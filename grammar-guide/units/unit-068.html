68

-ing clauses (He hurt his knee playing football.)

-ing li bo‘laklar (ravishdosh oborotlar)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • -ing clause (-ing li bo‘lak) nima ekanini: ega yo‘q, fe’l -ing shaklida, ega esa bosh gapdagi ega bilan bir xil.
  • Ikki ish bir vaqtda bo‘lganda: Sardor twisted his ankle playing basketball.
  • Ikki ish ketma-ket bo‘lganda: Taking off his shoes, Jasur walked in. va Having finished her report, Malika sent it to her boss.
  • Sabab ma’nosida: Feeling cold, I closed the window. va inkor shakl: Not knowing the way, ...
  • while, before, after, since, when dan keyin -ing: Be careful when using this knife.

Clause (bo‘lak, gap qismi) — o‘z fe’liga ega bo‘lgan gap qismi. -ing clauseda esa to‘liq fe’l (is playing, played) o‘rniga faqat fe’l + -ing turadi, ega aytilmaydi. Ega nima ekani bosh gapdan ma’lum: Sardor twisted his ankle playing basketball — basketbol o‘ynagan ham Sardor. Zamon ham bosh gapdan olinadi (o‘tgan zamon: hurt).

O‘zbek tilida bunday ma’noni ravishdosh beradi: -ib (eshikni ochib, kirdi), -ayotib (basketbol o‘ynayotib, oyog‘ini qayirib oldi), -gach (ishni tugatgach, uyga ketdi), -ganligi uchun / -gani sababli (charchaganligi uchun erta yotdi). Ingliz tilida bularning hammasi uchun asosiy vosita — -ing; ba’zan having + V3. Ammo har bir o‘zbekcha -ib inglizcha -ing bo‘lagiga to‘g‘ri kelavermaydi (pastdagi “Savol-javob”ga qarang).

Ikki ish bir vaqtda Two things happening at the same time

Bir kishi bir vaqtning o‘zida ikki ish qilsa, birinchisini oddiy fe’l bilan, ikkinchisini -ing bo‘lagi bilan aytish mumkin. Asosiy ish bosh gapda, hamroh ish -ing bo‘lagida beriladi:

Ikki gap → bitta gap
Ikki fe’l bilan-ing bo‘lagi bilan
Dilnoza was sitting in the garden and (she was) drinking tea.Dilnoza was sitting in the garden drinking tea.
Sardor twisted his ankle while he was playing basketball.Sardor twisted his ankle playing basketball.
A boy ran across the yard and (he) laughed.A boy ran across the yard laughing.
  • Aziz lay on the sofa watching TV.Aziz divanda yotib, televizor ko‘rdi.
  • Kamola cut her finger chopping onions.Kamola piyoz to‘g‘rayotib barmog‘ini kesib oldi.
  • Bekzod walked along the river listening to music.Bekzod musiqa tinglab, daryo bo‘ylab yurdi.
  • Don’t hurt your back lifting that heavy box.Anavi og‘ir qutini ko‘tarayotib belingizni shikastlab olmang.
  • She stood at the door waiting for her friend.U do‘sti kelishini kutib, eshik oldida turardi.

Qoida: -ing bo‘lagining egasi bosh gapning egasi bilan bir xil. Bosh gapdagi fe’l zamonni belgilaydi: -ing bo‘lagi o‘zi zamon bildirmaydi. He twisted (o‘tgan) ... playing; He sometimes twists ... playing (hozirgi).

-ing bo‘lagi gapning oldida ham kelishi mumkin

Odatda hamroh ish gapning oxirida turadi (He twisted his ankle playing basketball). Yozma, kitobiy uslubda -ing bo‘lagi boshda ham kelishi mumkin; bunda u vergul bilan ajratiladi:

  • Playing basketball, Sardor twisted his ankle.Basketbol o‘ynayotib, Sardor oyog‘ini qayirib oldi.
  • Walking along the street, I saw an accident.Ko‘cha bo‘ylab ketayotib, avariyani ko‘rdim.

Ma’no aniq bo‘lishi uchun -ing dan oldin while yoki when qo‘yish mumkin (D qismga qarang): Sardor twisted his ankle while playing basketball.

Ikki ish ketma-ket One action after another: -ing and having done

Ikki ish ketma-ket bo‘lsa, birinchi ish -ing bo‘lagida, ikkinchi ish bosh gapda beriladi. Bu uslub asosan hikoya va yozma nutqda uchraydi:

Ketma-ket ishlar
Ikki fe’l bilan-ing bo‘lagi bilan
Jasur took off his shoes and then walked into the room.Taking off his shoes, Jasur walked into the room.
Malika opened the envelope and took out a letter.Opening the envelope, Malika took out a letter.

Diqqat: bir vaqtda va ketma-ket ma’nolar bir xil shaklga ega, ma’noni mazmundan bilasiz. Walking along the street, I saw an accident — yurish ham, ko‘rish ham bir vaqtda. Taking off his shoes, Jasur walked in — avval yechdi, keyin kirdi. Ketma-ketlikda -ing bo‘lagidagi ish odatda qisqa va bosh gapdagi ishdan darhol oldin bo‘ladi.

having + V3: avval to‘liq tugagan ish

Birinchi ish ikkinchi boshlanishidan oldin tamom bo‘lgan bo‘lsa, ko‘pincha having + V3 (past participle) ishlatiladi. Bu ma’no jihatidan past perfectga yaqin: “avval qilib bo‘ldi, keyin boshqasini qildi”.

Having+V3+ ...,ega+asosiy fe’l
  • Having finished her report, Malika sent it to her boss.Hisobotini tugatgach, Malika uni rahbariga yubordi.
  • Having read the instructions, Bekzod started the machine.Yo‘riqnomani o‘qib chiqqach, Bekzod mashinani ishga tushirdi.
  • Having bought the tickets, we looked for our seats.Chiptalarni sotib olgach, o‘rinlarimizni qidirdik.
-ing yoki having + V3?
ShaklQachonMisol
V-ingbir vaqtda yoki qisqa ish darhol keyingisidan oldinOpening the window, she looked out.
having + V3birinchi ish to‘liq tugagan, ikkinchisi undan keyinHaving opened all the windows, she began to clean.

O‘zbekchada shu farq qo‘shimchalarda: -ib — ishlar bir-biriga yaqin yoki bir vaqtda (eshikni ochib, kirdi); -gach — birinchi ish tugagandan keyin (ishni tugatgach, ketdi). Inglizcha having finished ≈ o‘zbekcha tugatgach. Shuningdek, after finishing ham xuddi shu ma’noni beradi (D qism).

Sabab ma’nosi -ing clauses meaning “because”

-ing bo‘lagi sababni ham bildirishi mumkin. Bunday bo‘lak because / as / since bilan boshlanadigan gapga teng, lekin ancha ixcham va kitobiy:

Sabab ma’nosidagi -ing bo‘laklari
-ing bo‘lagi bilanSabab gap bilan
Feeling cold, I closed the window.Because I felt cold, I closed the window.
Being a nurse, Dilnoza knows what to do.As she is a nurse, Dilnoza knows what to do.
Not knowing the way, Nodira asked a policeman.Because she didn’t know the way, Nodira asked a policeman.
Having lost his keys, Aziz couldn’t open the door.Because he had lost his keys, Aziz couldn’t open the door.
  • Not wanting to be late, Jasur took a taxi.Kechikishni xohlamay, Jasur taksi oldi.
  • Being a student, Kamola gets a discount on train tickets.Talaba bo‘lgani uchun Kamola poezd chiptalariga chegirma oladi.
  • Knowing that the market closed at six, Sardor left work early.Bozor soat oltida yopilishini bilgani uchun Sardor ishdan erta chiqdi.
  • Having no money, we couldn’t buy anything.Pulimiz yo‘q bo‘lgani uchun hech narsa sotib ola olmadik.

Qoida: Inkor shaklda not -ing dan oldin turadi: Not knowing ✅ — Knowing not ❌, Feeling not well ❌. Sabab -ing bo‘lagidan keyin so yoki because qo‘shilmaydi: Not knowing the way, so I asked ❌.

Sabab ma’nosidagi -ing bo‘laklari ko‘pincha feel, know, realise, want, be, have kabi fe’llar bilan keladi va asosan yozma / rasmiy uslubga xos. Gapirganda odatda Because I felt cold ... yoki I felt cold, so ... deyiladi.

while, before, after, since, when + -ing Conjunctions with -ing clauses

Bog‘lovchi so‘zlardan keyin ham -ing bo‘lagi keladi. Bunda egani qayta aytish shart emas, chunki u bosh gapdagi ega bilan bir xil:

Bog‘lovchi + -ing
To‘liq gapQisqa shakl
I read the newspaper while I was waiting for the bus.I read the newspaper while waiting for the bus.
Be careful when you use this knife.Be careful when using this knife.
Check the windows before you leave.Check the windows before leaving.
We went for a walk after we finished the exam.We went for a walk after finishing the exam.
Kamola has made many friends since she started university.Kamola has made many friends since starting university.
  • Do you often sing while cooking?Ovqat pishirayotganda tez-tez qo‘shiq aytasizmi?
  • Before leaving the office, Malika switched off the lights.Idoradan chiqishdan oldin Malika chiroqlarni o‘chirdi.
  • After eating, we washed the dishes.Ovqatlangandan keyin idish-tovoqlarni yuvdik.
  • Take your passport when travelling abroad. (AmE: traveling)Chet elga sayohat qilayotganda pasportingizni oling.
  • On arriving at the airport, they went straight to the hotel.Aeroportga yetib kelishlari bilan ular to‘g‘ri mehmonxonaga ketishdi. (rasmiy)
-ing dan oldin keladigan so‘zlar:
whilewhenbeforeaftersinceon (rasmiy)withoutinstead of

O‘zbek tilida “ovqat pishirayotganda” (-ayotganda), “chiqishdan oldin” (-dan oldin), “ovqatlangandan keyin” (-gandan keyin) kabi shakllar bor. Inglizchada ularning o‘rnini while / before / after + -ing egallaydi. Bog‘lovchidan keyin bo‘sh (oddiy) fe’l ishlatmang: before to leave ❌, after finish ❌ — to‘g‘risi before leaving, after finishing (Unit 60).

Eng muhim cheklov: ega bir xil bo‘lishi shart The subject must be the same

-ing bo‘lagi o‘z egasiga ega emas, shuning uchun uni bosh gapning egasi bajarishi kerak. Aks holda gap kulgili yoki noto‘g‘ri chiqadi:

Tuzatish usullari
❌ Noto‘g‘ri✅ To‘g‘ri
Walking down the street, a dog attacked Aziz.Walking down the street, Aziz was attacked by a dog. / While Aziz was walking down the street, a dog attacked him.
Waiting for the bus, it started to rain.While I was waiting for the bus, it started to rain. / Waiting for the bus, I got wet when it started to rain.
Having finished the exam, the teacher collected the papers.When the students had finished the exam, the teacher collected the papers. (imtihonni tugatganlar — o‘quvchilar, o‘qituvchi emas)

Tekshirish: gapni o‘qing va o‘zingizdan so‘rang: “-ing ishini kim qilyapti?” Javob gapning egasi bo‘lishi kerak.

Tinish belgilari va -ing bilan maqsad farqi Commas and purpose

  • -ing bo‘lagi gap boshida kelsa, undan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi: Feeling cold, I closed the window.
  • Gapning oxirida yaqin bog‘liq hamroh ishdan oldin vergul shart emas (twisted his ankle playing basketball); qo‘shimcha axborot bersa, vergul qo‘yilishi mumkin: She sat there, waiting for the phone to ring.

-ing bo‘lagi

Ikkala ish ham sodir bo‘lgan; -ing — hamroh ish.

  • He ran out shouting. (qichqirib yugurdi)

to + fe’l (maqsad)

To-infinitive — maqsad (nima uchun?). Unit 64.

  • He ran out to shout at them. (ularga baqirish uchun yugurib chiqdi)

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Walking down the street, a dog attacked me.Walking down the street, I was attacked by a dog.-ing bo‘lagining egasi bosh gapning egasi bilan bir xil bo‘lishi kerak (ko‘chada yurgan odam — men, it emas).
Waiting for the bus, it started to rain.While I was waiting for the bus, it started to rain.Kutayotgan odam yomg‘ir emas. Egalar har xil bo‘lsa, to‘liq gap ishlating.
He twisted his ankle to play basketball.He twisted his ankle playing basketball.To + fe’l maqsadni bildiradi. “O‘ynayotib” ma’nosi uchun -ing kerak.
Not knowing the answer, so I asked my friend.Not knowing the answer, I asked my friend.-ing bo‘lagi o‘zi sababni bildiradi. so / because qo‘shish ortiqcha.
Finished my work, I went home.Having finished my work, I went home.Avval tugagan ish uchun having + V3 kerak. “Finished” yolg‘iz o‘zi bunday ishlatilmaydi.
Be careful when use this knife.Be careful when using this knife. / Be careful when you use this knife.when dan keyin yo -ing, yo ega + fe’l. Egasiz oddiy fe’l bo‘lmaydi.
Feeling not well, he stayed at home.Not feeling well, he stayed at home.Inkor not -ing dan oldin turadi.
After to finish the exam, we went out.After finishing the exam, we went out.Bog‘lovchi/predlogdan keyin to + fe’l emas, -ing keladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

-ing bo‘lagi qaysi zamonni bildiradi?

Hech qaysini — u zamonni bosh gapdan oladi. He twisted his ankle playing basketball (o‘tgan), He often twists his ankle playing basketball (hozirgi), He’ll twist his ankle playing basketball if he isn’t careful (kelasi). Faqat having + V3 shakli “avval tugagan ish” ma’nosini qo‘shimcha beradi.

“Playing basketball, he twisted his ankle” va “He twisted his ankle playing basketball” — farqi bormi?

Ma’no bir xil. Birinchisi boshida -ing bo‘lagi bo‘lgani uchun kitobiyroq eshitiladi. Og‘zaki nutqda ikkinchi variant ko‘proq ishlatiladi.

“Having done” va “after doing” — qaysi biri yaxshi?

Ma’no bir xil: Having finished the exam, we went out = After finishing the exam, we went out. After finishing kundalik nutqda keng tarqalgan, having finished esa yozma nutqda ko‘proq uchraydi.

Har bir o‘zbekcha “-ib” inglizcha -ing bo‘lagiga to‘g‘ri keladimi?

Yo‘q. Do‘konga borib, non sotib oldim degan gapni Going to the shop, I bought bread deb tarjima qilmang: ketish va sotib olish bir vaqtda emas, alohida ishlar. Bunday vaqtda and yoki then ishlating: I went to the shop and bought bread. -ing bo‘lagi ishlar bir vaqtda yoki bir-biriga juda yaqin bo‘lganda qulay.

Ega bir xil bo‘lmasa nima qilaman?

-ing bo‘lagini ishlatmang. Egasi bilan to‘liq ergash gap tuzing: While I was waiting for the bus, it started to rain. Yoki gapning egasini o‘zgartiring: Waiting for the bus, I felt the first drops of rain.

“Being tired” va “Because I was tired” — farqi nimada?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil. Being tired, I went to bed — ixchamroq va yozma uslubga yaqin. Because I was tired, I went to bed yoki I was tired, so I went to bed — kundalik nutqda tabiiyroq.

“He ran out shouting” va “He ran out to shout” orasidagi farq nima?

Birinchisida u yugurayotganda qichqirgan (ikki ish bir vaqtda). Ikkinchisida u qichqirish uchun yugurib chiqqan (maqsad, Unit 64).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • -ing clauseda ega yo‘q: egasi bosh gapning egasi, zamoni ham bosh gapdan olinadi.
  • Ikki ish bir vaqtda: He twisted his ankle playing basketball.
  • Ikki ish ketma-ket: Taking off his shoes, he walked in. Avval tugagan ish: Having + V3.
  • Sabab: Feeling cold, I closed the window. Inkor: Not feeling ... (so / because qo‘shilmaydi).
  • while, when, before, after, since dan keyin ham -ing: Be careful when using this knife.
  • Egalar har xil bo‘lsa, -ing bo‘lagi ishlatilmaydi: Walking down the street, a dog attacked me ❌.
  • -ing bo‘lagi (hamroh ish) ≠ to + fe’l (maqsad).
