Passive 1 (is done / was done)

Majhul nisbat — 1 (is done / was done)
Passive · Majhul nisbat (Passive)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Majhul nisbat (passive voice) nima ekanini va u aniq nisbat (active voice) dan qanday farq qilishini.
  • Passive shaklini: be + past participle (V3) — present simple da am / is / are + V3, past simple da was / were + V3.
  • Passive gapning inkor va so‘roq shakllarini (isn’t cleaned, Was it built?).
  • by bilan ishni bajaruvchini qachon aytish, qachon aytmaslikni.
  • Nima uchun passive ishlatilishini va nima uchun happen, die, arrive kabi fe’llardan passive yasab bo‘lmasligini.

Ikki gapni solishtiring: Malika cleans the room. va The room is cleaned. Birinchi gapda ishni bajaruvchi (Malika) — ega. Ikkinchi gapda esa ish ta’siriga uchragan narsa (the room) — ega. Ikkinchi turdagi gap passive (majhul nisbat) deyiladi: diqqat “kim qildi?” da emas, “nima bo‘ldi?” da.

O‘zbek tilida majhul nisbat fe’lga -il / -in (-n) qo‘shimchasi qo‘shilishi bilan yasaladi: yoz-il-di, qur-il-di, och-il-di, qil-in-di, tayyorla-n-di. Ingliz tilida fe’lga qo‘shimcha qo‘shilmaydi. Buning o‘rniga be fe’li va asosiy fe’lning V3 shakli (past participle) ishlatiladi: The house was built. Zamonni ham be ko‘rsatadi: is built (hozir) / was built (o‘tgan). O‘zbekcha “Bu uy 1990-yilda qurilgan” gapida yordamchi fe’l yo‘q, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar inglizchada ham be ni tushirib qoldiradi: ❌ This house built in 1990. Ingliz tilida be majburiy! Ishni bajaruvchi o‘zbekchada “…tomonidan” bilan, inglizchada by bilan beriladi: Malika tomonidan tozalandi = cleaned by Malika.

Aniq va majhul nisbat: shakli Active and passive: present simple and past simple

Bitta voqeani ikki xil aytish mumkin:

  • Active: Malika cleans the room every day.Malika xonani har kuni tozalaydi. (diqqat: Malikada)
  • Passive: The room is cleaned (by Malika) every day.Xona har kuni (Malika tomonidan) tozalanadi. (diqqat: xonada)

Aniq gapni majhul gapga aylantirish uch qadamdan iborat:

Qoida: (1) Aniq gapdagi to‘ldiruvchi (object) majhul gapda ega bo‘ladi. (2) Fe’l be + V3 ga aylanadi; be aniq gapdagi zamonni saqlaydi. (3) Aniq gapdagi ega by bilan oxiriga o‘tadi — lekin uni aytish shart emas (C bo‘limga qarang).

Ega (oldingi to‘ldiruvchi)+am / is / are / was / were+V3+(by + ish bajaruvchi)

Diqqat: be fe’li yangi egaga moslashadi, ish bajaruvchiga emas! Two cars were stolen (ega — two cars, ko‘plik). Ish bajaruvchi (by thieves) bu tanlovga ta’sir qilmaydi.

Qachon present simple, qachon past simple?

Present simple passive — takrorlanadigan ishlar, umumiy faktlar va jarayonlar uchun (Cotton is grown ...). Past simple passive — o‘tmishda tugagan voqea uchun (The bridge was opened last Friday.). Tanlov aniq nisbatdagi bilan bir xil.

Eng ko‘p xato: majhul so‘roq va inkorda do / did ishlatilmaydi. ❌ Did the window broken? — ✅ Was the window broken?   ❌ The room doesn’t cleaned. — ✅ The room isn’t cleaned.

Ish bajaruvchini so‘rash: Who ... by?

“Kim tomonidan?” deb so‘ramoqchi bo‘lsangiz, by gap oxirida qoladi. Rasmiyroq variant By whom ...? kamdan-kam ishlatiladi:

  • Who was this song written by?Bu qo‘shiqni kim yozgan?
  • By whom was this song written? (juda rasmiy)Bu qo‘shiq kim tomonidan yozilgan? (rasmiy)
  • Who wrote this song? (aniq nisbat — eng tabiiy)Bu qo‘shiqni kim yozgan?

O‘zbek tilida so‘roq yuklamasi -mi gap oxiridagi so‘zga qo‘shiladi: Xona tozalandimi? Inglizchada esa so‘roq so‘z tartibi bilan yasaladi: be egadan oldinga chiqadi: Was the room cleaned? “-mi” ga o‘xshash alohida so‘z yo‘q.

Savolning egasi o‘zi who / what / which bo‘lsa, so‘z tartibi darak gapdagidek qoladi: Who was invited? (kim taklif qilindi?), What was stolen? Bu haqda Unit 49 da (ega va to‘ldiruvchiga berilgan savollar) batafsil.

by va ish bajaruvchi; passive nima uchun kerak? Passive with by; why we use the passive

Majhul gapda ish bajaruvchini aytish shart emas. Ko‘pchilik passive gaplarda by iborasi umuman bo‘lmaydi. Uni faqat ish bajaruvchi haqidagi ma’lumot muhim yoki yangi bo‘lsa qo‘shamiz.

❌ by-iborasi kerak emas, tushirib qoldiring

  • Kim qilgani noma’lum: My phone was stolen. (kim o‘g‘irlagani — noma’lum)
  • Kim qilgani ma’lum / o‘z-o‘zidan tushunarli: The thief was arrested. (albatta, politsiya)
  • Umuman odamlar yoki ahamiyatsiz: English is spoken here.

✅ by-iborasini qo‘shing

  • Ma’lumot yangi va qiziq: “Hamlet” was written by Shakespeare.
  • Muallif, rassom, quruvchi (kim yaratgani muhim): This picture was painted by my sister.
  • Sabab (tabiat kuchi, hodisa): The roof was damaged by the storm.

Aniq gapda somebody, someone, they, people, you kabi noaniq ega bo‘lsa, passive da ular tushib qoladi: Somebody stole my bag → My bag was stolen (❌ by somebody demang).

  • The old fortress was destroyed by an earthquake.Qadimiy qal’a zilziladan vayron bo‘lgan (zilzila tomonidan vayron qilingan).
  • The bridge was opened on Friday. It was built by a local company.Ko‘prik juma kuni ochildi. U mahalliy kompaniya tomonidan qurilgan.
  • Two valuable carpets were taken from the museum last night.Kecha kechasi muzeydan ikkita qimmatbaho gilam olib ketildi.
  • These carpets are made by hand.Bu gilamlar qo‘lda to‘qiladi.

Faqat o‘timli fe’llardan passive yasaladi Only transitive verbs have a passive

Passive da aniq gapdagi to‘ldiruvchi ega bo‘ladi. Demak, passive faqat to‘ldiruvchi oladigan fe’llardan (o‘timli fe’l, transitive verb) yasaladi. To‘ldiruvchi olmaydigan fe’llar (o‘timsiz fe’l, intransitive verb) passive bo‘la olmaydi:

The train was arrived late.The train arrived late.Poyezd kechikib keldi.

Passive bo‘lmaydigan o‘timsiz fe’llar (eng ko‘p uchraydiganlari):

O‘zbek tilida ba’zi o‘timsiz fe’llardan ham majhul shakl yasash mumkin: kelindi, borildi, ishlandi, uxlanadi kabi. Inglizchada bunday imkon yo‘q. ❌ It was worked until midnight. deb bo‘lmaydi; ✅ We worked until midnight. yoki Work continued until midnight. deyiladi. Shuning uchun fe’lni tarjima qilishdan oldin o‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Bu fe’l biror narsaga (kimgadir) ta’sir qiladimi, ya’ni to‘ldiruvchisi bormi?”

Ba’zi o‘timli fe’llar ham passive bo‘lmaydi: have (egalik ma’nosida), resemble, suit, fit. I have a car → ❌ A car is had by me. She resembles her mother → ❌ Her mother is resembled by her.

Past participle (V3) shakllari Past participles you need for the passive

Passive da asosiy fe’l doim V3 shaklida turadi. Muntazam fe’llarda V3 = -ed (V2 bilan bir xil): clean → cleaned, paint → painted, invite → invited. Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarda V3 ko‘pincha V2 dan farq qiladi. Quyida passive da eng ko‘p uchraydigan fe’llar:

Muntazam fe’llarda imloga e’tibor bering: use → used (e + d), stop → stopped (undosh ikkilanadi), carry → carried (y → ied), lekin play → played (unlidan keyingi y o‘zgarmaydi). Britaniya ingliz tilida cancel → cancelled (AmE: canceled). -ed qo‘shishdagi imlo qoidalari Unit 5 da batafsil berilgan.

V2 va V3 ni almashtirib yubormang: ❌ The window was broke. — ✅ The window was broken.   ❌ The letter was wrote. — ✅ The letter was written.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

This house built in 1960.This house was built in 1960.“Be” fe’li (was) tushib qolgan. Majhul gapda u majburiy; zamonni ham u ko‘rsatadi. O‘zbekcha “qurilgan” da bunday yordamchi yo‘q, shuning uchun xato qilinadi.
The accident was happened yesterday.The accident happened yesterday.happen — o‘timsiz fe’l, passive bo‘lmaydi. (die, arrive, fall, come, go ham shunday.)
The window was broke.The window was broken.Passive da V2 emas, V3 (past participle) kerak: break – broke – broken.
Did the letter sent yesterday?Was the letter sent yesterday?Passive so‘roqda “did” emas, “be” fe’li egadan oldinga chiqadi.
English speaks in many countries.English is spoken in many countries.Ingliz tili o‘zi gapirmaydi — unda gapirishadi. Ega ish ta’sirini oladi, shuning uchun passive kerak.
Two cars was stolen.Two cars were stolen.“Be” yangi egaga (two cars — ko‘plik) moslashadi: were.
The book was written from Alisher Navoi.The book was written by Alisher Navoi.Ish bajaruvchi oldidan by ishlatiladi (“…tomonidan”), from emas.
My bag was stolen by somebody.My bag was stolen.Kim qilgani noma’lum bo‘lsa, by-iborasi qo‘shilmaydi (by somebody / by someone / by people ortiqcha).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Passive ni qachon, aniq nisbatni qachon ishlatish kerak?

Aniq nisbat — odatiy, kundalik tanlov: Malika wrote this email. Passive ni quyidagi hollarda tanlang: (1) ish bajaruvchi noma’lum yoki muhim emas (My phone was stolen); (2) ish yoki natija ish bajaruvchidan muhimroq (The road is closed); (3) rasmiy, ilmiy yoki yangiliklar uslubi (Visitors are asked to ...). Ish bajaruvchi muhim bo‘lsa, ko‘pincha aniq nisbat yaxshiroq. Hamma narsani passive qilish uslubni og‘irlashtiradi.

Passive gapda zamon qanday aniqlanadi?

Zamonni be fe’li ko‘rsatadi, V3 esa hamisha o‘zgarmaydi. Aniq gapda present simple bo‘lsa, passive da am / is / are; past simple bo‘lsa — was / were. They make cars here → Cars are made here. They made cars here → Cars were made here. Boshqa zamonlar (is being done, has been done, will be done) Unit 43 da.

by-iborasi har doim kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Inglizcha passive gaplarning ko‘pchiligida by-iborasi yo‘q. U faqat ish bajaruvchi haqidagi ma’lumot yangi, muhim yoki qiziq bo‘lsa qo‘shiladi (Hamlet was written by Shakespeare). by somebody / by people / by them deb aytish deyarli hech qachon kerak emas.

“The door opened” va “The door was opened” orasida qanday farq bor?

The door opened. — eshik ochildi (kim ochgani haqida gap yo‘q; o‘timsiz fe’l ishlatilgan). The door was opened. — eshikni kimdir ochdi (passive; ish bajaruvchi bor, lekin aytilmayapti). The shop opens at nine — “do‘kon soat to‘qqizda ochiladi” (jadval). Ba’zi fe’llar (open, close, break, start, stop) ikkala tuzilishda ham ishlatiladi, ma’nosi esa farq qiladi.

Nima uchun “was happened” deb bo‘lmaydi? Axir o‘zbekchada “sodir bo‘ldi” deymiz-ku.

Passive da to‘ldiruvchi ega bo‘ladi. happen fe’lining to‘ldiruvchisi yo‘q (“kimni sodir qildi?” deb so‘rab bo‘lmaydi), demak uni ega qilib ko‘tarish ham mumkin emas. Inglizcha happened — o‘zi to‘liq o‘tgan zamon shakli: What happened? Majhul shakl faqat kimdir biror narsani qilgan bo‘lsa mumkin.

V3 ni qayerdan bilaman? Hamma fe’lni yodlash shartmi?

Muntazam fe’llarda V3 = V1 + -ed, yodlash shart emas. Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarning V3 shaklini lug‘atdan o‘rganish kerak, lekin eng tez-tez uchraydiganlari kam (yuqoridagi jadvalga qarang). Har safar yangi noto‘g‘ri fe’lni uchratganda uchta shaklni birga yozib oling: steal – stole – stolen.

made of, made from, made in, made by — farqi nima?

made of — material o‘zgarmaydi, uni ko‘rib tanish mumkin: a table made of wood. made from — material boshqa narsaga aylanadi: cheese is made from milk. made in — joy: made in Uzbekistan. made by — ishlab chiqaruvchi yoki yaratuvchi: made by hand, made by Dilnoza.

“Who was it written by?” va “By whom was it written?” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham grammatik to‘g‘ri. Birinchisi kundalik nutqda, ikkinchisi juda rasmiy yozuvda uchraydi. Aslida eng tabiiy variant — aniq nisbat: Who wrote it? Ish bajaruvchini so‘rayotgan bo‘lsangiz, ko‘pincha passive kerak emas.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Passive: be + V3. Present simple da am / is / are + V3, past simple da was / were + V3. be yangi egaga moslashadi.
  • Aniq gapdagi to‘ldiruvchi passive da ega bo‘ladi; ish bajaruvchi kerak bo‘lsa, by bilan beriladi.
  • Inkor: isn’t / wasn’t + V3. So‘roq: Is / Was + ega + V3? — do / did ishlatilmaydi.
  • by faqat ish bajaruvchi haqidagi ma’lumot muhim yoki yangi bo‘lsa qo‘shiladi; by somebody / by people kerak emas.
  • Passive ishlatiladi: kim qilgani noma’lum yoki muhim emas; ish yoki natija muhimroq; umumiy fakt va jarayonlar; rasmiy uslub.
  • Faqat o‘timli fe’llardan passive yasaladi: ❌ was happened / was died / was arrived.
  • Passive da V3 ishlatiladi, V2 emas: broken, stolen, written, built.
