Present perfect continuous and simple (I have been doing and I have done)

Present perfect continuous va present perfect simple
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • I have been doing (present perfect continuous) va I have done (present perfect simple) farqini.
  • Continuous — harakatning o‘zi muhim (u tugaganmi-yo‘qmi, ahamiyatsiz); simple — tugagan natija muhim.
  • How long ...? → continuous; How much / How many / How many times ...? → simple.
  • Continuous shaklda kelmaydigan fe’llarni (know, like, believe ...) va istisnolarni (want, mean).

Ikkala zamon ham o‘tmish va hozirni bog‘laydi. Farq — nimaga e’tibor berayotganimizda. have been doing — “nima bilan band bo‘ldim, qancha vaqt” (jarayon). have done — “nima qilib qo‘ydim, nima natija bor” (natija).

🔄 have been doing — jarayon

Harakat davom etgan; tugagan bo‘lishi ham, tugamagan bo‘lishi ham mumkin. Biz uning davomiyligi va izlari haqida gapiramiz.

  • I’ve been painting the kitchen. (Ko‘ylagim bo‘yoq.)

✅ have done — natija

Harakat tugagan. Biz uning natijasi, qancha / necha marta qilingani haqida gapiramiz.

  • I’ve painted the kitchen. (Oshxona endi yangidek.)

O‘zbekchada bu farq ko‘pincha fe’l shakli bilan seziladi: oshxonani bo‘yab chiqdim / bo‘yab bo‘ldim (tugatdim, natija bor) → I’ve painted; ertalabdan beri oshxonani bo‘yayapman / bo‘yash bilan bandman (jarayon) → I’ve been painting. Agar o‘zbekchada -ib bo‘ldim, -ib tugatdim, -ib qo‘ydim deyish mumkin bo‘lsa — odatda present perfect simple kerak.

Jarayon yoki natija? I have been doing (activity) and I have done (completed action)

Ikki vaziyatni solishtiring. Bekzodning ko‘ylagi va qo‘llari bo‘yoq bo‘lib ketgan:

  • Bekzod has been painting the kitchen.Bekzod oshxonani bo‘yayapti / bo‘yash bilan band bo‘ldi.

has been painting — present perfect continuous. Bizni harakatning o‘zi qiziqtiradi. Ish tugaganmi yoki yo‘qmi — muhim emas. Bu misolda ish hali tugamagan bo‘lishi ham mumkin.

Endi oshxonaga qarang: devorlar yangidek yaltirab turibdi. Ish tugagan:

  • Bekzod has painted the kitchen.Bekzod oshxonani bo‘yab chiqdi. (ish tugadi, oshxona endi yangidek)

has painted — present perfect simple. Bu yerda muhimi — tugallangan ish va uning natijasi (bo‘yalgan oshxona), jarayon emas.

Taqqoslang
🔄 have been doing (jarayon, iz)✅ have done (tugagan ish, natija)
Jasur is hot and his face is red. He’s been digging in the garden.
Jasur terlab ketgan, yuzi qizargan. U bog‘da yer chopayotgan edi.		Look at the garden! Jasur has dug all the flower beds.
Bog‘ni qara! Jasur hamma gulzorlarni chopib chiqdi.
Nodira looks tired. She’s been cleaning the house all morning.
Nodira charchaganga o‘xshaydi. U ertalabdan beri uy tozalayapti.		The house looks great. Nodira has cleaned every room.
Uy ajoyib ko‘rinyapti. Nodira hamma xonani tozalab chiqdi.
I’ve been reading the book you lent me, but I haven’t finished it yet.
Bergan kitobingni o‘qiyapman, lekin hali tugatmadim.		I’ve read the book you lent me, so you can have it back now.
Bergan kitobingni o‘qib bo‘ldim, endi qaytarib olsang bo‘ladi.
Who’s been using my laptop? It’s still warm.
Noutbukimni kim ishlatgan? U hali ham issiq. (iz)		Who has used all the paper in the printer? There’s none left.
Printerdagi hamma qog‘ozni kim ishlatib qo‘ydi? Birorta ham qolmabdi. (natija)

Qoida: e’tibor jarayonga (nima bilan band bo‘ldim, izi qolgan) bo‘lsa — have been + -ing. E’tibor tugallangan ishga, natijaga bo‘lsa — have + V3.

Qisqa, bir lahzalik harakatlar (lose, break, find, start, stop, arrive, die) odatda continuous bo‘lmaydi — ularda “jarayon” yo‘q: I’ve lost my keys ✅ — I’ve been losing my keys (faqat “qayta-qayta yo‘qotib yuribman” ma’nosida). The film has started ✅.

How long? yoki How much / How many? Duration or quantity

Continuous — qancha vaqt (how long) davom etayotganini aytish uchun. Simple — qancha, nechta, necha marta (how much, how many, how many times) bajarilganini aytish uchun:

Davomiylik ↔ miqdor
⏳ How long? → have been + -ing🔢 How much / How many? → have + V3
How long have you been learning to play the dutar?
Qancha vaqtdan beri dutor chalishni o‘rganyapsan?		How many songs have you learnt (AmE: learned)?
Nechta kuy o‘rganding?
Nodira has been making somsa all morning.
Nodira ertalabdan beri somsa yopyapti.		She has made fifty somsa so far.
U hozircha ellikta somsa yopdi.
Jasur has been running since six o’clock this morning.
Jasur bugun ertalab soat oltidan beri yuguryapti.		He’s run fifteen kilometres so far.
U hozircha o‘n besh kilometr yugurdi.
I’ve been trying to call Aziz for an hour, but he isn’t answering.
Bir soatdan beri Azizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishga urinyapman, lekin u javob bermayapti.		I’ve called him five times.
Unga besh marta qo‘ng‘iroq qildim.

Qoida: gapda son yoki miqdor bo‘lsa (20 emails, three games, five times, a lot, how many) — present perfect simple: She’s written twenty emails ✅ — She’s been writing twenty emails ❌.

O‘zbekchada “Ertalabdan beri xat yozyapman” va “Bugun yigirmata xat yozdim” — har xil fe’l shakllari. Inglizchada ham farq bor: birinchisi I’ve been writing (jarayon, davomiylik), ikkinchisi I’ve written twenty (son, natija).

live, work, study kabi fe’llar bilan how long savoliga ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lishi mumkin: How long have you lived / been living here? Bu — Unit 11 mavzusi.

Continuous shaklda kelmaydigan fe’llar know, like, believe … (but want and mean)

Ba’zi fe’llar harakat emas, holatni bildiradi va odatda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi (ro‘yxat — Unit 4). Ular bilan “... dan beri” ma’nosida faqat present perfect simple ishlatiladi:

  • I’ve known Nodira since we were at school together. (not I’ve been knowing)Nodirani maktabda birga o‘qiganimizdan beri taniyman.
  • How long have you had this flat? (not have you been having)Bu kvartira sizda qachondan beri bor? (= qancha vaqtdan beri egasisiz?)
  • Aziz has always liked football. (not has been liking)Aziz har doim futbolni yaxshi ko‘rgan.
  • My parents have been married for thirty years. (not have been being)Ota-onam turmush qurganiga o‘ttiz yil bo‘ldi.
Odatda continuous bo‘lmaydigan fe’llar (holat fe’llari)
Bilish, fikrHis-tuyg‘uEgalik, boshqa
know, understand, believe, remember, realise (AmE: realize)like, love, hate, preferhave (egalik), own, belong, be, seem, need

Istisno: want va mean

want va mean (= niyat qilmoq) present perfect continuous da ishlatilishi mumkin. Ular ko‘pincha “ko‘pdan beri niyatim bor edi, lekin hali qilmadim” degan ma’noni beradi:

  • I’ve been meaning to fix that shelf for weeks.Haftalardan beri o‘sha javonni tuzatmoqchi bo‘lib yuribman.
  • I’ve been wanting to visit Khiva for years.Yillardan beri Xivaga borishni xohlab yuraman.

have faqat egalik ma’nosida continuous bo‘lmaydi. Harakat ma’nosida (have lessons, have problems, have a good time) continuous mumkin: I’ve been having driving lessons since March. (Martdan beri haydovchilik darslariga qatnayapman.) Batafsil — Unit 17.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’ve been reading a book” desam, kitobni tugatganmanmi?

Odatda yo‘q — bu gap jarayon haqida, ko‘pincha ish hali tugamagan. Agar tugatgan bo‘lsangiz: I’ve read the book (o‘qib bo‘ldim). Ish tugagan bo‘lsa ham continuous ishlatish mumkin — agar sizni natija emas, jarayon va uning izi qiziqtirsa: My eyes are tired. I’ve been reading.

Ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lgan holatlar bormi?

Ha. live, work, study, teach kabi fe’llar bilan for / since bo‘lganda: I’ve worked / I’ve been working here since 2020. Ma’no deyarli bir xil. Bu Unit 11 da batafsil.

“I’ve cleaned the room” va “I’ve been cleaning the room” — qachon qaysi biri?

Xonaga qarang: toza bo‘lsa va buni aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — I’ve cleaned (natija). O‘zingizga qarang: terlagan, charchagan bo‘lsangiz, nega charchaganingizni tushuntirmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — I’ve been cleaning (jarayon, iz).

Nega “I’ve been losing my keys” deyish g‘alati?

Chunki lose — bir lahzada sodir bo‘ladigan harakat, uning “davomiyligi” yo‘q. Bunday fe’llar (lose, break, find, arrive, start) odatda simple da keladi: I’ve lost my keys. Continuous faqat takrorlanish ma’nosida: I’ve been losing a lot of things lately (so‘nggi paytlarda ko‘p narsani yo‘qotib yuribman).

“want” holat fe’li-ku, nega “I’ve been wanting” to‘g‘ri?

Bu — istisno. want va mean present perfect continuous da “uzoq vaqtdan beri niyatim bor, lekin hali amalga oshirmadim” ma’nosini beradi: I’ve been meaning to tell you something. (Senga bir narsa aytmoqchi bo‘lib yurgandim.) Lekin present continuous da I’m wanting deyarli ishlatilmaydi.

“How long have you known him?” — bu yerda nega continuous emas?

Chunki know holat fe’li. How long bilan ham holat fe’llari simple da qoladi: How long have you known him? How long have you had this car? How long have you been here?

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Present perfect continuous (have been + -ing) yoki present perfect simple (have + V3)?

  1. You’re hot and out of breath. What (you / do)?
  2. I (lose) my keys. Have you seen them anywhere?
  3. Grandma (bake) bread all morning.
  4. Grandma (bake) twelve loaves this morning.
  5. I (read) the book you lent me, but I haven’t finished it yet.
  6. I (read) the book you lent me, so you can have it back now.
  7. Bekzod (know) Jasur since they were children.
  8. Somebody (eat) my sandwich! There’s nothing left.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi so‘zlardan savol tuzing.

  1. Aziz collects stamps. (how long / he / collect / them?) →
  2. (how many stamps / he / collect?) →
  3. Kamola and Nodira are picking apples. (how long / they / pick / apples?) →
  4. (how many kilos / they / pick?) →
  5. You are learning to drive. (how many lessons / you / have?) →

3-mashq. Vaziyatni o‘qing va gapni tugating. Sardor started painting the fence at 9 o’clock. It’s 12 o’clock now. He has finished half of it.

  1. He (paint) the fence for three hours.
  2. He (paint) half of the fence.
  3. He (use) three tins of paint so far.
  4. He’s tired because he (work) all morning.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. I’ve been drinking five glasses of water today. →
  2. How long have you been knowing Aziz? →
  3. Look! I’ve been finishing my project! →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Ertalabdan beri uy vazifasini qilyapman. →
  2. Men uy vazifamni qilib bo‘ldim. →
  3. Bu hafta uchta kitob o‘qidim. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • have been + -ing — jarayon, davomiylik, izi: I’ve been painting (my clothes are dirty). Ish tugagan bo‘lishi shart emas.
  • have + V3 — tugagan ish, natija: I’ve painted the kitchen (it looks new).
  • How long? → continuous; How much / How many / How many times? → simple.
  • Son va miqdor bilan doim simple: I’ve written ten emails.
  • Holat fe’llari (know, like, believe, have = egalik, be) → faqat simple: I’ve known him for years.
  • Istisno: want va mean: I’ve been meaning to call you.
  • Bir lahzalik harakatlar (lose, break, start) odatda simple: I’ve lost my keys.
