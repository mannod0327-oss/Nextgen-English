Relative clauses 2: clauses with and without who/that/which

Nisbiy gaplar — 2: who / that / which bilan va ularsiz
Relative clauses · Nisbiy ergash gaplar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • who / that / which ni nisbiy gapdan qachon tushirib qoldirish mumkin ekanini: faqat ular nisbiy gapning to‘ldiruvchisi (object) bo‘lsa — the film (that) we saw.
  • Ular nisbiy gapning egasi bo‘lsa, ularni hech qachon tushirib bo‘lmasligini: the girl who came.
  • Predlog nisbiy gapning oxirida qolishini: the man (who) I was talking to — va uni unutmaslikni.
  • everything, all, something, nothing va the first / the best … dan keyingi nisbiy gaplarni.
  • O‘zbekcha biz ko‘rgan film bilan inglizcha the film we saw ning o‘xshashligini va farqini.

Unit 92 da who / that / which nisbiy gapning egasi bo‘lgan hollarni ko‘rdingiz. Lekin bu so‘zlar ba’zan nisbiy gapning to‘ldiruvchisi ham bo‘ladi. Ana shunda ularni aytmasa ham bo‘ladi.

1. who / that / which = EGA

  • The woman who lives upstairs is a nurse.
  • who = she (u yashaydi)
  • Tushirib bo‘lmaydi ❌

2. who / that / which = TO‘LDIRUVCHI

  • The woman (who) I met is a nurse.
  • who = her (men uni uchratdim)
  • Tushirsa bo‘ladi ✅ — The woman I met

O‘zbek tilida nisbiy olmosh umuman yo‘q, shuning uchun men uchratgan ayol deganda biz hech narsani “tushirmaymiz”. Inglizchada to‘ldiruvchi vazifasidagi who / that / which ni tushirsak, gap aynan shu o‘zbekcha tuzilishga o‘xshab qoladi: men uchratgan ayol → the woman I met (faqat tartib teskari: gap otdan keyin keladi). Ega bo‘lsa, aksincha: o‘zbekchada yashaydigan qiz deymiz va ega ko‘rinmaydi, inglizchada esa ega who aytilishi shart: the girl who lives ….

Ega yoki to‘ldiruvchi: tushirib qoldirsa bo‘ladimi? Leaving out who / that / which

Nisbiy gap ham oddiy gap kabi qurilgan: unda ega (kim? nima?), fe’l va ba’zan to‘ldiruvchi (kimni? nimani?) bor. Who / that / which shu ega yoki to‘ldiruvchining o‘rnini bosadi. Qaysi vazifada ekanini bilish uchun nisbiy gapga qarang:

who / that / which ning ikki vazifasi
VazifasiQanday bilamiz?Tushirib bo‘ladimi?Misol
Ega (subject)who / that / which dan keyin darrov fe’l keladi❌ Yo‘qThe tram that stops near my school is always full.
Maktabim yonida to‘xtaydigan tramvay doim gavjum.
To‘ldiruvchi (object)who / that / which dan keyin yangi ega (I, we, Bekzod, the teacher …) va undan keyin fe’l keladi✅ HaThe tram (that) I take every day is always full.
Men har kuni minadigan tramvay doim gavjum.

Nega to‘ldiruvchini tushirsa bo‘ladi? Chunki gapda baribir ega ham, fe’l ham bor, ma’no aniq: the shoes Malika wore — “Malika kiygan tufli”. Ega bo‘lsa, who tushib qolsa fe’lning egasi yo‘qoladi va gap buziladi: the girl lives upstairs ❌.

Ega vazifasi: who / that / which majburiy

To‘ldiruvchi vazifasi: who / that / which ixtiyoriy

  • The shoes (that) Malika wore to the wedding were blue and gold.Malika to‘yga kiyib borgan tufli ko‘k va tillarang edi.
  • The cake (that / which) Nodira baked was delicious.Nodira yopgan tort juda mazali edi.
  • Where are the photos (that) you took in Khiva?Xivada olgan suratlaringiz qani?
  • The people (who / that) we met in Bukhara were very hospitable.Buxoroda tanishgan odamlarimiz juda mehmondo‘st edi.
  • The song (that) Jasur is singing is my favourite.Jasur kuylayotgan qo‘shiq mening sevimli qo‘shig‘im.
ot+(who / that / which)+yangi ega+fe’l+…

Qoida: nisbiy gapda who / that / which dan keyin darrov fe’l kelsa, ular ega — tushirib bo‘lmaydi. Ulardan keyin yangi ega (ot yoki olmosh) kelsa, ular to‘ldiruvchi — tushirib qoldirish mumkin (aytsangiz ham xato emas).

Ikki gapni birlashtiramiz

Ikkinchi gapdagi to‘ldiruvchi (him, her, it, them) ni qanday almashtirish mumkinligi:

him / her / it / them → who / that / which / hech narsa
Ikkinchi gapdaMumkin bo‘lgan variantlarMisol
him / her / them (odam)who / that / — (juda rasmiy: whom)The photographer was late. We hired him.
→ The photographer (who / that) we hired was late.
Biz yollagan fotograf kechikdi.
it / them (narsa)which / that / —The jacket is too small. I bought it last year.
→ The jacket (that / which) I bought last year is too small.
O‘tgan yili sotib olgan kurtkam juda kichik.

Diqqat: him / her / it / them ni hech qachon qoldirmang — ular who / that / which bilan almashadi, ular bilan birga kelmaydi: The jacket (that) I bought it last year … ❌. Ot jacket allaqachon gapning boshida turibdi.

Gap oxiridagi predlog Prepositions in relative clauses

Ba’zi fe’llar predlog bilan ishlatiladi: talk to, wait for, look for, stay at, listen to. Nisbiy gapda ham ular o‘z predlogini saqlaydi, va predlog nisbiy gapning oxirida qoladi:

Predlog qanday “qoladi”

  • The taxi (that) we were waiting for never came.Biz kutgan taksi hech kelmadi.
  • The colleague (who) I share an office with is very quiet.Men bilan bir xonada ishlaydigan hamkasbim juda kamgap.
  • Tashkent is the city (that) I grew up in.Toshkent — men katta bo‘lgan shahar.
  • The music (that) we listened to last night was relaxing.Kecha tinglagan musiqamiz odamni tinchlantirardi.
  • The classmate (who) I borrowed the notes from lives near me.Men konspektini so‘rab olgan sinfdoshim yaqin atrofda yashaydi.

O‘zbek tilida predlog yo‘q — uning vazifasini qo‘shimchalar va ko‘makchilar bajaradi (mehmonxonada, taksini kutmoq, hamkasb bilan). Sifatdosh gapda ham shunday: biz to‘xtagan mehmonxona — bu yerda “-da” ko‘rinmaydi, ma’no fe’lning o‘zidan chiqadi. Shuning uchun o‘zbek o‘quvchilari ko‘pincha predlogni tushirib qoldiradi: ❌ the hotel we stayed. Inglizchada esa predlog shart: the hotel we stayed at. Nisbiy gapni yozib bo‘lgach, o‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Bu fe’l predlog bilan keladimi?”

Predlog nisbiy olmoshdan oldin kelsa, faqat whom (odam) yoki which (narsa) ishlatiladi va olmoshni tushirib bo‘lmaydi. The man to who I was talking ❌, The man to that I was talking ❌ — The man to whom I was talking ✅. Bu shakl Unit 94 da yana ko‘riladi.

“Gap predlog bilan tugamaydi” — afsonami? Ha. Ingliz tilida nisbiy gapni predlog bilan tugatish mutlaqo to‘g‘ri va juda keng tarqalgan. Predlog bu yerda gapning emas, nisbiy gapning oxirida turibdi: The film we talked about was great. Rasmiy yozuvda esa about which shaklini ishlatish mumkin.

Joy ma’nosida where ham ishlatiladi: the hotel where we stayed. Bu holda at ni qaytadan qo‘shmang: ❌ the hotel where we stayed at. Buni Unit 94 da o‘rganamiz.

Everything, all, something, nothing … + nisbiy gap Relative clauses after everything, all, nothing …

Unit 92 da bilganingizdek, bu so‘zlardan keyin that ishlatiladi (what emas). To‘ldiruvchi vazifasida bu that ni ham tushirsa bo‘ladi:

Shunday so‘zlar:
everythingallanythingsomethingnothinglittlemuchthe first / the last / the only …the best / the funniest … (orttirma daraja)

✅ To‘ldiruvchi — that ni tushirsa bo‘ladi

  • Everything (that) Sardor told me was true.
  • Is there anything (that) you want to ask?
  • All (that) I want is a quiet evening.
  • Nothing (that) he said made any sense.
  • It was the funniest film (that) I have ever watched.

❌ Ega — that majburiy

  • Everything that is on the table is for the guests.
  • There is something that smells wonderful in the kitchen.
  • All that remains of the old fortress is one tower.
  • The only person who can open this door is the caretaker.
  • Everything Sardor told me was true.Sardor menga aytgan hamma narsa rost edi.
  • All I want is a quiet evening.Men xohlagan yagona narsa — tinch bir kechqurun.
  • Nothing he said made any sense.U aytgan hech narsa mantiqqa to‘g‘ri kelmasdi.
  • It was the funniest film I have ever watched.Bu men umrimda ko‘rgan eng kulgili film edi.
  • The first person I called was my brother.Men birinchi qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan odam akam bo‘ldi.

Eslatma: all, everything va nothing dan keyin what aytilmaydi: ❌ Everything what he said. Faqat oldida ot yo‘q bo‘lsa what ishlatiladi: What he said made no sense. (= the thing that he said).

Vaqt ma’nosidagi so‘zlardan keyin ham that ni tushirish mumkin: the first time (that) I met her, the last time (that) we spoke. Bu when ning o‘rniga ishlatilgan holat — batafsil Unit 94 da.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

who / that / which ni qanday qilib tez aniqlayman — ega yoki to‘ldiruvchi?

Olmoshdan keyingi so‘zga qarang. Agar darrov fe’l kelsa (who lives, that stops, which won) — olmosh ega. Agar yangi ega kelsa (that I take, who we met, which Nodira baked) — olmosh to‘ldiruvchi. Ikkinchi holatda uni tushirish mumkin. Faqat I think / I know kabi kiritmalar bo‘lsa (the man who I think lives …), olmosh baribir ega bo‘ladi va tushmaydi.

“Tushirsa bo‘ladi” degani “tushirish kerak” degani emasmi?

Yo‘q. Ikkala variant ham to‘g‘ri: the cake that Nodira baked ham, the cake Nodira baked ham. Kundalik nutqda tushirib qoldirish keng tarqalgan, chunki gap qisqaroq chiqadi. Rasmiy matnda yoki gap uzun bo‘lsa, olmoshni saqlash ma’noni aniqroq qiladi.

Gapni predlog bilan tugatish xato emasmi? Maktabda “bunday qilmang” deyishgan.

Nisbiy gapda — xato emas. The man I was talking to mutlaqo to‘g‘ri va juda tabiiy. “Gap predlog bilan tugamasin” — lotin tilidan olingan eski qoida, ingliz tili uchun u shart emas. Rasmiy yozuvda predlogni olmoshdan oldinga ko‘chirish mumkin: the man to whom I was talking.

Odam to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lsa who yoki whom? Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Qat’iy qoida bo‘yicha to‘ldiruvchi uchun whom kerak, lekin hozirgi ingliz tilida u asosan rasmiy yozuvda ishlatiladi. Kundalik nutqda who, that yoki olmosh umuman aytilmaydi: the man I met. Imtihonda ham “the man (who / that) I met” to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi. Predlog olmoshdan oldin kelsa, faqat whom mumkin (Unit 94).

O‘zbekchada ham “yashaydigan qiz” deymiz, egani aytmaymiz-ku. Nega inglizchada “the girl lives” bo‘lmaydi?

O‘zbek tilida fe’l sifatdosh shaklga o‘tadi (yasha-ydigan) va u otni bevosita aniqlaydi. Inglizchada esa nisbiy gap to‘liq gap bo‘lib qoladi, uning fe’li esa ega talab qiladi. Who / that / which ana shu ega vazifasini bajaradi. Shuning uchun the girl who lives deyiladi.

Vergulli gapda ham olmoshni tushirsa bo‘ladimi: “My uncle, I met yesterday, is a doctor”?

Yo‘q. Vergul bilan ajratilgan qo‘shimcha axborot gaplarida who / which ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi: My uncle, who I met yesterday, is a doctor. Bu qoidani Unit 95 da batafsil ko‘rasiz.

“The girl sitting next to me” da who is tushib qolgan. Bu ham shu qoidami?

Yo‘q, bu boshqa qoida. The girl who is sitting next to me → the girl sitting next to me: bunda olmosh va is birga tushadi, bunga faqat -ing / -ed shakli bor gaplarda ruxsat beriladi. Buni Unit 97 da o‘rganamiz. Bizning mavzumizda esa faqat to‘ldiruvchi olmoshi tushadi, fe’l qoladi.

The film (that) I saw — “that” ni tushirsam, ma’no o‘zgaradimi?

Yo‘q, ma’no aynan bir xil. Faqat uslub o‘zgaradi: that bor variant biroz to‘liqroq, that siz variant — qisqaroq va so‘zlashuvga yaqin.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • who / that / which dan keyin darrov fe’l kelsa — ular ega: tushirib bo‘lmaydi (the girl who lives).
  • Ulardan keyin yangi ega + fe’l kelsa — ular to‘ldiruvchi: tushirib qoldirish mumkin (the film (that) we saw).
  • Olmosh qoldirilganda ham, qoldirilmaganda ham him / her / it / them qayta yozilmaydi: ❌ the film I saw it.
  • Predlog nisbiy gap oxirida qoladi va uni tushirib bo‘lmaydi: the hotel we stayed at. Predlog oldinda bo‘lsa — faqat whom / which (rasmiy).
  • everything, all, nothing, the best … + to‘ldiruvchi that tushishi mumkin: Everything (that) he said.
  • Vergul bilan ajratilgan (qo‘shimcha axborot) gapda, whose da va predlog + whom / which da (to whom, in which) olmoshni tushirib bo‘lmaydi.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 92 (who / that / which — ega), Unit 94 (whose, whom, where), Unit 95 va Unit 96 (qo‘shimcha axborot gaplari), Unit 97 (-ing va -ed bo‘laklari), Unit 132 (fe’l + predlog: to va at).
