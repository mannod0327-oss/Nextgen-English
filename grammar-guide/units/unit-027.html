Could (do) and could have (done)

Could (do) va could have (done)
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • could + fe’l — hozir yoki kelajakda mumkin bo‘lgan ish: taklif, variant va taxmin (We could go to the cinema.).
  • could have + 3-shakl — o‘tmishda mumkin edi, lekin bo‘lmadi (You could have called me.).
  • couldn’t have + 3-shakl — o‘tmishda mumkin emas edi.
  • Nega could bu yerda “o‘tgan zamon” emas, vaqtni esa keyingi shakl belgilashini.
  • could have, should have va would have farqini.

Unit 26 da could o‘tmishdagi qobiliyat sifatida o‘rganilgan (I could swim when I was five). Endi uning boshqa vazifasi: imkoniyat. Bu yerda could — o‘tgan zamon emas! Vaqtni could dan keyingi qism ko‘rsatadi:

QolipVaqtMa’no
could + fe’lhozir / kelajakBuni qilish mumkin (imkoniyat, taklif)
could have + 3-shaklo‘tmishBuni qilish mumkin edi, lekin qilinmadi

O‘zbek tilida taklif “-sak bo‘ladi”, “-ishimiz mumkin” bilan beriladi: Kinoga borsak bo‘ladi = We could go to the cinema. O‘tmishdagi amalga oshmagan imkoniyat esa “-ishing mumkin edi”, “-sang bo‘lardi”: Menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilsang bo‘lardi = You could have phoned me. Xato ko‘p shundan chiqadi: o‘quvchi “mumkin edi” ni ko‘rib ❌ You could call me yesterday deydi. Ingliz tilida o‘tmishdagi imkoniyat uchun could have + 3-shakl kerak.

could dan keyin to kelmaydi va fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi: She could go ✅ — She could to go ❌, She could goes ❌.

could — real bo‘lmagan, xayoliy ishlar

Real bo‘lmagan, mubolag‘ali ishlar haqida faqat could ishlatiladi (can emas):

  • I’m so hungry I could eat a whole pot of plov! (not I can eat)Shunchalik ochmanki, bir qozon palovni yeb qo‘yishim mumkin!
  • This garden is so peaceful. I could sit here all day.Bu bog‘ juda osoyishta. Shu yerda kun bo‘yi o‘tirsam ham bo‘lardi.

can (umuman) va could (hozir/kelajakda, aniq holatda)

“Umuman olganda, ba’zan shunday bo‘ladi” degan ma’noda can ishlatiladi. Hozirgi yoki kelajakdagi aniq bir holat haqida “bo‘lishi mumkin” deganda esa could (can emas):

  • Winters in Tashkent can be very cold. (umuman)Toshkentda qish ba’zan juda sovuq bo‘ladi.
  • The sky is clear now, but it could change in the afternoon. (not it can change)Hozir osmon ochiq, lekin tushdan keyin o‘zgarishi mumkin.

Could ning ikki xil ma’nosini qanday ajratamiz?

Vaqtga qarang: o‘tmishni bildiruvchi so‘z bo‘lsa — Unit 26 dagi qobiliyat; hozir/kelajak bo‘lsa — bu unitdagi imkoniyat.

🕰️ O‘tmish: qobiliyat (Unit 26)

When I was young, I could run very fast.

Yoshligimda juda tez yugura olardim.

▶️ Hozir/kelajak: imkoniyat

Tomorrow we could run in the park.

Ertaga parkda yugursak bo‘ladi.

Could have + 3-shakl — o‘tmishdagi amalga oshmagan imkoniyat Could have (done): it was possible, but it didn’t happen

Biror ishni o‘tmishda qilish mumkin edi, lekin qilinmadi — shunda could have ishlatamiz. Gap odatda “aslida esa ...” degan ma’noni ham ichiga oladi:

Ega+could have+V3 (fe’lning 3-shakli)
  • We could have taken the train, but we took the bus.Poyezdda borishimiz mumkin edi, lekin avtobusda bordik.
  • Kamola could have been a doctor, but she became a painter.Kamola shifokor bo‘lishi mumkin edi, lekin rassom bo‘ldi.
  • I didn’t know you needed a lift. I could have taken you.Sizga mashina kerakligini bilmasdim. Sizni olib borishim mumkin edi.

1. Aybdorlik, ranjish: “qilsang bo‘lardi-ku!”

You could have ... ko‘pincha “nega qilmading?” degan ranjish yoki tanqidni bildiradi:

  • You could have told me you were late. I was so worried!Kechikayotganingni aytishing mumkin edi-ku. Juda xavotir olgandim!
  • Why did you shout at him? You could have asked him politely.Nega unga baqirding? Muloyimlik bilan so‘rashing mumkin edi.

2. Qutulib qolish: “bo‘lishi mumkin edi (lekin bo‘lmadi)”

  • The car skidded and hit a wall. The driver could have been killed.Mashina sirpanib, devorga urildi. Haydovchi halok bo‘lishi mumkin edi.
  • I nearly slept through the alarm. I could have missed my flight!Budilnikni eshitmay deyarli uxlab qolay dedim. Reysga kechikishim mumkin edi!

3. Shakl: inkor va so‘roq

Could have: shakllari
✅ Darak❌ Inkor❓ So‘roq
She could have come.She couldn’t have come. (C bo‘limiga qarang)Could she have come?

Og‘zaki nutqda could have qisqa aytiladi: could’ve /ˈkʊdəv/. Yozuvda esa ❌ could of yozmang! To‘g‘ri yozilishi — could have yoki could’ve. Fe’lning 3-shakli (V3) kerak: gone, taken, been, told, won (went, took, was emas).

Taxmin ma’nosi: o‘tmishda nima bo‘lgan bo‘lishi mumkin?

could have yana bir ma’noda ham keladi: nima bo‘lganini bilmasak, o‘tmish haqida taxmin qilamiz. Bu — might have ga yaqin (Unit 29, 30):

  • Where’s Kamola? She could have missed the bus. (= Maybe she missed it.)Kamola qani? U avtobusga ulgurmagan bo‘lishi mumkin.

couldn’t + fe’l (hozir) va “couldn’t be better”

couldn’t + asl fe’l hozir yoki kelajakda imkonsiz, tasavvur qilib bo‘lmaydigan ishni ham bildiradi. O‘tmish uchun — couldn’t have + V3:

  • I couldn’t work in an office all day. I’d go crazy.Men kun bo‘yi ofisda ishlay olmasdim. Aqldan ozardim.
  • My new job is great. It couldn’t be better!Yangi ishim zo‘r. Bundan yaxshisi bo‘lishi mumkin emas!
  • Dilnoza’s wedding was perfect — the food, the music, the weather. It couldn’t have been better.Dilnozaning to‘yi a’lo o‘tdi — taomlar ham, musiqa ham, havo ham. Bundan yaxshiroq bo‘lishi mumkin emas edi.

Qoida: Uchala qolipni ajrating: could + fe’l — mumkin (hozir/kelajak); could have + V3 — mumkin edi, lekin bo‘lmadi; couldn’t have + V3 — mumkin emas edi (yoki “iloji bo‘lmasdi”).

couldn’t + asl fe’l o‘tgan zamonda oddiy inkor ham bo‘lishi mumkin: I couldn’t open the door (eshikni ocholmadim) — bu Unit 26 dagi inkor qobiliyat. couldn’t have + V3 esa boshqa: I couldn’t have opened the door — “eshikni ochgan bo‘lishim mumkin emas” (masalan, kalit menda bo‘lmagan) yoki “eshikni ochishning iloji bo‘lmasdi”.

Could have, should have, would have Compare the three

Uchala qolip ham o‘tmishdagi “bo‘lmagan” narsa haqida, lekin ma’nolari har xil:

QolipMa’nosiMisol
could have + V3bu mumkin edi (imkoniyat bor edi)You could have called me. (Qo‘ng‘iroq qilishing mumkin edi.)
should have + V3bu kerak edi (qilish to‘g‘ri bo‘lardi)You should have called me. (Qo‘ng‘iroq qilishing kerak edi.)
would have + V3shunday bo‘lganida, bo‘lardi (xayoliy natija)If you had called, I would have come. (Qo‘ng‘iroq qilganingda, kelardim.)

should have haqida — Unit 33 da, would have haqida — Unit 36 da o‘rganasiz.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

You could to ask him.You could ask him.could modal fe’l: undan keyin to kelmaydi.
She could has come.She could have come.Modal fe’ldan keyin have (has emas), keyin V3.
We could of gone to the lake.We could have gone to the lake. / We could’ve gone ...Tovushi o‘xshash, lekin yozuvda of emas, have.
Yesterday you could call me.Yesterday you could have called me.O‘tmishdagi amalga oshmagan imkoniyat — could have + V3.
Tomorrow we could have visited Bukhara.Tomorrow we could visit Bukhara.Kelajakdagi imkoniyat — could + asl fe’l.
He could have went to the party.He could have gone to the party.could have dan keyin fe’lning 3-shakli (gone).
The driver could be killed, but luckily he wasn’t.The driver could have been killed, but luckily he wasn’t.Bo‘lmagan o‘tmish xavfi — could have been.
Could she came with us?Could she come with us?could dan keyin fe’lning asl shakli, o‘tgan zamon shakli emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Bu yerda could o‘tgan zamon emasmi? Nega “We could go” hozir/kelajak haqida?

Ha, shu ma’noda could o‘tmishni bildirmaydi. Ingliz tilida “muloyim, ehtiyotkor” gapirish uchun ko‘pincha o‘tgan zamon shakllaridan foydalaniladi (could, would, might). We could go — “borsak bo‘lardi (bir variant sifatida)”, We can go — “bora olamiz (aniq imkoniyat)”. O‘tmishni esa could have + V3 bildiradi.

“You could have called me” — qo‘ng‘iroq qildimi yoki yo‘qmi?

Qilmagan. could have + V3 da “mumkin edi, lekin bo‘lmadi” ma’nosi ichida. Agar qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan bo‘lsa, You called me yoki You managed to call me deyiladi. “U buni uddalay oldi” ma’nosi uchun could have ishlatilmaydi.

Could have (amalga oshmagan imkoniyat) bilan could have (taxmin) ni qanday farqlayman?

Kontekstga qarang: nima bo‘lganini bilsangiz (ish bajarilmagan), bu — amalga oshmagan imkoniyat (You could have helped me. You didn’t.). Bilmasangiz va sababni o‘ylab ko‘rayotgan bo‘lsangiz, bu — taxmin (She’s late. She could have missed the bus.) va might have bilan almashtirish mumkin.

Could have va should have farqi nima?

could have — “mumkin edi” (imkoniyat bor edi, lekin majburiyat yo‘q). should have — “kerak edi, to‘g‘ri bo‘lardi” (xatoni tan olish yoki tanqid). Masalan: You could have waited (kutsang bo‘lardi) va You should have waited (kutishing kerak edi).

Could have bilan savol qanday tuziladi?

Could + ega + have + V3: Could he have forgotten the key? (U kalitni unutgan bo‘lishi mumkinmi?) Bunday savol odatda taxmin haqida. Amalga oshmagan imkoniyat haqida ko‘pincha to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri gap aytiladi, savol emas.

Couldn’t have bilan can’t have bir xilmi?

“Aqlga sig‘maydi” ma’nosida — ha: It couldn’t have been Bekzod = It can’t have been Bekzod (Unit 28). Lekin “bunga iloji bo‘lmasdi” ma’nosida (I couldn’t have done it without you) faqat couldn’t have ishlatiladi.

“Could” bilan “was able to” bu ma’noda almashadimi?

Yo‘q. Imkoniyat/taklif uchun faqat could (We could go); We were able to go bo‘lib o‘tgan voqeani bildiradi. O‘tmishdagi amalga oshmagan imkoniyatni esa We could have gone yoki We would have been able to go deyish mumkin.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. could + fe’l bilan taklif yozing.

  1. “Where shall we eat?” (go / the new café) → We
  2. “I don’t know what to give Malika for her birthday.” (buy / a book) → You
  3. “Our bus is late.” (take / a taxi) → We
  4. “I need some advice.” (ask / your teacher) → You
  5. “What shall we do on Saturday?” (visit / the museum) → We

2-mashq. could have + fe’lning 3-shakli bilan to‘ldiring (amalga oshmagan imkoniyat).

  1. Why didn’t you phone me? I (come) and pick you up.
  2. Bekzod (win) the race, but he fell.
  3. We (fly) to Urgench, but we went by train.
  4. The taxi nearly hit her. She (be) killed.
  5. I didn’t know you needed a dictionary. I (lend) you mine.
  6. You (tell) me the truth!

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri qolipni yozing: could + fe’l, could have + V3 yoki couldn’t have + V3.

  1. Tomorrow we (go) to the lake if it’s warm.
  2. Jasur was in hospital on Sunday, so he (be) at the party.
  3. Without your help I (finish) the project.
  4. The two cakes were equally good. Either of them (win) the competition.
  5. I’m so tired that I (sleep) for a week!
  6. Our holiday in Khiva was perfect. It (be) better.
  7. I have no plans for Friday. We (meet) for lunch.
  8. Yesterday you (call) me, but you didn’t.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

  1. You could to ask me. →
  2. She could of come. →
  3. Yesterday I could help you, but you didn’t ask. →
  4. Next week we could have visited Bukhara. →
  5. He could have went to the party. →
  6. They could have walk home. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Bugun kechqurun kinoga borsak bo‘ladi. →
  2. Sen menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishing mumkin edi. →
  3. U (erkak) halok bo‘lishi mumkin edi. →
  4. Biz poyezdda borishimiz mumkin edi, lekin avtobusda bordik. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • could + fe’l (hozir/kelajak): imkoniyat, taklif, variant. We could go to the cinema.
  • could have + V3 (o‘tmish): mumkin edi, lekin bo‘lmadi. You could have called me.
  • could have taxmin ma’nosida ham keladi (nima bo‘lganini bilmaymiz): She could have missed the bus.
  • couldn’t have + V3: bo‘lishi mumkin emas edi; yoki “... bo‘lmasa, uddalay olmasdim”.
  • Xayoliy ish uchun faqat could: I could eat a whole pot of plov! couldn’t + fe’l — hozir imkonsiz: I couldn’t work in an office. It couldn’t be better.
  • Yozuvda could have yoki could’ve — could of ❌. could dan keyin to yo‘q, fe’lga -s yo‘q.
  • Qiyoslash: could have = mumkin edi; should have = kerak edi; would have = shunday bo‘lganda bo‘lardi.
