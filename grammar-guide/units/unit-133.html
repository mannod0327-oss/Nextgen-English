Verb + preposition 2: about/for/of/after

Fe’l + predlog — 2: about / for / of / after
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • about bilan keladigan fe’llar: talk about, read about, know about, hear about va do something about (“muammoga qarshi biror chora ko‘rmoq”).
  • for bilan keladigan fe’llar: ask (sb) for, apply (to sb) for, wait for, search (a place) for, leave (a place) for.
  • of va after bilan keladigan birikmalar: take care of, care about, care for, look after — hamda look for bilan look after orasidagi katta farq.
  • ask for (nimanidir so‘ramoq, iltimos qilmoq) bilan ask a question (savol bermoq) nega har xil tuzilishini.
  • O‘zbekcha -ni, -ga, -dan qo‘shimchalari qachon to‘g‘ri yo‘l ko‘rsatishini va qachon xatoga olib kelishini.

Oldingi darsda (Unit 132) listen to, look at kabi fe’llarni o‘rgandingiz. Bu darsda ham xuddi shu g‘oya davom etadi: ba’zi fe’llar o‘zidan keyin aniq bir predlog talab qiladi. Predlogni mantiq bilan topib bo‘lmaydi, shuning uchun fe’lni predlogi bilan birga, bitta “juftlik” sifatida yodlaymiz: wait for, look after, talk about.

O‘zbek tilida “kimni? nimani?” degan savolga javob beruvchi so‘z odatda -ni bilan keladi: avtobusni kutmoq, kalitni qidirmoq. Inglizchada esa shu o‘rinda ko‘pincha predlog bor: wait for the bus, look for the keys. Shuning uchun “-ni” ni predlog bilan almashtirishni unutib, ❌ I’m waiting the bus deb yuborish juda keng tarqalgan xato. Quyidagi jadvalda o‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha qayerda yordam berishini, qayerda aldashini ko‘ring.

About: gaplashmoq, o‘qimoq, bilmoq, eshitmoq talk / read / know / hear about; do something about

Biror mavzu haqida gapirish, o‘qish, bilish yoki eshitish haqida gap ketganda, fe’ldan keyin about keladi. O‘zbekchadagi “haqida” so‘zi bu yerda aniq yo‘l ko‘rsatadi: haqida = about.

about dan keyin ot, olmosh yoki -ing shakli keladi (to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri fe’l emas): We talked about going to Khiva in spring. — Biz bahorda Xivaga borish haqida gaplashdik. (Unit 60.) about dan keyin yana bir predlog qo‘shilmaydi: ❌ talk about of the exam → ✅ talk about the exam.

know va know about

know sb / sth

Shaxsan tanish bo‘lmoq, tajribadan bilmoq.

  • I know Kamola. We study together. (Kamolani taniyman. Biz birga o‘qiymiz.)
  • I know Chorsu bazaar very well. (Chorsu bozorini yaxshi bilaman.)

know about sth

Haqida ma’lumotga ega bo‘lmoq (o‘zingiz ko‘rmagan bo‘lishingiz mumkin).

  • I know about the Eiffel Tower, but I have never seen it. (Eyfel minorasi haqida bilaman, lekin uni hech ko‘rmaganman.)
  • Does your mother know about the exam? (Onangiz imtihon haqida biladimi?)

hear va hear about

hear (predlogsiz) — ovoz qulog‘ingizga o‘zi kirishi: I heard a strange noise. (G‘alati shovqin eshitdim.) hear about — voqea yoki yangilik haqida xabar topish: I heard about the accident on the radio. (Avariya haqida radiodan eshitdim.) Bu fe’lning of va from bilan kelishi Unit 134 da.

do something about — chora ko‘rmoq

Biror muammo bor va siz uni hal qilish uchun nimadir qilmoqchisiz (yoki hech narsa qila olmaysiz) — shunda do something about ishlatiladi:

do+something / nothing / anything / a lot+about+muammo
  • The street light has been broken for a month. Somebody should do something about it.Ko‘cha chirog‘i bir oydan beri buzuq. Kimdir bu haqda biror chora ko‘rishi kerak.
  • What are you going to do about your slow internet?Sekin internetingiz bilan nima qilmoqchisiz (qanday chora ko‘rasiz)?
  • It’s raining again, but there is nothing we can do about it.Yana yomg‘ir yog‘yapti, lekin bu haqda qo‘limizdan hech narsa kelmaydi.
  • The students complained about the rubbish, so the school finally did something about it.O‘quvchilar axlat haqida shikoyat qilishdi, shunda maktab nihoyat chora ko‘rdi.

do something about ≠ do something with. What did you do with my pen? — “Qalamimni nima qilding?” (ya’ni “qayerga qo‘yding?”). What did you do about the problem? — “Muammoga qarshi nima chora ko‘rding?”

For: so‘ramoq, ariza bermoq, kutmoq, qidirmoq, jo‘nab ketmoq ask for, apply for, wait for, search for, leave for

ask for, ask about va ask + savol

Inglizchada ask uch xil tuzilmada keladi, o‘zbekcha esa hammasi uchun “so‘ramoq” deydi:

Bir xil so‘ramoq degani uchun ask for a question deyish xato: savol beriladi, u talab qilinmaydi. To‘g‘risi: ask a question. Aksincha, I asked him a pen ham xato: qalamni iltimos qilgan bo‘lsangiz — I asked him for a pen.

apply (to sb) for sth — ariza bermoq

apply for — biror narsani olish uchun rasmiy ariza yuborish (ish, viza, grant, o‘quv joyi). Kimga ariza berilsa, uning oldidan to keladi (tashkilot yoki shaxs).

apply+to tashkilot+for nima olish uchun
  • Nodira applied for a scholarship to study in Seoul.Nodira Seulda o‘qish uchun grantga ariza berdi.
  • Sardor applied to three universities for a place on the engineering course.Sardor muhandislik yo‘nalishidagi o‘rin uchun uchta universitetga ariza berdi.
  • You must apply to the embassy for a visa at least a month before the trip.Safardan kamida bir oy oldin elchixonaga viza uchun ariza berishingiz kerak.
  • Kamola applied for a job at a bank in Tashkent.Kamola Toshkentdagi bankka ishga ariza berdi.

Tartibni almashtirish mumkin: apply to the embassy for a visa = apply for a visa at the embassy. Ot shakli: an application for a job, an applicant. apply to yana bitta ma’noga ega — “taalluqli bo‘lmoq”: This rule applies to all students. (Bu qoida barcha o‘quvchilarga tegishli.)

wait for — kutmoq

wait dan keyin kutilayotgan kishi yoki narsa oldidan for keladi. Kutilayotgan voqea esa wait for + kim + to + fe’l tuzilmasi bilan ifodalanadi:

wait+for+kim / nima(+to+fe’l)
  • We waited for Jasur for an hour, but he never came.Biz Jasurni bir soat kutdik, lekin u kelmadi.
  • We’re waiting for the shop to open.Do‘kon ochilishini kutyapmiz.
  • Please wait for me to finish.Iltimos, men tugatishimni kuting.
  • Wait until the water boils, then add the rice.Suv qaynaguncha kuting, keyin guruchni soling.

for dan keyin to‘liq gap (ega + kesim) kelmaydi. “…bo‘lguncha / …gacha kutmoq” ma’nosida wait until / till + gap ishlatiladi: ✅ wait until he comes — ❌ wait for he comes. Predlogsiz wait ham bor: Wait a minute! (Bir daqiqa kuting!), I can’t wait to see you. (Sizni ko‘rishni sabrsizlik bilan kutyapman.)

search (a place) for sth — qidirmoq

Biror joyni diqqat bilan, sinchiklab ko‘zdan kechirmoq uchun search ishlatiladi. Joy yoki odam predlogsiz keladi, qidirilayotgan narsa esa for dan keyin aytiladi.

  • I searched all my pockets for the ticket.Chiptani topish uchun hamma cho‘ntaklarimni qidirib chiqdim.
  • The police searched the forest for the missing boy.Politsiya yo‘qolgan bolani topish uchun o‘rmonni qidirdi.
  • The guard searched every passenger for knives.Qorovul pichoq bor-yo‘qligini bilish uchun har bir yo‘lovchini tintuv qildi.
  • Aziz is searching for a cheap flat near the university.Aziz universitet yaqinidan arzon kvartira qidiryapti.

Joy aytilmasa, search for degan oddiy shakl ishlatiladi: She searched for information online. Kitobiy uslubda in search of ham uchraydi: Many people moved to the city in search of work. (Ko‘p odamlar ish izlab shaharga ko‘chib o‘tdi.)

leave (a place) for … — jo‘nab ketmoq

  • We are leaving Tashkent for Bukhara tomorrow morning.Ertaga ertalab Toshkentdan Buxoroga jo‘nab ketamiz.
  • Bekzod leaves for work at seven o’clock.Bekzod soat yettida ishga ketadi.
  • The plane left for Istanbul ten minutes late.Samolyot Istanbulga o‘n daqiqa kechikib uchdi.

leave + joy

Joyni tark etmoq. Predlog yo‘q.

  • She left the office at six.
  • (Ofisdan chiqdi.)

leave for + manzil

Shu tomonga qarab yo‘lga chiqmoq. Yetib borgani aytilmaydi.

  • She left for the office at six.
  • (Ofisga qarab yo‘lga tushdi.)

leave for “manzil tomon yo‘lga chiqdi”, go to esa “bordi” degani (Unit 126). Transport qayerdan jo‘nashini aytganda from ham ishlatiladi: The train to Fergana leaves from platform 3. (Poyezd 3-platformadan jo‘naydi.)

O‘zbekcha uch xil qo‘shimcha — avtobusni kutmoq, undan pul so‘ramoq, ishga ariza bermoq — inglizchada bitta for bilan beriladi: wait for, ask him for, apply for. Aksincha, Toshkentdan ketmoq deganda “-dan” inglizchada ifodalanmaydi: leave Tashkent. Demak, qo‘shimchani so‘zma-so‘z o‘girmang — butun birikmani yodlang.

Of va after: g‘amxo‘rlik va qidiruv take care of, care about, care for; look for, look after

take care of — qarab turmoq, hal qilmoq

take care of — birovga yoki biror narsaga qarab turmoq (look after bilan bir xil ma’no) yoki biror ishni o‘z zimmangizga olib hal qilmoq.

  • Who is taking care of your garden while you’re away?Siz yo‘qligingizda bog‘ingizga kim qarayapti?
  • You book the hotel, and I’ll take care of the train tickets.Siz mehmonxonani band qiling, poyezd chiptalarini esa o‘zim hal qilaman.
  • You must take care of yourself and eat properly.O‘zingizga qarashingiz va to‘g‘ri ovqatlanishingiz kerak.

Alohida: Take care! (of siz) — xayrlashayotganda “Ehtiyot bo‘ling! / Salomat bo‘ling!” deganidir: Bye, take care!

care about va care for

care about sb / sth

Muhim deb bilmoq, qayg‘urmoq, e’tibor bermoq.

  • Sardor cares about nature; he plants trees every spring.
  • I don’t care about the score.
  • Who cares what he thinks?

care for sb (qarash)

Qarab turmoq (ko‘proq rasmiy yoki kasbiy uslub). Yana: yaxshi ko‘rmoq.

  • Nurses care for the sick.
  • He cares for his mother very much. (qaraydi / yaxshi ko‘radi)

care for sth (yoqtirmoq)

Asosan inkor, savol yoki xushmuomala taklifda.

  • I don’t care for loud music.
  • Would you care for some more soup?
  • Dilnoza really cares about her students’ progress.Dilnoza o‘quvchilarining o‘sishiga chin dildan qayg‘uradi.
  • Our grandfather’s nurse cares for him day and night.Bobomizning hamshirasi unga kechayu kunduz qaraydi.
  • I don’t really care for fast food.Men tez tayyorlanadigan ovqatni unchalik yoqtirmayman.
  • Would you care for a piece of cake?Bir bo‘lak tort olasizmi? (xushmuomala taklif)
  • I couldn’t care less about the result.Natija menga mutlaqo baribir.

“Qarab turmoq” ma’nosida kundalik nutqda look after va take care of tabiiyroq; care for kasbiy (hamshira, tarbiyachi) yoki biroz ko‘tarinki uslubda uchraydi. Imtihon va suhbatda eng kerakligi: take care of, look after, care about, hamda taklifdagi Would you care for…?

look for va look after

Ikki ibora bir-biriga juda o‘xshaydi, ma’nosi esa butunlay boshqa. O‘zbekchada “-ga qaramoq” ham “biror joyga qarash”, ham “birovga qarab turish” degani, shu sababli ayniqsa chalkashtiriladi.

look at (Unit 132)

Ko‘zni qaratmoq.

  • Look at the map.

look for

Topishga urinmoq = qidirmoq.

  • She is looking for a new flat.
  • I’ve been looking for you everywhere!

look after

G‘amxo‘rlik qilmoq = take care of.

  • He looks after his two younger brothers.
  • Look after yourself!
  • Sardor lost his wallet, so he is looking for it in the car.Sardor hamyonini yo‘qotdi, shuning uchun uni mashinadan qidiryapti.
  • Who is going to look after the children while you are at work?Siz ishda bo‘lganingizda bolalarga kim qaraydi?
  • Bekzod looks after his grandfather every weekend.Bekzod har dam olish kuni bobosiga qaraydi.
  • Nargiza has been looking for a job since June.Nargiza iyundan beri ish qidiryapti.

look for umumiy, kundalik “qidirmoq”; search (for) esa sinchiklab, tizimli qidirishni bildiradi. look after Britaniya ingliz tilida ko‘proq, Amerika ingliz tilida esa take care of ko‘proq uchraydi, lekin ikkala variant ham tushuniladi.

I’m looking my phone.I’m looking for my phone.“Qidirmoq” = look for. Look at ko‘z tashlash, look for esa topishga urinish.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

wait for, wait until, expect va await orasida qanday farq bor?

wait for + kishi/narsa: kutib turish, vaqt o‘tkazish (wait for the bus). wait until + gap: biror voqea sodir bo‘lguncha kutish (wait until the bus comes). expect — biror narsa bo‘lishini taxmin qilish, o‘tirib kutish shart emas (I expect it will rain). await — rasmiy uslubda wait for ning o‘rniga, predlogsiz: We await your reply. (Javobingizni kutamiz.)

ask for, ask about va ask a question ni qanday farqlayman?

Savol bering: “Men nimanidir so‘rayapmanmi (olish uchun)?” — ask for. “Biror mavzu haqida ma’lumot so‘rayapmanmi?” — ask about. “Shunchaki savol (vaqt, narx, yo‘l) beryapmanmi?” — ask + savol, predlogsiz. Taqqoslang: ask for the price (narxni aytishlarini so‘rash) va ask the price (“bu qancha?” deb so‘rash) — ikkalasi ham mumkin, ma’nosi deyarli bir xil.

look for va search for bir xilmi?

Ma’no yaqin: ikkalasida ham biror narsani topishga urinasiz. look for kundalik va oddiy. search esa ko‘proq tizimli, sinchiklab qidirishni bildiradi va joy bilan birga ishlatiladi: search the drawer for the scissors. Joy bilan look for ishlatsangiz: look for the scissors in the drawer.

care for ni qachon ishlataman? U qanday ma’nolarga ega?

Uch ma’no: (1) qarab turmoq, asosan rasmiy yoki kasbiy uslubda: care for the elderly; (2) yoqtirmoq, asosan inkor yoki savolda: I don’t care for coffee; (3) xushmuomala taklif: Would you care for some tea? Dars yoki imtihonda eng ko‘p kerak bo‘ladigani — take care of, look after va care about; care for ni asosan taklif (Would you care for…?) va don’t care for shaklida yodlab oling.

“Take care!” deganda “of” kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Take care! — xayrlashuv iborasi: “Ehtiyot bo‘ling! / Salomat bo‘ling!” of faqat kimga yoki nimaga g‘amxo‘rlik qilinishi aytilganda keladi: Take care of your health. Xuddi shunday: Look after yourself = Take care of yourself.

leave for va go to bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. go to Bukhara — “Buxoroga bormoq” (yetib borishi mumkin). leave for Bukhara — “Buxoro tomonga jo‘nab ketmoq”: gap yo‘lga chiqish haqida, yetib borgani aytilmaydi. Shuning uchun He left for Bukhara but never arrived (U Buxoroga jo‘nab ketdi, lekin yetib bormadi) degan gap mantiqan to‘g‘ri.

know about bilan know ning farqi nimada?

know + shaxs/joy — tanish bo‘lish, yaxshi bilish: I know Kamola. know about + narsa — haqida ma’lumotga ega bo‘lish: I know about the plan. Birinchisida shaxsiy tajriba yoki tanishlik bor, ikkinchisida faqat axborot.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • about (haqida): talk, read, know, hear, tell, write, ask about; do something about = muammoga qarshi chora ko‘rmoq (do with emas).
  • for: wait for the bus (❌ wait the bus), look for / search for, ask sb for sth, apply (to sb) for sth, leave (a place) for (a place).
  • ask: ask for = iltimos qilmoq; ask about = mavzu haqida so‘ramoq; ask a question = predlogsiz.
  • search: search the bag for the keys — joy predlogsiz, qidirilayotgan narsa for dan keyin.
  • look at = qarash; look for = qidirish; look after = qarab turish (= take care of).
  • care about = qayg‘urmoq, muhim deb bilmoq; care for = qarab turmoq yoki yoqtirmoq (Would you care for…?).
  • “…gacha kutmoq” — wait until + gap; wait for + kim + to + fe’l.
  • Predlogdan keyin ot yoki -ing keladi: talk about going.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 55 (ask sb to do), Unit 60 (predlog + -ing), Unit 62 (fe’l + predlog + -ing), Unit 126 (to / at / in / into), Unit 132 (look at, talk to), Unit 134 (hear of / hear from, think about / of), Unit 137 (frazali fe’llar).
