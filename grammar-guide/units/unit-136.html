Verb + preposition 5: in/into/with/to/on

Fe’l + predlog — 5: in / into / with / to / on
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • in bilan keladigan fe’llar: believe in, specialise in, succeed in (doing).
  • into bilan keladigan fe’llar: crash / drive / run / bump / break into; divide / cut / split into; translate from … into ….
  • with bilan keladigan fe’llar: collide with, fill … with, provide / supply sb with sth.
  • to bilan keladigan fe’llar: happen to (ikki ma’nosi) va prefer X to Y.
  • on bilan keladigan fe’llar: spend / waste money (time) on, concentrate on, insist on (doing).
  • O‘zbekcha qo‘shimchalar (-ga ishonmoq, -ga ixtisoslashmoq, -ga sarflamoq, -ga diqqatni qaratmoq, -ni -ga tarjima qilmoq) qayerda to‘g‘ri yo‘l ko‘rsatishini va qayerda xatoga olib borishini.

Oldingi darslarda (Unit 132–135) fe’l va predlogning “juftligi” haqida gaplashdik. Bu darsda yana beshta predlog — in, into, with, to, on — bilan keladigan fe’llarni guruhlab o‘rganamiz. Predlogni mantiq bilan topib bo‘lmaydi: fe’l va predlogni bitta juftlik sifatida yodlaymiz. Tuzilma ko‘pincha bir xil:

Ega+fe’l+predlog+ot / olmosh / fe’l-ing

Predlogdan keyin fe’l kelsa, u -ing shaklida bo‘ladi: succeed in passing, insist on paying, concentrate on studying (Unit 60, Unit 62).

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar kelishik qo‘shimchalari bilan beriladi: ruhlarga ishonmoq, huquqqa ixtisoslashmoq, daraxtga urilmoq, kiyimga pul sarflamoq, darsga diqqatni qaratmoq. Ko‘pchiligida bir xil “-ga” bor, lekin inglizchada in, into, on kabi turli predloglar kelishi mumkin. Jadvalga qarang: ba’zan mos keladi (✅), ba’zan chalg‘itadi (⚠️) — xatolar aynan ⚠️ qatorlardan chiqadi.

In: ishonmoq, ixtisoslashmoq, muvaffaq bo‘lmoq believe in, specialise in, succeed in

Fe’l + in
BirikmaMa’nosiMisolTarjima
believe in sb / sthborligiga yoki to‘g‘riligiga ishonmoq; kimningdir qobiliyatiga ishonmoqMy little brother still believes in monsters under the bed.Ukam hali ham karavot ostida maxluqlar bor deb ishonadi.
specialise in sth…ga ixtisoslashmoqDr Umarov specialises in children’s allergies.Doktor Umarov bolalar allergiyasiga ixtisoslashgan.
succeed in (doing) sth…ga erishmoq, uddalamoqJasur succeeded in passing the driving test.Jasur haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘tishga muvaffaq bo‘ldi.

believe in va believe

believe IN sb / sth

Uch xil ma’no: (1) bor deb hisoblamoq; (2) yaxshi, to‘g‘ri deb hisoblamoq; (3) kimdir uddalaydi deb ishonmoq.

  • Do you believe in ghosts?
  • Our coach believes in giving every player a chance.
  • I believe in you, Dilnoza. You will pass!

believe sb / sth (predlogsiz)

Aytganini rost deb qabul qilmoq.

  • I believe you. (Gapingga ishonaman.)
  • I don’t believe his story.
  • Nobody believed the news at first.

O‘zbekcha “-ga ishonmoq” ikkala ma’noni ham beradi: “Unga ishondim” (gapiga) = I believed him, lekin “Unga ishonaman, u uddalaydi” (qobiliyatiga) = I believe in him. Shuning uchun ❌ believe to xato; in yoki predlogsiz tanlanadi. “Ishonmoq, tayanmoq” ma’nosida trust sb ham predlogsiz keladi: I trust you.

specialise in

  • That café specialises in Khorezm dishes.Bu kafe xorazmcha taomlarga ixtisoslashgan.
  • Our shop specialises in repairing old watches.Do‘konimiz eski soatlarni ta’mirlashga ixtisoslashgan.
  • She is a specialist in heart surgery.U yurak jarrohligi bo‘yicha mutaxassis.

Britaniya ingliz tilida specialise, Amerika ingliz tilida specialize. Ot shakli: specialist in (mutaxassis), speciality (AmE: specialty) — mutaxassislik yoki “firmaviy taom”: Plov is the speciality of this café. Fe’ldan keyin in + ot yoki -ing.

succeed in

  • After three attempts, Malika succeeded in finding a cheap flat.Uch urinishdan keyin Malika arzon kvartira topishga muvaffaq bo‘ldi.
  • Did you succeed in getting tickets for the concert?Konsertga chipta olishga muvaffaq bo‘ldingizmi?
  • He succeeded in business without any help from his family.U oilasining yordamisiz biznesda muvaffaqiyatga erishdi.
succeed, manage va fail
Ma’noQolipMisol
muvaffaq bo‘ldisucceed in + -ingShe succeeded in opening the door.
muvaffaq bo‘ldi (so‘zlashuv)manage to + fe’lShe managed to open the door.
uddalay olmadifail to + fe’lShe failed to open the door.

❌ He succeeded to open the door — succeed dan keyin infinitiv emas, in + -ing keladi. Teskari ma’noda esa aksincha: fail to + fe’l. succeed predlogsiz ham ishlatiladi: (1) “natija bergan”: The plan succeeded. (2) “o‘rnini egallagan”: Who succeeded Mr Ahmedov as director?

(Itbaliqlar baqaga aylanadi.)

With: to‘qnashmoq, to‘ldirmoq, ta’minlamoq collide with, fill with, provide / supply with

collide with

  • A motorbike collided with a minibus at the crossroads.Chorrahada mototsikl marshrutka bilan to‘qnashdi.
  • Two cars collided on the ring road this morning.Bugun ertalab aylanma yo‘lda ikki mashina to‘qnashdi.

crash INTO …

Kundalik nutq. Harakatdagi narsa devor, daraxt yoki boshqa narsaga urilgan.

  • The van crashed into a gate.

collide WITH …

Rasmiyroq, yangiliklarda ko‘p uchraydi; odatda ikki harakatdagi narsa.

  • A motorbike collided with a minibus.
  • Two cars collided. (kimga ekani aytilmasa — predlogsiz)

Ot shakli: a collision with a lorry, a collision between two cars. “Bilan to‘qnashmoq” — with ✅.

fill … with

  • Fill the kettle with water, please.Iltimos, choynakni suv bilan to‘ldiring.
  • Nodira filled her bag with apples from the orchard.Nodira sumkasini bog‘dan olgan olmalar bilan to‘ldirdi.
  • The hall was filled with laughter and music.Zal kulgi va musiqaga to‘ldi.
  • Her eyes filled with tears.Ko‘zlari yoshga to‘ldi.
fill with va full of
Fe’l (harakat)Sifat (holat)
Aziz filled the jar with honey.The jar is full of honey. (Unit 131)
The room was filled with smoke.The room was full of smoke.

fill in a form (AmE ko‘pincha: fill out) — boshqa birikma, “anketa to‘ldirmoq”; u frazali fe’llarda o‘rganiladi (Unit 138). U yerda with emas, in / out keladi.

Ikki to‘ldiruvchili fe’l give: The club gives us uniforms. Ehtiyot bo‘ling: provide for sb (to‘ldiruvchisiz) — “oilani boqmoq, ta’minlamoq”: He works two jobs to provide for his family.

QolipMa’nosiMisolTarjima

QolipMisolTarjima

Birinchi ikki qolipdagi to — predlog, shuning uchun undan keyin -ing keladi: ✅ prefer walking to driving; ❌ prefer walking to drive. ❌ prefer rice than pasta, ❌ prefer rice from pasta. “Guruchni makarondan afzal ko‘rmoq” — “-dan” inglizchada from emas, to: prefer rice to pasta. O‘quvchilar “-dan ko‘ra” ni than deb tarjima qilib xato qiladi (than faqat better than, more than kabi qiyoslashda).

On: spend, waste, concentrate, insist spend / waste (money, time) on; concentrate on; insist on

spend … ON …

Pul yoki vaqt nimaga ketdi: sotib olingan narsa, mashg‘ulot.

  • I spent 200,000 so‘m on a jacket.

pay … FOR …

Sotib olingan narsa yoki xizmat evaziga to‘lov (Unit 135).

  • I paid 200,000 so‘m for a jacket.

“Kiyimga pul sarflamoq” — “-ga” bu yerda on: spend money on clothes. ❌ spend money for clothes / to clothes. Pul haqida gapirganda for — pay for bilan keladi. Vaqt haqida: spend two hours doing homework — bu qolipda predlog yo‘q (Unit 63).

concentrate on

  • Dilnoza can’t concentrate on her homework when the TV is on.Televizor yoqilgan bo‘lsa, Dilnoza uy vazifasiga diqqatini jamlay olmaydi.
  • Aziz concentrated on repairing the bike and ignored the noise.Aziz velosipedni ta’mirlashga diqqatini qaratdi va shovqinga e’tibor bermadi.
  • Please concentrate! The exam starts in a minute.Iltimos, diqqatingizni jamlang! Imtihon bir daqiqadan keyin boshlanadi.

Nimaga diqqat qaratilayotganini aytsangiz — concentrate on + ot yoki -ing. Nimaga ekani aytilmasa, fe’l predlogsiz: I can’t concentrate. Sinonim: focus on. ❌ concentrate to, ❌ concentrate in. pay attention to da esa attention ot bo‘lgani uchun to keladi.

insist on

  • Sardor insisted on carrying my suitcase.Sardor chamadonimni ko‘tarishda turib oldi.
  • The customer insisted on seeing the manager.Mijoz menejer bilan ko‘rishishni qat’iy talab qildi.
  • She insisted on a full refund.U pulini to‘liq qaytarishlarini talab qildi.
insist: ikki qolip
QolipMa’nosiMisol

❌ He insisted to pay — insist dan keyin infinitiv kelmaydi: insisted on paying. that bilan gap kelsa, on tushadi: ❌ insisted on that I stay.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I believe you” bilan “I believe in you” ning farqi nimada?

I believe you — aytgan gapingiz rost deb hisoblayman. I believe in you — sizning kuchingiz, qobiliyatingiz yoki yaxshi insoniy fazilatingizga ishonaman (“uddalaysiz”). Birinchisi gapga, ikkinchisi odamning o‘ziga ishonishdir. Narsa uchun ham shunday: I don’t believe the story (hikoya rost emas) — I believe in hard work (mehnatning kuchiga ishonaman).

in bilan into ni qanday farqlayman?

in — joy yoki holat (“ichida”), into — harakat, urilish yoki o‘zgarish (“ichiga”). Taqqoslang: The children are playing in the park. — The car drove into the park. Shu darsdagi believe in, specialise in, succeed in — qat’iy juftliklar, yodlanadi. crash into, cut into, divide into, translate into da esa urilish yoki o‘zgarish ma’nosi bor.

run into va bump into bir xilmi? Nega ularni “uchratmoq” deb ham tarjima qilamiz?

Ikkalasi ham ikki ma’noda ishlatiladi: (1) tasodifan urilmoq (bump — yengil, run/crash — kuchliroq); (2) tasodifan uchratmoq. Ikkinchi ma’noda ular norasmiy bo‘lib, o‘zbekchadagi “yo‘lda duch kelib qoldim” iborasiga yaqin. Rejalashtirilgan uchrashuv uchun oddiy meet ishlatiladi. run into “qiyinchilikka duch kelmoq” ma’nosida ham bor: run into trouble.

crash into bilan collide with ning farqi bormi?

Ma’no yaqin. crash into — kundalik nutq, harakatdagi narsa devor, daraxt yoki boshqa narsaga urilgan. collide with — rasmiyroq, ko‘proq yangiliklarda; odatda ikkala narsa ham harakatda. Ikkala narsa ham ega o‘rnida kelsa, collide predlogsiz ishlatiladi: The two cars collided.

provide for bilan provide with ning farqi nima?

provide sb with sth — kimgadir nimadir berish (provide guests with towels). provide for sb — to‘ldiruvchisiz, “boqmoq, moddiy ta’minlamoq”: He provides for his family. Yana provide sth for sb (provide towels for guests) — birinchisi bilan ma’nosi bir xil, so‘z tartibi boshqa.

“translate in English”, “translate to English” va “translate into English” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Standart va xavfsiz shakl — translate into English. “In English” faqat nima ingliz tilida yozilgan yoki aytilganini ko‘rsatadi (Write it in English). translate to norasmiy nutqda, ayniqsa Amerikada eshitilishi mumkin, lekin imtihonda into yozing. Qolipi: translate X from A into B.

prefer dan keyin than ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q: prefer X to Y. than — better than, rather than-dagi qiyoslash so‘zi. Faqat to-infinitiv bilan rather than kelishi mumkin: I prefer to walk rather than take a taxi. I’d rather walk than take a taxi ham to‘g‘ri, lekin u boshqa qolip (Unit 59).

spend, waste va pay — farqi nimada? “spend time doing” da on shartmi?

spend — pul yoki vaqt sarflash: spend money on books. waste — behuda sarflash: waste time on gossip. pay — sotib olingan narsa evaziga to‘lov: pay for the book (Unit 135). Vaqt va fe’l bilan: spend time doing — predlog kerak emas (spent two hours cleaning), ya’ni spend time on cleaning bilan ma’nosi bir xil (Unit 63).

insist on bilan insist that ning farqi nimada?

insist on + ot yoki -ing — o‘z talabingiz yoki qat’iy niyatingiz (He insisted on paying). insist that + gap — (1) boshqaga talab qo‘yish (She insisted that I stay) yoki (2) qat’iy ta’kidlash (He insists that he is innocent). Gap oldidan on qo‘yilmaydi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • in: believe in (sb / sth), specialise in, succeed in + -ing. ❌ believe to, ❌ succeed to do.
  • into: crash / drive / run / bump / walk / break into; cut / chop / divide / split into; translate from … into …. ❌ translate in English.
  • with: collide with; fill sth with sth; provide / supply sb with sth (yoki provide sth for sb, supply sth to sb). ❌ provide us uniforms.
  • to: what happened to sb; happen to + fe’l (tasodifan); prefer X to Y (than emas!); prefer walking to driving.
  • on: spend / waste money (time) on; concentrate on; insist on + -ing. ❌ spend for, ❌ concentrate to, ❌ insist to do.
  • run into / bump into = urilmoq yoki tasodifan uchratmoq. believe sb = gapiga ishonmoq; believe in sb = qobiliyatiga ishonmoq.
  • O‘zbekcha “-ga” har xil bo‘ladi: in, into, to yoki on — juftlikni bir butun qilib yodlang.
