Adjectives and adverbs 1 (quick/quickly)

Sifat va ravish — 1 (quick / quickly)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Sifat (adjective) otni, ravish (adverb) esa fe’lni tasvirlashini: a polite boy — answers politely.
  • Sifatdan ravishni qanday yasash: -ly va uning imlo qoidalari (easy → easily, terrible → terribly, true → truly).
  • Ravishlar sifat va boshqa ravishlar oldida ham kelishini: terribly sorry, badly damaged, incredibly well.
  • Fe’ldan keyin qachon sifat, qachon ravish kelishini va -ly bilan tugaydigan sifatlarni (friendly, lovely).

Ikkala so‘z turi ham tasvirlaydi, lekin turli narsani. Sifat savol beradi: “qanday bola?” — polite. Ravish esa: “qanday javob beradi?” — politely. Ko‘pchilik ravishlar sifatga -ly qo‘shilib yasaladi.

O‘zbek tilida sifat va ravish ko‘pincha bir xil so‘z bo‘ladi: tez haydovchi (sifat) — tez haydaydi (ravish); chiroyli qo‘shiq — chiroyli kuylaydi. Shuning uchun o‘zbek o‘quvchilari ingliz tilida ham He drives careful yoki She sings beautiful deb adashadi. Ingliz tilida esa fe’lni tasvirlayotgan so‘z ravish shaklida (carefully, beautifully) bo‘lishi shart.

Sifat va ravish: nimani tasvirlaydi? Adjective or adverb?

Sifat → ot yoki olmosh

  • We had a quick lunch.
  • The exam was easy.
  • He was angry.

Ravish → fe’l

  • We ate quickly.
  • She passed the exam easily.
  • He shouted angrily.
  • The film was so exciting that the two hours went very quickly.Film shunchalik qiziqarli ediki, ikki soat juda tez o‘tib ketdi.
  • Jasur is a fluent speaker of English. He speaks English fluently.Jasur ingliz tilida ravon so‘zlashadigan odam. U inglizchada ravon gapiradi.
  • The teacher spoke clearly, so everybody understood.O‘qituvchi aniq gapirdi, shuning uchun hamma tushundi.
  • There was a sudden noise. The bus stopped suddenly.To‘satdan shovqin bo‘ldi. Avtobus birdan to‘xtadi.

-ly qo‘shishning imlo qoidalari

Sifatdan ravish yasash
QoidaMisollar
Ko‘pchilik sifatlarga shunchaki -ly qo‘shiladislow → slowly, quiet → quietly, bad → badly, sudden → suddenly, perfect → perfectly
-l bilan tugasa, -ly qo‘shilganda ll bo‘ladicareful → carefully, beautiful → beautifully, real → really, usual → usually, final → finally

Ravishning joyi: Fe’l va to‘ldiruvchi orasiga ravish qo‘yilmaydi. She speaks English fluently. ✅ — She speaks fluently English. ❌. Fe’l to‘ldiruvchisiz bo‘lsa: She speaks fluently. Ravishlarning boshqa o‘rinlari uchun Unit 109, 110 ga qarang.

Sifat, ravish va boshqa so‘zlar oldidagi ravishlar Adverbs before adjectives, adverbs and participles

Ravish nafaqat fe’lni, balki sifat, boshqa ravish va -ed shakldagi so‘zni ham kuchaytiradi yoki aniqlashtiradi. Bu holda ravish ularning oldida turadi:

Ravish + sifat / ravish / -ed so‘z

  • Dilnoza is unusually quiet today. Is something wrong?Dilnoza bugun odatdagidan ancha jim. Biror narsa bo‘ldimi?
  • The film was really good, but the ending was slightly disappointing.Film haqiqatan ham yaxshi edi, lekin oxiri biroz ko‘ngilsiz bo‘ldi.
  • It’s perfectly clear: we can’t go on Friday.Hammasi mutlaqo aniq: juma kuni borolmaymiz.

Bu ravishlarning ko‘pi darajani bildiradi (juda, ancha, biroz): extremely, really, terribly, incredibly, reasonably, slightly, unusually. Terribly va awfully “dahshatli” ma’nosini yo‘qotgan va oddiy ravishda “juda” ni bildiradi: terribly sorry — “juda afsusdaman”, awfully nice — “juda yoqimli”. Really o‘rniga Amerika so‘zlashuvida real ham uchraydi (real good), lekin standart til va imtihon uchun really good ishlating.

Fe’ldan keyin sifat yoki ravish Adjective or adverb after a verb?

Fe’ldan keyin sifat yoki ravish kelishi fe’l ma’nosiga bog‘liq. Savol oddiy: so‘z egani tasvirlaydimi (sifat) yoki harakat qanday bajarilganini (ravish)?

Holat (sifat) va harakat (ravish)
Sifat (ega qanday?)Ravish (ish qanday bajarildi?)
Bekzod was angry. (u jahldor edi)Bekzod shouted angrily. (u jahl bilan qichqirdi)
The band sounded terrible. (guruh yomon eshitildi)The band played terribly. (guruh yomon chaldi)
Be careful! (ehtiyot bo‘ling!)Drive carefully! (ehtiyotkorlik bilan haydang!)
Nodira looks happy. (xursand ko‘rinadi)Nodira smiled happily. (u xursand bo‘lib jilmaydi)

Qoida: be, seem, look, feel, sound, taste, smell, get, become kabi fe’llardan keyin (holat) — sifat (Unit 99). Boshqa harakat fe’llaridan keyin qanday? savoliga javob bo‘lsa — ravish.

Make, keep, find + to‘ldiruvchi + sifat

Ba’zi fe’llardan keyin to‘ldiruvchi keladi, undan keyin esa shu to‘ldiruvchini tasvirlovchi sifat. Bu yerda ham ravish emas, sifat ishlatiladi:

  • This news made me sad.Bu xabar meni xafa qildi.
  • Please keep the door open.Iltimos, eshikni ochiq tuting.
  • I found the exam easy.Men imtihonni oson topdim (imtihon menga oson tuyuldi).
  • They painted the walls white.Ular devorlarni oq rangga bo‘yashdi.

O‘zbekchada “tez” so‘zi ham sifat (tez javob), ham ravish (tez javob berdi) bo‘la oladi. Inglizchada tarjima qilishda so‘zning gapdagi vazifasiga qarang: tez javob → a quick answer (ot oldida — sifat); tez javob berdi → answered quickly (fe’l bilan — ravish).

-ly bilan tugaydigan sifatlar Adjectives ending in -ly

Ba’zi so‘zlar -ly bilan tugaydi, lekin ular ravish emas, sifat: friendly, lovely, lonely, lively, silly, ugly, cowardly, elderly. Ularning odatda ravish shakli yo‘q. Ravish ma’nosi kerak bo‘lsa, in a ... way (“... tarzda”) ishlatiladi:

  • Kamola is a lively child. She never sits still.Kamola serharakat bola. U hech qachon bir joyda o‘tirmaydi.

She spoke friendlily. ❌ — She spoke in a friendly way. ✅. Boshqa yo‘l — gapni o‘zgartirish: She was friendly to us.

Ravishning boshqa shakllari (qisqa eslatma) A first look at other adverb forms

Hamma ravish ham -ly bilan tugamaydi. Ba’zi sifatlar ravish bo‘lib shu shaklda ishlatiladi (fast, hard, late), ba’zi sifatlarning ravishi butunlay boshqa so‘z (good → well). Ba’zi juftliklar esa ma’nosi bilan chalkashtiriladi (hard / hardly, late / lately). Bu mavzu Unit 101 da batafsil tushuntiriladi.

Qisqa ko‘rinish (to‘liq izoh Unit 101 da)
SifatRavish
a fast trainThe train goes fast.
a good singerShe sings well.
a hard examHe studies hard. (hardly — boshqa ma’no!)

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

He drives very careful.He drives very carefully.Fe’l (drives) ni tasvirlaydigan so‘z ravish bo‘lishi kerak.
She sings beautiful.She sings beautifully.O‘zbekchada “chiroyli” ikki ma’noda ham ishlatiladi, inglizchada esa fe’l bilan beautifully kerak.
He opened carefully the door.He opened the door carefully.Ravish fe’l va to‘ldiruvchi orasiga tushmaydi, to‘ldiruvchidan keyin turadi.
I’m terrible sorry.I’m terribly sorry.Sifat (sorry) oldidan ravish kerak: terribly, extremely, really.
The test was easily.The test was easy.Be fe’lidan keyin sifat keladi (egani tasvirlaydi).
The news made me sadly.The news made me sad.Make + to‘ldiruvchi dan keyin sifat keladi.
She behaved friendlily.She behaved in a friendly way.Friendly — sifat; uning -ly ravishi yo‘q. In a friendly way ishlating.
They live happyly in Samarkand.They live happily in Samarkand.Imlo: undosh + y → -ily (happy → happily).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Sifat bilan ravishni gapda qanday ajrataman?

So‘zning nimaga bog‘langanini tekshiring. Ot yoki olmosh haqida gapirsa (a quick lunch, he is tired) — sifat. Fe’l, sifat yoki boshqa ravish haqida gapirsa (ate quickly, very tired, quite quickly) — ravish. “Qanday?” savoli ikkalasiga ham mos, shuning uchun “nima haqida?” deb so‘rang.

Har bir sifatga -ly qo‘shsam, ravish bo‘ladimi?

Ko‘p hollarda ha (slow → slowly), lekin hammasida emas. -ly bilan tugaydigan sifatlar (friendly, lovely, lonely) ravishga aylanmaydi. Ba’zi sifatlarning ravishi shu so‘zning o‘zi (fast, hard, late), good ning ravishi esa butunlay boshqa so‘z — well (Unit 101).

Ravish gapda qayerda turadi?

Holat ravishi (carefully, quickly, beautifully) odatda fe’ldan keyin, agar fe’lning to‘ldiruvchisi bo‘lsa — to‘ldiruvchidan keyin: She sings beautifully. He opened the door carefully. Ba’zan ular fe’ldan oldin ham keladi (She carefully opened the door), bu haqda Unit 110 da. Eng muhimi: fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi orasiga qo‘ymang.

“Terribly sorry” — nega “terrible” (dahshatli) so‘zi bu yerda “juda” ma’nosida?

Ingliz tilida terribly, awfully, dreadfully, extremely kabi ravishlar o‘zining asl, salbiy ma’nosini yo‘qotib, “juda” ma’nosini beradi. terribly nice deganda “dahshatli yoqimli” emas, “juda yoqimli” deyilgan. Shu sababli sifat oldida “juda” deb tushunib o‘ting.

“Really good” desam bo‘ladimi yoki “real good”?

Standart ingliz tilida really good. Amerika so‘zlashuv tilida real good / real nice ham eshitiladi, lekin bu norasmiy va imtihon uchun xavfli. “Real” — sifat (a real friend), “really” — ravish.

“He speaks English good” deyish mumkinmi?

Yo‘q. Fe’l (speaks) ni tasvirlaydigan so‘z ravish bo‘lishi kerak, good ning ravishi esa well: He speaks English well. Bu qoida Unit 101 da batafsil. Shuningdek speaks good English to‘g‘ri: bu yerda good ot (English) ni tasvirlaydi.

Qiyosiy darajada ham ravish o‘zgaradimi? “More quickly” deymizmi?

Ha. -ly bilan tugagan ravishlar qiyosiy darajani more bilan yasaydi: more quickly, more carefully (Unit 105). Oddiy ravishlar esa -er qo‘shadi: faster, harder. Bu bobda faqat oddiy darajani o‘rgandik.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Sifat otni tasvirlaydi (a polite boy), ravish fe’lni (answers politely).
  • Ravish ko‘pincha sifat + -ly: quick → quickly. Imlo: easy → easily, terrible → terribly, careful → carefully, true → truly.
  • Ravish sifat, boshqa ravish va -ed so‘zlar oldida ham keladi: terribly sorry, incredibly well, badly damaged.
  • be, seem, look, feel, sound, taste, smell + sifat; make / keep / find + to‘ldiruvchi + sifat.
  • Fe’lni tasvirlasa — ravish: shouted angrily, played terribly.
  • -ly bilan tugaydigan sifatlar (friendly, lovely, lonely) ravish emas; ravish uchun in a friendly way.
  • Ravish fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi orasiga qo‘yilmaydi: opened the door carefully.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 99 (look / feel + sifat), Unit 101 (well, fast, late, hard / hardly), Unit 105 (qiyosiy daraja), Unit 109 va Unit 110 (so‘z tartibi, ravishlar).
