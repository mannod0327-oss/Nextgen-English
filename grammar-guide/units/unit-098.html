98

Adjectives ending in -ing and -ed (boring/bored etc.)

-ing va -ed bilan tugaydigan sifatlar (boring / bored)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Nega boring va bored, interesting va interested, tiring va tired kabi juftliklar bor va ular nimasi bilan farq qilishini.
  • -ing sifat — his-tuyg‘uni keltirib chiqargan narsa yoki shaxsni; -ed sifat — his-tuyg‘uni his qilgan odamni tasvirlashini.
  • Eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan juftliklarni va ularning predloglarini (interested in, worried about).
  • Nega I am boring deb aytsangiz, “men zerikdim” emas, balki “men zerikarli odamman” bo‘lib qolishini.

Ingliz tilida ko‘plab his-tuyg‘u fe’llaridan ikki xil sifat yasaladi. Masalan, to bore (zeriktirmoq) fe’lidan: The film bores me (film meni zeriktiradi). Natijada film — boring (zerikarli), men esa — bored (zerikkan). Bu yerda odatda mantiq oddiy: nima his-tuyg‘u qildiradi → -ing, kim his qiladi → -ed.

O‘zbek tilida bu ikki ma’noni bir-biridan alohida so‘zlar ajratadi: zerikarli film — zerikkan tomoshabin; qiziqarli kitob — kitobga qiziqqan o‘quvchi; charchatadigan yo‘l — charchagan yo‘lovchi. Ingliz tilida esa juftlik bitta o‘zakdan yasalgani uchun adashish oson. “Men zerikdim” deb I am boring aytsangiz, inglizlar buni “men zerikarli odamman” deb tushunadi. To‘g‘ri variant: I am bored.

-ing sabab, -ed his-tuyg‘u Cause (-ing) and feeling (-ed)

Bir voqeani ikki tomondan ko‘rish mumkin: nima sodir bo‘ldi (sabab) va odam nima his qildi (natija). Ingliz tili har ikki tomon uchun alohida sifat beradi.

narsa / shaxs+ be +-ing sifat→ odam +be / feel / look+-ed sifat

Misollar

  • The lesson was interesting, so the students were interested.Dars qiziqarli edi, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar qiziqib qolishdi.
  • The exam results were surprising. Everybody was surprised.Imtihon natijalari kutilmagan edi. Hamma ajablandi.
  • It is a worrying situation. Mum is worried.Vaziyat tashvishli. Onam xavotirda.
  • What a tiring day! I’m really tired.Qanday charchatadigan kun bo‘ldi! Men juda charchadim.
  • Jasur is excited because the trip to Bukhara is going to be exciting.Jasur hayajonda, chunki Buxoroga sayohat qiziqarli (hayajonli) bo‘ladi.

Qoida: His-tuyg‘uni keltirib chiqargan narsa yoki shaxs → -ing (the film is boring). His-tuyg‘uni his qilgan odam → -ed (I am bored). Hayvonlar ham “his qiladi”, shuning uchun ular uchun ham -ed: a frightened cat.

Tez tekshirish: gapning egasi men / siz / u (odam) bo‘lib, “his qilyapti” ma’nosi bo‘lsa — deyarli har doim -ed. Ega narsa, voqea, vaziyat bo‘lsa (film, dars, natija, yangilik) — odatda -ing.

Ot oldida va odamlar haqida Before nouns, and with people

Ikkala turdagi sifat ham otdan oldin kelishi mumkin. -ing — shu narsa his-tuyg‘u uyg‘otishini, -ed — shu odam his qilayotganini bildiradi:

Ot oldida: narsa (-ing) va odam (-ed)

Odam haqida: he is boring va he is bored

Gap odam haqida bo‘lganda ham ikkala sifatdan foydalanish mumkin, lekin ma’no butunlay boshqacha:

He is boring.

U boshqalarni zeriktiradi — uning gaplari, xarakteri zerikarli.

My neighbour is boring. He only talks about the weather.

He is bored.

U o‘zi zerikyapti — hozirgi his-tuyg‘usi.

He is bored because there is nothing to do.

  • Our guide was interesting. He told us so many stories about Samarkand.Gidimiz qiziqarli odam edi. U bizga Samarqand haqida juda ko‘p hikoya aytib berdi.
  • I’m interested in history, so I enjoyed the tour.Men tarixga qiziqaman, shuning uchun ekskursiya menga yoqdi.
  • Why is Malika so annoying today? — Because she is annoyed with her brother.Nega Malika bugun shunchalik jonga tegyapti? — Chunki uning akasidan jahli chiqqan.

be + being + sifat shakli odamning hozirgi xatti-harakati haqida: You are being annoying — “sen hozir (shu xatti-harakating bilan) jahlimni chiqaryapsan”. You are annoying esa doimiy xislat haqida. Bunday shakl xatti-harakat sifatlari (annoying, boring, silly, kind) bilan keladi; his-tuyg‘u sifatlari (bored, tired) bilan odatda ishlatilmaydi.

O‘zbek tilidagi ba’zi fe’llar bu juftlikni aniq ko‘rsatadi: zerikmoq (his qilgan, -ed) va zeriktirmoq (his qildirgan, -ing); qiziqmoq va qiziqtirmoq. Ingliz tilida esa fe’lning o‘zi (bore) “zeriktirmoq” ma’nosini beradi, farqni faqat -ing yoki -ed qo‘shimchasi ko‘rsatadi.

Very, really, so — kuchaytiruvchilar

Ikkala turdagi sifatdan oldin ham very, really, extremely, so kelishi mumkin (so — Unit 102):

  • Aziz was extremely tired after the long journey.Aziz uzoq yo‘ldan keyin juda charchagan edi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I am boring. There is nothing to do.I am bored. There is nothing to do.“I am boring” = “men zerikarli odamman”. O‘zingiz zerikayotgan bo‘lsangiz, bored kerak.
The film was bored.The film was boring.Film his-tuyg‘u keltirib chiqargan narsa (sabab), shuning uchun -ing.
She is interesting in languages.She is interested in languages.Qiziqayotgan odam uchun -ed (interested in).
The exam results were surprised.The exam results were surprising.Natija ajablanmaydi, ajablantiradi — natija sabab, shuning uchun surprising.
We had a very tired day.We had a very tiring day.Kun charchatadi (sabab) → tiring. Charchaydigan odam — tired.
The lesson made me boring.The lesson made me bored.Dars meni zeriktirdi — men his qildim, shuning uchun bored.
I’m interested of football.I’m interested in football.Interested bilan in predlogi ishlatiladi.
The children were very excite about the trip.The children were very excited about the trip.Sifat -ed bilan tugashi shart; fe’l shakli (excite) sifat o‘rnida turmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

-ing yoki -ed ni qanday tezda tanlayman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Bu so‘z kimning his-tuyg‘usini bildiryapti?” Agar odam o‘zi his qilayotgan bo‘lsa (I, we, the students) — -ed. Agar his-tuyg‘uni keltirib chiqargan narsa yoki voqea bo‘lsa (the film, the news, the exam) — -ing. Ikkalasini ham bir gapda ishlatib ko‘ring: The news was shocking. I was shocked. Gap to‘g‘ri chiqsa, tanlov ham to‘g‘ri.

“He is boring” va “He is bored” ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri-ku. Farqi nimada?

Ikkalasi ham grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri, lekin ma’nosi boshqa. He is boring — u o‘zgalarni zeriktiradi (uning xarakteri, gaplari). He is bored — u o‘zi hozir zerikyapti. Shuning uchun “men zerikdim” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, I’m bored deng. I’m boring — o‘zingizni zerikarli deb ta’riflash.

Nega bu sifatlar fe’lga o‘xshab ketadi? Ular fe’lmi, sifatmi?

Ular his-tuyg‘u fe’llaridan yasalgan (bore → boring / bored), lekin gapda sifatdek ishlaydi: a boring film, I am bored, very bored. Shuning uchun ularning oldida very, really, so ishlatish mumkin, fe’l bilan esa bunday bo‘lmaydi.

“Excited” doim yaxshi ma’nodami? “Exciting” ham?

Excited odatda qizg‘in kutish, quvonchli hayajon: I’m excited about the holiday. Exciting ham ko‘pincha yoqimli: an exciting match. Lekin yoqimsiz hayajon uchun boshqa so‘zlar bor: nervous, worried, frightening. Agar “qo‘rqinchli” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, exciting o‘rniga frightening yoki scary ishlating.

Qaysi predlogni ishlatishimni qanday bilaman? “Bored of” ham aytsa bo‘ladimi?

Predloglar ko‘p hollarda yod olinadi (D bo‘limdagi jadvalga qarang). Bored bilan an’anaviy variant with (bored with the lesson). Britaniya ingliz tilining so‘zlashuv uslubida bored of ham juda keng tarqalgan; AmE da bored with / by ishlatiladi. Imtihon va rasmiy yozuvda bored with xavfsizroq.

Hamma -ing sifatlarning -ed jufti bormi?

His-tuyg‘u fe’llaridan yasalgan sifatlarning ko‘pchiligida juftlik bor (amusing / amused, satisfying / satisfied). Lekin har bir -ing / -ed so‘zi juftlik emas: masalan a promising start (umidli boshlanish) va a promised gift (va’da qilingan sovg‘a) his-tuyg‘u juftligi emas, ma’nolari boshqa. Yangi so‘z uchrasa, lug‘atda ikkala shaklni ham tekshiring.

“Tired” va “tiring”, “exhausted” va “exhausting” — qaysi birini qachon aytaman?

Hammasi bir mantiq bo‘yicha. Tired / exhausted — odam (yoki hayvon) holati: I’m tired, The hikers were exhausted. Tiring / exhausting — charchatadigan narsa: a tiring journey, an exhausting week. Exhausted — tired dan kuchliroq (butunlay holdan toygan).

1-mashq. To‘g‘ri sifatni tanlang.

  1. The lecture was so (boring / bored) that half the students fell asleep.
  2. Bekzod was (excited / exciting) about the trip to Khiva.
  3. It was a (tired / tiring) journey: twelve hours by bus.
  4. I was (surprised / surprising) to see Nodira at the market.
  5. The news about the floods is (worried / worrying).
  6. Dilnoza looked (annoying / annoyed) when she saw the mess.
  7. The children found the museum very (interesting / interested).
  8. We were (shocking / shocked) when we heard the news.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’ldan -ing yoki -ed sifat yasab, bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring.

  1. This film is really (frighten). I don’t want to watch it alone.
  2. Jasur is (interest) in ancient history.
  3. The result was (disappoint): we lost 4–0.
  4. Sardor felt (embarrass) when he forgot the teacher’s name.
  5. What an (amaze) view of the mountains!

  8. After the long walk, the (exhaust) hikers went to bed early.

3-mashq. Ikkinchi gapni birinchisiga ma’nodosh qilib to‘ldiring (-ing yoki -ed sifat).

  6. The magic trick amazed everyone. → Everyone was by the magic trick.
  7. The news shocked Malika. → Malika was by the news.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • -ing sifat — his-tuyg‘uni keltirib chiqargan narsa yoki shaxs: a boring film, a tiring day.
  • -ed sifat — his-tuyg‘uni his qilgan odam: I am bored, she is tired.
  • I am boring = “men zerikarli odamman”. “Men zerikdim” = I am bored.
  • Ikkala turdagi sifat ham otdan oldin keladi: a boring lesson, bored students.
  • Predloglarni birga yodlang: interested in, excited about, worried about, bored with, surprised at / by, frightened of.
  • Bu sifatlar very, really, so bilan birga ishlatiladi: very tired, really exciting.
  • Make / feel / look / get bilan: The news made me worried. You look tired.
