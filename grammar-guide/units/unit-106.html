106

Comparison 2 (much better / any better / better and better / the sooner the better)

Qiyosiy daraja — 2 (much better, better and better ...)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Qiyosiy darajani kuchaytirish yoki yumshatish: much better, a lot easier, far more expensive (katta farq) va a bit better, slightly warmer (kichik farq).
  • very qiyosiy daraja bilan kelmaydi: very better ❌.
  • any va no + qiyosiy daraja: Is it any better? It’s no better.
  • Takroriy qiyosiy daraja — o‘sib borayotgan o‘zgarish: better and better, more and more expensive.
  • The + qiyosiy ..., the + qiyosiy ... qurilmasi: The sooner, the better.
  • older va elder farqi.

Bu bo‘limda siz 105-darsdagi qiyosiy daraja (bigger, more expensive) bilan ishlashni davom ettirasiz: farq qanchalik katta ekanini aytishni, o‘zgarish davom etayotganini bildirishni va ikki narsaning bir-biriga bog‘liqligini ifodalashni o‘rganasiz.

O‘zbekchada ham shunday: “ancha kattaroq”, “sal kattaroq”, “yanada yaxshiroq”, “tobora sovuqroq”, “qancha ertaroq bo‘lsa, shuncha yaxshi”. Inglizchada: much bigger, slightly bigger, even better, colder and colder, the sooner, the better. Eng katta xavf — o‘zbekcha “juda” ni qiyosiy daraja oldida very deb tarjima qilish: very better ❌, to‘g‘risi much better.

Much better, a bit better Much / a lot / far / a bit / slightly + comparative

Qiyosiy darajadan oldin farq qanchalik katta yoki kichik ekanini bildiruvchi so‘z qo‘yish mumkin:

Farq qanchalik katta?
FarqIshlatiladigan so‘zlarMisol
🔺 katta farqmuch, a lot, far (a great deal — rasmiyroq)much better, a lot easier, far more expensive
🔹 kichik farqa bit, a little, slightly (a little bit — norasmiy)a bit better, a little cheaper, slightly warmer
A+be+much / a lot / far / a bit / slightly+qiyosiy daraja+than+B
  • Kazakhstan is much bigger than Uzbekistan.Qozog‘iston O‘zbekistondan ancha-ancha katta.
  • Turkmenistan is slightly bigger than Uzbekistan.Turkmaniston O‘zbekistondan sal kattaroq.
  • My new phone is far better than my old one.Yangi telefonim eskisidan ancha yaxshi.
  • Jasur is a lot taller than his brother.Jasurning bo‘yi akasinikidan ancha baland.
  • The bazaar is a bit more crowded on Sundays.Bozor yakshanba kunlari biroz gavjumroq.
  • Today is slightly warmer than yesterday.Bugun kechagidan sal issiqroq.

Very qiyosiy daraja bilan kelmaydi: very better ❌, very bigger ❌. To‘g‘ri: much better, a lot bigger. Lekin very much better to‘g‘ri (very much — ikki so‘z birga). Very faqat asosiy daraja bilan: very good, very big.

More / less + ot

More va less ot bilan ham ishlatiladi. Oldidan: much / a lot (sanalmaydigan), many / a lot (sanaladigan ko‘plik), a bit / a little / a few / two ...:

  • There are many more tourists in Samarkand in May than in January.Samarqandda mayda yanvardagiga qaraganda ancha ko‘proq sayyoh bo‘ladi.
  • We need a lot more time to finish the project.Loyihani tugatish uchun bizga ancha ko‘proq vaqt kerak.
  • Can I have a little more tea, please?Yana ozgina choy olsam bo‘ladimi?
  • Wait two more minutes, please.Iltimos, yana ikki daqiqa kuting.

Farq oshganini ta’kidlash uchun even va still ham ishlatiladi: It was cold yesterday, but today it’s even colder. (Unit 112).

Any better, no better Any / no + comparative

Qiyosiy daraja oldida any va no ham kelishi mumkin. Bu “umuman biror farq bormi?” degan ma’noni beradi:

Any va no
So‘zQayerda?Misol
any + qiyosiyso‘roq gapdaIs your cough any better?
Yo‘talingiz biroz bo‘lsa ham yaxshilandimi?
any + qiyosiyinkor gapdaI can’t walk any faster.
Bundan tezroq yura olmayman.
no + qiyosiydarak shaklida, ma’no inkorThe second hotel was no cheaper than the first.
Ikkinchi mehmonxona birinchisidan umuman arzon emas edi.
  • Do you feel any better after the medicine?Dori ichgandan keyin o‘zingizni biroz yaxshiroq his qilyapsizmi?
  • We can’t wait any longer. The train leaves soon.Biz bundan ortiq kuta olmaymiz. Poyezd hademay jo‘naydi.
  • I’m no better at chess than I was last year.Men shaxmatda o‘tgan yilgidan hech yaxshiroq emasman.
  • The new road is no faster than the old one.Yangi yo‘l eskisidan umuman tez emas.

Not … any + qiyosiy bilan no + qiyosiy bir xil: It isn’t any cheaper = It’s no cheaper. Bu any more / any longer / no longer bilan bog‘liq (Unit 111).

Better and better Repeated comparatives show continuing change

Qiyosiy darajani and bilan ikki marta takrorlasak, “tobora ...roq bo‘lyapti” ma’nosi chiqadi — o‘zgarish davom etmoqda:

Takroriy qiyosiy daraja
SifatShaklMisol
qisqa (-er)sifat-er and sifat-erIt’s getting colder and colder.
Havo tobora sovib bormoqda.
uzun (more)more and more + sifatThe city is getting more and more crowded.
Shahar tobora gavjumlashib bormoqda.
otmore and more + otMore and more people are buying electric cars.
Tobora ko‘proq odamlar elektromobil sotib olmoqda.
kamayishless and less + ot/sifatWe have less and less free time.
Bo‘sh vaqtimiz tobora kamayib bormoqda.
  • The days are getting longer and longer.Kunlar tobora uzayib bormoqda.
  • Dilnoza’s English is getting better and better.Dilnozaning ingliz tili tobora yaxshilanib bormoqda.
  • It’s getting harder and harder to find a cheap flat in Tashkent.Toshkentda arzon kvartira topish tobora qiyinlashib bormoqda.
  • He became more and more nervous before the exam.U imtihon oldidan tobora asabiylashib bordi.

More and more better ❌, more and more cheap ❌. Qisqa sifat: cheaper and cheaper; uzun sifat: more and more expensive. Better o‘zi qiyosiy: better and better.

O‘zbekchada bu ma’no tobora, borgan sari, -ib bormoq bilan beriladi: yomonlashib bormoqda, tobora qimmatlashmoqda. Inglizchada fe’l ko‘pincha get / become bo‘ladi: get worse and worse.

The sooner, the better The + comparative ..., the + comparative ...

Ikki o‘zgarish bir-biriga bog‘liq bo‘lsa, ikkala gap ham the + qiyosiy daraja bilan boshlanadi: biri ortsa, ikkinchisi ham o‘zgaradi.

The+qiyosiy daraja+ega + fe’l,the+qiyosiy daraja+ega + fe’l
  • The more you practise, the better you will speak.Qancha ko‘p mashq qilsangiz, shuncha yaxshi gapirasiz.
  • The earlier we leave, the less traffic there will be.Qancha erta chiqsak, yo‘lda shuncha kam tirbandlik bo‘ladi.
  • The longer I stayed in Bukhara, the more I liked it.Buxoroda qancha uzoq tursam, shuncha ko‘p yoqdi.
  • The hotter the day, the more water I drink.Kun qancha issiq bo‘lsa, shuncha ko‘p suv ichaman.
  • The higher you climb, the colder it gets.Qancha baland chiqsangiz, shuncha sovuqroq bo‘ladi.

Qisqa shakllar

Agar fe’l be bo‘lsa, uni tushirib qoldirish mumkin: The hotter the day (is), ..., The bigger the city, the more expensive the flats. Kundalik nutqda esa fe’lsiz, juda qisqa shakllar ko‘p ishlatiladi:

Mashhur iboralar:
The sooner, the better.The more, the merrier.The bigger, the better.The cheaper, the better.
  • “When shall I send the documents?” “The sooner, the better.”— Hujjatlarni qachon yuboray? — Qancha tez bo‘lsa, shuncha yaxshi.
  • “Can I bring my cousins to the party?” “Of course! The more, the merrier.”— Ziyofatga amakivachchalarimni olib kelsam bo‘ladimi? — Albatta! Odam qancha ko‘p bo‘lsa, shuncha yaxshi.

Qoida: the bu yerda artikl emas — “shuncha / qancha” ma’nosidagi so‘z. Ikkala qismda ham so‘z tartibi oddiy: The more you practise ... (savol tartibi emas: The more do you practise ❌). Kelasi zamon ma’nosida birinchi qismda present simple, ikkinchisida odatda will ishlatiladi.

O‘zbekcha “qancha … shuncha …” (yoki “qanchalik … shunchalik …”) aynan shu qurilmaga mos keladi: qancha ko‘p o‘qisangiz, shuncha ko‘p bilasiz = The more you read, the more you know.

Older va elder Older / elder

Older va elder ikkalasi ham “katta yoshdagi” ma’nosini beradi, lekin ishlatilishi turlicha:

Elder — Britaniya ingliz tilida ko‘proq; Amerika ingliz tilida odatda older. Elders (ot) — “kattalar, oqsoqollar” (jamiyatda hurmatli yoshi kattalar) ma’nosida ishlatiladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Your English is very better than last year.Your English is much better than last year.Qiyosiy daraja oldida very emas, much / a lot / far.
She is a lot more taller than me.She is a lot taller than me.Taller o‘zi qiyosiy daraja — more kerak emas.
Is your cough more better?Is your cough any better?“Biroz bo‘lsa ham yaxshilandimi?” so‘roqda any better.
I can’t walk more fast.I can’t walk any faster.More fast emas, any faster.
It’s getting hot and hot.It’s getting hotter and hotter.Takroriy qiyosiy daraja: ikkala joyda ham qiyosiy shakl.
The cars are getting more and more cheap.The cars are getting cheaper and cheaper.Qisqa sifatda more and more emas, -er and -er.
The more you study, the more better you speak.The more you study, the better you speak.Better o‘zi qiyosiy daraja; the more better emas.
The more do you practise, the better you speak.The more you practise, the better you speak.“The + qiyosiy” dan keyin savol tartibi emas, oddiy tartib.
He is elder than me.He is older than me.Elder than bilan ishlatilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega very better deb bo‘lmaydi, very much better esa bo‘ladi?

Very asosiy darajadagi sifat bilan keladi (very good). Qiyosiy daraja bilan bu so‘zning o‘rniga much ishlatiladi. Very much esa bir butun kuchaytiruvchi ibora bo‘lib, qiyosiy darajani ham kuchaytira oladi: very much better. Lekin ko‘pincha oddiygina much better deyiladi.

A lot, far va much farqi bormi?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil — “ancha katta farq”. A lot — kundalik nutqda keng tarqalgan; much — neytral; far — biroz kuchliroq va yozuvda ko‘p uchraydi (far better, far more expensive).

No better va not better bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. No better (= not any better) — “zarracha ham yaxshiroq emas, avvalgidek”, ya’ni farq umuman yo‘q: The new road is no faster. Not better esa shunchaki “yaxshiroq emas” degani va ko‘pincha qarama-qarshi fikr bilan keladi: It’s not better — it’s worse! “Hech qanday farq yo‘q” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, no better yoki not any better deng.

The sooner, the better da the nima?

Bu artikl emas. Qadimiy ingliz tilidagi “shu qadar” ma’nosidagi so‘zdan qolgan. Shuning uchun “the” ni tushirib qoldirib bo‘lmaydi: Sooner, better ❌.

Takroriy qiyosiy daraja (better and better) qaysi zamonda ishlatiladi?

Ko‘pincha present continuous bilan get / become: It’s getting colder and colder. O‘tmish uchun past continuous yoki past simple: The noise got louder and louder.

Elder bilan older o‘rtasida qaysi birini tanlayman?

Aniq bilmasangiz — older. U har doim to‘g‘ri. Elder faqat oila a’zolari uchun, otdan oldin ishlatiladi (my elder sister) va than bilan kelmaydi.

Much more bilan many more farqi nima?

Much more + sanalmaydigan ot (much more money), many more + sanaladigan ko‘plik ot (many more tourists). Kundalik nutqda ikkalasi o‘rniga a lot more ishlatish mumkin.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. This hotel is (very / much) cheaper than that one.
  2. I feel (very better / a lot better) today.
  3. Our new flat is (a bit / very) bigger than the old one.
  4. She is (much more / very more) experienced than I am.
  5. It’s (slightly / very) warmer today than yesterday.
  6. There are (much more / many more) tourists here in May than in January.

2-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga any yoki no ni yozing.

  1. Is your headache better today?
  2. The new road is faster than the old one. (umuman tezroq emas)
  3. I can’t walk further.
  4. Can’t you drive faster?
  5. This hotel is cheaper than the last one. (umuman arzonroq emas)
  6. We can’t wait longer.

3-mashq. Takroriy qiyosiy daraja yordamida gapni to‘ldiring. Har bir gapda nimadir ortib (kuchayib) bormoqda.

  1. The days are getting and . (long)
  2. Life in the city is becoming and expensive.
  3. The weather is getting and . (cold)
  4. and people are buying electric cars.
  5. Bekzod’s driving is getting and . (good)
  6. It’s getting and difficult to find a parking space near Chorsu bazaar.

4-mashq. The … the … qurilmasini to‘ldiring. Qavsdagi sifat yoki ravishning qiyosiy shaklini yozing.

  1. The (early) you go to bed, the (good) you’ll feel in the morning.
  2. The (much) you eat, the (fat) you get.
  3. The (big) the city, the (expensive) the flats.
  4. The (old) he gets, the (wise) he becomes.

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘rilab yozing.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Katta farq: much, a lot, far + qiyosiy; kichik farq: a bit, a little, slightly + qiyosiy. Very emas: very better ❌.
  • Ot bilan: much more + sanalmaydigan, many more + sanaladigan ko‘plik; a lot more ikkalasi bilan.
  • any + qiyosiy — so‘roq va inkorda (any better); no + qiyosiy — “umuman emas” (no cheaper).
  • O‘sib boruvchi o‘zgarish: colder and colder, more and more expensive, more and more people.
  • The + qiyosiy ..., the + qiyosiy ... = qancha ... shuncha ...: The sooner, the better. Oddiy so‘z tartibi.
  • older — hamma holda va than bilan; elder — faqat oila a’zosi, faqat otdan oldin: my elder sister.
