Must, mustn't, needn't

Must, mustn’t, needn’t
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • must (qilish zarur) va mustn’t (qilish mumkin emas) farqini.
  • needn’t va don’t need to = “shart emas, hojati yo‘q”.
  • needn’t have done = “qildim, lekin bunga hojat yo‘q ekan”.
  • didn’t need to do va needn’t have done ning nozik farqini.

Uchta asosiy so‘z — uchta “svetofor chirog‘i”: must = “qiling!” (yashil), mustn’t = “qilmang!” (qizil), needn’t = “qilish shart emas, o‘zingiz bilasiz” (sariq).

O‘zbekcha “-ishingiz shart emas”, “-ishning hojati yo‘q”, “-masangiz ham bo‘ladi” → needn’t / don’t need to. O‘tmish uchun: “-ishning hojati yo‘q ekan”, “bekorga ...-ibman” → needn’t have done: Bekorga shoshilibmiz = We needn’t have hurried.

must va mustn’t must = it is necessary to do it; mustn’t = it is necessary NOT to do it

You must do something = buni qilish zarur. You mustn’t do something = buni qilmaslik zarur (ya’ni “qilmang!”).

  • The baby’s asleep. We must be quiet. We mustn’t wake her up.Chaqaloq uxlayapti. Jim bo‘lishimiz kerak. Uni uyg‘otib yubormasligimiz kerak.
  • I must remember to buy a present for Malika. I mustn’t forget again!Malikaga sovg‘a olishni yodda tutishim kerak. Yana unutib qo‘ymasligim kerak!
  • You mustn’t use your phone while you’re driving.Mashina haydayotganda telefondan foydalanmaslik kerak.
  • Jasur must study hard this year. He mustn’t fail his exams again.Jasur bu yil qattiq o‘qishi kerak. U yana imtihonlardan yiqilmasligi kerak.

needn’t va don’t need to needn’t do / don’t need to do = it is not necessary

You needn’t do something = buni qilish shart emas, zarurat yo‘q. Xuddi shu ma’noni don’t / doesn’t need to ham beradi:

  • The film doesn’t start until nine. We needn’t leave yet. (= We don’t need to leave yet.)Kino to‘qqizgacha boshlanmaydi. Hozircha chiqishimiz shart emas.
  • You needn’t take a jacket. It’s really warm outside.Kurtka olishingiz shart emas. Tashqarida juda iliq.
  • Aziz needn’t come to the office tomorrow. He can work from home.Aziz ertaga ofisga kelishi shart emas. U uydan ishlashi mumkin.
  • You don’t need to bring a towel. The hotel gives you one.Sochiq olib kelishingiz shart emas. Mehmonxona beradi.
  • Dilnoza doesn’t need to buy a new laptop. She can use mine.Dilnoza yangi noutbuk sotib olishi shart emas. Meniki bilan ishlashi mumkin.
Grammatikasiga e’tibor bering!

Qoida: needn’t dan keyin to yo‘q: You needn’t hurry. don’t need dan keyin esa to bor: You don’t need to hurry. Uchala shakl — needn’t, don’t need to, don’t have to — bu ma’noda deyarli bir xil.

needn’t asosan Britaniya ingliz tilida ishlatiladi va biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi. Amerikada va kundalik nutqda ko‘pincha don’t need to yoki don’t have to deyiladi.

Ijobiy gapda need — oddiy fe’l: I need to go ✅ (I need go ❌). Modal needn’t faqat inkor shaklda keng tarqalgan.

needn’t have done: qildim, lekin hojati yo‘q ekan I needn’t have done it = I did it, but it wasn’t necessary

Siz o‘tmishda biror ishni qildingiz. Keyin ma’lum bo‘ldiki, bu ish kerak emas edi — vaqt yoki kuch bekorga ketdi. Shunda needn’t have + V3 ishlatiladi:

Ega+needn’t+have+V3
  • I cooked a huge pot of plov, but only three guests came. I needn’t have cooked so much.Katta qozonda osh qildim, lekin faqat uch mehmon keldi. Shuncha ko‘p qilishimning hojati yo‘q ekan.
  • Jasur was very worried about the test, but it was really easy. He needn’t have worried.Jasur test haqida juda xavotirda edi, lekin test juda oson ekan. Bekorga xavotir olgan ekan.
  • We rushed to get to Chorsu bazaar before it closed, but it was open until eight. We needn’t have hurried.Yopilib qolmasin deb Chorsu bozoriga shoshildik, lekin bozor soat sakkizgacha ochiq ekan. Shoshilishimizning hojati yo‘q ekan.
  • Thank you for the flowers! You really needn’t have brought anything.Gullar uchun rahmat! Hech narsa olib kelmasangiz ham bo‘lardi-ku.

Oxirgi misol — juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan xushmuomala ibora. Mehmon sovg‘a olib kelsa: Oh, you needn’t have done that! (Ovora bo‘lib nima qilardingiz!) Bu tanbeh emas, minnatdorlik.

O‘zbekchadagi “bekorga” va “... ekan” so‘zlari yaxshi ishora: bekorga shoshilibmiz, olib kelishning hojati yo‘q ekan → needn’t have + V3.

didn’t need to do va needn’t have done I didn’t need to ... / I needn’t have ...

I didn’t need to do something = buni qilish shart emas edi. Bu shakl qilganmisiz yoki yo‘qmi — buni o‘zi aytmaydi. Ko‘pincha siz buni oldindan bilgansiz va shuning uchun qilmagansiz:

  • Mum had already made plov, so Nodira didn’t need to cook dinner.Oyim allaqachon osh qilib qo‘ygan edi, shuning uchun Nodira kechki ovqat pishirishi shart emas edi. (Pishirmadi.)
  • My uncle gave us free tickets, so we didn’t need to buy any.Amakim bepul chipta berdi, shuning uchun chipta sotib olishimiz shart bo‘lmadi. (Sotib olmadik.)
  • We didn’t need to buy any bread, but the bakery smelled so good that we bought two loaves anyway.Non olishimiz shart emas edi, lekin novvoyxonadan shunday yoqimli hid kelardiki, baribir ikkita non oldik. (Oldik!)

needn’t have done

Qildim, lekin bu keraksiz bo‘lib chiqdi (keyin bildim).

  • I needn’t have bought tickets. (Sotib oldim — bekorga.)

didn’t need to do

Zarurat yo‘q edi. Odatda qilmaganman; ba’zan qilganman — kontekst aytadi.

  • I didn’t need to buy tickets. (Shart emas edi — odatda: sotib olmadim.)

didn’t have to ham didn’t need to bilan bir xil ma’noda: We didn’t have to buy tickets = We didn’t need to buy tickets.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

needn’t, don’t need to va don’t have to — qaysi birini ishlatay?

Hozirgi va kelasi zamonda uchalasi ham deyarli bir xil: “shart emas”. don’t have to va don’t need to — eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan, universal shakllar. needn’t — ko‘proq Britaniyada, biroz rasmiyroq. Ikkilansangiz, don’t need to yoki don’t have to ni tanlang.

“needn’t have done” va “didn’t need to” — eng oson farqlash yo‘li?

Savol bering: ish qilinganmi? Agar ha va keyin bekorga ekani ma’lum bo‘lgan bo‘lsa → needn’t have done. Agar shunchaki “zarurat yo‘q edi” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz (ko‘pincha ish qilinmagan) → didn’t need to. I needn’t have taken a taxi — taksiga o‘tirdim, bekorga. I didn’t need to take a taxi — taksi kerak emas edi (odatda: o‘tirmadim).

Nega need ba’zan “to” bilan, ba’zan “to”siz?

Chunki need ikki xil ishlaydi. 1) Oddiy fe’l sifatida: I need to, she needs to, don’t need to, did you need to? — “to” bilan. 2) Modal fe’l sifatida (asosan inkorda): needn’t — “to”siz, -s olmaydi. Ikkalasini aralashtirmang: needn’t to ❌, don’t need worry ❌.

“Need I ...?” deb so‘rash to‘g‘rimi?

Grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri, lekin juda rasmiy va kam ishlatiladi (Britaniya): Need I say more? (Yana gapirishimga hojat bormi?) — bu tayyor ibora sifatida uchraydi. Odatdagi nutqda: Do I need to ...? yoki Do I have to ...?

must va need to — farqi bormi?

Ikkalasi ham zaruratni bildiradi: I must go = I need to go. must — so‘zlovchining kuchli his-tuyg‘usi yoki buyrug‘i; need to — biror sababga ko‘ra kerakligi (ehtiyoj). Inkorda esa farq juda katta: mustn’t = taqiq, don’t need to = shart emas.

“You needn’t have bothered” nima degani?

Bu xushmuomala ibora: “Ovora bo‘lmasangiz ham bo‘lardi” — kimdir siz uchun biror ish qilgan bo‘lsa (sovg‘a olib kelgan, yordam bergan). Ma’nosi minnatdorlik bilan aralash: “bunga hojat yo‘q edi, lekin rahmat!”. Ba’zan qattiq ohangda aytilsa, “foydasi bo‘lmadi” degan norozilikni ham bildirishi mumkin — ohangga qarang.

“mustn’t” va “needn’t” — ikkalasi ham inkor, nega ma’nolari bunchalik farq qiladi?

Chunki “not” turli narsani inkor qiladi. mustn’t = must + not do → “qilmaslik zarur” (taqiq). needn’t = not necessary → “zarurat yo‘q” (ixtiyoriy). You mustn’t tell her — aytmang! You needn’t tell her — aytishingiz shart emas (aytsangiz ham bo‘ladi).

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga must, mustn’t yoki needn’t ni qo‘ying.

  1. The baby is sleeping. You play loud music.
  2. The museum is open until eight, so we hurry.
  3. It’s Mum’s birthday tomorrow. I remember to call her.
  4. You bring food to the party. There will be plenty.
  5. This medicine is dangerous. You take more than two tablets a day.
  6. The exam starts at nine. You be late.
  7. Aziz wear a suit to the party. It’s very informal.
  8. The road is very icy. You drive carefully.

2-mashq. needn’t li gaplarni don’t / doesn’t need to bilan qayta yozing.

  1. You needn’t worry. → You
  2. Kamola needn’t come tomorrow. → Kamola
  3. We needn’t buy any milk. → We

3-mashq. Vaziyatni o‘qing va needn’t have + V3 bilan gap tuzing.

  1. Sardor rushed to the airport, but his flight was delayed by three hours. (hurry) → He
  2. I bought a new phone charger, but then I found my old one in a drawer. (buy a new charger) → I
  3. Malika was worried about the interview, but it went very well. (worry) → She
  4. We booked a table at the restaurant, but it was almost empty. (book a table) → We
  5. Nodira wrote the report twice, but the first version was fine. (write it twice) → She

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang. Ish qilingan, lekin bekorga bo‘lsa — needn’t have + V3; ish umuman qilinmagan bo‘lsa — didn’t need to.

  1. It was a warm evening. I (needn’t have taken / didn’t need to take) a jacket, so I left it at home.
  2. I took a jacket, but it was hot all evening. I (needn’t have taken / didn’t need to take) it.
  3. Bekzod had already finished the work, so he (needn’t have stayed / didn’t need to stay) late. He went home at five.
  4. We cleaned the whole house, but the guests didn’t come. We (needn’t have cleaned / didn’t need to clean) it.

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Ertaga kelishingiz shart emas. →
  2. Bu haqda hech kimga aytmang (aytishingiz mumkin emas). →
  3. Bekorga shoshilibmiz — avtobus kechikdi. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • must = qiling (zarur); mustn’t = qilmang (taqiq).
  • needn’t + fe’l = don’t need to + fe’l = don’t have to + fe’l = shart emas.
  • needn’t to ❌, don’t need worry ❌ — “to” ni to‘g‘ri qo‘ying.
  • needn’t have + V3 = qildim, lekin bekorga (hojati yo‘q ekan).
  • didn’t need to = zarurat yo‘q edi (odatda qilinmagan, ba’zan qilingan).
  • Mehmonga: You needn’t have brought anything! — xushmuomala minnatdorlik.
