Comparison 1 (cheaper, more expensive etc.)

Qiyosiy daraja — 1 (cheaper, more expensive)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Qiyosiy daraja (comparative) qanday yasaladi: sifat + -er (cheaper) yoki more + sifat (more expensive).
  • Qaysi biri qachon ishlatiladi: sifatdagi bo‘g‘inlar soniga qarab.
  • Imlo qoidalari: nicer, bigger, happier.
  • Ravishlarning qiyosiy darajasi: faster, more carefully.
  • Qoidasiz shakllar: good → better, bad → worse, far → farther / further.
  • than bilan taqqoslash: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand.

Ikki narsani taqqoslaganda sifatning qiyosiy daraja shaklini ishlatamiz va taqqoslanayotgan ikkinchi narsadan oldin than qo‘yamiz.

O‘zbek tilida qiyosiy daraja -roq qo‘shimchasi bilan yasaladi va u barcha sifatlarga bir xil qo‘shiladi: kattaroq, arzonroq, qimmatroq, qiziqarliroq. Ikkinchi narsa -dan qo‘shimchasi bilan keladi: Toshkent Samarqanddan katta(roq). Ingliz tilida bu ikki tomon boshqacha: (1) sifat uzunligiga qarab yo -er, yo more tanlanadi; (2) “-dan” o‘rniga alohida so‘z than qo‘yiladi. Eslab qoling: -roq = -er yoki more (ikkalasi birga emas!).

-er yoki more? cheaper / more expensive: forming the comparative

Sifatning qiyosiy darajasi sifatning bo‘g‘inlar soniga qarab yasaladi. Bo‘g‘in (syllable) — bir marta ochilib aytiladigan tovush bo‘lagi: cheap (1), ea-sy (2), care-ful (2), ex-pen-sive (3).

Qiyosiy daraja: asosiy qoidalar
Sifat turiQiyosiy darajaMisollar
1 bo‘g‘inlisifat + -ercheap → cheaper, tall → taller, fast → faster, old → older
2 bo‘g‘inli, -y bilan tugaydi-iereasy → easier, busy → busier, heavy → heavier
2 bo‘g‘inli (boshqa)more + sifatcareful → more careful, modern → more modern, boring → more boring
3 va undan ortiq bo‘g‘inlimore + sifatexpensive → more expensive, interesting → more interesting, comfortable → more comfortable
2 bo‘g‘inli, ba’zilari (clever, narrow, quiet, simple, gentle, polite, common)ikkalasi ham mumkinclever → cleverer / more clever; narrow → narrower / more narrow; quiet → quieter / more quiet

-ed va -ing bilan tugaydigan sifatlar (tired, bored, boring, interesting) odatda more bilan keladi: more tired, more boring (tireder ❌).

  • July is hotter than May in Bukhara.Buxoroda iyul mayga qaraganda issiqroq.
  • This bag is cheaper than that one.Bu sumka anavisidan arzonroq.
  • A taxi is more expensive than a bus.Taksi avtobusdan qimmatroq.
  • The new bridge is more modern than the old one.Yangi ko‘prik eskisidan zamonaviyroq.
  • English is easier than I expected.Ingliz tili men o‘ylaganimdan osonroq.
  • Samarkand is higher above sea level than Tashkent.Samarqand dengiz sathidan Toshkentga qaraganda balandroqda joylashgan.

Ravishlarning qiyosiy darajasi Comparative of adverbs

Ravishlar (quickly, carefully, well) ham taqqoslanadi. Qoida o‘xshash:

Ravishlar
Ravish turiQiyosiy darajaMisollar
-ly bilan tugaydigan ravishlarmore + ravishslowly → more slowly, carefully → more carefully, quickly → more quickly; shuningdek often → more often
Sifat bilan bir xil shaklli ravishlar+ -erfast → faster, hard → harder, early → earlier, late → later, high → higher
Qoidasizwell → better, badly → worse, far → farther / further
  • Please speak more slowly. I’m learning English.Iltimos, sekinroq gapiring. Men ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman.
  • Sardor runs faster than Bekzod.Sardor Bekzoddan tezroq yuguradi.
  • You must work harder if you want to pass.O‘tmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, ko‘proq mehnat qilishingiz kerak.
  • Can you come earlier tomorrow?Ertaga ertaroq kela olasizmi?
  • Dilnoza drives more carefully than her brother.Dilnoza akasidan ehtiyotkorroq haydaydi.

O‘zbekchada ravish ham sifat kabi -roq oladi: sekinroq, tezroq, ehtiyotkorroq. Inglizchada esa -ly ravishlar more bilan keladi (more slowly), sifat shaklidagi ravishlar -er bilan (faster). Norasmiy nutqda Speak slower ham eshitiladi, lekin yozuvda more slowly to‘g‘ri.

Farther va further masofa haqida ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi. Lekin further yana “qo‘shimcha, yana” ma’nosini ham beradi: For further information, please call this number. (farther information ❌).

Better va worse ning o‘zi qiyosiy daraja, shuning uchun more better, more worse, gooder, badder ❌.

Less — “kamroq”

Less + sifat more ning teskarisi: less expensive (kamroq qimmat). Odatda uzun sifatlar bilan ishlatiladi:

  • This hotel is less expensive than the one near the Registan.Bu mehmonxona Registon yaqinidagidan kamroq qimmat (arzonroq).
  • The exam was less difficult than I thought.Imtihon men o‘ylaganimdan kamroq qiyin edi.

Qisqa sifatlar bilan less o‘rniga odatda qarama-qarshi sifat ishlatiladi: smaller (less big tabiiy emas), shorter (less long emas). Uzun sifatlarda ham qarama-qarshi so‘z qulay bo‘lishi mumkin: cheaper = less expensive.

Teng narsalarni taqqoslang

Taqqoslanayotgan narsalar bir xil turda bo‘lishi kerak. “Havo”ni “shahar” bilan taqqoslab bo‘lmaydi:

❌ Mantiqiy xato (imtihonda xato hisoblanadi)

  • The weather in Bukhara is hotter than Tashkent.

(havo ↔ shahar)

✅ To‘g‘ri

  • The weather in Bukhara is hotter than in Tashkent.
  • The weather in Bukhara is hotter than the weather in Tashkent.

O‘zbekcha “Samarqanddan kattaroq” — -dan. Inglizchada from emas, than: bigger than Samarkand ✅, bigger from Samarkand ❌. From esa different from kabi iboralarda keladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

This bag is more cheaper than that one.This bag is cheaper than that one.-er va more ni birga ishlatmang: bittasi yetadi.
My English is more better now.My English is better now.Better — o‘zi qiyosiy daraja.
He is more tall than me.He is taller than me. / He is taller than I am.Qisqa sifat (tall) → -er.
This book is interestinger than that one.This book is more interesting than that one.3 bo‘g‘inli sifat (in-ter-est-ing) → more.
Tashkent is bigger from Samarkand.Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand.O‘zbekcha “-dan” inglizchada than bilan beriladi, from bilan emas.
The weather here is hotter than Tashkent.The weather here is hotter than in Tashkent.Havoni shahar bilan emas, “Toshkentdagi havo” bilan taqqoslang.
She speaks more slow than me.She speaks more slowly than me. / She speaks more slowly than I do.Fe’lni ravish aniqlaydi: slow emas, slowly.
The weather is more bad today.The weather is worse today.Bad → worse (qoidasiz shakl).
Bukhara is hoter than Samarkand.Bukhara is hotter than Samarkand.1 bo‘g‘inli hot da undosh ikkilanadi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

-er ni ishlatsam bo‘ladimi yoki more ni? Qanday bilaman?

Bo‘g‘inlarni sanang. 1 bo‘g‘inli sifat → -er. -y bilan tugaydigan 2 bo‘g‘inli → -ier. Qolgan hamma uzunroq sifat → more. Ba’zi 2 bo‘g‘inli sifatlar (clever, narrow, quiet, simple, gentle, polite, common) ikkalasini ham oladi. 2 bo‘g‘inli, -y bilan tugamaydigan sifatda shubha bo‘lsa, more odatda xavfsiz tanlov.

Farther bilan further farqi nima?

Masofa haqida ikkalasi ham bo‘ladi: The station is farther / further than I thought. Faqat further “qo‘shimcha” ma’nosida ham keladi: further information, further delay. Shuning uchun further xavfsizroq tanlov.

Than me yoki than I am — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. He is taller than me — kundalik nutqda keng tarqalgan; He is taller than I am — yozuvda va imtihonda xavfsizroq. Batafsil — Unit 107.

Qiyosiy daraja faqat ikki narsa uchunmi?

Ko‘pincha ikki narsa taqqoslanadi, lekin birdan ko‘pi bilan ham taqqoslash mumkin: Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand and Bukhara. Uch va undan ko‘p narsadan eng kattasini aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, orttirma daraja kerak (Unit 108).

Less o‘rniga nima deyish mumkin?

Qisqa sifatlar uchun qarama-qarshi sifat: smaller, cheaper, shorter. Uzun sifatlar uchun less expensive / less interesting. Yana not as … as (Unit 107): not as expensive as.

More better kabi shakllar ba’zi eski matnlarda uchraydi-ku?

Ha, eski inglizchada (masalan, Shekspir asarlarida) more va -er ba’zan birga ishlatilgan (more braver). Hozirgi standart ingliz tilida bu xato hisoblanadi: better, braver.

Ravishning qiyosiy darajasi sifatnikidan farq qiladimi?

Yasalish qoidasi o‘xshash. Farq faqat ravish -ly bilan tugasa: more slowly (slowlier ❌). Ba’zi ravishlar sifat bilan bir xil shaklda va -er oladi: faster, harder, earlier (Unit 101).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • 1 bo‘g‘inli sifat + -er (cheaper); -y → -ier (easier); qolgan uzun sifatlar more + sifat (more expensive).
  • -er va more ni birga ishlatmang: more cheaper ❌.
  • Imlo: nicer (e tushadi), bigger (undosh ikkilanadi), happier (y → ier).
  • Qoidasiz: good/well → better, bad/badly → worse, far → farther/further.
  • Ravish: -ly → more slowly; sifat shaklidagi ravish → faster, harder, earlier.
  • Ikkinchi narsadan oldin than (from emas!): bigger than Samarkand.
  • Less + sifat — “kamroq”; taqqoslanayotgan narsalar bir turda bo‘lsin: than in Tashkent.
