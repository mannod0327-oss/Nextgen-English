Wish

Wish — istak va afsus
If and wish · Shart gaplar va wish

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • I wish I had ... — hozirgi vaziyatdan norozi bo‘lishni va “qani endi ...” deyishni: I wish I had a car.
  • I wish I had done ... — o‘tmishdagi ishdan afsuslanishni: I wish I had studied harder.
  • I wish ... would ... — birovning xatti-harakatidan shikoyat qilishni va biror narsa o‘zgarishini istashni: I wish the rain would stop.
  • I wish I could ... va was / were ning ishlatilishini.
  • wish bilan hope ning farqini hamda If only ... iborasini.

wish — “qani endi”, “koshki” degan ma’noni beradi. Siz haqiqatdan norozisiz va u boshqacha bo‘lishini istaysiz, lekin haqiqat o‘zgarmaydi. Shuning uchun wish dan keyin fe’l haqiqatga nisbatan bir qadam orqaga suriladi — xuddi Unit 39 va Unit 40 dagi if gaplaridagi kabi. Eng muhim narsa: fe’lning shakli o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa ham, ma’no ko‘pincha hozirgi vaqtga tegishli.

O‘zbek tilida istak va afsus -sa edi shakli bilan, ko‘pincha qani endi yoki koshki so‘zlari bilan beriladi. Qani endi mashinam bo‘lsa edi (hozir yo‘q) = I wish I had a car. Koshki kecha uyda qolgan bo‘lsam edi (o‘tmishga afsus) = I wish I had stayed at home yesterday. Koshki yomg‘ir to‘xtasa edi = I wish it would stop raining. O‘zbekchada bu uch ma’no uchun deyarli bir xil qolip (-sa edi) bor, ingliz tilida esa uch xil tuzilma: past simple, past perfect va would. Qaysi birini tanlashni vaqt va vaziyat hal qiladi.

I wish I had ... — hozirgi vaziyatdan norozilik wish + past simple

Vaziyat: Bekzod Samarqandda yashaydi, lekin u Toshkentda yashashni orzu qiladi. Haqiqat — u hozir Samarqandda yashaydi. Istak — u Toshkentda yashasa edi. U shunday deydi:

  • I wish I lived in Tashkent.Qani endi Toshkentda yashasam. (aslida Samarqandda yashayman)

Gapdagi lived o‘tgan zamon shaklida, lekin Bekzod o‘tmish haqida emas, hozir haqida gapiryapti. Bu — “haqiqatga zid o‘tgan zamon”: u real o‘tmishni emas, xayoliy hozirni bildiradi.

Umumiy xarita: haqiqat va istak

I+wish+ega+past simple / could / were

Qoida: Hozirgi (yoki kelasi) vaziyat haqida “qani endi ...” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, wish dan keyin past simple (had, lived, knew), could yoki were / was ishlating. wish dan keyin hozirgi zamon (have, can, am) kelmaydi: I wish I have a car ❌ — I wish I had a car ✅.

wish + past simple: haqiqat → istak
Haqiqat (hozir)IstakO‘zbekcha
I don’t have a bigger flat.I wish I had a bigger flat.Qani endi kattaroq xonadonim bo‘lsa.
I don’t know how to play the dutar.I wish I knew how to play the dutar.Qani endi dutor chalishni bilsam.
It’s very hot today.I wish it wasn’t / weren’t so hot today.Qani endi bugun bunchalik issiq bo‘lmasa.
Dilnoza lives far from the university.Dilnoza wishes she lived closer to the university.Dilnoza universitetga yaqinroq yashasa edi.
I have to work tomorrow.I wish I didn’t have to work tomorrow.Qani endi ertaga ishlashim shart bo‘lmasa.
You aren’t coming with us.I wish you were coming with us.Qani endi biz bilan kelsangiz.

wish + could

Hozir qila olmaydigan ishingizni orzu qilsangiz, could ishlatiladi. Bu could o‘tmish emas, “hozir qila olsam edi” ma’nosini beradi:

  • I wish I could play the piano.Qani endi pianino chala olsam. (chala olmayman)
  • I wish I could come to your party, but I’m working tomorrow evening.Qani endi ziyofatingizga kela olsam, lekin ertaga kechqurun ishlayman.
  • Do you ever wish you could fly?Siz ham ba’zan “qani endi ucha olsam” deb orzu qilasizmi?

I wish I can ... ❌ — to‘g‘risi I wish I could ... ✅. Shuningdek I wish I didn’t had to ... ❌ — I wish I didn’t have to ... ✅ (didn’t dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda turadi).

I wish I was yoki I wish I were?

wish dan keyin I / he / she / it bilan ham was, ham were ishlatish mumkin. were rasmiyroq va an’anaviy hisoblanadi, was esa suhbatda tez-tez uchraydi.

  • I wish I was / were taller.Qani endi bo‘yim balandroq bo‘lsa.
  • I wish it was / were Friday today.Qani endi bugun juma bo‘lsa.
  • I wish Malika was / were here with us.Qani endi Malika ham biz bilan shu yerda bo‘lsa.

Imtihonda yoki rasmiy yozuvda were ni tanlash xavfsizroq. Javoblar kalitida har ikkala variantni ham to‘g‘ri deb hisoblang.

I wish I had known ... — o‘tmishdan afsus wish + past perfect

Agar o‘tmishdagi ishdan afsuslansangiz, ya’ni “o‘shanda shunday bo‘lmaganidan” xafa bo‘lsangiz, wish dan keyin had + V3 (past perfect) ishlatiladi. Bu Unit 40 dagi If I had known ... bilan bir xil mantiq.

I+wish+ega+had (not)+V3
  • I wish I had studied harder at school.Qani endi maktabda ko‘proq o‘qigan bo‘lsam. (kam o‘qiganman)
  • I feel awful. I wish I hadn’t eaten so much plov.O‘zimni yomon his qilyapman. Qani endi shuncha ko‘p palov yemagan bo‘lsam.
  • Jasur wishes he had listened to his mother’s advice.Jasur onasining maslahatiga quloq solmaganidan afsuslanadi. (quloq solmagan)
  • I wish we had taken a taxi. Now we’re going to be late.Qani endi taksi olganimizda. Endi kech qolamiz.
  • I wish I could have gone to the wedding.Qani endi to‘yga bora olganimda. (bora olmadim)
Bir xil fe’l: hozir va o‘tmish
VaqtTuzilmaMisol
Hozirwish + past simpleI wish I knew the answer. Qani endi javobni bilsam.
O‘tmishwish + had V3I wish I had known the answer. Qani endi javobni bilgan bo‘lsam.
Hozirwish + couldI wish I could help you. Qani endi sizga yordam bera olsam.
O‘tmishwish + could have V3I wish I could have helped you. Qani endi sizga yordam bera olganimda.

wish dan keyin would have ishlatilmaydi: I wish I would have studied ❌ — I wish I had studied ✅. Qisqa shaklda ’d bu yerda had ni bildiradi: I wish I’d known = I wish I had known.

Ko‘p o‘quvchilar “o‘tmishda” so‘zini ko‘rishi bilan past simple ishlatib yuboradi: I wish I studied harder last year ❌. O‘tmishdan afsus — doim had + V3: I wish I had studied harder last year ✅.

I wish ... would ... — shikoyat va o‘zgarishni istash wish + would

Uchinchi tuzilma — wish + ega + would + fe’l. U ikki holatda ishlatiladi:

1. Birovning odatidan norozilik

Biror kishi doim bir ishni qiladi (yoki qilmaydi) va bu sizni bezovta qiladi. Siz u o‘zgarishini istaysiz:

  • The neighbours play loud music every night. I wish they would turn it down.Qo‘shnilar har kecha baland musiqa qo‘yadi. Qani endi ular uni pasaytirsa.
  • I wish you wouldn’t leave your dirty cups in the sink.Qani endi iflos piyolalaringizni rakovinada qoldirib ketmasangiz.
  • My teacher talks too fast. I wish she would speak more slowly.Ustozim juda tez gapiradi. Qani endi sekinroq gapirsa.

2. Biror narsa sodir bo‘lishini istash

Biror ish bo‘lishini yoki biror holat o‘zgarishini kutyapmiz, lekin u bo‘lmayapti. Bu yerda ega — odam ham, narsa ham (yomg‘ir, avtobus, telefon) bo‘lishi mumkin:

  • Nobody has replied to my message all day. I wish somebody would write back.Kun bo‘yi xabarimga hech kim javob bermadi. Qani endi kimdir javob yozsa.
  • It has been raining all week. I wish it would stop.Bir haftadan beri yomg‘ir yog‘yapti. Qani endi to‘xtasa.
  • It’s freezing at the bus stop. I wish the bus would come.Bekatda sovuq qotib ketdim. Qani endi avtobus kelsa.

I wish I would ... ❌ — o‘zingiz haqingizda wish ... would ishlatilmaydi. Bu yerda would “qilishga rozi bo‘lmoq” ma’nosida: boshqa kishining xohishiga ta’sir qila olmasangiz, “qani endi u qilsa” deysiz. O‘zingizning ishingiz bo‘lsa — shunchaki qilasiz. O‘zingiz haqingizdagi istakni past simple yoki could bilan ayting: I wish I had more patience ✅, I wish I could relax ✅.

Holat (situation) haqida — bo‘y, kimdadir biror narsa borligi, kimningdir qayerdaligi — would emas, past simple ishlatiladi: I wish I were taller ✅, I wish Malika was / were here now ✅ (I wish Malika would be here ❌). wish ... would esa harakatlar va o‘zgarishlar uchun: stop, come, answer, listen, turn off. Solishtiring: I wish Malika would come (kelishini istayman — harakat) va I wish Malika were here (shu yerda bo‘lishini istayman — holat).

wish ... would ko‘pincha jahl yoki norozilikni bildiradi. Muloyim iltimos uchun Could you turn the music down, please? (Unit 37) degan shaklni ishlating.

Uchala tuzilma: qaysi birini tanlash kerak?
VaziyatTuzilmaMisol
Hozirgi holat yoqmaydiwish + past simple / could / wereI wish I had more free time.
O‘tmishdagi ishdan afsuswish + had V3I wish I had gone to bed earlier.
Boshqaning xatti-harakatidan norozilik; o‘zgarishni istashwish + would (not) + fe’lI wish he would stop shouting.

wish va hope farqi wish or hope?

Ikkalasi ham “umid qilmoq, istamoq” deb tarjima bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin farqi katta:

hope — bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan narsa

Natija noma’lum yoki imkoniyat bor. Keyin oddiy zamon yoki will keladi.

  • I hope you have a good trip.
  • I hope it doesn’t rain tomorrow.
  • I hope Sardor passed the exam. (natijani hali bilmaymiz)

wish — haqiqatga zid yoki bo‘lmagan narsa

Haqiqat boshqacha ekanini bilamiz. Keyin past simple / had V3 / would keladi.

  • I wish you could stay longer. (qola olmaysiz)
  • I wish it would stop raining. (yog‘yapti)
  • I wish Sardor had passed the exam. (o‘ta olmadi)
  • I hope you can come to my birthday party.Umid qilamanki, tug‘ilgan kunimga kela olasiz. (kelishingiz mumkin)
  • I wish you could come to my birthday party, but I know you’re abroad.Qani endi tug‘ilgan kunimga kela olsangiz, lekin xorijda ekaningizni bilaman.

Kelajakdagi tilak bildirishda hope ishlatiladi: I hope you have a nice holiday ✅ — I wish you will have a nice holiday ❌.

If only ... va wish ning boshqa ma’nolari If only; wish somebody something; wish to

If only ...

If only ... — I wish ... ning kuchliroq, hissiyotliroq varianti (“bo‘lsa edi!”). Zamon shakllari wish dagidek:

If only ...I wish ...O‘zbekcha
If only I had more time!I wish I had more time.Qani endi vaqtim ko‘proq bo‘lsa!
If only I had listened to you!I wish I had listened to you.Qani endi sizga quloq solganimda!
If only it would stop raining!I wish it would stop raining.Qani endi yomg‘ir to‘xtasa!

wish + kishi + ot: tilak bildirish

Bu boshqa tuzilma: wish dan keyin kishi va ot keladi, fe’l shaklini o‘zgartirish kerak emas. Ma’nosi — “tilamoq”:

  • I wish you a happy birthday!Tug‘ilgan kuningiz muborak bo‘lsin! (so‘zma-so‘z: sizga baxtli tug‘ilgan kun tilayman)
  • We wish all our students every success in the exam.Barcha o‘quvchilarimizga imtihonda omad tilaymiz.
  • She wished me good luck before the interview.U suhbatdan oldin menga omad tiladi.

I wish to ... — rasmiy “xohlamoq”

  • I wish to speak to the manager, please.Menejer bilan gaplashmoqchiman. (rasmiy uslub)

Bu yerda wish to = want to, lekin rasmiyroq. Bunda afsus yoki xayoliy holat yo‘q, shuning uchun “bir qadam orqaga” qoidasi ham ishlamaydi.

I wish I have a car.I wish I had a car.wish dan keyin hozirgi zamon emas, past simple keladi.
I wish I would have a car.I wish I had a car.O‘zingiz haqingizda wish + would ishlatilmaydi.
I wish I can swim.I wish I could swim.can → could: istak haqiqatga zid bo‘lgani uchun bir qadam orqaga.
I wish I studied harder last year.I wish I had studied harder last year.O‘tmishdan afsus — had + V3.
I wish I would have studied harder.I wish I had studied harder.wish dan keyin would have ishlatilmaydi.
I wish it stops raining. / I wish it will stop raining.I wish it would stop raining.Biror narsa o‘zgarishini istasak — wish + would + fe’l.
I wish you are here.I wish you were here.“Siz shu yerda emassiz” — are emas, were.
I wish I didn’t had to work.I wish I didn’t have to work.didn’t dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda: have (had emas).
I wish you will have a nice trip.I hope you have a nice trip.Real tilak uchun hope ishlatiladi, wish emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “I wish I had a car” da o‘tgan zamon bor, lekin ma’no hozirgi?

Chunki istak haqiqatga zid. Ingliz tilida haqiqatga zid narsalar haqida gapirganda fe’l bir qadam “orqaga” suriladi: hozirgi vaziyat → past simple, o‘tmishdagi vaziyat → past perfect. Bu If I had a car, I would ... (Unit 39) dagi mantiq bilan bir xil. had bu yerda o‘tmish emas, “xayoliy hozir”ni bildiradi.

“I wish I was” va “I wish I were” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. were an’anaviy va rasmiyroq, was esa suhbatda ko‘proq uchraydi. Boshqa egalar bilan farq yo‘q: I wish we were / you were / they were ... Imtihonda ikkala variantni ham yozish mumkin; were xavfsizroq.

Nega “I wish I would have a car” deb bo‘lmaydi?

wish ... would boshqa kishining (yoki narsaning) xatti-harakati o‘zgarishini istashda ishlatiladi, chunki bu yerda would “rozi bo‘lmoq, xohlamoq” ma’nosini bildiradi. Mashinaning bor-yo‘qligi esa holat, shuning uchun I wish I had a car deyiladi.

“I wish” va “If only” orasida farq bormi?

Grammatika bir xil, faqat If only kuchliroq hissiyotni beradi (hasrat, afsus, umidsizlik) va ko‘pincha undov belgisi bilan yoziladi: If only I had more time! Oddiy gapirishda I wish ko‘proq ishlatiladi.

“I want a car” bilan “I wish I had a car” farqi nima?

I want a car — oddiy istak, uni amalga oshirish mumkin deb o‘ylaysiz (hozircha yo‘q, lekin olishingiz mumkin). I wish I had a car — haqiqatda mashinangiz yo‘q va yaqin orada bo‘lmaydi; siz shu holatdan afsuslanyapsiz yoki orzu qilyapsiz.

wish ning o‘zi o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa-chi (He wished ...)?

Unda ham xuddi shu shakl saqlanadi: When I was a child, I wished I could fly. (Bolaligimda uchishni orzu qilardim.) She wished she had a bigger room. (U kattaroq xonasi bo‘lishini istar edi.) Orzu qilgan paytdan ham oldin sodir bo‘lgan voqeaga afsus qilinsa, had + V3 keladi: He wished he had told her the truth.

O‘zbekcha “qani endi ...” ni inglizchaga qanday o‘giraman?

Avval vaqtni aniqlang. Hozirgi holat: Qani endi uyda bo‘lsam → I wish I were at home. O‘tmishdagi holat (kecha, o‘tgan yili): Qani endi kecha uyda qolganimda → I wish I had stayed at home. Biror kishi/narsa o‘zgarsin desangiz: Qani endi u to‘xtasa → I wish he would stop.

“I wish you a happy birthday” dagi wish ham xuddi shu qoidaga bo‘ysunadimi?

Yo‘q. Bu — boshqa tuzilma: wish + kishi + ot (“tilamoq”). Unda zamon siljishi yo‘q. Fe’l shaklini o‘zgartirish kerak bo‘lgan wish + gap tuzilmasi faqat afsus yoki xayoliy istak haqida.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Hozirgi vaziyat haqida wish-gapni to‘ldiring (past simple, could yoki were / was).

  1. I don’t have a laptop. → I wish I a laptop. (have)
  2. Sardor can’t drive. → Sardor wishes he drive.
  3. It’s very cold today. → I wish it so cold. (not / be)
  4. Nodira lives far from her friends. → She wishes she closer to them. (live)
  5. I have to get up at five tomorrow. → I wish I get up at five.
  6. I don’t know the answer. → I wish I the answer. (know)
  7. We aren’t going to Bukhara this weekend. → I wish we to Bukhara this weekend. (go)
  8. Malika is shy. → Malika wishes she so shy. (not / be)

2-mashq. O‘tmishdagi afsusni wish + had V3 bilan bildiring.

  1. I forgot Malika’s birthday. → I wish I it. (not / forget)
  2. We didn’t take a taxi, so we were late. → I wish we a taxi. (take)
  3. Bekzod said something rude to his teacher. → Bekzod wishes he that. (not / say)
  4. The weather was terrible on our trip. → I wish the weather better. (be)
  5. I didn’t go to the concert yesterday. → I wish I to the concert. (go)
  6. Dilnoza lost her phone on the bus. → She wishes she it at home. (leave)
  7. I didn’t help you yesterday. → I wish I you. (help)
  8. Jasur didn’t listen to his mother. → He wishes he to her. (listen)

3-mashq. wish + would yordamida norozilikni bildiring.

  1. The neighbours play loud music every night. → I wish the neighbours . (turn the music down)
  2. A taxi driver keeps hooting outside and nobody is telling him to stop. → I wish somebody him to stop. (tell)
  3. It has rained all week. → I wish it raining. (stop)
  4. Aziz comes late every time. → I wish Aziz late every time. (not / come)
  5. The teacher speaks too fast. → I wish she more slowly. (speak)
  6. You leave your shoes in the hall. → I wish you your shoes in the hall. (not / leave)
  7. The bus is very late and it’s cold. → I wish the bus . (come)

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. I wish I (have / had / would have) a bigger flat. →
  2. I wish I (can / could / would) play the dutar. →
  3. I wish I (didn’t eat / hadn’t eaten / wouldn’t eat) so much plov yesterday. →
  4. I wish it (stops / will stop / would stop) raining. →
  5. I wish I (am / was / would be) taller. →
  6. I wish you (are / were / would be) here with us now. →
  7. I wish I (would have listened / had listened) to my father. →
  8. I (wish / hope) you have a lovely holiday. →
  9. I (wish / hope) I could come to your party, but I’m busy. →

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • wish — haqiqatdan norozilik: fe’l shakli haqiqatga nisbatan bir qadam orqaga suriladi.
  • Hozirgi vaziyat: wish + past simple / could / were — I wish I had a car. I wish I could swim. (I wish I have / can ❌)
  • O‘tmishdan afsus: wish + had (not) + V3 — I wish I had studied harder. (wish ... would have ❌)
  • Birovning odatidan norozilik yoki o‘zgarishni istash: wish + would (not) + fe’l — I wish he would stop. O‘zingiz haqingizda I wish I would ❌.
  • was va were ikkalasi ham mumkin: I wish I was / were taller.
  • hope — bo‘lishi mumkin narsa (I hope you have a nice trip); wish — haqiqatga zid narsa.
  • If only ... = kuchliroq I wish ...; I wish you luck = “omad tilayman” (boshqa tuzilma).
