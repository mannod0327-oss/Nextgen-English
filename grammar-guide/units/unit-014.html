Present perfect and past 2 (I have done and I did)

Present perfect va past simple — 2
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Nega tugagan vaqt (yesterday, last week, in 2019, two days ago) bilan present perfect ishlatib bo‘lmasligini.
  • When ...? va What time ...? savollari nega doim past simple bilan kelishini.
  • Hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr (today, this week, since ..., for ..., recently, never) bilan present perfect ishlatilishini.
  • this morning, today, for an hour kabi iboralar bilan ikkala zamon ham qachon to‘g‘ri bo‘lishini.
  • Signal so‘zlar ro‘yxatini va eng ko‘p uchraydigan xatolarni.

Asosiy g‘oya: zamonni tanlashda vaqt oralig‘iga qarang. Agar gapdagi vaqt tugagan bo‘lsa (kecha, o‘tgan yil, 2019-yil) — past simple. Agar vaqt oralig‘i hozirgacha davom etsa (bugun, shu hafta, hayotim davomida, 2019-yildan beri) — present perfect.

O‘zbek tilida “Malikani kecha ko‘rdim” va “Malikani bu hafta ko‘rmadim” — ikkalasi ham -di bilan. Ingliz tilida esa ular har xil: I saw Malika yesterday (kecha — tugagan) — I haven’t seen Malika this week (bu hafta — hali tugamagan). O‘zbekchadagi “ko‘rganman” (tajriba ma’nosida) esa ko‘pincha I have seen ga mos keladi: Samarqandda bo‘lganman = I’ve been to Samarkand.

Tugagan vaqt bilan — faqat past simple Finished time: yesterday, last week, in 2019 …

Agar gapda tugagan vaqt ko‘rsatilgan bo‘lsa, present perfect ishlatilmaydi. Present perfect hozirni o‘z ichiga oladi, “kecha” esa hozirni o‘z ichiga olmaydi — ular bir-biriga zid.

Tugagan vaqtni bildiruvchi so‘zlar (→ past simple):
yesterdaylast nightlast week / yeartwo days agoin 2019on Mondayat 5 o’clockwhen I was a childjust nowthe other daythen
  • The shops were very busy yesterday. (not have been)Kecha do‘konlarda odam juda ko‘p edi.
  • Our guests left twenty minutes ago. (not have left)Mehmonlarimiz yigirma daqiqa oldin ketishdi.
  • Did you learn to swim when you were little? (not have you learnt)Kichikligingizda suzishni o‘rganganmisiz?
  • We had a big family dinner on Friday. Everyone came, and we talked until midnight.Juma kuni katta oilaviy kechki ovqat qildik. Hamma keldi va yarim tungacha gaplashdik.
  • I saw Jasur just now. He’s in the library.Jasurni hozirgina ko‘rdim. U kutubxonada.

When ...? va What time ...? — past simple bilan

Bu savollar aniq vaqtni so‘raydi, aniq vaqt esa doim tugagan nuqta. Shuning uchun:

  • When did the parcel arrive? (not has the parcel arrived)Posilka qachon keldi?
  • What time did the match end last night?Kecha o‘yin soat nechada tugadi?
  • When did you last go to the cinema?Oxirgi marta qachon kinoga borgansiz?

Solishtiring

Present perfect — vaqt ko‘rsatilmagan, natija hozir

  • Jasur has broken his glasses. He can’t read the board.

Jasur ko‘zoynagini sindirib qo‘ydi. Doskani o‘qiy olmayapti.

Past simple — tugagan vaqt

  • Jasur broke his glasses last week. He couldn’t read the board for two days.

Jasur o‘tgan hafta ko‘zoynagini sindirdi. Ikki kun doskani o‘qiy olmadi.

Qoida: Tugagan vaqt (yesterday, ... ago, last ..., in 2019, when ...) ko‘rsatilgan yoki nazarda tutilgan bo‘lsa — past simple. When / What time bilan savol — past simple. ❌ I have seen him yesterday. ❌ When have you arrived?

Hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr va tugagan davr Present perfect (have done) and past simple (did) compared

Present perfect — hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr haqida (today, this week, since 2019, for an hour, recently, never — “hayotim davomida”). Past simple — tugagan davr haqida (yesterday, last week, from 2015 to 2019).

Bir xil fe’l — ikki xil davr
Present perfect (davr hozirgacha davom etmoqda)Past simple (davr tugagan)
Kamola has made three cakes today.
Kamola bugun uchta tort pishirdi.		Kamola made three cakes on Saturday.
Kamola shanba kuni uchta tort pishirdi.
We haven’t had any visitors this month.
Bu oy bizga hech kim mehmonga kelmadi.		We had a lot of visitors last month.
O‘tgan oy bizga ko‘p mehmon keldi.
Has Aziz called this morning? (hozir ham ertalab)Did Aziz call this morning? (hozir tushdan keyin)
Have you been to the new park recently?Did you go to the new park on Sunday?
The baby has been crying for an hour. (hali yig‘layapti)The baby cried / was crying for an hour, then fell asleep. (tugagan)
Nodira hasn’t answered my message. (hali javob yo‘q)Nodira didn’t answer my message yesterday, but she called me this morning.
My aunt teaches in Namangan. She has taught there for twenty years.My aunt taught in Kokand for six years. Then she moved to Namangan.
I’ve never tried qazi. (hayotim davomida)I didn’t try the qazi at the wedding — I wasn’t hungry.
Kursning oxirgi darsida: This course has been really useful. I’ve learnt a lot.Kurs tugaganidan bir oy keyin: The course was really useful. I learnt a lot.

“this morning” — soatga qarang!

this morning, this afternoon, today kabi iboralarda zamon gapirayotgan vaqtingizga bog‘liq. Agar ertalab hali tugamagan bo‘lsa — present perfect; ertalab o‘tib ketgan bo‘lsa — past simple.

  • (11 a.m.) I’ve drunk three cups of coffee this morning.(Soat 11) Bugun ertalab uch finjon qahva ichdim. (Ertalab hali davom etyapti.)
  • (4 p.m.) I drank three cups of coffee this morning.(Soat 16) Bugun ertalab uch finjon qahva ichdim. (Ertalab tugagan.)

O‘zbekchada “Samarqandda bo‘lganmisiz?” (hayotingiz davomida) va “O‘tgan yili Samarqandga borganmisiz?” deb so‘raymiz — ikkalasida ham -gan. Inglizchada ular farq qiladi: Have you been to Samarkand? (davr — butun hayotingiz, hali tugamagan) — Did you go to Samarkand last year? (o‘tgan yil — tugagan).

for ikkala zamon bilan ham keladi! Farq — davr tugaganmi yoki yo‘qmi: I’ve lived here for ten years (hali ham shu yerda yashayman) — I lived there for ten years (endi u yerda yashamayman).

just va just now ni farqlang: just (hozirgina) odatda present perfect bilan: I’ve just seen Jasur. just now (bir lahza oldin) — past simple bilan: I saw Jasur just now. Amerika inglizchasida just ham ko‘pincha past simple bilan keladi: I just saw Jasur.

Vafot etgan odamlar va tugagan davrlar

Inson hayoti tugagan bo‘lsa, uning tajribasi haqida past simple ishlatiladi: My grandfather travelled a lot. He visited twenty countries. Tirik odam haqida esa: My father has travelled a lot. He has visited twenty countries.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I have seen Aziz yesterday.I saw Aziz yesterday.“Yesterday” — tugagan vaqt, present perfect bilan ishlatilmaydi.
When have you arrived?When did you arrive?When / What time savollari — doim past simple.
I’ve finished school in 2018.I finished school in 2018.“In 2018” — aniq, tugagan vaqt.
Have you been to Samarkand last year?Did you go to Samarkand last year? / Were you in Samarkand last year?“Last year” bilan present perfect bo‘lmaydi. “Have you been...?” — vaqt ko‘rsatilmagan tajriba savoli.
I didn’t see Malika this week. (bugun chorshanba)I haven’t seen Malika this week.“This week” hali tugamagan → present perfect. (Amerika og‘zaki nutqida past simple ham eshitiladi, lekin standart britancha shakl — present perfect.)
It was a great trip so far.It has been (It’s been) a great trip so far.“So far” = hozirgacha → present perfect.
My aunt has taught in Kokand for six years, but now she teaches in Namangan.My aunt taught in Kokand for six years, but now she teaches in Namangan.Qo‘qondagi davr tugagan → past simple (for bilan ham).
I’ve seen him just now.I saw him just now. / I’ve just seen him.“Just now” = bir lahza oldin (tugagan) → past simple; “just” → present perfect.
What time have you got up today?What time did you get up today?“What time” — aniq vaqt; turish voqeasi tugagan.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“today” bilan qaysi zamon ishlatiladi?

Ikkalasi ham, ma’noga qarab. Bugungi kun hali tugamagan, shuning uchun “bugun hozirgacha” natijasi haqida — present perfect: I’ve had three lessons today. Lekin bugun ichida aniq, tugagan vaqtdagi voqea haqida — past simple: I got up at six today. I had a big breakfast. (Turish va nonushta allaqachon tugagan, biz ularning aniq vaqtini nazarda tutyapmiz.)

“for” bilan past simple ishlatish mumkinmi?

Ha. for faqat davr uzunligini bildiradi; zamon esa davr tugagan-tugamaganiga bog‘liq. We’ve been waiting for an hour (hali kutyapmiz) — We waited for an hour and then went home (kutish tugagan).

Nega “When have you arrived?” xato? “Arrived” natijasi hozir ham bor-ku — siz shu yerdasiz.

Chunki When so‘zining o‘zi aniq vaqtni — o‘tmishdagi bitta nuqtani so‘raydi. Aniq o‘tgan nuqta = past simple. “Natija hozir ham bor” degan ma’no bu yerda muhim emas, chunki savol vaqt haqida. When did you arrive? To‘g‘ri savollar: Have you arrived? (keldingizmi?) va When did you arrive? (qachon keldingiz?).

“I’ve been to Samarkand” va “I went to Samarkand” — farqi nima?

I’ve been to Samarkand — hayotimdagi tajriba, vaqt muhim emas va aytilmaydi. I went to Samarkand — aniq bir safar haqida, odatda vaqt aytiladi yoki ma’lum: I went to Samarkand last spring. Odatda suhbat shunday boradi: Have you ever been to Samarkand? — Yes, I have. I went there in 2022.

“recently” bilan qaysi zamon?

Ikkalasi ham bo‘ladi. “So‘nggi paytlarda (hozirgacha)” ma’nosida — present perfect: Have you seen Malika recently? “Yaqinda, ko‘p o‘tmay” ma’nosida, aniq bir voqea haqida — past simple ham to‘g‘ri: I recently moved to Tashkent. / I moved to Tashkent recently.

Amerikaliklar “Did you eat yet?” deyishadi. Bu xato emasmi?

Amerika inglizchasida bu odatiy: yet, already, just bilan ko‘pincha past simple ishlatiladi (Did you eat yet? I already did it. I just saw him.). Britaniya inglizchasida esa present perfect: Have you eaten yet? I’ve already done it. I’ve just seen him. Lekin tugagan vaqt bilan present perfect (❌ I have seen him yesterday) — ikkala variantda ham xato.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Tugagan vaqt (yesterday, last week, ... ago, in 2019, when I was a child, just now) → past simple. I have seen him yesterday ❌
  • When ...? / What time ...? → doim past simple: When did you arrive?
  • Hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr (today, this week, since, recently, so far, ever, never) → present perfect.
  • this morning / today: davr hali tugamagan bo‘lsa — present perfect; tugagan bo‘lsa yoki aniq vaqt nazarda tutilsa — past simple.
  • for ikkala zamon bilan: I’ve lived here for ten years (hali ham) / I lived there for ten years (endi emas).
  • Have you (ever) been ...? — tajriba; Did you go ... last year? — aniq tugagan vaqt.
