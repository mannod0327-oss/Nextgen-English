Phrasal verbs 8: up (2)

Frazali fe’llar — 8: up (2)
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • up bilan yasalgan frazali fe’llarning ikkinchi guruhini: uy ishlari (tidy up, clean up, clear up, wash up), ishlatib yoki yeb bo‘lish va pul yig‘ish (use up, eat up, save up), tuzatish, mahkamlash va zaxira nusxa (fix up, do up, back up).
  • Munosabatlar va his-tuyg‘ularga oid fe’llarni: break up / split up, cheer up, blow up, beat up, tear up.
  • Ma’lumot, adashish, kechikish va mehmon qilishga oid fe’llarni: look up, mix up, hold up, put sb up.
  • Qaysi fe’l to‘ldiruvchisiz (∅), qaysi biri ajraladi (⇄), qaysi biri ajralmaydi (✗) va olmosh qayerga tushishini: tidy it up, cheer her up.
  • Inglizcha up ning o‘zbekchadagi yordamchi fe’llarga (-ib qo‘ymoq, -ib bo‘lmoq, -ib yubormoq) qanday mos kelishini.

Unit 143 da up ko‘proq “yaqinlashmoq, hamqadam bo‘lmoq, paydo bo‘lmoq” ma’nolarini berdi. Bu unitdagi ko‘p fe’llarda esa up ishning oxirigacha, to‘liq bajarilganini ko‘rsatadi: eat up (hammasini yeb bo‘lmoq), use up (ishlatib bo‘lmoq), tidy up (hammasini tartibga keltirmoq). Lekin up har doim ham “to‘liq” degani emas: look up, hold up, put sb up kabi fe’llarning ma’nosi butun birikmadan chiqadi. Shuning uchun har bir fe’lni misol bilan, bitta so‘z kabi o‘rganing. Umumiy qoidalar — Unit 137 da.

Belgilar: ∅ — to‘ldiruvchisiz; ⇄ — ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: tidy it up); ✗ — ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida). (norasmiy) deb belgilangan fe’llar kundalik suhbatda tabiiy, rasmiy yozuvda esa ko‘pincha boshqa so‘z tanlanadi.

O‘zbek tilida tugallanganlik ma’nosi fe’lga qo‘shiladigan yordamchi fe’l bilan beriladi: yig‘ishtirib qo‘ymoq (tidy up), tozalab qo‘ymoq (clean up), ishlatib bo‘lmoq (use up), yeb bitirmoq (eat up), adashtirib yubormoq (mix up). Inglizchada shu vazifani fe’ldan keyingi up bajaradi. Solishtiring: Eat your plov — “Palovingni ye” (jarayon), lekin Eat up your plov — “Palovingni oxirigacha yeb tugat”.

Tuzoq: o‘zbekcha yig‘moq ham “pul yig‘moq”, ham “yig‘ishtirmoq”da keladi, inglizchada esa bular boshqa-boshqa fe’l: pulni asta-sekin yig‘ish — save up; xonani tartibga keltirish — tidy up. Collect esa narsalarni to‘plash (markalar, tangalar) yoki boshqalardan pul yig‘ish (collect money for charity) uchun ishlatiladi — o‘zingiz uchun asta-sekin pul jamg‘arish uchun emas.

Tartib va tozalik: uy ishlari tidy up, clean up, clear up, wash up

tidy up

  • There are books and papers all over the floor. Please tidy them up before dinner.Polda kitoblar va qog‘ozlar sochilib yotibdi. Iltimos, kechki ovqatdan oldin ularni yig‘ishtirib qo‘ying.

  • After the lesson, the children tidied up the classroom.Darsdan keyin bolalar sinfni tartibga keltirishdi.

clean up

  • Sardor spilled tea on the carpet and cleaned it up with a cloth.Sardor gilamga choy to‘kib yubordi va uni mato bilan artib tozaladi.
  • After the wedding party we spent two hours cleaning up.To‘y ziyofatidan keyin ikki soat tozalash bilan band bo‘ldik.
  • Volunteers cleaned up the park after the festival.Ko‘ngillilar bayramdan keyin parkni tozalashdi.

clear up

  • When the party was over, we cleared up and went to bed.Ziyofat tugagach, hammayoqni yig‘ishtirib, uxlagani yotdik.
  • Let’s wait a little — the sky over Chimgan is starting to clear up.Biroz kutib turaylik — Chimyon ustidagi osmon ochila boshladi.

wash up

  • My mother cooked dinner, so I’ll wash up.Onam kechki ovqat pishirdi, shuning uchun idish-tovoqni men yuvaman.
  • Who’s going to wash up tonight?Bugun kechqurun idish-tovoqni kim yuvadi?

  • (AmE) “Go and wash up before lunch — your hands are dirty!”(AmE) “Tushlikdan oldin borib qo‘l-betingni yuv — qo‘llaring kirlangan!”

tidy up 🧺

Narsalarni tartibga solish: kitoblar, o‘yinchoqlar, qog‘ozlar. Natija: tartibli (neat).

  • tidy up the books and papers
  • tidy up your desk

clean up 🧽

Iflosni yo‘qotish: to‘kilgan suyuqlik, axlat, kir. Natija: toza (clean).

  • clean up the spilt milk
  • clean up the river bank

wash up ning ikki ma’nosi mintaqaga bog‘liq: Britaniya inglizchasida — idish-tovoq yuvish, Amerika inglizchasida — qo‘l-betni yuvish. Idish-tovoqni nomlab aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, oddiygina wash the dishes deng; mashinani, kiyimni, qo‘lni yuvish uchun ham wash up emas, oddiy wash ishlatiladi: wash the car, wash your hands (✅), wash up the car (❌).

tidy va clean o‘zi ham fe’l: Tidy your room! / Clean the window. up qo‘shilganda ish “hammasini, oxirigacha” qilinadigandek eshitiladi. Spelling: tidy – tidied – tidying (y → ied).

Ishlatib va yeb bo‘lish, pul yig‘ish use up, eat up, save up (for)

Bu guruhda up “hammasi” degan tasavvurni beradi: use up va eat up — hech narsa qolmaguncha; save up — idish asta-sekin to‘lib borguncha.

B guruh: ishlatib bo‘lish va yig‘ish
Fe’lMa’nosiTo‘ldiruvchiBirikma

eat up1) ovqatni oxirigacha yeb bo‘lmoq;
2) (pul, vaqt, joyni) ko‘p qismini yutib yubormoq		∅ / ⇄Eat up! / eat it up

use up

  • Who has used up all the milk? There is none left for my tea.Sutning hammasini kim ishlatib qo‘ydi? Choyim uchun hech narsa qolmabdi.
  • I bought a big bag of rice, and now I have almost used it up.Katta xalta guruch olgandim, endi uni deyarli ishlatib bo‘ldim.
  • We used up the paint before we finished the second wall.Ikkinchi devorni tugatmasimizdan bo‘yoq tugab qoldi.

eat up

  • Come on, children, eat up! The plov is getting cold.Qani, bolalar, yeb oling! Palov sovib qolyapti.
  • My grandmother will be happy if you eat it all up.Hammasini yeb tugatsangiz, buvim xursand bo‘ladi.
  • The new project ate up all our free time.Yangi loyiha butun bo‘sh vaqtimizni yeb qo‘ydi.

save up

  • Dilnoza is saving up for a new laptop.Dilnoza yangi noutbuk uchun pul yig‘yapti.
  • I’ve been saving up to buy a bike since March.Mart oyidan beri velosiped sotib olish uchun pul yig‘yapman.
  • Sardor saved up enough money in a year to visit Istanbul.Sardor bir yilda Istanbulga borish uchun yetarli pul yig‘di.

use va use up ni aralashtirmang: I used the flour — unni ishlatdim (qolgan bo‘lishi mumkin); I used up the flour — unning hammasini ishlatib bo‘ldim (hech narsa qolmadi). Xuddi shunday eat — yemoq, eat up — oxirigacha yemoq. eat up imperativda ko‘pincha bolalarga aytiladi: Eat up!

save up bilan save deyarli bir xil: I’m saving for a bike va I’m saving up for a bike — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri; up “asta-sekin to‘plab borish”ni ta’kidlaydi. Odatda for + ot yoki to + fe’l keladi. Pul to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lsa, particle’dan keyin turadi: save up money for a trip. Spelling: save – saving, use – using.

Tuzatish, mahkamlash, zaxira fix up, do up, back up

Bu guruhdagi fe’llarning hammasi to‘ldiruvchi bilan keladi va ajraladi. Har birining bir nechta ma’nosi bor, shuning uchun jadvalni diqqat bilan o‘qing.

fix up

  • Let’s fix up a time to meet next week.Kelasi hafta uchrashadigan vaqtni kelishib olaylik.
  • Malika has fixed up an appointment with the dentist for Thursday.Malika payshanbaga tish shifokori qabuliga yozilib qo‘ydi.
  • They bought an old house in the village and fixed it up themselves.Ular qishloqdan eski uy sotib olib, uni o‘zlari tuzatib yangilashdi.
  • Can you fix me up with a room for the night?Menga bir kechalik joy topib bera olasizmi?

do up

  • Bekzod did up the buttons of his new shirt and looked in the mirror.Bekzod yangi ko‘ylagining tugmalarini qadab, oynaga qaradi.
  • The little girl couldn’t do up her shoelaces.Kichkina qiz oyoq kiyimining bog‘ichlarini bog‘lay olmadi.
  • Wait, my zip is open. Can you do it up for me?Kuting, molniyam ochiq qolibdi. Uni tortib qo‘ya olasizmi?
  • They bought an old farmhouse near Samarkand and did it up.Ular Samarqand yaqinidan eski qishloq uyini sotib olib, uni ta’mirlab, chiroyli qilishdi.

back up

  • Always back up your files before you update the computer.Kompyuterni yangilashdan oldin fayllaringizdan doim zaxira nusxa oling.
  • I lost my phone, but luckily I had backed everything up.Telefonimni yo‘qotdim, yaxshiyamki, hamma narsadan zaxira nusxa olgan edim.
  • Aziz said he had been at home all evening, and his sister backed him up.Aziz kechqurun uyda bo‘lganini aytdi, singlisi uning gapini tasdiqladi.

Ot shakllari: fe’l ikki so‘z, ot esa bitta so‘z (yoki defis bilan): Back up your photos (fe’l) — Do you have a backup? (ot, zaxira nusxa). Amerika inglizchasida back up avtomobilni “orqaga yurgizmoq” ma’nosida ham ishlatiladi: Back up a little — you’re too close to the wall. Britaniya inglizchasida bunga reverse deyiladi.

do up ning qarama-qarshisi — undo: undo your buttons (tugmalarni yechmoq). Amerika inglizchasida tugma/zip uchun odatda button up, zip up, fasten ishlatiladi; eski uyni yangilash uchun esa fix up.

break up va split up

Aziz and Kamola+broke up = Aziz+broke up+with+Kamola
  • Aziz and Kamola broke up last month, but they are still friends.Aziz bilan Kamola o‘tgan oy ajralishdi, lekin hamon do‘st.
  • Nodira split up with her boyfriend after two years.Nodira ikki yildan keyin yigiti bilan ajrashdi.
  • The band broke up in 2015 after ten years together.Guruh o‘n yil birga bo‘lgandan keyin 2015-yilda tarqalib ketdi.
  • Let’s split up: you check the library, and I’ll check the café.Ikki tomonga ajralaylik: sen kutubxonaga qara, men kafeni ko‘rib chiqaman.

cheer up

  • Cheer up, Sardor! You’ll pass the exam next time.Ko‘nglingni ko‘tar, Sardor! Keyingi safar imtihondan o‘tasan.
  • Malika took her sister to the cinema to cheer her up.Malika singlisining ko‘nglini ko‘tarish uchun uni kinoga olib bordi.
  • She cheered up when she saw the flowers.U gullarni ko‘rib, xursand bo‘lib ketdi.

blow up

1. portlamoq 💥

Portlash natijasida yo‘q bo‘lmoq yoki yo‘q qilmoq.

  • Workers used explosives to blow up the old chimney.
  • A gas cylinder blew up in the garage.

2. shishirmoq 🎈

Havo bilan to‘ldirmoq: shar, suzish halqasi, havo to‘shagi.

  • Can you help me blow up these balloons?
  • She blew the balloon up and tied it.

3. jahli chiqmoq 😡

(norasmiy) Birdan qattiq g‘azablanib, baqirib yubormoq.

  • My father blew up when he saw the broken window.
  • She blew up at me for being late.

beat up

  • Two men beat up the shop owner and ran away with the money.Ikki kishi do‘kon egasini do‘pposlab, pul bilan qochib ketdi.
  • A group of older boys beat him up behind the school.Kattaroq bolalar guruhi uni maktab orqasida do‘pposlashdi.
  • Don’t beat yourself up about the mistake. Everybody makes mistakes. (norasmiy)Xato uchun o‘zingni ko‘p koyima. Xato qilmaydigan odam yo‘q.

tear up

tear up — qog‘oz, xat, rasm yoki hujjatni mayda-mayda qilib yirtib tashlamoq. Ajraladi (⇄): tear up the letter / tear the letter up / tear it up. Fe’l shakllari: tear – tore – torn.

  • Kamola was so angry that she tore up his letter without reading it.Kamolaning jahli chiqqanidan xatni o‘qimasdan yirtib tashladi.
  • I made a mistake on the form, so I tore it up and started again.Anketada xato qildim, shuning uchun uni yirtib tashlab, qaytadan boshladim.

beat va beat up butunlay boshqa fe’llar. beat — musobaqada yoki o‘yinda yutmoq: Uzbekistan beat Turkmenistan 2–1. beat up esa — kaltaklamoq. Shuning uchun “jamoani 3:0 hisobida yutdik” — We beat the other team 3–0 (✅), We beat up the other team 3–0 (❌). Fe’l shakllari: beat – beat – beaten (They beat him up, He was beaten up).

break up ning boshqa ma’nolari ham bor. Britaniya inglizchasida maktab haqida: When does your school break up for the summer? (ta’tilga chiqadi). Yana: break up a fight — janjalni to‘xtatmoq, break sth up — bo‘laklarga bo‘lmoq (break the chocolate up). cheer ning o‘zi esa “qiyqirib qo‘llab-quvvatlamoq”: The crowd cheered when Uzbekistan scored.

mix up

  • My grandmother always mixes up my name and my cousin’s name.Buvim doim mening ismim bilan amakivachchamning ismini adashtirib yuboradi.
  • I mixed up the dates and arrived a day early.Sanalarni adashtirib yubordim va bir kun oldin kelib qoldim.

hold up

  • An accident on the road to Samarkand held up the traffic for an hour.Samarqand yo‘lidagi avariya transportni bir soatga to‘xtatib qo‘ydi.
  • Sorry I’m late. I was held up at work.Kechikkanim uchun uzr. Ishda ushlanib qoldim.
  • I don’t want to hold you up, so please go ahead.Sizni kechiktirib qo‘ymay, iltimos, oldinga o‘ting.
  • Two men held up a bank in the city centre last night.Kecha tunda ikki kishi shahar markazidagi bankni qurol bilan talashdi.

put sb up

  • We have a spare room, so we can put you up for a few days.Bizda ortiqcha xona bor, shuning uchun sizni bir necha kun uyimizda joylashtira olamiz.
  • When I visited Khiva, my friend put me up for the night.Xivaga borganimda do‘stim meni bir kecha uyida tunatdi.
  • The conference guests were put up in a nearby hotel.Konferensiya mehmonlari yaqin atrofdagi mehmonxonaga joylashtirildi.

look up, look up to, look for ni adashtirmang: look up a word — so‘zni lug‘atdan topmoq; look up to sb — havas qilmoq, hurmat qilmoq (Unit 142); look for sth — yo‘qolgan narsani qidirmoq (Unit 133). put sb up ham put up with (chidamoq, Unit 137) bilan aralashtirilmaydi: put me up ✅ — mehmon qilmoq; put up with me — menga chidamoq.

hold up va mix up dan yasalgan otlar: a hold-up (kechikish yoki qurolli talonchilik), a mix-up (chalkashlik). hold noto‘g‘ri fe’l: hold – held – held. put up ning boshqa ma’nolari ham bor: put up a tent (chodir tikmoq), put up a notice (e’lon osmoq) — bular mehmon qilishga aloqasiz.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Tidy up” bilan “clean up” ning farqi nimada?

tidy up — narsalarni joy-joyiga qo‘yib, tartib o‘rnatish (kitoblar, o‘yinchoqlar, qog‘ozlar). clean up — iflosni yo‘q qilish (to‘kilgan suyuqlik, axlat, kir). Xona toza, lekin narsalar sochilib yotgan bo‘lsa — tidy up; polga choy to‘kilgan bo‘lsa — clean up. Amerika inglizchasida bolalarga “o‘yinchoqlaringni yig‘ishtir” deyilganda ham ko‘pincha clean up aytiladi.

“Wash up” nega Britaniya va Amerika inglizchasida har xil ma’no beradi?

Bu tarixiy farq. Britaniya inglizchasida wash up — ovqatdan keyin idish-tovoq yuvish (I’ll wash up, the washing-up). Amerika inglizchasida u qo‘l va betni yuvish: Go and wash up before dinner. Amerikaliklar idish-tovoq uchun do the dishes yoki wash the dishes deyishadi. Ikkala holatda ham wash the dishes hamma joyda tushuniladi, shuning uchun unutsangiz, shuni ishlating.

“Use” bilan “use up”, “eat” bilan “eat up” o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ha. use / eat — jarayonni aytadi (qolgan bo‘lishi mumkin). use up / eat up — “hammasi tugadi” degan natijani ta’kidlaydi. I ate the soup — sho‘rvani yedim (hammasi bo‘lmasligi mumkin); I ate up the soup — sho‘rvani oxirigacha yeb bo‘ldim. Eat up! deyilsa, “qolmasin, hammasini ye” degan ma’no bor.

“Break up”, “split up” va “break down” ni qanday ajrataman?

break up va split up — munosabatning tugashi: Aziz and Kamola broke up / split up. Ikkalasi deyarli bir xil; split up yana “ikki tomonga ajralmoq” (Let’s split up) ma’nosiga ega. break down esa mashina yoki asbobning buzilishi, yoki odamning o‘zini tutolmay yig‘lab yuborishi (Unit 142): Our car broke down. Munosabat uchun break down demang.

“Blow up” ning uch ma’nosini qanday farqlayman?

Kontekst ko‘rsatadi. Portlash yoki bino, ko‘prik, mo‘rini portlatish haqida bo‘lsa — portlamoq. Shar, suzish halqasi, havo to‘shagi bo‘lsa — shishirmoq. Odamning g‘azabi haqida bo‘lsa — jahli chiqmoq (odatda blow up at sb, norasmiy). Ikkinchi ma’noda to‘ldiruvchi bor va u ajraladi: blow it up.

“Look up”, “look up to”, “look for” ning farqi nimada?

look up — so‘z yoki ma’lumotni lug‘atdan, internetdan, jadvaldan topish (look the word up). look up to — kimgadir hurmat yoki havas bilan qarash (Unit 142). look for — yo‘qotilgan yoki kerakli narsani qidirish (Unit 133). Yana bir ma’no: uzoq ko‘rishmagan tanishni borib ko‘rish (look me up, norasmiy).

“Save” bilan “save up” ni qachon ishlataman?

Ikkalasi ham “pul jamg‘armoq” ma’nosida ishlatiladi: I’m saving for a bike = I’m saving up for a bike. save up asta-sekin, uzoq vaqt yig‘ishni ta’kidlaydi. Lekin save boshqa ma’nolarda ham keladi — vaqt yoki pulni tejash (save time), qutqarish (save a life); save up esa faqat yig‘ish ma’nosida.

“Put sb up” va “put up with” bir xilmi? Yana “put up” nimani anglatadi?

Yo‘q. put sb up — kimnidir uyda mehmon qilish (We can put you up). put up with — uch so‘zli fe’l, “chidamoq” (I can’t put up with this noise, Unit 137). Yana put up chodir, javon, e’lon kabi narsalarni o‘rnatish yoki osish ma’nosida ishlatiladi: put up a tent, put up a notice.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • up ko‘pincha “oxirigacha, to‘liq” degani: tidy up, clean up, use up, eat up. Lekin look up, hold up, put sb up kabi fe’llarning ma’nosi butun birikmadan chiqadi.
  • Ajraladigan fe’llarda olmosh o‘rtada turadi: tidy it up, look it up, cheer her up, mix them up, put you up (❌ tidy up it).
  • tidy up / clear up — tartibga solmoq (clear up — havo ochilmoq ham), clean up — iflosni tozalamoq, wash up — idish yuvmoq (BrE) / qo‘l-bet yuvmoq (AmE).
  • use up va eat up — “hammasini tugatdim”; save up (for) — pul yig‘moq.
  • break up / split up — ajralishmoq (break up with sb); beat — yutmoq, beat up — kaltaklamoq (norasmiy).
  • blow up: portlamoq / havo bilan shishirmoq / jahli chiqmoq. tear up = yirtib tashlamoq. fix up va beat up norasmiy.
  • Noto‘g‘ri fe’llar: blow – blew – blown, break – broke – broken, beat – beat – beaten, eat – ate – eaten, hold – held – held, tear – tore – torn.
