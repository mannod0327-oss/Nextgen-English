Prefer and would rather

Prefer va would rather — afzal ko‘rish
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • prefer bilan umumiy did haqida gapirishni: I prefer tea to coffee, I prefer swimming to running, I prefer to swim rather than run.
  • would prefer va would rather bilan aniq bir vaziyatdagi tanlovni: I’d rather stay at home tonight.
  • Muloyim rad etishni: I’d rather not.
  • Boshqa odam nimadir qilishini (yoki qilmasligini) xohlashni: I’d rather you waited outside — bu yerda o‘tgan zamon shakli hozirgi yoki kelasi zamon ma’nosida keladi.

Ingliz tilida “afzal ko‘rish” uchun uchta asosiy yo‘l bor. prefer — umuman, doim nimani yoqtirishingiz haqida. would prefer va would rather — hozir, shu safar, shu vaziyatda nimani tanlashingiz haqida. Bu farq Unit 58 dagi like ↔ would like farqiga juda o‘xshaydi.

O‘zbek tilida “choyni qahvadan ko‘ra afzal ko‘raman” deymiz: -dan ko‘ra “qaysi biriga nisbatan” degan ma’noni beradi. Inglizchada bu vazifani to bajaradi (prefer tea to coffee), than emas! Shuning uchun ❌ I prefer tea than coffee juda ko‘p uchraydigan xato. Afzal ko‘rish iboralarida than faqat would rather ... than ..., ... rather than ... va like ... more than ... tarkibida keladi. I’d rather stay ni esa o‘zbekchada “uyda qolganim ma’qul / uyda qolsam yaxshi bo‘lardi” deb aytamiz.

Odatda, umuman, doim nimani afzal ko‘rishingizni aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, prefer ishlatiladi. Undan keyin uch xil tuzilma keladi:

Misollar

  • Dilnoza prefers dogs to cats.Dilnoza itlarni mushuklardan afzal ko‘radi.
  • My grandfather prefers walking to taking the bus.Bobom avtobusda ketgandan ko‘ra piyoda yurishni afzal ko‘radi.
  • I prefer living in a small town to living in a big city.Katta shaharda yashagandan ko‘ra kichik shaharda yashashni afzal ko‘raman.
  • At weekends Bekzod prefers to stay in bed rather than get up early.Dam olish kunlari Bekzod erta turgandan ko‘ra yotoqda yotishni afzal ko‘radi.
  • As a child, Nodira preferred reading to playing outside.Bolaligida Nodira tashqarida o‘ynagandan ko‘ra kitob o‘qishni afzal ko‘rardi.

prefer A to B dagi to — predlog (Unit 60), shuning uchun undan keyin ot yoki -ing keladi, infinitiv emas. Ikki tomon bir xil shaklda bo‘lsin: ot — ot, -ing — -ing. ❌ I prefer swimming to run. → ✅ I prefer swimming to running.

prefer to do va prefer doing umumiy did haqida deyarli bir xil ma’no beradi: I prefer to walk = I prefer walking. Lekin rather than bilan odatda prefer to + fe’l ishlatiladi: I prefer to walk rather than take a taxi. (Og‘zaki nutqda prefer walking rather than taking ham uchraydi, lekin o‘quv va imtihon ishlarida yuqoridagi uch tuzilmadan birini yozing.)

prefer — holat fe’li (Unit 4), shuning uchun odatda davomli zamonda ishlatilmaydi: I prefer tea ✅, I’m preferring tea ❌. Britaniya ingliz tilida ham, Amerika ingliz tilida ham tuzilma bir xil.

would prefer: hozirgi tanlov Would prefer (I’d prefer ...)

Gap “umuman” haqida emas, hozir, shu safar, shu vaziyatda nimani xohlashingiz haqida bo‘lsa, would prefer (yoki C qismdagi would rather) ishlatiladi. Ular taklif va tanlovga javob berishda juda qulay.

would prefer dan keyin to + fe’l keladi, -ing emas: I’d prefer to walk ✅ — I’d prefer walking ❌. (-ing faqat umumiy did haqidagi prefer bilan: I prefer walking.)

would rather: hozirgi tanlov (to siz) Would rather (I’d rather ...)

would rather + fe’l = would prefer to + fe’l. Ma’no bir xil, faqat would rather dan keyin to qo‘yilmaydi.

Ega+would (’d) rather+fe’l (to siz)

  • Would you rather sit by the window or near the door?Deraza yonida o‘tirganingiz ma’qulmi yoki eshik yonidami?

would rather dan keyin to ham, -ing ham kelmaydi — faqat fe’lning asosiy shakli: ❌ I’d rather to stay, ❌ I’d rather staying → ✅ I’d rather stay. Sababi: would — modal fe’l, modal fe’llardan (can, must, would ...) keyin odatda to kelmaydi (Unit 36).

“ ... dan ko‘ra ...”: than va rather than

Ikkita imkoniyatni solishtirishda ikki xil naqsh bor. Ikkalasida ham keyingi fe’l to siz keladi:

would rather ... than ...

  • I’d rather cook at home than eat out.
  • Aziz would rather take the train than drive.

Restoranda ovqatlangandan ko‘ra uyda ovqat pishirganim ma’qul.

would prefer to ... rather than ...

  • I’d prefer to cook at home rather than eat out.
  • Aziz would prefer to take the train rather than drive.

Ma’no bir xil, lekin would prefer biroz rasmiyroq.

I’d rather not — muloyim rad etish

Biror narsani xohlamasangiz, lekin qo‘pol bo‘lmoqchi bo‘lmasangiz, I’d rather not deyiladi. Fe’lni takrorlash shart emas. Agar fe’l kerak bo‘lsa: I’d rather not + fe’l yoki I’d prefer not to + fe’l.

  • “Shall we invite Rustam?” “I’d rather not.”— Rustamni ham chaqiramizmi? — Chaqirmasak yaxshi bo‘lardi.
  • I’d rather not drive tonight. My eyes are tired.Bugun kechqurun mashina haydamasam yaxshi. Ko‘zlarim charchagan.
  • I’d prefer not to say how much it cost.Narxi qancha ekanini aytmasam yaxshi bo‘lardi.

I’d rather not o‘zbekchada ko‘pincha “unchalik xohlamayman”, “bo‘lmasa yaxshi edi”, “iloji bo‘lsa yo‘q” kabi yumshoq shaklda ifodalanadi. Inglizchada I wouldn’t rather go deb bo‘lmaydi — inkor rather dan keyin turadi: would rather not.

would sooner ham would rather bilan bir xil ma’noda ishlatiladi: I’d sooner walk than take a taxi. Lekin would rather ancha keng tarqalgan.

I’d rather you did something: boshqa odam haqida I’d rather somebody did something

Agar siz boshqa odam nimadir qilishini xohlasangiz (yoki qilmasligini), would rather dan keyin yangi ega va o‘tgan zamon shakli keladi:

Ega 1+would (’d) rather+Ega 2+V2 (past simple)

Eng muhim nuqta: bu yerdagi came, called, didn’t kabi shakllar o‘tmishni emas, balki hozir yoki kelajakni bildiradi. Bu yerda o‘tgan zamon shakli “haqiqatda hali bo‘lmagan, istalgan holat”ni ko‘rsatadi (xuddi Unit 39 dagi If I knew ... yoki Unit 41 dagi I wish I knew ... kabi).

  • I’d rather you stayed with us tonight. The roads are icy.Bugun kechqurun bizning uyda qolsangiz yaxshi bo‘lardi. Yo‘llar muzlagan.
  • “Shall I turn the heating off?” “I’d rather you didn’t. It’s freezing.”— Isitishni o‘chiraymi? — O‘chirmasangiz yaxshi. Juda sovuq.
  • “Can I borrow your phone for a minute?” “I’d rather you didn’t. The battery is almost dead.”— Telefoningizni bir daqiqaga olib tursam bo‘ladimi? — Olmasangiz yaxshi bo‘lardi. Batareyasi deyarli tugagan.
  • Would you rather I called you tomorrow?Ertaga sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilganim ma’qulmi?
  • I’d rather you didn’t share my number with anyone.Raqamimni hech kimga bermasangiz yaxshi bo‘lardi.
  • My parents would rather I studied medicine.Ota-onam tibbiyotda o‘qishimni xohlashadi.
  • I’d rather you were honest with me.Menga rostini aytganingiz ma’qul.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I prefer to walk” va “I prefer walking” — farqi bormi?

Umumiy did haqida gapirganda ma’no deyarli bir xil, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Lekin prefer A to B naqshida ikki tomon ham -ing bo‘lishi kerak (walking to driving), rather than naqshida esa odatda prefer to + fe’l (to walk rather than drive) ishlatiladi.

Nega “would rather” dan keyin “to” yo‘q?

Chunki would — modal fe’l, rather esa u bilan birga turadi: would rather bir butun. Modal fe’llardan (can, must, would ...) keyin odatda to kelmaydi (I can swim, I would rather swim). would prefer da esa prefer oddiy fe’l, shuning uchun to kerak: I’d prefer to swim.

“I’d rather” dagi ’d — “would” mi yoki “had” mi?

Har doim would. Qanday bilaman? would dan keyin fe’lning asosiy shakli keladi (I’d rather stay). had dan keyin esa V3 keladi (I’d stayed — past perfect). I’d rather deganda doim would rather.

“I’d rather you did” da nega o‘tgan zamon? Gap kelajak haqida-ku!

Ingliz tilida o‘tgan zamon shakli faqat “o‘tmish”ni emas, “haqiqat emas / hali bo‘lmagan / istalgan holat”ni ham bildiradi. Bu yerda came — “agar kelsangiz yaxshi bo‘lardi” degan taxminiy istak. Xuddi shu qoida If I knew ... (Unit 39), I wish I knew ... (Unit 41) va It’s time you went (Unit 35) da ham bor.

“I’d rather you were” yoki “I’d rather you was”?

Standart, ehtiyotkor ingliz tilida were ishlatiladi: I’d rather you were honest. Og‘zaki nutqda ba’zan was ham eshitiladi, lekin imtihon va yozma ish uchun were xavfsiz.

“Would you rather ... or ...?” savoliga qanday javob beraman?

Tanlovingizni I’d rather + fe’l bilan ayting: “Would you rather walk or take a taxi?” “I’d rather walk.” Qisqa javoblar: Either, I don’t mind (ikkalasi ham mayli), Neither, thanks (hech biri kerak emas). Diqqat: Yes, I would deb javob berilmaydi, chunki savol “ha / yo‘q” savoli emas, tanlov savoli.

“would prefer” va “would rather” — qaysi biri muloyimroq?

Ikkalasi ham muloyim. would prefer biroz rasmiyroq va yozma/xizmat ko‘rsatish tilida ko‘proq eshitiladi (Would you prefer a window seat?), would rather esa og‘zaki, do‘stona nutqda tabiiyroq. Rad etish uchun I’d rather not juda keng ishlatiladi.

Nega “I prefer tea than coffee” noto‘g‘ri, axir “I like tea more than coffee” to‘g‘ri-ku?

Ikkala gap ma’nosi bir xil, lekin tuzilishi boshqa: like ... more than — qiyoslashda than; prefer ... to — fe’lning o‘zi “ko‘proq” ma’nosini olib yuradi, shuning uchun ikkinchi tomon to bilan ulanadi. Aralashtirmang.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. to, than yoki rather than — bo‘sh joyga to‘g‘risini yozing.

  1. I prefer coffee tea.
  2. Sardor prefers chess football.
  3. Jasur prefers to take the bus drive.
  4. I’d rather walk wait for a taxi.
  5. Do you prefer living in a village living in a city?
  6. I’d prefer to stay at home go out in the rain.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi so‘zni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (asosiy shakl, to + fe’l yoki -ing).

  1. I’d rather (stay) at home tonight.
  2. Would you prefer (go) by train or by bus?
  3. Nodira prefers (cook) at home to eating in restaurants.
  4. Would you rather (walk) or take a taxi?
  5. I’d prefer (not / say) anything about it.
  6. She’d rather (not / eat) meat.
  7. I prefer (get) up late at weekends.

3-mashq. I’d rather you ...: qavs ichidagi so‘zni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. I’d rather you (come) at seven, not at six.
  2. “Do you mind if I turn on the TV?” “I’d rather you .”
  3. Would you rather I (call) you tomorrow?
  4. My mother would rather my brother (live) at home than in a hostel.
  5. I’d rather you (not / tell) my parents about the broken vase.
  6. It’s cold. I’d rather you (wear) a coat.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • prefer — umumiy did: prefer A to B (ot — ot yoki -ing — -ing) va prefer to do rather than do.
  • than prefer bilan ishlatilmaydi: I prefer tea to coffee ✅.
  • would prefer to + fe’l va would rather + fe’l (to siz!) — hozirgi aniq tanlov.
  • Solishtirish: would rather ... than ... va would prefer to ... rather than ....
  • Inkor va rad etish: I’d rather not (wouldn’t rather ❌).
  • Boshqa odam haqida: I’d rather you did / didn’t — o‘tgan zamon shakli, lekin ma’no hozir yoki kelajak.
  • O‘tmish haqida afsus: I’d rather have + V3, I’d rather you had + V3.
