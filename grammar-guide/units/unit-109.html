Word order 1: verb + object; place and time

So‘z tartibi — 1: fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi; joy va vaqt
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Fe’l va uning to‘ldiruvchisi (object) ingliz tilida yonma-yon turishini: I like football very much ✅ — I like very much football ❌.
  • Joy (qayerda?) odatda vaqt (qachon?) dan oldin keladi: We went to the market on Saturday.
  • Uchta ma’lumot birga kelganda tartib: tarz (qanday?) → joy → vaqt.
  • O‘zbekcha (fe’l oxirda) va inglizcha (fe’l egadan keyin) tartib farqini.
  • give / send / show kabi fe’llardan keyin ikki to‘ldiruvchi qanday keladi.

Ingliz tilida gap odatda shunday quriladi: ega → fe’l → to‘ldiruvchi → qo‘shimcha ma’lumotlar (qanday, qayerda, qachon). Bu tartibni buzish — o‘zbek tilini bilganlar uchun eng ko‘p uchraydigan xatolardan biri.

O‘zbek tilida fe’l gap oxirida turadi: Men futbolni juda yaxshi ko‘raman (ega – to‘ldiruvchi – ravish – fe’l). Inglizcha gap esa teskari: I like football very much (ega – fe’l – to‘ldiruvchi – ravish). Shuning uchun o‘zbekcha o‘ylab inglizcha gapirsangiz, ravishni (very much, very well, quickly) fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchining o‘rtasiga qo‘yib yuborasiz: I like very much football ❌. Eslab qoling: fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi — bir butun, ular orasiga hech narsa kirmaydi.

Fe’l va to‘ldiruvchi yonma-yon Verb + object

Fe’l va uning to‘ldiruvchisi (kimni? nimani?) gapda odatda birga turadi. Ravishlar yoki boshqa so‘zlarni ular orasiga qo‘ymaymiz — ularni to‘ldiruvchidan keyin qo‘yamiz.

Ega+fe’l+to‘ldiruvchi+ravish / qo‘shimcha ma’lumot
Ravish to‘ldiruvchidan KEYIN keladi
❌ Noto‘g‘ri✅ To‘g‘riTarjima
Sardor ate quickly his dinner.Sardor ate his dinner quickly.Sardor kechki ovqatini tez yedi.
I like very much football.I like football very much.Men futbolni juda yaxshi ko‘raman.
Malika speaks very well English.Malika speaks English very well.Malika inglizchani juda yaxshi gapiradi.
She opened slowly the door.She opened the door slowly.U eshikni sekin ochdi.
I don’t know well him.I don’t know him well.Men uni yaxshi tanimayman.
Did you call yesterday your parents?Did you call your parents yesterday?Kecha ota-onangga qo‘ng‘iroq qildingmi?

Qoida: Fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi orasiga ravish, vaqt yoki joy so‘zi tushmaydi. Gap tartibi: fe’l → to‘ldiruvchi → (qanday / qayerda / qachon).

Ko‘proq misollar

  • Dilnoza reads books every evening.Dilnoza har kecha kitob o‘qiydi.
  • Do you like Samarkand a lot?Samarqand sizga juda yoqadimi?
  • She speaks Russian fluently.U ruscha ravon gapiradi.
  • I cleaned the room carefully.Men xonani sinchiklab tozaladim.
  • They sell fresh bread here.Ular bu yerda yangi non sotishadi.

Ba’zi ravishlar fe’ldan oldin ham turishi mumkin (She quickly opened the door. — Unit 110), lekin hech qachon fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi o‘rtasida emas: She opened quickly the door ❌. Rasmiyroq uslubda very much ham fe’ldan oldin keladi (I very much enjoyed the concert.); lekin enjoyed very much the concert ❌.

Joy va vaqt tartibi Place and time: where? before when?

Gapda joy (to school, in Bukhara, at home) va vaqt (yesterday, on Saturday, at 8 o’clock, every day) birga kelsa, ular odatda fe’l/to‘ldiruvchidan keyin turadi, va avval joy, keyin vaqt keladi.

Ega+fe’l+(to‘ldiruvchi)+JOY+VAQT
  • Sardor cycles to college every day.Sardor har kuni kollejga velosipedda boradi.
  • We went to the cinema on Saturday.Biz shanba kuni kinoga bordik.
  • She arrived in Bukhara last night.U kecha kechqurun Buxoroga yetib keldi.
  • I saw Dilnoza at the library yesterday.Men Dilnozani kecha kutubxonada ko‘rdim.
  • The meeting will take place in my office at 8 o’clock.Yig‘ilish soat 8 da mening xonamda bo‘ladi.
  • They are flying to Istanbul tomorrow morning.Ular ertaga ertalab Istanbulga uchishadi.

Uchta ma’lumot birga: tarz → joy → vaqt

Gapda qanday? (tarz — beautifully, hard, well), qayerda? (joy) va qachon? (vaqt) birga kelsa, odatdagi tartib shunday:

  • Malika sang beautifully at the concert yesterday.Malika kecha konsertda chiroyli kuyladi.
  • Sardor works hard in the garden every Saturday.Sardor har shanba bog‘da qattiq ishlaydi.
  • I slept badly in the hotel last night.Kecha mehmonxonada yomon uxladim.

Harakat fe’llari: joy, keyin vosita, keyin vaqt

go, come, travel, drive, walk kabi harakat fe’llaridan keyin joy tezda keladi, by bus / by train / on foot kabi vosita odatda joydan keyin, vaqt esa eng oxirida turadi:

  • I go to work by bus every day.Men har kuni ishga avtobusda boraman.
  • We travelled to Samarkand by train last summer.O‘tgan yozda biz Samarqandga poyezdda bordik.

Vaqt gap boshiga ham chiqishi mumkin

Vaqtni ta’kidlamoqchi bo‘lsangiz yoki gapni vaqt bilan boshlamoqchi bo‘lsangiz, uni gap boshiga ko‘chirish mumkin. Qolgan tartib o‘zgarmaydi:

  • Yesterday I met Jasur at the library.Kecha men Jasurni kutubxonada uchratdim.
  • Every Saturday, Sardor works hard in the garden.Har shanba Sardor bog‘da qattiq ishlaydi.

Diqqat: Vaqt so‘zi (yesterday, every day, on Saturday kabi) ega bilan fe’l orasiga yoki fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi orasiga tushmaydi: I yesterday met Jasur ❌, I met yesterday Jasur ❌ → I met Jasur yesterday / Yesterday I met Jasur ✅. every day ham shunday: I every day go to school ❌.

Home, there, here — to siz

home, there, here, abroad, upstairs kabi so‘zlarning o‘zi joy ma’nosini beradi, shuning uchun ulardan oldin to kerak emas:

❌✅
I went to home early.I went home early yesterday.
Let’s go to there.Let’s go there.
She travels to abroad every year.She travels abroad every year.

O‘zbekcha va inglizcha tartib: oyna aksi Uzbek (SOV) vs English (SVO)

O‘zbekcha gapning odatdagi (neytral) tartibi: ega – vaqt – joy – tarz – to‘ldiruvchi – fe’l. Inglizcha tartib esa deyarli uning oyna aksi: ega – fe’l – to‘ldiruvchi – tarz – joy – vaqt.

Savol tug‘iladi: “O‘zbek tilida ham tartib erkin-ku?” Ha, o‘zbek tilida -ni, -ga, -da, -dan kabi qo‘shimchalar kim nima qilganini ko‘rsatadi, shuning uchun tartibni o‘zgartirsak ham ma’no saqlanadi. Ingliz tilida bunday qo‘shimchalar deyarli yo‘q — rolni so‘zning o‘rni belgilaydi: The dog bit the man (it odamni tishladi) ≠ The man bit the dog (odam itni tishladi). Shuning uchun inglizchada tartib qat’iyroq.

Ikki to‘ldiruvchi: give me the book Verb + two objects

give, send, show, bring, lend, tell, offer kabi fe’llardan keyin ikkita to‘ldiruvchi kelishi mumkin: kimga? va nimani? Ikki xil tartib bor:

Ikki to‘ldiruvchi tartibi
TartibMisolTarjima
fe’l + kimga + nimani (to yo‘q)She gave Jasur a book.U Jasurga kitob berdi.
fe’l + nimani + to + kimgaShe gave a book to Jasur.U Jasurga kitob berdi.
fe’l + kimga + nimaniI sent my mother a letter.Men onamga xat yubordim.
fe’l + nimani + to + kimgaI sent a letter to my mother.Men onamga xat yubordim.
  • I bought my sister a scarf. / I bought a scarf for my sister.Men singlimga ro‘mol sotib oldim.
  • Please explain the rule to me.Iltimos, qoidani menga tushuntiring.
  • I gave it to him.Men uni unga berdim.

Ehtiyot bo‘ling: buy, make, cook, find kabi fe’llar bilan to emas, for ishlatiladi (a scarf for my sister). explain, suggest, describe fe’llaridan keyin “kimga” faqat to bilan keladi: explain me the rule ❌ → explain the rule to me ✅. Ikkala to‘ldiruvchi olmosh bo‘lsa, I gave it to him deyiladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Ravishni fe’ldan oldin qo‘ysam bo‘ladimi?

Ba’zi ravishlar uchun ha: She quickly opened the door. yoki She opened the door quickly. — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Muhimi — ravish fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi orasiga tushmasin: She opened quickly the door ❌. Fe’l oldida turadigan ravishlar (always, often, also, just …) haqida Unit 110 da gaplashamiz.

Vaqtni gap boshiga qo‘ysam, vergul kerakmi?

Qisqa vaqt iboralaridan keyin (Yesterday, Last night, Every Saturday) vergul ixtiyoriy: Yesterday I met Jasur. / Yesterday, I met Jasur. Uzun iboralardan keyin vergul qo‘yish yaxshi: When I was a student in Tashkent, I lived with my aunt.

Joyni ham gap boshiga chiqarsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha, lekin vaqtdan kamroq. Ta’kid yoki tasvir uchun: In the garden, Sardor works hard every Saturday. Oddiy, neytral gapda joy fe’l (yoki to‘ldiruvchi)dan keyin, vaqtdan oldin turadi.

Nega o‘zbek tilida tartib erkin, inglizchada esa yo‘q?

O‘zbek tilida har bir so‘zning roli qo‘shimchalar bilan ko‘rsatiladi (-ni, -ga, -dan). Ingliz tilida bunday qo‘shimchalar deyarli yo‘q, shuning uchun so‘zning gapdagi o‘rni uning rolini belgilaydi: The man bit the dog ≠ The dog bit the man. Shu sababli ingliz tilida tartibni buzib bo‘lmaydi.

Savol gapda ham tartib shundaymi?

Ha, faqat yordamchi fe’l ega oldiga chiqadi, so‘roq so‘zi boshiga keladi: Where did you meet Jasur yesterday? Bu yerda ham fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi yonma-yon, keyin joy va vaqt.

Very much ni fe’ldan oldin ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ba’zi rasmiyroq gaplarda ha: I very much enjoyed the concert. I very much hope you can come. Lekin I like very much football kabi gap xato — bunda very much fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi o‘rtasida turibdi.

I gave him it deyish mumkinmi?

Ikkala to‘ldiruvchi ham olmosh bo‘lsa, standart shakl: I gave it to him. I gave him it ba’zi Britaniya shevalarida eshitiladi, lekin o‘rganuvchi uchun gave it to him xavfsiz.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Inglizcha asosiy tartib: ega – fe’l – to‘ldiruvchi. O‘zbekchada fe’l oxirda, inglizchada esa egadan keyin.
  • Fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi yonma-yon: I like football very much ✅ (like very much football ❌).
  • Ravish, joy, vaqt — to‘ldiruvchidan keyin.
  • Joy → vaqt: to the cinema on Saturday. Uchtasi birga: tarz → joy → vaqt.
  • Vaqt gap boshiga ham chiqishi mumkin: Yesterday I met Jasur. Lekin ega bilan fe’l orasiga emas.
  • home, there, abroad dan oldin to kerak emas.
  • give me the book = give the book to me; buy bilan for; explain bilan faqat to me.
