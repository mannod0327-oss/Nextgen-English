Present perfect continuous (I have been doing)

Hozirgi tugallangan davomli zamon
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Present perfect continuous (hozirgi tugallangan davomli zamon) qanday yasalishini: have / has been + fe’l-ing.
  • Uni qachon ishlatishni: (A) yaqinda tugagan, natijasi hozir ko‘rinib turgan davomli harakat; (B) how long, for, since bilan — hozirgacha davom etib kelayotgan harakat.
  • Takrorlanib kelayotgan harakatlar: She’s been taking dutar lessons since she was seven.
  • I am doing va I have been doing farqini (C).

Bu zamon davomli harakat va hozirgi paytni bog‘laydi. Harakat o‘tmishda boshlangan, ma’lum vaqt davom etgan va (1) hozirgina tugagan — izi hozir ko‘rinib turibdi, yoki (2) hozir ham davom etmoqda.

O‘zbek tilida “... dan beri” bilan odatda hozirgi zamon ishlatamiz: Ikki soatdan beri yomg‘ir yog‘yapti. Bir soatdan beri seni kutyapman. Ingliz tilida esa bunday gaplarda present continuous emas, present perfect continuous kerak: It’s been raining for two hours. I’ve been waiting for you for an hour. I am waiting for you for an hour ❌ — bu o‘zbek tilida so‘zlashuvchilarning eng ko‘p xatosi!

It has been raining — yaqinda tugagan harakat, izi hozir bor An activity that has recently stopped

Bu ma’noda present perfect continuous yaqinda tugagan (yoki hozirgina to‘xtagan) davomli harakat uchun ishlatiladi. Harakat va hozirgi payt o‘rtasida bog‘liqlik bor: uning izi, natijasi hozir ko‘rinadi.

  • Your face is red. Have you been sitting in the sun?Yuzing qizarib ketibdi. Quyoshda o‘tirdingmi?
  • Sardor is very tired. He’s been working very hard.Sardor juda charchagan. U juda qattiq ishladi.
  • “The kitchen smells wonderful! What have you been cooking?” “I’ve been making somsa.”— Oshxonadan ajoyib hid kelyapti! Nima pishirding? — Somsa yopdim.
  • Your eyes are red. Have you been crying?Ko‘zlaring qizarib ketibdi. Yig‘ladingmi?
  • I’ve been reading about Khiva on the internet. It sounds amazing!Internetda Xiva haqida o‘qidim. Juda ajoyib joy ekan!
  • Sorry about the mess — the children have been playing in here.Tartibsizlik uchun uzr — bolalar shu yerda o‘ynashdi.

Bu ma’no o‘zbekchada ko‘pincha oddiy o‘tgan zamon yoki -ibdi / -gan ekan bilan beriladi: yomg‘ir yog‘ibdi, yugurdingmi?, yig‘lagan ekan. Inglizchada esa bu — has been raining, have you been running, has been crying. Kalit savol: “Harakat davomli bo‘lganmi va uning izi hozir ko‘rinyaptimi?”

’s bu yerda doim has: She’s been working = she has been working. She is been working ❌ degan shakl yo‘q.

How long ...? for ..., since ... — hozirgacha davom etayotgan harakat It has been raining for two hours

Endi boshqa vaziyat: yomg‘ir ikki soat oldin boshlangan va hali ham yog‘yapti.

  • It’s raining. It has been raining for two hours.Yomg‘ir yog‘yapti. Ikki soatdan beri yog‘yapti.
  • How long has it been raining?Qancha vaqtdan beri yomg‘ir yog‘yapti?

Bu ma’noda present perfect continuous how long, for ..., since ... bilan keladi. Harakat hali davom etmoqda yoki hozirgina to‘xtagan:

Takrorlanib kelayotgan harakatlar

Present perfect continuous bir davr mobaynida qayta-qayta takrorlanib kelayotgan harakatlar uchun ham ishlatiladi:

  • Dilnoza has been taking dutar lessons since she was seven.Dilnoza yetti yoshidan beri dutor darslariga qatnab keladi.
  • My dad has been buying his bread from the same bakery for twenty years.Dadam yigirma yildan beri nonni bitta novvoyxonadan oladi.
  • I’ve been calling you all morning. Why didn’t you answer?Ertalabdan beri senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilyapman. Nega javob bermading?

O‘zbekcha “... dan beri ... -yapman / -aman” → inglizcha have been + -ing + for / since: Uch yildan beri ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman = I’ve been learning English for three years. Present simple (I learn English for three years ❌) ham, present continuous (I am learning English for three years ❌) ham bunday ma’noda xato.

I am doing va I have been doing Present continuous and present perfect continuous

Ikkala zamon ham davomli harakat haqida, lekin qarash nuqtasi boshqa:

I am doing (present continuous)

Faqat hozirga qaraymiz: harakat ayni paytda davom etmoqda. Qancha vaqtdan beri ekani aytilmaydi.

I have been doing (present perfect continuous)

Harakatning boshidan hozirgacha bo‘lgan davrga qaraymiz: qancha davom etgani yoki uning izi muhim.

Continuous shaklda kelmaydigan fe’llar State verbs: a reminder

know, like, love, want, believe, understand, own, belong, have (egalik ma’nosida) kabi holat fe’llari odatda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi (Unit 4). Ular bilan “... dan beri” ma’nosida oddiy present perfect ishlatiladi:

❌ Noto‘g‘ri

  • I’ve been knowing Aziz for ten years.
  • She’s been having this car since 2020.

✅ To‘g‘ri

  • I’ve known Aziz for ten years.
    Azizni o‘n yildan beri taniyman.
  • She’s had this car since 2020.
    Bu mashina unda 2020-yildan beri bor.

Bu mavzu Unit 10 va Unit 11 da davom etadi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “been” kerak? “I have working” desa bo‘lmaydimi?

Yo‘q. Bu zamon ikki qismdan “yig‘ilgan”: present perfect (have + V3) va continuous (be + -ing). be fe’lining V3 shakli — been. Natijada: have + been + -ing. Uchala qism ham shart.

Present perfect continuous: harakat tugaganmi yoki davom etyaptimi?

Ikkalasi ham bo‘lishi mumkin — kontekst hal qiladi. The streets are wet. It’s been raining. — yomg‘ir to‘xtagan (A). It’s been raining for two hours (and it’s still raining). — davom etyapti (B). for / since / how long bo‘lsa, ko‘pincha harakat hali davom etayotgan bo‘ladi.

“It has been raining” va “It rained” farqi nima?

It has been raining — yaqinda, hozir bilan bog‘liq (ko‘chalar hali ho‘l). It rained — o‘tmishdagi tugagan voqea, ko‘pincha aniq vaqt bilan: It rained a lot yesterday.

“all day”, “all morning” bilan ham ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha, bu juda tabiiy: I’ve been cleaning the house all morning. (Ertalabdan beri uy tozalayapman.) He’s been playing computer games all day.

“I’ve been living here for five years” va “I’ve lived here for five years” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va ma’nosi deyarli bir xil. live, work, study kabi fe’llar bilan ikki shakl ham ishlatiladi. Uzoq muddatli, doimiy holatlar uchun odatda oddiy shakl afzal: My grandparents have lived in Bukhara all their lives. Bu farq Unit 10 va Unit 11 da batafsil.

“I haven’t been sleeping well” nimani bildiradi?

“So‘nggi paytlarda (bir muddatdan beri) yaxshi uxlay olmayapman” — muammo bir davr davomida takrorlanib kelayotganini bildiradi. Inkor shakl: haven’t / hasn’t been + -ing.

Present perfect continuous bilan aniq o‘tgan vaqt (yesterday, last week) ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Bu zamon hozir bilan bog‘liq. I’ve been working yesterday ❌. Tugagan davr uchun past simple yoki past continuous: I worked all day yesterday. I was working at 6 pm yesterday.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: have / has + been + fe’l-ing. So‘roq: Have you been ...ing? Inkor: haven’t / hasn’t been ...ing.
  • (A) Yaqinda tugagan davomli harakat, izi hozir ko‘rinadi: The streets are wet. It’s been raining.
  • (B) how long / for / since bilan hozirgacha davom etayotgan harakat: It’s been raining for two hours.
  • Takroriy harakat ham: She’s been taking dutar lessons since she was seven.
  • (C) I’m waiting — faqat hozir; I’ve been waiting for an hour — boshlanishdan hozirgacha.
  • O‘zbekcha “... dan beri ... -yapman” → I’ve been ...ing for / since ... (I am ...ing for ... ❌).
  • Holat fe’llari (know, like, have = egalik) continuous bo‘lmaydi: I’ve known him for years.
