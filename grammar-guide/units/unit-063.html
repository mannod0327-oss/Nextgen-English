63

Expressions + -ing

Iboralar + -ing
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • It’s no use / it’s no good va there’s no point (in) dan keyin -ing: It’s no use crying.
  • It’s (not) worth dan keyin -ing: The museum is worth visiting.
  • have difficulty (in) / have trouble + -ing: I had difficulty finding the house.
  • a waste of time / money, spend / waste time, be busy iboralarini -ing bilan.
  • go + -ing faoliyatlarini: go swimming, go shopping, go fishing.

Ingliz tilida ba’zi tayyor iboralardan keyin ikkinchi fe’l doim -ing shaklida keladi. Bu iboralarda to + fe’l ishlatilmaydi. Ularni bitta “blok” sifatida yodlab olish kifoya: it’s no use + -ing, it’s worth + -ing, have difficulty + -ing...

O‘zbek tilida ham shunday iboralar bor va ularning hammasida “-ish” shakli keladi: kutishdan foyda yo‘q, borishga arziydi, topishda qiynalmoq, o‘qishga vaqt sarflamoq, ovqat tayyorlash bilan band. Ingliz tilida bu “-ish” hamisha -ing: no use waiting, worth visiting, difficulty finding, spend time reading, busy cooking. Xato odat: o‘zbekcha “-ish” ni to + fe’l deb tarjima qilish (❌ worth to visit).

It’s no use, there’s no point, it’s worth It’s no use / there’s no point (in) / it’s (not) worth + -ing

Birinchi guruh iboralar “foyda yo‘q”, “ma’nosi yo‘q”, “arziydi” ma’nolarini beradi. Hammasidan keyin -ing keladi:

It’s no use / it’s no good + -ing

“Foydasi yo‘q, behuda” degan ma’noda. Ma’nodosh: it’s useless, it’s pointless.

  • It’s no use arguing with him. He never changes his mind.U bilan bahslashishdan foyda yo‘q. U hech qachon fikrini o‘zgartirmaydi.
  • It’s no good crying over spilt milk.Allaqachon bo‘lib o‘tgan ishga yig‘lashdan foyda yo‘q.
  • What’s the use of worrying?Xavotir olishdan nima foyda?
  • It’s useless trying to explain it to him.Unga tushuntirishga urinishdan foyda yo‘q.

There’s no point (in) + -ing

“Ma’nosi yo‘q, hech qanday sabab yo‘q”. in ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin. Savol shakli: What’s the point of / in ...-ing?

  • There’s no point in waiting for the bus. The drivers are on strike today.Avtobus kutishdan ma’no yo‘q. Bugun haydovchilar ish tashlashgan.
  • There’s no point buying a gym membership if you never go.Hech qachon bormasangiz, sport zaliga abonement olishdan nima ma’no bor?
  • What’s the point of learning grammar if you never speak?Hech gapirmasangiz, grammatika o‘rganishdan nima ma’no?

It’s (not) worth + -ing

“Arziydi / arzimaydi”. worth dan keyin to + fe’l emas, -ing keladi. Jumladagi ot ko‘pincha -ing fe’lining to‘ldiruvchisi bo‘ladi, shuning uchun -ing dan keyin yana it yozilmaydi:

  • The Registan is worth visiting.Registonni borib ko‘rishga arziydi.
  • It’s worth visiting the Registan.Registonga borishga arziydi.
  • Is this film worth seeing? — It’s not worth the money.Bu film ko‘rishga arziydimi? — U pulga arzimaydi.
  • It isn’t worth repairing this old printer. A new one is cheaper.Bu eski printerni ta’mirlashga arzimaydi. Yangisi arzonroq.

Qoida: worth + -ing (worth visiting ✅; worth to visit ❌). Egasi narsa bo‘lsa: The book is worth reading ✅ — The book is worth reading it ❌. Shuningdek: worth + ot (worth the money, worth a try) va worth + pul miqdori (The car is worth £5,000).

Qiyinchilik va vaqtni yaxshi o‘tkazish Have difficulty / trouble; have fun

have difficulty, have trouble, have a problem, have a hard time — “qiynalmoq”. Keyin -ing keladi (to + fe’l emas). have difficulty dan keyin in ni qo‘yish ham, tushirish ham mumkin (in bilan rasmiyroq):

Ega+have difficulty / trouble+(in)+fe’l-ing
  • I had difficulty finding a parking space near the bazaar.Bozor yaqinida mashina qo‘yish joyini topishda qiynaldim.
  • Jasur has trouble sleeping before exams.Jasur imtihondan oldin uxlay olmaydi (uxlashda qiynaladi).
  • We had a hard time getting tickets for the concert.Konsertga chipta topish bizga qiyin bo‘ldi.
  • Did you have any difficulty opening a bank account?Bank hisobi ochishda biror qiyinchilik bo‘ldimi?
  • She had no difficulty understanding the lecture.U ma’ruzani tushunishda qiynalmadi.

Bu iborada difficulty odatda a siz ishlatiladi: I have difficulty understanding him ✅ — I have a difficulty to understand ❌. Hamda difficulty to find ❌, difficulty finding ✅.

have fun / have a good time + -ing

Xuddi shu qolip yaxshi vaqt o‘tkazish haqida ham ishlatiladi:

  • The children had fun playing in the snow.Bolalar qorda o‘ynab quvnashdi.
  • We had a great time visiting Bukhara.Buxoroga borib juda yaxshi vaqt o‘tkazdik.
Qiyinchilik: “to” yoki “-ing”? (Unit 65 bilan solishtiring)
QolipMisol
sifat + to + fe’l (Unit 65)It’s difficult to find a parking space.
have difficulty + -ing (shu dars)I had difficulty finding a parking space.

Vaqt va pulni sarflash, band bo‘lish A waste of time/money; spend / waste time; be busy

It’s a waste of time / money + -ing

  • It’s a waste of time reading books you don’t understand.Tushunmaydigan kitoblarni o‘qish — vaqtni behuda sarflash.
  • It’s a waste of money buying a new phone every year.Har yili yangi telefon sotib olish — pulni isrof qilish.

spend / waste time or money + -ing

Ega+spend / waste+vaqt / pul+fe’l-ing
  • I spent two hours looking for my passport.Pasportimni qidirib ikki soat sarfladim.
  • Kamola spends a lot of time watching videos.Kamola ko‘p vaqtini video ko‘rib o‘tkazadi.
  • Don’t waste your money buying cheap shoes.Arzon poyabzal sotib olib pulingizni isrof qilmang.
  • We wasted the whole morning waiting at the bank.Butun ertalabni bankda kutib behuda o‘tkazdik.
spend: -ing yoki on + ot
QolipMisol
spend time + -ingShe spends hours practising the piano.
spend time on + otShe spends hours on piano practice.
spend time to + fe’l ❌She spends hours to practise.

O‘zbekcha “Men soatlab dars qilishga vaqt sarflayman” yoki “dars qilish uchun vaqt sarflayman” deb aytganimiz uchun, o‘quvchilar inglizchada ❌ spend time to study deb yozishadi. To‘g‘ri: spend time studying.

be busy + -ing

  • Mum is busy cooking plov for the guests.Onam mehmonlar uchun palov pishirish bilan band.
  • The students were busy preparing for their exams.Talabalar imtihonlarga tayyorgarlik bilan band edi.

Ot bilan: busy with homework, busy with the guests. Fe’l bilan esa predlog yo‘q: busy cooking ✅ — busy with cooking ham mumkin, lekin kamroq uchraydi.

Sport, sayohat va dam olish faoliyatlari haqida gapirganda go + fe’l-ing ishlatiladi. Predlog kerak emas:

go for a + ot

Yana bir qolip: go for a + ot (bir martalik, qisqa faoliyat): go for a walk, go for a swim, go for a run, go for a drive, go for a ride.

  • After dinner we went for a walk in the park.Kechki ovqatdan keyin parkda sayr qilgani chiqdik.
  • It’s hot. Let’s go for a swim.Havo issiq. Keling, cho‘milaylik.

We went to swimming ❌, We went for swimming ❌ → We went swimming ✅ yoki We went for a swim ✅. Hamma fe’lga go qo‘shib bo‘lmaydi: go playing football ❌ → play football ✅.

Sport nomlari bilan fe’lni to‘g‘ri tanlang: play — to‘p o‘yinlari va o‘yinlar (play football, play tennis, play chess); do — shaxsiy mashqlar (do yoga, do karate, do judo); go — -ing faoliyatlar (go running, go swimming).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega bu iboralardan keyin “to” emas, “-ing” keladi?

Ingliz tilida shunday qolip o‘rnashgan. Bu iboralar -ing bilan birga bitta tayyor blok sifatida ishlatiladi: no use + -ing, worth + -ing, difficulty + -ing. Ularni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri tarjima qilib bo‘lmaydi — shunchaki yodlab oling. Eslatma: It’s difficult to find (sifat + to, Unit 65) bilan have difficulty finding ni adashtirmang.

“There’s no point in waiting” da “in” shartmi?

Yo‘q, in ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin: There’s no point (in) waiting. Xuddi shunday have difficulty (in) finding da ham in ixtiyoriy; in bilan gap biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi.

Nega “worth visiting it” deb bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki The castle is worth visiting gapida castle — borilayotgan joy, ya’ni -ing fe’lining to‘ldiruvchisi. To‘ldiruvchi allaqachon gapning boshida aytilgan, uni it bilan takrorlash ortiqcha: The castle is worth visiting. Agar it kerak bo‘lsa: It’s worth visiting the castle yoki It’s worth it (“arziydi”).

“worth” va “worthwhile” bir xilmi?

Ma’nosi yaqin, lekin qoliplari boshqa. worth dan keyin -ing yoki ot keladi (worth trying, worth the effort). worthwhile — sifat, bitta so‘z: It’s worthwhile (to learn / learning) a second language. It isn’t worthwhile to ... kabi infinitiv bilan ham uchraydi.

Qaysi faoliyatlar bilan “go + -ing” ishlatiladi?

Asosan sport, sayohat va dam olish: go swimming, fishing, shopping, skiing, running, camping. Futbol, tennis, shaxmat kabi o‘yinlar bilan play (play football), yoga, karate bilan do (do yoga) ishlatiladi. go playing football ❌.

“spend time on” va “spend time doing” farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil, qolipi boshqa: on + ot (spend time on homework) yoki fe’l-ing (spend time doing homework). Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. spend time to do ❌.

“waste of time” bilan “wasting time” farqi nima?

a waste of time — ot iborasi: It’s a waste of time. waste time — fe’l: You’re wasting your time. / She wasted time looking for it. Ikkalasidan keyin ham -ing kelishi mumkin.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • It’s no use / no good + -ing; There’s no point (in) + -ing; It’s (not) worth + -ing.
  • worth dan keyin to ham, it ham yo‘q: The book is worth reading.
  • It’s a waste of time / money + -ing; spend / waste time + -ing (spend time to do ❌); be busy + -ing.
  • have difficulty (in) / trouble / a hard time + -ing — difficulty dan oldin a yo‘q.
  • have fun / a good time + -ing.
  • go + -ing sport va dam olish uchun: go swimming, shopping, fishing. Bir martalik: go for a walk / swim / run.
  • Imlo: swim → swimming, run → running, shop → shopping, dance → dancing, hike → hiking.
