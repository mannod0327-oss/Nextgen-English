Much, many, little, few, a lot, plenty

Much, many, little, few, a lot, plenty
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • many — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan, much — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan; How many …? How much …?
  • a lot of, lots of, plenty of — “ko‘p”, ayniqsa darak gaplarda.
  • little / few (kam, yetarli emas) va a little / a few (bir oz, bir nechta — bor) farqi.
  • quite a few, only a few, very little, too much, so many, as much as.
  • Ot tushirib qoldirilganda: a lot, plenty, not much, not many.

Miqdorni aytishda inglizcha so‘z ot qanday turdaligiga bog‘liq: sanaladigan (friends, apples) yoki sanalmaydigan (money, water, time). Bu haqda Unit 69–70 da gaplashgan edik. Endi shu ma’lumotdan foydalanamiz.

O‘zbek tilida “ko‘p” bitta so‘z: ko‘p pul, ko‘p odam, ko‘p vaqt. Inglizchada esa ot turiga qarab tanlanadi: much money, many people, much time. Savolda ham shunday: “qancha” (How much?) va “nechta” (How many?). “Oz” — little / few, “ozgina, biroz” — a little, “bir nechta” — a few. Eslab qoling: o‘zbekchada “ko‘p” sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar uchun bir xil, inglizchada esa boshqa-boshqa.

Much va many Much and many

Many — ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan otlar bilan, much — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan:

Many va much: asosiy farq
MANY + ko‘plik ot (sanaladigan)MUCH + sanalmaydigan ot
❓ So‘roqHow many friends do you have?
Sizning nechta do‘stingiz bor?		How much time do you need?
Sizga qancha vaqt kerak?
❓ So‘roq (yes/no)Do you know many people here?
Bu yerda ko‘p odamni taniysizmi?		Do you drink much tea?
Ko‘p choy ichasizmi?
❌ InkorThere aren’t many hotels here.
Bu yerda mehmonxonalar ko‘p emas.		We don’t have much time.
Bizda vaqt ko‘p emas.
✅ DarakWe have a lot of friends. (many — rasmiyroq)We have a lot of time. (much ❌)

Qoida: many va much asosan savol va inkor gaplarda ishlatiladi. Darak gapda kundalik nutqda a lot of / lots of aytiladi: She has a lot of friends. Much oddiy darak gapda deyarli ishlatilmaydi: We have much time ❌. Many darak gapda to‘g‘ri, lekin biroz rasmiy: Many people think so.

  • How many languages do you speak?Siz nechta tilda gaplashasiz?
  • There weren’t many people at the concert.Konsertda odam ko‘p emas edi.
  • How much sugar do you take in your tea?Choyingizga qancha shakar solasiz?
  • I don’t spend much money on clothes.Men kiyimga ko‘p pul sarflamayman.
  • Dilnoza doesn’t have many hobbies, but she reads a lot.Dilnozaning ermaklari ko‘p emas, lekin u ko‘p kitob o‘qiydi.

Too much, so many, as much as

Too, so, as va very dan keyin much / many darak gapda ham ishlatiladi:

too / so / as + much, many
IboraSanaladigan (many)Sanalmaydigan (much)
too (me’yordan ortiq)There are too many cars in the city.There is too much traffic.
so (shunchalik)I have so many things to do!I have so much work!
as … as (…chalik)Take as many photos as you like.Eat as much as you want.
how (qancha / nechta)How many eggs do we need?How much flour do we need?

Ot bo‘lmasa ham ishlatiladi

  • “Did you take many photos?” “No, not many.”— Ko‘p rasmga oldingizmi? — Yo‘q, ko‘p emas.
  • “Have you got much homework?” “Quite a lot.”— Uy vazifangiz ko‘pmi? — Ancha ko‘p.

Much of / many of bilan the, my, these yoki them keladi: many of my friends, much of the time (Unit 88).

A lot of, lots of, plenty of A lot (of) / lots (of) / plenty (of)

A lot of va lots of ikkalasi ham sanaladigan ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatiladi. Lots of norasmiyroq. Plenty of — “yetarli yoki undan ham ko‘p” (more than enough).

  • There are a lot of tourists in Samarkand in May.May oyida Samarqandda sayyohlar juda ko‘p bo‘ladi.
  • Sardor has lots of cousins in Fergana.Sardorning Farg‘onada qarindoshlari juda ko‘p.
  • The train leaves at nine. We’ve got plenty of time for breakfast.Poyezd soat to‘qqizda jo‘naydi. Nonushta qilishga vaqtimiz bemalol yetadi (yetarlidan ham ko‘p).
  • There’s plenty of food for everybody.Hamma uchun ovqat yetarli.
  • Do you have a lot of homework tonight?Bugun kechqurun uy vazifangiz ko‘pmi?

A lot of savol va inkorda ham ishlatiladi: I don’t have a lot of time = I don’t have much time. Fe’l of dan keyingi otga moslashadi: A lot of people are waiting. A lot of money is needed.

A lot, lots, plenty — ot yo‘q

Ot tushunarli bo‘lsa yoki fe’lni bildirsa, of tushiriladi:

  • “Do you like football?” “Yes, I like it a lot.”— Futbolni yoqtirasizmi? — Ha, juda yoqtiraman.
  • My brother eats a lot but he never gets fat.Akam ko‘p yeydi, lekin hech semirmaydi.
  • Thanks a lot for your help.Yordamingiz uchun katta rahmat.
  • “More tea?” “No, thanks. I’ve had plenty.”— Yana choy? — Yo‘q, rahmat. Yetarli ichdim.

Much ravish sifatida odatda inkor va savolda: I don’t like jazz much. Do you travel much? Darak gapda a lot yoki very much aytamiz: I like jazz a lot. ✅, I like jazz much. ❌ (tabiiy emas).

Little va few; a little va a few Little / few; a little / a few

Bu bo‘limdagi eng muhim fikr: a harfi ma’noni butunlay o‘zgartiradi. Few / little — “kam, yetarli emas” (salbiy). A few / a little — “bir oz, bir nechta — bor, yetarli” (ijobiy).

Little, few, a little, a few

Juft-juft taqqoslash

😞 few / little

  • The new café isn’t popular. It gets few customers.
  • There’s little bread left — we need to buy some.
  • Jasur is always busy. He has little free time.

🙂 a few / a little

  • The café isn’t empty. There are a few customers inside.
  • There’s a little bread left — enough for breakfast.
  • Jasur has a little free time today, so he can help us.
  • Very few people came, so the meeting was cancelled.Juda kam odam keldi, shuning uchun yig‘ilish bekor qilindi.
  • The film starts in twenty minutes. We have a little time for an ice cream.Film yigirma daqiqadan keyin boshlanadi. Muzqaymoq yeyishga ozgina vaqtimiz bor.
  • I saw Kamola a few days ago.Kamolani bir necha kun oldin ko‘rdim.
  • I’ve got a few questions about the test.Test haqida bir nechta savolim bor.
  • There is little hope of finding the keys. We’ve looked everywhere.Kalitlarni topish umidi kam. Hamma joyni qidirdik.

O‘zbekchada oz va ozgina — ikki xil so‘z, farqi “a” ga o‘xshaydi: “Menda pul oz” (yetishmaydi) = I have little money. “Menda ozgina pul bor” (bir oz bor) = I have a little money. “Bir nechta” = a few, “oz sonli” = few.

Little ning ikkinchi ma’nosi — “kichkina”: a little girl = kichkina qiz, a little house = kichkina uy. Bu miqdor ma’nosidan boshqa. Taqqoslang: a little girl (kichkina qiz) — a little water (ozgina suv).

Few / little (a siz) rasmiyroq. Og‘zaki nutqda ko‘pincha not many / not much yoki hardly any aytiladi: We don’t get many visitors. A little sifat yoki ravish oldida “biroz” bo‘lib ham keladi: a little tired, a little better (norasmiy: a bit tired).

Less va fewer

Kamroq degan ma’noda: less + sanalmaydigan ot, fewer + ko‘plik sanaladigan ot (an’anaviy qoida):

  • I drink less coffee than I used to.Men avvalgidan kamroq kofe ichaman.
  • There are fewer students in class today.Bugun sinfda talabalar kamroq.

Og‘zaki nutqda less people deyuvchilar ham bor, lekin imtihonda va rasmiy yozuvda fewer people to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi. Pul, vaqt va masofa miqdori bilan less ishlatiladi: less than 20 pounds, less than ten minutes.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “a lot of” darak gapda yaxshi, “much” esa yomon?

Ingliz tilida shunday odat shakllangan: much va many asosan savol va inkorda ishlatiladi, darak gapda esa a lot of / lots of. Hech qanday sehr yo‘q — bu shunchaki tabiiy ohang. Many darak gapda ham to‘g‘ri, lekin rasmiyroq eshitiladi (Many people think so); much deyarli faqat too, so, as, very dan keyin darak gapda keladi.

“Few” va “a few” — aniq nechta?

Aniq son yo‘q. Ikkalasi ham kichik son, lekin few “yetarli emas, juda kam” degan salbiy munosabat bildiradi, a few esa “bir nechta, bor” degan ijobiy munosabat. Masalan, 3 ta mijoz: The café gets few customers (kam, afsus) yoki There are a few customers in the café (bor, bo‘sh emas). Shu sababli ular ko‘pincha mazmuniga qarab tanlanadi.

“A lot of” bilan fe’l birlikda yoki ko‘plikda keladimi?

Fe’l of dan keyingi otga moslashadi: A lot of people are here (people — ko‘plik), A lot of money is needed (money — birlik).

“Lots of” bilan “a lot of” farqi nima?

Ma’nosi bir xil. Lots of norasmiyroq, og‘zaki nutqda ko‘proq. Rasmiy yozuvda a great deal of (sanalmaydigan) yoki a large number of (sanaladigan) ishlatiladi.

“Plenty of” bilan “a lot of” bir xilmi?

Yaqin, lekin bir xil emas. Plenty of — “kerakdan yetarli yoki undan ko‘p”. We have plenty of chairs — hammaga yetadi, ortib ham qoladi. We have a lot of chairs ham “stul ko‘p” degani, lekin “yetarli” degan ma’no kuchsizroq.

Less bilan fewer-ni qachon ishlatish kerak?

An’anaviy qoida: sanaladigan ko‘plik uchun fewer (fewer cars), sanalmaydigan uchun less (less traffic). Kundalik nutqda less cars deganlar ham bor, lekin imtihonda qoidaga amal qiling. Pul, vaqt, masofa o‘lchovlari bilan less: less than 5 kilometres.

“Time” qachon sanaladigan, qachon sanalmaydigan?

“Vaqt” ma’nosida sanalmaydi: How much time? “Marta” ma’nosida sanaladi: How many times have you been to Bukhara? (Unit 70).

Ot tushib qolganda “much” bilan “many” ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha: “How many apples did you buy?” “Not many.”, “Have you got much homework?” “Not much.” Darak gapda esa a lot, plenty, lots aytiladi: “How many apples?” “A lot.”

🧠 Esda tuting

  • many + ko‘plik sanaladigan ot; much + sanalmaydigan ot. How many …? How much …?
  • Darak gapda odatda a lot of / lots of; plenty of = yetarlidan ham ko‘p.
  • Savol va inkorda much / many; too, so, as dan keyin darak gapda ham.
  • few / little = kam, yetarli emas 😞; a few / a little = bir oz, bor 🙂.
  • Few + sanaladigan ko‘plik; little + sanalmaydigan.
  • Quite a few = ancha ko‘p; only a few = juda oz; hardly any = deyarli yo‘q.
  • Kamroq: fewer + sanaladigan, less + sanalmaydigan.
