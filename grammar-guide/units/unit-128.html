By

By predlogi
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • by + transport: by car, by bus, by train, by plane, by bike, by taxi, by road / rail / air / sea va on foot; hamda aniq vosita bilan in my car, on the 8 o’clock train.
  • by + aloqa va to‘lov: by post, by email, by phone, pay by card / by cheque (naqd pul uchun esa in cash, Unit 127), by hand.
  • by accident, by chance, by mistake va by myself (yolg‘iz, hech kimsiz).
  • by + -ing va by bilan with farqi: by pressing the button — with a key.
  • a book by Navoi, majhul nisbatdagi written by, sit by the window va prices rose by 10%.

By — inglizchadagi eng ko‘p ma’noli predloglardan biri. Lekin uning ma’nolarini beshta savolga bog‘lab, tartibga solish mumkin:

Agar biror aniq mashina, avtobus yoki poyezd haqida gapirsak (egasi, raqami, vaqti, tavsifi bor), by ishlatilmaydi. Kichik, yopiq transportda (car, taxi) in, jamoat transporti va minib olinadigan vositada (bus, train, plane, bike) on ishlatiladi (Unit 124 ga qarang).

By yoki with?

O‘zbekcha bilan ikkiga bo‘linadi: umumiy usul (by) va aniq asbob (with). With dan keyin odatda a / the / egalik olmoshi keladi, chunki aniq asbob haqida gapiramiz.

Qoida: by + usul (odatda artiklsiz ot yoki -ing): by hand, by phone, by pressing. with + aniq asbob (a / the / my bilan): with a knife, with my key. with yana “biror kishi bilan birga” ma’nosida ham keladi: I went to Bukhara with my cousin.

Muallif va majhul nisbatda by A book by …; the passive with by

A book / painting / song by …

Kitob, rasm, qo‘shiq va filmning muallifini ko‘rsatish uchun ot dan keyin by qo‘yiladi. O‘zbekcha “Qodiriyning romani” izofa bilan aytiladi, inglizchada esa a novel by Qodiriy:

  • “O‘tkan kunlar” is a novel by Abdulla Qodiriy.“O‘tkan kunlar” — Abdulla Qodiriyning romani.
  • Have you ever read anything by Alisher Navoi?Alisher Navoiyning biror asarini o‘qiganmisiz?
  • This miniature was painted by an unknown artist in Bukhara.Bu miniatyurani Buxoroda noma’lum rassom chizgan.
  • The song was composed by my grandfather.Qo‘shiqni bobom bastalagan.

❌ a novel from Qodiriy deb aytmang: muallifni ko‘rsatishning odatiy yo‘li — by: a novel by Qodiriy. (the works of Navoi — “Navoiy asarlari” kabi umumiy iboralarda of ham uchraydi.)

Majhul nisbat + by

Majhul nisbatda (Unit 42) ishni kim bajarganini aytmoqchi bo‘lsak, by + bajaruvchi qo‘shamiz. Bajaruvchi inson bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin: tabiat hodisasi, sabab:

  • The letter was written by Malika.Xatni Malika yozgan.
  • The old gate was damaged by the storm.Eski darvoza bo‘ron tufayli shikastlandi.
  • Our house was designed by a young architect.Uyimizni yosh me’mor loyihalagan.

Asbob esa with bilan keladi: The rope was cut with a knife. Bajaruvchi noma’lum yoki ahamiyatsiz bo‘lsa, by tushirib qoldiriladi: The window was broken.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“by car” deymiz-u, nega “in my car”? Ikkalasi qanday farq qiladi?

by car — umumiy usul (qanday yo‘l bilan?): I go to work by car. Bu yerda aniq mashina ko‘zda tutilmaydi, shuning uchun a/the/my yo‘q. in my car — aniq mashina: I left my bag in my car. Aniq narsa haqida gapirsak (egasi, tavsifi, raqami), by ishlatilmaydi.

“by bus” va “on the bus” farqi nimada?

by bus — qanday borasiz (usul): She goes to school by bus. on the bus — avtobus ichida bo‘lish (joy/holat): I met Aziz on the bus. Xuddi shunday: by train — on the train; by plane — on the plane; by bike — on my bike. Mashina va taksi uchun in: by car — in the car; by taxi — in a taxi.

“by foot” ham deyiladimi?

Standart tilda yo‘q: on foot yoki fe’l bilan walk: We went on foot. / We walked. “Yurish orqali, piyoda yurib” desangiz — by walking (by + -ing): You can stay fit by walking every day.

Qachon “by”, qachon “with” ishlataman?

Oddiy test: gapda a / the / my bilan aniq asbob bo‘lsa — with (with a key); umumiy usul yoki -ing shakli bo‘lsa — by (by hand, by using a key). “Kim bilan birga” ma’nosida ham with: with my brother.

“by accident”, “by mistake” va “by chance” ni qanday ajrataman?

Ma’nolari yaqin. by accident — niyatsiz (bexosdan qo‘l tegib ketdi). by mistake — adashib (boshqa narsa deb o‘ylab). by chance — rejasiz uchrashuv yoki topilma. Qarama-qarshisi — on purpose (Unit 127).

“by myself”, “on my own” va “alone” bir xilmi?

Deyarli bir xil: by myself = on my own (yolg‘iz yoki hech kimsiz, Unit 83). alone — faqat “yolg‘iz”, yordamsiz ma’nosi yo‘q: I live alone. Hech kimsiz bajargan ishni aytsangiz by myself eng aniq: I fixed the bike by myself. lonely esa boshqa — “yolg‘izlikdan ezilgan”.

Majhul nisbatda by har doim kerakmi?

Yo‘q. by + bajaruvchi faqat bajaruvchi muhim yoki yangi ma’lumot bo‘lsa qo‘shiladi: The mosque was built by a famous architect. Bajaruvchi noma’lum yoki ahamiyatsiz bo‘lsa, tushiriladi: The shop was robbed. (Unit 42, Unit 43.)

“rose by 10%” bilan “rose to 10%” farqi nimada?

by 10% — o‘zgarish miqdori (10 foizga oshdi). to 10% — yakuniy daraja (10 foizga yetdi). Unemployment rose by 2% — to 8%. (Ishsizlik 2 foizga oshib, 8 foizga yetdi.)

“by Friday” (muddat) ham shu predlog-mi?

Ha, bu — by ning beshinchi ma’nosi: “juma kunigacha” (shu kuni yoki undan oldin). Bu Unit 120 da: Please reply by Friday. Uni until (“juma kunigacha davom etib”) bilan aralashtirmang.
