There ... and it ...

There ... va it ...
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • There is / there are — “…bor” degan ma’noni qanday aytishni (biror narsaning mavjudligi).
  • There bilan it o‘rtasidagi farq: there yangi narsani tanishtiradi, it allaqachon aytilgan narsaga qaytadi.
  • There ning boshqa shakllari: there was, there has been, there will be, there must be, there used to be ...
  • There’s no point in ..., there’s no need to ..., there’s nothing to ... kabi tayyor iboralar.
  • It ning vaqt, ob-havo va masofa haqidagi gaplardagi o‘rni: It’s raining. It’s five o’clock.

O‘zbek tilida “xonada stol bor” deymiz: stol — ega, bor — kesim, gapni joy bilan boshlash odatiy hol. Inglizchada esa kesimdan oldin doim ega turishi kerak, mavjudlik haqidagi gapda bu o‘rinni odatda there egallaydi (u “formal ega” vazifasini bajaradi): There is a table in the room.

O‘zbekchada “bor” bitta so‘z, lekin inglizchada uni ikki xil aytamiz. Xonada stol bor = There is a table in the room. (narsa shu yerda mavjud), Menda mashina bor = I have a car. (kimga tegishli). Ertaklarning boshlanishi ham shunday: “Bir bor ekan, bir yo‘q ekan…” = Once upon a time there was …. Shuningdek Yomg‘ir yog‘yapti, Sovuq kabi gaplarda inglizchada it degan formal ega kerak: It is raining. It is cold.

“…bor”: there is / there are There is / There are

There is / there are yangi narsaning mavjudligini bildiradi: “bunday narsa bor”, “shu yerda …”. Gap boshida there turadi, uning ortidan be fe’li, keyin esa nima borligi aytiladi:

There+is / are+a / some / any / no / son + ot+joy / vaqt
  • There is a bank near the station.Bekat yaqinida bank bor.
  • There are three bridges over the river.Daryo ustida uchta ko‘prik bor.
  • There is some milk in the fridge.Muzlatkichda ozgina sut bor.
  • There was a long queue at the bank this morning.Bugun ertalab bankda uzun navbat bor edi.
  • Once upon a time there was a king who had three sons.Bir bor ekan, bir yo‘q ekan, bir podshoh bo‘lgan ekan, uning uch o‘g‘li bor ekan.

So‘roq so‘zlari bilan

So‘roq so‘zi gapning boshiga chiqadi, there esa be dan keyin keladi:

  • How many students are there in your group?Guruhingizda nechta talaba bor?
  • How much milk is there in the fridge?Muzlatkichda qancha sut bor?
  • What is there to do in this village?Bu qishloqda nima qilsa bo‘ladi? (qanday ermaklar bor?)
  • What restaurants are there near here?Bu atrofda qanday restoranlar bor?

Tag-savol: There’s a problem, isn’t there? / There aren’t any tickets left, are there? — there tag-savolda ham ega vazifasida takrorlanadi (Unit 52).

Birlik yoki ko‘plik?

Fe’l there dan keyin keladigan otga moslashadi: bitta narsa — is / was, bir nechta narsa — are / were.

Birlik / sanalmaydigan

  • There is a mosque in my street.
  • There was a lot of noise.

Ko‘plik

  • There are five mosques in my district.
  • There were many people at the bazaar.

Og‘zaki nutqda there’s ko‘plik bilan ham aytilishi mumkin: There’s two people outside. Bu so‘zlashuvda uchraydi, lekin yozma ishda va imtihonda There are deyish kerak.

There dan keyin odatda noaniq ot keladi

There is bilan yangi, hali tinglovchi bilmagan narsani tanishtiramiz. Shuning uchun keyin odatda a / an, some, any, no, son yoki a lot of keladi. Aniq, ma’lum narsa haqida (the, my, this, Malika’s) “bor” demaymiz, balki oddiy gap tuzamiz: The key is on the table. ✅ — There is the key on the table. ❌ (oddiy gapda).

O‘zbek tilidagi gap tartibi: Stol ustida bitta kitob bor. (joy → narsa → bor). Inglizchada: There is a book on the table. (there is → narsa → joy). Fe’l (is/are) o‘zbekchadagidek oxirda emas, boshida turadi va uning oldida there bo‘lishi shart.

There va it: nima farq? There ... and it ...

Bu darsning asosiy g‘oyasi shu: there — yangi narsani sahnaga olib chiqadi; shundan keyin u haqida gapirsak, it / they / he / she ishlatamiz.

Mana kichik bir matn — there va it qanday almashinishini ko‘ring:

  • There’s a new café in Navoi Street. It opened last month. It’s very cheap, and it’s always full of students. There are only ten tables, so there’s often a queue. It’s worth waiting, though.Navoiy ko‘chasida yangi kafe bor. U o‘tgan oy ochilgan. U juda arzon va doim talabalarga to‘la. Atigi o‘nta stol bor, shuning uchun ko‘pincha navbat bo‘ladi. Lekin kutishga arziydi.

“It is …” — nima ekanini aytish; “There is …” — borligini aytish

Biror narsani nomlash va tavsiflash uchun it is, mavjudligini bildirish uchun there is ishlatiladi:

It is … (bu nima?)

  • “What’s that?” “It’s a mosque.”
  • It’s a beautiful old building.

There is … (bor-yo‘qligi)

  • “Is there a mosque near here?” “Yes, there is.”
  • There’s a beautiful old building in the square.

There — “o‘sha yerda” ma’nosida ham keladi

Yana bir there bor: u joy ravishi (adverb), “o‘sha yerda / o‘sha yerga” degan ma’noni beradi. Gap ichida ikkalasi bir vaqtda kelishi mumkin:

  • There are many old buildings there.U yerda ko‘plab eski binolar bor. (birinchi there — “bor” ma’nosida, ikkinchisi — “o‘sha yerda”)
  • I’m going to Samarkand. My sister lives there.Men Samarqandga ketyapman. Opam o‘sha yerda yashaydi.
  • Look! There’s Jasur!Qara! Anavi Jasur-ku!
Ikkita “there”
There 1 (gap egasi)There 2 (joy ravishi)
Mavjudlikni bildiradi: “…bor”. Deyarli doim be fe’li bilan keladi.“O‘sha yerda / o‘sha yerga”. Istalgan fe’l bilan keladi (go there, live there).
Tarjima qilinmaydi: There is a book. = Kitob bor.Tarjima qilinadi: She lives there. = U o‘sha yerda yashaydi.
Gap oxirida kelmaydi (tag-savoldan tashqari).Gap oxirida keladi: I met him there.

There ning boshqa shakllari There will be / there must be / there used to be ...

There + be faqat hozirgi zamonda emas. Be o‘rniga har xil zamon va modal shakllarni qo‘yish mumkin. Gapning qolgan qismi o‘zgarmaydi: there doim egalik qiladi.

Savol va inkor shakllari

Savolda yordamchi fe’l there dan oldin chiqadi: modal / have / will + there + be.

  • Will there be a party on Saturday?Shanba kuni ziyofat bo‘ladimi?
  • Has there been any news from Dilnoza?Dilnozadan biror xabar bo‘ldimi?
  • Was there enough time to finish the test?Testni tugatishga vaqt yetarli bo‘ldimi?
  • There won’t be any lessons tomorrow.Ertaga darslar bo‘lmaydi.
  • There hasn’t been any rain for weeks.Bir necha haftadan beri yomg‘ir yog‘gani yo‘q.

Qisqa shakllar: there’s (is yoki has), there’ll (will), there’s been (has been), there’d (would): There’ll be a test on Monday. There’s been a mistake.

be ni tushirib qoldirmang: There will have a party. ❌ — There will be a party. ✅. There must have a reason. ❌ — There must be a reason. ✅.

There must have been …, there’s bound to be …

O‘tgan zamon haqidagi taxmin yoki tanqid uchun there must have been / there might have been / there should have been ishlatiladi:

  • The streets are white this morning. There must have been snow in the night.Bugun ertalab ko‘chalar oppoq. Kechasi qor yoqqan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • The bus was full. There should have been more buses on that route.Avtobus to‘la edi. O‘sha yo‘nalishda avtobuslar ko‘proq bo‘lishi kerak edi.

“Albatta bo‘ladi / bo‘lishi ehtimoli katta” degan ma’noda there is sure / certain / bound / likely to be … deyiladi:

  • Don’t worry about dinner. There’s bound to be a café near the hotel.Kechki ovqatdan xavotir olmang. Mehmonxona yaqinida albatta biror kafe bo‘ladi.
  • There’s likely to be heavy traffic on Friday evening.Juma kuni kechqurun tirbandlik kuchli bo‘lishi ehtimoli katta.

Modal va used to bilan ham there / it farqi saqlanadi

There + modal + be + ot (borligi)

  • Their flat is next to the airport. There must be a lot of noise.
  • There used to be a lot of trees in this street.

It + modal + be + sifat / ot (qandayligi)

  • Their flat is next to the airport. It must be very noisy.
  • It used to be a quiet street, but now it’s full of cafés.

Bu yerda there bilan tayyor iboralar bor. Ular nutqda juda ko‘p uchraydi. Ularni bir butun qilib yodlab oling.

It: ob-havo, vaqt, masofa It is raining / It’s five o’clock / It’s far

Ba’zi gaplarda ma’lum bir narsa yo‘q, lekin ega baribir kerak. Bunday gaplarda ham it ishlatiladi. O‘zbek tilida bunday gaplarda it ga mos so‘z yo‘q (Yomg‘ir yog‘yapti. Sovuq. Kech bo‘ldi.), shuning uchun it ni tushirib qoldirish — eng ko‘p uchraydigan xatolardan biri.

Uzun ega o‘rnida “it”: It’s dangerous to …, It’s not worth …

Ega to + fe’l yoki that … bilan boshlanadigan uzun bo‘lak bo‘lsa, uni gap oxiriga suramiz va boshiga it qo‘yamiz. To swim in this river is dangerous. deyish mumkin, lekin odatda bunday demaymiz:

  • It’s not worth taking a taxi — the bazaar is very close.Taksiga o‘tirishga arzimaydi — bozor juda yaqin.

Ob-havoda: sifat bo‘lsa — it: It was very windy yesterday. Ot bo‘lsa — there: There was a strong wind yesterday. Xuddi shunday: It’s foggy. — There’s fog on the road.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchada “bor” bitta so‘z-ku. Inglizchada qachon “have”, qachon “there is” deyman?

Savolni o‘ylab ko‘ring: kimda bor? yoki qayerda bor? Menda mashina bor → kimda? → I have a car. Hovlida mashina bor → qayerda? → There is a car in the yard. Ba’zan ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi: Our town has a big park = There is a big park in our town.

There is va It is ning farqini qisqa qilib qanday eslab qolaman?

There — “mana bunday narsa bor!” deb yangi narsani tanishtiradi. It — “o‘sha narsa” haqida, ma’lum narsa haqida gapiradi. Birinchi marta aytyapsizmi — there; allaqachon aytilganmi — it.

“There’s two books” deb aytsam bo‘ladimi?

Og‘zaki nutqda ingliz tilida so‘zlovchilar shunday deyishadi, lekin yozma ishda, imtihonda va rasmiy nutqda There are two books deyish to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi. Shuning uchun o‘rganayotganda are ni ishlating.

There dan keyin “the” bo‘lishi mumkinmi?

Oddiy “…bor” ma’nosida odatda yo‘q. Ikki istisno: (1) ro‘yxat berganda: Who can help us? Well, there’s Dilnoza, there’s Bekzod … (2) there joy ravishi bo‘lganda, ko‘rsatib: Look, there’s the bus! (anavi avtobus!).

Nega “It is raining” da “it” kerak? U nimani bildiradi?

Hech narsani bildirmaydi. Inglizcha gapda ega bo‘lishi shart, ob-havo, vaqt, masofa haqida gapirganda esa aniq ega yo‘q. Shuning uchun it “quruq ega” vazifasini bajaradi. O‘zbekchaga tarjima qilinmaydi.

“Is there …?” bilan “Do you have …?” savollari qanday farq qiladi?

Is there a printer in the office? — ofisda printer bormi (joyda mavjudmi)? Do you have a printer? — sizda (yoki sizning ofisingizda) printer bormi? Ikkinchisi kimga tegishli ekanini so‘raydi.

“There” fe’l bilan boshqa o‘rinlarda ham kela oladimi (exist, live, seem)?

Ha, lekin faqat rasmiy yozuvda: There exists a problem …, There remains one question. Kundalik nutqda deyarli har doim be bilan ishlatiladi. Faqat there seems/appears to be kundalik nutqda ham uchraydi.

“There” va “their”, “they’re” ni qanday adashtirmayman?

Uchalasining talaffuzi deyarli bir xil: there — “bor” yoki “u yerda”; their — “ularning” (their house); they’re = they are (they’re students). Yozayotganda “ularning” bo‘lsa, ot oldida turadigan their ni, “ular …dir” bo‘lsa they’re ni tanlang.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • There is/are = “…bor”; yangi narsani tanishtiradi: There’s a bank near here. Birlikda is, ko‘plikda are.
  • Narsa haqida birinchi marta aytsangiz — there; keyingi gaplarda u haqida — it / they / he / she.
  • Savol: Is there ...? Are there any ...? Qisqa javob: Yes, there is. (Yes, there’s ❌).
  • Boshqa shakllar: there was, there has been, there will be, there must be, there used to be — be ni tushirmang.
  • Iboralar: There’s no point in …ing, no need to …, nothing to ….
  • Ob-havo, vaqt, masofa uchun it shart: It’s raining. It’s late. “Bor” = there is, “egalik” = have.
  • Uzun ega o‘rnida ham it: It’s dangerous to swim here. It’s not worth waiting. Ob-havo: It’s windy (sifat), lekin There’s a strong wind (ot).

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 17 (have va have got), Unit 18 (used to), Unit 28 (must), Unit 52 (question tags), Unit 63 (there’s no point -ing), Unit 85 (some va any), Unit 86 (no, nothing, nobody).
