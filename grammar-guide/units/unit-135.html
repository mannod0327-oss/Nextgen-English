Verb + preposition 4: of/for/from/on

Fe’l + predlog — 4: of / for / from / on
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • of bilan keladigan fe’llar: accuse / suspect sb of, approve / disapprove of, die of, consist of.
  • for bilan keladigan fe’llar: pay (sb) for, thank / forgive / apologise / blame / criticise sb for hamda blame sth on sb.
  • from bilan keladigan fe’llar: suffer from, protect sb from / against, prevent sb from doing.
  • on bilan keladigan fe’llar: depend / rely on, live on, congratulate / compliment sb on va it depends (on) what / how … qoidasi.
  • O‘zbekcha qo‘shimchalar (-ga bog‘liq, -dan aziyat chekmoq, -dan saqlamoq) qayerda to‘g‘ri yo‘l ko‘rsatishini va qayerda xatoga olib borishini.

Oldingi darslarda (Unit 132, 133 va 134) fe’l va predlogning “juftligi” haqida gaplashdik. Bu darsda to‘rtta predlog — of, for, from, on — bilan keladigan eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan fe’llarni guruhlab o‘rganamiz. Ko‘pchilik birikmalarda tuzilma bir xil:

Ega+fe’l+kishi+predlog+ot / -ing

Masalan: accuse him of lying, thank her for helping, protect children from danger, congratulate us on winning. Predlogdan keyin fe’l bo‘lsa, u -ing shaklida keladi (Unit 60, Unit 62).

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar turli qo‘shimchalar bilan beriladi: -da ayblamoq, uchun to‘lamoq, -dan aziyat chekmoq, -ga bog‘liq, bilan tabriklamoq. Ba’zan ular inglizcha predlogga mos keladi (✅), ba’zan esa chalg‘itadi (⚠️). Jadvalni diqqat bilan o‘qing: xatolarning ko‘pi ⚠️ qatorlardan chiqadi.

accuse va suspect

accuse sb OF …

Siz ochiq aytasiz: “Sen buni qilding!” Isbot bo‘lishi shart emas.

  • Dilnoza accused her brother of reading her diary.
  • He was accused of stealing a bicycle, but the police found no proof.

suspect sb OF …

Siz o‘ylaysiz, lekin ishonchingiz komil emas; ba’zan hech narsa demaysiz.

  • Nobody suspected the quiet new student of spreading the rumours.
  • She is suspected of taking the money.

Ikkala fe’ldan keyin ham ot yoki -ing keladi: accuse him of theft / of stealing. Majhul nisbatda (Unit 42–44) ham predlog saqlanadi: He was accused of theft. Huquqiy tilda yana charge sb with ishlatiladi: He was charged with theft. (politsiya yoki sud rasman ayb qo‘ygan.) suspect fe’li ham ot sifatida ishlatiladi, lekin urg‘u boshqa: fe’l — suspect [səˈspekt], ot — suspect [ˈsʌspekt]. Predlogsiz tuzilma ham bor: I suspect (that) he is lying.

approve of va approve

approve of — “yaxshi fikrda bo‘lmoq, ma’qullamoq”. Predlogsiz approve esa rasman tasdiqlamoq, ruxsat bermoq degani. Ikki ma’no butunlay boshqa:

  • Her father doesn’t approve of her working at night.Otasi uning kechasi ishlashini ma’qullamaydi. (otaning fikri)
  • The city council approved the plan for a new park.Shahar kengashi yangi bog‘ rejasini tasdiqladi. (rasmiy qaror)
  • Grandmother disapproves of loud music at dinner.Buvim kechki ovqat paytida baland musiqani yoqtirmaydi.

die of, die from, die in

die: qaysi predlog?
PredlogQachon?Misol
die ofkasallik, tabiiy sabab, his-tuyg‘uThe old man died of old age. / Many people died of hunger.
die fromjarohat, tashqi sababThe climber died from the cold. / He died from loss of blood.
die inhalokat, urush, yong‘inThree people died in the fire.
die forbiror maqsad yo‘lidaHe was ready to die for his country.

of va from ko‘p hollarda almashinadi: died of / from pneumonia ikkalasi ham tabiiy. Imtihonda sabab kasallik bo‘lsa of xavfsizroq, jarohat yoki tashqi sabab bo‘lsa from tabiiy. “Halokatda vafot etdi” — died in an accident (✅ “-da” = in).

consist of

consist of — “tarkibi … dan iborat”. Bu holat fe’li, shuning uchun odatda continuous (is consisting) va majhul nisbatda ishlatilmaydi (Unit 4).

  • The squad consists of twenty-three players.Jamoa tarkibi yigirma uch futbolchidan iborat.
  • The course consists of twelve lessons and a final test.Kurs o‘n ikkita dars va yakuniy testdan iborat.

Sinonimlari: be made up of (o‘zbekcha “tashkil topgan”), comprise (rasmiy). Material haqida gap ketganda esa be made of ishlatiladi: The bridge is made of stone. ❌ consist from. (Kitobiy consist in esa boshqa ma’noda keladi: “mohiyati … da”.)

For: to‘lamoq, rahmat aytmoq, kechirmoq, ayblamoq pay, thank, forgive, apologise, blame, criticise + for

Bu guruhda kishi (kimga to‘lasiz, kimga rahmat aytasiz, kimni ayblaysiz) fe’ldan keyin predlogsiz keladi, sabab yoki nima uchun to‘lanayotgani esa for dan keyin aytiladi:

thank / forgive / blame / criticise+kishi+for+ot / -ing

BirikmaMisolTarjima
pay (sb) for sthBekzod paid the waiter for the tea.Bekzod choy uchun ofitsiantga pul to‘ladi.
thank sb for sth / doingAziz thanked the teacher for helping him with the project.Aziz loyihada yordam bergani uchun ustozga rahmat aytdi.

pay va pay for

pay FOR sth

Sotib olingan narsa yoki olingan xizmat uchun to‘lamoq.

  • Who is paying for the tickets?
  • I paid the taxi driver for the ride.
  • She paid twenty dollars for the scarf.

pay + bill / rent / tax / fine

To‘lov turi o‘zi aytilsa, for kerak emas.

  • Bekzod pays the rent on the first of every month.
  • Please pay the bill at the desk.
  • He had to pay a fine.

O‘zbekchada “pitsaning pulini to‘ladim” deymiz, inglizchada esa sotib olingan narsa oldidan for kerak: I paid for the pizza. ❌ I paid the pizza. Lekin “hisobni to‘ladim” — I paid the bill (predlogsiz). To‘lov usuli haqida pay in cash, pay by card (Unit 128).

thank, forgive, apologise

  • Thank you for the lift. I really appreciate it.Mashinada olib borganingiz uchun rahmat. Juda minnatdorman.
  • We thanked our neighbours for looking after the cat.Mushukka qarab turgani uchun qo‘shnilarimizga rahmat aytdik.
  • Please forgive me for interrupting.Gapingizni bo‘lganim uchun kechiring.
  • He apologised to the neighbours for the noise.U shovqin uchun qo‘shnilardan uzr so‘radi.

thank bilan kishi darhol keladi: thank him, ❌ thank to him. thanks to esa boshqa ibora: “…ning sharofati bilan, tufayli”: Thanks to Sardor, we found the way. apologise haqida: kimdan uzr so‘rasangiz — to (Unit 132), nima uchun — for. Amerika ingliz tilida apologize. I’m sorry for / about sifat bilan (Unit 130).

blame sb for sth = blame sth on sb

blame bilan ikki xil tartib bor: ma’no bir xil, lekin predlog va so‘z tartibi o‘zgaradi.

  • The coach blamed the referee for the defeat. = The coach blamed the defeat on the referee.Murabbiy mag‘lubiyatda hakamni ayblab chiqdi.
  • Dilnoza blames herself for the accident.Dilnoza halokat uchun o‘zini ayblaydi.
  • Who is to blame for this mess?Bu tartibsizlikda kim aybdor?

criticise va shunga o‘xshash fe’llar

criticise (Amerika ingliz tilida va Oksford yozuvida ham criticize) — “tanqid qilmoq”. criticise sb for + ot / -ing. Xuddi shu qolipda: praise sb for (maqtamoq), punish sb for (jazolamoq), reward sb for (mukofotlamoq).

  • The teacher praised Zarina for her clear handwriting.O‘qituvchi Zarinani chiroyli yozuvi uchun maqtadi.
  • The club punished the player for missing training.Klub futbolchini mashg‘ulotni qoldirgani uchun jazoladi.

Eslatma: criticise o‘zi predlogsiz (criticise the plan). Ot shakli criticism of (Unit 129), sifat — critical of (Unit 131).

From: aziyat chekmoq, himoya qilmoq, to‘sqinlik qilmoq suffer from, protect from / against, prevent from

Fe’l + from
BirikmaMa’nosiMisolTarjima
suffer from sth…dan aziyat chekmoq, …i borMy uncle suffers from back pain.Amakim bel og‘rig‘idan aziyat chekadi.
protect sb / sth from / against sth…dan himoya qilmoqA hat protects your head from the sun.Shlyapa boshingizni quyoshdan himoya qiladi.
prevent sb / sth from doingto‘sqinlik qilmoq, qilishga qo‘ymaslikHeavy snow prevented the bus from reaching the village.Qalin qor avtobusning qishloqqa yetib borishiga xalaqit berdi.

suffer from

  • Many big cities suffer from air pollution.Ko‘plab katta shaharlar havo ifloslanishidan aziyat chekadi.
  • Is she still suffering from the flu?U hali ham gripp bilan og‘riyaptimi?
  • Jasur suffers from a lack of sleep before exams.Jasur imtihon oldidan uyqu yetishmasligidan aziyat chekadi.

suffer from ko‘pincha uzoq davom etadigan holat yoki muammoni bildiradi. Bir martalik hodisa uchun fe’l to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri ot bilan keladi: The team suffered a heavy defeat. He suffered serious injuries in the crash. ❌ suffer of.

protect from / against

  • Dilnoza locked the gate to protect the garden from thieves.Dilnoza bog‘ni o‘g‘rilardan saqlash uchun darvozani qulfladi.
  • Vaccines protect people against serious diseases.Emlar odamlarni og‘ir kasalliklardan himoya qiladi.
  • You should protect yourself from the cold in winter.Qishda o‘zingizni sovuqdan asrashingiz kerak.

Ko‘p hollarda from va against almashinadi. from — zarar yetkazadigan kuch, odam yoki tabiat hodisasidan (quyosh, sovuq, o‘g‘ri) saqlash; against — kasallik, xavf yoki tahdidga qarshi himoya (emlash, sug‘urta) uchun ko‘proq uchraydi.

prevent sb from doing

prevent+kishi / narsa+from+fe’l-ing
  • Nothing can prevent me from trying again.Hech narsa meni yana urinib ko‘rishdan to‘xtata olmaydi.
  • How can we prevent the fire from spreading?Yong‘inning tarqalishiga qanday yo‘l qo‘ymaslik mumkin?
  • Good habits can prevent many illnesses.Yaxshi odatlar ko‘p kasalliklarning oldini olishi mumkin.

prevent dan keyin darhol kim yoki nima keladi, undan keyin from + -ing: ✅ The rain prevented us from going out. ❌ The rain prevented to go out. ❌ The rain prevented from going out. Britaniya ingliz tilida from ba’zan tushiriladi (stop him leaving), lekin from bilan gap har doim to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi. stop sb from doing va keep sb from doing ham xuddi shu qolipda.

live on sth…ga kun ko‘rmoq, …ni yeb yashamoqMany students live on very little money.Ko‘p talabalar juda oz pul bilan kun ko‘radi.

  • Many families in the region depend on tourism for their income.Mintaqadagi ko‘p oilalar daromad uchun turizmga tayanadi.
  • I rely on my brother to wake me up.Meni uyg‘otishda akamga tayanaman.

Ko‘pincha “nimadan” bo‘yicha qo‘shimcha ham aytiladi: depend on sb for sth (nimadir uchun kimgadir tayanmoq), rely on sb to do sth (kimdir biror ish qilishiga ishonmoq).

It depends (on) what / how / who …

“Bu … ga bog‘liq” deyilganda it depends ishlatiladi. Bog‘liq bo‘lgan narsa ot bo‘lsa, on shart. Lekin so‘roq so‘zi (what, where, how, who, when, whether) kelsa, on ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin:

It depends(on)what / where / how / who / whether+gap
Keyin nima keladi?on kerakmi?Misol
ot yoki olmosh (the weather, the price, him)MajburiyIt depends on the price. / It depends on him. (❌ It depends the price.)
so‘roq so‘zi + gap (how much it costs, who comes)IxtiyoriyIt depends (on) how much it costs. / It depends (on) who comes.
whether + gapIxtiyoriyIt depends (on) whether Aziz can drive.
  • “Will you come to the picnic?” “It depends. I may have to work.”— Piknikka kelasizmi? — Vaziyatga qarab. Ishlashimga to‘g‘ri kelishi mumkin.
  • The ticket price depends on the season.Chipta narxi faslga bog‘liq.

depend holat fe’li bo‘lgani uchun odatda continuous shaklda emas: ✅ It depends. ❌ It is depending. depend upon va rely upon ham yozma, rasmiy uslubda uchraydi, ma’no bir xil. ❌ depend from / of / to.

live on

  • The family lives on rice and vegetables for most of the week.Oila haftaning ko‘p qismida guruch va sabzavot bilan kun ko‘radi.
  • My grandmother lives on a small pension.Buvim kichik pensiya hisobiga yashaydi.

live ON money / food

Shu pul yoki ovqat hisobiga kun ko‘rmoq.

  • They live on a small income.
  • Pandas live on bamboo.

live ON a place (joylashuv)

Yuza, ko‘cha, orol, ferma yoki qavatda yashamoq (Unit 123–125).

  • They live on a small island.
  • We live on the third floor.

Yana ikkita yaqin birikma: live off — birovning mablag‘i hisobiga yashamoq (He lives off his parents.); live on (to‘ldiruvchisiz, gap oxirida) — davom etmoq, saqlanib qolmoq: His songs still live on.

congratulate va compliment

  • I congratulated him on passing the driving test.Men uni haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘tgani bilan tabrikladim.
  • Congratulations on your new flat!Yangi kvartirangiz bilan tabriklayman!
  • Everyone complimented the cook on the plov.Hamma oshpazni palovi uchun maqtadi.

Congratulations (ko‘plik, -s bilan!) ham tabrik so‘zi, ham gap: Congratulations on your exam results! Tug‘ilgan kun yoki bayramda Congratulations emas, Happy birthday! / Happy New Year! deyiladi. compliment (“iltifot bildirmoq”) bilan complement (“to‘ldirmoq”) imlosini aralashtirmang. Maqtov: pay sb a compliment.

Majhul nisbatda

Predlog majhul nisbatda ham o‘z joyida qoladi (Unit 42–44):

  • The police accused him of theft. → He was accused of theft.Politsiya uni o‘g‘irlikda ayblab chiqdi. → U o‘g‘irlikda ayblandi.
  • People suspect the shop owner of lying. → The shop owner is suspected of lying.Odamlar do‘konchini yolg‘on gapirmoqda deb o‘ylaydi. → Do‘konchi yolg‘on gapirishda gumon qilinmoqda.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

He accused me for stealing his pen.He accused me of stealing his pen.O‘zbekcha “o‘g‘irlikda ayblamoq”dagi “-da” — of. Accuse bilan for ishlatilmaydi (for blame va criticise bilan).
Our teacher disapproves with using phones.Our teacher disapproves of using phones.Approve va disapprove har doim of bilan keladi.
My grandfather died with a heart attack.My grandfather died of (from) a heart attack.Vafot sababi — of yoki from, with emas.
The team consists from eleven players.The team consists of eleven players.“-dan iborat” — consist of. “From” bilan emas.
Who paid the dinner last night?Who paid for the dinner last night?Sotib olingan narsa yoki xizmat uchun to‘lov — pay for. Faqat bill, rent, tax, fine bilan for kerak emas.
Thank you to help me.Thank you for helping me.For dan keyin -ing keladi, to + fe’l emas. “Thank to” ham yo‘q: thank you.
They blamed the power cut to the storm.They blamed the power cut on the storm. / They blamed the storm for the power cut.Sabab blame dan keyin kelsa, kishi oldidan on keladi, to emas.
She suffers of headaches.She suffers from headaches.Aziyat chekmoq — suffer from. “-dan” = from.
The rain prevented us to go out.The rain prevented us from going out.Prevent + kim + from + -ing. Infinitiv (to go) kelmaydi.
It depends from the weather.It depends on the weather.“Bog‘liq” — depend on. From, of, to ishlatilmaydi.
Congratulations for your exam results!Congratulations on your exam results!Tabriklash — congratulate / congratulations on. “Bilan” ni with yoki for deb tarjima qilmang.
He lives with bread and tea.He lives on bread and tea.“Ovqat yoki pul hisobiga yashamoq” — live on.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

accuse, blame, suspect va charge ni qanday farqlayman?

accuse sb of — “sen qilding” deb aytmoq. suspect sb of — qilgan bo‘lishi mumkin deb o‘ylamoq. blame sb for — biror yomon natija uchun javobgar deb hisoblamoq (blame the storm for the power cut). charge sb with — politsiya yoki sud tomonidan rasmiy ayb qo‘yish. Predloglar ham har xil: of, of, for, with.

die of bilan die from ning farqi bormi?

Ko‘p hollarda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Odatda of kasallik yoki tabiiy sabab uchun (of cancer, of old age), from esa jarohat yoki tashqi sabab uchun (from his injuries, from the cold). Halokat, urush yoki yong‘inda vafot etilsa — die in. Fe’l die – died – dying; sifat dead (“o‘lik”), ot death. Taqqoslang: He died in 2010. / He is dead.

pay va pay for ni qachon ishlataman?

Oddiy qoida: sotib olingan narsa yoki xizmat bo‘lsa — pay for (pay for the tickets, pay for the taxi). To‘lov turi o‘zi aytilsa — for siz (pay the bill, pay the rent, pay tax, pay a fine). Kimga to‘lasangiz, o‘sha kishi pay dan keyin darhol keladi: pay the waiter; nima uchun to‘lasangiz, for bilan: pay the waiter for the tea.

“blame sb for sth” bilan “blame sth on sb” ma’no jihatidan farq qiladimi?

Yo‘q, ma’no bir xil: ikkalasida ham “aybni shu odamga yuklamoq”. Farq faqat tuzilmada. They blamed the driver for the delay = They blamed the delay on the driver. Ikkalasi ham keng tarqalgan; gapda nimani (kishini yoki voqeani) oldinroq aytmoqchi ekaningizga qarab tanlang.

protect from va protect against, qaysi birini tanlash kerak?

Ko‘p gaplarda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri: protect your skin from / against the sun. Boshlovchi uchun qoida: aniq zarar yetkazuvchi narsa yoki odam bo‘lsa (o‘g‘ri, sovuq, yomg‘ir) — from; kasallik, tahdid yoki xavfga qarshi vosita bo‘lsa (emlash, sug‘urta, qulf) — against.

prevent bilan stop ning farqi nimada?

Ma’no yaqin: ikkalasi ham “to‘sqinlik qilmoq”. prevent — oldindan oldini olmoq, rasmiyroq (prevent accidents). stop — jarayonni to‘xtatmoq yoki biror ishga yo‘l qo‘ymaslik (stop the car, stop him from leaving). Ikkalasida ham kim / nima + from + -ing qolipi bor.

“It depends what you mean” da on nega yo‘q?

Chunki depend on dan keyin so‘roq so‘zi (what, how, who, where, whether) bilan boshlanuvchi gap kelsa, on ni ba’zan aytmaymiz. Ikkala variant ham to‘g‘ri: It depends what you mean. = It depends on what you mean. Lekin ot oldidan on majburiy: It depends on the price.

approve va approve of o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ha. approve of — o‘z fikringiz (“menimcha, bu yaxshi”). approve (predlogsiz) — rasmiy qaror (“ruxsat berdim, tasdiqladim”): The manager approved my holiday request. (Menejer ta’til arizamni tasdiqladi.)

congratulate fe’li bilan congratulations otining ishlatilishida nima farq bor?

congratulate fe’l: We congratulated him on his success. congratulations — ot, har doim ko‘plikda, tabrik uchun: Congratulations on your success! Ikkalasida ham on. Tug‘ilgan kunda Happy birthday! deyiladi, Congratulations emas.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga of, for, from yoki on yozing.

  1. The neighbour accused the boys breaking the window.
  2. Who is paying the dinner tonight?
  3. Zarina suffers terrible headaches in spring.
  4. It all depends the weather.
  5. I thanked my aunt the lovely scarf.
  6. A good hat protects your head the sun.
  7. Our squad consists twenty players.
  8. My parents don’t approve my staying out late.
  9. They congratulated Dilnoza winning the competition.
  10. Heavy fog prevented the plane landing.
  11. The newspaper criticised the mayor ignoring the problem.
  12. Many students live very little money.
  13. Her grandfather died a heart attack.
  14. She complimented me my English.

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. The shop owner accused the man (for / of / with) stealing.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • of: accuse / suspect sb of, approve / disapprove of, die of (yoki from), consist of. Halokatda vafot etmoq — die in.
  • for: pay (sb) for, thank / forgive / apologise / blame / criticise sb for. blame sb for sth = blame sth on sb. Hisob-kitob bilan: pay the bill (for siz).
  • from: suffer from, protect sb from / against, prevent sb from doing.
  • on: depend / rely on, live on, congratulate / compliment sb on. It depends (on) what / how … — so‘roq so‘zidan oldin on ixtiyoriy.
  • O‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha aldaydi: -ga bog‘liq = depend on (❌ from / of), bilan tabriklamoq = congratulate on (❌ with), -da ayblamoq = accuse of (❌ for).
  • Predlogdan keyin ot yoki -ing: thank you for coming, prevent me from sleeping.
