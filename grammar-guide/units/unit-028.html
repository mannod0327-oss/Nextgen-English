Must and can't

Must va can’t — xulosa va taxmin
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • must bilan “men bunga ishonaman, bu aniq shunday” degan xulosa (deduction) chiqarishni: You must be tired.
  • can’t bilan “bunday bo‘lishi mumkin emas” deyishni: That can’t be true.
  • O‘tmish haqida xulosa: must have done va can’t have done / couldn’t have done.
  • must be, must be doing, must do, must have been, must have been doing kabi tuzilmalarning farqini.

Bu darsda must “majbur” degan ma’noda emas! Bu yerda u detektivning so‘zi: biz dalillarga (evidence) qaraymiz va mantiqiy xulosa chiqaramiz. Must = “aniq shunday bo‘lsa kerak, boshqacha bo‘lishi mumkin emas”. Can’t esa buning teskarisi = “bunday bo‘lishi imkonsiz”.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no “-sa kerak”, “aniq”, “shubhasiz” so‘zlari bilan beriladi: Charchagan bo‘lsangiz kerak = You must be tired. “Bunday bo‘lishi mumkin emas” esa can’t bilan: Bu rost bo‘lishi mumkin emas = It can’t be true. Diqqat: o‘zbekcha “kerak” so‘zi ikki ma’noli (“borishim kerak” — majburiyat; “uyda bo‘lsa kerak” — taxmin). Inglizchadagi must ham xuddi shunday ikki ma’noli!

Must va can’t: hozirgi holat haqida xulosa must (= I’m sure it’s true) and can’t (= I’m sure it’s not true)

Biror narsani o‘z ko‘zingiz bilan ko‘rmagansiz, lekin dalillarga qarab ishonch bilan xulosa qilyapsiz. Shunda must ishlatiladi:

  • Aziz has three cars and a big house in Tashkent. He must be rich.Azizning uchta mashinasi va Toshkentda katta uyi bor. U boy bo‘lsa kerak. (Aniq boy, deb hisoblayman.)
  • “I’ve had three exams today.” “You must be tired.”— Bugun uchta imtihon topshirdim. — Charchagan bo‘lsangiz kerak.
  • Nodira has got dozens of books about Japan. She must love Japanese culture.Nodiraning Yaponiya haqida o‘nlab kitoblari bor. U yapon madaniyatini juda yaxshi ko‘rsa kerak.

Agar dalillarga qarab biror narsa imkonsiz, rost emas deb ishonsangiz, can’t ishlatiladi:

  • This red bag can’t be Malika’s. She hates red.Bu qizil sumka Malikaniki bo‘lishi mumkin emas. U qizil rangni yomon ko‘radi.
  • Kamola can’t be at home. I’ve just seen her at Chorsu bazaar.Kamola uyda bo‘lishi mumkin emas. Uni hozirgina Chorsu bozorida ko‘rdim.
  • “That man is Sardor’s father.” “He can’t be. He looks younger than Sardor!”— Anavi kishi Sardorning otasi. — Bo‘lishi mumkin emas. U Sardordan ham yoshroq ko‘rinadi-ku!

Tuzilma: must / can’t + nima?

must va can’t dan keyin har doim fe’lning “to”siz oddiy shakli (bare infinitive) keladi. Lekin qaysi shakl ekaniga qarab ma’no o‘zgaradi:

  • “Malika lives on your street.” “Really? Then I must know her.”— Malika siz bilan bir ko‘chada yashaydi. — Rostdanmi? Unda uni tanisam kerak.

Qoida: Xulosa ma’nosidagi must ning inkori — can’t (mustn’t emas!). He must be at home ↔ He can’t be at home. Britaniya ingliz tilida He mustn’t be at home xulosa ma’nosida ishlatilmaydi — mustn’t “taqiqlangan” degan ma’noni beradi (Unit 31–32).

Amerika ingliz tilida inkor xulosa uchun must not ham ishlatiladi: He must not be home — his car isn’t there. (AmE) = He can’t be at home. (BrE). Imtihonlarda va Britaniya uslubida can’t ni tanlang.

Must ning ikki ma’nosi

1) Majburiyat (Unit 31)

“Buni qilishim zarur.”

  • I must go now. — Hozir ketishim kerak.
  • You must wear a seat belt. — Xavfsizlik kamarini taqishingiz shart.

2) Xulosa (shu dars)

“Bu aniq shunday bo‘lsa kerak.”

  • You must be tired. — Charchagan bo‘lsangiz kerak.
  • This must be the right address. — Bu to‘g‘ri manzil bo‘lsa kerak.

Qanday ajratish mumkin? Xulosa ma’nosida must dan keyin ko‘pincha be, be -ing, know / have / live / like kabi holat fe’llari yoki have + V3 keladi va gap dalilga asoslanadi. Majburiyat ma’nosida esa odatda harakat fe’li keladi: must go / must finish / must call.

O‘tmish haqida xulosa: must have done, can’t have done must have (done) and can’t have (done)

Agar xulosangiz o‘tgan voqea haqida bo‘lsa, must have + V3 (fe’lning 3-shakli, past participle) yoki can’t have + V3 ishlatiladi. Xulosa hozir chiqarilmoqda, lekin voqea o‘tmishda bo‘lgan.

Ega+must / can’t / couldn’t+have+V3
  • The streets are wet. It must have rained in the night.Ko‘chalar ho‘l. Kechasi yomg‘ir yog‘gan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • I can’t find my keys anywhere. I must have left them at the office.Kalitlarimni hech qayerdan topolmayapman. Ishxonada qoldirgan bo‘lsam kerak.
  • Jasur got 98% in the exam. He must have studied really hard.Jasur imtihondan 98% oldi. U juda qattiq o‘qigan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • The car isn’t in the garage. Dad must have taken it.Mashina garajda yo‘q. Dadam olib ketgan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • Kamola says she read the whole book in one evening. She can’t have read it carefully — it’s 600 pages long.Kamola butun kitobni bir kechada o‘qib chiqqanini aytyapti. Uni diqqat bilan o‘qigan bo‘lishi mumkin emas — kitob 600 betlik.
  • “I posted the letter on Monday.” “You can’t have posted it on Monday. The post office was closed all day.”— Xatni dushanba kuni jo‘natdim. — Dushanba kuni jo‘natgan bo‘lishingiz mumkin emas. Pochta kun bo‘yi yopiq edi.

  • There’s a football on the floor and the window is broken. The boys must have been playing in the garden.Polda futbol to‘pi yotibdi, deraza esa singan. Bolalar bog‘da to‘p o‘ynab yurishgan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • Nodira didn’t know who sang at the party. She can’t have been there.Nodira ziyofatda kim qo‘shiq aytganini bilmadi. U u yerda bo‘lgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas.
  • Kamola can’t have been listening. She asked me the same question again.Kamola tinglab o‘tirgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas. U menga yana o‘sha savolni berdi.

can’t have = couldn’t have

O‘tmish haqidagi inkor xulosada can’t have o‘rniga couldn’t have ham ishlatiladi — ma’nosi bir xil:

  • Bekzod couldn’t have taken your phone. He wasn’t even in the room.Bekzod telefoningizni olgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas. U hatto xonada ham yo‘q edi.
  • You couldn’t have seen Aziz yesterday. He’s been in London all week.Kecha Azizni ko‘rgan bo‘lishingiz mumkin emas. U butun hafta Londonda.

Must have og‘zaki nutqda must’ve /ˈmʌstəv/ deb talaffuz qilinadi. Shuning uchun ba’zi odamlar xato qilib must of deb yozadi. Bu xato! Har doim must have (must’ve) yozing. Xuddi shunday: can’t have, couldn’t have.

Hozir yoki o‘tmish? Taqqoslash jadvali Present vs past deduction

Eng muhim savol: xulosangiz qaysi vaqt haqida? Hozirgi holat haqida bo‘lsa — must be / must do. O‘tgan voqea haqida bo‘lsa — must have been / must have done.

Hozir (now)O‘tmish (past)
✅ ishonamanShe must be at home.

U uyda bo‘lgan bo‘lsa kerak.
✅ ishonaman (jarayon)He must be working.
U ishlayotgan bo‘lsa kerak.		He must have been working.
U (o‘sha paytda) ishlayotgan bo‘lsa kerak.
✅ ishonaman (harakat)They must know the way.
Ular yo‘lni bilsa kerak.		They must have lost their way.
Ular yo‘ldan adashgan bo‘lsa kerak.
❌ imkonsizIt can’t be true.
Bu rost bo‘lishi mumkin emas.		It can’t have been true.
Bu rost bo‘lgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas.
❌ imkonsiz (harakat)She can’t mean that.
U buni nazarda tutishi mumkin emas.		She can’t / couldn’t have meant that.
U buni nazarda tutgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas.

Hozirgi holat + o‘tmishdagi sabab

Ba’zan bitta vaziyatda ikkala shakl ham kerak bo‘ladi:

  • “Malika works night shifts at the hospital. She must be exhausted all the time.” “Not really. She must have got used to it.”— Malika kasalxonada tungi smenada ishlaydi. Doim juda charchagan bo‘lsa kerak. — Unchalik emas. U bunga ko‘nikib qolgan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • Sardor looks very happy. He must have passed his driving test.Sardor juda xursand ko‘rinadi. Haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘tgan bo‘lsa kerak.

O‘zbekchada farq qo‘shimchada: uyda bo‘lsa kerak (hozir) — uyda bo‘lgan bo‘lsa kerak (o‘tmish). Inglizchada esa farq have + V3 da: must be — must have been. “-gan” qo‘shimchasini ko‘rsangiz, have + V3 ni eslang.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

He mustn’t be at home. His car isn’t here.He can’t be at home. His car isn’t here.Xulosa ma’nosidagi must ning inkori — can’t. Mustn’t = “taqiqlangan” (BrE).
She must to be tired.She must be tired.Modal fe’ldan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
They must went out.They must have gone out.O‘tmish haqidagi xulosa: must + have + V3. Must dan keyin o‘tgan zamon shakli (went) kelmaydi.
I must of left my bag on the bus.I must have (must’ve) left my bag on the bus.“Must of” — talaffuzdan kelib chiqqan imlo xatosi. To‘g‘risi: must have.
You can’t have see him yesterday.You can’t have seen him yesterday.“Have” dan keyin fe’lning 3-shakli (V3): seen, gone, done.
He must be phoned me while I was out.He must have phoned me while I was out.O‘tmishdagi harakat haqida xulosa — “be” emas, “have + V3”.
Aziz isn’t answering. He must be study.Aziz isn’t answering. He must be studying.“must be” dan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi (must be + -ing). Yoki shunchaki “must + fe’l” (holat fe’llari bilan).
Must he be at work now?Do you think he’s at work now? / Could he be at work now?Xulosa ma’nosidagi must deyarli so‘roq gapda ishlatilmaydi. “Do you think ...?” yoki “Could ...?” deymiz.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“He must be at home” va “He is at home” ning farqi nima?

He is at home — fakt, siz buni bilasiz (masalan, uni ko‘rdingiz). He must be at home — siz buni ko‘rmagansiz, lekin dalillarga qarab xulosa qildingiz: masalan, uyining chirog‘i yonib turibdi va mashinasi eshik oldida. Ya’ni must = “juda ishonaman, lekin bu mening mantiqiy xulosam”.

Nega xulosada “mustn’t” emas, “can’t” ishlatiladi?

Chunki mustn’t ning asosiy ma’nosi — taqiq: You mustn’t smoke here (Bu yerda chekish mumkin emas). “Bu rost bo‘lishi mumkin emas” degan xulosa uchun ingliz tili can’t ni ishlatadi: It can’t be true. Faqat Amerika ingliz tilida must not xulosa ma’nosida ham keladi (He must not be home), lekin Britaniya uslubida bu g‘alati eshitiladi.

“can’t have done” va “couldn’t have done” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va o‘tmish haqidagi xulosada deyarli bir xil ma’no beradi: She can’t have seen me = She couldn’t have seen me (U meni ko‘rgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas). Diqqat: couldn’t have done boshqa ma’noda ham keladi — “qila olmas edi” (Unit 27): I couldn’t have done it without your help (Yordamingizsiz buni qila olmagan bo‘lardim).

“You must have a ticket” — bu majburiyatmi yoki xulosami?

Kontekstga bog‘liq! Kassir sizga aytsa: You must have a ticket to enter — majburiyat (kirish uchun chiptangiz bo‘lishi shart). Do‘stingiz sizni konsert zalida ko‘rib: You’re here, so you must have a ticket — xulosa (shu yerdasiz, demak chiptangiz bor bo‘lsa kerak). Ma’noni doim vaziyatdan aniqlang.

“must have done” ni hozirgi zamon bilan aralashtirmaslik uchun nima qilish kerak?

O‘zingizga savol bering: men qaysi vaqt haqida taxmin qilyapman? Hozirgi holat → must be. O‘tgan voqea → must have been / must have done. Masalan, “U hozir uxlayotgan bo‘lsa kerak” → He must be sleeping. “U kecha uxlab qolgan bo‘lsa kerak” → He must have overslept yesterday. O‘zbekchadagi -gan qo‘shimchasi sizga yordam beradi.

Xulosa ma’nosida “must” bilan so‘roq gap tuziladimi?

Deyarli yo‘q. Taxmin so‘rash uchun boshqa yo‘llar bor: Do you think he’s at work? (Sizningcha, u ishdami?), Could he be at work? (U ishda bo‘lishi mumkinmi?), Can it be true? (Rostdan ham shundaymi? — hayratlanib). Must he be ...? esa odatda majburiyat haqida so‘raydi va bu ma’noda ham kam ishlatiladi.

“He must be working” va “He must work” — farqi bormi?

Ha. He must be working = “u hozir (ayni paytda) ishlayotgan bo‘lsa kerak” — hozirgi jarayon haqida xulosa. He must work esa ko‘pincha majburiyat sifatida tushuniladi (“u ishlashi shart”). Xulosa ma’nosida oddiy fe’l holat fe’llari va doimiy holat yoki odatlar bilan ishlatiladi: He must know, must have, must live, must like; He must work in IT (doimiy ish joyi). Ayni paytda bo‘layotgan harakat haqida xulosa uchun esa continuous kerak: He must be working late tonight.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga must yoki can’t + qavs ichidagi fe’lni yozing (hozirgi holat haqida xulosa).

  1. Dilnoza’s flat is full of plants and flowers. She (love) gardening.
  2. Jasur has only been learning Chinese for a month. He (speak) it fluently.
  3. Malika (be) in Samarkand. I saw her in Tashkent ten minutes ago.
  4. Nodira’s phone has been busy for an hour. She (talk) to somebody.
  5. That woman looks exactly like Sardor, and she’s about sixty. She (be) his mother.
  6. This restaurant is always empty. The food (be) very good.
  7. Bekzod knows everything about computers. He (work) in IT.
  8. It’s 35 degrees outside. You (be) cold!

2-mashq. must have yoki can’t have + qavs ichidagi fe’lni yozing (o‘tmish haqida xulosa).

  1. I can’t find my umbrella. I (leave) it on the bus.
  2. The ground is completely dry. It (rain) last night.
  3. The cake has disappeared from the fridge. Somebody (eat) it.
  4. Dilnoza didn’t react at all when I told her. She (hear) the news already.
  5. You look so tired. You (sleep) well last night.
  6. Jasur’s car is clean and shiny. He (wash) it this morning.

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. Look at the queue! The film (must be / must have been) very popular.
  2. Everybody left the cinema early yesterday. The film (must be / must have been) boring.
  3. Nodira (can’t be / can’t have been) at the party last night — she was ill in bed.
  4. The lights are on in the office. Somebody (must work / must be working) late.
  5. “I saw Aziz in Samarkand this morning.” “You (can’t / can’t have). He’s in London.”

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. She mustn’t be at work — it’s Sunday. →
  2. I must of dropped my wallet. →
  3. They must to be on holiday. →
  4. He can’t have went to bed so early. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. U (erkak) charchagan bo‘lsa kerak. →
  2. Ular uyda bo‘lishi mumkin emas — chiroqlar o‘chiq. →
  3. Men kalitlarimni mashinada qoldirgan bo‘lsam kerak. →
  4. Siz uni kecha ko‘rgan bo‘lishingiz mumkin emas — u Londonda. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • must (xulosa) = “aniq shunday bo‘lsa kerak”: You must be tired.
  • can’t (xulosa) = “bunday bo‘lishi mumkin emas”: It can’t be true. Inkori mustn’t emas!
  • Hozir: must / can’t + be, be -ing yoki holat fe’li (know, have, live).
  • O‘tmish: must / can’t / couldn’t + have + V3: They must have gone out. She can’t have seen me.
  • O‘tmishdagi jarayon: must have been + -ing: He can’t have been listening.
  • Hech qachon must of, must to, must went demang.
