If I knew ... I wish I knew ...

If I knew ... I wish I knew ... (hozirgi xayoliy holat)
If and wish · Shart gaplar va wish

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • If I knew ..., I would ... — hozirgi haqiqatga zid, xayoliy vaziyat haqida gapirishni.
  • I wish I knew ... — hozirgi holatdan afsuslanishni (“qani endi ...”).
  • If I were / If I was va I wish it were / was — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri va qachon.
  • Nega if qismida va wish dan keyin would ishlatilmasligini.
  • could ning ikki ma’nosini: “qila olardi” va “qila olsa”.

Bu unitning asosiy g‘oyasi: biz hozirgi haqiqatning teskarisini tasavvur qilamiz. Haqiqat: I don’t know the recipe. Tasavvur: If I knew the recipe, I would cook plov. Afsus: I wish I knew the recipe. Fe’l o‘tgan zamonda (knew), lekin gap hozir haqida!

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no -ganimda (edi) ... -ardim yoki -sa edi bilan beriladi: Retseptni bilganimda, palov pishirardim. Afsus esa “qani endi ...”, “koshki ...” bilan: Qani endi retseptni bilsam! = I wish I knew the recipe. Ingliz tilida ikkala holatda ham fe’l o‘tgan zamon shakliga o‘tadi — buni unutmang.

If I knew ... — hozirgi xayoliy vaziyat if + past: the meaning is present

Vaziyat: Sardor do‘stlariga buvisining mashhur palovini pishirib bermoqchi, lekin pishira olmaydi, chunki uning retseptini bilmaydi. U shunday deydi:

  • If I knew Grandma’s recipe, I would cook plov for my friends.Buvimning retseptini bilganimda, do‘stlarimga palov pishirib berardim.

If I knew ... degani — Sardor retseptni bilmaydi. U teskari vaziyatni tasavvur qilyapti. Haqiqiy holat: He doesn’t know the recipe.

Qoida: Hozirgi holatga zid vaziyatni tasavvur qilganda if + past (if I knew / if you were / if we didn’t ...) ishlatamiz. Ma’no hozirgi, o‘tgan emas. Ikkinchi qismda — would (’d) / wouldn’t.

Haqiqat ↔ tasavvur: “ha” “yo‘q”qa, “yo‘q” “ha”ga aylanadi

Haqiqiy holat (hozir)Xayoliy gap (if + past)
Nodira doesn’t have much free time.Nodira would take up tennis if she had more free time.
Bo‘sh vaqti ko‘proq bo‘lganida, Nodira tennis bilan shug‘ullanardi.
I like my job.If I didn’t like my job, I’d look for a new one.
Ishimni yoqtirmaganimda, yangisini qidirardim. (lekin yoqtiraman)
Bekzod lives far from his school.If Bekzod lived closer, he’d walk to school.
Bekzod yaqinroq yashaganida, maktabga piyoda borardi.
Aziz can’t swim.Aziz would come to the beach with us if he could swim.
Aziz suza olganida, biz bilan sohilga borardi.
It’s raining now.If it wasn’t raining, we’d go for a walk.
Hozir yomg‘ir yog‘mayotganida, sayrga chiqardik.
  • If you were the head teacher, what would you change at our school?Siz direktor bo‘lganingizda, maktabimizda nimani o‘zgartirardingiz?
  • Our garden wouldn’t look so nice if Dad didn’t work in it every weekend.Dadam har dam olish kuni bog‘da ishlamaganida, bog‘imiz bunchalik chiroyli bo‘lmasdi. (lekin ishlaydi)
  • It’s a pity Jasur isn’t here. He’d know what to do if he was here.Jasur bu yerda yo‘qligi achinarli. U shu yerda bo‘lganida, nima qilishni bilardi.

if qismida past continuous ham kelishi mumkin — hozir davom etayotgan holatning teskarisi uchun: If I wasn’t working tomorrow, I’d come to your party. (Ertaga ishlayman, shuning uchun kela olmayman.)

I wish I knew ... — hozirgi holatdan afsus wish + past

wish dan keyin ham xuddi shunday o‘tgan zamon shakli ishlatiladi. wish bilan biz biror narsa biz xohlagandek emasligidan afsuslanamiz:

I+wish+ega+past simple (knew, had, were, could ...)
  • I wish I knew Grandma’s recipe. (= I don’t know it, and I’m sorry about this)Qani endi buvimning retseptini bilsam. (bilmayman va afsusdaman)
  • Do you ever wish you could read minds? (you can’t)Hech odamlarning fikrini o‘qiy olishni xohlaganmisiz? (buni hech kim qila olmaydi)
  • It’s always windy in this town. I wish it wasn’t so windy.Bu shaharda doim shamol esadi. Qani endi bunchalik shamol bo‘lmasa.
  • There’s nowhere for the children to play. I wish there was a park near our house.Bolalarning o‘ynaydigan joyi yo‘q. Qani endi uyimiz yaqinida park bo‘lsa.
  • I wish I didn’t have to wear a uniform at work, but unfortunately I do.Qani endi ishda forma kiyishim shart bo‘lmasa, lekin afsuski, shart.
Haqiqat → wish gapi
Haqiqatwish bilanO‘zbekcha
I don’t know the answer.I wish I knew the answer.Qani endi javobni bilsam.
I can’t swim.I wish I could swim.Qani endi suza olsam.
My flat is very small.I wish my flat was (were) bigger.Koshki kvartiram kattaroq bo‘lsa.
I have to take the bus every day.I wish I didn’t have to take the bus every day.Qani endi har kuni avtobusda yurishim shart bo‘lmasa.
You live so far away.I wish you didn’t live so far away.Koshki sen bunchalik uzoqda yashamasang.

O‘zbekcha “qani endi ... -sa”, “koshki ... -sa (edi)” → inglizcha I wish + past. Diqqat: o‘zbekchada “bilsam” deymiz (o‘tgan zamon emas), inglizchada esa albatta knew: I wish I know ❌ → I wish I knew ✅.

Kuchliroq hissiyot uchun If only ... ham ishlatiladi: If only I knew the recipe! (Eh, qani endi retseptni bilsam!). Grammatikasi wish bilan bir xil.

If I were / If I was were or was after if and wish

if va wish dan keyin was o‘rniga were ishlatish mumkin — hatto I, he, she, it bilan ham. was ham to‘g‘ri. Ikkala variant ham standart ingliz tili:

were bilanwas bilanO‘zbekcha
If I were you, I wouldn’t lend him money.If I was you, I wouldn’t lend him money.Men sizning o‘rningizda bo‘lganimda, unga qarz bermasdim.
Aziz would buy that flat if it weren’t so expensive.Aziz would buy that flat if it wasn’t so expensive.O‘sha kvartira bunchalik qimmat bo‘lmaganida, Aziz uni sotib olardi.
I wish today were Friday.I wish today was Friday.Qani endi bugun juma bo‘lsa.

If I were you, ... — maslahat berishning juda keng tarqalgan usuli: If I were you, I’d see a doctor. (Men sizning o‘rningizda bo‘lganimda, shifokorga borardim.) Bu iborada were eng ko‘p ishlatiladi.

were biroz rasmiyroq va yozma nutqda afzal ko‘riladi (ayniqsa AmE rasmiy yozuvida). Og‘zaki nutqda was juda keng tarqalgan. Imtihonda were yozsangiz — hech qachon xato bo‘lmaydi.

if va wish dan keyin would ishlatilmaydi; could Not would after if / wish; could

Odatda if qismida va wish dan keyin would ishlatilmaydi:

  • If I were taller, I would play basketball. (not If I would be taller)Bo‘yim balandroq bo‘lganida, basketbol o‘ynardim.
  • I wish I had more friends here. (not I wish I would have)Qani endi bu yerda do‘stlarim ko‘proq bo‘lsa.

wish ... would ham mavjud, lekin uning ma’nosi boshqa — kimningdir harakati o‘zgarishini xohlash: I wish you would listen to me! Bu Unit 41 da tushuntiriladi.

could ning ikki ma’nosi

Bunday gaplarda could ba’zan would be able to (qila olardi), ba’zan was / were able to (qila olsa) ma’nosini beradi:

  • Malika could visit her cousins in London if she could get a visa.Viza ola olganida, Malika Londondagi amakivachchalarinikiga bora olardi.
Qismcould= ma’nosi
Asosiy qismshe could visitshe would be able to visit — bora olardi
if qismiif she could getif she was / were able to get — ola olganida

If I know ... va If I knew ... farqi Real or imagined?

Unit 38 dagi kabi, bu yerda ham hammasi haqiqatga bog‘liq:

If I have time ... (real)

If I have time later, I’ll help you.

Keyinroq vaqtim bo‘lishi mumkin — bilmayman.

If I had time ... (xayol)

If I had time, I’d help you.

Lekin vaqtim yo‘q — yordam bera olmayman.

Hozirgi xayoliy gaplar — qisqa xulosa
TuzilmaMisol
if + past simple, would + fe’lIf I knew, I would tell you.
if + past continuous, would + fe’lIf it wasn’t raining, we would go out.
if + could, would / could + fe’lIf I could drive, I would buy a car.
I wish + past simple / couldI wish I knew. / I wish I could help.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Hozir haqida gapiryapman — nega “knew” deyman?

Ingliz tilida o‘tgan zamon shakli “haqiqatdan uzoqlik”ni ham bildiradi. If I knew = “bilmayman, faqat tasavvur qilyapman”. Vaqt — hozir, lekin shakl o‘tgan zamon. Bu xuddi o‘zbekchadagi “bilganimda edi” ga o‘xshaydi: shakli o‘tgan zamonga o‘xshasa ham, gap hozir haqida.

“If I was you” xatomi?

Yo‘q, xato emas — og‘zaki nutqda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi. Lekin If I were you ko‘proq qo‘llanadi va rasmiyroq. Yozma ishda va imtihonda were ni tanlash xavfsizroq.

I wish va I hope ning farqi nima?

hope — bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan narsa uchun: I hope you pass the exam. wish — haqiqatga zid narsa uchun: I wish I knew the answer (bilmayman). Batafsil — Unit 41.

“I wish I didn’t have to go to the dentist tomorrow” — ertaga haqida-ku, nega o‘tgan zamon?

Chunki ertangi tashrif hozirdanoq belgilangan haqiqat: “ertaga tish shifokoriga borishim shart”. Siz shu hozirgi haqiqatdan afsuslanayapsiz. wish dan keyin past simple “haqiqat boshqacha bo‘lsa edi” degan ma’noni beradi.

if + past va wish + past — bir xil grammatikami?

Ha. Ikkalasida ham o‘tgan zamon shakli hozirgi xayoliy holatni bildiradi: If I had a car ... / I wish I had a car. Faqat if gapida ikkinchi qism (would ...) bor, wish gapida esa yo‘q.

O‘tmishdagi narsadan afsuslansam-chi? Masalan, “kecha bilganimda edi”?

Unda had + V3 kerak: I wish I had known yesterday. If I had known, I would have helped you. Bu — keyingi mavzu, Unit 40.

If only va I wish — farqi bormi?

Grammatikasi bir xil, faqat If only hissiyotliroq va ko‘pincha undov belgisi bilan keladi: If only I had more time! Kundalik gapda I wish ko‘proq ishlatiladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’llarni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (if qismida past, ikkinchi qismda would).

  1. I don’t know the way. If I (know) the way, I (drive) you there.
  2. We (go) out more often if we (not / live) so far from the city centre.
  3. If I (be) you, I (not / sign) that contract.
  4. My grandfather can’t hear well. He (enjoy) concerts more if he (can) hear better.
  5. If Kamola (have) a dog, she (walk) it every morning.
  6. If it (not / rain) right now, we (play) football.

2-mashq. Haqiqiy holatdan if bilan xayoliy gap tuzing.

  1. Bekzod doesn’t have a bike, so he walks to school. → If Bekzod (have) a bike, he (not / walk) to school.
  2. I can’t help you because I’m busy. → If I (not / be) busy, I (help) you.
  3. Dilnoza is ill, so she can’t go to the concert. → If Dilnoza (not / be) ill, she (can / go) to the concert.
  4. They don’t speak English, so they can’t understand the film. → If they (speak) English, they (can / understand) the film.

3-mashq. I wish ... bilan gap yozing.

  1. I don’t know how to fix my laptop. →
  2. I have to share a room with my brother. →
  3. I can’t speak Chinese. →
  4. It isn’t sunny today. →
  5. I don’t have a garden. →

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

  1. If I would know the answer, I would tell you. →
  2. I wish I can drive. →
  3. I wish I would have more time. →
  4. If I am you, I’d apologise. →
  5. If she had a phone, she will call us. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Qani endi Azizning manzilini bilsam! →
  2. Men sizning o‘rningizda bo‘lganimda, ko‘proq uxlardim. →
🔑 Javoblar (Answer key)

1-mashq

  1. knew, would drive (’d drive)
  2. would go (’d go), didn’t live
  3. were (was), wouldn’t sign
  4. would enjoy (’d enjoy), could
  5. had, would walk (’d walk)
  6. wasn’t raining (weren’t raining), would play (’d play)

2-mashq

  1. had, wouldn’t walk
  2. wasn’t (weren’t), would help (’d help)
  3. wasn’t (weren’t), could go
  4. spoke, could understand

3-mashq

  1. I wish I knew how to fix my laptop.
  2. I wish I didn’t have to share a room with my brother.
  3. I wish I could speak Chinese.
  4. I wish it was (were) sunny today.
  5. I wish I had a garden.

4-mashq

  1. If I knew the answer, I would tell you.
  2. I wish I could drive.
  3. I wish I had more time.
  4. If I were (was) you, I’d apologise. (AmE: apologize)
  5. If she had a phone, she would (she’d) call us.

5-mashq

  1. I wish I knew Aziz’s address! / If only I knew Aziz’s address!
  2. If I were (was) you, I’d (I would) sleep more.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • if + past, would + fe’l — hozirgi haqiqatga zid tasavvur: If I knew, I would tell you.
  • I wish + past — hozirgi holatdan afsus: I wish I knew. I wish I could swim.
  • Shakl o‘tgan zamon, ma’no hozirgi.
  • If I were / If I was — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri; If I were you eng ko‘p ishlatiladi.
  • if qismida va wish dan keyin would yo‘q: If I were taller, I wish I had.
  • could = would be able to (asosiy qismda) yoki was/were able to (if qismida).
