All, every and whole

All, every va whole
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • everybody / everyone / everything (birlik fe’l bilan) va all ning o‘rnini: all “hamma narsa” ma’nosida faqat All I want is …, all that … kabi tuzilmalarda keladi.
  • every + birlikdagi ot + birlikdagi fe’l (every student has) va all + ko‘plikdagi ot + ko‘plikdagi fe’l (all the students have) farqini.
  • whole = “butun, to‘liq”: the whole book, my whole family. U odatda birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan keladi, sanalmaydigan ot bilan emas.
  • every day (qanchalik tez-tez?) va all day / the whole day (qancha vaqt davomida?) farqini; every three days, every other day; all the time va every time farqini.
  • O‘zbekcha hamma, har bir, butun so‘zlarining inglizchadagi to‘g‘ri juftini.

Bu uch so‘z (all, every, whole) o‘zbekchaga o‘xshash tarjima qilinadi, lekin inglizchada qaysi so‘zni tanlash ot turiga bog‘liq: ko‘plik yoki sanalmaydigan ot bilan — all; birlik ot bilan — every yoki the whole. Bu darsning asosiy qoidasi shu.

O‘zbek tilida uchta tayanch so‘z bor: hamma, har bir va butun. Inglizchada ular quyidagicha bo‘linadi: hamma talabalar → all the students; hamma odam → everybody; hamma narsa → everything; har bir talaba → every student; butun shahar → the whole city. Eng xavfli tuzoq: o‘zbekchada butun shahar ham, butun pulim ham “butun” bilan aytiladi, lekin inglizchada shahar uchun the whole city, pul uchun esa all my money deyiladi (❌ my whole money). Yana bir farq: o‘zbekchada hamma keldi deymiz va fe’l birlikda turadi — inglizchada ham everybody bilan fe’l birlikda bo‘ladi: Everybody is here.

All va everybody / everyone All and everybody / everyone / everything

Everybody va everyone — “hamma odam” (all the people). Ikkalasining ma’nosi bir xil. Everything — “hamma narsa” (all the things). Uchalasi ham bir so‘z bo‘lib yoziladi va birlikdagi fe’l bilan keladi, garchi ma’nosi ko‘pchilikni bildirsa ham.

Qoida: everybody / everyone / everything + birlik fe’l: Everybody is, Everything was. Ular haqidagi olmosh ko‘pincha ko‘plikda: they / them / their (Everybody said they were tired).

Kundalik nutqda “hamma odam” uchun everybody / everyone aytiladi. Yolg‘iz all bu ma’noda faqat rasmiy yoki kitobiy uslubda uchraydi (All are welcome — e’lon va yozuvlarda). O‘rganuvchi uchun xavfsiz yo‘l: everybody, everyone, all of us, we all. We all, they all kabi o‘rin haqida Unit 88 ga qarang.

All va everything All (that) + clause; all = the only thing

Everything bilan all farqi: “hamma narsa” odatda everything deyiladi (Everything went wrong, All went wrong ❌). Lekin all bitta tuzilmada ot o‘rnida yolg‘iz keladi: all (that) + ega + fe’l.

Bu tuzilmaning ikki ma’nosi bor:

1. all = the only thing (yagona narsa)

  • All I need is a pen.
  • Menga kerak bo‘lgan yagona narsa — ruchka.
  • All Sardor did was smile.
  • Sardor faqat jilmayib qo‘ydi.

2. all = everything (hamma narsa)

  • I’ve told you all I know.
  • Bilganimning hammasini sizga aytdim.
  • Jasur gave me all he had.
  • Jasur menga bor narsasini berdi.

Birinchi ma’noda gap odatda is / was bilan davom etadi: All I need is a pen. Ikkinchi ma’noda all o‘rniga everything ham aytsa bo‘ladi: I’ve told you everything I know. Birinchi ma’noda esa everything mumkin emas, chunki ma’no “yagona narsa”: All I need is a pen ≠ Everything I need is a pen.

Bunday gaplarda all dan keyin what emas, that aytiladi yoki hech narsa aytilmaydi: all (that) I know ✅, all what I know ❌ (Unit 92 va Unit 93). Yana: That’s all. — “Hammasi shu.” all about ham ishlatiladi: Tell me all about your trip to Khiva. — “Xivaga sayohatingiz haqida hammasini aytib bering.”

All va every: ot va fe’l qanday o‘zgaradi All + plural, every + singular

All ham, every ham “hamma, istisnosiz” ma’nosiga yaqin. Lekin ular bilan ishlatiladigan ot va fe’l shakli butunlay boshqacha. Shuning uchun avval ot turiga qarab so‘zni tanlang, keyin fe’lni moslang.

Bir fikr — ikki xil tuzilma

Ko‘p gaplarda all va every bir xil voqeani bildiradi, faqat gap qurilishi boshqacha. Ot va fe’l birga o‘zgaradi:

  • All the guests were hungry.Hamma mehmonlar och edi.
  • Every guest was hungry.Har bir mehmon och edi.
  • All my cousins live in the Fergana Valley.Amakivachchalarimning hammasi Farg‘ona vodiysida yashaydi.
  • Every cousin of mine lives in the Fergana Valley.Amakivachchalarimning har biri Farg‘ona vodiysida yashaydi.

Qoida: every + birlikdagi ot + birlikdagi fe’l: Every window has a curtain. All + ko‘plikdagi ot + ko‘plikdagi fe’l: All the windows have curtains. All + sanalmaydigan ot + birlikdagi fe’l: All the information is correct.

Umumiy fikr va aniq guruh

All dan keyin the / my / these bo‘lmasa, gap umuman, “hamma …lar” haqida bo‘ladi: All children enjoy stories. The / my / these bo‘lsa, gap aniq guruh haqida: All the children in this kindergarten enjoy stories. Bu farq Unit 88 da batafsil berilgan. Every bilan esa har doim birlik ot keladi: Every child enjoys a good story. (Har bir bola yaxshi ertakni yoqtiradi.)

Birgalikda bajariladigan ish. Hamma bir joyga to‘planishi, yig‘ilishi kabi guruh harakati haqida gapirganda all ishlatiladi: All the guests gathered in the garden. ✅ Every guest gathered in the garden. — bu g‘alati eshitiladi, chunki every har bir kishiga alohida qaraydi, to‘planish esa jamoaviy ish. Shuningdek every olmosh (us, you, them) bilan of orqali kelmaydi: every of us ❌ → all of us ✅ yoki every one of us ✅ (Unit 91).

Birlik ot va every dan keyin keladigan olmosh ko‘pincha they / their bo‘ladi: Every student must bring their own pen. Rasmiy yozuvda his or her own pen ham yoziladi. Fe’l esa baribir birlikda: every student must, every student has.

Whole: butun, to‘liq Whole = complete, entire

Whole — sifat; “butun, yaxlit, to‘liq” degan ma’noni beradi. Uning gapdagi o‘rni aniqlovchi (the, a, my, this …) dan keyin, otdan oldin:

the / a / my / this+whole+birlikdagi sanaladigan ot
  • Jasur read the whole letter twice.Jasur xatni boshidan oxirigacha ikki marta o‘qidi.
  • My whole family came to the airport.Butun oilam aeroportga keldi.
  • The whole village was celebrating.Butun qishloq bayram qilardi.
  • Nodira ate a whole watermelon!Nodira butun bir tarvuzni yeb qo‘ydi!

The whole + birlik ot = all of the + birlik ot. Ma’no bir xil: the whole letter = all of the letter. A whole bilan gapirganda ma’no kuchayadi: “butun bir …, hatto butun …”, ya’ni kutilganidan ko‘p.

Every + son va every other

Oralig‘i bor takrorlanish uchun every + son + ko‘plik ot yoki every other + birlik ot ishlatiladi. Bu yerda all kelmaydi (each ham ishlatilmaydi — Unit 91 da tushuntirilgan):

  • The Olympic Games take place every four years.Olimpiya o‘yinlari har to‘rt yilda o‘tkaziladi.
  • Take one tablet every eight hours.Har sakkiz soatda bittadan tabletka iching.
  • Kamola visits her aunt every other Sunday.Kamola xolasinikiga ikki haftada bir yakshanba kuni boradi.
  • We water the flowers every few days.Biz gullarni har bir necha kunda sug‘oramiz.

All the time va every time

all the time — doim, tinmay

  • My little brother asks questions all the time.
  • Ukam tinmay savol beradi.

every time — har safar

  • Every time I call Jasur, he’s busy.
  • Jasurga har safar qo‘ng‘iroq qilsam, u band bo‘ladi.

Every other day = every second day = “kunora”. Every other Sunday = ikki haftada bir yakshanba. Rasm va to‘liq jadval Unit 91 da berilgan. Every day (ikki so‘z, “har kuni”) va everyday (bir so‘z, “kundalik”) farqi ham o‘sha yerda: I practise English every day, lekin everyday English.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Every student” bilan “all the students” ma’nosi bir xilmi?

Ko‘p gaplarda voqea bir xil: ikkalasi ham “hammasi, istisnosiz”. Farq grammatikada: every student is (birlik), all the students are (ko‘plik). Ma’nodagi nozik farq: every har bir kishiga alohida qaraydi, all guruhga bir butun sifatida. Shuning uchun birgalikda qilinadigan ishlarda (to‘planish, qo‘shilish) all tabiiyroq: All the guests gathered in the garden.

Everybody “hamma” — ko‘pchilik. Nega fe’l birlikda, lekin “they / their” ishlatiladi?

Grammatik jihatdan everybody birlik so‘z, shuning uchun fe’l birlikda: Everybody is here. Ma’noda esa u ko‘p odamni bildiradi, shuning uchun undan keyingi olmosh ko‘pincha they / their: Everybody brought their own lunch. Rasmiy yozuvda his or her ham uchraydi. Fe’lga hech qachon ko‘plik qo‘shmaymiz: Everybody are ❌.

“All I want is a cup of tea” gapidagi “all” nima? Uni “everything” bilan almashtirsa bo‘ladimi?

Bu yerda all = “the only thing” (yagona narsa): “Men xohlagan yagona narsa — bir piyola choy.” Everything bilan almashtirib bo‘lmaydi, chunki ma’no o‘zgaradi (hamma narsa, bir nechta). Boshqa gaplarda all (that) = “everything (that)”: I’ve told you all I know = I’ve told you everything I know. Ikkala holatda ham all dan keyin gap keladi (all I know).

“All day” va “the whole day” — farqi bormi? “All the day” deb bo‘ladimi?

Ma’no bir xil: kun bo‘yi, boshidan oxirigacha. The whole day ba’zan biroz ta’kidliroq tuyuladi. “All the day” kundalik tilda aytilmaydi, “all day” deymiz. A whole day ham mumkin: I waited a whole day! — “Men butun bir kun kutdim!” (ko‘p vaqt ekaniga urg‘u). Hamma ibora oldida predlog kerak emas.

Nega “the whole money” yoki “the whole water” deb bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki whole “bir butun, yaxlit narsa” degan ma’noni beradi va birlikdagi sanaladigan ot (a day, a book, a city) bilan keladi: bunday ot bitta yaxlit birlik sifatida ko‘rinadi. Pul va suv — sanalmaydigan otlar, ularni bitta yaxlit birlik deb sanab bo‘lmaydi. Ularning hammasi haqida gapirganda all ishlatiladi: all the money, all my water.

“Butun” so‘zini har doim “whole” deb tarjima qilsam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Quyidagicha tanlang: butun shahar, butun kun, butun kitob (birlik, sanaladigan) → the whole; butun pulim, butun suv, butun axborot (sanalmaydigan) → all; butun O‘zbekiston, butun Samarqand (joy nomi) → the whole of / all of; butun dunyo → the whole world yoki all over the world. Ba’zan “butun” ni umuman tarjima qilmasa ham bo‘ladi: butun kun = all day.

“The whole of” va “the whole” orasida farq nima?

Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan ikkalasi ham mumkin: the whole city = the whole of the city (ikkinchisi kitobiyroq). Joy nomi yoki olmosh bilan faqat of li shakl: the whole of Bukhara, the whole of it. The whole Bukhara ❌.

Every day va everyday qanday farq qiladi?

Every day (ikki so‘z) — “har kuni”: I practise English every day. Everyday (bir so‘z) — “kundalik, oddiy” degan sifat, otdan oldin keladi: everyday English, everyday life. Batafsil Unit 91 da. (Britaniya inglizchasida fe’l practise, ot practice; Amerika inglizchasida ikkalasi ham practice.)

  2. Every child has their own book. / Every child has his or her own book.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Every + birlik ot + birlik fe’l (every student has); all + ko‘plik ot + ko‘plik fe’l (all the students have); all + sanalmaydigan ot + birlik fe’l (all the water is).
  • Everybody / everyone / everything — bir so‘z, birlik fe’l; olmosh ko‘pincha they / their. Yolg‘iz all kundalik gapda “hamma narsa/odam” ma’nosida kelmaydi.
  • All faqat all (that) + gap tuzilmasida ot o‘rnida keladi: All I need is a pen (= yagona narsa), I’ve told you all I know (= hamma narsa).
  • Whole = butun: the / a / my + whole + birlik sanaladigan ot (the whole city = all of the city). Sanalmaydigan ot uchun all (all my money), joy nomi uchun the whole of (the whole of Samarkand).
  • Every day = har kuni (How often?); all day / the whole day = kun bo‘yi (How long?). Ularning oldida predlog kerak emas.
  • Every four years, every other Sunday, every few days — oraliq bilan takrorlanish; bunda all ishlatilmaydi.
