See somebody do and see somebody doing

See somebody do va see somebody doing
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • see / hear / watch / feel / smell / notice + kim + fe’l tuzilmasini: I saw Malika cross the road.
  • see somebody do (harakatni boshidan oxirigacha ko‘rdim) va see somebody doing (harakatning o‘rtasini, jarayonini ko‘rdim) farqini.
  • Qaysi fe’llar shu tuzilma bilan ishlatilishini va nega bu yerda to kerak emasligini.
  • Qachon do, qachon -ing tanlashni: qisqa voqea, davomli tovush, hid va his-tuyg‘u.

Biror odam nima qilganini o‘zimiz ko‘rgan, eshitgan yoki sezgan bo‘lsak, inglizchada buni qisqa tuzilma bilan aytamiz: see / hear / watch ... + shu odam + fe’l. Fe’l ikki xil shaklda kelishi mumkin — oddiy shaklda (cross) yoki -ing shaklida (crossing) — va bu ikki shakl ma’noni o‘zgartiradi.

I+saw / heard / watched+him / her / Malika+crossyokicrossing

O‘zbek tilida ham xuddi shu farq bor va u qo‘shimchalar bilan beriladi. Malikaning ko‘chadan o‘tganini ko‘rdim — o‘tib bo‘lganini, butun harakatni ko‘rdim (-gan). Malikaning ko‘chadan o‘tayotganini ko‘rdim — o‘tayotgan paytini ko‘rdim (-ayotgan). Inglizchada bu qo‘shimchalar yo‘q: kishi (Malika yoki her) + fe’lning oddiy shakli = -gan ma’nosi, fe’lning -ing shakli = -ayotgan ma’nosi. Shuningdek, o‘zbekchadagi “Malikaning” (qaratqich kelishigi) inglizchada ’s bo‘lmaydi, shunchaki Malika yoki her / him deyiladi.

Ikki shakl: see somebody do va see somebody doing The whole action and the action in progress

Tasavvur qiling, siz derazadan qarab turibsiz. Bekzod ko‘chaning bir tomonidan yo‘lga chiqdi, yo‘lni kesib o‘tdi va narigi tomonga yetib bordi. Siz buning hammasini ko‘rdingiz:

  • I saw Bekzod cross the road.Men Bekzodning yo‘ldan o‘tganini ko‘rdim. (U boshladi, o‘tdi va tugatdi.)

Endi boshqa holat: siz derazaga aynan Bekzod yo‘lning o‘rtasida ketayotgan paytda qaradingiz. Siz harakatning faqat bir qismini ko‘rdingiz — qachon boshlangani va qachon tugagani sizga noma’lum:

  • I saw Bekzod crossing the road.Men Bekzodning yo‘ldan o‘tayotganini ko‘rdim. (U o‘tish jarayonida edi.)

see somebody do

Fe’lning oddiy shakli (to‘siz). Harakatni boshidan oxirigacha, to‘liq ko‘rdim / eshitdim.

  • I saw him fall.
  • I heard her leave.

see somebody doing

Fe’lning -ing shakli. Harakat jarayonda edi; men uning faqat bir qismini ko‘rdim / eshitdim.

  • I saw him falling.
  • I heard her leaving.

Qoida: see somebody do = men harakatning hammasini ko‘rdim. see somebody doing = harakat ko‘z oldimda davom etayotgan edi (boshlanishi yoki oxirini ko‘rmagan bo‘lishim mumkin). Esda tutish uchun: do — butun film, doing — filmdan bitta lavha.

Ko‘rgan odamingiz ega emas, to‘ldiruvchi bo‘ladi, shuning uchun olmosh ham to‘ldiruvchi shaklida keladi: me, him, her, us, them. I saw him cross the road ✅ — I saw he cross the road ❌.

see, hear, smell, feel kabi fe’llar hozirgi zamonda odatda can bilan keladi: I can hear a baby crying ✅ — I am hearing a baby crying ❌ (bu fe’llar holat fe’llari, Unit 4).

Boshqa ba’zi fe’llar (catch, find, leave) ham “fe’l + kim + fe’l” shaklida keladi, lekin ular bilan faqat -ing ishlatiladi: I caught him reading my diary. (Uni kundaligimni o‘qiyotgan paytida ushladim.)

Bir vaqtda ikki voqea: fon va asosiy voqea

-ing shakli ko‘pincha “o‘sha paytda yana nimadir bo‘layotgan edi” degan fonni beradi:

  • While I was waiting for the bus, I saw Aziz cycling past, so I waved to him.Avtobus kutib turganimda Azizning velosipedda o‘tib ketayotganini ko‘rdim va unga qo‘l silkitdim.
  • I saw the thief run out of the shop and jump into a car.O‘g‘rining do‘kondan yugurib chiqib, mashinaga sakrab o‘tirganini ko‘rdim.

Ikkinchi gapda ikki ish ketma-ket yuz berdi (yugurib chiqdi, sakrab o‘tirdi) — shuning uchun ikkalasida ham oddiy shakl. Birinchi va uchinchi gapda esa -ing: harakat “jarayonda” edi.

Ba’zan ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri

  • I watched the boys play chess for an hour.Bolalarning bir soat shaxmat o‘ynashini kuzatdim. (butun o‘yinga e’tibor)
  • I watched the boys playing chess for a while, then went home.Bolalar shaxmat o‘ynayotganini birozgina tomosha qilib turdim, keyin uyga ketdim. (jarayon)

O‘zbekcha ikkala ma’no ham bitta fe’lning qo‘shimchasi bilan farqlanadi: jiringlaganini eshitdim / jiringlayotganini eshitdim. Inglizchada qo‘shimcha o‘rniga fe’l shaklini o‘zgartirasiz: hear it ring / hear it ringing. Gapni o‘zbekchadan tarjima qilishdan oldin “-gan”mi yoki “-ayotgan”mi, deb o‘ylab ko‘ring.

Majhul nisbatda to qaytib keladi Passive: He was seen to leave

Aktiv gapda see / hear dan keyin to bo‘lmaydi, lekin majhul nisbatda (passive) kishi ega bo‘lganda to + fe’l ishlatiladi. Bu uslub ko‘proq yangiliklar va rasmiy bayonlarda uchraydi. -ing shakli esa o‘zgarmaydi:

Aktiv va passiv
AktivPassiv
Somebody saw him leave the building.He was seen to leave the building.
Somebody saw him leaving the building.He was seen leaving the building.
They heard her say something strange.She was heard to say something strange.
  • The man was seen to enter the bank at ten o’clock.U kishining soat o‘nda bankka kirganini ko‘rishgan.
  • The boy was seen climbing over the fence.Bolaning to‘siqdan oshib o‘tayotganini ko‘rishgan.

I heard him sing va I heard that he sings Don’t mix them up

Bu tuzilmani that bilan boshlanadigan gap bilan almashtirib yubormang. Ma’nolari boshqa:

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I saw him to cross the road.I saw him cross the road. / I saw him crossing the road.see / hear / watch / feel dan keyin to ishlatilmaydi.
I heard her sang a song.I heard her sing a song. / I heard her singing a song.Zamon birinchi fe’lda (heard) allaqachon bor. Ikkinchi fe’l o‘tgan zamonga qo‘yilmaydi: oddiy shakl yoki -ing.
I could smell coffee to brew.I could smell coffee brewing.Davom etayotgan hid — -ing; to-infinitive ishlatilmaydi.
I saw her was crying.I saw her crying. / I saw (that) she was crying.“her” dan keyin “was” kelmaydi. Yo “her + -ing”, yo “that + ega + fe’l”.
I am hearing somebody knocking.I can hear somebody knocking.hear, see, smell holat fe’llari: odatda can bilan, continuous shaklda emas.
He was seen leave the building.He was seen to leave the building. / He was seen leaving the building.Passivda oddiy shakl kelmaydi: to leave yoki leaving.
I saw them that they were playing.I saw them playing. / I saw that they were playing.“them” va “that” gapi bir joyda kelmaydi. Birini tanlang.
I saw he cross the road.I saw him cross the road.Bu yerda kishi to‘ldiruvchi: him / her / them (he / she / they emas).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

see, watch va look at orasida qanday farq bor?

see — ko‘z oldingizda bo‘layotgan narsani ko‘rish (ko‘pincha ixtiyorsiz): I saw an accident. watch — e’tibor bilan, odatda harakatlanayotgan narsani kuzatish: watch TV, watch a match. look at — nigohni biror narsaga qaratish: Look at this photo! Uchalasi ham bu tuzilmada keladi; look at bilan ko‘pincha -ing ishlatiladi: Look at the children playing.

“I saw him fall” va “I saw him falling” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, lekin ma’no ozgina farq qiladi. I saw him fall — yiqilib tushganini (butun voqeani) ko‘rdim. I saw him falling — u yiqilayotgan paytini ko‘rdim. Juda qisqa voqealarda (fall, break, slam, explode) odatda oddiy shakl tabiiyroq eshitiladi.

“I heard him sing” va “I heard that he sings” orasidagi farq nima?

Birinchisida siz uning ovozini o‘zingiz eshitgansiz. Ikkinchisida bu xabar: kimdir sizga aytgan yoki siz biror joydan bilib olgansiz. Ikkinchi gapni o‘zbekcha “...ekan” yoki “...deb eshitdim” deb tarjima qilish mumkin.

Nega “I am hearing” emas, “I can hear” deyiladi?

Ingliz tilida see, hear, smell, feel, taste kabi fe’llar ko‘pincha can bilan, oddiy shaklda ishlatiladi: I can hear it. Can you see him? O‘tgan zamonda could yoki oddiy o‘tgan zamon: I could hear it. I heard it. Bu qoida Unit 4 da batafsil berilgan.

Bu tuzilma make va let bilan ham bir xilmi?

Qisman. make va let dan keyin ham oddiy shakl (to‘siz) keladi: She made me wait. He let us go. Lekin ularda “-ing” varianti yo‘q, ma’no ham boshqa: make — majburlamoq, let — ruxsat bermoq. Ko‘rish va eshitish fe’llarida esa ikkala shakl bor (Unit 55).

Bu tuzilmani o‘tgan zamondan boshqa zamonlarda ham ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha. Faqat birinchi fe’l zamonga qarab o‘zgaradi, ikkinchi fe’l (do yoki doing) o‘zgarmaydi: I can see him coming. I’ll watch you play. I’ve never heard her sing. She is watching them dance.

“I saw him running” desam, u keyin ham yugurdimi?

Gap buni aytmaydi. U faqat shuni bildiradi: men uni ko‘rganimda u yugurayotgan edi. U yugurishni to‘xtatdimi yoki yugurishda davom etdimi, noma’lum. Agar butun yugurishni, ya’ni boshidan oxirigacha ko‘rgan bo‘lsangiz, I saw him run deysiz.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (oddiy shakl yoki -ing). Qavsdagi izohga qarang: to‘liq = butun harakat; jarayonda = harakat davom etayotgan edi.

  1. We watched the plane (land) and then went to the arrivals hall. (to‘liq)
  2. As I walked past the café, I saw Kamola (read) a book at a table. (jarayonda)
  3. Nobody heard Sardor (leave) the party. He just disappeared. (to‘liq)
  4. Can you smell the samsa (bake)? (davom etmoqda)
  5. I looked out of the window and saw the children (play) in the snow. (jarayonda)
  6. I felt the table (shake) for a second, and then everything was quiet. (qisqa, tugagan)
  7. Listen! Can you hear somebody (knock) on the door? (davom etmoqda)
  8. Last night we all listened to Nodira (sing) the whole song from beginning to end. (to‘liq)

2-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni tuzating.

  1. I saw him to enter the shop. →
  2. She heard the baby cried. →
  3. I can smell something to cook. →
  4. We watched them played volleyball. →
  5. He was seen leave the building. →

3-mashq. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling (see / hear / watch / smell dan foydalaning).

  1. Men Dilnozaning rasm chizayotganini ko‘rdim. →
  2. Biz samolyotning boshidan oxirigacha qo‘nishini kuzatdik. →
  3. Men kimdir eshikni taqillatayotganini eshitdim. →
  4. Non yopilayotgan hidi kelyapti. (smell) →

4-mashq. Ikki gapni bitta gapga birlashtiring. Qavsdagi fe’ldan foydalaning; izohga qarang: to‘liq → oddiy shakl, jarayon → -ing.

  1. Kamola was talking on the phone. I saw her. (see; jarayon) →
  2. The glass fell off the table and broke. I heard it. (hear; to‘liq) →
  3. Somebody was following us. We noticed it. (notice; jarayon) →
  4. The children were singing in the next room. I could hear them. (hear; jarayon) →
  5. The sun went down behind the hills. We watched it. (watch; to‘liq) →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Tuzilma: see / hear / watch / feel / smell / notice + kim (him, her, them ...) + fe’l.
  • see somebody do = butun harakatni boshidan oxirigacha ko‘rdim (o‘zbekcha -gan).
  • see somebody doing = harakat jarayonda edi, uning bir qismini ko‘rdim (o‘zbekcha -ayotgan).
  • Qisqa voqealar uchun (fall, break, slam) odatda do; davomli tovush, hid, tuyg‘u uchun (burn, play, shout) doing.
  • Aktiv gapda to yo‘q: I saw him cross ✅. Passivda to bor: He was seen to cross.
  • Kishi to‘ldiruvchi: him, her, them (he, she, they ❌). I can hear, I am hearing ❌.
  • I heard him sing (o‘zim eshitdim) ≠ I heard that he sings (xabar eshitdim).

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 4 (holat fe’llari: can see / can hear), Unit 6 (Past continuous), Unit 53 (Fe’l + -ing), Unit 55 (Fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi + to; make / let), Unit 68 (-ing li bo‘laklar), Unit 97 (-ing va -ed li bo‘laklar).
