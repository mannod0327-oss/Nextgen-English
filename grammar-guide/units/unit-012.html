For and since, When ...? and How long ...?

For va since; When ...? va How long ...?
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • for va since farqini: for three hours (qancha vaqt) — since 9 o’clock (qachondan beri).
  • for ni qachon tashlab ketish mumkin va qachon mumkin emasligini; nega for all my life deyilmasligini.
  • When ...? (past simple) va How long ...? (present perfect) savollarining farqini.
  • It’s two years since I saw him tipidagi gaplarni va How long is it since ...? savolini.
  • ago, for, since ni chalkashtirmaslikni.

Oldingi darsda (Unit 11) biz How long have you been ...? savolini o‘rgandik. Endi “qancha vaqtdan beri” degan ma’noni inglizchada qanday so‘zlar bilan berishni aniq o‘rganamiz. Asosiy fikr: for — davrning uzunligi (soat, kun, yil), since — davrning boshlanish nuqtasi (soat, sana, voqea).

O‘zbek tilida ikkala ma’no ham bitta qo‘shimcha bilan beriladi: ikki yildan beri va 2020-yildan beri. Ingliz tilida esa “-dan beri” dan oldin nima turganiga qarab ikki xil so‘z tanlanadi: uzunlik bo‘lsa — for two years; boshlanish nuqtasi bo‘lsa — since 2020. Shuning uchun ❌ since two years yoki ❌ for 2020 deyish — o‘quvchilarning eng tipik xatosi.

for va since for + a period of time, since + the start of a period

Ikkalasi ham biror narsa qancha vaqtdan beri davom etayotganini aytish uchun ishlatiladi (ko‘pincha present perfect bilan). Farqi — ulardan keyin nima kelishida:

    The children have been playing outside since lunch.    Bolalar ikki soatdan beri tashqarida o‘ynashyapti. / Bolalar tushlikdan beri tashqarida o‘ynashyapti.
  • We’ve lived in Bukhara for many years / since 2015.Biz Buxoroda ko‘p yillardan beri / 2015-yildan beri yashaymiz.
  • I haven’t eaten anything for twelve hours / since breakfast.O‘n ikki soatdan beri / nonushtadan beri hech narsa yemadim.

Qoida: “Qancha?” deb so‘rash mumkin bo‘lsa (ikki soat, uch yil, ko‘p vaqt) — for. “Qachondan?” deb so‘rash mumkin bo‘lsa (soat 8 dan, dushanbadan, 2015-yildan, kelganimdan) — since.

since + gap (ergash gap)

since dan keyin butun gap ham kelishi mumkin. Bu gapda odatda past simple ishlatiladi, chunki u o‘tmishdagi boshlanish nuqtasini bildiradi:

  • I’ve known Nodira since we were at school.Nodirani maktabda o‘qiganimizdan beri bilaman.
  • Sardor has lived in Tashkent since he got married.Sardor uylanganidan beri Toshkentda yashaydi.
  • It’s been raining since we arrived.Biz kelganimizdan beri yomg‘ir yog‘yapti.

for ni tashlab ketish mumkinmi?

Darak gapda, ayniqsa og‘zaki nutqda, for ni ba’zan tushirib qoldirish mumkin:

  • Aziz and I have been friends (for) fifteen years.Aziz bilan o‘n besh yildan beri do‘stmiz.

Lekin inkor gapda for tushib qolmaydi:

  • I haven’t driven a car for five years. (not I haven’t driven a car five years)Besh yildan beri mashina haydamadim.

for + all ishlatilmaydi

all my life, all day, all week kabi iboralar oldidan for qo‘yilmaydi:

  • My grandmother has lived in Samarkand all her life. (not for all her life)Buvim butun umri Samarqandda yashab keladi.
  • Malika has been busy all day. (not for all day)Malika kun bo‘yi band.

When ...? va How long ...? When did it start? / How long has it been ...?

Bu ikki savol bir xil vaziyat haqida bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin ular boshqa-boshqa narsani so‘raydi va boshqa zamonda keladi:

When ...? + past simple

Ish qachon (qaysi nuqtada) boshlangan / bo‘lgan?

  • When did you arrive?
  • — I arrived two hours ago / at ten o’clock.

How long ...? + present perfect

O‘sha nuqtadan hozirgacha qancha vaqt o‘tdi?

  • How long have you been here?
  • — I’ve been here for two hours / since ten o’clock.

Juft misollar

  • A: When did you buy this flat?
    B: We bought it eight years ago / when our son was born.    — Bu kvartirani qachon sotib olgansizlar? — Sakkiz yil oldin / o‘g‘limiz tug‘ilganda sotib olganmiz.
  • A: How long have you had it?
    B: We’ve had it for eight years / since our son was born.    — U sizlarda qancha vaqtdan beri bor? — Sakkiz yildan beri / o‘g‘limiz tug‘ilganidan beri.
  • When did you start learning English? — Three years ago.Ingliz tilini qachon o‘rganishni boshlagansiz? — Uch yil oldin.
  • How long have you been learning English? — For three years.Qancha vaqtdan beri ingliz tilini o‘rganyapsiz? — Uch yildan beri.
  • When did Dilnoza move to Fergana? — In 2021.
    How long has she lived there? — Since 2021.    Dilnoza Farg‘onaga qachon ko‘chib borgan? — 2021-yilda. U yerda qachondan beri yashaydi? — 2021-yildan beri.
Qaysi savolga qanday javob beriladi?
SavolZamonJavobdagi tipik so‘zlar
When did ...? / What time did ...?past simpleago (two years ago), in 2019, on Monday, at 6 o’clock, when I was ..., yesterday, last week

O‘zbek tilida “ikki soat oldin keldim” va “ikki soatdan beri shu yerdaman” deb aniq farqlaymiz. Inglizchada ham xuddi shunday: I came two hours ago (past simple + ago) — I’ve been here for two hours (present perfect + for). “oldin” ni before deb tarjima qilmang: ❌ before two hours — to‘g‘risi two hours ago.

It’s two years since ... It’s (a long time / two years …) since something happened

Biror voqea oxirgi marta qachon bo‘lganini aytishning yana bir usuli bor:

It’s+vaqt (two years, ages ...)+since+past simple
  • It’s three years since I last visited my grandparents in Nukus.Nukusdagi bobom va buvimnikiga oxirgi marta borganimga uch yil bo‘ldi. (= I haven’t visited them for three years.)
  • It’s ages since we had a family picnic.Oilamiz bilan piknikka chiqqanimizga ancha bo‘ldi. (= We haven’t had a family picnic for ages.)
  • It’s twenty years since this school opened.Bu maktab ochilganiga yigirma yil bo‘ldi.
  • It’s only a week since Bekzod started his new job, but he already loves it.Bekzod yangi ishini boshlaganiga bor-yo‘g‘i bir hafta bo‘ldi, lekin unga allaqachon juda yoqib qoldi.

Savol: How long is it since ...?

  • How long is it since you last went to the dentist?Oxirgi marta tish shifokoriga borganingizga qancha bo‘ldi?
  • How long is it since Malika left school?Malika maktabni bitirganiga qancha bo‘ldi?

It’s ... = It’s been ...

It’s o‘rniga It’s been (= It has been) ham deyish mumkin, ma’nosi bir xil:

  • It’s been three years since I last visited my grandparents.Bobom va buvimnikiga oxirgi marta borganimga uch yil bo‘ldi.
  • How long has it been since you had a holiday?Ta’tilga chiqqaningizga qancha bo‘ldi?
Bitta fikr — uch xil aytish usuli
It’s ... since + past simplehaven’t + V3 + forThe last time ... was ... ago
It’s three months since I had a haircut.I haven’t had a haircut for three months.The last time I had a haircut was three months ago.
It’s ages since Jasur called me.Jasur hasn’t called me for ages.The last time Jasur called me was ages ago.

Bu tuzilma o‘zbekchadagi “-ganiga ... bo‘ldi” ga juda o‘xshaydi: “ko‘rganimga ikki yil bo‘ldi” = It’s two years since I saw him. E’tibor bering: since dan keyingi qism past simpleda (saw, went, got), chunki u o‘tmishdagi bitta nuqtani bildiradi.

during (davomida) — “qancha vaqt?” emas, “qachon?” degan savolga javob beradi: I visited Khiva during the summer holidays. I stayed there for five days. — Yozgi ta’tilda Xivaga bordim. U yerda besh kun qoldim. Batafsil — Unit 119.

for a long time / long

So‘roq va inkor gaplarda for a long time o‘rniga ko‘pincha qisqa long ishlatiladi:

  • Have you been waiting long?Ko‘p kutib qoldingizmi?
  • I haven’t lived here long — just a few months.Bu yerda ko‘p yashamadim — bor-yo‘g‘i bir necha oy.

Amerika inglizchasida inkor gaplarda for o‘rniga ko‘pincha in ishlatiladi: I haven’t seen him in years (BrE: for years). Hozir bu shakl Britaniyada ham tez-tez eshitiladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

When have you arrived?When did you arrive?When ...? — aniq vaqt haqida savol, u past simple bilan keladi.
How long did you live here? (hozir ham shu yerda yashasa)How long have you lived here?Davr hozirgacha davom etyapti → present perfect.
I’ve lived here for all my life.I’ve lived here all my life.All my life / all day oldidan for qo‘yilmaydi.
They haven’t visited us two years.They haven’t visited us for two years.Inkor gapda for tushirib qoldirilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

for va since ni qanday tez farqlayman?

“-dan beri” dan oldingi so‘zga qarang. Uni soat yoki kalendar bilan o‘lchash mumkin bo‘lsa (ikki soat, uch kun, besh yil, ko‘p vaqt) — for. U aniq nuqta bo‘lsa (soat 8, dushanba, 2015-yil, Navro‘z, kelganim) — since. Test: “qancha?” — for; “qachondan?” — since.

“We’ve been friends fifteen years” — bu yerda for qayerda? Xato emasmi?

Xato emas. Darak gapda (ayniqsa og‘zaki nutqda) for ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin. Lekin inkor gapda mumkin emas: I haven’t driven a car for five years. Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, for ni har doim yozing — bu hech qachon xato bo‘lmaydi (faqat all oldidan yozilmaydi).

since dan keyin qaysi zamon keladi?

since dan keyingi qism (ergash gap) odatda past simpleda bo‘ladi, chunki u o‘tmishdagi boshlanish nuqtasini bildiradi: since I was a child, since we moved here, since she got married. Asosiy gap esa present perfectda: I’ve known him since we were children.

“ago” va “before” ning farqi nima?

ago = hozirdan qancha oldin: I met him two years ago (bundan ikki yil oldin). before yolg‘iz kelsa = “ilgari, avval”: I’ve seen this film before. ... before (masalan, two years before) = o‘tmishdagi boshqa bir nuqtadan oldin — bu odatda hikoyada past perfect bilan keladi (Unit 15). “Hozirdan oldin” ma’nosida doim ago.

“It’s two years since...” va “It’s been two years since...” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va ma’nosi bir xil. It’s bu yerda It is ham, It has (It’s been) ham bo‘lishi mumkin. Savolda ham ikkala shakl bor: How long is it since ...? / How long has it been since ...?

“How long ago did you ...?” va “How long have you ...?” bir xil emasmi?

Yo‘q. How long ago did you leave school? = “Maktabni necha yil oldin bitirgansiz?” (javob: five years ago) — bu aslida When did you leave school? bilan bir xil. How long have you been at this school? = “Bu maktabda qancha vaqtdan beri o‘qiysiz?” (javob: for two years).

“since” ning “chunki” degan ma’nosi ham bormi?

Ha, bu — butunlay boshqa ma’no: Since it’s raining, let’s stay at home. (Yomg‘ir yog‘ayotgani uchun uyda qolaylik.) Bu ma’noda since because / as ga yaqin va zamon bilan bog‘liq qoidalar unga tegishli emas. Gapning mazmunidan qaysi ma’no ekanini bilib olasiz.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • for + davr uzunligi (for two hours, for ten years, for ages); since + boshlanish nuqtasi (since 9 o’clock, since 2015, since I was a child).
  • for ni darak gapda tushirish mumkin, inkor gapda — yo‘q. all oldidan for qo‘yilmaydi: all my life.
  • When ...? → past simple (When did it start?); How long ...? → present perfect (How long has it been raining?).
  • It’s / It’s been + vaqt + since + past simple: It’s two years since I saw him. = I haven’t seen him for two years.
  • ago = hozirdan oldin, past simple bilan, vaqtdan keyin keladi: two years ago (before two years ❌).
  • for har qanday zamon bilan kela oladi: I lived there for three years. I’m going there for a week.
