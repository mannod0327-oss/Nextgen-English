Will be doing and will have done

Kelasi davomli va kelasi tugallangan zamon
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • will be doing (future continuous — kelasi davomli zamon): kelajakdagi ma’lum paytda davom etayotgan ish.
  • will be doing va will do farqini.
  • will be doing ning yana bir ma’nosi: kutilayotgan, rejadagi ishlar va muloyim savollar (Will you be using the car tonight?).
  • will have done (future perfect — kelasi tugallangan zamon): kelajakdagi ma’lum paytga qadar tugagan ish.
  • by va by the time so‘zlarini, perfect shakllarini taqqoslashni.

Ikkala zamonning mantig‘i oddiy: kelajakda bir nuqta (vaqt) tanlaymiz va o‘sha nuqtada nima bo‘layotganini (will be doing) yoki nima allaqachon tugaganini (will have done) aytamiz.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar uchun qulay shakllar bor: Ertaga soat 10 da dars o‘tayotgan bo‘laman → I’ll be teaching at 10 tomorrow. Soat 9 gacha u ishga ketib bo‘lgan bo‘ladi → She’ll have gone to work by 9. Ya’ni -ayotgan bo‘laman ≈ will be doing, -ib bo‘lgan bo‘laman ≈ will have done. Bu o‘xshashlik juda foydali!

Bir misolda ikkala zamon An example: will be doing and will have done

Bekzod — uchuvchi. Ertaga u Toshkentdan Londonga uchadi. Samolyot ertalab soat 9:00 da ko‘tariladi va kunduzi soat 16:00 da (Toshkent vaqti bilan) qo‘nadi. Do‘sti Jasur unga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoqchi:

  • JASUR: Can I call you at about one o’clock tomorrow?
    BEKZOD: No, I’ll be flying then. Call me at five — I’ll have landed by then.    JASUR: Ertaga soat birlarda qo‘ng‘iroq qilsam bo‘ladimi? BEKZOD: Yo‘q, o‘shanda uchib ketayotgan bo‘laman. Soat beshda qo‘ng‘iroq qil — o‘shangacha qo‘nib bo‘lgan bo‘laman.

will be doing — kelajakdagi paytda davom etayotgan ish Future continuous: in the middle of doing something

I will be doing something = kelajakdagi ma’lum paytda men bu ishning o‘rtasida bo‘laman: ish o‘sha paytdan oldin boshlangan va hali tugamagan bo‘ladi.

Ega+will be+fe’l + ing+kelajakdagi vaqt

  • Don’t call me between 2 and 4. I’ll be teaching.Soat 2 bilan 4 orasida qo‘ng‘iroq qilmang. Dars o‘tayotgan bo‘laman.

  • In an hour we’ll be driving through the mountains.Bir soatdan keyin tog‘lar orasidan o‘tib ketayotgan bo‘lamiz.

will be doing va will do

⏳ will be doing — allaqachon boshlangan

When you arrive, we’ll be having dinner.

Siz kelganingizda biz kechki ovqatni yeyayotgan bo‘lamiz. (Ovqat siz kelishingizdan oldin boshlanadi.)

▶️ will do — keyin boshlanadi

When you arrive, we’ll have dinner.

Siz kelganingizdan keyin kechki ovqatni yeymiz. (Ovqat siz kelgach boshlanadi.)

O‘tmish, hozir, kelajak — davomli shakllar

Aziz — o‘qituvchi. Har kuni soat 11 da u dars o‘tadi.
VaqtGapZamon
Kecha soat 11 daAt 11 yesterday Aziz was teaching.past continuous
Hozir (soat 11)It’s 11 now. Aziz is teaching.present continuous
Ertaga soat 11 daAt 11 tomorrow Aziz will be teaching.future continuous

Holat fe’llari (know, want, like, understand, belong ...) bu shaklda ham odatda ishlatilmaydi: I’ll know the results tomorrow ✅ — I’ll be knowing ❌.

will be doing — rejadagi, kutilayotgan ishlar Future continuous for planned or expected actions

will be doing boshqacha ma’noda ham ishlatiladi: kelajakda to‘liq bajariladigan, rejalashtirilgan yoki “o‘z-o‘zidan” kutilayotgan ishlar haqida. Bu ma’noda u be going to ga yaqin, lekin ohangi neytralroq: niyatga emas, “ishlar shunday rejalashtirilgan, shunday bo‘ladi” degan ma’noga urg‘u beradi:

  • The mayor will be opening the new park in Fergana on Saturday.Shanba kuni Farg‘onadagi yangi bog‘ni hokim ochib beradi.
  • After the news we’ll be showing a film about the Silk Road.Yangiliklardan so‘ng Buyuk ipak yo‘li haqidagi filmni namoyish etamiz.
  • Nodira won’t be coming to the lesson tomorrow. She’s ill.Nodira ertaga darsga kelmaydi. U kasal.
  • The train will be arriving at platform 3 in five minutes.Poyezd besh daqiqadan keyin 3-platformaga kelib to‘xtaydi.

Muloyim savol: “Will you be ...ing?”

Will you be ...ing? — kishining rejasini muloyimlik bilan so‘rash usuli. Bu iltimos emas, shunchaki “rejangiz qanday?” degan savol:

🙂 Will you be using ...? — reja haqida savol

Will you be using the car tonight? (If not, can I borrow it?)

Bugun kechqurun mashinani ishlatasizmi? (Ishlatmasangiz, men olsam maylimi?)

🙏 Will you ...? — iltimos

Will you lend me your car tonight?

Bugun kechqurun mashinangizni berib turasizmi? (To‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri iltimos.)

  • Will you be going to the market later? Could you get me some apples?Keyinroq bozorga borasizmi? Menga biroz olma olib kela olasizmi?
  • Will you be staying in Tashkent for the holidays?Ta’tilda Toshkentda qolasizmi?

will have done — kelajakdagi paytgacha tugagan ish Future perfect: something will already be complete

will have done — ish kelajakdagi ma’lum vaqtgacha allaqachon tugagan bo‘ladi. Ko‘pincha by (... gacha, ... ga kelib), by then, by the time ... bilan keladi.

Ega+will have+V3 (past participle)+by + vaqt
  • The shop closes at 9. If we get there at 9.15, it will have closed.Do‘kon 9 da yopiladi. Agar 9:15 da yetib borsak, u yopilib bo‘lgan bo‘ladi.
  • Hurry up! By the time we get to the bus stop, the last bus will have gone.Tezroq! Bekatga yetib borgunimizcha oxirgi avtobus ketib bo‘lgan bo‘ladi.
  • By the end of this year I’ll have read thirty books.Shu yil oxirigacha o‘ttizta kitob o‘qib bo‘lgan bo‘laman.
  • Call me after 7. I won’t have finished my work before then.Soat 7 dan keyin qo‘ng‘iroq qil. Undan oldin ishimni tugatmagan bo‘laman.
  • Will you have finished the report by Friday?Hisobotni jumagacha tugatib bo‘lasizmi?

Perfect shakllarni taqqoslang

Malika bu kompaniyada to‘rt yildan beri ishlaydi
ZamonGapO‘zbekcha
present perfectMalika has worked here for four years.Malika bu yerda to‘rt yildan beri ishlaydi.
future perfectNext year she will have worked here for five years.Kelasi yil u bu yerda besh yil ishlagan bo‘ladi.
past perfectWhen she got her first promotion, she had worked here for two years.Birinchi marta lavozimi oshganda, u bu yerda ikki yil ishlagan edi.

by the time dan keyin kelajak ma’nosida present simple keladi: by the time we get there ✅ — by the time we will get there ❌. Bu qoida Unit 25 da batafsil.

Qo‘shimcha: by va until, will have been doing by / until; future perfect continuous

by yoki until?

by = ... gacha (kechiktirmay)

Ish o‘sha vaqtgacha tugaydi: I’ll have finished the essay by Friday.

Inshoni jumagacha tugatib bo‘laman.

until = ... gacha (davom etadi)

Ish o‘sha vaqtgacha davom etadi: I’ll be working until Friday.

Jumagacha ishlayotgan bo‘laman (juma kuni to‘xtayman).

will have been doing (future perfect continuous)

Kelajakdagi paytga qadar qancha vaqt davom etgan bo‘lishini aytish uchun: will have been + -ing. Bu shakl kam ishlatiladi, lekin bilib qo‘ygan foydali:

  • In June I’ll have been learning English for three years.Iyunda ingliz tilini o‘rganayotganimga uch yil bo‘ladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

This time tomorrow I will travel to Bukhara by train.This time tomorrow I’ll be travelling to Bukhara by train.Kelajakdagi ma’lum paytda davom etayotgan ish — will be + -ing.
Don’t call at 8. I will having a shower.Don’t call at 8. I’ll be having a shower.“be” tushib qolgan: will + be + -ing.
By the time you come, I will have finish.By the time you come, I will have finished.will have dan keyin V3 (past participle) keladi.
I’ll have finished the report until Friday.I’ll have finished the report by Friday.Tugash muddati — by. until davom etadigan ish uchun.
By the time we will arrive, the guests will have left.By the time we arrive, the guests will have left.by the time dan keyin present simple (Unit 25).
Tomorrow at this time I’ll be knowing my results.Tomorrow at this time I’ll know my results.“know” — holat fe’li, -ing olmaydi.
In June I have lived in Tashkent for ten years.In June I’ll have lived in Tashkent for ten years.Kelajakdagi paytgacha bo‘lgan muddat — will have + V3.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“At 8 I’ll watch TV” va “At 8 I’ll be watching TV” — farqi nima?

At 8 I’ll watch TV — soat 8 da televizor ko‘rishni boshlayman. At 8 I’ll be watching TV — soat 8 da men televizor ko‘rish jarayonida bo‘laman (oldinroq boshlaganman). Shuning uchun “Soat 8 da qo‘ng‘iroq qilsam bo‘ladimi?” degan savolga Sorry, I’ll be watching TV then deyish mumkin.

“Will you be using the car?” va “Are you going to use the car?” — farqi bormi?

Ikkalasi ham rejani so‘raydi. Will you be using ...? biroz muloyimroq va neytralroq: siz kishiga “niyatingiz nima?” deb bosim o‘tkazmaysiz, shunchaki “ishlar qanday bo‘ladi?” deb so‘raysiz. Shuning uchun biror narsa so‘rashdan oldin ko‘p ishlatiladi.

Future perfect og‘zaki nutqda ko‘p ishlatiladimi?

Present perfect’chalik ko‘p emas, lekin by, by then, by the time bilan juda tabiiy eshitiladi: Don’t worry, I’ll have finished by six. Tez nutqda ’ll have ko‘pincha /əl əv/ kabi qisqa talaffuz qilinadi.

“by” bilan har doim future perfect kerakmi?

Yo‘q. by bilan oddiy will ham ko‘p ishlatiladi: I’ll finish the report by Friday. (Jumagacha tugataman — va’da/reja.) I’ll have finished it by Friday esa “juma kuniga kelib bu ish tugagan holatda bo‘ladi” degan natijaga urg‘u beradi. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

“by the time” dan keyin nega “will” ishlatilmaydi?

Chunki by the time, when, before, after, until, as soon as kabi vaqt bog‘lovchilaridan keyin kelajak ma’nosida present simple ishlatiladi: By the time you wake up, I’ll have left. Bu Unit 25 ning asosiy qoidasi.

“will be doing” ni “going to” o‘rniga har doim ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. C bo‘limdagi ma’no (kutilayotgan, rejadagi ish) faqat ma’lum vaziyatlarda tabiiy: e’lonlar, yangiliklar, rasmiy rejalar, muloyim savollar. Shaxsiy niyat uchun (I’m going to buy a car) going to ishlatgan ma’qul.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Ular o‘sha paytda nima qilayotgan bo‘ladi? will be + -ing bilan yozing.

  1. Kamola works at the hospital from 8 to 5. At 11 tomorrow she (work).
  2. Our flight leaves at 10 and lands at 2. At noon we (fly) over the mountains.
  3. Please don’t ring the doorbell after 9 — the baby (sleep).
  4. This time next week Jasur (ski) in Chimgan.
  5. The concert starts at 8 and finishes at 10. At 9 Sardor (play) the piano on stage.
  6. I (not / use) my laptop this evening, so you can borrow it.

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang: will do yoki will be doing.

  1. I start cooking at 6, so when you get home at 6.30, I (’ll cook / ’ll be cooking) dinner.
  2. As soon as the director arrives, we (’ll start / ’ll be starting) the meeting.
  3. (Will you lend / Will you be lending) me your umbrella, please?
  4. Phone me at 7. I (won’t do / won’t be doing) anything then.
  5. “I can’t open this jar.” “Give it to me. I (’ll open / ’ll be opening) it.”

3-mashq. will have + V3 bilan yozing.

  1. The lesson starts at 9. If we arrive at 9.20, it (start).
  2. Dilnoza is reading a long book. She (finish) it by Sunday.
  3. By the time you get up tomorrow, I (leave) for the airport.
  4. Next month Bekzod (be) a pilot for ten years.
  5. Don’t call at 5. I (not / finish) work by then.
  6. the builders (finish) the new school by September?

4-mashq. by yoki until ni qo‘ying.

  1. I’ll have repaired your bike Saturday.
  2. We’ll be staying in Bukhara Thursday, then we’re going to Khiva.
  3. Please send me your answers the end of the week.

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Ertaga soat 10 da men imtihon topshirayotgan bo‘laman. →
  2. Kechqurun soat 8 gacha men uyga qaytib bo‘lgan bo‘laman. →
  3. Bugun kechqurun kompyuteringizni ishlatasizmi? (muloyim savol) →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • will be + -ing — kelajakdagi paytda ish davom etayotgan bo‘ladi: At 1 I’ll be flying. (o‘zbekcha -ayotgan bo‘laman)
  • will be doing — ish allaqachon boshlangan; will do — keyin boshlanadi: we’ll be having dinner / we’ll have dinner.
  • will be doing — rejadagi, kutilayotgan ishlar va muloyim savollar: Will you be using the car tonight?
  • will have + V3 — kelajakdagi paytgacha tugagan bo‘ladi: By 5 he’ll have landed. (o‘zbekcha -ib bo‘lgan bo‘laman)
  • by = kechiktirmay ... gacha (tugash); until = ... gacha (davom etish).
  • by the time / when dan keyin present simple: by the time we get there.
