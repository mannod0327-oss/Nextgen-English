53

Verb + -ing (enjoy doing / stop doing etc.)

Fe’l + -ing (enjoy doing / stop doing)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Qaysi fe’llardan keyin ikkinchi fe’l -ing shaklida keladi: enjoy doing, stop doing, finish doing, suggest doing.
  • Bu fe’llarni ma’no bo‘yicha guruhlab eslab qolishni.
  • Ikkinchi fe’lning “egasini” ko‘rsatishni: I don’t mind you coming.
  • being done va having done shakllarini.
  • -ing va to + fe’l orasidagi farqni (Unit 54 bilan solishtirib).

Ikki fe’l ketma-ket kelganda, ikkinchi fe’l ingliz tilida ko‘pincha yo -ing (I enjoy dancing), yo to + fe’l (I want to dance) shaklida bo‘ladi. Qaysi shakl kerakligi birinchi fe’lga bog‘liq. Bu darsda -ing talab qiladigan fe’llarni o‘rganamiz. Bunday -ing shakliga gerund ham deyiladi: fe’l ot kabi ishlatiladi (enjoy tea → enjoy dancing).

O‘zbek tilida fe’lni otga aylantiradigan -ish / -moq qo‘shimchasi bor: raqsga tushishni yoqtiraman, yozishni tugatdim, kechikishdan qochdi — bular inglizchada -ing (enjoy dancing, finish writing, avoid being late). Lekin o‘zbekchadagi bir xil -ish shakli inglizchada ikki xil bo‘ladi: raqsga tushishni xohlayman = want to dance (to bilan!). Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha ❌ I enjoy to dance deb xato qiladi. Yechim — fe’llarni guruhlab yodlash.

Fe’l + -ing Verbs followed by -ing

Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin ikkinchi fe’l -ing shaklida keladi:

Ega+enjoy / finish / avoid ...+fe’l-ing+(to‘ldiruvchi)
  • I enjoy cooking for my family.Men oilam uchun ovqat pishirishni yoqtiraman.
  • Have you finished writing your essay?Inshoyingni yozib bo‘ldingmi?
  • Please stop texting and listen.Iltimos, xabar yozishni bas qiling va tinglang.
  • Sardor suggested having a picnic by the lake.Sardor ko‘l bo‘yida piknik qilishni taklif qildi.

Fe’llar ma’no guruhlari bo‘yicha

😊 Hissiyot va munosabat

  • enjoy — zavqlanmoq: I enjoy swimming.
  • mind — qarshi bo‘lmoq: Do you mind waiting?
  • fancy (BrE) — xohlamoq: Do you fancy going out?
  • miss — sog‘inmoq: I miss living in Samarkand.
  • dislike — yoqtirmaslik: He dislikes getting up early.
  • can’t stand — chidolmaslik: I can’t stand waiting.
  • can’t help — o‘zini tutolmaslik: I couldn’t help laughing.

🛑 To‘xtatish, tugatish, qochish

  • finish — tugatmoq: She finished reading.
  • stop — to‘xtamoq: Stop shouting!
  • give up — voz kechmoq: He gave up smoking.
  • quit — tashlamoq: She quit working there.
  • avoid — qochmoq: Avoid driving at night.
  • delay / postpone / put off — kechiktirmoq: They postponed leaving.

💭 O‘ylash, taklif, tan olish

  • consider — o‘ylab ko‘rmoq: I’m considering moving.
  • imagine — tasavvur qilmoq: Imagine living on Mars!
  • suggest — taklif qilmoq: He suggested taking a taxi.
  • recommend — tavsiya qilmoq: I recommend visiting Khiva.
  • admit — tan olmoq: He admitted breaking the window.
  • deny — inkor qilmoq: She denied taking the money.

🔁 Davom etish, mashq, oqibat

  • keep / keep on — davom etmoq: He kept talking.
  • go on / carry on — davom etmoq: Carry on working.
  • practise — mashq qilmoq: Practise speaking every day.
  • risk — xavf ostiga qo‘ymoq: Don’t risk missing the train.
  • involve — o‘z ichiga olmoq: The job involves travelling.
  • mean (= involve) — degani: It means getting up early.

Yana: resist (I couldn’t resist buying it), appreciate (I appreciate your helping me). Practise — fe’l (Britaniya imlosi); Amerika ingliz tilida fe’l ham practice deb yoziladi.

Inkor shakli: not + -ing

-ing shaklini inkor qilish uchun uning oldiga not qo‘yiladi:

  • He admitted not knowing the answer.U javobni bilmasligini tan oldi.
  • I suggest not telling her yet.Hozircha unga aytmaslikni taklif qilaman.

Qoida: Yuqoridagi fe’llardan keyin to + fe’l ishlatilmaydi: I enjoy to read ❌ — I enjoy reading ✅; She finished to write ❌ — She finished writing ✅.

enjoy, mind, suggest — alohida eslatmalar

Fe’l✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘ri
enjoyI enjoyed the party. / I enjoyed it. / I enjoyed myself. / I enjoy swimming.I enjoyed. (to‘ldiruvchisiz) · I enjoy to swim.
mindWould you mind opening the window?Would you mind to open the window?
suggestShe suggested going by bus.She suggested to go by bus. / She suggested me to go.
She suggested (that) we go / we should go by bus.
She suggested the bus. (ot bilan)

admit, deny va suggest dan keyin that + gap ham ishlatiladi: He denied that he had taken the money. Nodira admitted that she was wrong.

Do you mind ...? savoliga javob: No, not at all yoki No, go ahead — “Qarshi emasman, mayli”. Yes, I do — “Qarshiman”. O‘zbekcha “mayli” deb Yes aytmang: Do you mind if I sit here? — Yes deganingiz “yo‘q, o‘tirmang” degani bo‘lib qoladi! (Unit 37)

Fe’l + kim? + -ing Verb + object / possessive + -ing

Ikkinchi fe’lning bajaruvchisini ko‘rsatish kerak bo‘lsa, -ing dan oldin shaxs qo‘yiladi. Bu mind, imagine, can’t stand, dislike, appreciate kabi fe’llar bilan keng ishlatiladi:

Ega+mind / imagine / can’t stand+me / him / her / Aziz (yoki my / his / Aziz’s)+fe’l-ing
  • I don’t mind you coming late.Kech kelishingizga qarshi emasman.
  • Do you mind me asking?Men so‘rasam, qarshi emasmisiz?
  • I can’t imagine Malika living in a village.Malikaning qishloqda yashashini tasavvur qila olmayman.
  • I can’t stand people shouting.Odamlarning baqirishiga chidolmayman.

Qoida: Kundalik nutqda -ing dan oldin obyekt shakli (me, him, her, us, them, Aziz) ishlatiladi: Do you mind me asking? Rasmiy uslubda egalik shakli (my, his, her, our, their) yaxshiroq: Do you mind my asking? Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

being done va having done Passive and perfect forms

-ing shaklining ikki qo‘shimcha ko‘rinishi bor:

ShaklMa’noMisol
being + V3majhul nisbat: “bir kishi tomonidan -ilmoq”I hate being told what to do.
She doesn’t enjoy being photographed.
having + V3oldin bo‘lgan ish (perfect)He denied having seen her.
She admitted having lied.
  • Nobody likes being laughed at.Hech kim ustidan kulishlarini yoqtirmaydi.
  • The child avoided being seen by the teacher.Bola o‘qituvchi ko‘rib qolishidan qochdi.
  • He admitted having eaten / eating the last piece of cake.U tortning oxirgi bo‘lagini yeganini tan oldi.

Oddiy suhbatda having done o‘rniga ko‘pincha oddiy -ing ishlatiladi: He admitted eating the cake. ham to‘g‘ri va tabiiyroq. Having done rasmiyroq.

-ing va to ni qanday ajrataman? -ing or to?

Ba’zi fe’llar faqat -ing, boshqalari faqat to + fe’l talab qiladi. Quyidagi jadvalda ikkalasi yonma-yon:

-ing (shu dars)to + fe’l (Unit 54)
I enjoy swimming.I want to swim.
She finished reading.She decided to read.
He avoided answering.He refused to answer.
They suggested going.They agreed to go.
We kept walking.We managed to walk home.
I don’t mind waiting.I offered to wait.

Taxminiy yordamchi qoida (istisnolari bor): reja, istak, qaror, va’da ma’nosidagi fe’llar (decide, plan, hope, want, promise, refuse) ko‘pincha to bilan; zavq, qochish, to‘xtatish, tugatish, davom etish ma’nosidagilar (enjoy, avoid, stop, finish, keep) esa -ing bilan keladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi fe’l -ing talab qilishini qanday bilaman?

Asosan yodlash kerak, lekin ma’no guruhlari yordam beradi: zavq/yoqtirmaslik (enjoy, mind, miss), to‘xtatish/tugatish/qochish (stop, finish, avoid, give up, postpone), o‘ylash/taklif/tan olish (consider, imagine, suggest, admit, deny), davom etish (keep, go on, carry on). Yuqoridagi kartochkalardan foydalaning va har kuni bir-ikkita jumla tuzing.

-ing shu yerda “hozirgi davomli” zamon emasmi?

Yo‘q. Present continuous am/is/are + -ing bilan keladi (I am dancing). Bu yerda esa -ing fe’l o‘rnida emas, ot kabi ishlatilmoqda: enjoy dancing ≈ enjoy music. Shuning uchun bu shaklni gerund deyishadi.

“Would you mind opening the window?” ga qanday javob beraman?

Rozi bo‘lsangiz: No, not at all yoki Of course not — “Qarshi emasman”. Rad etmoqchi bo‘lsangiz: I’m sorry, but I’d rather you didn’t yoki Actually, I do mind. Faqat Yes deb qo‘ysangiz, “qarshiman” degan ma’no chiqadi.

“stop doing” bilan “stop to do” ning farqi nima?

stop doing — ishni to‘xtatish: He stopped smoking (chekishni tashladi/bas qildi). stop to do — boshqa ish uchun to‘xtash: He stopped to smoke (chekish uchun to‘xtadi). Batafsil — Unit 56.

“I look forward to seeing you” da nega “to” dan keyin -ing?

Chunki bu yerdagi to infinitiv emas, predlog. Predlogdan keyin har doim -ing (yoki ot) keladi: look forward to the party → look forward to seeing you. Bu Unit 61–62 da.

“practise” va “practice” farqi nima?

Britaniya ingliz tilida practise — fe’l (practise speaking), practice — ot (a lot of practice). Amerika ingliz tilida ikkalasi ham odatda practice deb yoziladi.

Ikkinchi fe’lning egasi birinchinikidan boshqa bo‘lsa-chi?

-ing dan oldin obyekt yoki egalik olmoshini qo‘ying (B bo‘lim): I don’t mind him coming. (Uning kelishiga qarshi emasman.) Do you mind me opening the window?

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (-ing).

  1. Aziz enjoys (play) chess with his grandfather.
  2. Have you finished (write) your essay?
  3. The doctor suggested (take) a long holiday.
  4. Would you mind (turn) the music down?
  5. I can’t imagine (live) without a phone.
  6. They postponed (go) to Khiva because of the rain.
  7. She avoids (eat) fast food.
  8. He admitted (take) my pen.
  9. Stop (run) in the corridor!

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang va yozing.

  1. I enjoy (to cook / cooking) for my family.
  2. Bekzod decided (to study / studying) medicine.
  3. She refused (to answer / answering).
  4. Do you fancy (to go / going) for a walk?
  5. We hope (to see / seeing) you soon.
  6. They kept (to talk / talking) all night.
  7. Malika can’t afford (to buy / buying) a car.
  8. He avoided (to look / looking) at me.

3-mashq. being + V3 yoki having + V3 shaklini yozing.

  1. I hate (tell) what to do.
  2. She doesn’t enjoy (photograph).
  3. He denied (see) her. (having + V3 shaklida)
  4. Nobody likes (laugh) at.

4-mashq. Berilgan fe’l va -ing shakli bilan gapni qayta yozing (faqat bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring).

  1. “Let’s go to the park,” said Malika. (suggest) → Malika suggested .
  2. “Could you open the window, please?” (mind) → Would you mind ?
  3. “I didn’t break the vase,” said Sardor. (deny) → Sardor denied .
  4. “I’ll learn to drive later,” said Nodira. (put off) → Nodira put off .

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin -ing keladi: enjoy, mind, fancy, miss, finish, stop, give up, avoid, delay, postpone, put off, consider, imagine, suggest, recommend, admit, deny, keep, go on, carry on, practise, risk, involve, mean, can’t stand, can’t help.
  • Bu fe’llardan keyin to + fe’l ishlatilmaydi: enjoy reading, avoid asking.
  • Inkor: not + -ing (suggest not telling).
  • Bajaruvchi: mind me / my going (kundalik: obyekt shakli).
  • Majhul: being done. Oldin bo‘lgan ish: having done.
  • suggest: suggest doing / suggest that we go, lekin suggest to go ❌, suggest me to go ❌.
  • enjoy to‘ldiruvchisiz kelmaydi: I enjoyed it / myself.
