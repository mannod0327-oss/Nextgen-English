78

Names with and without the 2

Atoqli otlar the bilan va the siz — 2
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Qaysi joy nomlari the siz keladi: ko‘cha, maydon, park, vokzal, aeroport, universitet, saroy, qal’a (Registan Square, Heathrow Airport, Samarkand State University).
  • Qaysi nomlar the bilan keladi: mehmonxona, restoran, teatr, kino, muzey, mashhur bino va yodgorlik, gazeta, tashkilot (the Hilton, the British Museum, the Guardian, the BBC).
  • of bor nomlar the bilan keladi: the University of Oxford, lekin Oxford University — the siz.
  • ’s bilan tugaydigan do‘kon va restoranlar the siz: Harrods, McDonald’s, St Paul’s.
  • Kompaniya va aviakompaniyalar: Samsung, Toyota, Turkish Airlines — the siz.

Oldingi darsda (Unit 77) mamlakat, shahar, daryo, tog‘ nomlarini ko‘rdik. Bu darsda binolar, joylar va tashkilotlar nomlari. Bu yerda ham aniq qoida bor: nom qanday tuzilganiga va nima turidagi joy ekaniga qaraymiz. Qisqa qilib aytsak: joy nomi + Street / Square / Park / Station / Airport / University / Palace / Castle bo‘lsa — the yo‘q; hotel, restaurant, theatre, cinema, museum, gallery bo‘lsa yoki of ishtirok etsa — the bor.

O‘zbek tilida bunday nomlarning hammasi artiklsiz: Registon maydoni, Hilton mehmonxonasi, Milliy universitet, Britaniya muzeyi. Shuning uchun o‘quvchi “the” ni yo qo‘ymaydi, yo hamma joyga qo‘yadi. Qiziq tomoni: o‘zbekchada Oksford universiteti deymiz (ikki xil ingliz shakli uchun bitta tarjima): Oxford University (the siz) va the University of Oxford (the bilan). Inglizchada bu ikki tuzilmaning har biri o‘z qoidasiga ega.

The ishlatilmaydi: ko‘cha, park, vokzal, aeroport, universitet, saroy No “the”: streets, parks, stations, airports, universities, palaces

Nom joy yoki odam nomi + turni bildiruvchi so‘z (Street, Square, Park, Station, Airport, University, Palace, Castle ...) shaklida tuzilgan bo‘lsa, the qo‘yilmaydi.

Samarkand State University nomida of yo‘q, shuning uchun the yo‘q. Agar nom the National University of Uzbekistan kabi of bilan bo‘lsa, the kerak — C qismga qarang.

Mehmonxona nomi ko‘pincha qisqartiriladi: the Hilton Hotel = the Hilton, the Savoy Hotel = the Savoy. Har ikkala shaklda ham the bor. Kinoteatr va teatr nomlarida ham shunday: the Odeon Cinema = the Odeon, the Palace Theatre = the Palace.

Saroy va qal’a nomlari: joy nomi + Palace / Castle bo‘lsa the yo‘q (Buckingham Palace, Windsor Castle). Boshqa tuzilishdagi nomlarda esa the bor: the Royal Palace, the Kremlin, the Alhambra, the Tower of London. Bunday nomlarni alohida yodlash kerak.

Gazeta, tashkilot, kompaniya Newspapers, organisations and companies

1. Gazeta va jurnallar

Gazetalar the bilan keladi: the Times, the Guardian, the Washington Post, the New York Times, the Daily Telegraph. Jurnallarda umumiy qoida yo‘q: ko‘pchiligi the siz (Time, Newsweek, Vogue, Cosmopolitan), ba’zilarining nomida esa the bor (The Economist). Jurnal nomini yodlab oling.

  • My father reads the Guardian online every day.Dadam har kuni Guardian gazetasini internetda o‘qiydi.
  • There was an article about Khiva in the Times.Times gazetasida Xiva haqida maqola bor edi.
  • Bekzod buys Time every week.Bekzod har hafta Time jurnalini sotib oladi.

2. Tashkilotlar

Tashkilot, partiya, idora va xalqaro tashkilot nomlari the bilan keladi: the United Nations (the UN), the European Union (the EU), the World Bank, the Labour Party, the Red Cross. Harfma-harf o‘qiladigan qisqartmalar ham the oladi: the BBC, the FBI, the UN, the EU, the IMF.

Lekin qisqartma bitta so‘z kabi o‘qilsa, the yo‘q: NATO, UNESCO, UNICEF, FIFA, NASA, OPEC.

  • Kamola works for the BBC in London.Kamola Londonda BBC da ishlaydi.
  • Her aunt works for the United Nations.Uning xolasi Birlashgan Millatlar Tashkilotida ishlaydi.
  • UNESCO has included Khiva, Bukhara and Samarkand on its World Heritage List.YUNESKO Xiva, Buxoro va Samarqandni Butunjahon merosi ro‘yxatiga kiritgan.
  • FIFA organises the World Cup.FIFA Jahon chempionatini tashkil qiladi.

3. Kompaniya va aviakompaniyalar

Kompaniya, brend va aviakompaniya nomlari the siz: Samsung, Toyota, Google, Microsoft, Lufthansa, Turkish Airlines, British Airways.

  • My cousin works for Samsung in Seoul.Amakivachcham Seulda Samsungda ishlaydi.
  • Sardor flew to Istanbul with Turkish Airlines.Sardor Istanbulga Turkish Airlines bilan uchib bordi.
  • Malika’s mother shops at Korzinka.Malikaning onasi Korzinkadan xarid qiladi. (do‘konlar tarmog‘i — kompaniya nomi)

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega the Hilton, lekin Harrods va McDonald’s?

Qoida nom turiga bog‘liq. Mehmonxona, teatr, muzey kabi bino turlari the bilan keladi (the Hilton). Odam ismidan olingan do‘kon va restoran nomlari (-s, ’s) the siz (Harrods, McDonald’s). Kompaniya va brendlar ham the siz (Samsung, Toyota).

Oxford University va the University of Oxford — farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil, faqat tuzilishi har xil. Qoida oddiy: of bo‘lsa — the bor (the University of Oxford); of bo‘lmasa — the yo‘q (Oxford University). Cambridge uchun ham xuddi shunday.

Nega the Times (gazeta), lekin Time (jurnal)?

Gazeta nomlari odatda the bilan, jurnal nomlarida esa umumiy qoida yo‘q. Time jurnalining rasmiy nomida the yo‘q. Shuning uchun jurnal nomini alohida yodlang.

the White House the bilan, lekin Buckingham Palace the siz — nega?

White House — tavsifiy nom (“oq uy”), shuning uchun the bor, xuddi the Royal Palace kabi. Buckingham Palace esa joy nomi (Buckingham) + Palace tuzilishida, the siz. Qoida: nom joy yoki odam nomi bilan boshlansa (Buckingham, Windsor) — the yo‘q.

Registonni the Registan ham, Registan Square ham deyishadi. Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham uchraydi. Registan Square — joy nomi + Square tuzilishi, the siz (A qism). The Registan esa mashhur me’moriy majmuaning qisqa nomi sifatida ishlatiladi. Shubha bo‘lsa, Registan Square shaklini tanlang; kerak bo‘lsa Registan Square in Samarkand deb aniqlashtiring.

Yangi nomni uchratganda the kerakmi-yo‘qmi, qanday bilaman?

Quyidagi tartibda o‘ylang: (1) nomda of bormi? → the. (2) nom joy yoki odam ismi + Street / Square / Park / Station / Airport / University / Palace / Castlemi? → the yo‘q. (3) Hotel / theatre / cinema / museum / gallerymi? → the. (4) ’s bilan do‘kon yoki restoran, kompaniya, aviakompaniyami? → the yo‘q. (5) Gazeta yoki tashkilotmi? → the. Shubha qolsa, ingliz tilidagi matnda shu nom qanday yozilganini qidiring.

Nega I’m going to the airport deymiz, lekin Heathrow Airportda the yo‘q?

The airport — suhbatdoshlar biladigan aeroport (oddiy ot, Unit 73). Heathrow Airport — nom, shuning uchun the kerak emas. Ikkalasini aralashtirmang: the Heathrow Airport noto‘g‘ri.

Gazeta nomidagi The katta harfda yoziladimi?

Gazetaning o‘z sarlavhasida The Guardian deb katta harf bilan yoziladi. Gap ichida ko‘pchilik the Guardian deb kichik harfda yozadi. Ikkala yozilish ham uchraydi; asosiysi — the ni tushirib qoldirmaslik.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • The yo‘q: ko‘cha, maydon, park, vokzal, aeroport, universitet, saroy, qal’a — joy yoki odam nomi + tur so‘zi (Oxford Street, Registan Square, Heathrow Airport, Harvard University, Buckingham Palace).
  • The bor: mehmonxona, restoran, pab, teatr, kino, muzey, galereya va mashhur binolar (the Hilton, the Odeon, the British Museum, the Eiffel Tower, the Taj Mahal).
  • Of bor nomlar the bilan: the Bank of England, the University of Oxford; of yo‘q shakl the siz: Oxford University.
  • Odam ismidan olingan ’s / -s li do‘kon va restoran nomlari the siz: Harrods, McDonald’s, St Paul’s.
  • Gazeta va tashkilotlar the bilan: the Guardian, the UN, the BBC; so‘z kabi o‘qiladigan qisqartma va kompaniyalar the siz: NASA, UNESCO, Samsung, Lufthansa.
  • Ko‘priklarda yagona qoida yo‘q: Tower Bridge (the yo‘q), the Golden Gate Bridge (the bor).
  • Yangi nomni uchratsangiz: of → the; Street/Square/Airport/University → the yo‘q; hotel/theatre/museum → the.
