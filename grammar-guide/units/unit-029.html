May and might 1

May va might — 1 (ehtimollik)
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • may va might bilan “balki shundaydir, bo‘lishi mumkin” deyishni (hozirgi holat haqida).
  • Inkor: may not / might not = “balki ... emasdir”.
  • O‘tmish haqida taxmin: may have done / might have done.
  • could ham “bo‘lishi mumkin” ma’nosida kelishini, lekin couldn’t ning might not dan butunlay farq qilishini.

Siz biror narsaga to‘liq ishonmaysiz: u rost bo‘lishi ham, rost bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin — taxminan “50 ga 50”. Ana shunda may yoki might ishlatiladi. Bu Unit 28 dagi must (aniq shunday) va can’t (aniq unday emas) ning o‘rtasidagi joy.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no “balki”, “ehtimol”, “... bo‘lishi mumkin” bilan beriladi: U kutubxonada bo‘lishi mumkin = She may / might be in the library. Balki u kasaldir = She might be ill. Ingliz tilida “balki” uchun alohida so‘z (maybe, perhaps) ham bor, lekin ko‘pincha uning o‘rniga fe’l oldiga may / might qo‘yiladi.

May va might: hozirgi holat haqida taxmin may / might = it is possible that ...

Biror narsa mumkin deb o‘ylasangiz, lekin aniq bilmasangiz, may yoki might ishlatiladi. Odatda ikkalasining ham ma’nosi bir xil — qaysi birini ishlatsangiz ham to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi:

  • “Where’s Dilnoza?” “I’m not sure. She may be in the library.” (= She might be in the library.)— Dilnoza qayerda? — Aniq bilmayman. Kutubxonada bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • “Is that Bekzod over there?” “It might be. I can’t see very well from here.”— Anavi Bekzodmi? — Bo‘lishi mumkin. Bu yerdan yaxshi ko‘rinmayapti.
  • Malika has lived in Bukhara, so she may know a good hotel there.Malika Buxoroda yashagan, shuning uchun u yerdagi yaxshi mehmonxonani bilishi mumkin.
  • Don’t call Aziz now. He might be sleeping — he worked all night.Azizga hozir qo‘ng‘iroq qilmang. Uxlayotgan bo‘lishi mumkin — u tun bo‘yi ishladi.
Ega+may / might+(not)+be / be -ing / fe’l

Inkor: may not / might not

may not / might not = “balki ... emasdir”, “... bo‘lmasligi mumkin”:

  • Sardor might not be at home. Let’s phone him first.Sardor uyda bo‘lmasligi mumkin. Avval unga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaylik.
  • This café may not be open on Mondays.Bu kafe dushanba kunlari ochiq bo‘lmasligi mumkin.
  • Kamola might not want to come with us. She looks very tired.Kamola biz bilan borishni xohlamasligi mumkin. U juda charchagan ko‘rinadi.
May / might + nima? (hozir)
TuzilmaQachon?Misol
may / might (not) + beholat, joy, kim/nima ekaniHe might be ill. / It may not be true.
may / might (not) + be + -ingayni paytda bo‘layotgan ishShe may be working. / They might not be listening.
may / might (not) + fe’lbilish, xohlash, odat va h.k.He might know. / She may not want to come.

Qoida: may / might modal fe’l: undan keyin to qo‘yilmaydi, 3-shaxsda -s qo‘shilmaydi, inkori not bilan: She may be ✅ (She mays be / may to be ❌).

may be yoki maybe?

may be (ikki so‘z)

Modal fe’l + fe’l. Gapda kesim vazifasini bajaradi.

  • She may be busy.
  • U band bo‘lishi mumkin.

maybe (bitta so‘z)

Ravish = perhaps, “balki”. Odatda gap boshida turadi.

  • Maybe she is busy.
  • Balki u banddir.

Ikkala gap ham bir xil ma’noni beradi: Maybe she is busy = She may be busy. Lekin yozganda farqni unutmang: may be — fe’l, maybe — ravish.

O‘tmish haqida taxmin: may have done, might have done may / might have (done)

O‘tgan voqea haqida “balki shunday bo‘lgandir” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, may have + V3 yoki might have + V3 ishlatiladi:

Ega+may / might+(not)+have+V3
  • “I wonder why Kamola didn’t come to the lesson yesterday.” “She might have been ill.”— Kamola kecha nega darsga kelmadi ekan? — Kasal bo‘lgan bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • “The window was open when I got home.” “Mum may have opened it to let in some fresh air.”— Uyga kelganimda deraza ochiq edi. — Oyim toza havo kirsin deb ochgan bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • “I heard a strange noise in the night.” “It might have been the wind.”— Kechasi g‘alati tovush eshitdim. — Shamol bo‘lgan bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • Jasur hasn’t paid me back yet. He might not have had time to go to the bank.Jasur hali qarzini qaytarmadi. Bankka borishga vaqti bo‘lmagan bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • “Why was Bekzod smiling all through the lesson?” “He may have been thinking about his holiday.”— Bekzod dars davomida nega jilmayib o‘tirdi? — Ta’tilini o‘ylab o‘tirgan bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • I’m not sure where I bought this scarf. It might have been at Chorsu bazaar.Bu sharfni qayerdan sotib olganimni aniq eslolmayman. Chorsu bozoridan bo‘lishi mumkin.
O‘tmish haqida taxmin
TuzilmaMisolTarjima
may / might (not) have beenShe might have been at work.U ishda bo‘lgan bo‘lishi mumkin.
may / might (not) have been + -ingHe may have been driving too fast.U (o‘sha paytda) juda tez haydagan bo‘lishi mumkin.
may / might (not) have + V3They might not have heard the news.Ular yangilikni eshitmagan bo‘lishi mumkin.

O‘zbekcha “-gan bo‘lishi mumkin” deyilgan joyda inglizchada deyarli har doim may / might have + V3 keladi: ko‘rgan bo‘lishi mumkin = might have seen; ketgan bo‘lishi mumkin = may have gone. “-gan” → have + V3.

may / might dan keyin o‘tgan zamon shakli (went, was, saw) hech qachon kelmaydi: He might went ❌ → He might have gone ✅. She may was ❌ → She may have been ✅.

Could ham “bo‘lishi mumkin” degani, lekin couldn’t — boshqa narsa could, could have and couldn’t

could ham may / might ga o‘xshab “mumkin” ma’nosida ishlatiladi:

  • Don’t throw that old coin away. It could be valuable. (= It may / might be valuable.)U eski tangani tashlab yubormang. Qimmatbaho bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • The phone’s ringing. It could be Malika.Telefon jiringlayapti. Malika bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • “Who broke the vase?” “It could have been the cat.” (= It might have been the cat.)— Guldonni kim sindirdi? — Mushuk bo‘lishi mumkin.

Lekin inkor shaklda katta farq bor! couldn’t = “imkonsiz” (Unit 28 dagi can’t ga o‘xshaydi). might not / may not esa = “balki ... emasdir” (ya’ni balki shunday, balki unday).

  • I wonder why Nodira hasn’t replied. She might not have read my message yet.Nodira nega javob bermadi ekan? Balki xabarimni hali o‘qimagandir. (O‘qigan bo‘lishi ham mumkin.)
  • Nodira couldn’t have read my message. Her phone was broken all week.Nodira xabarimni o‘qigan bo‘lishi mumkin emas. Uning telefoni butun hafta buzuq edi.
  • Aziz might not have taken the money. Maybe somebody else did.Pulni Aziz olmagan bo‘lishi mumkin. Balki boshqa birov olgandir.
  • Aziz couldn’t have taken the money. He was in Bukhara all week.Pulni Aziz olgan bo‘lishi mumkin emas. U butun hafta Buxoroda edi.
Ijobiy va inkor shakllar: kim nimaga teng?
Ijobiy (bo‘lishi mumkin)Inkor (balki emasdir)Inkor (imkonsiz)
It may / might / could be true.It may not / might not be true.It can’t / couldn’t be true.
He may / might / could have gone.He may not / might not have gone.He can’t / couldn’t have gone.

Qoida: Ijobiy gapda could = may = might. Inkor gapda esa couldn’t ≠ might not: couldn’t “imkonsiz” degani, might not / may not esa “balki yo‘qdir”.

Qo‘shimcha: so‘roq gap, may well va boshqa ma’nolar Questions, may well, might have = criticism

Taxmin haqida so‘rash

“Bo‘lishi mumkinmi?” ma’nosidagi so‘roq gapda may deyarli ishlatilmaydi (May he be ...? g‘alati eshitiladi). Buning o‘rniga:

  • Do you think she’s at home?Sizningcha, u uydami?
  • Could she be at home?U uyda bo‘lishi mumkinmi?
  • Is it possible that he forgot?U unutib qo‘ygan bo‘lishi mumkinmi?

may well / might well = “ehtimoli katta”

  • Ask Sardor about the problem. He may well know the answer.Muammo haqida Sardordan so‘rang. U javobni bilishi ehtimoli katta.

“You might have told me!” — tanbeh

might have done (va could have done) ba’zan taxmin emas, norozilik bildiradi: “qilishingiz kerak edi-ku, nega qilmadingiz?”

  • You might have told me you were coming! I’d have cooked plov.Kelishingizni aytsangiz bo‘lardi-ku! Osh damlab qo‘ygan bo‘lardim.

Bu ma’noni ohangdan (intonatsiyadan) bilib olasiz: might so‘ziga urg‘u beriladi va gap norozi ohangda aytiladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

She mays be at home.She may be at home.Modal fe’llarga 3-shaxsda -s qo‘shilmaydi.
He might to be ill.He might be ill.may / might dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
May be she is at work.Maybe she is at work. / She may be at work.Gap boshidagi “balki” — bitta so‘z: maybe. “May be” — modal + fe’l, egadan keyin keladi.
He may has forgotten.He may have forgotten.Modaldan keyin har doim “have” (has emas).
She might was at the party.She might have been at the party.O‘tmish haqida taxmin: might + have + V3. Was / went kabi shakllar modaldan keyin kelmaydi.
He mayn’t be at home.He may not be at home.“Mayn’t” zamonaviy ingliz tilida deyarli ishlatilmaydi. To‘liq shakl: may not.
I don’t know why she’s late. She couldn’t have read my message.I don’t know why she’s late. She might not have read my message.Aniq bilmasangiz — might not (balki o‘qimagandir). Couldn’t = “o‘qigan bo‘lishi imkonsiz” (aniq bilasiz).
May he be in the office now?Do you think he’s in the office now? / Could he be in the office now?Taxmin haqidagi so‘roqda may ishlatilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

may va might ning farqi bormi?

Ushbu darsdagi ma’noda (hozirgi yoki o‘tgan holat haqida taxmin) farq juda kichik — ikkalasini ham ishlatsa bo‘ladi. Ba’zi odamlar might ni biroz kamroq ishonch bilan aytadi. Ammo ba’zi holatlarda faqat might to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi: real bo‘lmagan vaziyatlarda (If I were rich, I might buy a house in Samarkand) — bu Unit 30 da. Bundan tashqari may ruxsat so‘rashda ham ishlatiladi (May I come in?, Unit 37).

“Maybe” va “may be” ni qanday ajrataman?

O‘rniga perhaps qo‘yib ko‘ring. Agar gap to‘g‘ri bo‘lib qolsa — bu maybe (bitta so‘z): Maybe (= Perhaps) he’s right. Agar gapda kesim yetishmay qolsa — bu may be (ikki so‘z): He may be right → He perhaps right ❌. Demak bu yerda may be kerak.

Nega “couldn’t have” va “might not have” bir xil emas? Ikkalasida ham “not” bor-ku.

Chunki ular turli darajadagi ishonchni bildiradi. couldn’t have = 0% (“bu imkonsiz, men buni aniq bilaman”). might not have = taxminan 50% (“balki shunday bo‘lmagandir, lekin bo‘lgan bo‘lishi ham mumkin”). Diagrammaga qayta qarang: biri “eshikni yopadi”, ikkinchisi ikkala eshikni ochiq qoldiradi.

“might have done” ning boshqa ma’nolari ham bormi?

Ha. 1) Taxmin (shu dars): She might have missed the bus. 2) Norozilik: You might have told me! (Aytsangiz bo‘lardi-ku!) 3) Shart gaplarda “... bo‘lishi mumkin edi”: If you’d asked him, he might have helped you (Unit 40). Ma’noni kontekstdan aniqlang.

“may well” nima degani?

may well / might well = “ehtimoli ancha katta”. It may well rain this afternoon = tushdan keyin yomg‘ir yog‘ishi juda ehtimol. Bu oddiy may dan kuchliroq, lekin must darajasida emas.

“mightn’t” degan qisqa shakl bormi?

Britaniya ingliz tilida mightn’t uchraydi (og‘zaki nutqda), lekin u kam ishlatiladi. mayn’t esa amalda ishlatilmaydi. Eng xavfsiz yo‘l — to‘liq shakl: might not, may not.

may/might ni “maybe” bilan almashtirsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha, ma’nosi deyarli bir xil: She might be ill = Maybe she’s ill = Perhaps she’s ill. O‘tmishda: He may have forgotten = Maybe he forgot / Maybe he has forgotten. Faqat maybe dan keyin modal emas, odatdagi zamon shakli keladi: Maybe he forgot yoki Maybe he has forgotten ✅ (Maybe he have forgotten ❌).

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gaplarni may yoki might bilan qayta yozing (hozirgi holat).

  1. Perhaps Dilnoza is in the library. → Dilnoza
  2. Perhaps he knows the answer. → He
  3. Perhaps they are watching the match. → They
  4. Perhaps she isn’t at home. → She
  5. Perhaps Jasur doesn’t want to come. → Jasur

2-mashq. Endi o‘tmish haqida: may have yoki might have + V3 bilan yozing.

  1. Perhaps he missed the bus. → He
  2. Perhaps she was ill yesterday. → She
  3. Perhaps they didn’t hear the news. → They
  4. Perhaps I left my wallet at the café. → I
  5. Perhaps Aziz was driving too fast. → Aziz

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang: couldn’t have yoki might not have?

  1. I don’t know why Malika is upset. She (couldn’t have / might not have) understood my joke.
  2. Sardor (couldn’t have / might not have) stolen the bike. He was with me all evening.
  3. “Did Kamola see us?” “I’m not sure. She (couldn’t have / might not have) noticed us.”
  4. You (couldn’t have / might not have) seen Bekzod in Tashkent yesterday. He’s been in Moscow for a month.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. She mights be busy. →
  2. May be he is right. →
  3. They might went home. →
  4. He may has lost his phone. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling (may yoki might ishlating).

  1. U (ayol) ishda bo‘lishi mumkin. →
  2. Ular yangilikni eshitmagan bo‘lishi mumkin. →
  3. Ular adashib qolgan bo‘lishi mumkin. →
  4. Aziz hozir uxlayotgan bo‘lishi mumkin. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • may / might + be / be -ing / fe’l = hozir “bo‘lishi mumkin”: She may be at home.
  • Inkor: may not / might not = “balki ... emasdir”.
  • O‘tmish: may / might (not) have + V3: He might have missed the bus.
  • Ijobiy gapda could ham shu ma’noda: It could be true. You could have left it at home.
  • couldn’t = imkonsiz (0%); might not = balki yo‘qdir (≈ 50%).
  • maybe (ravish, bitta so‘z) ≠ may be (modal + fe’l).
