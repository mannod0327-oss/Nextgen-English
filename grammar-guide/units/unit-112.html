Even

Even — hatto
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Even (“hatto”) gapdagi biror narsaning kutilmagan, g‘alati yoki ajablanarli ekanini ta’kidlashini: Even Sardor came to the meeting.
  • Not even va n’t even (“hatto ... ham emas”) eng oson yoki eng kichik narsa ham bo‘lmaganini bildirishini: She didn’t even say goodbye.
  • Even + qiyosiy daraja (“yanada”): It’s cold today, but tomorrow it will be even colder.
  • Even though / even if / even when ning farqini va ulardan keyin qaysi zamon kelishini.
  • Even ning gapdagi o‘rnini: ta’kidlanayotgan so‘z oldida, oddiy fe’l oldida, be va yordamchi fe’ldan keyin.

Even gapning faktini o‘zgartirmaydi — u gapga so‘zlovchining munosabatini qo‘shadi: “Bu odatdagidan tashqari! Men buni kutmagan edim.” Tasavvur qiling: hamma narsa “kutilgan”dan “kutilmagan”gacha cho‘zilgan bir shkalada turibdi. Shkalaning eng chetidagi narsani — eng g‘alati, eng qiyin yoki eng oson narsani — biz even bilan ajratib ko‘rsatamiz.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no hatto so‘zi va ham yuklamasi bilan beriladi: Hatto Sardor ham keldi. Inglizchada esa kerakli so‘z bitta — even. “Ham” ning inglizcha juftini (also / too) qo‘shib yubormang: Even Sardor also came ❌ — Even Sardor came ✅. Yana bir farq: o‘zbekchada hatto so‘zdan oldin, ham esa undan keyin turadi; inglizchada even faqat ta’kidlanayotgan so‘zdan oldin (yoki fe’l oldida) keladi.

Even — kutilmagan, ajablanarli narsa Even: something unusual or surprising

Aziz mahalla yig‘ilishi haqida gapiryapti. Malika yig‘ilishlarga doim keladi, Bekzod ko‘pincha, Jasur ba’zan, Sardor esa hech qachon kelmaydi. Bu safar hamma, jumladan Sardor ham kelgan. Sardor shkalaning eng chetida turibdi — u eng kutilmagan odam. Shuning uchun biz uning oldiga even qo‘yamiz:

  • Everybody came to the neighbourhood meeting — even Sardor.Mahalla yig‘ilishiga hamma keldi — hatto Sardor ham.

Qoida: even = “ham” + “va bu kutilmagan!”. Demak, even faqat g‘alati, ajablanarli yoki ekstremal narsa haqida ishlatiladi. Oddiy qo‘shimcha ma’lumot uchun also / too / and yetarli.

Even ning gapdagi o‘rni Where does even go?

Even ravish (adverb), shuning uchun u o‘zi ta’kidlayotgan so‘zga yaqin turadi. Asosiy qoida oddiy: even ni ta’kidlanayotgan so‘z yoki bo‘lakning oldiga qo‘ying. Agar butun harakat kutilmagan bo‘lsa, even o‘rta o‘rinda (Unit 110) turadi: oddiy fe’ldan oldin, be dan keyin, birinchi yordamchi fe’ldan keyin.

“Even Sardor spoke” va “Sardor even spoke” — ma’no farqi

Even + ega

Ajablanarli — kim qilgani.

  • Even Sardor spoke at the meeting.
  • Hatto Sardor ham yig‘ilishda gapirdi (u odatda jim turadi).

Ega + even + fe’l

Ajablanarli — nima qilgani.

  • Sardor even spoke at the meeting.
  • Sardor yig‘ilishda hatto so‘zga ham chiqdi (kelganidan tashqari, bu ham kutilmagan).

Bir nechta yordamchi fe’l bo‘lsa, even birinchisidan keyin turadi: She has even been sleeping at the office. (Unit 110 dagi o‘rta o‘rin qoidasi.)

Even gap oxirida kelmaydi: Sardor came even. ❌ — Even Sardor came. ✅. Inkorda even yordamchi fe’l bilan n’t dan keyin turadi: She even didn’t call. ❌ — She didn’t even call. ✅. be bilan ham shunday: He even is late. ❌ — He is even late. ✅.

O‘zbek tilida ta’kidlanayotgan so‘zni hatto (oldidan) va ham (keyindan) o‘rab turadi, fe’l esa gap oxirida qoladi: U hatto qo‘ng‘iroq ham qilmadi. Inglizchada ham ning o‘rni yo‘q, even esa ta’kidlanayotgan so‘z yoki fe’l oldida turadi: She didn’t even call. So‘zma-so‘z ko‘chirib She didn’t call even. yoki She even didn’t call. deyish xato.

Not even — hatto ... ham emas Not even / n’t even

Salbiy gapda even shkalani teskari o‘girib qo‘yadi. Biz eng kichik, eng oson, eng oddiy narsani nomlaymiz — va hatto o‘sha ham bo‘lmaganini aytamiz. Agar eng oson narsa ham bo‘lmagan bo‘lsa, undan qiyinlari haqida gapirish ham ortiqcha.

  • Poor Jasur can’t swim. He can’t even float.Bechora Jasur suza olmaydi. U hatto suv yuzida qalqib ham turolmaydi.
  • I’m so tired that I can’t even think.Shu qadar charchadimki, hatto o‘ylay ham olmayapman.
  • It isn’t even eight o’clock, and Aziz is already asleep.Hali soat sakkiz ham bo‘lgani yo‘q, Aziz allaqachon uxlab qoldi.
  • Bekzod didn’t even look at the menu.Bekzod hatto menyuga ham qaramadi.

Even + inkor gapning ikki xil shakli deyarli bir xil ma’no beradi: Even the teacher didn’t know. va Not even the teacher knew. Ikkinchisi odatda biroz kuchliroq eshitiladi. Diqqat: Not even ega bilan kelganda fe’l ijobiy bo‘ladi — inkor ikki marta ishlatilmaydi.

Not even the teacher didn’t know. ❌ (ikkita inkor). To‘g‘risi: Not even the teacher knew. ✅ yoki Even the teacher didn’t know. ✅. Shuningdek can’t even dan keyin to kelmaydi: She can’t even to swim. ❌ — She can’t even swim. ✅.

Shuning uchun gapni inglizchaga o‘girayotganda “-b ham + inkor” ko‘rsangiz, n’t even deb o‘ylang.

Even though, even if, even when Even + conjunctions

Even bog‘lovchilar oldida ham ishlatiladi. Uchala holatda ham ma’no “... bo‘lsa ham”, lekin bog‘lovchi so‘z gapdagi vaziyat qanday ekanini ko‘rsatadi: haqiqiy faktmi, noaniq shartmi yoki takrorlanadigan holatmi.

Even though — bo‘lgan fakt

Even though = although, faqat kuchliroq: kutilmagan faktni ta’kidlaydi. U faqat haqiqatan bo‘lgan yoki bo‘layotgan narsa haqida ishlatiladi (batafsil — Unit 113). Even yolg‘iz o‘zi bog‘lovchi emas: Even he was tired, he worked. ❌ — Even though he was tired, he worked. ✅.

  • Even though she had never cooked plov before, Kamola made it perfectly.Kamola ilgari hech qachon osh pishirmagan bo‘lsa ham, uni a’lo darajada pishirdi.
  • My grandfather still works in the garden every day, even though he is eighty-five.Bobom sakson besh yoshda bo‘lsa ham, har kuni bog‘da ishlaydi.

Even if — shart: bo‘lsa ham, bo‘lmasa ham

Even if “baribir” ma’nosini beradi: shart bajarilsa ham, bajarilmasa ham natija o‘zgarmaydi. Shart noaniq yoki xayoliy bo‘ladi. Zamon if dagidek (Unit 38, Unit 40):

Even when — “... bo‘lganda ham”

Even when odatda sodir bo‘ladigan, takrorlanadigan vaziyatlarni ko‘rsatadi: kutilmagan paytda ham shunday. Zamon when dagidek (Unit 25): kelajak ma’nosida ham present simple.

  • Malika always smiles, even when she is exhausted.Malika charchab o‘lgan paytda ham doim jilmayib turadi.
  • The market stays open even when it snows heavily.Bozor qalin qor yog‘ganda ham ochiq turadi.
  • I’ll remember this day even when I’m an old man.Bu kunni keksa bo‘lib qolganimda ham eslab yuraman.

Tezkor test: (1) Bu hozir yoki o‘tmishda haqiqatan bo‘lgan faktmi? → even though. (2) Noaniq, kelajakdagi yoki xayoliy shartmi? → even if. (3) “Hatto shunday paytda ham” deb takrorlanuvchi holatni aytyapmanmi? → even when (ko‘pincha even if ham to‘g‘ri keladi).

O‘zbek tilida bularning hammasini bitta qo‘shimcha bilan aytamiz: -sa ham. Yomg‘ir yog‘sa ham boraman = I’ll go even if it rains; yomg‘ir yog‘ayotgan bo‘lsa ham bordik = we went even though it was raining. Inglizchada tanlash kerak: haqiqiy fakt → though, noaniq / xayoliy → if. Ikkinchi tuzoq: o‘zbekchada “yog‘sa” da zamon belgisi yo‘q, “yog‘adigan bo‘lsa ham” deyilsa esa kelasi zamon ohangi paydo bo‘ladi — shuning uchun o‘quvchi inglizchada will qo‘yib yuboradi. Inglizchada shart qismida present simple turadi: even if it rains.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Even va also / too ning farqi nimada?

Also / too oddiy qo‘shimcha ma’lumot beradi: “yana bittasi”. Even esa “yana bittasi, va bu kutilmagan” degan munosabatni qo‘shadi. Bekzod came, and Jasur came too. — oddiy fakt. Bekzod came, and even Sardor came. — Sardor kelishi ajablanarli. Ajablanish bo‘lmasa, even ishlatmang.

Even ni gapning qayeriga qo‘yaman?

Qoida: ta’kidlayotgan so‘zingizdan oldin. Ega ajablanarli bo‘lsa, Even + ega. Butun harakat ajablanarli bo‘lsa, o‘rta o‘rinda: oddiy fe’ldan oldin, be dan keyin, yordamchi fe’ldan keyin. Inkorda esa didn’t even, can’t even. Hech qachon gap oxirida emas.

“Even Sardor spoke” va “Sardor even spoke” bir xil ma’noni beradimi?

Yo‘q. Birinchisida ajablanarli narsa — kim gapirgani (Sardor: u odatda jim). Ikkinchisida — nima qilgani (Sardor shunchaki kelmadi, hatto so‘zga ham chiqdi). Shuning uchun even ning o‘rni ma’noni o‘zgartiradi.

Even though va even if o‘rtasidagi farqni qanday eslab qolaman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Bu haqiqatan bo‘lganmi / bo‘layotganmi?” Agar ha bo‘lsa, even though: Even though it was raining, we went out. (Yomg‘ir haqiqatan yog‘ayotgan edi.) Agar noaniq yoki xayoliy bo‘lsa, even if: We’ll play football even if it rains. (Yog‘adimi, yo‘qmi — bilmaymiz, baribir o‘ynaymiz.)

Even if ni o‘tgan zamondagi haqiqiy narsa uchun ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Odatda yo‘q, haqiqiy fakt uchun even though kerak. Lekin so‘zlovchi gapning rost-yolg‘onligiga ishonmasa yoki uni faqat faraz sifatida qabul qilsa, even if ishlatadi: Even if he said that, he didn’t mean it. (U shunday degan bo‘lsa ham — ehtimol deganmi, yo‘qmi — bu uning fikri emas edi.)

Even colder va much colder o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ha. Much colder — farq katta ekanini aytadi. Even colder — narsa allaqachon sovuq edi, va u yana bir pog‘ona sovuqroq bo‘ldi. Shuning uchun even faqat o‘sha xususiyat allaqachon mavjud bo‘lganda ishlatiladi. Still colder ham deyarli shunday ma’no beradi, lekin even colder ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi.

“Not even” bilan “even ... not” bir xilmi?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil: Not even the teacher knew. = Even the teacher didn’t know. Farq shundaki, not even biroz kuchliroq eshitiladi. Tuzilish jihatidan esa ehtiyot bo‘ling: not even dan keyin fe’l ijobiy (knew), even dan keyin esa inkor (didn’t know).

O‘zbekcha “hatto” ni har doim even deb tarjima qilsam bo‘ladimi?

Ko‘pincha ha, ayniqsa “hatto + ot + ham”. Lekin ikki holatga e’tibor bering: (1) qiyosiy darajada o‘zbekcha “yanada sovuqroq” even colder bo‘ladi, “hatto sovuqroq” demaymiz; (2) “-sa ham” ni vaziyatga qarab even if yoki even though deb tarjima qiling.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Even = “hatto”: kutilmagan, ajablanarli yoki ekstremal narsani ta’kidlaydi. Oddiy qo‘shimcha uchun also / too.
  • O‘rni: Even + ega; ega + even + oddiy fe’l; be + even; yordamchi + even + fe’l; inkorda n’t even. Gap oxirida emas.
  • Not even / n’t even eng oson narsa ham bo‘lmaganini bildiradi. Not even + ega bo‘lsa, fe’l ijobiy: Not even Sardor knew.
  • Even + qiyosiy daraja = “yanada”: even colder, even more difficult. Very colder va even more colder ❌.
  • Even though — haqiqiy fakt; even if — noaniq yoki xayoliy shart; even when — takrorlanuvchi holat.
  • Even if va even when dan keyin kelajak uchun present simple (will emas).
  • “Hatto ... ham” ni inglizchaga o‘girganda faqat even yozing (even ... also ❌).
