-ing and -ed clauses (the woman talking to Tom, the boy injured in the accident)

-ing va -ed li bo‘laklar (sifatdosh oborotlar)
Relative clauses · Nisbiy ergash gaplar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Otdan keyin keladigan -ing va -ed (past participle) bo‘laklarini: the girl dancing with Bekzod, the goalkeeper injured in the match.
  • Bunday bo‘lak nisbiy gap (who / which / that + be ...) o‘rnini qanday bosishini.
  • Qachon -ing (ot ishni o‘zi bajaradi), qachon -ed (otga nisbatan ish bajariladi) ishlatilishini.
  • Qaysi hollarda bu qisqa shaklni ishlatib bo‘lmasligini (masalan, bir marta bo‘lib tugagan ish haqida).

Biror ot haqida “qaysi?” yoki “qanday?” degan savolga javob berish uchun ikki yo‘l bor. Birinchisi — to‘liq nisbiy gap (Unit 92): the girl who is dancing with Bekzod. Ikkinchisi — who is so‘zlarini tushirib qoldirib, qisqa bo‘lak qoldirish: the girl dancing with Bekzod. Ikkalasining ma’nosi bir xil, lekin qisqa shakl ixchamroq va ko‘pincha tabiiyroq eshitiladi.

O‘zbek tilida bunday ma’noni sifatdosh beradi va u otdan oldin turadi: [Bekzod bilan raqsga tushayotgan] qiz, [o‘yinda jarohat olgan] darvozabon, [Margilonda to‘qilgan] atlas. Ingliz tilida esa xuddi shu bo‘lak otdan keyin keladi: the girl dancing with Bekzod. Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato — o‘zbekcha tartibni ko‘chirib, the dancing with Bekzod girl deyish. Bunday tartib ingliz tilida umuman bo‘lmaydi!

-ing va -ed li bo‘laklar nima? What are -ing and -ed clauses?

Clause (gap bo‘lagi) — o‘z fe’li bor so‘zlar guruhi. Quyidagi gaplarda otdan keyin fe’lning -ing yoki -ed shakli bilan boshlanuvchi bo‘lak turibdi. U shu otni aniqlaydi: “qaysi qiz?” — Bekzod bilan raqsga tushayotgan; “qaysi darvozabon?” — o‘yinda jarohat olgan.

  • Do you know the girl dancing with Bekzod? (= the girl who is dancing with Bekzod)Bekzod bilan raqsga tushayotgan qizni taniysizmi?
  • The goalkeeper injured in the match will miss the next game. (= the goalkeeper who was injured in the match)O‘yinda jarohat olgan darvozabon keyingi o‘yinni o‘tkazib yuboradi.

Qoida: -ing yoki -ed bo‘lagi aniqlayotgan otdan darhol keyin turadi: the girl dancing with Bekzod. Bo‘lak ichida ega yo‘q — uning egasi o‘sha ot (the girl is dancing, the goalkeeper was injured).

-ing → ot ishni o‘zi bajaradi (faol)

  • the people using the library (ular kutubxonadan foydalanadi)
  • a girl carrying a heavy bag

-ed → otga nisbatan ish bajariladi (majhul)

  • the books used in class (kitoblardan foydalaniladi)
  • a bag carried by a girl

Tekshirish usuli: bo‘lak oldiga who is / which was kabi so‘zlarni qo‘yib ko‘ring. Ma’no saqlansa, bo‘lak to‘g‘ri: the people (who are) using the library, the books (which are) used in class. Shu usul bilan siz ot ishni o‘zi qilayotganini (-ing) yoki unga ish qilinayotganini (-ed) aniq ajrata olasiz.

Tugagan, bir martalik ish uchun -ing ishlatilmaydi. Telefon qilish — o‘tmishda bir marta bo‘lib tugagan ish: The man who phoned you last night is my uncle. ✅ — The man phoning you last night is my uncle. ❌

There is / There was bilan

There is / there was dan keyin ot va -ing bo‘lagi juda ko‘p ishlatiladi. Bunda ham “biri nima qilib turibdi” degan ma’no bor. Xuddi shunday, there is / was dan keyin -ed bo‘lagi ham keladi (oxirgi misol):

  • There were three girls sitting on the bench.Skameykada uchta qiz o‘tirgan edi.
  • There’s a strange smell coming from the fridge.Muzlatgichdan g‘alati hid kelyapti.
  • There was a white Nexia parked outside our gate all night.Kechasi bilan darvozamiz oldida oq Nexia to‘xtatib qo‘yilgan edi.

O‘zbek tilidagi -ayotgan (gaplashayotgan, o‘ynayotgan) va -adigan / -uvchi (ketadigan, yuruvchi) sifatdoshlar ingliz tilida bitta shakl — -ing bilan beriladi. Masalan, buses going to the airport — “aeroportga ketadigan avtobuslar”, the man standing at the gate — “darvoza oldida turgan odam”.

-ed bo‘laklari: majhul ma’no -ed clauses (passive meaning)

-ed bo‘lagi — past participle (V3) bilan boshlanadi va majhul ma’noni beradi: ot ishni o‘zi bajarmaydi, unga nisbatan ish bajariladi. Bu who / which + was / were / is / are + V3 (Unit 42) ning qisqa shakli.

Past participle to‘g‘ri fe’llarda -ed bilan tugaydi (damaged, prepared, cooked), noto‘g‘ri fe’llarda esa alohida shaklga ega: take → taken, send → sent, write → written, steal → stolen, build → built, make → made, break → broken, hurt → hurt. Shuning uchun bu bo‘lakni “-ed bo‘lagi” deymiz, lekin taken, written, built kabi shakllar ham shu guruhga kiradi.

Left — “qolgan, ishlatilmagan”

Left (leave fe’lining V3 shakli) ham otdan keyin shu tarzda ishlatiladi va “hali ishlatilmay qolgan, bor” degan ma’noni beradi:

  • We’ve eaten almost all the samsas. There are only two left.Somsalarning deyarli hammasini yeb qo‘ydik. Faqat ikkitasi qoldi.
  • Is there any tea left in the pot?Choynakda choy qoldimi?

Called va named; being + V3

Ismi yoki nomini aytishda called / named ishlatiladi (“... deb ataladigan”, “... nomli”):

  • My cousin lives in a town called Zomin.Amakivachcham Zomin degan shaharchada yashaydi.
  • I’ve just spoken to a girl named Kamola.Hozirgina Kamola ismli qiz bilan gaplashdim.

Ish hozir bajarilayotgan bo‘lsa (who is being + V3), bo‘lak being + V3 bo‘ladi (Unit 43):

  • The man being questioned by the police is a suspect. (= the man who is being questioned)Politsiya so‘roq qilayotgan odam gumonlanuvchi.
  • The road being repaired is closed today.Ta’mirlanayotgan yo‘l bugun yopiq.

-ed bo‘lagini o‘tgan zamon fe’li (past simple) bilan adashtirmang. The man injured in the accident — “jarohat olgan” (majhul bo‘lak). Lekin The man who injured the cyclist — “velosipedchini jarohatlagan” (faol): bu yerda who ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi, chunki the man injured the cyclist to‘liq gap bo‘lib qoladi.

Qachon qisqartirib bo‘ladi, qachon yo‘q? Choosing between -ing / -ed, and when you cannot use them

Bo‘lakni tanlashda ikki savol yetarli:

  1. Ot ishni o‘zi qiladimi? Ha → -ing (the people using the library).
  2. Otga nisbatan ish bajariladimi? Ha → -ed (the books used by the students).

Qoida: Nisbiy gapdagi who / which / that gapning egasi bo‘lsa va fe’l is / was + -ing yoki is / was + V3 bo‘lsa, who/which + be ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin. Fe’l doimiy holatni bildirsa (which runs), uni -ing shakliga aylantiramiz (running). Boshqa hollarda to‘liq nisbiy gap qoldiring.

Vergul bilan: qo‘shimcha ma’lumot

Bo‘lak otni aniqlash uchun zarur bo‘lsa, vergul qo‘yilmaydi (the girl dancing with Bekzod — qaysi qiz). Agar shunchaki qo‘shimcha ma’lumot bo‘lsa, vergul bilan ajratiladi (Unit 95, 96):

  • Dilnoza, sitting at the back of the class, couldn’t hear the teacher.Dilnoza sinfning orqasida o‘tirib, o‘qituvchining gapini eshita olmadi.
  • The old bridge, damaged by the flood, is closed to traffic.Toshqinda shikastlangan eski ko‘prik transport uchun yopiq.

Bir so‘zli sifatdoshlar va Unit 68 bilan farqi Single participles; the difference from Unit 68

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

-ing va -ed ni qanday tanlayman? Ikkalasi menga bir xil ko‘rinadi.

Faqat bitta savolga javob bering: otning o‘zi shu ishni qilyaptimi, yoki unga shu ish qilinganmi? The boy kicking the ball — bola to‘pni tepyapti (o‘zi qiladi) → -ing. The ball kicked over the fence — to‘pni tepishgan (unga nisbatan qilingan) → -ed. Shubhangiz bo‘lsa, nisbiy gap bilan sinab ko‘ring: the boy who is kicking / the ball which was kicked.

Bu bo‘laklar o‘rniga har doim to‘liq nisbiy gap ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha, to‘liq nisbiy gap (the girl who is dancing with Bekzod) har doim to‘g‘ri. Qisqa shakl faqat ixchamlik va tabiiylik uchun. Bir so‘z bilan aytganda: qisqartirish — ixtiyoriy, lekin ayrim hollarda (masalan, o‘tgan zamondagi bir martalik ishda) to‘liq shakl majburiy.

“The man I saw yesterday” ham qisqa shaklmi? Nega uni -ing / -ed bilan qisqartirmaymiz?

Bu boshqa qoida: bu yerda who / that gapning to‘ldiruvchisi, gapning o‘z egasi (I) bor, shuning uchun that ni shunchaki tushirib qoldiramiz (Unit 93). -ing / -ed bo‘lagi esa faqat who / which / that gapning egasi bo‘lganda mumkin. Taqqoslang: the man who is waiting outside → the man waiting outside ✅ (who — ega); lekin the man I met yesterday — shundayligicha qoladi, chunki “I” o‘z egasi bor.

-ed bo‘lagi har doim majhul ma’nodami?

Otdan keyin kelgan -ed (V3) bo‘lagi odatda majhul ma’noni beradi: the letter sent by my aunt = xat yuborilgan (xolam yuborgan). Agar “ot o‘zi qilgan ish” ma’nosi kerak bo‘lsa, -ing yoki to‘liq nisbiy gap ishlatiladi: the aunt sending the letter / the aunt who sent the letter.

“A sleeping baby” va “a baby sleeping” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham ba’zan to‘g‘ri, lekin tabiiy tartib boshqa. Bitta so‘z bo‘lsa, odatda otdan oldin: a sleeping baby. Bo‘lak bir necha so‘zdan iborat bo‘lsa, otdan keyin: the baby sleeping in the next room. “a baby sleeping” (bitta so‘z, otdan keyin) odatda ishlatilmaydi.

Bunday bo‘laklarni so‘zlashuv tilida ishlatadilarmi, yoki faqat yozma tilda?

Ikkalasida ham. Ayniqsa -ing bo‘laklari so‘zlashuvda juda keng tarqalgan: Who’s that man standing over there? -ed bo‘laklari esa yangiliklar, e’lonlar va hisobotlarda ko‘p uchraydi: Most of the people injured in the crash .... Kundalik gapda ko‘pincha to‘liq nisbiy gap ham eshitiladi.

“The girl playing volleyball” va Unit 68 dagi “hurt her knee playing volleyball” — bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. Bu yerda -ing so‘zi otdan keyin turib, shu otni aniqlaydi (qaysi qiz?). Unit 68 dagi -ing bo‘lagi esa harakatni ta’riflaydi (qachon / qanday?): He hurt his knee playing football. Gapdagi bo‘lakning joyi va vazifasiga qarab farqlang.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • -ing yoki -ed bo‘lagi nisbiy gapning (who / which / that + be ...) qisqa shakli bo‘lib, aniqlayotgan otdan keyin turadi: the girl dancing with Bekzod.
  • -ing — faol ma’no: ot ishni o‘zi bajaradi (hozir yoki doimiy): buses going to the airport.
  • -ed (V3) — majhul ma’no: otga nisbatan ish bajariladi: the photos taken at the wedding. Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarda V3 shaklini ishlating (made, taken, written).
  • Tugagan, bir martalik ishda -ing ishlatilmaydi: the man who phoned you last night.
  • Bo‘lak ichida o‘z egasi bo‘lsa (the pictures I took), -ing / -ed ga o‘tkazib bo‘lmaydi.
  • Called / named: a town called Zomin. Hozir bajarilayotgan majhul ish: being + V3 (the road being repaired).
  • O‘zbekchada sifatdosh ot oldida, inglizchada bo‘lak otdan keyin — tartibni ko‘chirmang.
