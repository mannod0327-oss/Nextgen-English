To ... (afraid to do) and preposition + -ing (afraid of -ing)

afraid to do va afraid of doing
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • afraid to do (qilishga yuragi betlamaydi) va afraid of doing (biror yomon narsa bo‘lib qolishidan qo‘rqadi) farqini.
  • interested in doing (qilishni xohlaydi) va interested to hear / know (eshitib qiziq bo‘ldi) farqini.
  • sorry to do va sorry for / about doing — qachon qaysi biri ishlatilishini.
  • want to / think of -ing, fail to / succeed in -ing, allow to / prevent from -ing kabi juftliklarni.

Oldingi darslarda ko‘rdik: sifatdan keyin to + fe’l (Unit 65), predlogdan keyin esa -ing keladi (Unit 60). Ba’zi sifatlar ikkala tuzilma bilan ham ishlatiladi — lekin ma’no o‘zgaradi. Bu darsda aynan shu juftliklarni o‘rganamiz.

O‘zbek tilida ikkala ma’no ham ko‘pincha bitta fe’l bilan beriladi: qo‘rqmoq. Shuning uchun farqni ma’nodan ajrating: “...ga yuragim betlamadi / jur’at qilolmadim” → afraid to; “...ib qolamanmi deb qo‘rqdim” → afraid of -ing. Masalan: Daryoda suzishga yuragim betlamadi = I was afraid to swim in the river. Cho‘kib ketamanmi deb qo‘rqdim = I was afraid of drowning.

afraid to (do) va afraid of (doing) I’m afraid to do it / I’m afraid of something happening

afraid to + fe’l

Bu ishni qilishni xohlamayman, chunki u xavfli yoki oqibati yomon bo‘lishi mumkin. Ish — ataylab qilinadigan harakat, lekin men unga jur’at qilolmayman.

  • afraid to ask, afraid to go, afraid to tell, afraid to say

afraid of + -ing

Biror yomon narsa sodir bo‘lishi mumkin va men shundan qo‘rqaman. Bu narsa tasodifan, o‘z-o‘zidan yuz beradi — men uni ataylab qilmayman.

  • afraid of falling, of losing, of getting lost, of being bitten

afraid to + fe’l

  • The old bridge over the river looks unsafe. Many drivers are afraid to use it.Daryo ustidagi eski ko‘prik xavfli ko‘rinadi. Ko‘p haydovchilar undan o‘tishga qo‘rqadi.
  • Jasur scratched his dad’s car. He was afraid to tell him.Jasur dadasining mashinasini tirnab qo‘ydi. Unga aytishga qo‘rqdi (yuragi betlamadi).
  • Don’t be afraid to ask questions in class.Darsda savol berishdan tortinmang.

afraid of + -ing

  • It was raining hard, so Aziz drove very slowly. He was afraid of having an accident.Qattiq yomg‘ir yog‘ayotgan edi, shuning uchun Aziz juda sekin haydadi. U avariyaga uchrab qolishdan qo‘rqdi.
  • I save my work every ten minutes. I’m afraid of losing it.Ishimni har o‘n daqiqada saqlab qo‘yaman. Uni yo‘qotib qo‘yishdan qo‘rqaman.
  • I never go near the beehives. I’m afraid of being stung.Asalari uyalari yoniga hech qachon bormayman. Chaqib olishidan qo‘rqaman.
  • Let’s leave now. I’m afraid of missing the last bus.Hozir ketaylik. Oxirgi avtobusga ulgurmay qolishdan qo‘rqyapman.

Ko‘pincha ikkalasi bitta gapda keladi: odam biror ishni qilishga qo‘rqadi (afraid to), chunki uning natijasida yomon narsa bo‘lishidan qo‘rqadi (afraid of -ing):

Qoida: Agar qo‘rqinchli narsa o‘z-o‘zidan, tasodifan yuz beradigan bo‘lsa (yiqilish, adashib qolish, kasal bo‘lish, kechikish, tishlanish), afraid of + -ing ishlatiladi: We were afraid of falling ✅ (afraid to fall deb aytmang).

Ataylab qilinadigan ish haqida ba’zan afraid of -ing ham eshitiladi: She’s afraid of going out alone at night (= afraid to go out alone). Lekin “jur’at qilolmaslik” ma’nosida eng tipik shakl — afraid to. scared va frightened ham xuddi shunday ishlatiladi: scared to ask / scared of falling.

interested in (doing) va interested to (do) interested in joining / interested to hear

interested in + -ing = buni qilishni o‘ylayapman, xohlayman

  • If you’re interested in learning Korean, there’s a free course at the library.Koreys tilini o‘rganmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, kutubxonada bepul kurs bor.
  • Aziz is interested in studying abroad.Aziz chet elda o‘qishni xohlaydi (bu haqda o‘ylayapti).
  • Bekzod offered his old laptop to his classmates, but nobody was interested in taking it.Bekzod eski noutbukini sinfdoshlariga taklif qildi, lekin hech kim uni olishni xohlamadi.

interested to + hear / see / know / read / learn / find out

Bu shakl asosan hear, see, know, read, learn, find out bilan ishlatiladi va “eshitib / bilib qiziq bo‘ldi (yoki bo‘lardi)” degan ma’noni beradi:

  • I was interested to hear that Sardor has moved to Tashkent.Sardor Toshkentga ko‘chib o‘tganini eshitib, qiziq bo‘ldi.
  • I’d be interested to see the photos from your trip to Khiva.Xivaga qilgan safaringiz suratlarini ko‘rish menga qiziq bo‘lardi.
  • You’ll be interested to read this article about Bukhara.Buxoro haqidagi bu maqolani o‘qish sizga qiziq bo‘ladi.

I’d be interested to know o‘rniga I’d be interested in knowing ham deyish mumkin — ma’no bir xil. Lekin I was interested to hear ... (eshitdim va qiziq bo‘ldi) o‘rniga interested in hearing deyilmaydi.

interested in -ing → ...ishni xohlamoq, ...ishga qiziqmoq (kelajakdagi reja). interested to hear / learn → eshitib / bilib qiziq bo‘ldi (biror yangilikka munosabat). Ikkinchisi Unit 65 dagi surprised to hear, pleased to learn kabi tuzilma.

sorry to (do) va sorry for / about (doing) sorry to hear / sorry for breaking

1) sorry to + fe’l — hozir bo‘layotgan yoki eshitgan narsaga achinish

Biror narsa bizni xafa qilganda (ko‘pincha hear, see, learn, leave bilan):

  • I’m sorry to hear that you’ve been ill.Kasal bo‘lganingizni eshitib, xafa bo‘ldim.
  • The summer camp in Chimgan has been great. The children will be sorry to leave.Chimyondagi yozgi oromgoh ajoyib bo‘ldi. Bolalarga bu yerdan ketish og‘ir bo‘ladi.
  • We were sorry to see the old café close.Eski kafe yopilganini ko‘rib, achindik.

Shuningdek, ayni paytda qilayotgan ishimiz uchun uzr so‘raganda:

  • I’m sorry to call you so early, but the meeting has been moved to nine.Erta qo‘ng‘iroq qilayotganim uchun uzr, lekin majlis soat to‘qqizga ko‘chirildi.
  • Sorry to bother you, but could you help me with this form?Bezovta qilayotganim uchun uzr, shu anketani to‘ldirishga yordam bera olasizmi?
  • Sorry to interrupt, but your taxi is here.Gapingizni bo‘lganim uchun uzr, taksingiz keldi.

2) sorry for / about + -ing — avval qilgan ishimiz uchun uzr

  • I’m sorry for breaking your cup yesterday. I’ll buy you a new one.Kecha piyolangizni sindirib qo‘yganim uchun uzr. Sizga yangisini olib beraman.
  • Dilnoza said she was sorry about forgetting my birthday.Dilnoza tug‘ilgan kunimni unutgani uchun uzr so‘radi.

Ko‘pincha -ing o‘rniga oddiy gap ishlatiladi — bu og‘zaki nutqda eng tabiiy:

  • I’m sorry (that) I broke your cup yesterday.Kecha piyolangizni sindirib qo‘yganim uchun uzr.
  • “Sorry I’m late.” “That’s OK. We haven’t started yet.”— Kechikkanim uchun uzr. — Hechqisi yo‘q, hali boshlamadik.

O‘zbekchada “uzr” ikkala holatda ham bir xil. Inglizchada esa vaqtga qarang: hozir qilayotgan ishingiz yoki eshitgan xabaringiz — sorry to ...; kecha, avval qilgan ishingiz — sorry for / about -ing yoki sorry (that) I .... I’m sorry to break your cup yesterday ❌ — chunki “yesterday” o‘tgan ish!

Fe’l + to ... va fe’l + predlog + -ing want to / think of -ing, fail to / succeed in -ing ...

Ba’zi so‘zlar o‘xshash ma’noga ega bo‘lsa-da, biri to + fe’l, ikkinchisi esa predlog + -ing bilan ishlatiladi. Ularni juftlik qilib yodlash qulay:

to + fe’l ↔ predlog + -ing

I used to live alone. (avval)I’m used to living alone. (ko‘nikkanman)avval yolg‘iz yashardim / yolg‘iz yashashga ko‘nikkanman
  • Nodira dreams of becoming a famous singer.Nodira mashhur qo‘shiqchi bo‘lishni orzu qiladi.
  • Did you succeed in getting tickets for the concert?Konsertga chipta olishga muvaffaq bo‘ldingmi?
  • The heavy snow prevented the plane from landing.Kuchli qor samolyotning qo‘nishiga to‘sqinlik qildi.

Diqqat: to ham predlog bo‘lishi mumkin! look forward to, be used to, object to dagi to — predlog, shuning uchun undan keyin -ing keladi: I look forward to seeing you ✅ (to see you ❌). Tekshirish: to dan keyin otni qo‘yib ko‘ring. I look forward to the holidays — mumkin, demak bu predlog → -ing. I want to the holidays — mumkin emas, demak bu infinitive → to + fe’l.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

The floor was wet. We were afraid to slip.The floor was wet. We were afraid of slipping.Sirpanib ketish — tasodifiy narsa, ataylab qilinmaydi. Bunday hollarda afraid of + -ing.
I’m afraid of to go there alone.I’m afraid to go there alone.Predlog (of) dan keyin “to + fe’l” kelmaydi: yo afraid to go, yo afraid of going.
I’m interested to learn Japanese next year.I’m interested in learning Japanese next year.“Qilishni xohlayman” ma’nosida — interested in + -ing.
I’m sorry to break your cup yesterday.I’m sorry for breaking your cup yesterday. / I’m sorry I broke your cup yesterday.Avval qilingan ish uchun uzr — sorry for/about + -ing yoki sorry (that) + gap.
I was sorry for hearing that your father is ill.I was sorry to hear that your father is ill.Yomon xabarga munosabat — sorry to hear.
He succeeded to pass the exam.He succeeded in passing the exam.succeed in + -ing (fail esa to bilan: failed to pass).
The rain prevented us to play football.The rain prevented us from playing football.prevent + kishi + from + -ing.
I’m thinking to buy a new laptop.I’m thinking of (about) buying a new laptop.think of / about + -ing; “think to” bu ma’noda ishlatilmaydi.
I look forward to see you soon.I look forward to seeing you soon.look forward to dagi “to” — predlog, undan keyin -ing.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’m afraid I can’t come” — bu ham qo‘rquvmi?

Yo‘q! I’m afraid (that) ... — bu xushmuomala “afsuski, ...” degan ibora: I’m afraid I can’t come to your party. (Afsuski, ziyofatingizga bora olmayman.) “Is there any bread left?” “I’m afraid not.” (Afsuski, yo‘q.) Bu yerda hech qanday qo‘rquv yo‘q — faqat yomon xabarni yumshoq aytish.

“afraid of going out” deyish xato emasmi? Darslikda “afraid to go out” deyilgan.

Xato emas. Ataylab qilinadigan ish uchun ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi: She’s afraid to go out / afraid of going out alone at night. Muhim chegara boshqa joyda: tasodifan yuz beradigan narsalar (yiqilish, yo‘qotish, adashish, kasal bo‘lish) uchun faqat afraid of -ing to‘g‘ri. afraid to fall, afraid to get lost deyilmaydi.

afraid of dan keyin ot ham kelishi mumkinmi?

Albatta: afraid of dogs, afraid of the dark, afraid of heights, afraid of my boss. Ot o‘rniga harakat kelsa — -ing: afraid of flying (uchishdan qo‘rqadi).

“Sorry to keep you waiting” va “Sorry for keeping you waiting” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi. Sorry to keep you waiting — odatda kutish payti (hali kutib turibdi); Sorry for keeping you waiting — ko‘pincha kutish tugaganda, kelganingizda aytiladi. Asosiy qoida esda tursin: hozirgi ish — sorry to, avvalgi ish — sorry for -ing.

sorry for va sorry about — farqi bormi?

Uzr so‘rash ma’nosida deyarli farq yo‘q: sorry for / about being late. Ot bilan ikkalasi ham uchraydi: Sorry for the delay. Sorry about the mess. Lekin feel sorry for + kishi (kimgadir achinmoq) iborasida faqat for: I feel sorry for him.

“to” predlogmi yoki infinitive belgisimi — qanday bilaman?

to dan keyin ot qo‘yib ko‘ring. Agar ot qo‘ysa bo‘lsa (look forward to the trip, be used to the cold, object to the plan), bu predlog va fe’l -ing shaklida keladi: look forward to going. Agar ot qo‘yib bo‘lmasa (want to the trip), bu infinitive: want to go.

“I’m sorry to have kept you waiting” — bu qanday shakl?

Bu to have + V3 (perfect infinitive). U “avval qilgan ishim uchun” degan ma’noni beradi va biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi: I’m sorry to have bothered you. = I’m sorry I bothered you. Kundalik nutqda I’m sorry I ... ko‘proq ishlatiladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gapni afraid of + -ing bilan to‘ldiring (yomon narsa tasodifan sodir bo‘lishi mumkin).

  1. Malika walked very slowly across the wet floor. She was afraid (slip).
  2. Bekzod never swims far from the shore. He’s afraid (drown).
  3. I never stand near the goal when Sardor shoots. I’m afraid (hit) by the ball.
  4. Let’s leave now. I’m afraid (get) stuck in traffic.
  5. Speak quietly. I’m afraid (wake) the baby.

2-mashq. interested in + -ing yoki interested to + fe’l?

  1. Are you interested (take) part in the maths competition?
  2. I was interested (hear) that Kamola got the job.
  3. Bekzod is interested (open) his own café one day.
  4. I’d be interested (know) how much the tickets cost.
  5. Sardor is interested (study) medicine at university.

3-mashq. sorry to + fe’l yoki sorry for + -ing?

  1. I was sorry (hear) that your team lost the final.
  2. I’m sorry (be) rude to you yesterday.
  3. We’ll be sorry (say) goodbye to our teacher at the end of the year.
  4. Kamola was sorry (learn) that the museum was closed.
  5. I’m sorry (forget) your birthday last week.

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. Did you succeed (to find / in finding) a flat?
  2. Jasur failed (to pass / in passing) his driving test.
  3. The noise from the street prevented me (to sleep / from sleeping).
  4. I’m thinking (to buy / of buying) a new laptop.
  5. My parents allowed me (to go / from going) to the concert.
  6. I’m looking forward (to see / to seeing) you again.

5-mashq. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling.

  1. Men adashib qolishdan qo‘rqaman. →
  2. Bu xabarni eshitib xafa bo‘ldim. →
  3. Kecha kechikkanim uchun uzr. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • afraid to do — qilishga jur’at qilolmaslik; afraid of doing — yomon narsa (yiqilish, yo‘qotish) bo‘lib qolishidan qo‘rqish. Tasodifiy narsalar uchun faqat afraid of -ing.
  • interested in -ing = qilishni xohlamoq; interested to hear / know / learn = eshitib / bilib qiziq bo‘lmoq.
  • sorry to hear / to leave / to bother you — xabar yoki hozirgi ish; sorry for / about -ing yoki sorry (that) I ... — avvalgi ish uchun uzr.
  • Juftliklar: want to / think of -ing, fail to / succeed in -ing, allow to / prevent from -ing, encourage to / discourage from -ing.
  • look forward to, be used to dagi to — predlog → -ing.
