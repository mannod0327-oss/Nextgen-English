Verb + preposition 1: to and at

Fe’l + predlog — 1: to va at
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • to bilan keladigan fe’llar: talk to, listen to, write to, apologise to, explain … to, describe … to, reply to, happen to, invite … to.
  • at bilan keladigan fe’llar: look at, stare at, glance at, laugh at, smile at, aim at, point at.
  • shout at va shout to, throw … at va throw … to orasidagi muhim farq.
  • Predlog kerak bo‘lmaydigan fe’llar: phone, call, answer, discuss, enter — o‘quvchilar ularga ko‘pincha ortiqcha predlog qo‘shadi.
  • Nega ❌ explain me deyilmasligini va uni qanday to‘g‘ri aytishni.

Ba’zi fe’llar o‘zidan keyin predlog talab qiladi: listen to music, look at the picture. Boshqa fe’llar esa predlogsiz ishlatiladi: phone Aziz, discuss the plan. Qaysi fe’l qaysi guruhga kirishini o‘zbekchadan taxmin qilib bo‘lmaydi — fe’lni predlogi bilan birga yodlash kerak.

O‘zbek tilida “kimni?” va “kimga?” kelishiklari bor (-ni, -ga), inglizchada esa ularning o‘rniga ba’zan predlog, ba’zan hech narsa keladi. Shuning uchun o‘zbekcha gap tuzilishi aldaydi:

talk / speak to — gaplashmoq

  • I need to talk to you about tomorrow’s plan.Men siz bilan ertangi reja haqida gaplashishim kerak.
  • Why don’t you speak to your neighbour about the noise?Nega shovqin haqida qo‘shningiz bilan gaplashmaysiz?

Britaniya ingliz tilida odatda talk to / speak to aytiladi. Amerika ingliz tilida talk with / speak with ham keng tarqalgan: I talked with my advisor. O‘zbekchada “kim bilan gaplashmoq” deymiz, shuning uchun talk with aytish tabiiy tuyuladi — bu Amerika tilida to‘g‘ri, Britaniya tilida esa to yaxshiroq.

write to — yozmoq

  • Sardor writes to his grandmother every month.Sardor buvisiga har oy xat yozadi.
  • I’ve written to the university, but they haven’t answered yet.Universitetga xat yozdim, lekin ular hali javob berishmadi.

Amerika ingliz tilida to ni tushirish mumkin: I’ll write you next week. Britaniya tilida odatda write to you deyiladi (ikki to‘ldiruvchili write me a letter tuzilmasi esa ikkala tilda ham bor).

apologise, explain, describe — to + shaxs

❌ Xato

  • Explain me the rule.
  • He described us his trip.
  • She apologised the teacher.

✅ To‘g‘ri

  • Explain the rule to me.
  • He described his trip to us.
  • She apologised to the teacher (for being late).
  • The guide explained the history of the Registan to the tourists.Gid Registonning tarixini turistlarga tushuntirdi.
  • Please explain to me how this machine works.Iltimos, bu mashina qanday ishlashini menga tushuntiring.
  • Dilnoza apologised to her mother for forgetting the shopping.Dilnoza xaridni unutgani uchun onasidan uzr so‘radi.

Odatiy ketma-ketlik: explain + nima + to + kim (explain the rule to me). Agar explain dan keyin how / why / what bilan boshlanadigan gap kelsa, shaxs odatda oldinroq aytiladi: Explain to me how it works.

Qaysi fe’llarda “to” ni tushirib bo‘ladi?
Fe’lIkkala tuzilma ham mumkinFaqat to + shaxs
give, send, show, lend, offer, passShe sent me a message. = She sent a message to me.—
explain, describe, say, suggest, announce—He described the hotel to us. ✅ — He described us the hotel. ❌

say bilan ham shunday: She said something to me ✅. Shaxsni “to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri” aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, tell ishlating: She told me a story (Unit 48).

reply / respond / happen / invite

  • The bank has not yet responded to my complaint.Bank shikoyatimga hali javob bergani yo‘q.
  • I’ll reply to your message tonight.Xabaringizga bugun kechqurun javob yozaman.
  • Nobody knows what happened to the old teahouse by the canal.Ariq bo‘yidagi eski choyxonaga nima bo‘lganini hech kim bilmaydi.
  • Nodira has been invited to a conference in Istanbul.Nodira Istanbuldagi konferensiyaga taklif qilingan.

happen to — “kimgadir/nimagadir sodir bo‘lmoq”. Joy yoki payt aytilsa, happen in / at ishlatiladi: The accident happened at the crossroads. Bu fe’lning boshqa ma’nosi (happen to + fe’l, “tasodifan”) Unit 136 da qayta eslatiladi.

O‘zbekchada “-ga qaramoq”, “-ga kulmoq”, “-ga tikilmoq” ham “-ga” bilan aytiladi, shu sababli look me yoki smile me deb, at ni unutish mumkin. Eslab qoling: qaratilgan nishon bor — at bor. Eng ishonchli yo‘l — fe’lni at bilan birga yodlash: look-at, smile-at, laugh-at.

At yoki to? shout va throw shout at / shout to; throw at / throw to

Ba’zi fe’llar at bilan ham, to bilan ham keladi, lekin ma’no butunlay boshqacha bo‘ladi:

shout AT sb

Jahl bilan, g‘azab bilan baqirmoq. Odam yaqin turishi mumkin.

  • The teacher shouted at the noisy boys.
  • Don’t shout at me!

shout TO sb

Eshitsin deb, uzoqdan baqirib aytmoq. G‘azab yo‘q.

  • I shouted to Aziz across the river.
  • She shouted to us that dinner was ready.

throw sth AT sb

Tegsin deb, urish maqsadida otmoq.

  • Some boys threw snowballs at the parked cars.
  • Don’t throw stones at the dog!

throw sth TO sb

Ushlab olsin deb, uzatmoq. Zarar yetkazish niyati yo‘q.

  • Jasur threw an apple to his sister.
  • Throw the rope to me — I’ll hold it!

Misollar

  • The coach was furious and shouted at the players for ten minutes.Murabbiy g‘azablanib, o‘yinchilarga o‘n daqiqa baqirdi.
  • The boy was on the roof, so I shouted to him to be careful.Bola tomda edi, shuning uchun unga ehtiyot bo‘lishini baqirib aytdim.
  • Please don’t throw paper balls at your classmates.Iltimos, sinfdoshlaringizga qog‘oz to‘pchalar otmang.
  • Bekzod threw the bottle of water to the thirsty runner.Bekzod chanqagan yuguruvchiga suv shishasini otib berdi.

throw bilan predlogsiz tuzilma ham bor: Throw me the ball = Throw the ball to me. Bu ikkala holatda ham ushlab olish ma’nosi bor. Tegish ma’nosida esa faqat at: throw a stone at the window. Shunga o‘xshash: shoot at — nishonga o‘q uzmoq (tegishi shart emas): He shot at the target. shoot (predlogsiz) — otib urmoq: He shot the bird.

Ogohlantirish: ba’zi fe’llar boshqa ma’noda predlog bilan keladi. attend to = “e’tibor bermoq, hal qilmoq” (The nurse attended to the patient), enter into = “(muzokara, shartnoma) boshlamoq” (enter into an agreement). Joy ma’nosida (xonaga kirmoq, ma’ruzaga qatnashmoq) predlog ishlatilmaydi.

discuss fe’li predlogsiz, lekin discussion oti bilan about / of kelishi mumkin: We had a long discussion about money. reach predlogsiz, lekin arrive bilan at / in, get bilan to kerak (Unit 126): arrive in Bukhara, get to Bukhara, reach Bukhara. Ma’lumot uchun, make a phone call otida to keladi: a call to my aunt.

Yangi fe’l uchragan har safar lug‘atda uning tuzilishini ham o‘qing: listen (to) sb/sth. Gap ichida yodlang: I listen to music every day — shunda predlog fe’l bilan birga yodingizda qoladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Please explain me the rule.Please explain the rule to me.Explain, describe, say, suggest bilan shaxs oldida to kerak; “explain me” deyilmaydi.
She listens music every evening.She listens to music every evening.O‘zbekcha “musiqani tinglamoq”da predlog yo‘q, inglizchada listen to.
I phoned to my aunt yesterday.I phoned my aunt yesterday.Phone, call, ring — to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri to‘ldiruvchi bilan, predlogsiz.
They discussed about the plan.They discussed the plan.“Haqida” ni about deb qo‘shmang: discuss o‘zi “haqida muhokama qilmoq” degani.
He entered to the classroom.He entered the classroom.Enter bilan joy nomi to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri keladi. “Xonaga kirdi” deganda enter the room yoki went into the room deyiladi, lekin enter into the room emas.
Don’t look me like that!Don’t look at me like that!Look fe’lidan keyin to‘ldiruvchi oldida at kerak.

What happened your phone?What happened to your phone?“Telefoningga nima bo‘ldi?” — o‘zbekchadagi -ga qo‘shimchasi inglizchada to bo‘lib keladi: happen to sb/sth. Predlogni tushirib bo‘lmaydi.
The coach shouted to the players angrily.The coach shouted at the players angrily.G‘azab bilan baqirish — shout at. Shout to esa uzoqdan eshittirish uchun.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “explain me” xato, “tell me” esa to‘g‘ri?

Chunki tell o‘zidan keyin to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri shaxsni oladi (tell me the news), explain esa olmaydi. U shaxs oldidan to talab qiladi: explain the news to me. Xuddi shunday: say sth to sb, describe sth to sb, suggest sth to sb. Bu ro‘yxatni yodlab oling.

“talk to” bilan “talk with” orasida farq bormi?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil. Britaniya ingliz tilida talk to / speak to keng tarqalgan, Amerika ingliz tilida talk with / speak with ham ko‘p ishlatiladi. Imtihonda Britaniya variantiga amal qiling: talk to.

listen va hear ning farqi nimada?

listen to — ongli ravishda quloq solish: listen to the radio. hear — ovoz qulog‘ingizga o‘z-o‘zidan eshitiladi, predlogsiz: I heard a noise. “Eshitmadim, chunki quloq solmadim” — I didn’t listen; “eshita olmadim, ovoz kelmadi” — I couldn’t hear.

look at, see va watch qanday farq qiladi?

look at — ko‘zni biror narsaga qaratish (iroda bilan). see — ko‘z oldingizda paydo bo‘lgan narsani payqash. watch — harakatdagi narsani (film, o‘yin, bolalar) bir muddat kuzatish. see ham, watch ham predlogsiz ishlatiladi.

“answer” bilan “reply” ning farqi nimada?

Ma’no yaqin, lekin tuzilma boshqa: answer + to‘ldiruvchi (predlogsiz): answer the question, answer the phone, answer me. reply to + to‘ldiruvchi: reply to the email, reply to me. respond to ham shu kabi, lekin ko‘proq rasmiy.

Qaysi fe’l qaysi predlog bilan kelishini qanday bilaman?

Qat’iy qoida yo‘q, lekin bir yo‘l bor: fe’lni gap bilan yodlang va lug‘atdagi tuzilmaga e’tibor bering (listen to sb, discuss sth). Yangi fe’l o‘rganganingizda doim uch narsani tekshiring: predlog kerakmi? qaysi? kimga/nimaga?

“write to” ni “write” deyish mumkinmi (to siz)?

Amerika ingliz tilida ha: I’ll write you soon. Britaniya tilida odatda write to you deyiladi. “Write a letter to sb” va “write sb a letter” (ikkita to‘ldiruvchi) ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • to: talk, speak, listen, write, apologise, explain, describe, reply, respond, happen, invite. Explain the rule to me — ❌ explain me.
  • at: look, stare, glance, laugh, smile, aim, point. “Nishonga qaratilgan” — at.
  • shout at = jahl bilan baqirmoq; shout to = eshitsin deb uzoqdan baqirmoq.
  • throw … at = urish uchun otmoq; throw … to = ushlab olsin deb otmoq.
  • Predlogsiz: phone, call, answer, discuss, enter, marry, reach, mention, attend, resemble, contact.
  • ❌ discuss about, ❌ phone to, ❌ enter to — o‘zbekcha “-ga”, “haqida” inglizchaga ko‘chirilmaydi.
  • Britaniya tilida talk to / write to; Amerika tilida talk with / write sb ham bor.
