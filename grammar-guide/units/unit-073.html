The 1

The — 1
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Faqat bittasi bor narsalar bilan the: the only, the longest, the end of the month va the same.
  • Dunyoda, mamlakatda yoki shaharda yagona bo‘lgan narsalar bilan the: the sun, the moon, the sky, the world, the government; lekin space (the siz).
  • Kundalik hayotda hamma biladigan joylar bilan the: the cinema, the bank, the post office, the doctor’s; shuningdek the radio, lekin TV (the siz).
  • Ovqat nomlari (breakfast, lunch, dinner) artiklsiz ishlatilishini.
  • Ot + raqam the siz: Platform 2, Room 305, page 52.
  • next / last / this / every bilan the kerak emasligini: next week, last Friday.
  • Hikoyada esa the next day, the week before kabi shakllar ishlatilishini.

Unit 72 da the “tinglovchi qaysi ekanini biladi” degan ma’noda o‘rganildi. Bu darsda shu g‘oyaning eng ko‘p uchraydigan holatlari: biz hammamiz biladigan narsalar — osmon, quyosh, shaharning kinoteatri, bank — nima uchun the bilan aytilishini ko‘ramiz.

O‘zbek tilida bularning hammasi artiklsiz aytiladi: Quyosh chiqdi. Biz kinoga bordik. Nonushta qildik. Kelasi hafta ko‘rishamiz. Inglizchada esa har birining o‘z qoidasi bor: The sun came up. We went to the cinema. We had breakfast (the yo‘q). See you next week (the yo‘q). Shuning uchun bu darsdagi jadvallarni qoidaga qarab emas, guruhlarga qarab eslab qoling.

Weather so‘zi bilan ehtiyot bo‘ling: The weather is nice today ✅, lekin Weather is nice today ❌. Umumiy ma’noda, ayniqsa sifat bilan, the qo‘yilmaydi: I don’t like cold weather. (Unit 75).

Sifat qo‘shsangiz, a / an keladi. Yagona narsaning “turini” tasvirlaganda a / an ishlatiladi: There is a full moon tonight. Above us was a beautiful blue sky. Lekin shunchaki The moon is bright, The sky is blue.

space (koinot, fazo) — the siz: Yuri Gagarin was the first person to travel into space. “Bo‘sh joy” ma’nosida esa aniq joy haqida the: Put the box in the space under the stairs. (Qutini zinapoya ostidagi bo‘sh joyga qo‘ying.)

the country ikki ma’noli: a country — mamlakat; the country — shahar emas, qishloq joylar (= the countryside): Do you prefer living in a city or in the country? (Shaharda yashashni afzal ko‘rasizmi yoki qishloqdami?)

Earth sayyora nomi sifatida bosh harf bilan va the siz yoziladi, boshqa sayyoralar kabi: Venus, Earth and Mars. Lekin kundalik gapda the earth / the Earth ham uchraydi: The earth goes round the sun.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi narsalar “yagona” hisoblanadi? Ro‘yxatni to‘liq yodlash kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Savol oddiy: “Bizning dunyomizda / mamlakatimizda / shahrimizda bu narsadan nechta bor?” Bitta bo‘lsa — the: the sun, the president, the airport (agar shaharda bitta aeroport bo‘lsa). Boshqa joyda boshqa narsa yagona bo‘ladi: Toshkentda the metro bor, lekin har shaharda emas.

Shaharda kinoteatr ko‘p. Nega “go to the cinema” deymiz, “go to a cinema” emas?

Chunki biz “kino ko‘rish uchun odatda boriladigan joy” haqida gapiramiz, aniq bir binoni nazarda tutmaymiz. Go to the cinema = kinoga bormoq (umuman). Aniq bitta yangi kinoteatr haqida gapirsak, a: They opened a new cinema in our street.

Nega “watch TV” deymiz-u, “listen to the radio” deymiz?

Bu tilning odatiy qoidasi, aniq mantiq yo‘q. Television / TV — ko‘rsatuvlar, kanallar sifatida (the siz). The radio esa doim the bilan. Faqat televizor asbobi haqida gapirsak, the TV: Turn on the TV. Shunchaki yodlab oling.

“Earth” bilan “the earth” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham. Sayyora nomi sifatida (Mars, Venus bilan birga) Earth — the siz, bosh harf bilan. Oddiy gapda “yer” ma’nosida the earth / the Earth: The earth is round. Barqaror iboralarda on earth (What on earth are you doing? — “nima qilyapsan o‘zi?”).

“go to the doctor” bilan “see a doctor” bir xilmi?

Deyarli. Go to the doctor(’s) — odatdagi shifokorimizga borish: I have to go to the doctor tomorrow. See a doctor — shifokorga ko‘rinish, qaysi shifokor ekani muhim emas, maslahat sifatida ko‘p ishlatiladi: You should see a doctor. Kasalxona haqida — Unit 74.

“I love cinema” va “I love the cinema” farqi nima?

I love cinema — kino san’atini umuman yaxshi ko‘raman (umumiy tushuncha, the yo‘q; Unit 75). I love the cinema — kinoteatrga borishni yaxshi ko‘raman (kundalik joy sifatida).

Ovqat nomlari bilan the umuman ishlatilmaydimi?

Ishlatiladi, lekin faqat aniq bir ovqat haqida: The lunch we had at the hotel was excellent. Oddiy “ovqat qilmoq” ma’nosida (have lunch) — the siz. Sifat bilan esa a / an (a big breakfast).

“at night” deymiz-u, “in the morning” deymiz — nega?

Bu qotib qolgan iboralar: in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening, lekin at night. Ular aniq mantiqqa ega emas, ularni bir butun qilib yodlang. Batafsil — Unit 121.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Faqat bittasi bor bo‘lsa — the: the only, the highest, the end of the month; the same (same, a same ❌).
  • Yagona narsalar the bilan: the sun, the moon, the sky, the world, the environment, the government; sifat bilan tasvirlansa a / an: a full moon.
  • Kundalik joylar the bilan: the cinema, the bank, the post office, the doctor’s; the radio, lekin TV / television (the siz).
  • Ovqat nomlari artiklsiz: have breakfast, lunch, dinner; sifat bilan a lovely dinner; aniq ovqat — the lunch we had.
  • Ot + raqam — the siz: Platform 2, Room 305, page 52; koinot ma’nosida space — the siz.
  • next / last / this / every + vaqt so‘zi: the ham, predlog ham yo‘q: next week, last year.
  • Hikoyada: the next day, the day before, the following week. Hozirdan hisoblasak: tomorrow, yesterday, next week.
  • in the morning, lekin at night; at the weekend (BrE) — on the weekend (AmE).
