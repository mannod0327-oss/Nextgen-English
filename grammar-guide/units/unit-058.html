58

Verb + -ing or to ... 3 (like / would like etc.)

Fe’l + -ing yoki to ... — 3 (like / would like)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • like, love, hate dan keyin -ing ham, to ham kelishi mumkinligini — va qachon faqat -ing ishlatilishini.
  • I like doing va I like to do o‘rtasidagi nozik farqni.
  • would like / would love / would hate / would prefer dan keyin odatda to kelishini.
  • I would like to have done — “qilganimda edi, afsus” degan ma’noni.

Bu darsning eng muhim farqi: I like va I’d like butunlay boshqa narsalar. I like swimming = suzishni (umuman) yaxshi ko‘raman. I’d like to swim = (hozir / shu safar) suzgim kelyapti, suzmoqchiman.

O‘zbek tilida: suzishni yaxshi ko‘raman (umumiy odat, did) va suzgim keladi / suzmoqchiman / suzishni xohlardim (aniq bir paytdagi istak). Ingliz tilida bu farqni bitta kichik so‘z — would (’d) beradi: I like ↔ I’d like. ’d ni eshitmay qolish yoki tushirib qoldirish — juda ko‘p uchraydigan xato.

like / love / hate + -ing yoki to Like, love and hate

Takrorlanadigan ishlar, odatlar, umuman did haqida gapirganda like, love, hate dan keyin -ing ham, to ham ishlatish mumkin. Ma’no deyarli bir xil:

like / love / hate: ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri
+ -ing+ toO‘zbekcha
Do you like going for a walk after dinner?Do you like to go for a walk after dinner?Kechki ovqatdan keyin sayr qilishni yoqtirasizmi?
Nodira hates driving at night.Nodira hates to drive at night.Nodira kechasi mashina haydashni yomon ko‘radi.
I love reading before bed.I love to read before bed.Uxlashdan oldin kitob o‘qishni juda yaxshi ko‘raman.
I don’t like being interrupted.I don’t like to be interrupted.Gapimni bo‘lishlarini yoqtirmayman.

Fe’ldan oldin kimdir (to‘ldiruvchi) ham kelishi mumkin:

  • I don’t like people shouting at me. / I don’t like people to shout at me.Odamlarning menga baqirishini yoqtirmayman.

Mavjud vaziyat haqida — odatda -ing

Agar gap hozir mavjud bo‘lgan (yoki o‘tmishda mavjud bo‘lgan) vaziyat haqida bo‘lsa, -ing ishlatiladi. Bunda “bu vaziyat menga yoqadimi?” deb baho beramiz. (to bilan aytish ba’zan grammatik jihatdan mumkin, lekin kam uchraydi — -ing ni ishlating.)

  • Bekzod rents a flat in the old part of Bukhara. He likes living so close to the Ark.Bekzod Buxoroning eski qismida kvartira ijaraga olgan. Arkka shunchalik yaqin yashash unga yoqadi.
  • Do you like being the oldest child in the family?Oiladagi eng katta farzand bo‘lish sizga yoqadimi?
  • At my old school the classrooms were always cold. I hated sitting there in my coat all winter.Eski maktabimda sinflar doim sovuq edi. Qish bo‘yi u yerda paltoda o‘tirishni yomon ko‘rardim.

enjoy, mind, dislike — faqat -ing; can’t stand — odatda -ing

Ba’zi “yoqtirish / yoqtirmaslik” fe’llaridan keyin to ishlatilmaydi. can’t stand dan keyin ham odatda -ing keladi (can’t stand to ... ham uchraydi, ayniqsa AmE da, lekin -ing doim to‘g‘ri):

enjoy + -ing(don’t) mind + -ingdislike + -ingcan’t stand + -ing
  • I really enjoy cooking for my friends. (not enjoy to cook)Do‘stlarim uchun ovqat pishirishdan juda huzur qilaman.
  • I don’t mind working at weekends.Dam olish kunlari ishlashga qarshi emasman.
  • Sardor can’t stand waiting in queues.Sardor navbatda kutishga toqat qilolmaydi.

I like doing va I like to do farqi Enjoy it or think it’s a good idea

Ko‘p hollarda farq yo‘q. Lekin ba’zan like doing va like to do boshqacha ohang beradi:

😊 I like doing = zavq olaman

Men bu ishni qilaman va undan huzur qilaman.

  • I like gardening. It relaxes me. — Bog‘dorchilikni yaxshi ko‘raman. Bu meni tinchlantiradi.
  • Aziz likes cooking plov for his friends. — Aziz do‘stlariga palov damlashni yaxshi ko‘radi.

🧠 I like to do = to‘g‘ri deb bilaman

Bu ishni qilishni to‘g‘ri / foydali deb hisoblayman, shuning uchun qilaman. Zavq olishim shart emas.

  • I don’t enjoy going to the dentist, but I like to go every six months. — Tish shifokoriga borishni yoqtirmayman, lekin har olti oyda borib turishni ma’qul ko‘raman.
  • Dilnoza likes to check her emails before breakfast. — Dilnoza nonushtadan oldin xatlarini tekshirib olishni odat qilgan.

Bu farq asosan Britaniya ingliz tilida seziladi. Amerika ingliz tilida like to do “zavq olaman” ma’nosida ham juda keng ishlatiladi: I like to swim (AmE) = I like swimming.

Esingizda bo‘lsin: enjoy va mind dan keyin har doim -ing: I enjoy cleaning the house (enjoy to clean ❌), Do you mind cleaning the house? (mind to clean ❌).

would like / would love / would hate / would prefer + to Would like to ...

would like, would love, would hate, would prefer dan keyin odatda to + fe’l keladi. Ular aniq bir vaziyatdagi istak, taklif yoki xohish haqida:

Ega+would (’d)+like / love / hate / prefer+to+fe’l
  • I’d like to visit Khiva next spring.Kelasi bahorda Xivaga borishni xohlardim.
  • Would you like to come to our wedding in June?Iyundagi to‘yimizga kelishni xohlaysizmi? (taklif)
  • Kamola would love to study in Japan one day.Kamola bir kun kelib Yaponiyada o‘qishni juda-juda xohlaydi.
  • I’d hate to lose my passport abroad.Chet elda pasportimni yo‘qotib qo‘yishni sira istamagan bo‘lardim.
  • “Shall we eat now?” “I’d prefer to wait for Aziz.”— Hozir ovqatlanaveramizmi? — Azizni kutganimiz ma’qulroq.

would mind + -ing

would mind dan keyin esa -ing keladi. Would you mind ...ing? — xushmuomala iltimos:

  • Would you mind turning the music down a little?Musiqa ovozini biroz pasaytirsangiz, maylimi?
  • I wouldn’t mind having a cup of tea. (= I’d quite like a cup of tea.)Bir piyola choy ichsam yomon bo‘lmasdi.

Would you mind ...ing? so‘zma-so‘z “...ishga qarshi bo‘larmidingiz?” degani. Shuning uchun “Ha, mayli” deb rozilik bildirmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, No, not at all. / Of course not. deyiladi (ya’ni “Yo‘q, qarshi emasman”). Yes desangiz — “Ha, qarshiman” bo‘lib qoladi!

would like + kimnidir + to ham bor: I’d like you to meet my sister. (Singlim bilan tanishishingizni istardim.) Bu tuzilma Unit 55 da batafsil.

would like to have done: amalga oshmagan istak I would like to have done

I would like to have done something = o‘tmishda buni qilmadim (yoki qila olmadim) va hozir afsuslanaman. Bu — amalga oshmagan o‘tmishdagi istak.

  • Anvar was in hospital last month, but I was abroad on business. I’d like to have visited him.Anvar o‘tgan oy kasalxonada yotdi, lekin men xorijda xizmat safarida edim. Uni borib ko‘rsam bo‘lardi (afsuski, borolmadim).
  • We’d like to have stayed longer in Samarkand, but we had to come back to work.Samarqandda uzoqroq qolishni istardik, lekin ishga qaytishimiz kerak edi.

Xuddi shu tuzilma would love / would hate / would prefer bilan ham ishlatiladi:

  • I’d love to have met your grandfather, but he died before I was born.Bobongiz bilan tanishishni juda istagan bo‘lardim, lekin u men tug‘ilmasimdan oldin vafot etgan.
  • Jasur had to give a speech to 500 people with no preparation. I’d hate to have been in his place.Jasur hech qanday tayyorgarliksiz 500 kishi oldida nutq so‘zlashiga to‘g‘ri keldi. Uning o‘rnida bo‘lishni sira istamagan bo‘lardim.

Xuddi shu ma’noni would have liked to do bilan ham berish mumkin: I would have liked to see him = I would like to have seen him. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I like swimming” va “I like to swim” — qaysi birini ishlatay?

Umuman yoqtirish haqida gapirganda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Britaniya ingliz tilida “zavq olaman” ma’nosida -ing biroz tabiiyroq, Amerika ingliz tilida esa to juda keng tarqalgan. Imtihonda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi (agar gap mavjud vaziyat haqida bo‘lmasa — u holda -ing).

Nega “He likes living in Bukhara” deyiladi-yu, “He likes to live in Bukhara” emas?

Chunki u hozir Buxoroda yashayapti — bu mavjud vaziyat va biz unga baho beryapmiz (“bu hayot unga yoqadi”). Bunday hollarda -ing ishlatiladi. He likes to live in Bukhara grammatik jihatdan mumkin, lekin kam uchraydi va g‘alati eshitiladi. like to ko‘proq takrorlanadigan ishlar, tanlov va odatlar haqida.

“I’d like” dagi ’d nima? “had” mi yoki “would” mi?

Bu yerda ’d = would: I’d like = I would like. Qanday aniqlash mumkin? would dan keyin fe’lning asosiy shakli keladi (like, love, prefer), had dan keyin esa V3 (I’d liked — bu past perfect). I’d like to ... doim would.

“Would you like ...?” savoliga qanday javob beraman?

Rozi bo‘lsangiz: Yes, please. / I’d love to. / Yes, I’d like that. Rad etsangiz: No, thank you. / I’d love to, but I can’t. Diqqat: Yes, I would like. ❌ — oxirida to yoki to‘ldiruvchi kerak: Yes, I’d like to.

“would hate to” dan keyin ham -ing kelishi mumkinmi?

Odatda yo‘q — would like / love / hate / prefer dan keyin to ishlatiladi: I’d hate to live there. Faqat would mind dan keyin -ing keladi: Would you mind waiting?

“I would have liked to have seen him” deyish mumkinmi?

Bunday gapni eshitasiz, lekin unda “o‘tmish” ikki marta ifodalangan. Grammatika kitoblari soddaroq variantlarni tavsiya qiladi: I would like to have seen him yoki I would have liked to see him. Mashq va imtihonlarda shu ikkisidan birini yozing.

“I don’t like” va “I wouldn’t like” farqi nima?

I don’t like flying = umuman samolyotda uchishni yoqtirmayman. I wouldn’t like to fly in that old plane = aniq bir vaziyat haqida: o‘sha eski samolyotda uchishni istamagan bo‘lardim. Birinchisi — did, ikkinchisi — tasavvur qilingan vaziyat.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing. Ikkala shakl ham bir xil tabiiy bo‘lsa, ikkalasini ham yozing.

  1. I love (travel) by train.
  2. Our new flat is next to a park, and it’s very quiet. We like (live) there.
  3. You’ve been a taxi driver for five years now. Do you like (be) a taxi driver?
  4. My father hates (wait) in queues.
  5. I really enjoy (cook) for my friends.
  6. Do you mind (work) at weekends?

2-mashq. would like / would love / would mind ...: to‘g‘ri shaklni yozing.

  1. Would you like (come) to my birthday party on Sunday?
  2. I’d love (visit) Japan one day.
  3. Would you mind (open) the window? It’s very hot in here.
  4. “Shall we eat now?” “I’d prefer (wait) for Aziz.”
  5. I’d hate (live) in such a noisy city.

3-mashq. O‘tmishda amalga oshmagan istak: would ... to have + V3 shaklida yozing.

  1. We missed the parade. It’s a pity. We’d like (see) it.
  2. I’d love (meet) your grandfather, but he died before I was born.
  3. Bekzod’s flight was cancelled and he spent the whole night on a bench at the airport. I’d hate (be) in his place.
  4. I wanted to go to the concert, but I couldn’t. → I would

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘ri yozing.

  1. I would like going to the cinema tonight. →
  2. I enjoy to swim in the sea. →
  3. Would you mind to help me with this box? →
  4. Do you like to come to my party on Saturday? (taklif) →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Men erta turishni yoqtirmayman. →
  2. Bizga qo‘shilishni xohlaysizmi? (taklif) →
  3. Eshikni yopib qo‘ysangiz, maylimi? →
  4. Men o‘sha filmni ko‘rishni istardim (lekin ko‘rmadim). →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • like / love / hate + -ing yoki to — umumiy did va takrorlanadigan ishlar uchun ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.
  • Mavjud vaziyat haqida — -ing: He likes living there. Do you like being a taxi driver?
  • enjoy, mind, dislike — faqat -ing (can’t stand dan keyin ham odatda -ing).
  • like doing = zavq olaman; like to do = to‘g‘ri / foydali deb bilaman, odat qilganman (asosan BrE farqi).
  • would like / love / hate / prefer + to: I’d like to go. Lekin would mind + -ing: Would you mind waiting?
  • Do you like ...? = umuman yoqadimi; Would you like ...? = taklif / hozirgi istak.
  • would like to have + V3 (= would have liked to + fe’l) — o‘tmishda amalga oshmagan istak.
