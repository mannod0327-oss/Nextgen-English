72

A/an and the

A/an va the
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • a / an bilan the ning asosiy farqini: tinglovchi “qaysi biri?” ekanini biladimi?
  • Birinchi marta aytilgan narsa — a / an, ikkinchi marta — the (I bought a melon. The melon was sweet.).
  • Keyingi so‘zlar aniqlab bergan narsa uchun the: the girl in the red dress, the capital of Uzbekistan.
  • Vaziyatdan aniq bo‘lgan narsalar uchun the: Close the door. Where is the bathroom?
  • “Har bir” ma’nosida a / an: once a week, 15,000 so‘m a kilo.
  • first, last, same, only, best kabi so‘zlardan oldin the kelishini.
  • O‘zbekcha bir, o‘sha, shu, tushum kelishigi -ni va so‘z tartibi inglizcha a / the ga qanday to‘g‘ri kelishini.

Bu darsning asosiy savoli bitta: tinglovchi qaysi narsa haqida gapirayotganimni biladimi? Bilmasa (yangi, noaniq narsa) — a / an. Bilsa (aniq, ma’lum narsa) — the.

O‘zbek tilida artikl yo‘q, lekin “noaniq / aniq” farqi o‘zbekchada ham bor — faqat boshqa vositalar bilan:

• noaniq: bir (bir kitob oldim = I bought a book) yoki otning o‘zi (kitob oldim);
• aniq: o‘sha, shu, bu (o‘sha kitob = the book we both know) va tushum kelishigi -ni (kitobni oldim = I took the book);
• so‘z tartibi: gap boshidagi ega odatda ma’lum narsa (Mushuk uxlayapti = The cat is sleeping), yangi narsa esa ko‘pincha bir ... yoki ... bor bilan kiritiladi (Hovlida mushuk bor = There is a cat in the yard).

Ingliz tilida bu ma’nolar har doim a / an yoki the bilan aytilishi shart — o‘zbekcha o‘ylab ularni tushirib qoldirmang!

Birinchi marta — a / an, ikkinchi marta — the First mention and second mention

Yangi narsani gapga birinchi marta kiritganimizda tinglovchi uni hali bilmaydi, shuning uchun a / an aytamiz. Keyingi gapda o‘sha narsaga qaytganda tinglovchi endi qaysi narsa ekanini biladi — shuning uchun the.

  • In the park I saw a street musician and a clown. The musician was playing the dutar, and the clown was making the children laugh.Bog‘da bir ko‘cha sozandasi va bir masxarabozni ko‘rdim. Sozanda dutor chalayotgan, masxaraboz esa bolalarni kuldirayotgan edi.
  • Last weekend Dilnoza visited a village near Samarkand. In the village there was a small teahouse. The teahouse was very old.O‘tgan dam olish kuni Dilnoza Samarqand yaqinidagi bir qishloqqa bordi. Qishloqda kichkina choyxona bor edi. Choyxona juda eski edi.
  • Sardor ordered a kebab and a salad. The kebab was delicious, but the salad was too salty.Sardor kabob va salat buyurtma qildi. Kabob juda mazali edi, salat esa juda sho‘r edi.
  • Bekzod has a laptop and a phone. The laptop is old, but the phone is new.Bekzodning noutbuki va telefoni bor. Noutbuk eski, telefon esa yangi.

Mashhur ertak boshlanishini eslang: “Bir bor ekan, bir yo‘q ekan. Bir podshoh bor ekan. O‘sha podshohning uch o‘g‘li bor ekan.” Inglizchada xuddi shu mantiq: There was a king. The king had three sons. Birinchi gapda “bir” → a, ikkinchi gapda “o‘sha” → the.

Birinchi va ikkinchi marta: ot turlariga qarab
Ot turi1-marta (yangi)2-marta (ma’lum)
Birlik, sanaladiganI bought a bag.The bag is blue.
Ko‘plikI bought some apples.The apples were fresh.

Ikkinchi marta har doim “the + ot” aytish shart emas: ba’zan olmosh ishlatiladi. I bought a bag. It’s blue. Ikkala variant ham to‘g‘ri.

Keyingi so‘zlar aniqlab bersa — the The + a phrase that says which one

Biz bitta aniq narsani nazarda tutsak — the; “istalgan bittasi”, “qaysidir biri” bo‘lsa — a / an. Ko‘pincha narsa avval tilga olinmagan bo‘ladi, lekin gapning o‘zida “qaysi biri” aytib qo‘yiladi: of ..., who / that ..., in / on / near .... Shunda the aytiladi.

Diqqat: of yoki boshqa ibora keldi degani, avtomatik the degani emas. Agar shunday narsalardan ko‘p bo‘lsa va biz “bittasi” desak, a aytiladi: a friend of mine, a member of the club, a fan of this team. The faqat “yagona / aniq” narsa uchun.

The yoki a? Uchta savol

  1. Bu narsa gapda avval aytilganmi? → the.
  2. Men ham, tinglovchi ham vaziyatdan qaysi biri ekanini bilamizmi? → the.
  3. Narsa yagonami yoki keyingi so‘zlar uni yagona qilib aniqlab beradimi? → the.

Uchala javob ham “yo‘q” bo‘lsa — a / an (birlikda) yoki some / artiklsiz (ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlarda).

Vaziyatdan aniq bo‘lgan narsa — the It is clear from the situation which one

Narsa avval tilga olinmagan bo‘lsa ham, siz ham, tinglovchi ham qaysi biri ekanini bilsangiz — the aytiladi. Bu odatda bir xonada, bir uyda, bir oilada yoki bir ishxonada bo‘lgan narsalar:

  • “Where’s Dad?” “He’s in the garden.”— Dadam qayerda? — Bog‘da (bizning bog‘imizda).
  • Could you pass me the salt, please?Tuzni uzatib yuborasizmi? (dasturxondagi tuz)
  • Excuse me, where is the station? (in this town)Kechirasiz, bekat qayerda? (shu shaharda)
  • We stayed in a hotel. The manager was very kind.Biz mehmonxonada qoldik. Menejer juda mehribon edi. (shu mehmonxonaning menejeri)
  • I’ve got an exam tomorrow, so I’m studying in the library.Ertaga imtihonim bor, shuning uchun kutubxonada o‘qiyapman. (bizning kutubxona)

the bank, the post office, the doctor, the dentist

Bank, pochta, shifokor yoki tish shifokori haqida gapirganda odatda o‘zimiz boradigan, ma’lum bittasini nazarda tutamiz, shuning uchun the aytiladi. “Istalgan bittasi” yoki kasb haqida gapirsak — a:

  • After work Dilnoza went to the post office to send a parcel.Ishdan keyin Dilnoza posilka jo‘natish uchun pochtaga bordi.
  • Aziz has toothache, so he’s going to the dentist tomorrow.Azizning tishi og‘riyapti, shuning uchun u ertaga tish shifokoriga boradi.
  • Is there a post office near the hotel?Mehmonxona yaqinida pochta bormi? (istalgan bittasi)
  • Malika’s uncle is a dentist in Fergana.Malikaning amakisi Farg‘onada tish shifokori. (kasb)

a / an va the: qanday farq qiladi?

a / an

Tinglovchi qaysi biri ekanini bilmaydi; yoki “istalgan bittasi”.

  • Is there a pharmacy near here?
  • I need a pen.
  • Let’s sit on a bench.

the

Tinglovchi qaysi biri ekanini biladi (avval aytilgan, vaziyatdan ma’lum yoki yagona).

  • Where is the pharmacy? (bizning mahalladagi)
  • Can I borrow the pen? (anavi)
  • Let’s sit on the bench under the big tree.

Bu farq o‘zbekchada ham seziladi, faqat so‘z bilan emas. Solishtiring: Kitob oldim (a book — qaysi ekani muhim emas) va Kitobni oldim (the book — aniq bitta, ma’lum kitob). Inglizchada ikkinchisi I took the book bo‘ladi. Demak, tushum kelishigi -ni ko‘pincha the degan ma’noga yaqin keladi — lekin hamma vaqt emas, shuning uchun har gapda alohida o‘ylang.

“Har bir” ma’nosida a / an once a week, three times a day, 15,000 so‘m a kilo

Biror ish qanchalik tez-tez bo‘lishini yoki narx, tezlik, miqdor har bir vaqt yoki o‘lchov birligiga qancha to‘g‘ri kelishini aytganda a / an ishlatiladi. Bu yerda a / an = “har bir” (per). The ham, predlog ham qo‘yilmaydi.

three times a dayTake this medicine three times a day.Bu dorini kuniga uch mahal iching.

O‘zbekchada bu ma’no qo‘shimchalar bilan beriladi: haftasiga, kuniga, soatiga, kilosi. Inglizchada esa a / an + birlik: a week, a day, an hour, a kilo. three times in week ❌, twice the week ❌ → three times a week, twice a week ✅. Hour unli tovush bilan boshlangani uchun an hour.

Rasmiy matnlarda a / an o‘rniga per ham ishlatiladi: 100 kilometres per hour, 50,000 so‘m per person. Batafsil — Unit 71.

The bilan keladigan so‘zlar first, last, same, only, best ...

Ba’zi so‘zlar o‘zi “bittasi” degan ma’noni beradi, shuning uchun ulardan oldin odatda the keladi:

Odatda the bilan:

Qoida: first, last, next, same, only va orttirma daraja (the best, the oldest, the most beautiful) oldidan odatda the qo‘yiladi. Bu so‘zlar Unit 73 da ham uchraydi; orttirma daraja haqida — Unit 108.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega birinchi marta a, ikkinchi marta the aytiladi?

Chunki tinglovchining bilimi o‘zgaradi. Birinchi marta aytganingizda u “qaysi biri?” ekanini bilmaydi — siz uni gapga kiritasiz (a). Ikkinchi marta u allaqachon biladi — endi “o‘sha” deyishingiz mumkin (the). Ertaklarda ham shunday: Bir podshoh bor ekan... O‘sha podshoh...

Tinglovchi bilmasa-yu, men the aytsam nima bo‘ladi?

Gap tuzilishi buzilmaydi, lekin ma’no noto‘g‘ri chiqadi — tinglovchi darrov “Qaysi biri?” deb so‘raydi. Masalan, The man called you desangiz, u “Qaysi odam?” deydi. Agar kim ekanini u bilmasa, A man called you deyish kerak.

Ikkinchi marta ham a bo‘lishi mumkinmi?

Ha, agar siz aynan o‘sha narsani emas, istalgan bittasini nazarda tutsangiz: I need a pen. Have you got a pen? (har qanday ruchka bo‘laveradi). Aniq o‘sha ruchka haqida gapirsangiz — Can I borrow the pen? (anavi, stol ustidagi).

The va “o‘sha” bir xilmi? Men “o‘sha” ni har gal the deb tarjima qilsam bo‘ladimi?

Ko‘pincha bo‘ladi, lekin har doim emas. The “ma’lum” degan yengil signal; “o‘sha” esa ko‘rsatish ma’nosiga ega. Ko‘rsatib aytayotgan bo‘lsangiz (o‘sha uy — anavi uy), that house deyish kerak. Aksincha, o‘zbekchada “o‘sha” aytilmasa ham, inglizchada the kerak bo‘ladi: Eshikni yoping = Close the door.

Ikkinchi gapda the + ot o‘rniga it / he / she aytsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha. I bought a bag. It’s blue. yoki I bought a bag. The bag is blue. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Agar bir nechta narsa bo‘lsa va qaysi biri ekanini ajratish kerak bo‘lsa, ot takrorlanadi: The laptop is old, but the phone is new.

“a second chance” va “the second chapter” nega boshqacha?

The second chapter — tartibdagi ikkinchi (kitobda faqat bitta ikkinchi bob bor). A second chance — “yana bitta, qo‘shimcha imkoniyat” (another chance). Ya’ni a + tartib son “yana bittasi” ma’nosini beradi: a third attempt, a second cup of tea.

“I have a brother” desam bo‘ladi, nega “the brother” emas?

Chunki siz birinchi marta yangi axborot bermoqdasiz: tinglovchi sizning akangiz borligini bilmaydi. Keyin gapni davom ettirsangiz: I have a brother. He lives in Tashkent. Yoki: My brother lives in Tashkent. Bu yerda the o‘rniga my keladi: the va my bir otdan oldin birga ishlatilmaydi (the my brother ❌).

Ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ham the ishlatiladimi?

Ha. The birlik ham, ko‘plik ham, sanalmaydigan ot bilan ham keladi: the book, the books, the rice. A / an esa faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan. Ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlarda “noaniq” ma’no some yoki artiklsiz bilan beriladi (Unit 71).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • a / an — tinglovchi “qaysi biri?” ekanini bilmaydi (yangi, noaniq). The — biladi (ma’lum, aniq).
  • Birinchi marta a / an, ikkinchi marta the: I bought a melon. The melon was sweet.
  • Vaziyatdan ma’lum bo‘lsa the: Close the door. Where is the bathroom?
  • Keyingi ibora narsani yagona qilib aniqlasa the: the capital of Uzbekistan, the girl who sits next to me.
  • the first, the last, the next, the same, the only, the best — odatda the bilan (istisno: an only child, a second chance).
  • Kasbni oddiy aytsak — a: She is a nurse. Yagona lavozim — the: She is the manager of the bank.
  • O‘zbekcha: bir ≈ a; o‘sha, shu, -ni ≈ the; lekin inglizcha the o‘zbekcha “o‘sha” dan ancha kuchsiz.
  • a / an faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan; the — har qanday ot bilan.
