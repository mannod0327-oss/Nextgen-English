Will/shall 2

Will / shall — 2
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • will / won’t bilan kelajakni bashorat qilish (prediction): Don’t worry. You’ll pass.
  • will bilan ko‘p keladigan so‘zlar: probably, I expect, I’m sure, I think, I don’t think, I wonder.
  • Nega I hope dan keyin ko‘pincha present simple ishlatilishini: I hope it doesn’t rain.
  • will ning “hozir” haqidagi taxmin ma’nosini: Don’t call her now. She’ll be at work.
  • I shall / we shall va shan’t qachon ishlatilishini.

Unit 21 da will bilan gapirayotgan paytdagi qarorlar, takliflar va va’dalarni o‘rgandik. Endi will ning yana bir asosiy vazifasi — kelajakda nima bo‘lishini aytish, taxmin qilish. Bu yerda gap rejalar haqida emas, balki “menimcha, shunday bo‘ladi” degan fikr haqida.

O‘zbek tilida bashorat ham hozirgi-kelasi zamon bilan beriladi: Xavotir olma, imtihondan o‘tasan. → You’ll pass. Ehtimollik esa -sa kerak, ehtimol, shekilli bilan: Ular biroz kechroq yetib kelsa kerak → I expect they’ll arrive a bit later. Ammo diqqat: “Umid qilamanki, dam olish kunlari yomg‘ir yog‘maydi” inglizchada odatda present simple bilan aytiladi: I hope it doesn’t rain at the weekend.

will — bashorat, reja emas Predicting the future with will / won’t

Eslang: kelishilgan yoki oldindan qaror qilingan ish uchun will ishlatilmaydi (Unit 19–21):

  • Dilnoza is visiting her aunt next Saturday. (not Dilnoza will visit)Dilnoza kelasi shanba xolasinikiga boradi. (Kelishib qo‘yilgan — reja.)
  • Are you going to buy a new phone? (not Will you buy)Yangi telefon olmoqchimisan? (Niyat haqida so‘rayapmiz.)

Lekin kelajak haqida gapirganda biz har doim ham reja haqida gapirmaymiz. Ko‘pincha biz nima bo‘lishini aytamiz yoki taxmin qilamiz — bu bashorat (prediction). Bashorat uchun will / won’t ishlatiladi:

🗓️ Reja (plan)

I’m taking my driving test on Friday.

Juma kuni haydovchilik imtihonini topshiraman. (Yozilganman.)

🔮 Bashorat (prediction)

Don’t worry. You’ll pass.

Xavotir olma. O‘tasan. (Bu reja emas — bu mening fikrim.)

Misollar

  • Don’t worry about the interview. You’ll be fine.Suhbat haqida xavotir olmang. Hammasi yaxshi bo‘ladi.
  • Tashkent is changing fast. In ten years it will look completely different.Toshkent tez o‘zgaryapti. O‘n yildan keyin u butunlay boshqacha ko‘rinadi.
  • Put on a warm coat, or you’ll catch a cold.Issiq palto kiyib ol, bo‘lmasa shamollab qolasan.
  • We won’t get there before dark. We’ve still got 200 kilometres to go.U yerga qorong‘i tushguncha yetib bormaymiz. Hali 200 kilometr yo‘l bor.
  • When will the new metro station open?Yangi metro bekati qachon ochiladi?
  • A: How old will you be next year?
    B: I’ll be eighteen.    — Kelasi yil necha yoshga to‘lasan? — O‘n sakkizga.

Kelajakdagi oddiy faktlar uchun ham will ishlatiladi: I’ll be 30 next month. Tomorrow’s lesson will be in room 12.

probably qayerga qo‘yiladi?

Qoida: darak gapda probably will dan keyin: I’ll probably be late. Inkor gapda esa won’t dan oldin: I probably won’t be late. (I won’t probably be late ❌)

  • Kamola will probably call you tonight.Kamola bugun kechqurun senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilsa kerak.
  • It’s late. The shop probably won’t be open now.Kech bo‘ldi. Do‘kon hozir ochiq bo‘lmasa kerak.

I hope + present simple

I hope dan keyin kelajak ma’nosida odatda present simple ishlatiladi (will ham mumkin, lekin kamroq):

  • I hope it doesn’t rain on the wedding day. (or ... it won’t rain ...)To‘y kuni yomg‘ir yog‘masa edi.
  • I hope Jasur gets the job. (or ... Jasur will get ...)Jasur o‘sha ishga kirsa edi. / Umid qilamanki, Jasur ishga qabul qilinadi.
  • I hope you have a great time in London!Londonda yaxshi dam olishingizni tilayman!

I think it won’t ... dan ko‘ra I don’t think it will ... ancha tabiiy: I don’t think it’ll rain ✅ (eng odatiy). Inkor “think” ga o‘tadi — xuddi Unit 21 dagi I don’t think I’ll go kabi.

Do you think ...? va I wonder ... dan keyin so‘z tartibi darak gapdagidek: Do you think it will snow? ✅ (Do you think will it snow? ❌), I wonder what she will say. ✅ (I wonder what will she say ❌).

will — hozir haqida taxmin Will for the present: She’ll be at work now

Odatda will kelajak uchun. Lekin ba’zan will bilan hozirgi holat haqida ishonchli taxmin qilamiz: “bilmayman, lekin aniq shunday bo‘lsa kerak”.

  • Don’t call Dilnoza now. She’ll be at work. (= she’s probably at work now)Hozir Dilnozaga qo‘ng‘iroq qilma. U ishda bo‘lsa kerak.
  • It’s eight o’clock. Aziz will be home by now.Soat sakkiz bo‘ldi. Aziz hozir uyda bo‘lsa kerak.
  • (The doorbell rings.) That’ll be the courier with my parcel.(Eshik qo‘ng‘irog‘i chalinadi.) Bu posilkamni olib kelgan kuryer bo‘lsa kerak.

Bu ma’no o‘zbekchadagi “-sa kerak”, “bo‘lsa kerak” ga juda mos: U hozir ishda bo‘lsa kerak = She’ll be at work. Shunga yaqin ma’noni must be ham beradi (Unit 28).

I shall / we shall Shall with I and we

Shall faqat I va we bilan ishlatiladi va bu ma’noda will bilan bir xil. Ya’ni I shall = I will, we shall = we will:

  • I shall be away next week. (or I will be away ...)Kelasi hafta yo‘q bo‘laman (safarda bo‘laman).
  • We shall probably move to a bigger flat next year. (or We will probably ...)Kelasi yil kattaroq kvartiraga ko‘chsak kerak.

Og‘zaki nutqda odatda qisqa shakl I’ll / we’ll ishlatiladi — u ham I will, ham I shall o‘rnini bosadi: We’ll probably move next year.

shall ning inkori — shall not yoki qisqa shakli shan’t /ʃɑːnt/:

  • I shan’t need the car this weekend. (or I won’t need ...)Shu dam olish kunlari mashina menga kerak bo‘lmaydi.
will va shall: kim bilan?
Egawillshall
I / we✅ I will / we will (I’ll / we’ll)✅ I shall / we shall (biroz rasmiy, BrE)
you / he / she / it / they✅ They will be very surprised.❌ They shall be very surprised.

Zamonaviy ingliz tilida I shall / we shall asosan Britaniyada va biroz rasmiyroq uslubda uchraydi; shan’t esa kamroq ishlatiladi (asosan BrE). Amerika ingliz tilida bu ma’noda deyarli faqat will ishlatiladi. Savollardagi Shall I ...? / Shall we ...? esa Britaniyada juda faol (Unit 21).

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Don’t worry, you pass the exam.Don’t worry, you’ll pass the exam.Kelajak haqida bashorat — will. Present simple bu ma’noni bermaydi.
I won’t probably come.I probably won’t come.Inkorda probably won’t dan oldin turadi. Darakda esa will dan keyin: I’ll probably come.
Do you think will it rain?Do you think it will rain?“Do you think” dan keyingi qism — oddiy darak gap tartibida.
I wonder what will she say.I wonder what she will say.I wonder dan keyin so‘roq tartibi emas, darak tartibi.
Your parents shall be very proud of you.Your parents will be very proud of you.Kelajak ma’nosida shall faqat I va we bilan.
I’m sure you will to like it.I’m sure you’ll like it.will dan keyin to qo‘yilmaydi.
When will you be come back?When will you come back? / When will you be back?will + fe’l (come) yoki will + be + back. “be come” birikmasi yo‘q.
My sister will get married on Saturday — everything is organised.My sister is getting married on Saturday.Bu bashorat emas, kelishilgan reja — present continuous.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Bashorat uchun “will” va “going to” ning farqi nima?

Ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham mumkin. Farq: agar bashoratga hozirgi aniq belgi asos bo‘lsa (qora bulutlar, tirbandlik), going to afzal: It’s going to rain. Agar bu shunchaki fikr, ishonch, tajriba bo‘lsa, will: I think it will rain tomorrow. Batafsil — Unit 23.

“I hope” dan keyin “will” ishlatish xatomi?

Xato emas: I hope it won’t rain ham to‘g‘ri. Lekin ingliz tilida present simple ko‘proq ishlatiladi: I hope it doesn’t rain. Ikkalasini ham bilib qo‘ying, o‘zingiz gapirganda present simple’ni tanlang.

“I think it won’t rain” va “I don’t think it’ll rain” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham grammatik jihatdan mumkin, lekin I don’t think it’ll rain ancha tabiiy va ko‘p ishlatiladi. Ingliz tilida inkor odatda think, believe, expect fe’liga o‘tadi.

“Shall” eskirgan so‘zmi? Uni ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

I shall / we shall Britaniyada hali ham ishlatiladi, lekin ko‘proq rasmiy uslubda. Og‘zaki nutqda I’ll / we’ll deyish eng xavfsiz. Shall I ...? / Shall we ...? savollari esa (Unit 21) Britaniyada juda odatiy. Yana: huquqiy hujjatlar va qoidalarda shall boshqa shaxslar bilan “majbur” ma’nosida keladi: The tenant shall pay the rent monthly. — bu maxsus uslub.

“That’ll be the postman” — nega bu yerda kelajak zamon?

Bu kelajak emas, hozir haqida ishonchli taxmin (C bo‘lim). Eshik qo‘ng‘irog‘i chalindi — “bu pochtachi bo‘lsa kerak”. Inglizlar bunday paytda will bilan taxmin qilishni yaxshi ko‘rishadi.

“If it will rain, we’ll stay at home” deyish to‘g‘rimi?

Yo‘q. if, when, before, after, until, as soon as dan keyin kelajak ma’nosida present simple ishlatiladi: If it rains, we’ll stay at home. ✅ Will faqat gapning asosiy qismida. Bu qoida — Unit 25 da.

“I’ll be” va “I will be” orasida talaffuzda farq bormi?

Ma’no bir xil. Og‘zaki nutqda deyarli doim qisqa shakl I’ll /aɪl/ ishlatiladi. To‘liq will urg‘u bilan aytilsa — qat’iyat yoki ishonch kuchayadi: Don’t worry — it will work!

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bashoratni will / won’t + fe’l bilan yozing.

  1. Don’t worry about the test. You (pass) easily.
  2. Take an umbrella, or you (get) wet.
  3. It’s too late to call the bank now. They (answer).
  4. Malika is very good at her job. I’m sure she (get) a promotion soon.
  5. Don’t tell Aziz about the broken window. He (be) very angry.
  6. We (not / arrive) on time — the traffic is terrible today.

2-mashq. Qavsdagi so‘zlardan foydalanib gap tuzing (will / won’t bilan).

  1. (I / probably / see / you / tomorrow) →
  2. (I / probably / not / come / to the party) →
  3. (do you think / it / snow / tomorrow?) →
  4. (I / don’t think / the shops / be / open on Sunday) →
  5. (I wonder / what / Bekzod / say) →

3-mashq. “I hope” bilan gap tuzing. Present simple ishlating.

  1. (it / not / rain / on Saturday) → I hope
  2. (you / enjoy / the film) → I hope
  3. (Nodira / pass / her driving test) → I hope
  4. (the bus / not / be / late) → I hope

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang: bashorat (will) yoki kelishilgan reja (present continuous).

  1. I (’ll see / ’m seeing) my grandparents on Sunday — Mum has already told them we’re coming.
  2. Look at this traffic! I’m sure we (’ll be / ’re being) late.
  3. “Are you free on Thursday evening?” “No, sorry. I (’ll play / ’m playing) football with Sardor.”
  4. Who do you think (will win / is winning) the World Cup next time?
  5. Don’t phone Jasur now. He (’ll be / is going to be) asleep — it’s 2 in the morning.

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. He shall be very happy with the news. →
  2. I won’t probably have time tomorrow. →
  3. Do you think will they come? →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • will / won’t — kelajak haqida bashorat, fikr, fakt: You’ll pass. I’ll be 18 next year.
  • Kelishilgan reja uchun will emas: I’m playing football on Thursday.
  • will bilan tez-tez: probably, I expect, I’m sure, I think, I don’t think, I wonder.
  • I’ll probably ... lekin I probably won’t ....
  • I hope + present simple: I hope it doesn’t rain. (will ham mumkin)
  • will hozir haqida taxmin: She’ll be at work now. That’ll be the courier.
  • I shall / we shall = I will / we will (rasmiyroq, BrE); shan’t = won’t. He/she/you/they bilan shall ❌.
