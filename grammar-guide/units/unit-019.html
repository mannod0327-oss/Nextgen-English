19

Present tenses (I am doing / I do) for the future

Hozirgi zamonlarning kelasi zamon ma’nosida ishlatilishi
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Present continuous (I am doing) kelajakdagi kelishilgan rejalar (arrangements) uchun qanday ishlatilishini: I’m meeting Jasur tomorrow.
  • Present simple (I do) jadval va dasturlar (timetables, programmes) uchun qanday ishlatilishini: The train leaves at 7.30.
  • Bu ikki shaklning farqini va nima uchun bunday gaplarda odatda will ishlatilmasligini.
  • Kelajak haqida gapirishning boshqa yo‘llari (going to, will) bilan qisqacha taqqoslashni.

Ingliz tilida kelajak haqida gapirish uchun har doim ham will kerak emas. Agar ish allaqachon kelishib qo‘yilgan bo‘lsa (vaqti belgilangan, chipta olingan, kimdir bilan gaplashib qo‘yilgan), present continuous ishlatiladi. Agar ish jadval yoki rasmiy dastur bo‘yicha bo‘lsa (poyezd, samolyot, kino, dars), present simple ishlatiladi. Fe’l shakli “hozirgi” bo‘lsa ham, ma’nosi — kelajak. Buni gapdagi tomorrow, next week, on Friday kabi so‘zlar yoki vaziyatning o‘zi ko‘rsatib turadi.

O‘zbek tilida kelajak haqida ko‘pincha hozirgi-kelasi zamon bilan gapiramiz: Ertaga Samarqandga boraman. Poyezd soat 8 da jo‘naydi. Shu sababli o‘quvchilar inglizchada ham ❌ I go to Samarkand tomorrow deb yuborishadi. Ingliz tilida present simple kelajak ma’nosida asosan jadvallar uchun ishlatiladi: The train leaves at 8 ✅. Shaxsiy kelishilgan rejalar uchun esa present continuous kerak: I’m going to Samarkand tomorrow ✅. Qiziq tomoni, o‘zbek so‘zlashuvida ham “Ertaga Samarqandga ketyapman” deymiz — bu ingliz tilidagi qoidaga juda o‘xshaydi!

Present continuous: kelishilgan rejalar Present continuous with a future meaning: arrangements

Yuqoridagi rasmlarga qarang. Dilnoza bu ishlarning hammasini oldindan hal qilgan va kelishib qo‘ygan: tish shifokoriga yozilgan, samolyot chiptasini olgan, buvisiga qo‘ng‘iroq qilib, yakshanba kuni borishini aytib qo‘ygan. Bunday tayyor, “kalendarga yozib qo‘yilgan” rejalar uchun ingliz tili present continuous ishlatadi.

Qoida: I’m doing something (tomorrow) = men buni qilishga allaqachon qaror qilganman va kelishib qo‘yganman. Shakl oddiy present continuous bilan bir xil (am / is / are + fe’l-ing), faqat ma’nosi — kelajak.

Ega+am / is / are+fe’l + ing+tomorrow / on Friday / next week ...

Misollar

  • I’m meeting Jasur at Chorsu bazaar on Saturday morning.Shanba kuni ertalab Jasur bilan Chorsu bozorida uchrashaman. (Kelishib qo‘yganmiz.)
  • We’re flying to Istanbul next Friday. I’ve already booked the tickets.Kelasi juma Istanbulga uchamiz. Chiptalarni allaqachon bron qilib qo‘ydim.
  • My brother is getting married in September.Akam sentyabrda uylanadi.
  • A: What are you doing this evening?
    B: Kamola and I are going to a football match.    — Bugun kechqurun nima qilasan? — Kamola bilan futbol o‘yiniga boramiz.
  • I’m not working on Friday, so I can help you move house.Juma kuni ishlamayman, shuning uchun uy ko‘chirishingizga yordam bera olaman.
  • Bekzod isn’t coming to the party on Sunday. He’s visiting his relatives in Andijan.Bekzod yakshanbadagi ziyofatga kelmaydi. U Andijondagi qarindoshlarini ko‘rgani boradi.
  • Are you doing anything special for your birthday?Tug‘ilgan kuningizda biror maxsus narsa qilasizmi?

Qanday reja “arrangement” hisoblanadi?

Faqat “xayolda” bor fikr emas, balki amalda tayyorlangan reja: uchrashuv vaqti kelishilgan, chipta olingan, stol bron qilingan, shifokorga yozilgan, mehmonlar taklif qilingan, ishxonada ruxsat olingan va hokazo. Ko‘pincha bunday rejada boshqa odamlar ham ishtirok etadi yoki vaqt va joy aniq bo‘ladi.

Bu ma’noda ko‘p ishlatiladigan fe’llar:
meetsee (a doctor / a friend)visitgocomeleaveflytravelstayhave (dinner / a party)playget marriedstart

Vaqt ko‘rsatkichi muhim

Present continuous ko‘pincha “ayni hozir” ma’nosini beradi (Unit 1). Shuning uchun kelajak ma’nosida gapda odatda kelajak vaqti bo‘ladi yoki vaziyatdan aniq bo‘ladi:

🕒 Hozir (now)

I’m playing football.

Hozir futbol o‘ynayapman.

📅 Kelajak (future)

I’m playing football tomorrow.

Ertaga futbol o‘ynayman (kelishib qo‘yganmiz).

Going to ham mumkin, will esa — odatda yo‘q

Bunday gaplarda be going to ham ishlatsa bo‘ladi (Unit 20), ma’no deyarli o‘zgarmaydi. Lekin kelishilgan reja haqida gapirganda will odatda ishlatilmaydi:

Kelishilgan reja: qaysi shakl?
O‘zbekcha✅ Eng tabiiy✅ Bu ham mumkin❌ Odatda emas
Bugun kechqurun nima qilasan?What are you doing tonight?What are you going to do tonight?What will you do tonight?
Akam kelasi oy uylanadi.My brother is getting married next month.My brother is going to get married next month.My brother will get married next month.
Ertaga ishlamayman.I’m not working tomorrow.I’m not going to work tomorrow.I won’t work tomorrow.

Will boshqa vaziyatlar uchun: gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilingan yangi qaror (The phone’s ringing. I’ll answer it.) yoki bashorat (You’ll pass the exam.). Kelishib qo‘yilgan reja uchun will qo‘llansa, gap g‘alati yoki boshqa ma’noda eshitiladi. Batafsil — Unit 21 va Unit 22.

Present simple: jadval va dasturlar Present simple with a future meaning: timetables and programmes

Agar kelajakdagi ish rasmiy jadval yoki dastur bo‘yicha bo‘lsa, present simple ishlatiladi. Bular: poyezd, avtobus va samolyot qatnovi, kino va konsert boshlanishi, televizor ko‘rsatuvlari, muzey va do‘konlarning ish vaqti, o‘quv yili, imtihonlar jadvali. Bu vaqtlarni biz o‘zimiz belgilamaymiz — ular jadvalda yozilgan.

Ega+fe’l (he/she/it + -s)+vaqt
What time+does / do+ega+fe’l?
  • Our train leaves at 7.30 tomorrow morning, so we need to be at the station by 7.15.Poyezdimiz ertaga ertalab 7:30 da jo‘naydi, shuning uchun soat 7:15 gacha vokzalda bo‘lishimiz kerak.
  • What time does the match start on Saturday?Shanba kuni o‘yin soat nechada boshlanadi?
  • The flight from Tashkent lands in London at 6.15 pm.Toshkentdan kelayotgan reys Londonga kechki 6:15 da qo‘nadi.
  • The exhibition opens on Thursday and finishes at the end of the month.Ko‘rgazma payshanba kuni ochiladi va oy oxirida tugaydi.
  • The new term begins next Monday.Yangi chorak (semestr) kelasi dushanbadan boshlanadi.
  • Tomorrow is 1 September. / It’s Monday tomorrow.Ertaga 1-sentyabr. / Ertaga dushanba.
Jadval ma’nosida ko‘p keladigan fe’llar:
leavedepartarrivetake offlandstartbeginfinishendopenclosebe (calendar)

Odamlarning “jadvalga o‘xshash” rejalari

Ba’zan shaxsiy reja ham jadvaldek qat’iy belgilangan bo‘ladi (ish boshlanish kuni, ish tugash vaqti). Bunday hollarda ham present simple ishlatish mumkin:

  • I start university in September.Sentyabrda universitetda o‘qishni boshlayman.
  • What time does your shift finish tomorrow?Ertaga smenangiz soat nechada tugaydi?
  • Our exams begin next week.Imtihonlarimiz kelasi haftada boshlanadi.

Lekin boshqa shaxsiy kelishuvlar uchun present continuous ancha tabiiyroq:
Where are you having lunch with Malika tomorrow? ✅ (not Where do you have lunch ...)
I’m having dinner with my parents tonight. ✅ (not I have dinner ...)

Taqqoslang: I am doing yoki I do? Compare: present continuous and present simple

Eng oddiy tekshirish savoli: bu vaqtni kim belgilagan? Agar odamlar o‘zaro kelishgan bo‘lsa — present continuous. Agar jadval, tashkilot, transport yoki dastur belgilagan bo‘lsa — present simple.

Odamlar va jadvallar
Present continuous — odamlarning rejasi (arrangement)Present simple — jadval / dastur (timetable)

Namanganga qachon qaytasan?		When does the next bus to Namangan leave?
Namanganga keyingi avtobus qachon jo‘naydi?

Finalni Sardornikida ko‘ramiz.		The final starts at 9 p.m.

Ota-onam juma kuni kelishadi.		Their plane arrives at 9.40.
Ularning samolyoti 9:40 da qo‘nadi.

Hayotda bu ikki shakl ko‘pincha bitta hikoyada yonma-yon keladi:

  • I’m going to Bukhara on Friday. My train leaves at 8.05 and arrives at 12.30. My uncle is meeting me at the station.Juma kuni Buxoroga boraman. Poyezdim 8:05 da jo‘naydi va 12:30 da yetib boradi. Amakim meni vokzalda kutib oladi.
  • We’re having a family dinner tonight. The restaurant closes at 11, so we can’t stay too late.Bugun kechqurun oilaviy kechki ovqatga boramiz. Restoran 11 da yopiladi, shuning uchun juda kech qololmaymiz.

Ba’zan ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi va ma’no deyarli farq qilmaydi: I start university in September / I’m starting university in September. Birinchisi rejani jadvaldek qat’iy qilib ko‘rsatadi, ikkinchisi — shaxsiy reja sifatida.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I meet my friend tomorrow at 5.I’m meeting my friend tomorrow at 5.Bu shaxsiy kelishuv, jadval emas. O‘zbekchadagi “uchrashaman” ni present simple bilan tarjima qilmang.
What do you do this weekend?What are you doing this weekend?“What do you do?” — “Kasbingiz nima?” yoki odat haqida savol. Reja haqida so‘rash uchun present continuous kerak.
My sister will get married in June. (to‘y allaqachon belgilangan)My sister is getting married in June.Kelishilgan reja uchun will odatda ishlatilmaydi.
The flight leave at 6.30 tomorrow.The flight leaves at 6.30 tomorrow.Present simple: he/she/it bilan fe’lga -s qo‘shiladi (the flight = it).
What time does start the film?What time does the film start?So‘roq tartibi: does + ega + fe’l.
Are you work tomorrow?Are you working tomorrow?Present continuous: are + fe’l-ing. -ing tushib qolmasin.
I’m having a lot of work tomorrow.I’ve got / I have a lot of work tomorrow.“Have” egalik/holat ma’nosida (ko‘p ishim bor) continuous bo‘lmaydi. (Lekin: I’m having dinner with Aziz tomorrow ✅ — bu harakat.)
I’m being in Samarkand next week.I’ll be in Samarkand next week. / I’m going to be in Samarkand next week.“Be” bu ma’noda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi. Bunday holatda will be yoki going to be deyiladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchada “Ertaga Jasur bilan uchrashaman” deymiz. Nega inglizchada “I meet Jasur tomorrow” xato?

Chunki ingliz tilida present simple kelajak ma’nosida asosan jadval va dasturlar uchun ishlatiladi (poyezd, kino, dars). Odamlar o‘zaro kelishgan reja uchun present continuous kerak: I’m meeting Jasur tomorrow. O‘zbekcha “uchrashaman” so‘zini har safar “I meet” deb tarjima qilmang — avval o‘ylang: bu jadvalmi yoki kelishuvmi?

Present continuous gap hozir haqidami yoki kelajak haqida — qanday bilaman?

Gapdagi vaqt so‘zlariga va vaziyatga qarang. I’m cooking dinner (hozir) — I’m cooking dinner for ten people on Saturday (kelajak). Kelajak ma’nosida odatda tomorrow, tonight, on Friday, next week, at 6 o’clock kabi so‘zlar bo‘ladi. Agar suhbat rejalar haqida ketayotgan bo‘lsa (What are you doing at the weekend?), vaqt so‘zi bo‘lmasa ham ma’no aniq.

“I’m meeting Aziz” va “I’m going to meet Aziz” orasida farq bormi?

Farq juda kichik, ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. I’m meeting — reja kelishilgan (Aziz ham biladi, vaqt belgilangan). I’m going to meet — men bunga qaror qilganman; kelishilgan bo‘lishi ham, bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin. Bu farq Unit 20 da batafsil.

“I start university in September” va “I’m starting university in September” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. O‘qish boshlanish sanasi jadvaldek qat’iy bo‘lgani uchun present simple ham mumkin. Present continuous esa buni shaxsiy reja sifatida ko‘rsatadi. Lekin “I meet Malika at 6” kabi oddiy shaxsiy uchrashuvlar uchun present simple g‘alati eshitiladi — u yerda present continuous ishlating.

Nega “What will you do tonight?” deb so‘rash yaxshi emas?

Grammatik jihatdan gap buzilmagan, lekin reja haqida so‘rash uchun tabiiy emas. Inglizlar rejani bilmoqchi bo‘lsa: What are you doing tonight? yoki What are you going to do tonight? deydi. Will ko‘proq gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilinadigan qaror yoki bashorat uchun: You’ve missed the last bus! What will you do now? (— Oxirgi avtobusdan qolib ketding! Endi nima qilasan? — vaziyat yangi, reja yo‘q).

“Tomorrow is Sunday” — nega bu yerda present simple?

Hafta kunlari, sanalar — bu kalendar, ya’ni eng qat’iy “jadval”. Shuning uchun: Tomorrow is Sunday. / It’s my birthday next Tuesday. / Navruz is on 21 March. “Tomorrow will be Sunday” ham uchraydi, lekin present simple ancha tabiiyroq.

Har qanday fe’l bilan present continuous’ni kelajak uchun ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Faqat harakat fe’llari bilan (meet, go, play, have dinner ...). Holat fe’llari (be, know, like, have = egalik qilmoq) continuous shaklda kelmaydi: ❌ I’m being at home tomorrow → I’ll be at home tomorrow ✅. ❌ I’m knowing the results tomorrow → I’ll know the results tomorrow ✅.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Kelishilgan rejalarni present continuous bilan yozing.

  1. I (meet) Sardor at the cinema at 7 tonight.
  2. We (fly) to Dubai next Tuesday.
  3. Malika (not / come) to the lesson tomorrow. She’s ill.
  4. My parents (have) a party on Saturday.
  5. you (do) anything on Sunday afternoon?
  6. Aziz (play) tennis with his boss on Thursday.

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang (present continuous yoki present simple).

  1. The bus to Fergana (is leaving / leaves) at 6.45 every morning, so we can catch it tomorrow.
  2. (Are you doing / Do you do) anything this evening?
  3. The concert (starts / is starting) at 8 according to the programme.
  4. I (see / ’m seeing) the doctor at 10 tomorrow — I made an appointment yesterday.
  5. According to the timetable, the last bus (leaves / is leaving) at 11.40 tonight.
  6. Kamola (is getting / gets) married next month. Everybody is invited.
  7. Tomorrow (is / is being) Friday.
  8. We (have / ’re having) dinner with Nodira’s family tonight.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Present continuous (am/is/are + -ing) — kelishilgan shaxsiy reja: I’m meeting Jasur tomorrow.
  • Kelajak ma’nosida odatda vaqt ko‘rsatkichi bo‘ladi: tomorrow, tonight, on Friday, next week.
  • Present simple — jadval, dastur, kalendar: The train leaves at 7.30. Tomorrow is Friday.
  • Jadvaldek qat’iy shaxsiy rejalar uchun ham present simple mumkin: I start university in September.
  • Reja haqida so‘rash: What are you doing ...? (What do you do? — “kasbing nima?”).
  • Kelishilgan reja uchun will odatda ishlatilmaydi; going to esa mumkin.
