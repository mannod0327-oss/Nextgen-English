62

Verb + preposition + -ing (succeed in -ing / accuse somebody of -ing etc.)

Fe’l + predlog + -ing
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Fe’l + predlog + -ing: succeed in finding, insist on paying, think of buying, look forward to seeing.
  • Fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi + predlog + -ing: accuse somebody of stealing, thank somebody for helping.
  • stop / prevent / discourage somebody (from) -ing qolipini: from qachon shart, qachon tushirilishi mumkin.
  • Bu iboralar majhul nisbatda, inkor shaklda va boshqa ega bilan qanday ishlatilishini: He was accused of stealing, accused of not paying.
  • Qaysi fe’l qaysi predlog bilan kelishini va nega o‘zbekcha qo‘shimchaga qarab taxmin qilib bo‘lmasligini.

Unit 60 dagi qoida shu yerda ham amal qiladi: predlogdan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi. Bu unitning asosiy vazifasi esa — qaysi fe’l qaysi predlog bilan birga ishlatilishini bilish. Har bir fe’lning o‘z predlogi bor va uni almashtirib bo‘lmaydi: succeed in (succeed of ❌), accuse somebody of (accuse for ❌).

O‘zbek tilida fe’llar kelishik qo‘shimchalari bilan birga ishlatiladi: ayblamoq -da (o‘g‘irlikda ayblamoq), qiziqmoq -ga, qo‘rqmoq -dan, orzu qilmoq -ni. Inglizchada ularning o‘rnini predlog egallaydi. Lekin ular o‘zaro bir-biriga mos kelmaydi: o‘zbekcha -ga inglizchada concentrate on, object to ham, decide against ham bo‘lishi mumkin; -dan esa worry about, prevent from. Shuning uchun har bir fe’lni predlogi bilan birga, bitta ibora sifatida yodlang.

Fe’l + predlog + -ing Verb + preposition + -ing

Ko‘p fe’llar o‘zidan keyin muayyan predlogni talab qiladi. Predlogdan keyin esa ot yoki -ing turadi: We talked about the trip / We talked about going to Spain.

Ega+fe’l+predlog+fe’l-ing

O‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha — turli predloglar

Quyidagi to‘rt kartochkada bir xil o‘zbekcha qo‘shimchaga mos keladigan, lekin turli inglizcha predloglar ko‘rsatilgan. Shuning uchun o‘zbekchadan taxmin qilish xavfli:

-da (… topishda)

  • succeed in finding
  • specialise in treating
  • insist on paying
  • accuse / suspect somebody of lying

-ga (… ishlashga)

  • concentrate on studying
  • depend on finding
  • object to working
  • decide against buying

-dan (… kutishdan)

  • worry about making mistakes
  • complain about waiting
  • prevent / discourage somebody from going
  • warn somebody against driving fast

-ni (… ko‘rishni)

  • look forward to seeing
  • dream of becoming
  • approve of working
  • think of buying
  • confess to breaking

look forward to, object to, confess to dagi to — predlog, shuning uchun keyin -ing: ❌ I look forward to see you → ✅ I look forward to seeing you (Unit 60, “it” sinovi: look forward to it ✅).

think of va think about bu yerda “rejalashtirmoq, o‘ylab ko‘rmoq” ma’nosida bir xil: I’m thinking of / about buying a laptop. Fikr so‘ralganda esa think of ko‘proq ishlatiladi: What do you think of living abroad? (“chet elda yashash haqida nima deb o‘ylaysiz?”).

stop, prevent, discourage somebody (from) -ing Verb + object + from + -ing

Biror ishning bo‘lishiga to‘sqinlik qilish ma’nosidagi fe’llardan keyin from + -ing keladi. Ba’zilarida from ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin, ba’zilarida — yo‘q:

from qachon shart?
Fe’lMisolfrom
stop somebody (from) doingThe rain stopped us (from) going out.ixtiyoriy (BrE da keng tarqalgan)
prevent somebody (from) doingNothing can prevent them (from) winning.ixtiyoriy (BrE da keng tarqalgan)
discourage somebody from doingThe high price discouraged many people from buying the car.shart

  • The heavy snow prevented the bus from reaching the village.Qalin qor avtobusning qishloqqa yetib borishiga yo‘l qo‘ymadi.
  • The noise discouraged me from studying at home.Shovqin meni uyda dars qilishdan qaytardi.
  • Nothing can keep Malika from reading before bed.Hech narsa Malikani uxlashdan oldin kitob o‘qishdan to‘xtata olmaydi.

Amerika ingliz tilida stop va prevent dan keyin ham odatda from yoziladi (stopped us from going). Shubha qilsangiz, from ni yozing — bu har doim to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi.

keep somebody doing (from siz) boshqa ma’no beradi: She kept me waiting — “u meni kutdirib qo‘ydi”. To‘sqinlik ma’nosi uchun keep ... from kerak. Va esda tuting: The rain prevented us to go out ❌ — infinitiv emas, (from) going.

Majhul, inkor va boshqa ega: -ing ning shakllari Passive, negative and other subject

Majhul nisbatda

Bu iboralarning aksariyati majhul nisbatda ham ishlatiladi: kim? gapning egasiga aylanadi, predlog o‘zgarmaydi:

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi fe’l qaysi predlog bilan kelishini qanday eslab qolaman?

O‘zbekcha qo‘shimchaga qarab taxmin qilib bo‘lmaydi (A bo‘limdagi to‘rt kartochka buni ko‘rsatadi). Eng yaxshi usul — fe’lni predlogi va bitta qisqa jumla bilan yodlash: succeed in finding a job. Predlog bo‘yicha guruhlash ham foydali: on — insist, depend, concentrate; of — think, dream, approve, accuse, suspect; for — thank, blame, forgive, criticise, apologise; from — stop, prevent, discourage, protect.

“accuse” va “suspect” farqi nima?

accuse somebody of — kimnidir ayblash (“sen buni qilding” deb aytish). suspect somebody of — kimdir buni qilgan deb gumon qilish, hali ochiq aytmasdan yoki isbotsiz. Tuzilishi bir xil: accuse / suspect somebody of + -ing.

“think of” va “think about” orasida farq bormi?

Rejalashtirish, o‘ylab ko‘rish ma’nosida ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va bir xil: I’m thinking of / about moving. “Haqida fikr so‘rash” ma’nosida think of ko‘proq ishlatiladi: What do you think of this idea? think about esa uzoqroq o‘ylashni bildiradi: I thought about it all night.

Nega “congratulate” dan keyin “for” emas, “on”?

Chunki ingliz tilida bu fe’l on bilan o‘rnashgan: congratulate somebody on. Uni tushuntiradigan mantiq yo‘q, shunchaki ibora sifatida yodlang. Xuddi shunday accuse somebody of, thank somebody for.

“stop” dan keyin “from” ni tashlab ketsam bo‘ladimi?

stop somebody (from) doing va prevent somebody (from) doing da from ixtiyoriy, ayniqsa Britaniya ingliz tilida. Lekin keep somebody from, discourage somebody from, ban somebody from, protect somebody from da from shart. Shubha qilsangiz, from ni yozing, xato bo‘lmaydi.

“I apologise for” va “I’m sorry for” bir xilmi?

Ma’nosi o‘xshash, lekin apologise rasmiyroq va to‘liqroq: I apologise for being late. Kundalik nutqda I’m sorry I’m late yoki Sorry for being late. apologise to somebody for something: She apologised to the teacher for being late. (AmE: apologize.)

“look forward to” bu unitda ham, Unit 60 da ham bor. Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri — shu ibora ikkala unitda ham uchraydi. Unit 60 to ning predlog ekanini ko‘rsatadi, bu unit esa fe’l + predlog juftligi sifatida o‘rgatadi. Gap bir xil: I look forward to seeing you.

Bu iboralardan keyin oddiy ot ham kela oladimi?

Ha. Predlogdan keyin ot ham, -ing ham kelishi mumkin: We talked about the trip. / We talked about going there. He was accused of theft. / He was accused of stealing.

3-mashq. Predlog va fe’lni -ing shaklida yozing (qavs ichidagi fe’ldan foydalaning).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Predlogdan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi (Unit 60). Bu unitda esa qaysi fe’l qaysi predlog bilan kelishi o‘rganildi.
  • Fe’l + predlog + -ing: succeed in, believe in, insist on, concentrate on, think of / about, dream of, approve of, worry / complain about, look forward to, object to, confess to, decide against, apologise for, feel like.
  • Fe’l + to‘ldiruvchi + predlog + -ing: accuse / suspect somebody of; charge somebody with; congratulate somebody on; thank / blame / forgive / criticise / praise / punish somebody for; warn somebody against.
  • stop / prevent somebody (from) -ing — from ixtiyoriy; discourage, keep, ban, protect somebody from -ing — from shart.
  • Majhul nisbatda predlog saqlanadi: He was accused of stealing. Inkor: accused of not paying; majhul: insisted on being told.
  • Predlogni o‘zbekcha qo‘shimchaga qarab taxmin qilmang — ibora sifatida yodlang. enjoy, suggest, avoid, deny kabi fe’llardan keyin predlog yo‘q.
