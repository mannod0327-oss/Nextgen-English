134

Verb + preposition 3: about and of

Fe’l + predlog — 3: about va of
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • hear fe’lining uch xil ko‘rinishi: hear about (voqeani eshitib bilmoq), hear of (borligini bilmoq), hear from (undan xabar olmoq).
  • think about bilan think of farqi: qachon “o‘ylab ko‘rmoq”, qachon “o‘ylab topmoq, esga tushirmoq, fikr bildirmoq”.
  • dream about / of va remind sb about / of: predlog almashsa, gapning ma’nosi ham o‘zgaradi.
  • complain (to sb) about va warn sb about / of: o‘zbekcha “-dan” bu yerda from emas!
  • Rasmli qaror jadvali: qaysi vaziyatda qaysi predlogni tanlash kerak.

Oldingi darslarda (Unit 132 va Unit 133) fe’l o‘zidan keyin aniq bir predlogni talab qilishini ko‘rdingiz: listen to, wait for. Bu darsning o‘ziga xosligi shundaki, bitta fe’l ikki yoki uch xil predlog bilan kela oladi va har safar ma’no o‘zgaradi. Taqqoslang: I heard of him (u bor ekanini bilaman) va I heard from him (u menga yozdi yoki qo‘ng‘iroq qildi). Predlogni almashtirsangiz, gap boshqa narsa haqida bo‘lib qoladi.

O‘zbek tilida bunday farqlar ko‘pincha bir xil fe’l bilan, lekin turli kelishik yoki haqida kabi so‘zlar bilan beriladi. Ba’zan o‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha to‘g‘ri yo‘l ko‘rsatadi (✅), ba’zan aldaydi (⚠️). Jadvalni diqqat bilan o‘qing: xatolar aynan ⚠️ qatorlardan chiqadi.

Eshitmoq: hear about, hear of, hear from Three different ways to “hear”

Unit 133 da hear (predlogsiz) “ovozni eshitmoq”, hear about esa “voqea haqida xabar topmoq” ekanini ko‘rgan edingiz. Endi ikkita yangi birikma qo‘shiladi. Uchalasi ham “eshitmoq” bilan bog‘liq, lekin nimani eshitganingiz har safar boshqacha:

hear ABOUT sth

Voqea yoki yangilik haqida xabar topmoq (biror kishi aytgan, siz o‘qigan yoki radiodan eshitgan).

  • I heard about the new bus route from my neighbour.
  • Have you heard about Rustam’s promotion?

hear OF sb / sth

Biror kishi yoki narsa borligini bilmoq, nomini eshitgan bo‘lmoq. Tafsilot shart emas.

  • I’d never heard of this poet until our teacher mentioned her.
  • Few tourists have heard of this little village.

hear FROM sb

Undan xat, xabar yoki qo‘ng‘iroq olmoq (u siz bilan bog‘landi).

  • I haven’t heard from Kamola since she moved to Seoul.
  • You’ll hear from us within a week.

hear from ni present perfect bilan ko‘p ishlatamiz, chunki gap “shu paytgacha xabar bo‘ldimi?” haqida ketadi: I haven’t heard from her since June. (Unit 7, Unit 12.) look forward to hearing da to — predlog, shuning uchun undan keyin -ing keladi (Unit 60).

Qo‘shimcha ma’nolar

  • I heard from Jasur that the match was cancelled.Jasurdan eshitishimcha (Jasur aytdi), o‘yin bekor qilingan.
  • I offered to pay, but my uncle wouldn’t hear of it.Men to‘lashni taklif qildim, lekin amakim bunga umuman rozi bo‘lmadi.

hear from sb yana “kimdandir eshitmoq, kim aytdi” ma’nosida ham keladi. won’t / wouldn’t hear of — “eshitishni ham istamaslik, rad qilish” degani (qat’iy ruxsat bermaslik). Bu ikkala holat ham kundalik nutqda uchraydi.

O‘zbekchada “eshitgan” so‘zi uch holatda ham ishlatiladi: voqeani eshitdim, nomini eshitganman, undan xabar eshitdim. Inglizchada esa uch holat uch predlogga bo‘linadi. Savol bering: nimani eshitdim? Yangilikni — about; borligini — of; odamning o‘zidan kelgan xabarni — from.

O‘ylamoq: think about va think of Consider it, or have an idea?

O‘zbekchada “o‘ylamoq” bitta fe’l, inglizchada esa ikkita predlog. Asosiy farq mana bunday: think about — biror narsa ustida o‘ylab ko‘rish, fikrni unga qaratish; think of — fikr yoki g‘oya paydo bo‘lishi, eslab qolish, fikr bildirish.

think about — o‘ylab ko‘rmoq

  • “Why are you so quiet?” “I’m thinking about tomorrow’s interview.”— Nega bunchalik jimsan? — Ertangi suhbat haqida o‘ylayapman.
  • Before you sign anything, think about what it means.Biror narsaga imzo qo‘yishdan oldin, uning nimani anglatishini o‘ylab ko‘ring.
  • I can’t stop thinking about that film.O‘sha film haqida o‘ylashni to‘xtata olmayapman.

  • It was a clever idea. Why didn’t I think of it?Bu zo‘r g‘oya edi. Nega buni men o‘ylab topmadim?
  • I know that actor, but I can’t think of his name.Bu aktyorni bilaman, lekin ismi esimga tushmayapti.
  • Can you think of a word that rhymes with “night”?“Night” bilan qofiyalanadigan so‘zni o‘ylab topa olasizmi?

Fikr bildirish: think of / about

Birovdan fikrini so‘rasak, ikkala predlog ham to‘g‘ri: What do you think of the idea? = What do you think about the idea? Ammo “yaxshi / yomon fikrdaman” deyiladigan ba’zi iboralarda faqat of ishlatiladi:

IboraMa’nosiMisol
think much of (inkorda)yuqori baho bermaslikI don’t think much of the food in this canteen.
think highly ofyuqori baholamoq, hurmat qilmoqEveryone thinks highly of Mr Rahimov.
think the world ofjuda yaxshi ko‘rmoq, qadrlamoqMalika thinks the world of her grandfather.
think of sb as sthkimnidir … deb hisoblamoqI think of Samarkand as my second home.

think of / about + -ing — rejalashtirmoq

“Biror ishni qilishni o‘ylayapman (hali qaror qilmadim)” deyish uchun think of doing yoki think about doing ishlatiladi. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ma’no deyarli bir xil:

  • We’re thinking of going to Khiva in May. = We’re thinking about going to Khiva in May.Biz may oyida Xivaga borishni o‘ylayapmiz.
  • Is Sardor thinking about changing his job?Sardor ishini almashtirishni o‘ylayaptimi?

Predlogdan keyin -ing keladi (Unit 60): ✅ thinking of going — ❌ thinking to go. Agar qaror allaqachon qabul qilingan bo‘lsa, think ishlatilmaydi: We’re going to Khiva in May. “Menimcha, …” degan fikr uchun predlogsiz: I think (that) it’s a good plan. ❌ I think of it’s a good plan.

O‘zbek tilida “bu haqda o‘ylab ko‘ring” = think about it (haqida = about). “Ism o‘ylab toping”, “esimga tushmayapti” esa think of bilan aytiladi, o‘zbekchada bunday ajratuvchi so‘z yo‘q. Shuning uchun ❌ think for, ❌ think on, ❌ think from kabi xatolar uchrab turadi: inglizchada faqat about va of bor.

Orzu qilmoq, eslatmoq, shikoyat qilmoq, ogohlantirmoq dream, remind, complain, warn

dream about va dream of

dream ABOUT — tush ko‘rmoq

Uyqu paytida ko‘rgan tush. (Bu ma’noda of ham uchraydi, lekin about tabiiyroq.)

  • Last night I dreamed about a huge white horse.
  • Malika had a strange dream about her old school.

dream OF — orzu qilmoq

Bo‘lishini istagan narsa, katta niyat. (Bu ma’noda about ham uchraydi.)

  • Bekzod has always dreamed of becoming a pilot.
  • She dreams of a quiet life in the mountains.

Eslab qoling: I wouldn’t dream of + -ing — “Buni aslo qilmayman, xayolimga ham kelmaydi” degani. Bu iborada faqat of bor:

  • I wouldn’t dream of telling your secret to anyone.Sirni hech kimga aytish xayolimga ham kelmaydi.
  • I never dreamed (that) our little shop would grow so big.Kichik do‘konimiz bunchalik kattalashadi deb tasavvur ham qilmagan edim.

Fe’lning o‘tgan zamon shakli: dreamed yoki dreamt [dremt]. Britaniya ingliz tilida ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, Amerika ingliz tilida odatda dreamed. Ot shakli: a dream about my school, a dream of becoming a doctor. I never dreamed (that) … gapida predlog yo‘q: gap bo‘laki to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri keladi.

remind sb about va remind sb of

O‘zbekcha “eslatmoq” ikki xil narsani bildiradi. Inglizchada ular ajratiladi:

remind sb ABOUT sth

Biror narsani unutmasligi uchun esiga solmoq.

  • Please remind me about my dentist appointment on Thursday.
  • Aziz reminded us about the football match at nine.

remind sb OF sb / sth

O‘xshashligi tufayli kimnidir yoki nimanidir eslatmoq, xotirani uyg‘otmoq.

  • This smell reminds me of my grandmother’s kitchen.
  • You remind me of your father when you laugh.
remind: asosiy tuzilmalar
TuzilmaMa’nosiMisolTarjima
remind sb about sthunutmasligi uchun eslatmoqRemind me about the party on Saturday.Shanba kungi ziyofatni eslatib qo‘ying.
remind sb to do sthqilish kerakligini eslatmoq (Unit 55)Remind me to buy bread on the way home.Uyga qaytishda non olishni eslatib qo‘ying.
remind sb of sb / stho‘xshashlik, xotirani uyg‘otishHe reminds me of my cousin from Fergana.U menga farg‘onalik amakivachchamni eslatadi.

That reminds me — “Yodimga tushdi” (predlogsiz): That reminds me — I must phone the bank. Rasmiy uslubda remind sb of “biror faktni esiga solmoq” ma’nosida ham keladi: Let me remind you of the rules: no phones in the exam.

remind bilan remember ni aralashtirmang. remember — o‘zingiz esda tutasiz; remind — boshqa odamning esiga solasiz: ✅ Remind me to call Mum. — ❌ Remember me to call Mum.

complain (to sb) about sth

Shikoyat kimdan qilinsa, uning oldidan to keladi; shikoyat mavzusi oldidan esa about:

ega+complain(+to shikoyat qilinadigan kishi)+about mavzu
  • The guests complained to the hotel manager about the noise.Mehmonlar mehmonxona boshqaruvchisiga shovqindan shikoyat qilishdi.
  • Jasur complained about his low salary all day.Jasur kun bo‘yi kam maoshidan shikoyat qildi.
  • Bekzod complained that the tea was cold.Bekzod choy sovuq ekanidan shikoyat qildi.
  • Grandma complained of a headache and went to bed early.Buvim boshi og‘riyotganidan shikoyat qilib, erta yotdi.

complain of + kasallik belgisi (og‘riq, bosh aylanishi) — tibbiy yoki rasmiy uslub. Boshqa hamma holatda — complain about. Gap bo‘lagi kelsa, predlog yo‘q: complain that + gap. ❌ complain him (to‘g‘risi: complain to him), ❌ complain from / for.

warn sb about / of sth

Xavf yoki muammodan ogohlantirsak, warn sb about yoki warn sb of deyiladi. about hamma narsa uchun (odam, joy, muammo) tabiiy; of ko‘pincha xavf yoki yaqinlashayotgan zararli holat uchun ishlatiladi va biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi.

  • The ranger warned the tourists about the wild boars near the river.Qo‘riqchi sayyohlarni daryo bo‘yidagi yovvoyi cho‘chqalardan ogohlantirdi.
  • The sign warns drivers of icy roads ahead.Belgi haydovchilarni oldinda muzlagan yo‘l borligidan ogohlantiradi.
  • My brother warned me about Rustam: he never returns borrowed books.Akam meni Rustamdan ogohlantirdi: u olgan kitoblarini hech qaytarmaydi.
warn: boshqa tuzilmalar
TuzilmaMisolTarjima
warn sb against doing sthDoctors warn people against eating too much sugar.Shifokorlar odamlarni juda ko‘p shakar yeyishdan ogohlantiradi.
warn sb (not) to do sthI warned Sardor not to touch the wire.Sardorni simga tegmaslik haqida ogohlantirdim.
warn sb that …The teacher warned us that the test would be difficult.O‘qituvchi bizni test qiyin bo‘lishidan ogohlantirdi.

“Xavfdan ogohlantirmoq” — o‘zbekcha “-dan” sizni warn from deyishga undaydi, lekin bu xato. To‘g‘risi: warn sb about / of danger. Xuddi shunday, “xizmatdan shikoyat qilmoq” — complain about, complain from emas.

Predlogdan keyin nima keladi?

Bu birikmalarda predlogdan keyin ot, olmosh yoki fe’l-ing keladi. Infinitiv (to do) kelmaydi:

✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Xato
I’m thinking of buying a bicycle.I’m thinking to buy a bicycle.
She dreams of becoming a doctor.She dreams to become a doctor.
They complained about waiting too long.They complained about to wait too long.
He warned us against swimming there.He warned us against to swim there.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

A shop called Bahor? I’ve never heard about it.A shop called Bahor? I’ve never heard of it.“Bunday joy (odam, narsa) borligini bilmayman” degan javob uchun standart ibora — hear of. Hear about voqea haqida xabar topishni bildiradi va bu yerda g‘alati eshitiladi.
I haven’t heard of Bekzod for a month.I haven’t heard from Bekzod for a month.Undan xat yoki qo‘ng‘iroq kelmagan — hear from. Hear of bunday ma’no bermaydi.
What do you think for the new bus station?What do you think of (about) the new bus station?Think bilan faqat of yoki about keladi: for, on, from emas.
I can’t think about his name.I can’t think of his name.Ism, so‘z yoki g‘oya esga tushishi — think of. Think about o‘ylab ko‘rishni bildiradi.
I dreamed my grandfather last night.I dreamed about my grandfather last night.“Tushimda ko‘rdim” uchun predlog kerak: dream about (yoki of). O‘zbekchada to‘ldiruvchi “-ni” bilan keladi, inglizchada esa predlog.
I wouldn’t dream about asking him for money.I wouldn’t dream of asking him for money.“Xayolimga ham kelmaydi” — qat’iy ibora: wouldn’t dream of + -ing.
This song reminds me about my school days.This song reminds me of my school days.Xotirani uyg‘otadigan yoki o‘xshash narsa — remind of. About “unutmasin” ma’nosida.
Remember me to buy milk, please.Remind me to buy milk, please.Boshqa odamning esiga solish — remind. Remember o‘zingiz eslab qolishingiz.
They complained from the noise to the landlord.They complained to the landlord about the noise.O‘zbekcha “-dan” — about. Kimga aytilsa, oldidan to. “Complain from” inglizchada yo‘q.
The guide warned the tourists from the danger.The guide warned the tourists about (of) the danger.“Xavfdan ogohlantirmoq” — warn about yoki of. “From” ishlatilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’ve heard of him” bilan “I know him” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. I’ve heard of him — “uning nomini eshitganman, bunday odam borligini bilaman”, lekin u bilan tanish bo‘lmasligingiz mumkin. I know him — “uni taniyman, u bilan tanishman”. Masalan, mashhur yozuvchi haqida: I’ve heard of him, but I don’t know him.

hear about va hear from ni qanday ajrataman?

Predlogdan keyin kelgan so‘zga qarang. about dan keyin odatda voqea yoki yangilik keladi (the accident, the exam results). from dan keyin esa kishi keladi (Aziz, my cousin, the bank). Ya’ni hear from — odam sizga xabar yuborgan.

think of va think about, ikkalasi ham “fikr”da bo‘lsa, ma’no farq qiladimi?

Fikr bildirish uchun (What do you think of / about it?) va reja uchun (thinking of / about going) ma’no deyarli bir xil, ikkalasini ham ishlatish mumkin. Farq asosan boshqa joylarda: “o‘ylab ko‘rmoq, mulohaza qilmoq” — about (Let me think about it); “o‘ylab topmoq, esga tushirmoq” — of (I can’t think of the word). Shuningdek, think highly of, think much of faqat of bilan.

“I’m thinking of you” deyish mumkinmi? Bu qanday ma’no beradi?

Ha, bu juda tabiiy: I was thinking of you — “sizni esladim, xayolimda siz edingiz”. Ko‘pincha mehr yoki g‘amxo‘rlik bilan aytiladi, masalan, kartochkada: Thinking of you. I was thinking about you ham bo‘ladi, ma’no deyarli shunday.

“I wouldn’t dream of it” nimani anglatadi?

Bu qat’iy rad etish: “Buni aslo qilmayman, xayolimga ham kelmaydi”. Odatda biror taklif yoki ayb qo‘yilgan paytda aytiladi: “Would you ever sell your grandfather’s watch?” “I wouldn’t dream of it!” Gap bu yerda orzu haqida emas, balki “mutlaqo yo‘q” degan ma’noda.

remind sb about va remind sb of o‘rtasida chegara aniqmi? Ba’zan ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri bo‘ladimi?

Asosiy qoida: unutmaslik uchun esiga solish — about yoki to + fe’l; o‘xshashlik yoki xotira uyg‘onishi — of. Rasmiy uslubda remind you of the deadline deyiladi (muddatni yana bir bor eslatmoq), bu about bilan ham mumkin. Boshlovchi uchun eng ishonchli yo‘l: kundalik so‘rovda remind me about / to, o‘xshashlik uchun reminds me of.

warn about, warn of va warn against farqi nimada?

warn about / of — nimadan ogohlantirmoq (xavf, muammo, kishi). warn against doing — nimadir qilmaslikka undamoq: warn against smoking. warn sb not to do — buyruq kabi ogohlantirish. Yana warn sb that + gap. Bularning hammasida from yo‘q.

dreamed va dreamt orasida farq bormi?

Ma’no bir xil. Britaniya ingliz tilida ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi: I dreamed / dreamt about you. Amerika ingliz tilida odatda faqat dreamed. Talaffuzda dreamt [dremt] — “ea” qisqa o‘qiladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga about, of yoki from yozing (hear).

  1. “Have you read anything by this poet?” “No, I’ve never heard him. Is he famous?”
  2. Dilnoza hasn’t heard her cousin since June: he hasn’t written or phoned.
  3. Did you hear the accident on the road to Samarkand? It was on the news.
  4. I had never heard that brand of phone; I didn’t even know it existed.
  5. We will email all the winners. If you don’t hear us by Friday, you haven’t won.
  6. We heard the new rules from our teacher this morning; she explained them all.
  7. I wanted to pay, but my grandfather wouldn’t hear it.
  8. I look forward to hearing you soon.

2-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga about yoki of yozing. Agar ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ikkalasini yozing.

  1. I can’t think the name of that film.
  2. “Let me think your offer,” said Sardor. “I’ll answer on Monday.”
  3. What did you think the concert? (siz uning haqida fikringizni so‘rayapsiz)
  4. Dilnoza is thinking becoming a vet. (hali qaror qilmagan)
  5. Last night I dreamed my old school. (uyquda)
  6. I wouldn’t dream lying to you.
  7. This street reminds me my village.
  8. Please remind me the dentist tomorrow; I always forget.

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. The guests complained (to / for / from) the manager about the cold room.
  2. She complained (about / from / for) the slow service.
  3. The sign warns drivers (of / from / to) icy roads.
  4. The doctor warned him (against / from / for) eating too much salt.
  5. “Remind me (to / of / for) call Mum tonight.”

🧠 Esda tuting

  • hear about = voqeani eshitib bilmoq; hear of = borligini bilmoq; hear from = undan xat, qo‘ng‘iroq yoki xabar olmoq.
