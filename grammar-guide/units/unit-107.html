Comparison 3 (as ... as / than)

Qiyosiy daraja — 3 (as ... as / than)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • as … as bilan tenglikni ifodalashni: Malika is as tall as Dilnoza.
  • not as … as bilan kamroq darajani bildirishni: This hotel isn’t as expensive as that one.
  • twice as … as, half as … as, three times as … as — necha baravar ekanini aytishni; nearly, just, not quite, nowhere near bilan aniqlashtirishni.
  • the same (as) va different (from) ni to‘g‘ri ishlatishni.
  • than me yoki than I am? — than va as dan keyin olmosh qanday shaklda keladi.

105- va 106-darslarda qiyosiy daraja (taller than) bilan ish ko‘rgan edik. Bu darsda taqqoslashning yana ikki yo‘li bor: as … as (ikki narsa teng) va not as … as (birinchi narsa ikkinchisidan kam).

O‘zbek tilida tenglik -dek, -day, -chalik, kabi, xuddi so‘zlari bilan beriladi: Malika Dilnozadek baland, Sardorchalik tez yugura olmayman. Inglizchada buning uchun ikkita as kerak: as + sifat/ravish + as. Birinchi as sifatdan oldin, ikkinchisi taqqoslanayotgan narsadan oldin turadi. Bu yerda than ishlatilmaydi! O‘zbekcha “-dan” (Dilnozadan baland) = than, “-dek / -chalik” (Dilnozadek baland) = as … as. Ikkalasini aralashtirmang.

Tenglik: as … as Malika is as tall as Dilnoza

Ikki narsa biror jihatdan bir xil darajada bo‘lsa, as + sifat + as ishlatamiz. Ikkala as o‘rtasidagi sifat oddiy shaklda turadi: tall, cheap, fast — taller yoki more expensive emas.

A+be+as+sifat+as+B

Qoida: as + sifat/ravish (oddiy shakl) + as. Sifat o‘zgarmaydi: as tall as ✅, as taller as ❌, as more expensive as ❌.

Sifat bilan

  • Dilnoza is as tall as Malika.Dilnoza Malikadek baland.
  • This laptop is as expensive as that one.Bu noutbuk anavisichalik qimmat.
  • The new route is as short as the old one.Yangi yo‘l eskisidek qisqa.
  • Your English is as good as mine.Sizning inglizchangiz meniki kabi yaxshi.

Ravish bilan

  • Sardor runs as fast as his brother.Sardor akasidek tez yuguradi.
  • I get up as early as my father.Men otamdek erta turaman.
  • Please speak as clearly as you can.Iltimos, qo‘lingizdan kelgancha aniq gapiring.

Miqdor bilan: as much as / as many as

Sanalmaydigan ot bilan as much … as, sanaladigan ko‘plik ot bilan as many … as (Unit 87):

  • I don’t drink as much tea as my grandmother.Men buvimchalik ko‘p choy ichmayman.
  • Malika has as many books as Dilnoza.Malikaning kitoblari Dilnozaning kitoblari qadar ko‘p.

Kuchaytirish: just as … as, every bit as … as

just yoki every bit qo‘shsangiz, “mutlaqo teng” degan ma’no kuchayadi:

  • This sofa is just as comfortable as the expensive one.Bu divan ham xuddi anavi qimmat divandek qulay.
  • She is every bit as clever as her brother.U zakovatda akasidan hech kam emas.

Diqqat: as dan keyin than ham, like ham kelmaydi: as tall as me ✅ — as tall than me ❌ — as tall like me ❌.

Kamroq daraja: not as … as This hotel isn’t as expensive as that one

Birinchi narsa ikkinchisiga yetmasa (kam bo‘lsa), not as … as ishlatiladi. Bu qiyosiy daraja (than) bilan bir xil ma’noni beradi, faqat gap “teskari” tuziladi:

Bir fikr — ikki xil aytilishi
than bilannot as … as bilan
Tashkent is bigger than Bukhara.Bukhara is not as big as Tashkent.
The bus is slower than the train.The bus is not as fast as the train.
Sardor is older than Jasur.Jasur is not as old as Sardor.
Bukhara is less crowded than Tashkent.Bukhara is not as crowded as Tashkent.

O‘zbekchada: Buxoro Toshkentchalik katta emas yoki Buxoro Toshkentdan kichikroq. Ikkalasi ham bitta fikr. Inglizchada ham shunday: not as big as = smaller than.

  • This hotel isn’t as expensive as I thought.Bu mehmonxona men o‘ylaganchalik qimmat emas.
  • The film wasn’t as good as the book.Film kitobchalik yaxshi emas edi.
  • I can’t run as fast as Sardor.Men Sardorchalik tez yugura olmayman.
  • I’m not as tired as I was yesterday.Men kechagidek charchagan emasman.
  • We didn’t spend as much money as last year.Biz o‘tgan yilchalik ko‘p pul sarflamadik.

Farq qanchalik katta? — nearly, just, not quite, nowhere near

as … as ning oldiga so‘z qo‘yib, farq yoki o‘xshashlik darajasini aniqroq aytish mumkin:

as … as oldidan keladigan so‘zlar
IboraMa’nosiMisol
just as … asaynan tengThis one is just as good as that one.
nearly / almost as … asdeyarli tengMy old phone is almost as fast as the new one.
not quite as … asjuda oz kamMy room is not quite as big as yours.
not nearly as … asancha kamIt’s not nearly as cold as last winter.
nowhere near as … as (norasmiy)juda-juda kamThis café is nowhere near as busy as the one in Chorsu.

Inkor gapda not so … as ham aytish mumkin (It isn’t so hot as yesterday), lekin hozirgi ingliz tilida not as … as ancha keng tarqalgan. Ijobiy gapda faqat as … as ishlatiladi (so … as emas).

Boshqa shakl: twice the size of …

Shu ma’noni ko‘paytiruvchi + the + ot + of bilan ham aytish mumkin:

  • The new room is twice the size of the old one.Yangi xona eskisidan ikki baravar katta (maydoni).
  • This jacket is half the price of that one.Bu kurtka anavisining yarim narxida.

Eng ko‘p xato: twice taller than me ❌ → twice as tall as me ✅. Kundalik nutqda three times more expensive than ham eshitiladi, lekin u noaniq bo‘lishi mumkin; three times as expensive as eng aniq va xavfsiz shakl.

Bir xil yoki boshqacha: the same (as) the same as / different from

Ikki narsa aynan bir xil bo‘lsa, the same ishlatiladi. The so‘zi har doim kerak: same age ❌, the same age ✅.

the same va different
QolipMisolTarjima

the same + ot + asMalika is the same age as Dilnoza.Malika Dilnoza bilan tengdosh (bir yoshda).

Ikkala narsa gapning egasi bo‘lsa, as kerak emas: Malika and Dilnoza are the same age. (Malika va Dilnoza tengdosh.)

as … as va the same — bir ma’no

  • Malika is the same height as Dilnoza.Malikaning bo‘yi Dilnozaning bo‘yi bilan bir xil.
  • Malika is as tall as Dilnoza.Malika Dilnozadek baland.
  • Your pronunciation is different from mine.Sening talaffuzing menikidan farq qiladi.

Than me yoki than I am? Than / as + me or I am

than va as dan keyin olmoshni ikki xil shaklda aytish mumkin:

1) Obyekt olmosh (norasmiy)

me, him, her, us, them

  • He is taller than me.
  • I’m not as patient as her.

Kundalik nutqda juda keng tarqalgan.

2) Ega olmosh + fe’l (rasmiyroq)

I am, he is, she does, we can …

  • He is taller than I am.
  • I’m not as patient as she is.

Yozuvda va imtihonda xavfsiz tanlov.

Ikkala shakl yonma-yon
NorasmiyTo‘liq shakl
Jasur is taller than me.
Jasur mendan baland.		Jasur is taller than I am.
She speaks English better than him.
U ingliz tilida undan yaxshiroq gapiradi.		She speaks English better than he does.
The Karimovs have a bigger garden than us.
Karimovlarning bog‘i biznikidan kattaroq.		The Karimovs have a bigger garden than we do.
I can’t cook plov as well as her.
Men u kabi mazali palov pishira olmayman.		I can’t cook plov as well as she can.

Qoida: To‘liq shaklda than / as dan keyingi fe’l birinchi gapdagi fe’lga mos keladi. Birinchi gapda be bo‘lsa — am / is / are / was; oddiy fe’l bo‘lsa — do / does / did; can bo‘lsa — can; have bo‘lsa — have / do.

  • She sings better than I do. (sings → do)U mendan yaxshiroq kuylaydi.
  • He was more nervous than I was. (was → was)U mendan ko‘ra ko‘proq xavotirda edi.
  • My brother can swim faster than I can.Akam mendan tezroq suza oladi.

Than I (fe’lsiz, He is taller than I.) juda rasmiy va eskirgan tuyuladi; tabiiy variantlar: than me yoki than I am. Than me am, than I does kabi aralash shakllar xato.

O‘zbekchada olmosh “-dan” bilan keladi: mendan, undan, bizdan — ya’ni bitta shakl. Inglizchada esa than dan keyin ikki yo‘l bor: than me yoki than I am. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, faqat ohangi (norasmiy / rasmiyroq) farq qiladi.

as … as ko‘p qotib qolgan iboralarda uchraydi. Ularni yodlab oling:

  • Please reply as soon as possible.Iltimos, iloji boricha tezroq javob bering.
  • Take as many apples as you like.Xohlagancha olma oling.
  • Drink as much water as you can.Qo‘lingizdan kelgancha ko‘proq suv iching.
  • Call me as often as you want.Xohlagancha tez-tez qo‘ng‘iroq qiling.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

as … as bilan than ni qachon ishlataman?

Ikki narsa teng bo‘lsa — as … as. Biri ortiq bo‘lsa — qiyosiy daraja + than (taller than, more expensive than). Biri kam bo‘lsa — yoki not as … as, yoki qarama-qarshi sifat bilan than. Masalan, Bukhara is not as big as Tashkent = Bukhara is smaller than Tashkent = Tashkent is bigger than Bukhara.

Not as … as va not so … as bir xilmi?

Ma’no bir xil. Not so … as eskiroq, kitobiy ohangga ega; hozirgi nutq va yozuvda not as … as ko‘proq ishlatiladi. Ijobiy gapda faqat as … as: so tall as ❌.

“Ikki baravar baland” — twice taller deyish mumkinmi?

Ingliz tilida standart shakl — twice as tall as. Twice taller than grammatika bo‘yicha noto‘g‘ri deb hisoblanadi. “Uch baravar qimmat” uchun ham three times as expensive as deng. Kundalik nutqda three times more expensive than eshitiladi, lekin ba’zida “3 baravar” mi yoki “4 baravar” mi (asl narx + 3 baravar ko‘p) degan noaniqlik tug‘iladi.

than me va than I am — imtihonda qaysi birini yozay?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, shuning uchun testlarda odatda ikkalasi ham javob sifatida qabul qilinadi. Og‘zaki nutqda than me juda tabiiy; yozma ishda va rasmiy vaziyatda than I am xavfsizroq va ancha ravon ko‘rinadi.

Different from, different to va different than — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Britaniya ingliz tilida different from eng xavfsiz; different to ham eshitiladi. Amerika ingliz tilida different from yoki different than. O‘quvchi sifatida different from ni o‘rganing — u hamma joyda to‘g‘ri.

as much as bilan as many as ning farqi nima?

Sanalmaydigan otlar (water, money, time, rice) bilan — as much … as. Sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar (books, apples, students) bilan — as many … as. Xuddi much / many (Unit 87) kabi.

Malika is the same height as Dilnoza bilan Malika is as tall as Dilnoza farq qiladimi?

Ma’no bir xil. Birinchisida the same + ot (height) + as, ikkinchisida as + sifat (tall) + as. Ikkalasida ham keyingi so‘z as bilan boshlanadi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga to‘g‘ri so‘zni yozing (qavs ichidagi variantlardan tanlang).

  1. Dilnoza is 170 cm and Malika is 170 cm. Malika is as as Dilnoza. (tall / taller)
  2. This café is as as the one near the station. (cheap / cheaper)
  3. Please send the documents as as possible. (soon / sooner)
  4. I don’t drink as coffee as my father. (many / much)
  5. Malika has as friends as Dilnoza. (many / much)
  6. Our new flat is the same size the old one. (as / than / like)

2-mashq. Gapni not as … as bilan qayta yozing (ma’no o‘zgarmasin).

  1. Tashkent is bigger than Samarkand. → Samarkand Tashkent.
  2. The train is faster than the bus. → The bus the train.
  3. Sardor is older than Jasur. → Jasur Sardor.
  4. Winter is colder than autumn. → Autumn winter.
  5. My old phone was more expensive than my new one. → My new phone my old one.

3-mashq. Bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring: twice / half / three times / ten times / sixty times.

  1. A marathon is about 42 km and a half-marathon is about 21 km. A marathon is as long as a half-marathon.
  2. A kilometre is 1,000 metres. A kilometre is as long as a hundred metres.
  3. I’m 10 and my brother is 20. I’m as old as my brother.
  4. A taxi ride costs 30,000 soums and a bus ride costs 10,000 soums. The taxi is as expensive as the bus.
  5. An hour has 60 minutes. An hour is as long as a minute.
  6. Our old flat was 80 square metres and the new one is 40. The new flat is as big as the old one.

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. She is as (tall / taller) as her mother.

  5. He earns twice (as much / more much / much more) as I do.
  6. The film wasn’t as good as (I / me) expected.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • as + sifat/ravish + as = teng. Sifat oddiy shaklda: as tall as (as taller as ❌). As dan keyin than yoki like emas.
  • not as … as = kamroq: Bukhara is not as big as Tashkent = Bukhara is smaller than Tashkent.
  • Aniqlashtirish: just / almost / not quite / not nearly / nowhere near as … as.
  • Necha baravar: twice / three times / half as … as (twice taller ❌).
  • the same as — bir xil (the shart!); different from — boshqacha.
  • than / as dan keyin: me (norasmiy) yoki I am / he does / she can (to‘liq, fe’l birinchi gapga mos).
  • much — sanalmaydigan otlar bilan, many — sanaladigan ko‘plik otlar bilan: as much water as, as many books as.
