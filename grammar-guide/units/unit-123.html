In/at/on (position) 1

In / at / on — joy predloglari 1
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • in — biror narsaning ichida: in a room, in a box, in a garden, in a town, in a country, in the water.
  • at — joyni bir nuqta sifatida ko‘rish: at the bus stop, at the door, at the window, at the traffic lights, at the top / bottom of, at the end of the street.
  • on — biror narsaning sirtida, yuzasida: on the ceiling, on the wall, on the floor, on the table, on a shelf, on a plate, on a balcony, on a page, on an island.
  • in the corner of a room bilan at / on the corner of a street ni.
  • in the front / back of a car, at the front / back of a building va on the front / back of a letter o‘rtasidagi farqni.

Asosiy g‘oya oddiy. Narsaning joyini tasvirlayotganda uch xil fikr bo‘lishi mumkin: u biror chegara ichida (in), biror sirtga tegib turibdi (on) yoki shunchaki ma’lum nuqtada, masalan uchrashadigan yoki biror ish bo‘ladigan joyda (at). Bu darsda eng ko‘p uchraydigan joylar bilan boshlaymiz, davomi Unit 124 da.

O‘zbek tilida bu uchala ma’no bitta -da qo‘shimchasi bilan beriladi: xonada, devorda, bekatda. Shuning uchun “-da” ni ko‘rganda qaysi predlog kerakligini o‘zbekchadan bilib bo‘lmaydi, masalan, stolda ham on the table (stol ustida), ham at the table (stol atrofida o‘tirib) bo‘lishi mumkin. Yaxshi usul: gapni “ichida / ustida / yonida” so‘zlari bilan qayta aytib ko‘ring. Ko‘pincha ichida → in, ustida → on, yonida, oldida → at chiqadi. Yana bir tuzoq: stolga qo‘ydim, devorga osdim da -ga qo‘shimchasi bor, lekin inglizchada baribir on ishlatiladi: I put it on the table (❌ to the table).

In — ichida Inside a space or an area

In bilan biror narsa chegaralangan joyning ichida bo‘ladi: yopiq makon (xona, quti, sumka), o‘ralgan hudud (hovli, bog‘) yoki katta hudud (shahar, davlat). Suyuqlik bilan ham in: odam yoki baliq suvning ichida.

At — nuqta A point where people meet or things happen

At bilan joyni bir nuqta sifatida ko‘ramiz: odamlar u yerda kutadi, to‘xtaydi, uchrashadi yoki biror ish qiladi. Biz uning ichki tuzilishiga e’tibor bermaymiz. Shuning uchun at ko‘pincha yonida, oldida ma’nosiga yaqin.

O‘zbek tilida bekatda, eshikda, derazada deymiz, lekin ma’no ko‘pincha bekat yonida, eshik oldida, deraza oldida. Ingliz tilida ham shu mantiq: at the door — eshik oldida (eshikning o‘zi emas). Eshikning sirti haqida bo‘lsa, on the door deymiz.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

In, on, at ni tez ajratishning qulay usuli bormi?

Ha, o‘zingizdan so‘rang: bu narsa ichida mi (in), sirtiga tegib turibdimi (on) yoki shunchaki shu nuqtada, uning yonidami (at)? Gapni o‘zbekcha “ichida / ustida / yonida” bilan aytib ko‘ring. Bu 100% qoida emas, chunki ba’zi iboralar qotib qolgan (masalan, in bed, on page 5, at night), lekin ko‘p hollarda to‘g‘ri tanlashga yordam beradi.

“At the table” bilan “on the table” ning farqi nimada?

At the table — stol atrofida o‘tirgan odamlar haqida: We were sitting at the table. On the table — stolning ustida yotgan narsa haqida: The keys are on the table. O‘zbekchada ikkalasi ham “stolda” bo‘ladi.

“On the wall” deymiz, lekin “in the wall” ham bormi?

Bor, lekin boshqa ma’noda. Devorga osilgan yoki yopishgan narsa — on the wall (rasm, soat, plakat). Devorning ichida bo‘lgan narsa — in the wall: There is a crack in the wall (devorda yoriq bor), a small safe in the wall (devor ichiga o‘rnatilgan seyf).

Nega “on page 48” deymiz-u, “in the book” deymiz?

Sahifa — yassi yuza, shuning uchun on page 48 (sahifaning sirtida). Kitob esa ko‘p sahifalardan iborat “idish” kabi: in the book (kitobning ichida). Ikkalasini birga aytsa ham bo‘ladi: It’s in this book, on page 48.

“On an island” deymiz, lekin “in Japan” — nega?

Orol — quruqlikning kichik bo‘lagi, biz uning sirtida yashaymiz: on an island. Lekin Yaponiya, Kuba kabi orol davlatlar uchun davlat sifatida qaraladi, shuning uchun in Japan, in Cuba.

“At the corner”, “on the corner” va “in the corner” ni qanday ajrataman?

In the corner — xonaning, qutining, ekranning ichki burchagi. Ko‘cha burchagi uchun at the corner (siz o‘sha nuqtada turibsiz yoki kutyapsiz) va on the corner (burchakda joylashgan bino yoki do‘kon). Ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri (There is a café at / on the corner), lekin in the corner of the street deyilmaydi.

“In front of the cinema” bilan “at the front of the cinema” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. In front of the cinema — kinoteatr binosining oldida, tashqarida (kirish oldida). At the front of the cinema — zalning ichida, ekran tomondagi old qismda. Yana: in the front of a car — mashinaning ichidagi old o‘rindiqlar.

“At the traffic lights” da nega “lights” ko‘plikda?

Britaniya inglizchasida svetofor odatda traffic lights (bir nechta chiroq) deyiladi, shuning uchun ko‘plik. Amerika inglizchasida traffic light yoki stoplight ham ishlatiladi. Predlog ikkalasida ham at.

Bino ichida bo‘lish uchun “in” mi yoki “at” mi (masalan, at the cinema)?

Bu juda ko‘p so‘raladigan savol, uning batafsil javobi Unit 125 da: at home, at work, at school, at a party, at the cinema va in the cinema farqi shu yerda tushuntirilgan.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • in — ichida: in a room, in a box, in a garden, in a town, in a country, in the water.
  • on — sirtida: on the ceiling, on the wall, on the door, on the floor, on the table, on a shelf, on a plate, on a balcony, on page 5, on an island.
  • at — nuqta: at the bus stop, at the door, at the window, at the traffic lights, at the top / bottom of, at the end of the street.
  • Bir so‘z, uch xil ma’no: at the table (atrofida) / on the table (ustida); at the door (oldida) / on the door (sirtida) / in the doorway (oralig‘ida).
  • Front / back: in the back of a car; at the back of a cinema, a class, a house; on the back of a letter, a card, a door.
  • Corner: in the corner of a room; at / on the corner of a street. ❌ in the corner of the street.
  • O‘zbekcha -da hammasini qoplaydi, shuning uchun ichida / ustida / yonida bilan tekshiring. stolga qo‘ydim = I put it on the table (❌ to the table).
