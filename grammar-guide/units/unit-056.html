56

Verb + -ing or to ... 1 (remember/regret etc.)

Fe’l + -ing yoki to ... — 1 (remember / regret)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Qaysi fe’llardan keyin -ing, qaysilaridan keyin to + fe’l kelishini qisqacha takrorlaysiz (Unit 53, Unit 54).
  • remember va forget: remember doing va remember to do ma’nosi butunlay boshqa ekanini.
  • regret va go on (hamda qo‘shimcha ravishda stop) bilan ham shunday farq borligini.
  • begin, start, continue, intend, bother dan keyin esa ikkala shakl ham deyarli bir xil ma’no berishini.

Ko‘p fe’llar faqat bitta shakl bilan keladi: enjoy doing, decide to do. Lekin ba’zi fe’llar ikkala shakl bilan ham keladi — va shakl o‘zgarsa, ma’no ham o‘zgaradi. Bu darsning “kaliti” bitta savol: qaysi harakat oldin bo‘ldi?

Asosiy g‘oya: -ing odatda orqaga qaraydi — harakat allaqachon bo‘lgan yoki davom etib kelyapti. to + fe’l esa oldinga qaraydi — harakat keyin bo‘ladi (yoki bo‘lishi kerak).

O‘zbek tilida bu farqni qo‘shimchalar beradi: dazmolni o‘chirganimni eslayman (o‘tmishdagi xotira) va dazmolni o‘chirishni unutmayman / esdan chiqarmayman (kelgusidagi vazifa). Ingliz tilida esa fe’l bir xil — remember — faqat undan keyingi shakl o‘zgaradi: remember switching off va remember to switch off. O‘zbekcha -ganim ko‘pincha -ing ga, -ish(ni) esa ko‘pincha to ga to‘g‘ri keladi.

-ing bilan keladigan va to bilan keladigan fe’llar Verb + -ing and verb + to ...

Avval eslab olaylik: ba’zi fe’llardan keyin boshqa fe’l faqat -ing shaklida keladi, boshqalaridan keyin esa faqat to + fe’l (infinitive) shaklida. Bu yerda mantiq qidirish shart emas — bu fe’llarni ro‘yxat sifatida yodlash kerak.

Fe’l + -ing

  • admit, avoid, consider, deny
  • enjoy, fancy, finish, imagine
  • keep (on), mind, miss, risk
  • postpone, put off, suggest
  • give up, carry on, stop

Fe’l + to ...

  • afford, agree, arrange, attempt
  • decide, deserve, expect, fail
  • forget, hope, learn, manage
  • offer, plan, promise, refuse
  • seem, tend, threaten, want
  • I don’t mind waiting. Take your time.Kutsam ham mayli. Shoshilmang.
  • Have you finished cleaning your room?Xonangni yig‘ishtirib bo‘ldingmi?
  • Would you ever consider moving to Samarkand?Qachondir Samarqandga ko‘chib o‘tishni o‘ylab ko‘rarmidingiz?
  • Bekzod has decided to study medicine.Bekzod tibbiyotni o‘qishga qaror qildi.
  • We can’t afford to buy a new car this year.Bu yil yangi mashina sotib olishga qurbimiz yetmaydi.
  • Malika promised to call me after the exam.Malika imtihondan keyin menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishga va’da berdi.

Bu ro‘yxatlar Unit 53 (fe’l + -ing) va Unit 54 (fe’l + to) da batafsil berilgan. Bu darsda esa ikkala shakl bilan ham keladigan fe’llarni o‘rganamiz. Ularning bir qismida ma’no o‘zgaradi (B, C bo‘limlar), bir qismida esa deyarli o‘zgarmaydi (D bo‘lim).

remember va forget remember doing / remember to do

remember + -ing: avval qildingiz, endi eslaysiz

I remember doing something = men buni qilganman va hozir o‘sha voqeani eslayman. Harakat oldin, eslash keyin. Bu — xotira haqida.

  • Don’t worry, the gas is off. I remember turning it off before we left.Xavotir olma, gaz o‘chiq. Chiqishimizdan oldin uni o‘chirganimni eslayman.
  • Do you remember meeting Sardor at the wedding last summer?O‘tgan yozda to‘yda Sardor bilan tanishganingni eslaysanmi?
  • I don’t remember saying that. Are you sure it was me?Bunday deganimni eslolmayman. Aniq men aytganmanmi?
  • I remember my grandfather telling me stories about old Bukhara.Bobomning menga eski Buxoro haqida hikoyalar aytib berganini eslayman.

Oxirgi misolga e’tibor bering: remember + kimdir + -ing ham bo‘ladi — I remember him crying = “uning yig‘laganini eslayman”.

remember + to: avval eslaysiz, keyin qilasiz

I remember to do something = men buni qilishim kerakligini eslayman va shuning uchun qilaman. Eslash oldin, harakat keyin. Bu — vazifa, majburiyat haqida.

  • Please remember to buy some bread on your way home.Iltimos, uyga qaytayotganda non olishni esdan chiqarma.
  • “Did you remember to send the email?” “Yes, I sent it this morning.”— Xatni yuborish esingda bo‘ldimi? — Ha, bugun ertalab yubordim.
  • I remembered to take my umbrella, so I didn’t get wet.Soyabonni olishni unutmadim, shuning uchun ho‘l bo‘lmadim.

forget + to va forget + -ing

forget to do — remember to do ning teskarisi: qilish kerak edi, lekin esdan chiqdi, demak qilinmadi.

  • I forgot to call my mother yesterday. She was a bit upset.Kecha onamga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishni unutibman. U biroz xafa bo‘ldi.
  • Don’t forget to lock the door when you leave.Chiqib ketayotganda eshikni qulflashni unutma.

forget doing esa “qilganini unutmoq” degani. U asosan never / will never forget bilan, unutilmas xotiralar haqida ishlatiladi:

  • I’ll never forget seeing the Registan at night for the first time.Registonni ilk bor tunda ko‘rganimni hech qachon unutmayman.
remember / forget: qaysi harakat oldin?
ShaklNima oldin bo‘ladi?MisolO‘zbekcha
remember doingharakat → keyin eslashI remember paying the bill.Hisobni to‘laganimni eslayman.
remember to doeslash → keyin harakatRemember to pay the bill.Hisobni to‘lashni unutmang.
forget to doesdan chiqdi → harakat bo‘lmadiI forgot to pay the bill.Hisobni to‘lashni unutibman.
never forget doingharakat → unutilmas xotiraI’ll never forget meeting her.U bilan tanishganimni hech unutmayman.

regret va go on regret doing / regret to say · go on doing / go on to do

regret + -ing: qildingiz va endi afsuslanasiz

I regret doing something = men buni qilganman va endi bundan afsusdaman. Yana harakat oldin, afsus keyin.

  • I regret selling my old car. It never broke down.Eski mashinamni sotganimdan afsusdaman. U hech qachon buzilmasdi.
  • Dilnoza regrets not learning French at school.Dilnoza maktabda fransuz tilini o‘rganmaganidan afsuslanadi.
  • Do you regret leaving your job in the bank?Bankdagi ishingizdan ketganingizdan afsuslanasizmi?

Inkor shakli: regret not doing (not -ing dan oldin keladi). regret having done ham to‘g‘ri, ma’nosi bir xil: I regret having said that = I regret saying that.

regret + to: “afsus bilan aytishim kerakki ...”

I regret to say / to tell you / to inform you ... = “Sizga ... deyishga majburman, bundan afsusdaman.” Bu yerda afsus hozir, aytish esa endi bo‘ladi. Bu — rasmiy uslub, ko‘pincha yomon xabar berishda: e’lonlarda, rasmiy xatlarda.

  • We regret to inform you that your flight to Urgench has been cancelled.Afsus bilan ma’lum qilamizki, Urganchga parvozingiz bekor qilindi.
  • I regret to say that the museum is closed for repairs.Afsuski, muzey ta’mirlash uchun yopiq.

regret to asosan say, tell, inform, announce fe’llari bilan keladi. Oddiy suhbatda ko‘pincha I’m sorry to tell you ... yoki I’m afraid ... deyiladi.

go on + -ing va go on + to

go on doing = o‘sha ishni davom ettirmoq (to‘xtamasdan). go on to do = bir ishni tugatib, keyin yangi ishga o‘tmoq.

  • The teacher asked us to be quiet, but some students went on talking.O‘qituvchi jim bo‘lishimizni so‘radi, lekin ba’zi o‘quvchilar gaplashishda davom etishdi.
  • If you go on eating so many sweets, you’ll have problems with your teeth.Shuncha shirinlik yeyishda davom etsang, tishlaring bilan muammo bo‘ladi.
  • The guide talked about the history of the city and then went on to show us the old madrasas.Gid shahar tarixi haqida gapirib berdi, so‘ng bizga eski madrasalarni ko‘rsatishga o‘tdi.
  • Jasur finished school in Fergana and went on to study law in Tashkent.Jasur Farg‘onada maktabni tugatdi, keyin Toshkentda huquqshunoslikni o‘qidi.

Ot bilan esa go on with ishlatiladi: Go on with your work. (Ishingizni davom ettiravering.)

Ma’no deyarli o‘zgarmaydigan fe’llar begin, start, continue, intend, bother

Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin -ing ham, to ham kelishi mumkin va ma’no deyarli bir xil bo‘ladi:

beginstartcontinueintendbother
Ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri
Fe’l+ -ing+ toO‘zbekcha
startIt started raining.It started to rain.Yomg‘ir yog‘a boshladi.
beginWhen did you begin learning English?When did you begin to learn English?Ingliz tilini qachon o‘rgana boshlagansiz?
continueAziz continued working after he turned seventy.Aziz continued to work after he turned seventy.Aziz yetmish yoshga to‘lgandan keyin ham ishlashda davom etdi.
intendWe intend visiting Khiva in May.We intend to visit Khiva in May.May oyida Xivaga borish niyatidamiz.
botherDon’t bother calling a taxi.Don’t bother to call a taxi.Taksi chaqirib ovora bo‘lma.

intend dan keyin to ko‘proq ishlatiladi. bother asosan inkor va so‘roq gaplarda keladi: Don’t bother ..., He didn’t even bother to say goodbye. (U hatto xayrlashishni ham lozim topmadi.)

Ammo ikki holatda faqat to ishlatiladi

1) -ing dan keyin yana -ing ishlatilmaydi. Agar start / begin / continue o‘zi -ing shaklida bo‘lsa, keyingi fe’l to bilan keladi:

  • Look out of the window. It’s starting to snow. (not It’s starting snowing)Derazadan qara. Qor yog‘a boshlayapti.
  • The children are beginning to get tired.Bolalar charchay boshlashyapti.

2) understand, realise, know kabi “bilish” fe’llari bilan odatda to ishlatiladi:

  • I’m beginning to understand how this system works.Bu tizim qanday ishlashini tushuna boshlayapman.
  • After a few weeks, she started to realise that the job wasn’t right for her.Bir necha haftadan keyin u bu ish o‘ziga to‘g‘ri kelmasligini anglay boshladi.

Britaniya inglizchasida realise, Amerika inglizchasida realize deb yoziladi (AmE: realize).

Qo‘shimcha: stop doing va stop to do stop + -ing / stop + to

stop fe’li ham shu mantiqqa bo‘ysunadi va o‘quvchilar uni juda ko‘p adashtiradi:

stop + -ing = o‘sha ishni to‘xtatmoq

Harakat davom etib kelgan va endi tugadi.

  • My father stopped smoking ten years ago. — Dadam o‘n yil oldin chekishni tashlagan.
  • Please stop making that noise! — Iltimos, shovqin qilishni bas qil!

stop + to = boshqa ish qilish uchun to‘xtamoq

Bir ishni to‘xtatib, maqsad bilan yangi ish qilmoq.

  • We stopped to buy some water. — Suv olish uchun to‘xtadik.
  • On the way to Samarkand we stopped to have lunch. — Samarqandga ketayotib tushlik qilish uchun to‘xtadik.

O‘zbekchada farq aniq: chekishni to‘xtatdi / tashladi = stopped smoking; chekish uchun to‘xtadi = stopped to smoke. Ya’ni stop + to doim “... uchun to‘xtamoq” deb tarjima qilinadi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

-ing yoki to ni tez tanlash uchun qanday savol beraman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Qaysi harakat oldin?” Agar asosiy harakat (eslanayotgan, afsuslanilayotgan) allaqachon bo‘lib o‘tgan bo‘lsa — -ing. Agar u hali keyin bo‘ladigan bo‘lsa — to. Bu qoida remember, forget, regret, go on va stop uchun ishlaydi.

“I remember having switched off the iron” deyish mumkinmi?

Ha, to‘g‘ri. Lekin oddiy I remember switching off the iron ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi va ma’nosi xuddi shu. having done shakli harakat oldin bo‘lganini alohida ta’kidlaydi, lekin bu shart emas: -ing ning o‘zi “oldin bo‘lgan” ma’nosini beradi.

“I forgot doing my homework” desa bo‘ladimi?

Agar “uy vazifasini qilishni unutibman (qilmadim)” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz — yo‘q, I forgot to do my homework deyiladi. forget + -ing asosan I’ll never forget ...ing (hech qachon unutmayman) shaklida ishlatiladi.

“remember” dan keyin “that” ham kelishi mumkinmi?

Ha: I remember that I switched off the iron = I remember switching off the iron. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Vazifa ma’nosida ham mumkin: Remember that you have to switch off the iron. Lekin qisqa va tabiiy variantlar — remember switching off va remember to switch off.

“regret to” dan keyin istalgan fe’lni qo‘ysa bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. regret to amalda faqat say, tell, inform, announce bilan ishlatiladi va rasmiy eshitiladi: We regret to announce that ... Boshqa holatlarda “afsuslanaman” ma’nosi uchun regret + -ing yoki I’m sorry (that) ... ishlatiladi.

“start doing” va “start to do” o‘rtasida umuman farq yo‘qmi?

Amalda yo‘q — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Faqat ikki holatda to tanlang: (1) start / begin o‘zi -ing shaklida bo‘lsa (It’s starting to rain); (2) understand, realise, know bilan (I began to understand).

“go on doing”, “keep doing” va “continue doing” — farqi bormi?

Uchalasi ham “davom etmoq” ma’nosini beradi va ko‘p hollarda almashtirsa bo‘ladi: She went on / kept / continued working. keep (on) doing ko‘pincha takrorlanish yoki qat’iyatni ta’kidlaydi (He keeps forgetting his keys — kalitlarini tez-tez unutib qo‘yadi). Lekin keep faqat -ing bilan keladi (keep to do ❌), go on to do esa butunlay boshqa ma’no beradi.

“stop to do” ni qanday tez tushunsam bo‘ladi?

Uni “... uchun to‘xtamoq” deb tarjima qiling. We stopped to take photos = surat olish uchun to‘xtadik. We stopped taking photos = surat olishni bas qildik. Bu yerdagi to — maqsadni bildiruvchi to (Unit 64 ga qarang).

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni -ing yoki to shaklida yozing (remember / forget).

  1. Please remember (buy) some milk on your way home.
  2. I’m sure the window is closed. I remember (close) it.
  3. “Did you remember (call) the dentist?” “Yes, my appointment is on Friday.”
  4. I’ll never forget (ride) a camel in the desert near Bukhara.
  5. Oh no! I forgot (bring) my passport.
  6. My grandmother remembers (live) in Bukhara when she was a little girl.
  7. Don’t forget (switch off) the lights when you go out.

2-mashq. regret, go on, stop: to‘g‘ri shaklni yozing.

  1. I now regret (not / study) harder at school.
  2. We regret (inform) you that tonight’s concert has been cancelled.
  3. Jasur didn’t answer me. He just went on (read) his newspaper.
  4. After university, Kamola went on (become) a famous surgeon.
  5. The car was making a strange noise, so we stopped (find out) what was wrong.
  6. My father stopped (smoke) when I was born.
  7. Do you regret (move) to Tashkent?

3-mashq. Gapni to‘ldiring. Agar ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ikkalasini ham yozing.

  1. It started (snow) just after midnight.
  2. Turn off the gas — the water is starting (boil).
  3. Don’t bother (wash) the dishes — I’ll do it later.
  4. I’m beginning (understand) why she was so upset.
  5. The company continued (grow) even during the crisis.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘ri yozing.

  1. I remember to visit Khiva when I was ten. →
  2. Don’t forget sending me a message when you arrive. →
  3. I regret to buy this expensive phone last month. →
  4. The baby is beginning crying. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Eshikni qulflashni unutmang. →
  2. Men Sardor bilan Samarqandda tanishganimni eslayman. →
  3. Ular yo‘l so‘rash uchun to‘xtashdi. →
  4. Bekzod ishdan ketganidan afsuslanadi. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Ba’zi fe’llar faqat -ing bilan (enjoy, mind, finish ...), ba’zilari faqat to bilan (decide, hope, promise ...) keladi.
  • Asosiy savol: qaysi harakat oldin? -ing — harakat oldin bo‘lgan; to — harakat keyin bo‘ladi.
  • remember doing = qilganini eslamoq; remember to do = qilishni unutmaslik. forget to do = qilishni unutmoq; never forget doing = qilganini hech unutmaslik.
  • regret doing = qilganidan afsuslanmoq; regret to say / tell / inform = afsus bilan ma’lum qilmoq (rasmiy).
  • go on doing = o‘sha ishni davom ettirmoq; go on to do = keyin yangi ishga o‘tmoq.
  • stop doing = to‘xtatmoq / tashlamoq; stop to do = ... uchun to‘xtamoq.
  • begin, start, continue, intend, bother + -ing yoki to — ma’no deyarli bir xil. Lekin It’s starting to rain (-ing + -ing ❌) va begin to understand.
