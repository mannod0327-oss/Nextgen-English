99

Adjectives: a nice new house, you look tired

Sifatlar tartibi; look / feel + sifat
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Ot oldida bir nechta sifat kelganda ularning ingliz tilidagi odatiy tartibini: a lovely old mosque, a small square glass table.
  • Nega ot oldidagi sifatlar orasiga odatda and qo‘yilmasligini (rang bundan mustasno) va qachon vergul yoki and ishlatilishini.
  • be, seem, look, feel, sound, taste, smell, get, become kabi fe’llardan keyin sifat (ravish emas!) kelishini: You look tired, It smells delicious.
  • the first two days, the next few weeks tartibini.
  • something strange, nothing special kabi qolipni va faqat fe’ldan keyin turadigan sifatlarni (asleep, afraid).

Sifat (adjective) otni tasvirlaydi. U ikki joyda turishi mumkin: 1) ot oldida — a nice house; 2) bog‘lovchi fe’ldan keyin — The house is nice, The house looks nice. Ikkinchi holatda sifat ega haqida gapiradi, “qanday ish qilindi?” haqida emas.

O‘zbek tilida sifat doim ot oldida turadi: chiroyli yangi uy. Ingliz tilida ham shunday, lekin bir nechta sifat bo‘lsa, ularning tartibi deyarli qat’iy. Ikkinchi qiyinchilik: o‘zbekchada “u charchagan ko‘rinadi”, “bu sho‘rva yaxshi hid taratadi” desak, charchagan va yaxshi sifat ham, ravish ham bo‘la oladi. Inglizcha esa look tired, smells good — sifat! Ravish (-ly) qo‘shib looks tiredly, smells deliciously deyish xato.

Bir nechta sifat: tartib Adjectives before a noun: order

Ba’zan ot oldida ikki, hatto uchta sifat keladi. Ingliz tilida ular odatda quyidagi tartibda turadi: avval fikr-mulohaza (opinion), keyin faktlar — o‘lcham, yosh, shakl, rang, kelib chiqish, material va oxirida maqsad.

Sifatlar tartibi: misollar
TartibMisolO‘zbekcha
fikr + yosha cosy old teahouseshinam eski choyxona
o‘lcham + ranga small red bagkichkina qizil sumka
o‘lcham + yosha huge new stadiumulkan yangi stadion

Qoida: Ot oldida bir nechta sifat bo‘lsa, odatda fikr (nice, lovely, ugly) faktdan (size, age, colour ...) oldin keladi. Faktlar ichida: o‘lcham → yosh → shakl → rang → qayerlik → material → maqsad → ot.

  • Aziz drives a big old Russian truck.Aziz katta eski rus yuk mashinasini haydaydi.
  • She was wearing a beautiful long silk dress.U chiroyli uzun ipak ko‘ylak kiygan edi.
  • There is a small square glass table in the corner.Burchakda kichkina to‘rtburchak shisha stol bor.
  • I’ve just bought a cheap plastic watch.Hozirgina arzon plastik soat sotib oldim.

Bu tartib — odatiy tendensiya: ona tili egalari ham ba’zan biroz farq qiladi. Bir otga uchtadan ortiq sifat qo‘yish ham g‘ayritabiiy: a lovely big old round green wooden table o‘rniga gapni bo‘lib ayting. Ikki sifat o‘rtasida shubha bo‘lsa, fikrni oldinga qo‘ying, materialni esa otga eng yaqin joyga.

And, vergul va a/an

  • Ot oldidagi turli turdagi sifatlar orasiga and qo‘yilmaydi: a big red bus ✅ — a big and red bus ❌.
  • Ikkita rang orasida and ishlatiladi: a red and white flag, a black-and-white photo.
  • Agar ikki sifat bir xil turdagi (ikkalasi ham fikr yoki ikkalasi ham ob-havo tasviri) bo‘lsa, ularni vergul yoki and bilan ajratish mumkin: a friendly, helpful guide = a friendly and helpful guide; a cold, wet day = a cold and wet day.
  • a / an ibora boshidagi birinchi so‘zning tovushiga qarab tanlanadi: a nice old house, an old house, an expensive new car, a very expensive car.

Fe’ldan keyin sifat: look, feel, sound, taste, smell Adjectives after linking verbs

Ba’zi fe’llar ishni emas, holat yoki taassurotni bildiradi. Ulardan keyin sifat keladi va u egani tasvirlaydi:

Sifat keladigan fe’llar
Fe’lMisolO‘zbekcha
beThe exam was difficult.Imtihon qiyin edi.
seemYour brother seems friendly.Akangiz samimiy ko‘rinadi.
lookYou look pale. Are you OK?Rangingiz oqargan. Yaxshimisiz?
feelI feel sleepy after lunch.Tushlikdan keyin mudrayman.
soundThat plan sounds interesting.Bu reja qiziqarli eshitilyapti.
tasteThis tea tastes sweet.Bu choy shirin ta’m beradi.
smellThe bread smells fresh.Non yangi hid taratyapti.
get / becomeIt’s getting dark. He became famous.Qorong‘i tushyapti. U mashhur bo‘ldi.
stay / keep / remainKeep quiet, please. Stay calm.Iltimos, jim turing. Xotirjam bo‘ling.

O‘zbekchada “ko‘rinmoq” (look), “tuyulmoq” (seem), “hid taratmoq” (smell) fe’llari ham sifat bilan keladi: charchagan ko‘rinadi, yoqimli hid taratadi. Fikringizni shu tarzda inglizchaga o‘tkazing: looks tired, smells nice. Lekin ba’zan o‘zbekchada yaxshi va tez kabi so‘zlar ham sifat, ham ravish bo‘la oladi, shuning uchun ingliz tilida bu farqni alohida o‘rganish kerak (Unit 100, 101).

Look like, sound like, taste like + ot

Bu fe’llardan keyin ot kelsa, like kerak: look like (o‘xshab ko‘rinmoq), sound like, taste like, smell like:

  • Malika looks like her mother.Malika onasiga o‘xshaydi.
  • That sounds like a good idea.Bu yaxshi g‘oyaga o‘xshaydi.
  • The juice tastes like apricots.Sharbatning ta’mi o‘rikka o‘xshaydi.
  • It looks like rain. Take an umbrella.Yomg‘ir yog‘adiganga o‘xshaydi. Soyabon oling.

Sifat bilan like ishlatilmaydi: You look tired ✅ — You look like tired ❌. Ot bilan esa like kerak: You look like a ghost ✅ (“sen arvohga o‘xshaysan”).

Holat va harakat: sifat yoki ravish?

look, feel, smell, taste fe’llari ikki xil ma’noda keladi. Ega haqida taassurot bo‘lsa — sifat. Ega ish bajarayotgan bo‘lsa — ravish (Unit 100):

Holat → sifat

  • Bekzod looked angry. (u jahldor ko‘rindi)
  • The soup tastes salty. (sho‘rva sho‘r)
  • I felt nervous. (men hayajonlandim)

Harakat → ravish

  • Bekzod looked at me angrily. (u menga g‘azab bilan qaradi)
  • She tasted the soup carefully. (u sho‘rvani ehtiyotkorlik bilan tatib ko‘rdi)
  • The doctor felt my forehead gently. (shifokor peshonamni ohista paypaslab ko‘rdi)

Well ba’zan sifat bo‘ladi va “sog‘lom” ma’nosini beradi: You don’t look well (siz sog‘ ko‘rinmayapsiz). She’s very well now — u hozir yaxshi (sog‘). Bu haqda Unit 101 da. Shuningdek: I feel bad about it (men bundan o‘zimni yomon his qilyapman) — bad sifat.

Fe’ldan keyin ikki sifat: and Two adjectives after the verb

Ot oldida ikki sifat orasiga odatda and qo‘yilmaydi (a cold wet day), lekin fe’ldan keyin ikki sifat orasiga and kerak. Uch sifat bo‘lsa — vergul, oxirgi ikkitasi orasida and:

Ot oldida va fe’ldan keyin
Ot oldidaFe’ldan keyin
a cold, wet day (and ixtiyoriy)The day was cold and wet.
a long narrow streetThe street was long and narrow.
a clean, modern, comfortable roomThe room was clean, modern and comfortable.
  • After the match, the players felt hot and tired.O‘yindan keyin futbolchilar o‘zlarini issiq va charchagan his qilishdi.
  • The old bazaar was noisy, crowded and colourful.Eski bozor shovqinli, gavjum va rang-barang edi.
  • Nodira looked pale and worried.Nodiraning rangi oqargan va xavotirda ko‘rinardi.

Qoida: Fe’ldan keyin: A and B / A, B and C. Ot oldida: and yo‘q (faqat ikki rang yoki bir xil turdagi sifatlar bo‘lganda).

The first two days, the next few weeks first / next / last + number

first, next, last so‘zlari raqam yoki few bilan birga kelsa, ular raqamdan oldin turadi:

the+first / next / last+raqam / few+ot
  • I was ill for the first three days of the holiday.Ta’tilning dastlabki uch kunida kasal bo‘ldim.
  • We’ll be very busy for the next few months.Keyingi bir necha oy juda band bo‘lamiz.
  • Please read the last two pages again.Iltimos, oxirgi ikki betni qayta o‘qing.
  • The first ten customers will get a free cup of tea.Dastlabki o‘nta xaridor bir piyola choyni tekin oladi.

the first two days ✅ — the two first days ❌; the next few weeks ✅ — the few next weeks ❌; the last ten minutes ✅ — the ten last minutes ❌.

Bu yerda o‘zbekcha tartib yordam beradi: dastlabki ikki kun, keyingi bir necha hafta, oxirgi o‘n daqiqa — ingliz tilida ham xuddi shunday: the first two days, the next few weeks, the last ten minutes.

Something + sifat; faqat fe’ldan keyin keladigan sifatlar Something strange; adjectives that never go before a noun

Something, anything, nothing, somebody + sifat

something, anything, nothing, everything, somebody, anyone, nobody ... so‘zlaridan keyin sifat keyin keladi (oldin emas):

  • I’d like to eat something sweet.Biror shirin narsa yegim kelyapti.
  • There was nothing special on TV.Televizorda alohida hech narsa yo‘q edi.
  • Did you meet anyone interesting at the conference?Konferensiyada qiziqarli biror kishi bilan tanishdingizmi?

Faqat fe’ldan keyin keladigan sifatlar

Ba’zi sifatlar otdan oldin kelmaydi, faqat be / seem / look kabi fe’llardan keyin ishlatiladi. Ular o‘rniga boshqa so‘z ishlatiladi:

Ot oldida ishlatilmaydigan sifatlar
Fe’ldan keyin ✅Ot oldida ❌Ot oldida ✅
The baby is asleep.an asleep babya sleeping baby
The child is afraid of dogs.an afraid childa frightened child
The fish is alive.an alive fisha live / living fish

Shu guruhga odatda yana awake va alone ham kiradi: The children are awake. I’m alone. (an awake child, an alone man demaymiz.)

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Sifatlar tartibini yod olishim shartmi? Tartib buzilsa nima bo‘ladi?

Yod olish shart emas, lekin eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan juftliklarni sezish kerak: fikr oldin, material va maqsad ot yoniga yaqin. Tartib buzilsa, gap tushunarli bo‘ladi, lekin g‘ayritabiiy eshitiladi: a wooden big table o‘rniga a big wooden table aytiladi. Ko‘p hollarda ikki sifat yetarli, uchtadan ko‘p sifatni bir otga qo‘llamang.

“Opinion” va “fact” sifatlari nima? Qaysi birini qaysi guruhga qo‘yaman?

Opinion — sizning fikringiz: nice, lovely, beautiful, ugly, delicious, boring, comfortable. Bu fikr bilan boshqalar rozi bo‘lmasligi mumkin. Fact — tekshirib ko‘rsa bo‘ladigan xususiyat: big, old, round, red, Italian, wooden. Fikr sifatlari fakt sifatlaridan oldin turadi: a lovely old house.

Nega “look happy” deymiz, lekin “look at me angrily” deymiz?

Birinchi gapda look — “ko‘rinmoq”, ya’ni ega haqidagi taassurot: she looks happy (sifat). Ikkinchi gapda look at — “qaramoq”, ya’ni harakat, uni qanday bajarganini ravish ko‘rsatadi: angrily. Qisqa qoida: “ko‘rinmoq / eshitilmoq / tuyulmoq” ma’nosida — sifat; “harakat qilmoq” ma’nosida — ravish.

“You look good” va “You look well” — farqi bormi?

Ha. You look good — siz chiroyli, yaxshi kiyingansiz yoki ko‘rinishingiz yoqimli. You look well — siz sog‘ ko‘rinasiz (kasallikdan keyin ayniqsa). Birinchisida good oddiy sifat, ikkinchisida well “sog‘lom” ma’nosidagi sifat. Qarama-qarshi: You don’t look well — sog‘ ko‘rinmayapsiz.

Sifatlar ko‘plik oladimi? “Two bigs dogs” deyish kerakmi?

Yo‘q, ingliz tilida sifat hech qachon ko‘plik qo‘shimchasini olmaydi: two big dogs, three tall girls. Faqat ot ko‘plikda bo‘ladi. Xuddi o‘zbek tilidagi kabi: ikkita katta it.

“Bukhara carpet” va “Bukharan carpet” — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘rimi?

Ha. Ba’zan joy nomi (ot) ham sifat vazifasida keladi: a Bukhara carpet (Buxoro gilami). Bukharan — haqiqiy sifat. Ikkalasi ham “qayerlik” o‘rniga tushadi: a beautiful old Bukharan silk carpet. Tayyor sifat shakli bo‘lsa (Italian, Turkish, French) — shuni ishlating; bo‘lmasa yoki noaniq bo‘lsa, ot ham ishlaydi (a Tashkent hotel).

“Something” dan keyin sifat qo‘ysam, uni qanday tarjima qilaman?

Oddiy: something strange = “g‘alati bir narsa”, nothing special = “alohida hech narsa yo‘q”, anyone interesting = “qiziqarli biror kishi”. O‘zbek tilida sifat oldin keladi, ingliz tilida esa keyin. Faqat something / anything / nothing / everything, somebody kabi so‘zlarda shunday.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Ot oldida sifatlar tartibi: fikr → o‘lcham → yosh → shakl → rang → qayerlik → material → maqsad → ot: a lovely old Bukharan silk carpet.
  • Ot oldidagi turli turdagi sifatlar orasiga and qo‘yilmaydi. Ikkita rang orasida va fe’ldan keyin and kerak: red and white; cold and wet.
  • be, seem, look, feel, sound, taste, smell, get, become fe’llaridan keyin sifat keladi: looks tired, smells delicious.
  • Harakat ma’nosida ravish: looked at me angrily, tasted the soup carefully.
  • Ot bilan like kerak: looks like rain. Sifat bilan like kerak emas.
  • something, nothing, anyone + sifat: something strange.
  • asleep, afraid, alive, alone, awake kabi sifatlar ot oldida kelmaydi.
  • the first / next / last + raqam (few): the first two days, the next few weeks (the two first days emas).
