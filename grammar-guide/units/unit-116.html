As (As I walked along the street ... / As I was hungry ...)

As — payt va sabab ma’nosida
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • as so‘zining payt ma’nosini: ikki ish bir vaqtda yuz berganda (“…ayotganda”, “…gan paytda”) — As Malika was walking home, it started to rain.
  • as yordamida asta-sekin o‘zgarishni aytishni: As the evening went on, ... (“kech kirgan sari ...”).
  • as ning sabab ma’nosini: “chunki”, “…gani uchun” — As it was late, we took a taxi.
  • Qachon as, qachon when, while yoki because ishlatilishini.
  • Gapdagi as payt ma’nosida yoki sabab ma’nosida ekanini qanday ajratishni.

As inglizcha eng ko‘p ma’noli so‘zlardan biri. Bu darsda uning ikkita muhim ma’nosi o‘rganiladi. Birinchisi — payt: as bilan boshlangan bo‘lak asosiy gapdagi ish aynan o‘sha vaqtda yuz berganini bildiradi. Ikkinchisi — sabab: as bilan boshlangan bo‘lak asosiy gapdagi ishning sababini aytadi.

As — ikki ish bir vaqtda As = at the same time as / while

as + ega + fe’l tuzilishi “shu paytning o‘zida” degan ma’noni beradi. Ikki ish bir vaqtda bo‘ladi: biri as li bo‘lakda, ikkinchisi asosiy gapda. Bu qurilish ko‘pincha o‘tgan zamon haqidagi hikoyada uchraydi, shuning uchun past simple va past continuous (Unit 5, Unit 6) bilan ishlatiladi.

As+ega+fe’l (past simple / past continuous),asosiy gap

1. Uzun fon harakati + qisqa hodisa

Eng ko‘p uchraydigan holat: biror ish davom etib turgan payt (uzun harakat), uning o‘rtasida boshqa bir qisqa hodisa yuz beradi. Uzun harakat as dan keyin odatda past continuous bilan, qisqa hodisa asosiy gapda past simple bilan beriladi.

  • As Bekzod was crossing the bridge, his cap fell into the river.Bekzod ko‘prikdan o‘tayotganda qalpog‘i daryoga tushib ketdi.
  • As I was leaving the office, my boss called me back.Idoradan chiqayotganimda boshliq meni qaytarib chaqirdi.
  • The lights went out as we were having dinner.Kechki ovqat yeyotganimizda chiroq o‘chib qoldi.
  • As Nodira was reading the letter, she started to smile.Nodira xatni o‘qiyotganda jilmaya boshladi.

As li bo‘lakda past simple ham kela oladi: As Bekzod crossed the bridge, his cap fell into the river. Past continuous harakat “jarayonda bo‘lganini” ko‘proq ta’kidlaydi. Lekin asosiy gapdagi qisqa hodisa odatda past simple bo‘ladi: ❌ ... his cap was falling into the river (agar bu bir martalik qisqa hodisa bo‘lsa).

2. Ikkita qisqa harakat bir vaqtda — va just as

Ikki qisqa harakat bir lahzada yuz bersa, ikkalasi ham past simple bilan beriladi. Just as “aynan shu lahzada” ma’nosini kuchaytiradi:

  • Nodira waved as the train pulled out of the station.Poyezd vokzaldan jo‘nab ketayotganda Nodira qo‘l silkidi.
  • Just as we reached the bus stop, the bus drove away.Biz bekatga yetib kelishimiz bilanoq avtobus jo‘nab ketdi.
  • The phone rang just as I was leaving the house.Uydan chiqayotgan paytimda telefon jiringladi.

3. Ikkita uzun harakat bir vaqtda

Ikkita ish uzoq vaqt yonma-yon davom etsa, as “…ayotib, …ayotganda” ma’nosini beradi:

  • Jasur sang as he washed the car.Jasur mashinani yuvayotib qo‘shiq aytdi.
  • As Dilnoza was cooking, her little brother was doing his homework at the kitchen table.Dilnoza ovqat pishirayotganda ukasi oshxona stolida uy vazifasini qilayotgan edi.

Hozirgi zamonda ham shu ma’noda ishlatiladi:

  • Sardor always hums as he cooks.Sardor ovqat pishirayotib doim ohista xirgoyi qiladi.
  • Please buy some bread as you pass the bakery.Novvoyxona yonidan o‘tayotganingizda non olib keling.

4. Asta-sekin o‘zgarish: as the evening went on

as bilan ikkita narsaning birga o‘zgarishini ham aytamiz: bittasi o‘zgargan sari ikkinchisi ham o‘zgaradi. O‘zbekchada bu -gan sari yoki -ib borgan sari bilan beriladi. Gapda odatda o‘zgarishni bildiruvchi fe’llar (get, grow, become, increase, improve) yoki qiyosiy daraja (colder, harder, better) keladi.

  • As the evening went on, the guests grew sleepy.Kech kirgan sari mehmonlarni uyqu bosa boshladi.
  • As the exam came closer, Aziz studied harder.Imtihon yaqinlashgan sari Aziz tobora qattiqroq o‘qidi.
  • As Kamola became more confident, her English improved.Kamola o‘ziga ishonchi ortgan sari uning inglizchasi yaxshilanib bordi.
  • As I get older, I understand my parents better.Yoshim ulg‘aygan sari ota-onamni yaxshiroq tushunaman.
  • As time goes by, we forget small problems.Vaqt o‘tgan sari mayda muammolarni unutamiz.

Qoida: as + ega + fe’l — “bir vaqtda”. Uzun ish + qisqa hodisa: as ... was/were + -ing, ... past simple. Ikkita ish birga o‘zgarsa, as (“-gan sari”) ishlatiladi; bu ma’noda when yoki while yaramaydi.

As — sabab: “chunki” As = because / since

as sabab ma’nosida ham ishlatiladi. Bunday as bo‘lagi ishning sababini aytadi, asosiy gap esa uning natijasini. Sabab odatda tinglovchiga ma’lum yoki tushunarli bo‘ladi; asosiy fikr — natijada.

  • As it was already midnight, we took a taxi home.Allaqachon yarim tun bo‘lgani uchun biz uyga taksida qaytdik.
  • As Dilnoza didn’t have a ticket, she couldn’t go in.Dilnozada chipta bo‘lmagani uchun u ichkariga kira olmadi.
  • As the road was icy, Sardor drove very slowly.Yo‘l muzlagan bo‘lgani uchun Sardor juda sekin haydadi.
  • As I’m a little tired, I’ll stay at home tonight.Biroz charchaganim uchun bugun kechqurun uyda qolaman.
As+ega+fe’l (sabab),natija

O‘zbek tilida sabab va natijani ko‘pincha “..., shuning uchun ...” bilan bog‘laymiz, shu sababli o‘quvchilar as bilan birga so ni ham qo‘shib yuborishadi. Inglizchada as (yoki because) bo‘lagi bilan asosiy gap orasiga so qo‘yilmaydi: ❌ As it was raining, so we stayed at home. → ✅ As it was raining, we stayed at home. Ikkita bog‘lovchi bir gapda kerak emas.

Agar sabab yangi va asosiy ma’lumot bo‘lsa yoki “Nega?” savoliga javob bersangiz, faqat because ishlating: “Why are you smiling?” “Because I passed the exam!” ✅. As sababni ikkinchi darajali, “hammaga ma’lum” deb beradi.

Sabab ma’nosidagi as bo‘lagi gap oxirida ham kelishi mumkin (ko‘pincha vergul bilan): We didn’t go out, as it was very cold. Bu to‘g‘ri, ayniqsa yozma nutqda. Lekin og‘zaki nutqda bu o‘rinda because ko‘proq ishlatiladi, chunki gap oxiridagi as ba’zan payt ma’nosida tushunilishi mumkin.

Payt yoki sabab? Qanday ajratamiz?

Ba’zi gaplarni ikki xil tushunish mumkin: As she was leaving, she saw a stranger. — payt. As she was tired, she went to bed early. — sabab. Ajratishning oddiy yo‘li — as dan keyingi fe’lga qarang:

As, when va while: qaysi birini tanlaymiz? as / when / while

Payt ma’nosida as ko‘pincha when yoki while bilan almashadi, lekin hamma joyda emas. Bitta muhim farq bor: as “aynan bir vaqtda” degan ma’noni beradi, when esa yana ketma-ket ishlarni ham bildirishi mumkin.

Ketma-ket ishlarda when

Avval bitta ish tugasa, keyin ikkinchisi boshlansa, when ishlatiladi. as bu yerda ma’noni buzadi — go‘yo ikkala ish bir vaqtda bo‘lgandek chiqadi.

  • When I got home, I took a shower.Uyga kelgach, dush qabul qildim. (avval keldim, keyin dush)
  • When the film finished, we went home.Film tugaganda uyga qaytdik.
  • As the film was finishing, people started to leave.Film tugab borayotganda odamlar chiqa boshladi. (bir vaqtda)

Ikki gapni solishtiring: When I opened the door, the cat ran out. (eshik ochildi, keyin mushuk chiqdi) — As I was opening the door, the cat ran out. (eshik ochilayotgan paytda mushuk chiqdi). Ma’no biroz farq qiladi, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

Hayot davri va holatlar: when

Hayotning uzoq davri yoki holat haqida gapirsak, when yoki while ishlatamiz. as bu yerda “chunki” deb tushunilib ketishi mumkin:

  • When I was a student, I lived in a dormitory in Tashkent.Talaba bo‘lganimda Toshkentda yotoqxonada yashaganman.
  • As I was a student, I got a discount on the train.Talaba bo‘lganim uchun poyezdda chegirma oldim. (sabab!)

As va while

while asosan davom etayotgan ish bilan keladi: While I was waiting, I read a magazine. as esa qisqa hodisa, ikkita qisqa harakat va asta-sekin o‘zgarish bilan ham ishlatiladi. while ning boshqa ishlatilishlari (qarama-qarshilik, for / during / while farqi) — Unit 119 da.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Payt ma’nosida as ni when bilan almashtirsam bo‘ladimi?

Ikki ish bir vaqtda yuz bersa, ko‘pincha ha: As I was leaving, the phone rang. ≈ When I was leaving, the phone rang. Lekin ikki holatda almashtirib bo‘lmaydi: (1) ishlar ketma-ket bo‘lsa — faqat when; (2) asta-sekin o‘zgarish (-gan sari) bo‘lsa — faqat as.

As dan keyin nega ko‘pincha past continuous ishlatiladi?

Chunki as ko‘pincha “uzun harakat davom etayotganda” degan ma’noni beradi, uzun harakatni ko‘rsatish uchun esa past continuous eng qulay shakl (Unit 6). Lekin majburiy emas: As I walked home, I met Bekzod. ham to‘g‘ri.

Just as nimani anglatadi?

“Aynan shu lahzada”, “…ishi bilanoq”. Ikkita hodisa deyarli bir lahzada yuz berganini kuchli ta’kidlaydi: Just as I got into bed, the doorbell rang. (Endi yotishim bilanoq eshik qo‘ng‘irog‘i jiringladi.) Bunda just ni tushirib qoldirsa ham gap to‘g‘ri, faqat ma’no kuchsizroq bo‘ladi.

Gapdagi as payt ekanini yoki sabab ekanini qanday bilaman?

As dan keyingi fe’lga va asosiy gapga qarang. Harakat fe’li (walk, cook, cross, leave) va asosiy gapda hodisa bo‘lsa — payt. Holat (be tired, be hungry, be closed) va asosiy gapda qaror yoki natija bo‘lsa — sabab. Shubha tug‘ilsa, o‘zbekchaga tarjima qiling: “-ayotganda” chiqsa — payt, “-gani uchun” chiqsa — sabab.

Nega “Why?” savoliga As ... deb javob berib bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki as li bo‘lak mustaqil javob emas: u sababni “allaqachon ma’lum narsa” sifatida, natija bilan birga beradi. “Nega?” savoliga yangi sabab aytiladi, shuning uchun because kerak: — Why did you stay at home? — Because I was ill.

As time goes by nima degani?

“Vaqt o‘tgan sari” — tayyor ibora: As time goes by, the pain gets weaker. (Vaqt o‘tgan sari og‘riq susayib boradi.) O‘tgan zamonda: As time went by, ...

As li bo‘lak gap oxirida kelsa, vergul kerakmi?

Payt ma’nosida odatda kerak emas: I met Aziz as I was crossing the road. Sabab ma’nosida vergul bilan ajratish mumkin: We didn’t go out, as it was very cold. Gap boshidagi as bo‘lagidan keyin esa vergul qo‘yiladi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • As + ega + fe’l payt ma’nosida: ikki ish bir vaqtda — As I was crossing the bridge, I dropped my phone.
  • Uzun harakat + qisqa hodisa: as ... was/were + -ing, ... past simple. Aynan bir lahza: just as.
  • Birga o‘zgarish (“-gan sari”): as + o‘zgarish, o‘zgarish — As the evening went on, it got colder. Bu yerda when yoki while ishlamaydi.
  • As = because (sabab): ko‘pincha gap boshida, ma’lum sabab. “Nega?” savoliga faqat because bilan javob beriladi.
  • Ketma-ket ishlar uchun when: When I got home, I took a shower.
  • As bo‘lagi bilan asosiy gap orasiga so qo‘yilmaydi; gap boshidagi as bo‘lagidan keyin vergul bor.
  • Payt bo‘lagida kelajak haqida gapirsak ham will emas, hozirgi zamon: as I leave.
