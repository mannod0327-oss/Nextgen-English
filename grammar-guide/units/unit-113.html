Although / though / even though, In spite of / despite

Although / though / even though; in spite of / despite
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Although / though / even though (“garchi ... bo‘lsa ham”) dan keyin to‘liq gap — ega + fe’l — kelishini: Although it was cold, we went out.
  • In spite of / despite (“... ga qaramay”) dan keyin esa ot, fe’l + -ing yoki the fact that + gap kelishini: Despite the cold, we went out.
  • Though so‘zi gap oxirida ham kelib, “lekin, baribir” ma’nosini berishini: It was cold. We enjoyed the walk, though.
  • Nega although ... but ..., despite of, in spite the rain kabi gaplar xato ekanini va bu xatolardan qanday qochishni.

Bu so‘zlar qarama-qarshilik (contrast) bildiradi: gapning ikkinchi qismi birinchisidan kutilmagan bo‘ladi. Sovuq bo‘lsa, odam odatda uyda qoladi — lekin biz sayrga chiqdik. Ana shu kutilmagan natijani although yoki despite bilan bog‘laymiz.

O‘zbek tilida qarama-qarshilik uch xil yo‘l bilan beriladi: -sa ham / -sa-da (sovuq bo‘lsa ham), -ga qaramay (sovuqqa qaramay) va -ganiga qaramay (sovuq bo‘lganiga qaramay). Inglizchada tanlov so‘zdan keyin nima kelishiga bog‘liq: gap bo‘lsa — although, ot yoki -ing bo‘lsa — despite / in spite of. O‘zbekchada “qaramay” har ikkala holatda ham o‘zgarmaydi, inglizchada esa despite dan keyin to‘liq gap qo‘yib bo‘lmaydi.

Although, though, even though + gap Although / though / even though + clause

Uchala so‘z ham “garchi ... bo‘lsa-da” ma’nosini beradi va ular ikki fikrni bog‘laydi. Birinchi fikrdan keyin boshqacha natija kutgan bo‘lardingiz, ammo ikkinchi fikr kutilmagan chiqadi. Masalan: Malika juda charchagan (odatda odam dam oladi) — lekin u hisobotni tugatdi (kutilmagan).

Although / though / even though+ega+fe’l ...,asosiy gap

Qoida: although / though / even though dan keyin har doim ega + fe’l (to‘liq gap) keladi. Ot yoki -ing shaklini yolg‘iz qo‘yib bo‘lmaydi: Although the rain ❌. Ot bo‘lsa — despite / in spite of (B bo‘lim).

  • Although Malika was very tired, she finished the report.Malika juda charchagan bo‘lsa-da, hisobotni tugatdi.
  • Though the hotel was cheap, the rooms were spotless.Mehmonxona arzon bo‘lsa-da, xonalar juda toza edi.
  • Even though he has lived in Tashkent for ten years, he still gets lost in the metro.U Toshkentda o‘n yildan beri yashasa ham, hamon metroda adashib qoladi.
  • Bekzod bought the jacket although it was expensive.Bekzod qimmat bo‘lsa-da, kurtkani sotib oldi.
  • They enjoyed the trip to Bukhara even though the hotel was noisy.Mehmonxona shovqinli bo‘lishiga qaramay, ular Buxoroga safardan zavqlanishdi.
  • The shop was open although it was a public holiday.Bayram kuni bo‘lsa-da, do‘kon ochiq edi.

Although va because ni adashtirmang. Sardor went to school although he had a cold. (shamollagan bo‘lsa ham, baribir bordi — kutilmagan natija). Sardor stayed at home because he had a cold. (shamollash — sabab). Ot bilan ham shunday: Sardor went to school in spite of his cold. — Sardor stayed at home because of his cold.

Gapdagi o‘rni va vergul

Although bilan boshlangan qism gapning boshida ham, oxirida ham turishi mumkin. Ma’no o‘zgarmaydi. Boshida tursa — qismdan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi; oxirida tursa, odatda vergul kerak emas.

Boshida (vergul bilan)

  • Although it was late, we kept talking.
  • Even though he was ill, Sardor came to class.

Oxirida (odatda vergulsiz)

  • We kept talking although it was late.
  • Sardor came to class even though he was ill.

Even yolg‘iz bog‘lovchi emas: Even he was tired, he kept working. ❌ — Even though he was tired, he kept working. ✅. Even though ni ajratib bo‘lmaydi. (even haqida — Unit 112.)

Although + but: ikkalasi birga emas

Inglizchada qarama-qarshilikni gapda bitta so‘z ko‘rsatadi: yoki although, yoki but. O‘zbekchada “garchi ... bo‘lsa-da, lekin ...” deyish odat tusiga kirgan, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar inglizchada ham ikkalasini qo‘shib yuboradi.

Bitta bog‘lovchi yetarli

Rasmiy yozuvda although / though dan keyin ega va be fe’li tushib qolishi mumkin: Although tired, she went on working. (= Although she was tired, ...). Bunda ikkala qismning egasi bir xil bo‘lishi shart. Bu uslub kitobiy; o‘zingiz to‘liq gap yozsangiz, xato qilmaysiz.

In spite of va despite In spite of / despite + noun, -ing, the fact that

In spite of va despite ham “... ga qaramay” degan ma’noni beradi. Ularning ma’nosi bir xil, ammo ulardan keyin gap emas, ot, olmosh (this, that, what ...), -ing shakli yoki the fact that + gap keladi. Despite ko‘pincha biroz rasmiyroq va qisqaroq tuyuladi.

To‘rttasida ham in spite of o‘rniga despite qo‘ysangiz, ma’no va grammatika o‘zgarmaydi.

Uch tuzoq:
1) despite of ❌ — despite dan keyin of yo‘q: despite the rain ✅.
2) in spite ❌ — in spite of uchta so‘z: in spite of the rain ✅.
3) despite / in spite of + ega + fe’l ❌ — Despite it was raining ❌. To‘g‘risi: Although it was raining yoki Despite the fact that it was raining.

-ing ning egasi. Despite / in spite of + -ing qolipida -ing ning egasi odatda bosh gapning egasi bilan bir xil bo‘ladi. In spite of being tired, Malika finished. — charchagan ham, tugatgan ham Malika. Egalar turlicha bo‘lsa, -ing ishlatmang: Despite raining, Sardor went jogging. ❌ — Despite the fact that it was raining, Sardor went jogging. ✅

  • Despite earning a good salary, Bekzod never has any money left.Yaxshi maosh olsa ham, Bekzodning hech qachon puli qolmaydi.
  • Our picnic was a great success in spite of the wind.Shamolga qaramay, piknikimiz juda yaxshi o‘tdi.
  • In spite of the fact that it was her birthday, Dilnoza worked all day.Tug‘ilgan kuni bo‘lishiga qaramay, Dilnoza kun bo‘yi ishladi.

O‘zbekchada “qaramay” ni ot ham (yomg‘irga qaramay), fe’l nomi ham (yomg‘ir yog‘ishiga qaramay) bilan ishlataveramiz. Inglizchada esa shunday: yomg‘irga qaramay → despite the rain; yomg‘ir yog‘ishiga qaramay → despite the fact that it was raining yoki although it was raining.

Gap oxiridagi though Though at the end of a sentence

Though faqat bog‘lovchi emas, ravish (adverb) sifatida ham ishlatiladi. Gap oxirida (yoki gap bo‘lagining oxirida) turib, “lekin, baribir, ammo” ma’nosini beradi. Bunda u oldingi gapga qarama-qarshi qo‘shimcha fikr aytadi. Bu usul asosan og‘zaki nutqda uchraydi.

  • The film was very long. I enjoyed it, though.Film juda uzun edi. Lekin menga yoqdi.
  • Dilnoza isn’t very talkative. She’s always ready to help, though.Dilnoza unchalik ko‘p gapirmaydi. Biroq doim yordamga tayyor.
  • I didn’t win the race. I got a medal, though.Men poygada g‘olib bo‘lmadim. Lekin medal oldim.
  • “It’s a long walk to the bazaar.” “It’s a nice walk, though.”— Bozorgacha piyoda uzoq. — Lekin yurish yoqimli.

Joyialthoughthough
Gap boshida / o‘rtasida (+ gap)✅ Although it was late, ...✅ Though it was late, ...
Gap oxirida (yolg‘iz)❌ I enjoyed it, although.✅ I enjoyed it, though.

Gap oxiridagi though dan oldin odatda vergul qo‘yiladi. Yana bir muhim narsa: Although so‘zini However kabi alohida ishlatib bo‘lmaydi: It was late. Although, we kept talking. ❌

Gap oxiridagi though o‘zbekchadagi “lekin, baribir”, ba’zan “-ku” yuklamasiga to‘g‘ri keladi: Film uzun edi, menga yoqdi-ku. Inglizcha qoliplarda esa u faqat oldingi gapdan keyin, odatda yangi gap oxirida turadi.

Qarama-qarshilik so‘zlarini taqqoslash But, although, despite, however, even though / even if

Qarama-qarshilikni bildiradigan so‘zlar ma’nosi o‘xshash, lekin gapda boshqacha ishlatiladi. Jadvalda ularni yonma-yon ko‘ring:

Even though va even if

even though = haqiqatda shunday bo‘lgan

  • Even though it was raining, we walked home.

even if = bo‘lishi ham, bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin

  • We’ll walk home even if it rains.
  • (Yomg‘ir yog‘adimi, yo‘qmi — noma’lum, biz baribir yurib ketamiz.)

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Although, though va even though — qaysi birini tanlayman?

Oddiy holatda although xavfsiz tanlov: u har joyda to‘g‘ri. Though ma’nosi bir xil, lekin so‘zlashuvga yaqinroq. Even though — kuchaytirilgan variant: kutilmagan fakt ustida to‘xtalmoqchi bo‘lsangiz ishlating. Uchalasidan keyin ham ega + fe’l keladi.

In spite of va despite o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ma’no va grammatika bir xil: ikkalasidan keyin ot, -ing yoki the fact that + gap keladi. Farq faqat shaklda: in spite of — uch so‘z, despite — bitta so‘z, uning ketidan of qo‘yilmaydi. Despite ko‘pincha biroz rasmiyroq tuyuladi.

Nega although bilan but ni birga ishlatib bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki ikkalasi ham bir ishni bajaradi: ikki fikrni qarama-qarshi bog‘laydi. Ikkita bog‘lovchi bir xil vazifani ikki marta bajarsa, gap ortiqcha bo‘ladi. Although it was cold, we went out yoki It was cold, but we went out — bittasini tanlang. Xuddi shu qoida because ... so ... da ham bor.

Gap oxiridagi “though” nimani anglatadi? Uni qachon ishlataman?

U “lekin, baribir” degan ma’noni beradi va oldin aytilgan fikrga kutilmagan qo‘shimcha kiritadi: The room was small. It was clean, though. Asosan og‘zaki nutqda va norasmiy yozuvda qo‘llaniladi. Bu o‘rinda although ishlatilmaydi.

Even though bilan even if o‘rtasidagi farq nima?

Even though — bo‘lgan yoki bo‘layotgan haqiqiy fakt: Even though it was raining, we left. Even if — shart, bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan holat: We’ll leave even if it rains. Even if dan keyin kelasi zamon ma’nosida ham present simple ishlatiladi (will emas).

Vergulni qachon qo‘yaman?

Although / though / even though bilan boshlangan qism gap boshida tursa, undan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi: Although it was late, we stayed. Gap oxirida tursa, vergul odatda kerak emas: We stayed although it was late. Despite / in spite of bilan ham xuddi shunday: Despite the rain, we stayed.

“Although tired” kabi qisqa shaklni ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha, bo‘ladi: Although tired, she went to work. Lekin bu rasmiy, kitobiy uslub, va faqat ikkala qismning egasi bir xil bo‘lsa to‘g‘ri. Oddiy nutqda to‘liq shaklni yozing: Although she was tired, she went to work.

Despite dan keyin nega fe’l faqat -ing shaklida keladi?

Chunki despite va in spite of — predlog, predloglardan keyin fe’l -ing (gerund) shaklida bo‘ladi, xuddi before leaving, without asking kabi. Despite to be tired ❌, Despite be tired ❌, Despite being tired ✅.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Although / though / even though + ega + fe’l — “garchi ... bo‘lsa ham”. Even though eng kuchlisi; though so‘zlashuvga yaqin.
  • In spite of / despite + ot yoki -ing; gap bo‘lsa — the fact that + gap. Despite of, in spite deb bo‘lmaydi.
  • Although / despite ning o‘rnida ot yoki gapning to‘g‘ri kelishini tekshiring: Although the rain ❌, Despite it was raining ❌.
  • Although ... but ... ❌ — bitta bog‘lovchi yetarli.
  • Though gap oxirida “lekin, baribir” bo‘ladi: It was nice, though. Although bunday ishlatilmaydi.
  • Even though — bo‘lgan fakt; even if — bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan shart.
  • Although bilan boshlangan qism gap boshida turganda uning ketidan vergul qo‘yiladi.
