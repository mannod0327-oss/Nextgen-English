Past perfect continuous (I had been doing)

O‘tgan tugallangan davomli zamon
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Past perfect continuous (o‘tgan tugallangan davomli zamon) qanday yasalishini: had been + -ing.
  • Uni qachon ishlatishni: o‘tmishdagi bir paytgacha (yoki undan biroz oldin) davom etib kelgan harakat haqida.
  • “...dan beri ...ayotgan edi, keyin ...” tipidagi gaplarni: We’d been working in the garden for an hour when it started to rain.
  • have been doing, was doing va had done bilan farqlarini.
  • know, believe, have (egalik) kabi fe’llar bilan nima uchun bu zamon ishlatilmasligini.

Past perfect continuous — present perfect continuous ning (have been doing) o‘tmishdagi “egizagi”. U o‘tmishdagi biror paytga qadar bir muddat davom etgan harakatni bildiradi. Ko‘pincha biz o‘sha paytda harakatning izini, natijasini ko‘ramiz: ho‘l yer, qizargan ko‘zlar, charchoq.

O‘zbek tilida bunday ma’noni ko‘pincha -gan ekan / -ibdi (izidan bilib olish) yoki -dan beri ...ayotgan edi bilan beramiz: Yer ho‘l edi — yomg‘ir yoqqan ekan = It had been raining. Biz bir soatdan beri bog‘da ishlayotgan edik, yomg‘ir boshlandi = We’d been working in the garden for an hour when it started to rain. Ikkinchi gapni inglizchaga ❌ We were working in the garden for an hour when... deb o‘girish — eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato. Davomiylik (for an hour) + o‘tmishdagi keyingi voqea = had been + -ing.

Shakli va asosiy ma’nosi had been + -ing: It had been raining

Kecha kechqurun Aziz uyga kirib keldi. Yuzi qizargan, futbolkasi terdan ho‘l, nafasi tiqilib qolgan edi. U o‘sha paytda yugurmayotgan edi — eshik oldida turgan edi. Lekin bundan oldin u bir muddat yugurgan:

  • Aziz was out of breath. He had been running.Aziz hansirab qolgan edi. U yugurgan ekan. (Uyga kirishidan oldin bir muddat yugurgan.)

Misollar

  • Jasur’s trainers were covered in mud and his knee was bleeding. He’d been playing football in the rain.Jasurning krossovkalari loyga belangan, tizzasi qonagan edi. U yomg‘irda futbol o‘ynagan ekan.
  • My sister’s fingers hurt. She’d been practising the piano for three hours.Singlimning barmoqlari og‘riyotgan edi. U uch soat davomida pianino chalishni mashq qilgan edi.
  • When I walked into the flat, it smelt of paint. Somebody had been painting.Kvartiraga kirganimda bo‘yoq hidi kelardi. Kimdir bo‘yoq ishlari qilgan ekan.
  • There were puddles everywhere when we went outside. It had been raining.Tashqariga chiqqanimizda hamma yoqda ko‘lmaklar bor edi. Yomg‘ir yoqqan ekan.
  • Malika’s eyes were red. She had been crying.Malikaning ko‘zlari qizargan edi. U yig‘lagan ekan.

Qoida: O‘tmishdagi bir paytda biz biror harakatning izini ko‘rsak (charchoq, ho‘l yer, hid), va o‘sha harakat undan oldin bir muddat davom etgan bo‘lsa — had been + -ing. Harakat o‘sha paytga kelib to‘xtagan yoki endigina to‘xtagan bo‘lishi mumkin.

Qancha vaqt davom etgan edi? had been doing for … when …

Past perfect continuous bilan biror harakat boshqa bir voqea sodir bo‘lgunga qadar qancha vaqt davom etganini aytish mumkin. Ko‘pincha for / since / all day va when bilan keladi:

  • We’d been working in the garden for about an hour when it started to rain.Taxminan bir soatdan beri bog‘da ishlayotgan edik, yomg‘ir yog‘a boshladi.
  • We’d been walking for two hours when we finally found a café.Ikki soatdan beri yurayotgan edik, nihoyat bir kafe topdik.
  • Dilnoza finally saw a dentist on Monday. Her tooth had been hurting for a week.Dilnoza dushanba kuni nihoyat tish shifokoriga bordi. Bir haftadan beri tishi og‘riyotgan edi.
  • How long had you been waiting when the bus finally came?Avtobus nihoyat kelganida qancha vaqtdan beri kutayotgan edingiz?

have been doing ↔ had been doing

had been -ing — have been -ing (Unit 9) ning o‘tmishdagi shakli. Hozirga nisbatan — have been; o‘tmishdagi paytga nisbatan — had been:

Present perfect continuous (hozirgacha)Past perfect continuous (o‘sha paytgacha)
I hope the doctor calls me in soon. I’ve been sitting here for 40 minutes.At last the doctor called me in. I’d been sitting there for 40 minutes.
Aziz’s hands are dirty. He’s been repairing his bike.Aziz’s hands were dirty. He’d been repairing his bike.
Kamola has been learning Korean since January.When she went to Seoul in July, Kamola had been learning Korean for six months.

was doing va had been doing Past continuous and past perfect continuous

Bu ikki zamonni chalkashtirish oson. Farqi — o‘sha paytda harakat hali davom etayotgan edimi yoki undan oldin davom etgan edimi:

was / were + -ing

O‘sha paytda harakat ayni jarayonda edi.

  • When I arrived, Kamola was cooking dinner, so I helped her.

Men kelganimda Kamola kechki ovqat pishirayotgan edi.

had been + -ing

Harakat o‘sha paytdan oldin bir muddat davom etgan.

  • Kamola was tired because she had been cooking all afternoon.

Kamola charchagan edi, chunki tushdan keyin to kechgacha ovqat pishirgan edi.

  • It wasn’t snowing when we left the house — the sky was clear. But it had been snowing all night, so the roads were white.Uydan chiqqanimizda qor yog‘mayotgan edi — osmon musaffo edi. Lekin tun bo‘yi qor yoqqan ekan, shuning uchun yo‘llar oppoq edi.
  • Nodira was lying on the sofa, watching TV. She was exhausted because she’d been cleaning the house all day.Nodira divanda yotib televizor ko‘rayotgan edi. U juda charchagan edi, chunki kun bo‘yi uy tozalagan edi.

Continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydigan fe’llar Verbs not normally used in the continuous

know, like, believe, own, have (egalik) kabi holat fe’llari continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi (Unit 4). Ular bilan “...dan beri ... edi” ma’nosi uchun past perfect simple (had + V3) ishlatiladi:

  • Aziz and Sardor were close friends. They had known each other since childhood. (not had been knowing)Aziz va Sardor yaqin do‘st edi. Ular bir-birini bolalikdan beri bilishardi.
  • Our old car broke down last week. We’d had it for fifteen years. (not we’d been having)Eski mashinamiz o‘tgan hafta buzildi. U bizda o‘n besh yildan beri bor edi.
  • I’d wanted to visit Khiva for years, and last summer I finally went.Ko‘p yillardan beri Xivaga borishni xohlardim va o‘tgan yozda nihoyat bordim.
❌ Noto‘g‘ri✅ To‘g‘ri
We had been knowing each other ...We had known each other ...
She had been having that bag for years.She had had that bag for years.
My uncle had been owning the shop since 1990.My uncle had owned the shop since 1990.

want odatda simple shaklda keladi (I’d wanted to visit Khiva for years), lekin og‘zaki nutqda I’d been wanting to tell you for weeks (“haftalardan beri aytmoqchi edim”) kabi gaplar ham to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi. know, own, believe va have (egalik) bilan esa continuous shakl ishlatilmaydi.

had been doing yoki had done? Past perfect continuous or simple

Bu farq Unit 10 dagi have been doing / have done farqiga o‘xshaydi:

had been doing — jarayon, davomiylik

  • Nodira was tired. She had been writing emails all morning.
  • How long had you been reading when you fell asleep?

Harakat o‘zi muhim; tugagan-tugamagani noma’lum yoki muhim emas.

had done — natija, miqdor, tugallanganlik

  • By lunchtime she had written thirty emails.
  • When I had read the book, I gave it back.

Ish tugagan; how many / how much — simple bilan.

  • I’d been reading the book for a week, but I still hadn’t finished it.Kitobni bir haftadan beri o‘qiyotgan edim, lekin hali tugatmagan edim.
  • I’d read the book twice, so I knew the story well.Kitobni ikki marta o‘qigan edim, shuning uchun voqeani yaxshi bilardim.

Barcha “o‘tmish” zamonlari bir jadvalda

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“was doing” va “had been doing” ni qanday farqlayman?

O‘tmishdagi tayanch paytni (masalan, “men kelganimda”) tasavvur qiling. Shu paytda harakat hali ketayotgan bo‘lsa — was doing: When I arrived, she was cooking. Harakat undan oldin davom etib, o‘sha paytga kelib to‘xtagan (yoki uning izi qolgan) bo‘lsa — had been doing: She was tired because she had been cooking.

“It had been raining” o‘rniga “It rained” desa bo‘lmaydimi?

The ground was wet. It had rained. (past perfect simple) — ham to‘g‘ri. had been raining esa yomg‘ir bir muddat yoqqanini, jarayonni ta’kidlaydi va eng tabiiy variant. Oddiy It rained esa voqealar tartibini ko‘rsatmaydi va bu kontekstda unchalik tabiiy emas.

“had been doing” va “had done” — qaysi birini tanlayman?

Jarayon, davomiylik muhim bo‘lsa — had been doing: She had been writing emails all morning. Natija, miqdor, tugallanganlik muhim bo‘lsa — had done: She had written thirty emails. “Qancha vaqt?” → continuous; “nechta? qancha?” → simple.

Bu zamon og‘zaki nutqda ko‘p ishlatiladimi?

Hikoya qilganda, voqea tasvirlaganda juda tabiiy. Og‘zaki nutqda deyarli doim qisqa shakl ishlatiladi: I’d been waiting, she’d been crying, it’d been raining. Yozma rasmiy matnda to‘liq shakl yoziladi: had been waiting.

live va work bilan simple shaklni ham ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha, Unit 11 dagidek, live va work bilan ikkala shakl ham ishlatiladi: Sardor had worked / had been working in the bank for ten years when he decided to start his own business.

O‘zbekchada “-ayotgan edi” deyman. Bu har doim past continuous emasmi?

Ko‘pincha shunday: o‘ynayotgan edik = we were playing. Lekin agar gapda “...dan beri” yoki davomiylik (yarim soat, kun bo‘yi) bo‘lsa va keyin boshqa voqea sodir bo‘lsa, inglizchada had been + -ing kerak: Bir soatdan beri bog‘da ishlayotgan edik, yomg‘ir boshlandi = We’d been working in the garden for an hour when it started to rain.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Vaziyatni o‘qing va had been + -ing bilan gap yozing.

  1. Aziz was out of breath. (run) → He .
  2. The children’s clothes were muddy. (play football in the rain) → They .
  3. Malika’s eyes were red. (cry) → She .
  4. When I came into the kitchen, there was a smell of burnt onions. (cook) → Somebody .
  5. Sardor’s back hurt when he got home. (carry heavy boxes all day) → He .
  6. The streets were wet and there were puddles everywhere. (rain) → It .

2-mashq. Ikki gapni for ... when yordamida bitta gapga birlashtiring.

  1. We were working in the garden. After an hour it started to rain. →
  2. Sardor was working in a bank. After ten years he decided to start his own business. →
  3. I was waiting for the bus. After 40 minutes it finally came. →
  4. Dilnoza was driving. After three hours she stopped for a rest. →

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. Aziz is out of breath. He (has been running / had been running).
  2. Aziz was out of breath. He (has been running / had been running).
  3. When I arrived, Kamola (was cooking / had been cooking) dinner, so I helped her.
  4. Kamola was tired because she (was cooking / had been cooking) all afternoon.
  5. I hope Jasur gets here soon. I (’ve been waiting / ’d been waiting) for him for half an hour.
  6. At last Jasur arrived. I (’ve been waiting / ’d been waiting) for him for half an hour.
  7. It (wasn’t snowing / hadn’t been snowing) when we left, but the roads were still white.
  8. Malika and I were close. We (had known / had been knowing) each other since school.

4-mashq. had been + -ing yoki had + V3 shaklida yozing.

  1. Nodira was tired. She (write) emails all morning.
  2. By lunchtime she (write) thirty emails.
  3. I (read) the book for a week, but I still hadn’t finished it.
  4. I (read) the book twice, so I knew the story well.
  5. Our old car broke down last week. We (have) it for fifteen years.

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Biz bir soatdan beri bog‘da ishlayotgan edik, yomg‘ir yog‘a boshladi. →
  2. Yer ho‘l edi. Yomg‘ir yoqqan ekan. →
  3. Sardor charchagan edi, chunki u kun bo‘yi ishlagan edi. →
  4. Biz bir-birimizni bolalikdan beri bilar edik. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: had been + -ing — barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil (I’d been working, she’d been crying).
  • O‘tmishdagi bir paytgacha bir muddat davom etgan harakat, ko‘pincha izi ko‘rinib turadi: The road was wet. It had been raining.
  • Davomiylik + keyingi voqea: We’d been working in the garden for an hour when it started to rain. (We were working ... for an hour ❌)
  • have been doing (hozirgacha) → had been doing (o‘sha paytgacha).
  • was doing — o‘sha paytda jarayonda; had been doing — o‘sha paytdan oldin davom etgan.
  • Holat fe’llari bilan (know, believe, have) — had + V3: They had known each other since childhood.
