Enough and too

Enough va too
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • enough (yetarli) — sifat va ravishdan keyin, otdan oldin keladi: big enough, enough time.
  • too (me’yoridan ortiq) — sifat va ravishdan oldin keladi: too big, too quickly; ot oldida too much / too many.
  • too bilan very ning farqini: too doim “bu ortiqcha, muammo bor” degan ma’no beradi.
  • too … to do va … enough to do qurilmalarini, for somebody bilan birga.
  • Muhim qoida: too hot to drink ✅ — too hot to drink it ❌.

Ikkala so‘z ham bitta savolga javob beradi: “Kerakli miqdor yoki daraja bormi?” enough — daraja yetarli ✅. not enough — daraja yetmaydi (kam) ❌. too — daraja kerakdan ortiq ❌. Shuning uchun too deyarli doim “bu yaxshi emas, chunki haddan ortiq” degan ma’noni beradi.

O‘zbekchada “yetarlicha katta” deymiz: yetarlicha sifatdan oldin turadi. Inglizchada aksincha: big enough — enough sifatdan keyin keladi. Otlar bilan ikki til bir xil: yetarli pul = enough money. Yana bir tuzoq: o‘zbekcha “juda” ham very ga, ham too ga to‘g‘ri keladi. “Bu kurtka menga juda katta” — inglizchada too big for me (very big emas!), chunki bu yerda “juda” = “kerakdan ortiq, mos kelmaydi”. Va “ichish uchun juda issiq” = too hot to drink (too hot for drinking tabiiy emas).

Enough — yetarli Enough: adjective + enough, enough + noun

Enough “kerakli miqdorda yoki darajada” degan ma’noni beradi. Avval umumiy rasmni ko‘ring: enough va too bir o‘lchov ustida turadi.

Endi enough ning o‘zi. U uchta joyda uchraydi:

Enough ijobiy va inkor gapda

✅ enough (yetarli)

  • The soup is hot enough.
  • We have enough time.
  • She runs fast enough.

❌ not enough (yetarli emas)

  • The soup isn’t hot enough.
  • We don’t have enough time.
  • She doesn’t run fast enough.

Enough of + the / my / this / them

Aniq bir narsaning qismi haqida gapirsak, enough of + the, my, this, these, him, them ishlatiladi:

  • I’ve read enough of the book to know how it ends.Men kitobning yetarli qismini o‘qidim, oxiri nima bo‘lishini bilaman.
  • Malika has seen enough of them for one day.Malika bir kun uchun ularni yetarlicha ko‘rdi (ulardan to‘ydi).

Diqqat: “Enough” sifatdan oldin ham kelishi mumkin, lekin bu boshqa holat — u otning miqdorini bildiradi: We haven’t got enough big boxes = “yetarli miqdordagi katta qutilar yo‘q”. Solishtiring: The boxes aren’t big enough = “qutilar yetarlicha katta emas”. Birinchisida enough “qutilar”ga tegishli (soni yetmaydi), ikkinchisida “katta”ga (o‘lchami yetmaydi).

Too — me’yoridan ortiq Too + adjective/adverb; too much, too many

Too “kerak yoki yaxshi bo‘lgandan ortiq” degan ma’noni beradi va odatda muammoni ko‘rsatadi. U sifat yoki ravishdan oldin turadi:

too+sifat / ravish
  • This soup is too salty. I can’t eat it.Bu sho‘rva haddan tashqari sho‘r. Men uni yeya olmayman.
  • You are driving too fast. Please slow down.Siz juda tez haydayapsiz. Iltimos, sekinroq.
  • We were too late for the first train.Biz birinchi poyezdga kech qoldik (u ketib bo‘lgan edi).
  • It’s too early to phone Aziz. He is still asleep.Azizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilish uchun hali juda erta. U hali uxlayapti.
  • These jeans are too tight for me.Bu jinsi shim menga tor.

Too little va too few — “juda oz” demak, ya’ni not enough bilan bir xil: too little time = not enough time; too few chairs = not enough chairs.

Too va enough — bir fikrning ikki yuzi

Ko‘p hollarda too + sifat va not + qarama-qarshi sifat + enough deyarli bir xil fikrni beradi:

Ikki aytilish usuli
too …not … enough
The film is too long.The film isn’t short enough.
This street is too narrow for a bus.This street isn’t wide enough for a bus.
You are too young to rent a car.You aren’t old enough to rent a car.
The exam was too difficult.The exam wasn’t easy enough.
We have too few chairs.We don’t have enough chairs.

Kundalik nutqda too qisqa va ochiq-oydin eshitiladi; not … enough esa ko‘pincha yumshoqroq, xushmuomalaroq.

Too ning yana bir ma’nosi bor — “ham”: I’m hungry too. (Men ham ochman.) Bu holda too gap oxirida turadi. Imlo: to (yo‘nalish/infinitiv), too (ortiq; ham), two (ikki).

To‘ldiruvchini takrorlamang; predlog oxirida qoladi

Agar gapning egasi (the tea, the bag) to + fe’l ning to‘ldiruvchisi ham bo‘lsa, to‘ldiruvchi olmoshi (it, them) qo‘shilmaydi. Fe’l predlog bilan kelsa, predlog gap oxirida qoladi:

✅ To‘g‘ri

  • The tea is too hot to drink.
  • This bag is too heavy for me to lift.
  • This bench is too wet to sit on.
  • The music is too loud to listen to.

❌ Noto‘g‘ri

  • The tea is too hot to drink it.
  • This bag is too heavy for me to lift it.
  • This bench is too wet to sit.
  • The music is too loud to listen.

O‘zbekchada “Bu choy ichish uchun juda issiq” deymiz va “uni ichish” deb takrorlamaymiz. Inglizchada ham xuddi shunday: too hot to drink, to drink it emas. Lekin to‘ldiruvchi egadan boshqa narsa bo‘lsa, u yoziladi: I’m too tired to cook dinner (ega — men, to‘ldiruvchi — dinner).

Too … to va so … that

too … to ko‘pincha so … (that) … can’t / couldn’t bilan bir xil fikrni beradi (Unit 102):

too … toso … that
It was too hot to sleep.It was so hot that I couldn’t sleep.
The soup is too salty to eat.The soup is so salty that nobody can eat it.

Too … that deb bo‘lmaydi: It was too hot that I couldn’t sleep ❌. To‘g‘ri: too hot to sleep yoki so hot that I couldn’t sleep.

For + ot yoki -ing

Aniq harakat emas, balki shaxs yoki narsa bo‘lsa, for + ot yetadi: The room is too small for a big family. It’s too cold for football. Lekin harakat haqida gapirganda to + fe’l ishlatiladi, for + -ing emas: I haven’t got enough money to buy a new phone. ✅ — enough money for buying a new phone ❌.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“It’s very hot, so I can’t drink it” deyish mumkinmi? Unda “too” nega kerak?

Ha, bu gap to‘g‘ri. Very hot — shunchaki darajani aytadi, natijani esa keyingi bo‘lakda alohida bildirasiz (so I can’t drink it). Too hot esa natijani o‘zining ichiga oladi: It’s too hot to drink — qisqaroq va tabiiyroq. Ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi.

“Juda” so‘zini qachon very, qachon too deb tarjima qilaman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Bu ortiqcha, mos kelmaydi, xalaqit beradimi?” Ha bo‘lsa — too: “Bu uy bizga juda katta (ortiqcha)” = This house is too big for us. Faqat darajasi baland bo‘lsa, muammo yo‘q bo‘lsa — very: “Bu uy juda chiroyli” = This house is very beautiful. Too beautiful odatda natija ham aytilganda keladi: too beautiful to touch.

“Enough” nega sifatdan keyin, otdan oldin?

Ingliz tilida shunday odat shakllangan. Sifat/ravish bilan enough darajani o‘lchaydi va ularning orqasidan turadi (warm enough). Otlar oldida esa enough miqdorni bildiruvchi aniqlovchi (some, many kabi) vazifasini bajaradi va otdan oldin turadi (enough money). O‘zbekchada “yetarlicha” har ikkala holatda ham oldinda turadi, shuning uchun inglizcha tartibni alohida yodlash kerak.

“Too many” va “too much” farqi nima? “Many too” bormi?

Too many + sanaladigan ko‘plik ot (too many people), too much + sanalmaydigan ot (too much noise) yoki fe’ldan keyin (he talks too much). Much too + sifat (much too late). “Many too” bunday ishlatilmaydi.

“He is too young to drive” bilan “He is not old enough to drive” bir xilmi?

Ma’no jihatidan ha, ikkalasi ham “u mashina haydash uchun hali yosh” degani. Too young qisqa va to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri, not old enough esa ko‘pincha yumshoqroq eshitiladi. Ikkalasini ham ishlatsangiz bo‘ladi.

“Too” gap oxirida kelsa, nima bo‘ladi?

U boshqa ma’no beradi — “ham” (also): “I’m tired.” “Me too.” (— Men charchadim. — Men ham.) Bu yerda “ortiqcha” ma’nosi yo‘q. Inkor gapda “ham” uchun either ishlatiladi: I’m not hungry, either.

“I’ve had enough” deganda “enough” nima o‘rnida turibdi?

Bu yerda enough otsiz, olmosh sifatida turibdi: “yetarli (miqdor)”. Aniq narsani aytmay, “yetarlicha yedim, ichdim, ko‘rdim” demoqchimiz. Yana: That’s enough! (Bas!) — g‘azablanib to‘xtatish ham shu.

“Not enough” va “too little / too few” bir xilmi?

Ha, ma’no jihatidan deyarli bir xil: not enough time = too little time, not enough chairs = too few chairs. Faqat too little sanalmaydigan otlar bilan, too few — sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan keladi (Unit 87).

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  2. We haven’t got (enough chairs / chairs enough) for everyone.

  4. She didn’t speak (loudly enough / enough loudly).
  5. There wasn’t (enough time / time enough) to see everything.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • sifat / ravish + enough (big enough); enough + ot (enough time). Enough big ❌.
  • too + sifat / ravish (too big); ot oldida too much (sanalmaydigan) / too many (sanaladigan ko‘plik). Kuchaytirish: much too, far too.
  • too = ortiqcha, muammo; very = shunchaki juda. “Juda” → very yoki too, ma’nosiga qarab.
  • too … (for somebody) to do — “… qila olmaydi”; … enough (for somebody) to do — “… qila oladi”.
  • Ega to‘ldiruvchi vazifasini ham bajarsa, to‘ldiruvchi olmoshi qo‘shilmaydi: too hot to drink. Predlog oxirida qoladi: to sit on.
  • too young ≈ not old enough; too few ≈ not enough.
  • Gap oxiridagi too = “ham”.
