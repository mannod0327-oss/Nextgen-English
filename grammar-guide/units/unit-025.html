When I do / When I've done; When and if

When I do / When I’ve done; when va if
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Kelajak haqidagi gapda when, while, before, after, until, as soon as dan keyin nega present simple ishlatilishini: I’ll call you when I get home.
  • Bu so‘zlardan keyin present perfect qachon ishlatilishini: when I’ve finished.
  • if dan keyin ham kelajak ma’nosida present simple: If it rains, we’ll stay at home.
  • when va if ning farqini: aniq bo‘ladigan ish va bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan ish.
  • Qachon when / if dan keyin will ishlatsa bo‘lishini (savol va bilvosita savollarda).

Bu unitning eng muhim qoidasi: kelajak haqidagi gap ikki qismdan iborat bo‘lsa — asosiy qism va vaqt yoki shart qismi (when-, if-, before- ... qismi) — will faqat asosiy qismda bo‘ladi. When/if qismida esa hozirgi zamon ishlatiladi, garchi ma’no kelajak bo‘lsa ham.

Yaxshi xabar: o‘zbek tilida ham shunday! Biz “Uyga kelganimda qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman” deymiz — ergash qismda “kelaman” (kelasi zamon) emas, -ganda shakli. “Agar yomg‘ir yog‘sa, uyda qolamiz” — shart qismida “yog‘adi” emas, yog‘sa. Ya’ni ergash gapda odatda kelasi zamon shakli ishlatilmaydi. Muammo faqat tarjimada: o‘quvchi “kelaman” ni eslab, ❌ when I will come deb yuboradi. Esda tuting: when / if qismida will yo‘q!

when + present simple (kelajak ma’nosida) I’ll call you when I get home

Mana bu suhbatga qarang:

  • MALIKA: Will you let me know when you arrive in Istanbul?
    KAMOLA: Of course. I’ll text you when the plane lands.    MALIKA: Istanbulga yetib borganingni menga xabar qilasanmi? KAMOLA: Albatta. Samolyot qo‘nganda senga xabar yozaman.

I’ll text you when the plane lands — ikki qismdan iborat gap. Ikkala qism ham kelajak haqida, lekin when-qismda present simple (lands), asosiy qismda esa will.

Qoida: kelajak haqida gapirganda when dan keyin will ishlatilmaydi — present simple ishlatiladi. Asosiy qismda esa will, going to, buyruq gap va h.k. bo‘lishi mumkin.

  • When Dilnoza gets her exam results, she’ll call her parents. (not When Dilnoza will get)Dilnoza imtihon natijalarini olganda, ota-onasiga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi.
  • When you get to the hotel, send me a photo of the view. (not When you will get)Mehmonxonaga yetib borganingizda, menga manzaraning suratini yuboring.
  • (to a teenager) What will you do when you finish school?(o‘smirga) Maktabni bitirganingdan keyin nima qilasan?

while, before, after, until, as soon as ... ham xuddi shunday

Bu qoida boshqa vaqt bog‘lovchilariga ham tegishli:

Keyin kelajak ma’nosida present ishlatiladigan so‘zlar:

Vaqt bog‘lovchilari + present simple
So‘zMisolO‘zbekcha

Vergul: agar when-qism gap boshida kelsa, undan keyin odatda vergul qo‘yiladi: When I get home, I’ll call you. Agar oxirida kelsa, vergul shart emas: I’ll call you when I get home.

when / after / until / as soon as + present perfect When I’ve done

Bu so‘zlardan keyin present perfect (have done) ham ishlatilishi mumkin. U birinchi ish ikkinchisidan oldin tugagan bo‘lishini ta’kidlaydi:

  • You can use my laptop when I’ve finished my essay.Inshoimni yozib bo‘lganimdan keyin noutbukimni ishlatishing mumkin.
  • Don’t start eating until everybody has sat down.Hamma o‘tirib bo‘lmaguncha ovqatni boshlamang.
  • As soon as you’ve read the letter, give it back to me.Xatni o‘qib bo‘lishing bilan menga qaytarib ber.

Ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham mumkin

Ko‘p hollarda present simple ham, present perfect ham to‘g‘ri va ma’no deyarli bir xil:

  • I’ll send you the file as soon as I finish. / I’ll send you the file as soon as I’ve finished.Tugatishim bilan faylni yuboraman.
  • The exam will seem easier after you do a few practice tests. / The exam will seem easier after you’ve done a few practice tests.Bir nechta mashq testlarini ishlaganingizdan keyin imtihon osonroq tuyuladi.

Ikki ish bir vaqtda bo‘lsa — present perfect emas

Present perfect “birinchi ish tugaydi, keyin ikkinchisi boshlanadi” degan ma’noni beradi. Agar ikki ish bir vaqtda sodir bo‘lsa, present perfect ishlatilmaydi:

1️⃣ → 2️⃣ ketma-ket: present perfect

When I’ve finished this email, I’ll make some tea.

Bu xatni yozib bo‘lgach, choy damlayman. (Avval xat tugaydi, keyin choy.)

1️⃣ + 2️⃣ bir vaqtda: present simple

When I visit Grandma on Sunday, I’ll show her the photos. (not When I’ve visited)

Yakshanba kuni buvimnikiga borganimda, unga suratlarni ko‘rsataman. (Suratlarni uning oldida bo‘lganimda ko‘rsataman.)

if + present simple (kelajak ma’nosida) If it rains, we’ll stay at home

if (agar) dan keyin ham kelajak ma’nosida odatda present simple ishlatiladi, will emas:

If+present simple,will / won’t+fe’l
  • If it rains tomorrow, we’ll stay at home. (not If it will rain)Agar ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘sa, uyda qolamiz.
  • If you don’t take a map, you’ll get lost.Xarita olmasang, adashib qolasan.
  • I’ll be very upset if you forget my birthday again.Agar yana tug‘ilgan kunimni unutsang, juda xafa bo‘laman.
  • If Jasur calls, tell him I’m at the library.Agar Jasur qo‘ng‘iroq qilsa, uni kutubxonadaman deb ayt.

Bunday gaplar birinchi tur shart gap (first conditional) deyiladi. If-gaplar haqida batafsil — Unit 38 dan boshlab.

when yoki if? Compare when and if

when — aniq bo‘ladigan ish uchun (faqat vaqti haqida gap ketyapti). if — bo‘lishi ham, bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin bo‘lgan ish uchun.

✅ when — albatta bo‘ladi

Dilnoza is coming tomorrow. When she comes, I’ll give her the book.

Dilnoza ertaga keladi. U kelganda, kitobni beraman.

🤷 if — balki bo‘ladi, balki yo‘q

Dilnoza might come tomorrow. If she comes, I’ll give her the book.

Dilnoza ertaga kelishi mumkin. Agar kelsa, kitobni beraman.

  • If it’s cold tomorrow, we won’t go to the park. (not When it’s cold)Agar ertaga sovuq bo‘lsa, parkka bormaymiz.
  • Don’t wait for me if I’m not there by six. (not when I’m not there)Agar oltigacha u yerda bo‘lmasam, meni kutmanglar.
  • If the bus doesn’t come in ten minutes, we’ll take a taxi. (not When the bus doesn’t come)Agar avtobus o‘n daqiqada kelmasa, taksiga o‘tiramiz.

O‘zbekchada -sa ba’zan ikkala ma’noni beradi: “U kelsa, kitobni beraman” — “kelganda” ham, “agar kelsa” ham bo‘lishi mumkin. Inglizchada tanlash shart: ishonchingiz komil bo‘lsa — when, ishonchingiz bo‘lmasa — if. O‘zbekchada “-ganda” desa bo‘lsa → when; “agar ...-sa” desa bo‘lsa → if.

Qachon when / if dan keyin will ishlatiladi? When and if in questions and reported questions

Yuqoridagi qoida faqat when va if vaqt yoki shart bog‘lovchisi bo‘lganda ishlaydi. Agar when so‘roq so‘zi bo‘lsa yoki if “-mi, yo‘qmi” (= whether) ma’nosida bo‘lsa, kelajak uchun will ishlatiladi:

Bir xil so‘z — ikki xil vazifa
VazifasiMisolwill?
when — vaqt bog‘lovchisi (“-ganda”)I’ll call you when I get home.❌ will yo‘q
when — so‘roq so‘zi (“qachon?”)When will you get home?✅ will bor
when — bilvosita savol (“qachon ... ekanini”)I don’t know when he’ll be back.✅ will bor
if — shart (“agar”)If it rains, we’ll stay at home.❌ will yo‘q
if = whether (“-mi, yo‘qmi”)I wonder if it’ll rain tomorrow.✅ will bor

Tekshirish usuli: agar if o‘rniga whether qo‘yish mumkin bo‘lsa yoki when “qachon?” deb tarjima qilinsa — will mumkin. Agar if = “agar”, when = “-ganda” bo‘lsa — will yo‘q.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I’ll call you when I will get home.I’ll call you when I get home.when-qismda will ishlatilmaydi, kelajak ma’nosi bo‘lsa ham.
If it will rain, we will stay at home.If it rains, we will stay at home.“agar” ma’nosidagi if dan keyin present simple.
When I will be 18, I will learn to drive.When I’m 18, I’ll learn to drive.when-qismda present simple: I am (I’m).
Don’t go until I will tell you.Don’t go until I tell you.until dan keyin ham will yo‘q.
As soon as I will finish, I’ll call you.As soon as I finish / I’ve finished, I’ll call you.as soon as + present simple yoki present perfect.
When I’ve visited Grandma on Sunday, I’ll show her the photos.When I visit Grandma on Sunday, I’ll show her the photos.Ikki ish bir vaqtda (uning oldida bo‘lganimda ko‘rsataman) — present perfect emas.
When it rains tomorrow, we won’t go out. (yomg‘ir aniq emas)If it rains tomorrow, we won’t go out.Bo‘lishi noaniq ish — if.
I don’t know when he comes back. (kelajak haqida)I don’t know when he’ll come back.Bu yerda when — “qachon” (bilvosita savol), shuning uchun will kerak.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Ma’no kelajak-ku, nega when dan keyin will ishlatilmaydi?

Chunki gapning kelajak ekanligini asosiy qism (I’ll call you) allaqachon ko‘rsatib turibdi. Ingliz tilida vaqt va shart ergash gaplarida kelajak uchun hozirgi zamon ishlatiladi — bu qat’iy qoida. O‘zbek tilida ham xuddi shunday: kelganimda, yog‘sa — kelasi zamon qo‘shimchasi yo‘q.

“When will you come?” da will bor. Bu qoidaga zid emasmi?

Yo‘q. Bu yerda when — so‘roq so‘zi (“qachon?”), vaqt bog‘lovchisi emas. Savolda will ishlatiladi: When will you come? Bilvosita savolda ham: Tell me when you’ll come. Qoida faqat “-ganda” ma’nosidagi when uchun.

“I don’t know if she’ll come” — if dan keyin will to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, to‘g‘ri. Bu yerda if = whether (“kelarmikin, yo‘qmi”). Bunday bilvosita savollarda kelajak uchun will ishlatiladi: I wonder if it’ll snow. Ask him if he’ll be at home.

“when I finish” va “when I’ve finished” — qaysi birini tanlay?

Ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Present perfect ish tugashiga urg‘u beradi (“tugatib bo‘lganimdan keyin”). Agar ikki ish bir paytda bo‘lsa (When I see him, I’ll tell him) — faqat present simple.

until va till ning farqi bormi?

Ma’no bir xil. till biroz norasmiy, ko‘proq og‘zaki nutqda ishlatiladi. Yozma va rasmiy matnda until afzal. Diqqat: untill, til deb yozilmaydi.

“if” dan keyin will hech qachon ishlatilmaydimi?

Shart ma’nosida deyarli hech qachon. Faqat maxsus holat bor: muloyim iltimosda if you will / if you’ll (“agar xohlasangiz, rozi bo‘lsangiz”) ishlatiladi: If you’ll wait here a moment, I’ll get the manager. Bu yerda will — kelajak emas, “rozilik, xohish” ma’nosida. Oddiy gaplarda esa if + present.

“by the time”, “once”, “the next time” dan keyin ham present ishlatiladimi?

Ha, ular ham vaqt bog‘lovchilari: By the time we get there, the shop will have closed. Once you learn the rules, it’s easy. The next time I see him, I’ll ask him.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavsdagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (present simple yoki will).

  1. I’ll send you a message when I (arrive) in Bukhara.
  2. Tomorrow we (start) the lesson as soon as everyone is here.
  3. Before you (go) to bed, please switch off the lights.
  4. If you (not / hurry), you’ll miss the train.
  5. Aziz will look after the children while we (be) at the cinema.
  6. I (not / leave) until I get an answer.
  7. When Kamola (be) in London, she’ll visit the British Museum.
  8. If it (be) sunny tomorrow, we (go) to the mountains.

2-mashq. Present simple yoki present perfect? To‘g‘ri variant(lar)ni tanlang.

  1. Can I use the printer when you (finish / ’ve finished)?
  2. When I (see / ’ve seen) Jasur at the meeting tomorrow, I’ll give him your message.
  3. After you (’ve paid / pay) for the tickets, I’ll give you the money back.
  4. When I (meet / ’ve met) the director tomorrow, I’ll ask her about the job.

3-mashq. when yoki if ni qo‘ying.

  1. I’m going to the bank this afternoon. I go, I’ll pay the bills.
  2. I might go to the bank this afternoon. I go, I’ll pay the bills.
  3. the weather is bad tomorrow, the match will be cancelled.
  4. Sardor finishes university next year. he finishes, he wants to work abroad.
  5. Please let me know you can’t come to the party.

4-mashq. Ikki gapni qavsdagi so‘z bilan bitta gapga birlashtiring.

  1. I’ll finish my homework. Then I’ll go out. (after) → I’ll go out
  2. The guests will arrive. We’ll start dinner immediately. (as soon as) → We’ll start dinner
  3. You’ll be in Fergana. Phone me. (when) → Phone me

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating (agar xato bo‘lsa).

  1. I’ll wait here until you will come back. →
  2. Do you know when the next bus will leave? →
  3. If you will see Malika, say hello from me. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Kelajak haqidagi gapda when, while, before, after, until, as soon as dan keyin present simple: I’ll call you when I get home.
  • will faqat asosiy qismda bo‘ladi, when-qismda — yo‘q.
  • when / after / until / as soon as + present perfect — birinchi ish tugagach: when I’ve finished. Ikki ish bir vaqtda bo‘lsa — present simple.
  • if + present simple (kelajak ma’nosida): If it rains, we’ll stay at home.
  • when — aniq bo‘ladigan ish; if — bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan ish.
  • Savolda va bilvosita savolda will mumkin: When will you come? I don’t know if he’ll come.
