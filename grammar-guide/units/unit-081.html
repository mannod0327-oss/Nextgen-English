81

-'s (your sister's name) and of ... (the name of the book)

-’s va of — qaratqich
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Egalikni ingliz tilida ikki yo‘l bilan ifodalashni: -’s (my sister’s name) va of (the name of the book).
  • Qaysi biri kimga / nimaga mos kelishini: odam va hayvon — -’s, narsa — odatda of, tashkilot va joy — ikkalasi ham.
  • -’s ni to‘g‘ri yasashni: sister’s (bitta), sisters’ (bir nechta), children’s (-s siz ko‘plik).
  • Ot tushib qolgan shakllarni: Is it yours or Sam’s? — at the doctor’s.
  • Vaqt bilan -’s ni: yesterday’s newspaper, a week’s holiday, ten minutes’ walk.
  • -’s ning boshqa vazifalarini (is / has) va its / it’s, whose / who’s farqini.

O‘zbek tilida egalik -ning va -i / -si qo‘shimchalari bilan yasaladi: Malikaning kitobi. Inglizchada bu ma’noni berishning ikki yo‘li bor. Odam yoki hayvon egasi bo‘lsa, egasi + ’s + narsa: Malika’s book. Narsa egasi bo‘lsa, odatda narsa + of + egasi: the cover of the book.

Tartibga e’tibor bering! Malikaning kitobi — Malika’s book: tartib bir xil (egasi → narsa). Lekin kitobning muqovasi — the cover of the book: bu yerda tartib teskari (narsa → egasi). Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha ❌ the book of the cover deb xato qiladilar. O‘zbekchadagi -ning egasidan keyin qo‘shiladi, inglizcha of esa egasidan oldin turadi: kitobning — of the book.

-’s: odamlar va hayvonlar Possessive -’s with people and animals

Egasi odam yoki hayvon bo‘lsa, ot oxiriga -’s qo‘shiladi va keyin narsa nomi keladi. Narsa nomi oldidan alohida the yoki a qo‘yilmaydi, chunki -’s o‘zi aniqlik beradi: the Malika’s book ❌, Malika’s the book ❌. (Egasining o‘z artikli bo‘lishi mumkin: the children’s toys, a friend’s house — bu yerda the / a egasiga tegishli.)

Ot tushib qolgan -’s: Sam’s, the doctor’s -’s without a noun

Ma’no tushunarli bo‘lsa, -’s dan keyingi ot tushirib qoldiriladi. Bu ayniqsa “kimniki?” savoliga javobda va do‘kon / uy / klinika ma’nosida uchraydi.

  • “Whose bag is this?” “It’s Malika’s.” (= Malika’s bag)— Bu kimning sumkasi? — Malikaniki.
  • Is this your pen or Bekzod’s?Bu sening ruchkangmi yoki Bekzodnikimi?
  • I’m going to the dentist’s tomorrow. (= the dentist’s surgery)Ertaga tish shifokoriga boraman.
  • We stayed at my aunt’s in Fergana. (= my aunt’s house)Biz Farg‘onada xolamnikida qoldik.
  • I bought some bread at the baker’s.Nonni novvoyxonadan (novvoyning do‘konidan) oldim.

Bu o‘zbekchadagi -niki qo‘shimchasiga mos keladi: Malikaniki, Bekzodniki, xolamnikida. Inglizchada maxsus shakl yo‘q — shunchaki ’s qoladi, ot tushib qoladi.

Whose? (kimning?) bilan so‘raymiz: Whose umbrella is this? Javob: It’s mine / yours / hers / Sam’s. Egalik olmoshlari (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) -’s olmaydi: hers ✅, her’s ❌.

Odamlar bilan ham of ishlatiladi

Egasi uzun ibora bo‘lsa yoki lavozim, rol aytilsa, of qulay: the name of the man who repaired our car (mashinamizni tuzatgan odamning ismi), the father of the bride, the president of the club, the mother of three children. Qisqa egalar uchun esa -’s tabiiy: the man’s name.

Vaqt miqdori: a week’s holiday, two weeks’ holiday

Vaqt miqdori (a week, two weeks, ten minutes, an hour ...) bilan ham -’s ishlatiladi. Birlik bo‘lsa ’s, ko‘plik bo‘lsa ’:

  • We had a week’s holiday in Khiva.Biz Xivada bir haftalik ta’tilni o‘tkazdik.

Bu Unit 80 dagi son + birlik ot bilan bir xil ma’no beradi: a two-week holiday = two weeks’ holiday, a ten-minute walk = ten minutes’ walk. Aralashtirmang: a two weeks’ holiday ❌.

-’s odatda aniq vaqt bilan keladi: yesterday’s newspaper. “Boshi, o‘rtasi, oxiri” kabi qism ma’nolarida esa of: the end of the week, the beginning of January, the middle of the night.

✅ its — uning (egalik)

  • The dog wagged its tail.
  • The company changed its name.
  • Egalik olmoshlarida apostrof yo‘q: his, hers, its, ours, theirs.

✅ it’s — it is / it has

  • It’s a nice dog. (= It is)
  • It’s stopped raining. (= It has)
  • Whose? — kimning? Who’s = who is / who has.
  • Whose phone is on the table? Who’s calling?Stol ustidagi telefon kimniki? Kim qo‘ng‘iroq qilyapti?

Sinflash uchun -’s

Ba’zan -’s kimga tegishli ekanini emas, kimlar uchun ekanini bildiradi: a women’s magazine (ayollar jurnali), a children’s hospital (bolalar kasalxonasi), a boys’ school (o‘g‘il bolalar maktabi), men’s shoes (erkaklar poyabzali). Bunda ko‘plik egasi tanlanadi, shuning uchun women’s, children’s, boys’.

Quyidagilar ham to‘g‘ri: somebody else’s umbrella (birovning soyaboni), nobody else’s fault. Bu shaklda -’s else so‘ziga qo‘shiladi, ot ga emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

-’s va of ni qanday tanlayman? Qisqa qoida bormi?

Ha: odam yoki hayvon — -’s (the teacher’s book). Tashkilot, joy, vaqt — ko‘pincha -’s (yoki of). Narsa (bino, kitob, ko‘cha) — of yoki noun + noun (the roof of the house). Qism ma’nosi (the top of, the end of, the back of) bo‘lsa — of.

Odam bilan of ishlatsam xato bo‘ladimi (the book of my brother)?

Grammatik jihatdan tushunarli, lekin g‘ayritabiiy eshitiladi. Ona tilida so‘zlashuvchilar my brother’s book deydilar. Of faqat egasi uzun yoki lavozim bo‘lsa yaxshi: the father of the bride, the name of the man who repaired our car.

James’s yoki James’ — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Britaniyada ko‘pincha James’s yoziladi va /ˈdʒeɪmzɪz/ deb o‘qiladi. Yozuvingizda bir xil shaklga amal qiling. Ko‘plik ismlarda esa faqat apostrof: the Smiths’ house.

“Is it Bekzod’s?” — nega narsa nomi aytilmaydi?

Chunki gapda narsa nomi allaqachon aytilgan yoki tushunarli. Whose pen is this? — It’s Bekzod’s. Takrorlash shart emas: It’s Bekzod’s pen ham to‘g‘ri, lekin og‘ir eshitiladi. O‘zbekchada ham “Bekzodniki” deymiz.

“Malika and Dilnoza’s flat” — bitta kvartirami yoki ikkita?

Bitta: ikkalasi birgalikda egalik qiladi. Agar har birining alohida kvartirasi bo‘lsa, ikkalasiga ’s qo‘shiladi: Malika’s and Dilnoza’s flats (ko‘plik flats ga e’tibor bering).

Nega “yesterday’s newspaper” da ’s bor, “a newspaper of yesterday” demaymizmi?

Vaqt so‘zlari bilan -’s inglizcha odat: today’s, tomorrow’s, Monday’s, last week’s. “A newspaper of yesterday” ❌. O‘zbekchada “kechagi gazeta” deymiz, inglizchada esa yesterday’s newspaper.

“The car’s engine” desam xato bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q, kundalik nutqda bunday shakl keng tarqalgan: the car’s engine, the film’s ending. Lekin qoidani o‘rganayotganda of (the engine of the car) yoki noun + noun (the car engine) ishonchliroq. Qism ma’nosi uchun of deyarli doim tabiiy: the top of the page ✅, the page’s top ❌.

Egalik olmoshlari (hers, ours) bilan nima farq?

-’s faqat ot (yoki ism) ga qo‘shiladi. Olmoshlarning o‘z shakli bor: my / mine, your / yours, his, her / hers, our / ours, their / theirs. Apostrof ishlatilmaydi: The bag is hers ✅, her’s ❌.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Odam, hayvon → -’s: Malika’s book. Egasi oldin, narsa keyin (o‘zbekchadagidek). Narsa nomi oldida the / a yo‘q.
  • Yasalishi: sister’s (birlik), sisters’ (-s li ko‘plik), children’s (-s siz ko‘plik). Ikki egadan birgalikda: Malika and Dilnoza’s flat.
  • Narsa → odatda of: the top of the page, the name of the street. Tartib teskari: narsa oldin, egasi keyin.
  • Tashkilot, joy → ikkalasi ham: the government’s decision = the decision of the government.
  • Vaqt → -’s: yesterday’s newspaper, a week’s holiday, ten minutes’ walk.
  • Ot tushishi: Is it yours or Sam’s? — at the doctor’s.
  • its (egalik) ≠ it’s (it is / it has); whose (kimning) ≠ who’s (who is). Egalik olmoshlarida apostrof yo‘q: hers, ours, theirs.
