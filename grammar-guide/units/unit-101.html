Adjectives and adverbs 2 (well/fast/late, hard/hardly)

Sifat va ravish — 2 (well, fast, late, hard / hardly)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • good (sifat) bilan well (ravish) farqini: a good cook — cooks well. Shuningdek, I’m well (“sog‘man”) gapida well sifat ekanini.
  • fast, hard, late, early kabi so‘zlar ham sifat, ham ravish bo‘lishini (fastly ❌ degan so‘z yo‘q!).
  • hard bilan hardly, late bilan lately ma’nolari butunlay boshqa ekanini.
  • hardly (“deyarli ... emas”) ni any, anyone, anything, anywhere, ever hamda can / could bilan ishlatishni.

Unit 100 da ko‘pchilik ravish sifatga -ly qo‘shib yasalishini o‘rgandingiz (quick → quickly). Bu bobda esa shu qoidaning istisnolari bilan tanishasiz: good ning ravishi well; fast va hard shaklini o‘zgartirmaydi; hardly va lately esa “hard” va “late” ning ravishi emas, balki boshqa ma’noli so‘zlar.

O‘zbek tilida yaxshi so‘zi ham sifat (yaxshi o‘qituvchi), ham ravish (yaxshi o‘qitadi) vazifasini bajaradi; tez, kech, qattiq so‘zlari ham shunday. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ingliz tilida ham He speaks English good yoki She works hardly deb adashadi. Ingliz tilida esa fe’lni tasvirlayotgan so‘z ravish bo‘lishi kerak: good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard (hardly emas!).

Good va well Good (adjective) and well (adverb)

good — sifat: u otni tasvirlaydi (a good player) yoki be fe’lidan keyin keladi (The idea is good). well — ravish: u fe’lni tasvirlaydi (plays well). “good + -ly” yasalmaydi: good ning ravishi butunlay boshqa so‘z — well.

Sifat good va ravish well

Dilnoza dutorni yaxshi chaladi.

smell, taste, look, sound, feel kabi fe’llardan keyin “qanday?” holatni bildirgan so‘z sifat bo‘ladi (Unit 99, 100): The soup tastes good ✅ — The soup tastes well ❌. Harakatni bajarish sifati haqida esa well: She cooks well.

Qiyosiy va orttirma darajada ikkalasi ham bir xil o‘zgaradi: good → better → best (sifat) va well → better → best (ravish): She is a better player than me. She plays better than me.

Well + -ed shakl (V3): well-known, well-paid ...

-ed (V3) shaklidagi so‘zlar oldida good emas, well ishlatiladi. Ular ot oldida ko‘pincha defis bilan yoziladi:

Well — “sog‘, tetik” ma’nosida (sifat)

well yana bir ma’noda sifat bo‘ladi: “sog‘, kasal emas”. Bu ma’noda u asosan be, feel, look, get, seem fe’llaridan keyin keladi va odatda ot oldida ishlatilmaydi.

  • “How are you?” “I’m very well, thank you.”— Ahvolingiz qanday? — Juda yaxshi, rahmat.
  • Grandma doesn’t look well today. Let’s call the doctor.Buvim bugun sog‘ ko‘rinmayapti. Shifokorni chaqiraylik.
  • I didn’t feel well yesterday, so I stayed in bed.Kecha o‘zimni yaxshi his qilmadim, shuning uchun yotib qoldim.
  • Get well soon!Tezroq tuzalib keting!

O‘zbekcha “Men yaxshiman” deganda ingliz tilida I’m well / I’m fine deymiz. I’m goodly degan gap yo‘q. Suhbatda I’m good ham juda ko‘p ishlatiladi (ayniqsa Amerika inglizchasida), lekin sog‘liq haqida an’anaviy to‘g‘ri javob — well.

Fast, hard, late, early — sifat ham, ravish ham Fast, hard, late: same form as adjective and adverb

Bu so‘zlar ham sifat, ham ravish bo‘la oladi, shakli esa bir xil. Ularga -ly qo‘shilmaydi. So‘z ot haqida bo‘lsa — sifat, fe’l haqida bo‘lsa — ravish.

The train goes fastly ❌ → The train goes fast ✅. He came earlily ❌ → He came early ✅. Bu so‘zlarga yana -ly qo‘shmang! (daily va early -ly bilan tugasa ham, ular ham sifat, ham ravish bo‘la oladi.)

hard uch ma’noda keladi: (1) ravish — “zo‘r berib, qattiq”: works hard; (2) sifat — “qiyin”: a hard question; (3) sifat — “qattiq (yumshoq emas)”: a hard bed. Ravish ma’nosida hardly emas, faqat hard ishlatiladi.

Ikki ravish shakli — ikki xil ma’no

Ba’zi so‘zlarning -ly siz ham, -ly li ham ravishi bor. Ular bir xil ma’noni bermaydi: -ly li shakli ko‘pincha “juda”, “diqqat bilan” kabi boshqa ma’no oladi.

Ikki xil ravish, ikki xil ma’no
-ly siz-ly li
hard — zo‘r berib: She works hard.hardly — deyarli ... emas: She hardly works.
late — kech: He came late.lately — yaqinda, so‘nggi paytlarda: I haven’t seen him lately.
high — baland: The eagle flew high.highly — juda: a highly successful company.
deep — chuqur: They dug deep.deeply — chuqur (his-tuyg‘u): I’m deeply sorry.
wide — keng: Open your mouth wide.widely — keng tarqalgan: English is widely spoken.
close — yaqin: Stay close.closely — diqqat bilan: Watch closely.
near — yaqin: He lives quite near.nearly — deyarli: It’s nearly ten o’clock.

Late va lately

late — “kechikib, kech” (sifat va ravish). lately — “yaqinda, so‘nggi paytlarda” (recently ma’nosida). Odatda lately hozirgi tugallangan zamon (present perfect) bilan keladi (Unit 7, 8): savol va inkorda, yoki “bu kunlarda ...” ma’nosida.

  • The film started at eight, but we arrived late.Film sakkizda boshlandi, lekin biz kech qoldik.
  • Have you seen Bekzod lately?Bekzodni yaqinda ko‘rdingizmi?
  • I’ve been very tired lately.So‘nggi paytlarda juda charchayapman.
  • I met Kamola recently. (not lately)Men yaqinda Kamolani uchratdim.

Oddiy o‘tgan zamonda (I met ...) “yaqinda” uchun recently ishlatiladi; I met Kamola lately tabiiy emas. recently hamma zamonlarda bo‘la oladi, lately esa asosan present perfect bilan.

O‘zbek tilida “kech” ham sifat (kech kuz), ham ravish (kech keldi). “Yaqinda” esa boshqa so‘z. Inglizchada ham shunday ajrating: kech keldi → came late, yaqinda uni ko‘rmadim → I haven’t seen him lately / recently. “Kech keldim” demoqchi bo‘lib I came lately desangiz, xato bo‘ladi: lately “kech” emas, “so‘nggi paytlarda” degani. To‘g‘risi: I came late.

Hardly — “deyarli ... emas” Hardly = almost not

hardly “deyarli ... emas, zo‘rg‘a” degan ma’noni beradi. U hard ning ravishi emas; ma’nosi ham, ishlatilishi ham boshqa. Gapning o‘zi ijobiy shaklda bo‘ladi, lekin ma’nosi inkor (deyarli yo‘q).

  • I hardly know my new neighbours. We have only said hello.Yangi qo‘shnilarimni deyarli tanimayman. Faqat salomlashganmiz.
  • We hardly spoke during the journey.Yo‘lda biz deyarli gaplashmadik.
  • The soup is so spicy that I can hardly eat it.Sho‘rva shunchalik achchiqki, uni zo‘rg‘a yeyapman.
  • It was so noisy that I could hardly hear her.Shu qadar shovqin ediki, uni zo‘rg‘a eshitdim.

Hardly + any / anyone / anything / anywhere / ever

hardly ni “any-” so‘zlari va ever bilan birga ishlatamiz. Har bir qolipning “inkorli” sinonimi bor:

Tumanda deyarli hech narsa ko‘rinmasdi.

Qoida: hardly o‘zi inkor ma’nosida, shuning uchun uni not, no, nobody, nothing, never bilan birga ishlatmang: There was hardly nothing ❌ → There was hardly anything ✅. I can’t hardly see ❌ → I can hardly see ✅. I don’t hardly know him ❌ → I hardly know him ✅.

hardly gapda always, never kabi o‘rnida turadi: oddiy fe’l oldida, be dan keyin va birinchi yordamchi fe’ldan keyin (Unit 110): She hardly speaks. He is hardly ever late. I can hardly believe it. any / anything bilan ikki o‘rin ham mumkin, ma’nosi bir xil: I ate hardly anything. = I hardly ate anything.; There’s hardly any sugar left.

hardly ba’zan “aslo, shubhasiz emas” ma’nosida ham keladi: That’s hardly surprising. (= bu mutlaqo ajablanarli emas). Yaqin ma’noli so‘zlar: barely (“zo‘rg‘a, oz farq bilan”: We barely caught the train.) va rasmiy scarcely.

O‘zbekchada “deyarli” va “zo‘rg‘a” so‘zlari ko‘pincha inkor fe’l bilan keladi: deyarli tanimayman, deyarli hech kim kelmadi. Inglizchada hardly o‘zi inkor ma’nosini olib yuradi, shuning uchun fe’l ijobiy shaklda bo‘ladi: I hardly know him (I don’t hardly know him ❌). Yana: “hard” = qattiq, zo‘r berib, “hardly” esa -ly qo‘shilgani bilan “qattiq” emas, “deyarli emas”.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

She speaks English good.She speaks English well.Fe’lni (speaks) tasvirlaydigan so‘z ravish bo‘ladi: good → well.
He is a well footballer.He is a good footballer.Ot (footballer) oldida sifat kerak: good. Well — ravish.
The train goes fastly.The train goes fast.fast ning o‘zi ravish; -ly qo‘shilmaydi.
Nodira works hardly every day.Nodira works hard every day.hardly = deyarli ... emas. “Qattiq ishlaydi” uchun hard.
I arrived lately, so I missed the start.I arrived late, so I missed the start.“Kech” = late; lately = “yaqinda, so‘nggi paytlarda”.
I don’t hardly know him.I hardly know him.hardly o‘zi inkor ma’nosida; ikkinchi inkor (don’t) kerak emas.
There was hardly nothing in the fridge.There was hardly anything in the fridge.hardly dan keyin nothing emas, anything (yoki any-) keladi.
I can’t hardly breathe.I can hardly breathe.can + hardly (can’t + hardly emas).
She is a good-known actress.She is a well-known actress.V3 shakli (known) oldida well ishlatiladi: well-known, well-paid.
Hardly anybody didn’t come.Hardly anybody came.“Deyarli hech kim kelmadi” — fe’l ijobiy shaklda.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’m good” deyish mumkinmi, yoki faqat “I’m well” to‘g‘rimi?

Salomatlik haqida an’anaviy to‘g‘ri javob — I’m well (yoki I’m fine). Lekin suhbatda “I’m good, thanks” ham juda ko‘p ishlatiladi va xato hisoblanmaydi. Yozma imtihonda xavfsiz variant — I’m well / I’m fine.

Nega “fastly” yo‘q? Axir quick → quickly bor-ku?

Chunki fast ning o‘zi uzoq vaqtdan beri ravish ham bo‘lib kelgan. Ba’zi so‘zlar (fast, hard, late, early) sifat va ravish uchun bitta shaklni saqlagan. Eng yaxshi usul — ularni yodlab olish: drive fast, work hard, arrive late.

“Hard” bilan “hardly” ni qanday adashtirmayman?

hard — “qattiq, zo‘r berib” (ko‘p). hardly — “deyarli ... emas” (juda oz). Ular qarama-qarshi: He worked hard. (juda ko‘p ishladi) — He hardly worked. (deyarli ishlamadi). Shkala rasmiga qarang: bir chetda hardly, ikkinchi chetda hard.

“Lately” va “recently” bir xilmi?

Ma’nosi o‘xshash (“yaqinda, so‘nggi paytlarda”). recently har qanday zamonda bo‘la oladi (I recently moved). lately esa asosan present perfect bilan, savol va inkorda yoki “so‘nggi paytlarda” ma’nosida keladi: I haven’t been sleeping well lately. Oddiy o‘tgan zamonda recently ishlating.

“Hardly ever” bilan “never” orasida qanday farq bor?

never — nol marta, hech qachon. hardly ever — juda kamdan-kam, lekin ba’zan bo‘ladi. Sardor never eats meat — u go‘sht yemaydi. Sardor hardly ever eats meat — yilda bir-ikki marta yeydi.

Hardly, barely va scarcely o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Uchalasi ham “deyarli yo‘q / zo‘rg‘a” ma’nosiga yaqin. barely “oz farq bilan, zo‘rg‘a” ma’nosida ijobiy natija uchun ham ishlatiladi: We barely caught the train (zo‘rg‘a ulgurdik). hardly bu ma’noda tabiiy emas. scarcely — rasmiy, kitobiy. Oddiy nutqda hardly va barely yetarli.

“Hardly had he arrived when ...” degan gap nima?

Bu rasmiy uslub. Hardly gap boshida kelsa, yordamchi fe’l egadan oldin chiqadi: Hardly had I closed the door when the phone rang. (= Eshikni yopishim bilanoq telefon jiringladi.) Oddiy nutqda: As soon as I closed the door, the phone rang.

“As well” va “well” ning aloqasi bormi?

Yo‘q. as well “ham, shuningdek” (= also, too) ma’nosida: I’ll bring juice and some cake as well. Bu good / well farqiga aloqador emas.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • good — sifat (a good cook); well — ravish (cooks well). good + -ly yo‘q.
  • -ed (V3) so‘zlar oldida well: well-known, well-paid, well-dressed. “Sog‘” ma’nosida well — sifat: I’m well, Get well soon!
  • fast, hard, late, early sifat ham, ravish ham: drive fast, work hard, arrive late. fastly ❌.
  • hardly — hard ning ravishi emas: “deyarli ... emas”. lately — late ning ravishi emas: “yaqinda, so‘nggi paytlarda”.
  • Ikki ravish shakli: high / highly, deep / deeply, wide / widely, close / closely, near / nearly — ma’nolari har xil.
  • hardly + any, anyone, anything, anywhere, ever va can / could: hardly any money, I can hardly see.
  • hardly ni not / no / nobody / nothing / never bilan birga ishlatmang: I hardly know him ✅.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 99 va Unit 100 (sifat va ravish), Unit 7 va Unit 8 (present perfect: lately), Unit 105 (better, faster, harder), Unit 110 (hardly, always, never gapda qayerda turadi).
