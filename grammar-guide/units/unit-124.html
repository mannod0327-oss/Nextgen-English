In/at/on (position) 2

In / at / on — joy predloglari 2
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • in bilan: in a line / in a row / in a queue, in the sky, in the world, in the country(side), in an office / in a department, in a photo / picture / mirror, in a book / newspaper / magazine / letter, in a car / taxi.
  • on bilan: on the left / on the right, on the ground floor / on the first floor, on a map / on the menu / on a list / on a website, on a farm, on a bus / train / plane / ship / bike / horse, on the way.
  • Qavatlar Britaniya (BrE) va Amerika (AmE) inglizchasida boshqacha sanalishini va o‘zbekcha “1-qavat” qaysi biriga mos kelishini.
  • queue (BrE) bilan line (AmE) hamda in the street (BrE) bilan on the street (AmE) farqini.

Unit 123 da xona, quti, devor, eshik, burchak kabi eng oddiy joylarni o‘rgangansiz. Unit 125 da at home, at work, at a party kabi iboralar bor. Bu darsda esa shu ikki dars orasida qolgan, kundalik hayotda juda ko‘p uchraydigan birikmalar: navbat, qator, chap va o‘ng, qavat, xarita, menyu, osmon, rasm, gazeta va transport. Asosiy fikr avvalgidek: in — biror narsaning ichida (qator, to‘plam yoki katta joyning ichida); on — yuzada, chiziqda, ma’lumot ko‘rsatilgan sathda (xarita, menyu, ro‘yxat, sayt) yoki jamoat transportida.

O‘zbek tilida bularning hammasi bitta -da qo‘shimchasi bilan aytiladi: navbatda, xaritada, gazetada, saytda, avtobusda, mashinada, chapda. Inglizchada esa -da ba’zan in, ba’zan on bo‘ladi, shuning uchun o‘zbekchadan qaysi predlog kerakligini bilib bo‘lmaydi. Eng ko‘p xato shu yerdan chiqadi: ❌ in the bus, ❌ on the taxi, ❌ in the map. Har bir birikmani predlogi bilan birga yodlang.

O‘zbekcha navbat so‘zi ikki ma’noda keladi. “Navbatda turmoq” — stand in a queue. “Sizning navbatingiz” esa It’s your turn (queue emas): It’s your turn to answer. — Javob berish navbati sizniki.

Shuning uchun inglizcha “first floor” ni eshitganda adashmang: qaysi inglizcha ekaniga qarang. Chalkashlik bo‘lmasligi uchun shunday deyish mumkin: It’s one floor up from the street. Transport va yo‘l: on a bus, in a car, on the way Vehicles and journeys

Transportda bo‘lish uchun ikki predlog bor. Qaysi biri kerakligi transport turiga bog‘liq:

on — jamoat va minib olinadigan transport

  • on a train, on a plane, on a ship, on a ferry
  • on a bike, on a motorbike, on a horse, on a camel

in — kichik, yopiq transport

  • in a car, in a taxi, in a van
  • in an ambulance, in a lorry

  • Nodira met her old teacher on the bus.Nodira eski o‘qituvchisini avtobusda uchratdi.

Qo‘shimcha: katta yo‘lovchi samolyoti uchun on the plane, lekin kichik shaxsiy samolyot va vertolyot uchun in a small plane, in a helicopter. Kema: on a ship, on a ferry, lekin kichik qayiq uchun in a rowing boat. Transportga chiqish va tushish — get in / get on (Unit 126); “avtobusda boraman” — by bus (Unit 128).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “on the bus” deymiz? Axir avtobusning ichida o‘tiramiz-ku?

Predlogni joyning haqiqiy shakli emas, ingliz tilining odatiy tasavvuri belgilaydi. Avtobus, poyezd, samolyot, kema katta transport: ularda yurib, turib ketish mumkin, ularda “paluba, pol” bor. Shuning uchun on. Mashina va taksi kichik, unga “kirib o‘tirasiz”: in. Velosiped va otda esa ustiga o‘tirasiz: on.

“In the bus” hech qachon aytilmaydimi?

Ba’zan avtobusning ichki makoni haqida gapirganda aytiladi: It was very hot in the bus. Lekin yo‘lovchi sifatida ketayotganingizni aytish uchun standart shakl — on the bus. Imtihonda va yozma ishda on the bus yozing.

First floor — bu 1-qavatmi yoki 2-qavatmi?

Bu inglizchaning turiga bog‘liq. Britaniyada ground floor — ko‘cha sathidagi qavat, first floor — uning ustidagi (o‘zbekcha 2-qavat). Amerikada ko‘cha sathidagi qavat first floor, o‘zbekcha 1-qavatga to‘g‘ri keladi. Adashmaslik uchun: one floor up from the street (ko‘chadan bir qavat yuqorida) deb aniqlashtiring.

Velosiped va otga nega “on” deymiz?

Chunki ularga ustiga o‘tirasiz, ichiga kirmaysiz. Mototsikl, skuter va tuya ham shunday: on a motorbike, on a scooter, on a camel. “Ustida, minib” ma’nosida ular bilan faqat on ishlatiladi (❌ in a bike).

On the way, in the way va by the way ni qanday ajrataman?

On the way — yo‘lda ketayotib (on the way to school). In the way — yo‘lga to‘sqinlik qilib (You’re in my way). By the way — suhbatda “aytgancha” deb mavzuni o‘zgartirish. Uchalasi har xil ma’no beradi, predlogni almashtirib bo‘lmaydi.

Nega “in the newspaper”, lekin “on the website”?

Gazeta — maqolalar yig‘ilgan bosma nashr, maqola uning ichida. Veb-sayt va ekran — ma’lumot ko‘rsatiladigan sath. Bu qoida mutlaq emas, shuning uchun birikmani yodlang: in a newspaper / in a magazine / in a book, lekin on a website / on Telegram / on the screen.

“In the country” “davlatda” degani ham, “qishloqda” degani ham bo‘ladimi?

Ha. In the country (the bilan) — qishloq joyda, shahardan tashqarida: He grew up in the country. In this country / in Uzbekistan — davlatda. Qishloq ma’nosi uchun in the countryside ham aytiladi. Qaysi ma’no ekani gapdan tushunarli bo‘ladi.
