Present perfect and past 1 (I have done and I did)

Present perfect va past simple — 1
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • The lift has broken down va The lift broke down gaplari o‘rtasidagi farqni.
  • Nega present perfect aslida hozirgi zamon ekanini — u doim hozirgi holat haqida xabar beradi.
  • Holat o‘zgargan bo‘lsa, nega present perfect ishlatib bo‘lmasligini.
  • Yangilik (yangi voqea) va eski voqea / tarix haqida qaysi zamonda gapirishni.
  • Yangilikni present perfect bilan aytib, keyin tafsilotlarni past simpleda davom ettirishni.

Asosiy g‘oya: present perfect (have/has + V3) — o‘tmishdagi voqeani hozirgi holat bilan bog‘laydi: “bo‘ldi, va natijasi hozir ham bor”. Past simple (V2) esa faqat o‘tmish haqida gapiradi: “bo‘ldi” — hozir nima bo‘layotgani haqida hech narsa demaydi.

O‘zbek tilida ikkala holatda ham ko‘pincha -di ishlatamiz: telefonimni yo‘qotdim. Lekin bizda ham natijani ta’kidlaydigan shakl bor: telefonimni yo‘qotib qo‘ydim (hozir ham yo‘q) — bu inglizcha I’ve lost my phone ga juda yaqin. Yo‘qotdim, keyin topdim esa — I lost my phone, but I found it (past simple). Ingliz tilida bu farqni har doim ko‘rsatish kerak: “Natija hozir ham to‘g‘rimi?” deb o‘zingizdan so‘rang.

has broken down va broke down: hozirgi holat haqida nima deyiladi? It has broken down / It broke down

Vaziyatni kuzating. Bugun ertalab uyimizdagi lift buzilib qoldi. U hozir ishlamayapti — hamma zinadan chiqyapti:

  • The lift has broken down.Lift buzilib qoldi. (Hozir ham ishlamayapti.)

Tushdan keyin usta kelib, liftni tuzatdi. Endi lift ishlayapti:

  • The engineer has repaired the lift.Usta liftni tuzatdi. (Hozir lift ishlayapti.)

Endi ikki savolni solishtiring:

SavolJavobNima uchun?
Has the lift broken down?No, it hasn’t. The engineer has repaired it.Savol hozirgi holat haqida: lift hozir buzuqmi? — Yo‘q, ishlayapti.
Did the lift break down?Yes, it did.Savol o‘tmish haqida: o‘tmishda buzilganmi? — Ha.

Qoida: Present perfect — hozirgi zamon. U doim hozirgi holat haqida ham xabar beradi: The lift has broken down = lift hozir ishlamayapti. Past simple esa faqat o‘tmish haqida: The lift broke down — bu gapdan lift hozir ishlayaptimi yoki yo‘qmi bilib bo‘lmaydi; u faqat o‘tmishda buzilganini aytadi.

  • I’ve forgotten her name. What is it?Uning ismini unutib qo‘ydim. Ismi nima edi? (Hozir ham eslay olmayapman.)
  • I forgot her name, but then I remembered it.Uning ismini unutgandim, lekin keyin esladim. (Hozir eslayman.)
  • Kamola has gone to the bank. She’ll be back in twenty minutes.Kamola bankka ketdi. Yigirma daqiqadan keyin qaytadi. (Hozir u yo‘q.)

Holat o‘zgargan bo‘lsa — present perfect emas Don’t use the present perfect if the situation now is different

Present perfect hozirgi holatni bildirgani uchun, agar o‘sha holat endi to‘g‘ri bo‘lmasa, uni ishlatib bo‘lmaydi. Bunday paytda past simple kerak:

Present perfect — holat hozir ham shunday

  • The shop has closed, so we can’t buy bread now. (Do‘kon hozir yopiq.)
  • The power has gone off. I can’t use my computer. (Hozir svet yo‘q.)

Past simple — holat endi boshqacha

  • The shop closed for a month in the summer, but it’s open again now. (Hozir ochiq.)
  • The power went off for an hour, but it’s back on now. (Hozir svet bor.)
  • Sardor has broken his leg, so he can’t play football this month.Sardor oyog‘ini sindirib oldi, shuning uchun bu oy futbol o‘ynay olmaydi. (Oyog‘i hozir ham singan.)
  • Sardor broke his leg two years ago, but it’s fine now.Sardor ikki yil oldin oyog‘ini sindirgan edi, lekin hozir hammasi joyida.
  • Nodira has dyed her hair red. It looks great!Nodira sochini qizilga bo‘yabdi. Juda chiroyli!
  • Nodira dyed her hair red last summer, but now it’s dark again.Nodira o‘tgan yozda sochini qizilga bo‘yagan edi, hozir yana qora.

Eslatma (Unit 7): has gone to = ketgan, hali qaytmagan; has been to = borib, qaytib kelgan. Kamola has gone to London (hozir Londonda). Kamola has been to London (u yerda bo‘lgan, hozir qaytgan).

Yangi voqealar va eski voqealar New or recent happenings / things that are not new

Yangi yoki yaqinda bo‘lgan voqealar (yangilik) haqida xabar berganda present perfect ishlatiladi:

  • I’ve fixed the tap in the kitchen. It doesn’t drip any more.Oshxonadagi jo‘mrakni tuzatdim. Endi tomchilamayapti.
  • Guess what! Dilnoza has won a scholarship to study in Korea!Bilasanmi? Dilnoza Koreyada o‘qish uchun grant yutibdi!
  • Scientists have discovered a new kind of frog in the rainforest.Olimlar tropik o‘rmonda baqaning yangi turini kashf qilishdi.

Bunday yangiliklarda past simple ham ishlatilishi mumkin (ayniqsa Amerika inglizchasida): I fixed the tap in the kitchen. It doesn’t drip any more. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

Lekin yangi bo‘lmagan, ancha oldingi voqealar, tarix haqida faqat past simple ishlatiladi:

  • Ulugh Beg built an observatory in Samarkand. (not has built)Ulug‘bek Samarqandda rasadxona qurdirgan.
  • Alisher Navoi wrote many famous poems. (not has written)Alisher Navoiy ko‘plab mashhur she’rlar yozgan.
  • My grandfather worked as a teacher for forty years. (not has worked)Bobom qirq yil o‘qituvchi bo‘lib ishlagan. (U allaqachon nafaqada.)
Solishtiring
Yangilik → present perfectTarix / eski voqea → past simple

They’ve opened a new metro station near our house.Ulugh Beg built an observatory in Samarkand. (not has built)

Eyfel minorasi hozir ham turibdi, lekin uni loyihalash voqeasi — ancha oldingi, tugagan tarix. Shuning uchun Who designed the Eiffel Tower? Biz voqeaning hozirgi natijasi haqida emas, tarix haqida gapiryapmiz.

Yangilik — present perfect, tafsilot — past simple Giving new information, then continuing

Biror yangilikni birinchi marta aytganda ko‘pincha present perfect ishlatamiz. Lekin suhbat davom etib, tafsilotlar (qachon? qayerda? qanday? kim?) haqida gapirila boshlasa, odatda past simplega o‘tamiz. Chunki endi voqea aniq, o‘tmishdagi bitta hodisa sifatida ko‘riladi:

  • A: Oh no! I’ve dropped my phone in the water!
    B: How did that happen? (not has that happened)
    A: I was taking a photo of the river, and it slipped out of my hand.    — Voy! Telefonimni suvga tushirib yubordim! — Bu qanday bo‘ldi? — Daryoni suratga olayotgan edim, qo‘limdan sirpanib ketdi.
  • A: Somebody has eaten my yoghurt!
    B: Sorry, it was me. I was really hungry.    — Kimdir yogurtimni yeb qo‘yibdi! — Kechirasan, men yedim. Qornim juda och edi.
  • A: Malika has passed her driving test!
    B: Really? When did she take it?
    A: Last Thursday. She was very nervous, but she did really well.    — Malika haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘tibdi! — Rostdanmi? Qachon topshirdi? — O‘tgan payshanba. Juda hayajonlangan edi, lekin a’lo topshirdi.
  • A: I’ve bought a new laptop.
    B: Nice! Where did you buy it? How much did it cost?    — Yangi noutbuk sotib oldim. — Zo‘r! Qayerdan olding? Qancha turdi?
Yangilik: have/has + V3→When? Where? How? Who?→Tafsilot: past simple (did / V2)

Yangiliklar ham shunday tuziladi: “A plane has landed safely at Tashkent airport after a problem with one of its engines. It landed at 10.30 this morning. The pilot told passengers ...” Sarlavha va birinchi gap — present perfect (yangilik), keyingi tafsilotlar — past simple. O‘zbekcha yangiliklarda ham dastlab “...qo‘ndi”, keyin “...soat 10:30 da qo‘ngan” kabi tafsilotlar keladi.

Qaysi birini tanlayman? — qaror sxemasi Choosing between have done and did

Britaniya va Amerika inglizchasi

Amerika inglizchasida yangilik va yaqinda bo‘lgan voqealar uchun ko‘pincha past simple ishlatiladi. Britaniya inglizchasida esa present perfect odatiyroq:

BrE (odatda)AmE (ko‘pincha)
I’ve lost my keys.I lost my keys.
Have you had lunch yet?Did you have lunch yet?
I’ve just seen Jasur.I just saw Jasur.

Imtihon va rasmiy yozuvda Britaniya variantiga amal qilganingiz ma’qul. Lekin tarix va tugagan voqealar uchun (Ulugh Beg built ...) ikkala variantda ham faqat past simple.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’ve lost my keys” va “I lost my keys” — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, ikkalasi ham grammatik to‘g‘ri, lekin ma’no farqi bor. I’ve lost my keys — kalit hozir ham yo‘q (shuning uchun yordam kerak, eshikni ocholmayman). I lost my keys — o‘tmishdagi voqea; hozir topilgan bo‘lishi ham mumkin. Amerika inglizchasida “I lost my keys” hozirgi natija ma’nosida ham keng ishlatiladi; Britaniya inglizchasida esa bu ma’noda present perfect afzal.

Eyfel minorasi hozir ham turibdi-ku. Nega “Who designed the Eiffel Tower?” da present perfect emas?

Chunki biz minoraning hozir borligi haqida emas, loyihalash voqeasi haqida so‘rayapmiz. Bu voqea ancha oldin bo‘lgan va tugagan — bu tarix. Tarix haqidagi gaplarda past simple ishlatiladi. Present perfect esa yangi voqea uchun: Someone has designed a new bridge for our city.

Nega yangilikning birinchi gapi present perfect, keyingilari esa past simple?

Birinchi gap “nima yangilik bor?” degan savolga javob beradi va voqeani hozir bilan bog‘laydi. Keyin esa biz o‘sha voqeaga aniq, o‘tmishdagi bir hodisa sifatida qaraymiz: qachon, qayerda, qanday bo‘ldi. Aniq o‘tgan hodisa = past simple. Masalan: I’ve hurt my knee. — How did you do that?

Vafot etgan odamlar haqida present perfect ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Odatda yo‘q. Ularning hayoti — tugagan davr, shuning uchun past simple: My grandfather visited Moscow many times. Navoi wrote many poems. ❌ Navoi has written many poems. Present perfect bilan hayotiy tajriba haqida faqat tirik odamlar uchun gapiramiz: My father has visited Moscow many times.

“Have you seen my keys?” va “Did you see my keys?” — qaysi biri?

Kalitni hozir qidirayotgan bo‘lsangiz — Have you seen my keys? (hozirgi holat: “bilasizmi, ular qayerda?”). Did you see ...? esa ma’lum o‘tgan vaziyat haqida: Did you see my keys on the table this morning? (Amerika inglizchasida “Did you see my keys?” hozirgi ma’noda ham ko‘p ishlatiladi.)

O‘zbekcha gapni tarjima qilganda qanday tanlayman?

O‘zbekchada -ib qo‘ydi, -ib oldi, -ibdi (yo‘qotib qo‘ydim, sindirib oldi, farzand ko‘ribdi) ko‘pincha natija hozir ham borligini bildiradi — bu present perfect ga mos keladi. Oddiy -di esa ikkala zamonga ham to‘g‘ri kelishi mumkin. Shuning uchun har gal so‘rang: natija hozir ham bormi? Yangilikmi? Yoki tugagan, eski voqeami?

“He has gone” va “He went” — qaysi biri “u ketdi”?

Agar u hozir ham bu yerda bo‘lmasa (hali qaytmagan) — He’s gone (BrE): Jasur isn’t here. He’s gone home. Agar tafsilot aytsangiz yoki u allaqachon qaytgan bo‘lsa — He went: He went home at five. / He went to Samarkand last week, but he’s back now.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. Ulugh Beg (built / has built) an observatory in Samarkand.
  2. Aziz (has gone / went) to Moscow last month, but he’s back in Tashkent now.
  3. The water (has gone off / went off) for a few hours, but it’s back on now.
  4. “Has Dilnoza finished her report?” “Yes, she (did / has).”
  5. “Did you see the match?” “No, I (didn’t / haven’t).”
  6. Who (has painted / painted) the Mona Lisa?
  7. My grandfather (has been / was) a pilot. He loved flying. (He died in 2015.)
  8. “I’ve hurt my knee!” “Oh no! How (did you do / have you done) that?”
  9. Sardor (has lived / lived) in London for two years, but now he lives in Samarkand.

2-mashq. Vaziyatni o‘qing va present perfect bilan gap yozing.

  1. Malika is looking for her keys. She can’t find them. → She .
  2. Sardor weighed 80 kilos. Now he weighs 72 kilos. → He .
  3. Yesterday the window was fine. Now it is broken. → Somebody .
  4. Last month a bus ticket cost 2,000 so‘m. Now it costs 2,500. → The price of a ticket .

3-mashq. Dialoglarni to‘ldiring. Qavs ichidagi so‘zlardan foydalaning.

  1. A: I’ve bought a new laptop. B: Really? Where (you / buy) it?
  2. A: Malika has passed her driving test! B: That’s great! When (she / take) it?
  3. A: Someone has moved my bike. B: Sorry, I (move) it this morning. It was in the way.
  4. A: Bekzod has broken his arm. B: Oh no! How (it / happen)?
  5. A: Have you heard? Nodira (get) a new job.
  6. B: Has she? How (she / find) it?
  7. A: Jasur isn’t here. He’s gone home. B: When (he / leave)?

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Present perfect — hozirgi zamon: u hozirgi holat haqida ham xabar beradi. The lift has broken down = lift hozir ishlamayapti.
  • Past simple faqat o‘tmish haqida: The lift broke down — hozir tuzatilgan bo‘lishi ham mumkin.
  • Holat o‘zgargan bo‘lsa — present perfect emas: The power went off for an hour, but it’s back on now.
  • Yangilik → present perfect (yoki past simple, ayniqsa AmE); eski voqea va tarix → faqat past simple: Ulugh Beg built ...
  • Yangilikni aytgach, tafsilot (when? where? how?) → past simple: I’ve dropped my phone. — How did it happen?
  • Qisqa javob: Has he ...? — Yes, he has. / Did he ...? — Yes, he did.
