If I had known ... I wish I had known ...

If I had known ... I wish I had known ... (o‘tgan xayoliy holat)
If and wish · Shart gaplar va wish

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • If I had known ..., I would have ... — o‘tmishda bo‘lmagan narsani tasavvur qilishni.
  • ’d qachon had, qachon would ekanini ajratishni.
  • I wish I had known ... — o‘tmishdagi ishdan afsuslanishni.
  • would do (hozirgi natija) va would have done (o‘tgan natija) farqini.
  • would have / could have / might have ning ma’nolarini.

Unit 39 da hozirgi haqiqatning teskarisini tasavvur qildik (If I knew ...). Endi esa o‘tmishni “qayta yozamiz”: o‘tmishda bir narsa bo‘lmagan — biz u bo‘lganida nima bo‘lishini tasavvur qilamiz. O‘tmishni o‘zgartirib bo‘lmaydi, shuning uchun bu gaplar ko‘pincha afsus yoki tanqidni bildiradi.

O‘zbek tilida “Bilganimda, borardim” gapi ham hozir, ham o‘tmish haqida bo‘lishi mumkin — kontekst hal qiladi. Ingliz tilida esa tanlash shart: hozir haqida — If I knew, I would go; o‘tmish haqida — If I had known, I would have gone. O‘tmish uchun o‘zbekchada ko‘pincha “-gan bo‘lardim” ishlatiladi: bilganimda, borgan bo‘lardim.

If I had known ... — o‘tmishdagi xayoliy vaziyat if + had (done) ..., would have (done)

Vaziyat: O‘tgan shanba kuni Jasur aeroportga metro o‘rniga taksida ketdi. Yo‘lda katta tirbandlik bo‘ldi va u samolyotdan qolib ketdi. Endi u shunday deydi:

  • The traffic was terrible. If I had taken the metro, I would have caught my plane.Tirbandlik dahshatli edi. Metroga o‘tirganimda, samolyotga ulgurgan bo‘lardim.

Haqiqatda Jasur metroga o‘tirmagan va samolyotga ulgurmagan. U o‘tmishdagi teskari vaziyatni tasavvur qilyapti.

If+ega+had+V3,ega+would have+V3

Qoida: O‘tmish haqida tasavvur qilganda if + had (’d) + V3 ishlatamiz (if I had known / been / done ...). Ikkinchi qismda — would have + V3.

  • I didn’t know it was your birthday. If I’d known, I would have bought you a present.Tug‘ilgan kuning ekanini bilmagandim. Bilganimda, senga sovg‘a olgan bo‘lardim.
  • Bekzod failed his driving test. He would have passed if he hadn’t been so nervous.Bekzod haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘ta olmadi. Bunchalik hayajonlanmaganida, o‘tgan bo‘lardi.
  • If you had been listening, you would have understood the instructions.Quloq solib turganingizda, ko‘rsatmalarni tushungan bo‘lardingiz. (lekin quloq solmayotgan edingiz)
  • If we had left ten minutes earlier, we wouldn’t have missed the train.O‘n daqiqa ertaroq chiqqanimizda, poyezddan qolmagan bo‘lardik.

if qismida would yo‘q; ’d = had yoki would Not “if ... would have”

if qismida would ishlatilmaydi. would have faqat ikkinchi qismda keladi:

  • If I had heard the phone, I would have answered it. (not If I would have heard)Telefon jiringini eshitganimda, javob bergan bo‘lardim.

Diqqat: qisqa shakl ’d ham had, ham would bo‘lishi mumkin. Keyingi so‘zga qarang:

  • If I’d heard the phone, I’d have answered it.I’d heard = I had heard; I’d have answered = I would have answered.
’d ni qanday tanib olish mumkin?
’d dan keyin’d =Misol
V3 (seen, known, gone)hadIf I’d known ... = If I had known
have + V3wouldI’d have helped = I would have helped
fe’lning oddiy shakli (go, help)wouldI’d help = I would help (Unit 38–39)

Og‘zaki nutqda would have qisqa talaffuz qilinadi: would’ve /ˈwʊdəv/. Shuning uchun ba’zan noto‘g‘ri would of deb yoziladi. Bu — imlo xatosi! To‘g‘ri: would have yoki would’ve. Xuddi shunday: could’ve, should’ve, might’ve.

AmE og‘zaki nutqida ba’zan If I would have known ... eshitiladi, lekin bu standart ingliz tili hisoblanmaydi. Yozma ishda va imtihonda doim If I had known yozing.

I wish I had known ... — o‘tmishdan afsus wish + had (done)

wish dan keyin ham xuddi shunday had + V3 ishlatiladi. I wish something had happened = “bu bo‘lmaganidan afsusdaman”:

I+wish+ega+had (not)+V3
  • I wish I’d known about the meeting. I would have come. (but I didn’t know)Qani endi majlis haqida bilgan bo‘lsam. Kelgan bo‘lardim. (lekin bilmadim)
  • My feet hurt. I wish I hadn’t worn these new shoes. (I wore them)Oyog‘im og‘riyapti. Qani endi bu yangi tuflini kiymagan bo‘lsam.
  • I’ve lost all my photos. I wish I had made a backup copy. (I didn’t make one)Barcha suratlarimni yo‘qotdim. Qani endi zaxira nusxa olib qo‘ygan bo‘lsam.
  • Do you ever wish you had gone to university in another country?Hech qachon boshqa davlatda universitetga borganingizda edi, deb afsuslanasizmi?
  • Our hotel in Bukhara was very noisy. I wish we had stayed somewhere else. (not I wish we would have stayed)Buxorodagi mehmonxonamiz juda shovqinli edi. Qani endi boshqa joyda to‘xtagan bo‘lsak.

wish dan keyin would have ishlatilmaydi: I wish I had studied harder ✅ — I wish I would have studied ❌.

wish: hozir va o‘tmish
VaqtTuzilmaMisolO‘zbekcha
Hozirwish + pastI wish I had a car.Qani endi mashinam bo‘lsa.
O‘tmishwish + had V3I wish I had had a car last year.Qani endi o‘tgan yili mashinam bo‘lgan bo‘lsa.
Hozirwish + pastI wish I knew the answer.Qani endi javobni bilsam.
O‘tmishwish + had V3I wish I had known the answer.Qani endi javobni bilgan bo‘lsam.

O‘zbekcha “qani endi ... -gan bo‘lsam”, “koshki ... -maganimda edi” → I wish I had (not) + V3. Koshki unga aytmaganimda edi = I wish I hadn’t told him.

would (do) va would have (done); could have / might have would vs would have; would/could/might have

if qismi o‘tmish haqida bo‘lsa ham, natija hozir haqida bo‘lishi mumkin. Taqqoslang:

  • If Aziz had taken the job in Dubai, he would be rich now. (natija — hozir)Aziz Dubaydagi ishni olganida, hozir boy bo‘lardi. (hozir boy emas)
  • If Aziz had taken the job in Dubai, he would have earned a lot of money last year. (natija — o‘tmish)Aziz Dubaydagi ishni olganida, o‘tgan yili ko‘p pul ishlagan bo‘lardi.

would have / could have / might have

  • If Jasur hadn’t hurt his leg, he would have played in the final.Jasur oyog‘ini jarohatlamaganida, finalda o‘ynagan bo‘lardi. (aniq)
  • If Jasur hadn’t hurt his leg, he could have played in the final.... finalda o‘ynay olgan bo‘lardi. (= would have been able to)
  • If Jasur hadn’t hurt his leg, he might have played in the final.... balki finalda o‘ynagan bo‘lardi. (= perhaps he would have played)
ShaklMa’no
would have + V3aniq natija: “... gan bo‘lardi”
could have + V3imkoniyat: “... a olgan bo‘lardi” (= would have been able to)
might have + V3ehtimol: “balki ... gan bo‘lardi” (= perhaps would have)

If I had known yesterday, I would come.If I had known yesterday, I would have come.Natija o‘tmishda — would have + V3.
I wish I didn’t spend so much money yesterday.I wish I hadn’t spent so much money yesterday.O‘tmishdan afsus — wish + had (not) + V3.
I wish you would have told me earlier.I wish you had told me earlier.wish dan keyin would have ishlatilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

’d — had mi yoki would? Qanday bilaman?

Keyingi so‘zga qarang. ’d + V3 (known, seen) = had. ’d + have + V3 yoki ’d + oddiy fe’l (go, help) = would. Masalan: If I’d known (had), I’d have come (would).

If I knew va If I had known — qaysi birini qachon ishlataman?

Vaziyatning vaqtiga qarang. Hozir bilmaysizmi? — If I knew (Unit 39). O‘tmishda bilmaganmisiz? — If I had known. Masalan: If I knew his address, I’d write to him (hozir bilmayman) / If I had known his address, I’d have written to him (o‘sha paytda bilmagandim).

Bitta gapda o‘tmish va hozirni aralashtirsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha. if qismi o‘tmish haqida, natija esa hozir haqida bo‘lishi mumkin: If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now. (Tibbiyotni o‘qiganimda, hozir shifokor bo‘lardim.) Bu — D bo‘limdagi holat.

“I wish I had” va “I wish I had had” — farqi nima?

I wish I had a car — hozir mashinam yo‘q. I wish I had had a car — o‘tmishda (masalan, o‘tgan yili) mashinam yo‘q edi. Ikkinchisida birinchi had — yordamchi fe’l, ikkinchi had — have ning V3 shakli. G‘alati ko‘rinsa ham, to‘g‘ri!

“would have” qanday talaffuz qilinadi?

Tez nutqda would’ve /ˈwʊdəv/ yoki hatto /ˈwʊdə/. Inkori wouldn’t have /ˈwʊdntəv/. Yozganda would of deb yozmang — bu xato.

O‘zbekcha “bilganimda” ni qanday tarjima qilaman?

Kontekstga qarang. Hozir haqida bo‘lsa: Bilganimda, aytardim = If I knew, I’d tell you. O‘tmish haqida bo‘lsa (kecha, o‘shanda): Bilganimda, aytgan bo‘lardim = If I had known, I’d have told you.

could have va might have ning farqi nima?

could have — “qila olgan bo‘lardik” (imkon bo‘lardi): We could have gone. might have — “balki qilgan bo‘lardik” (aniq emas): We might have gone. would have esa aniq natija: We would have gone.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’llarni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (if + had V3, would have V3).

  1. Nobody told me about the party. If I (know) about it, I (come).
  2. We (not / miss) the train if we (leave) home earlier.
  3. If Malika (not / help) me, I (not / finish) the project on time.
  4. If you (tell) me about the problem, I (try) to help you.
  5. The accident (not / happen) if the driver (be) more careful.
  6. The shop was closed. If it (be) open, I (buy) some bread.

2-mashq. Vaziyatni o‘qing va if bilan gapni to‘ldiring.

  1. I didn’t have your number, so I didn’t call you. → If I (have) your number, I (call) you.
  2. Aziz was late because he overslept. → If Aziz (not / oversleep), he (not / be) late.
  3. We didn’t go to the beach because it was cold. → We (go) to the beach if it (not / be) so cold.
  4. She didn’t study, so she failed the exam. → If she , she the exam.

3-mashq. I wish ... bilan o‘tmishdagi afsusni yozing.

  1. I didn’t take my umbrella and I got wet. →
  2. I told Kamola your secret. →
  3. I didn’t study harder at school. →
  4. We didn’t book seats on the train, so we had to stand. →
  5. I didn’t go to the concert. →

4-mashq. would + fe’l (hozirgi natija) yoki would have + V3 (o‘tgan natija)?

  1. If I had charged my phone last night, I (not / need) to borrow yours now.
  2. If Kamola had set an alarm, she (not / oversleep) this morning.
  3. If Sardor had accepted the job in Dubai, he (live) there now.
  4. If you hadn’t reminded me, I (forget) about the meeting.

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

  1. If I would have known, I would have come. →
  2. I wish I didn’t spend so much money yesterday. →
  3. I would of helped you if you had asked. →
  4. If you had told me, I will help you. →
🔑 Javoblar (Answer key)

1-mashq

  1. had known (’d known), would have come (’d have come)
  2. wouldn’t have missed, had left (’d left)
  3. hadn’t helped, wouldn’t have finished
  4. had told (’d told), would have tried (’d have tried)
  5. wouldn’t have happened, had been (’d been)
  6. had been, would have bought (’d have bought)

2-mashq

  1. had had (’d had), would have called (’d have called)
  2. hadn’t overslept, wouldn’t have been
  3. would have gone (’d have gone), hadn’t been
  4. had studied (’d studied), would have passed / had studied (’d studied), wouldn’t have failed

3-mashq

  1. I wish I had (I’d) taken my umbrella.
  2. I wish I hadn’t told Kamola your secret.
  3. I wish I had (I’d) studied harder at school.
  4. I wish we had (we’d) booked seats on the train.
  5. I wish I had (I’d) gone to the concert.

4-mashq

  1. wouldn’t need / would not need
  2. wouldn’t have overslept / would not have overslept
  3. would be living (would live)
  4. would have forgotten (’d have forgotten)

5-mashq

  1. If I had (I’d) known, I would have come.
  2. I wish I hadn’t spent so much money yesterday.
  3. I would have (would’ve) helped you if you had asked.
  4. If you had told me, I would have helped you.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • O‘tmishdagi xayoliy vaziyat: if + had + V3, would have + V3 — If I had known, I would have come.
  • if qismida would yo‘q: If I would have known ❌.
  • ’d + V3 = had; ’d have + V3 = would have.
  • O‘tmishdan afsus: I wish + had (not) + V3 — I wish I hadn’t said it. (wish + would have ❌)
  • Natija hozir bo‘lsa: If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now.
  • would have (aniq), could have (qila olardi), might have (balki).
  • Yozuvda would of ❌ — faqat would have / would’ve.
