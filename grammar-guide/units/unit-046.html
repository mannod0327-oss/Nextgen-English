Have something done

Have something done — ishni boshqaga qildirish
Passive · Majhul nisbat (Passive)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • have + narsa + V3 tuzilmasini: I had my hair cut. — ishni o‘zingiz emas, boshqa kishi bajargan.
  • Bu tuzilmani turli zamonlarda yasashni: has, is having, had, will have, must have.
  • So‘roq va inkorni (Did you have your car repaired?) va norasmiy get variantini.
  • Yoqimsiz voqealarni ham shu shaklda aytishni: I had my bag stolen.
  • I had my room painted, I had painted my room va I painted my room orasidagi farqni.

Ba’zi ishlarni o‘zimiz qilmaymiz, balki mutaxassisga topshiramiz: sartarosh sochni kesadi, usta mashinani ta’mirlaydi, fotograf suratga oladi. Shunda ingliz tili have + narsa + V3 tuzilmasini ishlatadi. Muhim: ega ishni o‘zi qilmaydi, uni boshqa kishi qiladi (yoki ega bilan shunday voqea sodir bo‘ladi).

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no fe’lga -tir / -dir / -t / -ttir qo‘shimchasi (orttirma nisbat) qo‘shilishi bilan beriladi: soch kes-tir-dim, mashinani ta’mirla-t-dim, rasmga ol-dir-dim, kostyum tik-dir-di. Ingliz tilida fe’lga qo‘shimcha qo‘shilmaydi. Buning o‘rniga have + narsa + V3 ishlatiladi: I had my hair cut. Ko‘p o‘quvchilar “Men sochimni sartaroshxonada kesdim” ni ❌ I cut my hair at the barber’s deb tarjima qiladi. Bu gap “sochni o‘zim kesdim” degan ma’noni beradi! O‘zbekchada kes- va kes-tir- farqi inglizchada cut va have ... cut farqiga to‘g‘ri keladi.

have something done — ishni boshqaga qildirish have + object + past participle

Ikki gapni solishtiring:

  • Bekzod painted the gate.Bekzod darvozani bo‘yadi. (Bekzodning o‘zi bo‘yadi)
  • Bekzod had the gate painted.Bekzod darvozani bo‘yatdi. (usta bo‘yadi, Bekzod emas)

Ikkinchi gapda darvozani bo‘yagan kishi aytilmagan — chunki bu muhim emas yoki o‘z-o‘zidan tushunarli (bo‘yoqchi usta). Muhimi — Bekzodning darvozasi bo‘yaldi.

Ega+have (has / had / having ...)+narsa+V3

Qoida: Ishni boshqa kishi qilsa, have + narsa (object) + past participle (V3) ishlatiladi. Tartib qat’iy: have → narsa → V3. Zamon va shaxs faqat have ning shaklida ko‘rinadi: has / had / is having / will have. V3 hamisha o‘zgarmaydi.

  • Malika has her hair cut every month.Malika har oy sochini kestiradi.
  • I had my car repaired last week.O‘tgan hafta mashinamni ta’mirlatdim.
  • We’re having new windows put in this week.Bu hafta uyimizga yangi derazalar o‘rnattiryapmiz.
  • Have you ever had your eyes tested?Ko‘zlaringizni hech tekshirtirganmisiz?
  • I’m going to have the document translated.Hujjatni tarjima qildirmoqchiman.
  • Dilnoza had a dress made for her sister’s wedding.Dilnoza singlisining to‘yi uchun libos tikdirdi.

O‘zbekcha -tir / -dir qo‘shimchasi boshqa ma’nolarni ham beradi: kuldirmoq (kuldirib yubordi) = make somebody laugh, yig‘latmoq = make somebody cry. Faqat “xizmatdan foydalanish, ishni topshirish” ma’nosigina have + V3 ga mos keladi.

Zamonlar, so‘roq, inkor va get Tenses, questions, negatives and get

have bu tuzilmada oddiy (asosiy) fe’l, shuning uchun hamma zamon unga mos tuziladi. So‘roq va inkor present simple va past simple da do / does / did orqali yasaladi.

Savolda Had you your hair cut? ❌ deb bo‘lmaydi. have bu yerda asosiy fe’l, shuning uchun: Did you have your hair cut? ✅ Present perfect da esa Have you had ...? shakli bor: Have you had your hair cut?

get — norasmiy variant

Suhbatda ko‘pincha have o‘rniga get ishlatiladi. Ma’no bir xil, uslub norasmiyroq:

  • I need to get my hair cut.Sochimni kestirishim kerak.
  • She got her car fixed last week.U o‘tgan hafta mashinasini tuzattirdi.
  • We must get the carpets cleaned before the guests come.Mehmonlar kelishidan oldin gilamlarni tozalatishimiz kerak.

Shuningdek have a haircut, have a shave, have a check-up kabi ot bilan iboralar ham bor (I’m having a haircut tomorrow). Ular ham ishni boshqaga topshirishni bildiradi. Britaniya ingliz tilida hairdresser (ayollar va erkaklar uchun), barber (erkaklar sartaroshi) deyiladi.

Ismni qo‘shish uchun by ishlatish mumkin, lekin odatda kerak emas: I had my car repaired by a local mechanic. Agar kim bajargani muhim bo‘lsa — shunday yoziladi. Aks holda tushirib qoldiriladi.

I had my bag stolen — yoqimsiz voqea Things that happen to us

have + narsa + V3 ba’zan ishni buyurtma qilganingizni emas, balki sizning narsangiz bilan nimadir sodir bo‘lganini bildiradi. Bu ko‘pincha yoqimsiz voqea: o‘g‘irlik, sinish, shikast:

  • Nodira had her suitcase damaged on the flight to Istanbul.Istanbulga uchishda Nodiraning chamadoni shikastlandi.
  • Have you ever had your bank card blocked?Bank kartangiz hech bloklanganmi?
  • Jasur had his visa refused twice.Jasurning vizasi ikki marta rad etildi.
  • Malika had her poem published in a magazine.Malikaning she’ri jurnalda chop etildi. (yaxshi voqea)

Bunday gapda ega ishni o‘zi buyurtma qilmagan, ish unga “qilingan”. Passive bilan solishtiring:

Passivehave + V3Farqi
Aziz’s bag was stolen.Aziz had his bag stolen.Ikkinchisida diqqat Azizda: bu voqea u bilan sodir bo‘ldi.
Their windows were broken.They had their windows broken.Ma’no deyarli bir xil, lekin ikkinchisi egani tajribaga qo‘shadi.

Bu gaplarda ega aybdor degani emas, ishni o‘zi qilmagan ham: Aziz had his bag stolen — Aziz sumkasini o‘g‘irlatmagan (buyurtma bermagan), u shunchaki jabrlangan. Qaysi ma’no ekanini fe’l va kontekst ko‘rsatadi: haircut, repair, clean bilan — xizmat; steal, break, smash, refuse bilan — hodisa.

Taqqoslash: I painted / I had painted / I had it painted Don’t confuse the three forms

Ingliz tilidagi had ikki xil vazifada: yordamchi fe’l (past perfect) va asosiy fe’l (have something done). Natijada tashqi ko‘rinishi o‘xshash, lekin ma’nosi butunlay boshqacha gaplar paydo bo‘ladi:

O‘zim qildim

  • I cut my hair.
  • I cleaned the windows myself.
  • We repaired the roof ourselves.

Boshqaga qildirdim

  • I had my hair cut.
  • I had the windows cleaned.
  • We had the roof repaired.

Tartibni buzmang: I had cut my hair ❌ (agar “kestirdim” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz) — to‘g‘risi I had my hair cut ✅. Ketma-ketlik: had → hair → cut. V3 (cut, repaired, painted) narsadan keyin turadi.

have somebody do — kishiga buyurtma berish have + person + infinitive

Agar ishni bajaradigan kishi aytilsa, boshqa tuzilma ishlatiladi: have + kishi + oddiy fe’l (to siz). Bu have something done dan farq qiladi (u yerda kishi aytilmaydi):

TuzilmaMisolO‘zbekcha
have + narsa + V3I had my laptop fixed.Noutbukimni tuzattirdim.
have + kishi + VI had my brother fix my laptop.Noutbukimni akamga tuzattirdim.
get + kishi + to VI got my brother to fix my laptop.Akamni noutbukni tuzatishga ko‘ndirdim.

I had my brother to fix my laptop ❌ (have dan keyin to kelmaydi), I got my brother fix ❌ (get dan keyin to kerak).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I cut my hair” va “I had my hair cut” orasidagi asosiy farq nima?

I cut my hair — sochni o‘zingiz kesdingiz. I had my hair cut — sochingizni boshqa kishi (sartarosh) kesdi, siz buni buyurtma qildingiz. Shuning uchun sartaroshxonaga borganingizni aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, ikkinchi shaklni ishlating.

Nega ishni bajargan kishi (by ...) aytilmaydi?

Odatda u o‘z-o‘zidan ma’lum: soch kesadigan — sartarosh, mashinani ta’mirlaydigan — usta. Gapda uni takrorlash ortiqcha. Faqat u muhim bo‘lsa, by yoki at qo‘shiladi: I had my hair cut by a famous stylist. / I had it cut at Salon 7.

have va get ning farqi bormi?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil. have neytral, get esa norasmiyroq (suhbatda ko‘proq): I got my hair cut. get ni ishlatsangiz ham, V3 narsadan keyin keladi. Rasmiy yozuvda have yaxshiroq.

“I had my bag stolen” — bu o‘zim o‘g‘irlatganimni bildiradimi?

Yo‘q. Bu tuzilma ikkita ma’noga ega: (1) xizmat/buyurtma (had my hair cut), (2) boshingizga tushgan voqea (had my bag stolen). Ikkinchisida ega aybdor emas, ishni buyurtma ham qilmagan. Qaysi ma’no ekanini fe’l ko‘rsatadi: steal, break, damage bilan — voqea.

“I had my hair done” dagi “done” nimani bildiradi?

do one’s hair — sochni turmaklash, soch qilish (uslub berish). She has her hair done every Friday — u har juma sochini turmaklatadi (kesdirishi shart emas). Xuddi shunday have one’s nails done — manikyur qildirmoq.

Uyda biror ishni “o‘zim” qilganimni qanday ta’kidlayman?

myself qo‘shing: I painted the room myself. yoki I did it myself. Bunda have kerak emas.

“have + kishi + fe’l” bilan “have + narsa + V3” ni qanday ajratib olaman?

Keyingi so‘zga qarang. I had the car repaired — narsa + V3 (kim qilgani aytilmagan). I had the mechanic repair the car — kishi + oddiy fe’l (bajaruvchi aytilgan). Ikkinchisi kamroq ishlatiladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gapni have something done shaklida yozing (ishni boshqa kishi qiladi).

  1. A hairdresser cuts Malika’s hair every month. → Malika every month.
  2. A mechanic is repairing our car at the moment. → We at the moment.
  3. A painter painted the kitchen last week. → We last week.
  4. Someone will deliver the furniture tomorrow. → We’re going to tomorrow.
  5. A photographer took Dilnoza’s picture. → Dilnoza .
  6. A dry cleaner cleaned my coat. → I .
  7. A dentist is going to check my teeth tomorrow. → I’m going to tomorrow.
  8. Someone translated the contract for us. → We .

2-mashq. So‘roq va inkor gaplar tuzing.

  1. Where / you / have / your passport photo / take? (past simple) →
  2. How often / you / have / your carpets / clean? (present simple) →
  3. you / ever / have / your bank card / block? (present perfect) →
  4. When / Aziz / have / his eyes / test? (past simple) →
  5. We don’t pay anybody to clean our windows. → We our windows . (not / have; clean)
  6. I last went to the barber in March. → I my hair since March. (not / have; cut)

3-mashq. Ishni boshqa kishi qilgan gapni tanlang (a yoki b).

  1. (a) Jasur repaired his bike. (b) Jasur had his bike repaired. →
  2. (a) We had the carpets cleaned. (b) We cleaned the carpets. →
  3. (a) She did her nails herself. (b) She had her nails done. →
  4. (a) I fixed the roof. (b) I had the roof fixed. →
  5. (a) They had cut down the tree. (b) They had the tree cut down. →

4-mashq. Voqeani have + narsa + V3 bilan ifodalang (qavs ichidagi fe’ldan foydalaning).

  1. Sardor’s bike was stolen last week. → Sardor last week. (steal)
  2. Her phone was damaged in the rain. → She in the rain. (damage)
  3. Their windows were smashed in the storm. → They in the storm. (smash)
  4. Nodira’s suitcase was opened at the airport. → Nodira at the airport. (open)
  5. Aziz’s tooth was knocked out in a football match. → Aziz in a football match. (knock out)

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Ishni boshqa kishi qilsa: have + narsa + V3 — I had my hair cut. Tartib: have → narsa → V3.
  • O‘zbekcha -tir / -dir (kestirdim, ta’mirlatdim) ≈ inglizcha have ... done. I cut my hair — o‘zingiz kesgan bo‘lasiz.
  • Zamon have ning shaklida: has her hair cut, is having it cut, had it cut, will have it cut, should have it cut.
  • So‘roq va inkor — do / does / did bilan: Did you have your car repaired? (Had you ...? ❌)
  • Norasmiy: get + narsa + V3 — I got my hair cut.
  • Yoqimsiz hodisa ham shu shaklda: Aziz had his bag stolen. — u buyurtma qilmagan, jabrlangan.
  • I had my room painted (boshqa kishi) ≠ I had painted my room (past perfect, o‘zim) ≠ I painted my room (o‘zim).
