79

Singular and plural

Birlik va ko‘plik
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Faqat ko‘plik shaklida keladigan otlarni: trousers, jeans, shorts, glasses, scissors, clothes — va ularni a pair of bilan sanashni.
  • -s bilan tugaydi, lekin birlik bo‘lgan so‘zlarni: news, mathematics, physics, economics, athletics, measles, shuningdek the United States, the Netherlands.
  • -s siz, lekin ko‘plik bo‘lgan so‘zlarni: the police, people, cattle.
  • family, team, government, company, staff kabi guruh otlari bilan fe’l birlik yoki ko‘plik bo‘lishini.
  • ten pounds is, three weeks is — miqdorlar bilan birlik fe’l; a number of ... are va the number of ... is farqini.

Ingliz tilida ot birlik (singular) yoki ko‘plik (plural) bo‘ladi va fe’l unga moslashadi: The book is new. The books are new. Odatda bu oson, lekin ba’zi so‘zlarda shakl (-s) bilan ma’no (bitta yoki ko‘p) mos kelmaydi. Aynan shu istisnolar bu darsning mavzusi.

O‘zbek tilida shim, jinsi, qaychi, ko‘zoynak, kiyim, politsiya — hammasi birlik: shimim iflos, qaychi o‘tkir emas, politsiya keldi. Ingliz tilida esa bu so‘zlar ko‘plik: My trousers are dirty. The scissors are not sharp. The police have come. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ❌ My trouser is dirty yoki ❌ The police is coming deb xato qiladilar. Aksincha, o‘zbekchada yangiliklar, ma’lumotlar deymiz, inglizchada news va information birlik.

Faqat ko‘plik otlar va a pair of Plural nouns with no singular; a pair of

Ba’zi otlar ikki qismdan iborat narsalarni bildiradi (ikki oyoq, ikki shisha, ikki pichoq ...). Ular faqat ko‘plikda ishlatiladi va ko‘plik fe’l bilan keladi. Ularning birlik shakli yo‘q: a trouser ❌, a scissor ❌, a jean ❌.

a pair of ...

Bunday narsalarni sanash yoki “bitta” deyish uchun a pair of ishlatiladi: a pair of trousers, two pairs of jeans. Fe’l pair ga moslashadi (birlik): This pair of trousers is too tight., lekin These trousers are too tight.

a / two / three+pair(s)+of+trousers / jeans / scissors / glasses
  • I need a new pair of jeans for the winter.Qish uchun menga yangi jinsi shim kerak.
  • Dilnoza bought two pairs of shorts.Dilnoza ikkita shorti sotib oldi.
  • This pair of trousers is too tight.Bu shim juda tor.
  • There is a pair of scissors on the desk.Stol ustida qaychi turibdi.
  • We saw the eagle through a pair of binoculars.Burgutni durbin orqali ko‘rdik.

Pair ni ikki bo‘lakli buyumlar uchun ishlating. Shoes, socks, gloves, earrings ning birlik shakli bor (a shoe, a sock, a glove, an earring); a pair of socks = ikkita paypoq (bir juft). Trousers, jeans, scissors, glasses ning birlik shakli yo‘q, shuning uchun ularni sanashda a pair of shart: two pairs of scissors ✅, two scissors ❌.

Boshqa ot oldidan bu so‘zlar ko‘pincha birlikka o‘tadi: a trouser pocket (shimning cho‘ntagi), a trouser leg, a pyjama jacket. Lekin jeans va glasses shaklini saqlaydi: a jeans shop, a glasses case.

Clothes, cloth va clothing

clothes — faqat ko‘plik: My clothes are in the suitcase. A clothes deyilmaydi. Bitta narsa uchun: a piece of clothing yoki an item of clothing. Clothing — sanalmaydigan ot (kiyim-kechak): Warm clothing is expensive. Cloth — mato: a piece of cloth.

-s bilan tugaydi, lekin birlik Words ending in -s that are singular

Ba’zi so‘zlar -s bilan tugaydi, lekin ular ko‘plik emas. Ular birlik fe’l (is, was, has) bilan keladi.

The police is ❌ — to‘g‘risi The police are ✅. A police ❌ — bitta kishi uchun a police officer. People ko‘plik: People are, People were. Peoples (-s bilan) boshqa ma’noda — “xalqlar, millatlar”: the peoples of Central Asia.

O‘zbekchada politsiya va xalq birlik: politsiya keldi, xalq yig‘ildi. Ingliz tilida the police va the people ko‘plik fe’l bilan: The police have arrived. The people were angry.

Sifat bilan kelgan the rich, the poor, the young (Unit 76) ham ko‘plik fe’l oladi: The young are optimistic.

Guruh otlari: family, team, government Group nouns: singular or plural verb

Ba’zi otlar bitta so‘z, lekin ular bir guruh odamni bildiradi: family, team, class, club, government, company, committee, staff, audience, crew, orchestra, jury. Britaniya ingliz tilida bunday otlar bilan fe’lni birlik yoki ko‘plik qilish mumkin:

Birlik fe’l: guruh = bitta butun

  • My family is very important to me.
  • The team is doing its best.
  • The government has decided to build a new road.

Ko‘plik fe’l: guruh = alohida a’zolar

  • My family are all in Samarkand.
  • The team are doing their best.
  • The government have decided to build a new road.

Amerika ingliz tilida bunday otlar odatda birlik fe’l bilan keladi: The team is playing well. The government is considering the plan. Britaniyada ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri; sport jamoalari haqida ko‘pincha ko‘plik: England are playing Spain.

Izchil bo‘ling: birlik fe’l tanlasangiz, it / its / which ni, ko‘plik fe’l tanlasangiz, they / their / who ni ishlating. The team is playing well, and it hopes to win ✅; The team are playing well, and they hope to win ✅. Bitta gapda aralashtirmaslik ma’qul.

O‘zbek tilida oila, jamoa, hukumat bilan fe’l odatda birlik: hukumat qaror qildi. Inglizchada (Britaniya shaklida) ko‘plik ham mumkin, shuning uchun bu o‘zbek o‘quvchilari uchun yangi tanlov. Ishonchsiz bo‘lsangiz, birlik fe’lni tanlang — u ham Britaniya, ham Amerika ingliz tilida to‘g‘ri (faqat the police bundan mustasno: doim ko‘plik).

Bir nechta jamoa bo‘lsa, ot ko‘plikka o‘tadi: Two teams are playing.

Pul, vaqt, masofa: birlik fe’l Amounts, time and distance: singular verb

Pul miqdori, vaqt davri, masofa yoki og‘irlik haqida bitta umumiy miqdor sifatida gapirsak, ko‘plik shaklidagi so‘z bilan ham birlik fe’l ishlatiladi.

  • Ten pounds is a lot of money for a sandwich.Sendvich uchun o‘n funt — katta pul.
  • Three weeks is a long time to wait.Uch hafta kutish uchun uzoq vaqt.
  • Two hours isn’t enough for this exam.Bu imtihon uchun ikki soat yetmaydi.
  • Five kilometres isn’t far by bike.Velosipedda besh kilometr uzoq emas.
  • Twenty dollars was all he had.Uning hamma puli yigirma dollar edi.

O‘zbekchada uch hafta, o‘n funt kabi iboralarda son bilan ot birlikda turadi (uch hafta, o‘n funt), fe’l ham birlik. Inglizchada ot ko‘plikda (three weeks), lekin fe’l birlik: Three weeks is ... Fe’lga e’tibor bering.

Agar siz bitta umumiy miqdorni emas, ketma-ket kelgan alohida kunlar, haftalarni nazarda tutsangiz, ko‘plik fe’l ishlatiladi: The last three weeks have been very busy. — So‘nggi uch hafta juda band o‘tdi. Lekin miqdorga baho berganda (… is a long time, … is a lot of money, … is too far) birlik fe’l ishlating.

Birlik va ko‘plik shakli bir xil bo‘lgan otlar

Ba’zi otlarda ko‘plik uchun -s qo‘shilmaydi: one sheep — two sheep, one fish — two fish, one deer — two deer, one aircraft — two aircraft. Fe’lga qarab ma’no tushuniladi: The sheep is eating. The sheep are eating.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega shim, qaychi, ko‘zoynak inglizchada ko‘plik?

Bu narsalar ikki bo‘lakdan iborat (ikki oyoq, ikki shisha, ikki pichoq). Ingliz tili ularni ko‘plik shaklida ifodalaydi: trousers, scissors, glasses. O‘zbek tilida esa ularni birlik deb qaraymiz. Mantiqni izlamang — shunchaki yodlang: ikki bo‘lakli narsa = ko‘plik + a pair of.

“This pair of trousers” dan keyin is kerakmi, are?

Fe’l pair ga moslashadi: This pair of trousers is too tight. Lekin pair siz: These trousers are too tight. Ikkala gap ham to‘g‘ri. Ba’zi ona tili egalari so‘zlashuvda a pair of trousers are deydi, lekin imtihon va rasmiy yozuvda pair ... is ishlating.

Team va family bilan birlik fe’lni tanlasam, xato bo‘ladimi?

Odatda yo‘q. Birlik fe’l deyarli har doim to‘g‘ri va xavfsiz (ayniqsa Amerika ingliz tilida). Britaniyada ko‘plik fe’l ham to‘g‘ri, agar siz guruh a’zolarini alohida-alohida nazarda tutsangiz; a’zolar alohida ta’kidlanganda esa ko‘plik tabiiyroq: My family are all doctors. The team are arguing among themselves. Eng muhimi — izchillik: it / its bilan birlik, they / their bilan ko‘plik.

Nega “ten pounds” ko‘plik, lekin fe’l birlik?

“Ten pounds” — pul miqdori, ya’ni bitta umumiy summa. Biz o‘n dona funtni emas, “shu miqdorni” baholayapmiz: Ten pounds is a lot. Xuddi shunday: three weeks is, two hours is, five miles is. O‘zbekchada bu farq yo‘q, shuning uchun fe’lga alohida e’tibor bering.

People, persons va peoples orasida farq nima?

People — “odamlar” (eng keng tarqalgan, ko‘plik fe’l bilan). Person — bitta odam. Persons — rasmiy hujjatlarda (missing persons). Peoples — xalqlar, millatlar: the peoples of Asia.

A number of va the number of ni qanday adashtirmayman?

A number of = several / many, shuning uchun uning ortidan ko‘plik fe’l keladi: A number of students are late. The number of = “son”, ya’ni bitta raqam: The number of students is 25. Savol bering: “nima haqida gap ketyapti? Odamlarmi (ko‘plik) yoki raqammi (birlik)?”

Clothes, clothing va cloth qanday farqlanadi?

Clothes — kiyimlar (faqat ko‘plik): Her clothes are expensive. Clothing — kiyim-kechak (sanalmaydi): a piece of clothing. Cloth — mato (sanalmaydi): a piece of cloth. Eslab qolish: clothes = nima kiyasiz; cloth = nimadan tikiladi.

The police ko‘plik. Unda “politsiya bo‘limi” nima bo‘ladi?

“Bo‘lim” ma’nosida boshqa so‘z ishlating: a police station (politsiya bo‘limi, bino). The police faqat odamlar guruhi, tashkilot: The police are here. Bino haqida: The police station is near the market.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Faqat ko‘plik: trousers, jeans, shorts, scissors, glasses, clothes — ko‘plik fe’l (are); sanash uchun a pair of (two pairs of jeans).
  • -s bilan tugaydi, lekin birlik: news, mathematics, physics, economics, athletics, measles; davlatlar: the United States, the Netherlands — is.
  • -s yo‘q, lekin ko‘plik: the police, people, cattle — are. Bitta politsiyachi: a police officer.
  • Guruh otlari (family, team, government, staff): BrE da birlik yoki ko‘plik fe’l; AmE da odatda birlik. Izchil bo‘ling (it/its yoki they/their).
  • Pul, vaqt, masofa: Ten pounds is ... Three weeks is ... — birlik fe’l.
  • A number of students are ... (ko‘p); the number of students is ... (soni).
