70

Countable and uncountable 2

Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar — 2
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Ko‘p otlar ham countable, ham uncountable bo‘la oladi — ma’no o‘zgaradi: a paper (gazeta) va paper (qog‘oz).
  • Ichimlik va ovqat nomlari: I drink tea, lekin buyurtma berganda Two teas, please.
  • Ko‘plik shaklidagi, lekin birlik bo‘lgan so‘zlar: news, mathematics, physics, economics, politics.
  • Birlikdagi bitta narsa va umumiy tushuncha uchun boshqa-boshqa so‘zlar: a job / work, a trip / travel.
  • Mavhum otlar bilan a / an + sifat: a good knowledge of English.

Oldingi darsda (Unit 69) otlar ikki guruhga bo‘lindi. Lekin bitta so‘z ko‘pincha ikki xil ma’noda keladi: umumiy tushuncha / modda sifatida (uncountable) va aniq bitta narsa yoki hodisa sifatida (countable). Qaysi ma’no kerakligini gapdan bilib olasiz.

O‘zbekchada “soch” deganda uning sochlari uzun deymiz, inglizchada esa bu her hair is long (hairs ❌). Lekin “palovda bitta soch bor” — a hair in the plov. Shuningdek, “ikki choy” o‘zbekchada soni bilan birlikda aytiladi, inglizchada esa kafeda two teas (ko‘plikda!). Bu — ikki tilning mantig‘i qanday farq qilishini ko‘rsatadi.

Bir so‘z — ikki ma’no: uncountable va countable Nouns that can be countable or uncountable

Ko‘pchilik otlar ikkala shaklda ham ishlatiladi. Umumiy qoida:

Uncountable (U)

Umumiy tushuncha, modda, miqdor. A / an ❌, -s ❌.

  • We’ve run out of paper.
  • There’s too much noise.

Countable (C)

Bitta aniq narsa, hodisa yoki tur. A / an ✅, -s ✅.

  • Have you seen today’s paper?
  • I heard a strange noise.

Ichimliklar va ovqat: a coffee, two teas Drinks and food

Ichimliklar (tea, coffee, water, juice) odatda sanalmaydi. Lekin kafe yoki restoranda a / two + ichimlik deyiladi — bu a cup of ... yoki a glass of ...ning qisqa shakli:

O‘zbek tilida sondan keyin ot birlikda qoladi: ikki choy, uch qahva, besh kitob. Inglizchada esa son bilan ko‘plik ishlatiladi: two teas, three coffees, five books. Shuning uchun “two tea” ❌ — “two teas” ✅ (buyurtma ma’nosida).

Bu qisqa shakl asosan og‘zaki nutqda. Rasmiy yozuvda two cups of tea, a glass of water deyish aniqroq. Bundan tashqari, ba’zi ovqat nomlari ham shunday: cake (U: some cake) — a cake (butun tort, bitta pirojniy).

-s bilan tugaydigan, lekin birlik bo‘lgan so‘zlar Words ending in -s that are not plural

Ba’zi uncountable otlar -s bilan tugaydi, lekin ko‘plik emas. Ular birlik fe’l (is, has, was) bilan keladi:

Diqqat: fan nomi sifatida statistics ham birlik (Statistics is difficult), lekin “raqamlar, ma’lumotlar” ma’nosida ko‘plik (The statistics show that prices are rising). Politics fan yoki faoliyat sifatida birlik (Politics is a dirty game), “qarashlar” ma’nosida ko‘plik (His politics are very liberal). Shubhada fan ma’nosini yodda tuting: birlik.

Ko‘plik shaklida bo‘lgan va ko‘plik fe’l bilan keladigan otlar (trousers, scissors, jeans, glasses) boshqa guruh — ular Unit 79 da.

Bitta narsa va umumiy tushuncha: har xil so‘zlar Different words for one thing and for the general idea

Unit 69 dagi ro‘yxatni eslang: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage / baggage, bread, progress, permission, scenery, traffic, weather, work, accommodation — ular ingliz tilida sanalmaydi. Shu qatorga behaviour (xulq), damage (zarar), chaos (tartibsizlik), luck (omad) ham kiradi: The storm caused a lot of damage (damages ❌), His behaviour was terrible (a behaviour ❌), Good luck! (a good luck ❌).

Mavhum otlar + a / an + sifat Abstract nouns with a/an

Ba’zi mavhum otlar (knowledge, education, fear, love) odatda sanalmaydi. Lekin ular sifat bilan birga ma’lum bir daraja yoki turni bildirganda a / an olishi mumkin, ko‘pincha of bilan davom etadi:

  • Nodira has a good knowledge of French.Nodira fransuz tilini yaxshi biladi.
  • He has a fear of heights.U balandlikdan qo‘rqadi.
  • She has a great love of music.U musiqani juda yaxshi ko‘radi.
  • Sardor had a good education.Sardor yaxshi ta’lim olgan.

Bu advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, weather kabi so‘zlarga tegishli emas: a good advice ❌, a nice weather ❌.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

She has long black hairs.She has long black hair.Umuman soch haqida — hair sanalmaydi. Hairs faqat alohida tolalar haqida.
Can you give me a paper? I want to make some notes.Can you give me some paper? / Can you give me a piece of paper? / Can you give me a sheet of paper?Qog‘oz materiali sifatida paper sanalmaydi. A paper = gazeta yoki maqola.
There is too many noise in the street.There is too much noise in the street.noise (umumiy shovqin) sanalmaydi: much, many emas.
Two tea, please.Two teas, please.Son bilan ko‘plik kerak. O‘zbekchada “ikki choy” birlik, inglizchada esa -s qo‘shiladi.
Physics are difficult for me.Physics is difficult for me.Fan nomlari -s bilan tugasa ham birlik fe’l bilan keladi.
He has a big experience in teaching.He has a lot of experience in teaching.Tajriba ma’nosida experience sanalmaydi. “Katta tajriba” = a lot of experience.
I don’t have a time to cook.I don’t have time to cook.“Vaqt” ma’nosida time sanalmaydi. “Marta” yoki “lahza” ma’nosida sanaladi: three times, a great time.
I can’t see. I need a new glass.I can’t see. I need new glasses.Ko‘zoynak = glasses (ko‘plikda). Glass = shisha yoki stakan.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi ma’no ishlatilganini qanday bilaman?

Gapdagi ma’noga qarang. Agar bitta aniq narsa haqida gapirilsa (There’s a hair in my plov) — countable. Agar umumiy modda yoki tushuncha bo‘lsa (She has beautiful hair) — uncountable. Lug‘atda ham bunday so‘zlar ikki ma’no bilan [C] va [U] belgilari bilan beriladi.

“Two coffees, please” nega to‘g‘ri, axir coffee sanalmaydi-ku?

Bu two cups of coffeening qisqa shakli. Kafe va restoranlarda buyurtma berayotganda ichimlikni “porsiya” sifatida sanaymiz. Shuning uchun a coffee, two teas, three orange juices deyish mumkin. Umumiy gapirganda esa I drink coffee (a yo‘q).

“cheeses” yoki “wines” kabi ko‘plik shakllari nimani bildiradi?

Bunday ko‘plik turlarni bildiradi: French cheeses = fransuz pishloqlarining turlari. Umuman pishloq haqida gapirsangiz — cheese (ko‘plik yo‘q).

“News” ni “is” bilan ishlatamiz. “Mathematics” nega “is”, “trousers” esa “are”?

news va mathematics, physics, economics kabi so‘zlar uncountable — ular “bitta narsa” sifatida qaraladi. Trousers, scissors, jeans esa ikki bo‘lakdan iborat narsa bo‘lgani uchun doim ko‘plik fe’l bilan keladi (Unit 79).

work, job va works orasidagi farq nima?

work — umuman ish, mehnat, bajariladigan vazifalar (sanalmaydi): I have a lot of work. a job — bitta ish joyi, lavozim: She has a good job. works — ijodiy asarlar (the works of Navoi) yoki BrE da zavod (steelworks).

“Statistics” birlikmi yoki ko‘plikmi?

Fan sifatida — birlik: Statistics is a branch of mathematics. “Raqamlar, hisob-kitob ma’lumotlari” ma’nosida — ko‘plik: The statistics are interesting. Kontekstga qarang.

“a time” deb bo‘ladimi?

Ba’zan. “Lahza, davr” ma’nosida: We had a good time. It was a difficult time for the family. Lekin “vaqt” (soat, bo‘sh vaqt) ma’nosida time sanalmaydi: I haven’t got time. We have enough time.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Ko‘p otlar ikki ma’noda keladi: umumiy (U, a / an ❌) va aniq bitta (C, a / an ✅): paper / a paper, hair / a hair, light / a light.
  • time = vaqt (U), a time / times = lahza, marta (C). experience = tajriba (U), an experience = voqea (C).
  • Ichimlik: I drink tea (U), lekin buyurtma: a coffee, two teas (= cups). Turlar: cheeses, teas.
  • -s bilan tugaydigan birlik so‘zlar: news, mathematics, physics, economics, politics, athletics → is.
  • Bitta narsa va umumiy tushuncha uchun boshqa-boshqa so‘zlar: a job / work, a trip / travel, a chair / furniture.
  • Ba’zi mavhum otlar sifat bilan a / an oladi: a good knowledge of, a fear of.
