Relative clauses 1: clauses with who/that/which

Nisbiy gaplar — 1: who / that / which
Relative clauses · Nisbiy ergash gaplar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Nisbiy gap (relative clause) nima ekanini: otdan keyin turib, “qaysi?” yoki “qanday?” degan savolga javob beradigan gap — the girl who came yesterday.
  • who (odamlar), which (narsalar), that (ikkalasi uchun) qachon ishlatilishini.
  • Bu so‘zlar gapda ega vazifasida kelsa, ularni tushirib bo‘lmasligini va egani qayta takrorlamaslikni.
  • what bunday gaplarda ishlatilmasligini; all, everything, nothing dan keyin that kelishini.
  • O‘zbekchada ma’lumot otdan oldin (kecha kelgan qiz), inglizchada esa otdan keyin (the girl who came yesterday) kelishini.

Ba’zan bitta ot yetarli emas: the girl — qaysi qiz? the book — qaysi kitob? Ana shunda ot haqida aniqlik kiritadigan nisbiy gap qo‘shamiz. U who, which yoki that so‘zi bilan boshlanadi va aytilayotgan otning darrov orqasidan keladi.

O‘zbek tilida nisbiy olmosh yo‘q. Biz ot haqida ma’lumotni sifatdosh (-gan, -adigan, -yotgan) bilan, otning oldidan aytamiz: kecha kelgan qiz, Toshkentda yashaydigan do‘stim. Inglizchada tartib teskari: avval ot, keyin who / which / that + fe’l. Eng ko‘p xato shu yerda chiqadi: ❌ Came yesterday girl yoki ❌ The girl came yesterday is my cousin. To‘g‘risi: The girl who came yesterday is my cousin.

Nisbiy gap nima? Ot va gap qanday bog‘lanadi What is a relative clause?

Faraz qiling, siz “The girl is my cousin.” dedingiz. Tinglovchi so‘raydi: Which girl? (Qaysi qiz?). Siz shunday javob berasiz:

  • The girl who came yesterday is my cousin.Kecha kelgan qiz — mening amakivachcham.

Who came yesterday — nisbiy gap. U the girl otiga bog‘lanadi va “qaysi qiz?” degan savolga javob beradi. Nisbiy gap yana ot qanday ekanini ham aytishi mumkin:

  • People who exercise regularly usually feel better.Muntazam mashq qiladigan odamlar odatda o‘zini yaxshiroq his qiladi.
ot+who / that / which+fe’l+gapning qolgan qismi

Misollar

  • The shop that sells fresh bread is on the corner.Yangi non sotadigan do‘kon burchakda.
  • I know a man who speaks five languages.Men beshta tilda gapiradigan bir odamni bilaman.
  • She works for a company which makes mobile phones.U mobil telefon ishlab chiqaradigan kompaniyada ishlaydi.
  • Passengers who arrive late cannot board the plane.Kechikib kelgan yo‘lovchilar samolyotga chiqa olmaydi.
  • The tree that fell in the storm was very old.Bo‘ronda yiqilgan daraxt juda eski edi.
Nisbiy gap gapning qaysi joyida keladi?
Qaysi otga bog‘lanadiMisol
Ega (gap boshidagi ot) — gap o‘rtadaThe man who called you is waiting outside.
Sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan odam tashqarida kutyapti.
To‘ldiruvchi (fe’ldan keyingi ot) — gap oxiridaI like people who listen carefully.
Men diqqat bilan tinglaydigan odamlarni yaxshi ko‘raman.

Qoida: nisbiy gap odatda o‘zi tavsiflaydigan otning darrov yonida turadi. Who, which, that shu otning o‘rnini bosadi va nisbiy gapdagi egaga aylanadi: the girl who came = the girl + she came.

Who, that, which: qaysi birini tanlaymiz? Who, that or which?

👤 Odamwhothat (nutqda ko‘proq)❌ which, ❌ what
📦 Narsa, hayvonwhich yoki that—❌ who (odatda), ❌ what
  • A baker is a person who makes bread.Novvoy — non yopadigan kishi.
  • The man that robbed the bank was caught the next day.Bankni talagan odam ertasi kuni ushlandi.
  • A dishwasher is a machine that washes plates.Idish yuvish mashinasi — likopchalarni yuvadigan mashina.
  • Where is the umbrella which was by the door?Eshik yonida turgan soyabon qani?
  • Anybody who finds the lost dog will get a reward.Yo‘qolgan itni kim topsa, mukofot oladi.

Britaniya va Amerika: ingliz tilida (BrE) narsalar uchun that ham, which ham ishlatiladi. Amerika ingliz tilida (AmE) aniqlovchi nisbiy gapda ko‘pincha that afzal ko‘riladi, which esa vergul bilan keladigan gaplarda ishlatiladi (Unit 95). Odamlar uchun ikkala davlatda ham who eng yaxshi tanlov; that ham xato emas.

What bu yerda ishlatilmaydi

What o‘zi “the thing that” (… narsa) degan ma’noni beradi, shuning uchun uning oldida alohida ot turmaydi. Agar oldida ot yoki everything, all, nothing bo‘lsa, what emas, that kerak:

What yoki that?
❌ Noto‘g‘ri✅ To‘g‘riSabab
Everything what he said was true.Everything that he said was true.everything — ot o‘rnidagi so‘z, oldida turibdi
The film what I saw was boring.The film that I saw was boring. / The film I saw was boring.film — aniq ot
What he said was true.(to‘g‘ri gap)what = “the thing that”, oldida ot yo‘q

O‘zbekchada “nima” va “qaysi” boshqa so‘zlar, lekin o‘zbek sifatdoshida bunday so‘z umuman yo‘q, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar tasodifan what ni tanlab qo‘yadi. Eslab qoling: ot + who / that / which, ot yo‘q bo‘lsa — what.

Ega vazifasidagi who / that / which Who, that and which as the subject of the clause

Nisbiy gapda who / that / which fe’ldan oldin kelsa, ular nisbiy gapning egasi bo‘ladi. Shu sababli:

1. Tushirib bo‘lmaydi

  • The girl who came yesterday is my cousin. ✅
  • The girl came yesterday is my cousin. ❌

2. Ega qayta aytilmaydi

  • The girl who came yesterday … ✅
  • The girl who she came yesterday … ❌

Ikkinchi qoidaning sababi: who allaqachon “u” (she) vazifasini bajaryapti. Yana she qo‘shsangiz, ega ikki marta aytilgan bo‘ladi.

Fe’l oldingi otga moslashadi

Nisbiy gapdagi fe’l who / that / which ga emas, ulardan oldingi otga moslashadi. Bu yerda o‘zbek tilida moslik yo‘q, shuning uchun ehtiyot bo‘ling:

Fe’l oldingi otga moslashadi
OtMisol
birlikThe woman who lives on the third floor is a nurse.
Uchinchi qavatda yashaydigan ayol — hamshira.
ko‘plikThe people who live on the third floor are very friendly.
Uchinchi qavatda yashaydigan odamlar juda samimiy.
sanalmaydiganThe news that was on TV shocked everyone.
Televizorda ko‘rsatilgan xabar hammani larzaga soldi.

Zamon erkin: nisbiy gap istalgan zamonda bo‘la oladi

  • The woman who called yesterday wants to see you.Kecha qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan ayol siz bilan uchrashmoqchi.
  • The woman who is calling now is Mrs Karimova.Hozir qo‘ng‘iroq qilayotgan ayol — Karimova xonim.
  • The woman who will call tomorrow is the manager.Ertaga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladigan ayol — menejer.
  • The students who have finished can leave.Tugatgan talabalar chiqib ketishlari mumkin.

Who is …ing yoki who was …ed ni qisqartirish mumkin: the girl who is standing there = the girl standing there. Buni Unit 97 da o‘rganamiz.

That ayniqsa qachon ishlatiladi? When we prefer that

Quyidagi so‘zlardan keyin ko‘pincha that ishlatiladi (which emas):

that keladigan so‘zlar:
alleverythinganythingsomethingnothinglittlemuchnonethe only …the first / the last …the best / the most … (orttirma daraja)
  • Everything that happened yesterday was a surprise.Kecha sodir bo‘lgan hamma narsa kutilmagan hodisa bo‘ldi.
  • There is nothing that can stop us.Bizni to‘xtata oladigan hech narsa yo‘q.
  • It’s the only shop in our village that sells fresh fish.Bu bizning qishloqdagi yangi baliq sotadigan yagona do‘kon.
  • She is the best singer that has ever performed here.U bu yerda hozirgacha chiqish qilgan eng yaxshi qo‘shiqchi.
  • All that glitters is not gold.Yaltiraganning hammasi oltin emas.

Odam bo‘lsa va oldida someone, anyone, everyone, nobody turgan bo‘lsa, odatda who aytiladi: Is there anyone who can help me? That ham xato emas, lekin who ko‘proq uchraydi.

Ikki gapni birlashtirish Joining two sentences

Ikkita gapni bitta qilishning oson yo‘li:

Qadamma-qadam
QadamNima qilamizMisol

2Ikkinchi gapdagi he / she / it / they ni who / which / that ga almashtiring… who speaks five languages.

  • The man is my uncle. He called you. → The man who called you is my uncle.Bu odam — mening amakim. U sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildi. → Sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan odam — mening amakim.

The girl came yesterday is my cousin.The girl who came yesterday is my cousin.Ega vazifasidagi who / that / which ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi.
I have a friend who live in Samarkand.I have a friend who lives in Samarkand.Fe’l oldingi otga (a friend) moslashadi — birlik: lives.
The people who lives upstairs are friendly.The people who live upstairs are friendly.People — ko‘plik, shuning uchun live.
Came yesterday girl is my cousin.The girl who came yesterday is my cousin.O‘zbekcha tartib (gap oldin) inglizchada ishlamaydi: ot avval, nisbiy gap keyin.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Odamlar uchun who va that — qaysi biri yaxshi?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Who yozma va rasmiy matnlarda ko‘proq, that kundalik nutqda ham uchraydi. Imtihonda xavfsiz tanlov — who. Ammo which odamlar uchun noto‘g‘ri.

Narsalar uchun which va that o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Aniqlovchi nisbiy gapda (bizning mavzumiz) britaniya ingliz tilida ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi, ma’no bir xil. Amerika ingliz tilida odatda that afzal. Vergul bilan keladigan qo‘shimcha axborot gaplarida esa faqat which ishlatiladi, that emas (Unit 95).

Nega “Everything what he said” deb bo‘lmaydi?

What o‘zi “the thing that” degan ma’noni beradi, ya’ni u ot o‘rnida turadi. Everything allaqachon ot vazifasini bajarib turibdi, shuning uchun uning ortidan nisbiy olmosh kerak: that. What faqat oldida ot yo‘q joyda ishlatiladi: What he said was true.

Nisbiy gapdagi fe’l qaysi so‘zga moslashadi?

Fe’l who / which / that ning oldidagi otga moslashadi: The man who lives, The men who live. O‘zbek tilida sifatdosh moslashmaydi, shuning uchun bu qoidaga alohida e’tibor bering.

Who, which yoki that ni tushirib qoldirsam bo‘ladimi?

Faqat ular nisbiy gapda ega emas, to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lgandagina: The film (that) we saw. Ega bo‘lsa, tushirib bo‘lmaydi: The girl who came. Buni Unit 93 da batafsil o‘rganamiz.

“The girl who is wearing a red dress” bilan “the girl wearing a red dress” bir xilmi?

Ma’no bir xil, ikkinchisi qisqaroq shakl. Fe’l -ing shaklida bo‘lsa (yoki -ed), who is ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin. Buni Unit 97 da o‘rganamiz.

O‘zbekchada “Shunday odamlar borki, …” kabi -ki li gaplar bor. Bu who bilan bir xilmi?

-ki bilan tuzilgan bunday gaplar (Shunday odamlar borki, ular hech qachon kechikmaydi) kitobiy uslubga xos va inglizcha nisbiy gapga faqat qisman o‘xshaydi. Kundalik tilda o‘zbek sifatdosh qo‘llaydi: kecha kelgan qiz. Inglizchada esa teskari: sifatdosh yo‘q, who + fe’l ishlatiladi va otdan keyin keladi. Shuning uchun kelgan = who came deb tarjima qilish eng oson yo‘l.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga eng tabiiy nisbiy olmoshni yozing (who / that / which). Agar bir nechta variant to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, hammasini yozing.

  2. A kettle is a device heats water.
  3. The girl sits next to me is called Nodira.
  4. I’d like to buy the bag is in the shop window.
  5. Everything happened yesterday was a surprise.
  6. Is there anyone can help me?
  7. The train goes to Bukhara leaves at half past eight.
  8. The man robbed the bank was caught the next day.
  9. These are the children live next door.
  10. There is nothing can stop us.

2-mashq. Ikki gapni who / which / that yordamida bitta gapga birlashtiring.

  1. I know a woman. She works at the airport. →
  2. We stayed in a hotel. It was very cheap. →
  3. The man is my uncle. He sells fruit at Chorsu bazaar. →
  4. The bus was late. It goes to the university. →
  5. Sardor has a brother. He plays chess very well. →
  6. I read a book. It made me cry. →

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri so‘zni tanlang.

  1. I don’t understand (what / that) you mean.
  2. Everything (what / that) he said was true.
  3. The film (who / which) starts at nine is boring.
  4. She is the girl (who / which) won the competition.
  5. That is exactly (what / which) I meant.
  6. All (that / what) I know is that he left early.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘ri yozing.

  1. The woman which lives on the third floor is a nurse. →
  2. The girl who she came yesterday is my cousin. →
  3. The boys who plays football are my classmates. →
  4. Nothing what he said was interesting. →
  5. The man came here yesterday is my teacher. →
  6. I like people who is honest. →

5-mashq. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling (nisbiy gap ishlating).

  1. Kecha kelgan qiz mening amakivachcham. →
  2. Mening Toshkentda yashaydigan bir do‘stim bor. →
  3. Stol ustida turgan kitob menikidir. →
  4. Bu yerda ishlaydigan odamlar juda mehribon. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Nisbiy gap otdan keyin turadi va “qaysi? qanday?” degan savolga javob beradi: the girl who came yesterday. O‘zbekchada gap otdan oldin keladi.
  • who — odamlar; which — narsalar; that — ikkalasi (odamlar uchun which ❌).
  • Ega vazifasidagi who / that / which ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi va egani qayta aytmaymiz: the girl who came, ❌ who she came.
  • Fe’l oldingi otga moslashadi: the man who lives, the men who live.
  • What ot bilan birga kelmaydi; all, everything, nothing, the only va orttirma darajadan (the best …) keyin that.
  • Ikki gapni birlashtirish: he / she / it → who / which / that + otning yoniga qo‘yish.
