For, during and while

For, during va while
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • for + vaqt uzunligi (for two hours, for a week) — “qancha vaqt?” degan savolga javob.
  • during + ot (during the film, during the summer) — “qachon? qaysi davr ichida?” degan savolga javob.
  • while + ega + fe’l (while I was waiting) — boshqa bir ish davom etib turgan paytda.
  • Nega ❌ during three hours va ❌ while the lesson deyilmasligini.
  • While ning qo‘shimcha ishlatilishlarini: while driving, qarama-qarshilik ma’nosi.

Bu uch so‘z o‘zbekchada deyarli bir xil — “…davomida” yoki “…paytida” — deb tarjima qilinadi. Ingliz tilida esa ularning har biri o‘zidan keyin boshqa turdagi so‘z talab qiladi:

O‘zbek tilida davomida so‘zi ikkala holatda ham ishlatiladi: uch soat davomida (= for three hours) va dars davomida (= during the lesson). Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha ❌ during three hours deb xato qilishadi. Ingliz tilida avval o‘zingizdan so‘rang: “qancha vaqt?” bo‘lsa — for; “qachon, qaysi voqea paytida?” bo‘lsa — during. To‘liq gap (ega + kesim) bo‘lsa, o‘zbekchadagi -ayotganda / -gan paytda shakliga mos while kerak: kutayotganimda = while I was waiting.

for va during: qancha vaqt va qachon for + a length of time, during + a noun

for voqea qancha davom etganini o‘lchaydi. Undan keyin vaqt o‘lchovi keladi: a minute, two hours, three days, a month, ten years, a long time, ages. during esa voqea qachon — qaysi davr yoki voqea ichida — bo‘lganini ko‘rsatadi. Undan keyin muayyan voqea yoki davrni bildiruvchi ot keladi: the lesson, the film, the war, the summer, the night, our trip.

for va during bitta gapda

Ular bir-birini to‘ldirishi mumkin: during — qachon, for — qancha:

  • He slept for an hour during the flight.U parvoz paytida bir soat uxladi.
  • It rained for three days during our trip to Khiva.Xivaga safarimiz paytida uch kun yomg‘ir yog‘di.

Qoida: for dan keyin vaqt o‘lchovi (son + vaqt birligi yoki a long time, ages) keladi; during dan keyin esa nomi bor voqea yoki davr (odatda the / my / our / this bilan) keladi. during dan keyin son + vaqt birligi kelmaydi: ❌ during two hours → ✅ for two hours.

all bilan boshlanadigan iboralar oldidan for qo‘yilmaydi: all day, all night, all my life ✅ — for all day ❌. Bu qoida Unit 12 dagi bilan bir xil.

Nom bilan atalgan davr ichidagi qismni sanab ko‘rsatish mumkin: during the first three days of the festival ✅ — bunda “the first three days” muayyan davr, shuning uchun during to‘g‘ri. Lekin during three days ❌ deyilmaydi.

during va while: ot yoki gap during + noun, while + subject + verb

Ikkalasi ham “biror ish davom etib turgan payt ichida” degan ma’noni beradi. Farq faqat ulardan keyin nima kelishida: during dan keyin ot, while dan keyin ega + fe’l (to‘liq gap).

Savol va javob

While ning qo‘shimcha ishlatilishlari while + -ing; while = contrast; during, in, throughout

1. while + -ing (qisqartirilgan shakl)

Ikkala qismda ega bir xil bo‘lsa, while dan keyin ega va be tushirilib, faqat -ing shakli qoldirilishi mumkin:

  • Don’t use your phone while driving. (= while you are driving)Mashina haydab ketayotganda telefondan foydalanmang.
  • Malika hurt her knee while playing basketball. (= while she was playing)Malika basketbol o‘ynayotganda tizzasini shikastladi.

Egalar har xil bo‘lsa, bu qisqartma ishlatilmaydi — to‘liq gap yoziladi. Shu o‘xshash -ing tuzilmalar haqida — Unit 68.

2. while = qarama-qarshilik

While ba’zan vaqtni emas, ikki narsaning farqini bildiradi (“…, holbuki…”, “…, aksincha…”). Bunday gapda while odatda vergul bilan ajratiladi:

  • Bekzod likes tea, while his sister prefers coffee.Bekzod choyni yoqtiradi, singlisi esa qahvani afzal ko‘radi.
  • Summers in Khiva are very hot, while the winters can be surprisingly cold.Xivada yozlar juda issiq, qishlari esa kutilmaganda sovuq bo‘lishi mumkin.

Britaniya yozma va rasmiy nutqida whilst ham uchraydi: Whilst I understand your concern, we cannot change the date. Ma’nosi while bilan bir xil; oddiy suhbatda while ishlatiladi.

3. during, in va throughout

  • It’s very hot in the summer. = It’s very hot during the summer.Yozda juda issiq.
  • It was hot throughout the summer. (= from beginning to end)Butun yoz bo‘yi issiq bo‘ldi. (boshidan oxirigacha)

Fasl, oy va yil nomlari bilan in ham, during ham ishlatiladi; during “shu davr ichida” degan ma’noni biroz kuchaytiradi. throughout esa davrning boshidan oxirigacha hamma vaqt degani.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

for va during ni tez qanday ajrataman?

Gapni o‘zbekchada “qancha?” va “qachon?” savollari bilan tekshiring. “Qancha vaqt kutdim?” — “qirq daqiqa” → for forty minutes. “Qachon uxlab qoldim?” — “film paytida” → during the film. Bo‘sh joydan keyin son va vaqt birligi (two hours) bo‘lsa — for, nomi bor voqea (the film) bo‘lsa — during.

While dan keyin doim past continuous kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Past continuous eng ko‘p uchraydi, chunki while uzoq davom etgan ishni bildiradi (while I was waiting). Lekin holat va uzoq davrni bildiruvchi fe’llar bilan past simple ham to‘g‘ri: While we lived in Tashkent, we learnt Russian. Hozirgi zamonda — present simple yoki continuous: while I eat / while I’m eating. Kelasi ma’noda ham present: while you wait (will you wait emas).

While va when ning farqi nimada?

Ish davom etib turgan holatda ikkalasi ham mumkin: While / When I was shopping, I met Aziz. Lekin while faqat davom etuvchi jarayon bilan keladi. Bir lahzada bo‘ladigan voqea bilan faqat when: When I arrived, he was reading. ✅ — While I arrived… ❌.

“During” va “throughout” ning farqi nima?

during — voqea shu davr ichida (har qanday paytda) yuz berdi: It rained during the trip. (safarning qaysidir paytida yomg‘ir yog‘di). throughout — davrning butun uzunligi bo‘yi: It rained throughout the trip. (safar boshidan oxirigacha yomg‘ir yog‘di).

“I stayed there two weeks” deyish mumkinmi, for siz?

Ha, darak gapda va og‘zaki nutqda for ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin: I stayed (for) two weeks. Lekin inkor gapda tushirilmaydi: I haven’t seen him for two weeks. Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, for ni yozing — bu hech qachon xato bo‘lmaydi (faqat all day oldida emas).

While ning boshqa ma’nosi ham bormi?

Ha. “Bir vaqtda” ma’nosidan tashqari, while “…, aksincha …” ma’nosida ham keladi: Bekzod likes tea, while his sister prefers coffee. Gapda bir vaqtda bo‘layotgan ikki ish emas, ikki narsaning qarama-qarshiligi bo‘lsa — shu ma’no. Vergul ko‘pincha shuning belgisi.

“During the summer” va “in the summer” bir xilmi?

Ko‘pincha ha: It’s hot in the summer / during the summer. Fasl, oy va yil nomlari bilan ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi. Voqea nomlari bilan (the lesson, the exam, the war) vaqt ma’nosida odatda during aytiladi: during the exam.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • for + vaqt uzunligi: for two hours, for a week, for ages. Savol: How long?
  • during + ot (voqea yoki davr nomi): during the film, during the summer. Savol: When?
  • while + ega + fe’l: while I was waiting. Ot bilan while ishlatilmaydi.
  • ❌ during three hours, ❌ while the lesson, ❌ during I was sleeping.
  • O‘zbekcha davomida ikki xil bo‘ladi: uch soat davomida = for; dars davomida = during.
  • While dan keyin kelasi ma’no present bilan beriladi: while you buy the tickets (will emas).
  • Ega bir xil bo‘lsa: while driving. While ba’zan qarama-qarshilikni ham bildiradi.
