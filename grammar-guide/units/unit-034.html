Should 2

Should — 2
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • demand, insist, propose, recommend, suggest fe’llaridan keyin that ... should ... ishlatishni.
  • It’s important / essential / vital / necessary that ... (should) ... tuzilmasini.
  • should siz shakl — subjunctive (I insist that he be on time).
  • suggest bilan eng ko‘p qilinadigan xatoni (suggest me to ❌).
  • It’s strange that he should ..., If ... should ..., Should you need ... va I should ... (if I were you).

Unit 33 da should asosan maslahat edi. Bu darsda esa should ning “grammatik” vazifalari: u ba’zi fe’l va sifatlardan keyingi ergash gapda keladi, shartni “yumshatadi” yoki maslahatni boshqacha shaklda beradi.

O‘zbek tilida bunday gaplar ko‘pincha “-ishini talab qildi”, “-ishni taklif qildi”, “-ishi muhim” bilan beriladi: U kechirim so‘rashimni talab qildi = He demanded that I (should) apologise. O‘zbekchada ega ham, fe’l ham bitta so‘zga “yopishadi” (so‘rashimni), inglizchada esa that + ega + (should) + fe’l — to‘liq ergash gap kerak.

Fe’l + that + should demand / insist / propose / recommend / suggest + that ... should

Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin that bilan boshlanadigan ergash gapda ko‘pincha should keladi:

Asosiy fe’llar:
demand (talab qilmoq)insist (qattiq turib olmoq)propose (taklif kiritmoq)recommend (tavsiya qilmoq)suggest (maslahat bermoq, taklif qilmoq)
Ega+recommend / insist ...+(that)+ega+should+fe’l
  • The doctor recommended that Aziz should rest for a week.Shifokor Azizga bir hafta dam olishni tavsiya qildi.
  • Our teacher insisted that we should hand in our essays on Monday.O‘qituvchimiz insholarimizni dushanba kuni topshirishimizni qat’iy talab qildi.
  • The workers are demanding that their salaries should be increased.Ishchilar maoshlari oshirilishini talab qilmoqda.
  • Malika proposed that the meeting should be postponed until Friday.Malika majlisni jumagacha qoldirishni taklif qildi.
  • Where do you suggest we should stay in Samarkand?Samarqandda qayerda to‘xtashimizni maslahat berasiz?

It’s important / essential / vital / necessary that ... should ...

Xuddi shunday tuzilma “muhim, zarur” ma’nosidagi sifatlardan keyin ham keladi:

  • It’s essential that every student should have a dictionary.Har bir talabada lug‘at bo‘lishi juda zarur.
  • It’s important that everyone should know the safety rules.Hamma xavfsizlik qoidalarini bilishi muhim.
  • It’s vital that the doors should be locked at night.Kechasi eshiklar qulflanishi o‘ta muhim.

Bu yerda should maslahat emas! U “talab qilinyapti / taklif qilinyapti / zarur” degan fikrning ichidagi harakatni ko‘rsatadi. O‘zbek tiliga ko‘pincha “-ishni / -ishini” bilan tarjima qilinadi.

should ni tushirib qoldirish: subjunctive that he be / that she go — the subjunctive

A bo‘limdagi barcha gaplarda should ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin. Shunda fe’l “to”siz oddiy shaklda keladi — barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil, -s qo‘shilmaydi, be esa be bo‘lib qoladi. Bu shakl subjunctive (istak-shart mayli) deb ataladi:

  • The doctor recommended that Aziz rest for a week. (-s qo‘shilmaydi)Shifokor Azizga bir hafta dam olishni tavsiya qildi.
  • It’s important that he be on time. (subjunctive: be)Uning o‘z vaqtida kelishi muhim.
  • Where do you suggest we stay?Qayerda to‘xtashimizni maslahat berasiz?
  • The manager insisted that the report be finished by Friday.Menejer hisobot jumagacha tugatilishini qat’iy talab qildi.

Shuningdek, oddiy present yoki past zamon ham ishlatiladi (ayniqsa Britaniya og‘zaki nutqida):

  • It’s important that everyone knows the safety rules.Hamma xavfsizlik qoidalarini bilishi muhim.
  • The neighbours demanded that we turned the music down.Qo‘shnilar musiqani pasaytirishimizni talab qilishdi.
Uchta variant — bir xil ma’no
VariantMisolQayerda ko‘proq?
should + fe’lThe teacher demanded that Jasur should rewrite the essay.Britaniya
subjunctive (fe’lning asl shakli)The teacher demanded that Jasur rewrite the essay.Amerika; rasmiy Britaniya
oddiy zamonThe teacher demanded that Jasur rewrote the essay.Britaniya, norasmiy

Subjunctive da he / she / it bilan ham -s qo‘shilmaydi: It’s essential that she arrive early ✅ (arrives — bu “oddiy zamon” varianti bo‘ladi, u ham uchraydi). Subjunctive inkori: that he not be late — bu asosan Amerika uslubi; Britaniyada odatda that he shouldn’t be late deyiladi.

suggest bilan ehtiyot bo‘ling! suggest: not “suggest somebody to do”

suggest dan keyin to + fe’l (to do, to buy) ishlatilmaydi va suggest + kishi + to ham noto‘g‘ri. To‘g‘ri variantlar:

✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘ri
What time do you suggest we should leave? / What time do you suggest we leave?What time do you suggest us to leave?
Nodira suggested that I (should) take a taxi.Nodira suggested me to take a taxi.
Nodira suggested that I took a taxi.Nodira suggested to take a taxi.
Nodira suggested taking a taxi. (Unit 53)
  • “I’ve got a terrible toothache.” “I suggest you see a dentist as soon as possible.”— Tishim qattiq og‘riyapti. — Imkon qadar tezroq tish shifokoriga borishingizni maslahat beraman.
  • Jasur suggested that we should meet at the metro station.Jasur metro bekatida uchrashishimizni taklif qildi.
  • Kamola suggested going to the cinema.Kamola kinoga borishni taklif qildi.

O‘zbekchada “Menga taksiga o‘tirishni maslahat berdi” deymiz — shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ❌ She suggested me to take a taxi deb tarjima qiladi. Ingliz tilida suggest “kishiga” to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri bog‘lanmaydi. Agar “kishiga maslahat berdi” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, advise ishlating: She advised me to take a taxi ✅.

Sifat + that ... should: his-tuyg‘u bildirish It’s strange / odd / funny / typical / natural that ... should ...

Biror faktga hayron bo‘lish, g‘alati topish yoki uni tabiiy deb hisoblashni ifodalaganda, quyidagi sifatlardan keyin should ishlatish mumkin:

strangeoddfunnytypicalnaturalinterestingsurprisedsurprising
  • It’s strange that Bekzod should be so quiet today. He usually talks all the time.Bekzodning bugun bunchalik jim o‘tirgani g‘alati. U odatda tinmay gapiradi.
  • I was surprised that Nodira should refuse such a good offer.Nodiraning shunday yaxshi taklifni rad etganiga hayron bo‘ldim.
  • It’s only natural that parents should worry about their children.Ota-onalarning farzandlari haqida qayg‘urishi tabiiy hol.
  • It’s funny you should mention Samarkand — I’m going there tomorrow!Qiziq, Samarqandni tilga oldingiz — men ertaga u yerga ketyapman!

Bu gaplarda should siz ham aytsa bo‘ladi: It’s strange that he is so quiet. I was surprised that she refused. should faqat his-tuyg‘uni (hayrat, g‘alati) biroz kuchaytiradi. “Funny you should say that” — juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan tayyor ibora: “Qiziq, aynan shuni aytdingiz!”

If ... should ... va Should ... (inversiya) If something should happen ... / Should something happen ...

If + ega + should + fe’l — oddiy if ga o‘xshaydi, lekin ehtimol kamroq ekanini bildiradi: “agar mabodo ... bo‘lib qolsa”.

  • If Jasur should call, tell him the meeting has moved to Room 5. (= If Jasur calls ...)Agar mabodo Jasur qo‘ng‘iroq qilib qolsa, unga majlis 5-xonaga ko‘chganini ayting.
  • If you should see Kamola at the conference, give her my regards.Konferensiyada mabodo Kamolani ko‘rib qolsangiz, mendan salom ayting.
  • If there should be any problems, phone me at once.Agar mabodo biror muammo chiqsa, darhol menga qo‘ng‘iroq qiling.

Rasmiy uslubda if tushib qoladi va should gap boshiga chiqadi (inversiya):

Should+ega+fe’l,bosh gap
  • Should you need any help, please contact reception. (= If you need ...)Agar sizga biror yordam kerak bo‘lsa, iltimos, qabulxonaga murojaat qiling.
  • Should Jasur call, tell him I’m in a meeting.Jasur qo‘ng‘iroq qilsa, unga majlisda ekanimni ayting.

Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. — rasmiy xatlar va e’lonlarda juda ko‘p uchraydigan jumla: “Savollaringiz bo‘lsa, biz bilan bog‘lanishdan tortinmang.”

I should ... = “men sizning o‘rningizda ...” I should / I shouldn’t ... (= I’d ... if I were you)

Britaniya ingliz tilida I should ... / I shouldn’t ... maslahat sifatida ishlatiladi. Bu yerda I should wait = I would wait if I were you (“men sizning o‘rningizda kutardim”):

  • “Shall I phone Aziz now?” “No, I should wait until the evening. He’s at work.”— Azizga hozir qo‘ng‘iroq qilaymi? — Yo‘q, men sizning o‘rningizda kechgacha kutardim. U ishda.
  • I shouldn’t buy that car if I were you. It uses too much petrol.Men sizning o‘rningizda u mashinani sotib olmasdim. U juda ko‘p benzin yeydi.
  • It looks like rain. I should take an umbrella if I were you.Yomg‘ir yog‘adiganga o‘xshaydi. Men sizning o‘rningizda soyabon olardim.

Bugungi kunda bu ma’noda ko‘proq I’d ... (if I were you) deyiladi: I’d wait until the evening. I wouldn’t buy that car if I were you. I should shaklini o‘qiganda tushunishingiz kerak, lekin o‘zingiz I’d ni ishlatsangiz ham bo‘ladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

She suggested me to see a doctor.She suggested that I (should) see a doctor. / She advised me to see a doctor.suggest + kishi + to ishlatilmaydi. Yoki “that” ergash gap, yoki advise.
What do you suggest me to buy for Mum?What do you suggest I buy for Mum? / What do you suggest I should buy for Mum?suggest dan keyin: (that) + ega + (should) + fe’l.
I suggested to go to the park.I suggested going to the park. / I suggested that we (should) go to the park.suggest + to do ❌. To‘g‘ri: suggest + -ing yoki that-gap.
It’s essential that everyone should to arrive on time.It’s essential that everyone should arrive on time.should dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
The doctor recommended that he should goes to bed.The doctor recommended that he should go to bed.should dan keyin fe’l asl shaklda (-s siz).
They demanded that the price should lowered.They demanded that the price should be lowered.Majhul nisbat: should + be + V3.
If he should calls, tell him I’m out.If he should call, tell him I’m out.If ... should dan keyin ham fe’l asl shaklda.
Should you needed any help, call me.Should you need any help, call me.Inversiyada ham should + fe’lning asl shakli.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Bu gaplardagi should ham “maslahat” degan ma’nodami?

Yo‘q. They demanded that he should leave — bu yerda should “maslahat” emas, balki talab qilingan, taklif qilingan yoki zarur bo‘lgan harakatni ko‘rsatadi. Uni o‘zbekchaga alohida so‘z bilan tarjima qilmaysiz: “uning ketishini talab qilishdi”.

Nega “that he be”, “that she go” — grammatika buzilmayaptimi?

Buzilmayapti — bu maxsus shakl, subjunctive. U fe’lning “to”siz asl shakliga teng va shaxsga qarab o‘zgarmaydi: that I be, that he be, that they be; that she go, that he arrive. U asosan demand, insist, recommend, suggest, propose va important, essential, vital, necessary dan keyin keladi.

Britaniya va Amerika ingliz tilida farq bormi?

Ha. Amerikada subjunctive (I suggest he go) juda keng tarqalgan, should varianti kamroq. Britaniyada uchala variant ham ishlatiladi: should go, go (rasmiy), goes / went (norasmiy). Imtihonda should + fe’l yoki subjunctive — ikkalasi ham xavfsiz.

suggest va advise ning farqi nimada?

Ma’nosi o‘xshash, lekin grammatikasi har xil. advise: advise somebody to do ✅ — She advised me to rest. suggest: suggest that somebody (should) do yoki suggest doing — She suggested that I rest / She suggested resting. Suggest somebody to do ❌.

“It’s strange that he should be so quiet” — bu yerda should “kerak” degani emasmi?

Yo‘q. Bu gapda should ning “kerak” ma’nosi yo‘q — u hayrat, g‘alati topish hissini ifodalaydi. It’s strange that he should be so quiet = It’s strange that he is so quiet (Uning bunchalik jimligi g‘alati). should faqat emotsiyani biroz kuchaytiradi.

“If he should call” va “If he calls” — farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil, lekin should ehtimolni kamroq qilib ko‘rsatadi: “mabodo qo‘ng‘iroq qilib qolsa”. Bundan tashqari, should varianti biroz rasmiyroq. Should he call, ... (if siz) — yanada rasmiy, ko‘pincha yozma matnlarda.

Rasmiy xatlarda “I should be grateful if ...” ko‘rdim. Bu nima?

Bu eski, rasmiy Britaniya uslubi: I should = I would (1-shaxsda). I should be grateful if you would send me the details = I would be grateful if ... (Tafsilotlarni yuborsangiz, minnatdor bo‘lardim). Bugun ko‘pincha I would be grateful yoziladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gaplarni qavs ichidagi fe’l va that ... should bilan qayta yozing.

  1. The doctor said to Aziz, “You should rest for a week.” (recommend) → The doctor recommended
  2. The workers said, “Our salaries must be increased!” (demand) → The workers demanded
  3. Malika said, “Why don’t we postpone the meeting?” (propose) → Malika proposed
  4. The teacher said to us, “You must hand in your essays on Monday.” (insist) → The teacher insisted

2-mashq. should ni tushirib qoldiring (subjunctive).

  1. It’s important that he should be on time. → It’s important that he
  2. I recommend that she should take the train. → I recommend that she
  3. They insisted that the room should be cleaned. → They insisted that the room

3-mashq. suggest bilan to‘g‘ri gap tanlang.

  1. (a) Jasur suggested me to learn Spanish. (b) Jasur suggested that I should learn Spanish.
  2. (a) What do you suggest we cook for dinner? (b) What do you suggest us to cook for dinner?
  3. (a) Kamola suggested going to the cinema. (b) Kamola suggested to go to the cinema.
  4. (a) I suggest you see a dentist. (b) I suggest you to see a dentist.

4-mashq. If ... should bilan, keyin Should ... (inversiya) bilan qayta yozing.

  1. If Sardor calls, tell him I’m busy. (If ... should) →
  2. If you need anything, please ask. (Should ...) →
  3. If there are any problems, contact the manager. (Should ...) →

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. She suggested me to stay at home. →
  2. It’s vital that everyone should to wear a helmet. →
  3. Should you needed help, call me. →

6-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Shifokor menga ko‘proq yurishni tavsiya qildi (recommend ... that). →
  2. Uning bunchalik erta kelganiga hayron bo‘ldim (surprised ... should). →
  3. Men sizning o‘rningizda bu telefonni sotib olmasdim (I shouldn’t ...). →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • demand / insist / propose / recommend / suggest + that + ega + should + fe’l.
  • It’s important / essential / vital / necessary that ... (should) ...
  • should siz: subjunctive — that he be, that she go (-s yo‘q). Yoki oddiy zamon.
  • suggest somebody to do ❌ → suggest that somebody (should) do / suggest doing.
  • It’s strange / I was surprised that ... should ... — hayrat, his-tuyg‘u.
  • If ... should ... = mabodo; rasmiy: Should you need ....
  • I should / I shouldn’t ... = I’d ... if I were you (maslahat).
