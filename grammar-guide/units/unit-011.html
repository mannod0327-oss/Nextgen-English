How long have you (been) ...?

How long have you (been) ...? — qancha vaqtdan beri?
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • O‘tmishda boshlanib, hozir ham davom etayotgan holat haqida qanday gapirishni: They have been married for 15 years.
  • Nega “I live here for five years” deyish mumkin emasligini va o‘rniga nima deyish kerakligini.
  • How long ...? savolida qachon have been + -ing (continuous), qachon have + V3 (simple) ishlatilishini.
  • know, like, have kabi holat fe’llari, live / work fe’llari va always so‘zi bilan bog‘liq qoidalarni.
  • Inkor gaplarni: I haven’t seen him since his birthday party.

Asosiy g‘oya juda oddiy: agar biror holat o‘tmishda boshlangan bo‘lsa va hozir ham davom etsa, hamda biz uning qancha vaqtdan beri davom etayotgani haqida gapirsak, ingliz tilida present perfect (have/has + V3) yoki present perfect continuous (have/has been + -ing) ishlatiladi — hozirgi oddiy zamon (present simple) emas.

O‘zbek tilida bunday gaplarni hozirgi zamon + “-dan beri” bilan aytamiz: “Men Toshkentda besh yildan beri yashayman”, “Uni 2015-yildan beri taniyman”. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar inglizchada ham hozirgi zamonni ishlatib yuboradi: ❌ I live in Tashkent for five years. ❌ I know him since 2015. Ingliz tilida esa “qancha vaqtdan beri” degan ma’no bo‘lsa, fe’l present perfectda bo‘lishi shart: I have lived in Tashkent for five years. I have known him since 2015. Bu — shu darsdagi eng muhim qoida!

Hozirgi holat va “qancha vaqtdan beri?” They are married → They have been married for 15 years

Bir vaziyatni ko‘raylik. Sardor va Nilufar roppa-rosa 15 yil oldin turmush qurishgan. Bugun ularning to‘y kuni — 15 yilligi. Ular hozir ham er-xotin.

  • They are married.Ular turmush qurgan (er-xotin). — hozirgi holat
  • They have been married for 15 years.Ular turmush qurganiga 15 yil bo‘ldi. — holat 15 yil oldin boshlangan, hozir ham davom etmoqda
  • How long have they been married?Ular turmush qurganiga qancha bo‘ldi?

Diqqat qiling: They are married for 15 years deyish mumkin emas. “Are” faqat hozirgi holatni bildiradi, u o‘tmishdan hozirgacha cho‘zilgan davrni ifoda eta olmaydi. O‘tmishdan hozirgacha cho‘zilgan davr (for 15 years, since 2010) qo‘shilgan zahoti fe’l present perfect ga o‘tadi. (Kelajakka qaragan muddat — boshqa gap: I’m in London for two weeks = Londonda ikki hafta bo‘laman. Bu haqda ★ bo‘limda.)

Qoida: O‘tmishda boshlanib, hozir ham davom etayotgan holat yoki harakat haqida qancha vaqtdan beri ekanini aytganda yoki so‘raganda present perfect ishlatiladi: have/has + V3 yoki have/has been + -ing. Present simple (I live, they are) bu ma’noda ishlatilmaydi.

Hozirgi holat ↔ shu holat qancha vaqtdan beri davom etyapti
Present: hozir qanday?Present perfect: qancha vaqtdan / qachondan beri?
They are married.They have been married for 15 years.
Aziz is on holiday.He has been on holiday since Saturday.
We know each other well.We have known each other for ten years.
Are you waiting for someone?How long have you been waiting?
It’s raining.It’s been raining since this morning.
Malika has a cat.She’s had it for three years.
I live in Fergana.I’ve lived / I’ve been living in Fergana since 2019.

Gap tuzilishi

Ega+have / has+V3  yoki  been + -ing+for / since ...
How long+have / has+ega+V3  yoki  been + -ing?

Misollar

  • “How long have you been a teacher?” “For eight years.”— Qancha vaqtdan beri o‘qituvchisiz? — Sakkiz yildan beri.
  • “How long has Jasur been in London?” “Since September.”— Jasur qachondan beri Londonda? — Sentyabrdan beri.
  • I’ve been ill since Tuesday, so I haven’t been to work.Seshanbadan beri kasalman, shuning uchun ishga bormadim.
  • Dilnoza and I have been friends for twenty years.Dilnoza bilan yigirma yildan beri do‘stmiz.
  • My grandparents have lived in this house since 1975.Bobom va buvim bu uyda 1975-yildan beri yashaydilar.

’s been har doim has been degani (is been degan shakl yo‘q): It’s been raining = It has been raining. He’s been ill = He has been ill. Xuddi shunday ’s had = has had.

be fe’li bilan juda ko‘p ishlatiladi: be married, be ill, be in hospital, be here, be friends, be a teacher, be open → have been married / ill / in hospital ... Britaniya inglizchasida in hospital deyiladi (AmE: in the hospital).

Continuous yoki simple? I have been learning / I have known

Present perfect ning ikki shakli bor:

Present perfect simple

have/has + V3

  • I have known
  • I have had
  • I have lived

Present perfect continuous

have/has been + -ing

  • I have been learning
  • I have been waiting
  • I have been doing

How long haqida so‘raganda yoki gapirganda, harakat (faoliyat) fe’llari bilan odatda continuous shakl ishlatiladi (bu haqda Unit 9 va Unit 10 da ham gapirilgan):

  • I’ve been learning English for three years.Uch yildan beri ingliz tilini o‘rganyapman.
  • It’s been snowing since lunchtime.Tushlikdan beri qor yog‘yapti.
  • Nodira has been teaching at this school for twelve years.Nodira bu maktabda o‘n ikki yildan beri dars beradi.
  • How long have you been waiting?Qancha vaqtdan beri kutyapsiz?
  • Bekzod has been working on his project since eight o’clock.Bekzod soat sakkizdan beri loyihasi ustida ishlayapti.

Holat fe’llari — faqat simple

Lekin ba’zi fe’llar (know, like, believe, want, have egalik ma’nosida va boshqalar) odatda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi (Unit 4). Ular bilan how long gaplarida ham faqat simple shakl ishlatiladi:

  • How long have you known Malika? (not have you been knowing)Malikani qancha vaqtdan beri taniysiz?
  • I’ve had this phone since 2022. (not I’ve been having)Bu telefon menda 2022-yildan beri bor.
  • I’ve had a headache since I woke up this morning.Bugun ertalab uyg‘onganimdan beri boshim og‘riyapti.
  • Sardor has wanted to visit Japan for years.Sardor ko‘p yillardan beri Yaponiyaga borishni xohlaydi.
  • How long have they been in Bukhara? (not have they been being)Ular qachondan beri Buxoroda?

✅ Harakat fe’llari — odatda continuous

  • learn, study, teach, work
  • wait, rain, snow
  • play, read, watch, drive
  • try, look for, travel

I’ve been studying since 9.

⛔ Holat fe’llari — faqat simple

  • know, understand, believe
  • like, love, hate, prefer
  • have (= egalik), own, belong
  • be

I’ve known her since 2010.

have fe’liga e’tibor bering. Egalik ma’nosida (have a car, have a headache) — faqat simple: I’ve had this car for five years. Ammo harakat ma’nosida (have lessons, have problems, have a good time) — continuous ham bo‘ladi: I’ve been having driving lessons since June. — Iyundan beri haydovchilik darslariga qatnayapman.

live va work: ikkalasi ham bo‘ladi; always — faqat simple live / work + always

live (yashamoq) va work (ishlamoq) fe’llari bilan continuous ham, simple ham ishlatish mumkin. Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil:

  • Kamola has lived / has been living in a student flat for two years.Kamola ikki yildan beri talabalar kvartirasida yashaydi.
  • How long have you worked / have you been working as a driver?Qancha vaqtdan beri haydovchi bo‘lib ishlaysiz?
  • My father has worked / has been working at the same factory since 1998.Dadam 1998-yildan beri bitta zavodda ishlaydi.

Ammo always (doim, har doim) bilan faqat simple shakl ishlatiladi:

  • I’ve always lived in Tashkent. (not I’ve always been living)Men har doim Toshkentda yashaganman. (Butun umrim Toshkentda o‘tgan.)
  • Jasur has always worked in the family business.Jasur doim oilaviy biznesda ishlab kelgan.
  • I’ve always liked autumn in Fergana.Farg‘onaning kuzi menga doim yoqqan.
Qisqa xulosa: qaysi shakl qachon?
Fe’l turiSimple (have + V3)Continuous (have been + -ing)
Harakat fe’llari (learn, wait, rain ...)mumkin, lekin kamroq✅ odatiy
Holat fe’llari (know, like, have, be ...)✅ faqat shu❌ yo‘q

O‘zbekcha “har doim shu yerda yashaganman” yoki “doim shu yerda yashab kelaman” — ikkalasi ham inglizchada bitta shaklga tushadi: I’ve always lived here.

Inkor: “...dan beri qilmadim” I haven’t done something since / for ...

“Ma’lum vaqtdan beri biror ishni qilmadim” demoqchi bo‘lsak, present perfect simple ning inkor shakli ishlatiladi: haven’t / hasn’t + V3 + since / for ...

  • I haven’t seen Bekzod since his birthday party.Bekzodni tug‘ilgan kun ziyofatidan beri ko‘rmadim. (= Uni oxirgi marta o‘sha ziyofatda ko‘rganman.)
  • Malika hasn’t posted anything online for ages.Malika anchadan beri internetga hech narsa joylamadi. (= Oxirgi marta ancha oldin joylagan.)
  • We haven’t been to the cinema for months.Bir necha oydan beri kinoga bormadik.
  • I haven’t eaten anything since breakfast.Nonushtadan beri hech narsa yemadim.
  • It hasn’t rained here for three months.Bu yerda uch oydan beri yomg‘ir yog‘madi.

Bu gaplarni boshqacha ham aytish mumkin — ma’nosi bir xil:

Oxirgi marta qachon?= O‘shandan beri qilmagan
The last time I saw Bekzod was at his birthday party.I haven’t seen Bekzod since his birthday party.
Malika last posted something online ages ago.Malika hasn’t posted anything online for ages.
The last time we went to the cinema was in May.We haven’t been to the cinema since May.

O‘zbekchada “ko‘rmadim, yemadim, bormadik” — o‘tgan zamon shakli. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ❌ I didn’t see him since Monday deydi. Lekin “...dan beri” bor ekan, holat hozirgacha cho‘zilgan, demak inglizchada present perfect: I haven’t seen him since Monday. O‘zbekchada “ko‘rganim yo‘q” deb ham aytamiz — bu inglizcha shaklga ancha yaqin.

How long ...? — o‘tmish, hozir va kelajak How long did you ...? / have you ...? / are you ...?

How long savoli uch xil zamonda kelishi mumkin. Ma’no qaysi davr haqida so‘rayotganingizga bog‘liq:

SavolMa’nosiJavob
How long did you live in Bukhara?Tugagan davr — endi u yerda yashamaysiz (past simple, Unit 14)I lived there for five years.
How long have you lived in Tashkent?Hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr (present perfect — shu dars)I’ve lived here for seven years.
How long are you staying in London? / How long are you here for?Kelajakni ham o‘z ichiga olgan butun muddat (reja)I’m staying for two weeks. / For two weeks.

Qisqa javoblar

  • “How long have you been waiting?” “Not long. About five minutes.”— Qancha vaqtdan beri kutyapsiz? — Ko‘p emas. Besh daqiqacha.
  • “How long have you known Aziz?” “Since we were at university.”— Azizni qachondan beri taniysiz? — Universitetda o‘qiganimizdan beri.
  • “How long has she lived here?” “All her life.”— U bu yerda qachondan beri yashaydi? — Butun umri davomida.
  • “How long have you had that coat?” “For ages!”— Bu palto sizda qachondan beri bor? — Ancha bo‘ldi, juda uzoq vaqtdan beri!

for (+ davr: for two hours, for ten years) va since (+ boshlanish nuqtasi: since Monday, since 2019) farqi keyingi darsda — Unit 12 da batafsil o‘rganiladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchada “besh yildan beri yashayman” deymiz. Nega inglizchada “I live here for five years” xato?

Har bir til vaqtni o‘zicha “ko‘radi”. Ingliz tilida present simple (I live) faqat hozirgi holat yoki odatni bildiradi — unda o‘tmishga qarab cho‘zilgan davr g‘oyasi yo‘q. “Besh yildan beri” esa o‘tmishdagi nuqtadan hozirgacha bo‘lgan davr. O‘tmishni hozir bilan bog‘laydigan zamon — present perfect. Shuning uchun: I’ve lived here for five years. Eslab qolish uchun: for / since + hozirgacha davom etayotgan holat = have/has ...

“I have lived here” va “I have been living here” o‘rtasida farq bormi?

live va work fe’llari bilan farq juda kichik, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri (C bo‘lim). Ba’zan continuous shakl holatni biroz “vaqtinchalikroq” qilib ko‘rsatadi: I’ve been living with my aunt since September (balki keyin ko‘chib ketarman). Lekin always bilan faqat I’ve always lived.

“How long are you here for?” degan savolni eshitdim. Bu xato emasmi?

Xato emas, lekin boshqa savol! How long are you here for? = “Bu yerda (jami) qancha vaqt bo‘lasiz?” — ya’ni kelajakni ham qamrab olgan butun muddat haqida: “For two weeks. I’m going home on the 20th.” How long have you been here? esa = “Bu yerga kelganingizga qancha bo‘ldi?” — hozirgacha o‘tgan vaqt: “Since Saturday.”

“How long have you been married?” — nega bu yerda -ing yo‘q?

Chunki bu yerda asosiy fe’l — be (be married). Be holat fe’li, uning present perfect shakli — have been. “Have been being married” degan shakl yo‘q. Shuningdek: How long have you been ill / here / a student?

Inkor gapda continuous umuman ishlatilmaydimi?

Ishlatiladi, lekin boshqa ma’noda. D bo‘limdagi “oxirgi marta ...dan beri qilmadim” ma’nosida simple ishlatiladi: I haven’t seen him since the party. Continuous inkor esa ma’lum davr davomida harakat doimiy ravishda bo‘lmaganini bildiradi: I haven’t been sleeping well recently. (So‘nggi paytlarda yaxshi uxlayolmayapman.)

“How long did you live in Bukhara?” ham to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, agar siz endi Buxoroda yashamasangiz. Davr tugagan bo‘lsa — past simple: I lived in Bukhara for five years (then I moved to Tashkent). Davr hozir ham davom etsa — present perfect: I’ve lived in Tashkent for seven years. Batafsil — Unit 14.

Javobda “for” va “since” ni qanday tanlayman?

Qisqasi: for + davr uzunligi (for two years, for ten minutes, for ages); since + davr boshlangan nuqta (since 2019, since Monday, since I was a child). To‘liq qoidalar va istisnolar — Unit 12.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • O‘tmishda boshlanib hozir ham davom etayotgan holat + qancha vaqtdan beri → present perfect: They have been married for 15 years. (They are married for ... ❌)
  • Savol: How long have you + V3 / been + -ing ...? (How long do you ...? ❌)
  • Harakat fe’llari bilan odatda continuous: I’ve been learning English for three years.
  • Holat fe’llari (know, like, believe, have = egalik, be) — faqat simple: I’ve known her since 2010.
  • live / work — ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri; always bilan faqat simple: I’ve always lived here.
  • “...dan beri qilmadim” → haven’t + V3 + since / for: I haven’t seen him since the party. (I didn’t see him since ... ❌)
