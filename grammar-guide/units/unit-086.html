86

No/none/any, Nothing/nobody etc.

No / none / any; nothing / nobody
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • “Nol” (hech qancha, hech kim, hech narsa) ni aytishning ikki yo‘li: no / none / nothing / nobody / nowhere (fe’l ijobiy) yoki not … any / anything / anybody / anywhere (fe’l inkor).
  • no va none farqi: I have no money, lekin Money? I have none.
  • Nega I didn’t say nothing ❌ va Nobody didn’t come ❌ deb bo‘lmaydi (bitta gapda bitta inkor).
  • nothing to do, nobody to talk to, nowhere to go; nothing interesting, nobody else.
  • Nobody bilan they / their: Nobody phoned, did they?

Bu darsda “nol” haqida gaplashamiz. Inglizchada “hech narsa yo‘q” ni aytish uchun bitta inkor so‘z yetarli: yo no- so‘zi (nothing) va ijobiy fe’l, yo inkor fe’l (don’t) va any- so‘zi (anything). Ikkalasini bir gapda ishlatib bo‘lmaydi.

O‘zbek tilida “hech” so‘zi bor, va u inkor fe’l bilan keladi: Hech kim kelmadi (“kelmadi” — inkor!), Men hech narsa demadim (“demadim” — inkor!). Ingliz tilida bu ikkita inkor bitta bo‘lishi kerak: Nobody came. (fe’l ijobiy) yoki I didn’t say anything. (fe’l inkor, lekin any- so‘zi). Shuning uchun o‘zbek o‘quvchilari ko‘pincha I didn’t say nothing ❌ deb xato qiladi.

  • There is no milk in the fridge. (= There isn’t any milk in the fridge.)Muzlatkichda sut yo‘q.
  • She has no brothers. (= She hasn’t got any brothers.)Uning aka-ukalari yo‘q.
  • “How many mistakes did you make?” “None!”— Nechta xato qildingiz? — Hech nechta!
  • “Did you buy any apples?” “No, there were none at the market.”— Olma oldingizmi? — Yo‘q, bozorda olma topilmadi.

Qoida: no hamisha ot oldida turadi (no money). Ot bo‘lmasa, none (none left). No + birlikdagi sanaladigan ot = not a; no + ko‘plik yoki sanalmaydigan ot = not any. None ni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri ot oldida ishlatib bo‘lmaydi: none money ❌.

No + birlikdagi ot: I have no car = I haven’t (got) a car. Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan not … any odatda ishlatilmaydi — I haven’t got a car deymiz (not … any ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlar uchun). No yozuvda belgi sifatida ham keladi: No smoking, No parking, No entry.

No va none: tez yo‘riqnoma

no + ot

  • I’ve got no time.
  • There’s no bus today.

none (ot yo‘q)

  • Time? I’ve got none.
  • Buses? There are none today.

None of + the/my/these/them (none of the students) — Unit 88 da batafsil tushuntiriladi.

Eng muhim qoida: gapda faqat bitta inkor bo‘ladi. I didn’t say nothing ❌. To‘g‘ri: I said nothing ✅ yoki I didn’t say anything ✅.

Ega bo‘lganda faqat no- so‘zi

Agar so‘z gapning egasi bo‘lsa, any- so‘zi inkor fe’ldan oldin kela olmaydi. Shuning uchun faqat no- so‘zi ishlatiladi:

  • Nobody came to the meeting.Yig‘ilishga hech kim kelmadi.
  • Nothing happened yesterday.Kecha hech narsa bo‘lmadi.
  • No-one knows the answer.Javobni hech kim bilmaydi.
  • Anybody didn’t come to the meeting. ❌Bunday gap tuzilmaydi.

O‘zbekcha “Hech kim kelmadi” ning inglizchasi Nobody came: fe’l ijobiy (came), inkor ma’no esa nobody da. “Kelmadi”ga qarab didn’t come yozmang: Nobody didn’t come ❌. Xuddi shu: Men hech narsa bilmayman = I know nothing. yoki I don’t know anything.

Qisqa javoblar

Savolga bitta so‘z bilan javob berganda no- so‘zi ishlatiladi:

  • “What did you buy?” “Nothing.”— Nima sotib oldingiz? — Hech narsa.
  • “Who did you talk to at the party?” “Nobody.” / “No-one.”— Ziyofatda kim bilan gaplashdingiz? — Hech kim bilan.
  • “Where did you go at the weekend?” “Nowhere. I stayed at home.”— Dam olish kunlari qayerga bordingiz? — Hech qayerga. Uyda qoldim.
  • “How many tickets do you have?” “None.”— Nechta chiptangiz bor? — Hech nechta.

Nothing va none bir xil emas: nothing — “hech qanday narsa” (What did you eat? Nothing.), none — “hech qanchasi” (oldin aytilgan sanaladigan yoki sanalmaydigan narsa, How many sweets are left? None.).

None va no-one ham farq qiladi: none — How many? / How much? savoliga javob (narsa ham, odam ham bo‘lishi mumkin): “How many guests came?” “None.” No-one / nobody — Who? savoliga javob: “Who came?” “No-one.”

No-one ni no-one yoki no one deb yozish mumkin. Noone ❌ (bitta so‘z qilib). Amerika ingliz tilida odatda no one. Nobody va no-one ma’nosi bir xil.

Sifat nothing / anything / nobody dan keyin keladi: nothing interesting ✅, interesting nothing ❌. Predlog fe’lga tegishli bo‘lsa, gap oxirida qoladi: nobody to talk to, nothing to worry about.

Fe’l doim birlikda; they / their

Nobody, nothing, anybody, everybody bilan fe’l birlikda. Odamni bildiruvchi so‘zlardan (nobody, no-one, anybody, somebody, everybody) keyin esa olmosh uchun odatda they / them / their ishlatiladi (jinsi noma’lum yoki muhim emas). Nothing dan keyin — it: Nothing is impossible, is it?

  • Nobody likes waiting in queues.Navbatda kutishni hech kim yoqtirmaydi.
  • Nobody phoned, did they?Hech kim qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi, shundaymi?
  • No-one has paid for their ticket yet.Hali hech kim chiptasi uchun pul to‘lamadi.
  • If anybody asks, tell them I’m busy.Birov so‘rasa, band ekanimni ayting.

O‘zbek tilida hech kim bilan fe’l “ular”ga emas, yakka shaklda keladi (hech kim kelmadi). Inglizchada ham fe’l birlik (Nobody is …), lekin ortidan they / their kelishi mumkin. Buni xato deb o‘ylamang.

Anything, anybody, anywhere = istalgan Any- = it doesn’t matter which

Unit 85 da any “istalgan” ma’nosini bera olishini ko‘rdingiz. Anything, anybody, anywhere ham darak gapda shu ma’noda keladi:

  • You can ask anybody. Everyone here speaks English.Xohlagan odamdan so‘rashingiz mumkin. Bu yerda hamma inglizcha biladi.
  • I’m not fussy about food — I’ll eat anything.Ovqat tanlamayman — nima bo‘lsa ham yeyaveraman.
  • Sit anywhere you like.Xohlagan joyingizga o‘tiring.
  • Anyone can make a mistake.Har kim xato qilishi mumkin.

no- = hech kim / hech narsa

  • The road was blocked, so nobody could get through.
  • “What would you like?” “Nothing, thanks. I’m full.”

any- = istalgan

  • The door is never locked — anybody can come in.
  • “What would you like?” “I don’t mind. Anything.”

“Deyarli nol”: hardly any, hardly anybody Almost zero

Ba’zan miqdor nolga juda yaqin, lekin nol emas. Buning uchun hardly (“zo‘rg‘a, deyarli”) ishlatiladi, undan keyin esa any- so‘zlari keladi, no- so‘zlari emas:

Nol va nolga yaqin
Nol (0)Nolga juda yaqin

Vaqt deyarli qolmadi.
Nobody came.Hardly anybody came.
Deyarli hech kim kelmadi.
We saw nothing.We saw hardly anything.
Biz deyarli hech narsa ko‘rmadik.
He never calls.He hardly ever calls.
U deyarli hech qachon qo‘ng‘iroq qilmaydi.

Hardly o‘zi inkor ma’no beradi, shuning uchun not bilan birga ishlatilmaydi: We don’t have hardly any time ❌. Almost no va almost none ham “deyarli nol” (almost no money, almost none left). Little / few bilan farq haqida — Unit 87.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I didn’t say nothing” ba’zi qo‘shiqlar va filmlarda eshitiladi-ku. Xatomi?

Standart ingliz tilida bu xato. Ba’zi shevalarda va norasmiy nutqda (hamda qo‘shiqlarda) ikki inkor ishlatiladi, lekin imtihonda, ish xatlarida va rasmiy nutqda bitta inkor bo‘lishi shart. Shuning uchun I didn’t say anything yoki I said nothing deb o‘rganing.

“I didn’t see anybody” bilan “I saw nobody” — qaysi biri yaxshi?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ma’nosi bir xil. Og‘zaki nutqda inkor fe’l + any- (I didn’t see anybody) ko‘proq ishlatiladi. No- shakli esa qisqa javobda, ega bo‘lganda va ta’kid uchun yaxshi: Nobody came. / Who was there? Nobody.

No va none ni qanday ajratib olaman?

Savolga qarang: yonida ot bormi? Ot bo‘lsa — no (no money). Ot yo‘q, lekin ma’no tushunarli bo‘lsa — none (Money? None.). No hech qachon yolg‘iz, none hech qachon ot oldida kelmaydi.

Nothing bilan none o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ha. Nothing — “hech narsa”, o‘zi alohida tushuncha: I ate nothing. None — oldin eslatilgan guruhdan hech biri/hech qanchasi: I bought five apples, but none were ripe. Savolga qarab tanlang: What? → nothing; How many? / How much? → none.

Nega “Nobody likes …” da fe’l birlikda, lekin keyin “their” deyiladi?

Grammatik jihatdan nobody birlik, shuning uchun fe’l birlikda (likes, is). Lekin ma’no jihatdan u “hech bir odam” — jinsi noma’lum. Shu sababli keyingi olmosh they / their bo‘ladi: Nobody wants to lose their job. Bu hozirgi ingliz tilida odatiy hol.

Never va nobody/nothing bir oiladanmi?

Ha. Never = not ever, ya’ni “hech qachon”. U ham bitta inkor: I never go there ✅, I don’t never go there ❌. Boshqa “nol” so‘zlari: no, none, nothing, nobody, no-one, nowhere, neither, nor.

“No problem” va “Not a problem” — hammasi to‘g‘rimi?

Ha. No problem ham, not a problem ham ishlatiladi. Bu yerda no = “not a”. Ayniqsa No problem! kundalik nutqda “arzimaydi, muammo yo‘q” ma’nosida aytiladi.

“I have nothing to do” va “I don’t have anything to do” farqi?

Ma’nosi bir xil: ikkalasi ham “qiladigan ishim yo‘q”. Birinchisi qisqaroq va ta’kidliroq, ikkinchisi og‘zaki nutqda ko‘proq uchraydi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gapni inkor fe’lli shakldan no- so‘zli shaklga o‘tkazing (bo‘sh joyga bitta so‘z).

  1. There isn’t any milk. → There is milk.
  2. I didn’t see anybody. → I saw .
  3. We didn’t do anything. → We did .
  4. They aren’t going anywhere. → They are going .
  5. She hasn’t got any brothers. → She has got brothers.
  6. There weren’t any tickets left. → There were left.

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. I (don’t have / have) nothing to wear.
  2. Nobody (came / didn’t come) to the party.
  3. She didn’t tell (anybody / nobody) about the secret.
  4. “What’s in the bag?” “(Nothing / Anything).”
  5. “How many mistakes did you make?” “(No / None).”
  6. We went (nowhere / anywhere) last weekend.
  7. There isn’t (no / any) sugar in my tea.
  8. I’ve got (no / any) time to talk. Sorry!

3-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga nothing, nobody, nowhere, anything, anybody, anywhere dan mosini yozing.

  1. “Who is at the door?” “.” (hech kim)
  2. I’m bored. There’s to do. (hech narsa)
  3. The park was empty. There was there. (hech kim)
  4. He didn’t give me for my birthday.
  5. I’m so tired. I’m not going tonight.
  6. They knew about the problem. (hech narsa; fe’l ijobiy)
  7. Is there interesting on TV tonight?
  8. Sorry, I have to say about it. (hech narsa)

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. I don’t know nobody in this city. →
  2. Nobody didn’t answer the phone. →
  3. I have none money in my pocket. →
  4. There is interesting nothing in this book. →
  5. Anybody was at home. (= hech kim yo‘q edi) →

5-mashq. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling.

  1. Hech kim kelmadi. →
  2. Men hech narsa eshitmadim. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • “Nol” ni aytishning ikki yo‘li: ijobiy fe’l + no / none / nothing / nobody / nowhere yoki inkor fe’l + any / anything / anybody / anywhere.
  • Bitta gapda faqat bitta inkor: I didn’t say nothing ❌ → I said nothing / I didn’t say anything ✅.
  • no + ot (no money); ot bo‘lmasa none (Money? None.).
  • Ega bo‘lsa faqat no-: Nobody came ✅, Anybody didn’t come ❌.
  • Nothing to do, nobody to talk to, nothing interesting, nobody else: sifat va else keyin keladi.
  • Nobody bilan fe’l birlikda, keyin they / their: Nobody phoned, did they?
  • Darak gapda anything / anybody / anywhere = “istalgan”. Hardly any = deyarli nol.
