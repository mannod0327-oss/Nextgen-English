51

Auxiliary verbs (have/do/can etc.) I think so / I hope so etc.

Yordamchi fe’llar; I think so / I hope so
Questions and auxiliary verbs · Savollar va yordamchi fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Yordamchi fe’llar (be, have, do, can, will ...) asosiy fe’lsiz, yolg‘iz o‘zi qanday ishlatilishini: Yes, I have.
  • Qisqa javoblarni va I like tea but she doesn’t kabi qarama-qarshi gaplarni.
  • Qiziqish yoki hayrat bildiradigan javoblarni: “I’ve just won a prize.” “Have you?”
  • Rozilik bildirishni: So am I (men ham), Neither do I (men ham emas).
  • I think so, I hope so, I hope not, I’m afraid so kabi javoblarni.

Ingliz tilida gapni qaytarmaslik uchun fe’lning o‘rniga yordamchi fe’l (auxiliary verb) ishlatiladi. Savolda qaysi yordamchi fe’l bo‘lsa, javobda ham o‘sha: Have you ...? — Yes, I have. Can you ...? — Yes, I can. Yordamchi fe’l bo‘lmasa (present simple, past simple), do / does / did ishlatiladi: Do you ...? — Yes, I do.

O‘zbek tilida javobda asosiy fe’lni takrorlaymiz: Choy ichasanmi? — Ha, ichaman. Bordingmi? — Ha, bordim. Inglizchada esa fe’l takrorlanmaydi, uning o‘rniga yordamchi fe’l qo‘yiladi: Do you drink tea? — Yes, I do. (❌ Yes, I drink.). O‘zbekchada “men ham” deyish yetarli, inglizchada esa uni gap tuzilishi bilan aytish kerak: So do I / So am I / So did I / So can I — yordamchi fe’l oldingi gapga moslashadi. “Men ham emas” esa Neither do I.

Yordamchi fe’l gapning qolgan qismisiz Auxiliary verbs without the rest of the sentence

Ma’no tushunarli bo‘lsa, gapning qolgan qismini aytmaymiz. Faqat ega + yordamchi fe’l qoladi:

  • “Have you fed the cat?” “Yes, I have.” (= Yes, I have fed the cat.)— Mushukka ovqat berdingizmi? — Ha, berdim.
  • “Is Malika at home?” “No, she isn’t.”— Malika uydami? — Yo‘q, uyda emas.
  • “Can Bekzod swim?” “Yes, he can.”— Bekzod suza oladimi? — Ha, suza oladi.
  • “Do you like coffee?” “Yes, I do.”— Qahvani yoqtirasizmi? — Ha, yoqtiraman.

Qoida: Savolda be (am/is/are/was/were), have (perfect zamon uchun) yoki modal fe’l (can, will, should ...) bo‘lsa, javobda ham o‘sha fe’l. Savolda yordamchi fe’l bo‘lmasa (present simple, past simple), javobda do / does / did.

Ijobiy qisqa javobda yordamchi fe’l qisqartirilmaydi: Yes, I have. ✅ — Yes, I’ve. ❌; Yes, she is. ✅ — Yes, she’s. ❌. Inkor javobda esa qisqartirish odatiy: No, I haven’t.

✅ Ingliz tilida

  • Do you like tea? — Yes, I do.
  • Did you see him? — Yes, I did.
  • Are you ready? — Yes, I am.

❌ O‘zbekcha usulda

  • Do you like tea? — Yes, I like.
  • Did you see him? — Yes, I saw.
  • Are you ready? — Yes, I do. (be o‘rniga do bo‘lmaydi!)

Bir nechta yordamchi fe’l bo‘lsa

Fe’l birikmasida bir nechta yordamchi fe’l bo‘lsa, odatda birinchisi ishlatiladi: “Have you been waiting long?” “Yes, I have.” “Will you be working tomorrow?” “Yes, I will.” Modal + have bo‘lsa, odatda have ham qoladi: “Should he have called?” “Yes, he should have.”

Qarama-qarshi gaplarda (but, and)

Yordamchi fe’l but yoki and dan keyin ham fe’l o‘rnida keladi. Odatda ma’no qarama-qarshi bo‘lsa, gapning yo‘nalishi o‘zgaradi (ijobiy ↔ inkor):

Yordamchi fe’l qarama-qarshi gapda
GapTarjima
I like tea, but Dilnoza doesn’t.Men choyni yoqtiraman, Dilnoza esa yoqtirmaydi.
Bekzod hasn’t finished, but I have.Bekzod tugatmadi, men esa tugatdim.
Aziz can drive, but his wife can’t.Aziz mashina hayday oladi, xotini esa hayday olmaydi.
I wasn’t hungry, but Jasur was.Men och emas edim, Jasur esa och edi.
We have a dog, but they don’t.Bizda it bor, ularda esa yo‘q.

Bunday qisqa yordamchi fe’lga odatda ovozda urg‘u tushadi: I like tea but she DOESN’T.

Birovning gapini rad etish

Suhbatdosh noto‘g‘ri gap aytsa, uni ham yordamchi fe’l bilan rad etamiz (gapga teskari shaklda):

  • “Kamola isn’t at school today.” “Yes, she is! I’ve just seen her.”— Kamola bugun maktabda emas. — Yo‘q, maktabda! Hozirgina uni ko‘rdim.
  • “You’ve eaten all the grapes!” “No, I haven’t!”— Hamma uzumni yeb qo‘yibsiz! — Yo‘q, yemadim!

Qiziqish va hayrat: Have you? Is it? Did he? Showing interest or surprise

Suhbatdosh yangilik aytganda, uni qiziqish yoki hayrat bilan tinglayotganimizni ko‘rsatish uchun qisqa savol beramiz: yordamchi fe’l + ega olmoshi.

  • “I’ve just bought a new phone.” “Have you? Show me!”— Hozirgina yangi telefon sotib oldim. — Rostmi? Ko‘rsating-chi!
  • “It’s raining again.” “Is it? I thought it had stopped.”— Yana yomg‘ir yog‘yapti. — Shundaymi? To‘xtagan deb o‘ylagandim.
  • “Aziz doesn’t like football.” “Doesn’t he? I thought he did.”— Aziz futbolni yoqtirmaydi. — Yoqtirmaydimi? Yoqtiradi deb o‘ylagandim.
  • “I can’t swim.” “Can’t you? I’ll teach you.”— Men suza olmayman. — Suza olmaysanmi? Men o‘rgataman.

Qoida: Gap ijobiy bo‘lsa, javob ham ijobiy (Have you?); gap inkor bo‘lsa, javob ham inkor (Can’t you?). Yordamchi fe’l oldingi gapdagi bilan bir xil bo‘ladi; yo‘q bo‘lsa — do / does / did.

Gap → qiziqish bildiruvchi javob
GapJavob
Sardor passed the exam.Did he? Great!
My brother is a pilot.Is he? That sounds exciting!
I was born in Khiva.Were you? I didn’t know that.
My grandparents live in Bukhara.Do they? I love that city.
We didn’t get the tickets.Didn’t you? What a pity!
Nodira hasn’t called yet.Hasn’t she? That’s strange.

Bunday qisqa javoblar o‘zbekchadagi «Shundaymi?», «Rostdanmi?», «Ha-a?» ga o‘xshaydi. Inglizchada ular grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri bo‘lishi kerak: ega olmoshi va oldingi gapning yordamchi fe’li bilan. Faqat Really? ham deyish mumkin.

Qiziqish bildirish uchun ovozingizni biroz ko‘taring (↗). Past, yassi ohangda aytilsa, “menga baribir” degandek eshitilishi mumkin. Bu qisqa javoblar haqiqiy savol emas, shuningdek ajratilgan so‘roq (question tag) ham emas — farqi Unit 52 da.

So am I / Neither do I Agreeing with a statement

Birov bilan rozi bo‘lganda (“men ham”) quyidagi tuzilmalar ishlatiladi:

So+yordamchi fe’l+ega | Neither / Nor+yordamchi fe’l+ega
“Men ham” (ijobiy gapga) — So + yordamchi fe’l + ega
GapRozilik
I’m cold.So am I.
I passed the test.So did I.
I’ve been to Bukhara.So have I.
I can speak Russian.So can I.
I’ll be late.So will I.

Dilnoza works hard.So does Bekzod.
“Men ham emas” (inkor gapga) — Neither / Nor + yordamchi fe’l + ega
GapRozilik
I’m not hungry.Neither am I. / Nor am I.
I don’t like cold weather.Neither do I. / Nor do I.
I haven’t finished.Neither have I.
I didn’t see the film.Neither did I.
I can’t swim.Neither can I.
Malika doesn’t eat meat.Neither does her sister.
I never drink coffee at night. (never — inkor ma’noli)Neither do I.

Qoida: Tartib: So / Neither + yordamchi fe’l + ega (So am I, Neither do I). Yordamchi fe’l ega dan oldin turadi. Neither / Nor o‘zi inkor ma’noli, shuning uchun ikkinchi marta inkor qo‘shilmaydi: Neither do I ✅ — Neither don’t I ❌.

Qarshi fikr bildirish

Rozi bo‘lmasangiz, yordamchi fe’lni qarama-qarshi shaklda ishlatasiz:

GapRozi emasman
I’m tired.I’m not. / Are you? I’m not.
I like fish.I don’t.
I can swim.I can’t.
I don’t like fish.I do.
I haven’t been to Samarkand.I have.

Boshqa shakllar va gap ichida

  • “I don’t like it.” “I don’t either.” / “Me neither.”— Menga yoqmayapti. — Menga ham.
  • “I like it.” “I do too.” / “Me too.”— Menga yoqyapti. — Menga ham.
  • Aziz likes football, and so does Bekzod.Aziz futbolni yoqtiradi, Bekzod ham.
  • Malika can’t swim, and neither can her sister.Malika suza olmaydi, uning singlisi ham.

Me too / Me neither norasmiy og‘zaki nutqda juda keng tarqalgan. Bu to‘liq gap emas, lekin to‘g‘ri va tabiiy javob. Me too faqat ijobiy gapga, Me neither faqat inkor gapga javob bo‘ladi.

I think so / I hope so Replacing a whole clause with “so”

Ba’zi fe’llardan keyin butun ergash gap o‘rniga so ishlatiladi:

  • “Is Sardor at home?” “I think so.” (= I think he is at home.)— Sardor uydami? — Shekilli, uyda. (Uyda deb o‘ylayman.)
  • “Will Nodira come to the party?” “I hope so.”— Nodira ziyofatga keladimi? — Kelsa edi.
  • “Is the shop closed?” “I’m afraid so.”— Do‘kon yopiqmi? — Afsuski, ha.

Bu tuzilma think, hope, expect, suppose, imagine, believe, guess, assume, be afraid bilan ishlaydi. Inkor shakli esa fe’lga qarab farq qiladi:

Ijobiy va inkor javoblar
Fe’lIjobiyInkor (1-usul)Inkor (2-usul)
thinkI think so.I don’t think so. (eng keng tarqalgan)I think not. (rasmiyroq, kamroq)
believeI believe so.I don’t believe so.I believe not.
expectI expect so.I don’t expect so.I expect not.
supposeI suppose so.I don’t suppose so.I suppose not.
imagineI imagine so.I don’t imagine so.I imagine not.

hopeI hope so.I hope not. (faqat shu; I don’t hope so ❌)

Qoida: think bilan inkor odatda I don’t think so; hope va be afraid bilan inkor faqat I hope not, I’m afraid not. So gap oxirida turadi, undan keyin hech narsa qo‘shilmaydi: I think so. ✅ — I think so he is at home. ❌

➕ Ijobiy

  • “Is it going to rain?” “I think so.”
  • “Will you pass?” “I hope so.”
  • “Is she coming?” “I expect so.”

➖ Inkor

  • “Is it going to rain?” “I don’t think so.”
  • “Will you fail?” “I hope not.”
  • “Can I come in?” “I’m afraid not.”

afraid bu yerda “qo‘rqmoq” emas, “afsuski” ma’nosida: “Is there any milk?” “I’m afraid not.” = “Afsuski, yo‘q.” Muloyim rad etish uchun juda qulay.

If so / if not

Xuddi shunday, if so (“agar shunday bo‘lsa”) va if not (“agar bunday bo‘lmasa”) ham butun gapni qaytarmaslik uchun xizmat qiladi:

  • Are you free tonight? If so, let’s go to the cinema. If not, we can go tomorrow.Bugun kechqurun bo‘shmisiz? Bo‘sh bo‘lsangiz, kinoga boraylik. Bo‘lmasangiz, ertaga boramiz.

know bilan so ishlatilmaydi: “Is he married?” “I don’t know.” ✅ — I don’t know so ❌.

“to” bilan qisqa javoblar I’d love to. / I didn’t want to.

Fe’l to dan keyin ma’lum bo‘lsa, fe’lni tushirib, faqat to ni qoldiramiz:

  • “Are you coming to the party?” “I’d love to.”— Ziyofatga kelasizmi? — Albatta kelardim.
  • “Why didn’t you call me?” “I didn’t want to.”— Nega menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmading? — Xohlamadim.
  • “Did you clean your room?” “Not yet, but I’m going to.”— Xonangni tozaladingmi? — Hali yo‘q, lekin tozalayman.
  • I don’t smoke now, but I used to.Hozir chekmayman, lekin ilgari chekardim.

Bu yerda to ni tushirib qoldirib bo‘lmaydi: “I’d love.” ❌ — “I’d love to.” ✅. Shuningdek: “I didn’t want.” ❌ — “I didn’t want to.” ✅.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “Yes, I like” emas, “Yes, I do” deymiz?

Ingliz tilida javobda asosiy fe’lni takrorlash shart emas va odatiy ham emas. Present simple va past simple savollari do / does / did bilan tuzilgani uchun javobda ham shu yordamchi fe’l ishlatiladi: Do you like tea? — Yes, I do. Asosiy fe’lni qaytarish faqat ta’kid uchun, to‘liq gap sifatida mumkin: Yes, I like tea.

“So do I”, “I do too” va “Me too” orasida farq bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil — “men ham”. So do I va I do too (ega + yordamchi fe’l + too) har qanday vaziyatda to‘g‘ri; Me too esa norasmiy, og‘zaki nutq uchun. Inkor gapga javoblar: Neither do I (yoki Nor do I) / I don’t either / Me neither.

“So am I” bilan “So I am” ning farqi nima?

So am I = “men ham” (rozilik). So I am = “Ha, rostdan ham shunday” — oldingi gapning to‘g‘riligini tasdiqlaysiz: “You’re late.” “So I am.” (Rost, kechikibman.) Ikkalasi turli ma’noda, shuning uchun tartibni aralashtirmang.

Nega “I hope not” deymiz-u, “I don’t hope so” demaymiz?

Bu shunchaki odat: hope bilan inkor not orqali beriladi, think, believe, expect kabi fe’llarda esa odatda fe’lning o‘zi inkor bo‘ladi (I don’t think so). Xuddi shunday be afraid bilan faqat I’m afraid not.

“Is it?” “Did he?” — bu ajratilgan so‘roq (question tag)mi?

Yo‘q. Bu qiziqish bildiruvchi javob: biz boshqa odamning gapiga javob beryapmiz. Question tag esa o‘zimiz aytayotgan gapning oxirida keladi va ko‘pincha gap bilan qarama-qarshi shaklda bo‘ladi: It’s cold, isn’t it? (Unit 52).

“I’m afraid so” — qo‘rqayapman degani emasmi?

Yo‘q, bu yerda afraid “afsuski” ma’nosida, yomon yangilik yoki rad javobini muloyim aytishda ishlatiladi. “Can I pay by card?” “I’m afraid not.” = “Afsuski, bo‘lmaydi.”

Savolda “have got” bo‘lsa, javobda qaysi fe’l?

Savoldagi yordamchi fe’l bilan javob beriladi: “Have you got a pen?” “Yes, I have.” Savol Do you have a pen? bo‘lsa — Yes, I do. Rozilikda ham xuddi shunday: I’ve got a car. — So have I. / I have a car. — So do I.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Qisqa javobda asosiy fe’l o‘rniga yordamchi fe’l: Yes, I have. / No, she isn’t. Present/past simple uchun do / does / did.
  • Ijobiy qisqa javobda yordamchi fe’l qisqartirilmaydi (Yes, I am).
  • Qiziqish bildirish: ijobiy gap → Have you? Is it? Did he? Inkor gap → Can’t you? Doesn’t he?
  • Rozilik: So + yordamchi fe’l + ega (So am I), Neither / Nor + yordamchi fe’l + ega (Neither do I).
  • Rozi bo‘lmasangiz: I like it. — I don’t. / I don’t like it. — I do.
  • think, expect, suppose, believe: I think so / I don’t think so; hope va afraid: I hope so / I hope not, I’m afraid so / not.
  • to bilan: I’d love to. I didn’t want to. — to qoladi, fe’l tushadi.
