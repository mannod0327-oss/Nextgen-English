Present simple (I do)

Hozirgi oddiy zamon
Present and past · Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Present simple (oddiy hozirgi zamon) qanday yasalishini: I work, lekin he works.
  • Uni qachon ishlatishni: umumiy haqiqatlar, odatlar, takrorlanib turadigan ishlar va doimiy holatlar.
  • do / does yordamida so‘roq va inkor gaplar tuzishni (Do you ...? She doesn’t ...).
  • always, usually, often, never kabi so‘zlar bilan “qanchalik tez-tez” degan ma’noni berishni va ularning gapdagi o‘rnini.
  • I promise, I suggest, I apologise kabi “aytishning o‘zi bilan bajariladigan” iboralarni.
  • -s / -es qo‘shimchasining imlosi va talaffuzini (watch → watches, study → studies).

Present simple — narsalar haqida umuman olganda gapirish zamoni. U doimo, muntazam yoki odatda bo‘ladigan ishlarni bildiradi. Harakat aynan gapirayotgan paytda bo‘layotgani yoki bo‘lmayotgani muhim emas.

O‘zbek tilidagi -a/-y + shaxs qo‘shimchasi shakli (ishlayman, yashaysan, gapiradi) present simple ga juda yaqin: Akam bankda ishlaydi = My brother works in a bank. Farqi shundaki, o‘zbek tilida har bir shaxsning o‘z qo‘shimchasi bor (-man, -san, -di, -miz, -siz), ingliz tilida esa faqat bitta qo‘shimcha bor: he / she / it uchun -s. Boshqa hamma shaxslarda fe’l o‘zgarmaydi. Aynan shu sabab o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha -s ni unutadi: ❌ He work.

Shakli: I work — he works Form of the present simple

Mana bir vaziyat. Dilnoza — pianinochi, konsertlarda chiqish qiladi. Hozir esa u oshxonada kechki ovqat pishiryapti. Demak, ayni paytda u pianino chalmayapti. Lekin u pianino chaladi — bu uning kasbi, u buni muntazam qiladi:

  • Dilnoza is cooking dinner. She isn’t playing the piano now.Dilnoza kechki ovqat pishiryapti. U hozir pianino chalmayapti.
  • But she plays the piano. She’s a pianist.Lekin u pianino chaladi. U pianinochi.

play / plays, work / works, do / does — bular present simple shakllari. Fe’l o‘zgarmaydi, faqat he / she / it (va bitta odam yoki narsa) bilan oxiriga -s yoki -es qo‘shiladi.

I / we / you / they+fe’l
he / she / it+fe’l + s / es
Present simple: darak gap
EgaFe’lMisol
I / we / you / theywork, live, go, have, doThey live in Samarkand.
he / she / it (my father, Malika, the bus ...)works, lives, goes, has, doesMy father lives in Samarkand.

Qoida: Ega bitta odam yoki narsa bo‘lsa (he, she, it, my sister, the shop, Tashkent, water, everybody ...), fe’lga -s / -es qo‘shiladi. Ega ko‘plikda bo‘lsa (they, my parents, the shops) yoki I / you / we bo‘lsa — hech narsa qo‘shilmaydi.

Diqqat: everybody, everyone, nobody, somebody grammatik jihatdan birlikda: Everybody knows him. ✅ — Everybody know him. ❌

Umumiy narsalar, odatlar va haqiqatlar Things in general, repeated actions, facts

Present simple umuman olganda gapirish uchun ishlatiladi. U quyidagilarni bildiradi:

  • doimo yoki muntazam takrorlanib turadigan ishlarni (odatlar, kun tartibi, kasb);
  • umumiy haqiqatlarni (tabiat qonunlari, ilmiy faktlar);
  • doimiy holatlarni (qayerda yashash, qayerda ishlash).

Bunda harakat aynan gapirayotgan paytda bo‘layotgani muhim emas.

  • Bakers start work very early in the morning.Novvoylar ishni ertalab juda erta boshlashadi.
  • Malika is a vet. She looks after sick animals.Malika — veterinar. U kasal hayvonlarni davolaydi.
  • Water freezes at 0 degrees Celsius.Suv 0 darajada muzlaydi.
  • The sun rises in the east and sets in the west.Quyosh sharqdan chiqib, g‘arbga botadi.
  • My grandparents live in a village near Fergana.Bobom va buvim Farg‘ona yaqinidagi bir qishloqda yashaydi.
  • I usually visit them at weekends.Odatda dam olish kunlari ularni ko‘rgani boraman. (AmE: on weekends)
  • The museum opens at 9 o’clock and closes at 6.Muzey soat 9 da ochiladi va 6 da yopiladi.
  • Many people in Tashkent speak both Uzbek and Russian.Toshkentda ko‘p odamlar ham o‘zbek, ham rus tilida gapiradi.

Malika hozir uyda dam olayotgan bo‘lsa ham, She looks after sick animals deyish to‘g‘ri — chunki biz uning kasbi haqida umuman gapiryapmiz, hozirgi daqiqa haqida emas.

Eslab qoling: kimga -s kerak?

I / you / we / they

  • I work ...
  • They teach ...
  • We have ...
  • You go ...

he / she / it

  • but he works ...
  • but my sister teaches ...
  • but she has ...
  • but Jasur goes ...

So‘roq va inkor: do / does Questions and negatives with do / does

Present simple da so‘roq va inkor gap tuzish uchun yordamchi fe’l kerak: do yoki does. Asosiy fe’l esa doim lug‘at shaklida (infinitive, -s siz) qoladi.

Ega+don’t / doesn’t+fe’l (-s siz)
(Where / What ...)+do / does+ega+fe’l (-s siz)?
Present simple: to‘liq jadval
Ega✅ Darak gap❌ Inkor gap❓ So‘roq gap
I / we / you / theyI workI don’t (do not) workDo you work?
he / she / itShe worksShe doesn’t (does not) workDoes she work?

Qoida: -s faqat bir joyda turadi. Darak gapda u fe’lga qo‘shiladi (she works), so‘roq va inkorda esa does ga “ko‘chib o‘tadi” (does she work? she doesn’t work). Shuning uchun: Does she works? ❌, She doesn’t works. ❌

  • “Where do you live?” “I live in Chilanzar.”— Qayerda yashaysiz? — Chilonzorda yashayman.
  • “Does your brother work?” “No, he doesn’t. He’s still at school.”— Ukangiz ishlaydimi? — Yo‘q. U hali maktabda o‘qiydi.
  • I don’t eat meat. I’m a vegetarian.Men go‘sht yemayman. Men vegetarianman.
  • Penguins don’t live in the Arctic.Pingvinlar Arktikada yashamaydi.
  • “What does ‘xayrli tong’ mean?” “It means ‘good morning’.”— “Xayrli tong” nimani anglatadi? — “Good morning” degani.
  • How much does this melon cost?Bu qovun qancha turadi?

So‘roq gapda so‘z tartibi o‘zbek tilidan farq qiladi. What does this word mean? ✅ — What means this word? ❌. How much does it cost? ✅ — How much costs it? ❌

do — asosiy fe’l sifatida

do “qilmoq” ma’nosida asosiy fe’l ham bo‘la oladi. Shunda gapda ikkita do keladi: biri yordamchi, biri asosiy:

  • “What do you do?” “I’m an engineer.”— Kasbingiz nima? (= Nima ish qilasiz?) — Muhandisman.
  • What does your mother do?Onangiz nima ish qiladi? (kasbi nima?)
  • My cousin doesn’t do any housework. He just plays computer games.Amakivachcham uy ishlarini umuman qilmaydi. U faqat kompyuter o‘yinlarini o‘ynaydi.

What do you do? — bu “Kasbingiz nima?” degan savol. “Hozir nima qilyapsiz?” esa What are you doing? (present continuous, Unit 1).

“be” fe’li bilan do ishlatilmaydi

am / is / are — o‘zi yordamchi fe’ldek ishlaydi, unga do/does kerak emas:

✅ To‘g‘ri

  • Are you a student?
  • She isn’t at home.
  • Where are you from?

❌ Noto‘g‘ri

  • Do you are a student?
  • She doesn’t is at home.
  • Where do you are from?

Qanchalik tez-tez? How often: always, usually, often, sometimes, never

Biror ishni qanchalik tez-tez qilishimiz haqida gapirganda ham present simple ishlatiladi:

  • I leave home at half past seven every morning.Men har kuni ertalab soat yettiyarimda uydan chiqaman.
  • “How often do you call your grandparents?” “Twice a week.”— Bobongiz va buvingizga qanchalik tez-tez qo‘ng‘iroq qilasiz? — Haftada ikki marta.
  • Nodira hardly ever eats sweets.Nodira shirinlikni deyarli yemaydi.
  • Sardor goes to Samarkand on business once a month.Sardor oyiga bir marta ish yuzasidan Samarqandga boradi.
  • My family always has plov on Thursdays.Bizning oila har doim payshanba kuni osh qiladi.

Bu so‘zlar gapning qayerida turadi?

QoidaMisol
Oddiy fe’ldan oldinI often walk to work. She never drinks coffee.
am / is / are dan keyinJasur is always late. We are usually at home on Sundays.
don’t / doesn’t dan keyinI don’t often watch TV.
So‘roqda — egadan keyinDo you usually have breakfast?

usually, sometimes, occasionally gap boshida ham kela oladi: Sometimes I walk to work. Lekin always, never, hardly ever darak gapning boshida odatda turmaydi: Always I drink tea. ❌ → I always drink tea. ✅ (Buyruq gaplar bundan mustasno: Never give up!)

“Qanchalik tez-tez” ni bildiruvchi iboralar (odatda gap oxirida):
every day / week / yearonce a weektwice a monththree times a yearon Mondaysat weekendsin the morningevery other day

O‘zbek tilida “haftada ikki marta” deymiz — avval “hafta”, keyin “marta”. Inglizchada tartib teskari: twice a week (ikki marta + bir haftada). “Bir marta / ikki marta” uchun maxsus so‘zlar bor: once, twice; uchdan boshlab — three times, four times ...

I promise, I suggest, I apologise ... Doing things by saying something

Ba’zi ishlarni biz so‘z aytishning o‘zi bilan bajaramiz. “Va’da beraman” deganingizda — siz aynan shu so‘z bilan va’da berasiz. Bunday fe’llar bilan present simple ishlatiladi, continuous emas:

  • I promise I’ll phone you as soon as I arrive.Va’da beraman, yetib borishim bilan qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman. (I’m promising ❌)
  • “Where shall we eat?” “I suggest we try the new café near the metro.”— Qayerda ovqatlanamiz? — Metro yonidagi yangi kafeni sinab ko‘rishni taklif qilaman.
  • I apologise for being late.Kechikkanim uchun uzr so‘rayman. (AmE: apologize)
  • I agree with you.Siz bilan roziman. (I am agree ❌)
  • No, you can’t pay. I insist!Yo‘q, siz to‘lamaysiz. Men turib olaman!
  • I advise you to book your tickets early.Chiptalarni oldindan bron qilishingizni maslahat beraman.
  • I refuse to answer that question.Bu savolga javob berishdan bosh tortaman.
Shu guruhdagi fe’llar:
I promiseI suggestI apologiseI adviseI insistI agreeI refuseI swear

O‘zbek tilida “roziman” deymiz — bu sifatga o‘xshaydi, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ❌ I am agree deydi. Inglizchada agree — fe’l, unga am kerak emas: I agree. Inkori: I don’t agree (I am not agree ❌).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega -s faqat he / she / it ga qo‘shiladi?

Bu tarixiy qoldiq: qadimgi ingliz tilida har bir shaxsning o‘z qo‘shimchasi bo‘lgan (xuddi o‘zbek tilidagidek). Vaqt o‘tib ularning hammasi tushib qolgan, faqat 3-shaxs birlikdagi qo‘shimcha -s ko‘rinishida saqlanib qolgan. Shuni yodlab olish kifoya: he / she / it = -s.

“Ertaga boraman” ni “I go tomorrow” deb tarjima qilsa bo‘ladimi?

Odatda yo‘q. O‘zbekcha boraman ham hozirgi, ham kelasi zamonni bildiradi, inglizcha present simple esa asosan umumiy va takroriy ishlar uchun. Ertangi reja uchun I’m going tomorrow yoki I’ll go tomorrow deyiladi (Unit 19–22). Faqat jadval va rasmiy vaqtlar bundan mustasno: The train leaves at 7.15 tomorrow. (Unit 19)

“Who lives here?” — nega bu yerda does yo‘q?

Chunki who bu yerda gapning egasi: “Kim yashaydi?” Savol so‘zi ega bo‘lsa, do/does ishlatilmaydi va fe’l darak gapdagidek qoladi: Who lives here? (oddiy savolda Who does live here? deyilmaydi ❌). Lekin Who do you love? — bu yerda who to‘ldiruvchi, ega esa you. Bu mavzu Unit 49 da.

“Where are you from?” va “Where do you come from?” — farqi bormi?

Ikkalasi ham “Qayerliksiz?” degan ma’noni beradi va ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Where are you from? — be bilan, shuning uchun do yo‘q. Where do you come from? — oddiy fe’l come bilan, shuning uchun do kerak. Ularni aralashtirmang: Where do you from? ❌, Where are you come from? ❌

“My family live” yoki “My family lives”? “The team play” yoki “plays”?

Britaniya ingliz tilida ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi: guruhni bitta butun deb ko‘rsangiz — My family lives in Bukhara, uning a’zolari haqida o‘ylasangiz — My family live ... Amerika ingliz tilida odatda birlik: lives, plays. Batafsil — Unit 79.

“I don’t” va “I do not” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ma’nosi bir xil. don’t / doesn’t — og‘zaki nutq va oddiy yozuvda, do not / does not — rasmiy matnda yoki inkorni kuchli ta’kidlash uchun.

Gapda “always”, “every day” bo‘lsa, doim present simple ishlatilaveradimi?

Ko‘pincha shunday, chunki bu so‘zlar takrorlanishni bildiradi. Ammo istisno bor: You’re always losing your keys! — bu norozilik ifodasi (“doim yo‘qotasan-a!”). Bu ma’no Unit 3 da tushuntiriladi. Shuning uchun so‘zga emas, ma’noga qarang.

“I’m promising” deb umuman aytilmaydimi?

Va’dani ayni shu so‘zlar bilan berayotganingizda — yo‘q, faqat I promise. Xuddi shunday: I agree, I apologise, I suggest. Bu fe’llar bilan biz harakatni tasvirlamaymiz, balki uni aytish orqali bajaramiz.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: I / you / we / they work, lekin he / she / it works (-s / -es / -ies; have → has).
  • Present simple — umumiy haqiqatlar, odatlar, takroriy ishlar, doimiy holatlar uchun. Hozir bo‘layotgani muhim emas.
  • Inkor: don’t / doesn’t + fe’l. So‘roq: Do / Does + ega + fe’l? Fe’l -s olmaydi.
  • be (am/is/are) bilan do/does ishlatilmaydi: Are you ...? She isn’t ...
  • always / usually / often / never — oddiy fe’ldan oldin, am/is/are dan keyin.
  • “Qanchalik tez-tez?” — How often ...? → once / twice / three times a week.
  • I promise, I agree, I apologise, I suggest — doim present simple (I am agree ❌).
