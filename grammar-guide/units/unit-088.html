All / all of, most / most of, no / none of etc.

All / all of, most / most of, no / none of
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • all, most, some, any, no + ot — umumiy fikr (most people) va all of, most of, some of, any of, none of, half of + the / my / these … + ot — aniq guruh (most of the people I know).
  • of dan keyin olmosh: all of us, most of them, none of it.
  • Qaysi so‘zlardan keyin of ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin (all the, half my) va qaysilaridan keyin mumkin emas (most of the).
  • no va none farqi; none of dan keyin birlik yoki ko‘plik fe’l.
  • Miqdor shkalasi: all → most → many / much → some → (a) few / (a) little → none.

Miqdor so‘zlari (all, most, some, any, none, half …) bilan gapirishdan oldin o‘zingizdan so‘rang: gap umuman shu turdagi hamma narsa haqidami (qushlar, talabalar, tillar) yoki ma’lum, aniq guruh haqidami (shu bog‘dagi qushlar, mening guruhimdagi talabalar)? Birinchi holda ot to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri keladi: most birds. Ikkinchi holda of + the / my / these … kerak: most of the birds.

O‘zbek tilida ham xuddi shunday ikki qolip bor. Umumiy ma’noda miqdor so‘zi ot oldidan turadi: ko‘pchilik talabalar, ba’zi qushlar, hamma bolalar. Aniq guruh haqida esa egalik qo‘shimchasi ishlatiladi: talabalarimizning ko‘pchiligi, qushlarning ba’zilari, bolalarning hammasi. Inglizcha of ana shu -ning … -i qolipiga mos keladi: most of the students = talabalarning ko‘pchiligi. Yana bir muhim farq: o‘zbekchada hech biri bilan fe’l inkor bo‘ladi (hech biri kelmadi), inglizchada esa none bilan fe’l darak shaklida turadi: None of them came. ✅ — None of them didn’t come. ❌

Miqdor shkalasi

Miqdor so‘zlarini “hammadan hech narsagacha” bo‘lgan shkala bo‘yicha joylashtirish mumkin. Pastdagi rasmda har bir blokda 10 ta doira bor: to‘ldirilgani — gap tegishli bo‘lgan qism.

Shkala taxminiy. Most “yarmidan ko‘pi” degani; half — aniq yarmi; many va some orasidagi chegara so‘zlovchiga bog‘liq. Aniq son ma’lum bo‘lsa, sonni ayting: seven of the ten students (o‘ntadan yettitasi).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Most people” bilan “most of the people” ning farqi nimada?

Most people — odamlar umuman, butun insoniyat haqida: Most people sleep at night. Most of the people — oldindan ma’lum, aniq odamlar haqida: Most of the people in the hall were students. (zaldagi odamlar). Shuning uchun aniqlovchi (the, my, these) bo‘lsa, of kerak; aniqlovchi bo‘lmasa, of yo‘q: Most of people ❌.

“All of the students” bilan “all the students” — farq bormi?

Ma’no bir xil, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri (Britaniya va Amerika ingliz tilida ham). All va half dan keyin aniqlovchi (the, my, this …) kelsa, of ni tushirish mumkin. Olmosh bilan esa of doim kerak: all of them ✅, all them ❌.

“None of the students” dan keyin “is” kerakmi yoki “are”?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Rasmiy yozuvda birlik fe’l (None of the students is late) ko‘proq, kundalik nutqda esa ko‘plik fe’l (None of the students are late) juda keng tarqalgan. Ikkalasi ham Britaniya va Amerika ingliz tilida ishlatiladi. Ot sanalmaydigan bo‘lsa, fe’l faqat birlikda: None of the money was found.

“No” bilan “none” ni qanday ajrataman?

No ot oldida keladi va yolg‘iz qolmaydi: no time, no questions. None otdan oldin kelmaydi; u ot o‘rnini bosadi yoki of bilan keladi: “Any questions?” “None.”, none of the questions. Eslab qolish uchun: none = no + one (yoki no + ot), no esa har doim ot bilan birga.

“Half” bilan a/an va of qanday ishlatiladi?

Half of the cake va half the cake — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Ot oldida a / an bo‘lsa, half undan oldin keladi: half an hour, half a kilo; an half hour ❌. Olmosh bilan: half of it, half of them.

Nega “of them” deymiz, “of they” emas?

Of — predlog, predlogdan keyin olmoshning to‘ldiruvchi shakli turadi: of me, of us, of you, of him, of her, of it, of them. O‘zbekchada ham shunday: “ular” (ega) va “ularning” (qaratqich) boshqa shakllar.

“Most”, “almost” va “mostly” ni qanday adashtirmaslik kerak?

Most — “ko‘pchilik” (ot bilan): most students. Almost — “deyarli”, all, every, everyone, always kabi so‘zlar oldidan: almost all students. Mostly — “asosan”, ravish: The students are mostly girls. “The most” — orttirma daraja: the most expensive.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • all / most / some / any / no + ot — umumiy fikr: Most people …. All / most / some / any / none / half + of + the / my / these + ot — aniq guruh: Most of the people ….
  • Aniqlovchi (the, my, this …) yo‘q bo‘lsa, of ham yo‘q: most of people ❌.
  • Olmosh bilan of doim kerak: all of us, most of them, none of it.
  • Faqat all va half dan keyin (va both dan keyin, Unit 89) of ni tushirish mumkin: all the, half my. Most the ❌.
  • no + ot; none — ot yo‘q yoki none of. Fe’l inkor emas: None of them came.
  • None of + ko‘plik ot: fe’l birlik (rasmiy) yoki ko‘plik (kundalik). Boshqa so‘zlarda fe’l of dan keyingi otga moslashadi.
  • Ot tushib qolsa: Most were full. We all, they all — all olmoshdan keyin turadi.
