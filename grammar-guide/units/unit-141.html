Phrasal verbs 5: on/off (2)

Frazali fe’llar — 5: on / off (2)
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • on (davom etish, oldinga) va off (uzilish, ketish, tugash, ajralish) bilan yasalgan yana yigirmaga yaqin frazali fe’lni: go on, carry on, keep on, drive on, walk on, move on, hold on, get on, doze off, nod off, drop off, show off, tell off, see off, rip off, cut off, wear off, finish off, go off, be off, walk off, run off, drive off, take time off.
  • go on ning ikki asosiy ma’nosini (davom etmoq · sodir bo‘lmoq) va go on doing bilan go on to do farqini.
  • get on ning uch ma’nosini: ishlar qanday ketishi, birov bilan til topishish, ishni davom ettirish (get on with).
  • Odamlarga nisbatan ishlatiladigan fe’llarni: tell sb off (koymoq), see sb off (kuzatib qo‘ymoq), rip sb off (aldamoq, norasmiy) — ularda olmosh doim o‘rtada turadi.
  • go off va be off ning shu unitdagi ma’nolarini (sut buzilmoq, ketmoq, ishda yo‘q) va Unit 140 dagi ma’nolaridan farqini.

Oldingi unitda (Unit 140) on “ulangan, ustida”, off esa “uzilgan, ajralgan” tasavvurini berdi. Bu unitda ikkita yangi tasavvur bor. on — “oldinga, to‘xtamasdan”: ish yoki harakat davom etadi (go on, walk on) yoki biror narsa ketayotgan bo‘ladi (What’s going on?). off — “uzilish, ketish, tugash”: odam yoki narsa ketadi (be off), aloqa uziladi (cut off), ta’sir asta-sekin yo‘qoladi (wear off), odam uyquga “ketadi” (doze off). Frazali fe’llarning umumiy qoidalari — Unit 137 da.

Belgilar: ∅ to‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi · ⇄ ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: tell him off) · ✗ ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida) · informal norasmiy, asosan og‘zaki nutq.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar ko‘pincha yordamchi fe’llar bilan beriladi. on o‘zbekchadagi davom etmoq, -ib bormoq va -aver shakliga mos keladi: yuraver = walk on, gapiraver = go on talking, o‘qiyver = go on reading. off esa ko‘pincha -ib qolmoq / -ib qo‘ymoq / ketmoq ga to‘g‘ri keladi: uxlab qolmoq = doze off, kuzatib qo‘ymoq = see off, buzilib qolmoq = go off, uzib qo‘ymoq = cut off, jo‘nab ketmoq = be off. “Davom ettirmoq” — carry on yoki go on.

Tuzoq: tell off — “aytmoq” emas, “koymoq”; show off — “ko‘rsatmoq” emas, “maqtanmoq, ko‘z-ko‘z qilmoq”; see off — “ko‘rmoq” emas, “kuzatib qo‘ymoq”. Particle ma’noni butunlay o‘zgartiradi.

Davom etish: go on, carry on, keep on Continuing

Ish yoki holat to‘xtamay davom etsa, inglizchada uchta fe’ldan birini ishlatamiz: go on, carry on, keep on. Ular ko‘pincha bir-birini almashtira oladi. Hammasi to‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi (∅); davom etayotgan ishni aytsak, -ing yoki with + ot keladi (✗ ajralmaydi). Fe’ldan keyin -ing haqida — Unit 53.

Oldinga yurish va kutish: drive on, walk on, move on, hold on Moving forward and waiting

drive on · walk on — to‘xtamay yurmoq ∅

Harakat fe’li + on — “to‘xtamasdan davom ettirmoq, oldinga yurmoq”: drive on, walk on, go on, ride on. Joy aytilmasa — faqat on; borilayotgan joy to bilan: drive on to Samarkand.

  • We stopped for a drink, then drove on to Samarkand.Biz ichimlik ichish uchun to‘xtadik, so‘ng Samarqandga qarab yo‘limizni davom ettirdik.
  • The road was closed, so we couldn’t drive on.Yo‘l yopiq edi, shuning uchun biz yurishni davom ettira olmadik.
  • Jasur said hello but walked on without stopping.Jasur salom berdi, lekin to‘xtamay yurib ketaverdi.
  • Let’s walk on a little. The café is near the bridge.Biroz yana yuraylik. Kafe ko‘prik yonida.

Farqlang: He walked on. (u to‘xtamay yurdi — particle) va He walked on the grass. (u o‘t ustida yurdi — on predlog).

move on — o‘tmoq, ketmoq, oldinga yurmoq ∅

move on ning to‘rtta ma’nosi bor. Barchasida to‘ldiruvchi yo‘q; qayerga o‘tilishi to bilan, nimadan from bilan aytiladi (✗ ajralmaydi).

hold on — kutmoq; mahkam ushlamoq informal

hold on — (1) kutmoq: Hold on a minute! Telefonda ham: Hold on, please. (2) Hold on! — norozilik yoki ikkilanish: “Bir daqiqa, to‘xtang!” (3) mahkam ushlamoq: Hold on tight! Biror narsani ushlab turish bo‘lsa — hold on to + ot (✗ ajralmaydi).

  • Hold on a minute — I’ll get my coat.Bir daqiqa kuting — paltomni olib chiqaman.
  • Hold on, please. I’ll see if Mr Karimov is in.Iltimos, kutib turing. Janob Karimov joyidami-yo‘qmi, bilib beraman.
  • Hold on! That’s not what I said.To‘xtang! Men bunday demagan edim.
  • The bus is going very fast. Hold on to the rail!Avtobus juda tez ketyapti. Tutqichni mahkam ushlang!

“Kutmoq” ma’nosida hold on norasmiy; ma’nodoshlari: wait (neytral), hang on (norasmiy), telefonda hold the line (rasmiyroq).

Nima bo‘lyapti va ishlar qanday: go on, get on What’s happening, how things are going

go on — sodir bo‘lmoq, davom etmoq ∅

go on “nimadir sodir bo‘lyapti” degan ma’noda ham ishlatiladi — odatda What’s going on? shaklida. Bu hozir davom etayotgan voqea haqida so‘rash.

  • What’s been going on in the office this week?Bu hafta ofisda nimalar bo‘lyapti?
  • There’s something strange going on in that house.U uyda g‘alati bir narsa bo‘lyapti.

Uyqu, maqtanish, tanbeh, kuzatish, aldash: doze off, show off, tell off, see off, rip off People and behaviour

doze off · nod off — uxlab qolmoq ∅

doze off — o‘tirgan yoki yotgan holda istamasdan, yengil uxlab qolmoq, mudramoq. nod off va drop off — xuddi shu ma’no, lekin norasmiy informal: I dropped off in front of the TV. (Televizor oldida uxlab qolibman.) Hammasi to‘ldiruvchisiz.

  • Grandpa dozed off in front of the TV after lunch.Bobom tushlikdan keyin televizor oldida uxlab qoldi.
  • After the plov, two old men nodded off in the warm teahouse.Palovdan keyin ikki chol iliq choyxonada mudrab qolishdi.
  • I was so tired that I dozed off on the bus and missed my stop.Juda charchaganim uchun avtobusda uxlab qoldim va tushadigan joyimdan o‘tib ketdim.

tell sb off — koymoq informal ⇄

tell sb off (for sth / for doing sth) — kimnidir qilgan ishi uchun koymoq, tanbeh bermoq. To‘ldiruvchi — odam, shuning uchun u deyarli doim tell bilan off orasida turadi: tell him off, tell the boy off. Majhul nisbatda: be told off (koyilmoq). Ot: a telling-off. Fe’l: for + -ing (Unit 62).

  • The teacher told Jasur off for being late.O‘qituvchi Jasurni kechikkani uchun koyidi.
  • My mother told me off for coming home so late.Onam uyga juda kech qaytganim uchun meni koyidi.
  • I was told off for using my phone in class.Darsda telefon ishlatganim uchun meni koyishdi.

see sb off — kuzatib qo‘ymoq ⇄

see sb off — jo‘nab ketayotgan kishi bilan xayrlashish uchun vokzalga, aeroportga yoki bekatga borib, uni kuzatib qo‘ymoq. To‘ldiruvchi — odam, shuning uchun u odatda see bilan off orasida turadi (olmosh — doim).

  • Kamola is leaving for Seoul tomorrow, and her whole class is going to see her off.Kamola ertaga Seulga jo‘nab ketadi, butun sinfi uni kuzatib qo‘ymoqchi.
  • Her whole family came to the airport to see her off.Butun oilasi uni aeroportda kuzatib qo‘ygani keldi.
  • Who is going to see the guests off?Mehmonlarni kim kuzatib qo‘yadi?

O‘zbekchadagi kuzatib qo‘ymoq — inglizcha see off. Bu yerda see “ko‘rmoq” emas, “ko‘rib qo‘ymoq, kuzatmoq”.

rip sb off — aldamoq, qimmat narx olmoq informal ⇄

rip sb off — odamdan haddan tashqari ko‘p pul olmoq, uni aldamoq. To‘ldiruvchi — odam: rip him off, rip the tourists off. Majhul nisbatda: be ripped off. Ot: a rip-off (“bu — shilib olish!”).

  • The taxi driver ripped us off; he charged double.Taksichi bizni aldadi; ikki baravar pul oldi.
  • We paid far too much for those carpets. We were ripped off!U gilamlar uchun juda ko‘p pul to‘ladik. Bizni shilib olishdi!
  • Ten dollars for a cup of tea? What a rip-off!Bir piyola choy uchun o‘n dollar? Bu shunchaki shilib olish!

rip sth off (jismoniy ma’noda) — yirtib olmoq, sug‘urib olmoq: He ripped the label off. (U yorliqni yirtib oldi.) Bu ma’no norasmiy emas.

wear off — asta-sekin yo‘qolmoq ∅

wear off — dorining ta’siri, og‘riq, hayajon, yangilik hissi asta-sekin susayib, butunlay yo‘qolishi. To‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi; ega — yo‘qolayotgan narsa.

  • The effect of the medicine wore off after a few hours.Dorining ta’siri bir necha soatdan keyin tugadi.
  • The pain will wear off by tomorrow.Og‘riq ertagacha o‘tib ketadi.
  • At first she loved the city, but the novelty soon wore off.Avval u shaharni yaxshi ko‘rardi, lekin yangilik hissi tez orada so‘ndi.

finish off — oxirigacha tugatmoq ⇄

finish off — ishning yoki ovqatning qolgan qismini oxirigacha tugatmoq. Ajraladi: finish off the cake / finish the cake off / finish it off.

  • Who wants to finish off the plov? There’s only a little left.Palovni kim tugatadi? Ozgina qoldi.
  • I need one more hour to finish this report off.Bu hisobotni oxiriga yetkazish uchun menga yana bir soat kerak.

wear off bilan wear out ni aralashtirmang: wear out — kiyim yoki poyabzal eskirib yaroqsiz bo‘lmoq (My shoes have worn out). Ta’sir yoki og‘riq wear off qiladi.

go off — sut, go‘sht buzilmoq ∅ (BrE)

Ovqat yoki ichimlik (sut, baliq, go‘sht) eskirib, iste’mol qilib bo‘lmay qolsa — go off. Amerika inglizchasida: go bad, spoil. Sifat sifatida: This milk is off. go off ning “jiringlamoq, portlamoq, o‘chib qolmoq” ma’nolari — Unit 140 da.

  • Put the milk in the fridge or it will go off.Sutni muzlatgichga qo‘ying, aks holda buziladi.
  • The chicken has gone off; it smells terrible.Tovuq go‘shti buzilib qolibdi; hidi juda yomon.
  • Fish goes off quickly in hot weather.Issiq havoda baliq tez buziladi.

Sham yoki olov o‘chsa — go out (Unit 139): ❌ The milk has gone out.

walk off · run off · drive off — ketib qolmoq ∅

Harakat fe’li + off — joydan uzoqlashib ketmoq, ko‘pincha to‘satdan yoki hech narsa demasdan: walk off, run off, drive off, ride off. Qayerdan ketilgani aytilsa, off dan keyin ot keladi (bu yerda off — predlog): He walked off the stage.

  • Jasur got angry and walked off without saying goodbye.Jasurning jahli chiqib, xayrlashmasdan ketib qoldi.
  • The thief grabbed the bag and ran off.O‘g‘ri sumkani tortib olib, qochib ketdi.
  • Sardor got into his car and drove off.Sardor mashinasiga o‘tirib, jo‘nab ketdi.

Farqlang: drive off — joydan ketib qolmoq; drive on — to‘xtamay yo‘lni davom ettirmoq (B bo‘lim).

take time off — ta’til olmoq, dam olmoq ⇄

take time / a day / a week off — ishdan yoki o‘qishdan dam olish uchun ma’lum vaqt olmoq. Vaqt ifodasi take bilan off orasida turadi (take Friday off, take two days off). Ot: time off, a day off: Sunday is my day off. Kimdir sizga ruxsat bersa — have the afternoon off.

  • Sardor took two days off to help his parents move house.Sardor ota-onasiga ko‘chishda yordam berish uchun ikki kun ta’til oldi.
  • I’m taking Friday off.Men juma kuni ishga bormayman (dam olish kuni olaman).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Go on”, “carry on” va “keep on” — bir xilmi?

“Davom etmoq” ma’nosida ko‘pincha ha: She went on / carried on / kept on working. Farqlari: carry on Britaniya inglizchasida ko‘proq; keep on takrorlanishni yoki qat’iyatni ta’kidlaydi (He keeps on asking); go on yana “sodir bo‘lmoq” va “keyingisiga o‘tmoq” ma’nolariga ega. go on to do ni carry on yoki keep on bilan almashtirib bo‘lmaydi.

“Go on doing” va “go on to do” ni qanday ajrataman?

Savol bering: ish o‘zgardimi? O‘sha ish davom etsa — go on doing (went on talking). Bir ish tugab, boshqasi boshlansa — go on to do (finished school, went on to study medicine). Ikkinchisi ko‘pincha hayotdagi bosqichlar haqida.

“What’s going on?”, “What’s happening?” va “What happened?” ning farqi nima?

Birinchi ikkitasi hozir davom etayotgan voqeani so‘raydi (tashqarida shovqin, olomon). What happened? — tugagan voqea haqida (“Nima bo‘ldi?”): You’re wet. What happened? go on kundalik nutqqa xos.

“Get on” ning ma’nolarini qanday ajrataman?

Kontekstga qarang. Transport nomi bo‘lsa (bus, train) — chiqmoq (Unit 140). How are you getting on? — ishlar qalay. get on with + odam — til topishmoq. get on with + ish — ishni davom ettirmoq. Barchasida get on ajralmaydi.

“Hold on” va “wait” orasida farq bormi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil. wait — neytral va rasmiyroq; hold on — norasmiy, ayniqsa telefonda va suhbatda. hang on ham xuddi shunday norasmiy. Ushlab turish ma’nosi uchun hold on to + ot ishlatiladi: Hold on to my hand. (Qo‘limdan ushlang.)

“Tell sb off” qo‘pol so‘zmi? Rasmiy yozuvda ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

U qo‘pol emas, lekin norasmiy va asosan Britaniya inglizchasiga xos. Rasmiy yozuvda scold yoki reprimand afzal. Amerika inglizchasida kundalik nutqda scold yoki yell at ishlatiladi. “Tanbeh berilmoq” — be told off.

“Doze off”, “nod off”, “fall asleep” va “go to sleep” ni qanday ajrataman?

go to sleep — uxlashga yotmoq (ixtiyoriy). fall asleep — uxlab qolmoq. doze off — yengil va qisqa, istamasdan uxlab qolmoq. nod off — norasmiy doze off. Yakshanba kuni stulda mudrab qolsangiz — doze off; kechqurun yotsangiz — go to sleep.

“Take time off” bilan “take off” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. take time / a day / a week off — ishdan ta’til olmoq, vaqt ifodasi take bilan off orasida turadi. take off (Unit 140) esa kiyimni yechmoq yoki samolyotning ko‘tarilishi. Gapdagi vaqt ifodasi (Friday, two days) ma’noni ko‘rsatadi: I’m taking Friday off.

“Be off” desam, aynan nima demoqchi bo‘laman?

Kontekstga bog‘liq: I’m off — “ketyapman”; She’s off today — “bugun ishda yo‘q”; The milk is off — “sut buzilgan”; The match is off — “o‘yin bekor” (Unit 140). Gapdagi qolgan so‘zlar ma’noni ochiq ko‘rsatadi, shuning uchun adashish kamdan-kam.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • go on / carry on / keep on (doing) = davom etmoq. go on doing — o‘sha ishni davom ettirmoq; go on to do — keyingisiga o‘tmoq. What’s going on? = nima bo‘lyapti?
  • drive on, walk on, move on = to‘xtamay yurmoq, keyingi joyga yoki mavzuga o‘tmoq. hold on = kutmoq (norasmiy).
  • get on: ishlar qanday ketmoq · get on (well) with sb — til topishmoq · get on with sth — ishni davom ettirmoq. Ajralmaydi.
  • doze off / nod off / drop off = uxlab qolmoq; show off = maqtanmoq; tell sb off = koymoq; see sb off = kuzatib qo‘ymoq; rip sb off = aldamoq. Odam to‘ldiruvchisi o‘rtada: tell him off.
  • cut off: kesib olmoq · ta’minotni uzmoq · be cut off — aloqa uzilmoq, ajralib qolmoq. wear off = asta-sekin yo‘qolmoq (wear out emas).
  • finish off = oxirigacha tugatmoq; walk off / run off / drive off = ketib qolmoq. go off (sut) = buzilmoq; be off = ketmoq · ishda yo‘q · buzilgan · bekor (Unit 140). take time off = ta’til olmoq: take Friday off.
