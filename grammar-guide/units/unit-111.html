Still, yet and already, Any more / any longer / no longer

Still, yet, already; any more / any longer / no longer
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • still (hali ham, hamon) — ish davom etyapti, to‘xtamagan: Aziz is still asleep.
  • yet (hali) — ish hozirgacha bo‘lmagan, lekin kutilyapti; inkor va so‘roq gaplarda, odatda oxirida: He hasn’t called yet. Has he called yet?
  • already (allaqachon) — ish kutilganidan oldinroq bo‘ldi: He has already called.
  • not ... any more / not ... any longer / no longer (endi ... emas) — ilgari bor edi, endi to‘xtagan: He doesn’t play for our team any more. He no longer plays for our team.
  • To‘rttasining gapdagi o‘rni va inkor gapdagi farqi: still hasn’t va hasn’t ... yet.

Bu so‘zlarning hammasi bitta savolga javob beradi: «Hozirgi holat oldingisiga yoki biz kutganimizga nisbatan qanday?» Davom etyaptimi (still)? Hali bo‘lmadimi (yet)? Kutilganidan oldin bo‘ldimi (already)? Yoki to‘xtadimi (no longer)?

O‘zbek tilida «hali» so‘zi ikki xil ma’noda keladi: U hali uxlayapti (= still, davom etyapti) va U hali kelgani yo‘q (= not ... yet, hozirgacha bo‘lmadi). Inglizchada bu ikki ma’no uchun boshqa-boshqa so‘z bor. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha xato qiladi: He is yet sleeping ❌, He hasn’t come still ❌. «Allaqachon» = already, «endi ... emas / endi ... -maydi» = no longer / not ... any more.

Still — hali ham, hamon Still: something is continuing

Still ish yoki holat davom etayotganini, hali to‘xtamaganini yoki o‘zgarmaganini bildiradi. Ko‘pincha «kutganimizdan uzoqroq davom etyapti» yoki «hayratlanarli» degan ma’no ham bo‘ladi.

Ega+am / is / are+still+...
Ega+still+fe’l+...

Qoida: still o‘rta o‘rinda turadi (Unit 110): be dan keyin, oddiy fe’ldan oldin, birinchi yordamchi fe’ldan keyin. Gap oxirida ishlatilmaydi: He is asleep still ❌.

  • The sun is up, but Aziz is still asleep.Quyosh chiqib bo‘ldi, Aziz esa hamon uxlayapti.
  • My grandfather is eighty, but he still drives a car.Bobom sakson yoshda, lekin hali ham mashina haydaydi.
  • I’m still waiting for my friend.Men hamon do‘stimni kutyapman.
  • The shop is still open.Do‘kon hali ham ochiq.
  • Nodira still has her first phone.Nodira hali ham birinchi telefonidan foydalanadi.

Savol gapda

So‘roq gapda still egadan keyin turadi. Biz holat hamon shundaymi, deb so‘raymiz:

  • Do you still live in Namangan?Siz hali ham Namanganda yashaysizmi?
  • Is it still raining?Yomg‘ir hali ham yog‘yaptimi?
  • Are you still working on the report?Hisobot ustida hali ham ishlayapsizmi?

Inkor gapda: still hasn’t

Still inkor gapda ham bo‘ladi. U inkor yordamchi fe’ldan (hasn’t, isn’t, didn’t ...) oldin turadi va «bo‘lishi kerak edi, lekin hozirgacha bo‘lmadi» degan hayrat yoki sabrsizlikni bildiradi:

  • She still hasn’t answered my message.U hamon xabarimga javob bermadi.
  • The lift still isn’t working.Lift hamon ishlamayapti.
  • Sardor still doesn’t understand the rule.Sardor qoidani hamon tushunmayapti.

To‘liq not bilan (rasmiyroq yozuvda) still yordamchi fe’ldan keyin, not dan oldin turadi: She has still not answered. Ma’no bir xil. Qisqartma bo‘lsa — still uning oldida: She still hasn’t answered ✅, She hasn’t still answered ❌.

Yet — hali (hozirgacha) Yet: until now, in negative sentences and questions

Yet «hozirgacha» degan ma’noni beradi. Biz biror ish bo‘lishini kutayotganimizda yoki «bo‘ldimi?» deb so‘raganimizda ishlatamiz. Shuning uchun yet asosan inkor va so‘roq gaplarda keladi va odatda gap oxirida turadi.

Qisqa javob“Have you finished your homework?” “Not yet.”— Uy vazifasini tugatdingmi? — Hali yo‘q.
  • “Are we there yet?” “No, not yet.”— Yetib keldikmi? — Yo‘q, hali yo‘q.

Yet faqat present perfect bilan kelmaydi: be bilan ham, oddiy hozirgi zamon bilan ham ishlatiladi (Dinner isn’t ready yet. I don’t know yet.). Britaniya ingliz tilida present perfect (Have you eaten yet?) odatiy, Amerika ingliz tilida ko‘pincha past simple (Did you eat yet?). Bu haqda — Unit 7.

Yet oddiy darak gapda ishlatilmaydi: He has left yet ❌ → He has already left ✅. Darak gapda already yoki still kerak.

Hasn’t ... yet va still hasn’t: farqi

Ikkalasi ham «hozirgacha bo‘lmadi» degani. Farq — so‘zlovchining munosabatida:

Neytral yoki sabrsiz?
GapMa’nosiTarjima
The film hasn’t started yet.Neytral: tez orada boshlanadi, deb kutyapmiz.Film hali boshlanmadi.
The film still hasn’t started.Allaqachon boshlanishi kerak edi — hayron yoki sabrsizmiz.Film hamon boshlanmadi.

O‘zbekcha «hali ... -gani yo‘q / hali ... -madi» odatda not ... yet ga to‘g‘ri keladi: Hali ovqatlanmadim = I haven’t eaten yet. «Hamon / hanuz ... -madi» esa still hasn’t ga yaqin: U hamon qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi = He still hasn’t called.

Yet ning rasmiy o‘rni va boshqa ma’nolari

  • I haven’t yet decided what to wear. (rasmiyroq: yet o‘rtada)Nima kiyishni hali hal qilganim yo‘q.
  • We have yet to receive a reply. (= We haven’t received a reply yet.)Biz hali javob olganimiz yo‘q. (rasmiy)
  • The best is yet to come.Eng yaxshisi hali oldinda.

Already — allaqachon Already: earlier than expected

Already biror ish kutilganidan oldinroq yoki «o‘ylaganimizdan ertaroq» bo‘lganini bildiradi. Tarjimasi — «allaqachon».

Already still kabi o‘rta o‘rinda turadi (be dan keyin, oddiy fe’ldan oldin, yordamchidan keyin). Ta’kid yoki hayrat bo‘lsa, gapning oxirida ham kela oladi.

Ega+have / has+already+V3...
  • “Let’s buy Bekzod a ticket.” “No need — he’s already got one.”— Bekzodga chipta olib qo‘yaylik. — Shart emas, uning allaqachon chiptasi bor.
  • It’s only nine o’clock, and Malika has already finished her report.Soat atigi to‘qqiz, Malika esa hisobotini allaqachon tugatdi.
  • I’ve seen that film already. (oxirida)Men bu filmni allaqachon ko‘rganman.
  • We’re already late, so let’s take a taxi.Biz allaqachon kechikdik, taksida ketaylik.
  • When we reached the station, the train had already left.Stansiyaga yetib borganimizda, poyezd allaqachon ketib bo‘lgan edi.

Savol gapda already: hayrat

Savolda already «nahotki shunchalik tez?» degan hayratni bildiradi. Yet bilan so‘ralganda esa savol neytral:

Have you finished already?

Hayrat: juda tez tugatdingiz!

  • Nahotki allaqachon tugatdingiz?

Have you finished yet?

Neytral: tugatdingizmi, deb so‘rayapman.

  • Tugatdingizmi?

Already oddiy inkor darak gapda ishlatilmaydi: I haven’t finished already ❌ → I haven’t finished yet ✅. Inkorda «hali» = yet (yoki still).

Any more, any longer, no longer — endi ... emas Not ... any more / any longer, no longer: it has stopped

Ilgari shunday bo‘lgan, endi esa yo‘q: bu ma’noni uch shakl beradi. Ular still ning teskarisi: still — davom etyapti, bular — to‘xtadi.

Uch shakl: ma’no bir xil, o‘rni boshqa
ShaklO‘rniUslubMisol

not ... any longergap oxiridaoddiy nutqBekzod doesn’t play for our team any longer.

Ega+don’t / doesn’t / isn’t ...+fe’l ...+any more / any longer
Ega+no longer+fe’l (ijobiy shakl)+...

No longer ham o‘rta o‘rin qoidasiga (Unit 110) bo‘ysunadi: He no longer plays for our team (oddiy fe’ldan oldin), She is no longer a student (be dan keyin), I can no longer afford a car (yordamchidan keyin).

  • We don’t go to that café any more.Biz endi o‘sha kafega bormaymiz.
  • I can’t wait any longer.Men endi ortiq kuta olmayman.
  • She is no longer a student. She works at a bank.U endi talaba emas. Bankda ishlaydi.
  • They no longer make this model.Bu modelni endi ishlab chiqarishmaydi.
  • I can no longer afford a car.Endi mashina sotib olishga imkonim yo‘q.

Ikki marta inkor qilmang! no longer o‘zi inkor, shuning uchun fe’l ijobiy shaklda bo‘ladi: I no longer play tennis ✅. I don’t play tennis no longer ❌. Yoki bitta inkor + any more: I don’t play tennis any more ✅. no longer gap oxirida kelmaydi: He plays tennis no longer ❌.

O‘zbekcha «U endi bu yerda ishlamaydi» da inkor bitta (-ma-), «endi» esa «ilgari-ku bor edi» degan ma’noni qo‘shadi. Inglizchada ham inkor bitta bo‘lsin: yoki doesn’t ... any more, yoki no longer + ijobiy fe’l. O‘quvchilar ikkalasini qo‘shib yuboradi: doesn’t ... no longer ❌.

Any more — ikki xil ma’no

Any more: vaqt yoki miqdor
Ma’noMisolTarjima
Vaqt: endi (= no longer)I don’t drink coffee any more.Men endi qahva ichmayman.
Miqdor: qo‘shimcha, yanaI don’t want any more coffee.Menga yana qahva kerak emas.
Miqdor (so‘roq)Would you like any more tea?Yana choy ichasizmi?

Imlo: Britaniya ingliz tilida any more (ikki so‘z). Amerika ingliz tilida «endi» ma’nosida anymore (bir so‘z) yoziladi: I don’t live there anymore. «Yana, qo‘shimcha» ma’nosida Amerikada ham any more (ikki so‘z): any more tea.

No more bilan adashtirmang: no more miqdorga tegishli (There is no more bread — non qolmadi). Vaqt uchun — no longer: He is no longer here.

To‘rt so‘z yonma-yon Still, yet, already, no longer — which one?

Still, already va yet — o‘tgan zamonda ham

Bu so‘zlar faqat hozirgi zamonda emas, o‘tgan zamonda ham ishlatiladi. O‘rni o‘sha-o‘sha:

  • When I left the office at eight, Nodira was still at her desk.Soat sakkizda idoradan chiqqanimda, Nodira hamon ish stolida o‘tirgan edi.
  • By the time we arrived, they had already eaten.Biz yetib kelguncha, ular allaqachon ovqatlanib bo‘lgan edi.
  • The bell rang, but I hadn’t finished the test yet.Qo‘ng‘iroq chalindi, lekin men testni hali tugatmagan edim.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchada «hali» bitta, inglizchada still va yet — qanday ajrataman?

Gap ma’nosiga qarang. Ish davom etyaptimi (U hali uxlayapti)? — still. Ish hozirgacha bo‘lmadimi, lekin bo‘lishi kutilyaptimi (U hali kelgani yo‘q)? — not ... yet (yoki still hasn’t). Oddiy qoida: still — darak va so‘roqda «davom etish» uchun; yet — inkor va so‘roqda «hozirgacha bo‘lmadi» uchun.

«She still hasn’t called» va «She hasn’t called yet» farqi nima?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil — qo‘ng‘iroq hozirgacha bo‘lmagan. Lekin still hasn’t so‘zlovchining hayrati yoki sabrsizligini bildiradi («allaqachon qilishi kerak edi»), hasn’t ... yet esa neytral («hali qilmadi, kutyapmiz»).

Yet ni darak gapda ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Oddiy darak gapda yo‘q: He has left yet ❌. Faqat rasmiy yoki adabiy qoliplarda uchraydi: We have yet to decide (= hali qaror qilganimiz yo‘q), The best is yet to come (eng yaxshisi hali oldinda). Bular ham mazmunan «hali emas» degani.

«Have you finished already?» va «Have you finished yet?» — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ma’nosi biroz boshqa. yet — neytral savol («tugatdingizmi?»). already — hayrat: «nahotki shunchalik tez tugatdingiz?». Oddiy so‘raganda yet xavfsiz.

Any more, anymore va any longer — farqi bormi?

«Endi» ma’nosida any more va any longer bir xil: I don’t live there any more / any longer. Any longer ko‘pincha davomiylikka yaqin ma’noda ishlatiladi (I can’t wait any longer). Imlo: Britaniyada any more (ikki so‘z), Amerikada «endi» ma’nosida anymore (bir so‘z). «Yana, qo‘shimcha» ma’nosida har ikkala variantda any more (ikki so‘z).

No longer va not ... any more — qaysi birini tanlayman?

Ma’nosi bir xil. not ... any more — og‘zaki nutq uchun tabiiy. no longer — biroz rasmiyroq, yozuvda ko‘p uchraydi (xatlar, e’lonlar, maqolalar). Muhimi — qaysi birini tanlasangiz ham, inkor bitta bo‘lsin.

Amerika ingliz tilida ham «Have you eaten yet?» deyiladimi?

Deyiladi, lekin Amerikada past simple ham keng tarqalgan: Did you eat yet? I already ate. Britaniya ingliz tilida (va imtihonlarda) present perfect: Have you eaten yet? I’ve already eaten. Ikkala holatda ham yet va already ning o‘rni o‘zgarmaydi.

Gap boshidagi «Still,» va «Yet» nimani bildiradi?

Gap boshida Still, — «baribir, shunga qaramay»: It was raining. Still, we went out. yet bog‘lovchi sifatida — «lekin, shunga qaramay»: It was cheap, yet it was good. Bular «davom etish» yoki «hali emas» ma’nosidagi still / yet emas, boshqa ma’no.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • still — ish davom etyapti: o‘rta o‘rinda (is still asleep, still lives). Gap oxirida kelmaydi.
  • Inkor gapda still inkor yordamchidan oldin: He still hasn’t called (hayrat, sabrsizlik). Neytral: He hasn’t called yet.
  • yet — hozirgacha; faqat inkor va so‘roqda, odatda oxirida: Has it started yet? Not yet. Darak gapda yet yo‘q.
  • already — kutilganidan oldinroq: o‘rta o‘rinda yoki oxirida. Oddiy inkor gapda ishlatilmaydi (not yet).
  • not ... any more / any longer — gap oxirida; no longer — o‘rta o‘rinda, ijobiy fe’l bilan. Ikki inkor yo‘q.
  • «Hali» = still (davom etyapti) yoki yet (hozirgacha emas) — ma’noga qarab tanlang. «Allaqachon» = already; «endi ... emas» = no longer.
  • Britaniya ingliz tilida yet / already bilan present perfect; Amerikada past simple ham mumkin. any more (BrE) — anymore (AmE).
