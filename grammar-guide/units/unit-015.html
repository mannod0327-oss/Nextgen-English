Past perfect (I had done)

O‘tgan tugallangan zamon
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Past perfect (o‘tgan tugallangan zamon) qanday yasalishini: had + V3 (had gone, had seen, had finished).
  • Uni qachon ishlatishni: o‘tmishdagi bir voqeadan ham oldinroq bo‘lgan voqea haqida.
  • Nega had done — have done ning o‘tmishdagi shakli ekanini.
  • When I arrived, he left va When I arrived, he had left o‘rtasidagi katta ma’no farqini.
  • Past perfect kerak bo‘lmagan joylarni va o‘zbekcha -gan edi bilan bog‘liq tuzoqlarni.

Past perfect — “o‘tmishning o‘tmishi”. Hikoya o‘tmishda bo‘layotgan bo‘lsa, va biz o‘sha paytdan oldinroq bo‘lgan narsani aytmoqchi bo‘lsak, had + V3 ishlatamiz: When Malika arrived, Jasur had gone home. — Malika kelganida Jasur uyga ketib qolgan edi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no ko‘pincha -gan edi / -ib qolgan edi bilan beriladi: ketib qolgan edi, ko‘rgan edim, tugatgan edik. Bu juda qulay o‘xshashlik! Lekin ehtiyot bo‘ling: o‘zbekchada -gan edi boshqa ma’nolarda ham ishlatiladi (masalan, “Kecha sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan edim” — oddiy o‘tgan voqea). Inglizchada esa past perfect faqat boshqa o‘tgan voqeadan oldin bo‘lgan voqea uchun: I called you yesterday (had called ❌).

Shakli va asosiy ma’nosi had + past participle: When Malika arrived, Jasur had gone

O‘tgan shanba kuni Malika to‘yga bordi. Uning amakivachchasi Jasur ham o‘sha to‘yda edi, lekin ular bir-birini ko‘rishmadi: Jasur kechki soat 8:30 da ketdi, Malika esa soat 9:00 da keldi.

  • When Malika arrived at the wedding, Jasur wasn’t there any more. He had already gone home.Malika to‘yga kelganida Jasur u yerda yo‘q edi. U allaqachon uyga ketib qolgan edi.

Bu yerda hikoyaning boshlanish nuqtasi — Malika arrived (past simple). Undan oldinroq bo‘lgan voqea haqida gapirish uchun past perfect ishlatamiz: had gone.

Ega+had+V3 (past participle)
Past perfect: barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil — had
✅ Darak gap❌ Inkor gap❓ So‘roq gap
I / you / he / she / it / we / they had (’d) finished... had not (hadn’t) finishedHad you / she / they finished?
She’d already left.We hadn’t seen it before.Had he ever been there?

Qisqa javoblar: Had you met him before? — Yes, I had. / No, I hadn’t. Qisqartma ’d = had (I’d gone) yoki would (I’d go) bo‘lishi mumkin. Farqi: had dan keyin V3, would dan keyin oddiy fe’l keladi.

Misollar

  • When we came back from holiday, we found that a pipe had burst and the kitchen floor was wet.Ta’tildan qaytganimizda quvur yorilib ketgani va oshxona poli ho‘l ekanini ko‘rdik.
  • Dilnoza didn’t come to the museum with us because she’d already been there twice.Dilnoza biz bilan muzeyga bormadi, chunki u yerda allaqachon ikki marta bo‘lgan edi.
  • At first I thought I had locked the door, but then I realised I’d left it open.Avvaliga eshikni qulflaganman deb o‘yladim, keyin uni ochiq qoldirganimni tushundim.
  • Bekzod was very nervous on stage. He had never sung in front of so many people before.Bekzod sahnada juda hayajonlangan edi. U ilgari hech qachon bunchalik ko‘p odam oldida qo‘shiq aytmagan edi.
  • By the time we got to the station, the train had already left.Biz vokzalga yetib borganimizda poyezd allaqachon jo‘nab ketgan edi.

Qoida: O‘tmishdagi bir payt (hikoyaning “o‘sha payt”i) haqida gapirib, undan oldinroq bo‘lgan voqeani aytsak — had + V3. Past perfect uchun doim o‘tmishdagi tayanch nuqta kerak (aytilgan yoki tushunarli).

had done — have done ning o‘tmishdagi shakli had done is the past of have done

Present perfect (have done) — hozirgi paytgacha bo‘lgan voqealar haqida. Past perfect (had done) — xuddi shu, lekin “hozir” o‘rniga o‘tmishdagi bir payt olinadi. Hikoyani o‘tgan zamonga ko‘chirsangiz, have done → had done bo‘ladi:

Hozir ↔ o‘sha payt (o‘tmishda)
Present perfect (hozirga nisbatan)Past perfect (o‘tmishdagi paytga nisbatan)
This song sounds familiar. I’ve heard it before, but I can’t remember the singer’s name.The song sounded familiar. I’d heard it before, but I couldn’t remember the singer’s name.
Malika isn’t thirsty. She’s just drunk a glass of juice.Malika wasn’t thirsty. She’d just drunk a glass of juice.
The garden looks terrible. Nobody has watered it for weeks.The garden looked terrible. Nobody had watered it for weeks.
Sardor is tired. He’s worked hard all day.Sardor was tired. He’d worked hard all day.
  • I didn’t recognise Nodira at first. I hadn’t seen her for years.Nodirani avvaliga tanimadim. Uni yillar davomida ko‘rmagan edim.
  • It was the first time I had eaten sushi.Bu mening birinchi marta sushi yeyishim edi. (Hozirgi zamonda: It’s the first time I’ve eaten sushi.)
  • Kamola had lived in Fergana for ten years before she moved to Tashkent.Kamola Toshkentga ko‘chishdan oldin Farg‘onada o‘n yil yashagan edi.

Past simple va past perfect — farqi juda katta! left / had left

Bitta so‘z (had) gapning ma’nosini butunlay o‘zgartirishi mumkin. Solishtiring:

Past simple: avval men keldim, keyin u ketdi

  • A: Was Aziz still at the meeting when you got there?
    B: Yes, but he left a few minutes later.

— Siz borganingizda Aziz hali majlisda edimi? — Ha, lekin bir necha daqiqadan keyin ketdi.

Past perfect: avval u ketdi, keyin men keldim

  • A: Was Aziz still at the meeting when you got there?
    B: No, he’d already left.

— Siz borganingizda Aziz hali majlisda edimi? — Yo‘q, u allaqachon ketib qolgan edi.

Kinoteatr misoli

  • When I knocked on Kamola’s door, she wasn’t in. She was at the gym.Kamolaning eshigini taqillatganimda u uyda yo‘q edi. U sport zalida edi. (Men kelgan paytda o‘sha yerda.)
  • When I knocked on Kamola’s door, she had just got back. She had been at the gym.Kamolaning eshigini taqillatganimda u endigina qaytib kelgan edi. U sport zalida bo‘lgan edi. (Men kelishimdan oldin u yerda bo‘lgan.)
GapVoqealar tartibi
When I arrived, he left.1) men keldim → 2) u ketdi
When I arrived, he had left.1) u ketdi → 2) men keldim
When we got home, Mum cooked dinner.1) uyga keldik → 2) oyim ovqat pishirdi
When we got home, Mum had cooked dinner.1) oyim ovqat pishirdi → 2) biz uyga keldik (ovqat tayyor edi)

Past perfect qachon KERAK EMAS? When not to use the past perfect

1. Voqealarni tartib bilan aytganda

Agar voqealarni sodir bo‘lgan tartibda birin-ketin aytayotgan bo‘lsangiz, oddiy past simple yetarli:

  • I got up, had a shower and went to work. (not had had a shower)Turdim, dush qabul qildim va ishga ketdim.

2. after / before bilan tartib aniq bo‘lsa

after va before voqealar tartibini o‘zi ko‘rsatadi, shuning uchun past perfect ko‘pincha ixtiyoriy:

  • After Malika finished / had finished her homework, she went out.Malika uy vazifasini tugatgandan keyin tashqariga chiqdi. (Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.)
  • I left before the concert ended.Konsert tugashidan oldin ketdim.

3. “Juda uzoq o‘tmish” — past perfect sababi emas

Ko‘p o‘quvchilar voqea ancha oldin bo‘lgani uchun past perfect ishlatadi. Bu xato! Past perfect uzoqlikni emas, boshqa o‘tgan voqeadan oldin bo‘lishni bildiradi:

  • Ulugh Beg built his observatory in the 15th century. (not had built)Ulug‘bek o‘z rasadxonasini XV asrda qurdirgan.
  • My grandfather was born in 1940. (not had been born)Bobom 1940-yilda tug‘ilgan.

O‘zbekchada “Bobom 1940-yilda tug‘ilgan edi” yoki “Sizga kecha xat yozgan edim” deyishimiz mumkin — bu yerda edi hikoya ohangini beradi, “boshqa voqeadan oldin” degan ma’noni emas. Inglizchada bunday gaplar past simpleda: My grandfather was born in 1940. I wrote to you yesterday.

4. had had — xato emas

have fe’lining past perfect shakli — had had. Bu to‘g‘ri va oddiy shakl:

  • I wasn’t hungry. I’d had a big lunch.Och emas edim. Tushlikda ko‘p ovqat yegan edim.

Past perfect yana quyidagi mavzularda uchraydi: o‘zlashtirma gap (She said she had lost her keys — Unit 47), “if” va “wish” bilan o‘tmishdagi xayoliy holatlar (If I had known ... / I wish I had gone ... — Unit 40).

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I had called you yesterday, but you didn’t answer.I called you yesterday, but you didn’t answer.O‘zbekcha “qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan edim” — oddiy o‘tgan voqea. Undan oldingi boshqa voqea yo‘q.
My grandfather had been born in 1940.My grandfather was born in 1940.Uzoq o‘tmish — past perfect uchun sabab emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekcha “-gan edi” har doim past perfect bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Agar -gan edi “boshqa o‘tgan voqeadan oldin” ma’nosini bersa — ha: Men kelganimda u ketib qolgan edi = When I arrived, he had left. Lekin hikoya ohangi yoki muloyimlik uchun ishlatilsa (Kecha qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan edim) — inglizchada oddiy past simple: I called you yesterday. Har gal so‘rang: “Undan keyin bo‘lgan boshqa o‘tgan voqea bormi?”

Past perfect majburiymi? “After I finished...” ham to‘g‘rimi?

after, before bilan tartib aniq bo‘lgani uchun ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri: After I finished / had finished, I went home. Lekin when bilan farq muhim: When I arrived, he left (keyin ketdi) ≠ When I arrived, he had left (oldin ketgan). Ma’no o‘zgarsa — past perfect majburiy.

“I’d” — bu “I had” mi yoki “I would”?

Keyingi fe’lga qarang. I’d + V3 = I had: I’d seen it, I’d gone. I’d + oddiy fe’l = I would: I’d like a coffee, I’d go if I could. I’d better = I had better (Unit 35).

“had had” deyish to‘g‘rimi? Kulgili eshitiladi.

To‘liq to‘g‘ri. Birinchi had — yordamchi fe’l, ikkinchisi — have fe’lining V3 shakli: She’d had a terrible day. Og‘zaki nutqda odatda qisqartiriladi: I’d had, she’d had.

Past perfect ni hozirgi zamon bilan birga ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Odatda yo‘q. Past perfect uchun o‘tmishdagi tayanch nuqta kerak. Hozirgi paytga nisbatan esa present perfect ishlatiladi: I’ve seen this film before (hozir), I’d seen the film before (o‘sha paytda, o‘tmishda). ❌ I’m not hungry because I had eaten → I’m not hungry because I’ve eaten / I ate a lot at lunch.

“It was the first time I had ...” — nega bu yerda past perfect?

Hozirgi zamonda It’s the first time I’ve done this (present perfect) deymiz (Unit 8). Hikoya o‘tmishga ko‘chsa, ikkala qism ham bir qadam orqaga suriladi: It was the first time I had done this. Masalan: It was the first time Bekzod had sung on stage.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Fe’lni past perfect shaklida yozing (had + V3).

  1. Aziz wasn’t at home when I called. He (go) out.
  2. We couldn’t get into the hall because the concert (already / start).
  3. I wasn’t hungry. I (just / eat).
  4. Malika didn’t come to the party because nobody (invite) her.
  5. Bekzod was nervous at the interview. He (never / have) a job interview before.
  6. When I got to the shop, it (close).
  7. The car was very dirty. Sardor (not / wash) it for months.

2-mashq. Past simple yoki past perfect? To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. When I arrived at the party, Jasur (left / had left), so I didn’t see him.
  2. When I arrived at the party, Jasur (left / had left) almost immediately, so we only said hello.
  3. When we got to the cinema, the film (started / had started), so we missed the beginning.
  4. We sat down, and a minute later the film (started / had started).
  5. When I knocked on Kamola’s door, she wasn’t in. She (was / had been) at the gym.
  6. When I knocked on Kamola’s door, she had just got back. She (was / had been) at the gym.
  7. My grandfather (was / had been) born in 1940.
  8. I (called / had called) you yesterday, but you didn’t answer.
  9. I didn’t recognise Nodira because I (didn’t see / hadn’t seen) her for years.

3-mashq. Hikoyani o‘tgan zamonga o‘tkazing: present perfect → past perfect.

  1. Dilnoza isn’t thirsty. She’s just had some tea. → Dilnoza wasn’t thirsty. She .
  2. Sardor is very tired. He has worked all day. → Sardor was very tired. He .
  3. I don’t know this city. I’ve never been here before. → I didn’t know the city. I .
  4. The office is empty. Everyone has gone home. → The office was empty. Everyone .

4-mashq. Ikki gapni when va past perfect yordamida bitta gapga birlashtiring (gapni When ... bilan boshlang).

  1. I got to the station. The train left before that. →
  2. Dilnoza came home. Her children went to bed before that. →
  3. We arrived at the concert. It started before that. →
  4. Aziz phoned Malika. She left the office before that. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Men kelganimda Jasur allaqachon ketib qolgan edi. →
  2. Kecha sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan edim, lekin javob bermadingiz. →
  3. Bekzod ilgari hech qachon Samarqandda bo‘lmagan edi. →
  4. Biz och emas edik — hozirgina palov yegan edik. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: had + V3 — barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil. Inkor: hadn’t. So‘roq: Had you ...?
  • Ma’no: o‘tmishdagi bir paytdan oldinroq bo‘lgan voqea — “o‘tmishning o‘tmishi”.
  • had done = have done ning o‘tmishdagi shakli: The song sounded familiar — I’d heard it before.
  • When I arrived, he left (keyin ketdi) ≠ When I arrived, he had left (oldin ketib bo‘lgan).
  • Tartib bilan aytilgan voqealar va tarixiy faktlar uchun — past simple, past perfect emas.
  • O‘zbekcha -gan edi ning hammasi ham past perfect emas!
