Relative clauses 3: whose/whom/where

Nisbiy gaplar — 3: whose / whom / where
Relative clauses · Nisbiy ergash gaplar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • whose — egalikni bildiradigan nisbiy so‘z: the girl whose bag was stolen (= uning sumkasi). Whose ni who’s (= who is / who has) bilan adashtirmaslikni.
  • whom — who ning to‘ldiruvchi shakli (rasmiy), hamda predlog + whom / which: the man to whom I spoke.
  • where — joy bildiruvchi otdan keyin: the town where I grew up.
  • Vaqt va sabab: the day when we met, the reason why she left (ularda that yoki hech narsa ham bo‘ladi).
  • Oddiy nutq (the man I spoke to) va rasmiy uslub (the man to whom I spoke) farqini.

Unit 92 va Unit 93 da who / that / which ni o‘rgandingiz: ular nisbiy gapning egasi yoki to‘ldiruvchisi edi. Lekin ba’zan nisbiy gapda otning egaligi (“uning …i”), joyi yoki vaqti ifodalanishi kerak bo‘ladi. Ana shunda boshqa nisbiy so‘zlar ishlatiladi. Ularning hammasi oldingi otga “qaytadi” — o‘sha ot haqida qo‘shimcha aniqlik beradi:

O‘zbek tilida nisbiy olmosh yo‘q: ma’lumot otdan oldin, sifatdosh (-gan, -adigan) bilan beriladi. Eng qiziq jihati: o‘zbekchada uch xil ma’no bir xil shaklda yoziladi, inglizchada esa har xil so‘z kerak bo‘ladi:

biz sotib olgan uy → the house (that) we bought · biz yashagan uy → the house where we lived · egasi chet elda yashaydigan uy → the house whose owner lives abroad.

Demak, tarjima qilishdan oldin o‘zingizdan so‘rang: ot gapda nima bo‘lyapti — to‘ldiruvchimi (that / who / whom), joymi (where), vaqtmi (when) yoki egalikmi (whose)?

Whose: “…ning …i” Whose + noun

Whose egalikni bildiradi. U his / her / their / its o‘rnida keladi, lekin o‘zidan keyingi otni o‘zi bilan olib keladi. Ikki gapni birlashtirib ko‘ring:

Oxirgi ikki misolga e’tibor bering. Whose house we are renting — bunda whose dan keyin avval ot (house), keyin yangi ega va fe’l (we are renting) keladi: we are renting her house → whose house we are renting. Whose faqat odamlar uchun emas: narsalar, joylar va tashkilotlar uchun ham ishlatiladi (a village whose houses …, a company whose website …).

Who va whose ni solishtiring:

  • I know a girl who sings in a choir.Men xorda kuylaydigan bir qizni bilaman. (qizning o‘zi kuylaydi)
  • I know a girl whose mother sings in a choir.Men onasi xorda kuylaydigan bir qizni bilaman. (qizning onasi kuylaydi)

Qoida: whose dan keyin doim ot keladi (whose bag, whose brother). Ot oldida the / his / her kerak emas: the girl whose the bag ❌, the girl whose her bag ❌. Whose ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi, uni that bilan ham almashtirib bo‘lmaydi: the girl that her bag was stolen ❌.

O‘zbek tilida egalik qo‘shimcha bilan beriladi: sumkasi o‘g‘irlangan qiz, ota-onasi chet elda ishlaydigan bolalar. Inglizchada bu qo‘shimchaning o‘rnini whose egallaydi. O‘zbek o‘quvchilari ko‘pincha “uning” ni ikki marta aytib yuboradi: ❌ the girl that her bag was stolen (“qiz, uning sumkasi o‘g‘irlangan”). To‘g‘risi: the girl whose bag was stolen.

Whom va predlog + whom / which Whom and preposition + whom / which

Whom — who ning to‘ldiruvchi shakli (kimni? kimga?). Zamonaviy ingliz tilida u asosan rasmiy yozuvda uchraydi. Odam to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lganda (Unit 93) to‘rt yo‘l bor:

Whom hech qachon nisbiy gapning egasi bo‘lmaydi: the woman whom lives upstairs ❌ → the woman who lives upstairs ✅ (Unit 92). Qoida oddiy: whom dan keyin yangi ega (I, we, the manager …) keladi, fe’l emas.

Predlog + whom / which

Fe’l bilan birga keladigan predlog (talk to, work with, come from) ikki joyda turishi mumkin: nisbiy gapning oxirida (oddiy nutq) yoki nisbiy so‘zdan oldin (rasmiy uslub). Predlog oldinga o‘tganda whom (odam) yoki which (narsa) majburiy bo‘ladi:

Qoida: predlog + olmosh tuzilmasida olmosh faqat whom (odam) yoki which (narsa). Who, that yoki bo‘sh joy mumkin emas. Predlog oxirida bo‘lsa — who, that, which ixtiyoriy.

O‘zbekchada predlog yo‘q, uning vazifasini qo‘shimcha bajaradi: u bilan gaplashgan, uga aytgan, udan olgan. Shuning uchun ingliz tilida predlogni “qayerga qo‘yaman?” degan savol tug‘iladi. Oddiy javob: fe’l bilan birga, gap oxirida qoldiring (the man I spoke to). Rasmiy xat yoki insho yozsangiz — to whom shaklini ishlating.

Amerika ingliz tilida (AmE) kundalik nutqda whom deyarli aytilmaydi; rasmiy yozuvda esa saqlanadi, ayniqsa to whom, with whom kabi iboralarda. Britaniya ingliz tilida ham u asosan yozma uslub. Imtihonda the man (who) I met to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi.

Where: joy bildiruvchi otdan keyin Where after a place

Where joy bildiruvchi otdan keyin keladi (place, town, city, country, house, room, school, café, village, street …) va in / at / to which ma’nosini beradi. Where dan keyin to‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) keladi:

  • This is the café where Dilnoza works.Mana Dilnoza ishlaydigan kafe.
  • I’d like to visit the village where my grandfather was born.Bobom tug‘ilgan qishloqqa bormoqchiman.
  • Is there a shop near here where I can buy a SIM card?Shu yaqinda SIM karta sotib olsa bo‘ladigan do‘kon bormi?
  • They have moved to a town where there are no tall buildings.Ular baland binolar yo‘q shaharchaga ko‘chib ketishdi.
  • There are situations where it is better to say nothing.Hech narsa demagan ma’qul bo‘lgan holatlar ham bo‘ladi.

Where haqiqiy joydan tashqari, situation, case, point, stage kabi “joy” ma’noli mavhum otlardan keyin ham keladi (oxirgi misol).

Where nisbiy gapning egasi bo‘la olmaydi: the shop where sells fresh bread ❌ → the shop that sells fresh bread ✅. Tekshirish: where dan keyin darrov fe’l kelsa — xato; ega + fe’l kelsa — to‘g‘ri.

Somewhere, anywhere, nowhere

Bu so‘zlardan keyin ham where ishlatiladi. Kundalik nutqda where ko‘pincha tushiriladi:

  • I need somewhere (where) I can study in peace.Menga tinch o‘qiy oladigan joy kerak.
  • Is there anywhere (where) we can sit down?O‘tiradigan biror joy bormi?

When va why: vaqt va sabab The day when ..., the reason why ...

Vaqt: when

When vaqt bildiruvchi otdan keyin keladi (day, time, year, summer, moment, week …). Bunday gaplarda when o‘rniga that aytsa ham, hech narsa aytmasa ham bo‘ladi:

  • I’ll never forget the day (when / that) we climbed Chimgan.Chimyon tog‘iga chiqqan kunimizni hech qachon unutmayman.
  • Do you remember the summer (when / that) we went to Khiva?Xivaga borgan yozimiz esingdami?
  • There are days when I just want to stay at home.Ba’zi kunlar faqat uyda qolgim keladi.
  • It was the first time (that) I had seen snow.O‘shanda men birinchi marta qor ko‘rgan edim.

Rasmiy uslubda predlog + which ishlatiladi: the day on which we met, the year in which she was born.

Ehtiyot bo‘ling: agar vaqt oti nisbiy gapdagi fe’lning to‘ldiruvchisi bo‘lsa, when ishlatilmaydi — faqat that / which yoki hech narsa: the summer (that) we spent in Khiva ✅ (spend to‘ldiruvchi talab qiladi), the summer when we spent in Khiva ❌. Taqqoslang: the summer when we went to Khiva ✅ (went o‘zi to‘liq).

Sabab: why

The reason dan keyin why ishlatiladi; why o‘rniga that ham mumkin, yoki hech narsa aytilmaydi:

  • The reason (why) she left was never explained.Uning ketgan sababi hech qachon tushuntirilmadi.
  • I don’t know the reason why Jasur changed his mind.Jasur nima uchun fikrini o‘zgartirganini bilmayman.
  • That’s why I called you.Shuning uchun sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildim.

The reason because ❌ aytilmaydi. To‘g‘risi: The reason (why) I’m late is the traffic. yoki oddiygina: I’m late because of the traffic.

Qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi gaplarda The same words with commas

Bu darsda aytilgan whose, whom, where, when vergulli gaplarda ham ishlatiladi — u yerda ular ot haqida aniqlik emas, qo‘shimcha ma’lumot beradi:

  • Khiva, whose old town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracts many tourists.Eski shahri YUNESKOning Jahon merosi ob’ekti bo‘lgan Xiva ko‘plab sayyohlarni jalb qiladi.
  • We spent a week in Bukhara, where the old bazaars are still open.Biz Buxoroda bir hafta bo‘ldik; u yerda eski bozorlar hali ham ishlaydi.

Vergulli gaplarda that ishlatilmaydi, nisbiy so‘zni tushirib ham bo‘lmaydi. Buni Unit 95 da, predlog + whom / which va all of whom kabi tuzilmalarni esa Unit 96 da o‘rganamiz.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Whose faqat odamlar uchunmi? “A house whose roof is red” desam bo‘ladimi?

Bo‘ladi. Whose odamlar, hayvonlar, narsalar, joylar va tashkilotlar uchun ishlatiladi: a country whose economy is growing, a company whose website I visited. Ba’zi rasmiy matnlarda narsalar uchun the roof of which shakli ham uchraydi, lekin whose oddiy va to‘g‘ri.

Whose bilan who’s ni qanday adashtirmayman?

Ikkalasi bir xil o‘qiladi. Who’s ni who is yoki who has bilan almashtirib ko‘ring. Gap ma’noli bo‘lsa, who’s. Bo‘lmasa, whose yozing. Yana: whose dan keyin ot (whose bag), who’s dan keyin fe’l shakli yoki sifat keladi (who’s standing, who’s got, who’s tired).

Whom ni albatta ishlatishim kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Kundalik nutqda va ko‘p yozma matnlarda who, that yoki hech narsa ishlatiladi: the man I met. Whom majburiy bo‘ladigan yagona joy — predlog nisbiy so‘zdan oldin turganda: the man to whom I spoke. Bunda to who ❌.

Predlog gap oxirida turishi xato emasmi? Maktabda “bunday qilmang” deyishgan.

Nisbiy gapda — xato emas. The man I spoke to ingliz tilida juda tabiiy. “Gap predlog bilan tugamasin” degan eski qoida ingliz tili uchun majburiy emas (Unit 93). Rasmiy xat yoki insho yozsangiz, to whom shaklini ishlating — lekin oddiy nutqda the man I spoke to yaxshiroq eshitiladi.

“The hotel where we stayed” bilan “the hotel at which we stayed” bir xilmi?

Ma’no bir xil. Where — at / in / to which ning qisqa shakli, ya’ni where = predlog + which. Shuning uchun where dan keyin yana predlog aytilmaydi. Rasmiy uslubda at which ishlatiladi; kundalik nutqda where yoki the hotel we stayed at.

“The day when we met” da when o‘rniga that ishlatsam bo‘ladimi? Hech narsa aytmasam-chi?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri: the day when we met, the day that we met, the day we met. Kundalik nutqda oxirgisi ko‘p. Faqat bitta cheklov bor: vaqt oti fe’lning to‘ldiruvchisi bo‘lsa (the summer we spent in Khiva), when aytilmaydi — faqat that / which yoki hech narsa.

“The reason why” da why ni tushirsa bo‘ladimi?

Bo‘ladi: The reason I called you is simple. That ham mumkin: the reason that I called you. Lekin the reason because ❌ noto‘g‘ri. Sababni bayon qilishda because faqat fe’lli gap oldidan keladi: I called you because I need help.

O‘zbekchada “sumkasi o‘g‘irlangan qiz” deymiz. Inglizchada “the girl her bag was stolen” desa bo‘lmaydimi?

Bo‘lmaydi. O‘zbekchada sifatdosh oborot otdan oldin turadi va egalik qo‘shimchasi (-si) yetarli: sumkasi o‘g‘irlangan qiz. Inglizchada nisbiy gapni otga bog‘laydigan so‘z kerak (the girl her bag … ❌, the girl that her bag … ❌), va tartib teskari: avval ot, keyin whose + egalik qilingan ot + fe’l: the girl whose bag was stolen. Tarjima usuli: nisbiy gap ichida shu otga tegishli narsa egalik qo‘shimchasi bilan kelgan bo‘lsa (sumkasi, otasi, tomi), whose ishlating.

  1. The girl whose father is a doctor is my classmate.
  2. The city where we live is very big. / The city we live in is very big. / The city that we live in is very big. / The city in which we live is very big.
  3. The woman I spoke to is a doctor. / The woman I talked to is a doctor. / The woman who I spoke to is a doctor. / The woman to whom I spoke is a doctor. (who o‘rniga that ham to‘g‘ri)
  4. I will never forget the day we met. / I’ll never forget the day we met. / I will never forget the day when we met. / I will never forget the day that we met.
  5. That’s the reason why I was so busy. / That is the reason why I was so busy. / That’s the reason I was so busy. / That is why I was so busy. / That’s why I was so busy.
  6. Did you see the house whose roof is red? / Did you see the house whose roof was red? / Have you seen the house whose roof is red?

🧠 Esda tuting

  • whose + ot = egalik (his / her / their / its o‘rnida): the girl whose bag was stolen. Odamlar va narsalar uchun; who’s = who is / who has.
  • whom — to‘ldiruvchi, rasmiy. Egaga whom bo‘lmaydi. Kundalik nutqda: the man (who) I met.
  • Predlog nisbiy so‘zdan oldin kelsa, faqat whom (odam) yoki which (narsa): the man to whom I spoke. Oddiy nutqda: the man I spoke to.
  • where = in / at / to which, joy otlaridan keyin; yana predlog qo‘shilmaydi. Where dan keyin ega + fe’l.
  • when vaqt otlaridan, why “reason” dan keyin; ikkalasida ham that yoki hech narsa mumkin. The reason because ❌.
  • O‘zbekchada uch xil ma’no bir xil sifatdosh bilan aytiladi — inglizchada ot gapda nima ekaniga qarab (that / where / whose) tanlang.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 92 (who / that / which — ega), Unit 93 (to‘ldiruvchini tushirish, gap oxiridagi predlog), Unit 95 va Unit 96 (qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi gaplar), Unit 97 (-ing va -ed bo‘laklari), Unit 132 (fe’l + predlog).
