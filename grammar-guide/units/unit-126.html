To/at/in/into

To / at / in / into — harakat va joy
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • to — harakat yo‘nalishi (go to Khiva); at / in — turgan joy (be at school, live in Khiva).
  • been to: I’ve been to Bukhara — “Buxoroda bo‘lganman” deganda nega in emas, to kerak.
  • get to va arrive in / at (Unit 125 bilan bog‘liq): yetib borish.
  • home oldidan to qo‘yilmaydi: go home, get home.
  • into / out of (kirish va chiqish) hamda get in / out of a car, get on / off a bus.

Eng muhim savol: gap harakat haqidami (qayerga?) yoki joy haqidami (qayerda?). Harakat bo‘lsa — to (yoki into), joy bo‘lsa — at / in.

O‘zbek tilida bu farq qo‘shimchada ko‘rinadi: -ga (qayerga? — maktabga boraman) va -da (qayerda? — maktabda o‘qiyman), chiqish uchun -dan (mashinadan tushdim). Ingliz tilida ham shunday: go to school — be at school. Lekin ikkita tuzoq bor. Birinchisi: o‘zbekcha “Buxoroda bo‘lganman” (-da) inglizchada I’ve been to Bukhara bo‘ladi, in emas. Ikkinchisi: “uyga boraman” (-ga) inglizchada I’m going home — to yo‘q!

To — harakat; at / in — joy Movement and position

Go, come, travel, return, walk, run, drive, fly, move, take, send kabi harakat fe’llaridan keyin joy oldidan to keladi. “U yerda bo‘lish”, “yashash”, “ishlash”, “turish” esa joy ekanini bildiradi va at / in bilan aytiladi.

O‘zbekcha “Buxoroda bo‘lganman” — -da bilan, lekin inglizchada “borib kelganman” ma’nosida standart va eng tabiiy shakl — been to: ✅ I’ve been to Bukhara twice. Been in esa ko‘pincha biror joyda turish, yashash davomiyligi haqida keladi: I’ve been in Tashkent since Monday. Shuning uchun “-da” ni ko‘rib, avtomatik in qo‘ymang. Aniq o‘tgan vaqt aytilsa, oddiy o‘tgan zamon ishlatiladi: We went to Khiva last spring. (Present perfect emas. Unit 13 va Unit 14.)

Get in / out of, get on / off Cars, buses, trains, bikes

Transport vositasiga chiqish va tushish uchun ikki juft predlog bor:

get in(to) / get out of

Kichik, yopiq vositalar:

  • car, taxi, van, lorry
  • Bekzod got into the car and started the engine.
  • She got out of the taxi at the corner.

get on / get off

Jamoat transporti va minib olinadigan vositalar:

  • bus, train, tram, metro train, plane, ship
  • bicycle (bike), motorbike, horse
  • We got on the bus at Chorsu.
  • Jasur got off his bike and pushed it uphill.
  • The train stopped and everybody got off.Poyezd to‘xtadi va hamma tushdi.
  • Kamola got on her bike and rode to the park.Kamola velosipedga o‘tirib, bog‘ga ketdi.
  • I got into a taxi outside the station.Men vokzal oldida taksiga o‘tirdim.
  • After the long flight, we were happy to get off the plane.Uzoq parvozdan keyin samolyotdan tushganimizdan xursand bo‘ldik.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Go to the bank” va “go into the bank” — farqi bormi?

Ha. Go to the bank — bankka bormoq (maqsad: u yerda ish bitirish). Go into the bank — bank binosiga kirmoq (kirish harakatining o‘zi): He walked into the bank and asked for the manager. Odatiy rejalar haqida to, binoga kirish momentini ko‘rsatishda into.

“I’ve been to Khiva” va “I went to Khiva” ning farqi nima?

I’ve been to Khiva — hayotimda Xivaga borganman (qachon ekani muhim emas). I went to Khiva — aniq o‘tgan vaqtda bordim (last year, in May). Vaqt aytilsa — o‘tgan zamon; vaqt aytilmasa va tajriba haqida bo‘lsa — present perfect (Unit 13).

Nega “go home” deymiz, lekin “go to my home” ham to‘g‘rimi?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ammo qoida har xil. Home yolg‘iz kelsa — ravish, to kerak emas: go home. Oldida egalik olmoshi yoki sifat bo‘lsa, u ot: go to my home, go to a children’s home. Kundalik gapda go home eng tabiiy.

Qachon “get in” va qachon “get on” deyiladi?

Qoida shunday: kichik, yopiq vositalar (car, taxi) → in / out of; jamoat transporti va minib olinadigan narsalar (bus, train, plane, ship, bike, horse) → on / off. Sabab: avtobus yoki poyezdda yurib, turib yurish mumkin, mashinada esa o‘tiriladi.

“Arrive home” desa bo‘ladimi, “arrive at home” ham?

Standart shakl — arrive home (predlogsiz), xuddi get home, come home kabi. Arrive at home odatda aytilmaydi, arrive to home esa xato.

“Welcome to” va “welcome home” — nega farq?

Welcome to + ot (joy nomi): Welcome to Uzbekistan! Welcome to our school! Home ravish bo‘lgani uchun: Welcome home! (Uyingizga xush kelibsiz — qaytib kelganingiz bilan.)

“Go to school” bilan “go to the school” ni qanday ajrataman?

Artiklsiz go to school — o‘quvchi sifatida o‘qishga bormoq. The bilan — shunchaki o‘sha bino, boshqa maqsadda: I went to the school to talk to the director. Batafsil — Unit 74.

“Out of” o‘rniga “from” desam bo‘ladimi?

Ichidan chiqish va ichidan olish uchun out of: take a book out of the bag (take a book from the bag ham to‘g‘ri, lekin out of “ichidan” ma’nosini aniqroq beradi). From asosan boshqa ma’noda — boshlanish nuqtasi yoki manba: a letter from my uncle, a flight from Tashkent. Mashinadan tushganda ham get out of the car (get from emas).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Harakat (qayerga?) → to: go, come, travel, return, walk, fly to. Joy (qayerda?) → at / in: be at school, live in Khiva.
  • I’ve been to Khiva — borgan va qaytgan; he has gone to Khiva — hozir u yerda yoki yo‘lda.
  • get to + istalgan joy; arrive in (shahar, davlat) / arrive at (bino, tadbir) — arrive to ❌.
  • go / come / get home (to yo‘q), shuningdek here, there, abroad, upstairs oldidan ham to yo‘q.
  • into — ichiga kirish, out of — ichidan chiqish, in — ichida turish.
  • Car, taxi → get in(to) / get out of; bus, train, plane, ship, bike → get on / get off.
  • translate into English; go to school / bed / work (artiklsiz), lekin go to the bank.
