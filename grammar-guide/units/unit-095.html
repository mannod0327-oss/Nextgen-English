Relative clauses 4: extra information clauses (1)

Nisbiy gaplar — 4: qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi gaplar (1)
Relative clauses · Nisbiy ergash gaplar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Nisbiy gapning ikki turini: aniqlovchi (defining / identifying) va qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi (extra information / non-defining) gaplarni.
  • Qo‘shimcha axborot gapi vergul bilan ajratilishini: gap o‘rtasida — ikkita vergul, gap oxirida — bitta vergul.
  • Bunday gapda that ishlatilmasligini va who / which ni tushirib bo‘lmasligini.
  • Qo‘shimcha gapni olib tashlasak ham, asosiy ma’no saqlanib qolishini.
  • whose, whom, where, when shu gaplarda qanday ishlashini.
  • Vergul ma’noni o‘zgartirishini: My brother who lives in Bukhara … va My brother, who lives in Bukhara, …

Unit 92–94 da nisbiy gap otni aniqlar edi: the boy who sits behind me — qaysi bola? Endi boshqa holat bilan tanishamiz. Ba’zan ot allaqachon tanish bo‘ladi (ismi aytilgan yoki u bitta-yagona narsa), biz esa u haqida shunchaki qo‘shimcha ma’lumot beramiz. Ingliz tili bu ikki holatni vergul bilan farqlaydi.

O‘zbek tilida aniqlovchi nisbiy gap odatda sifatdosh (-gan, -adigan, -yotgan) bilan, otdan oldin keladi: orqamda o‘tiradigan bola (the boy who sits behind me). Vergulli gapning ma’nosini esa o‘zbek tilida ko‘pincha alohida gap yoki “u esa …”, “bu esa …” kabi so‘zlar bilan beramiz: Aziz orqamda o‘tiradi. U maktab jamoasida shaxmat o‘ynaydi. Ikki xavfli joy bor: (1) o‘quvchilar vergulli gapdan keyin ham Unit 92 dagi that ni qo‘yib yuboradi (❌ Aziz, that sits behind me); (2) ular inglizcha gapni o‘zbekchadagidek vergulsiz yozadi, natijada ma’no o‘zgarib qoladi (D bo‘lim).

Ikki xil nisbiy gap Two types of relative clause

Unit 92–94 da o‘rgangan gaplarimiz aniqlovchi nisbiy gaplar edi: ular “qaysi odam? qaysi narsa?” degan savolga javob berib, otning ma’nosini to‘ldirar edi. Ular gapning ajralmas qismi, shuning uchun vergulsiz yoziladi.

Ikkinchi tur — qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi nisbiy gap. Bunda ot allaqachon aniq, gap esa unga yana bir ma’lumot qo‘shadi. Bu gap xuddi qavs ichidagi izohga o‘xshaydi.

1. Aniqlovchi (defining)

  • “Qaysi?” degan savolga javob beradi.
  • Gapni olib tashlasak, ma’no chala qoladi.
  • Vergul yo‘q.
  • who / which / that.

2. Qo‘shimcha axborot (extra information)

  • Ot allaqachon tanish.
  • Gapni olib tashlasak ham, gap to‘liq.
  • Vergul bor.
  • who / which (that ❌).
  • The boy who sits behind me is called Aziz.Orqamda o‘tiradigan bolaning ismi Aziz. (aniqlovchi: sinfda ko‘p bola bor, bu gap qaysi ekanini aytadi)
  • Aziz, who sits behind me, plays chess for the school.Orqamda o‘tiradigan Aziz maktab jamoasida shaxmat o‘ynaydi. (qo‘shimcha: Azizni hamma taniydi)
  • The teacher who taught us grammar last year has moved to Samarkand.Bizga o‘tgan yili grammatikadan dars bergan o‘qituvchi Samarqandga ko‘chib o‘tdi. (qaysi o‘qituvchi?)
  • Mr Saidov, who taught us grammar last year, has moved to Samarkand.Bizga o‘tgan yili grammatikadan dars bergan Saidov ustoz Samarqandga ko‘chib o‘tdi. (ism allaqachon aniq)
  • I like cafés that stay open late.Men kechgacha ochiq turadigan kafelarni yoqtiraman.
  • Our local café, which stays open until midnight, is a great place to meet friends.Mahallamizdagi kafe yarim tungacha ochiq turadi — u do‘stlar bilan uchrashish uchun ajoyib joy.

Olib tashlash sinovi

Qaysi tur ekanini bilishning eng oson yo‘li: nisbiy gapni olib tashlab ko‘ring. Rasmda ikkala holat ko‘rsatilgan.

Qoida (sinov): nisbiy gapni olib tashlang. Agar tinglovchi “qaysi biri?” deb so‘rab qolsa — bu aniqlovchi gap, vergul yo‘q. Agar gap to‘liq qolsa va hech narsa yo‘qolmasa — bu qo‘shimcha axborot, vergul bor.

Qo‘shimcha axborot gapi odatda ma’lum bir odam yoki narsani bildiruvchi otlardan keyin keladi: ismlar (Aziz, Samarkand), yagona narsalar (the Registan, the capital of Uzbekistan) va my / this bilan boshlanuvchi otlar (my mother, this house). Umumiy otlar (a man, people, the girl) esa ko‘pincha aniqlovchi gap oladi.

Qo‘shimcha axborot gapining qoidalari Rules for extra information clauses

1. Vergul: o‘rtada ikkita, oxirida bitta

Qo‘shimcha gap asosiy gapdan vergul bilan ajratiladi. Gap o‘rtada bo‘lsa — uning oldida ham, keyin ham vergul turadi (ikkita). Gap oxirida bo‘lsa — oldiga bitta vergul, oxiriga nuqta qo‘yiladi.

Vergulning o‘rni
Qayerda turadi?VergullarMisol
Gap o‘rtasida (egadan keyin)ikkita: , …,Nodira, who sits next to me, is from Fergana.
Yonimda o‘tiradigan Nodira asli farg‘onalik.
Gap oxiridabitta: , … .I’d like you to meet Malika, who is my cousin.
Sizni amakivachcham Malika bilan tanishtirmoqchiman.
❌ Xatofaqat bitta (o‘rtada)Nodira, who sits next to me is from Fergana. ❌
Ikkinchi vergul unutilgan.

Qavs sinovi: qo‘shimcha gapni qavs ichiga olib ko‘ring: Nodira (who sits next to me) is from Fergana. Agar gap qavs bilan ham o‘qilsa, uni ikki vergul ichiga olish kerak.

2. That ishlatilmaydi

Aniqlovchi gapda odam uchun who yoki that, narsa uchun which yoki that mumkin edi (Unit 92). Vergulli gapda esa that ishlatib bo‘lmaydi: odam uchun faqat who, narsa uchun faqat which.

✅ To‘g‘ri

  • Sardor, who lives next door, is a pilot.
  • The Registan, which is in Samarkand, attracts many tourists.

❌ Noto‘g‘ri

  • Sardor, that lives next door, is a pilot.
  • The Registan, that is in Samarkand, attracts many tourists.
  • Sardor, who lives next door, is a pilot.Qo‘shnimiz Sardor uchuvchi.
  • The Registan, which is in Samarkand, attracts many tourists.Samarqanddagi Registon ko‘plab sayyohlarni jalb qiladi.

O‘zbek tilida aniqlovchi ham, qo‘shimcha ma’lumot ham bir xil sifatdosh bilan aytiladi: qo‘shni uyda yashaydigan uchuvchi (qaysi uchuvchi?) va qo‘shni uyda yashaydigan Sardor (Sardor haqida qo‘shimcha ma’lumot). Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar Unit 92 da o‘rgangan that ni “hamma nisbiy gap uchun” deb o‘ylab, vergul qo‘yilgan gapga ham qo‘shib yuboradi. Eslab qoling: vergul = who / which; that faqat vergulsiz (aniqlovchi) gapda.

Britaniya va Amerika: aniqlovchi gapda narsa uchun britaniya ingliz tilida that ham, which ham ishlatiladi. Amerika ingliz tilida (AmE) bunday gaplarda odatda that tanlanadi, which esa vergulli gaplarga qoldiriladi. Vergulli gapda ikkala variantda ham faqat which (narsa) va who (odam).

3. Who / which ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi

Aniqlovchi gapda to‘ldiruvchi vazifasidagi who / that / which ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin edi (Unit 93). Qo‘shimcha axborot gapida esa hech qachon:

Aniqlovchi gap va qo‘shimcha gap: olmoshni tushirish

Olmosh tushib qolsa, Mr Karimov, we met yesterday, is a lawyer gapi bir-biriga bog‘lanmagan ikkita gapga o‘xshab qoladi (yoki “Karimov janob, biz kecha uchrashdik” degan murojaatga). Shuning uchun vergulli gapda olmosh majburiy.

4. Who yoki whom?

Odam nisbiy gapning to‘ldiruvchisi bo‘lsa (kimni uchratdik?), ikki xil shakl bor:

Whom hech qachon nisbiy gapning egasi bo‘lmaydi: ega bo‘lsa — doim who (Mr Karimov, who works at a bank, …).

5. Fe’l va zamon

Fe’l oldingi otga moslashadi (Unit 92) va zamon erkin tanlanadi:

  • My cousins, who live in Namangan, are coming to the wedding.Namanganda yashaydigan amakivachchalarim to‘yga kelishadi.
  • Jasur, who has just passed his driving test, wants to buy a car.Haydovchilik imtihonidan hozirgina o‘tgan Jasur mashina sotib olmoqchi.

Whose, whom, where, when Whose, whom, where and when in extra information clauses

Unit 94 da o‘rgangan nisbiy so‘zlar vergulli gaplarda ham ishlaydi. Qoidalar o‘sha-o‘sha, faqat vergul bor, that yo‘q va nisbiy so‘z tushmaydi.

Vergulli gapda nisbiy so‘zlar
So‘zNimaning o‘rnida?Misol
whose + othis / her / their / its + otKamola, whose sister plays the violin, is in my class.
who / whomodam (to‘ldiruvchi)Dr Yusupova, whom I met at the conference, has written a book.

  • Kamola, whose sister plays the violin, is in my class.Opasi skripka chaladigan Kamola mening sinfimda o‘qiydi.
  • Dr Yusupova, whom I met at the conference, has written a book about teaching.Konferensiyada tanishgan Yusupova doktor o‘qitish haqida kitob yozgan.
  • My grandmother lives in Fergana, where I spent every summer as a child.Buvim Farg‘onada yashaydi; bolaligimda har yozni o‘sha yerda o‘tkazardim.
  • We usually travel in September, when the weather is cooler.Biz odatda sentyabrda sayohat qilamiz; o‘shanda havo salqinroq bo‘ladi.
  • Our school, whose library has over ten thousand books, is in the city centre.Kutubxonasida o‘n mingdan ortiq kitob bo‘lgan maktabimiz shahar markazida joylashgan.
  • Chorsu bazaar, which is in the old part of Tashkent, is busy every morning.Toshkentning eski qismidagi Chorsu bozori har kuni ertalab gavjum bo‘ladi.

Eslatma: where va when dan keyin to‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) keladi. Where ning o‘zi “o‘sha yerda” degan ma’noni beradi, shuning uchun there yoki predlog qo‘shilmaydi: Fergana, where my grandmother lives, … ✅ — Fergana, where my grandmother lives there, … ❌. Whose dan keyin esa doim ot keladi: Kamola, whose sister …

Predlog + whom / which tuzilmasi (Dilnoza, with whom I share a room, …) va all of whom, most of which kabi iboralar keyingi darsda — Unit 96 da.

Vergul ma’noni o‘zgartiradi Commas can change the meaning

Ba’zi gaplar vergul qo‘yilgan-qo‘yilmaganiga qarab butunlay boshqa ma’no beradi. Bu juda muhim: vergul faqat imlo emas, balki ma’noni ham belgilaydi.

Vergulsiz va vergulli: ma’no farqi
GapMa’nosi
My brother who lives in Bukhara is a doctor.Bir necha akam bor; Buxoroda yashaydigani shifokor. Gap qaysi akam ekanini aniqlayapti.
My brother, who lives in Bukhara, is a doctor.Bitta akam bor. U Buxoroda yashaydi — bu qo‘shimcha ma’lumot.
The roads which were flooded were closed.Faqat suv bosgan yo‘llar yopildi (boshqa yo‘llar ochiq).
The roads, which were flooded, were closed.Hamma yo‘llarni suv bosgan va hammasi yopilgan.

Qoida: ko‘plik otdan keyin vergul bo‘lsa — gap hammasi haqida ketadi; vergul bo‘lmasa — faqat ayrimlari (nisbiy gap bilan tavsiflangani) haqida. Birlik otda: vergulli gap “bu bitta-yagona” ekanini bildiradi (my brother, who …), vergulsiz gap esa “bir nechtasidan shu biri” ma’nosini beradi.

O‘zbek tilida Buxoroda yashaydigan akam shifokor gapi ikkala ma’noga ham mos keladi: qaysi ma’no ekanini kontekstdan, ya’ni “nechta akam bor?” degan savoldan bilamiz. Inglizchada bu farqni yozuvchi vergul bilan o‘zi belgilaydi. Shuning uchun yozishda o‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Mening bitta akam bormi yoki bir nechtami? Ularning hammasimi yoki ayrimlarimi?”

Yagona narsalarga vergulsiz gap qo‘shmang: Tashkent which is the capital of Uzbekistan … ❌. Toshkent bitta-yagona shahar, uni “aniqlash” shart emas, shuning uchun to‘g‘risi: Tashkent, which is the capital of Uzbekistan, has a metro system. ✅ Umumiy gaplarda esa aksincha, ortiqcha vergul xato ma’no beradi: Doctors, who work at night, need rest. — bu “hamma shifokor kechasi ishlaydi” degan noto‘g‘ri fikr; to‘g‘risi: Doctors who work at night need rest.

Yozma va og‘zaki nutq: qo‘shimcha axborot gaplari yozma matnlarda (maqola, xat, tavsif) juda ko‘p uchraydi. ❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Vergul kerak yoki kerak emasligini qanday bilaman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Tinglovchi bu odam yoki narsani allaqachon taniydimi?” Agar ha bo‘lsa (ism, yagona narsa, my mother), gap qo‘shimcha axborot — vergul kerak. Agar yo‘q bo‘lsa va nisbiy gap “qaysi biri?” degan savolga javob bersa, gap aniqlovchi — vergul kerak emas. Yana bir sinov: gapni olib tashlang. Ma’no chala qolsa — vergul yo‘q.

Nega vergulli gapda that bo‘lmaydi?

Bu ingliz tilining qoidasi: that o‘zi aniqlovchi (qaysi biri?) gaplar uchun ishlatiladi. Qo‘shimcha ma’lumot beradigan gapda esa who, which (va whose, whom, where, when) keladi. Amerika ingliz tilida bu farq yanada qat’iy: aniqlovchi gapda that, vergulli gapda which.

“Mr Karimov, who we met yesterday” dagi who ni tushirsam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Vergulli gapda nisbiy so‘z har doim aytiladi, hatto u to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lsa ham. Tushirish faqat aniqlovchi gaplarda mumkin: the man (who) we met yesterday (Unit 93).

Ismdan keyin aniqlovchi gap kela oladimi? Masalan, “the Aziz who sat next to me”?

Kela oladi, lekin kamdan-kam. Agar bir xil ismli bir necha odam bo‘lsa, “qaysi Aziz?” deb aniqlash kerak: the Aziz who sat next to me at school (maktabda yonimda o‘tirgan Aziz). Xuddi shunday: the Tashkent I remember from my childhood (bolaligimdan esimda qolgan Toshkent). Odatdagi holatda ismdan keyin vergulli gap keladi.

“My brother who lives in Bukhara is a doctor” gapi xatomi?

Yo‘q, gap grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri. Faqat u odatda bir nechta akam bor degan ma’noni beradi, chunki nisbiy gap “qaysi akam?” degan savolga javob beryapti (D bo‘lim). Agar sizning bitta akangiz bo‘lsa, vergul qo‘ying: My brother, who lives in Bukhara, is a doctor.

Og‘zaki nutqda vergul qanday eshitiladi?

Qo‘shimcha gap oldidan va keyin qisqa pauza qilinadi, gap esa biroz pastroq va tezroq aytiladi — xuddi qavs ichidagi izoh kabi. Aniqlovchi gap esa to‘xtovsiz, asosiy gapning bir qismidek aytiladi.

“Aziz, who is my cousin, lives in Bukhara” gapini qisqartirsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha. who is + ot o‘rniga shunchaki ot qo‘yish mumkin: Aziz, my cousin, lives in Bukhara. Bu juda keng tarqalgan qisqa shakl. -ing va -ed bilan qisqartirish (the girl sitting next to me) haqida Unit 97 da o‘rganasiz.

Qo‘shimcha axborot gapini ikkita gapga bo‘lib yozsam bo‘ladimi?

Bo‘ladi: Aziz sits behind me. He plays chess for the school. Ma’no bir xil. Nisbiy gap esa ikkita gapni bitta qilib bog‘laydi va yozma nutqni ravonroq qiladi. Faqat bir gapni boshqasiga vergul bilan ulab yubormang: Aziz sits behind me, he plays chess ❌.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gapni to‘g‘ri punktuatsiya bilan qayta yozing. Vergul kerak bo‘lmasa, “vergul kerak emas” deb yozing.

  1. Mrs Rahimova who teaches us English is very kind. →
  2. (Sinfda ko‘p o‘g‘il bola bor.) The boy who sits behind me is called Aziz. →
  3. We visited Bukhara which has many old madrasas. →
  4. (Javonda bir nechta chashka bor.) Don’t touch the cup that is on the top shelf. →
  5. (Mening bitta bobom bor.) My grandfather who is eighty-two walks to the park every morning. →
  6. Anyone who wants to join the club should sign up today. →
  7. Chorsu bazaar which is in the old part of Tashkent is always busy. →
  8. Dr Yusupova whom I met at the conference has written a book. →

2-mashq. Vergulli gap uchun to‘g‘ri so‘zni tanlang.

  1. Bekzod, (who / that) lives next door, is a pilot.
  2. The Registan, (which / that) is in Samarkand, is a famous square.
  3. Nodira, (whose / who’s) brother plays the violin, is in my class.
  4. We stayed in Bukhara, (where / which) we visited the old madrasas.
  5. Mr Aliyev, (whom / that) I met at the conference, is a lawyer.
  6. We usually travel in September, (when / that) the weather is cooler.
  7. My laptop, (which / what) I bought last year, is already slow.
  8. Dilnoza, (who / that) sits next to me, is from Namangan.

3-mashq. Ikkinchi gapni qo‘shimcha axborot gapiga aylantirib, qalin yozilgan otdan keyin (yoki gap oxirida) vergullar bilan joylashtiring.

  1. Malika is my neighbour. She works at the airport. →
  2. We visited Bukhara. We spent three days there. →
  3. My laptop is on the desk. I bought it last year. →
  4. Kamola is a violinist. Her sister lives in Fergana. →
  5. Today I phoned Mr Saidov. I met him at the conference. →
  6. We usually travel in September. The weather is cooler then. →

4-mashq. Vergul ma’noni o‘zgartiradi. To‘g‘ri ma’noni tanlang: a yoki b.

  1. My sister who lives in Fergana is a nurse. (a) Mening faqat bitta singlim bor. (b) Mening bir nechta singlim bor.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Aniqlovchi gap otni aniqlaydi (“qaysi biri?”), vergulsiz. Qo‘shimcha axborot gapi tanish otga ma’lumot qo‘shadi, vergul bilan.
  • Gap o‘rtada — ikkita vergul (Nodira, who sits next to me, is …); gap oxirida — bitta vergul.
  • Vergulli gapda that ❌: odam — who, narsa — which.
  • Vergulli gapda who / which / whom ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi (aniqlovchi gapda to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lsa tushadi).
  • Gapni olib tashlasak ham ma’no saqlansa — qo‘shimcha axborot. Whose, whom, where, when ham shu qoidaga amal qiladi.
  • Vergul ma’noni o‘zgartiradi: My brother who lives … (bir nechta akam bor) va My brother, who lives … (bitta akam bor).
  • Ismlar va yagona narsalardan keyin odatda vergulli gap keladi: Tashkent, which is the capital …
