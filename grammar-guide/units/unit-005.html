5

Past simple (I did)

O‘tgan oddiy zamon
Present and past · Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Past simple (oddiy o‘tgan zamon) nima va u qachon ishlatiladi: o‘tmishda tugagan ish.
  • To‘g‘ri fe’llar (-ed: worked, studied, stopped) va noto‘g‘ri fe’llar (went, saw, wrote).
  • did / didn’t yordamida so‘roq va inkor gap tuzishni: Did you go? I didn’t go.
  • be fe’lining o‘tgan zamoni: was / were — bu yerda did ishlatilmaydi.
  • Vaqt iboralari: yesterday, last week, two years ago, in 2010.

Past simple — o‘tmishda bo‘lib o‘tgan va tugagan ish yoki holat. Bu ish qachon bo‘lganini ko‘pincha aytamiz yoki kontekstdan bilamiz (kecha, o‘tgan yili, bolaligimda ...).

O‘zbek tilidagi -di shakli (bordi, o‘qidi, ko‘rdi) past simple ga juda yaqin: Kecha kinoga bordim = I went to the cinema yesterday. Lekin o‘zbek tilida so‘roq va inkor uchun fe’lning o‘ziga qo‘shimcha qo‘shamiz (bordingmi? bormadim), ingliz tilida esa alohida yordamchi so‘z kerak: did / didn’t. Va eng muhimi: did dan keyin fe’l o‘tgan zamon shaklida bo‘lmaydi: Did you go? ✅ — Did you went? ❌

Past simple nima? An example: the life of a famous poet

Mana buyuk shoir haqida qisqa matn. Qalin yozilgan fe’llarga e’tibor bering:

  • Alisher Navoi was a great poet and thinker. He was born in Herat in 1441. He studied in Herat, Mashhad and Samarkand, and he wrote in two languages, Turkic and Persian. He also built schools and a hospital. He died in Herat in 1501.Alisher Navoiy buyuk shoir va mutafakkir edi. U 1441-yilda Hirotda tug‘ilgan. U Hirot, Mashhad va Samarqandda tahsil olgan va ikki tilda — turkiy va forsiy tillarda ijod qilgan. U madrasalar va shifoxona ham qurdirgan. U 1501-yilda Hirotda vafot etgan.

was, was born, studied, wrote, built, died — bularning hammasi past simple. Hammasi o‘tmishda bo‘lib o‘tgan va tugagan.

Qoida: Past simple — o‘tmishda tugagan ish uchun. Ish uzoq o‘tmishda bo‘lishi ham (in 1441), yaqinda bo‘lishi ham (five minutes ago) mumkin — muhimi, u tugagan va o‘tmishdagi aniq vaqtga tegishli.

To‘g‘ri va noto‘g‘ri fe’llar Regular (-ed) and irregular verbs

To‘g‘ri fe’llar: -ed

Ko‘p fe’llarning o‘tgan zamon shakli -ed bilan tugaydi. Bu shakl barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil (I worked, she worked, they worked):

  • My sister moved to Tashkent in 2020. Before that, she lived in Termez.Opam 2020-yilda Toshkentga ko‘chib o‘tdi. Undan oldin u Termizda yashagan.
  • We invited all our relatives to the wedding.To‘yga barcha qarindoshlarimizni taklif qildik.
  • Jasur failed his driving test twice, but he passed the third time.Jasur haydovchilik imtihonidan ikki marta yiqildi, lekin uchinchi safar topshirdi.
  • A police officer stopped me and checked my documents.Politsiyachi meni to‘xtatib, hujjatlarimni tekshirdi.

Noto‘g‘ri fe’llar

Ko‘p muhim fe’llar noto‘g‘ri (irregular): ularning o‘tgan zamoni -ed bilan tugamaydi va har birini yodlash kerak:

  • write → wrote: Navoi wrote Khamsa in the 1480s.Navoiy “Xamsa”ni 1480-yillarda yozgan.
  • see → saw: We saw some beautiful old mosques in Khiva.Xivada go‘zal qadimiy masjidlarni ko‘rdik.
  • go → went: Last Friday Malika went to the theatre with her parents.O‘tgan juma Malika ota-onasi bilan teatrga bordi.
  • put → put: It started to rain, so we put up our umbrellas.Yomg‘ir yog‘a boshladi, shuning uchun soyabonlarimizni ochdik.
  • lose → lost, find → found: I lost my keys, but Aziz found them in his car.Kalitlarimni yo‘qotib qo‘ydim, lekin Aziz ularni mashinasidan topdi.

Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarning to‘liq ro‘yxati kitobning Appendix 1 qismida. Har kuni 5–10 tadan yodlang va ular bilan o‘zingiz haqida gap tuzing: Yesterday I woke up at 7, had breakfast and took the metro.

So‘roq va inkor: did / didn’t Questions and negatives

So‘roq va inkor gaplarda did / didn’t + fe’lning lug‘at shakli (infinitive: go, see, enjoy) ishlatiladi. O‘tgan zamon belgisi faqat did da bo‘ladi — fe’lning o‘zi o‘zgarmaydi.

Qoida: did dan keyin — doim V1. Did you go? ✅ Did you went? ❌ — I didn’t see. ✅ I didn’t saw. ❌

do — asosiy fe’l sifatida

“Qilmoq” ma’nosidagi do ham shu qoidaga bo‘ysunadi, shuning uchun gapda ikkita do bo‘ladi (did ... do, didn’t do):

  • What did you do on Sunday?Yakshanba kuni nima qildingiz? (What did you on Sunday ❌)
  • We didn’t do anything special. We just stayed at home.Hech qanday alohida ish qilmadik. Shunchaki uyda qoldik. (We didn’t anything ❌)

Qoida: was / were bilan did ishlatilmaydi. was / were o‘zi so‘roqda egadan oldinga chiqadi va inkorda not oladi: Were you tired? ✅ — Did you be tired? ❌; I wasn’t hungry. ✅ — I didn’t be hungry. ❌

  • The film was boring, so we left early.Film zerikarli edi, shuning uchun erta chiqib ketdik.
  • The shops weren’t open because it was a holiday.Bayram bo‘lgani uchun do‘konlar yopiq edi.
  • “Where were you yesterday?” “I was at my grandmother’s.”— Kecha qayerda eding? — Buvimnikida edim.
  • There weren’t many people at the concert.Konsertda odam ko‘p emas edi.
  • Were you at school yesterday, or did you stay at home?Kecha maktabda edingmi yoki uyda qoldingmi? (were — be bilan, did — oddiy fe’l bilan)

“Tug‘ilmoq”: I was born

  • I was born in Andijan in 2006.Men 2006-yilda Andijonda tug‘ilganman. (I born ❌, I am born ❌)
  • Where were your parents born?Ota-onangiz qayerda tug‘ilgan?

O‘zbekchada “tug‘ildim” — bitta fe’l. Inglizchada esa bu be born (majhul nisbat): I was born. Ko‘p o‘quvchilar I born yoki I am born deb xato qiladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchadagi “bordi” va “borgan” — ikkalasi ham past simple mi?

Ko‘pincha ha. Kecha bordim va 1990-yilda borganman — ikkalasi ham aniq o‘tgan vaqt bilan, demak past simple: I went. Lekin “-gan” ba’zan present perfect ga to‘g‘ri keladi: Samarqandga borganmisiz? (umuman hayotingizda) = Have you been to Samarkand? (Unit 8). Farqi: aniq o‘tgan vaqt bormi yoki yo‘qmi.

Past simple faqat uzoq o‘tmish uchunmi?

Yo‘q. Besh daqiqa oldin bo‘lgan ish ham past simple: I called you five minutes ago. Muhimi — ish tugagan va o‘tmishdagi aniq vaqtga tegishli.

“Who called you?” — nega bu yerda did yo‘q?

Chunki who — gapning egasi (kim qo‘ng‘iroq qildi?). Savol so‘zi ega bo‘lsa, did ishlatilmaydi: Who called you? Lekin Who did you call? — “Sen kimga qo‘ng‘iroq qilding?” — bu yerda ega you, who esa to‘ldiruvchi. Batafsil — Unit 49.

Qachon was/were, qachon did ishlataman?

Gapda boshqa fe’l bo‘lmasa va “edi / bo‘ldi” ma’nosi bo‘lsa — was / were: I was tired. Were you at home? Gapda oddiy fe’l bo‘lsa — so‘roq va inkorda did: Did you sleep well? I didn’t sleep.

Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarni qanday tez yodlash mumkin?

Ularni guruhlab yodlang (yuqoridagi jadvaldagi kabi: -ought guruhi, i → a guruhi ...). Har kuni bir nechtasini olib, kechagi kuningiz haqida gap tuzing. Eng muhim 30–40 tasi (go, see, have, get, make, take, come, say, tell, give, find, think ...) birinchi navbatda.

O‘tmishdagi odatlar uchun ham past simple ishlatiladimi?

Ha: When I was a child, I played football every day. Bunda used to ham ishlatiladi: I used to play football every day. (Unit 18)

“read” ning o‘tgan zamoni qanday?

Yozilishi bir xil — read, lekin talaffuzi boshqacha: hozirgi zamonda /riːd/, o‘tgan zamonda /red/ (xuddi “red” — qizil so‘zi kabi). I read (/red/) this book last year.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Past simple — o‘tmishda tugagan ish yoki holat: I went, she wrote, they lived.
  • To‘g‘ri fe’llar: -ed (imloga e’tibor: studied, stopped, played). Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarni yodlash kerak.
  • Shakl barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil (faqat be: was / were).
  • So‘roq: Did + ega + V1? Inkor: didn’t + V1. did dan keyin hech qachon went / saw emas!
  • was / were bilan did ishlatilmaydi: Were you ...? I wasn’t ...
  • I was born ✅ (I born ❌). two years ago ✅ (before two years ❌). last night ✅.
