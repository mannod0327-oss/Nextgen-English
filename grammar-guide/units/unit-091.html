Each and every

Each va every
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • each va every farqini: each — narsalarni birma-bir, alohida-alohida ko‘rish; every — guruhni butunlay, istisnosiz (hammasi) ko‘rish.
  • Ikkalasidan keyin ham birlikdagi ot va birlikdagi fe’l keladi: each student has, every student has.
  • each of + the / my / them (ko‘plik) va every one of; nega every of deb bo‘lmaydi.
  • Vaqt iboralarida every: every day, every other week, every two hours, almost every.
  • everyone, everybody, everything — birlik fe’l bilan; every day va everyday farqi.

Each ham, every ham o‘zbekchaga “har bir” deb tarjima qilinadi va ko‘p gapda ikkalasini ham aytish mumkin. Lekin ular orasida fikr farqi bor: each deganda biz guruhdagi narsalarni bittama-bitta ko‘z oldimizga keltiramiz; every deganda esa guruhni butunligicha, “bittasi ham chetda qolmaydi” degan ma’noda o‘ylaymiz (all ga yaqin).

O‘zbekchada “har bir” dan keyin ot birlikda turadi: har bir talaba, har bir uy. Inglizchada ham xuddi shunday: each student, every house — demak, bu yerda o‘zbek tili sizga yordam beradi. Xatolar boshqa joyda chiqadi: (1) “hamma bolalar” deb o‘ylab every children deyish; (2) “ularning har biri” ni every of them deb tarjima qilish — to‘g‘risi each of them; (3) “har ikki soatda” ni each two hours deyish — to‘g‘risi every two hours.

Each va every: ma’no farqi Each and every: the difference in meaning

Ba’zi gaplarda each ham, every ham to‘g‘ri va ma’no deyarli o‘zgarmaydi:

  • Each / Every time I visit Samarkand, I walk around the Registan.Samarqandga har borganimda Registon atrofida sayr qilaman.
  • Each / Every classroom in the new school has a whiteboard.Yangi maktabdagi har bir sinfxonada oq doska bor.

Lekin ko‘p gapda farq seziladi. Quyidagi rasmga qarang:

Qoida: narsalarni birma-bir, alohida ko‘rsangiz — each. Butun guruhni hammasi birga (istisnosiz) ko‘rsangiz — every. Ikki narsa haqida gapirsangiz — each (yoki both, Unit 89), every emas.

Biror ish qanchalik tez-tez takrorlanishini aytganda faqat every ishlatiladi: every day, every Friday, every ten minutes (each ten minutes ❌). Batafsil — quyidagi ★ bo‘limida.

Ma’no o‘zgarmaydigan gaplarda (masalan, each time / every time) ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Boshqa gaplarda javobni gapdagi so‘zlar ko‘rsatib turadi: separately, one by one bo‘lsa — each; all, without exception degan fikr bo‘lsa — every.

2. Each of + ko‘plik: the / my / these / them

Each of dan keyin aniqlovchi (the, my, these, our …) yoki olmosh (us, you, them) bo‘lishi shart, so‘ng ko‘plikdagi ot keladi. Lekin fe’l birlikda turadi, chunki gap “har bir (bittasi)” haqida.

Each of+the / my / these+ko‘plik ot+birlik fe’l
  • Each of the rooms has a balcony.Xonalarning har birida balkon bor.
  • Each of us has a different opinion.Har birimizning fikri har xil.
  • I have read each of these books twice.Bu kitoblarning har birini ikki marta o‘qiganman.
  • Each of them is good at something different.Ularning har biri boshqa-boshqa narsada kuchli.

Each of dan keyin aniqlovchi shart: each of the students ✅, each of students ❌. Rasmiy yozuvda va imtihonda fe’l birlikda: Each of the boys has … Og‘zaki nutqda ba’zan each of them have deb ham eshitasiz, lekin yozishda buni ishlatmang.

3. Each — yolg‘iz

Ot oldin aytilgan bo‘lsa, each yolg‘iz ham keladi:

  • Bekzod has three dogs. Each has its own name.Bekzodning uchta iti bor. Har birining o‘z ismi bor.
  • We tried four phones. Each was too expensive.To‘rtta telefonni ko‘rdik. Har biri juda qimmat edi.

Each o‘rniga each one deyish ham mumkin: Each one was too expensive. Ma’no bir xil.

4. Every + birlik ot + birlik fe’l; every yolg‘iz kelmaydi

  • Every seat in the hall was taken.Zaldagi har bir o‘rindiq band edi.
  • Every student needs a library card.Har bir talabaga kutubxona kartasi kerak.
  • Every one of the guests has arrived.Mehmonlarning bittasi ham qolmay hammasi yetib keldi.

Qoida: every hech qachon yolg‘iz yoki of bilan to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri kelmaydi: every of them ❌. Ot tushib qolsa, every one (har bir) deyiladi: every one of them ✅, yoki each of them ✅.

Everyone va every one Everyone (people only) and every one (people or things)

Bu so‘zlar bir so‘z bo‘lib yoziladi va birlik fe’l bilan keladi. Everyone va everybody bir xil, everything — hamma narsa, everywhere — hamma joyda.

  • Everyone knows Malika.Malikani hamma taniydi.
  • Everything is ready for the party.Ziyofat uchun hamma narsa tayyor.
  • Everybody has their own opinion.Hammaning o‘z fikri bor.

Everyone (bir so‘z) — “hamma odam”. Every one (ikki so‘z) — “har bir bittasi” (odam ham, narsa ham), ko‘pincha of bilan: every one of the letters (xatlarning har bir bittasi). Ikkalasining yozilishi boshqa!

  • Everyone enjoyed the concert.Konsert hammaga yoqdi.
  • There were twelve questions in the test, and Kamola answered every one.Testda o‘n ikkita savol bor edi va Kamola har biriga javob berdi.

Every: boshqa qo‘llanishlar almost every, every two hours, every reason

1. Almost every, not every

“Deyarli hamma” — almost / nearly / practically every; “almost each” deb aytilmaydi. “Hamma ham … emas” ma’nosi uchun not every ishlatiladi:

  • Almost every shop in this street is closed on Sundays.Bu ko‘chadagi deyarli hamma do‘kon yakshanba kuni yopiq.
  • Not every student passed the exam.Hamma talaba ham imtihondan o‘tmadi (ba’zilari o‘tdi).
  • Not everybody likes spicy food.Hamma ham achchiq ovqatni yoqtirmaydi.

2. Every + vaqt: takrorlanish

Biror ish muntazam takrorlanganda every ishlatiladi. Raqam kelganda every + son + ko‘plikdagi ot: every two hours. Bu yerda each ishlatilmaydi.

Each, every va all: taqqoslash Each, every and all compared

Uchala so‘z ham “hamma” ma’nosiga yaqin, lekin grammatik tuzilmasi farq qiladi. All — ko‘plikdagi (yoki sanalmaydigan) ot bilan; each / every — faqat birlikdagi ot bilan (all haqida Unit 88 va Unit 90 ga qarang).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Each va every farqini qanday eslab qolaman?

Ikki so‘zni eslang: each = one by one (birma-bir, alohida), every = all together (hammasi birga). Yana bir sinov: gapdagi so‘zni “hamma” (all) bilan almashtirib ko‘ring. Ma’no saqlansa — every. Agar fikr “har bir narsa alohida” bo‘lsa — each.

Every dan keyin ko‘plikdagi ot hech qachon kelmaydimi?

Kelmaydi, bitta istisno bor: every + son + ko‘plik ot vaqt yoki masofa iboralarida: every two hours, every ten minutes, every few days. Bu yerda ko‘plik “son”dan keyin kelyapti, every ning o‘zi esa baribir takrorlanish oralig‘ini bildiryapti. Every children ❌ — every child ✅.

“Each of the girls has” — fe’l birlikdami yoki ko‘plikdami?

Standart yozma ingliz tilida birlik: Each of the girls has a camera. Og‘zaki nutqda ba’zan ko‘plik ham eshitiladi, lekin imtihonda va yozuvda birlik yozing. Faqat The girls each have a camera tuzilmasida fe’l ko‘plikda bo‘ladi, chunki ega — the girls.

Everyone va every one — farqi nima?

Everyone (bir so‘z) = everybody, “hamma odam”: Everyone is here. Every one (ikki so‘z) — “har bir bittasi” va odatda of bilan keladi: every one of the players. Bu ikkinchi shaklni odam va narsa uchun ishlatish mumkin.

“Every other day” aniq nima degani?

“Kunora, bir kun oralatib”: bugun ha, ertaga yo‘q, indinga ha. Every other day = every second day = every two days. Every other week — haftaora (ikki haftada bir). Faqat “boshqa kunlar” degan ma’no emas, ritm haqida.

Everybody birlikda bo‘lsa, nega “their” ishlatiladi?

Ma’noda everybody ko‘pchilikni bildiradi, shuning uchun inglizcha they / their / them bilan davom ettirish keng tarqalgan va to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi: Everybody has their own opinion. Rasmiy yozuvda his or her ham yoziladi. Fe’l esa baribir birlikda: everybody has.

Each ikki narsa uchun ham ishlatiladimi? Every-chi?

Each ikki yoki undan ko‘p narsa uchun ishlatiladi (each of the two boxes, in each hand). Every odatda uch va undan ko‘p narsa uchun; ikki narsa haqida gapirganda both yoki each aytiladi (Unit 89).

Each other va each bir narsami?

Yo‘q. Each — “har biri”, each other — “bir-biri” (They help each other). Ikkinchisi olmosh va boshqa tuzilma, Unit 82 da tushuntirilgan.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Each = birma-bir, alohida (one by one); every = hammasi birga, istisnosiz (all together). Ikki narsa uchun — each.
  • Ikkalasidan keyin birlikdagi ot + birlikdagi fe’l: each / every student has.
  • each of + the / my / them (ko‘plik) + birlik fe’l; every of ❌ → every one of.
  • Ko‘plik ega + each: They each have; gap oxirida: £5 each.
  • Takrorlanish: every day / two hours / other day / few days; “almost every”, “not every”.
  • Everyone, everybody, everything — birlik fe’l; every day (har kuni) ≠ everyday (kundalik) ≠ all day (kun bo‘yi).
