Phrasal verbs 3: out

Frazali fe’llar — 3: out
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • out bilan yasalgan 19 ta eng ko‘p uchraydigan frazali fe’lni: go out, put out, blow out, run out (of), give out, find out, work out, sort out, point out, carry out, try out, turn out, leave out, cross out, cut out, hand out, break out, fall out, look out / watch out.
  • Particle out frazali fe’lda qanday ko‘chma ma’nolar berishini: tugash (run out), aniqlash, hal qilish (find out, work out), tushirish, tarqatish (leave out, hand out).
  • find out, work out va sort out ning bir-biridan farqini, go out bilan put out ning farqini.
  • Qaysi fe’llar ajraladi (put it out, work it out, hand them out) va qaysilari ajralmaydi (run out of sugar, look out for).

out ning asosiy ma’nosi — “tashqariga, chiqib”. Frazali fe’llarda bu ma’no kengayadi: narsa “tashqariga chiqib ketsa” — u tugaydi yoki yo‘qoladi; ma’lumot “tashqariga chiqarilsa” — u aniq bo‘ladi; narsalar “tashqariga berilsa” — tarqatiladi. Umumiy qoidalar (olmosh doim o‘rtada) — Unit 137 da; come in / go out kabi harakat fe’llari — Unit 138 da.

Belgilar: ∅ to‘ldiruvchisiz · ⇄ ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: put it out) · ✗ ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida) · informal norasmiy.

O‘zbek tilida out ma’nosi ko‘pincha yordamchi fe’llar bilan beriladi: -ib olmoq (bilib olmoq = find out), -ib chiqmoq (hisoblab chiqmoq = work out), -ib tashlamoq (chizib tashlamoq = cross out), tushirib qoldirmoq (= leave out), tarqatmoq (= hand out), -ib qolmoq (tugab qolmoq = run out). Ya’ni inglizchadagi out o‘zbekchada ko‘pincha chiqmoq, tashlamoq, olmoq, qolmoq yordamchilariga to‘g‘ri keladi.

Tuzoq: “o‘chirmoq” deganda hamma narsa put out emas! Olov, sigareta, sham — put out; elektr chiroq, televizor — odatda turn off / switch off (Unit 140). Yana: put out bilan put off (kechiktirmoq) ni adashtirmang.

Tugash, o‘chish, yo‘q bo‘lish: go out, put out, blow out, run out (of), give out Finishing and disappearing

go out — o‘chmoq (yonib turgan narsa)

Chiroq, olov, sham, o‘choq go out qilsa — yonishdan to‘xtaydi, o‘zi o‘chadi. Bu fe’l to‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi (∅). go out ning “uydan chiqmoq” ma’nosi — Unit 138 da.

  • The candle went out in the wind.Sham shamolda o‘chdi.
  • All the lights went out during the storm.Bo‘ron paytida hamma chiroqlar o‘chib qoldi.
  • The fire has gone out. Can you light it again?Olov o‘chib qoldi. Uni yana yoqa olasizmi?

put out — o‘chirmoq (olovni, sigaretani, shamni)

put out — kimdir olovni, sigaretani yoki shamni o‘chiradi. To‘ldiruvchi kerak va fe’l ajraladi (⇄): put out the fire / put the fire out / put it out. Britaniya inglizchasida put out the light (chiroqni o‘chirmoq) ham uchraydi, lekin elektr chiroq uchun odatda turn off / switch off deyiladi.

  • Please put your cigarette out before you come in.Kirishdan oldin sigaretangizni o‘chiring.
  • Jasur poured water on the campfire and put it out.Jasur gulxanga suv quyib, uni o‘chirdi.

put out bilan put off ni adashtirmang: put out the fire = olovni o‘chirmoq; put off the meeting = yig‘ilishni kechiktirmoq (Unit 140).

blow out — puflab o‘chirmoq

blow out — sham yoki gugurtni puflab o‘chirmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): blow out the candles / blow the candles out / blow them out.

  • Kamola made a wish and blew out the candles on her birthday cake.Kamola istak tilab, tug‘ilgan kun tortidagi shamlarni puflab o‘chirdi.
  • The match was burning my fingers, so I blew it out.Gugurt barmoqlarimni kuydirayotgan edi, shuning uchun uni puflab o‘chirdim.

give out — chidamay qolmoq, tugab qolmoq

give out ning ikkinchi ma’nosi: kuch, ovoz, zaxira yoki mashina ishdan chiqadi / tugab qoladi. To‘ldiruvchisiz (∅). (Birinchi ma’nosi — “tarqatmoq” — D bo‘limda.)

  • After ten kilometres, her legs gave out.O‘n kilometrdan keyin oyoqlari chidamay qoldi.
  • Our supplies gave out after a week.Zaxiramiz bir haftadan keyin tugab qoldi.

find out — bilib olmoq

find out — noma’lum faktni o‘rganib olmoq. Keyin ko‘pincha gap bo‘lagi (what, when, where, if, that) yoki about keladi. Nazariy jihatdan fe’l ajraladi, lekin amalda to‘ldiruvchi deyarli har doim particle’dan keyin turadi: find out the truth. Olmosh o‘rniga odatda find out about it yoki to‘ldiruvchisiz find out deyiladi.

  • I’d like to find out when the bank opens.Bank qachon ochilishini bilib olmoqchiman.
  • “Did you find out about the exam?” “Yes, it’s on Monday.”— Imtihon haqida bilib oldingmi? — Ha, dushanba kuni ekan.
  • Dilnoza found out the truth by accident.Dilnoza haqiqatni tasodifan bilib qoldi.
  • We found out that the shop closes at six.Do‘kon oltida yopilishini bilib oldik.

find bilan find out ni aralashtirmang. find = topmoq (yo‘qolgan yoki qidirilgan narsani): I found my keys. find out = bilib olmoq (ma’lumotni): I found out where the keys were.

sort out — tartibga solmoq, hal qilmoq

sort out — (1) narsalarni tartibga solmoq, ajratmoq; (2) muammo yoki tartibsizlikni hal qilmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): sort out the problem / sort the problem out / sort it out. Britaniya nutqida juda keng tarqalgan.

point out — ko‘rsatib o‘tmoq

point out — biror narsaga e’tiborni qaratmoq, uni ko‘rsatmoq yoki eslatib o‘tmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): point out the mistake / point the mistake out / point it out. Keyin that-gapi ham kelishi mumkin; kimga ko‘rsatilishi to bilan: point out sth to sb.

  • The teacher pointed out the mistake in my essay.O‘qituvchi inshoimdagi xatoni ko‘rsatib berdi.

Bajarish, sinash, natija: carry out, try out, turn out Doing, testing and results

carry out — reja, tajriba, so‘rovnoma, buyruq, ta’mirni amalga oshirmoq, o‘tkazmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): carry out an experiment / carry it out.

try out — yangi narsani, usulni, mashinani yoki retseptni ishlatib, sinab ko‘rmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): try out a recipe / try it out. (Kiyim-kechak uchun — try on, Unit 140.)

Tushirib qoldirish va tarqatish: leave out, cross out, cut out, hand out, give out Omitting, deleting, distributing

leave out — tushirib qoldirmoq

leave out — biror narsani qasddan yoki beixtiyor kiritmaslik, tushirib qoldirish. Ajraladi (⇄): leave out a word / leave it out. “Nimadan?” deb aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — leave sb / sth out of + ot.

  • You’ve left out a word in this sentence.Bu gapda bir so‘zni tushirib qoldiribsiz.
  • Don’t leave the date out.Sanani tushirib qoldirmang.
  • They left Bekzod out of the team because of his injury.Jarohati sababli Bekzodni jamoa tarkibiga kiritishmadi.
  • Nobody likes to feel left out.Hech kim chetda qolgandek his qilishni yoqtirmaydi.

cross out — chizib tashlamoq

cross out — yozilgan so‘z yoki javobning ustidan chiziq tortib o‘chirmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): cross out a word / cross it out.

  • Cross out the wrong answer and write the correct one.Noto‘g‘ri javobni chizib tashlang va to‘g‘risini yozing.
  • Dilnoza wrote the wrong date, so she crossed it out.Dilnoza sanani xato yozdi, shuning uchun uni chizib tashladi.

cut out — qirqib olmoq

cut sth out (of sth) — gazeta, jurnal yoki qog‘ozdan rasm, maqola, shaklni qaychi bilan qirqib olmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): cut out the picture / cut the picture out / cut it out.

  • Nodira cut an interesting article out of the newspaper.Nodira gazetadan qiziqarli maqolani qirqib oldi.
  • The children cut out paper stars for the New Year party.Bolalar Yangi yil bazmi uchun qog‘ozdan yulduzchalar qirqib olishdi.

hand out va give out — tarqatmoq

hand out va give out — bir nechta odamga bittadan tarqatmoq (varaqa, imtihon varaqalari, suv, sovg‘a). Ikkalasi ham ajraladi (⇄): hand out the papers / hand them out; give out leaflets / give them out.

  • The teacher handed out the exam papers.O‘qituvchi imtihon varaqalarini tarqatdi.
  • Volunteers gave out free water at the finish line.Ko‘ngillilar marra oldida bepul suv tarqatishdi.
  • A boy was handing leaflets out outside the station.Bir bola vokzal oldida varaqalar tarqatayotgan edi.

hand out ni hand in (topshirmoq, Unit 138) bilan adashtirmang: The teacher hands out the tests; the students hand them in. — O‘qituvchi testlarni tarqatadi; o‘quvchilar ularni topshiradi.

Birdan boshlanish, urishib qolish, ogohlantirish: break out, fall out, look out / watch out Sudden events, quarrels and warnings

break out — to‘satdan boshlanib ketmoq; qochmoq

break out — urush, yong‘in, janjal, kasallik kabi yoqimsiz voqea to‘satdan boshlanadi. Ega — voqea; fe’l ko‘pincha o‘tgan zamonda keladi va majhul nisbatda ishlatilmaydi (∅). Ikkinchi ma’nosi — qamoqdan qochib chiqmoq (break out of + joy). Ot: an outbreak (masalan, kasallik tarqalishi).

  • A fire broke out in the old market last night.Kecha eski bozorda yong‘in chiqdi.
  • War broke out in 1939.Urush 1939-yilda boshlandi.
  • Two prisoners broke out of the prison at night.Ikki mahbus tunda qamoqxonadan qochib chiqdi.

fall out (with sb) — urishib qolmoq, arazlashmoq

fall out — do‘stlar yoki qarindoshlar janjallashib, urishib qolmoq (ko‘pincha bir muddat gaplashmay yurishadi). To‘ldiruvchisiz (∅) yoki fall out with sb (✗ ajralmaydi); sababi over / about bilan aytiladi. Ayniqsa Britaniya inglizchasida keng tarqalgan. Ot: a falling-out.

  • Aziz and Bekzod fell out over a football match and didn’t speak for a month.Aziz bilan Bekzod futbol o‘yini sababli urishib qolishdi va bir oy gaplashmay yurishdi.
  • Dilnoza has fallen out with her best friend.Dilnoza eng yaqin dugonasi bilan arazlashib qoldi.

look out / watch out — ehtiyot bo‘ling!

Look out! va Watch out! — xavf haqida tez ogohlantirish: “Ehtiyot bo‘ling!” Ular ko‘pincha buyruq shaklida, to‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi (∅). Xavfning nima ekanini aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — look out for / watch out for + ot (✗ ajralmaydi).

  • Look out! A car is coming!Ehtiyot bo‘ling! Mashina kelyapti!
  • Watch out! The floor is wet.Ehtiyot bo‘ling! Pol ho‘l.
  • Look out for pickpockets in the bazaar.Bozorda cho‘ntakkesarlardan ehtiyot bo‘ling.

Look out of the window (“derazadan tashqariga qaramoq”) — bu frazali fe’l emas: look + out of (predlog) + window.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Find out” bilan “work out” ning farqi nimada?

find out — bilmagan faktni tashqaridan bilib olish (so‘rab, qo‘ng‘iroq qilib, o‘qib): I’ll find out when the shop opens. work out — javobni o‘zingiz o‘ylab yoki hisoblab topish: I worked out the total. Agar javobni kimdandir so‘rash yoki izlash kerak bo‘lsa — find out; faqat o‘ylash yoki hisoblash kerak bo‘lsa — work out.

“Go out” bilan “put out” ni qanday farqlayman?

go out — olov yoki chiroq o‘zi o‘chadi (to‘ldiruvchisiz): The fire went out. put out — kimdir uni o‘chiradi (to‘ldiruvchi bilan): They put the fire out. Ularni “o‘chmoq / o‘chirmoq” juftligi deb eslab qoling.

Elektr chiroqni o‘chirish uchun qaysi fe’l kerak: put out, turn out yoki turn off?

Eng keng tarqalgani — turn off yoki switch off (Unit 140): Turn off the light. Turn out the light va put out the light ham to‘g‘ri (Britaniya inglizchasi), lekin kamroq ishlatiladi. put out asosan olov, sigareta, sham uchun; televizor yoki kompyuter uchun esa faqat turn off / switch off.

“Run out” va “run out of” ning farqi nimada?

Ega tugagan narsaning o‘zi bo‘lsa — run out: The sugar has run out. (Shakar tugadi.) Ega odam bo‘lib, tugagan narsa oxirida kelsa — run out of: We have run out of sugar. (Bizda shakar qolmadi.) Ikkala gap deyarli bir xil narsani bildiradi, faqat nuqtai nazar boshqacha.

“Sort out” bilan “work out” ikkalasi ham “hal qilmoq” emasmi?

Ba’zan yaqin, lekin farqi bor. sort out — tartibsizlik yoki muammoni bartaraf etmoq (kelishmovchilik, bron, hujjat): sort out the booking. work out — javobni topmoq, hisoblamoq (raqam, sabab, usul): work out the answer. Narsalarni tartibga solish (kitoblar, qog‘ozlar) — faqat sort out.

“Turn out” dan keyin “to be” har doim kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Sifat yoki ravish bevosita kelishi mumkin: It turned out fine / well / badly. Ot yoki sifat bilan to be ishlatsangiz ham bo‘ladi: It turned out to be a mistake. Butun gap kelsa, that ishlatiladi (yoki tushiriladi): It turned out (that) he was right.

“Cross out” bilan “cross off” ning farqi nimada?

cross out — yozilgan so‘z yoki javob ustidan chiziq tortmoq (xatoni o‘chirish). cross off — ro‘yxatdagi bandni (masalan, sotib olingan narsani) chizib, ro‘yxatdan olib tashlamoq: She crossed bread off the shopping list. Amalda ular ba’zan almashadi, lekin asosiy farq shu.

“Hand out” bilan “give out” bir xilmi?

“Tarqatmoq” ma’nosida — ha, deyarli bir xil. Lekin give out ning yana bir ma’nosi bor: kuch, ovoz yoki zaxira tugab qolmoq / ishdan chiqmoq (Her voice gave out.). hand out faqat “tarqatmoq” ma’nosiga ega.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • go out — o‘zi o‘chadi; put out — kimdir o‘chiradi (olov, sigareta, sham). Elektr chiroq: turn off (Unit 140).
  • run out (ega = zaxira) · run out of + ot (ega = odam). of ni tushirib qoldirmang.
  • find out = tashqaridan bilib olmoq · work out = o‘ylab / hisoblab topmoq · sort out = tartibga solmoq, hal qilmoq · point out = ko‘rsatib o‘tmoq.
  • carry out = bajarmoq · try out = sinab ko‘rmoq · turn out = bo‘lib chiqmoq (turn out to be / turn out that).
  • leave out = tushirib qoldirmoq · cross out = chizib tashlamoq · cut out = qirqib olmoq · hand out / give out = tarqatmoq · blow out = puflab o‘chirmoq.
  • break out (urush, yong‘in) majhul nisbatda emas; fall out (with sb) = urishib qolmoq; Look out! / Watch out! = Ehtiyot bo‘ling!; look out for + xavf.
  • Olmosh to‘ldiruvchi o‘rtada: put it out, work it out, hand them out. Ajralmaydiganlar: run out of, look out for, break out of.
