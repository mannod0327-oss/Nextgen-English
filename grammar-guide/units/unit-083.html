A friend of mine, My own house, On my own / by myself

A friend of mine, my own, on my own / by myself
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • a friend of mine / a friend of Malika’s — “do‘stlarimdan biri” deyishning inglizcha yo‘li (of + mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs).
  • my own house / a room of my own — “o‘z uyim, o‘zimga tegishli xona”: own so‘zi qachon va qanday ishlatiladi.
  • on my own / by myself — “yolg‘iz o‘zim” va “hech kimning yordamisiz”.
  • Nega a friend of me, that my book, an own car deyish xato ekanini.

Bu darsda uchta kichik, lekin juda foydali konstruksiya bor. Uchalasi ham kimga tegishli yoki kim qilyapti degan savolga javob beradi: “mening do‘stlarimdan biri”, “faqat o‘zimniki”, “yolg‘iz o‘zim”.

O‘zbek tilida “do‘stlarimdan biri” deymiz — ya’ni “mening do‘stlarim ichidan bittasi”. Inglizchada bu a friend of mine: avval ot, keyin of, keyin egalik olmoshining mustaqil shakli (mine). “O‘z” so‘zi (o‘z uyim, o‘z xonang) esa inglizchada own bo‘ladi, lekin faqat ta’kidlash kerak bo‘lganda. “Yolg‘iz o‘zim” = on my own / by myself.

“Do‘stlarimdan biri”: a friend of mine a friend of mine / a friend of Tom’s

Inglizchada “mening do‘stlarimdan biri” deyish uchun my friend yetmaydi — chunki oldida a, this, that, some, two kabi so‘z bo‘lsa, my ni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri qo‘sha olmaymiz. Shunda of yordamga keladi:

a / this / that / some ...+ot+of+mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs
  • Bekzod is a friend of mine.Bekzod mening do‘stlarimdan biri.
  • I met a colleague of yours at the conference.Konferensiyada hamkasblaringizdan birini uchratdim.
  • Is Jasur a relative of hers?Jasur uning qarindoshlaridan birimi?
  • Where is that book of yours? I need it.Sizning o‘sha kitobingiz qani? Menga kerak.
  • Some friends of ours are coming for dinner tonight.Bugun kechqurun bizning do‘stlarimizdan ba’zilari kechki ovqatga keladi.
  • She wore a dress of her sister’s.U opasining ko‘ylaklaridan birini kiygan edi.

Qoida: of dan keyin egalik olmoshining mustaqil shakli keladi: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. of me ❌, of my ❌. his ikkala holatda ham bir xil: his book va a friend of his.

“Mine” o‘rniga ism + ’s

Egasi olmosh emas, ism yoki ot bo‘lsa, of + ism’s ishlatiladi (Unit 81 dagi ’s ni eslang):

  • She is a friend of Malika’s.U Malikaning do‘stlaridan biri.
  • I stayed with a friend of my father’s in Samarkand.Samarqandda otamning do‘stlaridan birinikida turdim.
  • A neighbour of my parents’ has bought the shop.Ota-onamning qo‘shnilaridan biri do‘konni sotib oldi.
  • It’s a favourite saying of my grandfather’s.Bu bobomning eng yaxshi ko‘rgan maqollaridan biri.

Ma’no farqi: a photo of Tom odatda “Tom tushgan surat”, a photo of Tom’s esa odatda “Tomga tegishli suratlardan biri”. Shuning uchun egalik ma’nosi kerak bo‘lsa, ’s qo‘shamiz.

a friend of mine = one of my friends

Ikki ibora bir xil ma’noda, lekin shakli boshqa: one of my friends da my (ot oldidagi shakl), a friend of mine da mine (mustaqil shakl) ishlatiladi. Aralashtirib yubormang: one of mine friends ❌, a friend of my ❌ (Unit 88 da one of the ... haqida yana gaplashamiz).

O‘zbekchada “do‘stlarimdan biri” deganda -dan va “biri” so‘zlarini aytamiz, “mine”ga o‘xshash maxsus shakl yo‘q. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar a friend of me deb xato qiladi: ular “do‘stlarimdan” → “of me” deb tarjima qiladi. Eslab qoling: of me degan birikma bu yerda ishlamaydi, faqat of mine.

“O‘z uyim”: my own house my own / a room of my own

own “faqat menga tegishli, boshqa birov bilan umumiy emas, birovdan olinmagan” degan ma’noni kuchaytiradi. U egalik olmoshidan keyin keladi:

my / your / his / her / our / their / its+own+ot
  • I’m tired of borrowing my brother’s bike. I want my own bike.Akamning velosipedini so‘rab olaverishdan charchadim. O‘z velosipedim bo‘lishini xohlayman.
  • Don’t borrow my laptop — use your own.Noutbukimni olmang — o‘zingiznikidan foydalaning.
  • Many students dream of having their own business.Ko‘p talabalar o‘z biznesiga ega bo‘lishni orzu qiladi.
  • The village has its own small school.Qishloqning o‘z kichik maktabi bor.
  • It was my own fault. Nobody else made the mistake.Bu mening o‘z aybim edi. Xatoni boshqa hech kim qilmagan.

Ikki shakl: my own + ot = ot + of my own

Xuddi shu ma’noni a / an + ot + of + my own shaklida ham aytish mumkin:

Ikkita to‘g‘ri shakl
my own + ota / an + ot + of my ownTarjima
She has her own flat.She has a flat of her own.Uning o‘z kvartirasi bor.
I’d like my own car.I’d like a car of my own.Men o‘z mashinamga ega bo‘lishni istardim.
They need their own office.They need an office of their own.Ularga o‘z ofislari kerak.

own dan oldin a, an, the kelmaydi: an own car ❌, the own house ❌, own house ❌ (egalik olmoshisiz). To‘g‘ri: my own car yoki a car of my own.

Nomsiz ishlatilishi

Ma’no tushunarli bo‘lsa, own dan keyin ot aytilmaydi:

  • “Can I use your pen?” “Why? You’ve got your own.”— Qalamingizni olsam bo‘ladimi? — Nega? Sizda o‘zingizniki bor-ku.
  • I don’t like other people’s ideas. I prefer my own.Men boshqalarning fikrini yoqtirmayman. O‘zimniki ma’qul.

“O‘zim qilaman, birov emas” ma’nosi

own yana “ishni boshqa odam emas, o‘zim bajaraman” ma’nosini beradi. Buni boshqa odam sizning o‘rningizga qiladigan holat bilan solishtiring:

O‘zim qilaman

  • I repair my own bike.
  • She makes her own clothes.
  • We bake our own bread.

Boshqa odam qiladi

  • I have my bike repaired at a shop. (Unit 46)
  • She buys her clothes in a shop.
  • We buy our bread at the baker’s.

my very own — “aynan o‘zimning” (his/her/our ... bilan ham): It’s my very own idea. Bu ta’kidni yanada kuchaytiradi. own yana fe’l ham: My uncle owns a shop (amakimning do‘koni bor, do‘kon unga tegishli). Owner — “egasi”.

O‘zbek tilida “o‘z” juda ko‘p ishlatiladi: U o‘z kitobini o‘qiyapti, Men o‘z ishimni qilaman. Inglizchada bu gaplarda oddiy egalik olmoshi yetarli: He is reading his book. I do my work. Own ni faqat “aynan o‘zimniki, boshqasiniki emas” ma’nosini ta’kidlamoqchi bo‘lganda qo‘shing. Har gapga own qo‘shsangiz, juda g‘alati eshitiladi.

“Yolg‘iz o‘zim”: on my own / by myself on my own / by myself

on my own va by myself ikkalasi ham bir xil ma’noga ega: yolg‘iz (boshqa hech kimsiz) yoki hech kimning yordamisiz.

Shakllar: on ... own va by ...self

Qoida: on bilan own (on my own), by bilan -self / -selves (by myself). Ularni aralashtirmang: on myself ❌, by my own ❌.

1-ma’no: yolg‘iz (alone)

  • Nodira lives on her own in a small flat near Chorsu.Nodira Chorsu yaqinidagi kichik kvartirada yolg‘iz yashaydi.
  • I like walking by myself in the evening.Men kechqurun yolg‘iz sayr qilishni yaxshi ko‘raman.
  • A little boy was sitting by himself on the bench.Kichkina bola o‘rindiqda yolg‘iz o‘tirgan edi.
  • Don’t leave the children on their own in the house.Bolalarni uyda yolg‘iz qoldirmang.

2-ma’no: hech kimning yordamisiz

  • Did you paint the whole room by yourself?Butun xonani o‘zingiz bo‘yadingizmi (hech kimsiz)?
  • The box is heavy. Can you carry it on your own?Quti og‘ir. Uni o‘zingiz ko‘tara olasizmi?
  • Sardor built the website all on his own.Sardor saytni butunlay o‘zi yasadi.
  • The door opened on its own. (= by itself)Eshik o‘z-o‘zidan ochilib ketdi.

Ta’kid uchun all qo‘shish mumkin: all on my own, all by myself — “mutlaqo yolg‘iz”, “batamom o‘zim”.

alone, lonely, on my own — farqi nimada?

O‘zbek tilidagi “yolg‘iz” so‘zi ham alone, ham lonely ma’nosida kelishi mumkin. Inglizchada bu ikki xil so‘z: yolg‘iz yashash — to live alone, yolg‘izlikdan azob chekish — to feel lonely. “Yolg‘iz o‘zim qildim” = I did it by myself / on my own (yoki I did it myself, Unit 82).

by myself va myself bir xil emas: I made the cake myself — tortni boshqa odam emas, aynan men tayyorladim (ta’kid). I made the cake by myself — hech kimning yordamisiz qildim. Ma’no juda yaqin, shuning uchun ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“A friend of mine” bilan “my friend” o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ma’no jihatidan juda yaqin, lekin my friend ko‘pincha aniq, tinglovchi biladigan odamni bildiradi (my friend Bekzod). A friend of mine esa “mening bir nechta do‘stlarimdan biri”. Eng muhimi — grammatika: a, this, that, some, two, another kabi so‘zlardan keyin my friend demaymiz, friend of mine deymiz.

“A friend of his” to‘g‘rimi? His da ‘s’ yo‘q-ku?

Ha, to‘g‘ri. His egalik olmoshining ikkala shakli (ot oldida va mustaqil) bir xil: his book va a friend of his. Faqat my → mine, your → yours, her → hers, our → ours, their → theirs o‘zgaradi.

Nega “a friend of Malika’s” da ham of, ham ’s bor? Ortiqcha emasmi?

Bu inglizchaning odatiy shakli. Of “…dan biri” ma’nosini beradi, ’s esa egasini ko‘rsatadi. Ikkalasi ham kerak: a friend of Malika’s = Malikaning do‘stlaridan biri. Shunga o‘xshash: a friend of my brother’s.

“One of my friends” va “a friend of mine” bir xilmi?

Ha, ma’nosi bir xil. Faqat shakli farq qiladi: one of my friends (ko‘plikdagi ot oldida my), a friend of mine (mine mustaqil shaklda). One of mine friends va a friend of my — ikkalasi ham xato.

“Own” ni “the” yoki “a” bilan ishlata olamanmi?

To‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri — yo‘q. Own egalik olmoshidan keyin keladi: my own, her own, their own. “A/an” kerak bo‘lsa, a + ot + of my own shaklini ishlating: a room of my own.

“On my own” bilan “alone” aynan bir xilmi? “Lonely” ham shumi?

On my own va alone deyarli bir xil: yoningizda hech kim yo‘q. Lonely esa boshqa: yolg‘izlikdan xafa, ko‘ngli g‘ash. Kishi alone bo‘lib, lonely bo‘lmasligi mumkin (yolg‘iz yashashni yaxshi ko‘radi) va aksincha — ko‘pchilik orasida ham o‘zini lonely his qilishi mumkin.

“I did it myself” bilan “I did it by myself” ning farqi?

Juda yaqin. Myself asosan “boshqa emas, aynan men” deb ta’kidlaydi. By myself “hech kimning yordamisiz (va ba’zan yolg‘iz)” ma’nosini beradi. Ko‘p hollarda ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi.

Narsa uchun ham “on its own” deyish mumkinmi?

Ha. On its own / by itself — “o‘z-o‘zidan, hech kim tegmasdan”. The computer switched off on its own. (Kompyuter o‘z-o‘zidan o‘chib qoldi.)

Amerika ingliz tilida farq bormi?

Asosiy farq yo‘q. A friend of mine, my own, on my own, by myself — Britaniya va Amerika inglizchasida bir xil ishlatiladi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • “Do‘stlarimdan biri” = a friend of mine: of dan keyin mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs (of me ❌, of my ❌).
  • Egasi ism bo‘lsa: a friend of Malika’s. a / this / that / some bilan my ni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri ishlatmaymiz: that book of mine.
  • own = “faqat o‘zimniki”: my own room = a room of my own. a / the dan keyin own kelmaydi.
  • O‘zbekcha “o‘z” har doim own emas: oddiy gapda his book yetarli. Own — ta’kid uchun.
  • on my own / by myself = yolg‘iz yoki yordamsiz. Aralashtirmang: on + own, by + -self.
  • alone = yolg‘iz (neytral); lonely = yolg‘izlikdan xafa.
