Phrasal verbs 7: up (1)

Frazali fe’llar — 7: up (1)
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • up bilan yasalgan frazali fe’llarning birinchi katta guruhini: yaqinlashish (walk up to), yetib olish va hamqadam bo‘lish (catch up with, keep up with), hayotdagi o‘zgarishlar (give up, take up, end up, grow up, bring up), paydo bo‘lish va yaratish (turn up, show up, set up, come up with, make up).
  • Particle up bu yerda ko‘pincha “yuqoriga” degani emas: u “yaqinga”, “ortda qolmay”, “oxiriga yetib”, “paydo bo‘lib” kabi ma’nolarni beradi.
  • Qaysi fe’l to‘ldiruvchisiz (∅), qaysi biri ajraladi (⇄), qaysi biri ajralmaydi (✗) — va olmosh qayerga tushishini: give it up, bring them up, lekin keep up with them.
  • give up va end up dan keyin qanday shakl kelishini (give up smoking, end up walking) hamda o‘zbekchadagi mos fe’llarni (ulg‘aymoq, voz kechmoq, o‘ylab topmoq).

Unit 142 da up asosan “yuqoriga” va “ko‘paytirish” ma’nosida edi (pick up, turn up the radio). Bu unitda esa fe’lning ma’nosi particle’dan emas, butun birikmadan chiqadi: give up — “taslim bo‘lmoq, tashlamoq”, come up with — “o‘ylab topmoq”. Shuning uchun har bir fe’lni misol bilan, bitta so‘z kabi o‘rganing. Umumiy qoidalar — Unit 137 da.

Belgilar: ∅ — to‘ldiruvchisiz; ⇄ — ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: give it up); ✗ — ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida: keep up with them). Norasmiy deb belgilangan fe’llar kundalik suhbatda tabiiy, rasmiy yozuvda esa bitta so‘zli sinonim afzal.

O‘zbek tilida bunday ma’nolar odatda bitta fe’l yoki yordamchi fe’l bilan beriladi: ulg‘aymoq (grow up), voz kechmoq / tashlamoq (give up), o‘ylab topmoq (come up with), to‘qib chiqarmoq (make up), kelib qolmoq (turn up), yetib olmoq (catch up with), tashkil qilmoq (set up). Inglizchada esa shu ma’no fe’ldan keyingi kichik so‘z bilan beriladi.

Tuzoq: o‘zbekcha “bolani o‘stirmoq” — inglizchada bring up (to‘ldiruvchi bor). “O‘smoq, ulg‘aymoq” — grow up (to‘ldiruvchi yo‘q). Bu ikkisini aralashtirmang (C bo‘lim). “Chekishni tashladim” — I gave up smoking, I threw away smoking emas (throw away — Unit 145).

Yaqinlashish: go up, come up, walk up (to) go up to, come up to, walk up to

Bu yerda up “yuqoriga” degani emas — tekis yerda ham ishlatiladi. U “bevosita yaqiniga borish” ma’nosini beradi. Odatda up bilan birga to keladi: fe’l + up + to + kim / nima.

Zina haqida esa: go up the stairs (zinadan yuqoriga chiqmoq). Qaysi ma’no ekanini keyingi so‘z ko‘rsatadi: up to + odam — yaqinlashmoq.

Yetib olish va hamqadam bo‘lish catch up (with), keep up (with)

Bu ikki fe’l ko‘pincha adashtiriladi. Farqi: catch up with — orqada qolgan kishi oldindagiga yetib oladi (masofa nolga tushadi); keep up with — kimdir yoki nimadir bilan bir xil tezlikda yurib, ortda qolmaydi (masofa o‘zgarmaydi).

Tekshirish: “Hozir men ortdaman, keyin yetib olaman” — catch up (until I catch up with you). “Men ortda qolmayapman” yoki “ortda qolib ketyapman” — keep up (I can’t keep up).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Give up” dan keyin “to + fe’l” kelishi mumkinmi?

Yo‘q. give up dan keyin faqat ot yoki -ing shakli keladi: give up chess, give up smoking, give up trying (urinishni tashlamoq). give up to smoke — xato. Eslatma: o‘zbekchada “chekishni tashlamoq” (harakat nomi + tushum kelishigi); inglizchada shu o‘rinda -ing shakli: smoking.

“Grow” bilan “grow up” ning farqi nimada?

grow — o‘smoq (bo‘y, soch, o‘simlik, miqdor): Tomatoes grow well here. You’ve grown! grow up — bolalikdan katta yoshga o‘tmoq: He grew up in Fergana. “Men Buxoroda o‘sganman” degan ma’noda I grew in Bukhara deb bo‘lmaydi, I grew up in Bukhara kerak.

“Turn up” bilan “show up” va “arrive” ning farqi nimada?

arrive — neytral, rasmiy yozuvda ham keladi (The guests arrived at eight). turn up va show up — norasmiy: ko‘pincha kutilmagan yoki kech kelishni, yoki umuman kelmaslikni ta’kidlaydi (He didn’t show up). turn up narsa uchun ham ishlatiladi (topildi), show up esa asosan odam uchun.

“Catch up with”, “catch up on” va “keep up with” ni qanday ajrataman?

catch up with — oldindagiga yetib olmoq (I’ll catch up with you). catch up on — qilolmay qolgan narsani to‘ldirmoq (catch up on sleep / on the news). keep up with — hamqadam bo‘lib, ortda qolmaslik (keep up with the class). Ikkita farqlovchi so‘roq: “Men ortdamanmi va yetib olmoqchimanmi?” — catch up. “Men tezlikka yetisha olamanmi?” — keep up.

“End up” faqat yomon natija uchunmi?

Yo‘q. Bu fe’l faqat “natijada shunday bo‘lib qoldi” deydi. Natija yaxshi bo‘lishi ham mumkin: She started as a cleaner and ended up as the manager. Yomon natijada ham ishlatiladi: He ended up in hospital. Uning o‘zbekcha ma’nosi: “oxiri … bo‘lib qoldi”, “… ga borib qoldi”.

“Take up” bilan “start” qanday farq qiladi?

start har qanday ishni boshlashga aytiladi (start a lesson, start the car). take up esa odatda yangi mashg‘ulot, hobbi, sport, fan bilan shug‘ullana boshlashni bildiradi (take up chess, take up yoga). “Darsni boshladik” deb took up the lesson bo‘lmaydi. Ikkinchi ma’no: take up + joy / vaqt = egallamoq.

“Make up” ning boshqa ma’nolari bormi?

Ha, bir nechta, ularni aralashtirmang: (1) to‘qimoq — shu unitdagi ma’no; (2) make up (with sb) — janjaldan keyin yarashmoq: Dilnoza and Malika quarrelled, but they made up the next day.; (3) make up for — o‘rnini qoplamoq (uch so‘zli fe’l); (4) be made up of — iborat bo‘lmoq: The team is made up of ten players. Yana: make-up (AmE: makeup) — ot, “pardoz-andoz”.

“Bring up” bilan “raise” ning farqi bormi?

Bolalarni tarbiyalab o‘stirish ma’nosida ikkalasi deyarli bir xil. Britaniya inglizchasida bring up ko‘proq uchraydi (She was brought up by her aunt), Amerika inglizchasida raise ko‘proq (She was raised by her aunt). Mavzuni ko‘tarish ma’nosida ikkalasi ham ishlatiladi: bring up / raise the question.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Bu guruhda up ko‘pincha “yuqoriga” emas: walk up to — yaqinlashmoq; catch up / keep up — ortda qolmaslik; turn up / show up — paydo bo‘lmoq; come up with / make up — yaratmoq.
  • catch up with — yetib olmoq (masofa qisqaradi); keep up with — hamqadam bo‘lmoq (masofa o‘zgarmaydi); catch up on — qoplamoq (on my sleep).
  • Ajraladi: give up, take up, bring up, set up, make up — olmosh o‘rtada (give it up). Ajralmaydi: walk up to, catch up with, keep up with, come up with.
  • To‘ldiruvchisiz: end up, grow up, turn up, show up.
  • give up va end up dan keyin -ing: give up smoking, end up walking.
  • grow up — o‘zi ulg‘aymoq (to‘ldiruvchisiz); bring up — bolani tarbiyalamoq yoki mavzuni ko‘tarmoq; majhul nisbat: I was brought up in …
  • turn up / show up norasmiy (rasmiy: arrive); come up with — g‘oya, reja, yechim; make up — hikoya, bahona, ism.
