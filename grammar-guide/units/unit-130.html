Adjective + preposition 1

Sifat + predlog — 1
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • kind / nice / generous / mean / stupid / silly / polite / rude / careless of sb (+ to fe’l) va nice / kind / polite / rude / friendly to sb farqini: It was kind of you to wait — He was rude to the waiter.
  • angry / annoyed / furious: about + narsa, with + kishi, with sb for doing sth.
  • excited / worried / upset / nervous / happy about — his-tuyg‘u nima haqida ekanini.
  • surprised / shocked / amazed / astonished at (by), impressed with (by), disappointed with / in (by, about), fed up / bored with.
  • sorry about, sorry for doing, sorry (that) I did va feel sorry for sb.

Kishining his-tuyg‘usi yoki xulq-atvorini bildiruvchi sifatlarning ko‘pi o‘zidan keyin predlog talab qiladi. Qaysi predlog kelishi ikki narsaga bog‘liq: (1) sifatning o‘ziga; (2) his-tuyg‘u kimga yoki nimaga qaratilganiga — ko‘pincha kishiga with / to / of, voqea yoki narsaga about / at / by keladi. Umumiy tuzilma:

Ega+be+sifat+predlog+ot / olmosh / fe’l-ing

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar kelishik qo‘shimchalari bilan beriladi: akamdan xafa bo‘ldim, imtihondan xavotirdaman, xabardan hayron qoldim, odamlarga mehribon. Ingliz tilida esa har bir sifatning o‘z predlogi bor. Quyidagi jadvalda ko‘rasiz: ba’zan o‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha va inglizcha predlog mos keladi (✅), ba’zan esa chalg‘itadi (⚠️) — xatolarning ko‘pi aynan shu qatorlardan chiqadi. Unit 131 da qolgan sifat + predlog juftliklari (afraid of, interested in, good at …) bor.

Nice of you va nice to you kind / rude of sb (to do sth) and kind / rude to sb

Kishining xatti-harakatini baholaydigan sifatlar (kind, nice, rude, silly …) ikki xil qolipda keladi. Predlog ham, ma’no ham har xil:

It is + sifat + OF + kishi + to + fe’l

Bitta aniq ishni baholaysiz: “u buni qildi — bu yaxshi (yoki yomon) ish edi”.

  • It was kind of you to wait for me.
  • It was careless of him to leave the door open.

kishi + be + sifat + TO + kishi

Kishi boshqa odamga qanday munosabatda ekanini aytasiz.

  • The nurse was kind to every patient.
  • Why are you so rude to your sister?

Sifat + of + kishi (+ to fe’l)

It is / was+sifat+of+kishi+to + fe’l
Sifat + of — ishni baholash
BahoSifatlarMisol

👎 Salbiymean, rude, stupid, silly, careless, selfish, foolish, unfairIt was silly of me to forget the umbrella.
  • It was kind of Malika to bring us tea while we were waiting.Biz kutib turganimizda Malikaning choy olib kelgani juda mehribonlik edi.
  • That was careless of Jasur — he left the oven on all night.Bu Jasurning beparvoligi edi — u tun bo‘yi pechni o‘chirmasdan qoldirgan.
  • It’s generous of you to lend me your bicycle.Menga velosipedingizni berib turishingiz — saxiylik.
  • How silly of me to lose the tickets!Chiptalarni yo‘qotib qo‘yganim qanday ahmoqona!

of dan keyin kishi nomi yoki object olmosh keladi: of you, of me, of him, of them, of Malika. ❌ of your. “Men” uchun of me: How stupid of me! Bu tuzilmani sifat + to-infinitiv (You were kind to wait) deb ham aytish mumkin: It was kind of you to wait = You were kind to wait. Ma’no bir xil.

Sifat + to + kishi

Sifat + to — munosabat
SifatlarMisolTarjima
nice, kind, good, generous, polite, friendly, helpfulThe staff at the hotel were friendly to every guest.Mehmonxona xodimlari har bir mehmonga xushmuomala edi.
mean, rude, unkind, unfriendly, cruel, nastyPlease don’t be cruel to animals.Iltimos, hayvonlarga shafqatsiz bo‘lmang.
  • Mrs Karimova was very good to us when we moved to the city.Karimova xonim shaharga ko‘chib kelganimizda bizga juda mehribon bo‘ldi.
  • The shop assistant was polite to everyone, even to the angry customers.Sotuvchi hamma bilan, hatto jahli chiqqan xaridorlar bilan ham muloyim edi.
  • Sardor was mean to his little sister and wouldn’t share his sweets.Sardor singlisiga xasislik qildi va shirinliklarini bermadi.

Bir hodisani ikki tomondan aytish mumkin — urg‘u boshqa joyda:

  • It was rude of him to shout at the waiter.Uning ofitsiantga baqirgani qo‘pollik edi. (ish baholanyapti)
  • He was rude to the waiter.U ofitsiantga qo‘pol munosabatda bo‘ldi. (kimga nisbatan ekani muhim)

to dan keyin kishi kelsa — munosabat: rude to the waiter. to dan keyin fe’l kelsa, bu — infinitiv: It was rude (of him) to interrupt. ❌ kind with me, ❌ rude with the waiter, ❌ kind for me — “bilan” va “uchun” ni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri tarjima qilmang. “Menga mehribon” = kind to me.

friendly to sb — kimgadir xushmuomala; friendly with sb — u bilan do‘st, yaqin munosabatda: She is friendly with her neighbours.

It’s nice to see you — It’s nice of you to come

nice to + fe’l va nice of + kishi + to + fe’l
GapMa’nosi
It’s nice to see you again.Sizni yana ko‘rish yoqimli (men xursandman). Kishi baholanmayapti.
It’s nice of you to come.Kelganingiz — sizning yaxshiligingiz. Siz baholanyapsiz va minnatdorlik bildirilyapti.

O‘zbekchada “Sizning yordam berganingiz juda mehribonlik” deymiz — kishi egalik qo‘shimchasi (-ingiz) bilan beriladi. Inglizchada bu o‘rinda egalik emas, of + kishi ishlatiladi: It was kind of you. ❌ It was kind from you / for you / with you. Qiyinlik haqida gapirganda esa for keladi: It was difficult for me to wait (Unit 65). Qoida oddiy: xarakter (kind, rude, silly) — of; qiyinlik, muhimlik (easy, hard, important) — for.

Angry, annoyed, furious: about va with angry about sth · angry with sb (for doing sth)

annoyed (jahli chiqqan, xafa) — angry (g‘azablangan) — furious (juda g‘azablangan): g‘azab kuchi shu tartibda oshadi. Uchalasi ham bir xil predlog oladi:

annoyed / angry / furious + ?
QolipMisolTarjima
… about + narsa, voqeaDilnoza was furious about the broken window.Dilnoza singan oynadan juda g‘azablandi.
… with + kishiDon’t be angry with me; I didn’t know.Mendan g‘azablanmang; men bilmagan edim.
… with + kishi + for + -ingBekzod is annoyed with his brother for losing the key.Bekzod kalitni yo‘qotgani uchun akasidan jahli chiqqan.
… with + kishi + about + narsaOur neighbour was angry with us about the noise.Qo‘shnimiz shovqin sababli bizdan g‘azablandi.
… that + gap (predlogsiz!)I’m angry that nobody told me.Hech kim menga aytmagani uchun jahlim chiqdi.
  • Are you still annoyed about yesterday’s argument?Kechagi tortishuvdan hali ham xafamisiz?
  • The manager was furious with Aziz for missing the meeting.Aziz yig‘ilishga kelmagani uchun menejer undan qattiq g‘azablandi.
  • I was annoyed about being kept waiting for an hour.Bir soat kutib qolganimdan jahlim chiqdi.
  • Nargiza is angry with herself for forgetting her passport.Nargiza pasportini unutgani uchun o‘ziga g‘azablanyapti.

Amerika ingliz tilida kishiga nisbatan ham ko‘pincha angry at aytiladi (angry at him); narsa uchun esa angry at / about ikkalasi ham uchraydi. Britaniya tilida kishi bilan with eng tabiiy. Imtihonda with sb va about sth deb yozsangiz — hech qachon xato bo‘lmaydi. upset ham shu qolipga yaqin: upset about the result; kishiga nisbatan upset with me ham bo‘ladi.

O‘zbekchada “akamdan xafaman” (-dan) va “akamga g‘azablandim” (-ga) deymiz, ikkalasi ham inglizchada with: angry with my brother. Shuning uchun ❌ angry from him va ❌ angry to him ikkalasi ham xato. Narsa uchun esa shovqindan jahli chiqmoq = annoyed about the noise; ❌ angry with the noise bo‘lmaydi, chunki with faqat kishi bilan.

happy / pleased ABOUT …

Voqea yoki vaziyatdan xursand (shu narsa bo‘lgani yaxshi).

  • I’m happy about the decision.
  • I’m not happy about this. (menga yoqmayapti)

Olgan yoki ega bo‘lgan narsangizdan mamnun.

  • I’m happy with my new phone.
  • The teacher was pleased with our project.

fed up with — norasmiy, “to‘ydim, bezdim”. bored with — standart. Norasmiy Britaniya nutqida bored of va fed up of ham eshitiladi, lekin yozma ishda va imtihonda with yozing. tired of ham xuddi shu ma’noda (Unit 131).

Predlogli tuzilma asosan his-tuyg‘u egasini bildiruvchi -ed sifatlar bilan ishlatiladi: I’m surprised at …, I’m bored with …. Sababni bildiruvchi -ing sifat (surprising, boring, disappointing) narsani tavsiflaydi: The film was boring. Bu farq Unit 98 da.

O‘zbekcha “-dan” bu yerda har xil: xabardan hayron qolmoq = surprised at / by; filmdan zerikmoq = bored with; natijadan hafsalasi pir bo‘lmoq = disappointed with; sizning ishingizdan taassurot olmoq = impressed with / by. Hech birida from yo‘q!

Sorry about, sorry for, feel sorry for apology, sympathy, pity

Siz o‘zingiz yoki sizning sababingizdan sodir bo‘lgan narsa uchun uzr so‘rayapsiz.

  • Sorry for the late reply.
  • I’m sorry for interrupting.

sorry for + kishi → achinish

Biror kishining og‘ir ahvoliga achinasiz; uzr so‘ramayapsiz.

  • I feel sorry for the tired farmers.
  • Don’t feel sorry for yourself.
  • I’m sorry about the smell of paint on the stairs; we’re decorating our flat.Zinapoyadagi bo‘yoq hidi uchun uzr; kvartiramizni ta’mirlayapmiz.
  • Bekzod is sorry for losing your book. He’ll buy a new one.Bekzod kitobingizni yo‘qotgani uchun afsusda. U yangisini sotib oladi.
  • We feel sorry for the farmers after the frost destroyed their crops.Sovuq hosillarni nobud qilgandan keyin dehqonlarga achinamiz.
  • I’m sorry (that) I interrupted you.Gapingizni bo‘lganim uchun uzr.

Rasmiy uzr uchun apologise (to sb) for ishlatiladi: I apologise for the delay. (Unit 132, Unit 135). sorry dan keyin darhol that-gap kelsa, predlog qo‘yilmaydi: ❌ sorry about that I was late ✅ sorry (that) I was late yoki sorry about being late.

“Kechikkanim uchun uzr” — uchun = for ✅. “Itlarga achinmoq” — achinish uchun ham for keladi (feel sorry for dogs), ❌ feel sorry to dogs. Hamdardlik bildirishda esa “bu voqeadan afsusdaman” = sorry about ….

Umumiy xarita va predlogdan keyin nima keladi? Quick map; what follows the preposition

Predlogdan keyin: ot, olmosh, -ing yoki that-gap

✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Xato
I’m annoyed about missing the bus.I’m annoyed about to miss the bus.
She is angry with me for lying.She is angry with me for to lie.
He is nervous about flying.He is nervous about to fly.
I’m sorry that I shouted. / I’m sorry about shouting.I’m sorry about that I shouted.
We are worried that he will be late.We are worried about that he will be late.

Qoida: predlogdan keyin fe’l bo‘lsa — -ing (Unit 60); gap bo‘lsa — that bilan, predlogsiz (angry that, sorry that, worried that), yoki whether/so‘roq so‘zi bilan predlog qoladi: worried about whether …

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

It was kind from you to help me.It was kind of you to help me.Xatti-harakatni baholashda “of + kishi” ishlatiladi. O‘zbekcha “sizning mehribonligingiz” ni from/for/with deb tarjima qilmang.
She is very rude with her teacher.She is very rude to her teacher.Kishiga munosabat — kind to, rude to, polite to, friendly to. “with” emas.
Why are you angry to me?Why are you angry with me?“Menga g‘azablandi” dagi “-ga” inglizchada with. Kishi bilan — angry with, narsa uchun — angry about.
I’m angry with the noise outside.I’m angry about the noise outside.With faqat kishi bilan ishlatiladi. Shovqin, kechikish, qaror kabi narsalar uchun — about.
We are worried from the exam results.We are worried about the exam results.“-dan xavotirlanmoq” inglizchada worried about. Excited, nervous, upset ham about bilan.
I was very surprised from his answer.I was very surprised at (by) his answer.“-dan hayron qolmoq” — surprised at yoki by. From ishlatilmaydi.
I’m bored from this lesson.I’m bored with this lesson.“-dan zerikmoq” — bored with (fed up with). Norasmiy nutqda bored of ham bor, lekin from yo‘q.
The teacher was disappointed from our work.The teacher was disappointed with our work.Hafsalasi pir bo‘ldi — disappointed with (kishiga ishonch yo‘qolsa, in: disappointed in you).
I’m sorry about that I was late.I’m sorry (that) I was late. / I’m sorry about being late.that-gapdan oldin predlog qo‘yilmaydi. Predlogdan keyin esa -ing (yoki ot) keladi.
I feel sorry about the lost puppy.I feel sorry for the lost puppy.Achinish — feel sorry for + kishi/jonivor. “About” uzr yoki hamdardlik uchun.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“kind of you” bilan “kind to you” ning farqi nimada?

It was kind of you to help — siz bitta aniq ishni qildingiz (yordam berdingiz) va men shu ishni baholayapman, minnatdorman. He is kind to you — u sizga qanday munosabatda ekanini aytyapman (mehribon). Birinchisida oxirida odatda to + fe’l keladi, ikkinchisida kishi. Taqqoslang: It was kind of Malika to help us. — Malika was kind to us.

“It’s nice to see you” va “It’s nice of you to come” — qaysi birini qachon aytaman?

Uchrashganda, o‘z xursandchiligingizni aytsangiz — It’s nice to see you. Kelgani yoki biror ish qilgani uchun odamga minnatdorchilik bildirsangiz — It’s nice of you to come. Ikkinchisida “siz yaxshi ish qildingiz” degan baho bor, shuning uchun of you shart.

angry with, angry at va angry about — imtihonda qaysi birini yozaman?

Kishi uchun angry with (Amerika ingliz tilida ko‘pincha angry at ham), voqea yoki narsa uchun angry about. Shu ikkisini yozsangiz, xato bo‘lmaydi. Kim nima qilgani uchun g‘azablangan bo‘lsa — with sb for doing sth. Umumiy qoida: kishi → with; narsa → about.

surprised at bilan surprised by ning farqi bormi?

Ma’no jihatidan farq yo‘q, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va tabiiy. shocked, amazed, astonished ham xuddi shunday: at yoki by. Yodlash uchun bittasini tanlang (masalan, at) va shuni ishlating; ikkinchisini eshitganda tanib oling.

disappointed with, in, by va about — qaysi birini tanlayman?

Aniq bilmasangiz, disappointed with deng — u narsa va odam uchun ham ishlaydi. Agar kishi sizning ishonchingizni oqlamagan bo‘lsa, disappointed in ko‘proq aytiladi (I’m disappointed in you). by — voqea sabab bo‘lganda; about — vaziyat haqida (disappointed about missing the match).

“bored of” va “fed up of” ni ishlatish mumkinmi?

Norasmiy Britaniya nutqida eshitiladi. Lekin standart va xavfsiz shakl — bored with va fed up with. Imtihon va yozma ishda faqat with yozing. tired of (Unit 131) esa har doim of bilan.

sorry about va sorry for — qanday farqlayman?

Uzr so‘raganda ular ko‘pincha almashinadi: sorry about / for the delay. Ammo uch farq bor: (1) sorry about hamdardlik ham bildiradi (sorry about your accident); (2) sorry for sb = kishiga achinmoq (feel sorry for the dogs); (3) sorry for doing — o‘zingiz qilgan ish uchun uzr (sorry for waking you). I’m sorry uzr, I apologise esa rasmiyroq.

“excited about” o‘rniga “excited for” desa bo‘ladimi?

Amerika nutqida I’m so excited for you! (“sen uchun xursandman”) kabi gaplar uchraydi. Lekin nima haqida hayajonda ekanini aytsangiz, standart ibora — excited about (excited about the trip). Imtihonda about yozing.

surprised bilan surprising ning farqi nima?

surprised — his-tuyg‘u egasi (men hayron bo‘ldim): I was surprised at the news. surprising — hayron qoldiradigan narsa: The news was surprising. Predlogli tuzilma (at, by, with, about) asosan -ed sifatlar bilan. Batafsil — Unit 98.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga of yoki to yozing.

  1. It was very kind Malika to carry my heavy bags.
  2. The shop assistant was very polite every customer.
  3. How careless me to leave my wallet on the bus!
  4. Why are you so rude your little sister?
  5. It was silly them to go out in the snow without coats.
  6. Our new neighbours are very friendly us.
  7. It was generous you to give your seat to the old man.
  8. Please be kind animals.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidan to‘g‘ri predlogni tanlang.

  1. Dilnoza was furious (about / with / for) the delay at the airport.
  2. Don’t be angry (about / with / for) me; it wasn’t my fault.
  3. Sardor is annoyed (about / with / for) his brother for losing the key.
  4. Bekzod was angry with his friend (of / to / for) telling a lie.
  5. Are you still upset (about / with / for) yesterday’s argument?
  6. I was annoyed (about / with / for) being kept waiting for an hour.
  7. The manager was furious with Aziz (of / to / for) forgetting the meeting.
  8. Nobody is angry (about / with / for) you. Everyone understands.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • It was kind / rude / silly of you to … — bitta ishni baholash; kind / rude / friendly to sb — kishiga munosabat. ❌ kind with me, ❌ kind from you.
  • angry / annoyed / furious: with + kishi (+ for -ing), about + narsa. ❌ angry to him, ❌ angry from him.
  • excited, worried, nervous, upset, happy + about. ❌ worried from. happy about (vaziyatdan xursand) ≠ happy with (olgan narsangizdan mamnun).
  • surprised, shocked, amazed, astonished + at / by; impressed + with / by; disappointed + with (in sb, by sth, about a situation); fed up / bored + with.
  • sorry about / for + narsa; sorry for doing; sorry (that) I did (predlogsiz); sorry to hear; feel sorry for sb = achinmoq.
  • O‘zbekcha “-dan” har doim from emas: xafa — with, xavotir — about, hayron — at/by, zerikish — with.
  • Predlogdan keyin fe’l — -ing; that-gapdan oldin predlog yo‘q.
