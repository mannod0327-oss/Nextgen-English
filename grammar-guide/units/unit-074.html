The 2 (school / the school etc.)

The — 2 (school / the school)
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • school / the school farqini: o‘quvchi sifatida o‘qish uchun borish (artiklsiz) va binoning o‘ziga boshqa sabab bilan borish (the bilan).
  • Xuddi shu qoida university, college, hospital, prison, church, court uchun ham amal qilishini.
  • bed, work, home so‘zlari bilan the ishlatilmasligini: go to bed, go to work, go home.
  • sea va town: go to sea / go to the sea, go into town.
  • Britaniya va Amerika ingliz tilidagi farqni: in hospital (BrE) va in the hospital (AmE).

Ba’zi so‘zlar bino ham, muassasa (uning asosiy vazifasi) ham bo‘la oladi. Agar biz muassasaga o‘z vazifasi bo‘yicha borsak (o‘quvchi — o‘qishga, bemor — davolanishga, mahbus — jazo o‘tashga), artikl qo‘yilmaydi. Agar bino yoki joy boshqa sabab bilan kerak bo‘lsa, the aytiladi.

O‘zbekcha maktabga bordim ikki xil ma’noda bo‘lishi mumkin: (1) o‘quvchi sifatida, dars uchun; (2) maktab binosiga, masalan, direktor bilan gaplashish uchun. O‘zbekchada ikkalasi bir xil, ingliz tilida esa farq qilinadi: I went to school (1) va I went to the school (2). Uyga bordim esa har doim I went home bo‘ladi (to home ❌): uyga dagi “-ga” inglizcha to ga o‘tkazilmaydi, home o‘zi yo‘nalishni bildiradi.

School / the school Going to school as a student, or to the school building

Maktab — bu bino, lekin ayni paytda o‘qish joyi (muassasa). Gap qaysi ma’no haqida ketayotganiga qarab, artikl o‘zgaradi:

  • Bekzod left school at seventeen and got a job in a bank.Bekzod maktabni o‘n yetti yoshida tugatib, bankda ishga kirdi.
  • The children play football in the yard after school.Bolalar maktabdan keyin hovlida futbol o‘ynashadi.
  • Parents’ evening will be held at the school on Thursday.Ota-onalar yig‘ilishi payshanba kuni maktabda bo‘ladi. (bino — yig‘ilish joyi)

Qoida: o‘quvchi sifatida — go to school, be at school, start school, leave school, after school (the siz). Ota-ona, mehmon, ishchi sifatida yoki bino haqida — the school.

University, hospital, prison, church: xuddi shu qoida university / hospital / prison / church / court

Maktab bilan bir xil mantiq boshqa muassasalarga ham tegishli: biz u yerga muassasaning o‘z vazifasi uchun borsak (talaba — o‘qishga, bemor — davolanishga, mahbus — jazo o‘tashga, dindor — ibodatga) — the yo‘q; boshqa sabab bilan borsak — the.

Qoida: school, university, college, hospital, prison, church, court so‘zlari muassasaning asosiy vazifasi haqida gapirilganda the siz ishlatiladi. Bino sifatida yoki boshqa sabab bilan borilganda the (yoki a) aytiladi.

Siz u yerda kimsiz?

Savolni shunday qo‘ying: “Men bu joyga qanday rolda kelyapman?” Rol — muassasaning o‘z vazifasiga mos kelsa, artikl kerak emas.

Bu ro‘yxatdagi so‘zlar bilan the faqat asosiy vazifa bo‘lmaganda qo‘yiladi. Boshqa bino nomlari (the bank, the post office, the library) bu guruhga kirmaydi — ular odatdagidek ishlatiladi (Unit 73).

Ish joyini ko‘rsatganda artikl qaytadi: Onam kasalxonada ishlaydi = My mother works in a hospital (yoki aniq kasalxona bo‘lsa, at the hospital). Chunki onam u yerda bemor emas, xodim. In hospital (artiklsiz, BrE) — faqat bemor sifatida yotish.

Diniy joylar haqida: go to church — ingliz tilida eng barqaror ibora. Masjid uchun odatda the bilan aytiladi: go to the mosque; go to mosque shakli ham uchraydi (ayniqsa musulmonlar nutqida), lekin the mosque hammaga tushunarli va ishonchli variant.

O‘zbekchada: uyga bordim, uyda o‘tirdim, uydan chiqdim — har biri alohida kelishik bilan. Inglizchada home bu yerda ravish kabi ishlaydi: go home (go to home ❌), come home, lekin be at home (bu yerda at kerak). Yotish uchun: yotdim = I went to bed; kasal yotibdi = He’s in bed.

be (at) home, stay (at) home: “uyda bo‘lmoq / qolmoq” ma’nosida at ni tushirib qoldirish ham mumkin, ayniqsa so‘zlashuvda va AmE da: Will you be home tomorrow? Let’s stay home tonight. To‘liq va eng ishonchli shakl — be at home, stay at home. Lekin go to home hech qaysi variantda to‘g‘ri emas.

Work va office bir narsa emas. I’m at work — ish joyidaman (qayerda bo‘lmasin: ofisda, zavodda, do‘konda). I’m in the office — aynan ofis xonasidaman. Office — oddiy ot, shuning uchun the / a bilan: the office, an office. Work — sanalmaydigan ot, job esa sanaladigan: a job, lekin a work ❌.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “go to school” deymiz-u, lekin “go to the shop” yoki “go to the bank” deymiz?

Maktab, kasalxona, qamoqxona kabi so‘zlar bir guruh muassasalar bo‘lib, ularning “asosiy vazifasi” aniq: o‘qish, davolanish, jazo o‘tash. Do‘kon, bank, pochta esa shunchaki joylar — ular bilan oddiy qoida: the (Unit 73). Do‘konga “do‘kon vazifasi” bilan borish degan alohida ma’no yo‘q.

Kasalxonada ishlasam ham “in hospital” deymanmi?

Yo‘q. In hospital (the siz) — faqat bemor sifatida yotish. Xodim bo‘lsangiz: My mother works in a hospital yoki She works at the hospital. Xuddi shunday, o‘qituvchi maktabda works at a school, lekin o‘quvchi is at school.

“Home” bilan nega “to” kerak emas?

Chunki home bu yerda otdan ko‘ra ko‘proq ravish (adverb) kabi ishlaydi va o‘zi “uyga” degan yo‘nalishni beradi: go home, come home, get home, bring something home. Xuddi go there (go to there ❌) kabi. At home da esa at kerak, chunki bu joy.

“Work” bilan “job” bir xilmi? “The work” qachon aytiladi?

Yo‘q. Work — sanalmaydigan ot: ish faoliyati, ish joyi yoki bajariladigan ish (I have a lot of work). Job — sanaladigan ot: lavozim, mashg‘ulot (She has a good job). The work — aniq bir ish yoki uning natijasi: The work is finished. Go to work iborasida the yo‘q, chunki bu ishga borish jarayoni.

Universitetning to‘liq nomi bilan the kerakmi? (Tashkent State University, the University of Tashkent)

Atoqli otlar qoidasi alohida (Unit 77 va Unit 78). Qisqacha: Tashkent State University (the siz), lekin the University of Tashkent (of bilan the). Bu dars qoidalari esa umumiy gapda ishlaydi: go to university — universitetda o‘qish.

Masjid uchun “go to mosque” deyish mumkinmi?

Ba’zi odamlar go to mosque deydi (go to church ga qiyos qilib), lekin eng ishonchli va hammaga tushunarli variant — the mosque: My grandfather goes to the mosque on Fridays. Imtihonda va yozma ishda shuni tanlang.

“In hospital” va “in the hospital” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, faqat variant farq qiladi. BrE: bemor sifatida in hospital, tashrif buyurgan yoki ishlovchi sifatida in / at the hospital. AmE: har ikki holatda ham in the hospital. Imtihonda “British English” so‘ralsa, birinchi qoida bo‘yicha javob bering.

“Go to sea” va “go to the sea” — ma’nosi farq qiladimi?

Ha. Go to sea = dengizchi bo‘lmoq (He wants to go to sea when he grows up). Go to the sea = dengiz qirg‘og‘iga, dam olgani bormoq (We go to the sea every summer). At sea = kemada, reysda.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • school, university, college, hospital, prison, church, court — o‘z vazifasi bo‘yicha: the siz (go to school, be in hospital, go to prison).
  • Bino yoki boshqa sabab bilan: the (I went to the school to see the headteacher).
  • Sifat yoki aniqlovchi gap qo‘shilsa, a / the qaytadi (a good school, the hospital where she works).
  • go to bed, be in bed; go to work, be at work; go home, be at home — the va to (home bilan) kerak emas.
  • go to sea = dengizchi bo‘lmoq; go to the sea = dengiz bo‘yiga bormoq; go into town = shahar markaziga bormoq.
  • Work — sanalmaydigan ot; the work — aniq ish; bitta lavozim — a job.
  • BrE: in hospital, at university; AmE: in the hospital, in college.
  • Xodim sifatida: works in a hospital / at the school (artikl bilan).
