54

Verb + to ... (decide to ... / forget to ... etc.)

Fe’l + to ... (decide to / forget to)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Qaysi fe’llardan keyin ikkinchi fe’l to + fe’l (infinitiv) shaklida keladi: decide to go, hope to see, refuse to pay.
  • Bunday fe’llarni ma’no bo‘yicha guruhlab eslab qolishni: qaror, va’da, umid, uddalash, ko‘rinish.
  • Inkor shaklini: decide not to go.
  • know how to, ask what to, decide whether to kabi “so‘roq so‘zi + to” tuzilmasini.
  • Unit 53 dagi -ing bilan bu darsdagi to ni qanday ajratishni.

Ba’zi fe’llar o‘zidan keyin ikkinchi fe’lni to bilan talab qiladi: I decided to go. Bu yerda to — predlog emas, infinitiv belgisi; undan keyin fe’lning asosiy shakli (V1) turadi: to go, to see, to pay. Bunday fe’llar odatda hali bajarilmagan ishga qaraydi: qaror, reja, va’da, umid, istak.

O‘zbek tilida borishga qaror qildim, borishni xohlayman, borishni yoqtiraman — hammasi bir xil “-ish” shakliga o‘xshab ko‘rinadi. Shu sababli o‘quvchilar inglizchada ham hammasi bir xil bo‘ladi deb o‘ylaydi. Aslida ingliz tilida fe’lning o‘ziga qarab ikkinchi fe’l yo to + fe’l, yo -ing bo‘ladi: want to go, lekin enjoy going (Unit 53). Yechim — fe’lni o‘z qolipi bilan birga yodlash.

Fe’l + to + fe’l Verb + to ...

Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin ikkinchi fe’l to + fe’l shaklida keladi. Ikkala ishni ham odatda bir kishi bajaradi: Malika decided to go — qaror qilgan ham, boradigan ham Malika.

Ega+decide / hope / refuse ...+to+fe’l (V1)+(to‘ldiruvchi)
  • Malika decided to study medicine.Malika tibbiyotni o‘qishga qaror qildi.
  • We planned to visit Khiva in spring.Biz bahorda Xivaga borishni rejalashtirgan edik.
  • Jasur promised to call me back.Jasur menga qayta qo‘ng‘iroq qilishga va’da berdi.
  • I can’t afford to buy a new phone.Yangi telefon sotib olishga qurbim yetmaydi.
  • He refused to say anything.U hech narsa deyishdan bosh tortdi.
  • We managed to catch the last bus.Biz oxirgi avtobusga ulgurdik.

Qoida: Zamon va shaxs faqat birinchi fe’lda o‘zgaradi (decides, decided). to dan keyingi fe’l esa hamisha asosiy shaklda turadi: She decided to go ✅ — She decided to went / to goes / to going ❌.

Vaqt chizig‘ida: qaror va keyingi ish

To‘ldiruvchi infinitivning ishi odatda birinchi fe’ldan keyin bajariladi yoki hali bajarilmagan bo‘ladi. Shu sababli decide, plan, hope, promise, offer, agree kabi fe’llar bilan to juda tabiiy:

Fe’llar ma’no guruhlari bo‘yicha

📋 Qaror va reja

  • decide — qaror qilmoq: He decided to stay.
  • plan — rejalashtirmoq: We plan to move in May.
  • intend — niyat qilmoq: I intend to apply.
  • arrange — kelishmoq: They arranged to meet at six.
  • prepare — tayyorlanmoq: She is preparing to leave.
  • choose — tanlamoq: He chose to walk.
  • mean (= intend) — niyat qilmoq: I didn’t mean to upset you.

🤝 Va’da, taklif, rozilik, rad etish

  • promise — va’da bermoq: She promised to write.
  • offer — taklif qilmoq (yordam): Bekzod offered to help.
  • agree — rozi bo‘lmoq: They agreed to wait.
  • refuse — rad etmoq: He refused to pay.
  • threaten — po‘pisa qilmoq: She threatened to leave.

🌟 Istak va umid

  • hope — umid qilmoq: I hope to see you soon.
  • want — xohlamoq: Nodira wants to be a doctor.
  • expect — kutmoq, deb o‘ylamoq: We expect to arrive at noon.
  • would like / would love — xohlar edim: I’d like to come. (Unit 58)

💪 Uddalash, o‘rganish, imkon

  • manage — uddalamoq: We managed to get tickets.
  • fail — uddalay olmaslik, qilmaslik: He failed to notice the sign.
  • forget — unutmoq: Don’t forget to call.
  • learn — o‘rganmoq: Kamola is learning to drive.
  • attempt — urinmoq: The man attempted to run away.
  • afford — qurbi yetmoq: We can’t afford to travel abroad.
  • deserve — loyiq bo‘lmoq: You deserve to win.

🎭 Ko‘rinish, tasodif, da’vo

  • seem — ko‘rinmoq: They seem to be lost.
  • appear — ko‘rinmoq: He appears to know everything.
  • pretend — o‘zini ko‘rsatmoq: The boy pretended to be asleep.
  • tend — moyil bo‘lmoq: Prices tend to rise in winter.
  • happen — tasodifan bo‘lmoq: I happened to see him there.
  • claim — da’vo qilmoq: She claims to speak six languages.

⚠️ Ikki xil shakl oladigan fe’llar

  • remember, forget, regret, stop — Unit 56
  • try, need, help — Unit 57
  • begin, start, continue — Unit 56; like, love, hate — Unit 58
  • prefer — Unit 59

Bu fe’llardan keyin ham to, ham -ing kelishi mumkin — ma’no yoki uslub o‘zgaradi.

manage va can/could. We managed to get tickets — “qiyinchilik bilan uddaladik” (bir aniq voqea). Bir marta bajarilgan, qiyin ish haqida o‘tmishda could emas, managed to / was able to deyiladi (Unit 26). Inkorda couldn’t ham to‘g‘ri: We couldn’t get tickets.

Inkor shakli: not to + fe’l

Ikkinchi fe’lni inkor qilish uchun to dan oldin not qo‘yiladi:

  • Sardor decided not to go to the party.Sardor ziyofatga bormaslikka qaror qildi.
  • She promised not to tell anyone.U hech kimga aytmaslikka va’da berdi.
  • We agreed not to be late again.Biz boshqa kechikmaslikka kelishib oldik.
Inkor: ma’no farqiga e’tibor bering
GapMa’nosi
He decided to go.U borishga qaror qildi.
He decided not to go.U bormaslikka qaror qildi (qaror qabul qilingan, qarori — bormaslik).
He didn’t decide to go.U borishga qaror qilmadi (borish haqida qaror umuman qabul qilinmagan).

Kundalik nutqda ba’zan decided to not go deyiladi, lekin o‘qish va yozishda, imtihonlarda not to + fe’l (decided not to go) shaklini qo‘llang — u har doim to‘g‘ri.

Boshqa infinitiv shakllari (qisqacha)

Ba’zan to dan keyin faqat V1 emas, be + -ing, have + V3 yoki be + V3 ham keladi. Ular ayniqsa seem, appear, claim, hope, happen bilan uchraydi:

To dan keyingi shakllar
ShaklMisolO‘zbekcha
to be + -ing (hozir jarayonda)He seems to be sleeping.U uxlayotganga o‘xshaydi.
to have + V3 (oldin bo‘lgan)She seems to have forgotten my name.U ismimni unutganga o‘xshaydi.
to have + V3He claims to have met the president.U prezident bilan uchrashganini da’vo qiladi.
to be + V3 (majhul)I hope to be invited.Meni taklif qilishlariga umid qilaman.

dare (jur’at qilmoq)

dare dan keyin to bilan ham, to siz ham aytish mumkin. Lekin daren’t (= dare not) dan keyin faqat to siz keladi:

  • I wouldn’t dare to argue with my grandfather. / I wouldn’t dare argue with my grandfather.Bobom bilan bahslashishga jur’at qilmasdim.
  • I daren’t look down. (daren’t to look ❌)Pastga qarashga yuragim betlamayapti.

Fe’l + so‘roq so‘zi + to Verb + question word + to ...

Ba’zi fe’llardan keyin what, where, how, which, who, when, whether kabi so‘z va to + fe’l keladi. Bu tuzilma o‘zbekchadagi “nima qilishni”, “qayerga borishni”, “qanday ishlatishni” iboralariga to‘g‘ri keladi.

Ega+know / ask / decide / tell ...+what / where / how / whether ...+to+fe’l (V1)
  • I don’t know what to do.Nima qilishni bilmayman.
  • Can you tell me where to catch the bus to the airport?Aeroportga boradigan avtobusga qayerdan chiqishni ayta olasizmi?
  • My brother showed me how to use the new washing machine.Akam menga yangi kir yuvish mashinasidan qanday foydalanishni ko‘rsatdi.
  • We can’t decide whether to buy it or not.Uni sotib olish-olmaslikni hal qila olmayapmiz.
  • I wonder who to invite to the wedding.To‘yga kimlarni taklif qilishni o‘ylayman.
  • She hasn’t decided which dress to wear.U qaysi ko‘ylakni kiyishni hali hal qilgani yo‘q.
Bu tuzilma bilan keladigan fe’llar
Fe’lMisol
know, have no idea, can’t thinkI have no idea what to buy her.
ask, wonder, find outAsk the driver where to get off.
decide, can’t decideThey haven’t decided where to have the party.
tell / show / teach + kim?Please tell me when to start.
learn, understand, remember, forgetI forgot how to open this app.
explain, discoverCan you explain how to log in?

Diqqat! 1) how dan keyin to kerak: I know how to swim ✅ — I know to swim ❌ (bu “suzishni bilaman” degan ma’noni bermaydi). 2) why bilan to ishlatilmaydi: I don’t know why I should go ✅. 3) if bilan ham odatda to kelmaydi: I wonder whether to go ✅ yoki I wonder if I should go ✅.

O‘zbekcha “nima qilishni” — bitta qo‘shimcha (-ni). Inglizchada u so‘roq so‘zi + to + fe’l: what to do. Ba’zan o‘quvchilar “-ni” ni -ing deb tarjima qiladi: ❌ I don’t know what doing. To‘g‘risi: I don’t know what to do.

Faqat “to” bilan tugagan gaplar Leaving out the verb after to

Fe’l oldin aytilgan bo‘lsa, takrorlamaslik uchun uni tushirib, faqat to ni qoldiramiz. Bu so‘zlashuvda juda ko‘p uchraydi:

  • A: Are you coming with us?
    B: I’d love to, but I have to work.    — Biz bilan borasizmi? — Juda xohlardim, lekin ishlashim kerak.
  • I didn’t phone her because I didn’t want to.Unga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadim, chunki xohlamadim.
  • You can borrow my bike if you want to.Xohlasang, velosipedimni olib turishing mumkin.
  • He passed the exam, although he didn’t expect to.U imtihondan o‘tdi, garchi kutmagan bo‘lsa ham.

Qoida: Fe’lni tushirsangiz ham, to ni tushirmang: I’d love to. ✅ — I’d love. ❌; I don’t want to. ✅ — I don’t want. ❌.

To yoki -ing: qanday tanlayman? To ... or -ing?

Fe’l aniq ro‘yxatda bo‘lmasa, quyidagi jadval yordam beradi. Ikkala ustundagi fe’llar ma’no jihatidan yaqin, lekin qolipi har xil:

to + fe’l (shu dars) va -ing (Unit 53)
to + fe’l-ing
I want to swim.I enjoy swimming.
She decided to read.She finished reading.
He refused to answer.He avoided answering.
They agreed to go.They suggested going.
We managed to walk home.We kept walking.
I offered to wait.I don’t mind waiting.

Taxminiy yordamchi qoida (istisnolari bor): reja, istak, qaror, va’da ma’nosidagi fe’llar odatda to bilan; zavq, qochish, to‘xtatish, tugatish, davom etish ma’nosidagilar -ing bilan keladi. Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, fe’lni lug‘atdan tekshiring.

Ikkita “to” ni aralashtirmang. Bu darsdagi to — infinitiv belgisi (decide to go). Boshqa to — predlog va undan keyin -ing keladi: look forward to seeing you, be used to working (Unit 60–62). Sinov: shu joyga it qo‘ysa bo‘ladimi? look forward to it ✅ — demak predlog → -ing; decide to it ❌ — demak infinitiv → V1.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I decided go to Samarkand.I decided to go to Samarkand.decide dan keyin to shart. O‘zbekchada “-ishga” o‘zi yetarli, inglizchada to kerak.
She decided to went home.She decided to go home.to dan keyin fe’l asosiy shaklda (V1). Zamon faqat decided da ko‘rsatiladi.
He refused answering.He refused to answer.refuse + to + fe’l. (deny + -ing bilan adashtirmang.)
We managed finding a taxi.We managed to find a taxi.manage + to + fe’l.
I can’t afford buying a car.I can’t afford to buy a car.afford + to + fe’l.
I hope seeing you soon.I hope to see you soon.hope + to + fe’l (yoki I hope I’ll see you soon).
She decided to don’t go.She decided not to go.Infinitivning inkori: not to + V1. don’t bu yerda ishlatilmaydi.
I don’t know what do.I don’t know what to do.So‘roq so‘zidan keyin to + V1 kerak.
I know to swim.I know how to swim.“Qanday qilishni bilaman” = know how to.
I can’t decide if to go.I can’t decide whether to go. / I can’t decide if I should go.if bilan to-infinitiv ishlatilmaydi; whether to deyiladi.
I enjoy to watch films.I enjoy watching films.enjoy — -ing fe’li (Unit 53), to emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi fe’l “to” bilan, qaysisi “-ing” bilan kelishini qanday bilaman?

Aniq qoida yo‘q, shuning uchun fe’lni qolipi bilan yodlaysiz: decide to, enjoy -ing. Yordam beradigan belgi: reja, qaror, istak, va’da, umid ma’nosidagi fe’llar odatda to bilan keladi (ish hali bajarilmagan). Yuqoridagi besh ma’no guruhini yodlab oling va Unit 53 dagi -ing guruhlari bilan solishtiring.

Bu yerdagi “to” predlogmi?

Yo‘q. Bu infinitiv belgisi, undan keyin fe’lning asosiy shakli keladi: decide to go. Predlog to dan keyin esa ot yoki -ing turadi: look forward to going (Unit 61–62). Farqlash uchun it sinovidan foydalaning (D bo‘lim).

“hope” dan keyin boshqa kishini qo‘yib “I hope you to come” deysam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. hope dan keyin “kim + to” kelmaydi. To‘g‘ri variantlar: I hope to see you. (men ko‘raman) yoki I hope (that) you will come. (siz kelasiz). “I want you to come” esa to‘g‘ri — bu Unit 55 da.

“forget to do” va “forget doing” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. forget to do — qilishni esdan chiqarish (qilmadingiz): I forgot to lock the door (eshikni qulflamadim). forget doing — qilganingizni eslay olmaslik, odatda inkorda: I’ll never forget meeting her (uni uchratganimni hech qachon unutmayman). Batafsil — Unit 56.

“managed to” va “could” ning farqi nima?

could umumiy qobiliyatni bildiradi: I could swim when I was five. managed to / was able to esa bir marta, qiyinchilik bilan bajarilgan aniq ishni bildiradi: We managed to open the door. Bunday ijobiy gapda We could open the door deyilmaydi (inkorda couldn’t to‘g‘ri). Batafsil — Unit 26.

Nega “why to do” deb bo‘lmaydi?

Ingliz tilida why to-infinitiv bilan odatda ishlatilmaydi. I don’t know why to go ❌ o‘rniga I don’t know why I should go yoki I don’t know why we’re going deyiladi. Qolgan so‘roq so‘zlari (what, where, how, which, who, when, whether) esa to bilan ishlatiladi.

“I’d love to.” — nega gap “to” bilan tugaydi?

Chunki fe’l oldingi gapda aytilgan va uni takrorlash shart emas: — Are you coming? — I’d love to (come). Bunda to ni tashlab bo‘lmaydi (C bo‘lim).

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (to + fe’l yoki -ing).

  1. Malika decided (study) medicine.
  2. They agreed (meet) at six o’clock.
  3. He refused (answer) my question.
  4. I can’t afford (buy) a new laptop.
  5. Jasur promised (call) me tonight.
  6. We managed (find) a taxi in the rain.
  7. Aziz enjoys (play) chess.
  8. The shop failed (deliver) my order on time.

2-mashq. Gapni not to yordamida yakunlang.

  1. “I won’t tell anyone,” said Sardor. → Sardor promised anyone.
  2. “I won’t forget your birthday again,” said Nodira. → Nodira promised my birthday again.
  3. Bekzod thought, “I won’t go to the party.” → Bekzod decided to the party.
  4. “Let’s not wait any longer,” they said. → They agreed any longer.

3-mashq. Ikki gapni bitta gapga birlashtiring (so‘roq so‘zi + to).

  1. I don’t know. What should I do? → I don’t know .
  2. Please tell me. Where should I park the car? → Please tell me .
  3. Could you tell me? How can I get to the Registan? → Could you tell me ?
  4. We can’t decide. Should we wait or leave? → We can’t decide .
  5. I wonder. Who should I ask? → I wonder .
  6. Jasur doesn’t know. Which road should we take? → Jasur doesn’t know .

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang va yozing.

  1. She suggested (to go / going) by train.
  2. We hope (to see / seeing) you again soon.
  3. Have you finished (to write / writing) the report?
  4. He pretended (to be / being) ill.
  5. Do you mind (to wait / waiting) a minute?
  6. Dilnoza is learning (to drive / driving).
  7. They kept (to talk / talking) all night.

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘ri yozing.

  1. He decided to went home. →
  2. I don’t know what do. →
  3. We managed finding a seat. →
  4. She decided to don’t go. →
  5. I know to swim. →
  6. I hope seeing you soon. →

6-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Men yangi ish qidirishga qaror qildim. →
  2. Bolalar shovqin qilmaslikka va’da berishdi. →
  3. Menga bu dasturni qanday ishlatishni ko‘rsating. →
  4. Biz uchrashuvga ulgurdik. (managed bilan) →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Fe’l + to + V1: decide, plan, intend, arrange, choose, promise, offer, agree, refuse, threaten, hope, want, expect, manage, fail, forget, learn, afford, deserve, seem, appear, pretend, tend, happen, claim.
  • to dan keyin fe’l asosiy shaklda: decided to go (to went ❌). Zamon faqat birinchi fe’lda.
  • Inkor: not to + V1 (decided not to go). didn’t decide to go — ma’nosi boshqa: qaror hali yo‘q.
  • So‘roq so‘zi + to: what / where / how / which / who / when / whether + to + V1. why va if bilan to ishlatilmaydi.
  • know how to (know to ❌).
  • Fe’l takrorlanmasa, to qoladi: I’d love to.
  • -ing talab qiladigan fe’llar (enjoy, finish, avoid, suggest, mind, keep) bilan to ishlatilmaydi — Unit 53.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 26 (can, could, be able to), Unit 53 (fe’l + -ing), Unit 55 (fe’l + kim? + to), Unit 56 va Unit 57 (to yoki -ing: remember, try, need, help), Unit 58 (like, would like), Unit 60 (predlog + -ing), Unit 64 (maqsad: to, for, so that), Unit 65 (sifat + to).
