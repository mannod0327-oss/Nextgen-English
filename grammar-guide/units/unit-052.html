Question tags (do you? isn't it? etc.)

Ajratilgan so‘roq (question tags)
Questions and auxiliary verbs · Savollar va yordamchi fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Question tag (ajratilgan so‘roq) nima ekanini va qanday yasalishini: You’re Aziz’s brother, aren’t you?
  • Asosiy qoidani: ijobiy gap → inkor tag, inkor gap → ijobiy tag.
  • Maxsus holatlarni: I am → aren’t I?, Let’s → shall we?, buyruq → will you?, nobody / never → ... do they / do you?
  • Ovoz ohangini: tushuvchi (↘) — rozilikni kutamiz; ko‘tariluvchi (↗) — haqiqiy savol beramiz.
  • Tagga qanday javob berishni: Yes, I do / No, I don’t.

Question tag — gap oxiriga qo‘shiladigan qisqa savol. Uning bilan biz (1) o‘zimiz aytgan gapni tasdiqlashni so‘raymiz yoki (2) suhbatdoshning roziligini kutamiz. Tag har doim yordamchi fe’l + ega olmoshidan iborat: The tea is hot, isn’t it?

O‘zbek tilida bunday vazifani o‘zgarmas so‘zlar bajaradi: ..., shunday emasmi? ..., to‘g‘rimi? ..., a? ..., mi? — Bugun havo yaxshi, shunday emasmi? Ular har qanday gapdan keyin bir xil turadi. Inglizchada esa tag gapga moslashadi: yordamchi fe’l, zamon va ega olmoshi har safar o‘zgaradi — isn’t it? / don’t you? / didn’t they? / can’t he? O‘zbekcha «shunday emasmi» ta’sirida hamma joyda isn’t it? deb qo‘yish xato (standart ingliz tilida tag gapga mos bo‘lishi shart).

Question tagni yasash: ijobiy ↔ inkor How to form a question tag

Tagni yasash uchun gapning yordamchi fe’lini topamiz va uning qarama-qarshi shaklini qo‘yamiz, so‘ng ega o‘rniga olmosh keltiramiz:

Ega + yordamchi fe’l ...,yordamchi fe’l (teskari)+ega olmoshi?

Qoida: Ijobiy gap → inkor tag (It’s cold, isn’t it?). Inkor gap → ijobiy tag (It isn’t cold, is it?). Tag gapdagi yordamchi fe’l bilan bir xil zamonda bo‘ladi. Yordamchi fe’l bo‘lmasa (present simple, past simple) — do / does / did.

Inkor ma’noli so‘zlar

Gapda never, nobody, nothing, no one, nowhere, hardly, scarcely, barely, rarely, seldom, few, little, no + ot bo‘lsa, gap ma’no jihatidan inkor hisoblanadi, garchi not bo‘lmasa ham. Shuning uchun tag ijobiy bo‘ladi:

  • You never eat meat, do you?Siz hech qachon go‘sht yemaysiz, shundaymi?
  • She hardly ever calls, does she?U deyarli hech qachon qo‘ng‘iroq qilmaydi, to‘g‘rimi?
  • Nobody was hurt, were they?Hech kim jarohat olmadi, shunday emasmi?
  • There’s no milk left, is there?Sut qolmagan, shunday emasmi?

have, used to, had better

  • You’ve got a car, haven’t you?Sizda mashina bor, shunday emasmi? (have got → have)
  • You have a car, don’t you?Sizda mashina bor, to‘g‘rimi? (have = ega bo‘lmoq → odatda do/does/did)
  • You have to leave early, don’t you?Erta ketishingiz kerak, shunday emasmi? (have to → do)
  • He used to live here, didn’t he?U ilgari shu yerda yashagan, shunday emasmi?
  • You’d better go, hadn’t you?Ketganingiz ma’qul, shunday emasmi?

’d qisqartmasi ikki xil fe’l bo‘lishi mumkin: had yoki would. Tagni qaysi biriga qarab yasashni ma’noga qarab bilasiz: He’d finished, hadn’t he? (had finished), lekin He’d like some tea, wouldn’t he? (would like).

Ohang va ma’no: ↘ yoki ↗ Intonation and meaning

Bir xil gap va tag ovoz ohangiga qarab ikki xil ma’no beradi:

↘ Tushuvchi ohang

Gap to‘g‘ri deb o‘ylaymiz, faqat tasdiq/rozilik kutamiz. Ko‘pincha suhbatni boshlash uchun:

  • The view is lovely, isn’t it? ↘
  • Malika is very clever, isn’t she? ↘

↗ Ko‘tariluvchi ohang

Aniq bilmaymiz, haqiqiy axborot so‘raymiz:

  • You haven’t seen my keys, have you? ↗
  • You’re Jasur’s cousin, aren’t you? ↗

Inkor gap + ijobiy tag: muloyim so‘rov

Biror narsa so‘rash yoki ma’lumot olish uchun ko‘pincha inkor gap + ijobiy tag (↗) ishlatiladi. Bu juda muloyim eshitiladi:

  • You couldn’t lend me your charger, could you?Zaryadlovchingizni berib turolmaysizmi?
  • You don’t know where Aziz is, do you?Aziz qayerdaligini bilmaysizmi?

Ijobiy gap + ijobiy tag

Ba’zan gap ham, tag ham ijobiy bo‘ladi. Bunda so‘zlovchi aytilgan gapga qiziqish, hayrat, shubha yoki istehzo bildiradi:

  • So you’re going to Bukhara, are you?Demak, Buxoroga ketyapsiz-da, a? (qiziqish)
  • Oh, you’ve lost your phone, have you?Voy, telefoningizni yo‘qotibsiz-da, a? (hayrat)
  • You think that’s funny, do you?Buni kulgili deb o‘ylaysizmi? (norozilik/istehzo)

Bunday ijobiy + ijobiy tag odatda yangi eshitilgan yoki xulosa qilingan ma’lumotga munosabat sifatida ishlatiladi. Oddiy tasdiq so‘rash uchun esa har doim ijobiy ↔ inkor qoidasini qo‘llang.

Tagga javob berish Answering question tags

Tagga Yes yoki No bilan javob berasiz. Muhim qoida: Yes dan keyin ijobiy fe’l, No dan keyin inkor fe’l keladi — bu savolning shakliga emas, sizning haqiqiy javobingizga bog‘liq.

SavolAslida shundayJavob
You like Chinese food, don’t you?YoqtirasizYes, I do.
You like Chinese food, don’t you?YoqtirmaysizNo, I don’t.
You aren’t coming, are you?KelasizYes, I am. (= kelaman)
You aren’t coming, are you?KelmaysizNo, I’m not. (= kelmayman)
You don’t mind, do you?Qarshi emassizNo, I don’t (mind).
You don’t mind, do you?QarshisizYes, I do (mind).

Bu yerda o‘zbek va ingliz tillari farq qilishi mumkin. O‘zbek nutqida inkor savolga ba’zan «Ha, kelmayman» deb javob beriladi (“ha” bu yerda “siz to‘g‘ri aytdingiz” ma’nosida). Inglizchada esa Yes faqat ijobiy javobga mos keladi: You aren’t coming, are you? — kelmasangiz, No, I’m not deyishingiz kerak; Yes desangiz, “kelaman” degan ma’no chiqadi.

  • “You haven’t finished, have you?” “No, I haven’t. I need ten more minutes.”— Tugatmagansiz, shunday emasmi? — Yo‘q, tugatmadim. Yana o‘n daqiqa kerak.
  • “Malika didn’t call, did she?” “Yes, she did. She called an hour ago.”— Malika qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi, a? — Aksincha, qildi. Bir soat oldin qo‘ng‘iroq qilgan edi.

Norasmiy tag va variantlar Informal “tags” and other varieties

Og‘zaki nutqda ba’zan qisqa, o‘zgarmas so‘zlar ham tag vazifasida keladi: right?, OK?, yeah?. Ular har qanday gapdan keyin ishlatiladi:

  • You’re coming tomorrow, right?Ertaga kelasiz, shundaymi?
  • Bring your passport, OK?Pasportingizni olib keling, mayli?

Ba’zi ingliz tili variantlarida (masalan, Hindistonda) hamma gapga isn’t it? qo‘shiladi, Londonning norasmiy nutqida esa innit? uchraydi. Bular standart emas. Imtihon va yozma ishda gapga mos tag yasang.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “I am” dan keyin “amn’t I” emas, “aren’t I” deymiz?

Standart ingliz tilida amn’t degan qisqa shakl yo‘q (u faqat ba’zi shevalarda uchraydi). Shuning uchun I’m next, aren’t I? deyiladi. Rasmiy variant — I am next, am I not? Inkor gapda esa muammo yo‘q: I’m not late, am I?

Ohangni qanday tanlayman?

Javobni bilsangiz va faqat rozilik kutsangiz — ovoz pastga tushadi (↘). Haqiqatan ham so‘rayotgan bo‘lsangiz, aniq bilmasangiz — ovoz yuqoriga ko‘tariladi (↗). Yozma holatda buni gap mazmunidan bilib olinadi.

Nega “somebody”, “nobody”, “everyone” dan keyin “they” ishlatiladi?

Bu so‘zlar grammatik jihatdan birlik, lekin ma’no jihatidan ko‘p odamni (yoki jinsi noma’lum odamni) bildiradi. Ingliz tilida bunday holatda they eng tabiiy olmosh: Everyone enjoyed the party, didn’t they? everything, nothing uchun esa it: Everything is OK, isn’t it?

Ijobiy gapdan keyin ijobiy tag qachon mumkin?

Faqat qiziqish, hayrat, shubha yoki istehzo bildirish uchun (C bo‘lim): So you’ve changed your mind, have you? Oddiy rozilik so‘rashda har doim qarama-qarshi shakl ishlatiladi.

Imperativdan keyin nega “will you?” deymiz?

Buyruq yoki iltimos qilganda, uning bajarilishiga oid taxminiy savol beramiz: Close the door, will you? (Eshikni yopasan-a?) Inkor buyruqda ham will you: Don’t touch the cake, will you? Won’t you? esa taklif ohangida: Have some tea, won’t you?

“right?” yoki “OK?” deyish xato emasmi?

Xato emas, lekin bu norasmiy og‘zaki uslub. Imtihonlarda va rasmiy yozuvda gapga mos question tag talab qilinadi.

Question tag yozma matnda ishlatiladimi?

Asosan yo‘q: bu og‘zaki nutq va dialoglarga xos. Ilmiy yoki rasmiy matnlarda deyarli uchramaydi, lekin hikoya va ssenariylardagi suhbatlarda ko‘p bo‘ladi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Tag = yordamchi fe’l + ega olmoshi. Yordamchi fe’l yo‘q bo‘lsa — do / does / did.
  • Ijobiy gap → inkor tag, inkor gap → ijobiy tag. Zamon va fe’l gapdagi bilan bir xil.
  • Maxsus: I am → aren’t I?; Let’s → shall we?; buyruq → will you?; there → isn’t there?
  • nobody, everyone, somebody → they; nothing, everything, this, that → it.
  • never, nobody, hardly, few, nothing, no + ot — inkor ma’no: tag ijobiy.
  • Ohang: ↘ rozilik kutamiz; ↗ haqiqiy savol.
  • Tagga javob: Yes + ijobiy fe’l, No + inkor fe’l (You aren’t coming, are you? — No, I’m not.).
