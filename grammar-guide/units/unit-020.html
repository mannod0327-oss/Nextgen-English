(I'm) going to (do)

Be going to — reja va bashorat
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • be going to + fe’l qanday yasalishini: I’m going to buy a bike.
  • Uni niyat va oldindan qilingan qaror (intention) uchun ishlatishni.
  • I’m doing (kelishilgan reja) va I’m going to do (qaror qilingan niyat) farqini.
  • Hozirgi belgilarga qarab bashorat qilishni: Look at the sky! It’s going to rain.
  • was / were going to — amalga oshmay qolgan niyatlar haqida gapirishni.

Be going to ingliz tilida kelajak haqida gapirishning eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan yo‘llaridan biri. Uning ikki asosiy ma’nosi bor: (1) “men buni qilishga allaqachon qaror qildim” va (2) “hozirgi vaziyatdan ko‘rinib turibdiki, bu albatta sodir bo‘ladi”.

O‘zbek tilida niyat ko‘pincha -moqchi bilan beriladi: Yangi telefon olmoqchiman → I’m going to buy a new phone. Bashorat esa “-adiganga o‘xshaydi”, “hozir ... -adi” bilan: Yomg‘ir yog‘adiganga o‘xshaydi → It’s going to rain. Amalga oshmagan niyat esa -moqchi edim: Sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoqchi edim, lekin ... → I was going to call you, but ... Shu uchta o‘xshashlikni eslab qolsangiz, bu unitning yarmini bilasiz!

I am going to do = qaror qildim, niyatim bor I am going to do something = I have already decided to do it

Agar biror ishni qilishga gapirishdan oldin qaror qilgan bo‘lsangiz, ya’ni bu sizning niyatingiz bo‘lsa, be going to ishlatiladi. Qaror allaqachon qabul qilingan — endi faqat bajarish qoldi.

Ega+am / is / are+going to+fe’l (1-shakl)
be going to: to‘liq jadval
Ega✅ Darak gap❌ Inkor gap❓ So‘roq gap
II’m going to workI’m not going to workAm I going to work?
He / She / ItShe’s going to workShe isn’t going to workIs she going to work?
We / You / TheyThey’re going to workThey aren’t going to workAre they going to work?

Qoida: going to dan keyin fe’l har doim 1-shaklda (infinitive, “to” siz asos) keladi: She’s going to buy ✅ — She’s going to buys / buying ❌. Shaxsga qarab faqat am / is / are o‘zgaradi.

Misollar

  • A: What are you going to do with your old phone?
    B: I’m going to give it to my little brother.    — Eski telefoningni nima qilmoqchisan? — Ukamga bermoqchiman.
  • Malika has been saving for months. Next week she’s going to buy a laptop.Malika bir necha oydan beri pul yig‘yapti. Kelasi hafta u noutbuk sotib olmoqchi.
  • Jasur is going to study in Korea next year.Jasur kelasi yil Koreyada o‘qimoqchi.
  • Wait a minute — I’m just going to charge my phone.Bir daqiqa kutib turing — telefonimni zaryadga qo‘yib olay.
  • The film got terrible reviews, so we’re not going to see it.Film haqida juda yomon fikrlar yozilgan, shuning uchun uni ko‘rmaymiz (ko‘rmoqchi emasmiz).
  • When are you going to tell your parents about the new job?Yangi ish haqida ota-onangga qachon aytmoqchisan?

Talaffuz: og‘zaki, norasmiy nutqda going to ko‘pincha gonna /ˈɡənə/ bo‘lib eshitiladi: I’m gonna call him. Lekin buni yozmang (faqat juda norasmiy chat yoki qo‘shiq matnlarida uchraydi). Imtihonda va rasmiy yozuvda — faqat going to.

I am doing yoki I am going to do? Arrangements and intentions

Unit 19 da present continuous kelishilgan rejalar uchun ishlatilishini ko‘rdik. Endi ikkalasini solishtiramiz:

📅 I am doing — kelishilgan reja (arrangement)

Reja tayyor: vaqt belgilangan, boshqalar bilan kelishilgan, chipta olingan.

  • I’m having my first driving lesson on Monday.
  • We’re flying to Khiva on Friday — the tickets are booked.

💭 I am going to do — qaror, niyat (intention)

Men qaror qildim. Hali hech narsa kelishilmagan bo‘lishi mumkin.

  • I’ve decided to learn to drive. I’m going to look for a driving school.
  • We’re going to visit Khiva one day.
  • I’m going to call Dilnoza tonight and invite her to the party.Bugun kechqurun Dilnozaga qo‘ng‘iroq qilib, uni ziyofatga taklif qilmoqchiman. (Qaror — hali kelishilmagan.)
  • I’m meeting Dilnoza at 6 — we agreed yesterday.Soat 6 da Dilnoza bilan uchrashaman — kecha kelishib oldik. (Kelishuv.)
  • Our flat is too small. We’re going to look for a bigger one.Kvartiramiz juda kichik. Kattaroq kvartira qidirmoqchimiz.

Ko‘p hollarda farq juda kichik va ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri: What time are you meeting Aziz? / What time are you going to meet Aziz? Kelishilgan reja odatda oldin qaror qilingan reja ham bo‘ladi, shuning uchun bunday rejalar haqida going to deyish ham xato emas.

“going to go” yoki shunchaki “going”?

go va come fe’llari bilan ko‘pincha going to go / going to come o‘rniga oddiy present continuous ishlatiladi — gap qisqa va tabiiyroq bo‘ladi:

  • I’m going to Samarkand next week. (= I’m going to go to Samarkand next week.)Kelasi hafta Samarqandga boraman.
  • Bekzod is coming to see us on Sunday. (= is going to come)Bekzod yakshanba kuni bizni ko‘rgani keladi.

Hozirgi belgilarga ko‘ra bashorat Something is going to happen: we can see it now

Be going to faqat odamlarning niyatlari uchun emas. Agar hozirgi vaziyatga qarab biror narsa albatta sodir bo‘lishini ko‘rib turgan bo‘lsak (belgilari, dalillari bor), ham going to ishlatamiz:

Qoida: Something is going to happen = hozirgi holatdan ko‘rinib turibdi: bu sodir bo‘ladi (ko‘pincha juda yaqinda).

  • The sky over the mountains is very dark. It’s going to rain.Tog‘ ustidagi osmon juda qorong‘i. Yomg‘ir yog‘adi.
  • Look at that boy on the bike! He isn’t looking. He’s going to hit the tree!Anavi velosipeddagi bolaga qarang! U oldiga qaramayapti. Daraxtga urilib ketadi!
  • I feel dizzy. I think I’m going to faint.Boshim aylanyapti. Hushimdan ketadiganga o‘xshayman.
  • The traffic isn’t moving at all. We’re going to be late.Tirbandlik umuman qimirlamayapti. Kechikamiz.
  • Kamola is going to have a baby in May.Kamola may oyida farzand ko‘radi. (U homilador — bu hozirdan ma’lum.)
  • Our team is losing 3–0 and there are only two minutes left. We’re going to lose.Jamoamiz 3:0 hisobida yutqazyapti, faqat ikki daqiqa qoldi. Biz yutqazamiz.

O‘zbekcha “Yiqiladi!”, “Kechikamiz!” deganimizda ham aslida hozirgi belgiga qarab gapiramiz. Ingliz tilida bunday paytda present simple ishlatilmaydi (❌ He falls!); eng tabiiy shakl — going to: He’s going to fall! (Ogohlantirishda will ham uchraydi: Careful — you’ll fall! Lekin belgi hozir aniq ko‘rinib tursa, going to eng yaxshi tanlov.)

was / were going to: amalga oshmagan niyat I was going to do something

I was going to do something = o‘tmishda men buni qilmoqchi edim, lekin qilmadim (fikrimni o‘zgartirdim, imkon bo‘lmadi, boshqa narsa xalaqit berdi).

Ega+was / were+going to+fe’l (1-shakl)+but ...
  • We were going to watch a film last night, but there was a power cut.Kecha kechqurun kino ko‘rmoqchi edik, lekin elektr o‘chib qoldi.
  • I was going to call you last night, but I fell asleep.Kecha kechqurun senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoqchi edim, lekin uxlab qolibman.
  • Nodira was going to study medicine, but she changed her mind and became a teacher.Nodira tibbiyotni o‘qimoqchi edi, lekin fikridan qaytib, o‘qituvchi bo‘ldi.
  • I was just going to leave when the phone rang.Endi chiqib ketmoqchi bo‘lib turgan edim, telefon jiringlab qoldi.

O‘tmishdagi bashorat ham shunday

O‘tmishda “bu sodir bo‘ladi” deb kutgan, lekin sodir bo‘lmagan narsalar haqida ham was / were going to ishlatiladi:

  • Everyone thought Bekzod was going to win, but he came second.Hamma Bekzod g‘olib bo‘ladi deb o‘ylagan edi, lekin u ikkinchi o‘rinni oldi.
  • The exam wasn’t as hard as I thought it was going to be.Imtihon men o‘ylagandek qiyin bo‘lmadi.

-moqchi edim / -moqchi edi → was / were going to. Bu juda aniq mos keladi: Ketmoqchi edim = I was going to leave. Ammo inglizchada gap ko‘pincha but ... bilan davom etadi, chunki asosiy ma’no — “bo‘lmadi”.

Chalkashtirmang: “going to + joy” va “going to + fe’l” I’m going to the bank / I’m going to open an account

Bir xil ko‘rinadigan ikki xil grammatika bor:

Ikki xil “going to”
GapTuzilishiMa’nosi
I’m going to the bank.present continuous (go) + to + joyBankka ketyapman / boraman.
I’m going to open a bank account.be going to + fe’lBankda hisob ochmoqchiman.
She’s going to Bukhara tomorrow.present continuous + to + joyU ertaga Buxoroga boradi.
She’s going to visit Bukhara.be going to + fe’lU Buxoroga borib, shaharni ko‘rmoqchi.

Qoida oddiy: to dan keyin ot / joy kelsa — bu “bormoq” fe’li; to dan keyin fe’l kelsa — bu “be going to” (niyat yoki bashorat).

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I going to buy a new phone.I’m going to buy a new phone.“Be” fe’li (am/is/are) majburiy. U tushib qolsa gap buziladi.
She is going to buys a car.She is going to buy a car.going to dan keyin fe’l doim 1-shaklda: -s qo‘shilmaydi.
We are going to visiting our grandmother.We are going to visit our grandmother.going to + fe’lning asosiy shakli, -ing emas.
What you are going to do?What are you going to do?So‘roq gapda am/is/are egadan oldin keladi.
He doesn’t going to come.He isn’t going to come.Inkor “be” bilan yasaladi (isn’t / aren’t / ’m not), “do” bilan emas.
Look at the clouds! It rains soon.Look at the clouds! It’s going to rain soon.Hozirgi belgiga qarab bashorat — be going to.
I’m going to call you yesterday, but I forgot.I was going to call you yesterday, but I forgot.O‘tmishdagi (amalga oshmagan) niyat — was / were going to.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’m going to call him” va “I’ll call him” ning farqi nima?

I’m going to call him — qaror avvaldan qabul qilingan (gapirishdan oldin o‘ylab qo‘ygansiz). I’ll call him — qaror ayni gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilinyapti: — Aziz sizni qidirdi. — Rostdanmi? Unda I’ll call him. Bu farq Unit 23 da batafsil o‘rganiladi.

“I’m going to go to Tashkent” deyish xatomi?

Xato emas, grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri. Lekin ko‘pincha qisqaroq shakl ishlatiladi: I’m going to Tashkent. Xuddi shunday: He’s coming tomorrow (= he’s going to come tomorrow).

“gonna” ni ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Gonna — bu “going to” ning tez og‘zaki nutqdagi talaffuzi. Filmlarda, qo‘shiqlarda ko‘p eshitasiz. Gapirganda shunday talaffuz qilishingiz mumkin, lekin yozganda (imtihon, xat, insho) har doim going to deb yozing. Eslatma: “gonna” faqat “going to + fe’l” o‘rnida keladi; I’m going to the shop ni ❌ I’m gonna the shop deb bo‘lmaydi.

“It’s going to rain” va “It will rain” — ikkalasi bir xilmi?

Ko‘pincha yaqin, lekin farqi bor. It’s going to rain — hozir dalil bor (bulutlarni ko‘ryapman). It will rain — umumiy fikr yoki bashorat (masalan, ob-havo ma’lumoti asosida: I think it will rain tomorrow). Agar hozir belgi ko‘rinib tursa, going to ishlating (Unit 23).

“was going to” doim amalga oshmagan ishni bildiradimi?

Odatda ha — “niyat bor edi, lekin bo‘lmadi”. Ba’zan esa natija noma’lum bo‘ladi yoki gap shunchaki o‘tmishdagi rejani bildiradi: She told me she was going to move to Bukhara. (U Buxoroga ko‘chib ketmoqchiligini aytdi — ko‘chdimi-yo‘qmi, gapdan bilinmaydi.) was just going to — “endi ... moqchi bo‘lib turgandim (shu payt ...)”.

Going to bilan holat fe’llari (be, have, know) ishlatiladimi?

Ha, bemalol. Going to o‘zi “be” bilan yasalsa ham, undan keyin har qanday fe’l kelishi mumkin: It’s going to be hot tomorrow. She’s going to have a baby. You’re going to love this film! Present continuous’dan farqli o‘laroq, bu yerda cheklov yo‘q.

Bashorat uchun “going to” ni har doim “very soon” ma’nosida tushunish kerakmi?

Ko‘pincha natija yaqin, lekin har doim emas. Muhimi — dalil hozir bor. Kamola is going to have a baby in May — may hali uzoq bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin dalil (homiladorlik) hozir mavjud.

  2. Aziz is running very fast towards the bus stop, but the bus is already leaving. (miss / the bus) → He .

  4. The shelf is full of heavy books and it is bending. (break) → It .

  2. We (have) a picnic, but it rained all day.
  3. Bekzod (come) with us, but he was ill.
  4. I thought the test (be) difficult, but it was easy.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: am / is / are + going to + fe’l (1-shakl): I’m going to buy ...
  • Niyat — qaror gapirishdan oldin qabul qilingan: We’re going to look for a bigger flat. (o‘zbekcha -moqchi).
  • I’m doing = kelishilgan reja; I’m going to do = qaror qilingan niyat. Ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham mumkin.
  • Hozirgi belgiga qarab bashorat: It’s going to rain. We’re going to be late.
  • was / were going to = niyat bor edi, lekin amalga oshmadi (o‘zbekcha -moqchi edim).
  • gonna — faqat og‘zaki talaffuz; yozmada going to.
