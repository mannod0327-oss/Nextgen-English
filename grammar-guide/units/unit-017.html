Have and have got

Have va have got
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • have va have got — egalik, qarindoshlik, kasallik va tashqi ko‘rinish haqida gapirganda ikkalasi ham ishlatilishini va ma’nosi bir xil ekanini.
  • Ikkalasining inkor va so‘roq shakllari qanday farq qilishini: Have you got ...? va Do you have ...?
  • O‘tgan zamonda faqat had ishlatilishini (had got emas!).
  • have breakfast, have a shower, have a good time kabi harakat ma’nosidagi iboralarda have got ishlatilmasligini.
  • have got faqat hozirgi zamonda ishlashini; boshqa zamonlarda have kerak.

Ingliz tilida “biror narsam bor” deyish uchun maxsus “bor” so‘zi yo‘q. Buning o‘rniga ega + have ishlatiladi: I have a brother. Britaniya ingliz tilida (BrE) shu ma’noni have got ham beradi: I’ve got a brother. Lekin have boshqa vazifada ham keladi — “qilmoq, yemoq, ichmoq, o‘tkazmoq” ma’nosida (have lunch, have a party). Bu ikkinchi vazifada got qo‘shib bo‘lmaydi. Unit shu farq haqida.

O‘zbek tilida egalik “menda ... bor” yoki “mening ... im bor” bilan beriladi: Menda yangi telefon bor. Inglizcha gapda “bor” so‘zi o‘rnida have turadi: I have a new phone. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ba’zan ❌ I am have a phone yoki ❌ At me a phone deb yuboradi. Ikkinchi tomondan, o‘zbekcha nonushta qilmoq, tushlik qilmoq, dush qabul qilmoq, dam olmoq iboralarining hammasi inglizchada bitta fe’l bilan aytiladi: have (have breakfast, have lunch, have a shower, have a rest). “Qilmoq”ni do yoki make deb tarjima qilish — xato (❌ I did breakfast).

Have va have got — egalik, qarindoshlik, kasallik Have and have got: possession, family, illness

Narsaga egalik, oila a’zolari, kasallik va tashqi ko‘rinish haqida gapirganda ikkita yo‘l bor, ma’nosi esa bir xil:

have got (BrE, so‘zlashuv)

  • She has got two brothers.
  • I’ve got a cold.
  • Have you got a pen?

have (AmE ko‘proq, neytral)

  • She has two brothers.
  • I have a cold.
  • Do you have a pen?
Quyidagilar haqida gapirganda ikkala shakl ham ishlatiladi:
narsalar: a car, a flat, a laptopoila: a sister, two sons, a lot of cousinskasallik: a cold, a headache, toothachetashqi ko‘rinish: blue eyes, long hairvaqt, g‘oya, reja: time, an idea, plans
  • We’ve got two sons and a daughter. / We have two sons and a daughter.Bizning ikki o‘g‘il va bir qiz farzandimiz bor.
  • Nodira has long dark hair and brown eyes. / Nodira has got long dark hair and brown eyes.Nodiraning uzun qora sochlari va jigarrang ko‘zlari bor.
  • Jasur has got a sore throat, so he can’t sing today.Jasurning tomog‘i og‘riyapti, shuning uchun bugun qo‘shiq ayta olmaydi.
  • Our flat hasn’t got a balcony. / Our flat doesn’t have a balcony.Kvartiramizda balkon yo‘q.
  • Have you got any brothers or sisters? — Yes, I have. I’ve got one sister.Aka-ukalaringiz yoki opa-singillaringiz bormi? — Ha, bor. Bitta singlim (yoki opam) bor.

Ikkala yo‘lni aralashtirmang: ❌ Do you have got a pen? — to‘g‘risi Have you got a pen? yoki Do you have a pen? Shuningdek ❌ She doesn’t has / ❌ Does she has — do/does dan keyin har doim have. Qisqa javobda ham savoldagi yordamchi fe’lni takrorlang: Have you got ...? — Yes, I have. (Yes, I’ve ❌).

’s qisqartmasiga e’tibor bering. She’s got a cat da ’s = has; She’s tired da ’s = is. Qanday ajratamiz? got yoki fe’lning 3-shakli kelsa — has; sifat yoki -ing kelsa — is. Og‘zaki nutqda deyarli har doim qisqa shakl ishlatiladi: I’ve got, she’s got, we’ve got.

O‘tgan zamon: had The past: had, didn’t have, did you have?

O‘tgan zamonda have va have got uchun faqat bitta shakl bor: had. Inkor va so‘roq esa did bilan quriladi, had o‘rniga have qoladi.

Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon
HozirO‘tgan zamon
✅ DarakI have a bike. / I’ve got a bike.I had a bike.
❌ InkorI don’t have a bike. / I haven’t got a bike.I didn’t have a bike.
❓ So‘roqDo you have a bike? / Have you got a bike?Did you have a bike?
Qisqa javobYes, I do. / Yes, I have.Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
  • My grandparents had a big garden in Fergana.Buvim va bobomning Farg‘onada katta bog‘i bor edi.
  • Did you have a mobile phone when you were ten?O‘n yoshingizda mobil telefoningiz bormidi?
  • We didn’t have a car, so we went everywhere by bus.Mashinamiz yo‘q edi, shuning uchun hamma joyga avtobusda borardik.
  • I had a terrible headache yesterday, but I’m fine now.Kecha boshim qattiq og‘rigan edi, lekin hozir yaxshiman.

O‘tgan zamonda egalik uchun had got demang: ❌ I had got a bike when I was young → I had a bike when I was young. I hadn’t a bike, Had you a bike? kabi shakllar eskirgan va hozir deyarli ishlatilmaydi — didn’t have va Did you have dan foydalaning. had past perfect ning yordamchi fe’li ham bo‘ladi (I had finished) — bu boshqa mavzu, Unit 15.

Have yoki take? BrE va AmE Small differences

  • BrE: Do you have a pen? / Have you got a pen?  |  AmE: Do you have a pen?Britaniyada ikkala shakl ham, Amerikada asosan Do you have ...?
  • I usually have a shower in the morning. (BrE) / I usually take a shower in the morning. (AmE)Odatda ertalab dush qabul qilaman.
  • Let’s have a rest. / Let’s take a rest.Keling, dam olaylik.

have a shower ham, take a shower ham ikkala davlatda tushuniladi; faqat birinchisi BrE da, ikkinchisi AmE da ko‘proq eshitiladi. AmE da get fe’lining 3-shakli (V3) ko‘pincha gotten: She’s gotten better (“yaxshilanib qoldi”). Bu egalik ma’nosidagi have got bilan bog‘liq emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Have va have got o‘rtasida ma’no farqi bormi? Qaysi birini ishlatay?

Egalik, oila, kasallik va tashqi ko‘rinish haqida ma’no bir xil. Farq faqat uslubda: have got Britaniyada so‘zlashuvda juda keng tarqalgan, Amerikada esa asosan have ishlatiladi. Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, have (Do you have ...? / I don’t have ...) ni tanlang — u hamma joyda to‘g‘ri. Harakat ma’nosida (have lunch) esa faqat have.

I’ve got dagi ’ve nima? She’s got dagi ’s nima?

’ve = have, ’s = has (bu yerda got kelgani uchun). Lekin ’s ba’zan is degani ham: She’s tired (= she is tired). Qoida: ’s dan keyin got yoki fe’lning 3-shakli kelsa — has (She’s got a cat; She’s finished); sifat yoki -ing kelsa — is (She’s kind; She’s working).

Nega “I’m having a car” deb bo‘lmaydi, lekin “I’m having breakfast” to‘g‘ri?

Mashinaga egalik — holat, u “jarayon” emas, shuning uchun continuous shakl yo‘q (holat fe’llari — Unit 4). Nonushta qilish esa harakat: u boshlanadi va tugaydi, shuning uchun I’m having breakfast ✅.

“Have a shower” bilan “take a shower” farqi nima?

Ma’nosi bir xil: dush qabul qilmoq. Ingliz tilining britaniya variantida ko‘proq have a shower / a bath, amerikacha variantida ko‘proq take a shower / a bath deyiladi. Ikkalasi ham hamma yerda tushuniladi va to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi.

“She has a baby”, “She’s having a baby” va “She had a baby” — farqi nima?

She has a baby — uning chaqalog‘i bor (holat). She’s having a baby — odatda “farzand ko‘rmoqchi / homilador, tez orada tug‘adi” (jarayon, ko‘pincha kelasi vaqt bilan: in June). She had a baby — farzand ko‘rdi (o‘tgan voqea).

“I have a cold” va “I’m cold” bir narsami?

Yo‘q! I have a cold — “men shamollaganman” (kasallik). I’m cold — “menga sovuq / sovuq qotdim” (hozirgi holat). O‘zbek tilida “shamollash” va “sovuq qotish” alohida, inglizchada ham shu farqni saqlang.

Nega “Did you have a good holiday?” dan keyin “Yes, I had” emas, “Yes, I did” deb javob beramiz?

Chunki bu savol did bilan tuzilgan, qisqa javobda esa savoldagi yordamchi fe’l takrorlanadi: Did you have ...? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t. “Yes, I had” deb ham gapirishmaydi: had bu yerda asosiy fe’l, yordamchi emas.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gapni to‘ldiring: have, has, ’ve got yoki ’s got (has got). Ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ikkalasini yozing.

  1. We two cats and a dog.
  2. Malika a sore throat today.
  3. My uncle a big farm near Namangan.
  4. I a lot of homework tonight.
  5. Our new teacher a very kind face.
  6. They a small house in Bukhara.

2-mashq. Savol yoki inkor gap tuzing. Ikki yo‘l ham mumkin bo‘lsa, ikkalasini yozing.

  1. (you / a pen?) →
  2. (Nodira / any brothers?) →
  3. (I / a car) — inkor →
  4. (our hotel / a swimming pool) — inkor →
  5. (they / a garden?) →
  6. (how many cousins / Jasur?) →

3-mashq. O‘tgan zamonga o‘tkazing: bo‘shliqqa to‘g‘ri shaklni yozing.

  1. Aziz has a bike. → Last year Aziz a bike.
  2. We haven’t got a car. → Ten years ago we a car either.
  3. Have you got a mobile phone? → you a mobile phone when you were twelve?
  4. I’ve got a headache. → Yesterday I a headache.
  5. She doesn’t have much time. → She much time then.

4-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni yozing (holat yoki harakat?).

  4. Right now we (have) a great time at the festival.
  5. Bekzod (has / is having) a new laptop.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Egalik, oila, kasallik, tashqi ko‘rinish: have = have got (ma’nosi bir xil). Shakllar: I have / I’ve got; she has / she’s got.
  • have got bilan inkor va so‘roq do siz: I haven’t got; Has she got ...? have bilan esa do / does / did: I don’t have; Does she have ...? Ikkalasini aralashtirmang.
  • O‘tgan zamon: had; inkor didn’t have; so‘roq Did you have ...? (had got ❌).
  • Harakat ma’nosida (have breakfast / a shower / a party / a good time) faqat have: do / does / did bilan, -ing ham mumkin; got yo‘q.
  • Egalik — holat: I’m having a car ❌. Harakat — jarayon: I’m having breakfast ✅.
  • have got faqat hozirgi zamonda; will / present perfect / infinitiv / -ing da have (will have, has had, to have, having).
  • Qisqa javob: Have you got ...? — Yes, I have. / Do you have ...? — Yes, I do.
