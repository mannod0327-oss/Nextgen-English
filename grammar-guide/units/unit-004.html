Present continuous and present simple 2 (I am doing and I do)

Present continuous va present simple — 2 (holat fe’llari)
Present and past · Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Holat fe’llari (state verbs) nima va nega ular odatda -ing shaklida ishlatilmaydi: I know ✅, I’m knowing ❌.
  • think fe’lining ikki ma’nosi: “fikrda bo‘lmoq” (I think) va “o‘ylamoq, mulohaza qilmoq” (I’m thinking).
  • He is selfish va He is being selfish farqini.
  • see, hear, smell, taste va can see / can hear ishlatilishini.
  • look va feel bilan ikkala zamon ham mumkin bo‘lgan holatlarni.

Continuous zamon — jarayonni bildiradi: harakat boshlangan, hali tugamagan, biz uning “o‘rtasidamiz”. Lekin ba’zi fe’llar jarayonni emas, holatni bildiradi: bilish, yoqtirish, xohlash, tegishli bo‘lish. Bilishning “o‘rtasida” bo‘lib bo‘lmaydi — siz yo bilasiz, yo bilmaysiz. Shuning uchun bunday fe’llar odatda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi.

O‘zbek tilida biz bemalol tushunyapman, xohlayapman, yoqyapti, ishonyapman deymiz. Inglizchada esa bu fe’llar odatda continuous bo‘lmaydi: Tushunyapsanmi? = Do you understand? (Are you understanding ❌); Menga yoqyapti = I like it (I’m liking it ❌). O‘zbekcha “-yapti” sizni aldab qo‘ymasin!

Odatda continuous bo‘lmaydigan fe’llar Verbs not normally used in the continuous

Continuous shakllar boshlangan, lekin tugamagan harakat va hodisalar uchun ishlatiladi: they are eating, it is raining. Lekin quyidagi fe’llar odatda bunday ishlatilmaydi. Biz I am knowing yoki they are liking demaymiz, balki I know, they like deymiz.

Odatda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydigan fe’llar
GuruhFe’llar
Yoqtirish, xohlash, hislike, love, hate, want, need, prefer
Bilish, fikr, xotiraknow, realise (AmE: realize), suppose, mean, understand, believe, remember (yana: forget, recognise)
Tegishlilik, tarkib, ko‘rinishbelong, fit, contain, consist, seem (yana: own, depend, matter)
  • My phone is very old. I want a new one.Telefonim juda eski. Yangisini xohlayman. (I’m wanting ❌)
  • Do you know what this word means?Bu so‘z nimani anglatishini bilasizmi? (Are you knowing ❌)
  • Kamola seems a bit tired today.Kamola bugun biroz charchagan ko‘rinadi. (is seeming ❌)
  • This bag belongs to Malika.Bu sumka Malikaniki.
  • These trousers don’t fit me. They’re too small.Bu shim menga to‘g‘ri kelmayapti. Juda kichkina.
  • The box contains old family photos.Qutida eski oilaviy suratlar bor.
  • I don’t believe him. He always exaggerates.Men unga ishonmayman. U doim oshirib gapiradi.
  • Do you remember my name?Ismimni eslaysizmi?
  • I suppose you’re tired after your journey.Safardan keyin charchagan bo‘lsangiz kerak.
  • “Shall we go to the park?” “It depends on the weather.”— Parkka boramizmi? — Ob-havoga bog‘liq.

Qoida: Holat fe’llari “hozir” ma’nosida ham present simple shaklida keladi: I want a coffee now. ✅ — I’m wanting a coffee now. ❌ “now, at the moment” kabi so‘zlar bu qoidani o‘zgartirmaydi.

Muloyim iltimos uchun want o‘rniga ko‘pincha I’d like ishlatiladi: I’d like a cup of tea, please.

Think: “fikrda bo‘lmoq” va “o‘ylamoq” Think

think ikki xil ma’noda keladi va har biri o‘z zamoniga ega.

think = believe, fikrim shunday

Fikr, ishonch, baho. Continuous ishlatilmaydi.

  • I think Aziz is at home now. His car is outside.
    Menimcha, Aziz hozir uyda. Mashinasi tashqarida turibdi.
  • What do you think of my new haircut?
    Yangi soch turmagim haqida fikringiz qanday?
  • I don’t think it will rain tomorrow.
    Menimcha, ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘maydi.

think = consider, o‘ylamoq

Miyada jarayon: mulohaza qilish, rejalashtirish. Continuous mumkin.

  • I’m thinking about my exam tomorrow.
    Ertangi imtihonim haqida o‘ylayapman.
  • Dilnoza is thinking of moving to Tashkent.
    Dilnoza Toshkentga ko‘chishni o‘ylab yuribdi.
  • What are you thinking about?
    Nima haqida o‘ylayapsan?

“O‘ylamoq” ma’nosida ham odat bo‘lsa — simple: I often think about my childhood in Bukhara. (Buxorodagi bolaligim haqida tez-tez o‘ylayman.)

O‘zbekcha “menimcha / nazarimda” = I think (simple). “o‘ylayapman / o‘ylab yuribman” = I’m thinking (continuous). I’m thinking you’re right ❌ — bu “fikrim” ma’nosi, shuning uchun: I think you’re right ✅.

think of / think about + -ing — “...ni o‘ylab yurmoq, rejalashtirmoq”: We’re thinking of buying a house. (Uy sotib olishni o‘ylab yuribmiz.)

He is selfish va He is being selfish am / is / are being

be fe’lining present continuous shakli — am / is / are being. He’s being ... = “u (hozir, shu vaziyatda) o‘zini ... tutyapti”, ya’ni He’s behaving / He’s acting ...

He is selfish

Bu uning xarakteri — umuman, doim shunday.

  • He is very selfish. He only cares about himself.
    U juda xudbin. Faqat o‘zini o‘ylaydi.

He is being selfish

Hozirgi xatti-harakati — odatda u bunday emas.

  • Why is he being so selfish today? He usually shares everything.
    Nega u bugun bunchalik xudbinlik qilyapti? Odatda u hamma narsasini bo‘lishadi.
  • Why are you being so rude to your sister? You’re usually so kind.Nega singlingga bunchalik qo‘pollik qilyapsan? Odatda juda mehribonsan-ku.
  • Jasur is being very helpful today. I wonder what he wants!Jasur bugun juda yordam beryapti. Qiziq, nima istayotgan ekan!
  • You’re being silly. Stop it!Bema’nilik qilyapsan. Bas qil!
  • Bekzod is being very careful with his money this month. He wants to buy a laptop.Bekzod bu oy pulini juda tejab sarflayapti. U noutbuk olmoqchi.

Qoida: am / is / are being faqat odamning xatti-harakati haqida (rude, polite, kind, silly, careful, lazy, selfish, quiet, difficult ...). Boshqa holatlarda — oddiy am / is / are:

✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘riNega?
It’s hot today.It’s being hot today.ob-havo — xatti-harakat emas
I’m very tired.I’m being tired.charchoq — holat, xatti-harakat emas
Sardor is very tall.Sardor is being tall.tashqi ko‘rinish
She’s hungry.She’s being hungry.his-tuyg‘u, holat

is being + V3 (majhul nisbat) — boshqa mavzu: The road is being repaired. (Yo‘l ta’mirlanmoqda.) Bu Unit 42–43 da.

See, hear, smell, taste Verbs of the senses

Sezgi fe’llari — see, hear, smell, taste — odatda present simple da ishlatiladi (continuous emas):

can + see / hear / smell / taste

Ko‘pincha bu fe’llar oldidan can qo‘yiladi — bu juda tabiiy va keng tarqalgan:

  • I can see the mountains from my window.Derazamdan tog‘lar ko‘rinib turibdi.
  • Can you hear me? The connection is bad.Meni eshityapsizmi? Aloqa yomon.
  • I can smell smoke. Is something burning?Tutun hidi kelyapti. Nimadir yonyaptimi?

O‘zbekchada ko‘ryapman, eshityapman deymiz, inglizchada esa I can see, I can hear yoki I see, I hear. I’m hearing a noise ❌ → I can hear a noise ✅.

Lekin: boshqa ma’noda continuous mumkin

Bu fe’llar ataylab qilinadigan harakat yoki boshqa ma’noni bildirsa, continuous ham ishlatiladi:

Holat (simple)Harakat / boshqa ma’no (continuous mumkin)
This soup tastes salty. (mazasi sho‘r)The chef is tasting the soup. (oshpaz sho‘rvani tatib ko‘ryapti)
The flowers smell lovely. (hidi yoqimli)Why are you smelling the milk? Is it bad? (nega sutni hidlayapsan?)
I see what you mean. (tushunaman)I’m seeing the doctor tomorrow. (shifokor qabuliga boraman — uchrashuv)

Look va feel How somebody looks or feels now

Kimningdir hozir qanday ko‘rinishi yoki o‘zini qanday his qilishi haqida gapirganda ikkala zamon ham to‘g‘ri, ma’no deyarli bir xil:

Present simplePresent continuousTarjima
You look tired.You’re looking tired.Charchagan ko‘rinasiz.
How do you feel after the match?How are you feeling after the match?O‘yindan keyin o‘zingizni qanday his qilyapsiz?
I feel much better today.I’m feeling much better today.Bugun o‘zimni ancha yaxshi his qilyapman.

Lekin odat, takrorlanadigan holat haqida — faqat simple: I usually feel sleepy after a big lunch. ✅ — I’m usually feeling sleepy ❌

look (ko‘rinmoq) va look at (qaramoq)

  • Malika looks happy today. / Malika’s looking happy today.Malika bugun xursand ko‘rinyapti. (ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri)
  • What are you looking at?Nimaga qarayapsan? (look at — harakat, odatdagi continuous)

feel “fikrda bo‘lmoq” ma’nosida (= think) — faqat simple: I feel (that) we should wait a little longer. (Menimcha, yana biroz kutishimiz kerak.)

Ikki ma’noli fe’llar: have, weigh va boshqalar Verbs with two meanings

Ba’zi fe’llar bir ma’noda holatni, boshqa ma’noda harakatni bildiradi. Qoida oddiy: holat → simple, harakat → continuous bo‘lishi mumkin.

Fe’lHolat (simple)Harakat (continuous mumkin)
haveI have two brothers. (egalik)We’re having lunch. / Are you having a good time? (ovqatlanmoq, vaqt o‘tkazmoq)
thinkI think it’s a good idea. (fikr)I’m thinking about it. (o‘ylash jarayoni)
seeI see the problem. (tushunaman / ko‘raman)She’s seeing a client at 3. (uchrashadi)
tasteIt tastes sweet.He’s tasting the sauce.
weighThis suitcase weighs 20 kilos. (og‘irligi)The shop assistant is weighing the apples. (tortyapti)

enjoy — holat fe’li emas, continuous bemalol keladi: Are you enjoying the film? (Film yoqyaptimi?) “Film yoqyaptimi?” ni Are you liking the film? deb emas, Are you enjoying the film? yoki Do you like the film? deb ayting. Batafsil “have” haqida — Unit 17.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I’m knowing your brother.I know your brother.know — holat fe’li.
I’m wanting a new phone.I want a new phone.want — holat fe’li, “hozir” ma’nosida ham simple.
This car is belonging to my uncle.This car belongs to my uncle.belong — holat fe’li.
I’m thinking you are right.I think you are right.Bu yerda think = “fikrimcha”, shuning uchun simple.
It’s being very cold today.It’s very cold today.“being” faqat odamning xatti-harakati haqida.
This soup is tasting very good.This soup tastes very good.“mazasi bor” — holat, simple.
Are you hearing that noise?Can you hear that noise? / Do you hear that noise?hear odatda can bilan yoki simple da.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega holat fe’llari continuous bo‘lmaydi?

Continuous “jarayonning o‘rtasida” degan ma’noni beradi. Bilish, yoqtirish, egalik qilish esa jarayon emas — ular bor yoki yo‘q. Siz biror narsani “bilishning yarmida” bo‘la olmaysiz. Shuning uchun I know, I like, it belongs — simple shaklda.

Reklamada “I’m loving it” deyiladi-ku. Bu xato emasmi?

Bu norasmiy, so‘zlashuv uslubi: “ayni paytda juda zavqlanyapman” degan kuchli emotsiyani beradi. Hozirgi jonli nutqda ba’zan uchraydi, lekin standart grammatikada va imtihonlarda I love it deb yozing.

“Film yoqyaptimi?” qanday deyiladi?

Are you enjoying the film? yoki Do you like the film? Are you liking the film? — noto‘g‘ri. enjoy — harakat fe’li, u bilan continuous bemalol keladi.

“How do you feel?” va “How are you feeling?” — farqi bormi?

Deyarli yo‘q, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri (E bo‘lim). Continuous variant biroz iliqroq, “aynan hozir” ga urg‘u beradi, shuning uchun kasal odamdan ko‘pincha How are you feeling? deb so‘raladi.

“She’s quiet” va “She’s being quiet” — farqi nima?

She’s quiet — u tabiatan kamgap odam. She’s being quiet — u hozir (odatdagidan farqli) jim o‘tiribdi: You’re being very quiet today. Is everything OK? (Bugun juda jimsan. Hammasi joyidami?)

“I’m seeing someone” degan gapni eshitdim. Bu to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, to‘g‘ri. Bu yerda see = “uchrashib yurmoq” (romantik munosabat) yoki “uchrashmoq” ma’nosida — bu harakat, holat emas. I’m seeing the dentist on Monday — dushanba kuni tish shifokoriga boraman.

Bu qoida boshqa continuous zamonlarga ham tegishlimi?

Ha. O‘tgan davomli (Unit 6) va present perfect continuous (Unit 10) da ham holat fe’llari odatda continuous bo‘lmaydi: We knew each other well (were knowing ❌). Lekin want va mean present perfect continuous da ham kela oladi: I’ve been meaning to call you.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni present simple yoki present continuous shaklida yozing.

  1. “Shall I turn on the TV?” “No, thanks. I (want) to read my book.”
  2. Nodira (think) of buying a new car.

  5. I (not / remember) his name.
  6. Look! The chef (taste) the soup.
  7. I (have) two sisters and a brother.
  8. Sorry, we (have) dinner at the moment. Can I call you later?

  2. Sardor very tall. He’s almost two metres.
  3. It very cold today. Wear a warm coat.
  4. You silly. Stop it!
  5. I very tired today. I need to go to bed early.
  6. My neighbours very friendly. They always say hello.

4-mashq. Fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing. Ikkala zamon mumkin bo‘lsa, ikkalasini ham yozing.

  2. How (you / feel) after your operation?
  3. I usually (feel) sleepy after a big lunch.
  4. What (you / look) at? There’s nothing there.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Holat fe’llari odatda continuous bo‘lmaydi: like, love, hate, want, need, prefer, know, realise, suppose, mean, understand, believe, remember, belong, fit, contain, consist, seem.
  • I think (= fikrimcha) — simple; I’m thinking (= o‘ylayapman) — continuous mumkin.
  • He is selfish — xarakter; He is being selfish — hozirgi xatti-harakat. “being” faqat xulq-atvor haqida.
  • see, hear, smell, taste — odatda simple yoki can see / can hear ...
  • look / feel — hozirgi holat haqida ikkala zamon ham mumkin; odat haqida — faqat simple.
  • Ikki ma’noli fe’llar (have, think, see, taste): holat → simple, harakat → continuous.
