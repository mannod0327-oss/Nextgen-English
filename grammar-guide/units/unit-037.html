37

Can/Could/Would you ...? etc. (Requests, offers, permission and invitations)

Iltimos, taklif, ruxsat va mehmonga chaqirish
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Birovdan nimadir qilishni so‘rash (iltimos): Can you ...? Could you ...? Would you mind ...ing?
  • Biror narsani so‘rash va ruxsat so‘rash: Can I have ...? Could I ...? May I ...? Do you mind if I ...?
  • O‘zingiz taklif qilish (offer): Can I ...? Shall I ...? Would you like me to ...?
  • Mehmonga chaqirish (invitation): Would you like to ...? Do you want to ...? va javob berish yo‘llari.
  • Qaysi shakl qanchalik muloyim ekanini va qanday javob berish kerakligini.

Ingliz tilida muloyimlik ko‘pincha so‘zlar tanlovida bilinadi. Open the window — buyruq. Could you open the window, please? — muloyim iltimos. Ma’no bir xil, lekin ohang butunlay boshqa. Notanish odam, o‘qituvchi yoki boshliq bilan gaplashganda buyruq shaklini ishlatish qo‘pol eshitiladi.

O‘zbek tilida muloyimlik ko‘pincha qo‘shimchalar (-ib bera olasizmi, -sam bo‘ladimi, marhamat) va “iltimos” bilan beriladi. Inglizchada buning o‘rniga modal fe’llar ishlatiladi: can, could, would, may. O‘zbekcha “Mumkinmi?” inglizchada Can I ...? / Could I ...? / May I ...?, “Marhamat” esa Go ahead / Please do / Here you are. Eng katta xavf — o‘zbekcha buyruq gapni so‘zma-so‘z tarjima qilish: “Qalamingni ber” → ❌ Give me your pen (qo‘pol) o‘rniga ✅ Could I borrow your pen?

Birovdan biror ish qilishni so‘rash Asking people to do things

Iltimos qilishning bir necha yo‘li bor. Ular muloyimlik darajasi bilan farq qiladi:

Qoida: Can / Could / Will / Would + ega (you) + fe’lning asl shakli. Bu yerda could va would o‘tmishni emas, muloyimlikni bildiradi: Could you help me now? — hozir yordam so‘rayapsiz. E’tibor bering: Do you think you could ...? deyiladi (Do you think you can ...? odatda ishlatilmaydi).

  • Could you speak a bit more slowly, please?Sal sekinroq gapira olasizmi?
  • Would you mind closing the window? It’s a bit cold.Derazani yopib qo‘ysangiz, qarshi bo‘lmaysizmi? Biroz sovuq.
  • Would you mind not smoking in here?Bu yerda chekmasangiz, qarshi bo‘lmaysizmi?

Would you mind dan keyin fe’lga -ing qo‘shiladi (Would you mind waiting?), to emas: Would you mind to wait? ❌ (Unit 53). Bu qolipga javob ham ehtiyotkorlik talab qiladi, pastdagi C bo‘limiga qarang.

Biror narsa so‘rash Asking for things

Biror narsani berishni so‘ralganda Can I have ...?, Could I have ...? yoki rasmiyroq May I have ...? ishlatiladi. Do‘kon va kafeda Can I get ...? ham ko‘p eshitiladi (ayniqsa AmE): Can I get a coffee, please?

  • Can I have a glass of water, please?Bir stakan suv olsam bo‘ladimi?
  • Could we have the bill, please?Hisobni olib kelsangiz.
  • May I have your name, please?Ismingizni aytsangiz.

Borrow va lend

Vaqtincha olish yoki berish haqida gapirganda borrow va lend ni aralashtirmang. O‘zbek tilida ikkalasi ham “qarz” bilan bog‘liq, ingliz tilida esa alohida fe’l:

Fe’lMa’nosiMisol
borrow (from)vaqtincha olmoq (men oluvchiman)Could I borrow your dictionary?
Lug‘atingizni vaqtincha olib tursam bo‘ladimi?
lend (to)vaqtincha bermoq (men beruvchiman)Could you lend me your dictionary?
Lug‘atingizni vaqtincha bera olasizmi?

Ikkala gap bir xil vaziyatni bildiradi: Could I borrow your pen? = Could you lend me your pen? Faqat egasi boshqa.

Ruxsat so‘rash va ruxsat berish Asking for and giving permission

Biror ishni qilishga ruxsat so‘rash uchun ham can, could, may ishlatiladi, lekin ega endi I / we:

Can I / we ...?

Can I park here?

Eng oddiy, norasmiy. Shu yerda mashinani qo‘ysam bo‘ladimi?

Could I / we ...?

Could I ask you a question?

Muloyimroq. Sizga savol bersam bo‘ladimi?

May I / we ...?

May I leave early today?

Rasmiy, odobli. Bugun ertaroq ketsam maylimi?

Would you mind if I opened ...? da opened o‘tgan zamon shaklida, lekin ma’no hozir/kelajak. Bu yerda o‘tgan zamon shakli xuddi Unit 38 dagi If I did ... kabi “xayoliy” ma’no beradi va gapni yumshatadi. (Og‘zaki nutqda Would you mind if I open ...? ham eshitiladi, lekin opened — standart shakl.)

Ikkita “Would you mind” — farqiga qarang!

Would you mind + -ing

Iltimos: Would you mind closing the door?

Eshikni yopib bersangiz, qarshi bo‘lmaysizmi?

Would you mind if I + o‘tgan zamon

Ruxsat: Would you mind if I closed the door?

Eshikni yopsam, qarshi bo‘lmaysizmi?

Ruxsat berish va rad etish

JavobO‘zbekcha
✅ RuxsatYes, of course. / Sure. / Certainly. / Go ahead. / Please do.Albatta. / Marhamat. / Bemalol.
❌ Rad etishI’m afraid not. / Sorry, I’d rather you didn’t. / I’m afraid you can’t.Afsuski, yo‘q. / Ochmasangiz yaxshi bo‘lardi.

Do you mind ...? / Would you mind ...? ga javob berishda o‘zbek o‘quvchilar xato qiladi. Bu savol “sizga xalaqit beradimi?” degan ma’noda, shuning uchun rozilik “No” bilan beriladi: Do you mind if I sit here? — No, not at all. / No, go ahead. / Of course not. ✅. Yes deb javob bersangiz, “xalaqit beradi, o‘tirmang” ma’nosi chiqadi: Yes, I do mind. Kundalik gapda Sure yoki No problem ham rozilik sifatida tushuniladi.

O‘zingiz taklif qilish (offer) Offering to do something

Birovga yordam taklif qilishning asosiy shakllari:

Taklif qilish
ShaklMisolO‘zbekcha
Can I ...?Can I help you? / Can I get you something to drink?Yordam beraymi? / Sizga ichimlik olib kelaymi?
Shall I ...? (BrE)Shall I carry your bag?Sumkangizni ko‘tarib beraymi?
Would you like me to ...?Would you like me to call a taxi?Taksi chaqirib beraymi?

Qoida: Shall faqat I / we bilan taklif va maslahat so‘rashda ishlatiladi: Shall I open the window? ✅ — Shall you open ...? ❌. Would you like me to + fe’l va Do you want me to + fe’l da me (olmosh) + to + fe’l keladi (Unit 55).

  • “Shall I open the window?” “Yes, please. It’s very hot in here.”— Derazani ochaymi? — Ha, iltimos. Bu yerda juda issiq.
  • “Would you like me to help you with your homework?” “No, thanks. I’ve nearly finished.”— Uy vazifangizda yordam beraymi? — Yo‘q, rahmat. Deyarli tugatdim.
  • “Can I give you a hand with those boxes?” “That would be great. Thank you!”— Bu qutilarni ko‘tarishda yordam beraymi? — Juda yaxshi bo‘lardi. Rahmat!

Amerika ingliz tilida taklif qilishda Shall I ...? o‘rniga ko‘pincha Should I ...? ishlatiladi. Britaniyada ikkalasi ham bor, Shall I — odatiyroq (Unit 21).

Mehmonga chaqirish va taklif Offers and invitations

Biror narsani yoki ish qilishni taklif qilish uchun Would you like ...? eng muloyim shakl:

Would you like+ot (a coffee) / Would you like+to + fe’l (to come)
  • Would you like some tea? (taklif: nimadir)Choy ichasizmi?
  • Would you like to come to my birthday party on Saturday?Shanba kuni tug‘ilgan kunimga kelasizmi?
  • Would you like to have dinner with us tonight?Bugun kechqurun biz bilan kechki ovqatga qolasizmi?

Taklif va iltimosda ko‘pincha some ishlatiladi, any emas: Would you like some tea? ✅ (biz “ha” javobini kutamiz). Batafsil — Unit 85.

Taklif qilganda Would you like ...? deyiladi, Do you like ...? emas: Would you like a biscuit? ✅ (Pechenye olasizmi?) — Do you like biscuits? esa “pechenyeni yoqtirasizmi?” (umuman, odat haqida) degan savol.

I’d like ... (= I would like) — nima xohlayotganingizni muloyim aytish usuli: I’d like a ticket to Bukhara, please. (Buxoroga bitta chipta bersangiz.) / I’d like to pay by card, please. (Karta orqali to‘lamoqchi edim.) I want ... ga qaraganda ancha muloyim.

Qisqa javobda I’d love to deyiladi, fe’l tushib qoladi: “Would you like to come?” “I’d love to.” ✅. Fe’l bilan: I’d love to come. ✅ — I’d love come ❌, I’d love coming ❌ (taklifga javobda). Would you like dan keyin ham to + fe’l, -ing emas: Would you like to go? ✅ — Would you like going? ❌.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Can, could va would — qaysi biri eng muloyim?

Taxminan shunday: Can you (oddiy) → Could you / Would you (muloyim) → Would you mind ...ing / Do you think you could ... / I was wondering if you could ... (juda muloyim). Lekin ohang ham, please ham muhim: Can you help me, please? aytilishiga ko‘ra qo‘pol ham, odobli ham bo‘lishi mumkin. Notanish yoki katta odam bilan Could you ...? eng xavfsiz tanlov.

“Could you ...?” da could o‘tgan zamonmi? Ya’ni “kecha”mi?

Yo‘q. Iltimosda could va would o‘tmishni emas, muloyimlikni bildiradi: Could you open the door now? — hozir. Ingliz tilida o‘tgan zamon shakllari ko‘pincha gapni “yumshatish” uchun ishlatiladi.

“May I” va “Can I” farqi nima?

Ma’nosi bir xil — ruxsat so‘rash. May I rasmiyroq va odobliroq eshitiladi (o‘qituvchi, rasmiy muassasa, restoran). Kundalik gapda Can I va Could I juda keng tarqalgan. Qat’iy qoida yo‘q, lekin rasmiy vaziyatda May I / Could I ni tanlang.

“Shall I” va “Will I” va “Should I” — taklif qilishda qaysi biri?

Taklif va “qilaymi?” deb so‘rash uchun Britaniyada Shall I ...? (Shall I open the window?). Amerikada ko‘proq Should I ...?. Will I ...? taklif emas — kelajak haqida savol (Will I pass the exam? — o‘tamanmi?). Shall faqat I/we bilan (Unit 21).

“Would you like” bilan “Do you want” — farqi nima?

Ikkalasi ham “xohlaysizmi?” Lekin Would you like ancha muloyim va mehmon bilan, notanish odam bilan, xizmat ko‘rsatishda ishlatiladi. Do you want do‘stlar orasida va norasmiy. Xuddi shuningdek, I’d like (muloyim) va I want (to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri, bolalarcha/qo‘pol eshitilishi mumkin): I’d like a coffee, please.

Taklifni muloyim rad etish uchun nima deyish mumkin?

Avval rahmat va xohish: Thanks for asking. / I’d love to, but ... Keyin sababi: ... I’m busy on Saturday. Faqat “No” yoki “I can’t” deyish quruq eshitiladi. Bundan tashqari Maybe another time. (Balki boshqa safar.) deyish ham odat.

Nega taklifda “some” deyiladi: “Would you like some tea?”

Taklif va iltimosda biz odatda “ha” javobini kutamiz. Bunday gaplarda some tabiiy. any esa asosan inkor va oddiy savolda: Would you like any tea? kam uchraydi, Have you got any tea? to‘g‘ri (Unit 85).

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. “Could you ...?” bilan muloyim iltimos yozing.

  1. open the window →
  2. repeat that →
  3. lend me your pen →
  4. speak more slowly →

2-mashq. “Would you mind + -ing” bilan iltimos yozing.

  1. close the door →
  2. turn the music down →
  3. wait a few minutes →
  4. help me with this bag →

3-mashq. Ruxsat so‘rash: fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. Would you mind if I (open) the window?
  2. Do you mind if I (sit) here?
  3. Would you mind if I (borrow) your umbrella?
  4. Do you mind if I (turn off) the TV?

4-mashq. Eng yaxshi javobni tanlang (a, b yoki c).

  1. “Would you like some tea?” — (a) Yes, I would like. (b) Yes, please. (c) Yes, I want.
  2. “Do you mind if I open the window?” — (a) Yes, go ahead. (b) No, not at all. (c) Yes, of course.
  3. “Shall I carry your bag?” — (a) Yes, please. That’s very kind of you. (b) Yes, I shall. (c) No, I don’t shall.
  4. “Would you like to come to the cinema tonight?” — (a) Yes, I’d love to. (b) Yes, I’d love. (c) Yes, I like.
  5. “Could you pass me the salt?” — (a) Yes, I could. (b) Sure, here you are. (c) I could not.

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

  1. Can I lend your pen? →
  2. Do you want that I help you? →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Iltimos: Can you ...? (oddiy) → Could / Would you ...? (muloyim) → Would you mind -ing? (juda muloyim). Ega — you.
  • Ruxsat so‘rash: Can / Could / May I ...?; Do you mind if I ...? (hozirgi zamon); Would you mind if I ...ed? (o‘tgan zamon shakli, hozirgi ma’no). Ega — I / we.
  • Do/Would you mind ...? ga rozilik No bilan: No, not at all.
  • Taklif: Can I ...? Shall I ...? Would you like me to ...?
  • Mehmonga chaqirish: Would you like to ...? / Would you like a ...? Javob: I’d love to. / Yes, please.
  • borrow = olmoq, lend = bermoq. Shall faqat I / we bilan.
  • could, would, may bu yerda o‘tmishni emas, muloyimlikni bildiradi.
