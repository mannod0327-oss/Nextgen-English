Unless, As long as, Provided/providing

Unless, as long as, provided / providing
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Unless — “... bo‘lmasa, faqat shu holat bundan mustasno” (= except if) ma’nosini va undan keyin nega ijobiy fe’l kelishini: We’ll start at nine unless it rains.
  • Unless bilan if ... not qachon bir xil, qachon bir xil emasligini (masalan, I’ll be surprised if he doesn’t come gapida unless tabiiy emas).
  • As long as, so long as, provided (that), providing (that) — “faqat ... bo‘lsa, ... sharti bilan” (= only if) ma’nosini.
  • Bu so‘zlardan keyin kelajak uchun will emas, present simple ishlatilishini.
  • O‘zbekcha -masa, -sa bo‘ldi, sharti bilan inglizcha gapda qanday xatolarga olib kelishini.

Bu so‘zlarning hammasi shart (condition) bildiradi, lekin oddiy if (“agar”) dan aniqroq. Unless — “reja yoki qoida shunday, faqat mana bu holat mustasno”. As long as va provided — “mumkin, lekin faqat shu shart bajarilsa”.

O‘zbek tilida unless uchun alohida so‘z yo‘q: bu ma’no inkor shart shakli bilan beriladi — -masa (kelmasangiz), “... bo‘lmasa” (chipta bo‘lmasa), ba’zan -maguncha (to‘lamaguningizcha kira olmaysiz). Inglizchada esa unless ning o‘zida inkor bor, shuning uchun undan keyin fe’l ijobiy bo‘ladi: Siz kelmasangiz, men ketaman = I’ll leave unless you come. (unless you don’t come ❌). “Faqat shu shart bilan” ma’nosi esa o‘zbekchada -sa bo‘ldi, sharti bilan, -sagina orqali beriladi — inglizchada bu as long as / provided.

Unless — “... bo‘lmasa” Unless = except if

Tasavvur qiling: siz do‘stlaringiz bilan shanba kuni hovlida futbol o‘ynashga kelishgansiz. Reja aniq — o‘ynaymiz. Rejani faqat bitta narsa buzishi mumkin: yomg‘ir. Mana shu “yagona istisno” fikrini unless beradi:

  • We’ll play football on Saturday unless it rains.Yomg‘ir yog‘masa, shanba kuni futbol o‘ynaymiz.
Asosiy gap+unless+ega+ijobiy fe’l (present simple)

Qoida: unless = except if (“... bo‘lgan holdan tashqari”). Asosiy gap — odatiy reja yoki qoida, unless qismi — undan yagona istisno. Uning ichida inkor ma’no allaqachon bor, shuning uchun undan keyin fe’l odatda ijobiy shaklda turadi: unless you hurry ✅ — unless you don’t hurry ❌.

Misollar

  • We’ll leave at seven unless the roads are icy.Yo‘llar muzlab qolmasa, soat yettida chiqamiz.
  • Malika won’t sign the contract unless the price goes down.Narx tushmasa, Malika shartnomani imzolamaydi.
  • Unless you water these roses every day, they’ll die.Bu atirgullarni har kuni sug‘ormasangiz, ular qurib qoladi.
  • Bekzod never eats out unless someone else pays.Boshqa birov to‘lamasa, Bekzod hech qachon restoranda ovqatlanmaydi.
  • I’ll call you tonight unless I get home very late.Uyga juda kech qaytmasam, kechqurun sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.
  • The bus to Khiva doesn’t leave unless at least ten people have bought tickets.Kamida o‘n kishi chipta olmaguncha, Xivaga avtobus jo‘namaydi.

Unless qismi gap boshida ham, oxirida ham turishi mumkin. Boshida tursa, undan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi (yuqoridagi atirgul haqidagi gapga qarang); oxirida tursa, odatda vergul kerak emas.

Juda kamdan-kam, rasmiy yozma matnlarda unless dan keyin ham inkor shakl uchraydi (... unless it is not practical). Bu murakkab hol; kundalik nutq va imtihon uchun qoida oddiy: unless + ijobiy fe’l.

O‘zbekchada -masa (kelmasangiz) ham unless ga, ham if ... not ga to‘g‘ri keladi — bizda farq yo‘q. Eng xavfli tuzoq: kelmasangiz so‘zida “-ma-” inkori bor, shuning uchun o‘quvchi unless you don’t come deb yozadi va inkorni ikki marta qo‘yadi. Unless allaqachon “agar ... bo‘lmasa” degani, shuning uchun fe’lni ijobiy qoldiring.

Unless ≠ otherwise (“bo‘lmasa” — aks holda)

O‘zbekchada gap boshidagi “bo‘lmasa” ko‘pincha “aks holda” degani: Shoshiling, bo‘lmasa kechikamiz. Inglizchada bunday ikkinchi qism or yoki otherwise bilan boshlanadi. Unless esa bog‘lovchi: u bitta gap ichida ikki qismni bog‘laydi.

  • Hurry up, otherwise we’ll miss the film.Shoshiling, bo‘lmasa kinodan qolamiz.
  • Hurry up, or we’ll miss the film.Shoshiling, aks holda kinodan qolamiz.
  • We’ll miss the film unless we hurry.Shoshilmasak, kinodan qolamiz.

Hurry up, unless we’ll miss the film. ❌ — unless mustaqil yangi gapni boshlamaydi, uning ortidan kelajak uchun will ham kelmaydi.

Unless o‘rniga faqat if ... not ishlatiladigan o‘rinlar

Unless = except if bo‘lgani uchun u faqat “odatiy holat + yagona istisno” gapida mos keladi. Quyidagi ikki holatda ingliz tilida odatda if ... not ishlatiladi, unless esa tabiiy emas:

Hayron qolish — kutilgan voqea (Nodira o‘tadi) yuz bermaganiga reaksiya, reja-qoidadan istisno emas. Savolda esa biz istisnoni emas, ehtimoliy vaziyatning o‘zini so‘raymiz. Shubhangiz bo‘lsa, if ... not ni tanlang: u xavfsizroq, unless esa faqat istisno bo‘lgandagina mos.

Qotib qolgan iboralar

  • Unless I’m mistaken, the museum opens at ten.Adashmasam, muzey soat o‘nda ochiladi.
  • All prices include VAT unless otherwise stated.Boshqacha ko‘rsatilmagan bo‘lsa, barcha narxlar QQS bilan.
  • Take one tablet a day unless told otherwise by your doctor.Shifokor boshqacha aytmasa, kuniga bitta tabletka iching.

Oxirgi gapda unless told otherwise = unless you are told otherwise: rasmiy matnlarda unless dan keyin ega va be tushib qolishi mumkin.

Bu iboralar only if — “faqat ... bo‘lsagina” ma’nosini beradi: biror narsa faqat shu shart bajarilsa mumkin yoki to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi. Oddiy if shunchaki “agar” desa, bular “mayli, lekin shu shart bilan” deb ta’kidlaydi — ayniqsa ruxsat, kelishuv, va’dalarda.

Misollar

  • Aziz can use my laptop as long as he brings it back by Friday.Juma kuniga qadar qaytarib bersa bo‘ldi, Aziz noutbukimdan foydalanishi mumkin.
  • Kamola is happy to cook for twenty guests so long as everybody helps to tidy up afterwards.Keyin hamma yig‘ishtirishga yordam bersa, Kamola yigirma mehmonga ovqat pishirishga tayyor.
  • We can have lunch in the garden provided it isn’t too windy.Shamol juda kuchli bo‘lmasa, tushlikni bog‘da qilishimiz mumkin.
  • Providing you book before Monday, the hotel in Bukhara will give you a discount.Dushanbagacha bron qilsangiz, Buxorodagi mehmonxona sizga chegirma beradi.
  • Sardor can play in the final provided that his ankle has healed.To‘pig‘i tuzalgan bo‘lsa, Sardor final o‘yinida o‘ynashi mumkin.
  • I don’t mind the noise as long as the children go to bed by nine.Bolalar to‘qqizgacha yotsa, shovqin menga xalaqit bermaydi.

Shart qismi gap boshida ham, oxirida ham kela oladi. Boshida kelsa, vergul qo‘yiladi: Providing you book before Monday, you’ll get a discount. Oxirida kelsa, odatda vergul shart emas.

Unless dan keyin ijobiy fe’l kerak edi, as long as / provided dan keyin esa inkor ham bemalol keladi: You can come in, provided you don’t make a noise. (Shovqin qilmasangiz, kirishingiz mumkin.)

As long as / provided va if: farq nimada?

if — oddiy shart

Shunchaki “agar”. Shart ma’lumot yoki ko‘rsatma uchun, “yagona shart” ta’kidlanmaydi.

  • If you see Malika, tell her to call me.
  • What happens if I press this button?

as long as / provided — “faqat shu shart bilan”

Ruxsat, kelishuv, va’da. Shart bajarilishi muhim.

  • You can come as long as you stay quiet.
  • I’ll lend you the money provided you pay it back in May.

As long as ning ikkinchi ma’nosi — davomiylik (“qancha vaqt ... shuncha vaqt”): You can stay here as long as you like. (Qancha xohlasangiz, shuncha turing.) As long as it rains, we’ll stay indoors. (Yomg‘ir yog‘ib turgan butun vaqt ichkarida o‘tiramiz.) If it rains, we’ll stay indoors esa shunchaki “yomg‘ir yog‘sa” degani. As far as bilan adashtirmang: as far as I know — “bilishimcha”.

O‘zbek tilida shartning “faqat” ma’nosi bir necha usul bilan beriladi: -sa bo‘ldi (vaqtida kelsangiz bo‘ldi), sharti bilan (jim o‘tirish sharti bilan), faqat ... -sa, -sagina (Dilnoza ham borsagina, men boraman). Inglizchada hammasi bitta guruh — as long as / provided. Birinchi tuzoq: o‘zbekcha -sa har joyda bir xil, shuning uchun o‘quvchi hamma joyda if deydi. I’ll go if Dilnoza goes grammatik to‘g‘ri, lekin “faqat shu shart bilan” ma’nosini alohida ta’kidlamaydi. Ikkinchi tuzoq: sharti bilan dagi “bilan” ni with deb tarjima qilmang: to‘g‘risi on condition that yoki provided that.

Zamon: will emas, present simple Which tense after unless / as long as / provided?

Gap kelajak haqida bo‘lsa ham, unless / as long as / so long as / provided / providing dan keyin will ishlatilmaydi — xuddi if, when va in case dan keyingidek (Unit 25, Unit 114). Asosiy gap kelajakda bo‘lishi mumkin, shart qismi esa oddiy zamonda turadi.

O‘zbek tilida hozirgi va kelasi zamon ko‘pincha bir xil shaklda beriladi (ertaga boraman — kelajak, har kuni boraman — odat). Shuning uchun shart qismida ham o‘quvchi “bu kelajak-ku” deb will qo‘yadi. Inglizchada shart qismi doim oddiy zamonda: kelajak ma’nosi asosiy gapdan va vaziyatdan bilinadi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Unless bilan if ... not ni qachon almashtirsa bo‘ladi?

Asosiy gap odatiy reja yoki qoida bo‘lsa, shart qismi esa uning yagona istisnosi bo‘lsa, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri: You’ll miss the bus unless you hurry = You’ll miss the bus if you don’t hurry. Lekin hayron qolish kabi reaksiyada va savolda faqat if ... not ishlatiladi. Ikkilansangiz, if ... not xavfsiz tanlov.

Nega unless dan keyin don’t / doesn’t qo‘yib bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki unless = except if, ya’ni inkor ma’no uning ichida. Unless you don’t hurry deyish “agar shoshilmasangiz bo‘lmasa” degandek ikki marta inkor qilishdir. Fe’lni ijobiy qoldiring: unless you hurry.

Unless dan keyin will ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Kelajak ma’nosida — yo‘q. Ingliz tilida if, when, unless, as long as, provided, in case dan keyin kelajak uchun present simple qo‘llanadi: unless it rains, as long as you pay. Shu qoida Unit 25 va Unit 38 da ham bor.

As long as, so long as, provided va providing o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ma’no bir xil: hammasi only if. Farq asosan uslubda: as long as eng keng tarqalgan; provided (that) va providing (that) ko‘pincha rasmiyroq, yozma nutq, qoidalar va shartnomalarda. That ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin: provided you pay = provided that you pay.

As long as bilan if ning farqi nimada?

If oddiy shart: If you see Malika, tell her to call me. As long as / provided esa “faqat shu shart bilan” deb ta’kidlaydi, ko‘pincha ruxsat va kelishuvda: You can come as long as you stay quiet. Ba’zan as long as davomiylikni ham bildiradi: As long as it rains, we’ll stay in — yomg‘ir yog‘ib turgan butun vaqt, If it rains — shunchaki “yomg‘ir yog‘sa”.

“Only if” bilan “if only” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. Only if = “faqat agar”, ma’nosi as long as ga yaqin. If only esa istak yoki afsus: If only I had more time! (Vaqtim ko‘proq bo‘lsaydi edi!). If only ishlatilishi jihatidan wish ga yaqin (Unit 41).

As long as bilan as far as ning farqi nimada?

As long as — shart yoki davomiylik: You can stay as long as you’re quiet. As far as — masofa yoki bilish doirasi: As far as I know, the shop is closed. (Bilishimcha, do‘kon yopiq.) Bu ikki ibora almashmaydi.

Shart qismi gap boshida kelsa, vergul kerakmi?

Ha, odatda kerak: Unless you hurry, you’ll miss the bus. Providing the weather is fine, we’ll go. Shart qismi gap oxirida kelsa, vergul odatda qo‘yilmaydi: You’ll miss the bus unless you hurry.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Unless yoki if? Qavs ichidagi izohga e’tibor bering.

  1. We’ll have the picnic tomorrow it rains. (Only rain would stop us.)
  2. I’ll be amazed Jasur doesn’t win the race. (You expect him to win.)
  3. You can’t borrow books from the library you have a card. (A card is required.)
  4. What will you do the shop is closed? (This is a question.)
  5. Dilnoza won’t sign the form somebody explains it to her. (She needs an explanation first.)
  6. Let’s go for a walk you are too tired. (Tiredness is the only reason not to go.)
  7. Call me you can’t find the address. (Call only in that case.)
  8. I’ll be very sorry you don’t come to my wedding.

2-mashq. Gaplarni unless bilan qayta yozing (ma’no o‘zgarmasin).

  1. You’ll fail the exam if you don’t study. →
  2. I won’t go to the party if Malika doesn’t go. →
  3. We’ll be late if we don’t take a taxi. →
  4. The shop doesn’t deliver if you don’t pay in advance. →
  5. Don’t open the door if you don’t know who it is. →
  6. Nodira will be cold if she doesn’t wear a coat. →

3-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. I’ll lend you my camera as long as you (look) after it.
  2. We’ll go to the lake tomorrow unless it (rain).
  3. Providing the train (be) on time, we’ll reach Samarkand by noon.
  4. Dilnoza won’t speak to him unless he (say) sorry.
  5. You can park here provided you (not / stay) longer than an hour.
  6. Last winter the school bus didn’t run unless the roads (be) clear.
  7. Unless you (hurry), you’ll miss your flight.

4-mashq. Qavs ichidagi so‘z bilan gapni qayta yozing (ma’no o‘zgarmasin).

  1. You can use the pool only if you wear a swimming cap. (provided) →
  2. I’ll go with you only if Nodira comes too. (as long as) →
  3. We can play outside, but only if it doesn’t rain. (providing) →
  4. The museum lets children in for free, but only if an adult is with them. (so long as) →
  5. Bekzod can keep the dog on condition that he walks it twice a day. (provided that) →

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘rilab yozing.

  1. We’ll be late unless we don’t take a taxi. →
  2. I’ll go jogging tomorrow unless it will rain. →
  3. Hurry up, unless we’ll miss the bus. →
  4. I’ll be amazed unless Sardor passes the exam. →
  5. What will we do unless the bus comes? →
  6. Provided you see Sardor, tell him to call me. →
  7. You can stay here as far as you are quiet. →

6-mashq. O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga tarjima qiling (qavs ichidagi ko‘rsatmaga amal qiling).

  1. Dars qilmasangiz, imtihondan o‘tmaysiz. (unless bilan) →
  2. Yomg‘ir yog‘masa, ertaga Samarqandga boramiz. (unless bilan) →
  3. Soat o‘ngacha qaytsangiz bo‘ldi, kinoga borishingiz mumkin. (as long as bilan) →
  4. Dilnoza ham borsagina, men boraman. (as long as bilan) →
  5. Tezroq yurmasak, poyezdga ulgurmaymiz. (unless bilan; “poyezdga ulgurmoq” = catch the train) →

  5. Bekzod can keep the dog provided that he walks it twice a day. / Provided that he walks it twice a day, Bekzod can keep the dog.

5-mashq

  1. We’ll be late unless we take a taxi. / We’ll be late if we don’t take a taxi.
  2. I’ll go jogging tomorrow unless it rains.
  3. Hurry up, or we’ll miss the bus. / Hurry up, otherwise we’ll miss the bus. / We’ll miss the bus unless we hurry.
  4. I’ll be amazed if Sardor doesn’t pass the exam.
  5. What will we do if the bus doesn’t come?
  6. If you see Sardor, tell him to call me.
  7. You can stay here as long as you are quiet. / You can stay here so long as you are quiet. / You can stay here provided (that) you are quiet. / You can stay here providing (that) you are quiet.

6-mashq

  1. Unless you study, you won’t pass the exam. / You won’t pass the exam unless you study.
  2. Unless it rains tomorrow, we’ll go to Samarkand. / We’ll go to Samarkand tomorrow unless it rains.
  3. You can go to the cinema as long as you come back by ten. / As long as you come back by ten, you can go to the cinema. (come back o‘rniga get back yoki are back ham to‘g‘ri)
  4. I’ll go as long as Dilnoza goes too. / As long as Dilnoza goes too, I’ll go.
  5. Unless we walk faster, we won’t catch the train. / We won’t catch the train unless we walk faster. (walk faster o‘rniga go faster yoki hurry ham to‘g‘ri)

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Unless = except if: “reja yoki qoida shunday, faqat shu istisno bundan mustasno”. Undan keyin fe’l odatda ijobiy: unless you hurry (unless you don’t hurry ❌).
  • Ko‘p gaplarda unless = if ... not. Lekin hayron qolish kabi reaksiyada va savolda faqat if ... not: I’ll be surprised if he doesn’t come.
  • O‘zbekcha gap boshidagi “bo‘lmasa” (aks holda) = otherwise / or, unless emas.
  • As long as, so long as, provided (that), providing (that) = only if: “faqat shu shart bilan”. Inkor fe’l ham kela oladi. Provided / providing ko‘pincha rasmiyroq.
  • Bu so‘zlardan keyin kelajak uchun present simple (o‘tgan zamonda — past simple). Will ishlatilmaydi.
  • As long as davomiylik ham bildiradi (stay as long as you like); as far as boshqa ibora (as far as I know).
  • Oddiy shart, savol, reaksiya — if; yagona istisno — unless; “faqat shu shart bilan” — as long as / provided; ehtiyot shart — in case.
