Both / both of, neither / neither of, either / either of

Both, neither, either
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • both, neither, either faqat ikkita odam yoki narsa haqida ishlatilishini: both — ikkalasi ham, neither — ikkalasi ham emas, either — ikkisidan biri (qaysi ekanining farqi yo‘q).
  • Uch qolipni: both / neither / either + ot (neither shop); + of + the / my / these + ko‘plik ot (neither of the shops); + of + us / you / them (both of us).
  • of ni faqat both dan keyin tushirib qoldirish mumkinligini (both my parents), neither va either dan keyin mumkin emasligini.
  • both … and, neither … nor, either … or juftliklarini va ularda fe’l qanday moslashishini.
  • Nega neither bilan fe’l darak shaklida turishini: Neither of us knew.

Gap ikkita narsa haqida bo‘lsa, both / neither / either ishlatamiz. Uchta va undan ko‘p narsa haqida gapirsak, ularning o‘rniga all / none / any keladi (Unit 88). Ikki shaxs yoki ikki narsa gap boshidan ma’lum bo‘lishi kerak: Malika has two phones. Both are new.

O‘zbek tilida bu so‘zlarga ikkalasi (ham) = both, ikkalasi ham … emas / hech biri = neither, ikkisidan biri / istalgani = either mos keladi. Eng katta farq inkorda: o‘zbekchada ikkalasi ham kelmadi deymiz — fe’l inkor shaklida. Inglizchada neither o‘zi inkor ma’noli, shuning uchun fe’l darak shaklida turadi: Neither of them came. ✅ — Neither of them didn’t come. ❌. Yana bir farq: o‘zbekchada ikkala dan keyin ot birlikda turadi (ikkala do‘kon), inglizchada esa both dan keyin ot ko‘plikda: both shops.

Ot aytilmasa ham bo‘ladi

Gapda nima haqida ketayotgani aniq bo‘lsa, ot aytilmaydi. Both / neither / either yolg‘iz o‘zi, ayniqsa qisqa javoblarda keladi:

  • “Shall we meet on Friday or Saturday?” “Either is fine for me.”— Juma kuni uchrashamizmi yoki shanbami? — Menga qaysi biri bo‘lsa ham bo‘laveradi.
  • “Which bus goes to Chorsu, the 12 or the 15?” “Neither. You need the 41.”— Chorsuga qaysi avtobus boradi, 12-mi yoki 15-mi? — Hech biri. Sizga 41-avtobus kerak.
  • “Do you speak Russian or English?” “Both.”— Rus tilida gapirasizmi yoki ingliz tilidami? — Ikkalasida ham.
  • I tried two keys, but neither worked.Ikkita kalitni sinab ko‘rdim, lekin ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri kelmadi.

Both / neither / either + ot Both, neither, either + noun

Ot oldidan aniqlovchi (the, my, these …) bo‘lmasa, of ham ishlatilmaydi — so‘z to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri otga qo‘shiladi. Faqat ot shakli har xil: both dan keyin ko‘plik, neither va either dan keyin birlik ot keladi.

O‘zbek tilida son yoki ikkala dan keyin ot birlikda turadi: ikkala o‘qituvchi, ikki kitob. Inglizchada both dan keyin ot ko‘plik bo‘ladi (both teachers), lekin neither / either dan keyin birlik (neither teacher). Shuning uchun both teacher ❌ va neither teachers ❌ xatolari juda ko‘p uchraydi.

Either inkor gapda ham keladi va neither bilan bir xil ma’no beradi: I didn’t like either hotel. = I liked neither hotel. (ikkalasi ham yoqmadi). Birinchisi — oddiy so‘zlashuv, ikkinchisi — rasmiyroq.

Neither bilan qo‘sh inkor bo‘lmaydi; both va either inkorda No double negatives; both and either in negative sentences

Neither (va nor) o‘zi inkor. Shuning uchun fe’lga not yoki didn’t / doesn’t qo‘shilmaydi. Agar inkor fe’l bilan gapirmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, either ishlating:

Bir ma’no — ikki to‘g‘ri yo‘l

Both inkor gapda — ehtiyot bo‘ling

Both ni inkor fe’l bilan ishlatsangiz, gap ko‘pincha “ikkalasi emas, bittasi” degan ma’noga ega bo‘lib qoladi — shuning uchun noaniq. “Ikkalasi ham emas” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, neither yoki not … either ishlating:

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Both, neither, either ni uchta narsa haqida ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Bu uch so‘z faqat ikkita narsa haqida ishlatiladi. Uchta va undan ko‘p bo‘lsa, all (hammasi), none (hech biri) va any (istalgani) ishlatiladi (Unit 88, Unit 86). Masalan: both of my parents (ikki kishi), lekin all three of my brothers.

“Neither of the students is” yoki “are” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Birlik fe’l (Neither of the students is late) an’anaviy va rasmiy yozuvda tavsiya etiladi. Ko‘plik fe’l (Neither of the students are late) esa so‘zlashuvda juda keng tarqalgan va xato hisoblanmaydi. Xuddi shunday either of bilan ham. Imtihonda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri deb qabul qilinadi, lekin bitta gapda birini tanlang.

Nega “Neither of them didn’t come” deb bo‘lmaydi? O‘zbekchada “ularning hech biri kelmadi” deymiz-ku.

O‘zbek tilida inkor ma’no bir necha marta ifodalanishi mumkin (hech biri + kelmadi) va bu normal. Ingliz tilida esa inkor bir marta aytiladi. Neither allaqachon “ikkalasi ham emas” degani, shuning uchun fe’l darak: Neither of them came. Agar inkor fe’l ishlatmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, neither o‘rniga either aytiladi: They didn’t come yoki I didn’t see either of them.

Both dan keyin of ni qachon tushirsa bo‘ladi?

Faqat the, my, these, your kabi aniqlovchi bor bo‘lsa: both (of) the shops, both (of) my parents — ikkala variant ham to‘g‘ri. Olmosh bilan of shart: both of us. Neither va either dan keyin of ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi: neither of the shops. Aniqlovchisiz otdan oldin of ishlatilmaydi: both shops, neither shop.

“Either” ning ma’nosi nima? U “any” ga o‘xshaydimi?

Ha, either — ikkita narsadan istalgani, any esa uchta va undan ko‘p narsadan istalgani: You can use either computer (ikkitadan biri) — You can use any computer (ko‘plab kompyuterlardan istalgani). Inkor gapda not … either “ikkalasi ham emas” bo‘ladi: I don’t like either of them = I like neither of them.

“Neither … nor” bilan fe’l birlikda bo‘ladimi yoki ko‘plikda?

Qoida: fe’l o‘ziga yaqin turgan otga moslashadi. Neither Sardor nor Bekzod has a car (ikkalasi birlik). Neither the teacher nor the students were ready (yaqin ot — students, ko‘plik). Kundalik nutqda ko‘pchilik ko‘plik fe’lni ishlatadi, lekin yuqoridagi qoida eng ishonchli. Either … or ham xuddi shunday. Both … and esa doim ko‘plik fe’l bilan: Both Malika and Sardor are students.

“Either” gap oxirida kelsa nima bo‘ladi? “Me too” bilan nima farqi bor?

Inkor gapda either “ham” (ham emas) ma’nosini beradi: I can’t swim. — I can’t either. Ijobiy gapda esa too ishlatiladi: I can swim. — I can, too. Qisqa javoblar: Neither can I = I can’t either = Me neither (so‘zlashuv). Bu mavzu Unit 51 da.

“Either side” va “both sides” bir xilmi?

Ko‘pincha ha. There are shops on either side of the street = on both sides of the street (ko‘cha ikki tomonida). Bunday ma’noda either “ikkitaning har biri” deganidir va odatda side, end, hand kabi ikki tomonli narsalar bilan, birlik otda ishlatiladi. Lekin You can park on either side — “ikki tomonning istalgani” ma’nosini ham beradi; ma’no kontekstdan chiqadi.

  4. We are both tired. / Both of us are tired. / We both feel tired. / We were both tired. / Both of us were tired.
  5. Neither Malika nor Bekzod was at home. / Neither Malika nor Bekzod were at home.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • both, neither, either — faqat ikkita narsa haqida. Uch va undan ko‘p bo‘lsa — all, none, any.
  • both + ko‘plik ot + ko‘plik fe’l (both shops are); neither / either + birlik ot + birlik fe’l (neither shop is).
  • Aniqlovchi (the, my, these …) yoki olmosh bilan of kerak: both of the shops, neither of us, either of them. Faqat both da of ni tushirish mumkin: both the shops, both my parents.
  • Neither of / either of dan keyin fe’l birlik (rasmiy) yoki ko‘plik (so‘zlashuv); both of dan keyin doim ko‘plik.
  • Juftliklar: both … and, neither … nor, either … or. Neither … or ❌ — neither … nor ✅.
  • Neither o‘zi inkor — fe’l darak: Neither of us knew. Inkor fe’l bilan: I don’t like either of them.
  • Both uchun gapdagi o‘rin: We both like tea. / They are both teachers. / I invited them both.
