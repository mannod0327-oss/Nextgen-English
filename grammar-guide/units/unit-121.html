At/on/in (time)

At / on / in — vaqt predloglari
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Vaqt bilan at (aniq payt), on (kun va sana) va in (uzoqroq davr) qanday tanlanishini.
  • Kun bo‘laklari: in the morning, at night, lekin on Monday morning.
  • Hafta oxiri va bayramlar: at the weekend (AmE: on the weekend), at Christmas va on Christmas Day.
  • in ten minutes — “o‘n daqiqadan keyin” va “o‘n daqiqada”: ikki xil ma’no.
  • Qaysi so‘zlardan oldin predlog umuman qo‘yilmasligini: next week, last night, every day, tomorrow.

Inglizchada vaqtning “kattaligi” predlogni belgilaydi. Eng katta davrlar (yil, oy, fasl) bilan in, bitta kun yoki sana bilan on, soat va qisqa lahzalar bilan at ishlatiladi. Buni bir-birining ichiga joylashgan uchta quti sifatida tasavvur qiling:

O‘zbek tilida bu uch predlog o‘rniga deyarli doim bitta -da qo‘shimchasi keladi: soat 7da, iyulda, 1991-yilda. “Kun” so‘zi esa ko‘pincha on ga mos: dushanba kuni = on Monday. Inglizchada har bir vaqt turi o‘z predlogini talab qiladi, shuning uchun “-da” ni ko‘rsangiz, o‘zingizdan so‘rang: bu soatmi (at), kun yoki sanami (on) yoki oy, yil, faslmi (in)? Yaxshi xabar: kelasi hafta, o‘tgan yil, har kuni kabi so‘zlarda o‘zbekchada odatda -da qo‘shilmaydi, inglizchada ham predlog qo‘yilmaydi (next week, last year, every day).

At, on va in: asosiy qoida at 7 o’clock, on Monday, in July

at — aniq payt

  • soat: at 6.30, at noon, at midnight
  • ovqat vaqti: at lunchtime, at bedtime
  • tong va kech: at dawn, at sunset
  • at night
  • at the weekend (BrE)
  • at Christmas, at Easter
  • at the age of six

on — kun va sana

  • hafta kunlari: on Tuesday, on Sundays
  • sana: on 5 July, on the 5th of July
  • maxsus kun: on my birthday, on Independence Day
  • kun + kun bo‘lagi: on Friday evening
  • on New Year’s Eve

in — uzoq davr

  • oy: in July
  • yil: in 1991
  • fasl: in winter / in the winter
  • asr va o‘n yillik: in the 21st century, in the 2000s
  • kun bo‘laklari: in the morning / afternoon / evening
  • in the past / the future
  • The lesson starts at half past eight.Dars sakkiz yarimda boshlanadi.
  • Grandma tells the children a story at bedtime.Buvi bolalarga uxlash vaqtida ertak aytib beradi.
  • Navruz falls on 21 March.Navro‘z 21-martga to‘g‘ri keladi.
  • Sardor has football practice on Tuesdays and Thursdays.Sardorning futbol mashg‘uloti seshanba va payshanba kunlari bo‘ladi.
  • Uzbekistan became independent in 1991.O‘zbekiston 1991-yilda mustaqil bo‘ldi.
  • It rains a lot in April here.Bu yerda aprelda ko‘p yomg‘ir yog‘adi.
  • My grandmother was born in the 1950s.Buvim 1950-yillarda tug‘ilgan.

Qoida: soat va qisqa lahza → at; kun yoki sana → on; oy, yil, fasl, asr va kun bo‘lagi (morning, afternoon, evening) → in.

O‘zbekchada bu ikki ma’no har xil aytiladi: o‘n daqiqada (ishga ketgan vaqt) va o‘n daqiqadan keyin (kelajak). Inglizchada esa ikkalasi ham in ten minutes; qaysi ma’no ekanini gapning ma’nosidan bilasiz (kelajak bo‘lsa — “keyin”, tugagan ish bo‘lsa — “ketgan vaqt”).

Kelajak ma’nosida in … ’s time shakli ham ishlatiladi (ayniqsa BrE): I’ll see you in a week’s time (bir haftadan keyin), The new bridge will be open in two years’ time (ikki yildan keyin). Ko‘plikda apostrof s dan keyin qo‘yiladi: two years’, birlikda — oldin: a week’s. Bu shakl faqat “keyin” ma’nosini beradi, “ketgan vaqt” ma’nosini emas.

within “… ichida, undan kechikmay” degani: The results will be ready within a week. (bir hafta ichida, undan kech emas). in a week esa aniq “bir haftadan keyin” bo‘lishi mumkin.

for (davomiylik) bilan aralashtirmang: He worked for two hours (ikki soat ishladi). He finished the work in two hours (ishni ikki soatda tugatdi). Batafsil — Unit 119.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “in the morning” lekin “at night” deyiladi?

Bu qoidadan emas, odatdan: ingliz tilida shunday o‘rnashgan. Tushuntirishga urinmang — shunchaki yodlab oling: in the morning / afternoon / evening, lekin at night. Aniq kun aytilsa, hammasi on ga o‘tadi: on Friday morning, on Friday night.

“In the morning” va “on Monday morning” ning farqi nima?

Birinchisi — umumiy yoki shu kunning ertalabi: I study in the morning. Ikkinchisida aniq bir kun bor, shuning uchun predlog on: I have a lesson on Monday morning. Qoida: kun nomi (yoki sana) qo‘shilsa, in → on.

“At the weekend” yoki “on the weekend” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Britaniya inglizchasida at the weekend / at weekends, Amerika inglizchasida on the weekend / on weekends. Bitta variantni tanlab, izchil ishlating. Imtihonda sizdan qaysi turdagi ingliz tili talab qilinsa, o‘shani yozing.

“In ten minutes” — “o‘n daqiqadan keyin” yoki “o‘n daqiqada”?

Ikkalasi ham bo‘lishi mumkin. Kelajak haqida gapirsangiz — “o‘n daqiqadan keyin” (The bus comes in ten minutes). Tugagan ish haqida gapirsangiz — “o‘n daqiqada” (I wrote the letter in ten minutes). “Keyin” ma’nosini after ten minutes deb tarjima qilmang, kelajakda in aytiladi.

Kun nomi oldidan “on” ni tushirib qoldirsam bo‘ladimi?

Og‘zaki nutqda ha, ayniqsa Amerika inglizchasida: See you Friday. Yozma, rasmiy va imtihon ishlarida on Friday deb yozing. Ko‘plik shakli ham shu: on Fridays (har juma kuni).

“At Christmas” bilan “on Christmas Day” ning farqi nima?

at Christmas — bayram davri (bir necha kun, bayramona vaqt): We visit my uncle at Christmas. on Christmas Day — aniq bir kun (25-dekabr). Shu mantiq at Easter / on Easter Sunday, at New Year / on New Year’s Day uchun ham ishlaydi. Navruz haqida gapirsangiz, sanani ayting: Navruz is on 21 March.

“Tonight”, “this evening” va “in the evening” ning farqi nima?

tonight va this evening — bugungi kechqurun, predlogsiz: I’m free tonight. in the evening — umuman kechqurun (har kuni yoki aniq kun nomisiz): I read in the evening. Kun nomi bilan: on Monday evening.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • at — soat va qisqa lahza: at 7.30, at noon, at night, at the weekend (BrE), at Christmas.
  • on — kun va sana: on Monday, on 5 July, on my birthday, on Friday evening.
  • in — uzoq davr: in July, in 1991, in winter, in the 21st century, in the morning.
  • Kun bo‘lagi: in the morning, lekin aniq kun bilan on Monday morning; faqat at night.
  • in ten minutes = o‘n daqiqadan keyin (kelajak) yoki o‘n daqiqada (ish uchun ketgan vaqt).
  • next, last, this, every, each, all, tomorrow, yesterday, tonight oldidan predlog qo‘yilmaydi.
  • BrE: at the weekend; AmE: on the weekend. Kun nomi oldidan on og‘zaki nutqda tushirilishi mumkin.
