By and until, By the time ...

By va until; by the time ...
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • by (kechi bilan, o‘sha payt yoki undan oldin) va until / till (o‘sha paytgacha uzluksiz) farqini.
  • Qaysi fe’llar bilan by, qaysilari bilan until ishlatilishini.
  • not … until va not … by ning ma’nolarini, by va before farqini.
  • By the time … iborasini: By the time we arrived, the match had started.
  • Nega until he doesn’t come deyilmasligini.

O‘zbekchada ikkala so‘z ham “-gacha” deb tarjima qilinadi, shuning uchun ular osongina aralashib ketadi. Lekin ular bir-biridan keskin farq qiladi:

by va until — bir qarashda farq
byuntil / till
Ma’nosio‘sha payt yoki undan oldin (muddat)o‘sha paytgacha uzluksiz davom etib, keyin tugaydi
Qanday ish?bir marta bo‘ladigan ish: finish, send, arrive, be backdavom etadigan ish yoki holat: wait, stay, work, sleep
MisolI’ll be back by five.I’ll wait until five.

O‘zbek tilida ikkala ma’no ham -gacha bilan beriladi, farqni fe’lning o‘zi ko‘rsatadi: soat beshgacha kutaman (davom etadi) va soat beshgacha qaytaman (bir marta, kechi bilan). Inglizchada esa farqni predlog bildiradi: I’ll wait until five. / I’ll be back by five. Yana bir tuzoq: o‘zbekchada u kelmaguncha kutaman deymiz — fe’l inkor shaklda (-maguncha). Inglizchada esa until he comes (darak shakl) aytiladi.

By — “…dan kechikmay” (until bilan solishtirib) by = not later than

Hozir soat 3 bo‘lsin, kelishilgan payt esa soat 5. Ikki xil gap aytish mumkin:

Qoida: by + vaqt = “o‘sha paytdan kech emas” (o‘sha payt yoki undan oldin); ish bir marta bajariladi va tugaydi. until + vaqt = “o‘sha paytgacha to‘xtamasdan”; o‘sha paytda holat yoki ish tugaydi.

Odatda by bilan keladigan fe’llar (bir marta bo‘ladigan ishlar):
finishsendhand insubmitpayarriveleavebe backbe readycall backreturn
Odatda until bilan keladigan fe’llar (davom etadigan ishlar):
waitstayworksleepstudykeep (doing)be awaybe opengo on / continue

Misollar: by

  • The library closes at eight, so I must return these books by 7.45.Kutubxona soat sakkizda yopiladi, shuning uchun bu kitoblarni 7.45 gacha qaytarishim kerak.
  • Please hand in your essays by Thursday.Insholaringizni payshanba kunigacha (payshanba yoki undan oldin) topshiring.
  • Can you finish the translation by tomorrow evening?Tarjimani ertaga kechgacha tugata olasizmi?
  • Our guests will arrive by seven.Mehmonlarimiz soat yettigacha yetib kelishadi.

Until (till) — o‘sha paytgacha davom etadi until = continuing up to a time

until (yoki till) biror holat yoki ish qancha vaqt davom etishini, ya’ni qachon to‘xtashini aytadi. Yuqoridagi vaqt chizig‘ini yana bir bor solishtiring: until — butun oraliq, by — oraliqning istalgan bir nuqtasi.

Misollar: until

  • The exhibition is open until the end of May.Ko‘rgazma may oyi oxirigacha ochiq.
  • Nodira slept until ten o’clock on Sunday.Nodira yakshanba kuni soat o‘ngacha uxladi.
  • I’ll stay in the library until it closes.Kutubxona yopilgunicha u yerda o‘tiraman.
  • My uncle is on a business trip until Monday.Amakim dushanbagacha xizmat safarida.

till va until ning ma’nosi bir xil. till ko‘proq og‘zaki nutqda; gap boshida va rasmiy yozuvda until yaxshiroq: Until you finish, you can’t go out.

from … until / to

Davrning boshi va oxiri birga aytilganda until (yoki to) ishlatiladi, by emas:

  • The market is open from seven until four. (= from seven to four)Bozor soat yettidan to to‘rtgacha ochiq.

until + gap (ergash gap)

until bog‘lovchi sifatida ham keladi: undan keyin ega + fe’l turadi. Kelasi zamon ma’nosida ham present simple ishlatiladi (will emas):

  • Wait here until I call you.Men sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilgunimcha shu yerda kuting.
  • We waited until the rain stopped.Yomg‘ir to‘xtaguncha kutdik.

by esa oddiy predlog: undan keyin gap kelmaydi. Gap kerak bo‘lsa, by the time aytiladi (C bo‘lim).

O‘zbekchada u kelmaguncha kutaman — fe’l inkor, lekin ma’no “u kelgan paytgacha”. Inglizchada inkor qo‘shilmaydi: I’ll wait until he comes. Until he doesn’t come degani “u kelmay qolgunicha” — bu g‘alati va boshqa ma’no.

not … until va not … by

Inkor gaplarda until va by
not … until = “…dan oldin emas”not … by = “…ga ulgurmayman”
The shops don’t open until ten. (o‘nda ochiladi, ungacha yopiq)I won’t finish by Friday. (jumaga ulgurmayman, jumadan keyin tugayman)
I can’t pay you until Friday. (eng erta payt — juma)I can’t pay you by Friday. (jumagacha to‘lay olmayman, keyinroq to‘layman)
I won’t be home until nine. (to‘qqizda yoki undan keyin kelaman)I won’t be home by nine. (to‘qqizgacha kela olmayman)

by va before

  • Send it by Friday.Uni juma kunigacha yuboring. (juma ham kiradi: payshanba, juma yoki undan oldin)
  • Send it before Friday.Uni juma kunidan oldin yuboring. (juma kirmaydi)

By the time … By the time + a clause: “already, by then”

By dan keyin gap kelmaydi, lekin by the time iborasidan keyin to‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) keladi. U “o‘sha lahzaga kelib” degan ma’noni beradi, asosiy gap esa o‘sha paytgacha nima allaqachon bo‘lganini aytadi.

  • By the time Aziz found his keys, the bus had already left.Aziz kalitlarini topgunicha avtobus allaqachon ketib bo‘lgan edi.
  • By the time you read this message, I’ll be on the plane to Istanbul.Siz bu xabarni o‘qiguningizcha, men Istanbulga uchayotgan samolyotda bo‘laman.

By the time va when

  • When we got to the theatre, we bought the tickets.Teatrga yetib kelgach, chiptalarni sotib oldik. (ketma-ket ishlar)
  • By the time we got to the theatre, the tickets had sold out.Teatrga yetib kelgunimizcha, chiptalar sotilib bo‘lgan edi. (oldin bo‘lgan ish)

By the time dan keyin kelasi zamonda will ishlatilmaydi: ❌ By the time you will arrive → ✅ By the time you arrive. O‘tgan zamonda esa asosiy gapda ko‘pincha past perfect kerak: ❌ By the time we arrived, the match started → ✅ …had started.

by then, by that time, by now

Gapda vaqt allaqachon aytilgan bo‘lsa, by the time … o‘rniga qisqa ibora ishlatiladi:

IboraMa’nosiMisol
by then / by that timeo‘sha paytga kelibWe got to the shop at nine, but by then it had closed.
by nowhozirga kelibJasur left at six, so he should be in Samarkand by now.
by the end of …… oxirigacha (kechi bilan)Please pay the fee by the end of the month.

Tarjimalar: Biz do‘konga to‘qqizda yetib bordik, lekin o‘sha paytga kelib u yopilgan edi. — Jasur oltida yo‘lga chiqqan, shuning uchun hozirga kelib Samarqandda bo‘lishi kerak. — Iltimos, to‘lovni oy oxirigacha to‘lang.

By ning boshqa ma’nolari Other meanings of by

Vaqt ma’nosidagi “…gacha” — by ning faqat bitta ma’nosi. Boshqa ma’nolari bilan aralashtirmang (ular Unit 128 da to‘liq o‘rganiladi):

Ma’noMisolTarjima

Rasmiy yozuvda by o‘rniga no later than ham ishlatiladi: Please reply no later than Friday. Ma’nosi bir xil: juma yoki undan oldin.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

by va until ni tez qanday ajrataman?

Fe’lga qarang. Ish bir marta bajarilib tugasa (yuborish, topshirish, yetib kelish, qaytish, to‘lash) va siz “qachongacha ulgurish kerak” deb o‘ylasangiz — by. Ish davom etsa (kutish, qolish, ishlash, uxlash, ochiq bo‘lish) va siz “qachon to‘xtaydi” deb o‘ylasangiz — until.

“till” va “until” ning farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil. till — og‘zaki nutqda ko‘proq; until — yozma va rasmiy nutqda, ayniqsa gap boshida yaxshiroq. Ikkalasi ham predlog ham, bog‘lovchi ham bo‘la oladi.

by va before ning farqi nimada?

by Friday — juma kuni ham kiradi (payshanba, juma yoki undan oldin). before Friday — juma kunidan oldin, juma o‘zi kirmaydi. Muddatlarni aytganda by aniqroq va keng tarqalgan.

By the time va when ning farqi nima?

when — shu paytda yoki shundan keyin ketma-ket bo‘lgan ish. by the time — “o‘sha payt kelganda allaqachon bo‘lib bo‘lgan” ish. When we arrived, the match started — biz kelganimizda o‘yin boshlandi. By the time we arrived, the match had started — biz kelganimizda o‘yin allaqachon boshlangan edi.

By the time dan keyin “will” ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Kelajak haqida gapirsangiz ham, by the time dan keyingi qismda present simple turadi: By the time you get here, I’ll have finished. Xuddi when, if va until dan keyingi kabi (Unit 25).

“By when …?” va “Until when …?” savollari to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, ikkalasi ham tabiiy va ularning ma’nolari alohida: By when do you need the report? — Hisobot sizga qachongacha kerak (muddat)? Until when is the museum open? — Muzey qachongacha ochiq (qachon yopiladi)? Soatni so‘rash uchun What time does it close? deyish ham mumkin.

“I’ll be in Bukhara until Thursday” va “I’ll be back in Tashkent by Thursday” — ikkalasi ham payshanba haqida. Farqi nima?

Birinchisida siz payshanbagacha Buxorodasiz — butun shu vaqt davomida (payshanba kuni jo‘nab ketasiz). Ikkinchisida siz payshanbagacha Toshkentga qaytib kelasiz — payshanbadan kechikmasdan; chorshanba yoki seshanba kuni ham qaytishingiz mumkin.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • by + vaqt = o‘sha payt yoki undan oldin (muddat): Send it by Friday. I’ll be back by five.
  • until / till + vaqt = o‘sha paytgacha uzluksiz: I’ll wait until five. She’s away until Monday.
  • Bir marta bo‘ladigan ish (finish, send, arrive) → by; davom etadigan ish (wait, stay, work, sleep) → until.
  • By dan keyin gap kelmaydi; gap kerak bo‘lsa by the time. Until esa gap bilan ham keladi: until I come back.
  • By the time … : o‘tmishda …, had + V3; kelajakda present simple … will be / will have + V3. Hech qachon by the time you will … emas.
  • not … until = “…dan oldin emas”; not … by = “…ga ulgurmayman”.
  • O‘zbekcha “kelmaguncha” = until he comes (inkor yo‘q!). by ≠ before: by Friday jumani o‘z ichiga oladi.
