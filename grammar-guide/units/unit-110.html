Word order 2: adverbs with the verb

So‘z tartibi — 2: fe’l bilan keladigan ravishlar
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Ba’zi ravishlar (always, never, often, usually, also, just, already, still, probably, certainly, really, almost) gapning o‘rtasida, fe’lning yonida turishini: I always walk.
  • Ularning o‘rni: oddiy fe’ldan oldin; am / is / are / was / were dan keyin; birinchi yordamchi fe’ldan keyin: She is always late. I have never been to Khiva.
  • probably, certainly, definitely inkor yordamchi fe’ldan oldin keladi: He probably won’t come.
  • all va both ham xuddi shu o‘rinlarda turadi: We all went. We were all tired. We have all finished.
  • Qaysi ravishlar gap boshida yoki oxirida ham kela olishini (sometimes, often, maybe) va qaysilari kelolmasligini.

Bunday ravishlar «qanchalik tez-tez?» (always, often, never), «qanchalik ishonch bilan?» (probably, certainly) yoki «hozirgina, allaqachon, ham, deyarli» (just, already, also, almost) kabi ma’nolarni beradi. Ular fe’lning yonida turgani uchun o‘rta o‘rin ravishlari (mid-position adverbs) deyiladi.

O‘zbek tilida ravish odatda fe’lning oldida turadi: Dilnoza universitetga doim piyoda boradi. Inglizchada ham ko‘p hollarda shunday: Dilnoza always walks to the university. Qiyinchilik boshqa joyda: inglizchada am / is / are va yordamchi fe’llar bor, ravish esa ularning orasiga kirib turadi (has never been). O‘zbek tilida bunday «orasiga kirish» yo‘q, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ravishni fe’ldan keyin qo‘yib yuboradi: I drink always tea ❌, She is late always ❌, I have been never to Khiva ❌.

O‘rta o‘rin: fe’ldan oldin, be dan keyin, yordamchi fe’ldan keyin Adverbs in mid-position: always, never, also, just ...

Quyidagi ravishlar gapning «o‘rta»sida, ya’ni fe’l bilan birga turadi. Ular qanday ma’no berishiga qarab uch guruhga bo‘linadi:

O‘rta o‘rinda keladigan asosiy ravishlar
GuruhRavishlarO‘zbekcha
Chastota (qanchalik tez-tez?)always, usually, normally, often, sometimes, occasionally, rarely, seldom, never, ever, hardly everdoim, odatda, ko‘pincha, ba’zan, kamdan-kam, hech qachon
Ishonch (qanchalik aniq?)probably, certainly, definitely, obviously, clearlyehtimol, albatta, shubhasiz, ravshanki
Boshqalaralso, just, already, still, almost, nearly, reallyham / bundan tashqari, hozirgina, allaqachon, hamon, deyarli, haqiqatan

Bu ravishlarning o‘rni fe’lning turiga bog‘liq. Quyidagi sxemada to‘q sariq ustun — ravish turadigan joy:

Ega+ravish+fe’l+...
Ega+am / is / are / was / were+ravish+...
Ega+yordamchi fe’l+ravish+asosiy fe’l+...

Qoida: O‘rta o‘rin ravishi oddiy fe’ldan oldin turadi, lekin am / is / are / was / were yoki birinchi yordamchi fe’l (have, has, can, will, do, must ...) bo‘lsa — ulardan keyin turadi.

1) Bitta oddiy fe’l — ravish fe’ldan oldin

  • Dilnoza always walks to the university.Dilnoza universitetga doim piyoda boradi.
  • Jasur often visits his grandparents in Fergana.Jasur Farg‘onadagi bobosi va buvisinikiga tez-tez boradi.
  • I also speak Russian.Men ruscha ham gapiraman.
  • We almost missed the last bus.Oxirgi avtobusdan qolib ketishimizga oz qoldi.

2) Be (am / is / are / was / were) — ravish be dan keyin

  • Sardor is never late for lessons.Sardor darsga hech qachon kech qolmaydi.
  • The market is usually crowded on Sundays.Yakshanba kunlari bozor odatda gavjum bo‘ladi.
  • Bekzod is a doctor. He is also a good guitarist.Bekzod shifokor. U yaxshi gitarachi ham.
  • They were certainly surprised.Ular, shubhasiz, hayron bo‘lishdi.

3) Yordamchi fe’l + asosiy fe’l — ravish ikkisining orasida

  • I have never been to Khiva.Men Xivada hech qachon bo‘lmaganman.
  • You can always call me after nine.Menga soat to‘qqizdan keyin istagan payt qo‘ng‘iroq qilishingiz mumkin.
  • They have just left.Ular hozirgina ketishdi.
  • Kamola is still waiting for the bus.Kamola hamon avtobus kutyapti.
  • The library will probably be closed on Monday.Kutubxona dushanba kuni, ehtimol, yopiq bo‘ladi.

4) So‘roq gapda — ravish egadan keyin

So‘roq gapda yordamchi fe’l (yoki be) eganing oldiga chiqadi, ravish esa egadan keyin turadi — ya’ni o‘sha «yordamchi fe’ldan keyin» qoidasi saqlanadi:

Do / Have / Is ...+ega+ravish+fe’l?
  • Do you often eat out?Siz tez-tez restoranda ovqatlanasizmi?
  • Have you ever been to Samarkand?Siz Samarqandda hech bo‘lganmisiz?
  • Is Aziz also coming to the party?Aziz ham ziyofatga kelyaptimi?
  • Did Malika really say that?Malika rostdan ham shunday dedimi?

5) Yordamchi fe’l yolg‘iz qolsa — ravish uning oldida

Qisqa javobda yoki fe’l takrorlanmasdan yordamchi fe’lning o‘zi qolganda (I do, he will, she is), ravish yordamchi fe’ldan oldin turadi. Gapdagi yordamchi fe’l ta’kidlansa ham shunday:

  • “Do you like green tea?” “I certainly do.”— Ko‘k choy yoqadimi? — Albatta yoqadi.
  • “Will Bekzod come?” “He probably will.”— Bekzod keladimi? — Ehtimol, keladi.
  • Sardor doesn’t often cook, but Nodira often does.Sardor kamdan-kam ovqat pishiradi, Nodira esa tez-tez pishiradi.
  • “Is the bazaar busy?” “It usually is.”— Bozor gavjummi? — Odatda gavjum bo‘ladi.
  • I definitely did lock the door!Eshikni qulfladim, bunga aminman!

Diqqat: ravish fe’l bilan to‘ldiruvchi orasiga tushmaydi (Unit 109): I often visit my aunt ✅ — I visit often my aunt ❌. Ravishni fe’ldan oldin qo‘ying yoki gapning oxiriga olib boring (agar shu ravish oxirida tura olsa — D bo‘limga qarang).

Inkor gaplarda: probably, certainly, definitely Adverbs and negative sentences

Inkor gapda ikki guruh ravish turlicha ishlaydi. probably, certainly, definitely, obviously, still kabi ravishlar inkor yordamchi fe’ldan (won’t, doesn’t, isn’t, haven’t ...) oldin turadi. always, often, usually kabi chastota ravishlari esa inkor yordamchi fe’ldan keyin keladi.

probably, certainly, definitely + inkor

Ishonch ravishlari inkor yordamchi fe’ldan oldin
❌ Noto‘g‘ri✅ To‘g‘riTarjima
He won’t probably come.He probably won’t come. / He will probably not come.U, ehtimol, kelmaydi.
I don’t definitely want that.I definitely don’t want that.Men buni mutlaqo xohlamayman.
They aren’t certainly ready.They certainly aren’t ready. / They are certainly not ready.Ular, shubhasiz, tayyor emas.
We haven’t still received the parcel.We still haven’t received the parcel.Biz posilkani hamon olganimiz yo‘q.
  • Nodira probably isn’t at home.Nodira, ehtimol, uyda emas.
  • Our team definitely won’t lose this match.Jamoamiz bu o‘yinda aniq yutqazmaydi.
  • I will probably not go to the concert.Men konsertga, ehtimol, bormayman.

Probably ham, certainly ham gap boshida kela oladi (Probably it won’t rain), lekin o‘rta o‘rin tabiiyroq. Qisqartma (won’t) bo‘lsa, ravish uning oldiga qo‘yiladi; to‘liq shakl (will not) bo‘lsa, ravish will bilan not orasiga ham tushishi mumkin (will probably not).

always, often, usually + inkor

Chastota ravishlari inkor yordamchi fe’ldan keyin keladi va ma’no «har doim ham emas» bo‘ladi:

  • Malika doesn’t always answer the phone.Malika telefonni har doim ham ko‘tarmaydi.
  • We don’t usually eat out on weekdays.Biz ish kunlari odatda tashqarida ovqatlanmaymiz.
  • I don’t often watch TV.Men televizorni tez-tez ko‘ravermayman.

always + inkor yordamchi fe’l deb bo‘lmaydi: She always doesn’t answer ❌. «Hech qachon» demoqchi bo‘lsangiz — never ishlating (She never answers the phone), «har doim ham emas» demoqchi bo‘lsangiz — doesn’t always. Va never bilan ikkinchi inkor ishlatilmaydi: I don’t never eat meat ❌ → I never eat meat / I don’t ever eat meat.

really: o‘rni ma’noni o‘zgartiradi

I don’t really like ...

«Unchalik yoqtirmayman» — yumshoq inkor.

  • I don’t really like football.
  • Men futbolni unchalik yoqtirmayman.

I really don’t like ...

«Mutlaqo yoqtirmayman» — kuchli ta’kid.

  • I really don’t like football.
  • Men futbolni umuman yoqtirmayman.

All va both All and both in the same positions

all (uch yoki undan ko‘p kishi/narsa — «hammasi») va both (ikkita — «ikkalasi») ega haqida gapirganda xuddi o‘rta o‘rin ravishlari kabi turadi. Ular egadan keyin, ya’ni fe’lning yonida joylashadi:

Boshqa shakllar — ma’no o‘zgarmaydi

  • The students all passed. = All the students passed. = All of the students passed.Talabalarning hammasi imtihondan o‘tdi.
  • My uncles are both engineers. = Both my uncles are engineers. = Both of my uncles are engineers.Ikkala amakim ham muhandis.
  • All of us went. = We all went. Both of them came. = They both came.Hammamiz bordik. Ikkalasi ham keldi.
  • I invited them all. / I like you both.Men ularning hammasini taklif qildim. / Ikkovingiz ham menga yoqasiz.

Qoida: both — faqat ikki kishi/narsa; uchtadan ko‘p bo‘lsa — all. Ikkitaga all demang: My two sisters are all doctors ❌ → My two sisters are both doctors ✅.

O‘zbekchada «hammamiz, ikkalamiz, hammasi, ikkalasi» so‘zlari egalik qo‘shimchasi bilan keladi: Biz hammamiz bordik — bu inglizchadagi We all went bilan bir xil tartib. Lekin Biz hammamiz charchagan edik ni inglizchada We were all tired deymiz: all were dan keyin turadi. Eng ko‘p xato: We went all ❌ yoki They are tired both ❌ — all/both gap oxiriga o‘tib ketadi.

Inkor gapda ehtiyot bo‘ling. We all didn’t go yoki They both didn’t come aniq emas. Aniq ma’no uchun: None of us went (hech birimiz bormadik), Neither of them came (ikkalasi ham kelmadi). We didn’t all go esa «hamma ham bormadi» degani: ba’zilar bordi, ba’zilar bormadi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega «I go there always» yoki «I eat breakfast always» deb bo‘lmaydi?

Inglizchada always va never oddiy darak gapda gapning o‘rtasida, fe’l bilan birga turadi: I always go. Ularni gap oxiriga ko‘chirib bo‘lmaydi. Gap oxirida faqat ba’zi ravishlar tura oladi (sometimes, often, quite often), ular D bo‘limdagi jadvalda ko‘rsatilgan. Shuning uchun inglizcha gap tuzayotganda «bu ravish qaysi guruhdan?» deb o‘ylang.

Ikkita yordamchi fe’l bo‘lsa, ravish qayerga qo‘yiladi?

Odatda birinchi yordamchi fe’ldan keyin: This room has never been painted. The bridge will probably be repaired next year. Passiv gapda completely, fully, carefully kabi ravishlar esa ko‘pincha asosiy fe’lga yaqin, ikkinchi yordamchidan keyin turadi: The old house has been completely destroyed. Shubha bo‘lsa, birinchi yordamchidan keyin qo‘yish xavfsiz.

Probably, maybe va perhaps farqi nima? Ularni qayerga qo‘yaman?

Ma’nosi o‘xshash («ehtimol, balki»), lekin o‘rni boshqa. probably o‘rta o‘rinda keladi: Aziz is probably ill. maybe va perhaps odatda gapning boshida turadi: Maybe Aziz is ill. Aziz is maybe ill tabiiy emas. O‘zbek tilida «balki» ham «ehtimol» ham boshida yoki o‘rtada kelaveradi, shuning uchun inglizchada ularni ajratib o‘rganish kerak.

«I don’t really like it» va «I really don’t like it» farqi bormi?

Ha. I don’t really like it — «unchalik yoqmaydi» (yumshoq). I really don’t like it — «umuman yoqmaydi, yomon ko‘raman» (kuchli ta’kid). Ravish inkor yordamchi fe’ldan oldin tursa, ta’kid kuchayadi; keyin tursa — inkor yumshaydi.

Also, too va as well — qaysi birini ishlataman?

Ma’nosi bir xil («ham»). also o‘rta o‘rinda turadi (She also speaks German), too va as well gap oxirida (She speaks German too / as well). Inkor gapda either ishlatiladi: She doesn’t speak French either. Inkor gapda «ham» ma’nosi uchun odatda either keladi, also emas.

«I just ate» deyish mumkinmi, yoki faqat «I’ve just eaten»?

Britaniya ingliz tilida odatda present perfect ishlatiladi: I’ve just eaten. Amerika ingliz tilida past simple ham juda keng tarqalgan: I just ate. Ikkala holatda ham just o‘rta o‘rinda turadi. Imtihonda (BrE) present perfect ni yozing (Unit 7).

Inkor gapda all yoki both ni qanday ishlataman?

Eng aniq yo‘l — none of us / neither of them deyish: None of us went. Neither of them came. We didn’t all go «hammamiz bormadik, ba’zilarimiz bordik» degani. We all didn’t go aniq emas, undan qoching.

«She is always losing her keys» — davomli zamon bilan always ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha. am / is / are + always + fe’l-ing qolipi tez-tez takrorlanadigan va ko‘pincha noxush ishni bildiradi: My brother is always losing his keys! («akam doim kalitlarini yo‘qotaveradi»). Ravish bu yerda ham be dan keyin turadi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • always, never, often, usually, also, just, probably, certainly ... o‘rta o‘rinda turadi: oddiy fe’ldan oldin, be dan keyin, birinchi yordamchi fe’ldan keyin.
  • So‘roq gapda ravish egadan keyin turadi: Do you often ...? Have you ever ...?
  • Yordamchi fe’l yolg‘iz qolsa, ravish uning oldida: I certainly do. He probably will.
  • probably, certainly, definitely, still — inkor yordamchi fe’ldan oldin: He probably won’t come. always, often, usually — inkordan keyin: She doesn’t always answer.
  • all (3+) va both (2) ham xuddi shu joylarda: We all went. They are both teachers. We have all finished.
