Relative clauses 5: extra information clauses (2)

Nisbiy gaplar — 5: qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi gaplar (2)
Relative clauses · Nisbiy ergash gaplar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Predlog + whom / which tuzilmasini: Dr Alimova, to whom I wrote, … (rasmiy) va uning kundalik varianti: Dr Alimova, who I wrote to, …
  • all of whom, most of which, neither of which kabi iboralarni: guruh haqida aytib, shu guruhning qismi haqida bitta gapning ichida fikr bildirish.
  • Which butun oldingi gapga ishora qilishini: Bekzod won the tournament, which surprised everyone. Bunda what ishlatib bo‘lmasligini.
  • Barcha nisbiy gap turlarining (Unit 92 dan Unit 96 gacha) bitta umumiy jadvalini.

Unit 95 da qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi gaplarning asosiy qoidalarini o‘rgandingiz: gap vergul bilan ajratiladi, that ishlatilmaydi, who / which ni tushirib bo‘lmaydi. Bu darsda shu gaplarning yana uchta muhim ko‘rinishi bor. Uchalasi ham asosan yozma va rasmiy nutqda uchraydi (faqat , which kundalik nutqda ham ko‘p ishlatiladi), uchalasida ham vergul shart.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar odatda ikkita alohida gap yoki vergul bilan ulangan ikki qism orqali beriladi: Uning uchta akasi bor, ularning hammasi Toshkentda yashaydi. / Bekzod g‘alaba qozondi, bu esa hammani hayron qoldirdi. Bu yerda ularning hammasi va bu gapni o‘zi bog‘lab turadi. Inglizchada esa all of them va it / this yangi mustaqil gapni boshlaydi, ikki mustaqil gapni faqat vergul bilan ulab bo‘lmaydi. Ularni ulash uchun nisbiy so‘z kerak: all of whom, which. Uchta xavfli joy: (1) all of whom o‘rniga all of them yozish; (2) butun gapga ishora qilganda which o‘rniga what yozish; (3) vergulni tushirib qoldirish.

Predlog + whom / which Prepositions before whom and which

Unit 93 va Unit 94 da aniqlovchi gaplarda predlog ikki joyda turishi mumkinligini ko‘rdingiz: the man I spoke to (oddiy nutq) va the man to whom I spoke (rasmiy uslub). Qo‘shimcha axborot beruvchi gaplarda ham xuddi shu ikki yo‘l bor. Lekin bitta muhim farq bor: bu yerda nisbiy so‘zni tushirib bo‘lmaydi (Unit 95). Shuning uchun the man I spoke to kabi “uchinchi yo‘l” vergulli gapda yo‘q.

Qoida: predlog nisbiy so‘zdan oldin kelsa, faqat whom (odam) yoki which (narsa) ishlatiladi: who, that, what mumkin emas. Predlog gap oxirida qolsa, odam uchun who (rasmiyroq: whom), narsa uchun which kerak; that yo‘q. Ikkala holatda ham nisbiy so‘z majburiy, predlog esa faqat bir joyda.

Misollar

  • My grandfather, from whom I learned to play chess, is eighty-five.Menga shaxmat o‘ynashni o‘rgatgan bobom sakson besh yoshda.
  • The conference, at which more than three hundred teachers were present, lasted three days.Uch yuzdan ortiq o‘qituvchi qatnashgan konferensiya uch kun davom etdi.
  • The scholarship, for which over two hundred students applied, was awarded to Malika.Ikki yuzdan ortiq talaba ariza topshirgan stipendiya Malikaga berildi.

Predlog fe’lning ajralmas qismi bo‘lsa

Look after, look for, look forward to, put up with, get on with kabi birikmalarda predlog fe’l bilan birga ma’no beradi (Unit 133, Unit 137). Bunday predloglar odatda gap oxirida qoladi; ularni which dan oldinga o‘tkazish g‘alati eshitiladi.

  • My neighbour’s dog, which I look after every Saturday, is called Max.Men har shanba qarab turadigan qo‘shnimning iti Maks deb ataladi.
  • The exhibition, which we are all looking forward to, opens on Monday.Hammamiz intiqlik bilan kutayotgan ko‘rgazma dushanba kuni ochiladi.

O‘zbek tilida predlog yo‘q: uning vazifasini ko‘makchi (bilan, haqida) yoki qo‘shimcha (-ga, -dan) bajaradi, nisbiy gap esa sifatdosh bilan otdan oldin keladi: men xat yozgan Alimova doktor. Bu yerda “-ga” ko‘rinmaydi, lekin ma’no ichida bor. Inglizchada predlog har doim ko‘rinib turishi kerak. Ikki xato: (1) predlogni tushirib qoldirish: Dr Alimova, who I wrote, … ❌ (to kerak); (2) predlogni ikki joyda yozish: to whom I wrote to ❌.

Eslatma: joy va vaqt uchun in / at / on which o‘rniga where va when ham ishlatiladi: The mahalla, where I grew up, … (Unit 94, Unit 95). Britaniya ingliz tilida Mr, Mrs, Dr nuqtasiz yoziladi, Amerika ingliz tilida nuqta bilan: Mr., Mrs., Dr. Qoidalar ikkala variantda bir xil; predlog + whom Amerikada ham yozma va rasmiy uslubga xos.

All of whom, most of which … Quantity word + of + whom / which

Ba’zan guruhni (odamlar yoki narsalar) tilga olib, darrov shu guruhning qismi haqida gapirish kerak bo‘ladi: “uning to‘rtta amakivachchasi bor, ularning hammasi …”. Inglizchada buni miqdor so‘zi + of + whom / which bilan, bitta gap ichida aytamiz. Bu gap ham qo‘shimcha axborot gapi, shuning uchun oldida vergul turadi.

Guruh (ot),all / most / two …+of+whom / which+fe’l…
  • Dilnoza has four cousins, all of whom live in Fergana.Dilnozaning to‘rtta amakivachchasi bor, ularning hammasi Farg‘onada yashaydi.
  • Our class has twenty-four students, most of whom come from Tashkent.Sinfimizda yigirma to‘rtta o‘quvchi bor, ularning ko‘pchiligi Toshkentdan.
  • Malika invited ten guests, only six of whom came.Malika o‘nta mehmonni taklif qildi, ularning faqat oltitasi keldi.
  • Jasur bought a lot of old books, some of which were very valuable.Jasur ko‘plab eski kitoblar sotib oldi, ulardan ba’zilari juda qimmatli edi.
  • We have two printers, neither of which works.Bizda ikkita printer bor, ularning hech biri ishlamaydi.
  • The company has two hundred employees, half of whom work from home.Kompaniyada ikki yuz nafar xodim bor, ularning yarmi uydan ishlaydi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’nolar ularning hammasi, ularning ikkalasi ham, ularning hech biri, ulardan biri, ularning ko‘pchiligi kabi iboralar bilan beriladi. Ular gapni o‘zlari bog‘lab turadi: Uning ikkita akasi bor, ularning ikkalasi ham shifokor. Inglizchada “ularning” = of them ni of whom / of which ga almashtirasiz (yuqoridagi jadvalga qarang). Shunda ikki gap bitta gapga aylanadi. Them ni qoldirsangiz, vergul bilan ulangan ikkita mustaqil gap hosil bo‘ladi, bu xato.

Foiz va ulush bilan: ten per cent of whom, a third of whom. Britaniya ingliz tilida per cent ikki so‘z, Amerika ingliz tilida percent bir so‘z. Bu iboralar asosan yozma nutqda uchraydi. Og‘zaki nutqda ko‘pincha ikkita gap ishlatiladi: She has four cousins. They all live in Fergana.

Which = butun gap Which referring to the whole clause

Ba’zan which bitta otga emas, butun oldingi gapga (voqeaga, fikrga) ishora qiladi. U shu voqea haqida izoh beradi: “bu hammani hayron qoldirdi”, “bu juda yoqimsiz”. Bunday which dan oldin doim vergul turadi.

To‘liq gap,which+fe’l (is / was / made / meant …)+izoh
  • Bekzod won the chess tournament, which surprised everyone.Bekzod shaxmat turnirida g‘alaba qozondi, bu hammani hayron qoldirdi.
  • The plane was delayed for five hours, which made us very tired.Samolyot besh soat kechikdi, bu bizni juda charchatdi.
  • Our neighbours play loud music every night, which is really annoying.Qo‘shnilarimiz har kecha baland ovozda musiqa qo‘yishadi, bu juda g‘ashga tegadi.
  • Nodira speaks fluent German, which helps her a lot at work.Nodira nemis tilida ravon gapiradi, bu unga ishda juda yordam beradi.
  • The bank was closed, which I hadn’t expected.Bank yopiq edi, buni kutmagan edim.
  • The lift wasn’t working, which meant we had to climb nine floors.Lift ishlamayotgan edi, bu esa to‘qqiz qavatga yurib chiqishimizni anglatardi.

Which va what: farqi

Qoida: butun oldingi gap haqida izoh bermoqchi bo‘lsangiz: , which + fe’l. What nisbiy so‘z sifatida ot yoki gapdan keyin kelmaydi; that, it, this bu yerda ikki gapni bog‘lay olmaydi. Fe’l doim birlikda bo‘ladi, chunki butun voqea bitta narsa deb olinadi: The prices went up, which was bad for everyone.

O‘zbek tilida “bu” (yoki “bu esa”) butun gapga ishora qilib, izoh boshlaydi: U imtihondan o‘tdi, bu hammani hayron qoldirdi. Ingliz tilida uning vazifasini , which bajaradi: u gapni ulaydi va “bu” ning o‘rnini bosadi. What esa “nima” ning nisbiy ma’nosiga mos keladi, ya’ni “o‘sha narsa” degani (nima desangiz, shuni qilaman = I’ll do what you say). Shuning uchun “bu” ni what deb tarjima qilmang. “Shuning uchun” esa , which is why bo‘ladi.

Which — oxirgi ot yoki butun gap?

Gapda ot bo‘lsa, which ikki ma’noga ega bo‘lishi mumkin. Qaysi ekanini ma’no ko‘rsatadi:

  • She sold her car, which upset her family.U mashinasini sotdi, bu oilasini xafa qildi. (xafa qilgan narsa — sotish, butun gap)
  • She sold her car, which was very old.U juda eski mashinasini sotdi. (eski bo‘lgan narsa — mashina, bitta ot)

Ma’no noaniq bo‘lsa, gapni qayta yozing: She sold her car, and that upset her family. (and dan keyin that yangi gapning egasi bo‘la oladi.)

Predlogni oldinga qo‘yish yaxshimi yoki oxirgami? Qaysi birini tanlayman?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Rasmiy yozma matnda (xat, hisobot, ilmiy ish) predlog + whom / which ishlating: Dr Alimova, to whom I wrote, … Suhbatda, shaxsiy xatda, norasmiy yozuvda who / which … predlog tabiiyroq: Dr Alimova, who I wrote to, … Muhimi — bir matn ichida uslubni aralashtirmaslik. Frazali fe’llarda (look after, look forward to) predlog odatda oxirida qoladi.

“All of whom” da nega “whom” ishlatiladi? Axir u gapning egasi-ku.

Chunki who predlogdan keyin kela olmaydi. All of whom live in Fergana gapida all of whom ega vazifasida, lekin uning ichida of predlogi bor, shuning uchun nisbiy so‘z whom bo‘ladi. Narsalar uchun which: all of which.

“Which” butun gap uchun ishlatilganda fe’l nega doim birlikda?

Chunki butun voqea bitta narsa deb olinadi. Oldingi gapda ko‘plik ot bo‘lsa ham, which dan keyin birlik fe’l keladi: The prices went up, which was bad for everyone. ✅ — which were bad ❌ (agar which “narxlar” ga emas, butun voqeaga ishora qilsa). Ko‘plik otga ishora qilsa: The prices, which were high, … ✅.

“That” yoki “this” bilan butun gapga ishora qilsam bo‘ladimi?

Vergul bilan ulab bo‘lmaydi: He passed the exam, that surprised everyone. ❌ Lekin ikkita gap qilsangiz bo‘ladi: He passed the exam. That surprised everyone. ✅ Yoki and bilan: He passed the exam, and that surprised everyone. ✅ Bitta gap ichida esa faqat , which.

“What” ni umuman qachon ishlataman?

What “o‘sha narsa(lar)” ma’nosida, oldida ot bo‘lmagan holda ishlatiladi: What surprised everyone was his speed. (Hammani hayron qoldirgan narsa — uning tezligi edi.) I didn’t understand what he said. Ya’ni what o‘zi ot vazifasida turadi, which esa oldingi gapga yoki otga qaytadi.

“None of whom” dan keyin fe’l birlikda bo‘ladimi yoki ko‘plikda?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Rasmiy uslubda ko‘pincha birlik (none of whom has), kundalik nutqda ko‘pincha ko‘plik (none of whom have). O‘tgan zamonda masala yo‘q: none of whom had any experience. Neither of whom va either of whom uchun ham xuddi shunday.

Bu tuzilmalar og‘zaki nutqda ishlatiladimi?

, which (butun gap) og‘zaki nutqda juda ko‘p uchraydi: He passed, which was great. Predlog + whom va all of whom kabi iboralar esa asosan yozma va rasmiy nutqqa xos. Suhbatda odamlar odatda ikkita gap aytishadi: She has four cousins. They all live in Fergana. Ammo ularni tushunish va yozma ishda qo‘llay olish muhim.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Predlog + whom / which (rasmiy): Dr Alimova, to whom I wrote, … Kundalik: Dr Alimova, who I wrote to, … Predlogdan keyin faqat whom / which; predlog bir joyda; nisbiy so‘z tushmaydi.
  • Miqdor so‘zi + of + whom / which: four cousins, all of whom live … Odamlar uchun whom, narsalar uchun which; all of them ❌ (vergul bilan ulab bo‘lmaydi).
  • Fe’l: each, one, much — birlik; both, many, several, two — ko‘plik; none, neither, either — birlik yoki ko‘plik; all, most, half — whom / which ishora qilgan otga qarab.
  • , which butun oldingi gapga ishora qiladi: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone. Fe’l birlikda. What, that, it ❌.
  • Qotib qolgan iboralar: which is why, which means, in which case, after which, at which point, by which time.
  • Barcha gaplarda vergul shart; that ishlatilmaydi (Unit 95).
  • Yozma nutq: of whom / of which, predlog + whom. Og‘zaki nutq: ikkita gap yoki who … to, , which.
