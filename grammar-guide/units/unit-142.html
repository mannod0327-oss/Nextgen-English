Phrasal verbs 6: up/down

Frazali fe’llar — 6: up / down
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • up (yuqoriga) va down (pastga) bilan yasalgan eng muhim frazali fe’llarni: stand up, sit down, bend down, pick up, put down, put up, take down, write down.
  • up / down miqdor va darajani ham o‘zgartirishini: turn up / turn down (ovoz, isitish), slow down, calm down, cut down on.
  • down ba’zan “to‘xtash, buzilish, vayron bo‘lish” ma’nosini berishini: break down, close down, knock down, blow down, burn down.
  • Odamlarga nisbatan ishlatiladigan fe’llarni: let sb down, turn sb down, look up to, look down on.
  • Qaysi fe’l to‘ldiruvchisiz, qaysi biri ajraladi, qaysi biri ajralmaydi — va olmosh qayerga tushishini (pick it up).

Particle up ko‘pincha “yuqoriga; ko‘proq; balandroq”, down esa “pastga; kamroq; pastroq; to‘xtash” degan tasavvurni beradi. Bu faqat umumiy yo‘l-yo‘riq: har bir frazali fe’lning ma’nosini alohida o‘rganish kerak (Unit 137).

Belgilar: ∅ — to‘ldiruvchisiz; ⇄ — ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: pick it up); ✗ — ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida: look down on them).

O‘zbek tilida “yuqoriga / pastga” ma’nosi odatda fe’l o‘zagiga qo‘shiladigan yordamchi fe’l bilan beriladi: ko‘tarib olmoq (pick up), yozib olmoq (write down), buzib tashlamoq (knock down), yonib ketmoq (burn down), buzilib qolmoq (break down). Ba’zan esa o‘zbekcha ham inglizchaga o‘xshaydi: past nazar bilan qaramoq ≈ look down on.

Tuzoq: inglizcha turn down “burilib tushmoq” emas — “pasaytirmoq” yoki “rad etmoq” degani. Put down ko‘pincha “qo‘ldagi narsani pastga qo‘ymoq”; “yozib olmoq” uchun eng ko‘p write down ishlatiladi (put sb’s name down — ismini ro‘yxatga yozib qo‘ymoq ham bor, lekin bu kamroq).

Yuqoriga va pastga: harakat va qo‘l bilan qilinadigan ishlar stand up, sit down, bend down, pick up, put down, put up, take down, write down

Eng oson guruh: particle yo‘nalishni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri ko‘rsatadi. up — yuqoriga (o‘rnidan turmoq, yerdan ko‘tarmoq), down — pastga (o‘tirmoq, pastga qo‘ymoq, qog‘ozga tushirmoq).

Qoida: pick up, put down, put up, take down, write down to‘ldiruvchi bilan ishlatiladi va ajraladi. Ot bo‘lsa — ikki o‘rin ham to‘g‘ri (pick up the pen / pick the pen up); olmosh bo‘lsa — faqat o‘rtada: pick it up, pick him up (❌ pick up it, pick up him). Stand up, sit down va bend down to‘ldiruvchi olmaydi.

Odamga “Sit!” demang — bu itga aytiladigan buyruq. Muloyim shakl: Sit down, please. yoki Take a seat, please.

pick up ning yana bir ma’nosi bor: tilni yoki odatni “o‘rganib olmoq” (norasmiy). Bu ma’no Unit 137 da misol sifatida keltirilgan: pick up some Turkish. Qaysi ma’no ekanini to‘ldiruvchi ko‘rsatadi: narsa — ko‘tarmoq, odam — olib ketmoq, til — o‘rganib olmoq.

Ko‘paytirish va kamaytirish: ovoz, tezlik, his-tuyg‘u turn up / turn down, slow down, calm down, cut down (on)

turn down ning ikki ma’nosi bir-biriga bog‘liq emas. Qurilma, ovoz, isitish bo‘lsa — pasaytirmoq (turn the radio down). Taklif, ish, ariza yoki odam bo‘lsa — rad etmoq (turn the offer down). Ikkalasida ham olmosh o‘rtada: turn it down.

look up to bilan look up ni adashtirmang. look up to him — unga havas qilmoq; look a word up — so‘zni lug‘atdan qidirmoq (Unit 144). look up to va look down on — uch so‘zli fe’llar, shuning uchun ajralmaydi (Unit 137): look up to her, look down on them.

Ingliz tilida “hafsalasi pir bo‘ldi” ma’nosini ko‘pincha majhul shaklda aytishadi: I felt let down. (Men umidim puchga chiqqanini his qildim.) The team was let down by its captain. (Jamoaning umidini kapitan puchga chiqardi.)

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Pick up” nechta ma’noga ega? Qaysi biri ekanini qanday bilaman?

Asosiy ma’nolari: (1) yerdan yoki ustidan ko‘tarib olmoq: pick up a pen; (2) odamni olib ketmoq, ko‘pincha mashinada: I’ll pick you up at six; (3) norasmiy: tilni yoki odatni o‘rganib olmoq: pick up some English. Ma’noni to‘ldiruvchi ko‘rsatadi: narsa bo‘lsa — ko‘tarmoq, odam bo‘lsa — olib ketmoq, til bo‘lsa — o‘rganib olmoq.

“Turn down” ni ovoz ma’nosida ham, rad etish ma’nosida ham ishlatsam, adashmaymanmi?

Kontekst ko‘rsatadi. Qurilma, ovoz, isitish yoki yorug‘lik bo‘lsa — pasaytirmoq: turn the radio down. Taklif, ish, ariza yoki odam bo‘lsa — rad etmoq: turn the offer down, turn him down. Ikkalasida ham ajraladi: turn it down.

“Break down” va “break up” ning farqi nimada?

break down — mashina yoki asbob buzilishi, yoki odamning o‘zini tutolmay yig‘lab yuborishi: Our car broke down. break up — munosabatning tugashi (Unit 144): Aziz and Kamola broke up.

“Close down” va “shut down” bir xilmi?

Do‘kon, zavod, korxona uchun deyarli bir xil: The factory closed down / shut down. (butunlay yopildi). Kompyuter yoki qurilma uchun ko‘proq shut down ishlatiladi: The computer shut down.

“Cut down” dan keyin “on” qachon kerak?

Nimani kamaytirayotganingizni aytsangiz, on kerak: cut down on sugar / salt / coffee. Agar nima ekani oldin aytilgan bo‘lsa, on siz ishlatish mumkin: I’m trying to cut down. Daraxt kesish ma’nosida esa cut down to‘ldiruvchi bilan keladi va on kerak emas: cut down a tree.

“Stand up” bilan “get up” ning farqi bormi?

stand up — oyoqqa turmoq, tikka turmoq: Everybody stood up. get up — o‘rnidan turmoq; yotoqdan turish uchun faqat get up ishlatiladi: I get up at six. O‘tirgan joyingizdan turib ketishda ham get up aytish mumkin: He got up and left.

“Look up to” va “look down on” nega ajralmaydi?

Chunki bu uch so‘zli fe’llar: fe’l + particle + predlog. To‘ldiruvchi doim eng oxirida turadi: look up to her, look down on them (Unit 137). Ularni look her up to deb bo‘lmaydi.

  • up — yuqoriga, ko‘paytirish; down — pastga, kamaytirish, to‘xtash. Lekin har bir fe’lni alohida o‘rganing.
  • To‘ldiruvchisiz: stand up, sit down, bend down, break down; ikkala usulda ham: slow down, calm down, close down, blow down, burn down.
  • Ajraladigan: pick up, put down, put up, take down, write down, turn up / down, knock down, pull down, let sb down, turn sb down — olmosh o‘rtada: pick it up (❌ pick up it).
  • Ajralmaydigan uch so‘zli fe’llar: cut down on, look up to, look down on — to‘ldiruvchi oxirda: look up to her.
  • turn down: ovoz, isitish — pasaytirmoq; taklif, ish, odam — rad etmoq.
  • close down — butunlay yopilmoq; close — ish vaqti tugab yopilmoq.
  • break noto‘g‘ri fe’l: broke down, has broken down.
