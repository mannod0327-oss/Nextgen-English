Reported speech 2

O‘zlashtirma gap — 2
Reported speech · O‘zlashtirma gap

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • O‘zlashtirma gapda zamonni o‘zgartirish qachon shart emasligini (gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa).
  • Qachon o‘tgan zamon majburiy ekanini (vaziyat o‘zgargan yoki gap noto‘g‘ri bo‘lib chiqqan bo‘lsa).
  • say va tell farqini: He said ... / He told me ...
  • Buyruq va iltimoslarni yetkazishni: tell somebody to do, ask somebody to do, not to do.

Unit 47 da o‘rgandik: said dan keyin zamon odatda bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi. Bu darsda qoidaning “nozik joylari”ni ko‘ramiz: qachon surmasa ham bo‘ladi, qachon albatta surish kerak, va say / tell / ask fe’llarini qanday to‘g‘ri ishlatish kerak.

O‘zbek tilida bitta aytmoq fe’li ham say, ham tell o‘rnida keladi: U charchaganini aytdi (said) — U menga charchaganini aytdi (told me). Ingliz tilida “kimga” ko‘rsatilsa, odatda tell kerak. Buyruqlarni o‘zbekchada -ishimni aytdi, -sin dedi bilan yetkazamiz: Shifokor menga dorini ichishimni aytdi = The doctor told me to take the medicine. Iltimoslarni esa -ishimni so‘radi bilan: Buvim mendan sumkalarni ko‘tarishga yordam berishimni so‘radi = Grandma asked me to help her with the bags. Diqqat: o‘zbekcha “mendan so‘radi” — inglizchada asked me (from yo‘q!).

Zamonni o‘zgartirish har doim shart emas It is not always necessary to change the verb

Bir oy oldin Kamola sizga shunday degan edi:

  • “I’m learning Korean. I really enjoy it.”«Koreys tilini o‘rganyapman. Menga juda yoqyapti.»

Bugun ham Kamola koreys tilini o‘rganyapti va bu unga hali ham yoqadi. Demak, uning gapi hozir ham to‘g‘ri. Shuning uchun siz ikkala usulda ham yetkaza olasiz:

  • Kamola said (that) she is learning Korean and she really enjoys it.Kamola koreys tilini o‘rganayotganini va bu unga juda yoqishini aytdi. (hozirgi zamon — “bu hali ham shunday” degan urg‘u)
  • Kamola said (that) she was learning Korean and she really enjoyed it.Xuddi shu ma’no. (o‘tgan zamon — oddiy, “hikoya” uslubi)

Qoida: Agar aytilgan gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, zamonni o‘zgartirish ixtiyoriy: hozirgi zamonni ham, o‘tgan zamonni ham ishlatishingiz mumkin. Agar vaziyat tugagan yoki gap noto‘g‘ri bo‘lib chiqqan bo‘lsa, faqat o‘tgan zamon to‘g‘ri.

  • Jasur told me he is / was saving money for a new car.Jasur menga yangi mashina uchun pul yig‘ayotganini aytdi. (u hali ham pul yig‘yapti)
  • Our teacher said that water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.O‘qituvchimiz suv 100 darajada qaynashini aytdi. (umumiy haqiqat — hozirgi zamon tabiiyroq, “boiled” ham xato emas)

Qachon o‘tgan zamon majburiy?

Nodira sizga kecha shunday degan edi: “Bekzod is in Moscow.” Bugun esa siz Bekzodni Chorsu bozorida ko‘rib qoldingiz. Siz unga aytasiz:

  • Bekzod! What are you doing here? Nodira said you were in Moscow.Bekzod! Bu yerda nima qilyapsan? Nodira Moskvadasan degandi.

Bu yerda Nodira said you are in Moscow ❌ deyish mumkin emas, chunki Bekzod aniq Moskvada emas — u qarshingizda turibdi! Aytilgan gap bilan haqiqat o‘rtasida farq bo‘lsa, faqat o‘tgan zamon ishlatiladi.

  • Aziz said he was going to Bukhara this weekend, but he changed his mind.Aziz bu dam olish kunlari Buxoroga borishini aytgan edi, lekin fikridan qaytdi. (reja endi kuchda emas)
  • I thought you said you didn’t eat meat. Why are you eating a kebab?Go‘sht yemayman degansan shekilli. Nega kabob yeyapsan? (gap endi to‘g‘ri emasdek)

Ikkilansangiz, o‘tgan zamonni tanlang: said dan keyin o‘tgan zamon deyarli har doim grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri. Hozirgi zamon esa faqat gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lgandagina mumkin.

say va tell say and tell

Ikkalasi ham “aytmoq” deb tarjima qilinadi, lekin grammatikasi har xil:

tell + KIMGA (odam)

Kimga aytilganini ko‘rsatsangiz — tell. Odam to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri, to siz keladi:

  • Nodira told me (that) you were in Moscow.
  • What did you tell your parents?
  • I told Aziz about the party.

say + NIMA (odamsiz)

Odamni ko‘rsatmasangiz — say:

  • Nodira said (that) you were in Moscow.
  • What did you say?
  • Odamni qo‘shmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — say ... to somebody: Aziz said thank you to the driver.
say / tell: to‘g‘ri va noto‘g‘ri
✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘ri
She told me (that) she was busy.She said me that she was busy.
She said (that) she was busy.She told that she was busy.
She said to me (that) she was busy.She told to me that she was busy.
What did he say to you? / What did he tell you?What did he say you?
  • Dilnoza told her parents she wanted to study medicine.Dilnoza ota-onasiga tibbiyotni o‘qimoqchi ekanini aytdi.
  • “What did the doctor say?” “He said it was nothing serious.”— Shifokor nima dedi? — Jiddiy narsa emasligini aytdi.
  • Sardor said sorry to his sister.Sardor singlisidan kechirim so‘radi.

Turg‘un iboralar

Ba’zi iboralarda tell odamsiz ham keladi, say esa o‘z iboralariga ega. Ularni yodlab oling:

tell + ...say + ...
tell the truth (haqiqatni aytmoq)say hello / goodbye (salom / xayr demoq)
tell a lie (yolg‘on gapirmoq)say sorry (kechirim so‘ramoq)
tell a story / a joke (hikoya / latifa aytib bermoq)say yes / no (ha / yo‘q demoq)
tell the time (soatni bilmoq, vaqtni aytmoq)say thank you (rahmat demoq)
tell the difference (farqini ajratmoq)say something / nothing (biror narsa / hech narsa demoq)

explain, describe, mention, suggest fe’llari ham say kabi ishlaydi — odamdan oldin to kerak: She explained the rule to me. ✅ — She explained me the rule. ❌

tell / ask somebody to do something Reporting orders and requests

Buyruq, ko‘rsatma yoki iltimosni yetkazganda to + fe’l (infinitive) ishlatiladi. Bu usul ayniqsa tell va ask bilan keng tarqalgan:

Ega+told / asked+kimga (me, us, Aziz)+(not)+to+fe’l
Buyruq va iltimoslar: ko‘chirma → o‘zlashtirma
Ko‘chirma gapO‘zlashtirma gap
The doctor: “Take this medicine three times a day.”The doctor told me to take the medicine three times a day.
The teacher: “Don’t be late.”The teacher told us not to be late.
Grandma: “Please help me with the bags.”Grandma asked me to help her with the bags.
Malika: “Could you open the window, please?”Malika asked me to open the window.
Dilnoza: “Please don’t read my diary.”Dilnoza asked me not to read her diary.
  • The doctor told me to take the medicine three times a day.Shifokor menga dorini kuniga uch mahal ichishimni aytdi.
  • The teacher told us not to be late.O‘qituvchi bizga kechikmasligimizni aytdi.
  • Dilnoza asked me not to read her diary.Dilnoza mendan kundaligini o‘qimasligimni so‘radi.

Qoida: Inkor buyruqda not to dan oldin keladi: told me not to go. tell — buyruq, ko‘rsatma, maslahat; ask — iltimos (odatda muloyim).

said (not) to do something

said bilan ham bu tuzilma mumkin, lekin faqat odamsiz:

  • Aziz said not to wait for him.Aziz o‘zini kutmaslikni aytdi.
  • The teacher said to bring our dictionaries tomorrow.O‘qituvchi ertaga lug‘atlarimizni olib kelishni aytdi.

Aziz said me not to wait ❌ — odam bo‘lsa, told: Aziz told me not to wait. ✅

ask somebody to do va ask for something

Agar kimdir narsa so‘rasa, ask for + ot ishlatiladi:

  • “Can I have a glass of water?” → The guest asked for a glass of water.Mehmon bir stakan suv so‘radi.
  • “Can you lend me your pen?” → Sardor asked me to lend him my pen.Sardor mendan ruchkamni berib turishimni so‘radi.

O‘zbekchada “mendan so‘radi” deymiz (chiqish kelishigi -dan). Inglizchada ask dan keyin odam to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri keladi: asked me ✅, asked from me ❌. Shuningdek, o‘zbekcha -ishimni so‘radi → inglizcha asked me to + fe’l, “that” bilan emas.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, qaysi zamonni tanlaganim yaxshi?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Hozirgi zamon “bu hali ham shunday” degan ma’noni ta’kidlaydi: She said she lives in Fergana. O‘tgan zamon esa oddiy hikoya uslubi: She said she lived in Fergana. Imtihonda ikkilansangiz, o‘tgan zamon xavfsizroq.

Nega “Nodira said you were in Moscow” — Bekzod hozir Moskvada emas-ku?

Aynan shuning uchun! were bu yerda “Nodira shunday degandi (lekin bu to‘g‘ri emas ekan)” ma’nosini beradi. are desangiz, go‘yo hozir ham u Moskvada, deb tasdiqlagandek bo‘lasiz — bu esa mantiqsiz.

say va tell ni qanday tez ajrataman?

Fe’ldan keyin odam keladimi? Kelsa — tell (told me, told Aziz). Kelmasa — say (said that ..., said hello). Odamni “say” bilan ishlatmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, to qo‘shing: said to me.

“tell” har doim odam bilan keladimi?

Deyarli har doim. Istisno — turg‘un iboralar: tell the truth, tell a lie, tell a story, tell a joke, tell the time, tell the difference. Masalan: Always tell the truth. (Har doim haqiqatni gapiring.)

“told me to” va “asked me to” ning farqi nima?

told me to — buyruq yoki ko‘rsatma (o‘qituvchi, shifokor, boshliq). asked me to — iltimos, odatda “please” yoki “Could you ...?” bilan aytilgan gap. “Open your books.” → The teacher told us to open our books. “Could you help me?” → She asked me to help her.

“said to me” to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, to‘g‘ri: She said to me that she was tired. Lekin bunday gaplarda told me ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi va tabiiyroq eshitiladi. Buyruqlarda esa faqat told me to: She told me to wait.

“told me to not go” deyish mumkinmi?

Standart va imtihon uchun to‘g‘ri shakl — not to go: He told me not to go. “to not go” norasmiy nutqda ba’zan eshitiladi, lekin uni ishlatmaganingiz ma’qul.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa: hozirgi yoki o‘tgan zamon — ikkalasi ham mumkin (said she lives / lived).
  • Gap endi to‘g‘ri emas yoki vaziyat tugagan bo‘lsa: faqat o‘tgan zamon (Nodira said you were in Moscow).
  • Xabar fe’li says (hozirgi) bo‘lsa — zamon o‘zgarmaydi.
  • tell + odam (told me), say — odamsiz yoki say to + odam. said me ❌, told that ❌.
  • Buyruq: told somebody (not) to do; iltimos: asked somebody (not) to do.
  • said (not) to do — faqat odamsiz: He said not to wait.
  • asked me (from emas!); narsa so‘ralsa — asked for + ot.
