117

Like and as

Like va as
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • like + ot / olmosh / -ing — “…ga o‘xshash”, “… kabi”: Dilnoza sings like an angel.
  • Misol keltirishda such as va like.
  • as + ega + fe’l — “…gandek”, “…gani kabi”: Cook the rice as the recipe says. Shuningdek as usual, as I said, as you know kabi tayyor iboralar.
  • as + ot — “…sifatida” (haqiqiy kasb yoki vazifa): She works as a nurse. Va like + ot — “…ga o‘xshab” (aslida u emas): He behaves like a child.
  • the same as iborasi (“aynan bir xil”).
  • Nega ❌ He works like a driver va ❌ She sings as an angel deyilmasligini.

Ikkala so‘z ham o‘zbekchada ko‘pincha kabi yoki -dek deb tarjima qilinadi. Inglizchada esa ular bir-biriga almashmaydi. Asosiy qoida: like dan keyin ot, olmosh yoki -ing keladi; as dan keyin esa odatda to‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) yoki kasb/vazifani bildiruvchi ot keladi.

O‘zbek tili bu farqni o‘zi ham ko‘rsatadi. Ot bilan “farishta kabi” (like an angel), fe’l bilan “men aytgandek, siz bilganingizdek” (as I said, as you know). Rol uchun esa “shifokor sifatida” (as a doctor) deymiz. Xato shundan chiqadi: o‘zbekcha “kabi” ham ot bilan (farishta kabi), ham fe’l bilan (men aytgan kabi) ishlatiladi, inglizchada esa ot oldidan like, ega + fe’l oldidan as kerak; rol uchun esa yana as.

Like — “o‘xshash”, “… kabi” like = similar to, in the same way as

like bu yerda predlog (preposition). Shuning uchun undan keyin ot, olmosh (me, him, her, us, them, it) yoki -ing shaklidagi fe’l keladi. U ikki narsa o‘xshash ekanini yoki biror ish bir xil usulda bajarilganini bildiradi.

What is it like? — qanday ekan?

be like “qanday?” (tabiati, tashqi ko‘rinishi, sifati) deb so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Bu yerda like — predlog, shuning uchun savolning oxirida turadi. Fe’l like (yoqtirmoq) bilan chalkashtirmang:

What’s the weather like in Samarkand in April?Havo qanday?It’s warm and sunny.
What does your teacher like?U nimani yoqtiradi? (fe’l like)She likes poetry and green tea.

Misol keltirish: such as va like such as / like = for example

Ro‘yxatdan misol keltirishda such as yoki like ishlatiladi. Ma’no bir xil: “… kabi”, “masalan”. such as biroz rasmiyroq, like og‘zaki nutqda ko‘proq uchraydi. as bu yerda yolg‘iz o‘zi ishlatilmaydi.

  • Many Uzbek cities, such as Bukhara and Khiva, attract tourists.Buxoro va Xiva kabi ko‘plab o‘zbek shaharlari sayyohlarni jalb qiladi.
  • Fruit like grapes and apricots is cheap in summer.Uzum va o‘rik kabi mevalar yozda arzon bo‘ladi.
  • She has visited countries such as Turkey, Egypt and Japan.U Turkiya, Misr va Yaponiya kabi mamlakatlarda bo‘lgan.
GapBaho
cities such as Bukhara and Khiva✅ to‘g‘ri

cities as Bukhara and Khiva❌ noto‘g‘ri

Juda rasmiy yozuvda such cities as Bukhara and Khiva shakli ham bor, lekin sizga such as va like ni bilish yetarli.

O‘zbek tilida misol keltirish uchun ham xuddi shu kabi ishlatiladi: Buxoro va Xiva kabi shaharlar. Inglizchada shu o‘rinda such as yoki like bo‘ladi, as emas.

As + ega + fe’l — “…gandek”, “…gani kabi” as = in the way that

Biror ish qanday usulda qilinganini to‘liq gap bilan tushuntirsak, as + ega + fe’l ishlatiladi. O‘zbekchada bu -gandek, -ganidek, -gani kabi qo‘shimchalariga to‘g‘ri keladi.

asosiy gap+as+ega+fe’l
  • Bekzod made the plov as his grandmother had taught him.Bekzod palovni buvisi o‘rgatganidek damladi.
  • Please write the report as your manager asked.Iltimos, hisobotni rahbaringiz so‘ragandek yozing.
  • Put the books back on the shelf as they were.Kitoblarni javonga avvalgi holicha qaytarib qo‘ying.
  • The meeting went as we planned.Yig‘ilish biz rejalashtirgandek o‘tdi.
  • Do as the doctor says and rest for a few days.Shifokor aytganidek qiling: bir necha kun dam oling.

Qoida: like + ot/olmosh, as + ega + fe’l. Taqqoslang: Nobody cooks like my mother. (ona — ot) — Make the tea as my mother makes it. (my mother makes it — to‘liq gap)

Tayyor iboralar: as usual, as I said ...

Quyidagi iboralar har doim as bilan keladi. Ularni bir butun qilib yod oling:

IboraO‘zbekchaMisol

Like o‘rniga as — rasmiy va norasmiy

Og‘zaki nutqda, ayniqsa Amerika inglizchasida, as o‘rniga gap oldidan ko‘pincha like ishlatiladi: Nobody plays like he does. Bu norasmiy uslub; ba’zi odamlar uni xato deb hisoblaydi. Imtihon, xat va rasmiy yozuvda as ishlating.

Rasmiy / imtihonNorasmiy (og‘zaki)
Cook the rice as the recipe says.Cook the rice like the recipe says.
Write your name as it appears in your passport.Write your name like it appears in your passport.
As I said, the bus leaves at six.Like I said, the bus leaves at six.

Tayyor iboralar ichida faqat as I said o‘rniga og‘zaki nutqda like I said ham deyiladi. As usual, as you know, as expected o‘rniga like ishlatilmaydi.

As + ot va like + ot as a teacher / like a teacher

Ot bilan ham ikkala so‘z ishlatiladi, lekin ma’nosi butunlay farq qiladi:

as + ot — “…sifatida”

  • Odamning haqiqiy kasbi yoki vazifasi
  • Narsaning vazifasi: nima uchun ishlatilishi
  • Rol, maqom: child, friend, guest
  • Jasur works as a driver. (u haydovchi)

like + ot — “…ga o‘xshab”

  • Boshqa narsaga o‘xshash, lekin aslida u emas
  • Xulq-atvor, usul, tashqi ko‘rinish
  • Jasur drives like a racing driver. (u poygachi emas, lekin xuddi shunday haydaydi)
Bir xil ot, ikki xil ma’no
as + ot (haqiqiy rol)like + ot (o‘xshashlik)
My cousin works as a guide in Bukhara. (u gid)Dilnoza speaks English like a native speaker. (u ingliz emas, lekin shunday gapiradi)
We use the spare room as a study. (bo‘sh xona ish xonasi vazifasini bajaradi)Our balcony is like a small garden. (bog‘ga o‘xshaydi, lekin bog‘ emas)
As a child, Nodira was very shy. (bola paytida)He treats me like a child. (men bola emasman)
I’m telling you this as a friend. (men sizning do‘stingizman)Jasur gave me advice like an older brother. (aka emas, aka kabi)
  • This old caravanserai is now used as a hotel.Bu eski karvonsaroy hozir mehmonxona sifatida ishlatiladi.
  • As your teacher, I must be honest with you.Sizning o‘qituvchingiz sifatida men sizga rost gapirishim kerak.
  • Don’t shout like that! You sound like a sergeant.Bunday baqirma! Serjantga o‘xshab gapiryapsan.
  • Like most teachers, Mr Karimov works long hours.Ko‘pchilik o‘qituvchilar kabi janob Karimov ham kuniga ko‘p soat ishlaydi.

Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato: ❌ He works like a driver (agar u haqiqiy haydovchi bo‘lsa). Bu gap “u haydovchi kabi ishlaydi” deganidir, ya’ni haydovchi emas. To‘g‘risi: ✅ He works as a driver. Aksincha, ❌ She sings as an angel noto‘g‘ri: ✅ She sings like an angel.

Qisqa maslahat: o‘zbekchada sifatida deya olsangiz — as; ga o‘xshab yoki kabi deya olsangiz — like. U gid sifatida ishlaydi → as a guide. U gid kabi gapiradi → like a guide.

The same as, similar to, like “bir xil” va “o‘xshash”

“Aynan bir xil” va “o‘xshash” ning farqini inglizcha ham alohida so‘zlar bilan beradi:

IboraMa’nosiMisol
the same asaynan bir xilYour phone is the same as mine. (bir xil model)
like / similar too‘xshash, lekin bir xil emasYour phone is like mine, but yours is newer.
different fromboshqachaYour phone is different from mine.

the same da the tushirilmaydi va undan keyin as keladi, like emas: ✅ the same as — ❌ same like, ❌ the same like. Amerika inglizchasida different than ham uchraydi, Britaniya inglizchasida different from (ba’zan different to) yaxshiroq.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

My uncle works like an engineer. (u haqiqiy muhandis)My uncle works as an engineer.Haqiqiy kasb yoki vazifa → as. Like esa “shunga o‘xshab” degani.
Dilnoza sings as an angel.Dilnoza sings like an angel.Farishtaga o‘xshashlik → like. U farishta emas.
Like usually, Aziz was late.As usual, Aziz was late.Tayyor ibora: as usual. “Usually” (ravish) emas, “usual” (sifat) ishlatiladi.
Please fill in the form like the instructions say. (imtihon yoki rasmiy xat)Please fill in the form as the instructions say.To‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) oldidan rasmiy uslubda as turadi. Like norasmiy.
Your phone is same like mine.Your phone is the same as mine.the same + as. “Same like” deyilmaydi.
Cities as Bukhara and Khiva are famous.Cities such as / like Bukhara and Khiva are famous.Misol keltirishda such as yoki like; as yolg‘iz ishlatilmaydi.
Like a teacher, I must be honest with you. (men haqiqatan o‘qituvchiman)As a teacher, I must be honest with you.O‘z rolingiz haqida gapirsangiz as a ... deysiz.
He doesn’t speak as me.He doesn’t speak like me.Olmosh (me) oldidan like keladi; as faqat ega + fe’l bilan: as I do.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Like va as o‘rtasidagi eng asosiy farq nima?

Like dan keyin ot, olmosh yoki -ing keladi va ma’nosi “o‘xshash”. As dan keyin ega + fe’l (“…gandek”) yoki a/an + ot (“sifatida”) keladi. Qisqa qilib: like — o‘xshashlik, as — usul yoki aniq rol.

Like dan keyin to‘liq gap kelishi mumkinmi?

Norasmiy nutqda ha: Nobody cooks like my mother does. Bunday gaplarni Amerika inglizchasida va og‘zaki nutqda tez-tez eshitasiz. Lekin ba’zi o‘qituvchilar va imtihon tuzuvchilari uni xato deb hisoblaydi, shuning uchun yozma ishlarda as ishlating: Cook the rice as my mother does. (Aslida bu yerda the way ham yaxshi: Nobody cooks the way my mother does.)

“As a child, ...” nimani anglatadi?

“Bola paytimda, ...” — ya’ni o‘sha davrda o‘sha rolda bo‘lganimda: As a child, I was afraid of dogs. = When I was a child, I was afraid of dogs. Bu as + ot (rol) ning bir ko‘rinishi.

Such as va like ning farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil — “masalan, … kabi”. Such as rasmiyroq va yozuvda ko‘proq uchraydi, like og‘zaki nutqda. Ikkalasi ham ro‘yxat oldidan keladi. Yolg‘iz as (cities as Bukhara) bu yerda ishlatilmaydi.

“I like” va “like” predlogi — bir narsami?

Yo‘q. I like pizza — fe’l (yoqtiraman). He eats like a horse — predlog (otdek yeydi). Savolda farq yaqqol: What does she like? (u nimani yoqtiradi?) va What is she like? (u qanday inson?). Birinchisida does bor, ikkinchisida is.

As I said, as you know — bu iboralarda as o‘rniga like qo‘ysam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. as I said, as you know, as usual, as expected, as planned iboralari doim as bilan aytiladi. Like I said og‘zaki nutqda uchrasa-da, yozma va rasmiy matnda as I said deyiladi.

Nega “He works like a driver” gapi “U haydovchi bo‘lib ishlaydi” degani emas?

Chunki like o‘xshashlikni bildiradi. Gap “u haydovchiga o‘xshab ishlaydi”, ya’ni u aslida haydovchi emas, degan fikrni beradi. Kasbini aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, He works as a driver. deng.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • like + ot / olmosh / -ing = “o‘xshash”, “… kabi”: like an angel, like me, like walking on sand.
  • as + ega + fe’l = “…gandek”: Cook the rice as the recipe says. Rasmiy uslubda like emas, as; og‘zaki nutqda like ham uchraydi.
  • Tayyor iboralar doim as bilan: as usual, as always, as expected, as planned, as I said, as you know.
  • as + a/an + ot = “…sifatida”, haqiqiy rol: She works as a nurse. like + ot = aslida u emas, o‘xshash: He behaves like a child.
  • Misol keltirish: such as yoki like; yolg‘iz as emas.
  • the same as = aynan bir xil (❌ same like); like / similar to = o‘xshash.
  • O‘zbekchada sifatida deya olsangiz — as; kabi, ga o‘xshab deya olsangiz — like.
