To ..., for ... and so that ... (purpose)

Maqsad: to ..., for ... va so that ...
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • “Nima uchun?” degan savolga javob berishning to‘rt yo‘lini: to + fe’l, for + ot / for + -ing, so that + gap, in order to / so as to.
  • Nega ❌ I went to the shop for buy bread xato, ✅ I went to the shop to buy bread esa to‘g‘ri ekanini.
  • to va for ning farqini: to buy some bread va for some bread.
  • Buyum nima uchun xizmat qilishini aytishni: This brush is for cleaning shoes.
  • so that qachon kerak bo‘lishini (ega boshqa bo‘lsa, can / could / will / would bilan) va inkor maqsadni (so as not to).

Maqsad (purpose) — biror ishni nima uchun, qanday natijaga erishish uchun qilganimizni bildiradi. Masalan, Dilnoza dorixonaga boradi — maqsadi: dori sotib olish. Ingliz tilida bu ma’no turli qurilmalar bilan beriladi va ularni tanlash — shu darsning asosiy vazifasi.

O‘zbek tilida maqsad asosan -ish uchun, -gani va -sin deb bilan beriladi: non sotib olish uchun, non sotib olgani, hech kim eshitmasin deb. Inglizchada uchun har doim for bo‘lmaydi! Fe’l oldidan — to (to buy bread), ot oldidan — for (for bread), yangi gap oldidan — so that (so that nobody can hear). “Uchun” ni for deb tarjima qilish — o‘zbek o‘quvchilarning eng ko‘p xatosi: ❌ for buy, ❌ for to buy.

To + fe’l: nima uchun? To ... to say why somebody does something

Birov biror ishni nima uchun qilganini aytmoqchi bo‘lsak, to + fe’lning asl shakli (infinitiv) ishlatiladi. Bu qurilma “Why ...?” savoliga javob beradi.

Ega+harakat+to+fe’l (asl shakl)+...
  • Dilnoza went to the pharmacy to buy some medicine.Dilnoza dori sotib olish uchun dorixonaga bordi.
  • I use this app to learn new words.Men bu ilovadan yangi so‘zlar o‘rganish uchun foydalanaman.
  • Sardor called the hotel to ask about a room.Sardor xona haqida so‘rash uchun mehmonxonaga qo‘ng‘iroq qildi.
  • We stood on the bridge to watch the sunset.Biz quyosh botishini tomosha qilish uchun ko‘prikda turdik.
  • Aziz is saving money to buy a laptop.Aziz noutbuk sotib olish uchun pul yig‘yapti.

Qoida: “nima uchun?” degan maqsad ma’nosida fe’ldan oldin to turadi va undan keyin fe’l asl shaklda keladi: to buy, to ask. to buying, to bought, for buy, for to buy — hammasi xato.

Maqsad ma’nosida for + fe’l ishlatilmaydi. ❌ I went to the shop for buy bread. — ✅ I went to the shop to buy bread. Shuningdek for -ing ham bunday holatda noto‘g‘ri: ❌ I went to the shop for buying bread.

Ot + to + fe’l: narsa nima uchun kerak?

need, have, use, take kabi fe’llardan keyingi ot ham “nima uchun?” degan ma’noda to + fe’l bilan davom etishi mumkin:

  • You need a password to log in.Tizimga kirish uchun parol kerak.
  • Nodira took a taxi to get to the airport on time.Nodira aeroportga o‘z vaqtida yetib borish uchun taksi oldi.
  • I need a pen to sign the form.Blankani imzolash uchun menga ruchka kerak.

Ot + to + fe’l: nima qilish mumkin / kerak?

to + fe’l otdan keyin kelib, shu narsa bilan nima qilish mumkin yoki kerakligini ham bildiradi. Ayniqsa something / anything / nothing, a place, work, time, money, chance kabi so‘zlardan keyin:

  • Would you like something to drink?Biror narsa ichasizmi? (ichadigan biror narsa)
  • It’s hard to find a place to park near Chorsu bazaar.Chorsu bozori yaqinida mashina qo‘yadigan joy topish qiyin.
  • I’ve got a lot of homework to do tonight.Bugun kechqurun qiladigan uy vazifam ko‘p.
  • Do you get much chance to speak English at work?Ishda ingliz tilida gaplashish imkoniyatingiz ko‘p bo‘ladimi?
  • I need something to cut this melon with.Bu qovunni kesadigan biror narsa kerak.

O‘zbekchada bu ma’no ko‘pincha -digan sifatdoshi bilan beriladi: ichadigan narsa = something to drink, qiladigan ish = work to do. Oxirgi misoldagi with ni unutmang: cut this melon with something — predlog gap oxirida qoladi.

Maqsad gapi gap boshida ham kelishi mumkin: To get a visa, you need a passport. (Viza olish uchun pasport kerak.) Bu holda maqsad iborasidan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi.

For + ot va for + -ing For + noun / for + -ing

1) For + ot

Maqsad narsa (ot) bilan aytilsa, for ishlatiladi — “nimani olish / nimaga erishish uchun”:

To + fe’l va for + ot — ma’nosi bir xil
to + fe’lfor + ot
I went to the market to buy some fruit.I went to the market for some fruit.
She went to the pharmacy to get some medicine.She went to the pharmacy for some medicine.
Bekzod phoned the hotel to ask for information.Bekzod phoned the hotel for information.
We stopped at a chaikhana to have a cup of tea.We stopped at a chaikhana for a cup of tea.
  • I’m going to the bazaar for some vegetables.Men bozorga sabzavot olgani ketyapman.
  • Madina went out for a walk.Madina sayr qilgani chiqdi.
  • What did you go to the bank for? — For advice about a loan.Bankka nima uchun bordingiz? — Kredit bo‘yicha maslahat uchun.

Bu yerda o‘zbekcha -gani va -ish uchun shakllari ham, ot bilan uchun ham ishlatiladi: bozorga sabzavot olgani bordim = sabzavot uchun bordim = for some vegetables / to buy some vegetables. Fe’lni aytsangiz — to, otni aytsangiz — for.

2) For + -ing: buyum nima uchun xizmat qiladi?

Narsaning umumiy vazifasi (u nima uchun mo‘ljallangan) haqida gapirganda for + -ing ishlatiladi. Savol: What is it for?

  • “What’s this tool for?” — “It’s for opening tins.”— Bu asbob nima uchun? — U konserva ochish uchun.
  • A thermometer is an instrument for measuring temperature.Termometr — haroratni o‘lchaydigan asbob.
  • Scissors are for cutting paper and cloth.Qaychi qog‘oz va mato kesish uchun.
  • I use my phone for reading the news.Telefonimdan yangiliklarni o‘qish uchun foydalanaman.

use bilan ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri: I use my phone for reading the news = I use my phone to read the news. Lekin odamning bitta aniq harakati uchun faqat to: I went to the library to study ✅ — I went to the library for studying ❌.

3) For + odam: kim uchun?

Diqqat: for + odam maqsad emas, balki kim uchun (oluvchi) degan ma’noni beradi. Ularni aralashtirmang.

Oluvchi va maqsad

So that + gap so that ... can / could / will / would

so that bilan maqsad to‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) shaklida aytiladi. U ayniqsa quyidagi hollarda kerak bo‘ladi:

  • maqsad gapining egasi boshqa bo‘lsa (bosh gapdagi odam emas): I spoke slowly so that she could understand.
  • gapda can / could / will / would kerak bo‘lsa: I’ll give you a map so that you won’t get lost.

Qoida: bosh gap hozirgi / kelasi zamonda bo‘lsa, so that dan keyin can / will (yoki oddiy present: so that you don’t forget). Bosh gap o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa, could / would. ❌ I left a note so that you will find it (o‘tgan zamonda) — ✅ ... so that you could find it.

To yoki so that?

To + fe’l

Maqsad egasi bosh gap egasi bilan bir xil.

  • I wrote it down to remember it.
  • Dilnoza stayed up to finish her essay.

So that + gap

Ega boshqa yoki can / could / would kerak.

  • I wrote it down so that you could read it.
  • I wrote it down so that I wouldn’t forget it. (ega bir xil, ham mumkin)

So that ning that qismi og‘zaki nutqda ko‘pincha tushirib qoldiriladi: I’ll leave the light on so you can see the stairs. Lekin so (natija) bilan adashtirmang: It was cold, so I closed the window. (Sovuq edi, shuning uchun derazani yopdim — bu natija.) I closed the window so that the room would stay warm. (Xona iliq tursin deb yopdim — bu maqsad.) “So” ning boshqa ishlatilishi — Unit 102.

Ega boshqa bo‘lganda ba’zan for + odam + to + fe’l ham ishlatiladi: Aziz left the door open for the cat to come in. Lekin so that eng oddiy va xavfsiz tanlov.

O‘zbek tilidagi -sin deb va -ishi uchun shakllari so that ga mos keladi: Hech kim eshitmasin deb pichirladim = I whispered so that nobody could hear. Bola uxlashi uchun = so that the baby could sleep. Ingliz tilida bunday gapda har doim ega + fe’l bo‘lishi kerak; faqat “uchun” ni for bilan tarjima qilsangiz, xato chiqadi.

In order to va so as to Formal alternatives and negative purpose

in order to va so as to — to bilan bir xil ma’noli, lekin ancha rasmiyroq shakllar. Ular yozma nutqda, ma’ruza va rasmiy xatlarda ko‘p uchraydi.

Maqsad shakllari: ijobiy va inkor
ShaklIjobiyInkor
toWe left early to avoid the traffic.— (inkor uchun boshqa shakl kerak)
in order toWe left early in order to avoid the traffic.We left early in order not to miss the first lesson.
so as toWe left early so as to avoid the traffic.She spoke quietly so as not to wake the baby.

  • Students must register online in order to take the exam.Imtihon topshirish uchun talabalar onlayn ro‘yxatdan o‘tishi shart.
  • Nodira walked slowly so as not to fall on the ice.Nodira muzda yiqilmaslik uchun sekin yurdi.
  • I set two alarms in order not to oversleep.Uxlab qolmaslik uchun ikkita budilnik qo‘ydim.
  • He closed the door quietly so as not to disturb anyone.U hech kimni bezovta qilmaslik uchun eshikni sekin yopdi.

Qoida: inkor maqsadni bildirish uchun in order not to / so as not to + fe’l, yoki so that ... won’t / wouldn’t ishlatiladi. Bu eng ishonchli shakllar.

in order that + gap — juda rasmiy yozuvda uchraydi (in order that all students may attend); kundalik nutqda so that ishlatiladi.

Maqsad, sabab va qaysi shaklni tanlash Purpose or reason? A quick decision guide

“Nima uchun?” savoliga ikki xil javob bo‘lishi mumkin: maqsad (nimaga erishmoqchi bo‘ldik — to ...) va sabab (nima bo‘lgani uchun — because ...).

because dan keyin to + fe’l kelmaydi: ❌ I went to the station because to meet my aunt. because dan keyin to‘liq gap (ega + fe’l) kerak: because I wanted to meet my aunt.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekcha “uchun” ni inglizchada har doim “for” deb aytsam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. “Uchun” dan oldin fe’l bo‘lsa (sotib olish uchun) — to buy; ot bo‘lsa (non uchun) — for bread; boshqa ega bilan gap bo‘lsa (bola uxlashi uchun) — so that the baby can sleep. Shuningdek kim uchun degan ma’noda (oluvchi) for ishlatiladi: a present for my mother.

“to” bilan “in order to” ning farqi nimada?

Ma’nosi bir xil, farqi faqat uslubda: in order to ancha rasmiy va yozma nutqqa xos, to esa kundalik nutqda keng ishlatiladi. Inkor gapda to yetarli emas: in order not to miss yoki so as not to miss kerak bo‘ladi.

Qachon “so that” ishlatishim shart?

Maqsad gapining egasi bosh gapning egasidan boshqa bo‘lsa (I spoke slowly so that she could understand), yoki can / could / will / would kerak bo‘lsa. Ega bir xil bo‘lsa, ko‘pincha to yetarli (I spoke slowly to be understood), lekin so that ham mumkin.

So that dan keyin qaysi zamon ishlatiladi?

Bosh gap hozirgi yoki kelasi zamonda bo‘lsa, can / will yoki oddiy present: Write it down so that you won’t / don’t forget. Bosh gap o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa, could / would: She whispered so that nobody could hear. O‘tgan zamon gapidan keyin will ishlatilmaydi.

“for + -ing” ni qachon ishlatsam bo‘ladi?

Buyumning umumiy vazifasi haqida: This tool is for cutting wood. A kettle is for boiling water. “Bu nima uchun?” degan savolga ham shunday javob beriladi. Odamning bitta aniq harakati uchun for -ing ishlatilmaydi — u yerda faqat to + fe’l.

“What for?” nima degani?

What ... for? — “nima uchun, qanday maqsadda?” What do you need the ladder for? (Narvon sizga nima uchun kerak?) — To paint the ceiling. (Shiftni bo‘yash uchun.) Bu Why do you need the ladder? bilan bir xil ma’noda, lekin ko‘proq maqsadni so‘raydi.

Maqsadni gap boshiga qo‘yish mumkinmi?

Ha: To get a good seat, you should come early. (Yaxshi joy olish uchun erta kelishingiz kerak.) Bunday gaplarda maqsad iborasidan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi. so that gapi esa odatda bosh gapdan keyin turadi.

“to” dan keyin fe’lga nega -ing yoki -s qo‘shilmaydi?

Maqsadni bildiruvchi to — infinitiv belgisi, shuning uchun fe’l asl shaklda turadi: She goes to the gym to keep fit (to keeps emas). Bu yerda to predlog emas, shuning uchun -ing kerak emas. Predlog bo‘lgan to dan keyin esa -ing keladi (look forward to meeting).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Biror kishi biror ishni nima uchun qilganini aytish: to + fe’l (I went to the shop to buy bread). for + fe’l ishlatilmaydi.
  • Maqsad ot bilan aytilsa: for + ot (I went to the shop for bread).
  • Buyumning vazifasi: for + -ing (What is it for? — It’s for cutting bread); use bilan to + fe’l ham mumkin.
  • So that + gap — ega boshqa bo‘lsa yoki can / could / will / would kerak bo‘lsa: so that you can find us / so that nobody could hear.
  • Inkor maqsad: so as not to / in order not to + fe’l yoki so that ... won’t / wouldn’t.
  • in order to va so as to — to ning rasmiyroq shakllari.
  • so — natija (It was cold, so I closed the window), so that — maqsad.
  • for + odam — “kim uchun?” (flowers for my mother), maqsad emas.
