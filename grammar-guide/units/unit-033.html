Should 1

Should — 1 (maslahat)
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • should / shouldn’t bilan maslahat berish va fikr bildirishni: You should take a break.
  • I think / I don’t think / Do you think ... should ...? tuzilmalarini.
  • should “bunday bo‘lishi kerak edi-ku” (kutilgan holat) va “bo‘lsa kerak” (bashorat) ma’nolarida.
  • should have done — “qilish kerak edi (lekin qilinmadi)” va shouldn’t have done.
  • ought to — should ning sinonimi.

You should do something = “buni qilish yaxshi bo‘ladi, to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi”. Bu maslahat yoki fikr, buyruq emas. must / have to (Unit 31) kabi kuchli emas.

O‘zbek tilida maslahat “-sangiz yaxshi bo‘ladi”, “-ganingiz ma’qul”, “-ish kerak” bilan beriladi: Dam olsangiz yaxshi bo‘ladi = You should rest. O‘tmishga afsus: “-ish kerak edi”, “-sa bo‘lardi”: Ertaroq kelishing kerak edi = You should have come earlier. Diqqat: o‘zbekcha “kerak” ham must, ham should bo‘lishi mumkin — kuchiga qarang.

should: maslahat va fikr You should do = it is a good thing / the right thing to do

Ega+should / shouldn’t+fe’l
  • You look exhausted. You should take a break.Juda charchagan ko‘rinasiz. Tanaffus qilsangiz yaxshi bo‘lardi.
  • The city should build more parks for children.Shahar bolalar uchun ko‘proq bog‘lar qurishi kerak. (Mening fikrim.)
  • “Should I call Malika and say sorry?” “Yes, I think you should.”— Malikaga qo‘ng‘iroq qilib, kechirim so‘rasammikan? — Ha, menimcha, so‘rashing kerak.
  • You shouldn’t eat so late at night. It’s bad for your sleep.Kechasi bunchalik kech ovqatlanmasligingiz kerak. Bu uyquga zarar.
  • Children shouldn’t spend so many hours on their phones.Bolalar telefonda shuncha ko‘p soat o‘tirmasligi kerak.
  • What should I wear to the interview?Suhbatga nima kiyib borsam yaxshi bo‘ladi?

I think / I don’t think / Do you think ...?

Maslahatni yumshatish uchun should ko‘pincha shu iboralar bilan keladi:

  • I think you should talk to your teacher about it.Menimcha, bu haqda o‘qituvchingiz bilan gaplashganingiz ma’qul.
  • I don’t think you should spend so much money on clothes.Menimcha, kiyimga bunchalik ko‘p pul sarflamasligingiz kerak.
  • Do you think I should accept the job in Tashkent?Sizningcha, Toshkentdagi ishni qabul qilsammikan?

Ingliz tilida “menimcha, ... qilmasligingiz kerak” deyish uchun odatda I don’t think you should ... ishlatiladi. I think you shouldn’t ... ham uchraydi, lekin kamroq va qattiqroq eshitiladi. Inkorni think ga qo‘yish — ingliz tilining odati.

should — must / have to dan kuchsizroq

  • You should tell your parents the truth. (= bu to‘g‘ri ish bo‘lardi)Ota-onangizga haqiqatni aytganingiz ma’qul (maslahatim shu).
  • You must tell the police the truth. / You have to tell the police the truth. (= boshqa yo‘l yo‘q)Politsiyaga albatta haqiqatni aytishingiz shart.

should: kutilgan holat va bashorat something is not right / we expect something to happen

1) Narsa joyida emas — “aslida bunday bo‘lishi kerak edi”

Biror narsa kutilganidek yoki to‘g‘ri bo‘lmasa, should bilan “aslida qanday bo‘lishi kerakligini” aytamiz:

  • Where’s Aziz? He should be here by now. The lesson started at nine.Aziz qani? Hozirgacha bu yerda bo‘lishi kerak edi. Dars soat to‘qqizda boshlandi. (U yo‘q — bu g‘alati.)
  • We only had two cups of tea. The bill should be 20,000 som, not 50,000!Biz faqat ikki piyola choy ichdik. Hisob 50 000 emas, 20 000 so‘m bo‘lishi kerak!
  • Those students shouldn’t be in the corridor. They should be in class.Anavi talabalar yo‘lakda bo‘lmasligi kerak. Ular darsda bo‘lishi kerak.

2) Bashorat — “... bo‘lsa kerak, kutyapman”

should bilan biror narsa bo‘lishini kutishimizni ham aytamiz (bu kelajak haqida):

  • Kamola has prepared very well, so she should do well in the interview.Kamola juda yaxshi tayyorlandi, shuning uchun suhbatdan yaxshi o‘tsa kerak.
  • The roads are quiet at this time. It shouldn’t take more than twenty minutes to get there.Bu paytda yo‘llar bo‘sh. U yerga yetib borish yigirma daqiqadan ko‘p vaqt olmasa kerak.
  • The weather should be fine tomorrow.Ertaga havo yaxshi bo‘lsa kerak.

Kutilmagan holat

Hozir narsa “to‘g‘ri emas”.

  • The train should be here by now. (lekin u yo‘q)

Bashorat

Men shuni kutyapman.

  • The train should arrive in five minutes. (kelishini kutyapman)

should have done va shouldn’t have done should have (done) = it would have been right, but you didn’t do it

You should have done something = siz buni qilmadingiz, lekin qilish to‘g‘ri bo‘lardi (afsus, tanbeh). You shouldn’t have done something = siz buni qildingiz, lekin qilmaslik kerak edi.

Ega+should / shouldn’t+have+V3
  • The wedding was wonderful. You should have come! Everybody asked about you.To‘y ajoyib bo‘ldi. Kelishing kerak edi! Hamma seni so‘radi.
  • The restaurant is full. We should have booked a table.Restoran to‘la. Joy band qilishimiz kerak edi.
  • Jasur failed the test. He should have studied harder.Jasur testdan yiqildi. Qattiqroq o‘qishi kerak edi.
  • My head hurts. I shouldn’t have stayed up so late.Boshim og‘riyapti. Shuncha kech yotmasligim kerak edi.
  • You shouldn’t have told Bekzod about the party. It was supposed to be a surprise!Bekzodga ziyofat haqida aytmasligingiz kerak edi. Axir bu syurpriz bo‘lishi kerak edi-ku!

B bo‘limdagi “kutilgan holat” ma’nosi o‘tmishda ham bor:

  • The parcel should have arrived on Monday. It’s Thursday now and it still isn’t here!Posilka dushanba kuni kelishi kerak edi. Bugun payshanba, u hali ham yo‘q!

Davomli shakl shouldn’t have been + -ing = o‘sha paytda biror ishni qilayotgan edi, lekin bu noto‘g‘ri edi:

  • The driver shouldn’t have been using his phone. That’s why he crashed.Haydovchi (o‘sha paytda) telefondan foydalanmasligi kerak edi. Avariyaga shuning uchun uchradi.
should do yoki should have done?
should + fe’l (hozir / kelajak)should have + V3 (o‘tmish)
Maslahat / afsusYou’re hungry. You should eat something.
Qorningiz och. Biror narsa yeb olsangiz yaxshi bo‘ladi.		You skipped breakfast. You should have eaten something before you left.
Nonushta qilmadingiz. Chiqishdan oldin biror narsa yeyishingiz kerak edi.
InkorYou shouldn’t drive so fast.
Bunchalik tez haydamasligingiz kerak.		You shouldn’t have driven so fast.
Bunchalik tez haydamasligingiz kerak edi.

O‘zbekchada “kerak edi” (“edi” — o‘tmish!) → should have + V3. Aytishing kerak edi = You should have told me. Aytmasliging kerak edi = You shouldn’t have told him.

ought to = should ought to do / ought not to do / ought to have done

Shu darsdagi barcha gaplarda should o‘rniga ought to ishlatsa bo‘ladi. Ma’no bir xil. Faqat to ni unutmang: ought to + fe’l.

shouldought to
You should call your grandmother more often.You ought to call your grandmother more often.
We shouldn’t waste so much water.We ought not to waste so much water.
You should have told me earlier.You ought to have told me earlier.
The bus should be here soon.The bus ought to be here soon.
  • You ought to call your grandmother more often.Buvingizga tez-tez qo‘ng‘iroq qilib turishingiz kerak.
  • We ought not to waste so much water.Shuncha ko‘p suvni isrof qilmasligimiz kerak.
  • You ought to have asked me before you took my car.Mashinamni olishdan oldin mendan so‘rashingiz kerak edi.

ought to talaffuzi: /ˈɔːt tə/ (og‘zaki nutqda ko‘pincha /ˈɔːtə/). So‘roq gapda Ought I to ...? juda rasmiy va kam ishlatiladi — odatda Should I ...? deyiladi. Inkorda ought not to yoki og‘zaki oughtn’t to (BrE).

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

You should to see a doctor.You should see a doctor.should dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
You ought see a doctor.You ought to see a doctor.ought dan keyin esa “to” majburiy!
He shoulds work harder.He should work harder.Modal fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi.
You should came to the party yesterday.You should have come to the party yesterday.O‘tmish haqida afsus: should + have + V3.
I shouldn’t have ate so many sweets.I shouldn’t have eaten so many sweets.have dan keyin V3: eaten (ate — V2).
Do you think I should to apply?Do you think I should apply?“Do you think” dan keyin ham should + fe’l, “to”siz.
Should have I called her?Should I have called her?So‘roqda faqat should egadan oldinga chiqadi: Should + ega + have + V3?
What I should do?What should I do?So‘roq gapda should egadan oldin keladi: so‘roq so‘zi + should + ega + fe’l.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

should va must — qachon qaysi birini ishlataman?

should — maslahat: “men shunday qilishni tavsiya qilaman, lekin qaror sizda”. must / have to — majburiyat: “boshqa yo‘l yo‘q”. You should wear a scarf — it’s cold (maslahat). You must wear a helmet on a motorbike (qoida, majburiy).

“I don’t think you should” va “I think you shouldn’t” — farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil, lekin ingliz tilida inkorni think fe’liga qo‘yish odatiyroq va yumshoqroq: I don’t think you should go. I think you shouldn’t go ham grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri, faqat kamroq ishlatiladi va qat’iyroq eshitiladi.

“should have done” tanbehmi yoki afsusmi?

Ikkalasi ham bo‘lishi mumkin. O‘zingiz haqida — afsus: I should have studied harder (Ko‘proq o‘qishim kerak edi). Boshqalar haqida — tanbeh yoki tanqid: You should have told me! (Menga aytishing kerak edi!). Ba’zan shunchaki kutilgan narsa bo‘lmagani: The parcel should have arrived on Monday.

should kelajak haqida ham gapiradimi?

Ha. 1) Maslahat kelajak uchun: You should visit Bukhara next summer. 2) Bashorat: Kamola should do well in the interview (yaxshi o‘tsa kerak — men shuni kutaman). Bu ikkinchi ma’no will probably ga yaqin.

ought to va should — qaysi biri ko‘proq ishlatiladi?

should ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi, ayniqsa so‘roq va inkorda. ought to ham to‘g‘ri, lekin og‘zaki nutqda kamroq, ayniqsa Amerikada. Imtihonda ikkalasini ham bilishingiz kerak — ma’nosi bir xil.

“Should I open the window?” va “Shall I open the window?” — farqi?

Shall I ...? (BrE) — ko‘pincha taklif: “Derazani ochib qo‘yaymi?” Should I ...? — ko‘proq maslahat so‘rash: “Derazani ochsam to‘g‘ri bo‘ladimi?” Amerika ingliz tilida Shall I kam ishlatiladi, uning o‘rniga ko‘pincha Should I yoki Do you want me to ...? deyiladi.

“should” ning “agar” ma’nosi ham bormi?

Ha, bu Unit 34 mavzusi: If anyone should call, tell them I’m out (Agar kimdir qo‘ng‘iroq qilib qolsa ...). Shuningdek I insist that he should apologise kabi gaplar ham Unit 34 da.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Maslahat bering: should yoki shouldn’t + qavs ichidagi fe’l.

  1. Aziz has a bad cough. He (smoke) so much.
  2. You look tired. You (go) to bed early tonight.
  3. Malika wants to improve her English. She (read) more books in English.
  4. You (leave) your bag in the car. Somebody might steal it.
  5. The new museum in Samarkand is wonderful. You (visit) it.
  6. Children (eat) so many sweets.

2-mashq. I think ... should yoki I don’t think ... should bilan javob bering.

  1. Kamola is going to drive home after only two hours’ sleep. (she / drive) →
  2. Bekzod wants to buy a new phone, but his old one works perfectly. (he / buy a new one) →
  3. Nodira feels very ill. (she / see a doctor) →

3-mashq. should have yoki shouldn’t have + qavs ichidagi fe’l.

  1. I missed the bus. I (leave) home earlier.
  2. Sardor is very tired today. He (play) computer games until 3 a.m.
  3. The concert was amazing. You (come) with us.
  4. I’m cold. I (bring) a warmer jacket.
  5. You (tell) him the secret. Now everybody knows!
  6. The parcel (arrive) yesterday, but it’s still not here.

4-mashq. should / shouldn’t bilan to‘ldiring (kutilgan holat yoki bashorat).

  1. Jasur has trained hard for months, so he win the race.
  2. There’s a mistake on my certificate. My name be “Dilnoza”, not “Dilfuza”.
  3. The instructions are very clear. It be difficult to assemble the shelf.

5-mashq. ought to bilan qayta yozing.

  1. You should drink more water. → You
  2. We shouldn’t waste food. → We
  3. He should have apologised. → He

6-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling (should ishlating).

  1. Sizningcha, men bu ishni qabul qilsammikan? →
  2. Senga oldinroq aytishim kerak edi. →
  3. Ular hozirgacha bu yerda bo‘lishi kerak edi. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • should / shouldn’t + fe’l = maslahat, fikr: You should rest.
  • I don’t think you should ... — inkor odatda think ga qo‘yiladi.
  • should must / have to dan kuchsiz: maslahat, majburiyat emas.
  • should = “aslida shunday bo‘lishi kerak” (He should be here by now) yoki “bo‘lsa kerak” (Kamola should do well).
  • should have + V3 = qilish kerak edi (qilinmadi); shouldn’t have + V3 = qilmaslik kerak edi (qilindi).
  • ought to = should: ought to do, ought not to do, ought to have done.
