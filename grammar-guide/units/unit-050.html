Questions 2 (Do you know where ...? / He asked me where ...)

So‘roq gaplar — 2 (bilvosita savollar)
Questions and auxiliary verbs · Savollar va yordamchi fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Do you know where ...?, Could you tell me ...?, I wonder ... kabi iboralardan keyin so‘roq gapning so‘z tartibi qanday o‘zgarishini.
  • do / does / did nega yo‘qolishini: Where does he live? → Do you know where he lives?
  • Ha/yo‘q savollarda if va whether ishlatilishini.
  • Birovning savolini yetkazishni: She asked me where I lived.
  • Who lives here? kabi savollar (so‘roq so‘zi ega vazifasida) nega o‘zgarmasligini.

Ba’zan savolni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri bermaymiz, balki uni Do you know ...? yoki Could you tell me ...? kabi iboraning ichiga joylaymiz. Bunda savol endi mustaqil savol emas, balki katta gapning bir bo‘lagi bo‘lib qoladi. Uni bilvosita yoki ichki savol deymiz (inglizchada indirect / embedded question). Shuning uchun uning tartibi oddiy darak gapdagidek bo‘ladi: avval ega, keyin fe’l.

O‘zbek tilida bu qiyinchilik umuman yo‘q: to‘g‘ri savolda ham, ichki savolda ham so‘z tartibi bir xil (ega + ... + fe’l oxirida). Qiyoslang: Sardor qayerda bo‘ldi? — Sardor qayerda bo‘lganini bilasizmi? Ikkala gapda Sardor boshida turibdi. Inglizchada esa to‘g‘ri savolda yordamchi fe’l egadan oldin keladi (Where has Sardor been?), ichki savolda esa ega oldin keladi (Do you know where Sardor has been?). O‘zbek o‘quvchilari buni sezmay, ko‘pincha ❌ Do you know where has Sardor been? deb xato qiladi. Ha/yo‘q savolda o‘zbekcha -mi yuklamasi o‘rnida ichki savolda if / whether ishlatiladi.

Do you know where ...? Questions inside another sentence

Axborot so‘raganda ko‘pincha muloyimroq bo‘lish uchun Do you know ...?, Could you tell me ...?, Can you tell me ...? iboralarini ishlatamiz. Taqqoslang:

  • Where has Sardor been?Sardor qayerda bo‘ldi? (oddiy, to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri savol)
  • Do you know where Sardor has been?Sardor qayerda bo‘lganini bilasizmi? (ichki savol)
Do you know / Could you tell me ...+so‘roq so‘zi+ega+(yordamchi fe’l)+fe’l ...

Qoida: Do you know ...? kabi iboradan keyin kelgan so‘roq so‘zidan so‘ng darak gap tartibi ishlatiladi: ega → fe’l. Ichki savolda do / does / did ishlatilmaydi; ularning vazifasini fe’lning o‘zi bajaradi (lives, left).

  • Could you tell me where the nearest pharmacy is?Eng yaqin dorixona qayerdaligini ayta olasizmi?

Tinish belgisi va zamon

Savol belgisi (?) qachon?Misol
Butun gap savol bo‘lsa (Do you know ...? Could you tell me ...?)Do you know where he lives?
Butun gap darak gap bo‘lsa (I wonder ..., I don’t know ..., Tell me ...)I don’t know where he lives.

Bosh gap hozirgi zamonda bo‘lsa (Do you know ...? Can you tell me ...? I wonder ...), ichki savolning zamoni haqiqiy vaqtga mos bo‘ladi, orqaga surilmaydi: Where does he live? → Do you know where he lives? (lived emas). Zamonni orqaga surish faqat o‘zlashtirma gapda kerak bo‘ladi (C bo‘lim).

Ichki savol muloyimroq eshitiladi. Where’s the toilet? biroz keskin, Could you tell me where the toilet is? esa notanish odamga murojaat uchun juda mos. Muloyim so‘rovlar haqida — Unit 37.

If va whether: ha/yo‘q savollar Do you know if / whether ...?

So‘roq so‘zi bo‘lmagan savollar (Is Aziz at home?, Did Kamola enjoy the concert?) ichki savolga aylanganda if yoki whether qo‘yiladi. So‘z tartibi yana oddiy darak gapdagidek.

Ha/yo‘q savollar: oddiy → ichki
Oddiy savolIchki savol
Is Aziz at home?Do you know if Aziz is at home?
Did Kamola enjoy the concert?Do you know if / whether Kamola enjoyed the concert?
Can I park here?I wonder whether I can park here.
Has the post arrived?Could you tell me if the post has arrived?
Does this bus go to Chorsu?Can you tell me if this bus goes to Chorsu?
Do you know+if / whether+ega+(yordamchi fe’l)+fe’l ...

O‘zbekcha -mi yuklamasi ichki savolda ham saqlanadi: U uydami? — U uydami-yo‘qmi, bilmayman. Inglizchada bu vazifani if / whether bajaradi. Ularni tushirib qoldirmang: Do you know he is at home? degani “U uyda ekanini bilasizmi?” (xabar), ya’ni boshqa ma’no; Do you know if he is at home? esa “U uydami, bilasizmi?” (savol).

if va whether farqi

Ko‘p hollarda ikkalasi o‘rnida ham bo‘ladi; whether biroz rasmiyroq. Lekin ba’zi o‘rinlarda faqat whether to‘g‘ri:

Qachon faqat whether?✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘ri
to + fe’l dan oldinI don’t know whether to go or stay.I don’t know if to go or stay.
Predlogdan keyinWe talked about whether the museum should close.We talked about if the museum should close.
whether or not birikmasiI wonder whether or not he’ll come.I wonder if or not he’ll come.
Gap ega vazifasidaWhether he comes doesn’t matter.If he comes doesn’t matter.

“or not” gap oxirida kelsa, if ham mumkin: I don’t know if he’s coming or not. ✅

Diqqat: savol ichidagi if shart gapdagi if emas! Shart gapda If he comes, tell me (will yo‘q — Unit 38), lekin ichki savolda will bemalol ishlatiladi, chunki bu savol, shart emas: I don’t know if he will come. ✅ Do you know if it will rain tomorrow? ✅

O‘zlashtirma savollar: He asked me where ... Reported questions

Birovning savolini boshqa odamga yetkazganda ham xuddi shu tartib ishlatiladi: ega + fe’l, do/did yo‘q. Bundan tashqari, o‘zlashtirma gapdagi odatiy o‘zgarishlar (Unit 47 va Unit 48) amal qiladi: zamon bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi, olmoshlar o‘zgaradi.

  • Direct: “Where do you live?” → Malika asked me where I lived.— Qayerda yashaysiz? → Malika mendan qayerda yashashimni so‘radi.
  • Direct: “Are you married?” → She asked me if I was married.— Uylanganmisiz? → U mendan uylangan-uylanmaganimni so‘radi.
Ko‘chirma savol → o‘zlashtirma savol
Ko‘chirma savolO‘zlashtirma savol
“What are you doing?”He asked me what I was doing.
“How old is your brother?”She asked how old my brother was.
“Where do you work?”He asked me where I worked.
“Why did you leave?”She asked me why I had left (/ left).
“Have you seen my phone?”She asked if / whether I had seen her phone.
“Can you swim?”Bekzod asked if I could swim.
“Will you help me?”Dilnoza asked if I would help her.
“Do you like plov?”He asked whether I liked plov.

Qoida: O‘zlashtirma savolda (1) savol belgisi qo‘yilmaydi, (2) do / does / did yo‘q, (3) so‘z tartibi ega → fe’l, (4) ha/yo‘q savolda if / whether, (5) zamon odatda bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi, (6) olmoshlar mantiqqa qarab o‘zgaradi (you → I, my → her).

  • The taxi driver asked Jasur where he wanted to go.Taksi haydovchisi Jasurdan qayerga bormoqchi ekanini so‘radi.
  • I asked her how she had got there.Men undan u yerga qanday borganini so‘radim.
  • Nodira wanted to know whether the shop was open on Sundays.Nodira yakshanba kunlari do‘kon ochiq bo‘ladimi-yo‘qmi, shuni bilmoqchi edi.
  • He wondered what time the train would arrive.U poyezd soat nechada kelar ekan, deb o‘ylardi.
Savolni yetkazadigan fe’llar:
ask (somebody)wonderwant to knowwanted to knowenquire / inquire (rasmiy)

ask dan keyin odam to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri keladi: She asked me ... ✅ — She asked to me ... ❌. Odamni aytmasangiz ham bo‘ladi: She asked where I lived. ✅ tell fe’li esa savolni emas, xabarni yetkazadi (She told me she was tired).

Agar gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, zamonni surmasa ham bo‘ladi (Unit 48): She asked me where I live. ham to‘g‘ri, agar siz hali ham o‘sha yerda yashayotgan bo‘lsangiz. Imtihonda ikkilansangiz, o‘tgan zamonni tanlang.

O‘zbek tilida o‘zlashtirma savol -ishini / -ganini so‘radi bilan beriladi: Mendan qayerda yashashimni so‘radi. Inglizchada bu asked me + alohida ichki savol: asked me where I lived. Bunda “that” yoki “to” ishlatilmaydi: ❌ She asked me that where I lived. ❌ She asked me to where I lived.

Who / what ega bo‘lsa — hech narsa o‘zgarmaydi Subject questions stay the same

Agar so‘roq so‘zi gapning egasi bo‘lsa (Who called? What happened? Which bus goes ...?), oddiy savolda ham do / does / did yo‘q va tartib allaqachon “ega + fe’l”. Shuning uchun ichki savolga o‘tganda faqat oldingi ibora qo‘shiladi, qolgan qismi o‘zgarmaydi:

Oddiy savolIchki savol
Who lives next door?Do you know who lives next door?
What happened?I don’t know what happened.
Which bus goes to Chorsu?Can you tell me which bus goes to Chorsu?
Who called you?Do you remember who called you?

Ikki xil savolni solishtiring. Ikkalasida ham who bor, lekin ega har xil:

Who = ega (kim?)

  • Who met Dilnoza? (Dilnozani kim uchratdi?)
  • Do you know who met Dilnoza?

Who = to‘ldiruvchi (kimni?)

  • Who did Dilnoza meet? (Dilnoza kimni uchratdi?)
  • Do you know who Dilnoza met?

Tekshirish usuli: so‘roq so‘zidan keyin darhol fe’l kelsa (who lives, what happened), u ega. So‘roq so‘zidan keyin boshqa ega kelsa (who Dilnoza met), u to‘ldiruvchi.

Do you think ...? va wh- + to ... Two special patterns

Do you think / believe / suppose ...?

think, believe, suppose, imagine, guess, expect fe’llari bilan savolni ha/yo‘q deb emas, fikr so‘rash uchun beramiz. Bunday savolda so‘roq so‘zi gapning eng boshiga chiqadi:

Do you know ...? (javob: ha/yo‘q)Do you think ...? (javob: fikr)
Do you know where he lives?Where do you think he lives?
Do you know how old she is?How old do you think she is?
Do you know what this word means?What do you think this word means?
Do you know who will win?Who do you think will win?

❌ Do you think where he lives? — noto‘g‘ri. Do you think dan keyin ichki savol emas, so‘roq so‘zi boshda: Where do you think he lives? ✅

So‘roq so‘zi + to + fe’l

Ba’zan ichki savol o‘rnida so‘roq so‘zi + to + fe’l ishlatiladi: I don’t know what to do. (= what I should do). Can you show me how to use this app? Bu tuzilma Unit 54 da batafsil o‘rganiladi. why bilan to ishlatilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega so‘z tartibi o‘zgaradi? “Where is the bank?” bilan “where the bank is” orasida ma’no farqi bormi?

Ma’no bir xil, farq faqat grammatikada. Where is the bank? mustaqil savol, shuning uchun yordamchi fe’l egadan oldin turadi. Where the bank is esa katta gapning bir bo‘lagi (ergash gap): inglizchada ergash gapning tartibi har doim darak gapniki — ega, keyin fe’l.

“Do you know where is the bank?” deb tez-tez eshitaman. Bu to‘g‘rimi?

Bu standart ingliz tilida xato hisoblanadi. Norasmiy nutqda ba’zi odamlar shunday deyishi mumkin, lekin imtihonda va yozma nutqda Do you know where the bank is? deyish kerak.

Savol belgisini qachon qo‘yaman?

Butun gap savol bo‘lsa. Do you know where he lives? (savol) — belgi bor. I don’t know where he lives. (darak gap) — nuqta. O‘zlashtirma savol (She asked where I lived.) ham nuqta bilan tugaydi.

If bilan whether ning farqi nima? Qaysi birini tanlasam xavfsiz?

Ko‘pchilik hollarda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri; whether biroz rasmiyroq. Xavfsiz yo‘l: whether har doim to‘g‘ri. Lekin to + fe’l oldidan, predlogdan keyin va whether or not birikmasida faqat whether ishlatiladi.

“Who lives here?” ni ichki savolga aylantirsam, nega hech narsa o‘zgarmaydi?

Chunki bunda so‘roq so‘zi (who) o‘zi ega, ya’ni u allaqachon fe’ldan oldin turibdi va do/does ishlatilmagan. Faqat boshiga Do you know qo‘shasiz: Do you know who lives here?

O‘zlashtirma savolda zamonni har doim orqaga surish kerakmi?

Odatda ha: “Where do you live?” → She asked me where I lived. Lekin gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa (masalan, siz hali ham o‘sha yerda yashasangiz), hozirgi zamonni qoldirsa ham bo‘ladi. Ichki savolda (Do you know where he lives?) esa zamon umuman surilmaydi, chunki bu o‘zlashtirma gap emas.

“Do you know...” va “Do you think...” ni qanday ajrataman?

Javobingiz ha/yo‘q bo‘ladimi yoki fikrmi, shunga qarang. Do you know where he lives? — javob: “Yes, in Samarkand” yoki “No”. Where do you think he lives? — javob: “In Samarkand, I think.” Ikkinchi holatda so‘roq so‘zi boshda turadi.

Ichki savoldan keyin “please” qo‘shsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha: Could you tell me where the station is, please? Bu juda muloyim shakl. Muloyim so‘rovlar haqida batafsil — Unit 37.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Do you know ...? / Could you tell me ...? / I wonder ... dan keyin tartib darak gapdagidek: so‘roq so‘zi + ega + fe’l.
  • Ichki savolda do / does / did yo‘q: Where does he live? → Do you know where he lives?
  • Ha/yo‘q savolda if / whether qo‘yiladi. to + fe’l oldida va predlogdan keyin faqat whether.
  • O‘zlashtirma savolda ham xuddi shunday: She asked me where I lived. (savol belgisi yo‘q, zamon orqaga suriladi)
  • So‘roq so‘zi ega bo‘lsa (Who lives here?), hech narsa o‘zgarmaydi: Do you know who lives here?
  • think / believe / suppose bilan so‘roq so‘zi boshga chiqadi: Where do you think he lives?
  • Savol belgisi faqat butun gap savol bo‘lganda: Do you know where he is? / I don’t know where he is.
