Would

Would
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • would + fe’l — tasavvur qilingan (xayoliy) hozirgi yoki kelasi vaziyat: It would be nice to travel around the world.
  • would have + 3-shakl — o‘tmishdagi tasavvur: “shunday bo‘lganida, ... bo‘lardi”.
  • would — will ning o‘tgan zamon shakli: He said he would come.
  • would bilan o‘tmishdagi takrorlangan ishlar (we would play) va wouldn’t bilan rad etish (the door wouldn’t open).
  • ’d qisqartmasi ba’zan would, ba’zan had ekanini qanday ajratishni.

Eng muhim g‘oya: will — haqiqiy, ishonchli kelasi zamon; would esa ko‘pincha “tasavvur qilaman, lekin bu hozircha haqiqat emas” degan ma’noni beradi. would — modal fe’l: u bilan to kelmaydi, fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi, so‘roq va inkorda do kerak emas.

O‘zbekcha -ardi / -ar edi / -gan bo‘lardi shakli inglizchada bir necha xil qolipga to‘g‘ri keladi. Dunyo bo‘ylab sayohat qilsam, yaxshi bo‘lardi → would be. Bolaligimizda har kuni futbol o‘ynardik → would play / used to play. Eng xavfli joyi: o‘zbek tilida kelasi zamon gap o‘zlashtirilganda ham o‘zgarmaydi: U keladi, dedi. Inglizchada esa o‘tgan zamon fe’lidan (said, thought, promised) keyin odatda will o‘rniga would keladi: He said he would come (o‘sha paytdan qaralganda kelasi zamon — would).

Would + fe’l — tasavvur qilingan vaziyat Would (do): imagined situations now or in the future

Bekzod dunyo bo‘ylab sayohat qilishni orzu qiladi. U hozir sayohatda emas — shunchaki tasavvur qilyapti va shunday deydi:

Bunday gaplarda ko‘pincha if qismi aytilmaydi, chunki shart gapdan o‘zi tushunilib turadi (agar shu orzu amalga oshsa ...):

  • Do you think Jasur would be a good teacher?Sizningcha, Jasur yaxshi o‘qituvchi bo‘larmidi?
  • There’s no point applying for that job. I wouldn’t get it anyway.Bu ishga hujjat topshirmayman. Baribir olmasdim (ololmasdim).
  • Would you like to be taller? Where would you go for a perfect holiday?Bo‘yingiz uzunroq bo‘lishini xohlarmidingiz? Ideal ta’til uchun qayerga borardingiz?

I wouldn’t ... ko‘pincha yumshoq maslahat: I wouldn’t phone him now — he’ll be asleep. (Men bo‘lganimda, hozir unga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmasdim — u uxlayotgan bo‘ladi.)

Will va would: haqiqat va tasavvur

will — haqiqiy kelajak

I’ll buy a car next year. (Rejam shunday, pulim bor.)

Kelasi yil mashina olaman.

would — tasavvur

I’d buy a car if I had the money. (Pulim yo‘q.)

Puli bo‘lsa, mashina olardim.

if bilan bunday gaplar Unit 38 va Unit 39 da batafsil o‘rganiladi.

’d — would yoki had?

Qisqa ’d ikki xil so‘zning qisqartmasi: would yoki had (past perfect, Unit 15). Qanday ajratamiz? ’d dan keyingi so‘zga qarang:

’d dan keyin’d = ?Misol
fe’lning asl shakli (go, be, like)wouldI’d go there. / She’d like it.
fe’lning 3-shakli (gone, been, finished)hadI’d gone already. / She’d finished before we came.
betterhad (had better, Unit 35)You’d better hurry.
ratherwould (would rather, Unit 59)I’d rather stay at home.

Would have + 3-shakl — o‘tmishdagi tasavvur Would have (done): imagining a past situation

O‘tmishda bo‘lmagan narsani tasavvur qilsak — “shunday bo‘lganida, ... bo‘lardi” — would have + V3 ishlatamiz:

Ega+would have+V3 (fe’lning 3-shakli)
  • I didn’t go to the party, but I’m sure I would have enjoyed it.Ziyofatga bormadim, lekin borganimda, menga albatta yoqqan bo‘lardi.
  • We left home early. Otherwise we would have missed the train.Uydan erta chiqdik. Bo‘lmasa, poyezdga kech qolardik.
  • Without the map we would have got lost. (AmE: gotten lost)Xaritasiz adashib qolardik.
  • Sardor wouldn’t have been late if the bus had come on time.Avtobus o‘z vaqtida kelganida, Sardor kechikmagan bo‘lardi.

Qoida: would + asl fe’l — hozir/kelajak tasavvuri; would have + V3 — o‘tmish tasavvuri. would dan keyin -ed / V2 kelmaydi: would went ❌, would have went ❌ — would have gone ✅.

Og‘zaki nutqda would have qisqa aytiladi: would’ve /ˈwʊdəv/. Yozuvda ❌ would of demang — would have yoki would’ve. Inkor: wouldn’t have (wouldn’t’ve faqat so‘zlashuvda aytiladi).

Would have, could have, should have, might have

QolipMa’nosiMisol
would have + V3shunday bo‘lganida, bo‘lardi (xayoliy natija)I would have helped you if you had asked.
could have + V3bu mumkin edi (Unit 27)I could have helped you. I was free.
should have + V3bu kerak edi (Unit 33)I should have helped you. I’m sorry.
might have + V3balki bo‘lgandir (Unit 29–30)She might have seen your keys. Ask her.

Would — will ning o‘tgan zamon shakli Would as the past of will

Biror kishi o‘tmishda kelajak haqida gapirgan yoki o‘ylagan bo‘lsa, will o‘rniga would ishlatamiz. Bunda “kelajak” — o‘sha paytga nisbatan kelajak:

Will → would
Asl so‘z yoki fikr (hozirgi zamon)Keyin aytilgan gap (o‘tgan zamon)
“I’ll send you the photos on Friday.”Dilnoza said she would send me the photos on Friday.
“It won’t rain.”I thought it wouldn’t rain.
“I’ll be late.”Aziz said he would be late.
“We’ll help you.”They promised they would help me.
  • I knew you would like this place.Bu joy sizga yoqishini bilgandim.
  • Nodira was sure she wouldn’t pass the exam, but she did.Nodira imtihondan o‘ta olmayman, deb amin edi, lekin o‘tdi.
  • I’d never imagined I would live in another country.Boshqa mamlakatda yashayman, deb hech tasavvur qilmagandim.

O‘zbek tilida “U ertaga keladi, dedi” deymiz — fe’l o‘zgarmaydi. Inglizchada said / thought / promised / knew kabi o‘tgan zamon fe’lidan keyin odatda will → would: He said he would come tomorrow. Bu qoida o‘zlashtirma gapda ham ishlaydi (Unit 47).

Hikoyalarda would o‘tmishdagi voqeadan keyin nima bo‘lganini aytish uchun ham ishlatiladi: In 2010 Bekzod moved to Tashkent. He would later become a famous actor. (U keyinchalik mashhur aktyor bo‘ldi.)

Would — o‘tmishdagi takroriy ish; wouldn’t — rad etish Would = used to; wouldn’t = refused

1. O‘tmishdagi odat: would = used to

O‘tmishda ko‘p marta takrorlangan ish-harakat haqida would ham ishlatiladi, ma’nosi used to ga yaqin (Unit 18):

  • When we lived in Fergana, we would swim in the canal every day after school.Farg‘onada yashaganimizda, har kuni maktabdan keyin kanalda cho‘milardik.
  • Every autumn my grandfather would bring us a basket of grapes from Samarkand.Har kuz bobom bizga Samarqanddan bir savat uzum olib kelardi.

would faqat takrorlangan harakat uchun; holat uchun (be, have, live, know, like ...) ishlatilmaydi. We used to have a dog ✅ — We would have a dog ❌ (bu holat). Shuningdek, bu ma’nodagi would dan oldin o‘tmish vaqti aytilgan bo‘lishi kerak (when I was young, every summer, in those days ...) — aks holda we would swim tasavvur (“cho‘milardik, agar ...”) deb tushuniladi.

2. Wouldn’t — rad etdi, ko‘nmadi

wouldn’t o‘tmishda kimdir yoki biror narsa biror ishni qilishni rad etganini bildiradi (won’t ning o‘tgan zamoni):

Hozir (won’t)O‘tmish (wouldn’t)
The door won’t open.
Eshik ochilmayapti.		The door wouldn’t open.
Eshik ochilmadi.
He won’t listen to me.
U gapimga quloq solmayapti.		I told him to stay, but he wouldn’t listen.
Qolishini aytdim, lekin u quloq solmadi.
  • I asked Sardor to help me, but he wouldn’t.Sardordan yordam so‘radim, lekin u ko‘nmadi.
  • The printer wouldn’t work, so I couldn’t print my essay.Printer ishlamadi, shuning uchun inshoimni chop eta olmadim.
  • Kamola wouldn’t tell me how old she was.Kamola necha yoshda ekanini aytishni xohlamadi.

Bu ma’no jonsiz narsalar uchun ham ishlaydi (eshik, mashina, telefon): “qanchalik urinmasam ham, ishlamadi” degan ohang bor.

Juda rasmiy britaniya uslubida I / we bilan would o‘rniga should ham uchraydi (I should be glad if ...). Siz uni ishlatishingiz shart emas — I would be glad to‘liq to‘g‘ri.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I thought it will be cold, but it was warm.I thought it would be cold, but it was warm.O‘tmishdagi fikr (thought) dan keyin will → would. O‘zbek tilidagi “sovuq bo‘ladi, deb o‘yladim” ta’siri.
I would went there if I had time.I would go there if I had time.would dan keyin fe’lning asl shakli.
I would of called you, but I had no credit.I would have called you, but I had no credit. / I would’ve called ...Yozuvda of emas, have.
If I would have more time, I would learn Chinese.If I had more time, I would learn Chinese.if qismida odatda would ishlatilmaydi (Unit 38, 39).
We would live in Bukhara when I was a child.We used to live in Bukhara when I was a child.Holat (live) uchun would emas, used to.
Would you to like some tea?Would you like some tea?would modal fe’l: to kelmaydi (would like bitta ibora).
The window didn’t would open.The window wouldn’t open.would o‘zi yordamchi fe’l: do / did kerak emas.
I would have gone yesterday if I am free.I would have gone yesterday if I had been free.Would have bilan if qismida past perfect (had been) bo‘ladi (Unit 40).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’d” ni would deb o‘qiymanmi yoki had deb? Qanday bilaman?

’d dan keyingi so‘zga qarang. Fe’lning asl shakli kelsa (I’d go, she’d like) — would. Fe’lning 3-shakli kelsa (I’d gone, she’d finished) — had. Ikki maxsus holat: I’d better — had better; I’d rather — would rather.

“I will buy” bilan “I would buy” o‘rtasida qanday farq bor?

will — haqiqiy reja yoki qaror: I’ll buy it (sotib olaman). would — shartli, xayoliy: I’d buy it if it were cheaper (arzonroq bo‘lsa, olardim — lekin arzon emas). Gapingizda “agar ... bo‘lsa” degan fikr bor-yo‘qligiga qarang.

Nega “He said he would come”, “will” emas?

Chunki “said” o‘tgan zamonda, va uning ichidagi “kelajak” o‘sha o‘tmishdagi paytga nisbatan olinadi. Ingliz tilida asosiy fe’l o‘tgan zamonga o‘tganda, will ham would ga o‘tadi (Unit 47). Istisno: gapirilgan voqea hali ham kelajakda va haqiqat bo‘lsa, ba’zan He said he will come deyiladi — lekin o‘quvchilar uchun would eng xavfsiz yo‘l.

Odatni aytishda would bilan used to farqi nima?

Takrorlangan harakat uchun ikkalasi ham mumkin, lekin would uchun o‘tmish vaqti gapda yoki oldingi gapda aytilgan bo‘lishi kerak. Holat (be, have, live, know ...) uchun faqat used to. Shubhalansangiz, used to ni ishlating (Unit 18).

“Would you help me” va “I wish you would help me” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. Would you help me? — muloyim iltimos (Unit 37). I wish you would help me — “qani endi yordam bersangiz”, norozilik/istak ohangi (Unit 41).

if qismida would ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Odatda yo‘q: If I had time, I would help. Lekin muloyim iltimosda ishlatiladi: I’d be grateful if you would send me the documents. Bu yerda would shartni emas, muloyim iltimosni bildiradi (“yuborsangiz, minnatdor bo‘lardim”) — rasmiy xatlarda ko‘p uchraydi.

“Wouldn’t” har doim rad etish ma’nosini beradimi?

Yo‘q. wouldn’t to‘rt joyda uchraydi: (1) tasavvur: I wouldn’t go there; (2) o‘tmish kelajagi: He said he wouldn’t come; (3) rad etish: The door wouldn’t open; (4) o‘tmishdagi odatning inkori. Ma’no gapning mazmunidan chiqadi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. would / wouldn’t + qavsdagi fe’l bilan to‘ldiring (tasavvur qilingan vaziyat).

  1. It (be) nice to have a garden.
  2. There’s no point in entering the competition. I (not / win) it.
  3. you (like) to live in another country?
  4. I (not / go) there alone at night. It isn’t safe.
  5. Where you (go) for a perfect holiday?

2-mashq. would have + V3 yoki wouldn’t have + V3 bilan to‘ldiring.

  1. I didn’t know about the party. I (come) if I’d known.
  2. We left early. Otherwise we (miss) the train.
  3. Nodira missed the concert because she was ill. It was great — she (enjoy) it.
  4. Without your map, we (get) lost.
  5. They (not / be) late if the bus had come on time.

3-mashq. Gapni o‘tgan zamonga o‘tkazing: would yoki wouldn’t yozing.

  1. “I’ll bring your book back tomorrow,” said Dilnoza. → Dilnoza said she bring my book back the next day.
  2. “It won’t rain,” I thought. → I thought it rain.
  3. “I’ll be late,” Aziz said. → Aziz said he be late.
  4. “We’ll help you,” they promised. → They promised they help me.
  5. “I won’t tell anyone,” she said. → She said she tell anyone.

4-mashq. Would, used to yoki ikkalasi ham to‘g‘rimi? Ikkalasi to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ikkalasini yozing.

  1. We (would / used to) have a dog when I was a child.
  2. When we were kids, we (would / used to) walk to school together every morning.
  3. Jasur (would / used to) be very shy when he was young.
  4. My grandfather (would / used to) tell us stories every evening.
  5. There (would / used to) be a cinema on this street.
  6. In summer my mother (would / used to) pick fruit from our garden every morning.

5-mashq. ’d = would yoki had? “would” yoki “had” deb yozing.

  1. I’d like some tea, please.
  2. By the time we arrived, she’d left.
  3. If I were you, I’d wait.
  4. He said he’d seen the film before.
  5. I’d rather stay at home today.
  6. They’d finished before we came.

6-mashq. Xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri gapni yozing.

  1. I thought it will be cold, but it was warm. →
  2. If I would have time, I would help you. →
  3. I would of gone with you. →
  4. We would live in a village when I was small. →
  5. The key didn’t would turn in the lock. →

7-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Tog‘da yashash zo‘r bo‘lardi. →
  2. Aziz ertaga kelishini aytdi. →
  3. Eshik (qancha urinsak ham) ochilmadi (wouldn’t). →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • would + asl fe’l — hozir/kelajak tasavvuri: It would be nice to ... / I wouldn’t go there. Ko‘pincha yashirin “agar ...” bor.
  • would have + V3 — o‘tmish tasavvuri: I would have helped you. Yozuvda would of ❌.
  • would = will ning o‘tgan zamoni: He said he would come. (O‘zbekchadan farqli, fe’l o‘zgaradi.)
  • would = used to (faqat takrorlangan harakat, o‘tmish vaqti aytilgan bo‘lishi kerak); holat uchun faqat used to.
  • wouldn’t = o‘tmishda rad etdi: The printer wouldn’t work.
  • ’d + asl fe’l = would; ’d + V3 = had; I’d rather = would rather; I’d better = had better.
  • would modal fe’l: to yo‘q, -s yo‘q, do yo‘q.
