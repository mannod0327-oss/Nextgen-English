It is said that ... He is said to ... He is supposed to ...

It is said that ... / He is said to ... / He is supposed to ...
Passive · Majhul nisbat (Passive)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • It is said that ... va He is said to ... — “aytishlaricha ...”, “... deb hisoblanadi” ma’nosidagi majhul gaplarni.
  • Qaysi fe’llar bilan ishlatilishini: say, think, believe, know, consider, report, expect, understand ...
  • Infinitivning to‘rt shaklini: to do, to be doing, to have done, to have been doing — qaysi birini qachon tanlash.
  • He is supposed to be ... — “uni ... deyishadi”.
  • I’m supposed to ... — “... shart / ... kutilmoqda / ... mumkin emas” (reja, qoida, kutish).

Yangiliklarda, gazeta va ilmiy matnlarda ko‘pincha “Odamlar aytishadi, ...”, “... deb hisoblashadi” degan ma’noni berish kerak bo‘ladi. Gapni kim aytgani noma’lum yoki ahamiyatsiz bo‘lgani uchun majhul nisbat ishlatiladi. Bu tuzilma rasmiy va yozma uslubga xos, lekin suhbatda ham supposed to shakli juda ko‘p uchraydi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no aytishlaricha, deyishadi, ... deb hisoblanadi, ... ekani ma’lum, ... xabar qilinishicha kabi iboralar bilan beriladi: “Uning juda boy ekani aytiladi.” Inglizchada ikki yo‘l bor: It is said that he is very rich va He is said to be very rich. Supposed to o‘zbekchada “kerak”, “lozim”, “bo‘lishi kutilgan” so‘zlariga yaqin: Poyezd soat sakkizda kelishi kerak → The train is supposed to arrive at eight.

It is said that ... / He is said to ... Reporting verbs in the passive: two patterns

Faraz qiling, shahar gazetasida shunday yozilgan: “Odamlar aytishadi, janob Rahimov o‘n ikki tilni biladi.” Inglizchada aniq nisbat shunday bo‘ladi:

  • People say that Mr Rahimov knows twelve languages.Odamlar janob Rahimov o‘n ikki tilni biladi, deyishadi.

“Odamlar” noaniq va muhim emas, shuning uchun say fe’lini majhul qilamiz. Buning ikki yo‘li bor:

It+is / was+said / thought / believed ...+that+gap
Ega+is / was+said / thought / believed ...+to + infinitiv
  • It is said that Mr Rahimov knows twelve languages.Aytishlaricha, janob Rahimov o‘n ikki tilni biladi.
  • Mr Rahimov is said to know twelve languages.Janob Rahimov o‘n ikki tilni biladi, deyishadi. (ma’no bir xil)
  • The company is expected to open a new factory next year.Kompaniya kelasi yil yangi zavod ochishi kutilmoqda.
  • The film isn’t expected to win any prizes.Filmning biror mukofot olishi kutilmayapti.
  • Who is thought to have taken the money?Pulni kim olgan deb hisoblanyapti?
  • It is estimated that thousands of tourists visit the city every month.Baholanishicha, shaharga har oy minglab sayyoh keladi.

Hamma reporting verb ikkala tuzilmaga ham mos kelmaydi. decide, suggest, announce, agree, explain kabi fe’llar faqat It was decided that ... shaklida keladi. He was decided to go ❌. Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, It is said / thought / believed / known / expected / reported that ... shaklini tanlang — u har doim xavfsiz.

Ega sifatida there ham kelishi mumkin: There is said to be hidden treasure under the old caravanserai. (Eski karvonsaroy ostida yashirin xazina bor, deyishadi.)

Qaysi infinitiv? to be / to be doing / to have done Choosing the right infinitive

He is said to ... dagi to dan keyingi shakl vaqtni ko‘rsatadi. Asosiy savol: ish qachon sodir bo‘lgan — aytilgan paytdami yoki ungacha?

O‘tgan zamonda: was said, was thought ...

Fe’lning o‘zi ham o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lishi mumkin (was said, was believed). U holda taqqoslash nuqtasi gap aytilgan (o‘ylangan) paytga ko‘chadi:

  • In the 19th century the village was believed to be cursed.XIX asrda qishloq la’natlangan deb hisoblangan. (hisoblangan paytda — la’natli)
  • The castle was said to have been built by a king.Qasrni qirol qurgan, deyilgan edi. (qurilish deyilishidan oldin bo‘lgan)
  • Before the match, the captain was thought to be injured, but he played.O‘yin oldidan sardor jarohatlangan deb o‘ylashgan, lekin u o‘ynadi.

O‘zbekcha gapda ko‘pincha -gan/-ligi shakllari ishlatiladi: qurilgan ekanligi aytiladi → is said to have been built. “Oldin bo‘lgan ish” ni ko‘rsatish uchun inglizcha to have + V3 shakli kerak: He is said to be a spy in the 1990s ❌ — He is said to have been a spy in the 1990s ✅.

He is supposed to be ... — “deb hisoblanadi” supposed to = said / believed to

supposed to ba’zan is said to / is believed to ma’nosida ishlatiladi. Bunda ko‘pincha “shunday deyishadi (lekin ishonmasligim mumkin)” ohangi bor:

  • Sardor is supposed to be a very good chess player.Sardor juda yaxshi shaxmatchi deyishadi.
  • That restaurant is supposed to be excellent, but I didn’t like the food.U restoran a’lo daraja deyishadi, lekin menga taomlari yoqmadi.
  • The old house is supposed to be haunted.Eski uyda arvoh bor, deyishadi.
  • He is supposed to have been the best football player of his generation.U o‘z avlodining eng yaxshi futbolchisi bo‘lgan, deyishadi.

supposedly ravishi ham shu ma’noda: The film is supposedly based on a true story. (Film haqiqiy voqeaga asoslangan deyishadi.) Xuddi shunday reportedly (xabar qilinishicha) va allegedly (da’vo qilinishicha): The company has reportedly lost millions.

I’m supposed to ... — reja, qoida, kutish supposed to = should (expected, planned, not allowed)

Ikkinchi, juda keng tarqalgan ma’no: be supposed to = “bo‘lishi kerak”, ya’ni reja, kelishuv, qoida yoki boshqalarning kutishiga ko‘ra kerak.

  • The meeting is supposed to start at ten.Yig‘ilish soat o‘nda boshlanishi kerak. (reja)
  • I’m supposed to meet Jasur at six.Men Jasur bilan soat oltida uchrashishim kerak. (kelishganmiz)
  • You’re supposed to wear a helmet on this building site.Bu qurilish maydonchasida shlem kiyish shart. (qoida)
  • Students aren’t supposed to use their phones in class.O‘quvchilarga darsda telefon ishlatish mumkin emas. (qoida)
  • Why aren’t you at school? You’re supposed to be in class now!Nega maktabda emassiz? Hozir darsda bo‘lishingiz kerak-ku!

was supposed to — ko‘pincha bo‘lmagan narsa

O‘tgan zamonda was / were supposed to “bo‘lishi kerak edi (lekin bo‘lmadi yoki boshqacha bo‘ldi)” degan ma’noni beradi:

  • The plane was supposed to land at 8.15, but it was two hours late.Samolyot 8.15 da qo‘nishi kerak edi, lekin ikki soat kechikdi.
  • I was supposed to return Malika’s book yesterday, but I forgot.Kecha Malikaning kitobini qaytarishim kerak edi, lekin unutdim.
  • You weren’t supposed to tell anybody about the surprise party!Syurpriz ziyofat haqida hech kimga aytmasligingiz kerak edi!

Ma’noMisolO‘zbekcha

Reja, kelishuvWe are supposed to meet at six.Soat oltida uchrashishimiz kerak.

Bo‘lmagan kutish (o‘tgan)He was supposed to be here at nine.U soat to‘qqizda shu yerda bo‘lishi kerak edi.

should

  • sizning fikringiz yoki maslahatingiz
  • You should wear a helmet. (men shunday o‘ylayman)

be supposed to

Talaffuzda supposed to “suppose-to” kabi eshitiladi (-d deyarli eshitilmaydi). Shu sababli yozuvda ko‘pincha I’m suppose to ❌ deb xato yozishadi. To‘g‘risi: I’m supposed to ✅. Shuningdek be fe’lini tushirib qoldirmang: The train supposed to arrive ❌.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

He is said that he is rich.It is said that he is rich. / He is said to be rich.Ikki tuzilmani aralashtirmang: yo It ... that, yo ega + to-infinitiv.
He is said to lost his job.He is said to have lost his job.Oldin bo‘lgan ish: to have + V3 (lost emas, have lost).
He is said to be a spy in the 1990s.He is said to have been a spy in the 1990s.O‘tmishdagi holat → to have been.
Prices are expected to going up.Prices are expected to go up.to dan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli (go), -ing emas.
I’m suppose to meet him at six.I’m supposed to meet him at six.supposed — V3, oxirida -d bor.
The train supposed to arrive at eight.The train is supposed to arrive at eight.be (is / was / are) tushib qolmasin: be + supposed to.
I am supposed to going to the bank.I am supposed to go to the bank.supposed to dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda.
He was decided to leave.It was decided that he would leave.decide, suggest, announce faqat It ... that tuzilmasida keladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“It is said that he is rich” va “He is said to be rich” orasida farq bormi?

Ma’no bir xil. Ikkinchisi ixchamroq va ko‘proq yozma uslubga xos. Birinchisi ham keng tarqalgan, ayniqsa gap uzun yoki murakkab bo‘lsa: It is believed that the fire started in the kitchen. Birinchi gapdagi It hech narsani bildirmaydi — u shunchaki “bo‘sh ega” (It is clear that ... dagi kabi).

To‘g‘ri infinitivni qanday tanlayman?

Ikki savolga javob bering: (1) Ish aytilgan paytdagi holatmi yoki undan oldin bo‘lganmi? Oldin bo‘lgan bo‘lsa — to have + V3. (2) Ish hozir davom etyaptimi? Ha bo‘lsa — to be + V-ing. Qolgan hollarda — to + V. Oldin bo‘lgan va davom etgan ish uchun — to have been + V-ing.

Nega “She is said to have been working ...” da have been va -ing birga keladi?

Chunki bu ikki ma’noni birlashtiradi: oldin (to have been) va jarayon (-ing). Aniq gap: People say that she was working in London. → She is said to have been working in London. Bunday shakl kam uchraydi, lekin rasmiy matnda bor.

“I was supposed to call” — qo‘ng‘iroq qildimmi yoki yo‘qmi?

Grammatik jihatdan was supposed to faqat “rejada / kutilgan shunday edi” deydi, natijani kontekst ko‘rsatadi. Lekin amalda ko‘pincha reja bajarilmagan bo‘ladi: I was supposed to call Malika — odatda “qo‘ng‘iroq qilmaganman”. Qilgan bo‘lsangiz, oddiy aytasiz: I called Malika yesterday.

“You should” va “You are supposed to” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. should — sizning fikringiz yoki maslahatingiz. be supposed to — boshqalar (qoida, reja, jamiyat) nimani kutsa shu. You’re not supposed to eat in the library — “kutubxonada ovqatlanish mumkin emas (qoida shunday)”, You shouldn’t eat in the library — “kutubxonada ovqatlanmaganingiz ma’qul (mening fikrim)”. Qoidani eslatish uchun be supposed to tabiiy yangraydi.

Qaysi fe’llar bilan He is ... to ... ishlatilmaydi?

decide, suggest, announce, agree, explain, propose kabi fe’llar bilan faqat It was decided that ... tuzilmasi bor. Ishonchsiz bo‘lsangiz, say, think, believe, know, expect, report fe’llarini tanlang — ular ikkala tuzilmaga ham mos.

“People say that ...” o‘rniga bu tuzilmalarni qachon ishlataman?

People say that ... — oddiy suhbat uslubi. It is said that ... / He is said to ... — yangiliklar, ilmiy yozuv, rasmiy nutq. Imtihon essesida ikkinchisini ishlatish yaxshi taassurot qoldiradi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • say, think, believe, know, consider, understand, expect, report majhul nisbatda ikki tuzilma beradi: It is said that he is rich / He is said to be rich.
  • 2-tuzilmada that tushadi, that-gapning egasi butun gapning egasi bo‘ladi, fe’l to + infinitiv ga aylanadi.
  • Infinitiv shakli vaqtni bildiradi: to do (hozir / odat; expect bilan — kelajak), to be doing (hozir davom etayotgan), to have done (oldin bo‘lgan), to have been doing (oldin davom etgan).
  • Fe’l o‘tgan zamonda (was said) bo‘lsa, taqqoslash nuqtasi o‘sha payt: was said to be — o‘sha payt, was said to have been — undan oldin.
  • He is supposed to be ... = “deyishadi, ... deb hisoblanadi” (ba’zan shubha bilan).
  • I’m supposed to ... = reja, qoida yoki kutish bo‘yicha “kerak”; was supposed to ... = “kerak edi (lekin bo‘lmadi)”; aren’t supposed to ... = mumkin emas.
  • Imlo: supposed to (suppose to ❌), va be ni tushirib qoldirmang: The train is supposed to arrive.
