Present continuous (I am doing)

Hozirgi davomli zamon
Present and past · Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Present continuous (hozirgi davomli zamon) qanday yasalishini: am / is / are + fe’l-ing.
  • Uni qachon ishlatishni: aynan hozir bo‘layotgan ish, “jarayonda” bo‘lgan ish, hozirgi vaqt atrofidagi davr va o‘zgarib borayotgan holatlar.
  • Inkor, so‘roq va qisqa javoblarni to‘g‘ri tuzishni.
  • -ing qo‘shishdagi imlo qoidalarini (make → making, run → running).

Present continuous — “hozir, ayni paytda davom etayotgan” harakatni bildiradi. Harakat allaqachon boshlangan, lekin hali tugamagan: u o‘rtasida turibdi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no -yapti / -yapman / -moqda qo‘shimchalari bilan beriladi: o‘qiyapman, ishlayapti, yomg‘ir yog‘moqda. Ingliz tilida esa buning uchun ikki qism kerak: be fe’li (am/is/are) + fe’lning -ing shakli. O‘zbek tilida “bo‘lmoq” fe’lini aytmaymiz, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha am/is/are ni tushirib qoldiradi: ❌ I working. Bu eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato!

Shakli va asosiy ma’nosi: ayni paytda bo‘layotgan ish Form and main use: something in progress now

Tasavvur qiling: Malika mashinada, ishga ketyapti. U yo‘lga chiqqan (harakat boshlangan), lekin hali ishxonaga yetib bormagan (harakat tugamagan). Biz bu haqda gapirayotgan paytda u haydash jarayonida:

  • Malika is driving to work.Malika ishga mashina haydab ketyapti.
Ega+am / is / are+fe’l + ing+...

Qoida: “Kim” ekaniga qarab be fe’li tanlanadi: I → am, he / she / it → is, we / you / they → are. Keyin asosiy fe’lga -ing qo‘shiladi. Ikkalasi ham shart!

Present continuous: to‘liq jadval

Og‘zaki nutqda deyarli har doim qisqa shakl ishlatiladi: I’m, you’re, he’s, she’s, it’s, we’re, they’re. Yozma rasmiy matnda to‘liq shakl (I am) yoziladi.

So‘roq gap va qisqa javoblar

So‘roq gapda am / is / are eganing oldiga chiqadi. So‘roq so‘zi (what, where, why...) bo‘lsa, u eng boshida turadi:

(What / Where ...)+am / is / are+ega+fe’l-ing?
SavolQisqa javob: ✅ HaQisqa javob: ❌ Yo‘q
Are you working?Yes, I am.No, I’m not.
Is he sleeping?Yes, he is.No, he isn’t. / No, he’s not.
Are they coming?Yes, they are.No, they aren’t. / No, they’re not.

Ijobiy qisqa javobda qisqa shakl ishlatilmaydi: Yes, I am. ✅ — Yes, I’m. ❌; Yes, she is. ✅ — Yes, she’s. ❌

Ish “jarayonda” — ayni daqiqada bo‘lmasa ham I am doing = I’m in the middle of doing it

I am doing something degani — “men bu ishni boshlaganman va hali tugatmaganman, u jarayonda” degani. Muhim nuqta: harakat aynan gapirayotgan soniyada bo‘layotgan bo‘lishi shart emas.

Masalan, Jasur choyxonada do‘sti bilan gaplashib o‘tiribdi. U shunday deydi:

  • I’m reading a great novel these days. You can borrow it when I finish.Shu kunlarda zo‘r bir roman o‘qiyapman. Tugatsam, senga berib turaman.

Jasur aynan shu daqiqada kitob o‘qimayapti — u gaplashyapti! Lekin u kitobni o‘qishni boshlagan va hali tugatmagan. Ya’ni u kitob o‘qish “jarayonining o‘rtasida”.

  • Dilnoza wants to study in Germany, so she’s learning German.Dilnoza Germaniyada o‘qimoqchi, shuning uchun nemis tilini o‘rganyapti. (Ayni paytda dars qilmayotgan bo‘lishi mumkin.)
  • My uncle is building a new house in Samarkand. He hopes to finish it next spring.Amakim Samarqandda yangi uy quryapti. Kelasi bahorda tugatishni umid qilyapti.
  • My brother is studying medicine at university.Akam universitetda tibbiyotni o‘qiyapti.
  • I’m looking for a new job.Men yangi ish qidiryapman (hozirgi davrda).

O‘zbek tilida ham xuddi shunday: “Akam tibbiyot institutida o‘qiyapti” deganda, u hozir darsda o‘tirgan bo‘lishi shart emas. Ingliz tilida ham shu mantiq: He is studying medicine.

“Hozir” atrofidagi davr: today, this week, this year Periods around now

Hozirgi paytni o‘z ichiga olgan vaqt oralig‘i haqida gapirganda (today, this week, this month, this year, these days, at the moment), ham present continuous ishlatiladi. Bu davr hali tugamagan.

Shu ma’noda tez-tez keladigan so‘zlar:

  • A: You’re working late today.
    B: Yes, I have a report to finish.    — Bugun kechgacha ishlayapsan. — Ha, tugatishim kerak bo‘lgan hisobot bor.
  • My dad’s shop isn’t making much money this year.Dadamning do‘koni bu yil unchalik ko‘p daromad keltirmayapti.
  • “Is Sardor working this week?” “No, he’s visiting his grandparents in Fergana.”— Sardor bu hafta ishlayaptimi? — Yo‘q, u Farg‘onaga bobosi va buvisini ko‘rgani ketgan.
  • These days more and more people are working from home.Hozirgi kunda tobora ko‘proq odamlar uydan ishlamoqda.

Diqqat: “every day / usually / always” kabi takroriy odatlar uchun present continuous emas, present simple ishlatiladi: I work every day ✅ — I’m working every day ❌ (odat ma’nosida). Bu farq Unit 3 da batafsil.

O‘zgarib borayotgan holatlar Changes happening around now

Hozirgi davrda asta-sekin o‘zgarayotgan, rivojlanayotgan narsalar haqida gapirganda present continuous ishlatiladi. Bunday gaplarda ko‘pincha quyidagi fe’llar keladi:

get (+ sifat)changebecomeincreaserisefallgrowimprovebeginstart
  • The number of tourists in Uzbekistan is increasing every year.O‘zbekistonga keladigan sayyohlar soni yildan yilga ko‘paymoqda.
  • Is your English getting better?Ingliz tilingiz yaxshilanyaptimi?
  • The traffic in Tashkent is bad, and it’s getting worse.Toshkentda tirbandlik yomon, u yanada yomonlashyapti.
  • The weather is getting colder. Winter is coming.Havo sovib boryapti. Qish kelyapti.
  • Prices are rising every month.Narxlar har oy oshib boryapti.

O‘zbek tilida bunday ma’no ko‘pincha “-ib bormoq” bilan beriladi: sovib bormoqda, oshib boryapti, yaxshilanib boryapti. Inglizchada bu — is/are getting / increasing / rising...

Present continuous ishlatilmaydigan fe’llar (qisqa eslatma) State verbs — a first look

Ba’zi fe’llar harakatni emas, holatni bildiradi (bilish, yoqtirish, egalik qilish). Ular odatda continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi, hatto “hozir” ma’nosida ham:

❌ Odatda -ing olmaydi

  • know, understand, believe
  • like, love, hate, want, need, prefer
  • mean, remember, forget
  • belong, own, seem, contain

✅ To‘g‘ri ishlatish

  • I know the answer. (I’m knowing ❌)
  • She wants a coffee now. (is wanting ❌)
  • Do you understand? (Are you understanding ❌)

Bu mavzu Unit 4 da to‘liq tushuntiriladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I working now.I am (I’m) working now.“Be” fe’li (am/is/are) tushib qolgan. U majburiy!
She is work at the moment.She is working at the moment.Asosiy fe’lga -ing qo‘shilmagan.
They is playing football.They are playing football.They bilan har doim “are”.
Do you working today?Are you working today?Present continuous so‘rog‘ida “do” emas, “am/is/are” ishlatiladi.
Where you are going?Where are you going?So‘roq gapda “are” egadan oldin keladi.
He is runing / He is writeing.He is running. / He is writing.Imlo: run → running (n ikkilanadi), write → writing (e tushadi).
I am knowing your sister.I know your sister.“Know” — holat fe’li, continuous shaklda kelmaydi (Unit 4).
Yes, I’m. (— Are you busy?)Yes, I am.Ijobiy qisqa javob oxirida qisqartma ishlatilmaydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I’m reading a book” desam-u, lekin ayni paytda o‘qimayotgan bo‘lsam, xato bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q, xato emas. Present continuous “ish jarayonda — boshlangan, lekin tugamagan” degan ma’noni beradi (B bo‘lim). Kitobni o‘qishni boshlagan bo‘lsangiz va hali tugatmagan bo‘lsangiz, I’m reading a book deyish to‘liq to‘g‘ri, hatto hozir ovqatlanayotgan bo‘lsangiz ham.

“I work” va “I’m working” ning farqi nimada?

I work — odat, doimiy holat: “men (umuman) ishlayman”. I’m working — hozir yoki hozirgi vaqtinchalik davrda: “men (hozir) ishlayapman”. Masalan: I work in a bank (doimiy ishim), lekin I’m working in a café this summer (shu yoz, vaqtincha). Batafsil — Unit 3.

“am not” ning qisqa shakli bormi? “I amn’t” deyiladimi?

Yo‘q, amn’t degan shakl standart ingliz tilida yo‘q. Faqat I’m not ishlatiladi: I’m not working. So‘roq-inkor shaklida esa Aren’t I ...? deyiladi: I’m late, aren’t I?

“He’s not working” va “He isn’t working” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va ma’nosi bir xil. He’s not inkorni biroz kuchliroq ta’kidlaydi, he isn’t esa neytral. You/we/they bilan ham ikkala variant bor: they’re not / they aren’t.

Present continuous kelasi zamon ma’nosida ham ishlatiladimi?

Ha! Agar ish allaqachon rejalashtirilgan / kelishilgan bo‘lsa: I’m meeting Tom tomorrow. (Ertaga Tom bilan uchrashaman — kelishib qo‘yganmiz.) Bu ma’no Unit 19 da o‘rganiladi. Bunda gapda odatda kelasi vaqtni bildiruvchi so‘z (tomorrow, next week) bo‘ladi.

“Look!” va “Listen!” dan keyin nega continuous ishlatiladi?

Chunki biz suhbatdoshning e’tiborini ayni paytda sodir bo‘layotgan narsaga qaratamiz: Look! It’s snowing. (Qara! Qor yog‘yapti.) Listen! Somebody is singing. (Eshit! Kimdir qo‘shiq aytyapti.)

“I’m having breakfast” to‘g‘rimi? “Have” holat fe’li emasmi?

“Have” ikki xil ma’noda keladi. Egalik ma’nosida (have a car) — continuous bo‘lmaydi: I have a car. Lekin harakat ma’nosida (ovqatlanmoq, dush qabul qilmoq, dam olmoq) — continuous bo‘ladi: I’m having breakfast / a shower / a good time.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

  4. We (wait) for the bus.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: am / is / are + fe’l-ing. Ikkala qism ham majburiy.
  • Ishlatilishi: (A) ayni paytda bo‘layotgan ish; (B) boshlangan, lekin tugamagan, “jarayonda” bo‘lgan ish; (C) today / this week / this year kabi hozirgi davr; (D) o‘zgarib borayotgan holatlar (getting better, increasing).
  • So‘roq: Are you ...ing? Inkor: I’m not / isn’t / aren’t ...ing.
  • Qisqa javob: Yes, I am. (Yes, I’m ❌)
  • Holat fe’llari (know, like, want, understand ...) odatda -ing olmaydi.
