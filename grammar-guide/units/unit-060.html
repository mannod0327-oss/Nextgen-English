Preposition (in/for/about etc.) + -ing

Predlog (in / for / about) + -ing
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Predlog (in, on, at, for, about, of, with, by, without, before, after ...) dan keyin fe’l kelsa, u -ing shaklida bo‘lishini: interested in learning.
  • Sifat + predlog + -ing (good at, fed up with, afraid of) va ot + predlog + -ing (the advantage of, the idea of).
  • by -ing (usul), without -ing, before / after -ing, instead of -ing, in spite of -ing, How about -ing?
  • to ba’zan infinitiv belgisi emas, predlog bo‘lishini: I’m looking forward to meeting you.

Qoida juda sodda: predlogdan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi. Infinitiv (to learn) yoki oddiy fe’l (learn) kelmaydi. Sababi: predlogdan keyin ot (yoki otga o‘xshash so‘z) turishi kerak, fe’lning -ing shakli esa shu yerda ot vazifasini bajaradi.

O‘zbek tilida ham fe’l -ish qo‘shimchasi bilan otga aylanadi va kelishik qo‘shimchalarini oladi: ishlashga qiziqaman, o‘rganishda yaxshiman, kutishdan charchadim, ko‘rishni kutyapman. Inglizchada ham xuddi shunday, faqat kelishik qo‘shimchasi o‘rniga alohida predlog turadi va fe’l -ing oladi: interested in working. Xato shundan chiqadi: o‘zbekchadagi ishlamoq shakliga qarab o‘quvchilar to work deb yuboradi: ❌ interested in to work. To‘g‘risi: ✅ interested in working.

Predlogdan keyin fe’l: -ing Preposition + -ing

Qoida: predlogdan keyin ot yoki fe’l-ing keladi. Oddiy fe’l ham, to + fe’l ham kelmaydi.

sifat / ot / fe’l+predlog+fe’l + ing

Istisnolar (kam uchraydi): but va except dan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli yoki to + fe’l kelishi mumkin — He did nothing but complain, I had no choice except to wait; be about to (I’m about to leave — “hozir ketmoqchiman”) esa alohida ibora. Asosiy qoida o‘zgarmaydi: predlog + -ing.

Misollar

  • Is your sister interested in joining the chess club?Singlingiz shaxmat to‘garagiga qo‘shilishga qiziqadimi?
  • I’m fed up with waiting for the bus every morning.Har tong avtobus kutishdan to‘yib ketdim.
  • What are the advantages of living near the metro?Metro yaqinida yashashning qanday afzalliklari bor?
  • Instead of buying a new phone, Aziz repaired the old one.Yangi telefon sotib olish o‘rniga Aziz eskisini ta’mirlatdi.
  • Thank you for showing me around Samarkand.Menga Samarqandni aylantirib ko‘rsatganingiz uchun rahmat.
  • Despite being tired, Nodira finished the report.Charchaganiga qaramay, Nodira hisobotni tugatdi.

Sifat + predlog + -ing (o‘zbekcha kelishik qo‘shimchalari bilan)

Quyidagi jadvalda ingliz predloglariga mos o‘zbekcha qo‘shimchalar ranglar bilan ajratilgan: -ga, -da, -dan, -ni. Ular ko‘pincha mos keladi, lekin har doim emas — ingliz predlogini ibora sifatida yodlash kerak.

Fe’l + predlog + -ing (succeed in, insist on, accuse somebody of ...) Unit 62 da alohida o‘rganiladi. Sifat va otlardan keyingi predloglar ro‘yxati Unit 129–131 da.

by, without, before, after, instead of, in spite of ... + -ing Common prepositions before -ing

Quyidagi predloglar -ing bilan eng ko‘p ishlatiladi. Ularning ko‘pchiligi o‘zbekchada alohida qo‘shimcha yoki so‘z birikmasi bilan beriladi:

Predlog + -ing: ma’no va o‘zbekcha muqobili

by -ing: usulni ko‘rsatadi

  • Aziz got rich by selling carpets in Samarkand.Aziz Samarqandda gilam sotib boyib ketdi.
  • You can open the file by double-clicking on it.Faylni ustiga ikki marta bosib ochishingiz mumkin.
  • The cat got into the kitchen by jumping through the open window.Mushuk ochiq derazadan sakrab oshxonaga kirib oldi.

without -ing: “...masdan”

  • Don’t leave the shop without paying.Do‘kondan pul to‘lamasdan chiqib ketmang.
  • He crossed the road without looking.U yo‘lni qaramasdan kesib o‘tdi.

O‘zbekcha -masdan (aytmasdan, qaramasdan) inglizchada without + -ing bo‘ladi. Inkor ma’no without ning ichida bor, shuning uchun qo‘shimcha not qo‘yilmaydi: ❌ without not saying. Xuddi shunday -ib / orqali (o‘qib, o‘qish orqali) — by reading.

before / after / since + -ing dan keyingi fe’l odatda asosiy gapning egasi qilgan ish bo‘ladi. After finishing school, Kamola moved ✅ (maktabni Kamola bitirdi). Ega boshqa bo‘lsa, to‘liq gap ishlating: After Kamola finished school, her parents moved to Tashkent. Aks holda gap chalkash chiqadi: After finishing school, her parents moved ... — go‘yo maktabni ota-onasi bitirgandek.

in spite of va despite — bir xil ma’no. Faqat despite dan keyin of kelmaydi: ✅ despite feeling ill, ❌ despite of feeling ill. Batafsil — Unit 113.

How about ...? / What about ...? + -ing

Taklif yoki maslahat berish uchun How about va What about ishlatiladi. Ulardan keyin ot yoki -ing keladi (ular ham predlog tuzilmasi):

  • How about asking Malika to come with us?Malikadan biz bilan kelishini so‘rasak-chi?
  • What about leaving at six instead of seven?Soat yettida emas, oltida chiqsak-chi?
  • How about a walk after dinner?Kechki ovqatdan keyin sayr qilsak-chi?

to: predlogmi yoki infinitiv belgisi? To + -ing (I’m looking forward to seeing you)

Inglizcha to ikki xil bo‘lishi mumkin. Aynan shu yerda xato eng ko‘p uchraydi:

to + fe’l (infinitiv belgisi)

  • I want to go.
  • We decided to wait.
  • She saves money to buy a car.

Keyin oddiy fe’l keladi.

to + -ing (to — predlog)

  • I’m looking forward to going.
  • He objects to paying more.
  • I’m used to waiting.

Keyin ot yoki -ing keladi.

“It” sinovi: gapda to dan keyingi fe’l o‘rniga it yoki biror ot qo‘ying. Gap to‘g‘ri chiqsa, to — predlog va fe’l -ing bo‘ladi. I’m looking forward to it ✅ → looking forward to seeing you. I want to it ❌ → I want to see (infinitiv).

Xat va elektron pochta oxirida: I look forward to hearing from you yoki qisqa Looking forward to hearing from you. — “Javobingizni intiqlik bilan kutaman”. Bu yerda ham to + -ing: ❌ I look forward to hear from you.

-ing ning boshqa shakllari not -ing, being + V3, having + V3, other subject

Agar -ing dagi ishni gapning egasi emas, boshqa odam qilsa, fe’ldan oldin shu odam yoziladi:

afraid of -ing va afraid to do ma’nosi turlicha (Unit 66). for -ing va maqsadni bildiruvchi to + fe’l farqi — Unit 64 da.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I’m interested in to learn English.I’m interested in learning English.Predlog (in) dan keyin infinitiv emas, -ing keladi.
She is good at to draw.She is good at drawing.O‘zbekcha “chizishda” → at drawing, at to draw emas.
He left without to say goodbye.He left without saying goodbye.without — predlog, keyin -ing.
Instead of go by bus, we walked.Instead of going by bus, we walked.instead of — predlog guruhi, keyin -ing.
Despite of being tired, she finished.Despite being tired, she finished. / In spite of being tired, she finished.despite dan keyin of yo‘q; in spite of da esa bor.
I’m looking forward to see you.I’m looking forward to seeing you.Bu yerda to — predlog (“it” sinovi: looking forward to it ✅), shuning uchun -ing.
Thank you for help me.Thank you for helping me.for predlogidan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida.
After to finish school, she moved.After finishing school, she moved.after dan keyin to kelmaydi: after + -ing.
How about to go to the cinema?How about going to the cinema?How about dan keyin -ing yoki ot keladi.
He left without not saying goodbye.He left without saying goodbye.without ning o‘zida inkor bor; qo‘shimcha not kerak emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega predlogdan keyin infinitiv (to + fe’l) emas, aynan -ing kerak?

Predlog o‘zidan keyin ot yoki otga teng keladigan so‘zni talab qiladi: interested in music. Fe’lning -ing shakli (gerund) ot vazifasini bajara oladi, infinitiv esa yo‘q. Shuning uchun interested in singing to‘g‘ri, interested in to sing noto‘g‘ri. O‘zbekcha -ish shakliga o‘xshatib eslab qoling: qo‘shiq aytishga.

“to” predlog ekanini qanday bilaman?

“It” sinovi: to dan keyin it yoki biror ot qo‘yib ko‘ring. I’m used to it, I object to it, I look forward to it — gap to‘g‘ri, demak to — predlog. I want to it — noto‘g‘ri, demak infinitiv. Lug‘atda ham look forward to something deb yoziladi: “something” (ot) kelishi predlog ekanini bildiradi.

“Before going” va “before I go” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Ish qiluvchi bir xil bo‘lsa, ixcham before -ing ishlatish mumkin: Before leaving, I locked the door. Ish qiluvchi boshqa bo‘lsa yoki zamonni aniq ko‘rsatmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, to‘liq gap kerak: Before the guests arrived, I cleaned the house.

“in spite of” va “despite” orasida farq bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil. in spite of da of bor, despite da yo‘q. Ikkalasidan keyin ot yoki -ing keladi: despite the rain, despite being tired. To‘liq gap qo‘shmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, although ishlating (Unit 113).

Predlogdan keyin -ing ning ega-si boshqa bo‘lsa, nima qilaman?

Fe’ldan oldin shu odamni yozasiz: We’re worried about Dad driving at night. Olmosh bo‘lsa, og‘zaki nutqda him / her / them, rasmiy yozuvda his / her / their ishlatiladi. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri (★ bo‘lim).

“I went there for buying bread” deb bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. Nima uchun borganingizni aytayotgan bo‘lsangiz (maqsad), to + fe’l ishlatiladi: I went there to buy bread. for + -ing faqat narsaning vazifasi haqida keladi: This knife is for cutting bread. Batafsil — Unit 64.

“How about” va “What about” farqi bormi?

Taklif va maslahat ma’nosida ikkalasi bir xil: How about / What about going to the cinema? Ikkalasidan keyin ot yoki -ing keladi. What about ba’zan “...-chi?” ma’nosida savol qaytarish uchun ham ishlatiladi: I’m hungry. What about you?

“on arriving” degani nima?

on + -ing — “shu ish qilingan zahoti” degani, asosan rasmiy yozuvda: On arriving at the airport, passengers must show their passports. Oddiy nutqda when yoki as soon as ishlatiladi: When we arrived, we ordered tea.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni predlogdan keyin to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. Are you interested in (join) our chess club?
  2. Dilnoza is very good at (draw).
  3. I’m fed up with (wait) for the bus every morning.
  4. What’s the main advantage of (work) from home?
  5. Bekzod is afraid of (lose) his job.
  6. Thank you for (show) me around the old city.
  7. We talked about (move) to Samarkand.
  8. Nodira is famous for (sing) old folk songs.

2-mashq. Predlog + -ing ni yozing (by, without, before, after, instead of, in spite of).

  1. We learned the poem (repeat) it again and again.
  2. She walked past me (say) a word.
  3. (go) to bed, I always check the door.
  4. Malika played in the final (have) a sore knee.
  5. (take) a taxi, Bekzod walked to the station.
  6. (finish) lunch, we drank green tea.

3-mashq. “to” dan keyin fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (infinitiv yoki -ing).

  1. I’m looking forward to (see) you.
  2. I want (see) the new film.
  3. She objects to (work) on Sundays.
  4. We decided (take) a taxi.
  5. Are you used to (get) up early?
  6. In addition to (teach), she writes books.
  7. He promised (call) me tonight.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘ri yozing.

  1. I’m interested in to learn Japanese. →
  2. He left without to say goodbye. →
  3. Instead of go by bus, we walked. →
  4. Despite of being tired, she finished the work. →
  5. I’m looking forward to meet you. →
  6. Thank you for help me. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Men ingliz tilini o‘rganishga qiziqaman. →
  2. U xayrlashmasdan ketdi. →
  3. Sizni ertaga ko‘rishni intiqlik bilan kutyapman. →
  4. Kinoga borsak-chi? →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Predlogdan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi: good at drawing, fed up with waiting, instead of going (istisno: did nothing but wait, be about to).
  • by -ing — usul; without -ing — “...masdan”; before / after / since -ing — vaqt; despite / in spite of -ing — “...ganiga qaramay”.
  • How about / What about + -ing? — taklif.
  • to ba’zan predlog: look forward to, object to, be used to, in addition to → keyin -ing. “It” sinovi: to it desa bo‘lsa — predlog.
  • Shakllar: not -ing, being + V3, having + V3; boshqa ega bo‘lsa: worried about Dad driving.
  • despite dan keyin of yo‘q; without bilan not ishlatilmaydi.
