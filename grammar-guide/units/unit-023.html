I will and I'm going to

Will va be going to farqi
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • will va be going to ni harakatlar (qarorlar) haqida gapirganda farqlashni: yangi qaror yoki avvalgi qaror.
  • will va be going to ni bashorat uchun farqlashni: hozirgi belgi bor-yo‘qligiga qarab.
  • Qachon ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lishini va qachon faqat bittasi to‘g‘ri ekanini.
  • Kelajak shakllarini tanlash uchun oddiy “ikki savol” usulini.

Bu unit Unit 20–22 dagi bilimlarni bir joyga yig‘adi. Kelajak haqidagi har bir gap uchun o‘zingizga ikki savol bering:

  1. Qaror qachon qabul qilindi? Hozir, gapirayotganda → will. Avvalroq → going to.
  2. Bashoratga hozirgi belgi asosmi? Ha, ko‘rib turibman → going to. Yo‘q, bu fikrim/ishonchim → will.

O‘zbek tilida ikkala holat ham ko‘pincha bitta shakl bilan aytiladi: Men unga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman (yangi qarormi yoki avvalgi rejami — farqi yo‘q). Shu sababli o‘zbek o‘quvchilari will va going to ni aralashtirib yuborishadi. Yordam beradigan maslahat: agar o‘zbekchada -moqchiman desa bo‘ladigan bo‘lsa (avvaldan niyat), bu ko‘pincha going to; agar “mayli, unda men ...” desa bo‘ladigan bo‘lsa (shu zahoti qaror), bu will.

Kelajakdagi harakatlar: yangi qaror yoki avvalgi qaror Future actions: will or going to?

Kichik bir voqeani kuzating. Malika va Kamola gaplashib o‘tirishibdi:

  • MALIKA: Let’s make plov on Sunday and invite everyone!
    KAMOLA: What a great idea! I’ll buy the rice and the carrots.    MALIKA: Yakshanba kuni palov qilib, hammani chaqiraylik! KAMOLA: Zo‘r fikr! Guruch bilan sabzini men olaman.

Palov g‘oyasi Kamola uchun yangi — u qarorni shu zahoti qabul qildi. Shuning uchun will (’ll).

Kechqurun Kamola akasi Jasur bilan gaplashadi:

  • KAMOLA: Guess what? Malika and I are going to make plov on Sunday. We’re going to ask all our classmates to come, and I’m going to buy the rice tomorrow.KAMOLA: Bilasanmi? Malika ikkalamiz yakshanba kuni palov qilmoqchimiz. Hamma sinfdoshlarimizni chaqirmoqchimiz, guruchni esa ertaga men olmoqchiman.

Endi qaror avvaldan qabul qilingan — Kamola Jasurga allaqachon tayyor rejani aytyapti. Shuning uchun going to.

Bir xil vaziyat — ikki xil javob
Gap⚡ will — hozir qaror qildim🗓️ going to — avval qaror qilganman
“Aziz needs help with his homework.”Does he? OK, I’ll help him after dinner.
Shundaymi? Mayli, kechki ovqatdan keyin unga yordam beraman.		Yes, I know. I’m going to help him after dinner.
Ha, bilaman. Kechki ovqatdan keyin unga yordam bermoqchiman.
“Our teacher’s birthday is tomorrow.”Really? I’ll buy her some flowers.
Rostdanmi? Unda unga gul olaman.		I know. I’m going to make her a card.
Bilaman. Unga tabriknoma yasamoqchiman.
“Your shoes are dirty.”Oh, so they are. I’ll clean them now.
Voy, rostdan. Hozir tozalayman.		I know. I’m going to clean them tonight.
Bilaman. Bugun kechqurun tozalamoqchiman.

Belgi so‘zlar: Did he? / Really? / Oh, ... / OK, ... / Right, ... — yangilikka reaksiya → ko‘pincha will. I know. / Yes, I heard. / I’ve decided ... — avvaldan biladi → going to.

Agar reja boshqalar bilan kelishilgan bo‘lsa (vaqt, joy belgilangan), present continuous ham ishlatiladi (Unit 19): I’m meeting Aziz at 3 to discuss it. Ammo yangi, shu soniyadagi qaror uchun faqat will.

Kelajakdagi voqealar: bashorat Future happenings and situations (predictions)

Bashorat qilganda will ham, going to ham ishlatiladi va ko‘pincha farq juda kichik:

  • I think Bekzod will win the chess tournament. / I think Bekzod is going to win the chess tournament.Menimcha, shaxmat turnirida Bekzod g‘olib chiqadi. (Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.)

going to — hozirgi vaziyatdan ko‘rinib turibdi

Agar bashoratni hozirgi vaziyatga qarab qilsangiz (belgisini ko‘rib, sezib turibsiz), going to ishlating. Bunday paytda will ishlatilmaydi:

  • Listen to that thunder! It’s going to rain. (not It will rain)Momaqaldiroqni eshityapsanmi? Yomg‘ir yog‘adi. (Momaqaldiroqni hozir eshityapman.)
  • My battery is at 2%. My phone is going to die.Batareyam 2% qoldi. Telefonim o‘chib qoladi.
  • Help! This box is too heavy. I’m going to drop it! (not I’ll drop it)Yordam bering! Bu quti juda og‘ir. Tushirib yuboraman!
  • The bus is full and it’s very slow. We’re going to be late.Avtobus to‘la va juda sekin ketyapti. Kechikamiz.

will — fikr, ishonch, tajriba

Boshqa hollarda — bashorat sizning fikringiz, ishonchingiz yoki bilimingizga asoslansa — will ishlatiladi:

  • I’m sure you’ll enjoy Samarkand. It’s a beautiful city.Ishonchim komil, Samarqand sizga yoqadi. U juda go‘zal shahar.
  • Robots will do many of our jobs in the future.Kelajakda robotlar ko‘p ishlarimizni bajaradi.
  • Don’t lend him any money. He won’t pay you back.Unga pul qarz berma. U qaytarmaydi.
  • Dilnoza will probably be late — she usually is.Dilnoza kechiksa kerak — u odatda kechikadi.

👀 going to — dalil HOZIR bor

The ice is very thin. Be careful — you’re going to fall in!

Muz juda yupqa. Ehtiyot bo‘l — tushib ketasan! (Yupqa muzni ko‘rib turibman.)

💭 will — mening fikrim

Don’t go skating there. I’m sure you’ll fall in.

U yerga konkida uchgani borma. Ishonchim komil, tushib ketasan. (Bu — fikr, ogohlantirish.)

Tekshirish usuli: gap oldidan Look! / Listen! / I can see ... / I feel ... qo‘yish mumkin bo‘lsa — ko‘pincha going to. I think / I’m sure / I expect / probably bilan boshlanadigan umumiy fikr bo‘lsa — ko‘pincha will (lekin going to ham mumkin).

Kelajak shakllarini tanlash xaritasi Choosing a future form

Qisqacha: will va going to
willbe going to
Harakat (qaror)yangi qaror, shu soniyada; taklif, va’da, rozilikqaror avvaldan qabul qilingan (niyat)
Bashoratfikr, ishonch, bilim (I think, I’m sure, probably)hozirgi belgi, dalil asosida (Look! I feel ...)
InkorI won’t lend him money. (rad etish / hozirgi qaror)I’m not going to lend him money again. (qat’iy qaror qildim)

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

A: I need a pen. B: I’m going to lend you mine.A: I need a pen. B: I’ll lend you mine.Taklif — shu soniyada qilingan qaror. Going to bu yerda “avvaldan rejalashtirgandim” degan g‘alati ma’no beradi.
The sky is black! It will rain soon.The sky is black! It’s going to rain soon.Belgi hozir ko‘rinib turibdi → going to.
We’ve decided: we will sell our car.We’ve decided: we’re going to sell our car.Qaror avvaldan qabul qilingan — going to.
I feel awful. I think I’ll faint.I feel awful. I think I’m going to faint.Hozirgi holatga (o‘zimni yomon his qilyapman) asoslangan bashorat — going to.
Oh, that’s the doorbell! I open it.Oh, that’s the doorbell! I’ll get it. / I’ll answer it.Shu soniyadagi qaror — will. Present simple ishlatilmaydi.
I think she is liking the scarf we bought.I think she’ll like the scarf we bought.Kelajak haqidagi fikr — will. “like” holat fe’li, continuous’da kelmaydi.
I will meet Sardor tomorrow at 5 — we agreed yesterday.I’m meeting Sardor tomorrow at 5.Kelishilgan reja — present continuous (yoki going to), will emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Ba’zan will ham, going to ham to‘g‘ri deyiladi. Unda qanday tanlayman?

Bashoratlarda ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri (I think it will be / is going to be cold). Faqat ikki holatda tanlov qat’iy: (1) shu soniyadagi qaror, taklif, va’da — faqat will; (2) hozir ko‘rinib turgan belgi asosidagi bashorat va avvalgi qaror — going to.

Ob-havo ma’lumotida “It will rain tomorrow” deyiladi. Bu xato emasmi?

Xato emas. Sinoptik bilim va prognozga asoslanib gapiradi — bu “fikr/bilim” turidagi bashorat: It will rain in the north tomorrow. Lekin derazadan qora bulutlarni ko‘rib turgan odam: It’s going to rain! deydi.

“I won’t” va “I’m not going to” — farqi nima?

I’m not going to ... — avvaldan qaror qildim: I’m not going to lend him money again. (Endi bermayman — qaror qilganman.) I won’t ... — hozirgi rad etish yoki qaror: “Can you lend me $100?” “No, I won’t.” (Yo‘q, bermayman.) Ma’nolar yaqin, lekin “qaror vaqti” farq qiladi.

Rejani birinchi marta aytayotgan bo‘lsam ham going to ishlatamanmi?

Ha! Muhimi — qaror qachon qabul qilingani, birinchi marta aytilishi emas. Agar siz kecha qaror qilib, bugun do‘stingizga birinchi marta aytayotgan bo‘lsangiz: I’m going to learn Chinese. Lekin suhbat davomida shu zahoti qaror qilsangiz: You know what? I’ll learn Chinese too!

Va’da berganda going to ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Va’da odatda will bilan beriladi: I promise I’ll be careful. I’m going to be careful — bu va’da emas, shunchaki niyatni aytish.

Imtihonda “will” yoki “going to” dan birini tanlash kerak bo‘lsa, qanday belgilarga qarayman?

Kontekstga qarang: Look! / Watch out! / I can see / I feel → going to; I’ve decided / I know / we’ve already planned → going to; Oh! / Really? / OK, then / I promise / Shall I ...? javobi → will; I think / I’m sure / probably / I expect → ko‘pincha will.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. will (’ll) yoki going to ni qo‘ying. Qaror qachon qilinganiga e’tibor bering.

  1. A: We haven’t got any bread.
    B: Oh, haven’t we? I (buy) some.
  2. A: We haven’t got any bread.
    B: I know. I (buy) some on my way home.
  3. A: Why are you taking the ladder?
    B: I (fix) the light in the kitchen.
  4. A: I can’t understand this maths problem.
    B: Let me see. I (explain) it to you.
  5. A: Have you decided what to study at university?
    B: Yes, I (study) economics.
  6. A: This suitcase is really heavy.
    B: Give it to me. I (carry) it.
  7. A: Did you know Nodira has passed her driving test?
    B: No, I didn’t! I (send) her a message to congratulate her.
  8. A: Why did you buy so many eggs?
    B: I (make) a big cake for Mum’s birthday.

2-mashq. Bashorat: will yoki going to? Hozirgi belgi bor bo‘lsa — going to, fikr bo‘lsa — will.

  1. Look! That car is going much too fast. It (crash)!
  2. I’m sure Kamola (like) the new teacher.
  3. Watch out! The baby (pull) the tablecloth off the table!
  4. Don’t worry about the trip. I think everything (be) fine.
  5. My eyes keep closing. I (fall) asleep at my desk!
  6. In fifty years, people (probably / live) longer than they do today.

3-mashq. Javobni to‘ldiring. Bitta vaziyat — ikki xil javob.

  1. “There’s a concert in Navoi Park on Friday.” (a) “Is there? Great, I (get) some tickets.”
  2. “There’s a concert in Navoi Park on Friday.” (b) “Yes, I know. I (go) with my sister.”
  3. “The car is very dirty.” (a) “I know. I (wash) it at the weekend.”
  4. “The car is very dirty.” (b) “Is it? I (wash) it now, then.”

4-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. — Kompyuterim ishlamayapti. — Mayli, hozir qarab beraman. →
  2. Biz yozda Xivaga bormoqchimiz. (avvaldan qaror qilingan) →
  3. Ehtiyot bo‘l! Stakan tushib ketadi! →
🔑 Javoblar (Answer key)

1-mashq

  1. ’ll buy / will buy
  2. ’m going to buy / am going to buy
  3. ’m going to fix / am going to fix
  4. ’ll explain / will explain
  5. ’m going to study / am going to study
  6. ’ll carry / will carry
  7. ’ll send / will send
  8. ’m going to make / am going to make

2-mashq

  1. ’s going to crash / is going to crash
  2. ’ll like / will like / ’s going to like / is going to like (will tabiiyroq)
  3. ’s going to pull / is going to pull
  4. ’ll be / will be / ’s going to be / is going to be
  5. ’m going to fall / am going to fall
  6. will probably live / ’ll probably live / are probably going to live

3-mashq

  1. ’ll get / will get
  2. ’m going to go / am going to go / ’m going / am going
  3. ’m going to wash / am going to wash
  4. ’ll wash / will wash

4-mashq

  1. “My computer isn’t working.” “OK, I’ll have a look at it now.” / “… I’ll look at it now.” / “… I’ll check it now.”
  2. We’re going to go to Khiva in the summer. / We are going to go to Khiva in the summer. / We’re going to Khiva in the summer. / We’re going to visit Khiva this summer.
  3. Be careful! The glass is going to fall! / Careful! That glass is going to fall!

🧠 Esda tuting

  • will — yangi qaror, shu soniyada: Oh, there’s no bread? I’ll buy some.
  • going to — qaror avvaldan qabul qilingan: I know. I’m going to buy some later.
  • Kelishilgan reja uchun present continuous ham: I’m meeting him at 3.
  • Bashorat: hozirgi belgi bor (Look! I feel ...) → going to: It’s going to rain.
  • Bashorat: fikr, ishonch (I think, I’m sure, probably) → will: You’ll love Samarkand.
  • Ko‘p bashoratlarda ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri; taklif, va’da va shu zahoti qarorda esa faqat will.
