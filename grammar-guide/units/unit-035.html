Had better, It's time ...

Had better va It’s time ...
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • had better (I’d better / you’d better) bilan “yaxshisi ..., bo‘lmasa muammo bo‘ladi” deyishni.
  • had better not inkor shaklini va had better ning hozirgi/kelasi zamon ma’nosini.
  • had better va should ning farqini.
  • It’s time to ..., It’s time for somebody to ... va It’s time + o‘tgan zamon (It’s time we went home).
  • It’s about time / It’s high time — kuchliroq tanqid.

I’d better do something = “buni qilganim ma’qul, aks holda muammo yoki xavf bo‘ladi”. It’s time you did something = “buni allaqachon qilishingiz kerak edi, endi kechikyapsiz”.

O‘zbek tilida had better ma’nosi “yaxshisi ...”, “-ganim ma’qul”, “-sam bo‘ladi, bo‘lmasa ...” bilan beriladi: Yaxshisi, hozir uxlay, bo‘lmasa ertaga charchab qolaman = I’d better go to bed now or I’ll be tired tomorrow. It’s time we went esa “... vaqti bo‘ldi / keldi”: Bozorga boradigan vaqtimiz keldi = It’s time we went shopping. Hayron bo‘ladigan joyi: “had” va “went” o‘tgan zamon shakli, lekin ma’no hozir haqida!

had better: “yaxshisi ..., bo‘lmasa muammo” I’d better do = it is advisable; if I don’t, there will be a problem

Ega+had better (’d better)+(not)+fe’l
  • My bus leaves in ten minutes. I’d better hurry, or I’ll miss it.Avtobusim o‘n daqiqadan keyin jo‘naydi. Shoshilganim ma’qul, bo‘lmasa qolib ketaman.
  • “Shall I book a table?” “Yes, you’d better. The restaurant is always busy on Fridays.”— Joy band qilaymi? — Ha, qilganingiz ma’qul. Juma kunlari restoran doim gavjum bo‘ladi.
  • My phone battery is almost dead. I’d better charge it before we go.Telefonim quvvati tugay deb qoldi. Ketishdan oldin quvvatlab olganim ma’qul.

Inkor: had better not

  • You’d better not tell Dad about the broken window. He’ll be furious.Yaxshisi, dadamga singan deraza haqida aytma. Uning jahli qattiq chiqadi.
  • “Would you like another piece of cake?” “I’d better not. I’m on a diet.”— Yana bir bo‘lak tort yeysizmi? — Yo‘q, yemaganim ma’qul. Parhezdaman.
  • The boss is in a bad mood. We’d better not be late today.Boshliqning kayfiyati yomon. Bugun kechikmaganimiz ma’qul.
had better haqida 4 ta muhim qoida
Qoida✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘ri
Har doim had (og‘zaki: ’d)You’d better go. / You had better go.You have better go.
Fe’l “to”siz keladiWe’d better take an umbrella.We’d better to take ...
Inkor: had better notYou’d better not go.You hadn’t better go. / You’d better don’t go.
Ma’no — hozir / kelajak (o‘tmish emas!)I’d better call the plumber now / tomorrow.I’d better call the plumber yesterday.

Ajratilgan so‘roq (question tag): We’d better leave now, hadn’t we? (Hozir chiqib ketganimiz ma’qul, shunday emasmi?) To‘liq so‘roq gap Had we better ...? juda kam ishlatiladi; odatda Do you think we should ...? deyiladi.

Og‘zaki nutqda ’d deyarli eshitilmaydi, shuning uchun ba’zan I better go deb yoziladi. Bu norasmiy — yozma ishda doim I’d better yoki I had better yozing.

had better va should: farqi nimada? had better vs should

had better should ga o‘xshaydi, lekin ikkita muhim farq bor:

had better

  • Faqat aniq bir vaziyat uchun (hozir, bugun, shu safar).
  • Maslahatga amal qilmasangiz — xavf yoki muammo bor.
  • It’s midnight and you’ve got an exam at eight. You’d better go to bed.

should

  • Har qanday vaziyatda: aniq yoki umumiy.
  • Shunchaki “bu yaxshi g‘oya”, majburiy xavf yo‘q.
  • You’re always tired. You should sleep more. (umumiy)
Vaziyathad bettershould
Aniq vaziyat: It’s snowing heavily. You ___ drive slowly.✅ You’d better drive slowly.✅ You should drive slowly.
Umumiy maslahat: People ___ eat more vegetables.❌✅ should eat
Xavf yo‘q, shunchaki tavsiya: The new café on Navoi Street is lovely. You ___ try it.❌ (g‘alati)✅ should try
Xavf bor: The last metro leaves at midnight. You ___ leave now or you’ll miss it.✅ You’d better leave.✅ You should leave.
  • It’s a beautiful city. You should visit it one day. (no danger — just a good idea)Bu go‘zal shahar. Bir kun albatta borib ko‘ring.
  • The library closes in ten minutes. You’d better return that book now, or you’ll have to pay a fine.Kutubxona o‘n daqiqadan keyin yopiladi. Kitobni hozir qaytarganingiz ma’qul, bo‘lmasa jarima to‘laysiz.

you’d better ba’zan ogohlantirish yoki hatto tahdid kabi eshitiladi: You’d better pay me back! (Pulimni qaytar, bo‘lmasa ...!). Shuning uchun katta yoshdagi yoki rahbar kishiga maslahat berganda should yoki I think you should ... xavfsizroq.

It’s time ... It’s time to ... / It’s time for ... to ... / It’s time + past

1) It’s time to do / It’s time for somebody to do

  • It’s eleven o’clock. It’s time to go home.Soat o‘n bir. Uyga ketish vaqti.
  • It’s time for the children to go to bed.Bolalarning uxlash vaqti bo‘ldi.

2) It’s time + ega + o‘tgan zamon (ma’no — hozir!)

Xuddi shu fikrni boshqacha ham aytish mumkin: It’s time + ega + past simple. Fe’l o‘tgan zamonda, lekin ma’no hozir yoki kelajak haqida:

It’s time+ega+past simple (V2)
  • It’s nearly eight. It’s time we left for the airport. (not It’s time we leave)Soat sakkizga yaqinlashdi. Aeroportga jo‘naydigan vaqtimiz bo‘ldi.
  • Bekzod has been playing computer games for five hours. It’s time he did some homework.Bekzod besh soatdan beri kompyuter o‘yini o‘ynayapti. Biroz uy vazifasi qiladigan vaqti allaqachon bo‘ldi.
  • It’s time the city did something about the traffic.Shahar tirbandlik bilan bog‘liq biror chora ko‘radigan vaqt allaqachon keldi.
  • Look at your hair! It’s time you had a haircut.Sochingga qara! Sartaroshga boradigan vaqting allaqachon kelgan.

Qoida: It’s time you did something = “buni allaqachon qilishingiz (yoki boshlashingiz) kerak edi”. Ko‘pincha tanqid yoki norozilik ohangida aytiladi. Fe’l doim o‘tgan zamonda: It’s time she went to the dentist ✅ (goes ❌).

3) It’s about time / It’s high time — kuchliroq

about yoki high qo‘shilsa, tanqid yanada kuchayadi: “allaqachon, ancha oldin qilinishi kerak edi!”

  • You’re thirty years old! It’s about time you learned to cook.O‘ttiz yoshga kirding! Ovqat pishirishni o‘rganadigan vaqting allaqachon o‘tib ketyapti-ku.
  • Aziz has been promising to fix the tap for weeks. It’s about time he actually did it.Aziz bir necha haftadan beri jo‘mrakni tuzataman deb va’da qilib yuribdi. Endi rostdan ham tuzatadigan vaqti allaqachon keldi-ku.
  • It’s high time the old bridge was repaired.Eski ko‘prikni ta’mirlash vaqti allaqachon kelgan (hatto o‘tib ketgan).

O‘zbekcha “... vaqti allaqachon keldi”, “qachon ... qilasan endi?!” kabi norozi gaplar → It’s (about / high) time + ega + V2. Oddiy, neytral “vaqt bo‘ldi” → It’s time to ...

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

You had better to go now.You had better (You’d better) go now.had better dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
You have better go now.You’d better go now.Bu iborada har doim “had” bo‘ladi — hatto ma’no hozir bo‘lsa ham.
You’d better don’t go out tonight.You’d better not go out tonight.Inkor: had better + not + fe’l. “don’t” ishlatilmaydi.
You hadn’t better tell her.You’d better not tell her.“not” better dan keyin keladi, had ga qo‘shilmaydi.
It’s time we go shopping.It’s time we went shopping. / It’s time to go shopping.It’s time + ega dan keyin fe’l o‘tgan zamonda.
It’s time he gets a job.It’s time he got a job.Ma’no hozirgi bo‘lsa ham, shakl — past simple.
It’s time for you go to bed.It’s time for you to go to bed. / It’s time you went to bed.“for + kishi” dan keyin “to + fe’l” keladi.
People had better drink more water. (umumiy maslahat)People should drink more water.Umumiy maslahat uchun had better emas, should.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “had” o‘tgan zamon shakli, lekin ma’no hozirgi?

Bu tarixiy sabablarga ko‘ra qotib qolgan ibora. had better ni bitta yaxlit modal ibora deb yodlang: u o‘zgarmaydi va har doim hozir yoki kelajak haqida gapiradi: I’d better go now / tomorrow. O‘tmishdagi maslahat uchun should have done ishlatiladi (Unit 33): You should have gone yesterday.

had better va should — qaysi biri kuchliroq?

had better kuchliroq, chunki unda “bo‘lmasa muammo bo‘ladi” degan ogohlantirish bor. should — oddiy maslahat. Shuningdek, had better faqat aniq vaziyat uchun, should esa umumiy gaplar uchun ham ishlatiladi.

“You’d better ...” qo‘pol eshitilmaydimi?

Do‘stlar orasida — yo‘q, bu oddiy. Lekin rahbaringizga yoki notanish kishiga You’d better ... deyish buyruq yoki tahdid kabi eshitilishi mumkin. Bunday hollarda yumshoqroq shakllarni tanlang: I think you should ..., It might be a good idea to .... O‘zingiz haqida esa I’d better — mutlaqo neytral: I’d better go.

Nega “It’s time we went”, “It’s time we go” emas?

Ingliz tilida ba’zi tuzilmalardan keyin o‘tgan zamon “real bo‘lmagan, hali sodir bo‘lmagan” narsani bildiradi (If I had ..., I wish I knew ... — Unit 38–41). It’s time + ega + V2 ham shu oilaga kiradi: “biz allaqachon ketishimiz kerak edi, lekin hali ketmadik”. Shuning uchun fe’l o‘tgan zamonda.

“It’s time to go” va “It’s time we went” — farqi bormi?

It’s time to go — neytral: “ketish vaqti bo‘ldi”. It’s time we went — biroz kechikkanlik yoki norozilik ohangi bor: “biz allaqachon ketishimiz kerak edi”. Ikkinchisi ko‘pincha tanqid uchun ishlatiladi: It’s time you found a job.

It’s about time va It’s high time — qaysi biri kuchliroq?

Ikkalasi ham oddiy It’s time dan kuchliroq va norozilikni ifodalaydi. It’s high time odatda eng kuchli va biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi; It’s about time kundalik nutqda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi. Ba’zan About time! qisqa javob sifatida ham aytiladi: “Nihoyat! Qachondan beri kutyapmiz!”

“It’s time + was” yoki “were”?

Bu yerda oddiy past simple ishlatiladi, shuning uchun ega bilan moslashadi: birlikda was — It’s time he was in bed; ko‘plikda were — It’s time they were in bed.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. ’d better yoki ’d better not + qavs ichidagi fe’l bilan to‘ldiring.

  1. The shop closes in five minutes. We (hurry).
  2. You’ve got an exam tomorrow. You (stay) up late tonight.
  3. My shoes are dirty and I’ve got an interview. I (clean) them.
  4. The soup is very hot. You (eat) it yet.
  5. Kamola has a sore throat. She (drink) cold water.
  6. I promised to call my mother. I (do) it now before I forget.

2-mashq. should yoki ’d better? Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ikkalasini yozing.

  1. Doctors say that we eat less salt.
  2. The taxi is waiting outside. We hurry or the driver will leave.
  3. Bukhara is a beautiful city. You visit it one day.
  4. Children always respect older people.

3-mashq. It’s time + ega + o‘tgan zamon bilan gap tuzing.

  1. You’re 25 and you still live with your parents. (you / find your own flat) → It’s time
  2. Your phone is five years old and very slow. (you / get a new one) → It’s time
  3. The traffic in the city is terrible. (the government / do something) → It’s time
  4. Our car is twenty years old and keeps breaking down. (we / buy a new one) → It’s time
  5. Aziz has been talking about learning English for years. (he / start) → It’s about time

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va tuzating.

  1. You’d better to call the doctor. →
  2. We’d better don’t wake the baby. →
  3. It’s time you go to bed. →
  4. It’s time for us leave. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Yaxshisi, taksi chaqiray, bo‘lmasa poyezdga kechikaman. →
  2. Yaxshisi, unga hech narsa demang. →
  3. Sening ish topadigan vaqting allaqachon keldi. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • had better (’d better) + fe’l = yaxshisi ..., bo‘lmasa muammo. “to” yo‘q!
  • Inkor: ’d better not + fe’l (hadn’t better ❌, ’d better don’t ❌).
  • had better — ma’no hozir / kelajak; faqat aniq vaziyat uchun. Umumiy maslahat — should.
  • It’s time to ... / It’s time for somebody to ... — neytral.
  • It’s time + ega + V2 = allaqachon qilinishi kerak edi (tanqid): It’s time we went.
  • It’s about time / It’s high time — yanada kuchli norozilik.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 33 (should), Unit 31 (have to va must), Unit 38 (If I did ... — o‘tgan zamon “xayoliy” ma’noda), Unit 41 (wish).
