Present continuous and present simple 1 (I am doing and I do)

Present continuous va present simple — 1
Present and past · Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Present continuous (I am doing) va present simple (I do) o‘rtasidagi asosiy farqni.
  • Qachon “hozir / shu kunlarda” (continuous), qachon “umuman / doim / odatda” (simple) ishlatilishini.
  • Vaqtinchalik va doimiy holatlar farqini: I’m living with friends ↔ I live in Tashkent.
  • I always do va I’m always doing — bir xil emas! Ikkinchisi ko‘pincha norozilikni bildiradi.

Ikkala zamon ham “hozirgi zamon”, lekin ular har xil savolga javob beradi. Continuous — “Hozir, shu atrofdagi vaqtda nima bo‘lyapti?” Simple — “Umuman, odatda, doim nima bo‘ladi?”

Xushxabar: o‘zbek tilida ham xuddi shunday farq bor! -yapti (o‘qiyapti, yog‘yapti) ≈ present continuous; -adi (o‘qiydi, yog‘adi) ≈ present simple. Qor yog‘yapti = It’s snowing; Qishda qor yog‘adi = It snows in winter. Agar o‘zbekchada qaysi shaklni ishlatishni sezsangiz, ko‘pincha inglizchada ham to‘g‘ri tanlaysiz.

Present continuous va present simple: taqqoslash Compare: I am doing and I do

Present continuous (I am doing)

Gapirayotgan paytda yoki shu atrofdagi vaqtda bo‘layotgan ish. Harakat tugamagan.

am / is / are + -ing

Present simple (I do)

Umuman olingan narsalar yoki takrorlanib turadigan ishlar.

I do / he does

1) Hozir bo‘layotgan ish ↔ umumiy holat

Look! It’s snowing.
Qara! Qor yog‘yapti.		It snows a lot in the mountains in winter.
Qishda tog‘larda qor ko‘p yog‘adi.
Listen! Somebody is playing the piano.
Eshit! Kimdir pianino chalyapti.		My sister plays the piano very well.
Singlim pianinoni juda yaxshi chaladi.
Why are you speaking so quietly? I can’t hear you.
Nega bunchalik sekin gapiryapsiz? Eshitmayapman.		My grandmother speaks Uzbek, Tajik and Russian.
Buvim o‘zbek, tojik va rus tillarida gapiradi.
Let’s stop for a break. I’m getting tired.
Biroz dam olaylik. Charchayapman.		I always get tired after long journeys.
Uzoq safarlardan keyin doim charchayman.
“What are you doing?” “I’m cooking dinner.”
— Nima qilyapsan? — Kechki ovqat tayyorlayapman.		“What do you do?” “I’m a chef.”
— Kasbingiz nima? — Oshpazman.
Dilnoza is learning Korean because she wants to work in Seoul.
Dilnoza koreys tilini o‘rganyapti, chunki Seulda ishlamoqchi.		Children usually learn languages more easily than adults.
Bolalar odatda tillarni kattalarga qaraganda osonroq o‘rganadi.

2) O‘zgarish jarayoni ↔ muntazam takrorlanadigan fakt

Ikkala gap ham o‘zgarish haqida bo‘lishi mumkin. Farqi: continuous — “o‘zgarish hozir sodir bo‘lyapti, davom etyapti”; simple — “bu har gal / har yili takrorlanadigan fakt”:

  • The river is rising fast after the heavy rain.Kuchli yomg‘irdan keyin daryo suvi tez ko‘tarilyapti. (hozir sodir bo‘lyapti)
  • Every spring the river rises when the snow in the mountains melts.Har bahorda tog‘lardagi qor eriganda daryo suvi ko‘tariladi. (har yili takrorlanadi)

3) Vaqtinchalik ↔ doimiy

Present continuous vaqtinchalik (temporary) holatlar uchun, present simple esa doimiy (permanent) holatlar uchun ishlatiladi:

  • I’m living with my aunt for a few months. My own flat isn’t ready yet.Bir necha oy xolamnikida yashab turibman. O‘z kvartiram hali tayyor emas. (vaqtincha)
  • My parents live in Namangan. They have their own house there.Ota-onam Namanganda yashaydi. Ularning o‘sha yerda o‘z uyi bor. (doimiy)
  • Sardor is working in a café this summer.Sardor bu yoz kafeda ishlayapti. (faqat shu yoz)
  • His father works in a bank.Uning otasi bankda ishlaydi. (bu uning doimiy ishi)
  • A: You’re eating a lot of fruit these days.
    B: Yes, I’m trying to be healthier.    — Shu kunlarda ko‘p meva yeyapsan. — Ha, sog‘lomroq bo‘lishga harakat qilyapman.
  • Aziz is a very good student. He studies every evening.Aziz juda yaxshi talaba. U har kuni kechqurun dars qiladi.

Signal so‘zlar — yordamchi, lekin hal qiluvchi emas

Ko‘pincha continuous bilanKo‘pincha simple bilan
now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, this year, currently, Look!, Listen!always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays, at weekends, once a week, in general

So‘zga emas, ma’noga qarang. Masalan: You’re always losing your keys! — continuous (B bo‘lim). I usually walk to work, but this week I’m taking the bus. — bitta gapda ikkala zamon.

Ba’zi fe’llar (know, like, want, understand ...) hozirgi ma’noda ham continuous shaklda kelmaydi: I know the answer now. Bu — Unit 4 mavzusi.

I always do va I’m always doing Always + present simple / present continuous

I always do something — “men buni har safar qilaman” degani. Bu oddiy odat, hech qanday his-tuyg‘u yo‘q:

  • I always walk to work. It takes twenty minutes.Ishga doim piyoda boraman. Yigirma daqiqa ketadi. (I’m always walking ❌)
  • My grandfather always reads the newspaper after breakfast.Bobom doim nonushtadan keyin gazeta o‘qiydi.

I’m always doing something — boshqa ma’no: “men buni juda tez-tez qilaman — ehtimol, haddan tashqari ko‘p, odatdagidan ko‘p”. Ko‘pincha bu norozilik, asabiylashishni bildiradi:

  • I left my umbrella on the bus again. I’m always forgetting things!Yana soyabonimni avtobusda qoldirib ketdim. Doim nimanidir esdan chiqaraman-a! (juda tez-tez, bu meni bezor qiladi)
  • You’re always looking at your phone. Put it down and talk to us!Doim telefoningga qarab o‘tirasan. Uni qo‘y-da, biz bilan gaplash! (= juda ko‘p qaraysan)
  • My neighbour is always borrowing things from us.Qo‘shnimiz doim bizdan nimadir qarz olib turadi. (bu bizga yoqmaydi)
  • Jasur is always interrupting people when they’re speaking.Jasur doim odamlarning gapini bo‘ladi. (bu yomon odat)

always + present simple

Har safar, odat. Neytral.

  • She always calls her mother on Sundays.
  • U yakshanba kunlari onasiga doim qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi.

always + present continuous

Juda tez-tez, haddan ortiq. Ko‘pincha norozilik.

  • She’s always calling me late at night!
  • U menga doim yarim tunda qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi! (bezor bo‘ldim)

Bu ma’noda always o‘rniga constantly yoki forever ham ishlatiladi: He’s constantly complaining. Ba’zan bu shakl norozilik emas, hayratni ham bildiradi: Malika is always helping other people. (U doim boshqalarga yordam beradi.) Ma’noni kontekst belgilaydi.

Chalkashtirmang: He is always late. — bu continuous emas! Bu be fe’lining present simple shakli (is) + always + sifat (late). Continuous bo‘lishi uchun -ing li fe’l kerak: He is always arriving late.

Bitta matnda ikkala zamon Both tenses together in real speech

Hayotda bu ikki zamon ko‘pincha yonma-yon keladi: biz odatimiz (simple) va hozirgi vaziyatimiz (continuous) haqida birga gapiramiz. Jasurning do‘stiga yozgan xabarini o‘qing va har bir fe’l nega shu shaklda ekanini tushunishga harakat qiling:

  • Hi Bekzod! I usually work in our Tashkent office, but this month I’m working in Samarkand. The company is opening a new branch here. I’m staying in a small hotel near the Registan. Every morning I have breakfast on the roof and look at the old city. Right now I’m sitting in a café and waiting for a colleague. He’s always late! He’s always telling me about the traffic, but the traffic here isn’t bad at all.Salom, Bekzod! Odatda Toshkentdagi ofisimizda ishlayman, lekin bu oy Samarqandda ishlayapman. Kompaniya bu yerda yangi filial ochyapti. Registon yaqinidagi kichkina mehmonxonada turibman. Har kuni ertalab tomda nonushta qilaman va eski shaharga qarayman. Hozir kafeda o‘tirib, hamkasbimni kutyapman. U doim kechikadi! Doim menga tirbandlik haqida gapiraveradi, lekin bu yerda tirbandlik umuman yomon emas.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Look! It snows.Look! It’s snowing.“Look!” — ayni paytda bo‘layotgan narsa → continuous.
She is usually going to bed at 11.She usually goes to bed at 11.Odat (usually) → present simple.
Water is freezing at 0 degrees Celsius.Water freezes at 0 degrees Celsius.Umumiy haqiqat → present simple.
What do you do now? (= hozir nima qilyapsan?)What are you doing now?“What do you do?” — kasb haqida savol. Hozirgi harakat haqida — “What are you doing?”
Why do you wear a coat today? It’s hot!Why are you wearing a coat today? It’s hot!Gap bugungi, hozirgi vaziyat haqida (odat haqida emas) → continuous.
I’m always go to work by bus.I always go to work by bus.“am” ortiqcha. Oddiy odat — present simple; “I’m always going” esa “juda ko‘p, norozilik” ma’nosini beradi.
My aunt is living in Bukhara. It’s her home town and she’ll never leave it.My aunt lives in Bukhara.Doimiy holat → present simple. “are living” vaqtinchalikni bildiradi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi zamonni tanlashni qanday bilaman?

O‘zingizga savol bering: “Bu ish hozir / shu kunlarda / vaqtincha bo‘lyaptimi?” — ha bo‘lsa, continuous. “Bu umuman, odatda, doim shundaymi?” — ha bo‘lsa, simple. Yana bir yo‘l: o‘zbekchaga tarjima qilib ko‘ring: -yapti → continuous, -adi → simple.

“I live in Tashkent” va “I’m living in Tashkent” — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, ikkalasi ham grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri, lekin ma’nosi farq qiladi. I live — bu sizning doimiy uyingiz. I’m living — vaqtincha, masalan o‘qish yoki ish uchun bir muddat: I’m living in Tashkent while I’m at university.

Bir necha yildan beri bankda ishlayman. “I’m working in a bank” desam bo‘ladimi?

Bu xato emas, lekin tinglovchi buni “vaqtincha ishlayapman” deb tushunishi mumkin. Doimiy ish uchun tabiiyroq variant: I work in a bank. Qancha vaqtdan beri ekanini aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — present perfect kerak: I’ve worked / I’ve been working in a bank for five years (Unit 9–11).

“always + -ing” har doim salbiy ma’noni bildiradimi?

Ko‘pincha — ha, norozilik yoki asabiylashishni bildiradi. Lekin ba’zan shunchaki “juda tez-tez” degan ma’noni, hatto maqtovni ham beradi: She’s always smiling. (U doim jilmayib yuradi.) Kontekstga va ohangga qarang.

“What do you do?” va “What are you doing?” — farqi nima?

What do you do? — “Kasbingiz nima? Nima ish qilasiz?” What are you doing? — “Hozir nima qilyapsiz?” Birinchisiga I’m a teacher, ikkinchisiga I’m reading deb javob beriladi.

“these days” bilan qaysi zamon ishlatiladi?

Ikkalasi ham bo‘lishi mumkin. Vaqtinchalik, o‘zgarib turgan holat haqida — continuous: I’m working from home these days. Hozirgi davrdagi umumiy odat yoki haqiqat haqida — simple ham ishlatiladi: These days most people pay by card.

“It’s getting dark” va “It gets dark early in winter” — nega har xil?

It’s getting dark — ayni paytda qorong‘i tushyapti (jarayon hozir). It gets dark early in winter — qishda har kuni shunday bo‘ladi (umumiy fakt). Bitta fe’l, ikki xil vaziyat.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. Hurry up! The taxi (waits / is waiting) outside.
  2. (Do you have / Are you having) breakfast every morning?
  3. A dentist (looks after / is looking after) people’s teeth.
  4. Be quiet, please. The baby (sleeps / is sleeping).
  5. The sun (rises / is rising) in the east.
  6. “What (do you do / are you doing)?” “I’m a doctor.”
  7. “What (do you do / are you doing)?” “I’m looking for my glasses.”
  8. Look at the thermometer! The temperature (falls / is falling) fast.
  9. It (doesn’t rain / isn’t raining) very often in the Kyzylkum Desert.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni present continuous yoki present simple shaklida yozing.

  1. Look! That man (try) to open your car.
  2. Bekzod (not / usually / drive) to work. He takes the metro.
  3. “ (you / play) chess?” “Yes, my grandfather taught me.”
  4. I (stay) with my cousins this month. My flat isn’t ready yet.
  5. It (get) dark. Let’s go home.
  6. My parents (live) in Namangan. They have a house there.
  7. “Where’s Dilnoza?” “She (have) a shower.”
  8. I usually walk to work, but this week I (take) the bus.

3-mashq. always bilan present simple yoki present continuous ishlating.

  1. Oops, I’ve dropped my phone again! I (always / drop) it.
  2. We (always / have) dinner together on Fridays. It’s a family tradition.
  3. Please stop it! You (always / interrupt) me when I’m speaking!
  4. My grandfather (always / read) the newspaper after breakfast.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Present continuous — hozir yoki shu atrofdagi vaqtda bo‘layotgan, tugamagan ish: It’s raining. I’m learning Korean.
  • Present simple — umumiy haqiqat, odat, takroriy ish: It rains a lot in April. Children learn quickly.
  • Vaqtinchalik → continuous (I’m living with my aunt); doimiy → simple (My parents live in Namangan).
  • What do you do? = kasbingiz nima; What are you doing? = hozir nima qilyapsiz.
  • I always do = har safar (odat). I’m always doing = juda tez-tez, ko‘pincha norozilik.
  • Signal so‘zlar yordam beradi, lekin hal qiluvchi — ma’no.
