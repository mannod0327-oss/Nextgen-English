May and might 2

May va might — 2
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • may / might bilan kelajakdagi ehtimoliy ish va voqealar haqida gapirishni: I might go to Samarkand next week.
  • will (aniq) va may / might (balki) farqini.
  • Real bo‘lmagan vaziyatlarda nega faqat might ishlatilishini: If I were rich, I might ...
  • may / might be doing shaklini va might as well iborasini.

Unit 29 da may / might hozir yoki o‘tmishdagi holat haqida taxmin edi. Bu darsda esa kelajak haqida: siz rejangizni hali aniq bilmaysiz yoki biror voqea bo‘lishi mumkin deb o‘ylaysiz. may / might + fe’l = “balki ... qilarman / bo‘lar”.

O‘zbek tilida kelajakdagi ehtimol “balki ...-arman”, “... -ishim mumkin”, “ehtimol” bilan beriladi: Balki Samarqandga borarman = I might go to Samarkand. O‘zbek o‘quvchilari ko‘pincha ❌ Maybe I go deydi — bu noto‘g‘ri. To‘g‘risi: I might go yoki Maybe I’ll go.

Kelajakdagi ehtimol: may / might + fe’l Possible future actions and events

Kelajakda biror ish bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin aniq emas — shunda may yoki might ishlatiladi:

Ega+may / might+(not)+fe’l+(kelasi vaqt)
  • I’m not sure what to do on Saturday. I might go to Chorsu bazaar with Mum. (= perhaps I will go)Shanba kuni nima qilishimni hali bilmayman. Balki oyim bilan Chorsu bozoriga borarman.
  • Charge your phone before you leave. You may need it on the way.Chiqishdan oldin telefoningizni quvvatlang. Yo‘lda kerak bo‘lib qolishi mumkin.
  • The traffic is terrible today. We might be late for the concert.Bugun tirbandlik dahshat. Konsertga kechikishimiz mumkin.
  • The museum is very popular. We might have to queue for a while.Muzey juda mashhur. Biroz navbatda turishimizga to‘g‘ri kelishi mumkin.
  • Sardor may get a new job next month.Sardor kelasi oy yangi ishga kirishi mumkin.

Inkor shakli — may not / might not:

  • Malika might not come to the party. She has an exam the next morning.Malika ziyofatga kelmasligi mumkin. Ertasiga ertalab uning imtihoni bor.
  • There may not be enough chairs for everyone, so bring a few from home.Hammaga stul yetmasligi mumkin, shuning uchun uydan bir nechtasini olib keling.

will yoki may / might?

  • I’ll be late this evening. (aniq)Bugun kechqurun kechikaman.
  • I may / might be late this evening. (balki)Bugun kechqurun kechikishim mumkin.

will bilan might ni birga ishlatmang: I will might go ❌. Ikkita modal fe’l ketma-ket kelmaydi. “Balki” ma’nosi uchun bitta might yetarli: I might go ✅. Yoki: Maybe I’ll go ✅ / Perhaps I’ll go ✅.

Real bo‘lmagan vaziyat: faqat might Only might for unreal situations

Odatda may ham, might ham to‘g‘ri. Lekin vaziyat real bo‘lmasa (xayoliy, “agar ... bo‘lganda edi” turidagi), faqat might ishlatiladi:

  • If I had more free time, I might learn Chinese. (not may learn)Bo‘sh vaqtim ko‘proq bo‘lganda, balki xitoy tilini o‘rganardim.
  • If Aziz lived nearer, he might visit us more often.Aziz yaqinroqda yashaganida, balki bizni tez-tez ko‘rgani kelardi.
  • If they paid us more, we might work on Saturdays too.Bizga ko‘proq maosh to‘lashganda, balki shanba kunlari ham ishlardik.

Bu gaplarda vaziyat real emas: menda bo‘sh vaqt ko‘p emas, Aziz yaqinda yashamaydi. Shuning uchun may to‘g‘ri kelmaydi.

Real yoki xayoliy shart?
VaziyatMisolmay?might?
Real, mumkin bo‘lgan (if + present)If it rains tomorrow, the match may / might be cancelled.✅✅
Xayoliy (if + past, Unit 38–39)If I won the lottery, I might travel round the world.❌✅

Qoida: “Agar ... bo‘lganda edi” turidagi xayoliy gaplarda might = would perhaps (“balki ... qilardim”). may bu ma’noni bermaydi.

may / might be doing: davomli shakl may / might be -ing

1) Kelajakdagi ma’lum paytda davom etayotgan bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lgan ish

  • Please don’t come round at seven tonight. I might be helping my little brother with his homework then.Iltimos, bugun soat yettida kelmang. O‘sha paytda ukamga uy vazifasida yordam berayotgan bo‘lishim mumkin.
  • Try to call Dilnoza after five. She may be working until then.Dilnozaga beshdan keyin qo‘ng‘iroq qilib ko‘ring. O‘sha vaqtgacha ishlayotgan bo‘lishi mumkin.

Solishtiring: I’ll be helping my brother at seven — aniq (Unit 24); I might be helping my brother at seven — balki.

2) Ehtimoliy rejalar

Kelajakdagi reja haqida, lekin reja hali aniq bo‘lmasa, may / might be + -ing ham ishlatiladi:

Aniq reja

  • I’m going to Bukhara next week. (Unit 19)
  • Kelasi hafta Buxoroga ketyapman.

Ehtimoliy reja

  • I might be going to Bukhara next week.
  • Kelasi hafta Buxoroga borishim mumkin.
  • Kamola might be moving to Tashkent later this year.Kamola shu yil oxirroq Toshkentga ko‘chib o‘tishi mumkin.
  • We may be visiting our grandparents in Fergana at the weekend.Dam olish kunlari Farg‘onaga buvi-bobomiznikiga borishimiz mumkin.

Bu ma’noda oddiy shakl ham to‘g‘ri: I might go to Bukhara next week = I might be going to Bukhara next week. Davomli shakl rejaning “tayyorlanayotganini” biroz ko‘proq his qildiradi.

might as well / may as well = there is no better alternative

We might as well do something = “boshqa yaxshiroq variant yo‘q, shuning uchun buni qilaylik”; “qilmaslikka hech qanday sabab yo‘q”. Bu juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan og‘zaki ibora. may as well ham to‘g‘ri, lekin might as well ko‘proq uchraydi.

Ega+might as well / may as well+fe’l
  • “The shop doesn’t open for another hour.” “Well, we might as well have a coffee while we wait.”— Do‘kon yana bir soatdan keyin ochiladi. — Unda kutib turgunimizcha qahva ichib olaylik (boshqa qiladigan ishimiz yo‘q).
  • A day ticket for the museum costs the same as a two-hour ticket. We may as well stay all day.Muzeyga bir kunlik chipta ikki soatlik chipta bilan bir xil narxda. Kun bo‘yi qolaqolaylik.
  • Nobody uses this old bike any more. You might as well take it.Bu eski velosipedni endi hech kim minmaydi. Olib ketaqoling.
  • “The lesson has been cancelled.” “Then we might as well go home.”— Dars bekor qilindi. — Unda uyga ketaveraylik.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no ko‘pincha “-a qolaylik”, “-averaylik”, “yaxshisi ...” bilan beriladi: ketaveraylik, olib ketaqoling, kun bo‘yi qolaqolaylik. Inglizchada esa: might as well + fe’l.

might as well “eng yaxshi g‘oya” degan ma’noni bermaydi. U biroz befarq, “mayli-da” ohangida aytiladi: “Boshqa ilojimiz yo‘q, shunday qilaqolaylik”. Kuchli maslahat uchun should yoki had better (Unit 33, 35) ishlatiladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Maybe I go to Bukhara next week.I might go to Bukhara next week. / Maybe I’ll go to Bukhara next week.Kelajak haqida “maybe” bilan oddiy hozirgi zamon emas, will kerak. Yoki shunchaki might ishlating.
I will might be late.I might be late.Ikkita modal fe’l (will + might) birga kelmaydi.
I may to visit my aunt tomorrow.I may visit my aunt tomorrow.may / might dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
It mights rain this afternoon.It might rain this afternoon.Modal fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi.
If I were rich, I may buy a house by the sea.If I were rich, I might buy a house by the sea.Xayoliy (real bo‘lmagan) vaziyatda faqat might.
I might going to the party.I might go to the party. / I might be going to the party.might + fe’l yoki might + be + -ing. “might going” degan shakl yo‘q.
We might as well to wait here.We might as well wait here.might as well dan keyin fe’l “to”siz keladi.
I might can come tomorrow.I might be able to come tomorrow.Ikki modal (might + can) birga kelmaydi. can o‘rniga “be able to” ishlatiladi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I will go”, “I’m going to go” va “I might go” ning farqi nima?

I’ll go — qaror yoki aniq bashorat. I’m going to go — oldindan o‘ylangan niyat (Unit 20). I might go — hali qaror qilinmagan, faqat ehtimol. “Balki” so‘zini qo‘shsangiz o‘zbekcha gap ma’noli bo‘lsa — might ni tanlang.

Nega “If I had money, I may buy ...” noto‘g‘ri?

Chunki if + past (if I had, if I were) xayoliy vaziyatni bildiradi (Unit 38). Bunday gaplarning ikkinchi qismida would / could / might ishlatiladi. may esa real, haqiqatda mumkin bo‘lgan narsalar uchun. Shuning uchun: If I had money, I might buy ...

“I might go” va “I might be going” — qaysi biri yaxshiroq?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ma’nosi deyarli bir xil: I might go to Bukhara next week = I might be going to Bukhara next week. Davomli shakl rejaga ishora qiladi. Lekin “ma’lum paytda jarayonda bo‘lish” ma’nosida faqat davomli shakl mos: Don’t come at 7 — I might be helping my brother (shu paytda ukamga yordam berayotgan bo‘lishim mumkin).

“might as well” va “should” ning farqi nima?

should — maslahat: “bu yaxshi g‘oya”. might as well — “boshqa yaxshiroq variant yo‘q, nima farqi bor, qilaqolaylik”. Masalan: You should see that film — it’s brilliant (Albatta ko‘ring, zo‘r film). There’s nothing else on TV. We might as well watch this film (Televizorda boshqa narsa yo‘q, shuni ko‘raqolaylik).

may va might — qaysi biri rasmiyroq?

may biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi va yozma matnlarda ko‘proq uchraydi: Flights may be delayed due to bad weather (aeroport e’loni). Og‘zaki nutqda ko‘pincha might ishlatiladi. Ma’nosi esa (kelajakdagi ehtimol) deyarli bir xil.

“might have to” to‘g‘rimi? Ikkita modal-ku?

To‘g‘ri! have to haqiqiy modal fe’l emas (Unit 31), shuning uchun might / may dan keyin kelishi mumkin: We might have to wait (Kutishimizga to‘g‘ri kelishi mumkin). Xuddi shunday: I may not be able to come (Kela olmasligim mumkin) — bu yerda can o‘rniga be able to ishlatiladi, chunki may can ❌ deyilmaydi.

“might as well have done” degan shakl bormi?

Ha, o‘tmish uchun: The film was so boring. We might as well have stayed at home. (Film shunchalik zerikarli ediki, uyda qolganimiz ham bo‘lardi — natija baribir bir xil.) Bu o‘tgan ish foydasiz bo‘lganini bildiradi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gaplarni might bilan qayta yozing (kelajakdagi ehtimol).

  1. Perhaps I’ll go to Samarkand at the weekend. → I
  2. Perhaps it will snow tonight. → It
  3. Perhaps Malika won’t come to the meeting. → Malika
  4. Perhaps we’ll have to wait. → We
  5. Perhaps there won’t be any tickets left. → There

2-mashq. Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri: faqat might yoki may ham, might ham? Javobda to‘g‘ri variant(lar)ni yozing.

  1. If it’s sunny tomorrow, we go to the mountains.
  2. If I had a car, I drive to work.
  3. Take a jacket. It get cold in the evening.
  4. If Jasur studied harder, he pass the exam.

3-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni might be + -ing shaklida yozing.

  1. Don’t call Bekzod at nine. He (play) chess at the club then.
  2. We (move) to a new flat next month — we’re still looking.
  3. Try to visit me before six. After that I (work).

4-mashq. Har bir vaziyat uchun might as well bilan gap tuzing (qavs ichidagi so‘zlardan foydalaning).

  1. The film we wanted is sold out, but another good film starts now. (we / see that one) →
  2. You’ve already paid for the ticket. (you / go to the concert) →
  3. Nobody is using this old laptop. (you / take it) →
  4. It’s raining and there’s nothing to do outside. (we / stay in and watch a film) →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘ishi mumkin. →
  2. Balki men kelasi yil Germaniyaga borarman. →
  3. Ular konsertga kechikishi mumkin. →
  4. Boyroq bo‘lganimda, balki Samarqandda uy sotib olardim. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • may / might + fe’l = kelajakda “balki ...”: I might go to Samarkand.
  • will — aniq; may / might — ehtimol. Ikkalasini birga ishlatmang (will might ❌).
  • Xayoliy vaziyatda faqat might: If I were rich, I might ...
  • may / might be + -ing: kelajakdagi paytda jarayon yoki ehtimoliy reja.
  • might as well / may as well + fe’l = “boshqa yaxshiroq variant yo‘q, shuni qilaqolaylik”.
  • Maybe I go ❌ → I might go / Maybe I’ll go ✅.
