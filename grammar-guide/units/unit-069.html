Countable and uncountable 1

Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar — 1
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Countable (sanaladigan) va uncountable (sanalmaydigan) otlar nima ekanini va ularni qanday ajratishni.
  • Sanalmaydigan otlar bilan a / an va ko‘plik -s ishlatilmasligini.
  • O‘zbek tilida sanaladigan, lekin ingliz tilida sanalmaydigan otlarni: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, money, work ...
  • Sanalmaydigan otni qanday “sanash” mumkinligini: a piece of advice, a bottle of water, a loaf of bread.
  • Fe’l, olmosh va much / many bilan moslashuvni.

Ingliz tilidagi har bir otni ikki guruhga ajratish mumkin. Countable otlarni birma-bir sanash mumkin: an apple, two apples, three apples. Uncountable otlar esa bo‘laklarga ajratilmaydigan “modda”, “umumiy tushuncha” sifatida qaraladi: water, rice, advice, furniture. Biz ularni sonlar bilan to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri sanamaymiz va ularda ko‘plik shakli bo‘lmaydi.

O‘zbek tilida deyarli har qanday otga -lar qo‘shish va bitta, ikkita deb sanash mumkin: maslahatlar, ma’lumotlar, mebellar, yuklar, pullar, ishlar. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar advices, informations, furnitures, luggages deb xato qilishadi. Ingliz tilida bu so‘zlar sanalmaydi. Ularni yodlash kerak (B qismdagi jadvalga qarang) va sanash kerak bo‘lsa, a piece of ... kabi iboralar ishlatiladi. Shuningdek, o‘zbekchada “bitta maslahat” deymiz, inglizchada a piece of advice.

Countable va uncountable: asosiy farq What is the difference?

Countable (C) — sanaladigan

Alohida, sanash mumkin bo‘lgan narsalar. Birlik va ko‘plik shakli bor.

  • a book — two books
  • an egg — six eggs
  • a friend — many friends
  • a coin — three coins

Uncountable (U) — sanalmaydigan

Bo‘linmas “modda” yoki umumiy tushuncha. Ko‘plik shakli yo‘q, a / an olmaydi.

  • water — a water, waters
  • rice — a rice, rices
  • advice — an advice, advices
  • furniture — a furniture

Birlik sanaladigan ot yolg‘iz kelmaydi

Sanaladigan otning birlik shakli gapda yolg‘iz turmaydi: oldida a / an, the, my, this kabi so‘z bo‘lishi shart. Ko‘plik shakli va sanalmaydigan ot esa yolg‘iz (artiklsiz) kela oladi:

Ot yolg‘iz kela oladimi?
Ot turi❌ Noto‘g‘ri✅ To‘g‘ri
Countable, birlikJasur has car. I’m looking for pharmacy.Jasur has a car. I’m looking for a pharmacy.
Countable, ko‘plik—Jasur likes cars. Pharmacies open at eight.
Uncountable—Malika drinks milk every day. There’s mud on your boots.

O‘zbek tilida artikl yo‘q: Jasurning mashinasi bor, dorixona qidiryapman. Inglizchada esa birlik sanaladigan ot oldida a / an (yoki the, my ...) albatta turadi: Jasur has a car, I’m looking for a pharmacy. Batafsil — Unit 71 va Unit 72.

Bu ro‘yxat to‘liq emas. Yangi so‘zni o‘rganganda lug‘atga qarang: [C] yoki [U] belgisi turadi. Ba’zi so‘zlar ikkala guruhda ham ishlatiladi (masalan, paper, glass, hair, time, work) — buni Unit 70 da ko‘rasiz.

Faqat “bitta maslahat”, “ikkita yangilik”, “uchta mebel” kabi o‘zbekcha sanashlarni inglizchada piece of bilan beramiz: a piece of advice, two pieces of news, three pieces of furniture.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Ot sanaladimi yoki yo‘qmi — qanday bilaman?

Uchta usul: (1) a / an va -s qo‘yib ko‘ring (a book, books ✅; a water, waters ❌); (2) How many ...? yoki How much ...? deb so‘rang; (3) inglizcha lug‘atga qarang: [C] = countable, [U] = uncountable. O‘zbekchaga qarab baho bermang — chunki o‘zbek tilida deyarli hamma narsa sanalishi mumkin.

Nega o‘zbekchada “maslahatlar” desak bo‘ladi-yu, inglizchada “advices” bo‘lmaydi?

Har bir tilda “nima sanaladigan” degan tushuncha o‘ziga xos. Ingliz tilida advice, information, furniture kabi so‘zlar “umumiy massa” sifatida qaraladi; ularni birma-bir emas, balki miqdor sifatida o‘ylaymiz. Shuning uchun sonni a piece of kabi iboralar bilan beramiz: two pieces of advice. Buning ortidagi mantiqni har doim topib bo‘lmaydi, shuning uchun bunday so‘zlarni ro‘yxatdan yodlash kerak.

“Information” ning ko‘plik ma’nosini qanday ifodalayman? O‘zbekchada “ma’lumotlar” deymiz-ku.

Inglizcha information yoki some information yetarli. Aniq narsalarni sanamoqchi bo‘lsangiz, boshqa so‘z ishlating: facts (faktlar), details (tafsilotlar), pieces of information (bir nechta ma’lumot), data (raqamli ma’lumotlar).

Pulni qanday sanayman?

money sanalmaydi: How much money do you have? Lekin pul birliklari sanaladi: a coin, three coins, a note (AmE: a bill), twenty dollars, five pounds. “Pullarim” deyish uchun ham my money ishlating, my moneys emas.

Ikkita non — “two breads” deymanmi?

Yo‘q. Standart ingliz tilida bread sanalmaydi: two loaves of bread (ikkita yaxlit non), two slices of bread (ikki bo‘lak). O‘zbek noni yassi bo‘lgani uchun ba’zan two flatbreads ham deyiladi. Breads shakli faqat “non turlari” ma’nosida uchraydi (Unit 70).

Sanalmaydigan ot hech qachon a / an yoki -s bilan kelmaydimi?

Ba’zan keladi, lekin ma’no o‘zgaradi: I’d like a coffee (= bir chashka qahva), two teas, please, a glass (stakan) va glass (shisha materiali). Bu holatlar Unit 70 da batafsil.

“a lot of” va “much” farqi nima?

a lot of ham sanaladigan ko‘plik, ham sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatiladi va darak gaplarda tabiiy: a lot of money, a lot of friends. much ko‘pincha so‘roq va inkor gaplarda: I haven’t got much money. How much time have we got? Sanaladigan ko‘plik uchun many (Unit 87).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Countable: a / an va -s bilan keladi (a book — books), How many.
  • Uncountable: a / an ❌, -s ❌, birlik fe’l, How much, it (water, rice, advice, furniture).
  • Inglizcha sanalmaydigan, o‘zbekcha sanaladigan: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, money, work, homework, progress, equipment ...
  • news — -s bilan tugaydi, lekin birlik: The news is good.
  • Sanash uchun: a piece of advice / news / furniture; a loaf of bread; a bottle of water.
  • work sanalmaydi (bitta ish = a job); travel sanalmaydi (bitta safar = a trip, a journey).
  • Shubha bo‘lsa lug‘atda [C] / [U] ni tekshiring.
