55

Verb (+ object) + to ... (I want you to ... etc.)

Fe’l (+ to‘ldiruvchi) + to ... (I want you to ...)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Fe’l + kim? + to + fe’l qolipini: want you to come, tell him to wait, advise us to go, expect them to arrive.
  • I want to go va I want you to go orasidagi farqni: ikkinchi ishni kim bajaradi?
  • make va let dan keyin to ishlatilmasligini: made me wait, let me go.
  • advise, allow, permit fe’llarini kim? bilan va kim?siz ishlatishni.
  • Majhul nisbatda: I was told to wait, We were made to wait.
  • Inkor shaklini: He told me not to be late.

Unit 54 da ikkala ishni ham bir kishi bajarardi: I want to go (xohlovchi ham, boruvchi ham — men). Bu darsda esa ikkinchi ishni boshqa kishi bajaradi: I want you to go (xohlovchi — men, boruvchi — siz). Shu boshqa kishi fe’ldan keyin turadi va to‘ldiruvchi (object) hisoblanadi: me, you, him, her, us, them yoki ot (Aziz, the teacher).

O‘zbek tilida “Men sizning kelishingizni xohlayman” yoki “Ustoz bizga uy vazifasini qilishni aytdi” deymiz. Inglizcha qolip butunlay boshqacha: I want you to come, The teacher told us to do the homework. Ikki tipik xato: ❌ I want that you come (want dan keyin that kelmaydi) va ❌ told to us (tell dan keyin kim? to siz turadi: told us).

Fe’l + kim? + to + fe’l Verb + object + to ...

Quyidagi fe’llardan keyin avval kim? (to‘ldiruvchi), undan keyin to + fe’l keladi:

Ega+want / tell / advise / expect ...+kim? (me / him / Aziz)+to+fe’l (V1)

Bir kishi yoki ikki kishi?

1 kishi: want to + fe’l

Xohlovchi va bajaruvchi bir xil.

  • I want to go. (men xohlayman, men boraman)
  • Aziz expects to win. (Aziz g‘alaba qozonishini kutadi)

2 kishi: want kim? to + fe’l

Xohlovchi bir kishi, bajaruvchi boshqa kishi.

  • I want you to go. (men xohlayman, siz borasiz)
  • Aziz expects his team to win. (jamoasi g‘alaba qozonishini kutadi)
  • I want you to come to my birthday party.Tug‘ilgan kunimga kelishingizni xohlayman.
  • Do you want me to help you with the bags?Sumkalarni ko‘tarishda sizga yordam berishimni xohlaysizmi?
  • I’d like you to meet my sister.Singlim bilan tanishishingizni istardim.
  • I don’t want you to worry.Xavotir olishingizni xohlamayman.

Fe’llar guruhlari

🌟 Istak va kutish

  • want — xohlamoq: I want you to come.
  • would like / would love — xohlar edim: I’d like you to stay.
  • expect — kutmoq: We expect them to arrive at eight.
  • need — kerak bo‘lmoq: I need you to sign here.

📣 Buyruq, so‘rov, ogohlantirish

  • tell — aytmoq, buyurmoq: She told me to wait.
  • ask — so‘ramoq: Aziz asked me to help.
  • order — buyurmoq: The officer ordered them to stop.
  • warn — ogohlantirmoq: He warned us not to swim in the river.
  • remind — eslatmoq: Remind me to water the plants.
  • invite — taklif qilmoq: They invited us to stay for dinner.

💡 Maslahat va ruxsat

  • advise — maslahat bermoq: He advised us to book early.
  • allow — ruxsat bermoq: They allow students to use the library.
  • permit — ruxsat bermoq (rasmiy): The rules permit visitors to take photos.
  • encourage — rag‘batlantirmoq: Her friends encouraged her to apply.

🎯 Ta’sir qilish, o‘rgatish

  • persuade — ko‘ndirmoq: I persuaded Dad to lend me his car.
  • force — majburlamoq: They forced him to leave.
  • teach — o‘rgatmoq: My uncle taught me to swim.
  • get — (ko‘ndirib) qildirmoq: I got my brother to fix my bike.
  • enable — imkon bermoq: This app enables users to send large files.

Qoida: Kim? o‘rnida to‘ldiruvchi olmosh turadi: me, you, him, her, it, us, them. I want him to come ✅ — I want he to come ❌.

want va hope bilan adashmang: I want you to come ✅, lekin I hope you to come ❌ — hope dan keyin kim? + to kelmaydi: I hope (that) you’ll come ✅. Shuningdek suggest dan keyin ham kim? + to kelmaydi: She suggested me to go ❌ (Unit 53).

make va let: to siz make and let + object + verb (no to)

Bu ikki fe’ldan keyin kim? va to siz fe’l (V1) keladi:

Ega+make / let+kim?+fe’l (V1, to siz)

make — majbur qilmoq

  • The teacher made us repeat the exercise.
  • The film made me cry.
  • What made you change your mind?

let — ruxsat bermoq, qo‘ymoq

  • Her teacher let her leave early.
  • Let me help you.
  • Don’t let him drive in this weather.
  • The boss made everyone work on Saturday.Boshliq hammani shanba kuni ishlashga majbur qildi.
  • My parents never let me stay out after ten.Ota-onam hech qachon soat o‘ndan keyin tashqarida qolishimga ruxsat bermaydi.
  • Don’t make me laugh!Meni kuldirmang!
  • Let him speak. He hasn’t finished.Qo‘ying, gapirsin. U hali gapini tugatmadi.

O‘zbekcha “majbur qildi”, “qo‘ydi”, “kuldirdi” inglizchada bitta qisqa qolip bilan beriladi. make me to go ❌, let me to go ❌ — to qo‘shilsa, gap noto‘g‘ri.

advise, allow, permit: kim? bilan yoki -ing advise / allow / permit + object + to or + -ing

Bu fe’llarda (forbid ham shunday) ikki qolip bor. Kim? bor bo‘lsa — to + fe’l. Kim? yo‘q bo‘lsa — -ing:

Kim? bor — to; kim? yo‘q — -ing
Fe’lKim? bilan: + toKim?siz: + -ing
adviseI’d advise you to take a taxi.I’d advise taking a taxi.

Kim?ni tushirib, to ni qoldirmang: They don’t allow to smoke here ❌. To‘g‘ri: They don’t allow smoking here. yoki They don’t allow people to smoke here. Xuddi shunday: I advise to take a taxi ❌ → I advise taking a taxi / I advise you to take a taxi ✅.

  • The hotel doesn’t allow guests to bring pets.Mehmonxona mehmonlarning uy hayvonlarini olib kelishiga ruxsat bermaydi.
  • The hotel doesn’t allow bringing pets.Mehmonxonada uy hayvonlarini olib kelish mumkin emas.
  • I wouldn’t advise you to go alone.Yolg‘iz borishingizni maslahat bermayman.
  • I wouldn’t advise going alone.Yolg‘iz borishni maslahat bermayman.

Advice va advise: advice [ədˈvaɪs] — ot, sanalmaydi (some advice, an advice ❌); advise [ədˈvaɪz] — fe’l. Imlo: advice / advise.

Majhul nisbat va ko‘chirma gap Passive and reported commands

Fe’l + kim? + to qolipining ko‘pchiligi majhul nisbatda ham ishlatiladi. Aktivdagi kim? majhul nisbatda ega bo‘lib qoladi, to + fe’l esa o‘z joyida turadi:

Aktiv → passiv
AktivPassiv

  • Visitors are not allowed to take photos in the museum.Muzeyda tashrifchilarning suratga olishi mumkin emas.
  • You are expected to wear a uniform.Sizdan forma kiyish talab qilinadi.

Kutmoq, kelishmoq: wait for / arrange for + kim? + to for + object + to

O‘zbekcha “sizning kelishingizni kutyapman” inglizchada wait for + kim? + to + fe’l bilan aytiladi. Bu yerda for shart:

  • I’m waiting for you to finish.Tugatishingizni kutyapman.
  • We waited for the rain to stop.Yomg‘ir to‘xtashini kutdik.
  • I’ve arranged for a taxi to pick us up at six.Soat oltida bizni taksi olib ketishini kelishib qo‘ydim.

I’m waiting you to come ❌ → I’m waiting for you to come ✅. Xuddi shunday: wait for the bus, ask for help. for + kim? + to qolipi Unit 64 va 65 da yana uchraydi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I want to go” bilan “I want you to go” ning farqi nimada?

Birinchi gapda xohlovchi ham, boruvchi ham — men. Ikkinchisida xohlovchi — men, boruvchi — siz. Ikkinchi ishni boshqa kishi bajarsa, kim? (you, him, them ...) fe’l bilan to orasiga qo‘yiladi.

Nega “I want that you come” deb bo‘lmaydi?

Ingliz tilida want dan keyin odatda that-gap kelmaydi. Uning o‘rniga kim? + to + fe’l ishlatiladi. O‘zbekcha (yoki ruscha) ta’sirda “xohlayman, sen kel” deb tuzilgan gaplar shu xatoga olib keladi. Esda tuting: I want you to come.

make va let dan keyin nega “to” yo‘q?

Bu ikki fe’l to siz infinitiv (bare infinitive) bilan keladi — shunday qolip o‘rnashgan. Lekin make majhul nisbatda to ni oladi: He was made to wait. let ning majhul shakli o‘rniga be allowed to ishlatiladi.

“tell” bilan “say” ni qanday ajrataman?

tell dan keyin odatda odam turadi: tell me, tell Aziz; buyruq ma’nosida: tell him to wait. say dan keyin odam turmaydi: He said (that) he was tired, yoki say to me. He said me to wait ❌.

“ask to do” va “ask somebody to do” ning farqi nimada?

She asked to leave early. — u o‘zi ketishga ruxsat so‘radi. She asked me to leave early. — u mendan ertaroq ketishimni so‘radi. Yana: ask for something — biror narsani so‘rab olmoq.

“make” bilan “force” orasidagi farq nima?

Ma’nosi o‘xshash, lekin force ko‘proq kuch yoki bosimni bildiradi va to bilan keladi: They forced him to sign. make kundalik nutqda keng ishlatiladi va to siz keladi: They made him sign.

“Do you want me to help?” va “Do you want to help?” nima uchun farq qiladi?

Birinchisi — taklif: “Yordam berishimni xohlaysizmi?” (yordam beradigan — men). Ikkinchisi — savol: “Yordam bermoqchimisiz?” (yordam beradigan — siz). Faqat bitta olmosh ma’noni butunlay o‘zgartiradi.

“expect” va “hope” ni qanday farqlayman?

expect — ish ro‘y berishiga ishonch yoki talab: I expect you to be on time. hope — umid, kafolat yo‘q: I hope you’ll be on time. hope dan keyin kim? + to kelmaydi; expect dan keyin keladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing (to + fe’l yoki to siz fe’l).

  1. The teacher told us (open) our books.
  2. Her parents let her (stay) at her friend’s house.
  3. I want you (listen) carefully.
  4. The guard made everyone (leave) the building.
  5. Dilnoza asked me (help) her with the homework.
  6. Jasur’s father won’t allow him (drive) at night.
  7. My boss expects me (be) on time.
  8. The sad film made me (cry).

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang va yozing.

  1. Our teacher doesn’t let us (use / to use) phones in class.
  2. They made him (sign / to sign) the contract.
  3. My uncle taught me (swim / to swim).
  4. I’d like you (meet / to meet) my sister.
  5. Did they allow you (take / to take) photos?
  6. We were made (wait / to wait) outside.
  7. She was allowed (go / to go) home early.

3-mashq. To‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri gapni o‘zlashtirma gapga aylantiring (told, asked, advised, reminded, warned).

  1. “Please close the window,” Malika said to Sardor. → Malika asked Sardor .
  2. “Don’t touch the machine,” the engineer said to us. → The engineer told us .
  3. “You should see a doctor,” I said to Aziz. → I advised Aziz .
  4. “Remember to buy bread,” Mum said to me. → Mum reminded me .
  5. “Don’t go out alone at night,” the police said to the tourists. → The police warned the tourists .

4-mashq. Gapni majhul nisbatga o‘tkazing.

  1. The police told the tourists to leave. → The tourists .
  2. They made us clean the classroom. → We the classroom.
  3. Her parents allowed her to travel alone. → She alone.
  4. The doctor advised Aziz to rest. → Aziz .
  5. The guard ordered the men to stop. → The men .

5-mashq. Kim?li va kim?siz variantlarni almashtiring (bo‘sh joyga -ing yoki to + fe’l yozing).

  1. They don’t allow people to smoke here. → They don’t allow here. (smoke)
  2. I’d advise you to take a taxi. → I’d advise a taxi. (take)
  3. The school doesn’t allow students to use phones. → The school doesn’t allow phones. (use)
  4. I wouldn’t advise taking the old road. → I wouldn’t advise you the old road. (take)

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Ikkinchi ishni boshqa kishi bajarsa: fe’l + kim? + to + V1 — want you to come, tell him to wait, advise us to go, expect them to arrive.
  • Kim? o‘rnida to‘ldiruvchi olmosh: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.
  • Bu qolipni oladigan fe’llar: want, would like, expect, need, tell, ask, order, warn, remind, invite, advise, allow, permit, encourage, persuade, force, teach, get, enable.
  • make va let + kim? + V1 (to siz): made me wait, let me go. Majhul: was made to wait, was allowed to go.
  • advise / allow / permit / forbid: kim? bilan — to; kim?siz — -ing.
  • Inkor: not to + V1 (told me not to be late).
  • want that ❌, hope you to ❌, said me to ❌, wait you ❌ (wait for).
