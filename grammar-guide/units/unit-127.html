In/on/at (other uses)

In / on / at — boshqa qo‘llanishlar
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • in bilan keladigan birikmalar: in the rain, in the sun, in the dark, in pencil, in capital letters, in cash, in love (with), in my opinion.
  • at bilan keladigan birikmalar: at the age of 21 / at 21, at 90 km an hour, at 100 degrees.
  • on bilan keladigan birikmalar: on holiday, on business, on a trip, on strike, on fire, on purpose, on the whole.
  • on bilan aloqa va axborot vositalari: on TV, on the radio, on the phone, on the internet.
  • O‘zbekcha bitta -da qo‘shimchasi bu yerda nega uch xil predlogga (in / at / on) aylanishini.

Unit 121 da vaqt predloglarini (at five, on Monday, in May), Unit 123–125 da joy predloglarini o‘rgangansiz. Bu darsda esa vaqt ham, joy ham emas, lekin kundalik nutqda juda tez-tez uchraydigan qotib qolgan birikmalar (fixed expressions) bor: in the rain, at 21, on holiday, on the phone. Ularda predlogni mantiq bilan topib bo‘lmaydi, shuning uchun har bir birikmani predlogi bilan birga yodlash kerak. Lekin ularni guruhlarga bo‘lsak, yodlash ancha yengillashadi.

O‘zbek tilida bu birikmalarning deyarli hammasi bitta -da qo‘shimchasi bilan aytiladi: yomg‘irda, qalamda, 21 yoshda, soatiga 90 km tezlikda, ta’tilda, televizorda, telefonda. Ingliz tilida esa -da o‘rniga uchta predlog turadi: in, at yoki on. Shuning uchun “-da = in” deb o‘ylab, ❌ in holiday, ❌ in TV, ❌ in the phone deyish eng keng tarqalgan xato. In: ob-havo, yozuv, pul, sevgi va fikr in the rain, in pencil, in cash, in love, in my opinion

1) Ob-havo va yorug‘lik: in the rain, in the sun, in the dark …

Ob-havo sharoitida yoki yorug‘lik va soya “ichida” bo‘lishni in bilan aytamiz — go‘yo shu sharoit odamni o‘rab olgandek.

Bekzod soyabonini unutib, uyga yomg‘irda piyoda ketdi.
in the snowqordaThe children played in the snow all morning.
Bolalar butun ertalab qorda o‘ynashdi.
in the sunquyoshda (to‘g‘ri nur ostida)Don’t leave your phone in the sun; it will get too hot.
Telefoningni quyoshda qoldirma, juda qizib ketadi.

Tushlikni qadimiy tut daraxtining soyasida qildik.

O‘zbek tilida “yomg‘ir ostida qoldik” deymiz, lekin inglizchada ❌ under the rain deyilmaydi: ✅ We got caught in the rain. “Quyosh ostida” ham odatda in the sun bo‘ladi.

in the dark yana ko‘chma ma’noda ishlatiladi: be in the dark (about something) — “biror narsadan bexabar bo‘lmoq”. The staff were kept in the dark about the plan. (Xodimlar reja haqida bexabar qoldirilgan.)

2) Yozuv va to‘lov: in pencil, in ink, in words, in cash …

Biror narsani qanday material, shakl yoki valyuta bilan yozishni yoki to‘lashni ham in bilan aytamiz. Bunday iboralarda odatda artikl yo‘q: in pencil, in ink, in cash.

Qoida: in + material/shakl (pencil, ink, cash, words, figures) — a/the siz. Qurol sifatida aytsak, with + a/the/egalik olmoshi ishlatiladi: write with a pencil (Unit 128). Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, lekin tuzilishi har xil: in pencil ✅, in a pencil ❌.

BrE / AmE: biro — Britaniya inglizchasida sharikli ruchka. in capital letters / in capitals / in block capitals (BrE), in block letters (AmE). “Raqam bilan” — in figures yoki in numbers.

3) In love (with)

be in love with — “sevib qolgan holatda”; fall in love with — “sevib qolmoq” (jarayon). Predlog har doim with, o‘zbekcha “-ga” bo‘lsa ham to emas.

  • Dilnoza fell in love with Samarkand at first sight.Dilnoza Samarqandni ilk ko‘rishdayoq sevib qoldi.
  • Kamola is in love with a boy from her class.Kamola sinfdoshi bo‘lgan bir yigitga oshiq.
  • They fell in love at university and got married in 2018.Ular universitetda bir-birini sevib qolishdi va 2018-yilda turmush qurishdi.

love (fe’l) bilan in love bir xil emas: I love my parents — ularni yaxshi ko‘raman (oilaviy mehr). I’m in love with him — unga oshiqman (romantik tuyg‘u). Norasmiy nutqda I’m in love with this song! deyish ham mumkin: “bu qo‘shiqqa shaydoman”.

4) In my opinion

O‘z fikringizni bildirish uchun in my opinion (“mening fikrimcha”) ishlatiladi. Egalik olmoshi o‘zgaradi: in her opinion, in our teacher’s opinion.

  • In my opinion, the new bridge is too narrow.Mening fikrimcha, yangi ko‘prik juda tor.
  • In Dilnoza’s opinion, the film was far too long.Dilnozaning fikricha, film haddan tashqari uzun ekan.
  • Why not ask Sardor? In my view, he knows the most about computers.Sardordan so‘ramaysizmi? Mening nazarimda, kompyuterni hammadan yaxshi biladi.

❌ by my opinion, ❌ on my opinion, ❌ according to my opinion. Faqat in my opinion (yoki in my view). According to boshqa odam yoki manba haqida gapirganda ishlatiladi: according to the weather forecast, according to Aziz — lekin o‘z fikringizni aytganda emas (according to me odatda aytilmaydi).

At: yosh, tezlik, harorat at the age of 21, at 90 km an hour, at 100 degrees

Aniq qiymat yoki “nuqta” — yosh, tezlik, harorat, narx — haqida gapirganda at ishlatiladi: shkalaning aniq bir nuqtasida turgandek.

1) At the age of … / at …

Voqea necha yoshda sodir bo‘lganini aytsak, at the age of yoki qisqaroq at + son:

  • Sardor started playing the dutar at the age of seven.Sardor dutar chalishni yetti yoshida boshlagan.
  • Malika became a head teacher at 35.Malika 35 yoshida maktab direktori bo‘ldi.
  • In Uzbekistan you can vote at 18.O‘zbekistonda 18 yoshda ovoz berish mumkin.
  • My grandfather learned to read at the age of eight.Bobom o‘qishni sakkiz yoshida o‘rgangan.
Yosh bilan: qaysi shakl to‘g‘ri?
ShaklIzoh
at the age of 21to‘liq, ancha rasmiy
at 21qisqa, kundalik nutqda eng ko‘p
at age 21asosan Amerika inglizchasida
in the age of 21 / in 21 yearsxato — faqat at
He is at 21.xato — hozirgi yoshni aytganda: He is 21. (yoki He is 21 years old.)

at yoshning aniq nuqtasi uchun, in esa o‘nlik uchun: in his twenties (yigirma yoshlarida), in her late thirties (o‘ttiz yoshlarining oxirida). She wrote her first novel in her twenties. (U birinchi romanini yigirma yoshlarida yozgan.)

2) At 90 km an hour — tezlik

  • Bekzod was driving at 110 km an hour on the motorway when the police stopped him.Bekzod magistralda soatiga 110 km tezlikda ketayotganda yo‘l patrul xizmati uni to‘xtatdi.
  • In the fog the bus moved at only 20 km an hour.Tumanda avtobus soatiga atigi 20 km tezlikda yurdi.
  • The plane was flying at a speed of 800 km an hour.Samolyot soatiga 800 km tezlikda uchayotgan edi.
  • Zarina ran at full speed to catch the bus.Zarina avtobusga ulgurish uchun butun tezligi bilan yugurdi.

Tezlik ifodalari: at 90 km an hour = at a speed of 90 km an hour = at 90 km/h (“kilometres per hour”). Amerika inglizchasida ko‘pincha miles per hour (mph). Yana: at high / low speed, at full speed, at top speed, at the speed of sound. ❌ with 90 km an hour deyilmaydi.

3) At 100 degrees — harorat

  • Ice melts at 0 degrees Celsius.Muz 0 daraja Selsiyda eriydi.
  • Bake the cake at 180 degrees for thirty-five minutes.Tortni 180 darajada o‘ttiz besh daqiqa pishiring.
  • Keep the medicine at room temperature.Dorini xona haroratida saqlang.
  • Wool should be washed at a temperature of 30 degrees.Jun matolarni 30 daraja haroratda yuvish kerak.

4) At bilan narx va stavka

  • A stallholder at Chorsu sells peaches at 14,000 soums a kilo.Chorsudagi sotuvchi shaftolini kilosi 14 000 so‘mdan sotadi.
  • I bought the coat at half price.Paltoni yarim narxda sotib oldim.

O‘zbekcha yosh, tezlik va harorat — hammasi “-da” (21 yoshda, 90 km tezlikda, 100 darajada) bilan aytiladi. Inglizchada bularning uchalasida ham at. Bu — “-da” = in/on/at ichida eng oson guruh: shkaladagi raqam bo‘lsa, at.

BrE / AmE: on holiday (BrE) = on vacation (AmE). “Ta’tilda” — on holiday (artiklsiz). Faqat sifat qo‘shilsa, a kerak bo‘ladi: on a skiing holiday, on a beach holiday. On purpose ning aksi — by accident / by mistake (Unit 128).

O‘zbekcha ikkalasi ham “-da”: televizorda, radioda, internetda — lekin gazetada, kitobda (bosma). Inglizchada bu ikkiga bo‘linadi: televizor, radio, internet, telefon — on; gazeta, jurnal, kitob — in.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “on holiday” deymiz, lekin “in the rain”? Buning qoidasi bormi?

Qat’iy qoida yo‘q: bular qotib qolgan birikmalar (collocations). Yodlashni yengillashtirish uchun guruhlarga bo‘ling: in — sharoit, material, fikr (the rain, pencil, my opinion); at — shkaladagi raqam (yosh, tezlik, harorat); on — faoliyat, holat va eshittirish (holiday, strike, TV, phone). Har yangi iborani to‘liq gapda yozib qo‘ying.

“on TV” bilan “on the TV” ning farqi nimada?

on TV — teleko‘rsatuv, kanal: There is a good film on TV. on the TV — televizor asbobining ustida, jismoniy joy: The remote is on the TV. Radio doim the bilan: on the radio (Unit 73).

“on the phone” bilan “by phone” ni qanday ajrataman?

on the phone — hozir suhbat jarayonida: She is on the phone. by phone — aloqa usuli (xat orqali emas, telefon orqali): You can book a table by phone. Birinchisi holatni, ikkinchisi vositani bildiradi (Unit 128).

“I’m 21” deymizmi yoki “I’m at 21”?

Hozirgi yoshni aytganda predlog yo‘q: I’m 21 (yoki I’m 21 years old). at 21 / at the age of 21 faqat o‘sha yoshda sodir bo‘lgan voqea uchun: I got my first job at 21.

“on holiday”, “on vacation” va “on holidays” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Britaniya inglizchasida on holiday, Amerika inglizchasida on vacation. “On holidays” ba’zi mintaqaviy variantlarda uchraydi, lekin standart emas; imtihonda on holiday (yoki AmE: on vacation) yozing. Ko‘plik holidays boshqa ma’noda: the school holidays (maktab ta’tili davri).

“pay in cash” o‘rniga “pay cash” desa bo‘ladimi? “by cash” chi?

pay in cash va pay cash ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. ❌ pay by cash deyilmaydi. Karta va chek uchun esa by: pay by card, pay by cheque (Unit 128). Ya’ni in cash, lekin by card.

“in my opinion” ni qachon ishlatish kerak, “I think” dan farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi yaqin. I think — oddiy suhbatda. In my opinion — yozma ish, esse va munozarada: u gapning boshida turadi va vergul bilan ajratiladi: In my opinion, school uniforms are a good idea. Boshqa odamning fikri uchun in her opinion, manba uchun according to the report.

“fall in love” bilan “be in love” ning farqi nimada?

fall in love (with) — jarayon, sevib qolish (fe’l o‘tgan zamonda: fell). be in love (with) — holat, sevib turish. They fell in love in 2015 and they are still in love. (Ular 2015-yilda sevib qolishgan va hanuz bir-birini sevishadi.)

“on fire” va “burning” farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi yaqin: The house is on fire = The house is burning. on fire ko‘pincha “yong‘in chiqqan, alanga olgan” holatini ta’kidlaydi. “Alangalanmoq” — catch fire, “o‘t qo‘ymoq” — set fire to.
