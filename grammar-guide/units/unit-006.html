6

Past continuous (I was doing)

O‘tgan davomli zamon
Present and past · Hozirgi va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Past continuous (o‘tgan davomli zamon) qanday yasalishini: was / were + fe’l-ing.
  • Uni qachon ishlatishni: o‘tmishdagi ma’lum bir paytda davom etayotgan, hali tugamagan ish.
  • Past continuous va past simple farqini: I was walking home ↔ I walked home.
  • Ikkalasini birga ishlatishni: Aziz called while we were watching the news. (when / while)
  • Qaysi fe’llar past continuous da ham ishlatilmasligini (know, want ...).

Past continuous — o‘tmishdagi biror paytda “jarayonning o‘rtasida” bo‘lgan ish. U Unit 1 dagi present continuous ning o‘tgan zamondagi “egizagi”: I am working (hozir) → I was working (o‘sha paytda).

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no -yotgan edi / -yotgan edim bilan beriladi: Soat 8 da kechki ovqat yeyayotgan edik = At 8 o’clock we were having dinner. Sen qo‘ng‘iroq qilganingda uxlayotgan edim = I was sleeping when you called. Agar o‘zbekchada “-yotgan edi” desangiz — inglizchada deyarli doim past continuous.

Shakli va asosiy ma’nosi Form: was / were + -ing

Mana bir vaziyat. Kecha Kamola televizorda film ko‘rdi. Film soat 8 da boshlanib, soat 10 da tugadi. Demak, soat 9 da u film ko‘rayotgan edi — film o‘rtasida edi, hali tugamagan edi:

  • Kamola watched a film last night. It started at 8 and finished at 10.Kamola kecha film ko‘rdi. U soat 8 da boshlanib, 10 da tugadi.
  • At 9 o’clock she was watching the film.Soat 9 da u film ko‘rayotgan edi.

-ing qo‘shishning imlo qoidalari (making, running, lying ...) Unit 1 dagi bilan bir xil.

“O‘sha paytda jarayonning o‘rtasida edim” I was doing = I was in the middle of doing

I was doing something — men o‘tmishdagi ma’lum bir paytda biror ishning o‘rtasida edim. Ish o‘sha paytdan oldin boshlangan, lekin o‘sha paytda hali tugamagan edi.

  • This time last year I was studying for my university exams.O‘tgan yili shu paytda universitet imtihonlariga tayyorlanayotgan edim.
  • At 6 o’clock this morning my parents were already working in the garden.Bugun ertalab soat 6 da ota-onam allaqachon bog‘da ishlayotgan edi.
  • I called Sardor’s name, but he wasn’t listening. He had his headphones on.Sardorni chaqirdim, lekin u tinglamayotgan edi. Quloqchinlarini taqib olgan edi.
  • At midnight we were still talking.Yarim tunda ham hali gaplashayotgan edik.
  • Where were you going when I saw you this morning?Bugun ertalab seni ko‘rganimda qayerga ketayotgan eding?

Past continuous va past simple: taqqoslash I was doing and I did

Past continuous: o‘rtasida

Ish o‘sha paytda davom etayotgan edi, tugamagan edi.

  • I was walking to the bus stop when it started to rain.
    Bekatga piyoda ketayotganimda yomg‘ir yog‘a boshladi. (yo‘lning o‘rtasida)
  • Kamola was painting the kitchen when we arrived.
    Biz kelganimizda Kamola oshxonani bo‘yayotgan edi. (ish tugamagan edi)

Past simple: to‘liq, tugagan

Ish boshidan oxirigacha bajarilgan.

  • Yesterday I walked to work. It took 25 minutes.
    Kecha ishga piyoda bordim. 25 daqiqa ketdi. (butun yo‘lni)
  • Kamola painted the kitchen last weekend.
    Kamola o‘tgan dam olish kunlari oshxonani bo‘yadi. (ishni tugatdi)
  • I was writing an email to my teacher when the electricity went off.Elektr o‘chib qolganda o‘qituvchimga xat yozayotgan edim. (xat tugamagan)
  • I wrote the email and sent it.Xatni yozib, jo‘natdim. (xat tugagan)

Ikkalasi birga: when va while Something happened in the middle of something else

Biror ish boshqa ishning o‘rtasida sodir bo‘lganini aytish uchun ikkala zamonni birga ishlatamiz: uzoqroq davom etgan “fon” ish — past continuous, uni “bo‘lgan” qisqa ish — past simple.

  • Aziz called while we were watching the news.Biz yangiliklarni ko‘rayotganimizda Aziz qo‘ng‘iroq qildi.
  • The sun was shining when we left the house.Uydan chiqqanimizda quyosh charaqlab turgan edi.
  • I saw Jasur at the market yesterday. He was buying melons.Kecha Jasurni bozorda ko‘rdim. U qovun sotib olayotgan edi.
  • Malika cut her finger while she was cooking.Malika ovqat pishirayotganda barmog‘ini kesib oldi.
while / when+was / were+-ing,past simple

while (… -yotganda) odatda davom etayotgan ish bilan keladi: while I was cooking. when (… -ganda) ikkala holatda ham ishlatiladi: when we left (qisqa ish), when I was cooking (davomli ish).

Ketma-ket ishlar — past simple

Agar bir ish ikkinchisidan keyin sodir bo‘lgan bo‘lsa, ikkalasi ham past simple:

  • I was waiting for the bus when I saw Jasur on the other side of the street. I waved, and he crossed the road to say hello.Avtobus kutayotganimda ko‘chaning narigi tomonida Jasurni ko‘rdim. Qo‘l siltadim, u esa salomlashish uchun yo‘lni kesib o‘tdi.
  • When the teacher came in, the students stood up.O‘qituvchi kirganda o‘quvchilar o‘rnidan turishdi. (avval kirdi, keyin turishdi)

Diqqat: ma’no butunlay o‘zgaradi!

When Dilnoza arrived, we were watching a film.

Biz filmni Dilnoza kelishidan oldin boshlagan edik. U kelganda biz film ko‘rayotgan edik.

When Dilnoza arrived, we watched a film.

Avval Dilnoza keldi, keyin biz film ko‘rdik.

Ikkita parallel jarayon

Ikki ish bir vaqtda, yonma-yon davom etgan bo‘lsa, ikkalasi ham past continuous bo‘lishi mumkin:

  • While I was cooking, my brother was cleaning the flat.Men ovqat pishirayotganimda akam kvartirani tozalayotgan edi.

Past continuous da ishlatilmaydigan fe’llar know, want etc.

Unit 4 dagi holat fe’llari (know, want, like, understand, believe, belong ...) past continuous da ham odatda ishlatilmaydi — past simple keladi:

  • My grandfather knew every street in the old city.Bobom eski shahardagi har bir ko‘chani bilardi. (was knowing ❌)
  • Everyone was dancing at the wedding, but my little sister wanted to sleep.To‘yda hamma raqs tushayotgan edi, lekin singlim uxlashni xohlardi. (was wanting ❌)
  • I didn’t understand what he was saying.U nima deyayotganini tushunmadim. (wasn’t understanding ❌)

O‘zbekchada xohlayotgan edi, tushunayotgan edim deyish mumkin bo‘lsa ham, inglizchada wanted, understood — oddiy past simple.

Hikoyada: fon va voqealar Past continuous and past simple in a story

Hikoya aytganda past continuous — manzara, fon (o‘sha paytda nimalar davom etayotgan edi), past simple esa — voqealar zanjiri (nima bo‘ldi, keyin nima bo‘ldi). Bekzodning hikoyasini o‘qing:

  • It was a cold evening. It was raining, and people were hurrying home from work. I was waiting for a taxi outside the metro station. Suddenly an old man slipped and fell on the wet steps. I ran to him and helped him up. He wasn’t hurt, but he was shaking. While we were talking, my taxi arrived, so I took him home in it.Sovuq oqshom edi. Yomg‘ir yog‘ayotgan, odamlar ishdan uylariga shoshilayotgan edi. Men metro bekati oldida taksi kutayotgan edim. To‘satdan bir qariya ho‘l zinapoyada sirpanib yiqildi. Men unga yugurib borib, turishiga yordam berdim. U jarohat olmagan edi, lekin qaltirayotgan edi. Biz gaplashayotganimizda taksim keldi, shuning uchun uni uyiga olib borib qo‘ydim.

Fon (past continuous)

  • It was raining
  • people were hurrying
  • I was waiting
  • he was shaking
  • we were talking

Voqealar (past simple)

  • an old man slipped and fell
  • I ran ... and helped
  • my taxi arrived
  • I took him home

Voqealar ketma-ket keladi (avval yiqildi → keyin yugurdim → keyin yordam berdim), shuning uchun ularning hammasi past simple. Fon esa voqealar paytida “orqada” davom etib turgan.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

when va while ning farqi nima?

while = “... -yotgan paytda”, ko‘pincha davomli ish bilan: while I was driving. when = “... -ganda”, ko‘pincha qisqa ish bilan: when the phone rang, lekin davomli ish bilan ham bo‘ladi: when I was driving. Shuning uchun ikkala variant ham to‘g‘ri: The phone rang while I was driving. / The phone rang when I was driving.

Past continuous har doim boshqa voqea bilan birga kelishi shartmi?

Yo‘q. Aniq bir o‘tgan payt bilan ham yolg‘iz keladi: At 7 o’clock I was having breakfast. This time last week we were travelling in Turkey.

Bitta gapda ikkala fe’l ham past continuous bo‘la oladimi?

Ha, agar ikkala ish bir vaqtda, parallel davom etgan bo‘lsa: While Malika was studying, her brother was playing computer games.

Hikoyalarda past continuous qanday ishlatiladi?

Hikoyaning “fonini”, manzarasini chizish uchun: It was raining. People were hurrying home. Suddenly a car stopped in front of me. Fon — past continuous, voqealar — past simple.

“I was wondering if you could help me” — bu nima uchun past continuous?

Bu juda muloyim iltimos shakli. Bu yerda o‘tgan zamon ma’nosi yo‘q — continuous va o‘tgan zamon shakli gapni yumshoqroq, odobliroq qiladi. Ma’nosi: “Menga yordam bera olmaysizmi?”

“He was always losing his keys” — bu to‘g‘rimi?

Ha. Xuddi present continuous dagi kabi (Unit 3B), was always + -ing o‘tmishda juda tez-tez takrorlangan, ko‘pincha bezor qilgan ishni bildiradi: “U doim kalitlarini yo‘qotardi”.

O‘zbekchada “o‘qiyotgan edim” va “o‘qir edim” — inglizchada qanday?

O‘qiyotgan edim — o‘sha paytda jarayonda: I was reading. O‘qir edim / o‘qirdim — o‘tmishdagi odat: I read / I used to read (Unit 18). Ularni aralashtirmang: o‘tmishdagi odat past continuous emas.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: was / were + fe’l-ing. So‘roq: Was she ...ing? Were you ...ing?
  • Ma’no: o‘tmishdagi paytda ish jarayonda edi — boshlangan, lekin tugamagan (= o‘zbekcha “-yotgan edi”).
  • Past continuous — “o‘rtasida”; past simple — “to‘liq, tugagan”.
  • Uzun ish + uni bo‘lgan qisqa ish: I was cooking when he called.
  • Ketma-ket ishlar — ikkalasi ham past simple: When she arrived, we watched a film.
  • while + davomli ish; when — ikkala holatda ham.
  • Holat fe’llari (know, want, understand ...) — past simple: I knew, not was knowing.
