Can, could and (be) able to

Can, could va (be) able to — qobiliyat
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • can — qobiliyat (ability), imkoniyat (possibility) va ruxsat (permission) ma’nolarida.
  • (be) able to — qachon can o‘rniga ishlatilishi shart ekanini (present perfect, infinitive, will, must ... dan keyin).
  • could — o‘tmishdagi umumiy qobiliyat, sezgi fe’llari (see, hear ...) bilan va o‘tmishdagi ruxsat.
  • could va was able to / managed to farqini — bu unitning eng muhim qismi.
  • couldn’t har qanday vaziyatda ishlatilishini.

Can — “qila olaman”, “mumkin”. U modal fe’l: undan keyin fe’l to siz keladi, uchinchi shaxsda -s olmaydi va uning faqat ikkita shakli bor: can (hozir) va could (o‘tmish). Boshqa barcha holatlarda yordamga be able to keladi.

O‘zbek tilida qobiliyat -a olmoq bilan beriladi: suza olaman, o‘qiy olardi, topa oldik. Muammo shundaki, -a oldi (aniq bir vaziyatda uddaladi) inglizchaga could deb tarjima qilinmaydi! Chipta qolmagan edi, lekin biz chipta topa oldik → We were able to get tickets / We managed to get tickets ✅ — We could get tickets ❌. could esa -a olardi (umuman qila olardi) ma’nosiga mos: Bolaligimda qo‘llarimda yura olardim → When I was a child, I could walk on my hands.

can — qobiliyat, imkoniyat, ruxsat Can: ability, possibility, permission

can uch asosiy ma’noda ishlatiladi: biror ishni qila olish (qobiliyat), biror narsa mumkinligi (imkoniyat) va ruxsat.

Qoida: can dan keyin to qo‘yilmaydi (I can swim, I can to swim ❌), uchinchi shaxsda -s qo‘shilmaydi (she can, she cans ❌), so‘roq va inkor uchun do kerak emas (Can you ...?, Do you can ...? ❌).

Talaffuz: darak gapda can ko‘pincha kuchsiz o‘qiladi /kən/: I can /kən/ swim. Inkor can’t esa doim urg‘uli: BrE /kɑːnt/, AmE /kænt/. Yozuvda cannot bitta so‘z qilib yoziladi.

(be) able to — can ning o‘rnini bosuvchi (Be) able to

can o‘rniga be able to ham ishlatish mumkin, lekin hozirgi zamonda can ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi:

  • Is Bekzod able to drive? (= Can Bekzod drive?)Bekzod mashina hayday oladimi?

Lekin can ning faqat ikki shakli bor: can (hozir) va could (o‘tmish). Uning infinitivi (to can), -ing shakli, perfect shakli yo‘q. Shuning uchun ba’zi joylarda be able to ishlatish shart:

Qachon can emas, be able to kerak?

can kelajak haqida ham ishlatiladi — agar gap hozirgi qaror yoki kelishuv haqida bo‘lsa: I can meet you tomorrow at 5. (Ertaga 5 da uchrasha olaman — bo‘sh vaqtim bor.) Lekin hozir yo‘q bo‘lgan, kelajakda paydo bo‘ladigan qobiliyat uchun will be able to: Next year I’ll be able to drive.

could — o‘tmishda Could: the past of can

could ko‘pincha can ning o‘tmish shakli bo‘ladi. U ayniqsa quyidagi hollarda ishlatiladi.

1. Sezgi va idrok fe’llari bilan

could bilan juda ko‘p keladigan fe’llar:
seehearsmelltastefeelrememberunderstand
  • From the train window we could see cotton fields for miles.Poyezd derazasidan kilometrlab cho‘zilgan paxta dalalari ko‘rinib turardi.
  • As we walked past the bakery, we could smell fresh bread.Novvoyxona yonidan o‘tayotganimizda, yangi non hidini sezdik.
  • The music was so loud that I couldn’t hear what Aziz was saying.Musiqa shunchalik baland ediki, Aziz nima deyayotganini eshita olmadim.
  • I could remember her face, but not her name.Uning yuzini eslay olardim, lekin ismini emas.

2. O‘tmishdagi umumiy qobiliyat

  • My grandmother could sew beautiful clothes.Buvim chiroyli kiyimlar tika olardi.
  • When I was five, I could already read.Besh yoshimda allaqachon o‘qiy olardim.

3. O‘tmishdagi ruxsat

  • When we were children, we could play outside until dark. (= we were allowed to)Bolaligimizda qorong‘i tushguncha tashqarida o‘ynashimizga ruxsat bor edi.
  • At my old school we couldn’t use our phones during the day.Eski maktabimda kun davomida telefon ishlatishga ruxsat yo‘q edi.

could yoki was able to / managed to? Could and was able to

Bu unitning eng muhim farqi:

Qoida: could — o‘tmishdagi umumiy qobiliyat (umuman qila olardi). Agar kimdir aniq bir vaziyatda biror ishni uddalagan bo‘lsa, was / were able to yoki managed to ishlatiladi — could emas.

  • The river was very high, but we were able to cross it. / ... we managed to cross it. (not we could cross it)Daryo juda to‘lib oqardi, lekin biz undan o‘ta oldik.
  • I lost my keys yesterday, but luckily I managed to find them in my bag. (not I could find)Kecha kalitlarimni yo‘qotib qo‘ydim, lekin baxtimga ularni sumkamdan topa oldim.
  • The exam was hard, but Dilnoza was able to answer all the questions.Imtihon qiyin edi, lekin Dilnoza hamma savollarga javob bera oldi.

🧠 could — umumiy qobiliyat

Malika was the best runner in her school. She could run 100 metres in 13 seconds.

Malika maktabidagi eng yaxshi yuguruvchi edi. U 100 metrni 13 soniyada yugura olardi.

🏆 was able to / managed to — aniq vaziyat

In yesterday’s race Malika fell at the start, but she still managed to win / was able to win.

Kechagi poygada Malika startda yiqilib tushdi, lekin baribir g‘olib chiqa oldi.

couldn’t — har qanday vaziyatda

Inkor shakl couldn’t esa ham umumiy, ham aniq vaziyat uchun ishlatiladi:

  • My grandmother couldn’t drive. (umumiy)Buvim mashina hayday olmasdi.
  • We tried for an hour, but we couldn’t open the door. (aniq vaziyat)Bir soat urindik, lekin eshikni ocholmadik.
  • Jasur trained very hard, but he couldn’t win a medal.Jasur juda qattiq mashq qildi, lekin medal ololmadi.

managed to ish qiyin bo‘lganini, ko‘p harakat talab qilganini ta’kidlaydi: The ticket office was closed, but we managed to buy tickets online. Sezgi fe’llari bilan esa aniq vaziyatda ham could mumkin: Suddenly I could hear voices. ✅

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

She cans speak English.She can speak English.Modal fe’llar uchinchi shaxsda -s olmaydi.
Do you can drive?Can you drive?So‘roqda do kerak emas — can o‘zi egadan oldin chiqadi.

I haven’t could finish my project yet.I haven’t been able to finish my project yet.can/could ning perfect shakli yo‘q → have been able to.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I can swim” va “I am able to swim” — farqi bormi?

Ma’no deyarli bir xil. can ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi va tabiiyroq. be able to hozirgi zamonda biroz rasmiyroq eshitiladi. Lekin can ishlatib bo‘lmaydigan joylarda (will, must, have been ...) faqat be able to.

Nega aniq vaziyatda “could” ishlatib bo‘lmaydi? Inkorda-ku bo‘ladi.

Ijobiy could “umuman qila olardi” degan umumiy qobiliyat ma’nosini beradi — u ish haqiqatan bajarildi deb aytmaydi. Aniq vaziyatda esa biz “uddaladi, qildi” demoqchimiz, shuning uchun was able to / managed to. Inkor couldn’t da bunday muammo yo‘q: “qila olmadi” — har ikkala holatda ham ma’no aniq.

“was able to” va “managed to” ning farqi nima?

Ikkalasi ham “uddaladi”. managed to qo‘shimcha ravishda ish qiyin bo‘lganini, harakat qilinganini bildiradi: After three attempts, I managed to pass my driving test.

Kelajak uchun “can” ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha, agar hozirgi imkoniyat yoki kelishuv haqida gap ketsa: I can see you tomorrow afternoon. (Ertaga tushdan keyin bo‘shman.) Hozir yo‘q, kelajakda paydo bo‘ladigan qobiliyat uchun esa will be able to: When I finish the course, I’ll be able to speak Korean.

“could” faqat o‘tmish uchunmi?

Yo‘q. could taklif (We could go to the cinema), imkoniyat (It could rain later) va muloyim iltimos (Could you help me?) uchun ham ishlatiladi — bular hozir yoki kelajak haqida. Bu ma’nolar Unit 27 va Unit 37 da.

“cannot” yoki “can not” — qanday yoziladi?

Odatda cannot — bitta so‘z. can not deb ajratib yozish juda kam, faqat maxsus urg‘u kerak bo‘lganda uchraydi. Og‘zaki va norasmiy yozuvda can’t.

“I could see the mountains” — bu ham aniq vaziyat-ku, nega could to‘g‘ri?

Sezgi va idrok fe’llari (see, hear, smell, taste, feel, remember, understand) — istisno. Ular bilan aniq vaziyatda ham could ishlatiladi: I could hear music from the next room. Bu ingliz tilining odatiy qo‘llanishi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • can + fe’l (to siz, -s siz): qobiliyat, imkoniyat, ruxsat. Inkor: can’t / cannot.
  • can ning faqat 2 shakli bor (can, could). Boshqa joylarda be able to: have been able to, will be able to, must be able to, to be able to, being able to.
  • could — o‘tmishdagi umumiy qobiliyat va ruxsat; sezgi fe’llari bilan (could see / hear / smell ...).
  • Aniq vaziyatda uddalangan ish: was/were able to yoki managed to (could ❌).
  • couldn’t — har qanday vaziyatda to‘g‘ri.
  • O‘zbekcha -a olardi ≈ could; -a oldi (aniq) ≈ was able to / managed to.
