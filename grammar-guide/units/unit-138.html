Phrasal verbs 2: in/out

Frazali fe’llar — 2: in / out
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • in (ichkariga, ichida) va out (tashqariga, tashqarida) bilan yasalgan eng kerakli frazali fe’llarni: come in, go out, get in(to), get out of, move in, check in, drop in, plug in, fill in, hand in, take in, join in, drop out.
  • come in bilan go in qanday farq qilishini (gapiruvchi qayerda turibdi?) va mashinaga get in, avtobusga get on deyilishini.
  • Qaysi fe’llarda to‘ldiruvchi kerak emas (come in), qaysilari ajraladi (plug it in) va qaysilari ajralmaydi (drop out of).
  • Norasmiy (informal) fe’llarni va Britaniya / Amerika ingliz tili farqini (fill in / fill out).

In va out — inglizchadagi eng oddiy “yo‘nalish” so‘zlari: ichkariga va tashqariga. Ko‘p frazali fe’llarda bu ma’no hamon aniq sezilib turadi (come in — kirib kelmoq, move out — ko‘chib chiqmoq). Boshqalarida particle ko‘chma ma’no beradi (take in — tushunib olmoq yoki aldamoq). Umumiy qoidalar — olmosh to‘ldiruvchi doim o‘rtada turishi (plug it in), fe’l + predlog ajralmasligi — Unit 137 da tushuntirilgan; bu yerda ularni amalda ko‘rasiz.

Belgilar: ∅ to‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi · ⇄ ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: plug it in) · ✗ ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida) · informal norasmiy, asosan og‘zaki nutq.

O‘zbek tilida in / out ma’nosi ko‘pincha kirmoq / chiqmoq fe’llari va yordamchi fe’llar bilan beriladi: kirib kelmoq (come in), kirib bormoq (go in), chiqib ketmoq (go out), chiqib kelmoq (come out). Inglizcha come ≈ “kelmoq” (gapiruvchi tomonga), go ≈ “bormoq, ketmoq” (gapiruvchidan uzoqqa) — shuning uchun o‘zbekcha -ib kelmoq va -ib ketmoq inglizcha come va go ni tanlashga yordam beradi.

Tuzoq: o‘zbek tilida mashinaga “o‘tirmoq”, avtobusga “chiqmoq” deymiz; ingliz tilida esa get in(to) a car, lekin get on a bus. Predlogni o‘zbekchadan so‘zma-so‘z olmang!

Gapiruvchi qayerda turibdi? come va go

Ingliz tilida “kirmoq” uchun ikki fe’l bor: come in va go in. Tanlov harakatga nisbatan gapiruvchi qayerda turganiga bog‘liq. Rasmda S — gapiruvchi, M — harakat qilayotgan odam.

Qoida: harakat gapiruvchiga qarab bo‘lsa — come in / come out; harakat gapiruvchidan uzoqlashsa — go in / go out. Gapiruvchining o‘zi tinglovchi turgan joyga borsa ham come ishlatiladi: ko‘chadagi do‘stiga uy ichidan Wait there, I’ll come out. (Shu yerda kutib turing, hozir chiqaman.)

  • Malika is inside the house. She sees Sardor at the gate and says: “Come in, Sardor!”Malika uyning ichida. U darvoza oldida Sardorni ko‘rib: “Kiring, Sardor!” deydi.
  • Don’t go in yet. The teacher is still talking to someone.Hozircha kirmang. O‘qituvchi hali kimdir bilan gaplashyapti.
  • Dilnoza is in the kitchen. Her brother has just gone out.Dilnoza oshxonada. Ukasi hozirgina chiqib ketdi.
  • Bekzod is waiting in the street. “Dilnoza, come out! The bus is coming!”Bekzod ko‘chada kutyapti. “Dilnoza, chiqing! Avtobus kelyapti!”

Joy nomi aytilsa: into va out of

Agar qayerga kirilgani yoki qayerdan chiqilgani aytilmasa, faqat in / out ishlatiladi (particle — ravish). Agar joy nomi aytilsa, predlog kerak: into yoki out of.

O‘zbekchadagi “-dan” qo‘shimchasini inglizchada from deb o‘ylamang: “mashinadan tushmoq” = get out of the car (❌ get out from the car).

Mashina va taksi: get in(to) / get out (of)

Kichik, yopiq transportga (car, taxi, van, lorry, ambulance) “o‘tirish” — get in yoki get into; undan “tushish” — get out yoki get out of. Avtobus, tramvay, poyezd, samolyot, velosiped uchun esa boshqa juftlik ishlatiladi: get on / get off (Unit 140).

Mehmonxona, aeroport, ofis: check in, drop in, plug in, fill in, hand in Everyday phrasal verbs with in

check in / check out — ro‘yxatdan o‘tmoq, hisob-kitob qilib ketmoq

check in — mehmonxonaga yoki aeroportga kelib, ro‘yxatdan o‘tmoq. check out — mehmonxonadan hisobni to‘lab chiqib ketmoq. Bu ma’noda ikkalasi ham to‘ldiruvchisiz (∅); joyni aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz: check in at the hotel yoki check into the hotel; check out of the hotel. (Aeroportda yukni topshirish ma’nosida esa to‘ldiruvchi oladi va ajraladi: check in your bags / check your bags in.)

  • We checked in at the hotel at two o’clock.Mehmonxonaga soat ikkida ro‘yxatdan o‘tdik.
  • Passengers must check in at least two hours before the flight.Yo‘lovchilar reysdan kamida ikki soat oldin ro‘yxatdan o‘tishi kerak.
  • Guests must check out before noon.Mehmonlar tushgacha mehmonxonadan chiqib ketishi (hisob-kitob qilishi) kerak.
  • She paid the bill and checked out of the hotel.U hisobni to‘ladi va mehmonxonadan chiqib ketdi.

Ot sifatida bu so‘zlar defis bilan yoziladi: the check-in desk (ro‘yxatdan o‘tish joyi), check-out time (mehmonxonadan chiqish vaqti).

informal check sth / sb out — “qarab ko‘rmoq, tanishib ko‘rmoq” (qiziq narsani): Check this video out! / Check it out! — Mana bu videoni ko‘ring! / Buni ko‘rib qo‘ying! Bu ma’noda fe’l ajraladi (⇄).

drop in — rejasiz kirib o‘tmoq informal

drop in — oldindan kelishmasdan, qisqa muddatga mehmonga yoki biror joyga kirib o‘tmoq. Odatda ikki shaklda: drop in on sb (kimnidir yoniga) va drop in at + joy. Ajralmaydi (✗): to‘ldiruvchi on yoki at dan keyin keladi.

  • I was near your office, so I dropped in for a minute.Ofisingiz yonidan o‘tayotgan edim, bir daqiqaga kirib o‘tdim.
  • Why don’t you drop in on us on your way home?Uyga ketayotib bizga kirib o‘tmaysizmi?
  • I’ll drop in at the pharmacy and buy some aspirin.Dorixonaga kirib, aspirin olaman.

Ma’nodoshlari: drop by (ayniqsa Amerika inglizchasida), pop in (Britaniya, norasmiy). Rasmiyroq: visit, call on.

plug in — rozetkaga ulamoq

plug sth in — elektr asbobni rozetkaga ulamoq. Ajraladi (⇄): plug in the kettle / plug the kettle in / plug it in. Teskarisi — bitta so‘z: unplug (rozetkadan uzmoq). “Rozetka”ga ulanayotganini aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz: plug the charger into the socket (AmE: outlet).

  • The TV screen is completely black. Is it plugged in?Televizor ekrani butunlay qora. U rozetkaga ulanganmi?
  • Plug the laptop in — the battery is almost empty.Noutbukni rozetkaga ulang — batareya deyarli bo‘sh.
  • Dilnoza plugged in the iron and started ironing.Dilnoza dazmolni rozetkaga ulab, dazmollashni boshladi.
  • Unplug the iron when you finish.Tugatgach dazmolni rozetkadan uzing.

fill in (BrE) / fill out (AmE) — anketa to‘ldirmoq

Blank, anketa, ariza yoki bo‘sh joylarni to‘ldirmoq: Britaniya inglizchasida fill in, Amerika inglizchasida fill out ko‘proq ishlatiladi. Hozir ikkala shakl ham ikkala mamlakatda uchraydi (fill out Britaniyada ham eshitiladi). Ajraladi (⇄): fill in the form / fill the form in / fill it in.

  • Please fill in this form in capital letters.Iltimos, ushbu anketani katta harflar bilan to‘ldiring.

  • (AmE) Please fill out the form.(AmE) Iltimos, anketani to‘ldiring.
  • Fill in the gaps with the correct words.Bo‘sh joylarni to‘g‘ri so‘zlar bilan to‘ldiring.

hand in — topshirmoq

hand sth in — uy vazifasi, hisobot, ariza, kalit kabi narsani biror kishiga (o‘qituvchi, boshliq, qabulxona xodimi) topshirmoq. Ajraladi (⇄): hand in your homework / hand your homework in / hand it in. Amerikada bu ma’noda turn in ham ko‘p ishlatiladi. Rasmiy: submit.

join in — qo‘shilmoq

drop out — o‘qishni, kursni, musobaqani tugatmasdan tashlab ketmoq, chiqib ketmoq. Nimadan chiqilganini aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz — drop out of + ot (✗ ajralmaydi). Ot: a dropout (o‘qishni tashlagan odam).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Come in” va “go in” — qaysi birini aytaman?

Gapiruvchi qayerda turganiga qarang. Gapiruvchi ichkarida bo‘lsa va birov unga qarab kirsa — come in (Malika uyda: Come in!). Gapiruvchi ham, tinglovchi ham tashqarida bo‘lsa va kirish undan uzoqda bo‘lsa — go in (Let’s go in). Chiqish uchun ham xuddi shunday: gapiruvchi tashqarida — come out; gapiruvchi ichkarida va odam chiqib ketsa — go out.

“Get in” va “get on” ning farqi nimada?

Kichik, yopiq transport (car, taxi, van) → get in(to) / get out (of). Katta transport yoki ustiga chiqiladigan transport (bus, train, plane, bicycle) → get on / get off (Unit 140). Eslab qolish usuli: mashinaga “ichiga” o‘tirasiz (in), avtobusga esa “sathiga” chiqasiz (on).

“Fill in” va “fill out” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va ma’nosi bir xil. Britaniya ingliz tilida (kitob shu variantga asoslangan) fill in ko‘proq ishlatiladi, Amerikada fill out. Hozirgi nutqda fill out Britaniyada ham eshitiladi. Imtihonda “bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring” topshirig‘ida fill in the gaps deyiladi.

“Fill in” faqat anketa to‘ldirish ma’nosidami? Boshqa ma’nolari bormi?

Bor. fill in for sb — vaqtincha birovning o‘rniga ishlamoq: I’m filling in for Kamola today because she’s ill. (Bugun Kamolaning o‘rniga ishlayapman, u kasal.) fill sb in (on sth) — o‘tkazib yuborgan narsani birovga aytib bermoq: I was late, so Bekzod filled me in on the meeting. (Men kechikdim, shuning uchun Bekzod yig‘ilishda nima bo‘lganini menga aytib berdi.) fill up esa boshqa: idishni “to‘la” qilmoq (fill up the tank).

“Drop in” bilan “visit” bir xilmi?

Deyarli, lekin drop in — rejasiz, qisqa va norasmiy kirib o‘tish. visit — rejalashtirilgan tashrif ham bo‘lishi mumkin va rasmiyroq. Kimnikiga kirsangiz — drop in on sb, biror joyga — drop in at a place. Do‘stingizga Drop in any time! desangiz, “istagan paytda kirib o‘ting” degan ma’noni beradi.

“Take in” ning ma’nolarini qanday ajrataman?

Gapdagi so‘zlarga qarang. Axborot, yangilik, ma’ruza haqida bo‘lsa — tushunib olmoq (odatda can’t take it all in shaklida). be taken in by sth / sb bo‘lsa — aldanmoq. Hayvon yoki muhtoj odam haqida bo‘lsa — uyiga olmoq, boshpana bermoq. Hammasida fe’l ajraladi: take it in.

“Join” va “join in” ni qanday farqlayman?

join + to‘ldiruvchi — odam yoki tashkilotga qo‘shilmoq: Can I join you? (Sizga qo‘shilsam bo‘ladimi?), join a club (klubga a’zo bo‘lmoq). join in — boshlangan harakatga (o‘yin, qo‘shiq, suhbat) qo‘shilmoq: We’re playing dominoes in the yard. Do you want to join in? (Hovlida domino o‘ynayapmiz. Qo‘shilasizmi?)

“In” va “out” har doim frazali fe’lmi? “She walked in the park” da ham?

Yo‘q. She walked in. (U kirib keldi) — bu yerda in ravish, frazali fe’l (walk in). Lekin She walked in the park. (U bog‘da sayr qildi) — bu yerda in predlog, “qayerda?” degan savolga javob. Tekshirish: in dan keyin joy nomi bo‘lsa va harakat shu joy ichida bo‘lsa — predlog. Joy nomisiz in — particle.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • come in / come out — harakat gapiruvchiga qarab; go in / go out — gapiruvchidan uzoqqa.
  • Joy nomi aytilsa: into va out of (❌ out from). Mashina, taksi: get in(to) / get out of; avtobus, poyezd, samolyot: get on / get off (Unit 140).
  • Is she in? — She’s out. — uyda yoki ofisda bor / yo‘q. Yana: go out, stay in, eat out.
  • Ajraladigan fe’llar (⇄): let sb in, plug in, fill in, hand in, take in — olmosh o‘rtada: plug it in, hand it in.
  • Ajralmaydiganlar (✗): get out of, drop out of, drop in on, join in — to‘ldiruvchi oxirida.
  • drop in — norasmiy, rejasiz kirib o‘tmoq (drop in on sb). fill in (BrE) = fill out (AmE).
  • take in: tushunmoq · aldamoq (be taken in) · uyiga olmoq.
