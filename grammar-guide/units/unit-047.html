Reported speech 1 (He said that ...)

O‘zlashtirma gap — 1 (He said that ...)
Reported speech · O‘zlashtirma gap

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Ko‘chirma gap (direct speech) va o‘zlashtirma gap (reported speech) farqini.
  • He said (that) ... tuzilishini va that qachon tushib qolishini.
  • Zamonni “bir pog‘ona orqaga surish” (backshift) qoidasini: am/is → was, will → would, can → could, have done → had done ...
  • Past simple o‘zgarmay qolishi yoki past perfect ga o‘tishi mumkinligini.
  • Olmoshlar (I → he/she), vaqt va joy so‘zlari (tomorrow, here) qanday o‘zgarishini.
  • Zamonni o‘zgartirish qachon shart emasligini (qisqacha; batafsil — Unit 48).

Kimdir sizga nimadir dedi. Keyinroq siz bu gapni boshqa odamga yetkazasiz. Buning ikki yo‘li bor: odamning so‘zlarini aynan takrorlash (ko‘chirma gap) yoki o‘z so‘zlaringiz bilan aytib berish (o‘zlashtirma gap). Ikkinchi holatda ingliz tilida asosiy fe’l odatda o‘tgan zamonda bo‘ladi (said), shuning uchun gapning qolgan qismidagi fe’llar ham odatda “orqaga suriladi”.

O‘zbek tilida biz zamonni o‘zgartirmaymiz: «Charchadim», — dedi Aziz → Aziz charchaganini aytdi. «Kelaman», — dedi Malika → Malika kelishini aytdi. O‘zbekcha -ganini / -ishini / -yotganini aytdi shakllari vaqtni gapirilgan paytga nisbatan ko‘rsatadi. Ingliz tilida esa said o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lgani uchun ergash gapdagi fe’l ham odatda o‘tgan zamonga o‘tadi: Aziz said he was tired. Malika said she would come. O‘zbekchada bunday “surish” yo‘qligi sababli o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha ❌ Malika said she will come deb yuboradi (garchi Malika allaqachon kelib-ketgan bo‘lsa ham).

Ko‘chirma gap va o‘zlashtirma gap Direct speech and reported speech

Kecha siz Sardorni ko‘rdingiz. U shunday dedi:

  • “I’m not feeling well. I have a terrible headache, so I can’t come to Nodira’s wedding.”«Mazam yo‘q. Boshim qattiq og‘riyapti, shuning uchun Nodiraning to‘yiga kela olmayman.»

Bugun siz Dilnozaga Sardor nima deganini aytib bermoqchisiz. Ikki yo‘l bor:

1. Ko‘chirma gap (direct speech)

Sardorning so‘zlarini aynan, qo‘shtirnoq ichida takrorlaysiz:

  • Sardor said, “I’m not feeling well. I have a terrible headache.”

2. O‘zlashtirma gap (reported speech)

Sardorning gapini o‘z so‘zlaringiz bilan yetkazasiz:

  • Sardor said (that) he wasn’t feeling well.
  • He said he had a terrible headache, so he couldn’t come to Nodira’s wedding.

O‘zlashtirma gapda uchta narsa o‘zgaradi:

Britaniya ingliz tilida qo‘shtirnoq ko‘pincha bittalik yoziladi: Sardor said, ‘I’m ill.’ Amerika ingliz tilida esa ikkitalik: “I’m ill.” Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri — bitta matnda bir xilini ishlating.

O‘zbek tilida ham ikki xil yo‘l bor: ko‘chirma gap — Sardor: «Mazam yo‘q», — dedi. O‘zlashtirma gap — Sardor mazasi yo‘qligini aytdi. Farqi shundaki, o‘zbekchada fe’l -ligini, -ganini, -ishini kabi qo‘shimchalar bilan o‘zgaradi, inglizchada esa alohida gap (that + ega + kesim) yasaladi va uning zamoni o‘zgaradi.

He said (that) ... va zamonning orqaga surilishi Reporting verb in the past → the rest usually moves back

O‘zlashtirma gapda asosiy fe’l (said, told me, explained ...) odatda o‘tgan zamonda bo‘ladi, chunki gap o‘tmishda aytilgan. Shu sababli gapning qolgan qismi ham odatda o‘tgan zamonga o‘tadi:

Ega+said+(that)+ega+o‘tgan zamondagi fe’l+...

Qoida: Ko‘chirma gapdagi hozirgi zamon shakli o‘zlashtirma gapda o‘tgan zamon shakliga aylanadi: am/is → was, are → were, have/has → had, will → would, can → could, do/does (live, want, know) → did (lived, wanted, knew).

that — ixtiyoriy so‘z

that ni qo‘yish ham, tushirib qoldirish ham mumkin. Og‘zaki nutqda ko‘pincha tushirib qoldiriladi, rasmiy yozuvda ko‘proq qo‘yiladi:

  • Dilnoza said that she was busy. = Dilnoza said she was busy.Dilnoza bandligini aytdi.

said dan boshqa “xabar fe’llari” ham xuddi shunday ishlaydi: told me (that), explained, mentioned, replied, added, complained, admitted, promised: He explained that the shop was closed. She complained that the room was too cold. (say va tell farqi — Unit 48.)

Har doim o‘zgartirish shartmi? Yo‘q. (1) Agar xabar fe’li hozirgi zamonda bo‘lsa (says), zamon o‘zgarmaydi: Malika says she is tired. (2) Agar aytilgan gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, o‘zgartirmasangiz ham bo‘ladi: Aziz said his sister lives in Fergana (u hali ham o‘sha yerda yashaydi). (3) Agar gap endi to‘g‘ri bo‘lmasa yoki vaziyat tugagan bo‘lsa — o‘tgan zamon majburiy. Bu qoidalar Unit 48 da batafsil tushuntiriladi.

Past simple: o‘zgarmaydi yoki past perfect ga o‘tadi did → did or had done

Agar ko‘chirma gapda past simple (did, missed, went ...) bo‘lsa, o‘zlashtirma gapda uni o‘zgartirmasdan qoldirish ham, past perfect ga (had done) o‘tkazish ham mumkin. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

  • Sardor: “I missed the bus, so I walked to school.”Sardor: «Avtobusdan qolib ketdim, shuning uchun maktabga piyoda bordim.»
  • → Sardor said (that) he missed the bus, so he walked to school.Sardor avtobusdan qolib ketganini, shuning uchun maktabga piyoda borganini aytdi. (past simple saqlangan)
  • → Sardor said (that) he had missed the bus, so he had walked to school.Xuddi shu ma’no. (past perfect ishlatilgan)

Past perfect “o‘tmishdagi o‘tmish”ni bildiradi (Unit 15): u aytilgan paytdan ham oldin bo‘lgan voqeani ko‘rsatadi. Agar voqealar ketma-ketligi kontekstdan aniq bo‘lsa, ko‘pincha past simple ni saqlab qo‘yish kifoya, ayniqsa og‘zaki nutqda. Agar tartibni aniq ko‘rsatish kerak bo‘lsa, past perfect qulayroq:

  • Nodira said she had already seen the film, so she didn’t come with us.Nodira filmni allaqachon ko‘rganini aytdi, shuning uchun biz bilan kelmadi.
  • Tom said he visited Khiva in 2019. / Tom said he had visited Khiva in 2019.Tom 2019-yilda Xivaga borganini aytdi. (aniq sana bilan past simple ko‘proq ishlatiladi)
O‘tgan zamon shakllari bilan nima bo‘ladi?

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“that” ni qo‘ysam yaxshimi yoki tushirib qoldirsammi?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Og‘zaki nutqda that ko‘pincha tushib qoladi: He said he was tired. Rasmiy yozuvda yoki gap uzun bo‘lsa, that qo‘yilsa, gap aniqroq o‘qiladi: The manager said that the new office would open in May.

Zamonni har doim orqaga surish kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Agar xabar fe’li hozirgi zamonda bo‘lsa (She says ...), zamon o‘zgarmaydi. Agar aytilgan gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, o‘zgartirish ixtiyoriy: Aziz said his sister lives / lived in Fergana. Lekin gap endi to‘g‘ri bo‘lmasa yoki vaziyat tugagan bo‘lsa, o‘tgan zamon majburiy. Bu farq Unit 48 da batafsil.

Past simple ni qachon past perfect ga o‘tkazishim kerak?

Ko‘pincha bu ixtiyoriy: She said she lost / had lost her keys. Past perfect voqea aytilgan paytdan oldin bo‘lganini aniq ko‘rsatadi, shuning uchun voqealar tartibi muhim bo‘lsa yoki chalkashlik bo‘lishi mumkin bo‘lsa, past perfect yaxshiroq. Aniq sana bo‘lsa (in 2019), ko‘pincha past simple qoladi.

could, would, should, might o‘zgaradimi?

Yo‘q. Ular allaqachon “o‘tgan zamon” ko‘rinishida, shuning uchun o‘zgarishsiz qoladi: “I could help you.” → He said he could help me. “You should rest.” → The doctor said I should rest.

“dedi” ni har doim “said” deb tarjima qilsam bo‘ladimi?

Ko‘pincha ha. Lekin “menga dedi / menga aytdi” bo‘lsa, ya’ni kimga aytilgani ko‘rsatilsa, inglizchada told me ishlatiladi: Malika menga charchaganini aytdi = Malika told me she was tired. said me deyish xato. Batafsil — Unit 48.

Gapda “tomorrow” bo‘lsa, uni har doim “the next day” ga o‘zgartiramanmi?

Yo‘q, faqat vaqt haqiqatan o‘zgargan bo‘lsa. Agar siz gapni o‘sha kuni yetkazsangiz va “ertaga” hali kelmagan bo‘lsa, tomorrow o‘z joyida qoladi. Qoida — mantiq: siz gapirayotgan paytdan qaraganda o‘sha kun qanday ataladi?

O‘zlashtirma gap faqat og‘zaki nutqdami?

Yo‘q, u yozma nutqda, ayniqsa yangiliklarda, hikoyalarda va rasmiy hisobotlarda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi: The minister said that prices would fall next year. Bunday matnlarda said, stated, explained, announced, claimed kabi fe’llar keladi.

🔑 Javoblar (Answer key)

1-mashq

  1. (that) he was hungry
  2. (that) she didn’t like horror films / (that) she did not like horror films
  3. (that) they were playing football
  4. (that) she had finished her project / (that) she’d finished her project
  5. (that) he would help me / (that) he’d help me
  6. (that) she couldn’t drive / (that) she could not drive
  7. (that) his brother was going to buy a car
  8. (that) they wanted to visit Khiva

2-mashq

  1. (that) she lost her keys / (that) she had lost her keys
  2. (that) he didn’t see the message / (that) he hadn’t seen the message
  3. (that) the shop closed early / (that) the shop had closed early
  4. (that) he met my cousin at the airport / (that) he had met my cousin at the airport

3-mashq

  1. I’m very busy. / I am very busy.
  2. I’ll phone later. / I will phone later.
  3. I can’t find my phone. / I cannot find my phone.
  4. We’re watching a cartoon. / We are watching a cartoon.

4-mashq

  1. Bekzod said that he was busy. / Bekzod said he was busy.
  2. Malika said (that) she was tired.
  3. Last month Aziz said he would visit us, but he never came.
  4. Jasur said he had gone home.

5-mashq

  1. Dilnoza said (that) she was ill. / Dilnoza said (that) she was sick.
  2. Bekzod said (that) he would help us. / Bekzod said (that) he’d help us. / Bekzod told us (that) he would help us. / Bekzod told us (that) he’d help us.
  3. The tourists said (that) they couldn’t find the hotel. / The tourists said (that) they could not find the hotel.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Ko‘chirma gap: aynan so‘zlar, qo‘shtirnoq ichida. O‘zlashtirma gap: said (that) + ega + fe’l, qo‘shtirnoqsiz va vergulsiz.
  • that — ixtiyoriy: He said (that) he was tired.
  • Xabar fe’li o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa, qolgan qism odatda orqaga suriladi: am/is → was, are → were, have → had, will → would, can → could, live → lived.
  • Past simple o‘zgarmasligi ham, past perfect ga o‘tishi ham mumkin: said he lost / had lost.
  • would, could, should, might, ought to va past perfect o‘zgarmaydi.
  • Olmoshlar, vaqt va joy so‘zlari vaziyatga qarab mantiqan o‘zgaradi (I → she, tomorrow → the next day).
  • Gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, zamonni o‘zgartirish shart emas; xabar fe’li says bo‘lsa — o‘zgarmaydi (Unit 48).
