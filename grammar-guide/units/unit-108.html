Superlatives (the longest / the most enjoyable etc.)

Orttirma daraja (the longest, the most ...)
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Orttirma daraja (superlative) qanday yasaladi: the + sifat-est (the tallest) yoki the most + sifat (the most interesting).
  • Qoidasiz shakllar: the best, the worst, the farthest / the furthest, the least, the most.
  • in va of farqi: the highest mountain in the world, the happiest day of my life.
  • Present perfect + ever: the best film I’ve ever seen.
  • one of the + orttirma daraja + ko‘plik ot: one of the oldest cities.

Ikki narsani taqqoslaganda qiyosiy daraja (taller than, Unit 105) kerak. Uch yoki undan ko‘p narsa/odam ichidan eng ... ni ajratib ko‘rsatsangiz — orttirma daraja ishlatiladi.

O‘zbek tilida “eng” so‘zi sifat oldiga qo‘yiladi: eng baland, eng qiziqarli, eng yaxshi. Inglizchada “eng” ning o‘rnini the + -est yoki the most bosadi. Eng muhimi — the ni unutmang! O‘zbekchada “the” degan so‘z yo‘q, shuning uchun o‘quvchilar He is tallest yoki She is best student deb xato qilishadi. Yana bir tuzoq: o‘zbekcha “dunyodagi / sinfdagi eng …” inglizchada in the world / in the class bo‘ladi, “of the world” emas.

Orttirma daraja yasalishi the longest / the most enjoyable

Yasalish qoidasi qiyosiy daraja bilan bir xil mantiqqa ega (Unit 105), faqat -er o‘rniga -est, more o‘rniga most, va oldiga har doim the qo‘yiladi.

Ravishlarning orttirma darajasi

Ravishlar ham xuddi shunday: -ly bilan tugaydiganlar most oladi (the most carefully), sifat bilan bir xil shakldagilar -est oladi (fastest, hardest, earliest), well → best, badly → worst. Ravish bilan the ko‘pincha tushiriladi:

  • Who works hardest in your office?Idorangizda kim eng astoydil ishlaydi?
  • Of the three, Sardor drives the most carefully.Uchovining ichida Sardor eng ehtiyotkor haydaydi.
  • I like this one best.Menga aynan shu eng ko‘p yoqadi.

Ikki xil “most”: the most = “eng ko‘pi” (orttirma): Malika has the most books. Faqat most (the siz) = “ko‘pchilik”: Most students like music. (Unit 88)

In va of: nimadan keyin qaysi biri? the oldest building in the town, the happiest day of my life

Orttirma darajadan keyin “ichida” ma’nosi uchun odatda in yoki of keladi. Ular o‘rnini almashtirmaydi.

In yoki of?

O‘zbekchada ikkalasi ham bir xil: “dunyodagi eng baland tog‘”, “umrimdagi eng baxtli kun”, “uchtasining ichidagi eng yaxshisi”. Inglizchada esa tanlash kerak: joy/guruh → in, vaqt davri va son bilan → of. Shuningdek, “hammasidan” — of all, from all emas.

Orttirma daraja + present perfect + ever the best film I’ve ever seen

“Men hayotimda ko‘rgan eng …” degan ma’noni berish uchun orttirma darajadan keyin present perfect (I’ve seen) va ko‘pincha ever ishlatiladi (Unit 7, Unit 8). Ever — “hech qachon / umr bo‘yi” ma’nosida.

the+orttirma daraja+ot+ega+have / has+ever+V3
  • It’s the most beautiful city I’ve ever visited.Bu men borgan eng go‘zal shahar.
  • That was the worst meal we’ve ever had.Bu biz yeb ko‘rgan eng yomon ovqat edi.
  • She’s the kindest person I’ve ever met.U men uchratgan eng mehribon inson.
  • This is the hardest exam we have ever taken.Bu biz topshirgan eng qiyin imtihon.

Ever ni tushirib qoldirsa ham bo‘ladi: It’s the best film I’ve seen. Agar gap o‘tmishdagi biror paytga nisbatan aytilsa (“o‘sha paytgacha ko‘rganlarim ichida”), ikkinchi qism past perfect bo‘ladi: It was the funniest film I had ever seen. Hozir ham shunday deb hisoblasangiz, present perfect qoladi: That was the worst meal we’ve ever had. Aniq o‘tgan vaqt aytilsa — past simple: It was the best film I saw last year.

the best film I ever see ❌ — the best film I have ever seen ✅. Fe’l uchinchi shaklda (V3) turadi.

One of the … one of the oldest cities in the world

“… ning eng … laridan biri” deyish uchun one of the + orttirma daraja + KO‘PLIK ot ishlatiladi. Ot doim ko‘plikda bo‘ladi, chunki “bir nechtasidan biri” haqida gapiryapmiz.

one+of the+orttirma daraja+ko‘plik ot
  • Samarkand is one of the oldest cities in the world.Samarqand dunyodagi eng qadimiy shaharlardan biri.
  • Chorsu is one of the best-known markets in Tashkent.Chorsu Toshkentdagi eng mashhur bozorlardan biri.
  • K2 is one of the highest mountains on Earth.K2 Yerdagi eng baland tog‘lardan biri.
  • She is one of the most talented players in the team.U jamoadagi eng iqtidorli o‘yinchilardan biri.

Fe’l haqida: Agar one of the … gapning egasi bo‘lsa, fe’l birlikda keladi: One of the oldest cities in Asia is Samarkand.

one of the oldest city ❌ — one of the oldest cities ✅.

Tartib raqami, kuchaytirish va ikki narsa second highest, by far the best, the older of the two

second, third + orttirma daraja

Ikkinchi, uchinchi o‘rinni aytish uchun orttirma darajadan oldin tartib son qo‘yiladi:

  • K2 is the second highest mountain in the world.K2 dunyodagi ikkinchi eng baland tog‘.
  • Canada is the second largest country in the world.Kanada dunyodagi maydoni bo‘yicha ikkinchi eng katta davlat.

by far, easily, nearly

Orttirma darajani by far (ancha-ancha), easily (shubhasiz), nearly / almost (deyarli) bilan kuchaytirish yoki yumshatish mumkin; ular the dan oldin turadi:

  • This is by far the cheapest hotel in town.Bu — shahardagi eng arzon mehmonxona, boshqalaridan ancha arzon.
  • Malika is easily the best singer in our school.Malika maktabimizdagi eng yaxshi qo‘shiqchi ekani shubhasiz.

Ikki narsa bo‘lsa — qiyosiy daraja

Faqat ikki narsa/odam bo‘lsa, rasmiy ingliz tilida orttirma emas, qiyosiy daraja ishlatiladi: the older of the two. Kundalik nutqda esa ko‘pincha oddiy qiyosiy: Which is better, A or B?

  • Of the two hotels, this one is the cheaper.Ikki mehmonxonaning ichida bunisi arzonroq.
  • Aziz is the older of the two brothers.Aziz ikki aka-ukaning kattasi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Orttirma darajadan oldin the nega shart?

Orttirma daraja bitta, aniq narsani ko‘rsatadi (eng baland, eng arzon), shuning uchun aniq artikl the kerak. Faqat egalik olmoshi (my best friend), yoki ravish (Who sings best?), yoki bir narsaning o‘zi turli sharoitda taqqoslanganda (Prices are lowest in January) the tushib qolishi mumkin.

In bilan of ni qanday ajrataman?

Savol bering: qayerda / qaysi guruh ichida? — in (in the world, in my class, in Tashkent). Qaysi davr yoki qaysilar ichidan? — of (of the year, of my life, of the three, of all). Sinash uchun: the best student ___ the class → sinf — guruh → in; the best of the three → uchta narsa → of.

The most bilan faqat most ning farqi nimada?

The most — “eng ko‘p” (bittasi hammadan ko‘p): Malika has the most books (kitoblari hammadan ko‘p). Most (the siz) — “ko‘pchilik, aksariyat”: Most students have a phone. Sifat bilan: the most interesting book (eng qiziqarli kitob), lekin most people are friendly (aksariyat odamlar do‘stona).

Ikki narsadan qaysi biri deb so‘rasam, orttirma ishlataymi?

Qoida bo‘yicha ikki narsa uchun qiyosiy daraja ishlatiladi: Which is better, tea or coffee? Rasmiy uslubda the better of the two ham aytiladi. Kundalik nutqda ba’zan ikki narsa uchun ham orttirma eshitiladi (Which is the best of the two?), lekin imtihonda qiyosiy daraja to‘g‘ri hisoblanadi.

Ever nima uchun kerak? Tushirib qoldirsam bo‘ladimi?

Ever “umr bo‘yi, hech qachon” ma’nosini kuchaytiradi. Uni tushirib qoldirsangiz ham gap to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi: It’s the best film I’ve seen. Lekin ingliz tilida I’ve ever seen juda keng tarqalgan qolip, uni bilish foydali.

Latest bilan last ning farqi nima?

Latest — “eng yangi, so‘nggi (eng yaqinda chiqqan)”: the latest news. Last — “oxirgi, keyingisi yo‘q”: the last bus. Ikkalasi ham o‘zbekchada “oxirgi” deb tarjima qilinishi mumkin, shuning uchun adashadi.

Farthest bilan furthest farqi bormi?

Masofa haqida ikkalasi ham bo‘ladi: the farthest / furthest village. Further esa “qo‘shimcha” ma’nosida ham keladi (further information), farther bunday ishlatilmaydi. Britaniya ingliz tilida further / furthest ko‘proq uchraydi, shuning uchun ular xavfsizroq tanlov.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Uch va undan ko‘p narsa ichidan eng …ni aytganda: the + sifat-est (the tallest) yoki the most + sifat (the most interesting). Ikkalasini birga ishlatmang.
  • Qoidasiz: good → the best, bad → the worst, far → the farthest / the furthest, little → the least, much / many → the most.
  • Joy yoki guruh bilan — in (in the world, in my class); vaqt davri va son/ko‘plik bilan — of (of my life, of the three, of all).
  • Orttirma daraja + present perfect (+ ever): the best film I’ve ever seen.
  • one of the + orttirma daraja + ko‘plik ot: one of the oldest cities.
  • Tartib: the second highest; kuchaytirish: by far the best, easily the best.
  • The tushib qoladi: my best friend, Who sings best?, Prices are lowest in January.
