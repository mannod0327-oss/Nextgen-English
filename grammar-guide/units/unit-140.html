Phrasal verbs 4: on/off (1)

Frazali fe’llar — 4: on / off (1)
Phrasal verbs · Frazali fe’llar (Phrasal verbs)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • on (ulangan, ishlab turgan, ustida) va off (uzilgan, o‘chirilgan, yechilgan, yo‘lga chiqqan, bekor bo‘lgan) bilan yasalgan eng kerakli frazali fe’llarni: turn on / off, switch on / off, put on, take off, try on, go off, set off, call off, put off, get on / off.
  • be on va be off iboralarini: The light is on (chiroq yoniq), What’s on tonight? (bugun kechqurun nima qo‘yilyapti?), The match is off (o‘yin bekor).
  • Bitta fe’lning bir necha ma’nosini ajratishni: put on (kiymoq · yoqmoq · semirmoq), take off (yechmoq · samolyot ko‘tarilmoq), set off (yo‘lga chiqmoq · signalni ishga tushirmoq), put off (kechiktirmoq · qaytarmoq, havasini qochirmoq).
  • Qaysi fe’llar ajraladi (turn it off, try them on) va qaysilari ajralmaydi (get on the bus).
  • call off (bekor qilmoq) bilan put off (keyinga qoldirmoq) farqini va nega put off the TV deb bo‘lmasligini.

Frazali fe’llarning umumiy qoidalari Unit 137 da tushuntirilgan: olmosh to‘ldiruvchi doim fe’l bilan particle orasida turadi (turn it off). on va off — bir juft qarama-qarshi particle. on asosan “ulangan, ishlab turgan, ustida” tasavvurini beradi, off esa “uzilgan, ajralgan, ketgan, to‘xtagan” tasavvurini. Shuning uchun bu fe’llar ko‘pincha juft-juft uchraydi: put on ↔ take off, get on ↔ get off, be on ↔ be off.

Belgilar: ∅ to‘ldiruvchisiz ishlatiladi · ⇄ ajraladi (olmosh o‘rtada: turn it off) · ✗ ajralmaydi (to‘ldiruvchi oxirida) · informal norasmiy, asosan og‘zaki nutq.

O‘zbek tilida “yoqmoq” va “o‘chirmoq” ikkita alohida fe’l bo‘lib, ko‘pincha yordamchi fe’l bilan aytiladi: yoqib qo‘ymoq, o‘chirib qo‘ymoq. Inglizchada esa bitta fe’l (turn, switch) ikkita qarama-qarshi particle bilan keladi: turn on = yoqmoq, turn off = o‘chirmoq. Ya’ni particle o‘zbekchadagi mos fe’lning o‘rnini bosadi. Kiyim-kechakda ham shunday: o‘zbekchada kiymoq, taqmoq, o‘ramoq va yechmoq deymiz, inglizchada hammasi put on va take off.

Tuzoq: “o‘chirib qo‘ymoq”dagi qo‘ymoqni put deb olmang! O‘chirmoq = turn off / switch off. put off esa “keyinga qoldirmoq” degani. Yana: “avtobusga chiqmoq, avtobusdan tushmoq” = get on / get off the bus; “mashinaga o‘tirmoq” esa get in(to) (Unit 138).

Yoqish va o‘chirish: turn on / off, switch on / off, put on, go off Switching things on and off

Chiroq, televizor, kompyuter, isitgich, telefon, gaz yoki suvni ishga tushirish va to‘xtatish uchun eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan juftliklar — turn on / turn off va switch on / switch off. Ma’nosi bir xil, ikkalasi ham ajraladi (⇄).

O‘zbekchada elektr asboblarni yoqamiz / o‘chiramiz, suv va gazni esa ochamiz / yopamiz. Inglizchada ikkala holatda ham bitta juftlik ishlatiladi: turn on the TV (televizorni yoqmoq), turn on the tap (jo‘mrakni ochmoq), turn off the tap (jo‘mrakni yopmoq). Demak on = yoqmoq / ochmoq, off = o‘chirmoq / yopmoq.

turn yoki switch? Ma’nosi bir xil, ikkalasi ham keng ishlatiladi. switch — tugma yoki tumbler (a light switch) bilan ishlaydigan narsalar uchun; turn burab ochiladigan jo‘mrak, gaz va suv uchun ham ishlatiladi: turn on the tap, turn off the water. Ikkilansangiz — turn ni tanlang. Amerika inglizchasida turn on / off ko‘proq eshitiladi.

put on — yoqmoq, musiqa qo‘ymoq BrE

put on ham “yoqmoq, ishga tushirmoq” ma’nosida keladi — ayniqsa Britaniya inglizchasida va chiroq, isitgich, choynak, radio, musiqa haqida. Ajraladi (⇄): put on some music / put some music on / put it on.

  • It’s getting dark. Put the light on, please.Qorong‘i tushyapti. Iltimos, chiroqni yoqing.
  • I’ll put the kettle on, and we can have some tea.Choynakni yoqaman (choy qo‘yaman), choy ichamiz.
  • Malika put on some folk music while she was cooking.Malika ovqat pishirayotganda xalq musiqasini qo‘ydi.
  • It’s freezing in here. Shall I put the heating on?Bu yerda juda sovuq. Isitishni yoqaymi?

Teskari ma’no uchun standart ingliz tilida put off ishlatilmaydi: ❌ put off the light. “O‘chirmoq” = turn off / switch off. put off boshqa ma’noga ega — “kechiktirmoq” (D bo‘lim). Amerika inglizchasida chiroq va televizor uchun asosan turn on ishlatiladi; put on some music esa ikkala davlatda ham keng tarqalgan.

be on / be off, leave sth on — yoniq yoki o‘chiq

on va off be fe’li bilan holatni bildiradi: narsa yoniqmi yoki o‘chiqmi. “Yoniq qoldirmoq” — leave sth on.

  • “Is the oven on?” “No, it’s off.”— Duxovka yoniqmi? — Yo‘q, o‘chiq.
  • Someone has left the tap on. The floor is wet!Kimdir jo‘mrakni ochiq qoldiribdi. Pol ho‘l!
  • Don’t leave the iron on when you go out.Chiqayotganda dazmolni yoniq qoldirmang.

“Yoniq qoldirmoq” o‘zbekchada ham qoldirmoq bilan: chiroqni yoniq qoldirmoq = leave the light on.

O‘zbekchada kiyim turiga qarab turli fe’llar ishlatiladi: palto kiymoq, soat taqmoq, sharf o‘ramoq. Inglizchada hammasiga bitta put on mos keladi: put on a coat / a watch / a scarf. Teskarisi ham bitta: take off (yechmoq, olmoq). “Kiyib olmoq” (-ib olmoq) ko‘pincha put on yoki have on ga to‘g‘ri keladi: Sharfimni kiyib olibsan-ku! = You’ve got my scarf on!

try on — kiyib ko‘rmoq

try on — do‘konda kiyim yoki poyabzalni o‘lchamiga mosligini bilish uchun kiyib ko‘rmoq. Ajraladi (⇄). Boshqa narsalarni sinash uchun try out ishlatiladi (Unit 139).

  • Malika tried on three dresses, but she didn’t buy any of them.Malika uchta ko‘ylakni kiyib ko‘rdi, lekin birontasini ham olmadi.
  • The jeans looked nice, so I tried them on.Jinsi shim chiroyli ko‘rindi, shuning uchun uni kiyib ko‘rdim.

have … on — kiyib turibdi

have sth on — “kiyib turibdi, ustida bor”: kiyimning o‘rni have bilan on orasida. Bu — holat fe’li, shuning uchun continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi: ❌ She is having a hat on (Unit 4). Kiyim haqida continuous kerak bo‘lsa — be wearing.

  • The shop assistant remembered the thief. “He had a grey hoodie on,” she said.Sotuvchi o‘g‘rini esladi: “U kulrang kapyushonli kofta kiygan edi”, dedi.
  • Where’s my scarf? You’ve got it on!Sharfim qani? Sen uni kiyib olibsan-ku!

have the TV / the radio on ham bor: “televizor yoqib qo‘yilgan”. They always have the TV on while they eat dinner. (Ular ovqatlanayotganda doim televizorni yoqib qo‘yishadi.) Yana norasmiy ma’no informal: have sth on = rejasi bor, band. I’ve got a lot on this week. (Bu hafta ishim ko‘p.) Have you got anything on tonight? (Bugun kechqurun rejangiz bormi?)

  • My uncle lost weight after he stopped eating sweets.Amakim shirinlik yeyishni to‘xtatgach ozdi.

Yo‘lga chiqish va transport: set off, take off, get on / get off Journeys and transport

set off — yo‘lga chiqmoq ∅

set off — safarni boshlamoq, yo‘lga chiqmoq. Qayerga ketilishi for bilan aytiladi: set off for Khiva. Safar nomi bilan: set off on a journey. Ma’nodoshlari: leave (oddiy), set out (ko‘proq uzoq safar haqida), rasmiy: depart.

  • We set off at five in the morning, while it was still cool.Hali salqin paytda, ertalab soat beshda yo‘lga chiqdik.
  • Sardor set off for Khiva on Friday.Sardor juma kuni Xivaga yo‘lga chiqdi.
  • They set off on a long journey across the Kyzylkum Desert.Ular Qizilqum cho‘li bo‘ylab uzoq safarga chiqishdi.

set off o‘zbekchadagi yo‘lga chiqmoq ga aniq mos keladi. Tuzoq: ❌ We set off the journey — safar to‘ldiruvchi emas; to‘g‘risi: We set off yoki We set off on the journey.

set off — signalni ishga tushirmoq ⇄

set off ning ikkinchi ma’nosi — signal, bomba yoki otashbozlikni ishga tushirmoq (u go off qiladi). Bu ma’noda to‘ldiruvchi bor va fe’l ajraladi: set off the alarm / set the alarm off / set it off.

  • Burnt toast set off the smoke alarm.Kuygan non tutun signalini ishga tushirdi.
  • Don’t smoke here; you’ll set the alarm off.Bu yerda chekmang; signalni ishga tushirib yuborasiz.

take off — samolyot ko‘tarilmoq ∅

Samolyot yerdan uzilib havoga ko‘tarilsa — take off (to‘ldiruvchisiz). Teskarisi — land (bitta so‘z). “Yechmoq” ma’nosi B bo‘limda: u yerda take off ajraladi.

  • Our flight to Tashkent took off half an hour late because of fog.Toshkentga reysimiz tuman sababli yarim soat kechikib ko‘tarildi.
  • The plane took off at eight and landed at ten.Samolyot soat sakkizda ko‘tarilib, o‘nda qo‘ndi.

get on / get off — avtobus, poyezd, samolyot, velosiped

Katta yoki “ustiga chiqiladigan” transportga chiqish — get on, undan tushish — get off. Kichik, yopiq transportga (mashina, taksi) o‘tirish uchun get in(to) / get out (of) ishlatiladi (Unit 138).

get on / get off

bus, coach, tram, train, metro, plane, ship, ferry, bicycle, motorbike, horse

  • Nodira got on the bus at Chorsu.
  • Everybody got off at the last stop.

get in(to) / get out (of)

car, taxi, van, lorry

  • Sardor got into the taxi.
  • She got out of the car.
  • Nodira got on the bus at Chorsu and got off near the university.Nodira avtobusga Chorsuda chiqib, universitet yonida tushdi.
  • Excuse me, where should I get off for the museum?Kechirasiz, muzeyga borish uchun qayerda tushishim kerak?
  • The train was so crowded that we couldn’t get on.Poyezd shu qadar gavjum ediki, biz chiqa olmadik.
  • Kamola got on her bicycle and rode away.Kamola velosipedini minib, jo‘nab ketdi.

get on / get off bu ma’noda ✗ ajralmaydi: on / off bu yerda predlog, to‘ldiruvchi undan keyin turadi. Olmosh ham: The bus was empty, so we got on it. To‘ldiruvchi aytilmasa (joy ma’lum) — Nodira got on at Chorsu. Bu unit oxiridagi “qiyoslash” jadvalida ko‘ring. get on ning boshqa ma’nolari (muvaffaqiyat, til topishish) — Unit 141 da.

O‘zbekchada “avtobusga chiqmoq, avtobusdan tushmoq” deymiz: chiqmoq = get on, tushmoq = get off. Shuning uchun ❌ get off from the bus demang — “-dan” ni from deb tarjima qilmang; to‘g‘risi: get off the bus.

Joylar haqida batafsil — Unit 123, Unit 124 va Unit 125 (in / at / on, joy predloglari).

Rejalar va tadbirlar: call off, put off, be on / be off Plans and events

Rejalashtirilgan voqea (o‘yin, yig‘ilish, konsert, to‘y) haqida gapirganda uch holat bo‘lishi mumkin: u bo‘ladi (be on), bekor qilinadi (call off / be off) yoki keyinga suriladi (put off).

call off — bekor qilmoq ⇄

call off — rejalashtirilgan voqeani bekor qilmoq: u endi bo‘lmaydi. Ko‘pincha majhul nisbatda keladi (Unit 42): The match was called off. Rasmiyroq: cancel.

  • The school called off the trip to Samarkand because the bus had broken down.Avtobus buzilib qolgani uchun maktab Samarqandga sayohatni bekor qildi.
  • The strike was called off at the last moment.Ish tashlash oxirgi daqiqada bekor qilindi.
  • It’s too windy. Let’s call the picnic off.Shamol juda kuchli. Piknikni bekor qilaylik.

put off — keyinga qoldirmoq ⇄

put off — voqeani keyinroqqa surmoq: u keyin bo‘ladi. Qaysi vaqtgacha ekanini until / till bilan aytamiz. Agar kechiktiriladigan narsa ish bo‘lsa, fe’ldan keyin -ing keladi: put off doing sth (fe’ldan keyin -ing haqida — Unit 53). Rasmiyroq: postpone, delay.

  • The meeting has been put off until next Thursday.Yig‘ilish kelasi payshanbagacha qoldirildi.
  • Malika put off her trip to Samarkand because her mother was ill.Onasi kasal bo‘lgani uchun Malika Samarqandga safarini keyinga qoldirdi.
  • We’ll have to put the football tournament off till spring.Futbol turnirini bahorgacha qoldirishimizga to‘g‘ri keladi.
  • Don’t put off doing your homework until the last minute.Uy vazifasini oxirgi daqiqagacha qoldirmang.

Inglizcha put off o‘zbekchadagi keyinga qoldirmoq (keyinga surmoq) ga mos keladi. call off esa bekor qilmoq. Ikkalasini aralashtirmang: qoldirilgan voqea keyin bo‘ladi, bekor qilingani bo‘lmaydi.

put sb off — qaytarmoq, havasini qochirmoq ⇄

put sb off (sth / doing sth) — kimnidir biror narsadan sovitmoq, qaytarmoq: odam endi uni xohlamaydi yoki qilishni istamaydi. To‘ldiruvchi — odam: olmosh bo‘lsa, u doim put bilan off orasida turadi (put me off, put them off); ot ko‘pincha ham o‘rtada keladi, lekin oxirida ham bo‘lishi mumkin: The high prices put off many customers. Majhul nisbatda: be put off (by sth).

  • The price put me off, so I didn’t buy the jacket.Narx meni qaytardi, shuning uchun kurtkani olmadim.
  • One bad lesson shouldn’t put you off learning English.Bitta yomon dars sizning ingliz tilini o‘rganishdan ko‘nglingizni sovutmasligi kerak.
  • The smell of the fish put us off our lunch.Baliq hidi tushlik ishtahamizni qochirdi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Turn on” bilan “switch on” o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil, ikkalasi ham ajraladi: turn the light on = switch the light on. switch tugma yoki tumbler bilan ishga tushadigan narsalar uchun; turn burab ochiladigan jo‘mrak, gaz va suv uchun ham ishlatiladi. Qaysi birini tanlashga ikkilansangiz — turn: u hamma joyda to‘g‘ri.

Nega “put on” bir paytda “kiymoq”, “yoqmoq” va “semirmoq” degani?

Chunki ma’no to‘ldiruvchiga bog‘liq: kiyim, ko‘zoynak, soat → kiymoq, taqmoq; chiroq, isitgich, choynak, musiqa → yoqmoq, qo‘ymoq; weight yoki kilogramm → semirmoq. Ularning teskarisi ham har xil: take off (yechmoq), turn off / switch off (o‘chirmoq), lose weight (ozmoq). put off hech qaysisining teskarisi emas.

“Put on” bilan “wear” o‘rtasidagi farq nimada?

put on — kiyish harakati (bir lahza): She put on her coat and left. wear — kiyib yurish holati: She wears a coat in winter. She is wearing a red coat today. have … on ham holat: She has a red coat on. Continuous shaklda (is wearing) wear ishlatiladi; have on continuous bo‘lmaydi.

“Call off” bilan “put off” ning farqi nimada? “Cancel” va “postpone” bilan bir xilmi?

Ha. call off = cancel (voqea bo‘lmaydi); put off = postpone / delay (voqea keyin bo‘ladi). Kundalik nutqda frazali fe’llar ko‘proq, rasmiy yozuvda cancel, postpone ko‘proq. Ertaroq muddatga ko‘chirish — bring forward.

Nega “on the bus”, lekin “in the car”?

Avtobus, poyezd, samolyot kabi katta transportda yurish, turish mumkin — u “platforma”ga o‘xshaydi, shuning uchun on: get on the bus. Mashina va taksida esa siz kichik yopiq joyga o‘tirasiz, shuning uchun in: get in the car. Velosiped va ot ustida ham on ishlatiladi: get on a bicycle.

“Take off” ning nechta ma’nosi bor?

Ko‘p ishlatiladigan uchtasi: (1) yechmoq (kiyim, poyabzal) — ajraladi: take your shoes off; (2) samolyot ko‘tarilmoq — to‘ldiruvchisiz: The plane took off; (3) tez rivojlana boshlamoq (biznes, loyiha) — to‘ldiruvchisiz: His business took off. Yana bir ma’no — take time off, ya’ni “ishdan dam olmoq” (Unit 141).

“Go off” yana qanday ma’nolarda uchraydi?

Bu unitda: budilnik jiringlaydi, bomba portlaydi, elektr yoki isitish o‘chib qoladi. Sut yoki go‘sht buziladi degan ma’no ham bor: The milk has gone off — bu Unit 141 da. Sham yoki olov “o‘chsa” — go out (Unit 139).

“What’s on?” desam, nima so‘rayapman?

Kino, teatr, televizor yoki shahar tadbirlarida “nima qo‘yilyapti, nima ko‘rsatilyapti?” deb so‘rayapsiz: What’s on at the cinema? What’s on TV tonight? Javobda ham on: There’s a good film on. Voqea haqida Is it still on? — “u hali bo‘ladimi?”, javob: Yes, it’s still on / No, it’s off.

Ovozni baland yoki past qilishni qanday aytaman?

Bunda turn up (balandlatmoq) va turn down (pasaytirmoq) ishlatiladi: Turn the music down, please. Ular Unit 142 da. turn on / off esa faqat qurilmani yoqadi yoki o‘chiradi.

“Put off” dan keyin “to” bilan fe’l ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. put off dan keyin faqat -ing: I put off calling him ✅ — I put off to call him ❌. until / till bilan vaqt ham qo‘shish mumkin: She put off signing the contract until Monday. (Fe’ldan keyin -ing — Unit 53.)

🧠 Esda tuting

  • turn on / switch on = yoqmoq, turn off / switch off = o‘chirmoq (ajraladi: turn it off). “O‘chirmoq” uchun put off ishlatilmaydi: put off = kechiktirmoq.
  • put on: kiymoq (↔ take off) · yoqmoq, musiqa qo‘ymoq · put on weight (semirmoq). try on = kiyib ko‘rmoq. Kiyib turish (holat): wear yoki have … on.
  • go off: budilnik jiringlaydi, bomba portlaydi, elektr o‘chadi (go off — sut buzilmoq: Unit 141). set off: yo‘lga chiqmoq; signalni ishga tushirmoq.
  • take off: yechmoq (ajraladi) · samolyot ko‘tarilmoq (to‘ldiruvchisiz).
  • get on / get off a bus, train, plane, bicycle — ajralmaydi (get off the bus, ❌ from). Mashina, taksi — get in(to) / get out of.
  • call off = bekor qilmoq (voqea bo‘lmaydi) · put off = keyinga qoldirmoq (put off doing); put sb off = qaytarmoq.
  • be on = ko‘rsatilmoqda, bo‘ladi (What’s on?) · be off = bekor. Olmosh doim o‘rtada: call it off, try them on.
