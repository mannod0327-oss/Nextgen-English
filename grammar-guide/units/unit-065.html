65

Adjective + to ...

Sifat + to ...
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • difficult to understand, easy to use, safe to drink — sifat + to + fe’l, va nega He is difficult to understand him deyilmaydi.
  • It’s nice of you to ... — birovning qilgan ishiga baho berish (kind of you, silly of me, typical of him).
  • I’m sorry to hear ..., glad to see ..., surprised to find ... — biror narsaga bo‘lgan munosabat.
  • the first / the last / the next / the only ... to do — “birinchi bo‘lib qilgan”, “oxirgi bo‘lib ketgan”.
  • sure to, certain to, likely to, unlikely to, bound to — ehtimollik haqida gapirish.

Bu darsning asosiy g‘oyasi oddiy: ingliz tilida sifatdan keyin ko‘pincha to + fe’l (infinitive) keladi. Lekin bu birikma bir necha xil ma’noda ishlatiladi va har birining o‘z “tuzog‘i” bor. Masalan, difficult to understand da oxirida to‘ldiruvchi takrorlanmaydi, nice of you da esa of, for emas.

O‘zbek tilida bunday ma’no ko‘pincha harakat nomi (-ish) + sifat bilan beriladi: tushunish qiyin, ichish xavfsiz, foydalanish oson. Ingliz tilida tartib teskari: avval sifat, keyin to + fe’l: difficult to understand, safe to drink, easy to use. O‘zbekcha “Uni tushunish qiyin” gapini inglizchada ikki xil aytish mumkin: It is difficult to understand him. yoki He is difficult to understand.

difficult to understand, easy to use He is difficult to understand. This water is safe to drink.

Jasur juda tez va noaniq gapiradi. Buni ikki xil aytish mumkin:

  • Jasur speaks very fast. It is difficult to understand him.Jasur juda tez gapiradi. Uni tushunish qiyin.
  • Jasur speaks very fast. He is difficult to understand.Jasur juda tez gapiradi. Uni tushunish qiyin. (so‘zma-so‘z: “U tushunish uchun qiyin”.)

Ikkala gapning ma’nosi bir xil. Ikkinchi gapda birinchi gapdagi to‘ldiruvchi (him) gapning egasiga aylandi (He). Shuning uchun gap oxirida u qaytadan yozilmaydi.

It+be+sifat+to + fe’l+to‘ldiruvchi
ega (sobiq to‘ldiruvchi)+be+sifat+to + fe’l.

Bu tuzilmada ko‘p ishlatiladigan sifatlar:

Predlog gap oxirida qoladi

Agar fe’l predlog bilan ishlatilsa (talk to, sit on, live in, listen to), ikkinchi usulda predlog gap oxirida qoladi:

  • My grandfather is very interesting to listen to. (= It’s interesting to listen to him.)Bobomni tinglash juda qiziqarli.
  • This old chair isn’t very comfortable to sit on.Bu eski stulda o‘tirish unchalik qulay emas.
  • Is this lake safe to swim in?Bu ko‘lda suzish xavfsizmi?
  • Dilnoza is very easy to talk to.Dilnoza bilan gaplashish juda oson (u ochiq odam).

Sifat + ot + to ...

Xuddi shu tuzilma sifat + ot bilan ham ishlatiladi. Bu yerda ham oxirida to‘ldiruvchi takrorlanmaydi:

  • That’s a hard word to pronounce. (to pronounce it)Bu talaffuz qilish qiyin bo‘lgan so‘z.
  • Bukhara is a wonderful city to visit.Buxoro — borib ko‘rishga arziydigan ajoyib shahar.
  • It was an exciting match to watch.Bu tomosha qilish maroqli bo‘lgan o‘yin edi.

Qoida: Ikkinchi usulda (He is difficult to understand) gap egasi aslida fe’lning to‘ldiruvchisi. Shuning uchun gap oxiriga him / her / it / them qo‘yilmaydi: These boxes were impossible to lift ✅ — impossible to lift them ❌.

Kim uchun? — for + kishi

Ish kim uchun qiyin yoki oson ekanini aytish kerak bo‘lsa, for + kishi qo‘shiladi:

  • It’s difficult for me to get up early in winter.Qishda erta turish men uchun qiyin.
  • This book is easy for children to read.Bu kitobni bolalar oson o‘qiydi.

Diqqat: ikkinchi usul (He is difficult to understand) hamma sifat bilan ishlamaydi. U asosan easy, hard, difficult, impossible, safe, dangerous, cheap, expensive, nice, interesting kabi sifatlar bilan ishlatiladi. possible, necessary, important bilan esa faqat It li gap mumkin:
✅ It is possible to read this book in a day. — ❌ This book is possible to read in a day.
✅ It is important to check your answers. — ❌ Your answers are important to check.
Qiziq: impossible bilan mumkin (This book is impossible to read ✅), possible bilan esa yo‘q!

It’s nice of you to ... Saying what we think of somebody’s action

Birov biror ish qildi va biz bu ish haqida (va shu odam haqida) fikr bildirmoqchimiz: “yaxshi qildingiz”, “ahmoqlik qildim”, “bu uning odati”. Buning uchun It + be + sifat + of + kishi + to + fe’l ishlatiladi:

It+is / was+sifat+of+kishi+to + fe’l
  • It was very kind of you to lend me your car. Thank you!Menga mashinangizni berib turganingiz uchun katta rahmat — juda mehribonlik qildingiz!
  • It was careless of me to leave the door unlocked.Eshikni qulflamay ketganim beparvolik bo‘libdi.
  • It was silly of Sardor to go out without a coat. It was freezing.Sardor paltosiz chiqib ketib, ahmoqlik qilibdi. Havo juda sovuq edi.
  • It was brave of Dilnoza to speak in front of the whole school.Butun maktab oldida gapirgani — Dilnozaning jasorati.
  • It was rude of him not to say goodbye.U xayrlashmay ketib, qo‘pollik qildi.
  • Bekzod is late again. It’s typical of him!Bekzod yana kechikdi. Bu uning odati!
Bu tuzilmada ko‘p keladigan sifatlar:
nicekindgoodgenerouspolitethoughtfulcleversensiblebravecarelesssillystupidrudemeanunfairwrongtypical

Qisqa javob sifatida ham juda ko‘p ishlatiladi:

  • “I’ve brought you some flowers.” “Oh, how kind of you!”— Sizga gul olib keldim. — Voy, qanday mehribonsiz!
  • “I’ll help you carry your bags.” “That’s very nice of you.”— Sumkalaringizni ko‘tarishga yordamlashaman. — Rahmat, juda yaxshi odamsiz.

Xuddi shu fikrni kishi + be + sifat + to ... shaklida ham aytish mumkin: It was stupid of me to trust him = I was stupid to trust him. (Unga ishonib ahmoqlik qildim.) Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

of yoki for? — eng ko‘p chalkashadigan joy

Kishi haqida baho: u qanday odam ekani uning ishidan ko‘rinadi.

  • It was kind of Aziz to help me. (Aziz — mehribon)
  • It was stupid of me to forget. (men — ahmoq)

Ish haqida baho: ish shu kishi uchun qanday (oson, qiyin, muhim).

  • It was easy for Aziz to help me. (ish — oson)
  • It’s important for you to sleep. (ish — muhim)

I’m sorry to hear ..., glad to see ... Adjective + to ... for reactions

Biror narsani eshitganda, ko‘rganda yoki bilib olganda qanday his qilganimizni aytish uchun ham sifat + to ishlatiladi:

  • I was sorry to hear that you didn’t get the job.O‘sha ishga olinmaganingizni eshitib, xafa bo‘ldim.
  • After three weeks abroad, Aziz was glad to be back in Tashkent.Uch hafta chet elda bo‘lgach, Aziz Toshkentga qaytganidan xursand edi.
  • Kamola was surprised to see her old teacher at the wedding.Kamola to‘yda eski o‘qituvchisini ko‘rib hayron bo‘ldi.
  • I was disappointed to learn that the concert had been cancelled.Konsert bekor qilinganini bilib, hafsalam pir bo‘ldi.
  • We were relieved to find that nobody was hurt.Hech kim jarohat olmaganini bilib, yengil tortdik.
  • Pleased to meet you. / Nice to meet you.Tanishganimdan xursandman.
Munosabat sifatlari:
sorrygladpleasedhappydelightedsadsurprisedamazedastonishedshockeddisappointedrelieved

Ko‘pincha ulardan keyin hear, see, find, learn, know, get, meet, read fe’llari keladi.

Tayyorlik: happy to help, glad to help

  • If you need anything, just ask. I’ll be happy to help.Biror narsa kerak bo‘lsa, ayting. Bajonidil yordam beraman.
  • Nodira is always ready to listen to her friends’ problems.Nodira do‘stlarining muammolarini tinglashga doim tayyor.

O‘zbekchada munosabat ko‘pincha -ib yoki -ganimdan bilan beriladi: eshitib xafa bo‘ldim, ko‘rganimdan xursandman, bilib hayron qoldim. Inglizchada esa tartib: his-tuyg‘u sifati + to + fe’l: I was sorry to hear ..., I’m glad to see you, I was surprised to learn ... “-gan” qo‘shimchasini ko‘rib, for seeing yoki of hearing deb tarjima qilmang.

the first / the last / the next / the only ... to ... the first person to walk on the moon

the first, the second, the last, the next, the only dan keyin to + fe’l kelishi mumkin. Bu “birinchi bo‘lib ...gan / oxirgi bo‘lib ...gan / ...gan yagona” degan ma’noni beradi:

the first / the last / the next / the only+(ot / one)+to + fe’l
  • Who was the first person to walk on the moon? — Neil Armstrong.Oyda birinchi bo‘lib yurgan odam kim edi? — Nil Armstrong.
  • Valentina Tereshkova was the first woman to fly into space.Valentina Tereshkova kosmosga uchgan birinchi ayol edi.
  • Aziz is always the last to leave the office.Aziz har doim ishxonadan eng oxirida ketadi.
  • Kamola was the only student to answer every question correctly.Hamma savolga to‘g‘ri javob bergan yagona talaba — Kamola edi.
  • The next flight to leave for Istanbul is at 9.40.Istanbulga uchadigan keyingi reys soat 9:40 da.
  • Bekzod was the second (one) to finish the test.Testni ikkinchi bo‘lib Bekzod tugatdi.

O‘zbekchada bu ma’no sifatdosh (-gan, -adigan) bilan beriladi: oyda yurgan birinchi odam, ishdan ketadigan oxirgi kishi. Inglizchada sifatdosh o‘rniga oddiygina to + fe’l qo‘yiladi va u o‘tmish, hozir, kelajak uchun bir xil: the first to walk (yurgan), the last to leave (ketadigan).

Orttirma daraja bilan ham shunday: She was the youngest player to win the tournament. (U turnirda g‘olib bo‘lgan eng yosh o‘yinchi edi.)

sure to, certain to, likely to, bound to Something is sure / likely to happen

Biror narsaning sodir bo‘lishi qanchalik ehtimol ekanini aytish uchun be + sure / certain / likely / unlikely / bound + to + fe’l ishlatiladi:

  • Malika has trained every day for months. She’s sure to win the race.Malika oylab har kuni mashq qildi. U poygada albatta g‘olib bo‘ladi.
  • The final is certain to be exciting.Final o‘yini, shubhasiz, qiziqarli bo‘ladi.
  • The meeting is likely to finish late, so don’t wait for me.Majlis kech tugashi ehtimoli katta, shuning uchun meni kutmang.
  • It’s unlikely to rain tomorrow.Ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘ishi dargumon.
  • Don’t tell anyone about the surprise. Somebody’s bound to tell Sardor.Kutilmagan sovg‘a haqida hech kimga aytmang. Kimdir Sardorga albatta aytib qo‘yadi.
Ehtimollik shkalasi
TuzilmaMa’nosiBoshqacha aytilishi
be sure / certain / bound toalbatta, aniq (~100%)She is sure to win. = I’m sure she will win.
be likely toehtimoli kattaHe’s likely to come. = He will probably come. / It’s likely that he’ll come.
be unlikely toehtimoli kam, dargumonIt’s unlikely to rain. = It probably won’t rain.

She is sure to win va She is sure (that) she will win farqli! Birinchisida gapiruvchi ishonchi: “Men aminmanki, u yutadi”. Ikkinchisida qizning o‘zi ishongan: “U yutishiga amin” (balki adashayotgandir). Shuningdek, probable bilan bunday tuzilma yo‘q: ❌ He is probable to come → ✅ He is likely to come / It is probable that he will come.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

Jasur is difficult to understand him.Jasur is difficult to understand. / It is difficult to understand Jasur.Ega (Jasur) aslida “understand” ning to‘ldiruvchisi — oxirida yana “him” qo‘yilmaydi.
Is this ladder safe to climb it?Is this ladder safe to climb?Yuqoridagi sabab: “this ladder” allaqachon gapda bor, “it” ortiqcha.
It was very kind for you to help me.It was very kind of you to help me.Kishining fe’l-atvoriga baho berilsa — of: kind of you, nice of you, silly of me.
It’s difficult of me to get up early.It’s difficult for me to get up early.Ish kim uchun qiyin/oson ekani — for + kishi.
This book is possible to read in a day.It is possible to read this book in a day.“possible” bilan faqat It li gap tuziladi (impossible bilan esa ikkalasi ham mumkin).
I was surprised for seeing him there.I was surprised to see him there.Munosabat sifatidan keyin to + fe’l keladi (surprised to see, sorry to hear).
She was the first finishing the test.She was the first to finish the test.the first / the last / the only dan keyin to + fe’l.
He is likely come tomorrow. / He is probable to come.He is likely to come tomorrow.likely dan keyin “to” majburiy; “probable” bunday ishlatilmaydi.
Thank you, it’s very nice from you.Thank you, that’s very nice of you.O‘zbekcha “sizdan” so‘zma-so‘z “from you” emas — “of you”.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“It is difficult to understand him” va “He is difficult to understand” — qaysi birini ishlatgan ma’qul?

Ma’no bir xil, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. Farq — nima haqida gapirayotganingizda. Agar gap Jasur haqida ketayotgan bo‘lsa, uni ega qilish tabiiyroq: Jasur? Oh, he’s difficult to understand. Agar umuman bir ish haqida gapirsangiz, It li gap qulayroq: It’s difficult to understand people who speak fast.

Nega gap oxiriga “him” yoki “it” qo‘yib bo‘lmaydi? O‘zbekchada “uni” deymiz-ku.

Chunki He is difficult to understand gapida He o‘zi “understand” ning to‘ldiruvchisi vazifasini bajaryapti. Bitta to‘ldiruvchini ikki marta yozish — xuddi “Men uni uni ko‘rdim” deyishdek. O‘zbekchada Uni tushunish qiyin gapida esa “uni” faqat bir marta keladi — inglizchada ham bir marta: yo boshida (He is ...), yo oxirida (It is ... him).

“of” va “for” ni qanday ajrataman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: baho kimga berilyapti? Agar sifat odamni ta’riflasa (u mehribon, ahmoq, saxiy, qo‘pol) — of: It was generous of your parents to pay. (Ota-onangiz saxiy.) Agar sifat ishni ta’riflasa (oson, qiyin, muhim, zarur) — for: It’s important for students to sleep well. (Uxlash — muhim.) Tekshirish usuli: Your parents were generous — mantiqli ✅, demak of. Students are important — bu boshqa ma’no ❌, demak for.

“the first person who walked on the moon” deb aytsam xato bo‘ladimi?

Xato emas, bu ham to‘g‘ri gap (nisbiy gap — Unit 92). Lekin the first / the last / the only dan keyin to + fe’l ancha qisqa va tabiiy: the first person to walk on the moon. Faqat the first walking yoki the first who walk deyish noto‘g‘ri.

“Nice to meet you” gapida ega va fe’l qayerda?

Bu qisqartirilgan gap: to‘liq shakli It’s nice to meet you. Og‘zaki nutqda It’s tushib qoladi. Xuddi shunday: (I’m) pleased to meet you, (I’m) glad to hear that, (It’s) good to see you.

“I’m glad to see you” va “I’m glad that you came” — ikkalasi ham to‘g‘rimi?

Ha. Ega bir xil bo‘lsa (men xursandman — men ko‘ryapman), to + fe’l qulay: I’m glad to see you. Ega boshqacha bo‘lsa (men xursandman — siz keldingiz), that li gap kerak: I’m glad (that) you came. “that” ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin.

“sure to” va “bound to” ning farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil — “albatta sodir bo‘ladi”. bound to og‘zaki, norasmiy nutqda ko‘proq ishlatiladi va ko‘pincha “muqarrar, bo‘lmay qolmaydi” ohangini beradi: If you leave your bike there, it’s bound to get stolen. (Velosipedni u yerda qoldirsangiz, uni albatta o‘g‘irlab ketishadi.)

“too difficult to” ham shu mavzugami?

Yaqin mavzu. too + sifat + to “haddan tashqari ..., shuning uchun qilib bo‘lmaydi” degani: This box is too heavy to carry. Bu yerda ham oxirida “it” qo‘yilmaydi. Batafsil — Unit 103.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Gapni boshqacha yozing: It is ... to ... → ... is ... to ...

  1. It’s hard to read Aziz’s handwriting. → Aziz’s handwriting .
  2. It isn’t easy to find this street. → This street .
  3. It was impossible to open the window. → The window .
  4. Is it safe to eat these mushrooms? → Are these mushrooms ?
  5. It’s really easy to get along with Kamola. → Kamola .
  6. It’s expensive to live in big cities. → Big cities .

2-mashq. of yoki for ni qo‘ying.

  1. It was nice you to call me on my birthday.
  2. It’s difficult me to get up early.
  3. It was careless Jasur to lose his passport.
  4. It isn’t easy young children to sit still for an hour.
  5. It was very generous your parents to pay for the trip.
  6. It’s important students to sleep well before an exam.

3-mashq. Qavs ichidagi so‘zlardan foydalanib gapni to‘ldiring (the first / the last / the only ... + to + fe’l).

  1. Nobody else got full marks. Kamola was full marks. (the only student / get)
  2. Neil Armstrong was on the moon. (the first person / walk)
  3. Aziz always works late. He’s usually the office. (the last / leave)
  4. Sardor is very helpful. He’s always help. (the first / offer)

4-mashq. Qavs ichidagi so‘zlar bilan gapni qayta yozing.

  1. I’m sure that Dilnoza will win. → Dilnoza . (sure / win)
  2. It will probably rain tomorrow. → It tomorrow. (likely / rain)
  3. The bus probably won’t be on time. → The bus on time. (unlikely / be)
  4. The shops will probably be closed on Navruz. → The shops closed on Navruz. (likely / be)

🧠 Esda tuting

  • It is difficult to understand him = He is difficult to understand. Ikkinchi gapda oxiriga him/it qo‘yilmaydi; predlog esa qoladi (interesting to talk to).
  • possible / necessary / important bilan faqat It li gap: It is possible to ...
  • Odamning ishiga baho: It was kind / silly / typical of + kishi + to ... (of, for emas!).
  • Ish kim uchun qanday: It’s easy / difficult / important for + kishi + to ...
  • Munosabat: sorry to hear, glad to see, surprised to learn, relieved to find.
  • the first / the last / the next / the only + to + fe’l = “birinchi / oxirgi / yagona bo‘lib ...gan”.
  • Ehtimollik: sure / certain / bound to (albatta), likely to (ehtimol), unlikely to (dargumon).
