Noun + preposition (reason for, cause of etc.)

Ot + predlog (reason for, cause of)
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • for bilan keladigan otlar: reason for, demand for, need for, a cheque for (summa).
  • of bilan: cause of, advantage / disadvantage of, a photo / picture / map / plan / drawing of.
  • in bilan: an increase / decrease / rise / fall / drop in prices va miqdor uchun of: an increase of 10%.
  • to bilan: invitation to, solution to, answer / reply to, key to, reaction to, attitude to / towards, damage to.
  • with va between bilan: connection / contact / relationship / link with (ikki tomon orasida: between), difference between.

Sifatlar va fe’llar kabi (Unit 130–136), ko‘p otlar ham o‘zidan keyin aniq bir predlogni talab qiladi: the reason for the delay, the cause of the fire. Predlogni mantiq bilan topib bo‘lmaydi, shuning uchun ot va predlogni bitta juftlik sifatida yodlaymiz. Tuzilishi shunday:

ot+predlog+ot / olmosh / fe’l-ing

O‘zbek tilida “kechikishning sababi”, “eshikning kaliti”, “yong‘inning sababi” kabi birikmalar -ning (qaratqich) bilan tuziladi. Shuning uchun o‘quvchi har safar of deb yuboradi: ❌ the reason of the delay, ❌ the key of the door. Aslida inglizchada of faqat ba’zi otlar bilan keladi (cause of), boshqalar bilan for (reason for) yoki to (key to) kerak. Jadvalga qarang:

For bilan: sabab, talab, ehtiyoj, chek reason for, demand for, need for, a cheque for

Reason for

Reason — biror ish yoki holatning izohi: nima uchun shunday bo‘ldi yoki qilindi. Undan keyin turli qolip kelishi mumkin:

Reason + qaysi qolip?
QolipMisolTarjima
reason for + otThe reason for the delay was a technical problem.Kechikish sababi texnik nosozlik edi.
reason for + -ingWhat is your reason for learning Korean?Koreys tilini o‘rganishingizning sababi nima?

Demand for, need for

  • There is a growing demand for electric cars in Tashkent.Toshkentda elektromobillarga talab ortib bormoqda.
  • Is there much demand for hand-made carpets this year?Bu yil qo‘lda to‘qilgan gilamlarga talab kattami?

O‘zbekchada “…ga talab”, “…ga ehtiyoj” deymiz, shuning uchun -ga ni to deb tarjima qilib yuborish oson: ❌ demand to cars, ❌ need to a jacket. Inglizchada ikkalasida ham for.

Need haqida: no need for a taxi (ot bilan) = no need to take a taxi (fe’l bilan, bu yerda to — infinitiv). need of esa odatda faqat qotib qolgan in need of (“…ga muhtoj”) iborasida keladi: The old roof is in need of repair.

A cheque for (summa)

Chek, hisob-kitob yoki to‘lov hujjatining summasi ham for bilan aytiladi:

  • Bekzod wrote the builder a cheque for two million soums.Bekzod quruvchiga ikki million so‘mlik chek yozib berdi.
  • The invoice for the repairs arrived yesterday.Ta’mirlash uchun hisob-faktura kecha keldi.
  • Please keep the receipt for your new phone.Iltimos, yangi telefoningiz uchun kvitansiyani saqlab qo‘ying.

BrE / AmE: Britaniya inglizchasida cheque, Amerika inglizchasida check: a check for $200. Ikkalasida ham predlog for.

Of bilan: sabab, afzallik, rasm, xarita cause of, advantage / disadvantage of, a photo / map / plan of

Cause of

Cause — voqeani keltirib chiqargan narsa (yong‘in, avtohalokat, kasallik, muammo). Undan keyin of:

  • The cause of the fire was a short circuit.Yong‘inning sababi qisqa tutashuv edi.
  • Speeding was the main cause of the accident.Tezlikni oshirish halokatning asosiy sababi edi.
  • Doctors still do not know the cause of her illness.Shifokorlar hali ham uning kasalligi sababini bilmaydi.

reason for …

Nima uchun shunday bo‘lgani yoki qilingani izohi.

  • the reason for the delay
  • his reason for leaving
  • a good reason for being late

cause of …

Voqeani keltirib chiqargan narsa.

  • the cause of the fire
  • the cause of death
  • the cause of the problem

Qotib qolgan iboralarda cause for ham uchraydi: There is no cause for alarm (“vahimaga asos yo‘q”), cause for concern. Bu yerda “his-tuyg‘uga asos, sabab” ma’nosi bor. ❌ the cause for the accident deyilmaydi: cause of.

Advantage of, disadvantage of

  • The main advantage of this flat is its location.Bu kvartiraning asosiy afzalligi uning joylashuvi.
  • What is the advantage of living in the old city?Eski shaharda yashashning qanday afzalligi bor?
  • A disadvantage of this phone is its short battery life.Bu telefonning kamchiligi — batareyasi tez tugashi.

In va of: o‘sish va kamayish an increase / decrease / rise / fall / drop in … (of …)

Biror narsa ko‘paysa yoki kamaysa, ikki narsani aytish mumkin: nima o‘zgargani va qanchaga o‘zgargani. Ular ikki xil predlog bilan beriladi:

Increase, rise, fall, drop, decrease + in / of
Ot… in + nima… of + miqdor
an increase / a riseThere was a sharp rise in the price of petrol.
Benzin narxi keskin oshdi.		There was a rise of 10% last year.
O‘tgan yili 10 foizlik o‘sish bo‘ldi.
a decrease / a fall / a dropThere has been a fall in the number of visitors.
Tashrif buyuruvchilar soni kamaydi.		A drop of five degrees is expected tonight.
Bugun kechasi harorat besh daraja pasayishi kutilmoqda.
birgaThere was an increase of 15% in the number of tourists. = There was a 15% increase in the number of tourists.
Sayyohlar sonida 15 foizlik o‘sish bo‘ldi.

Qoida: increase, decrease, rise, fall, drop, growth + in + o‘zgargan narsa; + of + miqdor (of 10%, of five degrees). ❌ a rise of prices, ❌ a rise in 10%.

Ot va fe’l: predlog o‘zgaradi
Fe’l (by)Ot (of)
Prices rose by 10%. Narxlar 10 foizga oshdi.There was a rise of 10% in prices. Narxlarda 10 foizlik o‘sish bo‘ldi.
Sales fell by a third. Sotuv uchdan bir qismga kamaydi.There was a fall of a third in sales. Sotuv uchdan bir qismga kamaydi (pasayish bo‘ldi).

BrE / AmE: maosh oshishi — Britaniya inglizchasida a pay rise, Amerika inglizchasida a raise: She asked her boss for a pay rise. Fe’l bilan by — Unit 128 da.

O‘zbekchada “narxlardagi o‘sish” (-dagi = in) va “10 foizlik o‘sish” (-lik = of) bir-biridan farq qiladi, bu esa inglizchaga to‘g‘ri mos keladi. Xatolar ko‘pincha o‘rinlarni almashtirishdan kelib chiqadi: ❌ an increase of prices, ❌ an increase in 10%.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Reason for” bilan “cause of” ning farqi nimada?

Reason — biror ish yoki holatning izohi: the reason for the delay, the reason for his absence. Cause — voqeani keltirib chiqargan narsa: the cause of the fire, the cause of the accident. Savol sifatida: reason — “nima uchun shunday qilindi?”; cause — “nima buni sodir qildi?”. Ikkalasining predlogi har xil: reason for, cause of.

“Increase in” bilan “increase of” ni qanday ajrataman?

In dan keyin nima o‘zgargani keladi (increase in prices, a fall in the number of visitors). Of dan keyin qanchaga o‘zgargani keladi (an increase of 10%). Ikkalasi bitta gapda ham kelishi mumkin: an increase of 10% in prices yoki a 10% increase in prices.

“Increase by” ham to‘g‘rimi? Uning “increase of” dan farqi bor?

Ha, to‘g‘ri, lekin so‘z turkumi har xil. Increase fe’l bo‘lsa — by: Prices increased by 10%. Increase ot bo‘lsa — of: There was an increase of 10%. Fe’l bilan of, ot bilan by ishlatilmaydi.

Attitude to yoki attitude towards — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, ma’nosi bir xil. Britaniya inglizchasida attitude to / towards, Amerika inglizchasida ko‘pincha attitude toward (s siz). Imtihonda ikkalasi ham qabul qilinadi. Faqat ❌ attitude of demang (attitude of mind kabi boshqa ma’no bor).

“Advantage of”, “advantage in”, “advantage to” va “advantage over” — qaysi birini aytaman?

Umumiy afzallik haqida: the advantage of + ot / -ing (the advantage of living alone). “Bunda afzallik bor / yo‘q” deganda there is an advantage in / to + -ing (There is no advantage in waiting). Biror kishidan ustunlik: advantage over (an advantage over other candidates). Qiyin bo‘lsa, birinchisidan foydalaning: advantage of.

“A plan of” bilan “a plan for” ning farqi nimada?

A plan of — chizma, nimaning tuzilishini ko‘rsatadi (a plan of the house). A plan for — niyat, rejalashtirilgan ish (a plan for the weekend). Xuddi shunday: a map of Samarkand (xarita), lekin a reason for the delay.

“Relationship with” va “relationship between” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham. Bir kishi nuqtai nazaridan: my relationship with my sister. Ikki tomon birga aytilsa: the relationship between me and my sister yoki between the two sisters. Connection, contact, link ham xuddi shunday ishlaydi.

Ot va predlog juftliklarini qanday yodlayman?

Qoida deyarli yo‘q, shuning uchun juftlikni to‘liq ibora sifatida yodlang: the key to the door, a cheque for $50, a rise in prices. Yangi ot o‘rgansangiz, lug‘atdagi misol gapni ham yozib oling. O‘zbekcha qo‘shimchaga (-ning, -ga) qarab predlogni taxmin qilmang: u ko‘pincha aldaydi (darsning boshidagi jadvalga qarang).

“Cheque” va “check” farqi nimada?

Bir xil so‘z, imlo har xil: Britaniya inglizchasida cheque, Amerika inglizchasida check. Predlog ikkalasida for: a cheque / check for $100.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • for: reason for, demand for, need for, a cheque (invoice, receipt) for + summa.
  • of: cause of, advantage / disadvantage of, a photo / picture / map / plan / drawing of.
  • in — nima o‘zgardi: a rise in prices; of — qanchaga: a rise of 10%. Fe’l bilan by: rose by 10%.
  • to: invitation to, solution to, answer to, reply to, key to, reaction to, attitude to / towards, damage to.
  • with / between: connection, contact, relationship, link with (ikki tomon: between … and …); difference between.
  • Predlogdan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida: reason for being late, key to learning.
  • O‘zbekcha -ning ≠ of: reason for, key to; damage sanalmaydi; answer fe’li predlogsiz.
