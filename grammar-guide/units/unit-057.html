Verb + -ing or to ... 2 (try/need/help)

Fe’l + -ing yoki to ... — 2 (try / need / help)
-ing and to ... · -ing shakli va to + fe’l

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • try to do (harakat qilmoq, urinmoq) va try doing (sinab ko‘rmoq) farqini.
  • need to do (kimdir biror ishni qilishi kerak) va need doing (narsaga nimadir qilinishi kerak) farqini.
  • help (to) do — to ni qo‘yish ham, tushirish ham mumkinligini.
  • can’t help doing — “o‘zimni tutolmayman, beixtiyor qilaman” degan iborani.

Unit 56 dagidek, bu fe’llar ham -ing bilan ham, to bilan ham keladi va ma’no o‘zgaradi. Bu safar farq “qaysi harakat oldin?” degan savolda emas — har bir fe’lning o‘z mantig‘i bor. Shuning uchun ularni birma-bir, misollar bilan o‘rganamiz.

Yaxshi xabar: o‘zbek tilida ham shunga o‘xshash farq bor! try to open = ochishga harakat qildim (urindim); try opening = ochib ko‘rdim. O‘zbekcha -ib ko‘rmoq deyarli doim inglizcha try + -ing ga to‘g‘ri keladi. can’t help laughing esa kulmasdan turolmadim / kulib yubordim degani.

try to do va try doing Try to ... / try -ing

try to do = harakat qilmoq, urinmoq

try to do something = biror ishni qilishga kuch sarflamoq, urinmoq. Ko‘pincha bu ish qiyin bo‘ladi va natija noaniq: uddalashingiz ham, uddalay olmasligingiz ham mumkin.

  • Bekzod tried to push the car out of the snow, but it wouldn’t move.Bekzod mashinani qordan itarib chiqarishga urindi, lekin u qimirlamadi.
  • Please try to be quiet on the stairs — the baby has just fallen asleep.Iltimos, zinapoyada shovqin qilmaslikka harakat qiling — chaqaloq hozirgina uxlab qoldi.
  • Nodira wanted to see the end of the match. She tried to stay awake, but she fell asleep in front of the TV.Nodira o‘yinning oxirini ko‘rmoqchi edi. U uxlamaslikka harakat qildi, lekin televizor oldida uxlab qoldi.
  • I’ll try to finish the report by Friday, but I can’t promise.Hisobotni jumagacha tugatishga harakat qilaman, lekin va’da berolmayman.

try doing = sinab ko‘rmoq (tajriba sifatida)

try doing something = muammoni hal qilish uchun biror ishni sinab ko‘rmoq: “Shunday qilib ko‘ray-chi, yordam berarmikan?” Bu yerda harakatni qilish oson — savol shundaki, u natija beradimi.

  • My little brother had hiccups. He tried holding his breath, but that didn’t help, so he drank some water.Ukamni hiqichoq tutdi. U nafasini ushlab ko‘rdi, lekin foydasi bo‘lmadi, shuning uchun suv ichdi.
  • “My laptop is very slow.” “Have you tried restarting it?”— Noutbukim juda sekin ishlayapti. — Uni o‘chirib-yoqib ko‘rdingmi?
  • If you can’t sleep, try drinking a glass of warm milk before bed.Uxlay olmasangiz, yotishdan oldin bir stakan iliq sut ichib ko‘ring.
  • Jasur didn’t answer his phone, so I tried sending him a message on Telegram.Jasur telefonini olmadi, shuning uchun unga Telegramda xabar yuborib ko‘rdim.

💪 try to do — urinish

Ishning o‘zi qiyin. Uddalay olasizmi?

  • I tried to open the jar, but the lid was too tight. — Bankani ochishga urindim, lekin qopqog‘i juda qattiq ekan (ochilmadi).

🧪 try doing — sinab ko‘rish

Ishni qilish oson. Lekin muammoni hal qiladimi?

  • I tried putting the jar under hot water, and then it opened easily. — Bankani issiq suv ostiga tutib ko‘rdim — keyin u osongina ochildi.

Tekshirish usuli: gapni o‘zbekchaga o‘girib ko‘ring. Agar “-ishga harakat qildim / urindim” mos kelsa — try to. Agar “-ib ko‘rdim” mos kelsa — try + -ing.

try + ot: tatib ko‘rmoq, sinab ko‘rmoq

try dan keyin ot ham kelishi mumkin. Ma’nosi — “sinab ko‘rmoq, tatib ko‘rmoq, kiyib ko‘rmoq”:

  • You must try this plov. My mother made it.Bu palovdan albatta tatib ko‘ring. Uni onam pishirgan.
  • We tried three shops, but none of them had the book.Uchta do‘konga kirib ko‘rdik, lekin hech birida o‘sha kitob yo‘q edi.
  • Can I try these shoes on?Bu tuflilarni kiyib ko‘rsam bo‘ladimi?

need to do va need doing Need to ... / need -ing

need to do: kimdir biror ishni qilishi kerak

I need to do something = men buni qilishim zarur. Bu yerda ega — ishni bajaradigan odam.

  • I need to charge my phone. The battery is almost dead.Telefonimni quvvatlashim kerak. Batareyasi deyarli tugadi.
  • Bekzod needs to practise more if he wants to pass his driving test.Bekzod haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘tmoqchi bo‘lsa, ko‘proq mashq qilishi kerak.
  • You don’t need to come early. We start at ten.Erta kelishing shart emas. Soat o‘nda boshlaymiz.

need doing: narsaga nimadir qilinishi kerak

Something needs doing = narsaga nimadir qilinishi kerak. Bu yerda ega — ishni bajaradigan odam emas, balki ish ustida bajariladigan narsa. Ma’nosi majhul nisbatga (passive) teng: needs doing = needs to be done.

  • After our trip to Chimgan the car is covered in mud. It needs washing. (= It needs to be washed.)Chimyonga borib kelganimizdan keyin mashina loyga belanib ketgan. Uni yuvish kerak.
  • Your hair is getting long. It needs cutting.Sochingiz uzun bo‘lib ketyapti. Uni oldirish kerak.
  • Do you think these trousers need ironing?Sizningcha, bu shimni dazmollash kerakmi?
  • We can’t decide this today. The plan needs discussing with the whole team.Buni bugun hal qila olmaymiz. Rejani butun jamoa bilan muhokama qilish kerak.
need: uch xil shakl
ShaklEga kim?MisolO‘zbekcha
need to + fe’lodam (bajaruvchi)I need to paint the fence.Men panjarani bo‘yashim kerak.
need + -ingnarsaThe fence needs painting.Panjarani bo‘yash kerak.
need to be + V3narsaThe fence needs to be painted.Panjarani bo‘yash kerak.

O‘zbekchada “Mashinani yuvish kerak” gapida ega yo‘q — kim yuvishi aytilmaydi. Ingliz tilida esa gapda ega bo‘lishi shart, shuning uchun narsaning o‘zi ega bo‘ladi: The car needs washing. ❌ Need to wash the car (egasiz) deb bo‘lmaydi.

The car needs to wash ❌ — bu “mashina (o‘zi) nimanidir yuvishi kerak” degan bema’ni ma’no beradi. Narsa ega bo‘lsa: needs washing yoki needs to be washed.

need doing Britaniya ingliz tilida juda tabiiy. Amerika ingliz tilida ham ishlatiladi, lekin u yerda needs to be done ko‘proq uchraydi. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri — shubhangiz bo‘lsa, needs to be + V3 ni ishlating.

help va can’t help help (to) do / can’t help doing

help + (to) + fe’l

help dan keyin fe’l to bilan ham, to siz ham kelishi mumkin. Ma’no bir xil:

help+(kimnidir)+(to)+fe’l
  • All the neighbours helped (to) plant trees in the park.Barcha qo‘shnilar parkda daraxt ekishga yordam berishdi.
  • Can you help me (to) fix my bike?Velosipedimni tuzatishga yordam berib yuborasanmi?
  • Kamola helped her grandmother (to) cook dinner for the whole family.Kamola buvisiga butun oila uchun kechki ovqat pishirishga yordam berdi.
  • Regular exercise helps (to) reduce stress.Muntazam jismoniy mashq stressni kamaytirishga yordam beradi.

Britaniya ingliz tilida ikkala variant ham keng tarqalgan. Amerika ingliz tilida ko‘pincha to siz aytiladi: Can you help me fix my bike? (AmE)

help dan keyin -ing qo‘yilmaydi: He helped me carry the bags ✅ — He helped me carrying the bags ❌.

can’t help + -ing = o‘zimni tutolmayman

I can’t help doing something = men buni qilmaslikka o‘zimni majburlay olmayman, beixtiyor qilib qo‘yaman. Bu yerda help “yordam bermoq” emas, balki “oldini olmoq, to‘xtatmoq” ma’nosida.

  • The film was so sad that I couldn’t help crying.Film shunchalik g‘amgin ediki, yig‘lamasdan turolmadim.
  • When Sardor saw his little sister in his huge coat, he couldn’t help laughing.Sardor singlisini o‘zining katta paltosida ko‘rib, kulmasdan turolmadi.
  • I can’t help thinking that we’ve made a mistake.Xato qildik, degan fikr xayolimdan ketmayapti.
  • Sorry I keep checking my phone — I’m waiting for my exam results. I can’t help it.Kechirasiz, telefonimga qarayveryapman — imtihon natijalarimni kutyapman. Nima qilay, o‘zimni tutolmayapman.
help ning ikki xil ishlatilishi
ShaklMa’nosiMisol
help (sb) (to) doyordam bermoqShe helped me (to) find a flat.
can’t / couldn’t help doingo‘zini tutolmaslik, beixtiyor qilmoqI couldn’t help smiling.
can’t help it“Nima qilay, qo‘limdan hech narsa kelmaydi”Sorry, I can’t help it.

O‘zbekchada bu ma’no inkor + -masdan turolmaslik bilan beriladi: kulmasdan turolmadim, so‘ramasdan turolmadim. Ingliz tilida esa inkor can’t / couldn’t da bo‘ladi, keyingi fe’l esa ijobiy -ing shaklida: couldn’t help laughing (couldn’t help not laughing ❌).

Qo‘shimcha: try and do, want doing Informal British English

Og‘zaki Britaniya ingliz tilida yana ikki shakl uchraydi. Ularni bilish kerak, chunki filmlar va suhbatlarda eshitasiz:

  • Try and come early tomorrow. (= Try to come early.)Ertaga erta kelishga harakat qil.
  • The grass wants cutting. (= The grass needs cutting.)O‘tni o‘rish kerak.

try and do faqat try ning asosiy shaklida ishlatiladi (try and ..., I’ll try and ..., Please try and ...). He tried and came ❌ — bunda He tried to come deyiladi. Yozma va rasmiy matnda doim try to ni ishlating.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

The door was locked. I tried opening it, but I couldn’t.The door was locked. I tried to open it, but I couldn’t.Ochishga urindingiz, lekin uddalay olmadingiz — bu try to. try opening = ochib ko‘rdim (ochildi), natijasini kuzatdim.
He tried and finished the report on time.He tried to finish the report on time.try and faqat try ning asosiy shaklida ishlatiladi (try and ..., I’ll try and ...). tried, tries, trying dan keyin faqat to.
The windows need to clean.The windows need cleaning. / The windows need to be cleaned.Derazalar o‘zi hech narsani tozalamaydi — ularni tozalash kerak. Narsa ega bo‘lsa: need + -ing yoki need to be + V3.
I need washing my car.I need to wash my car.Ega — odam (men yuvaman). Odam ega bo‘lsa: need to + fe’l.
Need to repair the roof.The roof needs repairing. / We need to repair the roof.Ingliz tilida gapda ega bo‘lishi shart. O‘zbekcha “Tomni tuzatish kerak” ni so‘zma-so‘z tarjima qilmang.
I couldn’t help to laugh.I couldn’t help laughing.“O‘zimni tutolmadim” ma’nosida can’t help dan keyin faqat -ing.
She helped me carrying the bags.She helped me carry / to carry the bags.“Yordam bermoq” ma’nosidagi help dan keyin (to) + fe’l, -ing emas.
I couldn’t help not laughing.I couldn’t help laughing.O‘zbekchadagi “kulmasdan” inkorini ko‘chirmang. Inkor faqat couldn’t da.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I tried calling you, but your phone was off” — bu to‘g‘rimi? Axir qo‘ng‘iroq bo‘lmadi-ku.

Ha, bu gap so‘zlashuvda juda keng tarqalgan va to‘g‘ri. Siz raqamni terdingiz (harakatni bajardingiz), lekin natija bo‘lmadi — buni “qo‘ng‘iroq qilib ko‘rdim” deb tushunish mumkin. I tried to call you ham to‘g‘ri va bir xil ma’no beradi. Farq faqat aniq vaziyatlarda muhim: ishning o‘zi qiyin bo‘lsa — to, oson ish natija beradimi degan savol bo‘lsa — -ing.

try to do va try doing ning farqini qanday tez eslab qolaman?

O‘zbekchaga o‘giring: -ishga harakat qilmoq / urinmoq = try to; -ib ko‘rmoq = try + -ing. Masalan: Uxlamaslikka harakat qildim → I tried to stay awake. Choy ichib ko‘rdim → I tried drinking tea.

“needs cleaning” va “needs to be cleaned” — qaysi biri yaxshiroq?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri va ma’nosi bir xil. needs cleaning Britaniya ingliz tilida, ayniqsa og‘zaki nutqda, juda tabiiy. needs to be cleaned esa hamma joyda (BrE va AmE) va har qanday uslubda to‘g‘ri — shuning uchun shubha bo‘lsa, shuni ishlating.

need ning “needn’t” shakli bilan bu darsdagi need bir xilmi?

Bu darsda need oddiy fe’l sifatida ishlatilgan: I need to go, She needs to go, Do you need to go? I don’t need to go. needn’t esa need ning modal fe’l sifatidagi shakli: You needn’t come (= You don’t need to come). Bu haqda Unit 32 da batafsil.

“help” dan keyin “to” ni qachon qo‘yish kerak?

Hech qachon shart emas — help (to) do ikkala variantda ham to‘g‘ri. Amerika ingliz tilida ko‘pincha to siz aytiladi. Ma’no farqi yo‘q, shuning uchun qaysi biri sizga qulay bo‘lsa, shuni ishlating.

“I can’t help but laugh” degan gapni eshitdim. Bu to‘g‘rimi?

Ha, can’t help but + fe’l (to siz, -ing siz) ham to‘g‘ri, ayniqsa Amerika ingliz tilida va adabiy uslubda: I couldn’t help but notice ... Ma’nosi couldn’t help noticing bilan bir xil. Lekin aralashtirib yubormang: couldn’t help but noticing ❌.

“Help yourself!” nimani anglatadi?

Bu mehmonga aytiladigan ibora: “Olavering, tortinmang!” (ovqat, ichimlik haqida). Masalan: There’s tea and samsa on the table. Help yourself! (Stolda choy va somsa bor. Olavering!) Bu yerda -ing yoki to umuman yo‘q.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. try + to yoki try + -ing: qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. Sardor tried (lift) the sack of rice, but he couldn’t even move it.
  2. “The printer isn’t working.” “Have you tried (turn) it off and on again?”
  3. Please try (be) on time tomorrow. The bus leaves at seven.
  4. If you can’t sleep, why don’t you try (drink) a glass of warm milk? It often helps.
  5. Aziz was very nervous, but he tried (stay) calm during the interview.
  6. The soup tasted a bit bland, so I tried (add) some black pepper. It was much better.

2-mashq. need + -ing yoki need to: gapni to‘ldiring. Narsa ega bo‘lsa, ikkala to‘g‘ri variantni ham yozing.

  1. We’ve just driven all the way from Khiva on dusty roads, and now the car (wash).
  2. Sardor can hardly see — his fringe is in his eyes. His hair (cut).
  3. My phone battery is at 3%. The phone (charge).
  4. These shirts are creased. They (iron).
  5. I (finish) this report by Friday.
  6. The concert is next week, so Malika (practise) the piano every day.

3-mashq. help yoki can’t help: to‘g‘ri shaklni yozing. Ikkala variant mumkin bo‘lsa, ikkalasini ham yozing.

  1. Could you help me (carry) these bags upstairs?
  2. The film was so sad that I couldn’t help (cry).
  3. I’m sorry I keep yawning. I can’t help .
  4. He’s so funny that you can’t help (laugh) at his jokes.
  5. Kamola helped her younger brother (do) his homework.

4-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘ri yozing.

  1. The windows need to clean. →
  2. I couldn’t help to laugh when he fell off his chair. →
  3. The door was locked. I tried opening it, but I couldn’t. →
  4. I need washing my car this weekend. →

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Mashinani yuvish kerak. (ega — the car) →
  2. Men kulmasdan turolmadim. →
  3. Derazani ochib ko‘ring. →
  4. Aziz menga uy vazifamni qilishga yordam berdi. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • try to do = harakat qilmoq, urinmoq (ish qiyin): I tried to lift it, but I couldn’t.
  • try doing = sinab ko‘rmoq (natija beradimi?): Try restarting it. O‘zbekcha -ib ko‘rmoq.
  • need to do — ega odam: I need to wash the car.
  • need doing = need to be done — ega narsa: The car needs washing. (needs to wash ❌)
  • help (sb) (to) do — to ixtiyoriy: She helped me (to) find it. -ing emas!
  • can’t / couldn’t help doing = o‘zini tutolmaslik: I couldn’t help laughing.
