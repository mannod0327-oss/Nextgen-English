Quite, pretty, rather and fairly

Quite, pretty, rather va fairly
Adjectives and adverbs · Sifatlar va ravishlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • quite, pretty, rather, fairly — sifat va ravishdan oldin keladigan, “ancha” ma’nosidagi daraja so‘zlari: very dan kuchsizroq, a bit dan kuchliroq.
  • Ularning farqi: fairly eng kuchsiz, rather kuchliroq, pretty esa norasmiy (kundalik so‘zlashuv).
  • a / an bilan so‘z tartibi: quite a nice day, a fairly nice day.
  • quite ning ikkinchi ma’nosi — “butunlay” (quite right) va not quite — “to‘liq emas”.
  • Inglizcha rather ning nozikliklari va Britaniya hamda Amerika ingliz tilidagi farq.

Bu so‘zlarning hammasi bitta shkalada turadi: ular a bit (biroz) dan kuchliroq, lekin very (juda) dan kuchsizroq. Ya’ni “yo‘q” ham emas, “juda” ham emas — “o‘rtadan yuqoriroq”.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no asosan bitta so‘z bilan beriladi: “ancha” (ancha sovuq, ancha qimmat, ancha yaxshi). Inglizchada esa to‘rtta so‘z bor va ular uslub hamda nozik ma’nosi bilan farq qiladi. Ko‘pincha hammasini “ancha” deb tarjima qilish mumkin. Yana bir ehtiyot bo‘lish kerak bo‘lgan joy: quite ba’zan “ancha”, ba’zan “butunlay, mutlaqo” ma’nosini beradi (quite right = mutlaqo to‘g‘ri). O‘zbekchada bu ikki ma’no ikki xil so‘z bilan aytiladi, shuning uchun adashmang.

Qoida: bu so‘zlar very bilan birga kelmaydi: quite very good ❌, very rather tired ❌. Bittasini tanlang: quite good yoki very good.

Bu so‘zlar odatda qiyoslanadigan darajali (ya’ni “ko‘p-kam” bo‘ladigan) sifatlar bilan ishlatiladi: cold, good, expensive, tall, interesting. Quite “o‘rtacha” va “butunlay” ma’nolarining farqi C bo‘limda.

Quite a + ot (sifatsiz) = “ajoyib, g‘aroyib”

Sifat bo‘lmasa, quite a / quite an + ot “oddiy emas, e’tiborga loyiq” degan ma’noni beradi:

  • That was quite a party!Bu ajoyib ziyofat bo‘ldi!
  • Bekzod is quite a singer.Bekzod zo‘r xonanda-ku.

Quite a lot, quite a few, quite a bit — qat’iy iboralar, “ancha ko‘p” ma’nosida: quite a lot of money, quite a few people (Unit 87).

Quite = “butunlay”; not quite Quite meaning “completely”; not quite

Quite ikki xil ma’noda keladi. Qaysi ma’no ekani sifat turiga bog‘liq:

1) Oddiy (darajali) sifat → “ancha”

  • quite good, quite tall, quite cold
  • quite expensive, quite tired
  • quite slowly, quite well

Ma’no: fairly ga yaqin — “ancha”.

2) Chegaraviy / mutlaq sifat → “butunlay”

  • quite right, quite wrong, quite true
  • quite sure, quite impossible, quite perfect
  • quite empty, quite exhausted

Ma’no: completely — “mutlaqo, butunlay”.

Ikkinchi guruhdagi sifatlar “yarim”, “ko‘p-kam” bo‘lmaydi: biror narsa yo butunlay bo‘sh, yo bo‘sh emas; yo butunlay to‘g‘ri, yo to‘g‘ri emas. Shuning uchun quite bunday sifatlarga “butunlay” ma’nosini beradi.

  • It’s quite impossible to finish this report by tomorrow.Bu hisobotni ertagacha tugatish mutlaqo mumkin emas.
  • The two photos are quite different.Ikkala surat butunlay boshqacha.

Not quite = “deyarli, lekin to‘liq emas”

Not quite “hali to‘liq emas, biroz yetmaydi” degan ma’noni beradi — maqsadga juda yaqin, lekin hali yetmagan:

  • “Have you finished packing?” “Not quite. I still need to find my passport.”— Narsalaringizni yig‘ib bo‘ldingizmi? — Hali to‘liq emas. Pasportimni topishim kerak.
  • I haven’t quite finished my homework.Uy vazifamni hali to‘liq tugatmadim (tugatishga oz qoldi).
  • I don’t quite understand this word.Bu so‘zni to‘liq tushunmadim.
  • The shirt isn’t quite big enough.Ko‘ylak yetarlicha katta emas (biroz kichik).

Diqqat: not quite bilan not very ni adashtirmang. Not very good — “unchalik yaxshi emas”. Not quite ready — “deyarli tayyor, lekin hali to‘liq emas”.

Britaniya ingliz tilida “Quite.” (yoki Quite so) — suhbatdoshga “aynan shunday, roziman” degan javob. “It’s very expensive.” “Quite.” Bu biroz rasmiy va eski uslubda eshitiladi.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

She is fairly a good teacher.She is a fairly good teacher.Fairly va pretty faqat a dan KEYIN keladi. Quite esa a dan oldin keladi, rather — oldin ham, keyin ham.
It was pretty a nice day.It was a pretty nice day. / It was quite a nice day.Pretty ham a dan keyin turadi.
The film was quite very good.The film was quite good. / The film was very good.Quite va very birga kelmaydi — bittasini tanlang.
My brother is quite taller than me.My brother is a bit taller than me. / My brother is much taller than me.Quite qiyosiy daraja bilan odatda kelmaydi. Qiyosiy darajadan oldin a bit, much, rather ishlatiladi (Unit 106).
I’m not quite understand.I don’t quite understand.Inkor do not bilan tuziladi; quite fe’l oldida turadi.
You are fairly right.You are quite right.“Mutlaqo haqsiz” ma’nosi uchun quite right deyiladi; fairly right tabiiy emas.
The hotel is fair expensive.The hotel is fairly expensive.Sifatni kuchaytirish uchun ravish fairly kerak; fair — sifat (adolatli).
I’m quite agree with you.I quite agree with you.Quite bilan fe’l (agree) ishlatilsa, am kerak emas.
She is a quite tall.She is quite tall.Sifatdan keyin ot bo‘lmasa, a qo‘yilmaydi. Ot bo‘lsa: She is quite a tall girl (quite — a dan oldin).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi biri kuchliroq: quite yoki rather?

Umuman olganda rather kuchliroq: fairly < quite < pretty < rather < very. Lekin bu aniq tartib emas: ma’no ohangga, gap mazmuniga va hatto so‘zlovchining mamlakatiga qarab biroz o‘zgaradi. Muhim narsa — ularning hammasi a bit dan kuchli va very dan kuchsiz.

“It’s quite good” degan gap maqtovmi yoki yo‘qmi?

Britaniya ingliz tilida bu “yomon emas, yaxshi” degani — odatda ijobiy baho, lekin “a’lo” ma’nosida emas. Ba’zan ohangga qarab “o‘rtacha” degan sovuq taassurot ham qoldirishi mumkin. Chin dildan maqtamoqchi bo‘lsangiz very good, really good, excellent deng.

Quite ni qachon “ancha”, qachon “butunlay” deb tushunaman?

Sifatga qarang. Agar sifat “ko‘p-kam” bo‘ladigan oddiy sifat bo‘lsa (good, tall, cold) — “ancha”. Agar sifat chegaraviy bo‘lsa (right, wrong, impossible, perfect, empty, exhausted) — “butunlay” (C bo‘lim).

Pretty bir vaqtda “chiroyli” va “ancha” bo‘la oladimi?

Ha, bu ikki alohida ma’no. Ot oldidagi sifat: a pretty girl (chiroyli qiz). Sifat yoki ravish oldida ravish: pretty good (ancha yaxshi). Qaysi ma’no ekani gap tuzilishidan ko‘rinadi.

“I quite like it” nima degani?

Britaniya ingliz tilida: “menga ancha yoqadi” — yaxshi, lekin ishtiyoq darajasi o‘rtacha. Agar “juda yoqadi” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz, I like it a lot yoki I really like it deng.

Quite ni qiyosiy daraja bilan ishlatsam bo‘ladimi (quite better)?

Odatda yo‘q: zamonaviy ingliz tilida quite qiyosiy daraja oldida deyarli ishlatilmaydi. O‘rniga: a bit better (biroz yaxshiroq), much better (ancha yaxshiroq), rather better. Quite a bit better iborasi esa to‘g‘ri (“ancha yaxshiroq”). Qiyosiy daraja haqida batafsil — Unit 105 va Unit 106.

Quite a few va quite a lot bu bo‘limga tegishlimi?

Ha, ular quite bilan tuzilgan qat’iy iboralar: quite a few = ancha ko‘p (sanaladigan), quite a lot (of) = ancha ko‘p. Batafsil — Unit 87.

1-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. It’s (a fairly long way / fairly a long way) from here.
  2. She is (pretty a good singer / a pretty good singer).
  3. We stayed in (quite a nice / quite nice a) small hotel.
  4. He is (a fairly / fairly a) experienced driver.
  5. They live in (a pretty / pretty a) big house.
  6. This is (quite a / a quite a) surprise!

2-mashq. Quite bu gapda qanday ma’noda? “ancha” yoki “butunlay” deb yozing.

  1. The film was quite good.
  2. You are quite wrong.
  3. It’s quite cold today.
  4. This is quite impossible.
  5. The room was quite empty.
  6. She is quite tall for her age.
  7. I’m quite sure that I locked the door.

3-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga quite yoki not quite ni yozing.

  1. “Is the plov ready?” “. Give it ten more minutes.”
  2. “Was Malika right?” “Yes, she was right.”
  3. I haven’t finished my homework.
  4. I don’t understand this word.
  5. He was exhausted, so he went straight to bed.

4-mashq. Qavsdagi izohga eng mos so‘zni yozing: pretty, fairly, rather, quite.

  3. (xohlaganimdan ortiq, yoqmaydigan) The room was small for four people.
  4. (“butunlay”) That’s impossible.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • quite, pretty, rather, fairly — “ancha”: a bit dan kuchli, very dan kuchsiz. Taxminiy tartib: fairly < quite < pretty < rather.
  • pretty — norasmiy; rather — ko‘pincha “xohlagandan / kutganimdan ortiq”; fairly — eng kuchsiz.
  • So‘z tartibi: quite a / rather a + sifat + ot; a fairly / a pretty + sifat + ot (fairly a ❌).
  • quite + oddiy sifat = “ancha”; quite + chegaraviy sifat (right, impossible, perfect, empty) = “butunlay”.
  • not quite = “to‘liq emas, biroz yetmaydi”: not quite ready.
  • Qiyosiy daraja bilan quite kelmaydi: a bit better, much better.
  • Amerika ingliz tilida ko‘pincha pretty; Britaniya ingliz tilida quite, rather, fairly.
