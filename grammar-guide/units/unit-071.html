71

Countable nouns with a/an and some

Sanaladigan otlar bilan a/an va some
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot (a book, an apple, a doctor) ingliz tilida yolg‘iz kela olmaydi: oldida a / an (yoki the, my, this ...) bo‘lishi shart.
  • a va an ni qanday tanlashni: harfga emas, tovushga qarab (a university, an hour).
  • Ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan ot a / an olmasligini: apples yoki some apples.
  • some nimani bildirishini: noaniq miqdor — “bir necha”, “bir oz”.
  • a / an ning boshqa o‘rinlarini: kasb va “bu kim / nima?” (She’s a nurse), sifat bilan (a nice day), “har bir” ma’nosida (twice a week).

Ingliz tilida birlikdagi sanaladigan otni “yalang‘och” qoldirib bo‘lmaydi. Har safar aniqlashimiz kerak: bu bitta noaniq narsami (a / an) yoki ma’lum narsami (the, my, this)? Bu darsda birinchisi — a / an va some — o‘rganiladi. The haqida Unit 72 da gaplashamiz.

O‘zbek tilida ot yolg‘iz kelaveradi: Menda mashina bor. U kitob o‘qiyapti. Akam shifokor. Bu gaplarni so‘zma-so‘z o‘girsak: ❌ I have car. She is reading book. My brother is doctor. To‘g‘risi: I have a car. She is reading a book. My brother is a doctor. O‘zbekchadagi bir (bir mashina, bir kitob) ko‘pincha a / an ga to‘g‘ri keladi, lekin o‘zbek tilida bir ni aytmasak ham gap to‘g‘ri bo‘laveradi, ingliz tilida esa a / an ni tushirib qoldirib bo‘lmaydi. Ko‘plikda esa o‘zbekcha kitoblar — inglizcha books: artikl kerak emas. “Bir necha, bir oz” ma’nosi uchun some ishlatiladi.

Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot: a / an Singular countable nouns need a / an

Ot bitta bo‘lsa va uni sanash mumkin bo‘lsa (pen, car, girl, idea), ingliz tilida uning oldida albatta aniqlovchi turadi. Eng oddiy aniqlovchi — a yoki an (“bir, qandaydir”).

a / an+birlikdagi sanaladigan ot
  • I need a pen.Menga ruchka kerak.
  • There is a mosque near our house.Uyimiz yaqinida masjid bor.
  • Have you got a brother or a sister?Aka-ukangiz yoki opa-singlingiz bormi?
  • Sardor wants a bicycle for his birthday.Sardor tug‘ilgan kuniga velosiped olishni xohlaydi.

Qoida: Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot oldida a / an (yoki the, my, your, this, that, each, every, another ...) bo‘lishi shart. ❌ I need pen. ✅ I need a pen.

Birlikdagi ot oldida “bo‘sh o‘rin” bo‘lmasligi kerak
❌ Aniqlovchisiz✅ a / an bilan✅ Boshqa aniqlovchi bilan
I bought bag.I bought a bag.I bought my / the / this / another bag.
Is there bank here?Is there a bank here?Is the bank open?
She is eating egg.She is eating an egg.She is eating her egg.

a yoki an? Imloga emas, tovushga qarang

Qoida oddiy: a — undosh tovush oldidan, an — unli tovush oldidan. Lekin gap harf haqida emas, aytilishi haqida ketadi. Bundan tashqari, a / an o‘zidan keyingi birinchi so‘zga qarab tanlanadi — bu ot bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin.

a yoki an?
Keyingi so‘z qanday tovush bilan boshlanadi?ArtiklMisollar
Undosh tovush (b, c, d, k, t, p ...)aa book, a car, a teacher, a young man
Unli tovush (a, e, i, o, u kabi)anan apple, an egg, an idea, an orange, an umbrella, an uncle
Harf unli, lekin tovush y / w kabi undoshaa university, a European, a unit, a useful tool, a one-way street, a one-day trip
Harf undosh (h), lekin h o‘qilmaydi — tovush unlianan hour, an honest man, an honour
Qisqartma yoki harf: harfning nomi qanday boshlansa, shunga qarangan / aan MBA (em-bi-ey), an SMS (es-em-es), an HR manager (eych-ar), an X-ray; a PhD student (pi-eych-di), a UN meeting (yu-en)
Oraga so‘z kirsa, shu keyingi so‘zga qaranga / ana book — an old book — a very old book; an exam — a difficult exam

O‘zbek tilida yozilishi va aytilishi deyarli bir xil, shuning uchun ko‘pchilik harfga qarab xato qiladi. Inglizchada esa university “u” bilan yoziladi, lekin “yu-” deb o‘qiladi (a university), hour “h” bilan yoziladi, lekin “au-” deb o‘qiladi (an hour). Shubha bo‘lsa, so‘zni ovoz chiqarib ayting va birinchi tovushni eshiting.

Kasb ko‘plikda: kasb nomi ko‘plikka o‘tsa, a / an tushib qoladi: She is a doctor → They are doctors ✅ (They are a doctors ❌).

Tashqi ko‘rinish: odamning yuzi, burni, ko‘zlari haqida gapirganda ham birlikda a / an, ko‘plikda artiklsiz: Bekzod has a long nose and a friendly face. Malika has blue eyes. (the long nose, the blue eyes ❌).

Tasvirlangan narsaga hayratlanganda What a ... ! ishlatiladi. a / an faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan otdan oldin keladi. Ko‘plik va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan a yo‘q.

Ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan otlar: a / an yo‘q Plural countable nouns

Ko‘plikdagi ot oldida a / an hech qachon kelmaydi, chunki a / an “bitta” degani. Ko‘plik otdan oldin nima kelishi mumkin?

Hech narsa (–)

Umumiy ma’no yoki aniqlanmagan miqdor.

  • I like apples.
  • Dogs are loyal animals.

some

Noaniq miqdor: “bir necha”.

  • I bought some apples.
  • There are some dogs in the yard.

Son yoki miqdor so‘zi

Aniq yoki taxminiy miqdor.

  • two apples, three dogs
  • many / a few / several apples

the / my / these ...

Aniq, ma’lum narsalar.

  • The apples are fresh.
  • My dogs are big.

Ko‘plik shaklining yasalishi (bus → buses, city → cities, child → children) Unit 79 da to‘liq berilgan.

  • I bought a postcard. Malika bought some postcards.Men otkritka sotib oldim. Malika bir necha otkritka sotib oldi.
  • Jasur has a good friend. I have good friends.Jasurning yaxshi do‘sti bor. Mening yaxshi do‘stlarim bor.
  • Is that man a tourist? — No, he’s a student. Those women are tourists.U odam sayyohmi? — Yo‘q, u talaba. Anavi ayollar sayyoh.

Qoida: Kim yoki nima ekanini, qanday ekanini aytganda ko‘plikda otning o‘zi ishlatiladi, some qo‘yilmaydi: Those are nice chairs. My parents are doctors. Dogs are animals. (Those are some nice chairs. — bu ma’noda ❌)

O‘zbekchada ko‘plik shakli son aytilganda ishlatilmaydi: ikkita kitob, lekin inglizchada two books (ko‘plik -s shart). Aksincha, “ular shifokor” deganda o‘zbekchada -lar shart emas, inglizchada They are doctors (ko‘plik shart).

Some: noaniq miqdor Some = an unspecified number or amount

Some “qancha ekani muhim emas” yoki “aniq ayta olmayman” degan ma’noni beradi. U ham sanaladigan ko‘plik, ham sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatiladi.

some+ko‘plik sanaladigan ot/sanalmaydigan ot
  • There are some tomatoes in the fridge.Muzlatkichda bir nechta pomidor bor.
  • There is some milk in the fridge.Muzlatkichda ozgina sut bor.
  • I met some interesting people at the conference.Anjumanda bir necha qiziqarli odam bilan tanishdim.
  • Bekzod gave me some good advice.Bekzod menga yaxshi maslahat berdi.
  • I need some new sunglasses. (= a new pair of sunglasses)Menga yangi quyosh ko‘zoynagi kerak.
a / an, some va “hech narsa” (–) ni solishtirish
a / ansome– (artiklsiz)
Ma’nobitta, noaniqnoaniq miqdor (bir necha / bir oz)umumiy ma’no — hammasi haqida
Sanaladigan birlik✅ I need an egg.❌❌
Sanaladigan ko‘plik❌✅ I need some eggs.✅ Eggs are healthy.
Sanalmaydigan❌✅ I need some milk.✅ Milk is healthy.

Muhim farq: Apples are healthy = hamma olma foydali (umumiy qoida). Some apples are healthy = ba’zi olmalar foydali (hammasi emas). Umumiy gapda some ni qo‘shsangiz, ma’no o‘zgaradi!

Oddiy gaplarda, ayniqsa aniq bir vaziyatda, some ni tushirib qoldirish ham mumkin: I bought (some) apples — ma’no deyarli bir xil. Some “bir necha, ozroq” ekanini biroz ko‘proq ta’kidlaydi.

Some qayerda ishlatiladi, any qayerda?

Some va any (qisqacha; batafsil — Unit 85)
Gap turiOdatdaMisol

Gapda some odatda kuchsiz, qisqa aytiladi (taxminan “sm”): some eggs. Agar kuchli aytilsa, “ba’zilar” ma’nosi paydo bo‘ladi: SOME people like it, but others don’t.

Qaysi birini tanlayman? Choosing: a / an, some or nothing

Avval ot qaysi guruhga kirishini aniqlang, so‘ng jadvaldan foydalaning.

  • Jasur is eating a sandwich, some grapes and an apple. He is also drinking some water.Jasur sendvich, uzum va olma yeyapti. U yana suv ham ichyapti.
  • Malika bought a notebook and some pens at the bazaar.Malika bozorda daftar va bir necha ruchka sotib oldi.
  • Is there a chemist near here? — Yes, there are some on Navoi Street.Bu yerda dorixona bormi? — Ha, Navoiy ko‘chasida bir nechtasi bor.

a / an va one When do we say “one” instead of “a”?

O‘zbek tilida bir ham “bitta”, ham “noaniq” ma’nosini beradi. Inglizchada bu ikkiga bo‘lingan: a / an — oddiy, neytral; one — “aynan bitta, ikkita emas” deb sonni ta’kidlash.

a / an — neytral

  • I have a sister.
  • She lives in a small village.

Soni muhim emas, shunchaki bor deymiz.

one — son ta’kidlanadi

  • I have one sister, not two.
  • I’ve had only one cup of tea today.

“Bitta” — ko‘p yoki kam emas.

  • I’d like one kilo of tomatoes, not two.Menga ikki kilo emas, bir kilo pomidor kerak.
  • One day, Nodira found a key in the garden.Bir kuni Nodira bog‘da kalit topib oldi.
  • One of my friends is a pilot.Do‘stlarimdan biri uchuvchi.

a va one bir vaqtda ishlatilmaydi: a one book ❌, one a book ❌. “Bir kuni” (hikoya boshlanishi) ma’nosida one day deyiladi, a day emas.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

a yoki an — qanday tez aniqlayman?

Keyingi so‘zni ichingizda aytib, birinchi tovushga e’tibor bering. Unli tovush bo‘lsa — an (an apple, an hour), undosh tovush bo‘lsa — a (a book, a university). Harfga qarab xato qilmang: university (yu-), one (u-an), hour (au-), honest (o-).

O‘zbekcha “bir” har doim a / an ga teng bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. “Bir” ba’zan a / an (bir kitob = a book), ba’zan one (faqat bir kitob = only one book), ba’zan esa “taxminan” (bir soat chamasi = about an hour) ma’nosini beradi. Muhimi: o‘zbekchada “bir” ni tushirib qoldirishingiz mumkin, inglizchada a / an ni tushirib qoldirib bo‘lmaydi.

Nega “She is a teacher” deymiz, “She is teacher” emas?

Inglizchada kasb ham “sanaladigan ot”, uning oldida birlikda a / an kerak: bu odam “o‘qituvchilar to‘plamidan bittasi” deb qaraladi. Ko‘plikda esa a / an yo‘q: They are teachers. Millat nomi sifat sifatida kelsa (She is Uzbek.), a yo‘q, chunki u holda so‘z ot emas, sifat.

Some bilan any ning farqi nima?

Qisqacha: some — darak gaplarda, shuningdek taklif va iltimoslarda; any — inkor va oddiy savollarda. Batafsil — Unit 85. Masalan: I have some friends there. I don’t have any friends there. Would you like some coffee?

“I like apples” bilan “I like some apples” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q. I like apples — olmani umuman yaxshi ko‘raman. I like some apples — ba’zi olmalar yoqadi (hammasi emas, yoki aynan shu bir necha olma). Umumiy ma’noda some ishlatilmaydi.

some ni birlikdagi ot bilan ishlatish mumkinmi?

Oddiy ma’noda yo‘q. Lekin kuchli ohang bilan some + birlik ot “qandaydir, noma’lum” ma’nosini beradi: Some man phoned you. (Qandaydir odam sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildi — kimligini bilmayman.) Bu kam uchraydi; oddiy gapda A man phoned you deymiz.

“a lot of”, “a few” da a nima qiladi? Nega bu ibora ko‘plik ot bilan kelishi mumkin?

Bunda a / an ot bilan emas, ibora (a lot of, a few, a couple of) ning o‘zi bilan bog‘liq: butun ibora “ko‘p / bir necha” ma’nosida miqdor so‘zi. Shuning uchun a lot of books, a few books to‘g‘ri, lekin a books noto‘g‘ri. Ularning qoidalari Unit 87 da.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot yolg‘iz kelmaydi: a / an (yoki the, my, this ...) shart. ❌ I have car. ✅ I have a car.
  • a — undosh tovush oldidan, an — unli tovush oldidan: a university, an hour, an old car, a very old car.
  • Ko‘plik sanaladigan ot a / an olmaydi: books, some books, two books.
  • some = noaniq miqdor; ko‘plik sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatiladi. Umumiy ma’noda ishlatilmaydi.
  • Kasb: She is a nurse — lekin They are nurses.
  • “Har bir” ma’nosida: twice a week, 60 km an hour.
  • What a ...! faqat birlikdagi sanaladigan ot bilan: What a view! — What lovely weather!
  • Sonni ta’kidlash uchun one (only one), oddiy gapda a / an.
