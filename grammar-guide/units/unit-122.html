On time and in time, At the end and in the end

On time va in time; at the end va in the end
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • on time — “o‘z vaqtida, kechikmasdan” (rejadagi paytda) va in time — “ulgurib, kech qolmasdan” (muhim narsaga ulgurish) o‘rtasidagi farqni.
  • in time for + ot va in time to + fe’l qoliplarini hamda just in time — “oxirgi daqiqada ulgurib” ma’nosini.
  • at the end of — biror narsaning (dars, film, oy, ko‘cha) oxirida; in the end — uzoq jarayondan keyin oxir-oqibat.
  • Nega in the end of the lesson deyish xato ekanini va at the beginning of bilan qarama-qarshilikni.

Bu darsda ikki juft o‘xshash ibora bor, ular bir-biriga juda yaqin ko‘rinadi, lekin ma’nosi boshqa. Birinchi juft vaqt bilan munosabat haqida: on time — reja yoki jadval bo‘yicha aniq paytda; in time — kech qolmaslik, biror muhim narsaga ulgurish. Ikkinchi juft tugash haqida: at the end — biror narsaning oxirgi nuqtasi; in the end — voqealar zanjirining yakuni, ya’ni yakuniy natija.

O‘zbek tilida o‘z vaqtida va ulgurmoq alohida so‘zlar, lekin kundalik nutqda ko‘pincha bitta “vaqtida” aytiladi: Vaqtida keldi (rejadagi paytda keldi) ham, vaqtida yetib keldi (ulgurdi) ham. Ingliz tilida bu ikkisini aralashtirib bo‘lmaydi: o‘z vaqtida → on time, ulgurmoq → in time, kech qolmoq → late / too late. Ikkinchi tuzoq: o‘zbekcha oxirida (-da qo‘shimchasi bilan) ikkala ibora uchun ham ishlatiladi. Dars oxirida = at the end of the lesson, lekin oxir-oqibat = in the end. O‘quvchilar dars oxirida ni ❌ in the end of the lesson deb tarjima qiladi, bunday ibora yo‘q!

On time va in time Punctual or soon enough?

On time — rejadagi paytda, kechikmasdan

On time degani biror ish rejalashtirilgan yoki kelishilgan paytda bo‘ladi, ya’ni kechikmasdan. Bunda doim biror reja, jadval yoki kelishuv bor: poyezd jadvali, dars boshlanishi, uchrashuv vaqti. Qarama-qarshi ma’no — late (kech). Bu ibora punctual (aniq, vaqtga rioya qiladigan) so‘ziga yaqin: She is always punctual = She always arrives on time.

Ega+arrive / start / finish / leave / pay / be+on time(+forot)
  • The lesson started on time, at exactly nine.Dars aniq soat to‘qqizda, o‘z vaqtida boshlandi.
  • Malika is never late. She always arrives on time.Malika hech qachon kechikmaydi. U doim o‘z vaqtida keladi.
  • The train from Bukhara reached Tashkent on time.Buxorodan kelgan poyezd Toshkentga jadval bo‘yicha yetib keldi.
  • Please pay the bill on time, or you will have to pay a fine.Hisobni o‘z vaqtida to‘lang, aks holda jarima to‘laysiz.
  • “Is the 6.20 bus running on time today?” “No, it’s ten minutes late.”— Soat 6.20 dagi avtobus bugun jadval bo‘yicha yuryaptimi? — Yo‘q, o‘n daqiqa kechikyapti.
  • Bekzod was on time for his interview.Bekzod suhbatga o‘z vaqtida keldi.

Kundalik nutqda rejadagi paytdan bir necha daqiqa oldin kelgan odam ham on time hisoblanadi, chunki u kechikmagan. Agar kutilganidan ancha oldin kelsangiz, early (erta) deyish aniqroq. Exactly / right on time — “aniq o‘z vaqtida”.

In time — kech qolmasdan, ulgurib

In time (for something / to do something) degani kech bo‘lib qolishidan oldin yetib kelish yoki ulgurish. Biz biror muhim narsaga (poyezd, imtihon, ovqat, yordam) kech qolmaymiz. Bu yerda aniq soatga rioya qilish emas, balki ulgurish muhim. Qarama-qarshi ma’no — too late (juda kech, ulgurmadi).

Ega+be / get / arrive / come+in time+forot/tofe’l
  • Dilnoza ran to the platform and got there in time for the train.Dilnoza platformaga yugurib bordi va poyezdga ulgurdi.
  • We left home early to be in time to find good seats.Yaxshi joy topishga ulgurish uchun uydan erta chiqdik.
  • Will you be back in time for the wedding on Saturday?Shanba kungi to‘yga ulgurib qaytasizmi?
  • The ambulance got there in time to save the old man.Tez yordam keksa kishini qutqarib qolishga ulgurib yetib keldi.
  • The doors were closing, but Jasur jumped on just in time.Eshiklar yopilayotgan edi, lekin Jasur oxirgi daqiqada sakrab chiqib ulgurdi.
  • We were too late to see the beginning of the film.Filmning boshlanishini ko‘rishga kech qoldik.

Just in time — “zo‘rg‘a ulgurib, oxirgi daqiqada”. In good time esa aksincha: “ancha oldin, ortiqcha vaqt bilan”: We got to the airport in good time for our flight. (Aeroportga reysdan ancha oldin yetib keldik.)

At the end va in the end The last part, or the final result?

At the end of — biror narsaning oxirida

At the end of degani biror narsa tugaydigan paytda yoki tugaydigan joyda. Undan keyin doim biror ot keladi: davr (the lesson, the week, the year), tadbir (the film, the concert, the match) yoki joy (the street, the corridor, the platform). Qarama-qarshi ma’no — at the beginning of / at the start of (… ning boshida).

In the end — oxir-oqibat, nihoyat

In the end degani uzoq vaqtdan keyin yoki ko‘p o‘zgarishlar, qiyinchiliklar, ikkilanishlardan so‘ng — “oxir-oqibat, oxiri”. U vaziyatning yakuniy natijasini bildiradi. In the end dan keyin of va ot kelmaydi, u butun gapga tegishli ravish: ko‘pincha gap boshida (vergul bilan) yoki gap oxirida turadi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

On time va in time ni qisqacha qanday ajrataman?

O‘zingizga ikkita savol bering. 1) Reja yoki jadvaldagi aniq payt bormi va ish o‘shanda bo‘ldimi? → on time (qarama-qarshisi: late). 2) Biror muhim narsa (poyezd, imtihon, tushlik, yordam) bor va men undan oldin yetib keldimmi, ulgurdimmi? → in time for / to (qarama-qarshisi: too late).

Rejadagidan oldinroq kelsam ham “on time” deyish mumkinmi?

Kundalik nutqda ha: kelishilgan paytdan besh daqiqa oldin kelgan odam ham kechikmagan, shuning uchun on time. Agar juda erta kelsangiz, early (erta) deb aytish aniqroq. In time esa erta yoki aniq payt degan ma’no emas, “kech qolmadim” degan ma’no.

“In time for” va “in time to” ning farqi nimada?

Ma’nosi bir xil, faqat keyin nima kelishiga bog‘liq. In time for dan keyin ot keladi: in time for lunch, in time for the train. In time to dan keyin fe’lning boshlang‘ich shakli keladi: in time to catch the train, in time to say goodbye. Agar nimaga ulgurish gapdan ma’lum bo‘lsa, ikkalasini tushirib, shunchaki We got there just in time deyish mumkin.

“Just in time” va “in good time” bir xilmi?

Yo‘q, deyarli aksincha. Just in time — oxirgi daqiqada, zo‘rg‘a ulgurish (bir daqiqa kech bo‘lsa, ulgurmasdik). In good time — kerakli vaqtdan ancha oldin, ortiqcha vaqt bilan: We left in good time for the airport.

At the end va in the end ikkalasi ham “oxirida” emasmi? Nega inglizcha ikkita?

O‘zbekchada ikkalasi uchun ham oxirida / oxir-oqibat ishlatamiz, ingliz tilida esa ular har xil. At the end of + ot — biror narsaning oxirgi qismi yoki nuqtasi (dars, film, ko‘cha). In the end — uzoq jarayondan keyingi natija, “oxir-oqibat”. Tez tekshirish: “nimaning oxirida?” degan savolga javob bor bo‘lsa — at the end of; yo‘q bo‘lsa va “natijada” ma’nosi bo‘lsa — in the end.

“At the end” ni “of” siz ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha, faqat nimaning oxiri ekani kontekstdan aniq bo‘lsa: I read the whole novel. At the end, the hero dies. Lekin “oxir-oqibat, nihoyat” desangiz at the end demang, in the end deng.

“In the end” dan keyin nega “of” bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki in the end — qotib qolgan ravish (adverbial ibora), u “finally” kabi butun gapga tegishli. Ot bilan bog‘lash uchun at the end of kerak. ❌ in the end of the film, ✅ at the end of the film.

In the end, at last, finally va eventually bir xilmi?

Yaqin, lekin bir xil emas. In the end — yakuniy natija, neytral. At last — uzoq kutilganidan keyin “nihoyat” (his-tuyg‘u bilan). Finally — “nihoyat” yoki ro‘yxatda oxirgi qadam. Eventually — vaqt o‘tishi bilan tabiiy ravishda sodir bo‘lgan narsa. Jadval — B bo‘limning oxirida.

Nega “on time” va “in time” da artikl yo‘q?

Bular qotib qolgan iboralar, ichidagi time so‘zi artiklsiz keladi. ❌ on the time, ❌ in the time. In the time of degan boshqa ibora bor, u “… davrida” ma’nosida: in the time of my grandfather (bobom davrida).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • on time = rejadagi paytda, kechikmasdan (qarama-qarshisi: late). Artiklsiz: on time.
  • in time (for / to) = kech bo‘lishidan oldin, ulgurib (qarama-qarshisi: too late). Qolip: in time for + ot, in time to + fe’l.
  • just in time = oxirgi daqiqada, zo‘rg‘a ulgurib; in good time = ancha oldin, ortiqcha vaqt bilan.
  • at the end of + ot = biror narsaning (vaqt yoki joy) oxirida: at the end of the lesson, at the end of the street. Qarama-qarshisi: at the beginning of.
  • in the end = oxir-oqibat, yakuniy natija. Of olmaydi: ❌ in the end of the film.
  • Tez test: “nimaning oxirida?” degan savolga javob bor → at the end of; yo‘q va ma’no “natijada” → in the end.
  • O‘zbek tilidagi bitta “vaqtida” va “oxirida” inglizchada ikkitadan ibora bo‘lishini unutmang.
