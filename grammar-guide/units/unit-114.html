In case

In case — ehtiyot shart
Conjunctions and prepositions · Bog‘lovchilar va predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • In case — “ehtiyot shart, ... bo‘lib qolsa deb” ma’nosini: hozir biror ish qilamiz, chunki keyin biror narsa bo‘lishi mumkin.
  • In case bilan if (“agar”) farqini: biri ehtiyot chorasi, ikkinchisi shart.
  • In case dan keyin qaysi zamon kelishini: kelasi zamon uchun present simple, o‘tgan zamon uchun past simple (will va would emas).
  • In case of + ot (“... bo‘lgan taqdirda”) va just in case (“har ehtimolga qarshi”) ni.

Tasavvur qiling, siz tog‘ga ketyapsiz. Yomg‘ir yog‘ishi ham mumkin, yog‘masligi ham. Siz soyabon olasiz — chunki yog‘ib qolishi mumkin. Inglizchasi: I’ll take an umbrella in case it rains. Soyabonni yomg‘ir yog‘gandan keyin emas, oldindan olasiz. Mana shu — in case ning asosiy g‘oyasi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no “-ib qolsa deb” yoki “-ar deb” bilan beriladi: Yomg‘ir yog‘ib qolsa deb soyabon oldim. Yo‘lda och qolarman deb non oldim. Ayni shu -sa qo‘shimchasi “agar” ma’nosida ham ishlatilgani uchun o‘quvchilar in case ni if bilan adashtiradi. Esda tuting: -sa deb = in case, “agar ... -sa” = if.

In case — ehtiyot chorasi In case = because something might happen

In case bilan gap ikki qismdan iborat: (1) biz hozir yoki oldin qiladigan ish; (2) keyin bo‘lishi mumkin (lekin bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin) voqea. Birinchi ish ikkinchi voqeaga javob emas, balki unga oldindan tayyorgarlik.

ish (oldindan)+in case+ega+present simple / past simple
  • Take a power bank in case your phone dies on the train.Poyezdda telefoningiz o‘chib qolsa deb, power bank oling.
  • Malika always carries a spare pen in case hers runs out of ink.Malika o‘zinikining siyohi tugab qolsa deb, doim zaxira ruchka olib yuradi.
  • I’m going to cook extra rice in case my cousins stay for dinner.Amakivachchalarim kechki ovqatga qolib ketsa deb, ko‘proq guruch pishiraman.
  • Let’s leave early in case there’s a lot of traffic.Yo‘lda tirbandlik bo‘lib qolsa deb, barvaqt chiqaylik.
  • Keep the receipt in case the shoes don’t fit.Poyabzal to‘g‘ri kelmasa deb, chekni saqlab qo‘ying.

Zamon: kelasi uchun present, o‘tgan uchun past

Asosiy gap qaysi zamonda bo‘lishidan qat’i nazar, in case dan keyin kelajakdagi imkoniyat ham oddiy zamon bilan aytiladi. Will yoki would kelmaydi.

Will / would ishlatmang: I’ll take an umbrella in case it will rain. ❌ — ... in case it rains. ✅. I took a map in case I would get lost. ❌ — ... in case I got lost. ✅. Bu qoida if va when dan keyingi qoidaga o‘xshaydi (Unit 25).

Rasmiyroq uslubda in case dan keyin should ham keladi: I’ll leave the spare key with Aziz in case you should need it. Ma’nosi o‘zgarmaydi, shunchaki rasmiyroq va “ehtimoli kamroq” ohang beradi.

In case va if In case or if?

Ikkalasi ham “bo‘lishi mumkin” voqea haqida, lekin ishning vaqti turlicha. In case da ish voqeadan oldin qilinadi (tayyorgarlik). If da ish voqea sodir bo‘lgandan keyin qilinadi (javob, natija).

In case — oldindan tayyorgarlik

Voqea bo‘lmasa ham, biz ishni baribir qilamiz.

  • I’ll take an umbrella in case it rains.
  • Save your work in case the computer crashes.

If — shart va natija

Voqea bo‘lmasa, ishni qilmaymiz.

  • If it rains, we’ll move the party indoors.
  • Call the technician if the computer crashes.

Tezkor test: “Agar bu voqea bo‘lmasa-yu, men baribir shu ishni qilamanmi?” — Ha bo‘lsa, in case. Yo‘q bo‘lsa, if. Soyabon olish — yomg‘ir yog‘masa ham olinadi (in case). Tadbirni ichkariga ko‘chirish — faqat yomg‘ir yog‘sa (if).

Bir xil ibora, ikki xil ma’no
in case (oldin)if (keyin)
I’ll put a torch by the bed in case there’s a power cut.
Elektr o‘chmasligi ham mumkin, lekin fonarni oldindan karavot yoniga qo‘yaman.		I’ll light a candle if there’s a power cut.
Avval elektr o‘chsin, keyin sham yoqaman.
Keep a first-aid kit in the car in case someone gets hurt.
Birov jarohat olmasa ham, dori qutisi mashinada turadi.		Use the first-aid kit if someone gets hurt.
Dori qutisini faqat kimdir jarohat olsa ishlating.
Keep the receipt in case you want to return the shirt.
Qaytarish kerak bo‘lmasa ham, chekni oldindan saqlab qo‘yasiz.		Bring the receipt if you want to return the shirt.
Ko‘ylakni qaytarmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, chekni olib keling.

Ikkalasi bir gapda ham uchrashi mumkin: I’ll put a torch by the bed in case there’s a power cut. If there is, I’ll use it. (Fonarni oldindan qo‘yaman. Elektr o‘chsa, undan foydalanaman.)

Amerika ingliz tilida (AmE) va norasmiy nutqda in case ba’zan if o‘rnida ham eshitiladi. Standart ingliz tilida va imtihonda esa ikkalasini yuqoridagidek ajratish kerak.

O‘zbekchada ham ikki xil: “yomg‘ir yog‘ib qolsa deb soyabon oldim” (in case) va “yomg‘ir yog‘sa, uyda qolamiz” (if). Birinchisida “deb” bor — u oldindan qilingan ish sababini bildiradi.

In case of va just in case In case of + noun; (just) in case

In case of + ot

In case of dan keyin ot keladi. Ko‘pincha e’lon, yo‘riqnoma va rasmiy matnlarda “... bo‘lgan taqdirda” ma’nosida uchraydi (= if there is ...).

  • In case of an earthquake, stay away from windows.Zilzila bo‘lgan taqdirda derazalardan uzoqroq turing.
  • In case of bad weather, the concert will be held indoors.Ob-havo yomon bo‘lsa, konsert ichkarida o‘tkaziladi.
  • In case of emergency, press the red button.Favqulodda holat yuz bersa, qizil tugmani bosing.

In case of dan keyin gap kelmaydi: in case of you need help ❌. Ot bo‘lsa — in case of + ot, gap bo‘lsa — in case + gap: in case of an emergency ✅, in case there is an emergency ✅.

Ba’zan in case of + ot ehtiyot chorasi ma’nosida ham keladi: Keep a torch in the car in case of power cuts. Ma’noni matn aniqlaydi. Yo‘riqnomalarda esa odatda “... bo‘lsa” degani.

(Just) in case — alohida

Just in case — “har ehtimolga qarshi, ehtiyot shart”. Gap oxirida yolg‘iz ham keladi, uning ortidan gap kelishi shart emas.

  • We probably won’t need the tent, but let’s pack it just in case.Chodir kerak bo‘lmasa kerak, lekin ehtiyot shart uni ham olaylik.
  • Take my number — just in case.Raqamimni oling — ehtiyot shart.
  • “Why did you bring a map?” “In case we get lost.”— Nega xarita oldingiz? — Adashib qolsak deb.

Savolga javob berayotganda Because in case ... demang. To‘g‘risi: In case we get lost. yoki Because we might get lost.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

In case bilan if ni qanday ajrataman?

O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Agar o‘sha voqea bo‘lmasa, men shu ishni baribir qilamanmi?” Ha — in case (ehtiyot chorasi, ish oldin qilinadi). Yo‘q — if (shart: ish faqat voqea bo‘lsa qilinadi). Take an umbrella in case it rains — yomg‘ir yog‘masa ham soyabon olinadi. If it rains, we’ll go by taxi — taksi faqat yomg‘irda.

Nega in case dan keyin “will” kelmaydi?

Ingliz tilida if, when, as soon as, before kabi so‘zlardan keyin kelajak uchun present simple ishlatiladi. In case ham shunday: in case it rains, in case you need me. “Kelajak” ma’nosi asosiy gapdan va vaziyatdan tushuniladi. Shuning uchun in case it will rain ❌.

O‘tgan zamonda “would” emas, nega past simple?

Chunki o‘sha paytga nisbatan voqea hali kelajakda edi, lekin biz uni oddiy o‘tgan zamon shaklida aytamiz: We took a map in case we got lost. Asosiy gap o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa, in case dan keyin ham o‘tgan zamon keladi. Would bu o‘rinda ishlatilmaydi.

In case gapning boshida kela oladimi?

Ha, kela oladi: In case you need me, I’ll be in the next room. Bu holda in case qismidan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi. Lekin ko‘pincha asosiy gap avval keladi: I’ll be in the next room in case you need me.

In case va in case of o‘rtasida farq bormi?

Ha. In case dan keyin gap (ega + fe’l) keladi: in case it rains. In case of dan keyin ot keladi: in case of rain. In case of ko‘pincha rasmiy yo‘riqnomalarda “... bo‘lgan taqdirda” ma’nosida uchraydi: In case of fire, use the stairs.

“Just in case” ni yolg‘iz ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Ha. Just in case alohida gap bo‘lagi sifatida kelishi mumkin: I’ll take a jacket, just in case. Ya’ni “har ehtimolga qarshi”. Bunda “nimadan ehtiyot bo‘lyapman” savoli kontekstdan ma’lum bo‘ladi.

In case, in any case va in that case bir xil emasmi?

Yo‘q. In case = ehtiyot shart; in any case = har holda, baribir; in that case = unda, u holda. Birinchisidan keyin gap kelishi mumkin, qolgan ikkitasi esa alohida iboralar (★ bo‘lim).

In case dan keyin “should” ham keladimi?

Ha, rasmiyroq uslubda: In case you should need any help, call me. Ma’nosi in case you need bilan bir xil. Kundalik nutqda esa oddiy present simple yetarli.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. In case yoki if? Qavs ichidagi izohga e’tibor bering.

  1. Write the address down you forget it. (You write it now, before you forget.)
  2. Phone the police you see anything suspicious. (You phone only after you see something.)
  3. I always take a charger my battery dies. (I take it every day; the battery may die.)
  4. We’ll cancel the picnic it rains. (We decide only when it rains.)
  5. Put the cake in the fridge it goes bad. (You put it there now.)
  6. Press this button there is a fire. (Only after a fire starts.)
  7. Take some cash with you the card machine isn’t working. (You take it now.)
  8. I’ll lend you my notes you miss the lesson. (I lend them before the lesson.)

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. I’ll take my charger in case I (need) it.
  2. Yesterday she took a map in case she (get) lost.
  3. Write down my number in case you (forget) it.
  4. Last night he locked the bike in case somebody (try) to steal it.
  5. Keep the receipt in case the shoes (not / fit).
  6. On our last trip we bought extra water in case the bus (break down).
  7. Take your jacket in case it (get) cold tonight.
  8. Last year Sardor wrote the password on a piece of paper in case he (forget) it.

3-mashq. Ikki gapni in case bilan bitta gapga birlashtiring.

  1. Take a coat. It might get cold. →
  2. I’ll leave the key with Mrs Karimova. You might arrive early. →
  3. Last week Nodira packed some sandwiches. It was possible that she would get hungry on the train. →
  4. Let’s book a table. The restaurant might be full. →
  5. Yesterday they saved the document twice. It was possible that the computer would crash. →

4-mashq. In case of bilan qayta yozing.

  1. If there is a fire, use the stairs. →
  2. If there is an emergency, break the glass. →
  3. If there is heavy rain, the concert will take place indoors. →
  4. If there is an earthquake, stay away from windows. →

5-mashq. Xatoni toping va gapni to‘g‘rilab yozing.

  1. I’ll take an umbrella in case it will rain. →
  2. She took a jacket in case it would get cold. →
  3. In case it rains tomorrow, we’ll stay at home. →
  4. Take my number in case of you lose your way. →
  5. I brought a book if I get bored. →
  6. I’m taking my passport in the case I need it. →

6-mashq. O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga tarjima qiling.

  1. Yomg‘ir yog‘ib qolsa deb, soyabon oldim. →
  2. Telefoningiz o‘chib qolsa deb, power bank oling. →
  3. Yana mehmonlar kelib qolsa deb, ko‘proq non oldik. →
  4. Yong‘in bo‘lsa, zinadan foydalaning. →
  5. Ehtiyot shart, men raqamingizni yozib qo‘yaman. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • In case = ehtiyot shart: ish oldin qilinadi, voqea keyin bo‘lishi mumkin (bo‘lmasligi ham mumkin).
  • If = shart: ish faqat voqea sodir bo‘lgandan keyin qilinadi. Test: voqea bo‘lmasa ham ishni qilamanmi? Ha — in case; yo‘q — if.
  • In case dan keyin present simple (kelajak uchun) yoki past simple (o‘tgan uchun). Will / would yo‘q.
  • In case of + ot = “... bo‘lgan taqdirda” (e’lonlar): In case of fire .... Gap bo‘lsa — in case + gap.
  • Just in case = har ehtimolga qarshi; yolg‘iz ham ishlatiladi.
  • In case ≠ in any case ≠ in that case.
