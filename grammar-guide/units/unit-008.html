8

Present perfect 2 (I have done)

Hozirgi tugallangan zamon — 2
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Hayotiy tajriba haqida gapirishni: Have you ever ...? I’ve never ...
  • been to = borib kelgan, bo‘lgan; the best ... I’ve ever ... (orttirma daraja + ever).
  • Hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr bilan keladigan so‘zlarni: recently, lately, so far, since breakfast, for a long time.
  • today, this morning, this week, this year — davr hali tugamagan bo‘lsa, present perfect.
  • It’s the first time I’ve ... qurilmasini (“birinchi marta ...”).

Bu unitning asosiy g‘oyasi bitta: biz o‘tmishdan boshlanib, hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr (masalan, butun hayotimiz, shu hafta, bugun, nonushtadan beri) haqida gapirganda present perfect (have / has + V3) ishlatamiz. Harakat qachon bo‘lgani muhim emas — muhimi, u shu tugamagan davr ichida bo‘lgan yoki bo‘lmagan.

Ega+have / has+(ever / never)+V3+... (davr: so far, today, this week ...)

O‘zbek tilida hayotiy tajriba ko‘pincha -gan shakli bilan aytiladi: Samarqandda bo‘lganman, sumalak yemaganman, hech tuya minganmisiz? Ingliz tilida bu — present perfect: I’ve been to Samarkand. I’ve never eaten sumalak. Have you ever ridden a camel? Ehtiyot bo‘ling: “Samarqandga birinchi marta kelishim” o‘zbekchada hozirgi zamonga o‘xshaydi, lekin inglizchada It’s the first time I’ve come to Samarkand — present perfect bilan aytiladi (D bo‘lim).

Hayotiy tajriba: ever, never, been to Have you ever ...? I’ve never ...

Mana kichik suhbat. Sardor yangi tanishi Kamoladan sayohatlari haqida so‘rayapti:

  • Sardor: Do you like travelling, Kamola?
    Kamola: I love it! I’ve visited nearly every region of Uzbekistan.
    Sardor: Have you ever been to Khiva?
    Kamola: Yes, twice — it’s beautiful. But I’ve never seen the Aral Sea.    Sardor: — Sayohat qilishni yoqtirasizmi, Kamola? Kamola: — Juda yoqtiraman! O‘zbekistonning deyarli barcha viloyatlarida bo‘lganman. Sardor: — Xivada hech bo‘lganmisiz? Kamola: — Ha, ikki marta — juda chiroyli shahar. Lekin Orol dengizini hech ko‘rmaganman.

Sardor va Kamola Kamolaning butun hayoti haqida gaplashyapti. Hayot — o‘tmishda boshlangan va hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr. Kamola Xivaga qachon borgani muhim emas; muhimi — u hayoti davomida u yerda bo‘lgan. Shuning uchun present perfect ishlatilyapti.

Yana misollar

  • “Have you ever ridden a camel?” “No, never.”— Hech tuya minganmisiz? — Yo‘q, hech qachon.
  • My grandmother has never used a computer.Buvim hech qachon kompyuterdan foydalanmagan.
  • This puzzle is far too easy. It’s the easiest puzzle I’ve ever done.Bu boshqotirma juda oson. Men yechgan eng oson boshqotirma shu.
  • Bekzod is the funniest person I’ve ever met.Bekzod — men hayotimda uchratgan eng kulgili odam.
  • How many times have you been to Bukhara?Buxoroda necha marta bo‘lgansiz?

been (to) = borib kelgan, bo‘lgan

been to — “biror joyga borgan va qaytib kelgan”, ya’ni u yerda bo‘lgan, u joyni ko‘rgan:

  • I’ve never been to India. Have you been there?Men hech qachon Hindistonda bo‘lmaganman. Siz u yerda bo‘lganmisiz?
  • Dilnoza has been to London three times.Dilnoza Londonda uch marta bo‘lgan.

gone to va been to farqini eslang (Unit 7): She’s gone to London — ketgan, hozir u yerda; She’s been to London — borib kelgan. Tajriba haqida gapirganda doim been: I’ve been to Khiva twice ✅ — I’ve gone to Khiva twice ❌.

Qoida: ever va never — have / has bilan V3 orasida turadi. ever asosan so‘roq gapda (Have you ever ...?) va orttirma darajadan keyin (the best ... I’ve ever ...) ishlatiladi. Oddiy darak gapda I’ve ever been to Khiva ❌ deyilmaydi — I’ve been to Khiva ✅.

never o‘zi inkor ma’noli. Unga yana not qo‘shilmaydi: I’ve never seen snow ✅ — I haven’t never seen snow ❌. (I haven’t ever seen snow ham to‘g‘ri, lekin kamroq ishlatiladi.)

Qisqa javoblar

Agar odam endi tirik bo‘lmasa yoki davr tugagan bo‘lsa, “hayot hozirgacha davom etmayapti” — shuning uchun past simple: My great-grandfather never travelled abroad. (Bobokalonim hech qachon chet elga chiqmagan — u allaqachon vafot etgan.) Bu farq Unit 13–14 da batafsil.

Hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr: recently, so far, since ... A period of time that continues until now

Quyidagi misollarda ham gap hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr haqida: “yaqin kunlarda”, “shu paytgacha”, “nonushtadan beri (hozirgacha)”, “ancha vaqtdan beri”. Shuning uchun present perfect:

  • Have you spoken to Jasur recently? Is he OK?Yaqinda Jasur bilan gaplashdingizmi? U yaxshimi?
  • Our little shop has had a lot of new customers in the last few weeks.So‘nggi bir necha hafta ichida kichkina do‘konimizga ko‘plab yangi xaridorlar keldi.
  • We started building the house in May, and so far everything has gone well.Uyni qurishni may oyida boshladik va hozircha hammasi yaxshi ketyapti.
  • I haven’t checked my email since Friday. There must be a lot of messages.Jumadan beri elektron pochtamni tekshirmadim. Xatlar ko‘p yig‘ilgan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • Nodira and I were best friends at school, but we haven’t spoken to each other for years.Nodira bilan maktabda eng yaqin dugona edik, lekin yillardan beri gaplashmadik.
  • Kamola has been very busy lately.Kamola so‘nggi paytlarda juda band.

recently past simple bilan ham kelishi mumkin — agar biz o‘tmishdagi bitta aniq voqeani nazarda tutsak: Aziz moved to Fergana recently. (Aziz yaqinda Farg‘onaga ko‘chdi.) lately esa deyarli doim present perfect (yoki present perfect continuous) bilan keladi. for va since farqi — Unit 12 da.

O‘zbekchada “dushanbadan beri ko‘rmadim” yoki “dushanbadan beri ko‘rmayapman” deymiz — hozirgi yoki o‘tgan zamon bilan. Inglizchada since / for bilan “hozirgacha” ma’nosi bo‘lsa, present perfect kerak: I haven’t seen him since Monday ✅ — I don’t see him since Monday ❌, I didn’t see him since Monday ❌.

today, this week, this year — tugamagan davr Present perfect with today / this evening / this year etc.

today, this morning, this evening, this week, this month, this year, this term kabi so‘zlar bilan present perfect ishlatamiz — agar gapirayotgan paytimizda bu davr hali tugamagan bo‘lsa:

  • I’ve had three lessons today.Bugun uchta darsim bo‘ldi. (kun hali tugamagan, yana dars bo‘lishi mumkin)
  • Have you been to the theatre this year?Bu yil teatrga bordingizmi?
  • Bekzod hasn’t called me this morning. I hope everything is OK.Bekzod bugun ertalab menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi. Umid qilamanki, hammasi joyida. (hali ertalab)
  • Dilnoza has got top marks in every test this term.Dilnoza bu chorakda har bir testdan eng yuqori baho oldi.
  • It’s been very hot this week.Bu hafta havo juda issiq bo‘ldi.

Davr tugaganmi? — shu narsa zamonni hal qiladi

Davr hali tugamagan → present perfect

  • I’ve drunk three cups of coffee this morning.
    (soat 11 da aytilgan — hali ertalab)
  • We’ve had a lot of rain this month.
    Bu oy yomg‘ir ko‘p yog‘di. (oy hali tugamagan)

Davr tugagan → past simple

  • I drank three cups of coffee this morning.
    (soat 15 da aytilgan — ertalab o‘tib ketgan)
  • We had a lot of rain last month.
    O‘tgan oy yomg‘ir ko‘p yog‘di.

yesterday, last week, last year, in 2020, two days ago — bular tugagan davrlar. Ular bilan present perfect ishlatilmaydi: I saw him yesterday ✅ — I’ve seen him yesterday ❌.

O‘zbekchada “Bugun uch piyola choy ichdim” — bitta shakl. Inglizchada esa ikki variant bor (I’ve drunk / I drank) va tanlov davr tugagan-tugamaganiga bog‘liq. Kechqurun, kun hali tugamaganda ham today bilan odatda present perfect ishlatiladi: I’ve worked hard today.

It’s the first time ... — “birinchi marta” It’s the first time something has happened

Malika hozir samolyotda. U biroz hayajonda — chunki u hayotida birinchi marta samolyotda uchyapti. Inglizchada buni shunday aytamiz:

  • It’s the first time Malika has been on a plane.Malika birinchi marta samolyotda uchyapti. (= hayotida birinchi marta)
  • Malika has never flown before.Malika ilgari hech qachon samolyotda uchmagan.
It’s / This is / That’s+the first / second / third time+ega+have / has+V3
  • The lift isn’t working again. This is the second time it’s broken down this month.Lift yana ishlamayapti. Bu oy ikkinchi marta buzilishi.
  • The neighbours’ dog is barking again. That’s the third time it’s woken us up tonight.Qo‘shnilarning iti yana hurayapti. Bu kecha bizni uchinchi marta uyg‘otishi.
  • “Is this the first time you’ve been to Samarkand?” “No, I’ve been here twice before.”— Samarqandga birinchi marta kelishingizmi? — Yo‘q, bu yerda avval ikki marta bo‘lganman.
  • This is the first time I’ve cooked plov. I hope it’s OK!Birinchi marta palov pishiryapman. Umid qilamanki, yaxshi chiqadi!

Qoida: It’s the first time dan keyin present perfect keladi — present simple ham, present continuous ham emas: It’s the first time she has flown ✅ — she flies ❌, she is flying ❌.

O‘zbekchada: “Birinchi marta mashina haydayapman”, “Toshkentga birinchi kelishim” — hozirgi zamon yoki harakat nomi. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ko‘pincha It’s the first time I drive ❌ deb yuborishadi. To‘g‘risi: It’s the first time I’ve driven ✅. Mantiq: “hozirgacha bo‘lgan tajribamni sanasam — bu birinchisi”.

O‘tmish haqida gapirsak, It was the first time + past perfect (had + V3) ishlatiladi: It was the first time she had flown. Bu — Unit 15 mavzusi.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“Have you been to London?” va “Have you ever been to London?” — farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil: ikkalasi ham hayotiy tajriba haqida. ever “hayotingizda hech” degan ma’noni biroz kuchaytiradi. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

Nega “It’s the first time” dan keyin present perfect? Axir ish hozir bo‘lyapti-ku?

Chunki biz tajribani hozirgacha sanayapmiz: “hayotimda shu paytgacha necha marta mashina haydadim? — birinchi marta”. Sanash hozirgacha davom etayotgan davrni qamraydi — bu present perfect ning ishi. Shuning uchun It’s the first time I’ve driven a car.

“this morning” bilan qachon present perfect, qachon past simple?

Soatga qarang. Agar hali ertalab bo‘lsa (masalan, soat 10:00) — davr tugamagan: I’ve had two coffees this morning. Agar tushdan keyin bo‘lsa — ertalab tugagan: I had two coffees this morning. today esa kun oxirigacha odatda present perfect bilan ishlatiladi.

Vafot etgan odam haqida “He has never been to Mecca” deyish mumkinmi?

Yo‘q. Uning hayoti tugagan, “hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr” yo‘q. Shuning uchun past simple: My grandfather never went to Mecca. Tirik odam haqida esa: My grandmother has never been to Mecca.

“so far”, “up to now” va “until now” farqi nimada?

Uchalasi ham “shu paytgacha, hozircha” degani. so far eng ko‘p ishlatiladi va ko‘pincha ish hali davom etayotganini, keyin o‘zgarishi mumkinligini bildiradi: So far, so good (Hozircha hammasi yaxshi). until now ko‘pincha “hozirgacha shunday edi, endi o‘zgardi” ma’nosida ham keladi.

“the best film I’ve ever seen” — bu yerda “that” kerak emasmi?

that qo‘yish mumkin, lekin shart emas: the best film (that) I’ve ever seen. Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri; og‘zaki nutqda odatda tushirib qoldiriladi. Bu nisbiy gaplar mavzusi (Unit 92–93).

“Have you ever ...?” savoliga qanday javob beraman?

Qisqa: Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t. / No, never. To‘liqroq: Yes, I’ve been there twice. / No, I’ve never been there. Diqqat: Yes, I’ve. ❌ — ijobiy qisqa javobda qisqartma ishlatilmaydi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. “ever” bilan so‘roq gap tuzing.

  1. (you / ride / a camel?) →
  2. (she / be / to Japan?) →
  3. (they / lose / their passports?) →
  4. (you / win / a competition?) →

2-mashq. Gapni orttirma daraja + ever bilan qayta yozing.

  1. It’s a very good book. (read) → It’s the best book .
  2. She’s a very kind person. (meet) → She’s the kindest person .
  3. It’s a very expensive restaurant. (be to) → It’s the most expensive restaurant .
  4. It’s a very funny story. (hear) → It’s the funniest story .

3-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. Jasur is very nervous. It’s the first time he (speak) in public.
  2. I’m eating sushi. This is the first time I (eat) it.
  3. The phone is ringing again. This is the third time it (ring) this morning.
  4. You’ve forgotten your homework again! That’s the second time you (forget) it this week.

4-mashq. Present perfect yoki past simple? Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing.

  1. I (not / see) Aziz this week. Is he ill?
  2. I (not / see) Aziz last week. He was in Bukhara.
  3. We bought this car in March, and it (not / give) us any problems so far.
  4. I’m really thirsty. I (not / drink) anything since lunch.
  5. How many cups of tea (you / drink) today?
  6. My great-grandfather (never / travel) abroad. He died in 1990.
  7. We (go) to Khiva two years ago.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Present perfect — o‘tmishdan hozirgacha davom etayotgan davr haqida: hayot, today, this week, since ..., so far.
  • Tajriba: Have you ever ...? / I’ve never ... / I’ve been to ... twice. ever va never — have va V3 orasida.
  • Orttirma daraja + I’ve ever + V3: the best film I’ve ever seen.
  • recently, lately, so far, up to now, since ..., for ... (hozirgacha) → present perfect.
  • today / this week / this year — davr tugamagan bo‘lsa present perfect; yesterday / last week / ago — doim past simple.
  • It’s the first / second time + present perfect: It’s the first time I’ve flown.
  • never + not birga ishlatilmaydi: I’ve never ... ✅, I haven’t never ... ❌.
