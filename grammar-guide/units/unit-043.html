Passive 2 (be done / been done / being done)

Majhul nisbat — 2 (be done / been done / being done)
Passive · Majhul nisbat (Passive)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Passive ni hamma zamonlarda va modal fe’llar bilan yasashni: is being repaired, has been repaired, will be repaired, must be repaired.
  • Passive dagi be fe’lining uch shaklini: be, been va being — qaysi birini qachon tanlashni.
  • be done: will, can, must, should, going to, to dan keyin.
  • been done: have / has / had dan keyin (present perfect va past perfect passive).
  • being done: continuous passive (is being built) va -ing shakli (I hate being interrupted).
  • Barcha zamonlar uchun to‘liq passive jadvalini.

Unit 42 da passive = be + V3 ekanini bilib oldingiz va ikkita zamonni ko‘rdingiz. Bu darsda o‘sha formula boshqa zamonlarda ham ishlaydi, faqat be fe’li zamonga qarab o‘zgaradi: be / been / being. V3 hamisha o‘zgarmaydi.

O‘zbek tilida majhul nisbat bitta qo‘shimcha (-il, ba’zan -in / -n) bilan yasaladi, zamon va ma’no esa undan keyingi qo‘shimchalar bilan beriladi: tuzat-il-yapti (is being repaired), tuzat-il-gan (has been repaired), tuzat-il-adi (will be repaired), tuzat-il-ishi kerak (must be repaired). Inglizchada esa asosiy fe’l o‘zgarmaydi (repaired), zamon va ma’noni yordamchi fe’llar zanjiri beradi: is being repaired, has been repaired, will be repaired, must be repaired. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar be / been / being ni tushirib qoldiradi yoki adashtiradi.

be done: modal fe’llar, will, going to va to dan keyin Passive infinitive: modal / will / going to / to + be + V3

will, can, must, should kabi modal fe’llardan keyin, shuningdek (be) going to, have to, need to, want to dan keyin fe’lning infinitiv shakli keladi. Passive da u be + V3 bo‘ladi:

Ega+will / can / must / should / going to / to+be+V3
Aniq nisbat → majhul nisbat (be + V3)
Ma’noActivePassive
will (kelasi zamon)They will repair the road next month.The road will be repaired next month.
going to (reja)They are going to demolish the old school.The old school is going to be demolished.
can (imkoniyat)You can see the mountains from here.The mountains can be seen from here.
must (majburiyat)We must finish this work today.This work must be finished today.
should (maslahat)You should keep fish in the fridge.Fish should be kept in the fridge.
might (ehtimol)They might cancel the match.The match might be cancelled.
have to / need toSomeone has to clean the carpets.The carpets have to be cleaned.
to (infinitiv)She hopes (that) they will choose her.She hopes to be chosen.

  • The new metro line will be opened next year.Yangi metro liniyasi kelasi yil ochiladi.
  • The exam results won’t be announced until Friday.Imtihon natijalari juma kunigacha e’lon qilinmaydi.
  • Is anything going to be done about the noise?Shovqinga qarshi biror ish qilinadimi?
  • Nothing can be done now. It’s too late.Endi hech narsa qilib bo‘lmaydi. Kech bo‘lib qoldi.
  • The roof needs to be fixed before winter.Tomni qishgacha tuzatish kerak.
  • I don’t want to be disturbed while I’m studying.Dars qilayotganimda meni bezovta qilishlarini xohlamayman.
  • When will the winner be announced?G‘olib qachon e’lon qilinadi?

Inkor va so‘roq faqat birinchi yordamchi fe’l bilan yasaladi: The road won’t be repaired. Can this be repaired? When will it be finished? be va V3 o‘z joyida qoladi.

O‘zbekchadagi -ilishi kerak / -ilishi mumkin / -ilmaydi shakllari shu tuzilishga mos keladi: tuzatilishi kerak = must be repaired; ko‘rilishi mumkin = can be seen; bajarilmaydi = won’t be done.

been done: present perfect va past perfect passive have / has / had been + V3

have / has / had dan keyin fe’lning V3 shakli keladi. Passive da been (be ning V3 si) qo‘shiladi va keyin asosiy fe’lning V3 si keladi. Ikkita V3 ketma-ket: been + cleaned.

Ega+have / has / had+been+V3
Perfect passive
ZamonActivePassive
Present perfectSomebody has stolen my bag.My bag has been stolen.
Present perfectNobody has told me anything.I haven’t been told anything.
Past perfectThey had decorated the hall before we came.The hall had been decorated before we came.
Modal + haveSomeone must have seen the thief.The thief must have been seen.
Modal + haveThey should have finished the report yesterday.The report should have been finished yesterday.

  • Have the invitations been sent yet?Taklifnomalar jo‘natildimi hali?
  • I haven’t been told anything about the change.Menga o‘zgarish haqida hech narsa aytilmagan.
  • When we arrived, the hall had been decorated.Biz kelganimizda zal bezatib bo‘lingan edi.
  • Our flight had been delayed, so we had time for a coffee.Reysimiz kechiktirilgan edi, shuning uchun qahva ichishga vaqtimiz bo‘ldi.
  • Could this accident have been avoided?Bu avariyaning oldini olish mumkin edimi?

Qaysi biri: was done yoki has been done?

Zamonlarning farqi aniq nisbatdagi kabi: past simple — o‘tmishdagi, vaqti aniq voqea; present perfect — hozirgi vaqt bilan bog‘liq, natijasi hozir ko‘rinib turgan ish (Unit 7, 13).

  • The road was repaired in July.Yo‘l iyul oyida ta’mirlandi. (qachonligi aytilgan)
  • The road has been repaired. You can drive on it now.Yo‘l ta’mirlab bo‘lingan. Endi uning bo‘ylab yurish mumkin. (natija muhim)

has been being done (present perfect continuous passive) kabi shakllar grammatik jihatdan mumkin, lekin og‘ir va juda kam ishlatiladi. Bunday vaziyatda aniq nisbatni tanlang: They have been repairing the road for weeks. (Yo‘l bir necha haftadan beri ta’mirlanmoqda.)

being done: continuous passive va -ing shakli is / was being done; being done after prepositions and verbs

1. Continuous passive: is being / was being + V3

Hozir yoki o‘tmishdagi ma’lum paytda jarayonda bo‘lgan ish haqida gapirganda am / is / are being yoki was / were being + V3 ishlatiladi (continuous passive).

Ega+am / is / are / was / were+being+V3
Continuous passive
ZamonActivePassive
Present continuousThey are repairing the road.The road is being repaired.
Present continuousSomeone is watching us.We are being watched.
Past continuousThey were painting the classroom when I arrived.The classroom was being painted when I arrived.
  • A new hotel is being built near the station.Bekat yonida yangi mehmonxona qurilyapti.
  • The road is being repaired, so we can’t drive through.Yo‘l ta’mirlanyapti, shuning uchun u yerdan o‘ta olmaymiz.
  • Look at that man with the camera. I think we’re being watched.Anavi kamerali odamga qarang. Menimcha, bizni kuzatishyapti.
  • When I got to the garage, my car was still being repaired.Ustaxonaga borganimda, mashinam hali ham ta’mirlanayotgan edi.
  • Are the students being tested today?Bugun talabalar sinovdan o‘tkazilyaptimi?

O‘zbekcha “uy qurilyapti” — inglizchada The house is being built. ❌ The house is building deb bo‘lmaydi: bu “uy (o‘zi) nimadir qurayapti” degan ma’noni beradi. Jarayonni ko‘rsatish uchun being kerak.

2. being + V3: -ing shakli sifatida (predloglardan va ba’zi fe’llardan keyin)

Predloglardan keyin va like, hate, enjoy, remember, avoid kabi fe’llardan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi. Agar ega ish ta’sirini o‘zi oladigan bo‘lsa, being + V3 ishlatiladi:

  • I hate being interrupted when I’m reading.Kitob o‘qiyotganimda xalaqit berishlarini yomon ko‘raman.
  • Nobody likes being laughed at.Hech kim ustidan kulinishini yoqtirmaydi.
  • Dilnoza enjoys being asked for advice.Dilnozaga undan maslahat so‘rashlari yoqadi.
  • The cat stole the meat without being noticed.Mushuk go‘shtni hech kimga sezdirmasdan o‘g‘irlab ketdi.
  • Sardor still remembers being chosen as the best pupil in his class.Sardor sinfidagi eng yaxshi o‘quvchi deb tanlanganini hali ham eslaydi.
  • There is a risk of being misunderstood.Noto‘g‘ri tushunilish xavfi bor.
-ing: aniq va majhul
Active (-ing)Passive (being + V3)
I like asking questions. (men so‘rayman)I like being asked questions. (mendan so‘rashadi)
He left without noticing us. (u bizni sezmadi)He left without being noticed. (uni hech kim sezmadi)

Hamma zamonlar uchun passive jadvali The full passive table

Qoida (hamma zamon uchun bitta usul): (1) Aniq nisbatdagi yordamchi fe’l (is, was, has, had, will, can ...) o‘zgarmaydi. (2) Asosiy fe’l o‘rniga be ning kerakli shakli keladi: be / been / being. (3) Asosiy fe’lning o‘zi V3 bo‘lib oxirida turadi. Present simple va past simple da yordamchi fe’l yo‘q — u yerda be o‘zi zamonni ko‘rsatadi: cleans → is cleaned, cleaned → was cleaned.

Eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan passive shakllari: present simple (is made), past simple (was built), present perfect (has been sent), will (will be done) va can / must / should be done. Continuous passive (is being repaired) ham juda tanish, lekin har kuni ishlatilmaydi.

Predlogli fe’llarning passive shakli Verb + preposition in the passive

Predlogli fe’llarda (look after, talk about, laugh at, look at) predlog fe’l bilan birga qoladi. Aniq gapdagi predlogdan keyingi so‘z ega bo‘ladi, predlog esa V3 dan keyin turadi:

Predlog fe’ldan keyin qoladi
ActivePassive
Their grandmother looks after the children.The children are looked after by their grandmother.
People talked about the accident for weeks.The accident was talked about for weeks.
Everybody laughed at him.He was laughed at by everybody.
We must deal with this problem today.This problem must be dealt with today.

Frazali fe’llar (Unit 137–145) ham xuddi shunday: They turned off the lights → The lights were turned off.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

The road is repairing now.The road is being repaired now.Yo‘l o‘zi tuzatmaydi — uni tuzatishadi. Jarayondagi passive: is being + V3 (o‘zbekcha “ta’mirlanyapti”).
The parcel will delivered tomorrow.The parcel will be delivered tomorrow.will dan keyin be kerak: will + be + V3.
This work must finished today.This work must be finished today.Modal fe’ldan keyin be ni tushirib qoldirmang.
My phone has stolen.My phone has been stolen.has dan keyin been kerak: has + been + V3.
The room has being cleaned.The room has been cleaned.been va being ni adashtirmang: have / has / had dan keyin been, is / was dan keyin (jarayon) being.
I hate interrupted.I hate being interrupted.“Menga xalaqit berishlari” — passive -ing: being + V3.
The mistake could been avoided.The mistake could have been avoided.Modal + have been + V3. “Have” tushib qolmasin.
Will the results be announce tomorrow?Will the results be announced tomorrow?be dan keyin asosiy fe’l V3 bo‘lishi shart (announce emas, announced).

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

be, been va being ni qanday ajrataman?

Fe’l guruhida oldin kelgan so‘zga qarang. will / can / must / should / to / going to dan keyin — be. have / has / had dan keyin — been. am / is / are / was / were dan keyin (jarayon ma’nosida) yoki predlogdan keyin — being. Passive gapda asosiy fe’l har doim V3 bo‘ladi: will be done, has been done, is being done.

“is being repaired”, “has been repaired” va “was repaired” ning farqi nimada?

is being repaired — ish hozir jarayonda (hali tugamagan). has been repaired — ish tugagan va natijasi hozir ahamiyatli. was repaired — ish o‘tmishda, ma’lum vaqtda tugagan. The road is being repaired = hozir ta’mirlanyapti; The road has been repaired = ta’mirlab bo‘lishgan; The road was repaired in July = iyulda ta’mirlangan.

“The room’s been cleaned” dagi ’s — is mi yoki has mi?

Bu has. Qisqartma ’s ikki xil ma’noda keladi, lekin keyingi so‘z aniqlab beradi: The room’s been cleaned = has been cleaned (been ni ko‘rsangiz, “has”); The room’s being cleaned = is being cleaned (being ni ko‘rsangiz, “is”); The room’s cleaned every day = is cleaned.

Nima uchun “The house is building” deb bo‘lmaydi?

Chunki is building — aniq nisbat: “uy qurayapti” degan ma’no chiqadi, ya’ni uy ega, ish bajaruvchi. Uyni esa kimdir quradi. To‘g‘ri variant: The house is being built. Aniq nisbatni ishlatmoqchi bo‘lsangiz: They are building the house.

Nima uchun present perfect continuous va boshqalarning passive shakli yo‘q?

Grammatik jihatdan ular yasalishi mumkin (has been being repaired), lekin been va being ketma-ket kelib, gap og‘ir va noqulay eshitiladi. Ingliz tilida ular deyarli ishlatilmaydi. Bunday vaziyatda aniq nisbatni (They have been repairing it) yoki oddiyroq passive zamonni tanlang.

Predlogli fe’llardan passive yasash mumkinmi? Predlog qayerda turadi?

Mumkin. Predlog fe’ldan ajralmaydi va V3 dan keyin qoladi: She looks after the baby → The baby is looked after. We talked about it → It was talked about. Predlogni tushirib qoldirmang: ❌ The baby is looked.

“I like being asked” va “I like asking” ning farqi nimada?

Farq — kim ish bajaradi. I like asking questions — men savol beraman. I like being asked questions — menga savol berishadi. Xuddi shunday: without noticing us (u bizni sezmadi) va without being noticed (uni hech kim sezmadi).

Suhbatda qaysi passive shakllar eng ko‘p ishlatiladi?

Eng ko‘p: is / are + V3 va was / were + V3 (Unit 42), keyin has / have been + V3, will be + V3, can / must / should be + V3. Continuous passive (is being built) ham keng tarqalgan, lekin ancha kam. will have been done va having been done asosan rasmiy yozuvda uchraydi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Passive hamma zamonda be + V3; zamonni be ning shakli ko‘rsatadi, V3 o‘zgarmaydi.
  • be + V3: will, can, must, should, going to, to dan keyin (will be done, must be done, to be done).
  • been + V3: have / has / had dan keyin (has been done, had been done, should have been done).
  • being + V3: jarayondagi passive (is being done, was being done) va predlog / fe’ldan keyingi -ing (without being noticed, I hate being interrupted).
  • Inkor va so‘roq faqat birinchi yordamchi fe’l orqali: Has it been sent? It won’t be finished.
  • Present perfect continuous, past perfect continuous va future continuous passive shakllari ishlatilmaydi — aniq nisbatni tanlang.
  • Predlogli fe’llarda predlog V3 dan keyin qoladi: looked after, talked about.
