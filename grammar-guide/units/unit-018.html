Used to (do)

Used to — avvalgi odatlar
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • used to + fe’l — o‘tmishda takrorlanib turgan odat yoki o‘tmishdagi holat, hozir esa unday emas.
  • Inkor va so‘roq shakllari: didn’t use to, Did you use to ...? (used emas, use!).
  • used to va oddiy o‘tgan zamon (past simple) qachon farq qilishini.
  • Hozirgi odat uchun nega use to emas, present simple ishlatilishini.
  • used to ning would (o‘tmishdagi odat), was doing (past continuous) hamda be used to (ko‘nikkan bo‘lmoq) bilan farqini.

Bekzod bolaligida har kuni shaxmat o‘ynardi, hozir esa vaqti yo‘q. Ingliz tilida bu fikr bitta qolip bilan aytiladi: He used to play chess every day. Bu gap ikki narsani birdan bildiradi: (1) o‘tmishda shunday bo‘lgan; (2) hozir bunday emas.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no odatda “ilgari / bir vaqtlar ... -ardi” yoki “... -gan edi” bilan beriladi: Ilgari men tez-tez shaxmat o‘ynardim (hozir o‘ynamayman). Ingliz tilida buning uchun maxsus ibora bor: used to + fe’lning asl shakli, va u barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil. Ehtiyot bo‘ling: o‘zbekcha “ko‘nikkanman, o‘rganib qolganman” ma’nosi inglizchada boshqa qolip — be used to (I’m used to the heat). Ikkalasining talaffuzi bir xil, shuning uchun ularni aralashtirib yuborish oson (Unit 61).

Used to — o‘tmishdagi odat va holat Past habits and states that are no longer true

used to ni biz o‘tmishda ancha vaqt davomida takrorlanib turgan ish yoki o‘tmishda mavjud bo‘lgan holat haqida gapirganda ishlatamiz. Muhimi — hozir bu boshqacha:

  • Bekzod used to play chess every day, but he doesn’t have time now.Bekzod ilgari har kuni shaxmat o‘ynardi, lekin hozir vaqti yo‘q.
Ega+used to+fe’lning asl shakli+...

Qoida: used to faqat o‘tgan zamonni bildiradi. Shakli hamma shaxslar uchun bir xil (I / he / they used to ...), -s qo‘shilmaydi. Undan keyin fe’lning asl shakli keladi: used to play ✅ — used to playing ❌, used to played ❌.

Imlo: did / didn’t dan keyin used emas, use yoziladi, chunki o‘tgan zamon belgisi endi did da: He didn’t use to like fish ✅ — He didn’t used to like fish ❌. Did you use to ...? ✅ — Did you used to ...? ❌. Ko‘p ona tilida so‘zlashuvchilar ham bu yerda xato yozadi, lekin imtihonda to‘g‘ri shakl — use to.

Juda rasmiy yoki eskirgan uslubda I used not to like it deyiladi; o‘quvchilarga didn’t use to yoki never used to yetarli. So‘roq gapning rasmiy shakli Used you to ...? ham deyarli ishlatilmaydi.

Talaffuz: used to ham, use to ham bir xil o‘qiladi: /ˈjuːstə/ — “yus-tə” (s tovushi bilan, d eshitilmaydi). Shuning uchun yozishda adashish oson: did / didn’t dan keyin — use to, boshqa hollarda — used to. (“Ishlatmoq” ma’nosidagi use /juːz/, used /juːzd/ esa z bilan o‘qiladi — bu boshqa fe’l.)

Used to yoki past simple? Used to or past simple?

Odatda used to o‘rniga oddiy o‘tgan zamonni ham ishlatish mumkin. Quyidagi ikki gap deyarli bir xil ma’noda:

  • When I was a child, I used to go to the park every Sunday.Bolaligimda har yakshanba parkka borardim.
  • When I was a child, I went to the park every Sunday.Bolaligimda har yakshanba parkka borardim. (Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil.)

Farq: used to “ilgari shunday edi, hozir esa yo‘q” degan qarama-qarshilikni ta’kidlaydi; past simple esa shunchaki o‘tmishdagi fakt. Lekin past simple har doim ishlatiladigan holatlar bor:

Qoida: used to ni bitta aniq voqea yoki necha marta / qancha davom etgani aytilgan holatda ishlatmaymiz. Bunday gaplarda faqat past simple.

Hozirgi odat uchun used to yo‘q

used to faqat o‘tmish uchun. Hozirgi odatni present simple (ko‘pincha usually, often, every day bilan) aytamiz. I use to ... degan hozirgi shakl mavjud emas.

🕰️ O‘tmish — used to

  • I used to get up late.
  • We used to eat out a lot.

Endi unday emas.

▶️ Hozir — present simple

  • I usually get up early.
  • We eat at home most days.

Hozirgi odat. I use to get up ❌.

  • Jasur used to work at night, but now he works during the day.Jasur ilgari tunda ishlardi, hozir esa kunduzi ishlaydi.
  • We never used to eat out, but now we often eat in cafés.Ilgari hech qachon tashqarida ovqatlanmasdik, hozir esa tez-tez kafelarda ovqatlanamiz.

used to va was doing (past continuous)

Bu ikkisini ham aralashtirmang. used to — o‘tmishda takrorlanib turgan odat (hozir yo‘q). was / were + -ing — o‘tmishdagi bir paytda ayni jarayonda bo‘lgan ish (Unit 6).

  • I used to play computer games a lot. (ilgari tez-tez o‘ynardim, hozir unday emas)Ilgari kompyuter o‘yinlarini ko‘p o‘ynardim.
  • I was playing a computer game when my father came home. (o‘sha paytda o‘ynayotgan edim)Dadam uyga kelganida men kompyuter o‘yini o‘ynayotgan edim.

Used to va would Used to or would for past habits?

O‘tmishdagi takrorlangan ishlarni would bilan ham aytish mumkin. Ma’no deyarli bir xil, lekin would uchun shart bor: bu o‘tmishdagi vaqt gapda allaqachon aytilgan bo‘lishi kerak (when I was young, in those days …). would haqida batafsil — Unit 36.

  • When we were children, we used to visit / would visit our grandparents every summer.Bolaligimizda har yozda buvi-bobomiznikiga borardik.
  • In those days my mother used to bake / would bake bread every Friday.O‘sha paytlarda onam har juma non yopardi.

Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, used to ni tanlang: u har ikkala ma’noda (odat va holat) ham to‘g‘ri, would esa faqat odat uchun.

Used to (do) va be used to (doing) Don’t confuse them!

Shakli o‘xshash, ma’nosi mutlaqo boshqa ikki qolip bor. Ular Unit 61 da to‘liq o‘rganiladi, bu yerda asosiy farqni ko‘ring:

used to + fe’l

O‘tmishdagi odat/holat, hozir yo‘q.

  • I used to live in a village.

Ilgari qishloqda yashardim.

be used to + -ing / ot

Ko‘nikkan bo‘lmoq; hozirgi holat.

  • I’m used to living in a city.

Shaharda yashashga ko‘nikkanman.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I used to play tennis” desam, hozir o‘ynamayman degani-mi?

Ha, odatda shunday. used to “ilgari shunday edi” va “hozir esa yo‘q” ma’nolarini birga beradi. Agar hozir ham o‘ynayotgan bo‘lsangiz, I play tennis yoki I’ve played tennis for years deysiz. Faqat “o‘ynardim” deb o‘tmish haqida oddiy fakt aytmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, I played tennis a lot as a child ham to‘g‘ri.

Hozirgi zamonda “use to” bormi? Masalan, “I use to go” — to‘g‘rimi?

Yo‘q. Bunday hozirgi zamon shakli mavjud emas. Hozirgi odatni present simple bilan aytamiz: I usually go, I often go, I go every day. use to faqat did / didn’t dan keyin keladi: Did you use to go?

“Didn’t used to” bilan “didn’t use to” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Standart grammatikaga ko‘ra didn’t use to to‘g‘ri, chunki -d (o‘tgan zamon belgisi) endi did yordamchi fe’lida. Ba’zi gaplashuvchilar didn’t used to deb yozadi, lekin darslik va imtihonlarda bu xato hisoblanadi. Inkorni never used to bilan ham aytish mumkin, bunda hech qanday imlo muammosi yo‘q.

Qachon used to, qachon would?

Takrorlangan o‘tmish odati uchun ikkalasi ham bo‘ladi, lekin would uchun o‘tmishdagi vaqt gapda allaqachon aytilgan bo‘lishi kerak. Holat haqida (have, be, live, like, know …) faqat used to: I used to have a bike ✅ — I would have a bike ❌. Xavfsiz yo‘l — used to.

“Last year I used to work in a bank” deyish mumkinmi?

Mumkin, lekin kam ishlatiladi. used to ko‘pincha uzoqroq, noaniq o‘tmish davri uchun keladi. last year kabi yaqin, aniq davr bilan shunchaki fakt aytilsa, past simple tabiiyroq: Last year I worked in a bank ✅. Hozirgi holat bilan qarama-qarshilik ta’kidlansa, used to ham to‘g‘ri: Last year I used to walk to work, but now I drive ✅. Uzoqroq davr haqida esa: When I was a student, I used to work in a bank ✅.

“You used to live here, ...?” — oxirgi qisqa savol (question tag) qanday bo‘ladi?

Oddiy gapdagidek: You used to live here, didn’t you? Inkorda esa: You never used to like tea, did you?

Qisqa javobda “used to” ni to‘liq takrorlash kerakmi?

Yo‘q. Fe’lni tushirib qoldirib, faqat used to deyish mumkin: “Do you swim?” “Not now, but I used to.” (Hozir yo‘q, lekin ilgari suzardim.) Savol bo‘lsa, odatdagidek: Did you use to swim? — Yes, I did.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’ldan used to / didn’t use to shaklini yasang.

  1. Bekzod (play) chess every day, but he doesn’t have time now.
  2. We (have) a big dog, but it died last year.
  3. Dilnoza (like) coffee, but now she drinks it every morning.
  4. There (be) a cinema in our street, but they closed it.
  5. Where you (live) before you moved to Tashkent?
  6. My grandmother (wear) glasses, but now she can’t read without them.

2-mashq. Past simple yoki used to? Qaysi shakl(lar) to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, shuni yozing. Ikkalasi to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ikkalasini yozing.

  1. When I was a student, I (lived / used to live) in a small room near the university.
  2. I (went / used to go) to Chorsu bazaar twice last week.
  3. Aziz (worked / used to work) in a bank for five years, then became a teacher.
  4. Yesterday evening we (watched / used to watch) a film.
  5. When she was little, Kamola (hated / used to hate) vegetables.
  6. On 5 May 2019 my sister (got / used to get) married.

3-mashq. Hozirgi va avvalgi holatni solishtiring. “Hozir”ni bildiruvchi gapga qarab, used to bilan gap tuzing.

  1. Now Jasur lives in Tashkent. (Before: Samarkand) → Jasur in Samarkand.
  2. Now Kamola has short hair. (Before: long hair) → Kamola long hair.
  3. Now we never eat meat. (Before: often) → We meat.
  4. Now Sardor drives to work. (Before: by bus) → Sardor to work by bus.
  5. Now I’m not afraid of dogs. (Before: afraid) → I afraid of dogs.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • used to + fe’lning asl shakli = o‘tmishdagi odat yoki holat, hozir esa yo‘q: I used to play chess. There used to be a shop here.
  • Barcha shaxslarda bir xil: he used to, they used to (-s yo‘q).
  • Inkor: didn’t use to / never used to. So‘roq: Did you use to ...? (did dan keyin use, used emas).
  • Bitta aniq voqea, necha marta yoki qancha davom etgani aytilgan holatda used to emas, past simple: We lived there for three years.
  • Hozirgi odat — present simple (I usually ...); I use to degan shakl yo‘q.
  • Odat uchun would ham mumkin, holat uchun faqat used to (we used to have a dog).
  • be used to + -ing (ko‘nikkanman) — butunlay boshqa qolip.
