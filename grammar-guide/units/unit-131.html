131

Adjective + preposition 2

Sifat + predlog — 2
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • of bilan keladigan sifatlar: afraid of, fond of, proud of, jealous of, aware of, capable of, full of, short of, tired of …
  • to bilan keladigan sifatlar: similar to, married to, engaged to va different from / to / than farqi.
  • at, in, on, for, with bilan keladigan sifatlar: good at, interested in, keen on, famous for, responsible for, crowded with, dependent on.
  • O‘zbekcha qo‘shimchalar (-dan, -ga, -da) nega to‘g‘ri inglizcha predlogni topishga yordam bermasligini.
  • Bu predloglardan keyin fe’l kelsa, u -ing shaklida bo‘lishini: good at drawing.

Ingliz tilida ko‘p sifatlar o‘zidan keyin aniq bir predlog talab qiladi: afraid of, interested in, good at. Predlogni mantiq bilan topib bo‘lmaydi, shuning uchun sifat bilan predlogni bitta juftlik sifatida yodlaymiz. Tuzilishi oddiy:

Ega+be+sifat+predlog+ot / olmosh / fe’l-ing

Eslatma: Unit 130 da nice of / kind to, angry with / about, sorry about / for kabi birikmalar o‘rgangansiz. Bu darsda qolgan muhim birikmalar o‘rganiladi.

O‘zbek tilida bu ma’no kelishik qo‘shimchalari bilan beriladi: itdan qo‘rqmoq (-dan), shaxmatga qiziqmoq (-ga), matematikada kuchli (-da). Inglizchada esa har safar alohida predlog kerak. Quyidagi jadvalda ko‘rasiz: ba’zan o‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha va inglizcha predlog mos keladi (✅), ba’zan esa aldaydi (⚠️) — xatolar aynan shu yerdan chiqadi.

jealous of va envious of ma’nosi yaqin: boshqada bor narsani o‘zingiz ham xohlaysiz. jealous yana “sevgan odamimni yo‘qotib qo‘yaman” degan qo‘rquvni ham bildiradi. critical of — “kamchilikni topib tanqid qiladigan”; tolerant of — “chidaydigan, bag‘rikeng”.

Afraid haqida: afraid of + ot yoki -ing (afraid of flying), lekin afraid to do (I’m afraid to jump) — “jur’at qilolmayman” ma’nosida. Bu farq Unit 66 da batafsil.

tired of … (= fed up with)

Biror narsadan zerikish, to‘yish. Undan keyin sabab (ot yoki -ing) keladi.

  • I’m tired of this rain.
  • She’s tired of washing dishes.

tired (predlogsiz)

Charchagan, dam olish kerak. Predlog kerak emas.

  • I’m tired after the long walk.
  • The children look tired.

O‘zbekcha charchadim ham, zerikdim / to‘yib ketdim ham “-dan” bilan aytilishi mumkin. Inglizchada ikki ma’no ikkiga bo‘linadi: jismoniy charchoq — oddiy tired; “bezdim” — tired of yoki fed up with (Unit 98 dagi bored / boring ham shu ma’noga yaqin).

full of — sifat (“to‘la”), fill … with — fe’l (“to‘ldirmoq”): The glass is full of water. / Fill the glass with water. Fe’l haqida — Unit 136.

To: o‘xshashlik, farq, nikoh similar to, different from / to / than, married to, engaged to

similar to va the same as

  • Her style of embroidery is similar to the suzanis of Bukhara.Uning kashtachilik uslubi Buxoro suzanilariga o‘xshaydi.
  • My phone is similar to yours, but a little bigger.Telefonim senikiga o‘xshaydi, lekin sal kattaroq.

similar to — “o‘xshash” (aynan bir xil emas). “Aynan bir xil” uchun the same as ishlatiladi: My phone is the same as yours (ikkalasi bir model). ❌ the same like — xato (qiyoslash haqida Unit 107 ga qarang).

different from / to / than

different + qaysi predlog?
VariantQayerda ishlatiladi?Misol
different fromhamma joyda; eng xavfsiz variantLife in a village is very different from life in Tashkent.
different toBritaniya ingliz tilida ham bo‘ladiYour accent is different to mine.
different thanAmerika ingliz tilida ham bo‘ladiYour accent is different than mine. (AmE)

Imtihonda yoki rasmiy yozuvda different from yozing — bu hech qachon xato bo‘lmaydi. Agar different dan keyin to‘liq gap kelsa, Britaniya tilida different from what … deyiladi: Samarkand was different from what I had imagined. Amerika tilida shu o‘rinda different than I had imagined ham uchraydi.

married to va engaged to

  • Nargiza is married to an engineer from Fergana.Nargiza farg‘onalik muhandisga turmushga chiqqan.
  • Sardor got engaged to Kamola last spring.Sardor o‘tgan bahorda Kamolaga unashtirildi.
  • Bekzod has been married to Dilnoza for ten years.Bekzod Dilnoza bilan turmush qurganiga o‘n yil bo‘ldi.

married with ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin boshqa ma’noda: He is married with two children = “uning ikkita farzandi bor, uylangan”. Turmush o‘rtog‘ingiz nomi yoki kasbi aytilsa, doim to: married to a pilot ✅ — married with a pilot ❌. Fe’l shakli predlogsiz: She married Sardor (Unit 132 ga qarang).

good at … (qobiliyat)

  • I’m hopeless at remembering faces.Men yuzlarni eslab qolishga mutlaqo qobiliyatsizman.
  • Is your brother good at drawing?Akangiz rasm chizishga qobiliyatlimi?
  • Jasur is terrible at cooking; he even burns the toast.Jasur ovqat pishirishda juda yomon; hatto tostni ham kuydirib yuboradi.

good at — “biror ishni yaxshi uddalaydi”. Odamlar bilan munosabatda good with deyiladi: Aziz is good with children (bolalar bilan muomalasi yaxshi). Foydali narsa uchun good for: Walking is good for your health. Mehribon bo‘lish uchun good to/kind to — Unit 130.

interested in va keen on

  • Dilnoza is interested in old coins and stamps.Dilnoza eski tangalar va markalarga qiziqadi.
  • I’m not very keen on horror films.Qo‘rqinchli filmlar menga unchalik yoqmaydi.
  • Are you interested in joining the chess club?Shaxmat to‘garagiga qo‘shilishga qiziqasizmi?

interested in — “qiziqadi”. ❌ interested to music, ❌ interested on music. Faqat bitta istisno: interested to hear / know / see / find out (“eshitib qiziqib qoldim, bilgim keldi”): I’d be interested to hear your opinion. Boshqa fe’llar bilan in + -ing. keen on — asosan Britaniya ingliz tilida “juda yoqtiradi, ishqibozi”; keen to do (“qizg‘in xohlaydi”) esa boshqa tuzilma.

famous for, responsible for

  • Margilan is famous for its silk.Marg‘ilon ipagi bilan mashhur.
  • Who is responsible for the library keys?Kutubxona kalitlari uchun kim javobgar?
  • Sardor is famous as a chess player. He is also famous for his kindness.Sardor shaxmatchi sifatida mashhur. U mehribonligi bilan ham tanilgan.

famous for + nima bilan mashhur; famous as + kim sifatida mashhur (rol, kasb). responsible for + nima uchun javobgar; kimga hisobot berasiz — responsible to: Aziz is responsible to the manager for the shop.

crowded with

  • The beach was crowded with holidaymakers in August.Avgustda plyaj dam oluvchilar bilan gavjum edi.

dependent on va independent of

dependent on

Biror kishi yoki narsaga qaram, tayanadi.

  • Young children are dependent on their parents.
  • The harvest is dependent on the weather.

independent of

Biror kishi yoki narsadan mustaqil.

  • The newspaper is independent of any government.
  • Zarina wants to be independent of her family.

Fe’l ko‘rinishi: depend on — Unit 135 da. Diqqat, ikki sifatning predlogi har xil: dependent on (tayanadi) ↔ independent of (mustaqil).

Predlogdan keyin nima keladi? After the preposition: noun, pronoun or -ing

Bu sifatlar bilan kelgan predlogdan keyin ot, olmosh yoki fe’l-ing keladi. Infinitiv (to do) kelmaydi (Unit 60):

✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Xato
Aziz is good at fixing bikes.Aziz is good at to fix bikes.
I’m tired of waiting.I’m tired to wait.
She’s capable of running a company.She’s capable to run a company.
Are you interested in learning Korean?Are you interested in to learn Korean?

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I’m afraid from dogs.I’m afraid of dogs.O‘zbekcha “-dan” har doim from emas. Qo‘rquv sifatlari (afraid, scared, frightened, terrified) bilan of keladi.
She’s interested to music.She’s interested in music.“-ga qiziqmoq” inglizchada interested in. “to” faqat interested to hear/know kabi qisqa holatlarda.
He is married with a doctor.He is married to a doctor.Turmush o‘rtog‘i bilan — married to. “married with two children” boshqa ma’no (farzandlari bor).
Jasur is good in maths.Jasur is good at maths.Qobiliyat — good at / bad at / hopeless at. O‘zbekcha “-da” ni in deb tarjima qilmang.
The room was full with smoke.The room was full of smoke.Full — doimo of bilan. Fe’l fill esa with bilan keladi: fill the room with smoke.
Samarkand is famous by the Registan.Samarkand is famous for the Registan.“…i bilan mashhur” — famous for. “by” yoki “with” ishlatilmaydi.
My bag is different with yours.My bag is different from yours.Different bilan from (yoki Britaniya tilida to) keladi, with emas.
He is dependent from his parents.He is dependent on his parents.“Qaram” — dependent on; “mustaqil” — independent of.
I’m tired to wait for the bus.I’m tired of waiting for the bus.Predlogdan keyin fe’l -ing shaklida: tired of waiting.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Nega “qo‘rqmoq” -dan bo‘lsa-yu, inglizchada from emas, of deyiladi?

Chunki predlogni so‘zma-so‘z tarjima qilib bo‘lmaydi. O‘zbekchada ham fe’l bilan kelishik mustahkam bog‘langan (qo‘rqmoq — -dan, qiziqmoq — -ga), inglizchada esa har bir sifatning o‘z “sherigi” bor: afraid uchun of, interested uchun in. Shuning uchun sifat va predlogni birga, bitta so‘z kabi yodlang: afraid-of, good-at, interested-in.

Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri: different from, different to yoki different than?

Uchalasi ham ishlatiladi, lekin different from hamma joyda to‘g‘ri va imtihonda eng xavfsiz. different to asosan Britaniya ingliz tilida, different than esa Amerika ingliz tilida uchraydi (ayniqsa keyin to‘liq gap kelsa: different than I expected).

“I’m interested to learn English” deyish mumkinmi?

“O‘rganishga qiziqaman” ma’nosida standart va eng ishonchli shakl — interested in learning English; imtihonda va yozuvda shuni ishlating. Faqat interested to hear / know / see / find out kabi iboralarda to ishlatiladi: I was interested to hear that you had moved to Bukhara. (Siz Buxoroga ko‘chib o‘tganingizni eshitib qiziqdim.)

afraid of va afraid to farqi nimada?

afraid of + ot yoki -ing: qo‘rqish obyekti (afraid of dogs, afraid of falling). afraid to + fe’l: qo‘rqqanidan qilolmaydi (afraid to ask). Bu haqda Unit 66 da yana ko‘rasiz.

good at, good with va good for ning farqi nimada?

good at — biror faoliyatni uddalaydi (good at swimming). good with — odamlar yoki asboblar bilan muomalasi yaxshi (good with children). good for — foydali (good for your health). good to — biror kishiga mehribon (Unit 130).

“full of” va “fill with” ning farqi nimada?

full — sifat: “to‘la”, shuning uchun be full of. fill — fe’l: “to‘ldirmoq”, shuning uchun fill … with. Ikkalasi bir hodisani ko‘rsatadi, lekin so‘z turkumi har xil, predlog ham har xil: The bag is full of apples. — He filled the bag with apples.

Nima uchun sifat va predlog juftliklarini yodlashim kerak, qoida yo‘qmi?

Qisman guruhlash mumkin: qo‘rquv — of, qobiliyat — at, o‘xshashlik — to. Lekin istisnolar ko‘p. Eng yaxshi yo‘l — har juftlikni to‘liq gapda yodlash: Sardor is proud of his sister. Yangi sifat o‘rgansangiz, lug‘atda yonidagi predlogni ham yozib oling.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • of: afraid / frightened / scared / terrified of; fond, proud, ashamed, jealous, envious, suspicious, critical, tolerant of; aware, conscious, capable, incapable, full, short, tired of.
  • to: similar to, married to, engaged to; different from (BrE ham to, AmE ham than).
  • at: good / bad / brilliant / hopeless at. in: interested in. on: keen on, dependent on.
  • for: famous for, responsible for. with: crowded with. of: independent of.
  • O‘zbekcha qo‘shimcha aldaydi: -dan ≠ from (afraid of), -ga ≠ to (interested in), -da ≠ in (good at).
  • Predlogdan keyin fe’l bo‘lsa, -ing: good at drawing, tired of waiting.
  • tired of = bezgan; tired = charchagan. married to = turmush o‘rtog‘i; married with = farzandlari bor.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 60 (Predlog + -ing), Unit 66 (afraid to do va afraid of doing), Unit 129 (Ot + predlog), Unit 130 (Sifat + predlog — 1), Unit 135 (depend on), Unit 136 (fill with).
