In/at/on (position) 3

In / at / on — joy predloglari 3
Prepositions · Predloglar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • at bilan keladigan odatiy joylar: at home, at work, at school, at university, at sea.
  • Vokzal va aeroport: at the station, at the airport; birovnikida bo‘lish: at Dilnoza’s.
  • Tadbirlar: at a party, at a concert, at a conference, at a wedding, at a match.
  • Bino ichida (in) bo‘lish bilan binoda biror ish uchun bo‘lish (at) farqi: in the cinema va at the cinema.
  • Shahar va davlat bilan in; arrive in, arrive at va get to — hamda hech qachon arrive to emas.

Asosiy in / at / on qoidalari va ularning rasmlari — Unit 123 va Unit 124 da. Bu darsda esa joy nomlari va tadbirlar bilan ishlatiladigan in va at ni o‘rganamiz.

Asosiy fikr oddiy: in — biror narsaning ichida yoki katta hudud (shahar, davlat) ichida; at — joyni xaritadagi bir nuqta sifatida yoki u yerda nima ish qilinishi nuqtai nazaridan ko‘rish.

O‘zbek tilida bularning hammasi bitta -da qo‘shimchasi bilan aytiladi: uyda, ishda, maktabda, vokzalda, to‘yda, Toshkentda, O‘zbekistonda. Inglizchada esa har bir turga o‘z predlogi to‘g‘ri keladi: at home, at work, at school, at the station, at a wedding, lekin in Tashkent, in Uzbekistan. Yana bir tuzoq: o‘zbekchada “shaharga yetib keldim” deymiz (-ga), shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ❌ I arrived to Tashkent deb yozadi. Arrive dan keyin to hech qachon kelmaydi — in yoki at keladi.

At home, at work, at school … Everyday places with at

Ba’zi joylarni biz bino sifatida emas, odatiy faoliyat joyi sifatida tasavvur qilamiz: uyda dam olamiz, ishda ishlaymiz, maktabda o‘qiymiz. Bunday iboralar at bilan keladi va oldidan a / the qo‘yilmaydi.

Malika bugun uyda. U shamollagan.

Kema uch hafta dengizda (safarda) bo‘ldi.

at university (artiklsiz) — “talaba bo‘lib o‘qiyapti”. at the university (the bilan) — shunchaki “universitet binosida / hududida”: Let’s meet at the university at five. (Universitetda uchrashamiz.) Amerika inglizchasida talaba haqida ko‘pincha in college, in school deyiladi (BrE: at college, at school).

O‘zbekcha “dengizda” har ikki ma’noni beradi: kemada safarda (at sea) va suvning ichida (in the sea). Inglizchada ular farqlanadi: The sailors have been at sea for a month (safarda), lekin The children are swimming in the sea (suv ichida). Dengiz bo‘yida dam olish uchun by the sea (Unit 128).

At the station, at the airport

Vokzal va aeroportni biz odatda yo‘ldagi nuqta sifatida ko‘ramiz: u yerda kutamiz, uchrashamiz, chipta olamiz. Shuning uchun at ishlatiladi (bu yerda the bor!).

  • Sardor is waiting for his cousin at the station.Sardor amakivachchasini vokzalda kutyapti.
  • Our plane landed at Samarkand airport at dawn.Samolyotimiz tongda Samarqand aeroportiga qo‘ndi.
  • You can buy a ticket at the airport.Chiptani aeroportda ham sotib olsa bo‘ladi.

Birovnikida: at Dilnoza’s

Biror kishining uyida bo‘lsak, at + ism-’s deymiz. Egalik shaklidan keyin house, flat, home so‘zi tushib qoladi. Shifokor, tish shifokori, sartarosh kabi kasb egalarining joyi uchun ham shu qoida.

  • I’m at Dilnoza’s. We’re revising for the exam.Men Dilnozanikidaman. Imtihonga tayyorlanyapmiz.
  • We had dinner at my aunt’s on Friday.Juma kuni xolamnikida kechki ovqat qildik.
  • Where’s Nodira? — She’s at the dentist’s.Nodira qani? — U tish shifokorida.
  • Jasur spent the weekend at his parents’.Jasur dam olish kunlarini ota-onasinikida o‘tkazdi.

Egalik ’s haqida — Unit 81. ’s ni tushirib qoldirmang: at Dilnoza’s ✅, at Dilnoza ❌.

Tadbirlar: at a party, at a concert … At + event

Biror tadbir (to‘y, konsert, yig‘ilish, o‘yin) bo‘layotgan joyda ishtirokchi yoki tomoshabin bo‘lsak, at ishlatiladi. Tadbir — “xaritadagi nuqta” kabi: u yerda bo‘lish muhim, binoning ichki tuzilishi emas.

At + tadbir
IboraMisolTarjima
at a partyI met Nodira at a party last month.Nodirani o‘tgan oy ziyofatda uchratdim.
at a concertThere were thousands of people at the concert.Konsertda minglab odam bor edi.
at a conferenceA professor from Bukhara spoke at the conference.Buxorolik professor konferensiyada so‘zga chiqdi.
at a weddingEveryone cried at my cousin’s wedding.Amakivachchamning to‘yida hamma yig‘ladi.
at a matchWe were at the match when our team scored.Jamoamiz gol urganda biz o‘yinda edik.
at a lecture / meetingWere you at the lecture yesterday?Kecha ma’ruzada bo‘ldingizmi?

Tadbir → at

  • I saw her at the concert.
  • Who was at the wedding?

Tadbir o‘tgan bino → in

  • The concert is in the main hall of the theatre. (Konsert teatrning katta zalida bo‘ladi.)
  • It was very hot in the hall.

Meeting so‘zida ikkala shakl bor: I saw him at the meeting (yig‘ilish tadbirida) va Sorry, she is in a meeting (hozir yig‘ilishda band — ichida ketayotgan jarayon). Ma’nosi bir oz farq qiladi, ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri.

Tomoshabin emas, ijrochi bo‘lsangiz, in ham ishlatiladi: She sang in the concert (konsertda ijro etdi). Oddiy mehmon yoki tomoshabin uchun esa at.

Bino: in yoki at? In a building or at a building

Bino haqida gapirganda ikki nuqtai nazar bor:

in — binoning ichi

Siz binoning ichki makoniga e’tibor berasiz: nima bor, qanday, qancha.

  • It is cold in the library.
  • There are fifty tables in the restaurant.

at — joy va maqsad

Siz binoni u yerda nima qilinadigan joy sifatida ko‘rasiz: film ko‘rish, ovqatlanish, xarid qilish.

  • We were at the cinema. (Film ko‘rgani borgan edik.)
  • I’ll see you at the restaurant.
Bir bino — ikki nuqtai nazar

Kinoteatr ichi qorong‘i edi.

Kamola kutubxonada o‘qiyapti.		You must be quiet in the library.
Kutubxona ichida jim bo‘lish kerak.

Qoida: o‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Men binoning ichiga qarayapmanmi yoki binoni u yerda ish qilinadigan joy deb tasavvur qilyapmanmi?” Birinchisi → in, ikkinchisi → at. Muassasa nomi aytilsa (at the Hilton, at the Opera House) odatda at ishlatiladi.

Kasalxona: She is in hospital (BrE) — u bemor sifatida yotibdi. She is at the hospital — u kasalxonada, lekin bemor bo‘lmasligi mumkin (bemorni ko‘rgani kelgan yoki ishlaydi). Amerika inglizchasida bemor uchun in the hospital. Bu haqda ham Unit 74 ga qarang.

Shahar va davlat: in In + town, city, country

Shahar, qishloq, davlat va katta hududlar bilan in ishlatiladi: biz ularni “ichida yashaymiz, ichida bo‘lamiz”.

  • My grandparents live in a small village near Fergana.Bobom va buvim Farg‘ona yaqinidagi kichik qishloqda yashaydi.
  • Dilnoza was born in Bukhara, but she grew up in Tashkent.Dilnoza Buxoroda tug‘ilgan, lekin Toshkentda o‘sgan.
  • Samarkand is in Uzbekistan, which is in Central Asia.Samarqand O‘zbekistonda, O‘zbekiston esa Markaziy Osiyoda joylashgan.
  • We stayed in Khiva for three days.Biz Xivada uch kun turdik.

Sayohat yo‘lidagi bir to‘xtash joyi sifatida shahar ham nuqta bo‘lishi mumkin: We changed trains at Samarkand. (Samarqandda boshqa poyezdga o‘tdik.) Bunday gaplarda in ham to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi. Lekin kimdir qayerda yashasa yoki turgan bo‘lsa, har doim in: live in Bukhara ✅, live at Bukhara ❌.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“At the cinema” va “in the cinema” — qaysi birini aytsam bo‘ladi?

Ko‘pincha ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, faqat nuqtai nazar boshqacha. Film ko‘rish, ya’ni kinoteatrga borgan ish haqida gapirsangiz: We were at the cinema. Binoning ichki tomoni haqida (qorong‘i, sovuq, o‘rindiqlar) gapirsangiz: It was dark in the cinema. Gapirayotganda ichki makon emas, faoliyat muhim bo‘lsa — at tanlang.

Nega “at home” (the yo‘q), lekin “at the station” (the bor)?

Home, work, school, university, sea — bular “odatiy faoliyat joyi”ni bildiruvchi qotib qolgan iboralar, ular artiklsiz keladi. Station, airport, cinema, restaurant esa oddiy ot: aniq biror vokzal yoki aeroport haqida gapirasiz, shuning uchun the yoki a kerak: at the station, at a restaurant. Artikllar haqida — Unit 74.

O‘zbekchada “shaharga yetib keldim” deymiz. Nega “arrive to” xato?

Chunki inglizchada arrive fe’li to bilan ishlatilmaydi, o‘zbekcha -ga qo‘shimchasiga to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri mos kelmaydi. Shaharga yetib kelsangiz: arrive in Samarkand; binoga, vokzalga, tadbirga: arrive at the station. “To” kerak bo‘lsa, get to yoki go to ishlating.

“Arrive in” va “get to” ning farqi nimada?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil. Get to — kundalik, norasmiy uslub va har qanday joy bilan ishlaydi (get to the station, get to Termez). Arrive — biroz rasmiyroq, lekin in / at tanlash kerak. Undan keyin joy ko‘rsatilmasa, predlog umuman kerak emas: We arrived late.

“At university” va “at the university” farqi nima?

At university — talaba sifatida o‘qiyapti (umumiy holat). At the university — aniq bir universitet binosi yoki hududi: My sister studies at the National University yoki See you at the university. Talaba sifatida o‘qish haqida gapirsangiz, at university (artiklsiz) deyiladi.

“In a meeting” va “at a meeting” — qaysi biri to‘g‘ri?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. At the meeting — tadbir sifatida (I saw her at the meeting). In a meeting — hozir shu majlis jarayonida va band ekanini bildiradi: Please call later. He’s in a meeting.

“In hospital” bilan “at the hospital” farqi nimada?

Britaniya inglizchasida in hospital — bemor sifatida yotish. At the hospital — kasalxonada bo‘lish (ishlash, ko‘rgani borish, navbatda turish). Amerika inglizchasida bemor ham in the hospital deydi.

“At the sea”, “at sea”, “in the sea” va “by the sea” ni qanday ajrataman?

At sea — kemada safarda. In the sea — dengiz suvining ichida (cho‘milish). By the sea — dengiz bo‘yida (Unit 128). Dengiz bo‘yida dam olish haqida BrE da at the seaside ham deyiladi: We spent a week at the seaside. “At the sea” odatda aytilmaydi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • at + odatiy joy (artiklsiz): at home, at work, at school, at university, at sea.
  • at + nuqta: at the station, at the airport; birovnikida: at Dilnoza’s, at the dentist’s.
  • at + tadbir: at a party, at a concert, at a conference, at a wedding, at a match.
  • Bino: in — ichki makon (It’s cold in the library); at — u yerda qilinadigan ish (We were at the cinema).
  • Shahar, qishloq, davlat bilan yashash va turish: in (live in Bukhara).
  • arrive in + shahar / davlat; arrive at + bino, aeroport, vokzal, tadbir; get to + istalgan joy; arrive to ❌.
  • BrE: at school / at university / in hospital; AmE: in school / in college / in the hospital.
