Present perfect 1 (I have done)

Hozirgi tugallangan zamon — 1
Present perfect and past · Present perfect va o‘tgan zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Present perfect (hozirgi tugallangan zamon) qanday yasalishini: have / has + V3 (past participle).
  • U nimani bildirishini: o‘tmishdagi harakat + uning hozirgi natijasi (bog‘liqlik hozir bilan).
  • Yangi xabar berishda present perfect: Ow! I’ve burnt my hand! Our team has won the cup!
  • gone va been farqini: She has gone to Turkey ↔ She has been to Turkey.
  • just, already, yet so‘zlarini va ularning gapdagi o‘rnini.

Present perfect — o‘tmish va hozirni bog‘laydigan zamon. Harakat o‘tmishda bo‘lgan, lekin biz u haqida hozirgi nuqtai nazardan gapiramiz: uning natijasi hozir ham bor yoki u hozir uchun yangi, muhim xabar.

O‘zbek tilida alohida “present perfect” zamoni yo‘q. Biz Kalitimni yo‘qotdim deymiz — bu inglizchada ham I lost my key, ham I’ve lost my key bo‘lishi mumkin. Ma’no ko‘pincha -ib qo‘ydim, -ib bo‘ldim bilan seziladi: yo‘qotib qo‘ydim (hozir yo‘q), yeb bo‘ldim (tugatdim). Inkorda esa hali ... -ganim yo‘q / hali ... -madi: Hali kelmadi = He hasn’t come yet. Asosiy savol: “Bu hozir uchun muhimmi?” Agar shunday bo‘lsa va aniq o‘tgan vaqt aytilmagan bo‘lsa — present perfect.

Shakli: have / has + past participle Form of the present perfect

Mana vaziyat. Jasur telefonini qidiryapti, lekin topa olmayapti. U telefonini yo‘qotib qo‘ygan: o‘tmishda yo‘qotgan va hozir ham telefoni yo‘q.

To‘g‘ri fe’llarda V3 past simple bilan bir xil — -ed: finished, decided, opened. Noto‘g‘ri fe’llarda esa ko‘pincha boshqacha shakl bo‘ladi — uni alohida yodlash kerak:

’s ikki xil bo‘lishi mumkin: has yoki is. Fe’lga qarang: She’s gone = she has gone (V3 dan oldin); She’s going = she is going (-ing dan oldin).

Yangi xabar Something has happened = new information

“Nimadir bo‘ldi” deb yangilik aytganda ko‘pincha present perfect ishlatiladi. Bu xabar hozir uchun muhim:

  • Ow! I’ve burnt my hand.Voy! Qo‘limni kuydirib oldim.
  • Look at all those police cars! There’s been (there has been) an accident.Qara, qancha politsiya mashinasi! Avariya bo‘libdi.
  • Guess what! Dilnoza has passed her driving test!Bilasanmi! Dilnoza haydovchilik imtihonidan o‘tdi!
  • The government has announced new rules for drivers.Hukumat haydovchilar uchun yangi qoidalarni e’lon qildi. (yangiliklarda)
  • Look! Somebody has left a bag on the bus.Qara! Kimdir avtobusda sumkasini qoldirib ketibdi.

Yangiliklar (radio, televizor, sayt) ko‘pincha present perfect bilan boshlanadi, keyin tafsilotlar past simple da beriladi: A fire has destroyed a market in the city centre. The fire started at 3 am. (Bu farq — Unit 13–14 da.)

gone (to) va been (to)

has gone to

Ketgan va hali qaytmagan — hozir o‘sha yerda yoki yo‘lda.

  • Kamola isn’t at home. She has gone to Turkey for two weeks.
    Kamola uyda emas. U ikki haftaga Turkiyaga ketgan. (hozir u yerda)

has been to

Borgan va qaytib kelgan.

  • Kamola has just come back. She has been to Turkey. Look at her photos!
    Kamola hozirgina qaytdi. U Turkiyaga borib keldi. Suratlarini ko‘ring!

been to hayotiy tajriba haqida ham keladi: Have you ever been to Khiva? (Hech Xivada bo‘lganmisiz?) — bu Unit 8 mavzusi.

just = hozirgina, biroz oldin

  • “Would you like some tea?” “No, thanks. I’ve just had a cup.”— Choy ichasizmi? — Yo‘q, rahmat. Hozirgina bir piyola ichdim.
  • Your hair is wet. Have you just had a shower?Sochlaring ho‘l. Hozirgina dush qabul qildingmi?

already = allaqachon, kutilgandan oldinroq

  • “Don’t forget to call Grandma.” “I’ve already called her.”— Buvimga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishni unutma. — Allaqachon qo‘ng‘iroq qildim.
  • “Is the film starting soon?” “It’s already started! Hurry up!”— Film tez orada boshlanadimi? — Allaqachon boshlandi! Tezroq!

yet = hali (inkor), ...-mi? (so‘roq)

yet — “shu paytgacha”. U so‘zlovchi biror narsa bo‘lishini kutayotganini bildiradi. yet asosan so‘roq va inkor gaplarda ishlatiladi:

  • Has the parcel arrived yet?Posilka keldimi? (kutyapmiz)
  • I’ve cooked the rice, but I haven’t made the salad yet.Guruchni pishirdim, lekin salatni hali tayyorlamadim.
  • “Where’s Sardor?” “He hasn’t arrived yet.”— Sardor qani? — U hali kelmadi.
Ega+have / has+just / already+V3
Ega+haven’t / hasn’t+V3+...+yet

yet = hali (inkorda): Hali ovqatlanmadim = I haven’t eaten yet. So‘roqda esa yet ko‘pincha tarjima qilinmaydi: Ovqatlandingmi? = Have you eaten yet?

Past simple ham mumkinmi?

Bu sahifadagi misollarda past simple ham ishlatilishi mumkin, ayniqsa Amerika ingliz tilida: I just had a cup. He already left. Did the parcel arrive yet? Britaniya ingliz tilida esa bunday holatlarda present perfect odatiyroq. Imtihonlarda (BrE) present perfect ni tanlang.

Lekin aniq o‘tgan vaqt aytilsa (yesterday, last week, in 2020, two days ago) — faqat past simple: I lost my keys yesterday. ✅ — I’ve lost my keys yesterday. ❌ (Unit 13–14)

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchada bitta “-di” bor. Qachon past simple, qachon present perfect ishlataman?

Ikki savol bering: (1) Aniq o‘tgan vaqt aytilganmi yoki nazarda tutilganmi (kecha, 2019-yilda, o‘tgan hafta)? → past simple. (2) Gap hozirgi natija yoki yangi xabar haqidami, vaqt muhim emasmi? → present perfect. I’ve lost my keys (hozir yo‘q) ↔ I lost my keys yesterday (kecha bo‘lgan voqea).

“She’s gone” dagi ’s — is mi yoki has mi?

Bu yerda has, chunki keyin V3 (gone) kelgan. Agar keyin -ing yoki sifat kelsa — is: She’s going (is), She’s tired (is). She’s been — doim has.

gone va been ni qanday adashtirmaslik mumkin?

Oddiy test: “U hozir qayerda?” Agar u hali o‘sha yerda (yoki yo‘lda) bo‘lsa — gone. Agar u qaytib kelgan bo‘lsa — been. Aziz has gone to Bukhara — hozir Buxoroda. Aziz has been to Bukhara — borib kelgan.

already ni so‘roq gapda ishlatsa bo‘ladimi?

Ha, lekin u hayronlikni bildiradi (“shunchalik tez?”): Have you already finished? That was quick! (Allaqachon tugatdingmi? Tez-ku!) Oddiy so‘roqda yet ishlatiladi: Have you finished yet?

yet, already va still farqi nima?

already — kutilgandan oldin bo‘lib bo‘ldi; yet — kutilgan narsa hali bo‘lmadi (inkor/so‘roq); still — biror holat hali ham davom etyapti: I’m still waiting. Bular Unit 111 da batafsil.

Amerikaliklar “Did you eat yet?” deyishadi. Bu xatomi?

Amerika ingliz tilida bu normal. Britaniya ingliz tilida esa Have you eaten yet? deyiladi. Siz qaysi variantni o‘rganayotgan bo‘lsangiz, o‘shanga izchil amal qiling; bu kitob — BrE.

Present perfect “hozirgi” zamonmi yoki “o‘tgan”?

Grammatik jihatdan — hozirgi zamon (have/has — hozirgi shakl). Harakat o‘tmishda bo‘lgan, lekin biz u haqida hozirdan turib gapiramiz: “hozirgi holatim — kalitlarim yo‘q”. Shuning uchun u aniq o‘tgan vaqt bilan sig‘ishmaydi.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Shakl: have / has + V3. So‘roq: Have you ...? Has she ...? Inkor: haven’t / hasn’t.
  • Ma’no: o‘tmishdagi harakat + hozir bilan bog‘liqlik (natija hozir bor yoki bu yangi xabar).
  • gone to — ketgan, hali u yerda; been to — borib, qaytib kelgan.
  • just (hozirgina) va already (allaqachon) — have va V3 orasida; yet — gap oxirida, asosan so‘roq va inkorda.
  • Aniq o‘tgan vaqt (yesterday, ago, in 2020) bilan — faqat past simple.
  • AmE da bunday gaplarda past simple ham ko‘p ishlatiladi; BrE da present perfect odatiyroq.
