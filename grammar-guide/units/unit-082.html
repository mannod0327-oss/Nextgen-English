Myself/yourself/themselves etc.

O‘zlik olmoshlari (myself, yourself ...)
Pronouns and determiners · Olmoshlar va aniqlovchilar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • O‘zlik olmoshlari (reflexive pronouns): myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.
  • Ularni qachon ishlatishni: ega va to‘ldiruvchi bir xil odam bo‘lganda — I cut myself.
  • Qachon ishlatmaslikni: wash, shave, dress, meet, relax, concentrate, feel — o‘zbekchada -in / -sh bor, inglizchada olmosh yo‘q.
  • Ta’kid uchun: I baked it myself — “o‘zim pishirdim”.
  • themselves va each other (bir-biri) o‘rtasidagi farqni.
  • Tayyor iboralar: Enjoy yourself! Help yourself! Be yourself!

Ba’zan ish-harakat egaga o‘ziga qaytadi: men o‘zimni oynada ko‘rdim, u o‘zini kesib oldi. Bunday gaplarda inglizchada -self / -selves bilan tugaydigan maxsus olmosh ishlatiladi. Agar harakat boshqa odamga qaratilgan bo‘lsa, oddiy olmosh (me, him, her, them) ishlatiladi.

O‘zbekcha o‘zim, o‘zing, o‘zi, o‘zimiz, o‘zingiz, o‘zlari ga inglizcha -self olmoshlari mos keladi: Men o‘zimni ko‘rdim = I saw myself. Ikkita farq bor. 1) O‘zbekchada fe’lning o‘zida o‘zlik qo‘shimchasi (-in, -n: yuvindim, kiyindim, tarandim) va birgalik qo‘shimchasi (-sh: uchrashdik, xayrlashdik) bor. Inglizchada bunday qo‘shimcha yo‘q, shuning uchun ko‘pincha olmosh umuman kerak emas: I washed, I got dressed, we met. 2) Inglizchada yourself (bitta kishi) va yourselves (bir necha kishi) farqlanadi.

Shakli va asosiy ishlatilishi: ega = to‘ldiruvchi Reflexive pronouns: when subject and object are the same

O‘zlik olmoshi ega bilan bir xil odam bo‘lgan to‘ldiruvchi o‘rnida keladi. Xuddi oyna kabi: harakat egadan chiqib, yana egaga qaytadi.

Oddiy olmosh yoki o‘zlik olmoshi?

Boshqa odam (him, her, them) yoki o‘sha odamning o‘zi (himself, herself, themselves)?
GapKim kimni?
Dilnoza saw her in the shop.Dilnoza boshqa ayolni ko‘rdi.
Dilnoza saw herself in the shop window.Dilnoza o‘z aksini ko‘rdi.

O‘zlik olmoshi predloglardan keyin ham keladi, agar u ega bilan bir xil odamni bildirsa: She talks to herself. I’m proud of myself. He is angry with himself. Lekin joy predloglaridan keyin (behind, beside, next to, around ...) va with “birga” ma’nosida odatda oddiy olmosh ishlatiladi: I took my passport with me. She put the bag next to her. They looked around them.

O‘zlik olmoshi bilan keladigan fe’llar va iboralar Verbs and expressions with -self

Quyidagi fe’llar ko‘pincha o‘zlik olmoshi bilan ishlatiladi. Ularning ba’zilari o‘zbekchadan farq qiladi, shuning uchun alohida yodlang.

Help yourself! (bitta mehmon)

  • Please help yourself to some tea.
  • Choydan o‘zingiz oling, marhamat.

Help yourselves! (bir necha mehmon)

  • Please help yourselves to some fruit.
  • Mevadan o‘zingiz oling, marhamat.

Enjoy fe’lidan keyin to‘ldiruvchi shart: I enjoyed the party yoki I enjoyed myself (maza qildim). We enjoyed at the party ❌. Behave yolg‘iz ham keladi: Behave! = Behave yourself!

O‘zlik olmoshi kerak bo‘lmagan fe’llar Verbs that are usually NOT reflexive in English

O‘zbek tilida o‘zlik va birgalik nisbati fe’l qo‘shimchasi bilan yasaladi (yuvindi, kiyindi, uchrashdi). Inglizchada esa bu fe’llar olmoshsiz ishlatiladi: He washed. She got dressed. We met. Bu o‘zbek o‘quvchilari uchun eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato manbai.

Eng ko‘p xato feel fe’lida: o‘zbekchada o‘zimni yaxshi his qilyapman deymiz. Inglizchada esa I feel well / I feel fine / I feel tired — myself yo‘q! I feel myself tired ❌.

Shuningdek, get up, sit down, stand up, wake up, hurry up — olmoshsiz: She sat down. Hurry up! Tana a’zolari haqida egalik olmoshi ishlatiladi: I washed my hands (myself emas).

Bu fe’llardan keyin o‘zlik olmoshi faqat alohida ta’kid uchun keladi: My grandfather is 90 and he cannot dress himself without help. (U o‘zi kiyina olmaydi.) Yana: After swimming, I dried myself with a towel. Dry odatda o‘zlik olmoshi bilan keladi, agar tananing qismi aytilmasa.

Ta’kid uchun: I did it myself Reflexive pronouns for emphasis

O‘zlik olmoshi “aynan men / u o‘zi” ma’nosida ta’kid beradi: ish boshqa birov emas, o‘sha odamning o‘zi bajargan. Bunda olmosh otdan keyin yoki gap oxirida turadi va uni tushirib qoldirsa ham gap to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi.

  • “Did your mother bake this cake?” “No, I baked it myself.”— Bu tortni onang pishirdimi? — Yo‘q, o‘zim pishirdim.
  • The director himself opened the door for us.Eshikni biz uchun direktorning o‘zi ochdi.
  • I don’t need a plumber. I’ll fix the tap myself.Menga santexnik kerak emas. Jo‘mrakni o‘zim tuzataman.
  • The hotel itself was ordinary, but the view from our room was amazing.Mehmonxonaning o‘zi oddiy edi, lekin xonamizdan ko‘rinadigan manzara ajoyib edi.
  • Your mum is tired. Wash the dishes yourself tonight!Onang charchagan. Bugun idishlarni o‘zing yuv!

Bu o‘zbekchadagi “o‘zim, o‘zi” bilan to‘liq mos: direktorning o‘zi = the director himself; o‘zim pishirdim = I baked it myself.

By myself boshqa ma’no ham beradi: “yolg‘iz”. I live by myself = men yolg‘iz yashayman. Bu Unit 83 da. I did it myself esa asosan “boshqa hech kim emas, o‘zim” demak.

themselves yoki each other? Reflexive or reciprocal?

Ikki yoki undan ortiq odam bir-biriga nimadir qilsa, each other (yoki one another) ishlatiladi. Har biri o‘ziga qilsa — themselves / ourselves.

  • Malika and Dilnoza looked at themselves in the mirror.Malika bilan Dilnoza oynada har biri o‘ziga qaradi.
  • Malika and Dilnoza looked at each other and smiled.Malika bilan Dilnoza bir-biriga qarab jilmayishdi.
  • Jasur and Aziz have known each other since school.Jasur bilan Aziz maktab davridan beri bir-birini biladi.
  • We write to each other every week.Biz bir-birimizga har hafta yozamiz.
  • They borrow each other’s books.Ular bir-birining kitoblarini olib turishadi.
  • The students helped one another with the homework.Talabalar uy vazifasida bir-biriga yordam berishdi.

O‘zbekchada ikkalasini ham aytamiz: o‘zlariga (themselves) va bir-biriga (each other). Ingliz tilida ular butunlay boshqa so‘zlar. Inglizchada each other ni -sh qo‘shimchasiga ham solishtiring: ko‘rishdik — we saw each other, salomlashdik — we greeted each other.

Each other egalik shaklini ham oladi: each other’s (bir-birining). One another ma’nosi bir xil, ko‘pincha ikkitadan ortiq odam haqida va biroz rasmiyroq.

Yana bir necha eslatma Extra points

1. O‘zlik olmoshi ega bo‘lib kelmaydi. Ega o‘rnida faqat oddiy olmosh: My friend and I went to the market. ✅ — My friend and myself went ... ❌. Ta’kid uchun esa mumkin: I myself saw it.

2. Predloglardan keyin. proud of myself, angry with yourself, talk to himself — ega bilan bir xil odam bo‘lganda keladi. Lekin She looked behind her deyiladi (behind herself deyish odatiy emas).

3. Umumiy “kishi”. Rasmiy uslubda oneself uchraydi: It is hard to teach oneself a language. Kundalik nutqda yourself ishlatiladi: It’s hard to teach yourself a language.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

O‘zbekchada “yuvindim” deymiz. Nega inglizchada “I washed myself” demaymiz?

Chunki o‘zbekchada o‘zlik ma’nosi fe’l ichiga (-in) kiritilgan, inglizchada esa wash o‘zi yetarli: I washed before breakfast. “I washed myself” ham grammatik jihatdan mumkin, lekin odatda aytilmaydi (faqat o‘zini yuva olmaydigan bola yoki hayvon haqida alohida ta’kid uchun). Shuning uchun wash, shave, dress, meet, relax bilan olmosh qo‘shmang.

Yourself va yourselves o‘rtasida nima farq?

Yourself — “sen/siz” bitta kishi bo‘lsa. Yourselves — “sizlar”, bir necha kishi. Take care of yourself, Malika. Take care of yourselves, children. O‘zbekchada ikkalasi ham o‘zingiz bo‘lishi mumkin.

“They looked at themselves” va “They looked at each other” — qanday farq qiladi?

Birinchisida har kim o‘ziga qaradi (masalan oynada). Ikkinchisida bir-biriga qaradi: A B ni, B esa A ni ko‘rdi. Savol bering: “Kim kimga?” Agar harakat o‘zaro bo‘lsa — each other.

“I did it myself” bilan “I did it by myself” bir xilmi?

Yaqin, lekin bir xil emas. I did it myself — “o‘zim qildim, boshqa hech kim emas” (ta’kid). I did it by myself — “yolg‘iz, hech kimning yordamisiz qildim”. Ikkinchisi “yolg‘iz” ma’nosini ham beradi: She lives by herself (Unit 83).

“Help yourself” nimani anglatadi? Menga yordam bering degani emasmi?

Yo‘q! Bu — mehmonga aytiladigan taklif: “Marhamat, o‘zingiz oling”. Help yourself to some bread! = Nondan oling! Birovdan yordam so‘rash uchun esa Can you help me? deyiladi.

“Myself and Tom went” desam bo‘ladimi?

Yo‘q. O‘zlik olmoshi ega bo‘la olmaydi. To‘g‘risi: Tom and I went (ingliz tilida I oxirida aytiladi). To‘ldiruvchi o‘rnida ham Tom and me ishlatiladi: She invited Tom and me.

Behave va behave yourself — farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil: o‘zini yaxshi tutmoq. Behave! / Behave yourself! — ikkalasi ham bolalarga aytiladi. Oddiy gapda esa olmoshsiz: He behaves badly in class.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • O‘zlik olmoshlari: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves. Birlik — -self, ko‘plik — -selves.
  • Ega va to‘ldiruvchi bir xil odam bo‘lsa -self: I cut myself. Boshqa odam bo‘lsa — him, her, them.
  • Wash, shave, dress, meet, relax, concentrate, feel bilan olmosh yo‘q: I feel tired, We met. (O‘zbekcha -in / -sh ga to‘g‘ri keladi.)
  • Enjoy yourself, behave yourself, help yourself (to), blame yourself, introduce yourself, be yourself — tayyor iboralar.
  • Ta’kid: I did it myself, the director himself (otdan keyin yoki gap oxirida).
  • Each other (bir-biriga) ≠ themselves (har biri o‘ziga).
  • O‘zlik olmoshi ega bo‘lib kelmaydi: Tom and I ✅, Tom and myself ❌.
