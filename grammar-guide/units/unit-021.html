Will/shall 1

Will / shall — 1
Future · Kelasi zamon

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • I’ll (= I will) — ayni gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilingan qaror uchun: The phone’s ringing. I’ll get it.
  • I think I’ll ... / I don’t think I’ll ... va inkor shakli won’t.
  • Nega oldindan qaror qilingan yoki kelishilgan ishlar uchun will ishlatilmasligini.
  • will bilan taklif qilish, rozi bo‘lish, va’da berish, iltimos qilish (Will you ...?) va rad etish (won’t).
  • Shall I ...? / Shall we ...? — taklif va maslahat so‘rash.

Bu unitda will ning “kelajak zamoni” sifatida emas, balki munosabat bildirish vositasi sifatidagi ishlatilishini o‘rganamiz: biror narsani hozir hal qilish, yordam taklif qilish, va’da berish. Kelajak haqidagi bashorat ma’nosidagi will — keyingi unitda (Unit 22).

O‘zbek tilida “hozir hal qildim” ma’nosini hozirgi-kelasi zamon beradi: Telefon jiringlayapti. Men olaman. Shuning uchun o‘quvchilar ❌ I answer it deyishadi. Inglizchada bu yerda I’ll kerak: I’ll answer it. Yana bir qulay moslik: Derazani yopaymi? = Shall I close the window?, Qayerga boraylik? = Where shall we go?

I’ll — hozir qabul qilingan qaror I’ll = a decision at the time of speaking

Agar biror ishni qilishga aynan gapirayotgan paytda qaror qilsangiz (bir soniya oldin bu haqda o‘ylamagan edingiz), I’ll (I will) ishlatiladi.

Ega+will (’ll)+fe’l (1-shakl)
will: shakllari
✅ Darak gap❌ Inkor gap❓ So‘roq gap
I will (I’ll) helpI will not (I won’t) helpWill you help?
She will (she’ll) callShe won’t callWill she call?
They will (they’ll) comeThey won’t comeWill they come?

Qoida: will barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil, undan keyin fe’l 1-shaklda keladi (to siz): I’ll call ✅ — I’ll to call / I’ll calling ❌. Og‘zaki nutqda deyarli doim qisqa shakl: I’ll, you’ll, he’ll, she’ll, we’ll, they’ll; inkori — won’t /wəʊnt/.

Misollar

  • Oh, there’s no bread. I’ll go and buy some.Voy, non qolmabdi. Borib olib kelaman.
  • A: Are you ready to order?
    B: Yes, I’ll have the plov, please.    — Buyurtma berishga tayyormisiz? — Ha, menga palov bering.
  • A: Has anyone fed the cat?
    B: Oh, no. I’ll do it now.    — Mushukka kimdir ovqat berdimi? — Voy, yo‘q. Hozir o‘zim beraman.
  • It’s a bit cold in here. I’ll close the window.Bu yer biroz sovuq ekan. Derazani yopaman.
  • I’m not hungry, thanks. I won’t have any dessert.Rahmat, qornim to‘q. Shirinlik yemayman.

Bunday gaplarda present simple ishlatib bo‘lmaydi: I’ll go and buy some bread ✅ — I go and buy some bread ❌; I’ll close the window ✅ — I close the window ❌.

I think I’ll ... / I don’t think I’ll ...

Qaror qilayotganda ko‘pincha I think I’ll (... qilsam kerak, ... qilaman shekilli) va I don’t think I’ll deymiz:

  • I’m really tired. I think I’ll go to bed early tonight.Juda charchadim. Bugun erta yotsam kerak.
  • It’s too far to walk. I don’t think I’ll walk — I’ll take a taxi.Piyoda borishga juda uzoq. Piyoda bormayman, menimcha — taksiga o‘tiraman.
  • This jacket is nice, but it’s too expensive. I don’t think I’ll buy it.Bu kurtka chiroyli, lekin juda qimmat. Menimcha, uni olmayman.

Ingliz tilida I don’t think I’ll ... shakli I think I won’t ... ga qaraganda ancha ko‘p ishlatiladi. Inkorni “think” ga qo‘yish odatiy: I don’t think I’ll go (tabiiy) — I think I won’t go (kamroq ishlatiladi).

Oldindan qaror qilingan ishlar uchun will emas Don’t use will for things already decided or arranged

Will — yangi, shu soniyadagi qaror. Agar qaror avvaldan qabul qilingan yoki reja kelishilgan bo‘lsa, will emas, be going to yoki present continuous ishlatiladi (Unit 19, Unit 20):

  • I’m playing tennis with Aziz on Saturday. (not I’ll play)Shanba kuni Aziz bilan tennis o‘ynayman (kelishib qo‘yganmiz).
  • Are you doing anything on Sunday? (not Will you do)Yakshanba kuni biror rejang bormi?
  • I’ve decided to sell my bike. I’m going to put an advert online tonight. (not I’ll put)Velosipedimni sotishga qaror qildim. Bugun kechqurun internetga e’lon joylashtirmoqchiman.

⚡ will — qaror HOZIR

A: The bin is full.
B: Is it? OK, I’ll empty it.

— Chelak to‘lib qolibdi. — Shundaymi? Mayli, men to‘kib kelaman.

🗓️ going to / -ing — qaror OLDIN

A: The bin is full.
B: I know. I’m going to empty it after dinner.

— Chelak to‘lib qolibdi. — Bilaman. Kechki ovqatdan keyin to‘kmoqchiman.

Taklif, rozilik, va’da, iltimos va rad etish Offering, agreeing, promising, asking, refusing

Will ko‘pincha quyidagi vaziyatlarda ishlatiladi. Hammasida qaror yoki munosabat gapirayotgan paytda bildiriladi:

will ning vazifalari

B: Sure, I’ll bring them tomorrow.		— Konspektlaringni berib turasanmi? — Albatta, ertaga olib kelaman.

won’t = “xohlamaydi, ko‘nmaydi” (hozir ham)

won’t faqat kelajak uchun emas. U biror kishi yoki narsa biror ishni qilishni rad etayotganini bildiradi — ko‘pincha ayni hozir:

  • My little sister won’t eat her vegetables.Singlim sabzavotlarini yegisi kelmaydi (hech yemaydi).
  • My laptop won’t turn on. I think the battery is dead.Noutbukim hech yonmayapti. Batareyasi o‘tirib qolgan bo‘lsa kerak.
  • I’ve asked Bekzod to come with us, but he won’t.Bekzodni biz bilan borishga chaqirdim, lekin u ko‘nmayapti.

Odob haqida: Will you ...? — oddiy iltimos, ba’zan biroz buyruqdek eshitiladi (ayniqsa “please” siz). Begona odamga yoki rasmiy vaziyatda Could you ...? yoki Would you ...? ancha muloyimroq: Could you help me with this box, please? (Unit 37).

Shall I ...? Shall we ...? Offers and suggestions

Zamonaviy ingliz tilida shall asosan savollarda, faqat I va we bilan ishlatiladi: Shall I ...? / Shall we ...? Bu bilan biz suhbatdoshning fikrini so‘raymiz — ayniqsa yordam taklif qilganda yoki biror narsa taklif (suggestion) qilganda.

(What / Where ...)+shall+I / we+fe’l?
  • Shall I turn the heating on? (= Do you want me to turn it on?)Isitgichni yoqaymi? (= Yoqishimni xohlaysizmi?)
  • I’ve lost my bank card. What shall I do? (= What do you suggest?)Bank kartamni yo‘qotib qo‘ydim. Nima qilay? (= Nima maslahat berasiz?)
  • Shall we take a taxi? It’s raining.Taksiga o‘tiramizmi? Yomg‘ir yog‘yapti.
  • Where shall we have lunch — at home or in the café?Tushlikni qayerda qilamiz — uydami yoki kafedami?
  • Let’s visit the Registan this evening, shall we?Bugun kechqurun Registonga boraylik, a?

Shall I ...? va Will you ...? farqi

🙋 Shall I ...? — men qilaymi?

Shall I turn off the TV?

= Televizorni o‘chiraymi? (Siz xohlaysizmi, men o‘chiray?)

🙏 Will you ...? — siz qilasizmi?

Will you turn off the TV?

= Televizorni o‘chirib qo‘yasizmi? (Men sizdan so‘rayapman.)

O‘zbekchada bu ma’nolar fe’l shakli bilan beriladi: -aymi? (ochaymi?, olib kelaymi?) → Shall I ...?; -aylikmi? (boraylikmi?) → Shall we ...?; -asizmi? iltimos ma’nosida (yordam berasizmi?) → Will you ...?

BrE va AmE: Amerika ingliz tilida shall kam ishlatiladi. Uning o‘rniga Should I ...? yoki Do you want me to ...? deyiladi: Should I close the window? (AmE) = Shall I close the window? (BrE). Shall ning boshqa ishlatilishi (I shall / we shall) — Unit 22 da.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

The phone is ringing. I answer it.The phone is ringing. I’ll answer it.Shu soniyadagi qaror — will. Present simple bu ma’noni bermaydi.
I’ll see the dentist tomorrow at 10. I booked it last week.I’m seeing the dentist tomorrow at 10.Kelishilgan reja — present continuous (yoki going to), will emas.
I’ll to call you later.I’ll call you later.will dan keyin “to” qo‘yilmaydi.
I willn’t tell anybody.I won’t tell anybody.will not ning qisqa shakli — won’t.
He won’t to listen to me.He won’t listen to me.won’t dan keyin ham fe’l to siz keladi.
Will I carry your bag? (taklif)Shall I carry your bag?Yordam taklif qilish (men qilaymi?) — Shall I ...?
What I shall do?What shall I do?So‘roq gapda shall egadan oldin keladi.
Shall you help me, please?Will you help me, please?Bu ma’noda shall faqat I va we bilan. Sizdan iltimos — Will you ...? (yoki Could you ...?)

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I will” va “I’ll” — farqi bormi?

Ma’no bir xil. I’ll — og‘zaki nutq va norasmiy yozuvda odatiy shakl. I will to‘liq aytilsa, ko‘pincha urg‘u, qat’iylik bildiradi: I will finish it today, I promise! (Albatta tugataman!)

Nega “I answer the phone” xato? O‘zbekchada “men olaman” deymiz-ku.

Ingliz tilida present simple asosan odat va umumiy faktlar uchun (I answer the phone at work — ishda telefonga men javob beraman, odatda). Shu soniyada qilingan qaror uchun maxsus shakl bor — I’ll. O‘zbekcha “-aman” ni har doim “I + fe’l” deb tarjima qilmang: avval o‘ylang — bu odatmi yoki hozirgi qarormi?

“will” har doim kelajakni bildiradimi?

Yo‘q. Masalan, won’t rad etish ma’nosida ko‘pincha hozirni bildiradi: My laptop won’t turn on (noutbuk hozir yonmayapti). Will you be quiet? — iltimos, hozir jim bo‘ling. Unit 22 da will hozirgi taxmin ma’nosida ham ishlatilishini ko‘rasiz.

Shall Amerikada ishlatiladimi?

Juda kam. Amerikaliklar Shall I ...? o‘rniga Should I ...? yoki Do you want me to ...?, Shall we ...? o‘rniga Should we ...? yoki Let’s ... deyishadi. Britaniyada esa Shall I / Shall we juda odatiy.

“Shall we go?” va “Let’s go” ning farqi nima?

Let’s go — taklif, deyarli qaror: “Ketdik!”. Shall we go? — so‘rov: “Ketamizmi? Siz nima deysiz?” Ikkalasini birlashtirish ham mumkin: Let’s go, shall we?

Iltimos uchun “Will you ...?” odobli emasmi?

Tanish odamlar orasida Will you ...? normal, lekin “please” bilan aytgan ma’qul. Agar biroz norozilik bilan aytilsa (Will you stop talking!), bu buyruqqa yaqin. Notanish odamga, ustozga yoki rasmiy vaziyatda Could you ...? yoki Would you ...? afzal.

“I promise I’ll ...” deyiladimi yoki “I promise I ...”?

Va’dada will kerak: I promise I’ll be on time. ✅ I promise I’m on time ❌. Inkor: I promise I won’t be late.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Hozir qabul qilingan qarorni “I’ll + fe’l” bilan yozing.

  1. “I’m thirsty.” “Wait, I (get) you a glass of water.”
  2. “This box is too heavy for me.” “Don’t worry, I (carry) it.”
  3. “There’s someone at the door.” “OK, I (see) who it is.”
  4. “I don’t understand this exercise.” “Never mind, I (explain) it to you.”
  5. “Tea or coffee?” “I (have) coffee, please.”
  6. “I’m too tired. I don’t think I (go) to the gym today.”

2-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang. Qaror hozirmi yoki avval qilinganmi — o‘ylang.

  1. “Why are you wearing your best suit?” “I (’ll go / ’m going) to a wedding.”
  2. “Oh no, I’ve left my wallet at home!” “Don’t worry. I (’ll pay / ’m paying).”
  3. “Have you decided where to go on holiday?” “Yes, we (’ll go / ’re going to go) to Turkey.”
  4. “The bin is full.” “Is it? I didn’t notice. I (’ll empty / ’m going to empty) it.”
  5. I (’ll start / ’m starting) my driving lessons on Monday — I paid for them yesterday.
  6. “It’s getting dark in here.” “OK, I (’ll turn / turn) the light on.”

3-mashq. Vaziyatga mos savol yozing: Shall I ...? / Shall we ...? / Will you ...?

  1. Your friend looks cold. Offer to close the window. →
  2. You and your friend can’t decide where to eat tonight. Ask your friend. → Where ?
  3. You want your brother to turn the TV off. Ask him. →
  4. Your grandmother has a heavy bag. Offer to carry it. →
  5. It’s raining and you and Aziz are late. Suggest a taxi. →

4-mashq. Rad etishni “won’t + fe’l” bilan yozing.

  1. I’ve told Aziz to see a doctor many times, but he (go).
  2. My phone (switch on). I think the battery is dead.
  3. I’ve tried to open this jar, but the lid (come off).

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Eshikni ochaymi? →
  2. Va’da beraman, hech kimga aytmayman. →
  3. Qayerda uchrashamiz? (do‘stingizdan fikr so‘rab) →
  4. Charchadim. Uyga ketsam kerak. →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • I’ll + fe’l — qaror gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilinsa: I’ll get it. (I get it ❌)
  • I think I’ll ... / I don’t think I’ll ... — qaror qilayotganda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi.
  • Oldindan qaror qilingan yoki kelishilgan ish uchun will emas — going to yoki present continuous.
  • will: taklif (I’ll help you), rozilik (OK, I’ll do it), va’da (I’ll return it on Monday), iltimos (Will you ...?).
  • won’t = rad etish, ko‘nmaslik: He won’t listen. My laptop won’t turn on.
  • Shall I ...? = men qilaymi? Shall we ...? = qilaylikmi? (AmE: Should I/we ...?)
