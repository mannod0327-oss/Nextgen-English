Noun + noun (a tennis ball / a headache)

Ot + ot (a tennis ball / a headache)
Articles and nouns · Artikllar va otlar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Ikkita otni yonma-yon qo‘yib, bitta tushuncha yasashni: a tennis ball, a bus driver, a headache.
  • Qaysi ot asosiy so‘z ekanini (doim o‘ngdagi) va birinchi ot nimani bildirishini.
  • Birinchi ot nega odatda birlikda turishini: a shoe shop ✅, a shoes shop ❌.
  • -ing li otlarni: a swimming pool, a washing machine, a driving licence.
  • Son + ot + ot: a ten-minute walk, a two-hour journey, a five-pound note — bunda ot ko‘plikda bo‘lmaydi!
  • a coffee cup bilan a cup of coffee o‘rtasidagi farqni.

Ingliz tilida ikkita ot yonma-yon kelishi mumkin. Ikkinchi ot — gapning “ustuni”: nima haqida gap ketayotganini aytadi. Birinchi ot esa sifat kabi ishlaydi: u ikkinchi otning qanday turi ekanini, nima uchun mo‘ljallanganini yoki nimadan yasalganini ko‘rsatadi. A tennis ball — bu “to‘p” (ball), “tennis” emas.

O‘zbek tilida ham xuddi shunday: tennis to‘pi, bosh og‘rig‘i, avtobus haydovchisi — avval “qanday”, keyin asosiy so‘z. So‘z tartibi bir xil! Farq shundaki, o‘zbekchada asosiy so‘zga -i / -si qo‘shimchasi qo‘shiladi (to‘pi, og‘rig‘i, haydovchisi), inglizchada esa hech qanday qo‘shimcha yo‘q — ikki ot shunchaki yonma-yon turadi. Yana bir o‘xshashlik: o‘n daqiqalik yo‘l deymiz (“daqiqa” ko‘plikda emas, -lik bor) — inglizchada ham a ten-minute walk (“minute” birlikda, defis bilan).

Ot + ot: birinchi ot “qanday?” degan savolga javob beradi Noun + noun: the head noun is on the right

Ikki otdan iborat ibora (noun + noun) da ikkinchi ot asosiy so‘z (head noun), birinchisi esa uni aniqlaydi. Ma’noni o‘ngdan chapga o‘qing: a bus driver = haydovchi (driver), kimning haydovchisi? — avtobusning (bus).

A / an, ko‘plik va fe’l — asosiy so‘zga qarab

A / an butun ibora oldiga qo‘yiladi va u birinchi so‘zning tovushiga qarab tanlanadi: an apple pie (apple unli bilan boshlanadi), a university student (university “yu” tovushi bilan boshlanadi). Ko‘plik -s faqat oxirgi (asosiy) otga qo‘shiladi, birinchi ot o‘zgarmaydi. Fe’l ham asosiy so‘zga moslashadi.

  • two tennis balls, three bus drivers ✅ — three buses drivers ❌ikkita tennis to‘pi, uchta avtobus haydovchisi
  • Sardor has two car keys. The keys are on the table.Sardorda ikkita mashina kaliti bor. Kalitlar stol ustida.
  • An expensive leather jacket was on sale.Qimmat charm kurtka sotuvda edi.

Sifat butun iboradan oldin turadi: a small coffee cup, a good history teacher. Ikkitadan ko‘p ot ham ketma-ket kelishi mumkin: a road accident report (yo‘l hodisasi haqidagi hisobot), a Tashkent taxi driver. Ularni o‘ngdan chapga o‘qing: report → qaysi? accident haqida → qaysi? road (yo‘l). Uchtadan ko‘p ot ketma-ket kelsa, gap og‘ir o‘qiladi — uni qayta yozing.

Tartib ma’noni o‘zgartiradi. Otlarning o‘rnini almashtirsangiz, boshqa narsa hosil bo‘ladi, chunki asosiy so‘z almashadi: milk chocolate — sutli shokolad (bu shokolad), chocolate milk — shokoladli sut (bu sut); a horse race — ot poygasi (bu poyga), a racehorse — poyga oti (bu ot).

Birinchi ot odatda birlikda The first noun is usually singular

Birinchi ot sifat vazifasini bajaradi. Ingliz tilida sifat ko‘plikka o‘tmaydi (tall boys — “tall” ga -s qo‘shilmaydi). Xuddi shunday, noun + noun da birinchi ot birlikda qoladi — ma’no ko‘plik bo‘lsa ham: poyabzal do‘koni ko‘p poyabzal sotadi, lekin u a shoe shop.

Qanday yoziladi va qanday o‘qiladi? One word, two words, stress

Noun + noun uch xil yozilishi mumkin: ikki so‘z (tennis ball, bus driver, city centre), bir so‘z (headache, toothbrush, bedroom, newspaper) yoki ba’zan defis bilan. Yagona qoida yo‘q: juda tez-tez ishlatiladigan va qisqa so‘zlar odatda bir so‘z bo‘lib qoladi, qolganlari ikki so‘z. Ishonchingiz komil bo‘lmasa, lug‘atga qarang.

Urg‘u (stress) odatda birinchi so‘zga tushadi: TENnis ball, BUS driver, HEADache. Sifat + ot ibora bilan solishtiring: GREENhouse (issiqxona — o‘simlik uchun bino) va a green HOUSE (yashil rangli uy).

Bir so‘z yoki ikki so‘z bo‘lishi gapning ma’nosini o‘zgartirmaydi, lekin imlo xatosi bo‘lishi mumkin. O‘quvchilar ko‘pincha bus-driver yoki busdriver deb yozadi — to‘g‘risi bus driver.

Vaqt uchun yana bir yo‘l bor: It’s ten minutes’ walk from here — bu yerda ko‘plik ot bilan ’ (apostrof) ishlatiladi (Unit 81). Ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri: a two-week holiday = two weeks’ holiday.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

Qaysi ot asosiy so‘z ekanini qanday bilaman?

Har doim oxirgi (o‘ngdagi) ot. Savol bering: “Bu nima?” A bus driver — haydovchi. A driver bus deb bo‘lmaydi, chunki o‘ngdagi so‘z aniqlovchi emas, asosiy so‘z bo‘lishi kerak. A/an va ko‘plik shakli ham shu so‘zga qarab belgilanadi: two bus drivers.

Nega “shoe shop” deymiz, o‘zi ko‘p poyabzal sotadi-ku?

Chunki birinchi ot sifat vazifasini bajaradi, sifat esa inglizchada ko‘plikka o‘tmaydi (tall boys, tall ga -s qo‘shilmaydi). Shuning uchun a shoe shop, a flower shop, a vegetable garden. Istisnolar: sports, clothes, savings, customs, greetings kabi so‘zlar (B bo‘lim).

“A ten-minute walk” va “ten minutes’ walk” ning farqi bormi?

Ma’nosi bir xil. A ten-minute walk — son + birlik ot (sifat). Ten minutes’ walk — egalik shakli (Unit 81). Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri, lekin a ten minutes walk ❌ — bunday aralash shakl xato.

Defisni har doim qo‘yish kerakmi?

Rasmiy yozuvda ha: a ten-minute walk, a six-year-old boy. Sizga ba’zan defissiz yozuv ham uchraydi (a ten minute walk), lekin eng muhimi — o‘lchov so‘zi birlikda bo‘lishi. Imtihonda defis bilan yozing.

“Bir so‘z”, “ikki so‘z” yoki defis — qanday bilaman?

Yagona qoida yo‘q. Juda tez-tez ishlatiladigan, qisqa va ma’nosi “yangi” bo‘lib qolgan so‘zlar bir so‘z bo‘ladi (headache, bedroom, toothbrush). Ko‘pchiligi ikki so‘z (tennis ball, bus driver, city centre). Lug‘atga qarang; ikki variantni ham uchratsangiz, ikki so‘zli yozuv ham ko‘pincha qabul qilinadi.

“I have toothache” desam bo‘ladimi, yoki “a toothache”?

Britaniya ingliz tilida ko‘pincha a siz: I’ve got toothache / earache / backache. Amerika ingliz tilida odatda a bilan: I have a toothache. Headache bilan esa a doim ishlatiladi: I have a headache. Ishonchsiz bo‘lsangiz, a qo‘shing — hamma joyda to‘g‘ri bo‘ladi.

“A cup of coffee” va “a coffee cup” ni nega farqlash kerak?

Chunki ma’no boshqa. A coffee cup — qahva ichiladigan chashka (bo‘sh ham bo‘lishi mumkin). A cup of coffee — chashkadagi qahva (siz uni ichasiz). Shishani, qadahni, kosani ham shunday farqlang: a wine bottle va a bottle of wine.

Nega “a swimming pool” da ikkinchi ot suzmayapti?

Chunki swimming bu yerda sifatdosh emas, ot (suzish): pool for swimming. Buni sinab ko‘rish oson: ikkinchi ot o‘sha ishni qiladimi? Hovuz suzmaydi — demak maqsad (swimming pool). Chaqaloq uxlaydi — demak harakat (a sleeping baby).

🧠 Esda tuting

  • Noun + noun: asosiy so‘z — o‘ngdagi ot; birinchi ot uning turi, maqsadi yoki materialini bildiradi: a tennis ball, a bus driver, a cheese sandwich.
  • Birinchi ot odatda birlikda: a shoe shop (✅), a shoes shop (❌). Istisno: sports, clothes, savings, customs, greetings, jeans, glasses.
  • A / an va ko‘plik -s asosiy so‘zga qarab: two tennis balls.
  • -ing li ot maqsad bildiradi: a swimming pool, a washing machine. Harakatni bildiruvchi -ing sifat: a sleeping baby.
  • Son + ot + ot: a ten-minute walk, a two-hour journey — ot birlikda, defis bilan. Oddiy gapda: The walk takes ten minutes.
  • A cup of coffee (ichidagi narsa) ≠ a coffee cup (idishning turi).
  • Bir so‘z / ikki so‘z: yagona qoida yo‘q — lug‘atga qarang.

Bog‘liq mavzular: Unit 79 (Birlik va ko‘plik: a trouser pocket, a jeans shop), Unit 81 (-’s va of: ten minutes’ walk, the roof of the house), Unit 98 (-ing va -ed sifatlar: boring / bored), Unit 99 (Sifatlar tartibi: a nice new house).
