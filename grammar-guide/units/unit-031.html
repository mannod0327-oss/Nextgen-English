Have to and must

Have to va must — majburiyat
Modals · Modal fe’llar

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • have to bilan majburiyatni ifodalashni: I have to work on Saturday.
  • have to ning barcha zamonlardagi shakllarini (had to, will have to, have had to) va do / does / did bilan so‘roq-inkorni.
  • must va have to qachon bir xil, qachon farqli ekanini.
  • mustn’t (taqiqlangan!) va don’t have to (shart emas) — butunlay boshqa-boshqa narsa ekanini.
  • have got to shaklini.

I have to do something = “buni qilishim shart, majburman, qilmasam bo‘lmaydi”. must ham shunga o‘xshaydi, lekin u ko‘proq so‘zlovchining o‘z fikrini bildiradi, have to esa ko‘pincha tashqi qoida yoki faktni.

O‘zbek tilida majburiyat “-ishim kerak”, “-ishim shart”, “majburman”, “-ishga to‘g‘ri keladi” bilan beriladi: Ertaga erta turishim kerak = I have to get up early tomorrow. Eng katta tuzoq — inkor: o‘zbekcha “kerak emas” ikki xil tarjima qilinadi: mustn’t (“qilmang, mumkin emas”) yoki don’t have to (“shart emas”). Bu farqni C bo‘limda ko‘ramiz.

Have to: majburiyat va uning barcha shakllari I have to do = it is necessary, I am obliged to do it

  • I can’t meet you tonight. I have to finish this report.Bugun kechqurun siz bilan uchrasha olmayman. Bu hisobotni tugatishim kerak.
  • In our school, students have to wear a uniform.Bizning maktabda o‘quvchilar forma kiyishi shart.
  • Bekzod has to get up at five — his work starts at six.Bekzod soat beshda turishi kerak — ishi oltida boshlanadi.
  • Our flight was cancelled, so we had to spend the night at the airport.Reysimiz bekor qilindi, shuning uchun tunni aeroportda o‘tkazishga majbur bo‘ldik.
  • Since we moved next to the metro, I haven’t had to drive to work.Metro yoniga ko‘chib o‘tganimizdan beri ishga mashinada borishimga to‘g‘ri kelmadi.
have to — zamonlar bo‘yicha
ZamonShaklMisol
Present simplehave to / has toShe has to work late today.
U bugun kech ishlashi kerak.
Past simplehad toWe had to wait for an hour.
Bir soat kutishga majbur bo‘ldik.
Futurewill have toThe car is broken. We’ll have to take the bus.
Mashina buzuq. Avtobusga chiqishimizga to‘g‘ri keladi.

Mashinamni sotishimga to‘g‘ri keladi.

U safarini bekor qilishga majbur bo‘ldi.
Modal bilanmay / might have toYou might have to wait a bit.
Biroz kutishingizga to‘g‘ri kelishi mumkin.

So‘roq va inkor: do / does / did

have to modal fe’l emas, oddiy fe’l kabi ishlaydi. Shuning uchun present simple va past simple da so‘roq va inkor do / does / did bilan yasaladi:

Do / Does / Did+ega+have to+fe’l ...?
✅ To‘g‘ri❌ Noto‘g‘ri
So‘roqDo you have to work on Saturdays?Have you to work ...?
So‘roqWhat does Aziz have to do?What has Aziz to do?
InkorNodira doesn’t have to cook today.Nodira hasn’t to cook.
O‘tgan zamonWhy did Sardor have to go back to Bukhara?Why had Sardor to go ...?
O‘tgan zamonWe didn’t have to pay for the tickets.We hadn’t to pay.

Talaffuz: have to odatda /ˈhæftə/, has to esa /ˈhæstə/ deb aytiladi — “v” tovushi “f” ga, “z” tovushi “s” ga aylanadi.

Must va have to: farqi nimada? must = my opinion; have to = a fact or an outside rule

Ko‘p hollarda must va have to ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri:

  • The shop closes at six. I must hurry. / I have to hurry.Do‘kon oltida yopiladi. Shoshilishim kerak.
  • My English is getting worse. I must practise more. / I have to practise more.Ingliz tilim yomonlashyapti. Ko‘proq mashq qilishim kerak.
  • You must try the samsa in this café — it’s delicious! / You have to try ...Bu kafedagi somsani albatta tatib ko‘ring — juda mazali!

Lekin ma’noda nozik farq bor:

must — so‘zlovchining fikri

Men o‘zim buni zarur deb hisoblayman, men o‘zimga yoki sizga “buyuraman”, tavsiya qilaman.

  • I must stop eating so much sugar. (o‘zim shunday qaror qildim)
  • You must come and see us in Samarkand! (chin dildan taklif)

have to — fakt, tashqi majburiyat

Qoida, ish, vaziyat meni majbur qiladi. Men shunchaki faktni aytyapman.

  • I have to start work at 7.45. (ish tartibi)
  • Doctors often have to work at night. (kasb talabi)
  • In my job I have to speak English every day.

Yozma qoida va ko‘rsatmalarda — must

Rasmiy qoidalar, e’lonlar, yo‘riqnomalarda ko‘pincha must ishlatiladi:

  • (on a sign) Passengers must show their tickets to the driver.(e’lon) Yo‘lovchilar chiptalarini haydovchiga ko‘rsatishi shart.
  • (exam instructions) Mobile phones must be switched off.(imtihon qoidasi) Mobil telefonlar o‘chirib qo‘yilishi shart.
  • Library books must be returned within two weeks.Kutubxona kitoblari ikki hafta ichida qaytarilishi shart.

O‘tmish uchun must ishlatilmaydi — had to

must ning o‘tgan zamon shakli yo‘q. O‘tmishdagi majburiyat uchun har doim had to:

  • The lift wasn’t working, so we had to walk up to the tenth floor. (not we must walk)Lift ishlamayotgan edi, shuning uchun o‘ninchi qavatga piyoda chiqishga majbur bo‘ldik.
must yoki have to: qisqa jadval
Holatmusthave to
O‘z fikrim, tavsiya✅ I must call her.✅ I have to call her.
Fakt, ish tartibi, boshqalarning qoidasikam✅ She has to wear a uniform.
Yozma qoida, e’lon✅ Visitors must ...mumkin
O‘tgan zamon❌ (yo‘q)✅ had to
Kelasi zamon✅ I must go tomorrow.✅ I’ll have to go tomorrow.

mustn’t va don’t have to — butunlay boshqa ma’no! mustn’t ≠ don’t have to

Bu darsdagi eng muhim farq. Ikkalasida ham “not” bor, lekin ma’nolari qarama-qarshi:

You mustn’t do something = buni qilmang (taqiq)

  • You mustn’t park here — it’s a fire exit.Bu yerga mashina qo‘yish mumkin emas — bu yong‘indan chiqish yo‘li.
  • Students mustn’t use their phones during the exam.Imtihon paytida talabalar telefondan foydalanishi mumkin emas.
  • I mustn’t forget Mum’s birthday on Friday.Juma kuni oyimning tug‘ilgan kunini unutmasligim kerak.

You don’t have to do something = shart emas (lekin qilsangiz ham bo‘ladi)

  • You don’t have to bring anything. We’ve got plenty of food.Hech narsa olib kelishingiz shart emas. Ovqatimiz yetarli.
  • Tomorrow is a holiday, so I don’t have to set an alarm.Ertaga bayram, shuning uchun budilnik qo‘yishim shart emas.
  • Kamola doesn’t have to come to the meeting, but she can if she wants to.Kamola majlisga kelishi shart emas, lekin xohlasa kelishi mumkin.

O‘zbekcha sinab ko‘ring: “-ma!”, “mumkin emas”, “-maslik kerak” → mustn’t. “shart emas”, “hojati yo‘q”, “majbur emassiz” → don’t have to. “Kerak emas” desangiz — o‘zingizdan so‘rang: bu taqiqmi yoki shunchaki zarurat yo‘qmi?

have got to = have to I’ve got to = I have to

Britaniya ingliz tilida, ayniqsa og‘zaki nutqda, have to o‘rniga have got to ham ishlatiladi. Ma’nosi bir xil:

  • I’ve got to finish this by Friday. (= I have to finish this by Friday.)Buni jumagacha tugatishim kerak.
  • What time have you got to be at the airport? (= What time do you have to be at the airport?)Aeroportda soat nechada bo‘lishingiz kerak?
  • We haven’t got to pay for parking here. (= We don’t have to pay ...)Bu yerda to‘xtash joyi uchun to‘lashimiz shart emas.
have tohave got to
DarakI have to go.I’ve got to go.
InkorI don’t have to go.I haven’t got to go.
So‘roqDo you have to go?Have you got to go?

have got to asosan hozirgi zamonda va bir martalik vaziyatlarda ishlatiladi. Takrorlanadigan majburiyat uchun ko‘proq have to qo‘llanadi: I have to get up at six every day. O‘tmish uchun esa doim had to. Amerika og‘zaki nutqida I’ve got to ko‘pincha I gotta deb talaffuz qilinadi — lekin uni yozma ishda ishlatmang.

⚠️ Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar Common mistakes

I must went to the bank yesterday.I had to go to the bank yesterday.must ning o‘tgan zamoni yo‘q. O‘tmish uchun: had to + fe’l.
Do you must work tomorrow?Do you have to work tomorrow?“do” faqat have to bilan ishlatiladi. Must bilan so‘roq: Must you ...? (kam ishlatiladi).
She have to wear glasses.She has to wear glasses.he / she / it bilan: has to.
He doesn’t has to come.He doesn’t have to come.doesn’t dan keyin fe’l asl shaklda: have.
You don’t have to smoke here! It’s a hospital.You mustn’t smoke here! It’s a hospital.Bu taqiq — “mumkin emas”. Don’t have to esa “shart emas” degani.
You mustn’t come if you’re busy.You don’t have to come if you’re busy.Bu taqiq emas, “shart emas, ixtiyoriy” — don’t have to.
I must to finish this today.I must finish this today.must dan keyin “to” yo‘q. (have to da esa “to” bor!)
I will must work late tomorrow.I will have to work late tomorrow. / I must work late tomorrow.will + must birga kelmaydi. Kelajak uchun: will have to.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

must va have to ni har doim almashtirsa bo‘ladimi?

Hozirgi va kelasi zamonda ko‘pincha ha: I must go = I have to go. Lekin: (1) o‘tmishda faqat had to; (2) kelasi zamonning boshqa shakllarida, modallardan keyin faqat have to (will have to, might have to); (3) faktlar, ish tartibi haqida have to tabiiyroq; (4) inkorda ma’no butunlay o‘zgaradi: mustn’t ≠ don’t have to.

Nega “must” ning o‘tgan zamoni yo‘q?

must o‘zgarmaydi: uning can → could kabi o‘tgan zamon juftligi yo‘q. Shuning uchun o‘tmishdagi majburiyatni have to ning o‘tgan zamoni — had to bilan beramiz. Eslatma: must have done ham bor, lekin u majburiyat emas, xulosa (Unit 28): He must have forgotten = U unutgan bo‘lsa kerak.

O‘zbekchadagi “kerak emas” ni qanday tarjima qilaman?

Ma’noga qarang. Bu yerda chekish kerak emas (= chekmang) → You mustn’t smoke here. Ertaga kelishing kerak emas (= kelmasang ham bo‘ladi) → You don’t have to come tomorrow. Birinchisi — taqiq, ikkinchisi — zarurat yo‘qligi.

“Must I ...?” deb so‘rasa bo‘ladimi?

Grammatik jihatdan mumkin, lekin bu rasmiy yoki biroz eski uslub: Must I sign here? Kundalik nutqda deyarli har doim Do I have to ...? deyiladi: Do I have to sign here? Ba’zan Must you ...? norozilik bildiradi: Must you play that music so loudly? (Musiqani shunchalik baland qo‘yish shartmi?!)

have to va have got to — qaysi birini ishlatay?

Ikkalasi ham to‘g‘ri. have to — universal (barcha zamonlar, Britaniya va Amerika). have got to — og‘zaki, ko‘proq Britaniyada, asosan hozirgi zamonda. Agar ikkilansangiz, have to ni tanlang — u hamma joyda to‘g‘ri.

need to va have to farqi bormi?

Juda o‘xshash: I need to buy some bread = I have to buy some bread. need to ko‘proq “menga bu kerak, zarur” degan shaxsiy ehtiyojni, have to esa majburiyatni bildiradi. Inkorda: You don’t need to come = You don’t have to come (shart emas). Batafsil — Unit 32.

“I have to” ni “I must” bilan almashtirsam, ma’no qattiqroq bo‘ladimi?

Biroz. must so‘zlovchining o‘z irodasini his qildiradi va rasmiyroq, kuchliroq eshitilishi mumkin: You must be here at 8 (o‘qituvchi talab qilyapti). have to neytralroq: You have to be here at 8 (shunday qoida). Kundalik nutqda have to ko‘proq ishlatiladi.

✍️ Mashqlar Practice

1-mashq. Bo‘sh joyga have to / has to / had to / will have to dan mosini qo‘ying.

  1. Bekzod is a doctor. He sometimes work at night.
  2. The museum was closed yesterday, so we come back today.
  3. My car is at the garage, so tomorrow I take the bus.
  4. In our school, all students wear a uniform.
  5. Malika leave early last night because she felt ill.
  6. We’ve missed the last train. We take a taxi.

2-mashq. Qavs ichidagi so‘zlar bilan so‘roq yoki inkor gap tuzing (do / does / did ishlating).

  1. (you / have to / work / on Saturdays?) →
  2. (Aziz / not / have to / wear a suit) →
  3. (why / Kamola / have to / leave early yesterday?) →
  4. (we / not / have to / pay / last time) →

3-mashq. To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang.

  1. Last week Jasur (must / had to) work late every day.
  2. (sign in a museum) Visitors (must not / don’t have to) touch the paintings.
  3. It’s Dilnoza’s birthday today. I (must / had to) call her tonight.
  4. When I was a child, I (must / had to) help my parents in the garden every weekend.

4-mashq. mustn’t yoki don’t / doesn’t have to ni qo‘ying.

  1. You open the oven door while the cake is baking.
  2. Tomorrow is Sunday, so I go to work.
  3. Passengers smoke on the plane.
  4. Bekzod wear a suit at work. He can wear jeans.
  5. We forget to buy bread on the way home.
  6. You pay for the museum. It’s free on Mondays.

5-mashq. Ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

  1. Kecha men ishni kech tugatishga majbur bo‘ldim. →
  2. Bu yerda telefonda gaplashish mumkin emas. →
  3. Menga sovg‘a olib kelishing shart emas. →
  4. U (ayol) har kuni ingliz tilida gaplashishi kerak (ishida). →

🧠 Esda tuting

  • have to = majburiyat. Barcha zamonlarda: has to, had to, will have to, have had to.
  • So‘roq / inkor: Do you have to ...? / She doesn’t have to ... / Did you have to ...?
  • must — o‘z fikrim, tavsiya, yozma qoidalar; have to — fakt, tashqi majburiyat.
  • must ning o‘tgan zamoni yo‘q → had to.
  • mustn’t = qilmang, mumkin emas. don’t have to = shart emas, ixtiyoriy.
  • have got to = have to (og‘zaki, BrE): I’ve got to go.
