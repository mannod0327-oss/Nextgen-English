Passive 3

Majhul nisbat — 3
Passive · Majhul nisbat (Passive)

🎯 Bu darsda nimani o‘rganasiz?

  • Ikki to‘ldiruvchili fe’llar (give, offer, tell, show, send, pay, teach, ask ...) dan majhul nisbat yasashni: I was offered a job. / A job was offered to me.
  • Qaysi shakl tabiiyroq ekanini va nima uchun ❌ I was explained the rule deb bo‘lmasligini.
  • be born iborasini: I was born in Samarkand.
  • get + past participle (get passive): Did anybody get hurt? My bag got stolen.
  • Qisqacha: being + V3 (I don’t like being photographed) va predlogli fe’llarning passive shakli (He hates being shouted at).

Unit 42 va 43 da majhul nisbat be + V3 ekanini o‘rgandingiz. Bu darsda uchta alohida holatni ko‘ramiz: (1) ikki to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lsa, qaysi biri ega bo‘ladi; (2) be born — har doim passive; (3) get ni be o‘rnida ishlatish.

O‘zbek tilida “Menga ish taklif qilindi” gapida ega — ish, menga esa jo‘nalish kelishigidagi to‘ldiruvchi bo‘lib qoladi; men ega bo‘la olmaydi. Ingliz tilida esa “kimga” bo‘lgan odam ham ega bo‘la oladi: I was offered a job (so‘zma-so‘z “men ish taklif qilindim”). Aynan shu shakl ingliz tilida ko‘proq ishlatiladi. Tug‘ilmoq fe’li esa o‘zbekchada ham majhul: tug‘- (bola tug‘moq) + -il → tug‘il-moq. Shuning uchun inglizcha was born ham majhul nisbatda — bu ikki tilda bir xil mantiq.

I was offered a job ... — ikki to‘ldiruvchili fe’llar Verbs with two objects

Ba’zi fe’llardan keyin ikkita to‘ldiruvchi keladi: odam (kimga?) va narsa (nimani?). Bunday fe’llar: give, offer, lend, send, show, tell, pay, teach, ask, promise, award, hand. Aniq nisbatda gap shunday bo‘ladi:

  • They offered Bekzod a job in Dubai.Ular Bekzodga Dubayda ish taklif qilishdi.
  • They offered a job in Dubai to Bekzod.Ular Dubaydagi ishni Bekzodga taklif qilishdi. (xuddi shu ma’no, to bilan)

Shuning uchun majhul nisbatda ikki xil gap yasash mumkin. Ikkala to‘ldiruvchi ham ega bo‘la oladi:

Bitta gap — uch shakl
ShaklGapDiqqat qaratilgan narsa
ActiveThey gave Malika a gold medal.kim bergan
Passive 1 (odam ega)Malika was given a gold medal.Malika — odatda asosiy mavzu
Passive 2 (narsa ega)A gold medal was given to Malika.medal

Qoida: Ikki to‘ldiruvchili fe’ldan majhul gap yasaganda, odatda odam ega bo‘ladi: I was given a key, She was offered a place. Narsa ega bo‘lsa, odamdan oldin to kerak: A key was given to me. Ikkala gap ham to‘g‘ri, lekin birinchisi tabiiyroq (chunki odatda “men bilan nima bo‘ldi” haqida gapiramiz).

Yaxshi bilish kerak: buy, make, cook, find kabi fe’llarda “kim uchun” for bilan beriladi (She made a cake for me). Majhul nisbatda odatda narsa ega bo‘ladi: A cake was made for me. Bunday fe’llarda odamni ega qilish (I was made a cake) tabiiy eshitilmaydi.

explain, describe, suggest, say, mention, announce fe’llaridan keyin odam doim to bilan keladi, shuning uchun odam ega bo‘la olmaydi:
The teacher explained the rule to us. → The rule was explained to us. ✅
❌ We were explained the rule.   ❌ I was suggested a hotel. → A hotel was suggested to me. ✅

Odam + that-gap yoki to + fe’l

tell, warn, advise kabi fe’llardan keyin odam ega bo‘lib, undan keyin that-gap yoki to + fe’l kelishi mumkin; ask va allow dan keyin odatda to + fe’l keladi:

  • We were told that the flight was delayed.Bizga reys kechikkani aytildi.
  • The students were asked to wait outside.O‘quvchilardan tashqarida kutib turish so‘raldi.
  • I was told to fill in a form.Menga anketani to‘ldirishni aytishdi.

O‘zbekchada “Menga aytildi”, “Bizdan so‘rashdi” kabi gaplarda odam jo‘nalish yoki chiqish kelishigida turadi. Inglizchada esa odam ega bo‘ladi: Menga aytishdi → I was told. Gapni o‘girishdan oldin “kimga?” bo‘lgan so‘zni ega qilib qo‘ying.

I was born ... — be born The verb “be born”

born bu yerda majhul nisbat: “tug‘ilmoq” ingliz tilida “dunyoga keltirildi” deb aytiladi. Odamning tug‘ilishi o‘tmishda bo‘lgan, shuning uchun oddiy gapda was / were born (past simple passive) ishlatiladi.

Ega+was / were+born+in / on / at+joy yoki vaqt
  • I was born in Bukhara in 2005.Men 2005-yili Buxoroda tug‘ilganman.
  • Where were you born?Siz qayerda tug‘ilgansiz?
  • When was your sister born?Singlingiz qachon tug‘ilgan?
  • Alisher Navoi was born in Herat in 1441.Alisher Navoiy 1441-yili Hirotda tug‘ilgan.
  • My parents were born in Fergana, but I was born in Tashkent.Ota-onam Farg‘onada tug‘ilgan, men esa Toshkentda tug‘ilganman.
  • Most babies are born in hospitals nowadays.Hozir ko‘pchilik bolalar tug‘ruqxonada tug‘iladi. (umumiy fakt — present simple)
be born: shakllar
Qachon?ShaklMisol

O‘zbekcha “Men Toshkentda tug‘ilganman” gapida tugallangan zamon (-gan) ishlatiladi, chunki biz hozirgi holat haqida gapiramiz. Inglizcha esa doim o‘tgan zamon: I was born in Tashkent ✅. I am born, I have been born, I born — hammasi ❌. Savolda ham: Where did you born? ❌ — Where were you born? ✅.

get + past participle — get passive Did anybody get hurt? My bag got stolen.

Og‘zaki nutqda be o‘rniga ko‘pincha get ishlatiladi: get / got + V3. Bunda ko‘pincha kutilmagan yoki yoqimsiz voqea sodir bo‘lgani aytiladi:

Ega+get / gets / got / getting+V3
  • My bag got stolen on the bus yesterday.Sumkam kecha avtobusda o‘g‘irlanib ketdi.
  • After the match, two fans got arrested.O‘yindan keyin ikki muxlis hibsga olindi.
  • Did anybody get hurt in the accident?Avtohalokatda kimdir jarohat oldimi?
  • The window got broken during the storm.Deraza bo‘ron paytida sindi.
  • Why do I never get chosen for the team?Nega meni hech qachon jamoaga tanlashmaydi?
  • Dilnoza got promoted last month.Dilnozaning lavozimi o‘tgan oy ko‘tarildi.

be + V3

  • neytral, yozma va rasmiy uslubda ham ishlatiladi
  • holatni ham, voqeani ham bildiradi
  • When I got home, the window was broken. (oyna singan edi — holat)

get + V3

  • asosan og‘zaki, norasmiy uslub
  • voqea sodir bo‘lishini (ko‘pincha tasodifan) bildiradi
  • The window got broken last night. (oyna kecha singan — voqea)

get passive faqat harakat-voqea bildiruvchi fe’llar bilan keladi. Holat fe’llari (want, like, love, need, know, own ...) bilan ishlatilmaydi: The suspect is wanted by the police ✅ — The suspect gets wanted by the police ❌.

Inkor va so‘roq: do / does / did kerak

be-passive da so‘roq va inkor be orqali yasaladi. get-passive da esa get oddiy fe’l, shuning uchun present simple va past simple da do / does / did kerak bo‘ladi:

be passiveget passive
Was anybody hurt?Did anybody get hurt?
The vase wasn’t broken.The vase didn’t get broken.
How was the window broken?How did the window get broken?
Nobody was injured.Nobody got injured.

get + V3 — passive bo‘lmagan iboralar

Ba’zi get + V3 iboralari aslida o‘zining ishini qilishni yoki o‘zida bo‘ladigan o‘zgarishni bildiradi (majhul emas). Ularni iboraga o‘xshab yodlash kerak:

IboraMisolO‘zbekcha
get married (to)Aziz and Nodira are getting married in June.Aziz va Nodira iyun oyida turmush qurishadi.
get divorcedThey got divorced last year.Ular o‘tgan yili ajrashishgan.
get dressedI got dressed and left the house.Kiyinib, uydan chiqdim.
get changedI must get changed before the party.Ziyofatdan oldin kiyimimni almashtirishim kerak.
get lostWe got lost in the old city.Eski shaharda adashib qoldik.
get stuckThe car got stuck in the mud.Mashina loyga botib qoldi.

get + sifat (get tired, get angry, get better) — bu boshqa narsa, u Unit 1 da ko‘rilgan o‘zgarish ma’nosi. Shuningdek get something done (I got my car repaired) — boshqa tuzilma: ishni boshqaga qildirish. U Unit 46 da o‘rganiladi.

being + V3 va predlogli fe’llarning passive shakli being done; verb + preposition in the passive

being + V3: -ing shaklining majhul ko‘rinishi

Ba’zi fe’llardan (like, hate, enjoy, remember, avoid) va predloglardan (without, after, before) keyin fe’l -ing shaklida keladi. Agar ega ish ta’sirini o‘zi oladigan bo‘lsa, -ing shaklining majhul ko‘rinishi — being + V3 ishlatiladi (batafsil — Unit 43):

  • Kamola doesn’t like being photographed.Kamola suratga olinishini yoqtirmaydi.
  • The children enjoyed being given so much attention.Bolalarga shuncha e’tibor berilgani ularga yoqdi.
  • Nobody can enter the building without being checked.Hech kim tekshiruvdan o‘tmasdan binoga kira olmaydi.

Predlogli fe’llar

Predlogli fe’llarda (look after, shout at, laugh at, talk about, deal with) predlog fe’l bilan birga qoladi va V3 dan keyin turadi. Bu Unit 43 da ham ko‘rilgan:

ActivePassive
Her grandmother looked after the baby.The baby was looked after by her grandmother.
The coach shouted at the players.The players were shouted at.
Everybody laughed at his joke.His joke was laughed at by everybody.
We must deal with this problem.This problem must be dealt with.
  • I hate being shouted at.Menga baqirishlarini yomon ko‘raman.
  • Nobody likes being laughed at.Hech kim ustidan kulinishini yoqtirmaydi.

Predlogni tushirib qoldirmang: The baby was looked after ✅ — The baby was looked ❌.

❓ Savol-javob Questions students often ask

“I was given a key” va “A key was given to me” — qaysi birini ishlatay?

Ikkalasi to‘g‘ri. Gapda kim haqida gapirayotgan bo‘lsangiz, o‘sha ega bo‘ladi. Ko‘pincha mavzu — odam (men, u, biz), shuning uchun I was given a key tabiiyroq. Agar asosiy e’tibor narsada bo‘lsa (masalan, kalit, hujjat yoki sovg‘a haqida gap ketsa), ikkinchi shakl ishlatiladi: The keys were given to the new manager.

Nega “I was explained the rule” noto‘g‘ri? Axir “I was told the news” to‘g‘ri-ku?

Fe’llar har xil tuzilishga ega. tell, give, show, teach kabi fe’llardan keyin odam to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri keladi (told me the news), shuning uchun u ega bo‘la oladi. explain, describe, suggest, say, mention fe’llaridan keyin odam faqat to bilan keladi (explained the rule to me). Odam ega bo‘la olmaydi. To‘g‘ri variant: The rule was explained to me.

“by” ni har doim yozish kerakmi?

Yo‘q. by faqat ishni kim bajargani muhim bo‘lsa yoziladi: Malika was given a medal by the president. Bajaruvchi noma’lum, ahamiyatsiz yoki ko‘rinib turgan bo‘lsa, tushirib qoldiriladi: I was offered a job.

“I was born” ni nega o‘tgan zamonda aytamiz, axir men hali tirikman?

Chunki tug‘ilish voqeasi o‘tmishda yuz bergan va tugagan. Hozirgi holat (“men tug‘ilganman”) inglizchada ham o‘tgan voqea orqali beriladi. O‘zbekchadagi tug‘ilganman shakli (-gan) inglizchada present perfect bo‘lmaydi: I have been born ❌. Faqat was born. Umumiy faktlar uchun esa hozirgi zamon mumkin: Most babies are born without teeth.

get-passive ni imtihonda yoki rasmiy xatda ishlatsam bo‘ladimi?

Rasmiy yozuvda (ariza, maqola, esse) be + V3 ishlating: Two men were arrested. get + V3 — og‘zaki, norasmiy: Two men got arrested. Imtihonning gapirish qismida get-passive ishlatish tabiiy yangraydi.

“get married”, “be married” va “marry” orasida qanday farq bor?

get married — voqea (to‘y qilish, turmush qurish): They got married in 2015. be married — holat: They have been married for ten years. marry — voqea, lekin predlogsiz va ko‘pincha yozma uslubda: She married Tom. / She got married to Tom. “Married with” deb aytilmaydi.

“My phone got stolen” va “I had my phone stolen” bir xilmi?

Ma’nosi deyarli bir xil, lekin tuzilishi boshqa. My phone got stolen — majhul nisbat: ega — telefon. I had my phone stolen — Unit 46 dagi have something done tuzilmasi: ega — men, “telefonim o‘g‘irlanib, men shunday holatda qoldim” degan ta’kid bor. Oddiy gapirishda eng tabiiysi: My phone got stolen yoki My phone was stolen.

🧠 Esda tuting

  • give, offer, lend, send, show, tell, pay, teach, ask, promise dan ikki xil passive: I was offered a job. (odam ega, ko‘proq ishlatiladi) / A job was offered to me. (narsa ega, to bilan).
  • explain, describe, suggest, say, mention, announce bilan odam ega bo‘lmaydi: The rule was explained to me ✅, I was explained the rule ❌.
  • be born — har doim majhul, odatda was / were born: Where were you born? (I am born, I born ❌). die esa majhul bo‘lmaydi: He died.
  • get + V3 — norasmiy, voqea (ko‘pincha kutilmagan) uchun: My bag got stolen. Did anybody get hurt?
  • get-passive da inkor va so‘roq do / does / did bilan tuziladi: Did anybody get hurt?
  • Holat fe’llari bilan get-passive ishlatilmaydi: is wanted ✅, gets wanted ❌.
  • get married (to), get dressed, get lost — iboralar; married with ❌.
